# Issue 1210: `Lie(GL_n) = gl_n`, the field `toMatrixLieHom.map_smul'`

Every declaration listed in the issue is now proved except the field `map_smul'` of
`AlgebraicGeometry.GeneralLinearGroup.toMatrixLieHom`, which is still `sorry`. Its statement is
true, but the statements that are currently available do not imply it.

## Why it does not follow

`toMatrix` is `Lie G → infPoints G → tangentGroup G → gl_n`. Here `lieEquiv` is the bridge `toLie`,
and the `k`-module structure on `Lie G` is the one on `Hom(ω_{G/S}, 𝒪_S)`. The only statement that
connects the module structure of `Lie G` with infinitesimal points is `lieEquiv_mul`, which handles
addition. No statement handles scalars.

The Lie structure does not determine the scalar action either. Additivity, bijectivity and
compatibility with brackets are proved here. Together with `lie_smul`, they only show that
`X ↦ toMatrix (c • toMatrix⁻¹ X)` commutes with every `ad A`. For `n = 1`, and in general on the
centre (`X ↦ tr(X) • 1`), many additive maps have that property.

## Proposed missing lemma (in `GroupScheme/Lie.lean`, section `Ring`)

```lean
theorem GrpObj.infPointsEquivTangentGroup_lieEquiv_symm_smul (c : R) (x : Lie G) :
    (infPointsEquivTangentGroup G ((lieEquiv G).symm (c • x)) : points G (DualNumber R)) =
      points.map G (DualNumber.scale c)
        (infPointsEquivTangentGroup G ((lieEquiv G).symm x))
```

This says that the bridge turns `ε ↦ cε` into multiplication by `c`. `DualNumber.scale` is in
`WeilConjectures/Mathlib/Algebra/DualNumber.lean`. The proof scales the derivation `infDerivation`
by `c`, then uses that `desc`, `sheafificationHomEquiv` and the adjunction are linear.

Given the lemma, `map_smul'` takes a few lines: `homMulEquiv_specMap_comp` turns `points.map`
into `Matrix.GeneralLinearGroup.map`, and `DualNumber.snd_scale` multiplies the `ε`-part by `c`.
