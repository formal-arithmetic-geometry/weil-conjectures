# Issue 1200: `sk-p-tensor` and `sk-p-exterior` stay `sorry`

This PR proves five of the seven declarations: `detWeight_mul_of_mem_ker`,
`detWeight_eq_of_isPositiveCentralPair`, and the three `SemidirectProduct` lemmas.
The two statements below cannot be proved as the branch states them. They are left `sorry`.
slopmaster proves both, but only after restating them.

## `Representation.exists_detWeight_eq_add_of_subquotient_tprod` (`sk-p-tensor`) is false

The conclusion asks for simple constituents `SV / UV` of `V` and `SE / UE` of `E` that have
finite geometric determinant. Nothing in the hypotheses makes this possible.

Counterexample: take `k = ℚ` (or `ℚ̄_ℓ`), `W = ℤ × ℤ` with `deg` the second projection, and
`P = ℤ × 0`. Let `V = k` with `(p, m) ↦ 2 ^ p` and `E = k` with `(p, m) ↦ 2 ^ (-p)`.
- `V ⊗ E` is trivial on `P`, so `Q = ⊤ / ⊥` is simple with finite geometric determinant.
- The only simple constituents of `V` and `E` are `V` and `E` themselves. Their determinants on
  `P` are `2 ^ (±p)`, an infinite set.

So no witnesses exist.

Proposed statement (slopmaster's): add `[IsAlgClosed k] [CharZero k]`, and assume finite geometric
determinant for every simple constituent of `V` and of `E`:
```
(hdetV : ∀ UV SV : Subrepresentation rV, IsIrreducible (SV.subquotient UV) →
  (SV.subquotient UV).HasFiniteGeometricDet deg)
(hdetE : ∀ UE SE : Subrepresentation rE, IsIrreducible (SE.subquotient UE) →
  (SE.subquotient UE).HasFiniteGeometricDet deg)
```
`hUS` is not needed. The proof on slopmaster then reads the weights off a positive central pair
for the semisimple datum `V_i ⊕ E_j`, transported along the Kronecker square.

## `Representation.exists_multiset_le_and_detWeight_eq_sum_of_exteriorPower` (`sk-p-exterior`)

The branch states this over an arbitrary field. The only available route is the blueprint's: read
every weight off a positive central pair `(N, z)`. That route needs
`RepresentedDegreeDatum.exists_isPositiveCentralPair`, which assumes `[IsAlgClosed k] [CharZero k]`.
It also needs Schur's lemma in its scalar form, which needs `k` algebraically closed.

I don't have a counterexample for a general field. Still, base change to `k̄` doesn't obviously
work, because the geometric constituents of the `V_j` can have infinite geometric determinant.

Proposed statement (slopmaster's): add `[IsAlgClosed k] [CharZero k]`, with nothing else changed.
The ported proof also needs these slopmaster helpers:
- `DegreeDatum.exists_ker_eq_and_eq_mul_zpow`, `RepresentedDegreeDatum.map` and
  `IsPositiveCentralPair.map` in `Monodromy.lean`;
- `exists_algHom_comp_eq_evalAlgHom_exteriorPower` in `GeneralLinearGroup/ExteriorPower.lean`;
- `exteriorPower.exists_eq_prod_of_hasEigenvalue_map`;
- the `Subrepresentation` API.
