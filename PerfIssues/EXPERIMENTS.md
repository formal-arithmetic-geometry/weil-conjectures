their original module identity and generated Lake setup, one Lean thread,
synchronous elaboration, ordinary Kernel checking, `nice -n 10`, and a
3600-second process bound. Times inside a probe are `IO.monoMsNow` intervals
around `Kernel.isDefEq` or `Kernel.check`. A row's wall/CPU/RSS belongs to
the whole invocation where known; `n/m` means not measured separately.
Diagnostic Kernel skip and `sorry` runs are never counted as proof success.
