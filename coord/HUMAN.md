For non-Noetherian `A` this is false: `A = ∏_ℕ 𝔽₂`, `I = 0`, `F = x_* ℤ/2` for a non-principal ultrafilter point `x` (closed, not open), `q = 0`, `c = 1`: a subsheaf of `F` with `1` in its `H^0` is all of `F`, whose support `{x}` is not constructible, so `F` is not constructible (unless the defective `IsConstructibleSheaf` of the earlier entry makes it so, which is that defect again).
