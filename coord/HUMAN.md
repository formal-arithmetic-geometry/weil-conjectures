False without flatness: for $D=V(x)\subset\mathbb A^1_k$ and $g$ the inclusion of the origin, $g^*\mathcal I_D\cong k$ while $\mathcal I_{D_Y}=0$ (found by the worker of lem-pc-divisor-line-bundle-properties). 01JY states only that immersions are stable under base change; the ideal sheaf is the image of $g^*\mathcal I_Z\to\mathcal O_{X'}$ (01JU (1)), equal to $g^*\mathcal I_Z$ when $g$ is flat. The node's statement is corrected to the image form with the flat case; its nine citers are flagged in its lead note. The blueprint's own uses (the $\kappa(x)$-point case, $X_K$) are flat base changes and unaffected.

## Blueprint statement error: thm-galois-fundamental, base-change clause (2026-10-10, found by the worker)

The blueprint's base-change clause says that for every connected $Y'\to Y$ the image of $\pi_1(Y')\to\pi_1(Y)$ is an open subgroup.
