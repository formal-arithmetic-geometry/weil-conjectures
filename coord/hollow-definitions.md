## Inventory and scope

The available diagnostics and original sources give 74 candidates in 43 modules. Z2 replaced 23 candidate definitions: 21 across its 11 fully repaired sweep modules and two in its partial FieldLinear delivery. The remaining inventory is **51 definitions in 32 modules**, not the supplied 45. Omitting every candidate in a Z2 delivery module instead gives 47 in 31. The discrepancy cannot be resolved by dropping names without evidence; all 51 are recorded below. Additional source-detected choices are recorded separately.

Users counts exclude the defining module and count source modules mentioning the distinctive declaration name; common names were checked against qualified uses/imports. They count callers, not their transitive import closure. Size is the estimate for replacing the choice: small confines the change to one module, medium has up to ten callers to adapt, large requires a refactor. Shared infrastructure work can exceed the apparent zero-caller size of one wrapper.

All Z2 delivery modules remain protected, including their untouched `SingleComplexBaseChange.counit`; no repair note or queue entry is added to them. A class-2 normalization defect can require work in shared upstream infrastructure before a wrapper is repaired.
