# Ledger

One line per change that needs a reason: date, node or file, what changed, why.

- 2026-10-08, dag/nodes: imported from the blueprint (`literature/blueprint/master/blueprint`, master 967897d1) by `tools/import_blueprint.py`; 2,943 files gave 2,320 nodes (558 definitions, 1,762 statements) and 623 notes. Nothing was written by hand.
- 2026-10-08, kinds: `definition` stays; `theorem` and `lemma` become `statement`; `thm-weil-final` is the one target. The blueprint kind is kept in `blueprint_kind`.
- 2026-10-08, remarks: the 220 files of kind `remark` are not propositions or definitions, so 218 of them are kept verbatim in `dag/notes/` and are not nodes; the 39 dependency edges that pointed at one are dropped and listed in `dag/notes/dropped_edges.tsv`, and the citing node carries `cites_remark`.
- 2026-10-08, thm-weil-pbc-stein-inputs, thm-weil-pbc-henselian-idempotent-lifting: remarks tagged `latex:openproblem` that node proofs cite as their input; kept as statements with no proof (`open_problem`), because dropping the edge would hide an unproved input.
- 2026-10-08, sections: the 405 files of kind `section` (16 chapters, 389 sketch blocks `sk-*`) are kept verbatim in `dag/notes/`; no node cites or uses one, so no edge is lost.
- 2026-10-08, versions: in the 86 files with `## Detailed form` the last version is the node's statement and proof; earlier versions are verbatim in `blueprint_sketch` and give no dependencies. Three files have two detailed forms (`multiple_detailed_forms`).
- 2026-10-08, embedded statements: nine files carry further `## <title>` blocks (sub-statements with their own proofs, one remark); they are kept in place as steps of the node's proof (`embedded_statements`), not made into nodes.
- 2026-10-08, dependencies: `stmt_deps` are the ids cited in the statement, a step's `uses` the ids cited in the step; an id cited in the text counts whether or not the frontmatter `uses` has it (`cites_outside_uses`). A frontmatter `uses` entry the text does not cite is kept and listed in `uses_not_cited`: as a statement dependency when the node or the entry is a definition, otherwise in `proof_deps` without a step. A `uses` list of more than 20 entries is not trusted: its uncited entries are dropped (`padded_uses`). The frontmatter list is always in `blueprint_uses`.
- 2026-10-08, self-citations: ten nodes cite their own id (files where sub-statements were merged under one id); the self-edge is dropped, listed in `dag/notes/dropped_edges.tsv`, flagged `self_citation`.
- 2026-10-08, cycles: 21 cycles (117 nodes, flag `in_cycle`) are left in the graph. The frontmatter `uses` graph has none; every cycle contains an edge that only the text gives (a forward pointer such as "proved in [lemma] below"). Which of these edges are pointers and not dependencies is a judgment for stage 1.
- 2026-10-08, steps: a proof is cut at its labelled paragraphs when it has at least two, else at the items of a numbered list, else at paragraphs; a displayed formula stays with its sentence. `elementary` is `false` for a step that cites a node and `null` otherwise: nobody has judged those steps.
- 2026-10-08, proofs that are references: a proof that opens by declaring itself a recorded input, says it is not discharged, or is "Omitted" is not a proof: `proof` is empty and the text is in `blueprint_proof_text` (`undischarged_input`, `citation_only`). The same is done when one step of a longer proof rests on a recorded input (`partial_undischarged_input`, 12 nodes); the cut steps are in `blueprint_proof_steps` and their citations stay in `proof_deps`.
- 2026-10-08, bypassed nodes: the 108 nodes whose status paragraph says "Bypassed by the formalized route" stay ordinary nodes with the flag `bypassed`; none is merged, because each names Lean declarations as its replacement and not a node with the same statement.
- 2026-10-08, meta paragraphs: paragraphs opening with `**Status.**`, `*Source:*`, `*Source note:*`, `*Recorded in advance*`, `*Mathlib:*`, `*Implementation note.*`, `Uses:` or `Unresolved dependencies:` are about the node, not part of its statement or proof; they are in `blueprint_notes` and give no dependencies (the ids of a `Uses:` line are treated as frontmatter `uses`).
- 2026-10-08, Mathlib leaves: seven nodes, those whose statement has the blueprint's own `*Mathlib:*` paragraph and whose named declarations were all found in the local Mathlib checkout (0df444a); each lookup is in the node's `mathlib_searches`. No other search was made, so every other node has no match.
- 2026-10-08, lean: a node has `lean` only when a declaration of its blueprint `lean` list was found in the source text of the repository (branch `master` first, then `slopmaster2`); `sorry_free` says the text of that declaration contains no `sorry`, nothing more. Names not found are in `blueprint_lean_not_found`.
