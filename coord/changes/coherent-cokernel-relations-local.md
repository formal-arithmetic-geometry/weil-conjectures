# AlgebraicGeometry.RingedSpace.coherent_cokernel_relations_local

## Reason

Repair of reviewer X-6acd0, head 6acd0cac5, point 4. Stacks Project, Modules,
Lemma 01BY first shrinks the original open before lifting sections and choosing
image generators. The old fixed-U assertion is false: on P¹_k take F = I_p ≅
O(-1), G = O, U = X, n = 1 and g the quotient onto k(p). Maps O^m → O are rows
of constants and their kernels are free; no free sheaf can surject onto O(-1),
since its global sections vanish. The corrected statement allows a neighbourhood
V ≤ U around every x. Empty U is vacuous and rank zero is allowed.

The only direct declaration user is Sheaf.AlgebraicGeometry.CoherentCokernelRelations
(unclaimed). Its existing theorem signature and visibility stay identical. No
target signature or definition changes. All downstream signatures retain their
meaning. The original proof fails against the corrected signature with:

```
CoherentCokernelRelations.lean:48:9: error: Tactic `rcases` failed:
`x✝ : ∀ x ∈ U, ∃ V, ∃ (hVU : V ≤ U), x ∈ V ∧ ∃ m ψ e, Epi e`
is not an inductive datatype
```

The forced changes are two proof-only `import all` lines for existing private
locality and transport helpers, and replacement of the old proof's fixed-U
unpacking and epimorphism application (original lines 48–52) by lines 50–92:
choose one neighbourhood for each point, verify the cover in Over U, use hG.2
and sheaf_finiteType_of_epi there, identify overMap with iterated restriction,
and use finiteType_of_cover. No other Lean lines are changed. The adapted file
compiles against the changed dependencies. The downstream node's proof metadata
is updated to this route; its statement and Lean signature are unchanged.
