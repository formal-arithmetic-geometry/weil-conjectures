# CategoryTheory.brownHypothesesFor_coefficientIso_iff and brownHypotheses_of_finiteFieldDatum

## Reason

The general Brown criteria imported field-specific compactness theory only for two reasons:
the coefficient-isomorphism proof used private helpers from BrownHypothesesGeneration,
and the geometric finite-field specialization lived in the general module. Neither is
needed by the general criteria or by the original disputed theorem. Replace the coefficient
compactness argument with the existing adopted-source compact-object adjunction theorem,
and move the supported specialization to its own module. Preserve the original disputed
statement and hole; the maintainer rollback explicitly requires keeping them visible.

## Old

The following theorem was declared in Sheaf.SixFunctorsBasics.BrownHypotheses:

```lean
theorem brownHypotheses_of_finiteFieldDatum
    (D : WeilConjectures.FiniteFieldDatum.{u}) (ℓ : ℕ) [Fact ℓ.Prime]
    (hℓ : ℓ ≠ D.p) {X : Scheme.{u}} (aX : X ⟶ Spec (.of D.k))
    [IsSeparated aX] [LocallyOfFiniteType aX] [QuasiCompact aX]
    (Λ : Sheaf (coherentTopology Profinite.{u}) Ab.{u + 1})
    [MonObj Λ] [IsCommMonObj Λ]
    (hΛ : IsLocalizingAdmissibleCoefficient ℓ Λ) :
    BrownHypothesesFor Λ X
```

The coefficient-isomorphism signature was:

```lean
theorem brownHypothesesFor_coefficientIso_iff
    {Λ Λ' : Sheaf (coherentTopology Profinite.{u}) Ab.{u + 1}}
    [MonObj Λ] [MonObj Λ'] [IsCommMonObj Λ] [IsCommMonObj Λ']
    (e : Mon.mk Λ ≅ Mon.mk Λ') (X : Scheme.{u}) :
    BrownHypothesesFor Λ X ↔ BrownHypothesesFor Λ' X
```

Its proof used compactness_of_brown_coefficientIso, which compared ambient canonical
Hom maps through private helpers of Sheaf.Paper.BrownHypothesesGeneration. The module
also directly imported BrownHypothesesArbitraryFieldCompactness for the finite-field
specialization.

## New

Both theorem types are exactly unchanged. The coefficient-isomorphism theorem and its
private proof helpers move to Sheaf.SixFunctorsBasics.BrownHypothesesCoefficientIso; no
current consumer uses its name. The finite-field theorem, with its docstring and
proof unchanged, now belongs to Sheaf.SixFunctorsBasics.BrownHypothesesFiniteField. Its
node's Lean attachment follows it. No live consumer uses its name, so no importer needs an
adaptation to find it. The new module publicly imports the general criterion module and
privately imports the finite-field compactness support.

The coefficient-isomorphism proof restricts the exact derived scalar equivalence to the
localizing full subcategories. Minimality of the localizing intersection gives preservation
in both directions; the unit gives reflection. Compactness transports by the existing
CategoryTheory.BrownRepresentability.IsCompactObj.of_adjunction theorem and the unit
isomorphism. This removes the private ambient-Hom transport helper, the field-specific
proof imports and the optional CondensedCoefficientIsoConstructible import from the general
module. A private, completely proved minimality helper performs the closure step.
Move the public FiniteFieldFiber import to the specialization, whose statement names D.
All remaining public imports are preserved. No source-library file, definition, global
instance, target, theorem signature, or disputed mathematical assertion is changed.
