# IntegralOpenReduction definitions and proof imports

## Reason

The item theorem uses the existing tensor-model and canonical restriction-model lemmas.
They imported the item for its definitions, which makes direct proof imports cyclic.
Move precisely the definition prefix to IntegralOpenReductionDefinitions and publicly
re-export it from the original item. All definition statements and bodies are byte-for-byte
unchanged; a local comparison of the entire declaration prefix verifies this. No choice,
mathematical predicate, or target signature changes. Existing definitions retain their names.

## New

The complete declaration prefix (through Comparison), with unchanged bodies and types, occurs in
`Sheaf.SixFunctors.IntegralOpenReductionDefinitions`. The original item and the three
adapted modules publicly import it:

```lean
public import Sheaf.SixFunctors.IntegralOpenReductionDefinitions
```

The extracted module adds local irreducibility attributes before the large comparison
signatures to avoid expanding concrete category presentations; these attributes do not
change the declarations or their exported attributes.

The item privately imports the tensor model, canonical restriction model,
restriction completeness, reconstruction and two proved bridge modules.
IntegralOpenExtensionComplete uses the proved principal-completion helpers of
CompletedDirectImagePreservesCompleteness and the independent-universe ringed open
product theorem; it has zero sorries. Generic private adjunction helpers in the item
construct the inverse-unit realizations. The item does not need the extra restriction
representative and completion model clauses of SfOpenReductionAdjunctionTransport.
That module is unchanged. The three adapted modules have only the forced import-line
redirect; OpenRestrictionCompleteness is held by E2, the other two are unclaimed.
No source-library file is changed.
