module adjunctions, and the closure-factorization extension. Without it, elaboration

proof elaboration only. Both existing definitions and every existing public signature stay identical. The two
direct consumers obtain the removed broad imports independently. No target changes
meaning and no consumer needs adaptation. New theorem modules are genuine separate argument steps.
