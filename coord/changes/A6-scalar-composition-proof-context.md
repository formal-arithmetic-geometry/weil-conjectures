## Reason

functors from unfolding, and generic tensor/derived/conjugation helpers. These commands
change the syntactic command inventory checked by preflight. All attributes are local;
the new helpers are private and proved. Public signatures, definitions, instances and
public imports retain their existing meaning. No downstream adaptation is needed.
