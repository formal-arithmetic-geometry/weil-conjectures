## Reason

this record. The original signature, universes, binders, attributes and public imports stay
verbatim. Only its proof changes. The new proof combines the geometric degree-zero base case
with a proved two-variable finite-truncation induction. One original hole is replaced by one
strictly smaller geometric hole; this is a decomposition, net zero, not a completed finiteness
proof. No definition or target statement changes.
