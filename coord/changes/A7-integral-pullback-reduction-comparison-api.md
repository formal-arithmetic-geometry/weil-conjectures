## Reason

term. The existing integralPullbackReductionIso keeps its full signature and delegates
to exactly that construction. This prevents elaboration of a beta projection equation
from opening the chosen derived pullback implementations. Local irreducibility attributes
in the proof keep those implementations abstract; no exported reducibility changes.

are unchanged. The target statements and meanings are unchanged.
