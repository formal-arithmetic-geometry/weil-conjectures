# Profinite.CondensedAction.InternalBar free-module data

## Reason

The three declared proof-dependency modules publicly imported the resolution merely for its free-module data. Importing them to prove exists_resolution therefore created cycles. Extract the unchanged data into CondensedInternalBarData and publicly reexport it from the item. Redirect only the dependency imports. No definition body, declaration name, target statement, or Resolution field changes. The extracted data has no postponed witness. All other existing public imports are retained. The remaining two construction lemmas are independent of exactness.
