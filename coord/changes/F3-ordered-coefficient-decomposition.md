# Ordered coefficient comparison module decomposition

## Reason

Separate the comparison interfaces from the final theorem so the strict-tower,
reconstruction and pointwise cancellation steps can be independent stage-3 items.
The final theorem combines three strictly smaller obligations, proves natural
transformation equality by component extensionality, and proves the witnessed
base-change identity by its definition. This is a decomposition, not a net proof
of the coefficient comparison. The earlier rho proof exploration is not delivered.

## Old

All declarations were in Sheaf.Paper.OrderedExchangeCoefficientComparison, which
publicly imported ProEtaleConstantReductionTower and KFlatIndependentCoefficients.
The coefficientComparison proof was sorry. Its signature is unchanged.

## New

All public declarations before coefficientComparison are preserved with identical
signatures and bodies, with only their blueprint attachment changed, in Sheaf.Paper.OrderedExchangeCoefficientData.
The original module publicly imports that data module. Its public declarations,
signatures and definition bodies remain available under exactly the same names.
No additional downstream import is needed. The final theorem imports the three
new proof obligations and combines their results. No upstream source module changes.
The private limit-preservation proof now transports preservation through an explicit
identity natural isomorphism instead of an implicit instance conversion. Its statement
is unchanged; scoped transparency prevents unnecessary unfolding. On the repair
recompile, profiling reached normalized_naturality before the fixed time limit.
The repair additionally scopes respectTransparency and its types variant to true
for this theorem, leaving its statement and proof argument unchanged.
The lambda_existsUnique proof keeps the reviewed argument, locally disables
GrothendieckTopology.hasSheafify_of_essentiallySmall (as completedRlim does),
and sets the two respectTransparency options to true. This prevents the expensive
conversion between different presentations of the sheafification instance.
Its public statement and specified comparison are unchanged. Exploratory private
unit helpers were removed. All five packet modules compile without warnings.
The five auxiliary holes remain under their original names, with their original
natural-language proofs. The definition node records their new Lean attachment.
