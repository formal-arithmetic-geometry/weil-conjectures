# CategoryTheory.BrownHypothesesFor.finite_nonzero_hom_of_degreewise_disjoint

## Reason

Extract and generalize the degreewise-disjoint-complex step of the existing Brown
compactness obstruction, then reuse it in the varying-heart Ext bound. The theorem
allows arbitrary complexes with disjoint degree supports and derived images in L.
All existing statements, definitions, visibility, public imports and instances stay
identical. No target meaning changes and no consumer adaptation is needed.

## Old

The new theorem was absent. The varying-heart Ext bound constructed the cochain
product-coproduct and restricted its universal properties to L inside its own proof.

      DerivedCategory.finiteTorAmplitude _ K.obj)

      (Mod (Sheaf (Scheme.ProEt.topology X) Ab.{u + 1}) (condensedRingObj Λ X)) ℤ)
