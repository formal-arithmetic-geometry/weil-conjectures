# ModuleCat.raw_transport_counit_equation

## Reason

The original non-target helper asserts compatibility for every natural isomorphism.
Replacing a comparison by its negative preserves all its hypotheses and negates only
its left side. With identity scalar changes over the integers and the complex Z[0],
the canonical counits are invertible, so this gives a false sign equation.
Naturality does not select a normalized comparison. The earlier attempt correctly
identified this error but merely moved the same false equation into a new hole.

This repair instead constructs the comparison from the right Kan extension universal
properties, retaining the original counits at every transport step. Every helper and
the corrected theorem are fully proved. No data is chosen from an unproved existence
statement. The existing caller has the identical mathematical statement and signature.
No definition, target statement or public import is changed. The generalized
owned theorem gains an explicit morphism universe; the caller signature is unchanged.

The theorem is generalized in place to arbitrary additive functors between abelian
categories. Its universes u and v now describe arbitrary object and morphism sizes;
the caller retains its original universes and signature. The two functor equivalences
replace ring equivalences, and t provides the ordinary comparison. No ring-square
hypothesis is required beyond t. This is a genuine generalization, rather than a
renamed copy of raw_compatibility.

The three existing private helpers kan_precomp_full, kan_postcomp_equivalence and
derived_equivalence are reused with a proof-only import all RawTransportIso.
Their duplicate declarations and proofs are removed. The two remaining local
helpers have stronger conclusions than their existing existential counterparts:
they retain the specific counit, which is essential for the final equation.

The caller specializes the generic theorem to extendScalars and the two
restrictScalarsEquivalenceOfRingEquiv equivalences. Its original selection of a,
refine and equation application fail against the corrected generic interface; only
the required application and imports are adapted. have _ := hRB prevents a new unused
variable warning while leaving its signature identical. RawTransportIso itself is
unchanged and remains used, so its module and node are not pruned.
