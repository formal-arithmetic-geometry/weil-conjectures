# WeilConjectures.ExceptionalIntegralScalar.TransportCoreData

## Reason

The system reduction theorem quantifies over any `TransportCoreData` while its old
`reductionF` and `reductionG` fields were independent of `realizeF` and `realizeG`.
Consequently the demanded equations were not enforced by the interface. For example,
a comparison can be composed with an automorphism of its target without changing
the realization isomorphism; the equation specifies one particular comparison.
The normalized reconstruction construction instead specifies the comparisons:
whisker the realization isomorphism and compose with the reconstruction counit.

This repair constructs those two comparisons using Mathlib's isomorphism
whiskering and right unitor. It adds no compatibility hypothesis, postponed proof,
or chosen data. The existence theorem's independent fields and construction are
retained, with its two redundant constructor assignments removed. The post-data
packages may still supply comparison fields, but the normalized core no longer
uses them. `TransportCoreData` was unclaimed on the current server claim check.

The source is Zheng, Constructions 4.1 and 5.25, Lemma 5.22 and Proposition 5.26.
The paper's induced operations use the specified comparison maps. The new
component calculations exactly reproduce the equations already required by
`ExceptionalIntegralScalar.Transported`. No target declaration or target
statement definition is changed: this record is internal proof construction data.

## Old

The following were independent fields of `TransportCoreData`:

```lean
  reductionF (E : FieldStage.{u} l) :
    (operation (.integral E)).F ⋙ (reconstruction E false).equivalence.functor ≅
      (reconstruction E true).equivalence.functor ⋙ F E
  reductionG (E : FieldStage.{u} l) :
    (operation (.integral E)).G ⋙ (reconstruction E true).equivalence.functor ≅
      (reconstruction E false).equivalence.functor ⋙ G E
```

Its existence theorem assigned them as follows:

```lean
    reductionF := p.reductionF,
    reductionG := p.reductionG,
```

## New

Remove the two record fields and constructor assignments and provide the following
canonical definitions under the same qualified names and with the same types:

```lean
section ReductionComparisons
variable {l : ℕ} [Fact l.Prime] {Ω : Type u} [Field Ω] [Algebra (Padic l) Ω]
  [IsAlgClosure (Padic l) Ω] {D : FiniteFieldDatum.{u}} {hl : l ≠ D.p}
  {X Y : Scheme.{u}} {f : X ⟶ Y}
  [X.Over (Spec (.of D.k))] [Y.Over (Spec (.of D.k))]
  [LocallyOfFiniteType (X ↘ Spec (.of D.k))] [QuasiCompact (X ↘ Spec (.of D.k))]
  [LocallyOfFiniteType (Y ↘ Spec (.of D.k))] [QuasiCompact (Y ↘ Spec (.of D.k))]
  [f.IsOver (Spec (.of D.k))] [IsSeparated f] [LocallyOfFiniteType f] [QuasiCompact f]
  {c : Compactification f}
  {operation : ∀ s : RingStage.{u} l, Operation.{u} l Ω X Y (s.stage l)}
  {left : ∀ s : RingStage.{u} l, LeftRealization.{u} l Ω c s (operation s)}
  (d : TransportCoreData l Ω D hl c operation left)

/-- The reduction comparison is the realization comparison followed by the target
reconstruction counit. It is constructed from these maps, rather than chosen independently.
Source: Zheng, Constructions 4.1 and 5.25, Lemma 5.22 and Proposition 5.26.
(blueprint: lem-sf-exceptional-ring-transport-core-data) -/
def TransportCoreData.reductionF (E : FieldStage.{u} l) :
    (operation (.integral E)).F ⋙ (d.reconstruction E false).equivalence.functor ≅
      (d.reconstruction E true).equivalence.functor ⋙ d.F E :=
  Functor.isoWhiskerRight (d.realizeF E) (d.reconstruction E false).equivalence.functor ≪≫
    Functor.isoWhiskerLeft ((d.reconstruction E true).equivalence.functor ⋙ d.F E)
      (d.reconstruction E false).equivalence.counitIso ≪≫ Functor.rightUnitor _

/-- The right-adjoint reduction comparison uses the realization comparison and the
source reconstruction counit, with the two endpoints interchanged.
Source: Zheng, Constructions 4.1 and 5.25, Lemma 5.22 and Proposition 5.26.
(blueprint: lem-sf-exceptional-ring-transport-core-data) -/
def TransportCoreData.reductionG (E : FieldStage.{u} l) :
    (operation (.integral E)).G ⋙ (d.reconstruction E true).equivalence.functor ≅
      (d.reconstruction E false).equivalence.functor ⋙ d.G E :=
  Functor.isoWhiskerRight (d.realizeG E) (d.reconstruction E true).equivalence.functor ≪≫
    Functor.isoWhiskerLeft ((d.reconstruction E false).equivalence.functor ⋙ d.G E)
      (d.reconstruction E true).equivalence.counitIso ≪≫ Functor.rightUnitor _

end ReductionComparisons
```

The constructor, recursor, and autogenerated structure field metadata necessarily
reflect the removal of the two redundant fields. Source searches find no external
construction or case analysis of `TransportCoreData`; its sole constructor is in
this module. Existing dot-notation callers continue to use the definitions.
