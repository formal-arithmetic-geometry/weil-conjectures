# sectionModuleEquivalence

## Reason

Projecting the functor of the original equivalence repeatedly expands its full fully-faithful and essentially-surjective construction. Retain that exact construction as `sectionModuleEquivalenceCore` and store the equivalence record with its forward functor explicitly equal to `sectionModuleFunctor`. Every other field is copied. The entire equivalence is definitionally equal to the original one, including its inverse, unit and counit. There is no new choice or axiom. The existing declaration signature and docstring remain verbatim. The module was unclaimed when adapted; no adopted source module is changed. A new helper is attached to the same existing definition node.

    (R : Sheaf J AddCommGrpCat.{u}) [MonObj R] [IsCommMonObj R] :

    ⟨fun M ↦ ⟨liftedSheafModule J R M, ⟨sectionModuleFunctor_counit J R M⟩⟩⟩
