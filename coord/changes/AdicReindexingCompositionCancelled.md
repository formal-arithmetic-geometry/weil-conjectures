# AdicReindexingCompositionCancelled

The original item theorem depended on one monolithic composition proof step. This delivery exposes that step as a finite-stage calculation followed by common-stage descent, with the cancelled-equation proposition in its own module. The item imports those modules and combines their results.

The item declaration keeps the original signature unchanged. The added declarations are `normalized_reindexing_composition_cancelled_equation`, `normalized_reindexing_composition_cancelled_stage`, and `normalized_reindexing_composition_cancelled_descent`; their DAG nodes are included in this delivery. Existing direct importers continue to compile against the unchanged item theorem signature.

Status: open pending global build and review.
