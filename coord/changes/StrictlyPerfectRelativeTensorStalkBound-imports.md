compile without Mathlib.Topology.Sheaves.Abelian. The only direct importer,
Sheaf.AlgebraicGeometry.PerfectRelativeTensorLocallyBounded, imports that Mathlib
module publicly itself and imports this item privately. Thus this removal does
not alter the visible interfaces of the direct importer or any transitive user;
no declaration statement or definition changes and no adaptation is required.

visibility are unchanged. The three new proof imports remain plain imports.
