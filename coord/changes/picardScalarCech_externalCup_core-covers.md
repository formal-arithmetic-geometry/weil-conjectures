# AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.picardScalarCech_externalCup_core

## Reason

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the former core omitted the cover equalities, although the
public enclosing theorem already assumed them. IsModuleCechComparison fixes the
composite with the resolution-to-Cech-total comparison, so a family that does not
cover leaves extensions outside its union unconstrained. Both cover equalities
are required by the ordinary external-pairing compatibility used in the proof.
This repairs a below-unit helper. It changes no definition or Challenge target.

The supplied route audit gives the corrected counterexample: X=Y=P¹_k, with two
rational points x₀,x₁; singleton U=V=P¹_k minus x₁; F=G=k(x₀)⊕k(x₁). The global
structure rings are k. Factor comparison extensions s↦(s,0) and a product
extension s↦(s,s,s,s) satisfy the visible restriction equations. At degree zero,
the cup of the factor extensions of 1 is (1,0,0,0), while the product extension is
(1,1,1,1). This is the corrected G3 scheme example supplied in the route note;
it has not been fully formalized as a Lean scheme counterexample. The earlier
disjoint Spec(k×k) example does not establish the claim: its idempotents constrain
linearity. The corrected core has a complete Lean proof from the existing
ordinary-pairing and scalar/module-comparison theorems.
