# AlgebraicGeometry.ProEtale.CondensedRing.IntegralPullbackIsCompleted

## Reason

The existing theorem interfaces use canonical intrinsic derived completeness, whereas
this module independently encoded a cone-product triangle using a representing complex.
Use the existing notion instead of maintaining a second representation of completion.
The theorem signature is unchanged, and its mathematical conclusion is unchanged.
This is an interface repair, not a claim that the two Lean definitions are definitionally equal.

The equivalence is as follows. Put H = Lg* K. The pulled-back coefficient square is
cartesian and module inverse image is exact. It carries the two-term free resolution
[O_E --ell^(n+1)--> O_E] to the same resolution on X. It therefore identifies the
localized pulled-back cone reductions with H tensor^L O_E/ell^(n+1), including their
cone-inclusion units and (ell,1) transition squares. On the pro-etale site countable
products are exact, so the product mapping fiber computes their derived limit.
The old distinguished-triangle condition says exactly that the canonical completion
unit of H is invertible: the first arrow has all its prescribed quotient projections.
A morphism of its triangle with the Roos triangle is the identity on the two product
vertices, so its comparison at H is invertible by two-out-of-three for triangle maps.

Choose a generator pi of the maximal ideal, possible because O_E is a principal
valuation ring. The ideals (pi^n) and (ell^n) are cofinal: ell is a unit times a positive
power of pi. Hence their canonical derived completion units agree under the cofinal
comparison. Conversely a generator always exists, so the new existential neither
adds a hypothesis nor drops a case. The quotient kernel of
completedCoefficientMap X (completedReduction ell E pi 0) is the ideal generated by
pi on the completed coefficient sheaf. The principal Koszul local-completeness
criterion (Stacks 0999, 0A0E), and its unit-preserving quotient/Roos comparison,
identify the existing IntegralModelsEmbedded.completedComplete predicate with
invertibility of this same completion unit. The exact equivalence with the small
ringed site preserves the scalar, products, cones and quotient maps. Restriction
along constant O_E to completed O_E is exact, preserves products, and reflects
isomorphisms, so the result is precisely the completed Stacks inverse image after
restriction of scalars. No freely chosen comparison map is substituted for its unit.

The replacement works for every g and K in the definition, including unbounded K;
no constructibility or scheme finiteness assumption is inserted into the definition.
Zero K, empty schemes and identity g use the same criterion. The theorem later uses
integral_models clause (2) only to prove this condition for bounded constructible H.
