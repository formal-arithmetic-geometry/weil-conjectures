# WeilConjectures.purNormalizedReconstructionOfScheme

## Reason

The assigned reflection item duplicates the complete normalized reconstruction proof because
`purNormalizedReconstruction` has an unused scheme invertibility binder. Expose the generality
already proved by that argument. The existing theorem keeps exactly its signature and docstring
and becomes an application of the generalized theorem. No downstream statement or definition
changes. This is reuse of an existing complete proof; no postponed reconstruction claim is added.
The reconstruction module is currently unclaimed.

```lean
theorem purNormalizedReconstruction

    (∀ (N : coefficientSequenceDerived l E W), IsNormalizedCoefficientSequence l E W N →
      IntegralOnSlice.IsComplete l E W none ((coefficientSequenceLimit l E W).obj N) ∧
        IsIso (S.adjunction.counit.app N)) ∧
    (∀ (K : DerivedCategory (scalarReductionSource l E W)),
      IntegralOnSlice.IsComplete l E W none K → IsIso (S.adjunction.unit.app K))
```

## New

The old signature stays verbatim, with proof `exact purNormalizedReconstructionOfScheme l E W S`.
The additional theorem is:

```lean
theorem purNormalizedReconstructionOfScheme
    (S : CoefficientDerivedSystemCalculus l E W) :
    (∀ (N : coefficientSequenceDerived l E W), IsNormalizedCoefficientSequence l E W N →
      IntegralOnSlice.IsComplete l E W none ((coefficientSequenceLimit l E W).obj N) ∧
        IsIso (S.adjunction.counit.app N)) ∧
    (∀ (K : DerivedCategory (scalarReductionSource l E W)),
      IntegralOnSlice.IsComplete l E W none K → IsIso (S.adjunction.unit.app K))
```

Its proof is the original complete reconstruction proof, verbatim. All definitions and
all public imports in that module stay unchanged. No target statement changes.
