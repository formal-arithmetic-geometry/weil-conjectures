## Review by Q43

I checked B–E with the item, the existing dependency statements and the sources open. The relative-nerve formula in Haugseng Definitions 4.2/4.4 and Lemma 4.8 gives exactly the ordinary total category in the item: a covariant transition H(f), followed by a fibre arrow. I checked the order of composition against `(g,w)(f,v)=(gf,w H(g)v)`. The corresponding marked relative-nerve theorem and HTT 3.2.5.20–21 apply to diagrams of arbitrary quasicategories, not only spaces or model categories. Thus they apply to the ordinary nerves here. I did not use Haugseng's total-localization result to assume what the item explicitly does not assume about q.

1. Step 1 cites `lem-cbc-marked-relative-nerve-quillen` for the actual subset-indexed simplex formula, not just the existence of a right adjoint. The inverse formulas recover all arrows and impose exactly the triple composition law. I checked face and degeneracy restriction, including identity arrows. This is the elementary coordinate calculation explaining Haugseng Lemma 4.8, not a newly named node.
2. Step 2 uses `lem-coef-coherent-nerve-comparison` for the nerve of a discrete enriched category being the ordinary nerve and being a quasicategory; `lem-coef-marked-point-cartesian-model` for fibrancy of the natural marking; and `lem-cbc-marked-relative-nerve-quillen` for objectwise projective fibrancy, the cocartesian marking and the derived classification comparison. That node explicitly states the identity fibre identification and naturality. These are the clauses needed here, not an identification inferred from its proof.
3. Step 3 uses `lem-coef-cocartesian-straightening` for its intrinsic mapping-square criterion and its equivalence on the category of cocartesian-preserving functors. An arbitrary cocartesian edge has fibre factor an isomorphism by steps 1–2. The displayed factorization and the mapping-square argument show why preserving the chosen lifts suffices; the hypothesis does not already say q preserves all lifts. No total-category localization is used.
4. Step 4 uses the system and mapping-space definitions in `lem-coef-system-localization-universal`, and the concrete coherent-nerve model of Cat_infinity from `lem-coef-marked-point-cartesian-model`. Each q_c inverts W_c by the given factorization. The other diagram arrows preserve the respective systems. The system-preserving mapping spaces are unions of whole components, so the existing higher coherence simplices remain in them. This is a check of the definition, not an unproved rule that arbitrary fibrewise functors assemble.
5. Step 5 uses `lem-cbc-enhanced-functor-cone-calculus` for actual functor quasicategories, and the specified L-adjoint-G from `lem-coef-system-localization-universal`. The unit, counit and both triangle homotopies are whiskered by K. This formal substitution proves the functor-category adjunct data rather than citing the ordinary Mathlib whiskering declaration as an infinity theorem.
6. Step 6 applies the exact statement of `lem-coef-infinity-adjunction-mapping-equivalence`: coherent functors with transformations satisfying both triangles yield an equivalence of full mapping spaces with the displayed inverse formula. Step 5 supplies those hypotheses. The systems-localization node identifies its unit with the desired localization units. I checked the direction: beta is epsilon_D composed with L(theta^W), and its adjunct is G(beta) composed with eta_A. Forgetting systems therefore gives beta composed with lambda, as required.
7. Step 7 cites the full functor-category restriction equivalence from `lem-coef-system-localization-universal` at target P_c. Both beta_c and J_c have the same domain, the localization of NH(c). Their specified restrictions are naturally equivalent; full faithfulness lifts that equivalence and its inverse and makes the retained lift space contractible. It is not enough here to cite only a fully faithful embedding of objects.
8. Step 8 uses `lem-coef-infinity-isofibration-exponentiation`, whose statement gives restriction isofibrations for every degreewise inclusion, and `lem-coef-marked-point-cartesian-model` for Cat_infinity as a quasicategory. The specified boundary B includes both full endpoint diagrams and all vertex intervals. The component comparison paths have fixed endpoints, so they agree with the identity on the intersections. Lifting their equivalence supplies a representative with components exactly J_c and keeps the comparison to theta. This is a direct application to the displayed inclusion, not an assumption that independently specified J_c form a natural transformation.
9. Step 9 uses the componentwise equivalence criterion in `lem-cbc-enhanced-functor-cone-calculus` and the inverse equivalence and fibre/base-change compatibility in `lem-coef-cocartesian-straightening`. This verifies an equivalence over NC and the required localized transport. Restricting the same transformation to every base simplex verifies compatibility for composites, rather than merely naming it natural.
10. Step 10 uses the mapping-space equivalence of the adjunct lemma, full faithfulness of fibre localization, and contractibility of classification with its equivalence from the straightening node. I checked that the homotopy-fibre descriptions retain the restriction homotopies. Otherwise the claimed space of choices could contain automorphisms and need not be contractible. The formulas are natural in the diagrams because they are the indicated functors and coherent units/counits, not separately selected inverses on fibres.

Hypotheses and small cases: I tested C empty, C terminal, a one-arrow category with an empty source fibre, W consisting only of isomorphisms, and W consisting of all arrows. For terminal C this is exactly uniqueness of a functor out of a localization with its restriction retained. For W equal to isomorphisms the localization is equivalent to NH(c), so beta recovers the original classifier map. For W all arrows it is the corresponding groupoid completion; the proof uses only the actual inverting property. An empty source fibre causes no choice of an object; an empty target fibre forces every incoming ordinary fibre to be empty when a transition exists, as already required by H. Universes are enlarged to contain K, the system category, Cat_infinity and their functor categories before applying the adjunct lemma, so no small-index result is used on an uncontained shape.
