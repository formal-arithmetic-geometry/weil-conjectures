## Supporting argument for the new statement-only lemma

This argument is recorded here for the future worker, not as a proof in the unsplit node. On the vertex set take the equivalence relation generated by unordered endpoint pairs. Choose a root in each nonempty class. For each vertex v choose a finite edge path from its root to v and let c_v be its signed integral edge sum; for a root take the empty path. Such paths exist by the definition of the generated relation, and telescoping gives boundary(c_v)=b_v-b_root(v). Set r(b_v)=c_v and extend linearly. Since s(e) and t(e) are in the same class, boundary(r(boundary(b_e)))=(b_t-b_root)-(b_s-b_root)=boundary(b_e). Equality on all standard vectors proves boundary r boundary=boundary. The empty graph uses the zero map. No acyclicity, chosen spanning-tree theorem, freeness of a quotient or rational division is needed. This is a genuine integral-lattice lemma with no coefficient group A, quotient, characters or equivariance clause; it is strictly smaller than the item. In particular it is not a restatement of one of the item's clauses.
