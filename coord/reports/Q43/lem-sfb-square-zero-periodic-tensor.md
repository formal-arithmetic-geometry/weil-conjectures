In each degree i<=0 put the coefficient object, put zero above zero, use multiplication by t in negative degrees and the quotient augmentation at zero. Check d squared is zero and the augmentation kills the last image. In each negative degree, the kernel/image hypothesis gives equality of boundaries and cycles. In degree zero the quotient map is an epimorphism with kernel image(t). Thus H^0 is the cokernel and all other cohomology is zero. For ordinary R the terms have the specified basis 1, so this is the exact free resolution, with its actual differential and actual quotient augmentation.
2. The flatness and K-flatness portion of old step 2 and all of old step 5 go to the same first node. For every module sheaf M the maps O(U) tensor M(U) -> M(U), a tensor m -> am, and m -> 1 tensor m are inverse: a tensor m = 1 tensor am. They commute with restrictions, so sheafification gives the unit tensor isomorphism. Tensor with O is the identity and tensor with zero is zero; both are exact, hence O and zero are flat. P is zero above degree zero. Apply the already existing bounded-above flat K-flat criterion (lem-pc-bounded-above-flat-kflat-site, or lem-pc-module-bounded-flat-kflat for ordinary modules). No global projectivity of the unit sheaf is claimed.
3. The tensor calculation in old step 2, old step 3, and the computational portion of old step 6 go to lem-sfb-periodic-tensor-normal-form. Since M[0] has only degree zero, in total degree i there is just one potentially nonzero summand P^i tensor M. In nonpositive degrees the explicit unit maps identify this summand with M, and in positive degrees it is zero. On a local tensor a tensor m the first differential becomes ta m = a(tm) = 0; the second differential is zero since M[0] has zero differential, so its sign contributes nothing. The inverse component maps are m -> 1 tensor m. Check both componentwise inverse identities, compatibility with the zero differentials, and naturality by f(am)=a f(m). This produces a chain isomorphism, not just a list of abstract term isomorphisms. Every degree -q has kernel M and incoming image zero, including degree zero where the outgoing target vanishes. Positive degrees vanish. There is no convergence question, infinite-sum rearrangement or boundedness assumption on the left tail.
