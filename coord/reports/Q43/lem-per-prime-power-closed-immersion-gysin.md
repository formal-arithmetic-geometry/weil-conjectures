I opened the statement of every cited node. For the seven steps I checked the following matches and hypotheses.

1. `lem-weil-pbc-flat-morphism-affine-criterion` gives ring flatness on the affine schemes; `lem-weil-pbc-regular-map-ascent` gives regularity when the source is regular, the target Noetherian and the fibres regular; `lem-weil-pbc-regular-base-change` gives permanence under finite-type ring base change. Here the base-change map is the quotient R to R/I, which is generated as an algebra by the empty set, so its finite-type hypothesis really holds. Geometric regularity implies ordinary fibre regularity by using the identity field extension. I read Stacks Tag 07C1 (`lemma-regular-base-change`) and the local-ascent source Tag 031E (`lemma-flat-over-regular-with-regular-fibre`). Applying ascent again to R/I to S/IS proves regularity of Z', with Noetherianity from the quotient of S. No excellence assumption occurs here.

2. `lem-weil-pbc-flat-tensor-exact` explicitly preserves injections after tensoring. Applying it to multiplication by each successive regular generator gives an actual pulled-back regular sequence wherever the final quotient is nonzero. This is not deduced just from the definition of flatness. `lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-conormal-flat-base-change` states that the canonical conormal map is invertible in a flat Cartesian square, and `lem-weil-pbc-regular-sequence-conormal-free` states that the generator classes form a basis. These are the precise rank and codimension comparisons used. Their sources are Tag 0473 (`lemma-conormal-functorial-flat`) and Tag 00LN (`lemma-regular-quasi-regular`, degree one). Empty upstairs support has no point at which to demand nonzero quotient or a codimension value.

3. `lem-asd-ordinary-supported-kummer` states pullback compatibility for every divisor square whose pullback is still Cartier; step 2 verifies that hypothesis for each successive flag inclusion. It does not require intermediate schemes to be regular. `lem-per-regular-immersion-class-calculus` explicitly expresses the prime-power class by the successive Cartier maps and fixes the sign; `lem-asd-etale-finite-twist-pullback` explicitly supplies the twist comparisons and their composition compatibility. The step applies the equalities to each factor of the nested composite and uses functoriality of composition, so it proves compatibility of the indicated map. It does not merely cite a new copy of the item's base-change clause.

4. `lem-per-regular-supported-base-change` states the canonical exchange, its invertibility for regular morphisms, and its compatibility with closed counits. This supplies exactly the displayed counit equation. `lem-per-regular-immersion-class-calculus` supplies the actual class being transposed; `lem-per-absolute-gysin-purity` gives the isomorphism for the regular affine pairs established in step 1, using the same ILO definition. Signed twist pullback is supplied by `lem-asd-etale-finite-twist-pullback`. The adjunction bijection is applied to the two explicit equal transposes. This proves equality after the specified exchange, rather than the existence of some isomorphism between the objects.

Constant-codimension pieces are open and closed, so the same argument handles varying locally constant c.
