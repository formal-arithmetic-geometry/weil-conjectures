## Problems found and corrected

There was no proof in the imported item. The main source trap is that the item uses Bennett's transcendence-degree normalization, while CJS use a component-dependent global normalization; CJS Remark 1.41 explicitly distinguishes them. I did not identify these functions. Steps 2 and 4 convert the actual equalities in the item to raw local equalities, by a dimension formula and injectivity of finite summation. The CJS raw function is the degree-wise Hilbert function, whereas the item uses quotient lengths; one summation, and its injectivity in the reverse direction, reconcile that offset.

CJS Definition 2.1 assumes that the centre contains no component. The item allows components in the centre. Step 6 proves that the local centre prime cannot be minimal when the given near point exists: normal flatness, reducedness, torsion-freeness and Nakayama force I=0 in that case, making the blowup empty. This is a check of an input hypothesis, not an added assumption or a change of statement.

The unrestricted Proj base-change node is used for the residue-field fibre. The flat-only clause of `prop-nagata-relative-proj-functoriality` would not justify that step. Its graded-isomorphism and graded-surjection clauses are used only in the final step.
