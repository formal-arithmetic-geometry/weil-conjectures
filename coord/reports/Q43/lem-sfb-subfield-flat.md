## Proof in words: lem-sfb-condensed-subfield-basis

Source read: main-20261015/blueprint/sec-proetale-coefficients/sk-prop-sfb-cartesian-coefficients/lem-sfb-subfield-flat.proof.md, first two sentences. This is the retained argument, with its continuity justification from the existing Mathlib leaf lem-weil-pbc-finite-dimensional-linear-homeomorphism.

Put k = Q_l. The coordinate map c:F to E^n is E-linear and invertible by the assumed basis; hence it is k-linear and invertible. Both its source and target have Hausdorff topological k-vector-space structures from their induced norms and the finite product topology. The addition and scalar multiplication inequalities ensure continuity of the operations. Its source F is finite-dimensional over k by hypothesis. The field k is complete by def-weil-l-adic-numbers and nontrivially normed since |l| = l^(-1) differs from 1. The exact statement of lem-weil-pbc-finite-dimensional-linear-homeomorphism therefore makes c and its inverse continuous, without any completeness assumption on the algebraic closure. In particular all q_i are continuous.

On each profinite S, postcomposition with q_i and finite linear combinations with the constants b_i define the two displayed maps. They preserve continuous functions, commute with precomposition, and their composites are identities by the two basis equations. They are C(S,E)-linear pointwise. By sk-def-sfb-condensed-ring they are inverse maps of the underlying abelian sheaves. By the sheafified tensor definition sk-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor, those pointwise bilinear action equations are precisely the internal module-morphism equations of sk-def-sfb-module-objects. This proves the asserted internal module isomorphism.

## Proof in words: lem-sfb-monoidal-finite-free-transport

Source read: the same blueprint proof, third sentence, together with the exact transported action in def-sfb-modobj-transport and its blueprint source under sk-def-sfb-transport. The argument is an agent expansion of that sentence.

Write u_i = theta iota_i and v_i = pi_i theta^(-1), so v_i u_j = delta_ij and sum_i u_i v_i = id_M. By def-sfb-modobj-transport, P(u_i) and P(v_i) are linear over P(R); the transported regular R-module has exactly the regular P(R)-action, since that transport formula also defines the multiplication of P(R). Additivity of P carries zero morphisms and finite sums to zero morphisms and finite sums. Functoriality therefore gives P(v_i)P(u_j) = delta_ij and sum_i P(u_i)P(v_i) = id. Define a map from P(R)^n to P(M) by the P(u_i), and a map back by the P(v_i). Their linearity follows coordinatewise from the componentwise regular-module action and additivity of tensor. The two identities just obtained show that they are inverse module morphisms. This also proves the asserted formulas for the comparison and its inverse. For n = 0, the empty sum identity forces M to be zero; its image is zero because an additive functor preserves zero objects (the image identity is the zero endomorphism), and the same equations apply.
