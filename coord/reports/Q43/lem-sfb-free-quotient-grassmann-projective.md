### Task lem-sfb-plucker-determinant-locus-charts

Source: Tags 089T (lemma-gkn-representable, normalized quotient charts), 01N0 (lemma-surjective-graded-rings-map-proj), 01M4 (lemma-standard-open). Agent-written homogeneous-equation and inverse chart-ring completion; extracted from former proof steps 2--4 of lem-sfb-free-quotient-grassmann-projective.

Former step 2:

Introduce degree-one variables $p_J$ for the increasing $r$-subsets of $\{1,\ldots,N\}$. Extend $p_{j_1\ldots j_r}$ to all ordered tuples by the sorting sign, and set it to zero when indices repeat. For increasing $I,K$, put $C_{I,K}(a,b)=p_{i_1\ldots i_{a-1}k_bi_{a+1}\ldots i_r}$ and
$$F_{I,K}=p_I^{r-1}p_K-\det C_{I,K}.$$
These are homogeneous of degree $r$. For any $r\times N$ matrix $M$, substitute $p_J=\det M_J$. The column-replacement identity of [lem-sfb-matrix-cramer-determinant-identities] gives $C_{I,K}=\operatorname{adj}(M_I)M_K$ entry by entry. Its two determinant identities therefore give $\det C_{I,K}=(\det M_I)^{r-1}\det M_K$. Thus every $F_{I,K}$ vanishes on every matrix of maximal minors, over every commutative ring, without cancellation. This equation is the only determinant-reconstruction fact used below; it is proved here from the cited Mathlib identities, rather than made a separate lemma.

Cited statements: lem-sfb-matrix-cramer-determinant-identities, def-weil-pbc-mv-polynomial-ring, def-weil-pbc-matrix-determinant.

Former step 3:

Let $B=\mathbf Z[p_J]$, $H=(F_{I,K}:I,K)$ and $A=B/H$ with the induced grading. Form $X=\operatorname{Proj}(A)$. The degree-preserving surjection $B\twoheadrightarrow A$ gives a closed immersion $j:X\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Proj}(B)=\mathbf P^{\binom Nr-1}_{\mathbf Z}$ by the surjective-map clause of [prop-nagata-relative-proj-functoriality], applied over $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf Z$. The standard opens $U_I=D_+(p_I)$ cover $X$ and have rings $A_{(p_I),0}$ by [prop-nagata-proj-standard-open]. All schemes and equations here are constructed; no existence of a Grassmannian or Hilbert scheme has been assumed.

Cited statements: prop-nagata-relative-proj-functoriality, prop-nagata-proj-standard-open, def-pc-proj, def-weil-pbc-algebra-hom-quotient.

Former step 4:

Here is the full chart-ring calculation. In $A_{(p_I),0}$ set $x_{aj}=p_{i_1\ldots i_{a-1}ji_{a+1}\ldots i_r}/p_I$ for $j\notin I$. Adjoin identity columns at $I$ to obtain an $r\times N$ matrix $M^{(I)}$. Dividing $F_{I,J}=0$ by $p_I^r$ gives
$$p_J/p_I=\det M^{(I)}_J.$$
Every homogeneous fraction of degree zero is a polynomial in these ratios, so the homomorphism $\mathbf Z[x_{aj}]\to A_{(p_I),0}$ is surjective. Conversely take the matrix with identity columns at $I$ and independent polynomial entries elsewhere. Step 2 makes evaluation $p_J\mapsto\det M_J$ annihilate all $F_{K,L}$; it sends $p_I$ to $1$, hence defines a map from the degree-zero localization to $\mathbf Z[x_{aj}]$. On the $x_{aj}$ its composite is the identity, since a single identity-column replacement has determinant equal to that entry. The reverse composite is the identity on every $p_J/p_I$ by the displayed formula. Thus $U_I\cong\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf Z[x_{aj}]$, including its full scheme structure.

Cited statements: def-weil-pbc-mv-polynomial-ring, def-pc-proj, lem-sfb-matrix-cramer-determinant-identities.

Former step 1:

First assume $0<r<N$. For any quotient $q:\mathcal O_T^N\twoheadrightarrow Q$ and increasing $r$-subset $I=(i_1,\ldots,i_r)$, let $T_I$ be the locus where $q(e_{i_1}),\ldots,q(e_{i_r})$ form a frame. In a frame of $Q$ it is the principal open of the corresponding determinant. These opens cover: at each point the images of the $N$ generators span the $r$-dimensional residue vector space, so $r$ of them form a basis. The selected determinant is then a unit in the local ring, hence in a neighbourhood. The adjugate identity supplies the inverse matrix there. On $T_I$ there is consequently a unique matrix $M_I$ for $q$ whose columns indexed by $I$ are the identity; its other $r(N-r)$ entries determine the quotient up to unique quotient-preserving isomorphism. Pullback replaces entries by their images in the new structure sheaf; the pivot identity and its splitting persist.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-matrix-adjugate-identity, def-weil-pbc-matrix-determinant, def-pc-module-pullback.

Former step 5:

On $U_I\cap U_K$, the square matrix $D=(M^{(I)})_K$ has determinant $p_K/p_I$, a unit. By [lem-weil-pbc-matrix-adjugate-identity], $D^{-1}=(\det D)^{-1}\operatorname{adj}(D)$. Determinant multiplicativity gives every $J$-minor of $D^{-1}M^{(I)}$ as $(p_J/p_I)/(p_K/p_I)=p_J/p_K$. Its $K$ columns are the identity, so its entry in row $a$, column $j$ equals the minor obtained by replacing the $a$-th $K$ column by $j$, with the ordered-tuple sign. Step 4 applied at $K$ gives exactly that entry for $M^{(K)}$. Therefore
$$M^{(K)}=D^{-1}M^{(I)}.$$
Write $g_{IK}=D^{-1}$. On a triple overlap both $g_{KL}g_{IK}$ and $g_{IL}$ send $M^{(I)}$ to $M^{(L)}$; applying them to the identity columns at $I$ proves equality. Also $g_{II}=1$. These are actual module transition maps, not an identification of point sets.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-matrix-adjugate-identity, lem-sfb-matrix-cramer-determinant-identities.

Former step 6:

The morphism-gluing clause of the same node gives $q_X:\mathcal O_X^N\to Q_X$. Each local map has its pivot-column right inverse, so it is surjective on sections locally, and $q_X$ is a surjection of sheaves. This constructs the candidate universal quotient with no sheaf-descent assumption left implicit.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-glue-sheaves-of-modules.

Step 7 and the coefficient formulas of Step 9 identify this map, including its line quotient, with the top exterior-power construction on the universal quotient. Consequently it is the required Plücker embedding, and [def-kun-projective] gives H-projectivity.

Former step 12:

For $r=0$ the quotient is necessarily the zero module and has exactly one isomorphism class. For $r=N$, in any local frame the surjection is square; at each point its reduction modulo the maximal ideal is a surjective square matrix, so its determinant is nonzero in the residue field and hence a unit in the local ring. Its adjugate gives a local inverse, and uniqueness glues these inverses. Thus $q$ itself identifies $Q$ with $\mathcal O_T^N$, giving again one quotient class. In both cases $S$ represents the functor, with its evident universal quotient, and arbitrary pullback preserves it. Finally $\mathbf P^0_{\mathbf Z}=\operatorname{Proj}\mathbf Z[t]$ has the single covering chart $D_+(t)=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf Z$, since its degree-zero localization is $\mathbf Z$; hence $\mathbf P^0_S=S$. The identity is a closed immersion, so $S\to S$ is H-projective by [def-kun-projective]. This includes $N=0$, empty bases and empty test schemes.
