## Corrections and other findings

The item's statement agrees with Stacks **0F1J**, `etale-cohomology-lemma-punctual-base-change-upgrade`, and is kept unchanged. I followed the current `main-20261015` statement and separate proof file, not just the old imported proof. The unit remains the ordinary Kunneth unit and no unit record is changed: this item is not its key node.

The imported proof was not a complete split:

1. Its citation `lem-kun-derived-tensor-t-est` asserts a false general lower bound for tensoring arbitrary cohomologically bounded-below complexes. For example, over `R = Z/l^2`, two copies of `R/(l)` in degree zero have nonzero `Tor_1`, so their derived tensor has nonzero degree -1 cohomology. The split removes this citation. It uses an actual K-flat flat-term model of E, proves the stupid tails K-flat by the split sequence, and counts the terms of total tensor complexes. I do not edit the other node.
2. The F reduction concealed both a truncation bound and the unjustified suggestion that a bounded object could simply be replaced by an arbitrary bounded flat model. The split uses a good upper truncation of a bounded-below representative, with quotient supported in degrees at least M; the bounded truncation is decomposed through its individual sheaf terms. F needs no flatness hypothesis.
3. `lem-kun-change-of-rings-tensor` now states a fact about ordinary modules, not sheaves or derived direct images. It cannot alone justify the coefficient step. Step 4 writes the balanced tensor maps as actual sheaf maps, uses the existing coefficient-forgetting comparison, and explicitly cites the bounded-below acyclic-complex computation theorem for the common resolutions.
4. `lem-kun-03sa` is only the finite-type-over-a-field specialization. Step 7 uses the already existing qcqs result `lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaf-colimit-constructible`. The original embedding citation `lemma-weil-pbc-constructible-finite-pushforward` is for abelian groups, not module-linear embeddings. Step 8 uses `lem-weil-pbc-constructible-module-finite-pushforward-embedding`, whose statement gives finite modules V_i. A finite basis over F_l gives finite sums of the coefficient field; I did not claim that its statement directly gives rank-one coefficient modules.
5. `lem-kun-0f1i` now assumes X finite type over K, whereas Stacks 0F1I and this item's proof need arbitrary X_i. Step 5 supplies the missing limit argument inside this item's proof using existing `lem-kun-0ezt`, the Spec-colimit theorem and fibre-product limit theorem. In particular I do not silently assert that a scheme finite over an arbitrary affine K-scheme is finite type over K.
6. The words "dimension shifting" hid the main argument. Step 9 proves injectivity for all degrees first, extends it from constructible to arbitrary sheaves by filtered colimits, and then proves surjectivity by induction on q. This uses the arbitrary quotient C only after the previous assertions have been proved for every sheaf. It neither assumes that C is constructible nor needs a new effacement node. The constructibility-of-P/cokernel citations, including the too narrow `lem-kun-095r`, are unnecessary and removed.
