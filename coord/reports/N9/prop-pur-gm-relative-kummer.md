## Statement correction and source discrepancies

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported final sentence said to take a geometric point as the base change in clause (4), although clause (4) quantified only separated finite-type k-schemes. For example Omega = an algebraic closure of k(t) is algebraically closed but Spec Omega is not of finite type over k, by the field form of Zariski's lemma. The geometric-point morphism into Y = Spec k is nevertheless a legitimate geometric point. I preserved clauses (1)–(4) and replaced the final sentence by an explicit additional geometric-point conclusion, proved by the same argument for arbitrary k-bases. This makes the source's intended specialization well scoped; it imposes no new hypothesis and removes no case. I removed the reference to `thm-pur-kummer-affine-line` from that sentence and from `stmt_deps`, since the proof now states the corresponding computation directly.

STATEMENT-DISPUTED (existing dependency, reported without editing it): `thm-pur-kummer-affine-line` assumes N is prime to char k, rather than assuming ell is different from char k. Within the present conventions, take char k = p, E = Q_ell, and n = p. Then e = 1 and N = p are allowed by the affine-line datum, but the theorem's written condition on N fails. As a standalone theorem its omission of ell != char k also makes it false: take ell = char k = p, N = 1, and A = Z/p. The etale roots-of-unity sheaf mu_p on the smooth reduced curve G_m over an algebraically closed field of characteristic p is zero, so its H^1 is zero, whereas the claimed answer A is nonzero. The appropriate condition is invertibility of ell^N. Steps 4–5 avoid invoking this mis-stated theorem, using exponent r = ell^N in the existing Kummer and affine-cohomology statements. This is the same computation already in the unit's route, not an alternative argument from another unit.

A separate notational error in `def-pur-affine-line-datum` identifies G_m(Y) with H^0(G_{m,Y}, G_m). For Y = Spec k the former is k^times and the latter has the additional t^Z factor. The coordinate is a global unit on G_{m,Y}; this is the object used throughout the split. I did not use that false identification or alter the dependency node.

The divisibility e(E/Q_ell) | n is already expressly required in the definition node `def-pur-affine-line-datum` invoked by the item's statement. Step 1 spells it out rather than inserting it silently. Thus A is free over Z/ell^N and the flat-coefficient alternative explicitly mentioned at the end of the unit's Coefficients paragraph applies. I do not claim to prove a new arbitrary-n extension of that datum. The unit's chapter, statement, source, route, and proof text are untouched.
