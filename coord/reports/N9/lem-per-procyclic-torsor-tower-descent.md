## New node: scope and small cases

The new lemma is purely algebraic/topological: it has a profinite group, a cofinal open-normal sequence, quotient representations N_r and equivariant transition maps. It mentions no schemes, torsors, geometric cohomology, spectral sequence or edge class. Thus it supplies an independent strictly smaller input, not a renamed clause of the item. It is not elementary: it includes simultaneous descent of the arguments and of the coefficients, with a joint finite-level equality argument when coefficient maps fail to be injective. It has a complete self-contained statement and explicit cochain transition maps; its proof is left for its own stage-1 item.

For the future split, the argument is as follows. A continuous cochain from compact G^a to discrete N has finite image and factors through Q_s^a for some s. Choose representatives of its finitely many values in a common N_t with t at least s, assigning them consistently to the Q_s^a fibres. This lifts the cochain at level t. A level-r cochain mapping to zero has finitely many values, each eventually zero in a common later N_t, proving injectivity. Addition and differential agree because the coefficient maps are linear and equivariant. This argument does not assume the maps N_r to N are injective. It works in degree zero, for N_r=0, for a constant finite quotient, and when a nonzero coefficient at one stage is killed at the next. Cofinality is essential: a fixed insufficient quotient would miss locally constant cochains. Infinite discrete coefficients are allowed, since compactness gives finite image rather than requiring N finite.

For the item, checked r=0 (trivial group and identity cover), a=0, the split tower over a separably closed point (higher continuous group cohomology cancels on the regular-function module), and disconnected schemes. The main spectral sequence does not require connectedness; the extra H^0=A hypothesis is used only for the final collapse. The empty scheme still gives the zero spectral sequence, but never satisfies the additional H^0=A hypothesis. No hypothesis that ell is invertible on V is used or added. The characteristic-ell case is included because finite etale torsors and their Cech covers still make sense. Odd ell checks the sign: replacing c by -c would produce the inverse torsor and is not what Step 9 does.
