## What was wrong and what changed

The returned split obtained an abstract adjoint isomorphism and then assumed its graph-section normalization, agreement with the actual derived compactification exchange, and agreement with the counit-defined projection. That is insufficient: Rr_* is derived even when e is etale, and projection invertibility/composition alone does not identify PF_e with sum-by-lifts projection.

I compared it with the current shared item (still imported), retained its statement and blueprint fields, and checked the repaired proof against the sources. The delivered version described by the return had no new nodes; the repaired split now has the following substantive changes:

1. Step 2 uses the existing Stacks-sourced lem-sfb-quasifinite-compact-support-exact for the exact, canonical compact-support-to-sum comparison, valid for every compactification. The new frontier specifies its graph normalization on presheaf summands before sheafification and its derived-functor normalization on complexes. The step explicitly derives H^q(Rr_*j_!M)=0 for q>0 from that comparison, without asserting that arbitrary r_* is exact.
2. The new frontier (1) states the equality carrying the proper-exchange/open-comparison composite to the inverse specified 03S6 sum comparison. The sum adjunction is transported along this particular c_e, and the written Hom bijections fix its counit. Equation (E) is then the evaluation/base-change counit identity, so beta_e is the unit identification.
3. The new frontier (2) states the separate equality carrying Rr_*(gamma_j) composed with the counit-defined pi_r to the sum-extension projection p^Sigma. This is the missing projection compatibility, not a consequence taken from fix-prop-projection-formula. Step 2 substitutes it into theta's defining transpose. Step 6 writes the typed base-change counit identity and uses clause (1), naturality and mate pasting to identify the two beta transposes. These are identities of derived transformations; no sheaf-level or stalk-level equality is substituted for a derived equality.
5. The retained repair also removed an extra separatedness assumption on Y and arbitrary base-change schemes. For example, a separated affine chart can map to the affine line with doubled origin. General admissibility, arbitrary pullback of constructible stratifications/flat models, arbitrary-base-change compact-support pasting, and covering-family conservativity give the statements actually needed without the extra assumption.
