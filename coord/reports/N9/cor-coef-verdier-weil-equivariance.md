## Extracted argument from returned step 2

The inverse of geometric Frobenius is again a scheme automorphism. The exact inverse-image equivalence of [lem-weil-proetale-isomorphism-inverse-image] and the identification with derived pullback on integral constructible objects in [lem-weil-isomorphism-derived-pullback] give mutually inverse pullback functors for $g_T$ and $g_T^{-1}$. Their inverse identities rationalize by the morphism formulas of [def-weil-derived-pullback-tensor-hom], so $P_T$ is an equivalence on $\mathcal C_T$. This uses geometric, not absolute scheme, Frobenius. For a morphism $f$ descended from $k_0$, its Frobenius square, with horizontal arrows $g_X,g_Y$ and vertical arrows $f$, is Cartesian because both horizontal arrows are isomorphisms. Give the two source corners the $k$-structures $a_Xg_X$ and $a_Yg_Y$; then all four arrows are separated finite-type $k$-morphisms of finite-type $k$-schemes, and the pro-etale coefficient categories do not depend on the $k$-structure. So [fix-lem-exchanges] gives the compact-support exchange $a_f=\operatorname{Ex}_{g}:P_YF_f\to F_fP_X$, $g=g_Y$, natural in the input by its clause 1. Its clause 2, applied to horizontal pasting with the square of $h=g_Y^{-1}$ (upper arrow $h'=g_X^{-1}$) in both orders, where the pasted square is the identity square with identity exchange, gives $(\operatorname{Ex}_h\,Lg_X^*)\circ(Lh^*\operatorname{Ex}_g)=1$ and $(\operatorname{Ex}_g\,Lh'^*)\circ(Lg_Y^*\operatorname{Ex}_h)=1$ under the pullback-composition identifications. As $Lh^*$ is fully faithful, the first equation makes every component of $\operatorname{Ex}_g$ a split monomorphism; the second makes its components at the objects $Lh'^*K$, which exhaust $\mathcal C_X$ up to isomorphism, split epimorphisms, hence by naturality all components. Thus $a_f$ is a natural isomorphism on all of $\mathcal C_X$, not only on Weil pairs. The tensor comparison $\mu:P_T(A\otimes B)\xrightarrow{\sim}P_TA\otimes P_TB$ preserves symmetry, associators and units by [lem-pur-derived-pullback-tensor-iso]. These facts hold for the semilinear scheme automorphisms: neither inverse-image lemma requires a $k$-linear isomorphism.

## Extracted argument from returned step 5

First the transpose bijection used here and in Step 7. Let $P,L,Q$ be integral bounded constructible objects on $X$ whose internal Hom $H(P,Q)=R\mathcal Hom_{\widehat R_X}(P,Q)$ is again constructible. The global-sections identification of [lem-pur-rhom-global-sections] and the currying isomorphism of [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-internal-hom-exact-restriction] give $\operatorname{Hom}(L,H(P,Q))\cong\operatorname{Hom}(P\otimes^L_{\widehat R_X}L,Q)$ for the ordinary ambient tensor, with evaluation as counit. The completed tensor of [def-weil-derived-pullback-tensor-hom, def-pur-integral-derived-operations] is the derived limit of the reductions of this ordinary tensor (balancing on K-flat representatives, compatibly with the quotient transitions); by [lem-weil-pbc-constant-completion-equivalence], for the Noetherian ring $\mathcal O_E$ and the ideal $(\varpi_E)$, this limit with its unit is the Koszul completion of [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-koszul-completion-reflector], and the derived-complete object $Q$ of [def-weil-derived-complete-integral-detailed] satisfies that lemma's local completeness condition. Its mapping property gives $\operatorname{Hom}(P\widehat\otimes L,Q)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(P\otimes^LL,Q)$. Hence $\varphi\mapsto e\circ(1_P\widehat\otimes\varphi)$ is an $\mathcal O_E$-linear bijection $\operatorname{Hom}(L,H(P,Q))\to\operatorname{Hom}(P\widehat\otimes L,Q)$. By [def-weil-rationalization-karoubi] rational Hom groups are these groups tensored with $E$, and the rational tensor, internal Hom and evaluation are the integral ones extended linearly [def-weil-derived-pullback-tensor-hom, thm-coef-rational-verdier]; tensoring the bijection with $E$ gives the rational transpose bijection. Step 1 makes it applicable with $Q=\omega_X$ and $P=K$ or $DK$. By [thm-coef-integral-verdier], the actual integral double-dual map is evaluation-defined. Its rationalization in [thm-coef-rational-verdier], identified with internal-Hom biduality by [lem-coef-rational-operations], is therefore characterized by
$$e_{DK}(1_{DK}\otimes\delta_K)=e_K\tau_{DK,K},$$
where $\tau$ is tensor symmetry. We check $\delta_K\theta_K=\theta_{DDK}P_X\delta_K$ by taking both tensor-Hom transposes and precomposing with the invertible map $\theta_{DK}\otimes1_{P_XK}$. The transpose of the left side becomes
$$e_K(\theta_K\otimes\theta_{DK})\tau_{P_XDK,P_XK}
=\theta_\omega P_X(e_K)\mu_{K,DK}^{-1}\tau_{P_XDK,P_XK}.$$
The transpose of the right side becomes
$$e_{DK}(\theta_{DK}\otimes\theta_{DDK})(1\otimes P_X\delta_K)
=\theta_\omega P_X\bigl(e_{DK}(1\otimes\delta_K)\bigr)\mu_{DK,K}^{-1}.$$
Step 4 applied to $K$ and $DK$ gives these equalities; symmetry compatibility of [lem-pur-derived-pullback-tensor-iso] and the characterizing equation identify their right sides. By the transpose bijection just proved, the original arrows are equal. The underlying $\delta_K$ is invertible by [thm-coef-rational-verdier]; its inverse is equivariant by rearranging this equation. This proves equivariance of the actual biduality map.
