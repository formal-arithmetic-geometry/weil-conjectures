## Review by N9

I checked B–E with the node statements and local Stacks sources open. The two new statements introduce all objects, retain all source hypotheses, cite the definition nodes for schemes, local finite type, local Noetherianity, etaleness, completions, residue fields and the G-ring where relevant, and contain no proof sketches or hollow constructions. The local-map lemma is exactly 0CAU, with elementary etale neighbourhood unpacked into etale plus unchanged residue field. Its congruence is deliberately stated on the original Y local ring; extending it to the completed source is proved in step 3 of the item, not assumed from the citation. The completed-stalk criterion concerns the map induced by f, not merely some abstract isomorphism between its two completions. This distinction is essential.

For the second new node I checked why the source's inference is true: local maps to Artinian test rings factor through a power of the maximal ideal and hence through the completion; an inverse of the completed map gives lifts for the tests of 02HX. The isomorphism also identifies residue fields and cotangent spaces. Nakayama applied to the finite maximal ideal gives equality of the source maximal ideal with the extended target maximal ideal, so the finite type morphism is unramified at that point; 02GK then makes it etale near the point. This justification is recorded here for future work; the new node itself is statement-only. No algebraic closure, rational-point, flatness, or finite-presentation hypothesis is inserted into it.

Step 1 uses only the statement of the new local-map lemma, whose base, G-ring and both local finite type hypotheses exactly match those in the item. A ring isomorphism of local rings is local, so restricting phi gives its required local map. N >= 2 implies the input's N >= 1.

Step 2 uses finite-type-over-noetherian on affine charts, and the Noetherian-localization clause of flat-local-ring-maps to obtain the Noetherian local rings needed by flat-local-completion-iso. It uses the actual etale definition for flatness and def-weil-flat-morphism for flatness of the stalk map. The etale-prime-generates-maximal statement gives precisely m_B C = m_C. The unchanged residue field comes from step 1, not from a separability theorem. All three hypotheses of the completion-isomorphism node therefore hold. Its conclusion supplies the actual completed map p-hat, so alpha uses exactly the originally specified phi.

Step 3 uses the maximal-ideal identification of local-completion-local-ring and the surjective-quotient completion statement of local-completion-quotient. For J = m_A^N its quotient is already complete because the quotient's maximal ideal is nilpotent. This proves the representative claim for a in A-hat, with kernel exactly m_A^N A-hat, not just a surjection in one direction. Locality of g and alpha then gives their congruence on the entire completed source. This is the compatibility clause that a mere existence of abstract isomorphisms would fail to prove.

Step 4 is an elementary expansion in ideal powers. The degree-zero assertion is included explicitly. For positive degrees a product difference has one factor in n² and the others in n, so the induced map is identity on gr_n. Absorbing coefficients into a factor makes this apply to every finite sum defining n^r. No unquoted theorem about graded algebras is being used.

Step 5 is the explicit elementary injectivity and surjectivity chase of successive quotients. The induction starts at r=1 and its correction term e is in n^r, so the step is valid also at r=1. It does not assume the desired completed isomorphism or flatness of f.

Step 6 uses only completeness for the extended ideal from adic-completion-mod-ideal and the compatible-sequence definition of completion. The original maximal ideal is finitely generated because C was proved Noetherian. Inverses of bijective compatible quotient maps are compatible; taking them componentwise gives an inverse without needing any general exactness assertion for inverse limits.

Step 7 uses the precise composition AND cancellation clauses (e),(f) of proper-fp-permanence to check local finite type of f. The base Y is locally Noetherian by the chart calculation of step 2. The new criterion therefore applies to g, which step 6 proves is the actual completed map of f. The etale-composition-cancellation statement says open immersions are etale and etale maps compose, so restricting p preserves etaleness. The local ring at u is unchanged by the open restriction. Thus both residue-field conclusions and the original N-th order congruence survive, with no change of phi.
