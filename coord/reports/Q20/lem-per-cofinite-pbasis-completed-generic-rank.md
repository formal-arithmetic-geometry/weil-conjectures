## Source error

The sentence in ILO IV 2.1.6 immediately before equation (2.b) reverses the subscript of the ordinary differential module on the right and describes a free module over the wrong ring. The right side of (2.a) is computed using Omega_{R_kappa/R_kappa,e}, a free R_kappa-module, rather than Omega_{R_kappa,e/R_kappa}. In general the latter relative module is not defined by the displayed inclusion: R_kappa,e is a subring of R_kappa, and the reverse algebra structure is not supplied. With a perfect field, one variable and J empty, R=k[[t]], R_J=k[[t^p]], the correct Omega_{R/R_J} has basis dt; reversing the inclusion loses this algebra map. Step 3 explicitly computes a relative p-basis of K/K_J and therefore gives the intended rank d+|J| directly. This typographical error does not change the item's statement. No STATEMENT-DISPUTED correction is required.
