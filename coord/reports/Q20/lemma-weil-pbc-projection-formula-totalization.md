## Review by Q20

I checked the item against the two blueprint proof paragraphs, with the node files open. The hypotheses are those of the arrow definition: small ringed sites in a suitable universe, a morphism of ringed topoi with its ring map, an arbitrary unbounded derived object E, and a bounded complex with displayed finite-free retracts in every degree. No properness, scheme hypothesis, boundedness of E, exactness of arbitrary module pullback, or global projectivity in the category of sheaves is added. The strict package is used as the node actually states it: a summand of a finite free sheaf on the chosen site. The proof does not turn an arbitrary locally free sheaf into such a global summand.

Step 1 takes existence of a K-injective representative from `prop-pur-kinjective-resolutions-exist` and its computation of Rh_* from `lem-weil-pbc-kinjective-total-derived`. The latter requires an exact functor on homotopy categories, not exactness on sheaves. I wrote the cone-matrix justification for the former requirement explicitly. The arrow definition supplies the setting and the canonical, rather than arbitrary, comparison maps.

Step 2 uses the splitting equations from `def-weil-pbc-strict-perfect-coefficient-package`, the bounded-above-flat conclusion of `lem-pc-bounded-above-flat-kflat-site`, and the preservation/computation statement of `lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-derived-tensor-pullback`. The flatness assertion is proved by a retract of finite exact sequences; it is not deduced merely from the word projective. Pulling back a split retract remains a split retract, so no unavailable exactness assumption enters.

Step 3 uses `lem-pc-kflat-resolution` for P and for a resolution of I, the actual acyclicity test in `def-pc-kflat-complex`, and the signed total in `def-pc-tensor-complex-sheaves`. The cone criterion is cited explicitly as `lem-pur-quasi-iso-cone-acyclic`; the cone definition is cited as `def-weil-abstract-derived-category`, and I checked the shifted coefficient sign -(-1)^(a+1)=(-1)^a directly. Step 4 uses the ordinary isomorphism actually stated in `lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-module-pullback-tensor`, naturality from `lem-weil-pbc-termwise-adjunction-homotopy`, and the finite-product case of `lem-weil-pbc-right-adjoint-preserves-limits`.
