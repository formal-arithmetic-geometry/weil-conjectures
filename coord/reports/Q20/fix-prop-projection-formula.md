## Source error

### STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the unqualified Weil restriction

The imported conclusion says that the formula restricts to Weil categories without requiring that f descend to the fixed finite field. This is false. Take the fixed models X_0 = Spec F_q, Y_0 = A^1 over F_q, and the k-morphism f: Spec k -> A^1_k selecting a point a in F_(q^2) minus F_q, where k is the fixed algebraic closure. The constant E-object on Spec k has its usual Weil structure. Its proper pushforward is the skyscraper at a, whose Frobenius pullback has support at a different point (a^q or its inverse-Frobenius conjugate, according to convention). Objects with different supports are not isomorphic, so this pushforward has no Weil structure for the fixed models. Thus there cannot be the stated restriction of functors. This issue persists although f is proper and L can be the constant unit.

Corrected **only the Weil clause**: X_0,Y_0 are separated finite-type schemes over the fixed k_0 and f is the geometric base change of a separated finite-type f_0 over k_0. This is exactly the hypothesis of `lem-fix-weil-operations` and the models required by `def-wf-weil-category`, and expresses the intended finite-field assertion. No new hypothesis was added to the underlying projection formula. Both arbitrary bounded constructible K at finite level and finite-Tor L remain unchanged. The chapter and unit statement are untouched.
