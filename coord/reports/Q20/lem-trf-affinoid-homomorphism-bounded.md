The intended later proof is as follows, recorded here rather than inside the unsplit statement. For a fixed maximal m, the filtration of R/m^n has pieces m^i/m^(i+1). Noetherianity makes these finite R-modules annihilated by m, hence finite-dimensional over R/m; since R/m is finite over K each piece is finite over K. Lift bases through the finite filtration to prove R/m^n finite-dimensional. If x belongs to every maximal-power ideal, its localization at every maximal m belongs to every power of m R_m and is zero by local Krull intersection. Thus x=0 by localization detection. Equivalently for x nonzero, choose a maximal ideal containing the proper annihilator of x: x/1 cannot be zero there, since a denominator outside m killing x would lie in its annihilator contained in m. This confirms separation without asserting that the intersection of maximal ideals is zero. The standard filtration/localization argument is substantial enough to be a node, and is independent of normed algebras and maps.
