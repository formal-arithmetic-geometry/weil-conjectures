### Every conclusion

1. Properness is `IsProper I.blowupMap`.
2. Flatness of X'/R is `Flat (I.blowupMap ≫ f)`.
3. Separatedness of X'/R is `IsSeparated (I.blowupMap ≫ f)`.
4. Finite type of X'/R is the pair `LocallyOfFiniteType (I.blowupMap ≫ f)` and
   `QuasiCompact (I.blowupMap ≫ f)`.
5. The isomorphism off the centre is `IsIso (I.blowupMap ∣_ I.support.compl)`.
   The support of the kernel ideal of a closed immersion is precisely the underlying
   closed subset of its source. The morphism restricts to the inverse image of that open.
6. The generic-fibre assertion is an explicit second `IsIso`, on
   `pullback.snd I.blowupMap (pullback.fst f (Spec.map ...))`, where the ring map is
   `algebraMap R (FractionRing R)`. This is exactly the base change of b along X_K -> X,
   so its source is the generic fibre of X' and its target is X_K.
7. The dilatation open is the witness `D : I.blowup.Opens`.
8. For EVERY affine open `U : X.affineOpens`, there is an isomorphism from
   `(D ⊓ (I.blowupMap ⁻¹ᵁ U.1)).toScheme` to
   `Spec (.of (AffineBlowup.chartRing (I.ideal U) π_B))`.
   Here pi_B is written out as
   `(f.appLE ⊤ U.1 le_top).hom ((Scheme.ΓSpecIso (.of R)).inv.hom π)`.
   This is precisely the image of pi in B = Gamma(X,U). Since I is the kernel of
   the given special-fibre centre, `I.ideal U` is the inverse image in B of the
   centre ideal in B/pi_B B. The existing `chartRing` is the B-subalgebra of
   `Localization.Away π_B` generated by the elements i/pi_B for i in this ideal.
9. The equation following that isomorphism requires its inverse, followed by
   the inclusion of the intersection into X' and b, to equal the spectrum of
   `algebraMap B (AffineBlowup.chartRing ...)` followed by `U.2.fromSpec`.
   Thus this is an identification over X (and hence over U), with the canonical
   B-structure on the chart algebra; it is not an arbitrary abstract scheme isomorphism.
10. The bijection on A-points is stated as `∃! a', a' ≫ I.blowupMap = a` for
    every R-point a and every A with the stated properties. This is exactly
    bijectivity of composition on R-points. Any such a' is automatically an R-point,
    since composing its displayed equality with f gives its structure-map condition.
11. Reduction into Z is `a (IsLocalRing.closedPoint A) ∈ I.support`.
    This is equivalent to scheme-theoretic factorization of the reduction
    `Spec (IsLocalRing.ResidueField A) -> X_k` through Z: its source is a field,
    and vanishing at that point means the entire defining ideal maps to zero.
    This equivalence does not require Z or X_k to be reduced.
12. For every a' satisfying the lift equation, the last clause supplies
    `d : Spec (.of A) ⟶ D.toScheme` with `d ≫ D.ι = a'`.
    Together with unique lifting, this is exactly the claimed factorization of
    the unique lift into the dilatation open.

Read the local Stacks source `literature/stacks-project/divisors.tex`,
`lemma-blowing-up-affine` (0804), `lemma-flat-base-change-blowing-up` (0805),
`lemma-blowing-up-gives-effective-Cartier-divisor` (02OS), and
`lemma-universal-property-blowing-up` (0806). The affine-chart and complement
constructions agree with the actual library implementations. The theorem's
seven-step proof docstring retains the reviewed DAG proof and names its dependencies.

- Empty X is allowed; its affine rings can be zero and all point assertions are vacuous.
- Empty centre: I is the unit ideal, b is an isomorphism, and B[I/pi] = B[1/pi].
  Thus D is the generic-fibre open, not necessarily all of X'. No A-point
  reduces into the empty centre, so the final implication is vacuous.
- Full special fibre: I = pi O_X. The blowup is an isomorphism and B[I/pi] = B,
  so D is all of X'. Every A-point reduces into this centre.
- For B = R[x], I = (pi,x), the designated chart is R[x/pi], as expected;
  a section reducing into the centre has x(a) in pi A and hence lies in that chart.
- Nonreduced special fibres and rings B with nilpotents are allowed. The chart
  construction is the subalgebra inside localization, not a naive polynomial
  quotient that might retain pi-torsion. Flatness is only over R.
- Disconnected X is allowed: affine restrictions and the gluing statement impose
  no connectedness condition. Zero charts cause no exception to the assertion.
- Arbitrary residue characteristic and residue extensions of A are allowed;
  no equality of residue fields, smoothness, or excellence is imposed.
