### Returned step 1

Put $Y=X\times_S S'$ with projections $p:Y\to X$ and $q:Y\to S'$, let $E=b^{-1}(Z)\subseteq S'$ (cut out by $\mathcal I\mathcal O_{S'}$, $\mathcal I$ the ideal of $Z$), and let $D=q^{-1}(E)=(f\circ p)^{-1}(Z)$. By [thm-nagata-blowup-principalizes-center], every point of $S'$ has an affine neighbourhood $V$ with $E\cap V=V(a)$ for one $a\in\mathcal O_{S'}(V)$; hence on an open of $Y$ mapping into $V$ the set $D$ is the zero set of the image of $a$. Let $W\subseteq Y$ be an affine open. Each point of $W$ has a principal open $D(g)\subseteq W$ mapping into such a $V$ ([lem-weil-pbc-affine-opens-basis] and the basic-open basis of [lem-weil-pbc-spec-localization-away-basic-open](i)), and on it $D=V(a_g)$; finitely many of them cover $W$ by [lem-weil-pbc-spec-basic-opens-unit-ideal-quasi-compact](iii). Thus $W\setminus D=\bigcup_g D(g)\cap D(a_g)$ is a finite union of principal opens, each quasi-compact as the homeomorphic image of the spectrum of a localization [lem-weil-pbc-spec-localization-away-basic-open](ii), [lem-weil-pbc-spec-basic-opens-unit-ideal-quasi-compact](iii). So the open immersion $j:Y\setminus D\hookrightarrow Y$ is quasi-compact; it is quasi-separated because, being a monomorphism, its diagonal is an isomorphism, hence quasi-compact [def-weil-quasi-separated-morphism].

### Returned step 2

The pullback $H=p^*\mathcal F$ is quasi-coherent by [lem-weil-pbc-module-pullback-quasi-coherent]. Let $K\subseteq H$ be the subsheaf of sections restricting to zero on $Y\setminus D$; a section has support in $D$ exactly when its germs vanish off $D$, i.e. (sheaf axiom) when it restricts to zero on the complement, so $K$ is the subsheaf of sections supported on $D$. It is the sectionwise kernel of $H\to j_*(H|_{Y\setminus D})$, since $(j_*(H|_{Y\setminus D}))(W)=H(W\setminus D)$. The restriction $H|_{Y\setminus D}$ is the module pullback $j^*H$ (because $j^{-1}\mathcal O_Y=\mathcal O_{Y\setminus D}$), hence quasi-coherent by [lem-weil-pbc-module-pullback-quasi-coherent] applied to $j$, and $j_*$ preserves quasi-coherence by [pqf-lem-pushforward-qcoh] because $j$ is quasi-compact and quasi-separated by Step 1. Therefore $K$ is quasi-coherent by [lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-module-kernel]. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-module-cokernel-image] to the inclusion $K\hookrightarrow H$: its cokernel $H/K$ is quasi-coherent by (2), the quotient map $\pi:H\to H/K$ is an epimorphism by (1), and its sectionwise kernel is $\operatorname{Im}(K\hookrightarrow H)$, which is $K$ itself because a section of $H$ that is locally in the sheaf $K$ lies in $K$. By [def-trf-module-strict-transform], $H/K$ is exactly $\mathcal F^{\mathrm{st}}$. So $\pi:p^*\mathcal F\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal F^{\mathrm{st}}$ is surjective with kernel $K$, the quasi-coherent subsheaf of sections supported on $D=(f\circ p)^{-1}(Z)$.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-module-pullback-quasi-coherent, pqf-lem-pushforward-qcoh, lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-module-kernel, lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-module-cokernel-image, def-trf-module-strict-transform.

### Returned step 3

Over $Y\setminus D$ the subsheaf $K$ restricts to zero (a section of $K$ over an open $O\subseteq Y\setminus D$ restricts to zero on $O$ itself), so $\pi|_{Y\setminus D}$ is an epimorphism with zero kernel in the abelian category of $\mathcal O_{Y\setminus D}$-modules, hence an isomorphism. For the absence of supported sections, let $O\subseteq Y$ be open and $t\in\mathcal F^{\mathrm{st}}(O)$ with $t|_{O\setminus D}=0$. Cover $O$ by affine opens $W\subseteq O$ [lem-weil-pbc-affine-opens-basis]. By [lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-module-cokernel-image](3) on the affine $W$, $t|_W=\pi_W(m)$ for some $m\in H(W)$. Then $\pi_{W\setminus D}(m|_{W\setminus D})=t|_{W\setminus D}=0$, so by part (1) of the same lemma (kernel of $\pi$ is $K$ on every open, Step 2) $m|_{W\setminus D}\in K(W\setminus D)=0$. Hence $m$ restricts to zero off $D$, i.e. $m\in K(W)$, and $t|_W=\pi_W(m)=0$. By the sheaf axiom $t=0$. This holds on every open $O$, so $\mathcal F^{\mathrm{st}}$ has no nonzero local section supported on $D$. (Algebraically, on $W=\operatorname{Spec}A$ with $D\cap W=V(I)$, $I$ finitely generated, this is the fact that $M/\Gamma_I(M)$ has no $I$-power torsion, the Tag 07ZP description; the argument above does not need that description.)
