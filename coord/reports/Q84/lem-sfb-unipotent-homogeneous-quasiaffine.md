## Source error

Milne iAG2017, Proposition 14.26, printed p.286 (PDF p.298), says that U acts trivially on its Chevalley stabilizer line L, “and so V^U=L”. The equality does not follow and need not hold. Take G=G_m, U=1, V=k^2 with representation t↦diag(t,1), and L=k(1,1). For every k-algebra A, stabilizing L_A forces t=1, so L has scheme-theoretic stabilizer U as required by Chevalley; but V^U=V≠L. Only L⊆V^U and the equality between the vector stabilizer of a generator of L and U are needed. Steps 3–4 supply exactly these true assertions. The proposition's statement is unchanged.
