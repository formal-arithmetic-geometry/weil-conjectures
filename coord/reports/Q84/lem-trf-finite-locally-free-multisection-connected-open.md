### Clarifications for the independent proofs

For the first node, flatness and finite presentation of X_Z over Z come from lem-weil-pbc-flat-morphism-base-change and clause (3) of lem-weil-pbc-qcqs-morphism-permanence; geometric reducedness persists by the iterated-extension definition. Set W equal to the intersection of the inverse images of these two opens under the opposite sections. Each point of the double fibre lies in W because its X-fibre is a field extension of the geometrically connected designated fibre. On W the two section points lie in the same connected component in each fibre, so the component sets agree. This is a genuine relative-locus comparison, not just the elementary equality of two topological components; no finiteness of p is needed at this stage.

For the second node, both p and q:Z times_B Z to B are finite locally free by the existing base-change permanence nodes and lem-trf-finite-locally-free-composition. The existing open-map and finite-closed-map leaves give p(Z) open and q(R minus W) closed. Since Z has a point over b_0 and W contains all of R over b_0, the open p(Z) minus q(R minus W) contains b_0. Choose an affine V inside it. Then p_V is surjective finite locally free, its pullback pi to X_V is again finite locally free and surjective, and both are fpqc covers. The given W-equality now holds on the whole double product. Lem-weil-pbc-open-subscheme-fpqc-descent produces the unique U with inverse image C_V. Crucially, Z_V is affine because a finite morphism to affine V has affine source (lem-weil-pbc-finite-morphism-permanence(b)); the base of the section family for the compactness step is Z_V, not just V. Its family is flat, finitely presented with geometrically reduced fibres by permanence, so lem-trf-section-component-quasi-compact applies. The restriction C_V to U is surjective: pi is surjective and C_V is its inverse image. Its continuous image is U, proving U quasi-compact. This is the complete cover construction and descent argument; the compactness node alone neither gives V nor constructs U.

For the third node, pullback pasting identifies the fibre of Y over t with X_b over K=kappa(t), compatibly with the open immersions. The equality of the inverse image of U with C restricts to that square, hence identifies (U_b)_K with C_t, carrying the section to a K-rational point. By hypothesis C_t is exactly its connected component and is nonempty. Lem-sbc-connected-rational-point-geometrically-connected states precisely that a connected K-scheme with a rational section point stays connected over every field extension. Thus (U_b)_K is geometrically connected. If X_b is geometrically connected, Y_t is connected, its section component is the whole fibre, and (U_b)_K=(X_b)_K. The projection (X_b)_K to X_b is surjective (a nonempty field extension is faithfully flat, and surjectivity is stable under scheme base change), so equality of the inverse-image open with the whole space forces U_b=X_b. These field-square and open-identification arguments, rather than the rational-point criterion alone, are the content of the third new node. Its surjectivity hypothesis ensures that a t above each b can be selected. Parent step 3 then uses the EXISTING geometric-connectedness reflection node to pass from K back to kappa(b); this implication is still an explicit citation in the parent's assembly.
