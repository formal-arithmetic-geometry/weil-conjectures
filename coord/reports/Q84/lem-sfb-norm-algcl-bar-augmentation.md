## Original argument, retained verbatim and allocated

### Original step 1

Allocation/correction: derived-function-columns: exact internal/section-module transport.

Let $\mathcal C$ be the small coherent profinite site and write $A$ also for its section-ring sheaf $V\mapsto C(V,\Omega)$. By [def-sfb-continuous-map-condensed-ring] this is a sheaf. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-sheaf-tensor-closed] with coefficient category $\operatorname{Ab}$: abelian sheafification is exact and strong monoidal, its local equivalences are monoidal, and abelian sheaves form a closed symmetric monoidal Grothendieck category. Thus [def-weil-pbc-internal-abelian-sheaf-module-equivalence] identifies internal $A$-modules with sectionwise $A$-module sheaves, preserving sections and maps. The equivalence is exact and monoidal by [lem-pe-internal-section-tensor-comparison]. The tensor right adjoint on the sectionwise side is sheaf Hom by [lem-pc-sheaf-modules-tensor-closed]; uniqueness of right adjoints therefore transports the internal Hom to that sheaf Hom. An exact equivalence preserves injectives, since its inverse carries monomorphisms to monomorphisms and the adjunction bijections preserve the extension property. Consequently it also transports the injective-resolution derived Hom and its augmentation.

Original citations: def-sfb-continuous-map-condensed-ring, lem-weil-pbc-sheaf-tensor-closed, def-weil-pbc-internal-abelian-sheaf-module-equivalence, lem-pe-internal-section-tensor-comparison, lem-pc-sheaf-modules-tensor-closed.

### Original step 2

Allocation/correction: derived-function-columns: finite-family identification, now with an explicit conditional Cech-vanishing premise; the old asserted Omega premise is false.

Let $\mathcal F$ be the underlying abelian sheaf $V\mapsto C(V,\Omega)$. Given a defining finite jointly surjective covering family $\{T_i\to V\}$, take $T=\coprod_i T_i$ and the surjection $q:T\to V$. Every $T_V^{n+1}$ is the finite disjoint union of the fibre products $T_{i_0}\times_V\cdots\times_VT_{i_n}$. Continuous functions on a finite disjoint union are precisely tuples of continuous functions on its components. Omitting the $j$-th coordinate restricts to the projection omitting the $j$-th component in the corresponding tuple. Thus the complex in [lem-sfb-norm-algcl-profinite-cech-exact] is precisely the covering Cech complex in [def-weil-pbc-site-cech-complex]. The new analytic input gives its exactness in every positive degree. It is applied to $\Omega$ itself; no completeness premise is supplied or needed by its statement.

Original citations: lem-sfb-norm-algcl-profinite-cech-exact, def-weil-pbc-site-cech-complex.

### Original step 3

Allocation/correction: derived-function-columns: conditional Cech criterion, enough injectives and module/abelian evaluation.

Use [lem-weil-pbc-site-cech-vanishing-criterion] with $\mathcal B$ all objects and $\mathrm{Cov}$ all defining finite jointly surjective families. Finite products and fibre products of profinite spaces are profinite; hence every object and fibre product in such a covering belongs to $\mathcal B$. These families define the coherent topology, so are cofinal among coverings (every covering sieve contains a defining family). Step 2 establishes the required positive Cech vanishing for each family. The criterion gives $H^r(V,\mathcal F)=0$ for every profinite $V$ and every $r>0$. The internal module category is Grothendieck by [lem-sfb-modobj-grothendieck], using the closed monoidal Grothendieck category from step 1, and has enough injectives by [lem-sp-grothendieck-enough-injectives]. Choose a nonnegative module injective resolution $A\to J^\bullet$ and transport it by step 1. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-site-module-abelian-evaluation-comparison] to the actual ring sheaf $A$, not a constant ring. It identifies the cohomology of $J^\bullet(V)$ with $H^r(V,\mathcal F)$ and is the identity in degree zero. Therefore $A(V)[0]\to J^\bullet(V)$ is a quasi-isomorphism for every $V$. All objects, covering indices and module values lie in the prescribed common universes.

Original citations: lem-weil-pbc-site-cech-vanishing-criterion, lem-sfb-modobj-grothendieck, lem-sp-grothendieck-enough-injectives, lem-weil-pbc-ringed-site-module-abelian-evaluation-comparison.

### Original step 4

Allocation/correction: derived-function-columns: free-generator evaluation and parameter naturality.

For any profinite $P$ and module sheaf $N$, [def-pur-sheaf-hom] and the free-module universal property give
$$\mathcal Hom_A(A[P],N)(S)=N(S\times P).$$
Explicitly $x\in N(S\times P)$ sends a generator $p:V\to P$ over $v:V\to S$ to $(v,p)^*x$, extended $A(V)$-linearly. Conversely evaluate such a map on the tautological $P$-coordinate over $S\times P$. Each construction reverses the other, including on generators after base change, and the free sheaf universal property extends these maps uniquely through sheafification. For $N=A$ this is the stated evaluation $C(S\times P,\Omega)$, with scalars pulled back from $S$. For $N=J^r$ the same formula identifies the Hom-resolution complex with $J^\bullet(S\times P)$, with identical differentials. Every continuous $v:P'\to P$ acts by pullback along $1_S\times v$, so these identifications and the resolution augmentations are contravariantly natural in $P$.

Original citations: def-pur-sheaf-hom.

### Original step 5

Allocation/correction: derived-function-columns: conditional sectionwise-to-sheaf quasi-isomorphism; not invoked unconditionally for Omega.

Apply step 3 to $V=S\times P$. Step 4 identifies its quasi-isomorphism with the sections of the canonical map
$$C(-\times P,\Omega)[0]\longrightarrow\mathcal Hom_A(A[P],J^\bullet).$$
It is a quasi-isomorphism of sheaf complexes: exactness of every section complex identifies each presheaf image with the corresponding kernel sheaf, and hence identifies the sheaf image with that kernel. In degree zero the same statement includes the augmentation. The resolution complex represents $R\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_A(A[P],A[0])$ after the exact transport of step 1. Thus its canonical unit from the underived Hom is an isomorphism in the ordinary derived category [def-weil-abstract-derived-category], naturally under every continuous parameter map as verified in step 4. This proves the needed termwise comparison as an intermediate consequence of the analytic input; the item itself requires only the total comparison.

Original citations: def-weil-abstract-derived-category.

### Original step 6

Allocation/correction: bar-augmentation-row-comparison: actual finite-total map; generalize the sufficient column hypothesis to positive-E2-row vanishing.

Now take $P=H^q$ for every $q\geq0$. Use the compatible nonnegative bar-resolution model of [lem-sfb-condensed-derived-invariant-bar]. Each column augmentation $U_A^q[0]\to R\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_A(A[\underline H^q],A[0])$ is the canonical unit proved invertible in step 5. Parameter naturality makes it commute with the bar faces: these are deletion of an end coordinate and addition of consecutive coordinates, all continuous on $H$. The first face has no action factor on $A$, since its action is trivial. The alternating sum is exactly the differential displayed in the item, including $d^0=0$. Regard the source as a double complex concentrated in resolution degree zero and the target as the compatible resolution double complex. They are first-quadrant, with finite total diagonals and total sign $d_{\mathrm{bar}}+(-1)^q d_{\mathrm{res}}$ as in [def-sp-double-complex-total]. [lem-sc-first-quadrant-total-column-comparison] applies to this double-complex morphism. It makes its total augmentation a quasi-isomorphism. Its $(q,0)$ component is induced by $A\to I^0$, and all other source components vanish; consequently its localization is exactly the specified $j_A$, which is therefore an isomorphism.

Original citations: lem-sfb-condensed-derived-invariant-bar, def-sp-double-complex-total, lem-sc-first-quadrant-total-column-comparison.

### Original step 7

Allocation/correction: bar-augmentation-row-comparison: lifts, natural comparisons and signed total homotopy.

The derived-unit interpretation of each column augmentation is independent of the chosen resolution. More concretely, apply [lem-weil-pbc-injective-resolution-lift-exists] in the equivariant category to the identity of $A$, obtaining a chain map between any two nonnegative equivariant injective resolutions with composite on $A$ equal to the second augmentation. Applying the compatible bar construction makes a map of double complexes commuting with the maps from $U_A^\bullet$; totalization preserves that equality. The comparison map is a quasi-isomorphism before applying the derived functors, since it induces the identity on the sole cohomology object $A$. The compatible derived model and its naturality identify it with the canonical change of resolution by [lem-sp-derived-injective-resolution-naturality]. Uniqueness follows from [lem-weil-pbc-lift-to-injective-unique-up-to-homotopy]: two augmentation-preserving lifts are homotopic. After the bar functors the homotopy is natural in the parameter and commutes with bar faces; multiplying its component in bar degree $q$ by $(-1)^q$ gives a total homotopy (the two horizontal cross terms cancel). Thus the total morphism from $U_A^\bullet$ is the same under the canonical resolution identifications.

Original citations: lem-weil-pbc-injective-resolution-lift-exists, lem-sp-derived-injective-resolution-naturality, lem-weil-pbc-lift-to-injective-unique-up-to-homotopy.

### Original step 8

Allocation/correction: bar-augmentation-row-comparison: degree zero and edge cases.

For $q=0$ the parameter $H^0$ is a point, so evaluation is $U_A^0=A$ and the column augmentation is $A[0]\to I^\bullet$. Its induced degree-zero map is the identity under $H^0(I^\bullet)=A$. The group action on $A$ is trivial, so the bar differential out of the constant degree-zero term is zero and this also gives $H^0(j_A)=1_A$, agreeing with the invariant-inclusion normalization of [lem-sfb-condensed-derived-invariant-bar]. For the empty test set all the section groups used above are zero. Negative degrees vanish throughout and no infinite total diagonal is used. The only analytic input was the new all-continuous $\Omega$-valued Cech exactness statement; the construction of $A$, all maps and all resolutions remain in the ordinary module categories of the item.

Original citations: lem-sfb-condensed-derived-invariant-bar.
