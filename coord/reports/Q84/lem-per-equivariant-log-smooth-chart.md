Thus character orders dividing the exponent of G put their values in the prescribed mu_N, without replacing the given base chart.

Step 3 checks every hypothesis of the divisible-unit lattice extension: L is finite free; B/D is C and torsion-free; the characteristic cokernel is finite killed by an invertible n; and A-units are n-divisible. Step 4 is the explicit missing equivariance argument: the ratio's m-th power is the old character, so it is an mN-root; inertia fixes it; the cocycle becomes a character. Multiplicativity in the lattice variable supplies the tensor pairing and preserves the base character. Steps 5--6 explicitly prove the inverse-image monoid and chart assertions. The subgroup-free leaf supplies the only group-structure fact used for K. The pushout calculation proves the chart at the stalk, not merely surjectivity on the characteristic. The torsion subgroup embeds into F/iL, so its finite order has only primes dividing m and is invertible. This includes r=0 and K=0.

Step 7 uses the stalk-spreading lemma with the precise pushout isomorphism established in Step 6; its conclusion preserves the specified germ, as required. I corrected the equivariant-spreading argument to use two finite products: first an equivariant product of translates of the chart neighbourhood, then the equivariant product of translates of a further neighbourhood realizing the finitely many germ equalities.
