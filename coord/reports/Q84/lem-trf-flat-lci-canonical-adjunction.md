### Former step 1 → lem-trf-lci-determinant-descent-smooth

Work locally with $I=(f_1,\ldots,f_c)$ on an affine piece of $P$. By [lem-weil-pbc-regular-sequence-koszul-regular] these equations are Koszul-regular; by [lem-weil-pbc-regular-sequence-conormal-free] their classes form a basis of $C=I/I^2$. On $P\subset\mathbf A^n_S$ the coordinate differentials $dx_1,\ldots,dx_n$ form a basis of $\Omega^1_{P/S}$, hence their pullbacks form a basis of $V$. Thus $C$ and $V$ have ranks $c$ and $n$, and $K_i=(\bigwedge^c C)^\vee\otimes\bigwedge^n V$ is locally free of rank one: the dual of $\bar f_1\wedge\cdots\wedge\bar f_c$ tensored with $dx_1\wedge\cdots\wedge dx_n$ is a local generator.

### Former step 4 → lem-trf-lci-koszul-duality-determinant-comparison

### Former step 6 → lem-trf-lci-koszul-duality-determinant-comparison

The polynomial-refinement comparisons of [lem-trf-relative-duality-polynomial-chart] reduce the preceding check to restriction, change of regular generators, and adjoining a coordinate $t$ mapping to a lift $a$ of a function on $U$. The generator change is covered by the ideal-independent top Ext identification of [lem-sc-koszul-regular-sequence-dual-complex]. For adjoining $t$, the new equation is $t-a$ and the extra conormal-differential part is $[\mathcal O_U\xrightarrow{1}\mathcal O_U]$, using $d(t-a)=dt-da$ for its degree-zero generator. Its determinant is trivialized by evaluation by [lem-trf-two-term-perfect-determinant]. The dualizing comparison contracts the dual of $t-a$ against $d(t-a)$ to $1$, which is exactly that evaluation. Iterating gives the same ordered wedge contractions, including the cohomological tensor signs. Locally any two polynomial presentations admit the combined-coordinate refinement: lift the second set of coordinate functions through the first quotient presentation, and conversely. Thus these checks compare any two charts, and the comparisons agree with $t_{ij}$. The local dualizing identifications therefore glue to $K|_T[d]$.

### Former step 7 → lem-trf-flat-lci-presentation-base-change

For an arbitrary base change $S'\to S$, choose affine base charts $\operatorname{Spec}R'\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ and principal affine pieces of the ambient polynomial charts, with ring $A$ and quotient $B=A/(f_1,\ldots,f_c)$. Write $A=R[x_1,\ldots,x_n]_h$. The polynomial ring is $R$-free on monomials, so tensoring an injection with it is a direct sum of injections. Hence $A$ is $R$-flat by [lem-pc-affine-flat-module-localization-preserves-flatness]. By [lem-weil-pbc-localization-away-polynomial-quotient], $A=R[x_1,\ldots,x_n,z]/(hz-1)$, a finite presentation. By [lem-weil-pbc-flat-morphism-affine-criterion], flatness of $p$ makes $B$ flat over $R$. Apply [lem-pur-relative-regular-equations-base-change]: at every prime of $A'=A\otimes_RR'$ containing the new equations $f'_j$, those equations are regular in the local ring. Their Koszul complex is consequently exact in positive degrees at these primes by [lem-weil-pbc-regular-sequence-koszul-regular]; at a prime outside their common zero locus it is contractible by contraction with an invertible equation. Exactness can be checked on stalks, so the pulled-back equations are Koszul-regular. By [lem-weil-pbc-regular-sequence-conormal-free] on those local rings, their classes give a basis of the conormal of $U'$. The natural map $g^*C\to C'$ sends one such basis to the other, hence is an isomorphism. Coordinate differentials likewise identify $g^*V$ with $V'$. Thus the same determinant and Koszul adjunction construction is available after base change, without a Noetherian hypothesis on $S'$.

### Former step 8 → lem-trf-flat-lci-presentation-base-change

Flatness of $W/S$ makes every such square Tor-independent: on affine charts $B\otimes_R^{\mathbf L}R'=B\otimes_RR'$. The natural cotangent comparison of [lem-trf-lci-cotangent-presentation] is therefore an isomorphism $Lg^*L_{W/S}\simeq L_{W'/S'}$. For two successive changes of base, the cotangent comparison for the composite is the composite of the two natural comparisons; the determinant preserves composition and its pullback comparison is coherent. Consequently the two resulting maps on $K$ agree, and identity base change gives the identity.
