## Problems found and corrections

The imported item was a statement-only child of `lem-per-rational-surface-gorenstein-blowups`, not a completed split. I retained its statement, original source, provenance, earlier Mathlib searches and blueprint ancestry. There is no statement dispute. The source's 01OF is the blowing-up section, while the precise away-from-center assertion used here is divisors.tex Tag 02OS, `lemma-blowing-up-gives-effective-Cartier-divisor` (1); the proof steps name that precise lemma. Blowup restriction is Tag 0805, `lemma-flat-base-change-blowing-up`.

I supplied two details that cannot be silently elided. First, transporting a closed point across an isomorphism of opens does not in general give a globally closed point. Here its closure lies in the closed singular locus, which is entirely inside the comparison open, so closedness in that open implies global closedness. Second, blowing up a normal surface need not preserve normality. Here the new retained blowup is normal on the comparison open because it is isomorphic to an open in the given normal next stage, and is normal elsewhere because the blowup is an isomorphism there. No general normal-blowup assertion is used.

The induction requires no ample line bundle and no assertion that later models all have global dimension two. Local dimension of retained centers is transported by the local-ring isomorphism. Thus the existing `lem-per-surface-point-blowup-geometry`, which requires an ample line bundle and a globally regular surface, was not used. The more directly applicable existing `lem-per-regular-point-blowup-acyclic` works on the regular open with exactly the hypotheses present. Only its regularity conclusion is used, not its cohomology clause.
