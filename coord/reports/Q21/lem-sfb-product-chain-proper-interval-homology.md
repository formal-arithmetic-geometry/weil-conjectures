Its standalone proof obligation is genuine: on weak chains the deletion and prism identities are alternating cancellations; a tuple with a repeated adjacent pair stays in the repeated-tuple submodule after applying a monotone map, and in its boundary the two exceptional deletion faces cancel. For the prism on such a tuple, terms splitting away from that repeated pair still have a repeat, while the two split positions adjacent to the pair yield the same tuple with opposite signs; thus it descends to strict chains. On a vertex the formula is $h[x]=[f(x),g(x)]$, whose boundary is $[g(x)]-[f(x)]$; in degree -1 both induced maps are the identity and the difference is zero. This explains why the additional normalized augmented node is needed beyond the existing unnormalized node, without putting its proof into a new unsplit node.
