## Argument retained for the new statement-only node

The new lemma is a synthesis of Milne 6.27 (p.131), 1.34 and 1.38--1.39 (pp.16--18), 1.90 (p.33), 5.39 (p.108), and the dimension argument in 1.63 (p.25). It does not assert that a quotient is reductive, does not construct a character sublattice quotient, and does not assert any root-data comparison.

For a central isogeny f:E→B and smooth connected solvable normal U⊂B, put P=f⁻¹(U). It is normal in E, and P→U is faithfully flat with finite commutative kernel K. Pull back a finite solvable series for U and append K⊃1. Each pullback quotient is commutative, so P is solvable (Milne 6.27). Over the algebraically closed, hence perfect, field, P_red is a closed subgroup by 1.38--1.39 and is smooth by 1.28; its identity component V is a smooth connected closed subgroup by 1.34. Subgroups of a solvable group are solvable by 6.27. Conjugation by every E(F)-point preserves P, its reduced subscheme and its identity component as schemes, so V is normal in the smooth group E by the normality criterion behind 1.90. This assertion would be unsafe in a nonsmooth ambient group, which is why smoothness of E is explicit.

The faithfully flat image I of V in U is closed by 5.39. Every fibre of P→U is a translate of finite K, so dim P=dim U. Reduction does not change dimension, and all components of an algebraic group have the dimension of its identity component, by 1.35--1.36. Hence dim V=dim U. The restriction V→I also has finite kernel V∩K, so dim I=dim V. The connected smooth group U is irreducible by 1.35; its reduced closed subgroup I of equal dimension is U. Here I is smooth because it is a quotient of the smooth V (1.62), so it cannot be a proper thickening with the same underlying space. Thus V→U is faithfully flat, and its kernel is the scheme-theoretic intersection V∩K. This proves every clause of the new statement, without assuming K reduced. This argument belongs here, in the report; the unsplit node itself contains only the self-contained statement and no proof or proof dependencies.
