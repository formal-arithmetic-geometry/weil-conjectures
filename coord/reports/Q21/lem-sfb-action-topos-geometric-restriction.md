### Original step 3 → lem-sfb-internal-restriction-geometric-coherence

Finite limits in the action categories are created in underlying sheaves. Explicitly the terminal sheaf has its unique action, a finite product has componentwise action, and the equalizer of equivariant $u,v:X\to Y$ has sections $\{x\mid u(x)=v(x)\}$ with the restricted action. These presheaves are sheaves: compatible tuples glue coordinatewise, and equalizer membership holds after gluing because equality of two sheaf sections can be checked on a cover [def-weil-pbc-sites-and-cohomology]. The universal maps into products and equalizers are equivariant by their component equations. Restriction along $\theta$ leaves the terminal object, these products and these equalizers unchanged, and hence preserves finite limits.
