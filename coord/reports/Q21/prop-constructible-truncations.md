## Source error

The imported **proof**, not the item's statement, contains three errors.

1. Canonical truncation is not generally triangulated. On a point, with a nonzero field-coefficient object E in degree zero, tau<=0(E)=E but tau<=0(E[-1])=0, whereas tau<=0(E)[-1]=E[-1]. It fails to commute with shift.
2. Triangulated exactness does not imply t-exactness or commutation with truncation. The shift [1] is an exact triangulated autoequivalence. For K=E[-1], tau<=0(K[1])=E but (tau<=0 K)[1]=0. The repaired proof instead checks cohomological bounds by exact module tensor and exact automorphism inverse image.
3. An arbitrary reduction tower cannot be truncated levelwise and remain normalized. Already for A a DVR and K=A/(varpi) in degree zero, K tensor^L_A A/(varpi) has nonzero H^-1 as well as H^0. Thus reduction of tau>=0 K is not tau>=0 of the reduction. The current integral and localized theorem statements explicitly restrict levelwise formulas to their Tor-compatible locus. The repaired proof uses their unconditional ambient canonical t-structure clauses and makes no unrestricted reduction/truncation assertion.

No statement weakening, added flatness hypothesis, or change to the chapter/unit route is used to repair these errors.
