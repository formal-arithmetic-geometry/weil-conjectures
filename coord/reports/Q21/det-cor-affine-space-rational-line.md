## Corrections and statement dispute

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the standalone import never introduces V as a scheme or fixes the prime, coefficient extension, or invertibility/finite-type setting required by the exceptional-inverse-image interface. The type check fails directly: sk-prop-sf-existence requires separated finite-type schemes over a field of positive characteristic different from ell, whereas the imported standalone statement provides none of these inputs. This is the standing chapter/unit setting, not a new proof convenience. The corrected statement restores it, introduces E and Qlb explicitly, and specifies the ambient module-object coefficient category, the chosen-compactification lower shriek, Tate twist and exceptional right adjoint. The condensed-coefficient statement dependencies directly cite the definitions of continuous E-valued functions, finite-subextension-valued Qlb functions, and pullback from the point, rather than attributing those coefficient definitions to the constructibility node. All three conclusions are retained, with the original proof's counit compatibility explicit.

Invertibility cannot simply be ignored: over Spec(F_ell), on the small pro-etale site every object is reduced (its maps to the field are weakly etale); hence the sheaf mu_(ell^n) is the zero abelian group, since u^(ell^n)=1 implies (u-1)^(ell^n)=0. The roots-of-unity rational twist is then zero, so the proposed twisted right adjoint would be zero. Compact-support pushforward of a rational sheaf supported on the zero section is nonzero, ruling out such an adjunction. This illustrates why an arbitrary unspecified V cannot be the intended statement.

The imported step (3) referred to sk-prop-p1-adjunction-rational, which is a unit rather than a node. Its existing node det-prop-p1-adjunction-rational-triangle explicitly supplies exactly the adjunction used. The old proof_deps instead listed cor-amb-affine-space-adjunction, an illustrative finite-coefficient analogue; it is not used in this proof. The imported proof also silently treated the standard compactification as the chosen one. Step 3 now cites independence of compactification. No chapter or unit route is changed: the proof is still extension by zero, projective-line adjunction, composition, and right-adjoint uniqueness.
