## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported statement calls a map canonical without supplying data defining it. The definition it cites (`def-weil-pbc-derived-inverse-limit`) expressly defines a Roos-triangle property rather than a functorial choice of an object. Also, a functor between the ordinary derived categories cannot literally be composed with evaluation from a tower-derived category until an action on towers is supplied. The imported proof's sentence "Cones of a fixed morphism in a triangulated category are canonically isomorphic" is false. Its description of `F(Rlim M)` as the third vertex also uses the wrong vertex: it is the first vertex in the displayed unrotated triangle, or a shifted third vertex after rotation.

Here is an explicit small counterexample to the cone-uniqueness inference. In `D(k)` for a field `k`, take `X=k[-1]`, `Y=k`, and the zero map `X -> Y`. Its cone is `Y direct-sum X[1] = k direct-sum k`. The nonidentity automorphism `(y,x) -> (y+x,x)` fixes the inclusion of `Y` and the projection to `X[1]`. It and the identity are therefore two different isomorphisms of the same cone triangle with identity on its first two vertices. Both maps differ already on degree-zero vector spaces. Thus TR3 does not pick a canonical isomorphism. This refutes the imported general cone argument; the separate type check for this item is that its stated data contain no chosen cone-comparison map.

The correction retains the intended preservation of derived limits and the countable-product hypothesis. It makes the tower/evaluation compatibility precise with a supplied action `F-tilde` and transition-compatible evaluation identifications, writes the canonical product comparison `phi`, and states that the image triangle is a Roos triangle. It then asserts existence of a comparison `c` extending `phi` on both products and proves that EVERY such comparison is an isomorphism. It does not choose `c` canonically from triangulated data alone. This is exactly the distinction made in Stacks Tag 08TC (`definition-derived-limit`, paragraph following the definition), Tag 0H9J (`remark-functoriality-derived-limit`), and Tag 014B (`lemma-cone-triangle-unique-isomorphism`). A comparison supplied by a further strict/enhanced model is covered as soon as it satisfies the two displayed equations; this item itself supplies no such model. No target or unit record was changed.

The unit `sk-prop-sf-cbc` has no proof steps in its unit record and calls for the route through finite reductions and derived limits. The correction keeps this route and the imported Roos-triangle input. I searched the whole DAG and the blueprint for derived limits and countable products.
