## What changed and why

Followed `blueprint_update` and the main-20261015 source, rather than retaining the older snapshot's concluding cross-reference to torsors and the Kummer sequence. The data, maps, hypotheses and four schemes are unchanged. The older statement mixed in its group-axiom proof; that verification is now in the proof steps, as in the current blueprint. Added actual notion dependencies for affine spectrum, polynomial/Laurent rings, quotient algebras, tensor algebras, and closed immersions. Removed `def-sc-gm-torsor` and `def-sc-kummer-sequence`: neither supplies data needed to construct these group schemes. Kept and cited the closed-subgroup definition rather than leaving it only in imported `uses_not_cited` metadata, which is preserved unchanged.

Clarified the existing phrase “Frobenius kernel”: it means relative Frobenius, with the affine-line twist identified using its coordinate. The coordinate map fixes k and sends z to y^p. This adds no perfectness or prime-field hypothesis and changes no intended object. Absolute Frobenius is not generally a k-map: for example over F_p(s) it sends the coefficient s to s^p. The statement makes no such assertion. The fifth proof step verifies the twist identification even over an imperfect field, by the two explicit mutually inverse algebra maps.

The current blueprint proves only the G_m/G_a group axioms and does not check its two closed-subgroup claims or the alpha_p kernel description. The split supplies those calculations, including the otherwise implicit surjection k[t,t^-1] -> k[t]/(t^n-1) and its kernel. The proof is marked agent-written because those expansions go beyond the blueprint's proof paragraph, while each step names its locally read source.
