### Returned Step 2; owner: lem-sp-proetale-scalar-balancing-chain

For any $A_Y$-module sheaf $M$, apply clause 1 of [lem-pur-proetale-inverse-image-tensor-comparison] to $(M,B_Y)$. Transport its coefficient ring along $c_A$ and its second factor along $c_B$. This gives an isomorphism
$$t_M:f_A^*r_Y(M\otimes_{A_Y}B_Y)\longrightarrow r_X(f_A^*M\otimes_{A_X}B_X).$$
Its formula is $f^{-1}(x\otimes s)\mapsto f^{-1}x\otimes c_B(f^{-1}s)$, with the understood transported module actions. For $a\in A_Y$, the balancing equality is preserved because $c_B(f^{-1}(a_Y(a)s))=a_X(c_A(f^{-1}a))c_B(f^{-1}s)$, by Step 1. Locally a section $v$ of the finite constant sheaf $B_X$ is a constant $b\in B$. On the unit-section tensors, multiplying by $v$ on the source replaces $s$ by $bs$; the formula sends this to $f^{-1}x\otimes c_B(f^{-1}b)c_B(f^{-1}s)$, which is $v$ times the original output. These tensors determine the map by the uniqueness clause of the cited comparison, and the equality glues. Thus $t_M$ respects the transported $B_X$-action. Consequently this also gives the $B_X$-linear map from $f_B^*(M\otimes_{A_Y}B_Y)$ after applying the ordinary restriction comparison. Naturality of $t_M$ in $M$ follows from the cited simultaneous naturality; the formula uses the sheafified tensors, not an identification of tensor sections with all sections of a sheaf tensor.
