## Comparison lemma: argument reserved for its later split

This paragraph is not a proof added to the new unsplit node. It explains the precise non-elementary analytic comparison that the item cites. For each i apply bounded Weierstrass division in Yi, regarding the other variables as coefficients. Its remainder projection Ri is linear over the restricted-series algebra in the variables other than Yi, has norm at most one, kills fi times any series, and is the identity on polynomials of Yi-degree less than di. Because the coefficients of fi lie in T and involve no other Yj, finite polynomial reductions in distinct variables commute; density and the norm bounds extend that equality to restricted series. Compose the Ri to get a projection R to the finite T-module of remainders with every Yi-degree less than di. Successive division writes h-R(h) in the algebraic ideal sum fi D, using finitely many convergent quotients. Commutation and the relevant linearities show R(fi h)=0 for every i,h. Thus the kernel of R is exactly that ideal, and R is the identity on the bounded-degree module. The same finite polynomial division gives that module for P/(fi), and the canonical map is the identity on its coefficient representatives. This proves the desired isomorphism when that node is split. The commuting projections and kernel comparison are the reason this is an analytic lemma rather than an unproved 'evaluate convergent series in B' in the item. B has no topology at that stage.
