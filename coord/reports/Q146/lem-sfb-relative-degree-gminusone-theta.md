## Auxiliary proof routes for later workers

For the determinant auxiliary, start with the existing two-term determinant functor and good surjective diagrams. In a kernel-first split surjection of rank-zero two-term models, the differential matrix is block upper triangular; its determinant is det(d_kernel)*det(d_quotient). Kernel normalization therefore sends det(d_source) to det(d_target). The existing good-diagram factorization makes this invariant for every derived isomorphism; equality of affine-local sections glues. The arbitrary pullback is the exterior scalar-extension comparison. Trivialize a twist N locally: changing the trivialization by a unit contributes the equal positive and negative ranks, so cancellation is independent of the frame. The zero complex and field-fibre acyclicity follow directly from the equal-size matrix formula. The shift convention is handled by the degreewise sign isomorphism before transporting the section.

For the first-order auxiliary, choose affine trivializing opens on the separated projective curve, whose intersections are affine. Write a proposed lift of s as s_i+epsilon*b_i. The overlap equation is b_i-t_ij*b_j=t_ij*a_ij*s_j, giving precisely the boundary cocycle. Pairing with t is functoriality of Serre duality for the morphism O_C -> L given by s: its dual is multiplication by s from omega*L^-1 to omega. For a two-term free complex computing derived sections, the same short exact sequence is termwise reduction modulo epsilon; the elementary boundary map is x -> [epsilon^-1*d_A(lift x)]. These descriptions prove equality with the sheaf boundary and show that changing the lift or the model does not change it. This route uses the existing cocycle realization but adds the section comparison needed here.
