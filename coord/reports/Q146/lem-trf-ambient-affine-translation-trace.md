Its proper part is finite type over the Noetherian scheme $P$, hence its source is Noetherian by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian]; the minimal condition on closed subsets [lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-scheme-closed-subsets-minimal] makes its topology Noetherian, and its open part is quasi-compact since all subsets of a Noetherian space are quasi-compact [lem-sfb-noetherian-space-compact-subsets]. This is an allowed compactification for the action lemma. The universal translation $\tau_P(z,b)=(z-b,b)$ has inverse $(z,b)\mapsto(z+b,b)$; both formulas glue since each coordinate is a global coordinate over $P$.

### Old step 3 — new scalar-rigidity node

Conjugate the action to $\alpha=t_P\circ\rho_P\circ t_P^{-1}:A_P[0]\to A_P[0]$. Full faithfulness [lem-weil-pbc-derived-single-fully-faithful] identifies this with a unique module-sheaf automorphism $\alpha_0:A_P\to A_P$. It is multiplication by $b=\alpha_0(1)\in A_P(P)$: for an etale object $U\to P$ and $c\in A_P(U)$, module linearity gives $\alpha_{0,U}(c)=c\alpha_{0,U}(1)=c\,b|_U$. The inverse map similarly gives a section $c$ with $bc=1$, so $b$ is a unit section, also when $A$ is the zero ring. By [lem-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf-sections-locally-constant], $b$ is a Zariski-locally constant $A$-valued function on $|P|$. Moreover the equations of step 2 give $\alpha_T=u^*\alpha$, since $\alpha_T=t_TC_u(u^*\rho_P)C_u^{-1}t_T^{-1}=u^*(t_P\rho_Pt_P^{-1})$. Thus the scalar pulls back to the scalar for the specialized translation.

Cited inputs: `lem-weil-pbc-derived-single-fully-faithful`, `lem-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf-sections-locally-constant`.

### Old step 4 — new scalar-rigidity node

For $s\in S$, let $K=\kappa(s)$ and consider $P_s=\mathbf A^d_K$. Its coordinate ring $K[x_1,\ldots,x_d]$ is a nonzero integral domain by [lem-weil-pbc-polynomial-ring-domain]. By [lem-weil-pbc-spec-irreducible-closed-subsets-primes], the zero prime has closure $V(0)=P_s$. Consequently every nonempty open contains that prime, so two disjoint nonempty opens cannot cover $P_s$: it is nonempty and connected. This includes $d=0$, when the ring is $K$. The restriction of the locally constant function $b$ to this fibre is constant by [lem-weil-pbc-connected-locally-constant-map]. Along the zero section $e:S\to P$ the specialized translation is the identity and its Tate identification is the identity; hence the identity normalization [lem-amb-compact-action-base-change], the Tate identity compatibility [lem-asd-etale-finite-twist-pullback], and step 3 give $e^*b=1$. Each fibre therefore has constant value $1$. Ordinary residue-field fibres suffice here: every point of $P$ over $s$ is the image of a point of $P_s$ (on affine charts the prime defining that point is disjoint from the denominators in $\kappa(s)$). Hence $b=1$ as a function, and [lem-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf-sections-locally-constant] gives $b=1$ as a section. Thus $\alpha=1$ and $t_P\rho_P=t_P$.

Cited inputs: `lem-weil-pbc-polynomial-ring-domain`, `lem-weil-pbc-spec-irreducible-closed-subsets-primes`, `lem-weil-pbc-connected-locally-constant-map`, `lem-amb-compact-action-base-change`, `lem-asd-etale-finite-twist-pullback`, `lem-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf-sections-locally-constant`.

### Old step 5 — parent specialization

Pull $t_P\rho_P=t_P$ back along the given section $a:S\to P$. Under the coordinate identification $W\times_{P,a}S=V$, the base change of $\tau_P$ is exactly $\tau_a(z)=z-a$. The Tate identity and composition compatibilities [lem-asd-etale-finite-twist-pullback] identify the specialized coefficient map with $\kappa_a$. Set $\rho_a=Rq_!(\kappa_a)\circ\operatorname{Ex}_{\tau_a}$, using [lem-amb-unbounded-compact-support-comparisons] to identify the bounded-below exchange with the stated one. The action equation [lem-amb-compact-action-base-change] and trace equation [lem-amb-affine-space-top-trace] give
$$t_q\rho_a C_a=t_q C_a a^*\rho_P=a^*t_P\,a^*\rho_P=a^*(t_P\rho_P)=a^*t_P=t_q C_a.$$
Cancel the isomorphism $C_a$ to obtain the required equality. When $d=0$, $P=S$ and $\tau_a=1$, so the same computation is the identity normalization; when $S$ is empty it is the unique equality of the indicated maps.

Cited inputs: `lem-asd-etale-finite-twist-pullback`, `lem-amb-unbounded-compact-support-comparisons`, `lem-amb-compact-action-base-change`, `lem-amb-affine-space-top-trace`.
