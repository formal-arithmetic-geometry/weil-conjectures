Here is the counterexample, including the action calculation. Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic different from 2 take E=G_m semidirect C_2, with the nonidentity sigma acting on G_m by inversion; equivalently this is split O(2). Write multiplication as (t,epsilon)(u,delta)=(t u^((-1)^epsilon),epsilon+delta). Let N=G_m and Q=C_2, isomorphic to mu_2, with q the projection. N and Q are diagonalizable and q is faithfully flat: its coordinate algebra is a product of two Laurent polynomial rings over the two quotient components. Let the smooth connected G=G_m act by conjugation through N. This action preserves N and is by automorphisms, but s sends (1,sigma) to (s^2,sigma). For example over C, s=2 gives (4,sigma), not (1,sigma). Thus all old hypotheses hold and the conclusion fails. N is not central in E, so the corrected node excludes this example.

In the item, N=ker f is normal in the connected group H0 and multiplicative. Corollary 12.37 applied to H0's conjugation action on N makes N central in H0, as also stated in 12.38.
