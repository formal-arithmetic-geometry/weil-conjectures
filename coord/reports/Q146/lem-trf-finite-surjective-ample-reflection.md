### Returned step 3

For a coherent module $\mathcal E$ on $X$, define $\mathcal P(\mathcal E)$ by eventual vanishing of $H^p(X,\mathcal E\otimes\mathcal L^{\otimes n})$ for every $p>0$, with one bound $n_0\ge0$ for all $p$. Cohomology is derived global sections [pqf-def-cohomology]; the module category has enough injectives [lem-sc-ringed-space-module-sheaves-enough-injectives] and global sections is additive and left exact [lem-sc-global-sections-left-exact]. Thus [lem-weil-pbc-right-derived-universal-delta-functor] gives additive cohomology functors and long exact sequences. The zero module has $\mathcal P$. Tensoring a short exact sequence by $\mathcal L^{\otimes n}$ is exact: on a rank-one trivializing cover [def-sc-invertible-module] the tensor functor [def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product] is the identity, so its stalk sequences are exact and [lem-weil-pbc-module-stalk-exact](5) gives sheaf exactness. If the end terms have $\mathcal P$, the segment $H^p(\mathcal E_1\otimes\mathcal L^{\otimes n})\to H^p(\mathcal E\otimes\mathcal L^{\otimes n})\to H^p(\mathcal E_2\otimes\mathcal L^{\otimes n})$ gives vanishing after the maximum of their two bounds. Finite sums are coherent [lem-weil-pbc-coherent-modules-kernel-cokernel-sum](3). For $r\ge1$, $\mathcal E$ is a retract of $\mathcal E^{\oplus r}$ using the first inclusion and projection; tensor and additive cohomology preserve that retraction, so $\mathcal P(\mathcal E^{\oplus r})$ implies $\mathcal P(\mathcal E)$. Let $\mathcal J\subseteq\mathcal O_X$ be quasi-coherent with $\mathcal J_\xi=\mathcal O_{X,\xi}$. Coherent modules on locally Noetherian schemes are quasi-coherent [pqf-lem-coherent-ft-locnoeth]. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-affine-pushforward-ideal-product] to the affine $f$, $\mathcal F$, and $\mathcal J$. Its clause (1) says that $\mathcal H:=f^{-1}\mathcal J\cdot\mathcal F$ is a quasi-coherent submodule of $\mathcal F$, and its clause (2) gives the equality $\mathcal J\mathcal G=f_*\mathcal H$ as submodules of $\mathcal G$. Since $Y$ is locally Noetherian, [lem-weil-pbc-coherent-kernel-cokernel-locnoeth](2) gives coherence of $\mathcal H$; finite coherent pushforward then gives coherence of $\mathcal J\mathcal G=f_*\mathcal H$. Thus both the identity and coherence on $Y$ used below are supplied by cited statements.

### Returned step 5

For $n\ge0$ put $\mathcal N=\mathcal L^{\otimes n}$ (with $\mathcal N=\mathcal O_X$ for $n=0$). It is invertible by its rank-one trivializations [def-sc-invertible-module]. The affine morphism $f$ is quasi-compact and quasi-separated by [lem-weil-pbc-affine-morphism-permanence](3), so the invertible-module clause of [lem-pc-projection-formula] applies to $E=\mathcal H[0]$ and $\mathcal N$. Tensoring by $\mathcal N$ and by $f^*\mathcal N$ is exact by the same local identity argument as Step 3, so the derived tensors here are ordinary tensors. The quasi-coherent tensor-product theorem [lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-tensor-product-affine-sections] applies to $\mathcal H$ and $f^*\mathcal N$. Affine higher-direct-image vanishing [lem-weil-pbc-affine-morphism-cohomology-edge] for $\mathcal H$ and $\mathcal H\otimes f^*\mathcal N$ therefore turns the derived projection isomorphism, in degree zero, into $f_*\mathcal H\otimes\mathcal N\cong f_*(\mathcal H\otimes f^*\mathcal N)$. Concretely this is the ordinary projection map: on any open $U$ trivializing $\mathcal N$, both sides are $(f_*\mathcal H)|_U$ and the map is the identity. This also proves compatibility on overlaps, since the projection map is defined by multiplication by the pulled-back local section. By the tensor-pullback definition [def-pc-module-pullback], $f^*(\mathcal L^{\otimes n})\cong(f^*\mathcal L)^{\otimes n}$: both are trivial on $f^{-1}U$ and have the same pulled-back $n$-th-power transition functions.

### Returned step 6

Apply [lem-weil-pbc-affine-morphism-cohomology-edge] to the quasi-coherent $\mathcal H\otimes(f^*\mathcal L)^{\otimes n}$ (quasi-coherence was checked in Step 5). Steps 4-5 give for every $p,n\ge0$ the isomorphisms $H^p(X,\mathcal J\mathcal G\otimes\mathcal L^{\otimes n})\cong H^p(X,f_*(\mathcal H\otimes(f^*\mathcal L)^{\otimes n}))\cong H^p(Y,\mathcal H\otimes(f^*\mathcal L)^{\otimes n})$. The sheaf $\mathcal H$ is coherent by Step 4, $Y$ is proper over the Noetherian ring $R$, and $f^*\mathcal L$ is invertible and ample. Consequently [lem-weil-pbc-coherent-serre-vanishing] gives one $n_0$ for which the right side vanishes for all $p>0$ and $n\ge n_0$. Hence $\mathcal P(\mathcal J\mathcal G)$ holds. By [lem-weil-pbc-ideal-module-product-stalk](2), $(\mathcal J\mathcal G)_\xi=\mathcal J_\xi\mathcal G_\xi=\mathcal G_\xi$, with the equality through its inclusion into $\mathcal G_\xi$. The coherent submodule $\mathcal G'=\mathcal J\mathcal G$ thus verifies devissage clause (3)(c), while Step 4 verified (3)(a) and (3)(b).

### Returned step 7

The property $\mathcal P$ satisfies every hypothesis of [lem-weil-pbc-coherent-property-devissage-integral-subschemes]: $X$ is Noetherian by Step 2, clauses (0)-(2) hold by Step 3, and the coherent $\mathcal G$ and $\mathcal G'$ required by (3) were verified in Steps 4-6. Hence $\mathcal P$ holds for every coherent module on $X$. In particular it holds for every quasi-coherent ideal $\mathcal I\subseteq\mathcal O_X$: $\mathcal O_X$ is quasi-coherent of finite type, hence coherent by [pqf-lem-coherent-ft-locnoeth], and [lem-weil-pbc-coherent-kernel-cokernel-locnoeth](2) makes $\mathcal I$ coherent. Choose $n\ge\max(1,n_0)$ for its bound; then $H^1(X,\mathcal I\otimes\mathcal L^{\otimes n})=0$. The exact ideal-twist vanishing hypothesis of [lem-trf-ideal-twist-vanishing-implies-ample] is now satisfied, so $\mathcal L$ is ample. Together with Step 1 this proves the stated equivalence.
