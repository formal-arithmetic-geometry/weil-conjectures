### Original step 1

Write $0=i_0$, and let $p_i:S_i\to S_0$ ($i\ge0$) and $p:S\to S_0$ be the transition morphisms and the limit projection. Cover $S_0$ by affine opens and their preimages in $V_0$ by affine opens; since $V_0$ is quasi-compact, finitely many of these give a finite affine open covering $V_0=\bigcup_{j=1}^mY_{j,0}$ in which each $Y_{j,0}$ maps into an affine open $W_{j,0}\subset S_0$. Since $U_0$ is quasi-compact and $V_0$ is quasi-separated, $g_0$ is quasi-compact by [lem-weil-pbc-qcqs-morphism-permanence] (1), so each $g_0^{-1}(Y_{j,0})$ is quasi-compact [def-weil-finite-type-morphism] and has a finite affine open covering $g_0^{-1}(Y_{j,0})=\bigcup_{k=1}^{n_j}X_{jk,0}$. Write $W_{j,0}=\operatorname{Spec}R^{(j)}_0$, $Y_{j,0}=\operatorname{Spec}B_{j,0}$ and $X_{jk,0}=\operatorname{Spec}C_{jk,0}$ [def-weil-affine-spectrum]; then $B_{j,0}$ is an $R^{(j)}_0$-algebra through $Y_{j,0}\to W_{j,0}$, and $g_0$ restricts to $\operatorname{Spec}$ of an $R^{(j)}_0$-algebra map $\varphi_{jk,0}:B_{j,0}\to C_{jk,0}$. Because $g_0$ is locally of finite type, $C_{jk,0}$ is a finitely generated $B_{j,0}$-algebra by [lem-weil-pbc-lft-affine-pairs].

### Original step 2

For $i\ge0$ put $W_{j,i}=p_i^{-1}(W_{j,0})\subset S_i$ and $W_j=p^{-1}(W_{j,0})\subset S$. Each $p_i$ is affine [pqf-def-affine-morphism], so $W_{j,i}=\operatorname{Spec}R^{(j)}_i$ is affine; by [lem-weil-pbc-limit-affine-transition] (its clause on $f_0^{-1}(U_0)$, then its affine clause for the system $(W_{j,i})_{i\ge0}$, whose transition morphisms are affine) $W_j=\operatorname{Spec}R^{(j)}$ with $R^{(j)}=\varinjlim_{i\ge0}R^{(j)}_i$. For $T=S_i$ or $T=S$, with $T_j\subset T$ the preimage of $W_{j,0}$, the preimage of $X_{jk,0}$ in $U_0\times_{S_0}T$ is the open subscheme $X_{jk,0}\times_{S_0}T$, and it equals $X_{jk,0}\times_{W_{j,0}}T_j$, since every morphism to it lands over $T_j$ (universal property [def-pc-fibre-product]); likewise for $Y_{j,0}$ in $V_0\times_{S_0}T$. By [lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra], $X_{jk,i}:=X_{jk,0}\times_{S_0}S_i=\operatorname{Spec}(R^{(j)}_i\otimes_{R^{(j)}_0}C_{jk,0})$ and $Y_{j,i}:=Y_{j,0}\times_{S_0}S_i=\operatorname{Spec}(R^{(j)}_i\otimes_{R^{(j)}_0}B_{j,0})$, and, comparing projections, the restriction $X_{jk,i}\to Y_{j,i}$ of $g_i$ is $\operatorname{Spec}(\mathrm{id}\otimes\varphi_{jk,0})$; the same holds over $S$ with $R^{(j)}$ in place of $R^{(j)}_i$, giving $X_{jk}\to Y_j$. As preimages of the coverings of step 1, the $X_{jk,i}$ cover $U_i$, the $Y_{j,i}$ cover $V_i$, and $g_i(X_{jk,i})\subset Y_{j,i}$; the same over $S$.

### Original step 3

Fix $(j,k)$. Since $g$ is unramified, its restriction $X_{jk}\to Y_j$ is unramified by [lem-weil-pbc-unramified-base-change-open] (2), so $R^{(j)}\otimes_{R^{(j)}_0}B_{j,0}\to R^{(j)}\otimes_{R^{(j)}_0}C_{jk,0}$ is an unramified ring map by [lem-weil-pbc-unramified-ring-map-iff-unramified-affine]. Apply [lem-trf-colimit-unramified-algebra] to the directed set $\{i\in I:i\ge0\}$, the rings $A_i=R^{(j)}_i$ with colimit $R^{(j)}$, and $\varphi_{jk,0}:B_{j,0}\to C_{jk,0}$, which is of finite type by step 1: there is $i_{jk}\ge0$ with $R^{(j)}_{i_{jk}}\otimes B_{j,0}\to R^{(j)}_{i_{jk}}\otimes C_{jk,0}$ unramified, so $X_{jk,i_{jk}}\to Y_{j,i_{jk}}$ is unramified by [lem-weil-pbc-unramified-ring-map-iff-unramified-affine]. There are finitely many pairs, so directedness of $I$ gives $i\ge0$ with $i\ge i_{jk}$ for all of them ($i=0$ if there are none). For $i'=i_{jk}\le i$, writing $R_\bullet=R^{(j)}_\bullet$, $B_0=B_{j,0}$, $C_0=C_{jk,0}$, associativity of tensor products gives $(R_{i'}\otimes_{R_0}C_0)\otimes_{R_{i'}\otimes_{R_0}B_0}(R_i\otimes_{R_0}B_0)\cong C_0\otimes_{B_0}(R_i\otimes_{R_0}B_0)\cong R_i\otimes_{R_0}C_0$ compatibly with the maps from $R_i\otimes_{R_0}B_0$, so by [lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra] $X_{jk,i}\to Y_{j,i}$ is the base change of $X_{jk,i'}\to Y_{j,i'}$ along $Y_{j,i}\to Y_{j,i'}$, hence unramified by [lem-weil-pbc-unramified-base-change-open] (1); by [lem-weil-pbc-unramified-ring-map-iff-unramified-affine] its ring map is of finite type with vanishing Kähler differentials. Thus $U_i$ is covered by the affine opens $X_{jk,i}$, each mapped by $g_i$ into the affine open $Y_{j,i}$ by a finite type ring map with $\Omega=0$; by the affine-cover characterization of unramified morphisms recorded in [def-weil-unramified-morphism] (Stacks 02GE with Definition 02G4), $g_i$ is unramified.
