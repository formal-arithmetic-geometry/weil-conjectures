Edge cases checked before defining:

1. h = identity: each etale object is sent to its identity-composed structure map, each section and tensor coefficient is unchanged, and the pointed germ is the identical germ (up to the standard category identity transport).
2. h swaps two components: evaluation at the first component is the old second-component evaluation. A trivializer is moved with h.inv, while the fibre is moved forward with h.hom. This distinguishes the two directions.
3. For Spec k and a field automorphism, the lift to U stays fixed and its structure map is postcomposed; the fibre is at y ≫ h.hom, as required by Stacks 03Q1(3).
4. Empty Y: covers and finite locally constant systems are still allowed. There is no geometric-point binder inhabited by force. All fibre constructions are conditional on an actual supplied point.
5. Zero modules, torsion integral systems and varying ranks are allowed: no finite-free, nonzero, connectedness or rank hypothesis was introduced. n = 0 is level one of the recorded integral system, and all transition formulas include it.
6. Identity and ramified coefficient inclusions use the existing ceiling indices and scalar maps; pullback does not alter them. Scalar comparison fixes both a and s, and hence also handles zero tensors and arbitrary finite common stages.
