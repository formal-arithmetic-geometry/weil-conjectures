## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the complement cannot assert that the coefficient dual is automatically an object of the bounded constructible finite-Tor category on every permitted T. Fix A=F_ell with ell different from p, take T0 to be the disjoint union of A^d over F_q for all d>=1, and take f0=id_T0, which is smooth. Let i:Z->T be the union of the origins after geometric base change and K=i_*A_Z in degree zero, with its canonical Weil structure. Constructibility is local on affine opens in the current condensed definition; K is constructible and flat over A, hence has the single cohomological and Tor interval [0,0]. On the d-th component, closed-immersion tensor/Hom adjunction and affine-space zero-section purity give

    RHom(i_*A, A) = i_* A(-d)[-2d].

I checked the sign and the hypotheses against cor-pur-affine-space-zero-section-purity: use its base B=Spec k separately for each finite d, not a fictitious single purity theorem on an infinite-dimensional scheme. Cohomology in degree 2d is nonzero. No finite interval bounds the dual. A fixed Tate twist does not change those degrees. The pair remains defined in the ambient derived category; it cannot be called an object of the small pair category. The statement now retains the ambient formula for every K and restricts to the small category only on its actual object domain. It imposes no global extra hypothesis on the pair definition and asserts no false all-object dual closure.
