## Review by Q160

I read the statement and proof of 0AT4 with the item open. It assumes a local essentially finite type map of arbitrary local rings, a B-linear map from a finite B-module, an A-flat target with no finiteness hypothesis, and injectivity modulo the base maximal ideal. These are exactly the item's hypotheses, and all three conclusions, including finite presentation over B rather than over A, are retained. I did not replace 0AT4 by the stronger A-linear general case: A-linearity is used only inside the henselian intermediate argument, where 0AT3 proves it. The final passage to henselization needs the original B-linearity to define the tensor map.

For the first new node I read 05U7 in flat.tex at its lemma-flat-finite-type-local-colimit-free label. The source requires the base henselian, the local map essentially of finite type and the module finite over B and flat over A; it gives free modules, not necessarily finite free ones, and injective maps after reduction modulo the maximal ideal. Those precise conditions and conclusions are written. A small filtered index is stated explicitly so both cited colimit nodes apply. I omit the unused Mittag-Leffler assertion. For the second new node I read 05PG at theorem-flattening-local. For A'=A/J with J contained in the maximal ideal, B'=B/JB is local, and the primes containing the two maximal-ideal images in the theorem consist of its maximal ideal alone. Localization there changes no module. Thus the source's condition is exactly flatness of P/JP over A/J. Its ideal is finitely generated under the two presentation assumptions in the new statement. Taking J equal to the maximal ideal makes P/JP a vector space, so the universal ideal is contained in that maximal ideal. No arbitrary quotient or Noetherian assumption is smuggled into the specialization.

Step 1: the new 05U7 node supplies the free filtered diagram. lem-weil-pbc-tensor-commutes-with-colimits states exactly the comparison used after reduction and for tensoring with Q. lem-weil-pbc-filtered-colimit-modules-elementwise supplies representation at a stage and eventual vanishing. lem-weil-pbc-free-module-projective supplies projectivity, and lem-trf-local-universal-injective-residue requires only projectivity of the source, flatness of the target and injectivity on the residue fibre. All hold at each stage. I wrote the representative-killing argument; there is no assumed commutation of kernels with a colimit or missing finite-rank hypothesis.

The definition def-weil-pbc-henselian-terminology actually states the finite-algebra idempotent lifting condition, which proves henselianity of A/I immediately, since finite A/I-algebras are finite A-algebras. def-weil-pbc-flat-ring-map states the injection-tensor criterion used to prove flatness of T/IT.
