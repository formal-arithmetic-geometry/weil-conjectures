### lem-pur-relative-kummer-coefficient-normalization

Finite coefficient freeness, natural root trivialization, module/abelian comparisons and natural Kummer boundary.

Original step 1 (verbatim):

Put $r=\ell^N$. The affine-line datum already assumes $e(E/\mathbf Q_\ell)\mid n$, so $N=n/e\geq1$, $rA=0$, and $A$ is finite free over $\mathbf Z/r\mathbf Z$ by [lem-pur-finite-kummer-level]. Since $\ell\neq p=\operatorname{char}k$, $r$ is invertible in every $k$-scheme; no condition on whether $p$ divides the exponent $N$ is needed. Choose a primitive $r$-th root $\zeta\in k$ by [lem-sc-primitive-root]. For any $k$-scheme $T$, the factorization $X^r-1=\prod_{a\in\mathbf Z/r}(X-\zeta^a)$ has pairwise invertible differences. Thus the roots-of-unity scheme is the disjoint union of $r$ copies of $T$, and $a\mapsto\zeta^a$ identifies the constant $\mathbf Z/r$-sheaf with $\mu_{r,T}$. Tensoring this map with $A$ identifies $A_T$ with $A(1)_T$. These identifications commute with every $k$-morphism because the same roots and the same polynomial factorization pull back. In particular all the twisted coefficient sheaves in the question are inverse images from the base, and the order of their underlying finite abelian group is a power of $\ell$.

The computations below may be made with underlying abelian etale sheaves. [lem-sbc-higher-direct-image-forget-comparison] identifies the underlying abelian sheaf of each module-valued higher direct image with the corresponding abelian higher direct image, including degrees zero and negative degrees. [lem-weil-pbc-etale-module-cohomology-forget] supplies the natural module/abelian comparison on global cohomology, also applied to each etale test scheme. The comparisons can be computed with resolutions: an injective module sheaf has acyclic underlying abelian sheaf for global sections by [lem-weil-pbc-etale-module-injective-forget-acyclic], and [src-right-derived-functor-plus-acyclic-unit] computes the derived functor on such a resolution.
