Hypothesis and clause correspondence:

- Algebraically closed field k: `(k : Type u) [Field k] [IsAlgClosed k]`.
- A k-scheme: `(X : Scheme.{u}) [X.Over (Spec (.of k))]`; its structure map is
  `X ↘ Spec (.of k)`. Both schemes and k have the standard scheme universe u.
- Reduced: `[IsReduced X]`.
- Finite type: `[LocallyOfFiniteType (X ↘ Spec (.of k))]` and
  `[QuasiCompact (X ↘ Spec (.of k))]`, the two recorded clauses of the dependency.
- Pure dimension one: `(hpure : X.IsPureDimension 1)`, with the recorded componentwise
  Krull-dimension definition. No separatedness, properness or connectedness is assumed.
- The closed rational point: `(x : X) (hx : IsClosed ({x} : Set X))`. Over an algebraically
  closed field, a closed point of a finite-type scheme has residue field k via its structure
  map (Mathlib `residueFieldIsoBase`); thus this specifies exactly the point in the node,
  without an extra arbitrary choice of a map Spec k → X.
- Componentwise normalization: `X.reducedNormalization`, `X.fromReducedNormalization`.
  Their definition is the coproduct of normalizations of the reduced irreducible components.
- b is the cardinality of the actual topological inverse-image fibre:
  `Nat.card {y : X.reducedNormalization // X.fromReducedNormalization y = x}`. This is
  neither the number of global components through x nor the cardinality of a set of lifts.
- The normalization quotient is the stalk of `conductorCokernel X`, which is the existing
  sheafified cokernel of `SheafOfModules.unitToPushforwardObjUnit`. Taking stalks is exact,
  so its stalk is precisely `(ν_* O)_x / O_x` for the canonical inclusion. The construction
  `normalizationDeltaModule p x` then uses `ModuleCat.restrictScalars
  (Orgogozo.localScalar p x)` to supply its structural k-vector-space structure.
- Finiteness is stated explicitly as `Finite` of the fibre type and `Module.Finite k` of
  that quotient. Delta is `Module.finrank k (normalizationDeltaModule ... x)`; its finite
  dimensionality is a conclusion, not a hypothesis or a convention hiding infinite rank.
- b ≥ 1: the third conjunct is `1 ≤ Nat.card ...`.
- Delta ≥ b-1: the fourth conjunct is `Nat.card ... - 1 ≤ Module.finrank k ...`.
  Natural-number subtraction is exact here because b ≥ 1 is a separate conclusion.
- Equality iff the completed local algebra is the axes algebra: the fifth conjunct uses
  `IsOrdinaryMultiplePoint ... (Nat.card ...)`. Its definition gives `1 ≤ n` and an actual
  ring equivalence from `AdicCompletion (IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal (X.presheaf.stalk x)) ...`
  to `MvPowerSeries (Fin n) k` modulo the ideal generated by all mixed `X i * X j`.
  The equation on each `a : k` explicitly preserves the structural scalar map, hence
  expresses a k-algebra equivalence. It is not merely an abstract ring equivalence.
  Positivity repeats the already asserted b ≥ 1 and does not change the criterion.
- Ordinary one-fold iff smooth: the sixth conjunct is equivalence with
  `∃ U : X.Opens, x ∈ U ∧ Smooth (U.ι ≫ ...)`, exactly smoothness at x.
- Ordinary two-fold iff node: the seventh conjunct is equivalence with the existing
  `Orgogozo.IsOrdinaryDoublePoint`, preserving its scalar-compatible completion definition.
- Smooth points have delta zero: the eighth conjunct is the implication from the same
  smooth-at-x predicate to `Module.finrank k ... = 0`.
- Nodes have b=2 and delta=1: the ninth conjunct gives both equalities under
