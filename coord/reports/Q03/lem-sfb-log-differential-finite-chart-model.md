1. **Ordinary etale derivations.** `def-weil-etale-morphism` actually includes flatness and unramifiedness, so `lem-weil-pbc-flat-unramified-formally-etale` applies. Its statement is unique lifting for affine first-order test thickenings, exactly the square-zero ring `B ⊕ H` used here. Applying uniqueness to the base etale algebra proves that the extended derivation kills all new base functions. This is ordinary etaleness, not an added log-lifting hypothesis.

2. **Chart neighbourhoods.** `def-trf-fs-log-blowup` explicitly supplies etale-local finitely generated integral charts and describes the chart pushout at geometric points. `lem-weil-pbc-etale-cover-geometric-point-lift` supplies the actual lift of the specified geometric point into a chart cover, with the same algebraically closed field. `lemma-weil-pbc-affine-neighbourhood-cofinal` supplies affine refinements. `lem-sfb-stack-chartwise-smooth-etale-calculus` states base-change and composition stability, including for schemes as a special case, and is not being used for a stronger log claim. No affine intersection is assumed to be affine.

3. **Base chart generators.** `lem-trf-associated-log-universal-stalks` states both the geometric-stalk pushout and its sheafification/restriction description. This is exactly what is used to express each image m_i as [v_i,u_i] locally. `def-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks`, the stated cofilteredness of pointed neighbourhoods, and `lem-weil-pbc-site-sheafification-sections-local` permit one common refinement for the finite list. 4. **Finiteness hypotheses.** The generic scheme calculus states local finite presentation is stable under the refinements. The affine finite-polynomial presentation then comes from the precise definition `def-sbc-locally-finite-presentation`, after shrinking both affine opens if needed. `pqf-lem-locnoeth-lft` has a locally Noetherian target and locally finite type source morphism, here U to X; both hypotheses hold. `pqf-lem-affine-open-locnoeth` then applies to U itself, and really concludes that A is Noetherian. 6. **Finite presentation.** Differentiating a polynomial expression gives generation of ordinary differentials by dx_j; the defining relations of group completion give generation by the finite p_j. 7. **The actual sheaf H.** `lem-weil-pbc-spec-module-tilde-sections` asserts quasi-coherence and the sections formula, including D(1). `lem-sc-etale-qcoherent-descent` is a sheaf theorem, unlike the definition-only node for the associated etale presheaf. `lem-weil-pbc-module-pullback-affine-sections` supplies the tensor identification with the pure-tensor map and its naturality, with both selected opens affine. Here they are the entire affine schemes. The restriction formula follows by composing pullback units on a section coming from W and multiplying by the restricted scalar.

8. **Derivation on every etale object.** This is an application of the already proved step 1 to the sheaf model's affine sections. `lem-weil-pbc-etale-composition-cancellation` says a morphism of etale U-objects is etale, and `lem-weil-pbc-affine-etale-essentially-small-cover` supplies affine cover density. 9. **Logification calculation.** I checked both representatives of the pushout relation [pe,u]=[p,beta(e)u]. Their assigned values agree because lambda_e=beta(e)^(-1)d beta(e) for a unit image.
