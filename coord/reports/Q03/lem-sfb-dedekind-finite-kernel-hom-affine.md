The existing `lem-a1-relative-affinization-compatible-charts`, `lem-trf-canonical-affine-charts-qc-open-immersion`, `lem-trf-quasi-affine-canonical-affine-embedding`, `lem-weil-pbc-flat-base-change-global-sections-qcqs` and `lem-sfb-dedekind-torsionfree-module-flat` give its geometric and flat algebra ingredients. The novel assertion is extension of the group operations, which those statements do not give.

Here is the construction to hand to its next worker. For each affine W in J, G_W is qcqs and flat over B, since it is an open of the separated finite-type flat G. Multiplication by any nonzero b in B is injective on its structure sheaf and hence on its sections. Thus A(W) is torsion-free and is B-flat by 0AUW. If X,Y are qcqs B-flat schemes, the canonical map Gamma(X,O) tensor_B Gamma(Y,O) -> Gamma(X x_S Y,O) is an isomorphism: over an affine open V of Y, use 02KH for X and the flat ring Gamma(V,O). These are the actual pullback product maps and agree on restrictions. Take the finite Cech equalizer for a finite affine cover of Y (and affine covers of its qc intersections). Tensoring that equalizer with the flat Gamma(X,O) preserves its kernel, so global sections give the claimed isomorphism. The same calculation holds for three or more factors.

Cover J^n by products of its affine opens. The preceding calculation identifies the functions on G^n over each such product with those on H^n, for n=1,2,3. On principal subopens the identification localizes, by the qcqs global-sections localization statement (the flat-base-change argument in 02KH). Consequently the sheaf map O_(H^n) -> (j^n)_*O_(G^n) is an isomorphism; the assertion is not merely an equality of a few global section rings. For the multiplication, regard m_J(q x q):H^2 -> J as the base map. The morphism G^2 -> G -> H over J therefore extends uniquely to H^2 -> H, since H is affine over J and functions agree on every inverse image of an affine open of J. The inverse extends in the same way, and the identity is j e_G. The three-factor equality of functions proves associativity by restriction to G^3; the one/two-factor equalities prove the identity and inverse diagrams. These maps make q,j homomorphisms. No affineness theorem for group homomorphisms was used. Finally, the n=1 sheaf identity commutes with flat base change B -> K by 02KH for the qcqs open immersion j, giving precisely the generic sheaf comparison in the statement. No comparison with nonflat special-fibre functions is asserted. No finite generation of A(W) or finite type of H is needed or claimed.

For `lem-sfb-field-open-group-image-closed`, I read groupoids.tex Tag 047S, `lemma-open-subgroup-closed-over-field`, including its proof using translation of an open. Its statement is used exactly, not the assertion that every underlying point set is a topological group. The reduced closed complement Z of the open image is stable under translations by image points, tested after common field extensions. The translation map Z x_k P -> Q has open image by the preceding translate-open lemma, and that image is Z: containment follows by multiplying by the inverse of the P-point; reverse containment follows by the unit section. Hence Z is open and the given image is closed. This needs no finite-type or reducedness assumption on either group.

For `lem-sfb-dedekind-vertical-clopen-open-affine`, I read divisors.tex Tag 07ZT, `lemma-complement-locally-principal-closed-subscheme`, and its principal-open proof. The additional assertion is the following local boundary reduction, independent of groups. On an affine W=Spec C of X let the closed complement of U be V(I). Its generic fibre is empty, so I tensor_B K is the unit ideal; explicitly some nonzero a in B belongs to I after mapping to C. Thus this part of the boundary lies over the finitely many closed points dividing a. Near a boundary point x over s, shrink the base to remove the other such closed points and make s principal (the maximal ideal of a Dedekind domain is invertible). Since U_s is closed in X_s, remove it to obtain an open neighbourhood of x. On an affine subneighbourhood V within that open, the remaining boundary is exactly V_s: there is no boundary outside s and there are no U-points in V_s. If t is the chosen local base equation of s, then U intersect V is D(t) in V and is affine. At points of U choose affine neighbourhoods contained in U. These neighbourhoods form a target cover on which the inclusion is affine, hence the inclusion is affine. Pullback of t need not be a nonzerodivisor, which is why 07ZT, not the effective-Cartier density assertion, is the appropriate final calculation. This argument allows non-Noetherian X, nonflat X, vertical X and empty fibres. The node retains the available quasi-compactness of i, although the local calculation needs no stronger finiteness on X.
