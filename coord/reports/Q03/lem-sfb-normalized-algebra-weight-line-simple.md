For the geometric auxiliary I read Milne's Grassmannian construction and Borel fixed point statement in full at the places above. The intended proof uses d equal to the least positive dimension of a B-stable subspace. On Gr_d(M), the equations that the composite universal subbundle -> M -> M/universal subbundle vanish after every basis operator of B cut out a closed subscheme X. It has a k-point by the choice of d, is projective, and is preserved scheme-theoretically by T because T normalizes B. Borel fixed point applies since a split torus is smooth, connected and solvable; its fixed k-point gives a T-stable B-submodule of dimension d, necessarily simple. This is a genuine geometric input, with no irreducibility, semisimplicity or chosen weight assumption. Its proof is not placed in the unsplit node's statement or proof fields.
