## Proof of lem-weil-pbc-exact-faithful-torsion

1. Additivity gives A(n id_F)=n id_AF. Preservation of kernels therefore identifies A(F[n]) with (AF)[n], including their inclusions into AF. For n dividing m, the transition F[n] → F[m] is uniquely determined by its composite with the monomorphism F[m] → F. These kernel identifications consequently respect every transition, not just the individual objects.
2. The positive integers under divisibility form a small directed poset. Apply lem-weil-pbc-left-adjoint-preserves-colimits to the given adjunction and this diagram. The resulting colimit isomorphism identifies A(c_F) with c_AF because both composites with each colimit structure map are the corresponding kernel inclusion. Lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-colimits-forgetful, clauses (1)–(3), identifies these module kernels and colimits with their underlying abelian ones on both sites.
3. If c_F is invertible, A(c_F), hence c_AF, is invertible. Conversely lift the inverse of A(c_F) through fullness to h:F → colim_n F[n]. Applying A gives A(c_F h)=id and A(h c_F)=id; faithfulness gives both inverse identities before applying A. Thus c_F is invertible. This is sheaf torsion, not a bound for all sections or a single annihilator of F.
