## Statement correction: STATEMENT-DISPUTED

The imported phrase “zero-dimensional” needs an explicit convention. If read as literal Krull dimension equal to zero, it is false: take any perfect field k, C the affine line, and U=C with j the identity. The reduced boundary is empty and has dimension minus infinity by `def-kun-topological-krull-dimension`. The corrected statement says dimension at most zero, explicitly giving the empty dimension. It retains the assertion of smoothness of pure relative dimension zero, which is vacuous on the empty scheme. This is faithful to the source's finite disjoint union (including the empty union) and the convention in `def-weil-pure-dimension`. No additional hypothesis, nonemptiness assumption, or new conclusion is introduced. The meaning of perfect is also written explicitly as every algebraic extension being separable.
