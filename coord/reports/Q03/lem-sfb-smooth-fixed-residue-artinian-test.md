## Arguments retained for the two statement-only nodes

For lem-sfb-fixed-residue-lifts-truncated-section, put E=C/(J^2+m^N), F=C/(J+m^N). A surjective local-to-local map C -> S pulls the maximal ideal of S back to m, so J lies in m, image(m) is the maximal ideal n of S, and F=S/n^N. Both E,F are nonzero Artinian local by lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-local-quotient-artinian-local. Their common residue is C/m=S/n=k. Filter the kernel K of E -> F using lem-weil-pbc-artinian-ideal-length-one-filtration. Each quotient E/K_i has that same specified residue k and each adjacent surjection has length-one kernel: submodules of K_(i+1)/K_i are the same whether scalars are restricted from E/K_i to E. Start with A -> F. Descending induction on the finite filtration lifts this map compatibly with R. Every lifted map reduces to the same canonical A -> k because all adjacent residue maps are identities. This is the extra invariant absent from the arbitrary-residue theorem's statement. The final map A -> E takes every element outside q to a unit, so extends to a local R-map S -> E. It kills n^N since the maximal ideal of E has Nth power zero. Hence it factors through F=S/n^N, giving sigma:F -> E. The composite with E -> F agrees with the identity on A, hence on its localization S, and finally on the quotient F. These are existence proofs, not definitions chosen from a sorried existential assertion.

For lem-sfb-truncated-polynomial-sections-smooth, C=P_Q is Noetherian by finite-type Noetherian permanence and localization, and J is finite. Apply lem-weil-pbc-artin-rees to J in C and m, choosing N>=2 large enough that m^N intersect J is contained in mJ. The assumed section at this N satisfies all the hypotheses of lem-weil-pbc-truncated-section-conormal-residue-kernel. Its conclusion says exactly that delta:J/J^2 -> S tensor_C Omega(C/R) is injective after quotienting both modules by n. Quotient by n is tensor with k, so this is residue conormal injectivity. Here C is a localization of a finite polynomial algebra, hence formally smooth over R with finite free differentials, and J is finitely generated. Mathlib's local Jacobian theorem Algebra.FormallySmooth.iff_injective_lTensor_residueField (Smooth/Local.lean:34) therefore gives formal smoothness of S=A_q. Equivalently the localized conormal complex has zero kernel and projective cokernel; over the local ring S the finite projective cokernel is free. Finite type over Noetherian R gives finite presentation by lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-noetherian-finite-presentation. Apply the smooth-at-prime criterion, or Mathlib Algebra.IsSmoothAt.exists_notMem_smooth, to obtain g outside q with A_g smooth. This proves a geometric conclusion from a specified family of algebra sections; it makes no Artinian lifting assumption and is strictly smaller than the item.
