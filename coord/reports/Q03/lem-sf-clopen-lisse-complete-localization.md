## Review by Q03

I checked the statement against the portion of 09C3 it extracts, and against the section-and-partition construction in 098F. The categorical hypothesis is enough: an epimorphism of represented sheaves is evaluated at T, lifting the identity. It is unnecessary to infer geometric w-contractibility first. T need not be Noetherian, connected, finite type, or have ell invertible. The coefficient ring is the actual inverse limit of constant quotient sheaves, not the constant sheaf of the completed ring. The original statement is preserved in full.

Step 1 uses the definition of locally constant sheaf for its actual trivializing covering, the explicit finite-affine-refinement clause of sk-def-pe-site, and the constant-coefficient/localization clauses of lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization. Finiteness is used only for the set J and then for a covering of the affine target. Successive pullbacks and composition of coverings provide a common covering; no unproved simultaneous trivialization lemma is assumed. The finite models are vector spaces, so choosing finite bases supplies the ranks. For empty J the identity covering is explicit.

Step 2 uses lem-weil-pbc-proetale-finite-coproduct-evaluation to form P as a site object and represent the sheaf coproduct. Its hypotheses hold because the selected V_alpha are affine, hence qcqs, and the index is finite. The local-epi criterion says exactly that the lifts on the pulled-back covering make h_P to h_T epic. The subcanonical node makes these actual sheaves, and the full Yoneda Hom/evaluation bijection, not mere full faithfulness, converts the identity lift to a scheme section. The scheme-coproduct node gives clopen, disjoint covering images. The open-factorization node applies to the restriction of s whose image lies in the chosen component, an actual open subscheme. Constant local models are pulled along these maps by the localization node. Closed subsets of the compact affine underlying space are quasi-compact. This step does not infer a factorization through an arbitrary locally closed stratum. Every theorem just described is in its uses list and its quoted statement is in citations.json.

Step 3 is an elementary application of the local-epi criterion to a displayed sheaf extension, not an invocation of the module-gluing theorem for sheaves of sets. The extension's value on V is F(V times_T U), and the sheaf condition follows from pullback of coverings. Local lifts on V times_T U plus the complementary open V times_T Z cover V. The value on the empty object is a singleton. Thus its map is epic on T and evaluation produces exactly F(U) to G(U). The localization node and localized-ringed-site definition provide the slice-site identifications. This checks inheritance of categorical weak contractibility without applying a theorem that assumes the desired property on U.

Step 4 applies the exact-evaluation node to U with the property just proved. Its statement allows any commutative ring sheaf C and says evaluation preserves homology, giving exactness. A finite-free map is determined by the displayed global basis sections; exactness lifts them along any module epimorphism. Rank zero gives the zero projective object. This elementary basis argument stays in the step.

Step 5 uses the constant-sheaf comparison in the localization node and the sectionwise formula of def-pur-sheaf-inverse-limit, whose last sentence explicitly includes rings.
