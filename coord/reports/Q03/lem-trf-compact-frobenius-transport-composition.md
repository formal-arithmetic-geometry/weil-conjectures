## Retained argument for the new point-transport node

For an isomorphism h^r, inverse-image evaluation at (U,u) is reindexing to (U,h^r u). In the inverse-image colimit, an index (V,phi) is the same as an arrow (U,h^r u) to V; the identity arrow is initial before taking the opposite. Consequently the distinguished insertion at the final object is the identity in these coordinates. The finite-level point map sends x to res_(h^r)(x) tensor 1. Successive restrictions for m and 1 compose along h^m composed with h, and the canonical inverse pullback-composition map t_m sends the two tensor units to the single unit. This gives exactly the stated equation in every complex degree. Restrictions and tensor insertion commute with differentials and maps of complexes, and are A-linear because the constant scalars restrict to themselves. Exactness of point sections over algebraically closed k and exactness of the pullbacks allow localization to the derived category. The localization universal property carries the equality and naturality to all derived morphisms. This is the original step 3 argument, preserved as preparation for splitting the new lemma; it is not hidden inside the new statement.
