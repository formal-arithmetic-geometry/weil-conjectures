## STATEMENT-DISPUTED: discarded prior sandbox helper

The earlier assertion that arbitrary finite-limit-sketch models are algebras of a finitary monad on sorted sets is false. Categories are models of a small finite-limit sketch (objects, arrows, composable pairs/triples, with the pullbacks and composition/unit/associativity equations). They cannot be equivalent to a many-sorted finitary variety: regular epimorphisms in a variety are stable under pullback, while this fails in Cat. Explicitly, identifying the two objects of the walking arrow gives a coequalizer `q:[1] -> B(N)`, the free one-object category on a loop. Pulling q back along `B(2N) -> B(N)` leaves a discrete two-object category, because the sole nonidentity arrow of [1] maps to 1, which is not even. Its map to B(2N) is not an epimorphism: the two functors to B(N) sending the generator 2 respectively to 0 and 1 agree after that map. Thus the regular epi q has a pullback that is not epi. This diagnoses the removed helper, not the imported item, and no changed item statement is being silently substituted.
