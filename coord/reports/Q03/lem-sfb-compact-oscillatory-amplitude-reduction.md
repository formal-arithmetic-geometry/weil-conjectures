## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the phrase "supports in the two spatial projections of supp a" is false if projection has its literal set-theoretic meaning. Replace it by "supports in the closures of the two spatial projections of supp a". This is the customary compact spatial support intended by the local quantization construction; every other hypothesis and conclusion is retained.

Explicit counterexample, m=p=q=1 and r=0: take nonnegative nonzero smooth compact bumps psi(y), chi(s), and h(s), with chi supported in (-1,1) and h supported in (-1/10,1/10), with h(0)>0. Put h_n(x)=h(n^2(x-1/n)) and

a(x,y,xi) = sum over n>=2 of exp(-n^4) h_n(x) psi(y) chi(xi-n) exp(-i(x-y)xi).

This sum is locally finite in xi, smooth, compactly supported in (x,y), and satisfies the symbol bounds of every order: each derivative contributes only polynomial powers of n, while exp(-n^4) defeats all such powers and every negative frequency weight. For every finite xi0 there is a product neighborhood of (0,y,xi0) on which a is zero: only finitely many n can meet its bounded frequency interval, and their x supports avoid zero. Hence zero is not in the x projection of supp a. But with f=psi, the phase cancels exactly and the absolutely convergent operator value is

A_a f(x) = (2pi)^(-1) (integral chi)(integral psi^2) sum exp(-n^4) h_n(x).

It is nonzero at x=1/n and smooth flat at zero. Its closed support contains zero, contradicting the unclosed-projection claim. The corrected compact sets P_x,P_y are the closures of the projections, contained in the fixed compact spatial envelope. The parent's use in lem-sfb-pseudodifferential-leading-calculus needs compact spatial supports and continuous transposes and is unaffected; no citer is edited.
