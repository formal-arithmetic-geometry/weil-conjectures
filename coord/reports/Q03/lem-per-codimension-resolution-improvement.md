## Faults and corrections

The item had no proof. Its statement is not weakened or changed. The main issue to make explicit is that correcting an entire old center by the lower-stage rule does not immediately show that the inserted centers all have pure codimension r over X. I first restrict that center to the finite disjoint union of local bases at the selected height-r points, apply the already defined lower-stage rule there, and take schematic closures of its synchronized centers. The resulting new centers all have image of pure X-codimension r. This uses exactly the codimension-induction and ordinary-blowup route of the parent, not the global resolution theorem or a new route through it. No assertion of stabilization or finite dimension of X is introduced.

The provisional globalization interface said merely that P factors through a closed W in an ambient scheme; that would not identify a tower on P with a strict-transform tower of W unless P=W. I corrected it to P=W embedded as a closed subscheme. The local dimension interface now expressly supplies localization and quasi-excellence, rather than tacitly using an excellent-scheme lemma on a merely quasi-excellent scheme. The generic dimension-function lemmas in the DAG assume excellence and do not supply this input.
