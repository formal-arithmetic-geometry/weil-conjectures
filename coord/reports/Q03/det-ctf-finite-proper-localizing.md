## STATEMENT-DISPUTED

The bare imported statement mentions only a finite ell-primary ring and a proper map. Taken without its chapter's standing setting, it is false. Let R be the product of countably many copies of F_p, and let m be the maximal ideal given by a nonprincipal ultrafilter. The residue field is F_p: a sequence with finitely many possible values takes one value on an ultrafilter-large set. Thus i: Spec(F_p) -> Spec(R) is a closed immersion, hence proper. For ell different from p, the constant F_ell sheaf on the source is constructible and flat. Its pushforward has support exactly {m}. The spectrum of this absolutely flat ring is a Stone space. For a sequence a, the principal ideal (a) is generated by the idempotent which is 1 where a is nonzero and 0 elsewhere (invert each nonzero coordinate); hence every principal open is clopen, and every constructible subset is clopen. The point m is not open: an ultrafilter-large set is infinite and each basic neighborhood also contains principal ultrafilter points. Hence {m} is not constructible, whereas a finite-stratified locally constant finite sheaf has constructible support. The pushforward fails constructibility already in degree zero.

The faithful correction makes explicit the ACTUAL standing setting: any field k of characteristic p>0 with p different from ell, separated finite-type k-schemes, k-morphisms, and finite commutative A annihilated by a power of ell. Ordinary unbounded constant-A module categories are identified with the pulled-back finite condensed coefficient categories. All three conclusions, including arbitrary small ambient sums and the specified adjunction mate, are retained.
