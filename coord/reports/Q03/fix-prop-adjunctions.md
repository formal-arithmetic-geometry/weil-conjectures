## Statement and source errors

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported ordinary composite unit `Lf^* eta_g Lf^* ∘ eta_f` is ill-typed. For distinct categories at X, Y, Z, eta_f has source id on Y, whereas the unit for the composite has source id on Z. Moreover the left functor Lf* cannot whisker the Z-endofunctor eta_g as written. The corrected typed formula is `eta_gf = (Rg_* eta_f Lg*) ∘ eta_g`. The corresponding counit is `epsilon_f ∘ (Lf* epsilon_g Rf_*)`.

The source's phrase “transpose of Ex_! is the corresponding exceptional-inverse-image identification” does not specify the adjunctions or even the type of the result. The Lean source above makes its intended reading explicit: conjugate the entire transformation between the two composite left adjoints. Its type is `Rg'_* Rf'^! → Rf^! Rg_*`. This is not the square mate obtained by reversing Ex_! and moving only the exceptional functors, and no assertion that every square mate of an isomorphism is invertible is valid. The corrected clause specifies the composite adjunctions and proves the identification whenever Ex_! is invertible on those domains. Base-change invertibility itself is not proved by formal mate calculus; it belongs to the compact-support base-change nodes.

The same clause now explicitly retains the coefficient/category qualifications of the already repaired `fix-lem-exchanges` and `def-exchange-projection`. In particular their completed/uniform coefficient domains are not silently identified with arbitrary unbounded pro-etale inputs. For composition I have likewise retained the corresponding domains of the higher-coefficient composition identifications; `thm-sf-comp-composition` alone asserts only the finite-coefficient comparison.

The ordinary composition formula is stated in the adjunction-compatible normalization (the direct-image comparison is conjugate to the pullback comparison). Equality of units would not follow merely from an arbitrary natural direct-image isomorphism with the same source and target. This makes the normalization in the source and Lean statement explicit.

The original proof also treated an underived inverse-image definition as if it stated the derived adjunction, and treated right-adjoint Tor preservation as enough for an adjunction on two finite-Tor categories. These inferences have been replaced by actual derived-adjunction statements and a separate left-functor Tor-preservation argument. No theorem's proof is used as if it were a clause of its statement.
