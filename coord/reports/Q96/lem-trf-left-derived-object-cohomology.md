### Returned step 1 — allocated to lem-trf-projective-replacement-initial-denominator

Construct an augmented projective complex by recursion on $n\in\mathbf N$: put $Z_0=M$, choose a projective epimorphism $p_n:P^{-n}\twoheadrightarrow Z_n$, and put $Z_{n+1}=\ker p_n$ with inclusion $k_{n+1}:Z_{n+1}\hookrightarrow P^{-n}$. Set $d^{-n-1}=k_{n+1}p_{n+1}$, put $P^q=0$ for $q>0$, and augment by $a^0=p_0$. Then $d^2=0$ since $p_nk_{n+1}=0$. Since $p_{n+1}$ is epic, $\operatorname{im}d^{-n-1}=Z_{n+1}$; since $k_n$ is monic, $\ker d^{-n}=\ker p_n=Z_{n+1}$ for $n\geq1$. Thus $P$ is exact in negative degrees. The epimorphism $p_0$ is a cokernel of its kernel in an abelian category, and hence a cokernel of $k_1p_1=d^{-1}$, because $p_1$ is epic. Therefore $H^0(a)$ is an isomorphism and $a:P\to M[0]$ is a quasi-isomorphism. This recursion uses only the given projective covers, kernels, and the kernel/image definition of cohomology; it requires no infinite products or colimits.

Existing nodes used in the original argument: def-sp-abelian, def-weil-abstract-derived-category.

Source: literature/stacks-project/derived.tex, Tag 0646, lemma-projective-resolutions-exist (1), explicit recursive construction for an object; the resolution definition immediately before it.

### Returned step 2 — allocated to lem-trf-projective-replacement-initial-denominator
