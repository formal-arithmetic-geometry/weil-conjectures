## Source and intended proof of the new node

For `lem-sfb-directed-ring-sheaf-module-colimit` I read Stacks `sites-modules.tex`, Tag 03DB (`lemma-limits-colimits`, module limits/colimits, around lines 1358-1400), Tag 03EP (`lemma-tensor-commute-colimits`, item (2), around lines 4001-4030), and the relative sheaf tensor definition in Section 03EK (`section-tensor-product`, around lines 3551-3586). These supply module colimits and their interaction with tensor, not the additional comparison with ring colimits. The following is the intended argument, recorded in the report rather than in the statement-only node.

Form the colimit C of the underlying R-module sheaves A_i, with maps j_i, using 03DB. For i,j choose k >= i,j and use multiplication in A_k to form A_i tensor_R A_j -> A_k -> C. The choice of k does not matter: any two choices admit a common upper bound, and the transition maps preserve multiplication and commute with the colimit maps. These maps are compatible separately in i,j. By 03EP and the balanced tensor swap, taking the colimit in both variables gives a bilinear product C tensor_R C -> C. The maps R -> A_i -> C agree, since I is nonempty directed, and define its unit R -> C. Each finite collection of indices has a common upper bound, so associativity, commutativity and unit equations reduce, by the tensor-colimit universal property, to those of a single A_k. This gives a sheaf of unital commutative rings C and makes every j_i a unital ring map.

For any compatible cocone of unital commutative ring-sheaf maps A_i -> T, the maps R -> T agree (choose an upper bound for any two indices). Give T the resulting R action. The unique R-linear map C -> T is multiplicative: precomposing its two multiplicativity expressions with A_i tensor_R A_j reduces them to multiplication after the common-upper-stage ring map A_k -> T. It preserves the unit by precomposing with any R -> A_i. Thus C is a colimit in commutative ring sheaves. Comparing with the given ring-colimit cocone at A gives an isomorphism C -> A respecting each A_i leg. It is under R since I is nonempty; consequently it is R-linear, as is its inverse. Transporting the module-colimit universal property gives precisely the statement of the new node, with the specified q_i. This argument also shows why no injectivity of the transition maps and no exactness or flatness hypothesis is needed.

Small cases: a singleton index gives the identity statement; if R is the zero ring all unital R-algebras and their module sheaves are zero; zero stages and noninjective transitions cause no problem, since no element of a stage is ever cancelled. The site need not have points or finite covering families. Nonemptiness cannot be dropped without changing the ring-colimit versus module-colimit comparison, so it is included explicitly. A common coefficient universe contains the site skeleton, all sheaves and I; the independent universe feature of 03EP is preserved rather than identifying site and coefficient universes.
