## Source error

The second reader found a slip in Higher Algebra (2017), Remark 4.2.2.26, printed p. 521. Its displayed factorization of a marked cross edge passes first along the bottom and then vertically at the target, and asserts both factors are inert. Take the cross edge $([2],0)\to([1],1)$ with opposite operator $\alpha:[1]\to[2]$, $\alpha(0)=0$, $\alpha(1)=1$. Its sole algebra-output fibre is $\{1\}$, so the cross edge is inert. In the source's bottom-first factorization, the bottom arrow $([2],0)\to([1],0)$ has module-output fibre $\{2,3\}$, so it is not inert. The correct factorization is the vertical edge at the source followed by the top interval-inclusion edge. The existing item and proof step 4 already state and prove this valid factorization. Their statements need no correction; this report records the source error rather than silently adopting its proof. This does not affect HA's approximation assertion in Remark 4.2.2.8.
