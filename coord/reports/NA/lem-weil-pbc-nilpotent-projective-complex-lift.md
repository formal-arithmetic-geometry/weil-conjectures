## Corrections and source precision

The original item is exactly the content of more-algebra.tex, Tag 09AR, lemma-lift-complex-projectives. Its statement, coefficient rings, nilpotence and quotient-complex conclusion are retained. No STATEMENT-DISPUTED is needed.

The source verifies hypothesis (5) of the general lifting lemma by invoking Tag 0H75. That tag's *statement* is about a finite Tor interval, and an arbitrary bounded-above projective complex need not have finite Tor amplitude. Accordingly the new upper-bound node is explicitly sourced to the upper-bound-only version of its proof, not presented as a literal specialization of the finite-interval assertion. This is a clarification of the inference in the proof, not a change of route or a dispute of 09AR.

For the second reader, the upper-bound argument is as follows. If the reduction is in degrees at most b, its derived tensor over A/I with any A/I-module M is also in degrees at most b, by a bounded-above free resolution and total tensor with a nonpositive free resolution of M. Associativity identifies this with C tensor_A^L M. Filter A by I^j, with quotients I^j/I^(j+1) annihilated by I. Apply exactness of derived tensor and the cohomology long exact sequence successively, starting from I^N=0. This shows C tensor_A^L A, hence C, has no cohomology above b. This proof does not use finite lower amplitude or finite generation, and works even when C was initially unbounded. The new node is intentionally not split further.
