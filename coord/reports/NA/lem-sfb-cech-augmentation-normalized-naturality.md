## STATEMENT-DISPUTED: degree-zero transpose needs the underlying unit

The original final sentence said that the Cech-degree-zero map has adjoint transpose the identity on `v_0^*I`. Literally its map is `f_*I -> (fv_0)_*v_0^*I`, in the module category on Y, whereas `v_0^*` is defined on the module category on X. Thus `v_0^*` cannot even be applied to that source when X and Y differ. This is a type failure, not a counterexample to the intended localization normalization. The source intent is the unit `I -> v_{0*}v_0^*I` on X followed by `f_*`, exactly as in the parent item's restriction-of-sections augmentation. The statement now says this explicitly; the adjoint transpose is taken before applying `f_*`. It also explicitly projects the total augmentation to column zero before invoking its derived normalization. This projection is a chain map because the horizontal differential only increases Cech degree. No assertion of an adjunction on Y with left adjoint `v_0^*` is made. All naturality, comparison, homotopy and refinement conclusions are retained. The domain of a refinement is explicitly another epimorphic covering, as the term refinement intended.
