### Former step 1

For $M\in\Mod_{\Lambda_X}$, take the functor of [def-sfb-module-lower-star]. Its underlying object is $f^{\Ab}_*M$ and its action is the composite $\Lambda_Y\otimes f^{\Ab}_*M\xrightarrow{\eta_f\otimes1}f^{\Ab}_*\Lambda_X\otimes f^{\Ab}_*M\xrightarrow{\mu}f^{\Ab}_*(\Lambda_X\otimes M)\xrightarrow{f^{\Ab}_*\gamma_M}f^{\Ab}_*M$. Thus it is precisely transport followed by restriction along $\eta_f$, on morphisms as well as objects. The same cited statement supplies additivity and left exactness for arbitrary $f$ and commutative condensed $\Lambda$. This defines the first functor and its asserted properties.

Cited nodes: def-sfb-module-lower-star.

### Former step 2

Take the functor of [def-sfb-module-upper-star]. It first transports $M\in\Mod_{\Lambda_Y}$ to an $f^*_{\Ab}\Lambda_Y$-module and then extends scalars along $\alpha_f$, giving $\Lambda_X\otimes_{f^*\Lambda_Y}f^*_{\Ab}M$. This is the displayed pullback functor, with the target and tensor formula stated there. The construction uses the morphism $\alpha_f$ in extension of scalars, rather than an inverse of that morphism.

Cited nodes: def-sfb-module-upper-star.

### Former step 3

Use the adjunction specified by [def-sfb-module-upper-lower-adjunction], for the functors constructed in steps 1 and 2. Its natural Hom bijection identifies a $\Lambda_X$-linear map $\Lambda_X\otimes_{f^*\Lambda_Y}f^*_{\Ab}M\to N$ first with an $f^*\Lambda_Y$-linear map from $f^*_{\Ab}M$ to the restriction of $N$, and then with a $\Lambda_Y$-linear map $M\to f_*N$. The same statement specifies the lifted monoidal adjunction followed by extension/restriction of scalars. The coefficient relation needed to identify its right adjoint with step 1 is exactly $\eta_f=f^{\Ab}_*(\alpha_f)\circ u_{\Lambda_Y}$. In [sk-def-sfb-coefficient-morphisms], the named adjunction is the abelian-sheaf pullback/pushforward adjunction on sheaves, not the module adjunction being constructed. Specializing its coefficient category to $\Ab$, $u_{\Lambda_Y}$ has type $\Lambda_Y\to f^{\Ab}_*f^*_{\Ab}\Lambda_Y$, and $f^{\Ab}_*(\alpha_f)$ then has target $f^{\Ab}_*\Lambda_X$. Thus this is the same coefficient map used by the module pushforward in step 1, before any module change of scalars. Hence the adjunction here is the required one.

Cited nodes: def-sfb-module-upper-lower-adjunction, sk-def-sfb-coefficient-morphisms.

### Former step 6

Write $a_M,b_M$ for the two module actions. The preceding linearity equation is $c_Ma_M=b_M(1\otimes c_M)$. Precompose with $1\otimes c_M^{-1}$ and postcompose with $c_M^{-1}$ to obtain $c_M^{-1}b_M=a_M(1\otimes c_M^{-1})$; hence the inverse is linear too by [sk-def-sfb-module-objects].
