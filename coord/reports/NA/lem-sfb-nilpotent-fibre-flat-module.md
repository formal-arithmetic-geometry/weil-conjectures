Step 2 cites the tensor-product definition, whose statement expressly supplies the universal property and generation by pure tensors. The map theta is R-balanced because f(ri) tensor m = f(i) tensor f(r)m. The written finite expansion of j in IA moves each coefficient a_t onto m by A-balancing and exhibits a preimage of every pure tensor. Step 3 again cites the tensor definition for multiplication and equality of maps on pure tensors. The equality mu_A theta = mu_R follows on i tensor m by both sides being f(i)m. Surjectivity from step 2 and injectivity from step 1 imply injectivity of mu_A by the displayed element chase. This is elementary and does not invoke a hidden change-of-rings Tor result.
