Take a nonzero finite coefficient ring A=A_{E,1}. If H_n is the pushforward of A from X_n, put M=(product H_n)/(direct sum H_n), with the direct sum formed as a sheaf. The diagonal (1,1,...) gives a nonzero class on affine X: a locally finite supported tuple on an affine has finite support after a finite affine refinement. Both j^*M and i^{-1}M vanish; for the latter, closed inverse image commutes with arbitrary products by Stacks 09AA and is exact and preserves direct sums. Thus G_Z M=M is nonzero, while i_*i^!M=0. This is the counterexample recorded in the already reviewed NN report for `def-pur-flasque-support`, checked against that node and Stacks 09AA. Even restricting the comparison to injective inputs does not fix it: embed M in an injective I; then M embeds in G_Z I, whereas any map M -> i_*N vanishes by i^{-1}M=0 and i^{-1} left adjoint to i_*.

The repaired steps use only the constant-sheaf cokernel A_X/(image j_!A_U)=i_*A_Z, whose proof is written in steps 1–3.
