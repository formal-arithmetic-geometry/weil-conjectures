## New node argument retained for its future worker

For arbitrary f, write u_(p,q)=ι_(p,q);f^(p+q). Transpose each u_(p,q) with the actual underived adjunction and use the product universal property to define φ^q. The chain-map equation for f on the (p,q) summand says, in conventional composition order,

d_I u_(p,q) = u_(p+1,q)(1⊗d_P) + (-1)^p u_(p,q+1)(d_Q⊗1).

Naturality and additivity of the underived adjunction transpose this to

(d_Q;φ^(q+1))_p = (-1)^p ((φ^q)_p;Hom(P^p,d_I) - (φ^q)_(p+1);Hom(d_P,I)).

This is exactly the p-th projection of φ^q;d_row. Equality of all product projections proves the chain condition. Applying underived uncurrying to the defining projection equation gives the statement, with no additional sign. Countable products/coproducts handle arbitrary unbounded complexes degree by degree; no exchange of a product with a coproduct is used. The node isolates this strict complex construction and its sign compatibility, not a clause of the derived-model uniqueness conclusion. Its statement contains neither a derived structure S, a representative system R, nor two comparison families.
