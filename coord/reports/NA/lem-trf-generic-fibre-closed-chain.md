### Old step 2: New affine-dimension-data task

By [def-weil-finite-type-morphism], choose a member $U=\operatorname{Spec}A$ of the affine finite-type cover of $X\to\operatorname{Spec}K$ containing $x$. Then $A$ is a finitely generated $K$-algebra and is a nonzero domain by [def-pc-integral-scheme]. The singleton $\{x\}$ is closed in $U$ because it is closed in $X$; write $\mathfrak m$ for its maximal ideal. The one-member affine cover shows that $U$ is of finite type over $K$. The stalks, maximal ideals and residue fields on $U$ and on $X$ at $x$ agree: neighbourhoods contained in $U$ are cofinal among neighbourhoods of $x$, so the same germ equivalence relation computes both stalks [def-weil-pbc-module-stalk, def-weil-residue-field]. Thus $\kappa_X(x)/K$ is finite.

Cited statements: def-weil-finite-type-morphism, def-pc-integral-scheme, lem-kun-closed-point-field, def-weil-pbc-module-stalk, def-weil-residue-field.

Indeed a set of nonnegative integers with finite supremum $r$ contains $r$: for $r>0$ otherwise all its members are at most $r-1$; for $r=0$ the maximal ideal $\mathfrak mA_{\mathfrak m}$ supplies a chain of length zero [def-weil-pbc-localization-at-prime].
