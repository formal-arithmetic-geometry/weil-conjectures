## Coefficient theorem: construction and source check

The new statement is a consequence assembled from Stacks tags, not presented as the text of one tag. I read proetale.tex Tag 098T, label lemma-morphism-big-small (2), which constructs scheme base change on small pro-etale sites; sites.tex Tag 04D3, label lemma-pullback-representable-sheaf, which identifies inverse image of a sheafified representable; proetale.tex Tag 097N, label lemma-ind-etale-implies; and limits.tex Tag 01YW, label lemma-directed-inverse-system-affine-schemes-has-limit. They give the following route for a later split of this statement-only node.

For a finite ring Q_n, let Z_{T,n} be the finite disjoint union of copies of T indexed by its underlying finite set. Its representable sheaf is the constant finite sheaf (Q_n)_T: morphisms W → Z_{T,n} over T are finite clopen partitions of W labelled by Q_n, equivalently continuous maps from its component space to the finite discrete set, locally on affine opens. The transition ring maps give transition scheme maps between these finite disjoint unions. On T = Spec B, the inverse-limit scheme Z_T is Spec of the filtered colimit of the rings B^{Q_n}, by 01YW. Every B → B^{Q_n} is finite etale. Thus the colimit is ind-etale and hence weakly etale by 097N. For general T these constructions glue on affine opens; the limits are relative affine schemes, so restriction to an affine open has the same formula. All the rings and the countable indexing category stay inside the prescribed universe. The representable of Z_T is the inverse limit of the representables of Z_{T,n}, by the universal property of this scheme limit. Hence it represents the underlying sheaf of R_T.

Base change of Z_{V,n} is Z_{U,n}. On an affine chart the limit base change is also the same: tensoring the filtered colimit of the B^{Q_n} with the new base ring commutes with that colimit, and the finite products B^{Q_n} base change to the corresponding finite products. These equalities glue and identify U ×_V Z_V with Z_U. Apply 04D3 to Z_V, which is an object of the small pro-etale site because it is weakly etale, using the site functor of 098T. It yields u^{-1}h_{Z_V} ≃ h_{Z_U}. Apply the same result at finite level. The resulting maps commute with every projection Z_T → Z_{T,n}, and with the transition maps. Ring operations are induced by the finite-level addition, multiplication, zero and unit maps, so naturality and finite-product preservation make these isomorphisms ring isomorphisms. This proves exactly the projection equation in the new statement. Naturality in ring towers and composition of base changes follows from the same projections and their universal properties. This argument does not assume that an arbitrary inverse image preserves infinite limits, and does not equate the completed sheaf with the discrete constant sheaf of the abstract completed ring.
