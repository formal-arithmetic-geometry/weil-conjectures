## STATEMENT-DISPUTED

The imported statement says only that `f` is separated of finite type and concludes that finitely many affine opens cover `X`. This is false without the standing scheme hypotheses. Let `X=Y` be the disjoint union of infinitely many copies of `Spec k` and let `f` be its identity. The identity is separated of finite type. Every affine open is quasi-compact and hence contains only finitely many of these discrete points; finitely many affine opens cannot cover `X`. Thus the first sentence of the imported proof, “Y is quasi-compact”, does not follow from its written hypotheses.

I restored the standing hypotheses actually required by the named right adjoint: a prime ell, a positive-characteristic field k with characteristic different from ell, and separated finite-type k-schemes X and Y, with rational condensed coefficients E or Qell-bar. These are exactly `sk-def-sfb-dc-setting` and the reviewed `sk-prop-sf-existence`. The restored scope is also the standing scope of `sk-thm-existence`. The conclusion, including finite charts, is retained. This is an explicit repair of an omitted context, not a change of proof route or a target weakening. The same missing hypothesis occurs in unit `sk-thm-restriction` step 1; its intended standing context makes that step valid. The unit record itself was not edited.
