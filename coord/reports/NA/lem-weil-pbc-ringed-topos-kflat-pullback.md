## Source error

The proof of Stacks 0G7E, sites-cohomology.tex, lines 3549 and 3579, twice says "bounded below" for complexes arising from bounded-above resolutions or their kernels. The construction only gives bounded above, and that is what Tag 06YQ requires. For example a free resolution of the degree-zero module Z/2 over Z has its free terms in negative cohomological degrees; the indexing direction is a bounded-above resolution, and over a ring where a module has infinite projective dimension it need not be bounded below. Explicitly, over k[e]/(e^2), the resolution of k with all free terms in degrees at most zero and negative-degree differentials multiplication by e continues indefinitely to the left. Every appeal in this split is to bounded-above complexes. This repairs the proof's two words, not the valid item statement; there is no STATEMENT-DISPUTED for the item.
