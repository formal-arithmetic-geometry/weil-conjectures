## Source error

Tag 09AC says the image of an ind-etale affine map is closed under specialization. The required property is closure under generalization, which is what going down gives. For example, the ind-Zariski (hence ind-etale and flat) map Spec(Frac(R)) to Spec(R), for a nonfield DVR R, has image only the generic point; its specialization to the closed point is absent. This refutes the asserted specialization direction for flat/ind-etale images. Step 2 instead lifts a prime below the chosen prime over a point of T, citing the existing flat-going-down node (Algebra 00HS). Since every point of B specializes into T, closure under generalization is exactly the direction that proves surjectivity. This corrects a sentence of the source proof, not the item's true statement or the unit/chapter route.
