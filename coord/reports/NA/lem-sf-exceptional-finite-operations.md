This establishes preservation before restricting the functor. It is not borrowed from the proof of a definition.

Step 4 uses thm-amb-etale-exceptional-adjoint for the ordinary etale right adjoint on Noetherian schemes, lem-sf-proetale-constructible-origin for all intrinsic bounded constructible inputs, lem-sf-class-compact-support-comparison for the proper/open comparison, and thm-weil-pbc-site-comparison for the bounded-below fully faithful Hom map and comparison unit. thm-sf-ambient-adjoint-class clause 2 states the natural witness-independent Hom bijection with precisely G_A and with bounded-below etale-origin left input.
