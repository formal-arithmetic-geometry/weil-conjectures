For the new node I read all three Properties criteria, not merely the title of 01ON. Its eventual proof is: a domain spectrum is reduced and irreducible. Reducedness is local on the affine covering by 01OL. For a closed covering Y=Z1 union Z2, each irreducible V_i lies in one Z_k. Fix i and suppose V_i is in Z1. Each nonempty overlap V_i intersection V_j is an open nonempty subset of the irreducible V_j, hence dense there: if an open nonempty subset were not dense, its complement of closure would be another disjoint nonempty open. Therefore Z1 intersection V_j, a closed subset of V_j containing the overlap, is all of V_j. Thus Y=Z1 and Y is irreducible by 01OM. Finally 01ON gives integrality. This is a genuine local-to-global lemma using more than the definition; it is not a new name for the item, which asserts existence of a base shrink and requires finite generation to construct it.

Step 3 uses precisely the finite-ideal-generation definition of Noetherianity and the zero-fraction criterion in the cited module-localization definition. Thus deleting exactly the empty localized charts does not lose any part of the covering of X_a. J is nonempty because X_p is integral and therefore nonempty. These arguments are written out rather than hiding an empty-scheme convention.
