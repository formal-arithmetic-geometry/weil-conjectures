## Source error

There is an auxiliary printing/mathematical error in Milne 14.51's first assertion: finitely generated modules need not be sums of cyclic modules with annihilator either zero or irreducible. Already for k=F_p, A=F_p[F], the module A/(F^2) has nonzero nilpotent F, whereas the F-primary part of a sum of the stated irreducible cyclic modules has F=0. Powers of irreducibles cannot be omitted. Proposition 14.52's classification statement still holds; indeed its alpha_(p^r) factors require those powers. The new decomposition node states 14.52, not the erroneous assertion of 14.51. A later split of that node must use the corrected elementary-divisor argument. This does not change the assigned item's statement or its route.
