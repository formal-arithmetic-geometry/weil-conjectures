This is the covering meaning of jointly surjective representable etale maps; all products here are the actual sheaf fibre products. Scheme products used below are the chosen products of [def-pc-fibre-product].

lem-trf-local-open-representatives-support-descent (etale-to-fpqc conversion); lem-trf-prescribed-open-fppf-sheaf-representation (all base-changed etale families are fppf).

### Old step 3: allocation

Assembly step 2 (formation of W_i); lem-trf-local-open-representatives-support-descent (overlap equality from the common restricted presheaf).

Write $Y_i=X\times_P P_i$, a scheme whose projection $j_i:Y_i\to P_i$ is an open immersion by hypothesis. By [lem-weil-pbc-open-immersion-base-change], the scheme $Y_i\times_{P_i}T_i\to T_i$ is an open immersion. Denote its image open subscheme by $W_i\subset T_i$. The fibre-product universal property identifies $h_{W_i}$ over $h_{T_i}$ with $F\times_{h_T}h_{T_i}$. For $R_{ij}=T_i\times_TT_j$, the two pullbacks of these open subschemes are open by the same base-change lemma, and both represent the identical subfunctor $F\times_{h_T}h_{R_{ij}}$ of $h_{R_{ij}}$. They are therefore equal as opens of $R_{ij}$: membership of a point $r$ in either open is exactly the factorization of $\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(r)\to R_{ij}$ through that subfunctor. Their structure sheaves are both restrictions of $\mathcal O_{R_{ij}}$.

### Old step 5: allocation

lem-trf-prescribed-open-fppf-sheaf-representation (agreement of local sections and fppf gluing).

The identifications of Step 3 give sections $b_i\in X(W_i)$, the maps $W_i\simeq Y_i\times_{P_i}T_i\to X$. On $W_i\times_UW_j$ they agree: after mapping to $R_{ij}$, both are the unique $X$-section corresponding to the inclusion of the same open in the common subfunctor of Step 3. The family $W_i=U\times_TT_i\to U$ is fppf by Step 2. Since $X$ is an fppf sheaf, its equalizer condition glues the $b_i$ to a unique $b\in X(U)$. Moreover $a(b)$ equals the image in $P(U)$ of $U\hookrightarrow T\xrightarrow{t}P$: they agree after restriction to every $W_i$, and $P$ is an fppf sheaf too. Consequently $b$ defines a map $h_U\to F$ over $h_T$, by pullback of this section.

### Old step 6: allocation

lem-trf-prescribed-open-fppf-sheaf-representation (arbitrary scheme tests, surjectivity, unique factorization, and sectionwise inverse).

For every scheme $g:Z\to T$ and $x\in F(Z)$, pull back along the fppf covering $Z_i=Z\times_TT_i\to Z$. The identification $F|_{T_i}=h_{W_i}$ gives a unique map $Z_i\to W_i$. Since $W_i=q_i^{-1}(U)$, the structural map $Z_i\to Z\xrightarrow{g}T$ has image in $U$. Joint surjectivity of $(Z_i\to Z)$ forces $g(Z)\subset U$. Restricting the continuous map and sheaf map of $g$ to this open gives a unique scheme morphism $\widetilde g:Z\to U$ with $g$ equal to its composite with $U\hookrightarrow T$. On every $Z_i$, the sections $x$ and $\widetilde g^*b$ agree, since each is the unique element over the fixed $Z_i\to T_i$ in the represented open $W_i$. Separatedness in the defining sheaf condition for $X$ gives $x=\widetilde g^*b$. Conversely every $Z\to U$ gives such an $x$ by pulling back $b$. Uniqueness of the factorization through an open shows that these constructions are inverse. The covers used here are exactly those supplied by [lem-weil-pbc-etale-covering-base-change].

### Old step 7: allocation

lem-trf-prescribed-open-fppf-sheaf-representation (naturality); assembly step 4 (scheme-test conclusion and P a scheme).

The bijections of Step 6 commute with pullback in $Z$: the forward map is pullback of $b$, and its inverse is the unique factorization through $U$. They therefore identify $F=h_T\times_PX$ with $h_U$, with its projection to $h_T$ equal to the open inclusion $U\hookrightarrow T$. Since $T\to P$ was arbitrary, the scheme-test definition of representability proves that $a$ is representable, and every scheme-test projection is an open immersion. This is precisely the asserted meaning of an open immersion of algebraic spaces. If $P$ is itself a scheme, take $T=P$ and $t$ the identity to obtain $X\simeq U\subset P$.
