Step 2 uses `lem-weil-pbc-scalar-extension-hom` for both ordinary transposition formulas, `def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification` for unique extension to sheaves, `lem-weil-pbc-equivalence-injective-resolutions` for preservation of all existing colimits by an additive equivalence, `def-sfb-modobj-relative-tensor` for the two-arrow balanced coequalizer, and `def-sfb-modobj-extend-restrict-adjunction` for the scalar-unit Hom bijection. The balancing equation and inverse-generator calculations are written out. This is not the false claim that a sheaf tensor is sectionwise tensor on every test; the formulas are on presheaf generators followed by module sheafification. The base equivalence preserves finite products and colimits, and its specified ring comparison transports precisely the two balancing arrows. Evaluating the transpose at an identity determines the actual unit and counit, so no arbitrary automorphism is inserted.

Step 3 is elementary relative to the stated K-flat definition and the exact equivalence: for each acyclic A, the inverse carries A to an acyclic complex, and the total tensor is identified with the image of that acyclic tensor. The converse uses the same test with the inverse. The four cited statements are `def-pc-kflat-complex`, equivalence colimit/exactness, exact-functor derived factorization, and `lem-weil-pbc-site-derived-tensor-descent`. The last explicitly says that localization of the flat-term K-flat homotopy subcategory is equivalent to the entire unbounded derived category. I use this full resolving-class assertion, not mere existence of one resolution. Tensor coproducts stay in the compatible coefficient universe. The analogous degree-zero short-exact-sequence test verifies preservation of flat terms. This is a short application of the definition, not a new lemma node.

Step 4 uses `lem-pur-sheaf-ring-derived-coefficient-adjunction`, whose statement gives exact restriction, universal K-flat scalar extension, and the normalized chain unit/counit with no flatness assumption. `lem-sfb-internal-section-scalar-adjunction` transfers that assertion to the actual internal module constructions. `lem-sfb-kflat-scalar-comparison-normalization` explicitly identifies the localized balanced map with the mate of restriction for an exact inverse image. Its hypotheses apply because a ringed equivalence over Cond carries both coefficient rings and their map to the indicated rings, while step 3 verifies the necessary resolution transport. Its conclusion is stronger than an unspecified isomorphism and proves the Hom normalization required here.

Step 5 applies that same scalar mate statement to the exact action-forgetting inverse image from step 1, not to a direct image mistakenly treated as a pullback. The coefficient-adjunction theorem supplies the representative map with a K-flat resolution of the restricted complex. The exact-functor theorem supplies the fixed signed shift for restriction.
