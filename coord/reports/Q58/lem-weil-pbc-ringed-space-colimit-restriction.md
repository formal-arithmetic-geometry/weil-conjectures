## Original four-step proof retained for the new workers

### Original step 1

*Restriction is a functor.* For $\mathcal M\in\Mod(\mathcal O_X)$, $\mathcal M|_U$ is a sheaf of $\mathcal O_U$-modules: an open covering of an open $W\subseteq U$ by opens of $U$ is an open covering in $X$, so the sheaf condition for $\mathcal M|_U$ on $W$ is that of $\mathcal M$, and $\mathcal M(W)$ is an $\mathcal O_X(W)=\mathcal O_U(W)$-module with semilinear restriction maps [def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category], [def-weil-pbc-module-finite-type-presentation]. For $\alpha:\mathcal M\to\mathcal N$ the components $\alpha_W$ ($W\subseteq U$) are $\mathcal O_U(W)$-linear and commute with restrictions, so $\alpha|_U$ is a morphism of $\mathcal O_U$-modules; $(\beta\alpha)|_U=\beta|_U\alpha|_U$ and $\mathrm{id}|_U=\mathrm{id}$ componentwise. So $(-)|_U:\Mod(\mathcal O_X)\to\Mod(\mathcal O_U)$ is a functor, and the $c_i|_U$ form a cocone on $i\mapsto\mathcal M_i|_U$: $c_j|_U\circ\mathcal M(a)|_U=(c_j\circ\mathcal M(a))|_U=c_i|_U$ for $a:i\to j$.

Cited nodes: def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category, def-weil-pbc-module-finite-type-presentation.

Source: literature/stacks-project/sheaves.tex, Sheaves, Lemma 00A0 (sheaves-lemma-j-pullback) (2) and its last sentence, Definition 00A1 (sheaves-definition-restriction) (3),(4).

### Original step 2

*Clause (1).* By [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-space-open-restriction-adjunction] the functor $(-)|_U:\Mod(\mathcal O_X)\to\Mod(\mathcal O_U)$ of Step 1 is left adjoint to the direct image $j_*:\Mod(\mathcal O_U)\to\Mod(\mathcal O_X)$, $(j_*\mathcal G)(V)=\mathcal G(V\cap U)$. A left adjoint carries a colimit cocone to a colimit cocone [lem-weil-pbc-left-adjoint-preserves-colimits]: applied with $L=(-)|_U$, $R=j_*$ and the colimit $(\mathcal M,(c_i)_i)$ of $i\mapsto\mathcal M_i$ [def-weil-pbc-category-colimit], it gives that $(\mathcal M|_U,(c_i|_U)_i)$ is a colimit of $i\mapsto\mathcal M_i|_U$ in $\Mod(\mathcal O_U)$.

Cited nodes: lem-weil-pbc-ringed-space-open-restriction-adjunction, lem-weil-pbc-left-adjoint-preserves-colimits, def-weil-pbc-category-colimit.

Source: literature/stacks-project/modules.tex, Modules, Lemma 01AJ (modules-lemma-exactness-pushforward-pullback) (2) and its proof (adjoint pair, Sheaves Lemma 0096, Categories Lemma 0038), for the open immersion j:(U,O_U)->(X,O_X) with j^* = restriction (Sheaves, Lemma 00A0).

### Original step 3

*The sheafified sectionwise colimit is a colimit.* Regard the $\mathcal M_i|_U$ as presheaves of $\mathcal O_U$-modules on the open subsets of $U$. By [lem-weil-pbc-presheaf-modules-colimits-sectionwise] the sectionwise colimit $\mathcal P$, $\mathcal P(W)=\varinjlim_i\mathcal M_i(W)$, is a presheaf of $\mathcal O_U$-modules and $(\mathcal P,(\iota_i)_i)$ is a colimit of $i\mapsto\mathcal M_i|_U$ in the category $\mathrm{PMod}(\mathcal O_U)$ of presheaves of $\mathcal O_U$-modules. Let $\eta:\mathcal P\to\mathcal P^\#$ be its sheafification as a presheaf of $\mathcal O_U$-modules [def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification] (the sheafification of the underlying presheaf of abelian groups on the site of open subsets of $U$ exists [def-weil-pbc-sheafification]). Claim: $(\mathcal P^\#,(\eta\circ\iota_i)_i)$ is a colimit of $i\mapsto\mathcal M_i|_U$ in $\Mod(\mathcal O_U)$ [def-weil-pbc-category-colimit]. It is a cocone, since the $\iota_i$ are. Let $(\mathcal G,(g_i)_i)$ be a cocone in $\Mod(\mathcal O_U)$. Morphisms of sheaves of $\mathcal O_U$-modules are the morphisms of their underlying presheaves of $\mathcal O_U$-modules [def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category], so $(\mathcal G,(g_i))$ is a cocone in $\mathrm{PMod}(\mathcal O_U)$ and there is exactly one $g:\mathcal P\to\mathcal G$ with $g\circ\iota_i=g_i$ for all $i$; as $\mathcal G$ is a sheaf, there is exactly one $\mathcal O_U$-linear $g^\#:\mathcal P^\#\to\mathcal G$ with $g^\#\circ\eta=g$ [def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification]. Then $g^\#\circ\eta\circ\iota_i=g_i$. If also $h:\mathcal P^\#\to\mathcal G$ has $h\circ\eta\circ\iota_i=g_i$ for all $i$, then $h\circ\eta=g$ by the uniqueness of $g$, hence $h=g^\#$ by the uniqueness of $g^\#$. This proves the claim.

*Clause (2).* By Step 2 and Step 3, $(\mathcal M|_U,(c_i|_U)_i)$ and $(\mathcal P^\#,(\eta\circ\iota_i)_i)$ are two colimits of the same diagram in $\Mod(\mathcal O_U)$. By the universal property of the colimit $\mathcal P^\#$ [def-weil-pbc-category-colimit] applied to the cocone $(\mathcal M|_U,(c_i|_U))$ there is exactly one $u:\mathcal P^\#\to\mathcal M|_U$ with $u\circ\eta\circ\iota_i=c_i|_U$ for all $i$, and by that of $\mathcal M|_U$ exactly one $v:\mathcal M|_U\to\mathcal P^\#$ with $v\circ c_i|_U=\eta\circ\iota_i$. Then $vu$ and $\mathrm{id}_{\mathcal P^\#}$ both satisfy $w\circ\eta\circ\iota_i=\eta\circ\iota_i$ for all $i$, so $vu=\mathrm{id}$ by uniqueness, and likewise $uv=\mathrm{id}$. Hence $u$ is an isomorphism.
