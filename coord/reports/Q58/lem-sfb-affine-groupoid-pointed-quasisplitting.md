## Mathematical structure

The finite-parts input is for an arbitrary separated flat locally finitely presented morphism equipped with a section, with no groupoid or affine-base requirement. It includes representability, separated etaleness, the universal finite clopen family, and a residue-field point where the section lies in the quasi-finite locus. Tags 04RI and 04RR supply representability and separation. For the point clause, the quasi-finite fibre has at the section a finite clopen component supported on that rational point; selecting this component gives a Spec kappa(y)-point of the parameter scheme. Its induced field maps kappa(y) -> kappa(m) -> kappa(y) compose to the identity, hence are isomorphisms: the second is an injective field map whose image contains all of kappa(y). This is exactly the local argument in the last paragraph of 04RW; no groupoid operation is used for this point clause.

The universal-groupoid input is a different theorem: conditional on a representing scheme for those finite parts, it constructs the translation maps and proves their groupoid laws. It assumes neither affine objects nor the quasi-finiteness or flatness of the original projections. The source is finite by the universal family; inversion identifies the target with the source. Its statement includes the actual formulas and all their compatibility claims, so the item does not have to cite the unrecorded proof of another node. These two inputs are not clauses of the item: neither produces an affine pointed neighborhood with an open subgroupoid of the restricted original relation. Steps 2--6 prove precisely the additional embedding, finite-locally-free upgrade, invariant affine shrinking, preservation under restriction, and pointed conclusion.

## Source error

The proof of Stacks lemma-strong-splitting-affine-scheme, used verbatim for Tag 04S0, writes the restricted full projections R' -> U' and then says "They are finite by assumption." Finiteness is known only for the splitting P -> U'. For example, over a field take U = Spec k and the constant infinite group scheme R = coproduct indexed by the integers of Spec k. Both projections are separated, flat, locally finitely presented and locally quasi-finite, and the identity subgroupoid is finite, while the full R -> U is not finite. The intended finite orbit and invariant-affine argument concerns P, not the full relation. Steps 3 and 4 explicitly use the finite projections of Q, identified with P_0, and never claim finiteness of H -> M or R_W -> W. This corrects a notational error in the source proof and does not dispute or weaken the item's statement.
