## Review by Q58

I checked the item against 08KB, 08W5 and 08KC with the node and TeX sources open. The hypotheses are a scheme S and a finitely presented morphism X → B of algebraic spaces; I did not add separation, Noetherianity, flatness of X → B, or finite presentation of an arbitrary test morphism. The finitely presented module is flat over the test scheme, not merely flat over X_T. The proper closed-carrier definition agrees with 0CZI because X_T → T is locally of finite type. This hypothesis is explicitly checked when the carrier lemma is used.

Step 1 uses exactly the assertion in `def-sfb-space-proper-quot-functor` that every base change of the specified finitely presented map is locally of finite type. It then applies `lem-sfb-space-module-base-change-flat-proper-support`, whose statement preserves all of quasi-coherence, finite presentation, relative flatness and the proper-carrier condition under arbitrary base change. I checked its locally-finite-type hypothesis and that it requires no flatness of the test map. A definition of the Quot functor is not being cited for an unstated descent theorem: the specific locally-finite-type assertion really is in its statement.

Step 2 uses the actual tensor composition formula of `lem-trf-space-chart-adjunction-tensor-realization`, not an informal assertion that pullbacks compose. I wrote the scalar multiplication check of the associativity and unit identities and the unique filler formula. `def-sfb-stable-pointed-moduli-groupoids` gives precisely the two lifting conditions that the filler checks. No representability or effectivity theorem is inferred from that definition. This is an elementary categorical verification from the cited comparison.

Step 3 uses the fppf sheaf condition in `def-trf-algebraic-space` to identify the two base maps. `lem-sfb-space-fppf-covering-calculus` supplies the covering after base change. Full faithfulness in the statement of `lem-sfb-space-fppf-qcoh-descent` descends compatible maps, including inverse maps; faithfulness checks the inverse equations. Thus the proof establishes the whole isomorphism sheaf condition without assuming that a diagonal is already representable.

Step 4 uses the same sheaf condition to glue b_i, the actual fibre-product assertion of `lem-trf-space-fibre-products-etale-covers` to identify double and triple overlaps, and covering calculus to obtain a genuine fppf cover of Y = X ×_B T. The essential-surjectivity clause of `lem-sfb-space-fppf-qcoh-descent` gives M and comparison isomorphisms inducing the original cocycle. Its statement includes both effectiveness and compatible morphisms and imposes no quasi-compactness on this cover. At this point I claim only quasi-coherence of M, not its extra properties.

Step 5 is an elementary reduction to an existing scheme theorem. U_i = U ×_T T_i is a scheme even though X and B are spaces. The maps U_i → U form an fppf scheme cover by covering calculus, hence an fpqc cover by `lem-trf-fppf-covering-is-fpqc`. `lem-trf-scheme-zariski-etale-quasi-coherent-equivalence` gives the ordinary pullback comparison, and `lem-sfb-scheme-etale-module-exact-finiteness-comparison` specifically identifies finite presentation on the two sites. Clause (2) of `lem-pc-fpqc-descent` reflects finite presentation of a quasi-coherent scheme module, with no Noetherian hypothesis. Finally `lem-sfb-space-qcoh-affine-chart-calculus` allows testing this property on an etale scheme atlas of Y. These statements really concern finite presentation of modules; I did not use finite presentation of algebras or morphisms in its place.

Step 6 uses the same chart and pullback comparisons, plus joint surjectivity from covering calculus, to choose u_i above each chart point u. Clause (2) of `lem-sfb-pointwise-module-flatness-base-change` is the exact flatness-reflection statement required. Its square is U_i → U over T_i → T, and its flatness hypothesis holds because T_i → T is fppf. Its input is the relative flatness of M_i over T_i on the etale chart U_i; its output is the relative flatness of M over T at u. I checked that it needs neither flatness of U → T nor a nonzero module stalk. This avoids mistakenly using only the forward base-change direction.

Step 7 proves the support identification by calculation, rather than assuming that arbitrary pullback commutes with scheme-theoretic support. `lem-sfb-space-module-proper-carrier-support` supplies the quasi-coherent annihilator and its closed subspace; finite type follows immediately from the finite presentation proved in step 5. On affine charts, `lem-sfb-space-qcoh-affine-chart-calculus` gives a finite section module N. Covering calculus and `lem-weil-pbc-flat-morphism-affine-criterion` give a flat ring map A → C. The tensor realization, scheme-site comparison, and `lem-weil-pbc-module-pullback-affine-sections` identify the pulled-back module with N ⊗_A C. `lem-trf-annihilator-finite-flat-base-change` then gives precisely Ann(N ⊗ C) = Ann(N) C, with both finite generation and flatness checked. `lem-sfb-space-closed-ideal-correspondence` explicitly states the ideal of a pulled-back closed subspace. Equality on covering charts proves equality of closed subspaces, not just equality of point sets. All the algebra is in existing cited statements, so no new node is warranted for this short chart calculation.

Step 8 applies `lem-sfb-space-module-proper-carrier-support` in both directions: first each local proper carrier makes its scheme-theoretic support proper; then the properness of the global scheme-theoretic support gives the required global carrier and finite type module. I checked local finite type for Y → T and Y_i → T_i using the same assertion as step 1. The properness clause of `lem-sfb-space-flat-proper-fp-locality` applies to Z → T with the actual fppf cover T_i → T and the closed subspaces identified in step 7; it imposes no finite-presentation hypothesis on Z. The resulting object and the comparison maps from step 4 give the prescribed datum, completing effectiveness.
