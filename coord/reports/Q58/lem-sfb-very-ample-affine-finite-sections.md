## Review by Q58

I read Morphisms Tag 02NP (`lemma-very-ample-finite-type-over-affine`), including its entire finite-chart/generator/numerator proof and omitted-twist sentence; Constructions 01OA/01OB's projective-bundle quotient discussion; Constructions 01N8's explicit homogeneous-fraction construction; and Morphisms 01QO's affine closed-immersion criterion. The nine steps follow 02NP's argument, with the final map supplied by the existing sections theorem rather than an unrecorded theorem about arbitrary graded Proj maps.

1. `def-trf-relatively-very-ample` supplies the witness immersion and isomorphism; `def-trf-canonical-symmetric-graded-algebra` gives the literal affine symmetric graded algebra on E(S); `lemma-nagata-relative-proj-exists` supplies its affine restriction identification; `lem-trf-projective-bundle-quotient-geometry` supplies the normalized quotient. None assumes E is finite or locally free. I use the canonical affine model rather than importing an unstated affine equivalence theorem.
2. `lem-weil-pbc-symmetric-algebra-graded` says positive homogeneous components are spanned by degree-one monomials, hence those elements generate the irrelevant ideal. `prop-nagata-proj-standard-open` gives their covering and localizations. `def-weil-pbc-proj-twisting-sheaf` describes sections as fractions of the specified degree and gives the global section b as b/1. Together with the normalized degree-one quotient of `lem-trf-projective-bundle-quotient-geometry`, these give the displayed basis m/1 and coefficient b/m^d. `def-nagata-homogeneous-localization-degree-zero` gives the ratios. Nonvanishing is checked on homogeneous primes, and after pullback on local rings. This is a direct computation in the definitions, not an invocation of an absent twist comparison or general graded-map theorem.
3. `def-weil-finite-type-morphism` gives quasi-compactness over the affine base, and `def-weil-pbc-quasi-compact-scheme` gives the finite subcover. The definition of immersion in `def-weil-closed-immersion` supplies an open W with a closed immersion into W. `lem-sc-proj-standard-open-basis` supplies basis refinement inside W, not merely a cover of the whole Proj. `lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-affine-cover-criterion` supplies affine inverse images and surjective maps for these affine opens contained in W. No claim that an arbitrary affine inverse image under an immersion is closed is made.
4. `lem-weil-pbc-lft-affine-pairs` explicitly applies to every affine pair, so it applies to each newly chosen U_j over S. Together with step 3 and the fractions in `def-nagata-homogeneous-localization-degree-zero`, it supplies all x_jk lifts and their exact homogeneous degrees. There is no finite-presentation or noetherian assumption.
5. The explicit monomial-spanning clause of `lem-weil-pbc-symmetric-algebra-graded` supplies homogeneous expressions, not just ungraded generation. Taking the finite union of the supports and replacing the module elements by variables is elementary; the lifted polynomials retain their degrees. Padding by zero handles an empty list without affecting the equations.
6. The rank-one local-freeness assertion of `def-sc-invertible-module` is precisely what turns a nonzero fibre value into a local generator. F_j has strictly positive degree, so nonzero evaluation implies a nonzero coordinate. This proves stalk surjectivity, which is the definition-level criterion for surjectivity of a sheaf map. The sections generate L itself, not a positive tensor power substituted for L.
7. `lem-trf-projective-bundle-quotient-geometry` states the natural quotient bijection, its quotient-compatible twist isomorphism, and its identification for the free module with the exact canonical finite projective-space twist. `lem-weil-pbc-sections-morphism-to-proj-polynomial-ring` explicitly gives coordinate ratios and the image of the pulled-back twist bases. These data identify its quotient with q, so uniqueness in the former cited statement identifies its map with a_s. I do not use uniqueness from the latter's proof.

The same sections theorem gives the degree-one substitutions. `def-pc-projective-space-twist` supplies the chosen local bases; `def-nagata-homogeneous-localization-degree-zero` supplies homogeneous fractions. Step 2 supplies the comparison with the original b_j,c_jk, so both equality of inverse-image opens and equality of the induced ring maps are justified, not just a comparison of point sets.

The ring-map image contains A and all its chosen algebra generators x_jk, hence is the whole A_j. `lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-affine-cover-criterion` now applies to an actual affine cover of that union.
