### Original step 1, assigned to `lem-zl-weil-heart-localization-exact`

All functors below act on geometric base changes to $k$, with the given $k_0$-structures retained for Frobenius. The schemes in the chain are separated and of finite type: on an affine chart a closed subscheme has ring $A/I$, which is generated over $k_0$ by the images of generators of $A$. Finite type over a field implies Noetherian by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian]; every open subscheme is quasi-compact by [lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-open-subscheme-noetherian], so its local finite-type presentations give a finite affine cover. Separatedness is inherited by these subschemes. [lem-pod-separated-finite-type-base-change] supplies the same hypotheses over $k$. By [lem-zl-immersion-weil-local-behavior], restrictions are exact heart functors. For every closed inclusion $i$, [lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-finite] and [lem-zl-finite-adic-direct-image] make $Ri_*$ an exact functor of constructible hearts. Its Weil lift is [prop-weil-operations]. Exactness is also valid on Weil sheaves: the underlying heart is abelian [def-weil-constructible-heart], and exact Frobenius pullback takes a kernel or cokernel diagram to a kernel or cokernel diagram. The commuting Weil square therefore induces invertible Weil maps on its kernel and cokernel; their universal properties are the same among commuting morphisms. This uses [lem-a1-constructible-pullback-t-exact] and the definition of Weil morphism [def-weil-weil-sheaf-complex]. Thus forming these subobjects and quotients stays in the stated Weil heart.

Cited interfaces: `lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian`, `lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-open-subscheme-noetherian`, `lem-pod-separated-finite-type-base-change`, `lem-zl-immersion-weil-local-behavior`, `lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-finite`, `lem-zl-finite-adic-direct-image`, `prop-weil-operations`, `def-weil-constructible-heart`, `lem-a1-constructible-pullback-t-exact`, `def-weil-weil-sheaf-complex`.

### Original step 2, assigned to `lem-zl-immersion-filtered-reduction-transport`

For an open inclusion $j$, [lem-zl-extension-by-zero-punctual] states exactness on constructible Weil sheaves and preservation of punctual purity of each real weight. For a closed inclusion $i$, its finiteness from [lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-finite] allows [lem-zl-finite-pushforward-punctual] to be applied: $i_*$ preserves punctual purity of the same weight. Together with Step 1, these facts prove preservation of a particular finite filtration. Namely, if $0=G_0\subset\cdots\subset G_n=G$, applying either exact functor $T$ gives monomorphisms $TG_s\to TG_{s+1}$ and short exact sequences $0\to TG_{s-1}\to TG_s\to T(G_s/G_{s-1})\to0$. Thus the new quotients are the images of the old quotients, pure of exactly the same assigned weights. All arrows have their induced Weil structures. This proves the component extension-by-zero assertion, as well as preservation of mixedness and every specified upper bound in [def-weil-punctual-weights].

Cited interfaces: `lem-zl-extension-by-zero-punctual`, `lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-finite`, `lem-zl-finite-pushforward-punctual`, `def-weil-punctual-weights`.

### Original step 3, assigned to `lem-zl-weil-heart-localization-exact`

For any of the complementary pairs $j:U_0\hookrightarrow S_0$, $i:C_0\hookrightarrow S_0$ in the argument, apply [prop-sf-open-closed-triangle-all-stages] at $\Lambda=\Qlb$ to a constructible Weil sheaf $H$. It gives the triangle $j_!j^*H\to H\to i_*i^*H\to j_!j^*H[1]$, with the actual counit and unit as its first two arrows. Steps 1 and 2 put its three vertices in the ordinary constructible heart. Clause 4 of [lem-weil-heart-short-exact-triangles] then gives $0\to j_!j^*H\to H\to i_*i^*H\to0$ in that heart. The triangle's first two arrows are Weil-equivariant because both immersions descend to $k_0$. The kernel and cokernel description of Step 1 therefore makes this a short exact sequence of Weil sheaves. Taking $S_0=Z_a$, $C_0=Z_{a+1}$ gives precisely every short exact sequence required in the statement.

Cited interfaces: `prop-sf-open-closed-triangle-all-stages`, `lem-weil-heart-short-exact-triangles`.

### Original step 4, assigned to `lem-zl-exact-functor-tower-filtration-splicing`

The splicing operation used below is clause 2 of [lem-a1-mixed-direct-summand], with its hypotheses checked in Step 3. Explicitly, for $0\to A\to B\xrightarrow{\pi}C\to0$ and filtrations $A_r,C_s$, first use the subobjects $A_r\subset B$, then the inverse-image subobjects $\pi^{-1}(C_s)\subset B$. The latter start at $\pi^{-1}(0)=A$ and end at $B$. Their successive quotients are $C_s/C_{s-1}$: the quotient map onto $C_s$ is surjective with kernel $A$, and then quotienting by the inverse image of $C_{s-1}$ has kernel exactly $\pi^{-1}(C_{s-1})$. The initial successive quotients are $A_r/A_{r-1}$. Kernels, images and these inverse images are constructible Weil subobjects by Step 1. Hence this finite filtration keeps the assigned pure weights of both input filtrations, even when their order is arbitrary, and keeps a common upper bound. No splitting of the short exact sequence is assumed.

Cited interfaces: `lem-a1-mixed-direct-summand`.

### Original step 5, assigned to `lem-zl-clopen-prescribed-weil-filtration`

For each $a$, apply finite induction on the number $N_a$ of clopen components of $U_a$. For $N_a=0$, $U_a=\varnothing$ and its only sheaf is zero, with the empty filtration: the empty covering forces a sheaf of modules on the empty site to have only its zero section, and all coefficient constructions preserve this zero object. For $N_a>0$, choose one component $V=U_{a,b}$ and let $C$ be the union of the remaining components. These are an open subscheme and its complementary closed subscheme of $U_a$, both defined over $k_0$. Put $H=\mathcal F|_{U_a}$. Inverse images compose: for the immersions $V\hookrightarrow U_a\hookrightarrow X_0$ the composition isomorphism $H|_V\cong\mathcal F|_{V}$ is a Weil isomorphism by [prop-weil-operations], and likewise for the components of $C$. Taking images of a filtration under a Weil isomorphism keeps its quotients up to isomorphism. So the given filtration supplies $H|_V$, and the induction hypothesis, applied to $C$ with its $N_a-1$ components, supplies $H|_C$. Step 2 extends their filtrations by $V\hookrightarrow U_a$ and $C\hookrightarrow U_a$, and Steps 3 and 4 splice them to a filtration of $H$. This proves that $\mathcal F|_{U_a}$ is mixed. Its filtration has the same list of pure weights as the component filtrations, concatenated in the chosen induction order, so a common bound $w$ is preserved.

Cited interfaces: `prop-weil-operations`.

### Original step 6, assigned to `lem-zl-exact-functor-tower-filtration-splicing`

Set $\mathcal F_a=\mathcal F|_{Z_a}$. The backward induction assertion is that $\mathcal F_a$ has a finite Weil filtration whose pure quotient weights belong to the concatenated lists of the prescribed component filtrations for indices $c\ge a$. Its base is $a=t$: $Z_t=\varnothing$ gives the zero sheaf and the empty filtration. Given the assertion for $a+1$, Step 5 supplies the open-stratum filtration at $a$. By the Weil composition isomorphisms of inverse images [prop-weil-operations], $j_a^*\mathcal F_a\cong\mathcal F|_{U_a}$ and $i_a^*\mathcal F_a\cong\mathcal F_{a+1}$, so these filtrations are filtrations of the two outer restrictions in Step 3. Apply Step 2 to extend it by $j_{a!}$, and to push the induction filtration forward by $i_{a*}$. The short exact sequence of Step 3 and the explicit splicing of Step 4 prove the induction assertion for $a$. There are only $t$ stages, so this proves the assertion for $a=0$, on $(X_0)_{\mathrm{red}}$. In particular the number of filtration steps is finite and every original upper bound is retained. If no upper bound was prescribed, the finitely many input weights have a common real upper bound (use $0$ if the list is empty), so this also proves unbounded mixedness in the stated sense.

Cited interfaces: `def-weil-punctual-weights`, `prop-weil-operations`.

### Original step 7, assigned to `lem-zl-immersion-filtered-reduction-transport`

By [lem-weil-pbc-reduction-universal-homeomorphism], $r:(X_0)_{\mathrm{red}}\hookrightarrow X_0$ is a surjective closed immersion. Its complementary open is therefore empty. Apply the construction of Step 3 with $i=r$ and $H=\mathcal F$: the open term is zero, so the resulting heart sequence is $0\to0\to\mathcal F\xrightarrow{\eta}r_*r^*\mathcal F\to0$. Thus the actual reduction unit $\eta$ is an isomorphism. It is Weil-equivariant by the Weil clause of [prop-sf-open-closed-triangle-all-stages]; its inverse is equivariant as well, by multiplying its commuting square by the inverse on both sides. Apply Step 2 to $r_*$ and the filtration of $r^*\mathcal F=\mathcal F_0$ from Step 6, and take inverse images along $\eta$. This yields the required filtration of $\mathcal F$ with the same quotient weights and upper bounds. This also treats nonreduced schemes without comparing coefficient models. If $t=0$, the reduction is empty and this isomorphism says $\mathcal F=0$; zero sheaves and empty components were already included in the two inductions. None of these steps uses reality.

Cited interfaces: `lem-weil-pbc-reduction-universal-homeomorphism`, `prop-sf-open-closed-triangle-all-stages`.
