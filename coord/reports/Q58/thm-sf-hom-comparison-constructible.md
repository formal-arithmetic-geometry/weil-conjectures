Finite type over $k$ makes $X$ quasi-compact, so [lem-weil-pbc-qcqs-finite-affine-open-cover] gives a finite affine open cover $j_i:U_i\hookrightarrow X$. We will apply the separated comparison theorem on these $U_i$; no assertion that intersections of affine opens of $X$ are affine is needed.

### Returned step 2 → lem-sf-hom-comparison-affine-inputs

Restriction to an open subscheme is localization of either small site at that open object: an object over $U_i$ is precisely an etale, respectively weakly etale, $X$-object equipped with a factorization through $U_i$. By [lem-pur-site-restriction-module-adjunction-exact] this restriction is exact. Therefore it commutes with cohomology, computed as kernels modulo images, and preserves the cohomological bounds. Intersecting a constructible stratification with $U_i$ preserves its finite locally constant restrictions; hence $K_i=K|_{U_i}$ and $M_i=M|_{U_i}$ are bounded constructible, using [def-weil-pbc-constructible-complexes]. To check finite Tor amplitude without any assumption on $M$, choose a bounded flat model $P$ for $K$ using the etale-site clause of [lem-sf-flat-model]. Restriction carries its quasi-isomorphism to a quasi-isomorphism, and each $P^r|_{U_i}$ is flat by [lem-weil-pbc-slice-restriction-flat]. The converse flat-model implication on $U_i$ gives $K_i\in D^b_{ctf}(U_{i,\mathrm{et}},A)$ with the same interval.

### Returned step 4 → lem-sf-comparison-open-monoidal-square

The comparison pullback also commutes canonically with open restriction. To see the actual identification, write $j_{\tau,*}$ for direct image from $U_\tau$ to $X_\tau$, given by $(j_{\tau,*}G)(V)=G(V\times_XU)$, for $\tau=\mathrm{et},\mathrm{proet}$. The restriction/direct-image adjunction follows by sending a natural map $F|_U\to G$ to its components $F(V)\to F(V\times_XU)\to G(V\times_XU)$, and recovering the former map at objects already over $U$. The formula $(\nu_*G)(V)=G(V)$ on etale $V$ in [def-weil-pbc-site-comparison-morphism] gives, componentwise,
$$\nu_{X,*}j_{\mathrm{proet},*}=j_{\mathrm{et},*}\nu_{U,*}.$$
Consequently the two left adjoints $j_{\mathrm{proet}}^*\nu_X^*$ and $\nu_U^*j_{\mathrm{et}}^*$ have the same Hom bijections, giving the canonical isomorphism between them (the unique map corresponding to the identity of the common right adjoint). Exactness of $\nu^*$ [lem-sf-comparison-pullback-exact] and of restriction makes this the same identification on complexes and derived categories.

Cited nodes: def-weil-pbc-site-comparison-morphism, def-pur-localized-ringed-site, lem-sf-comparison-pullback-exact, lem-pur-site-restriction-module-adjunction-exact.

### Returned step 8 → parent assembly step 4

Finally fix $r\in\mathbf Z$ and apply $\mathcal H^r$ to $\beta_X$. Exact restriction commutes with these cohomology sheaves, so steps 6 and 7 give an inverse on every $U_i$.
