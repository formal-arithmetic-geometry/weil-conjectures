Step 4 checks the ordinary comparison's normalization. `lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-module-adjunction` states the tensor-formula adjunction, and `def-weil-pbc-ringed-tensor-section-calculus` supplies both the scalar-extension unit at a local generator and the pure-tensor determination property.
