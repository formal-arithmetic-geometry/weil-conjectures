## Problems found and corrected

There is no statement dispute: the imported classification is precisely the module specialization of Toën's Lemma 6.2, with the homotopically projective variant justified by the already existing projective-resolution statement. The original node had no proof. Its source description alone did not expose the distinction between a cofibrant diagram and an arbitrary diagram with cofibrant values, nor the local cofibrancy hypotheses needed when changing its index. These hypotheses are now checked explicitly.

Toën's Sub-lemma 4.5 leaves the injective-arrow-model fibration case to the reader. The split does not conceal that missing case: it replaces every quasi-isomorphism by a span of pointwise trivial cofibrations, through a projective factorization of the map from a coproduct. It then computes the arrow-dg-category Hom complexes directly. This also avoids assuming that the saturated full subcategory S is stable, additive, or closed under coproducts.
