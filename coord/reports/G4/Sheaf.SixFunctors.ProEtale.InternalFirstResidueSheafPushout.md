## Where the brief, the DAG or earlier work was wrong

The original additive pushout claim was false: the additive image of the discrete
uniformizer ideal is locally constant, while the continuous quotient kernel contains
continuous uniformizer multiples with infinite image. In a profinite test the latter
cannot become locally constant on a finite jointly surjective covering.

The claim for every residue isomorphism also omitted normalization: twisting the
canonical isomorphism by a nontrivial residue-field Frobenius changes the image of
constant residue classes and makes the square fail to commute. The original split
therefore could not prove the ring pushout stated by its consumer.

The corrected theorem expresses the ring quotient universal property from the source,
and the unchanged consumer now supplies its normalization by a proved construction.
The changes and sole DAG citer are recorded explicitly; no unproved existence theorem
is used to choose arbitrary definition data.
