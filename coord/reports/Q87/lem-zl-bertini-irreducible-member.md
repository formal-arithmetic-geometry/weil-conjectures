## Source error

In more-morphisms.tex, proof of lemma-bertini-irreducible, the sentence “f_1 cannot be zero because this would mean v_1 = 0” is invalid when psi has a kernel. For example, on A^2 take V with basis e1,e2,e3, psi(e1)=1, psi(e2)=s, psi(e3)=t, and add a fourth basis vector e4 with psi(e4)=0: the nonzero e4 has zero section. This counterexample refutes the implication, not the theorem or its hypotheses. Step 4 instead uses the nonvanishing of psi(v1) at the generic point of W and the fact that a section of an invertible module on an integral scheme injects into its generic fibre. The local argument is written out with domain fraction fields and invertible transition functions. The item statement remains exactly the imported statement.
