## Preserved original proof and assignment

### Old step 1 — assigned to lem-trf-free-module-sheaf-universal-property

Write $L=f^{-1}$, $R=f_*$ and $B\{E\}$ for the free module sheaf in the statement. Its model is the module sheafification of $P(U)=B(U)[E(U)]$, with restriction $\sum b_e[e]\mapsto\sum(b_e|_V)[e|_V]$. For any $B$-module sheaf $M$ and sheaf map $a:E\to |M|$, the formula $\sum b_e[e]\mapsto\sum b_ea(e)$ defines a unique sectionwise linear map $P\to M$: it commutes with restrictions since $a$ does. The module-sheafification universal property extends it uniquely to $B\{E\}\to M$. Conversely restrict a linear map to the generator map $i:E\to|B\{E\}|$. These operations are inverse, giving $\operatorname{Hom}_B(B\{E\},M)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(E,|M|)$, natural in both arguments. This same construction applies on the target site with the ring $LB$.

### Old step 2 — assigned to lem-trf-geometric-free-module-hom-equivalence

Apply the geometric-module transport theorem with $A=S=LB$, $e_R$ the identity on the transported ring, and $c=\operatorname{id}_{LB}$. Its hypotheses are exactly the small sites, the adjunction $L\dashv R$, and finite-limit preservation of $L$ in the present geometric morphism. The theorem gives the transported module $LM$ with underlying sheaf $L|M|$ and the $B$-module $R_BH$ with underlying sheaf $R|H|$ (scalars act through the unit $B\to RLB$). It gives a natural bijection $\operatorname{Hom}_{LB}(LM,H)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_B(M,R_BH)$ whose underlying map is the original set-sheaf adjunction. Thus this is precisely inverse image with transported scalars; no extension to a different coefficient ring occurs.

### Old step 3 — assigned to lem-trf-geometric-free-module-hom-equivalence

For any $LB$-module sheaf $H$, apply step 2 with $M=B\{E\}$, then step 1, then the set-sheaf adjunction: $\operatorname{Hom}_{LB}(L(B\{E\}),H)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_B(B\{E\},R_BH)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(E,R|H|)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(LE,|H|)$. The normalization clause of the transport theorem and naturality of the set adjunction show that this composite sends $h$ to $|h|\circ Li$: the adjunct of $h$ composed with $i$ is the adjunct of $h\circ Li$. Hence $L(B\{E\})$, with generator map $Li$, has the free universal property on $LE$.

### Old step 4 — assigned to lem-trf-free-module-sheaf-universal-property

Let $j:LE\to |(LB)\{LE\}|$ be the canonical generator map. By step 1 there is a unique linear $q:(LB)\{LE\}\to L(B\{E\})$ with $qj=Li$; by step 3 there is a unique linear $p:L(B\{E\})\to(LB)\{LE\}$ with $p(Li)=j$. The identity $p(Li)=j$ is the precise generator-preservation assertion, formulated as a map of sheaves and therefore covering all generalized sections, without assuming that a section of an inverse image lifts globally.
