1. `sk-def-localizing-closure` fixes arbitrary finite commutative ell-primary rings and the integral/rational stages. `sk-def-sfb-dc-setting` supplies separated finite-type schemes over a field of characteristic different from ell and compactifications staying in that setting. `lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian` makes these schemes Noetherian, since a field is Noetherian. `det-proper-base-change-unbounded-pullback` explicitly states pullback preservation of Dctf. `det-def-localizing-closure-ctf-equals-bc` identifies Dctf with bounded constructible complexes only for integral and rational coefficients. No such identification is made for nonregular finite A.

2. `det-pe-finite-tor-comparison-noetherian` gives the finite Tor comparison equivalence, including tests against all pro-étale module sheaves. Its Noetherian coefficient hypothesis holds because A is finite. `lem-sf-etale-flat-constructible` gives the bounded flat constructible model in an ordered Tor interval. I read Stacks Tag 03TT, `etale-cohomology.tex`, `lemma-when-ctf`, which states that model theorem. The common stratification is exactly `lem-weil-pbc-constructible-stratification-intersection`; termwise local tensor constructibility is `lem-weil-pbc-locally-constant-tensor` (Tag 094H, `lemma-locally-constant-tensor-product`), applied to flat terms in degree zero. The finite sums and cohomology use `thm-constructible-serre` (Tag 03RZ, `lemma-constructible-abelian`). The flatness of a tensor of two flat modules is written as composition of the two exact tensor functors. Bounded-flat K-flatness is `lem-pc-bounded-above-flat-kflat-site`, whose actual source I read at Tag 06YQ. The support interval of the total tensor is [a+c,b+d]. `lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-derived-tensor-pullback` (Tag 07A4, `lemma-pullback-tensor-product`) identifies the tensor under the exact comparison pullback.

3. Clause (2) of `det-lem-integral-models-operations` states integral tensor closure; clause (1) of `det-thm-sf-pf-field-tensor` states rational tensor closure. Step 1's equality converts each to Dctf. Setting E equal to Q_l covers both Z_l and Q_l. No coefficient category is silently identified with a finite-coefficient one.

4. `lem-pqf-qc-immersion-open-closed-factor` applies because the source of the immersion and all its opens are Noetherian. I read Tag 01QV, `morphisms.tex`, `lemma-factor-quasi-compact-immersion`. The intermediate closed subscheme is Noetherian. `sk-prop-sfb-cartesian-coefficients` identifies inverse images of coefficient rings. `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-ringed-open-closed` states exactness, the identity restrictions on the support and zero restrictions off it; the quasi-compact-complement hypotheses hold here. I checked its source Tag 09BM, `lemma-closed-immersion-complement-retrocompact-exact`, and Tag 09BN, `lemma-open-immersion`. `lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-functor` permits termwise derivation. The finite stratification of `sk-def-sfb-constructible`, composed with the immersion using clause (e) of `lem-weil-pbc-proper-fp-permanence`, together with T minus S and X minus T proves constructibility directly. Finally `det-lem-int-tensor-projection` is stated for any condensed ring and arbitrary derived arguments, despite its integral-looking id. Its displayed projection isomorphism tests the Tor interval against every target module N. Exactness of a_! then preserves the same interval. This does not incorrectly test the cohomology modules of K for perfectness.

5. `cor-fin-deligne-open-finite-coefficients` gives bounded constructibility for arbitrary finite A killed by an invertible integer. The integer can be a power of ell in the standing field. `lem-kun-0f10` clause (1) gives finite relative cd(j) over an arbitrary field. I read Tag 0F10, `etale-cohomology.tex`, `lemma-finite-cd`; its absolute assertion (2) has an additional field-cd hypothesis and is not used. `det-geo-open-finiteness-ii-amplitude` clause (1) supplies vanishing for all étale module sheaves above d. The finite truncation argument explicitly derives the [a,b+d] complex bound. `lem-sf-projection-finite-constant` requires this uniform vanishing, a Noetherian ring, a bounded-above complex, and a finitely presented scalar module; all are available. It proves the constant-module projection comparison, not the arbitrary-sheaf test. That remaining test is supplied by exact geometric stalks (`prop-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks-exact`), derived tensor pullback, and exact filtered colimits. Every module over finite A is a filtered union of finitely generated, hence finitely presented, modules. `lem-pc-kflat-resolution` (Tag 06YS, whose proof I read) and `lem-pur-ringed-site-grothendieck-abelian`, applied on the one-point site, justify computing tensor and cohomology through that union. `lemma-weil-pbc-geometric-stalk-conservative` detects vanishing on the étale site. `prop-weil-pbc-comparison-direct-image` applies to the qcqs open map and bounded-below C; `lem-exc-comparison-tor-interval` transfers the result against every pro-étale test sheaf. 6. `det-thm-sf-pf-adic-open` states integral open preservation itself, including derived completeness and reduction comparison. It applies to the Noetherian quasi-compact open case. Its output is bounded constructible, hence bounded below; Step 1 converts this to Dctf.

7. `sk-def-sfb-change-of-scalars` defines restriction on the same underlying sheaves and explicitly gives exactness.
