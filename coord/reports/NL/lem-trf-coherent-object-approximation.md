## Counterexample to the imported generality

Here is an explicit example using only presheaves and finite presentations, independently of any prohibited source route.

Let C be a small skeleton of affine Q-schemes of finite presentation, and give C the trivial topology (identity coverings). This category has finite limits: on the algebra side they are finite colimits of finitely presented Q-algebras. The topos E = Set^(C^op) is coherent. Indeed its representables generate, their finite limits are representables, and they are coherent by the existing finite-cover-site criterion `lem-trf-finitary-site-coherent-generators` applied to this trivial site.

Put P = h_(A^1_Q) and let t:P→P be translation by 1. Let Z be the presheaf coequalizer of id_P and t. For U = Spec A one has Z(U)=A/Z as a set of additive orbits, so the kernel pair R=P ×_Z P has sections (a,b) with b−a an integer. For each n∈Z the graph Γ_n→R, a↦(a,a+n), is a representable subobject isomorphic to P. These graphs jointly cover R sectionwise. No finite subfamily covers: evaluate at Spec Q and choose the pair (0,N) for an integer N not among the finitely many chosen integers. Consequently R is not quasi-compact. Since P is quasi-compact, Z fails precisely the quasi-separatedness test for P→Z←P. In particular Z is not coherent.

Z is nevertheless finitely presentable in the categorical sense. Hom(Z,H) is the equalizer of the two maps H(A^1_Q)⇒H(A^1_Q). Evaluation preserves filtered colimits of presheaves, and filtered colimits of sets commute with finite equalizers: an equality of two representatives holds at one later stage, and finitely many such equalities hold at a common stage. Hence Hom(Z,−) preserves small filtered colimits.

If Z were a filtered colimit of coherent C_i, finite presentability would factor id_Z through one C_i: Z→C_i→Z with composite identity. A retract of a coherent object is coherent: the retraction is epic, so its target is quasi-compact; the section is monic, so its source inherits quasi-separatedness from C_i. This contradicts the computed non-quasi-separatedness of Z. Thus the original clause is false even on a coherent presheaf topos. The counterexample is not a claim that the source's geometric theorem is false.
