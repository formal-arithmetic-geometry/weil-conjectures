New restriction lemma: the exact extension/restriction adjunction of 03DJ lifts levelwise to towers, since natural adjuncts preserve the transition equations; exactness is levelwise. Hence 08BJ preserves K-injective tower complexes under restriction. For a tower resolution X to Q, restriction is still a resolution, and the ordinary limit comparison is the natural Hom bijection between maps from j_!B to lim Q and compatible families from B to j^*Q_n. Applying this degreewise is the asserted derived comparison, with every projection fixed. This is more than ordinary preservation of limits: it identifies the chosen total-derived comparison via preserved tower resolutions. It remains an unsplit statement, with this explanation only in the report. New image lemma: 0941's evaluation argument constructs the exact left adjoint of evaluation at n: it places B at positions 1 through n, with identity transitions, and zero above n. A tower map from it is determined by its nth component. Thus evaluation preserves K-injective complexes by 08BJ. For a K-injective tower resolution X to Q, each Q_n is K-injective; if each X_n is too, 070I makes q_n a homotopy equivalence. An additive functor preserves its homotopy inverse and equations, so G(q_n) is a quasi-isomorphism. The maps are evaluations of one strict tower map G(q), giving an actual derived-category isomorphism with all transitions, and functorial resolution makes it natural. The conclusion never asserts that X itself is K-injective in the tower category. This auxiliary computation says nothing about completion or the item's adjunction and is strictly smaller than that item. I generalized the extracted direct-image calculation to an additive functor only because its proof uses just additivity, so its independent hypotheses and scope are explicit.

For G=identity or G=zero the comparison gives the expected identity or zero result; a zero-transition tower stays zero-transition and the identity-transition tower stays constant.
