Step 4 applies the previously opened compactness node to P over Z_V, not to P over V. Flatness and finite presentation follow from the cited scheme-morphism permanence results. Finite presentation includes quasi-compactness and quasi-separatedness. The affine base needed by that compactness node is Z_V, proved affine in step 2. Geometric reducedness after field extension is checked directly using the definition: any L over kappa(z) is also an extension of kappa(b). Restriction from Z to its open Z_V changes neither those residue fields nor the fibre components. Consequently C_V is precisely the section locus to which the cited compactness node applies. This avoids the false inference that an arbitrary open in a quasi-compact scheme must be quasi-compact.
