## Statement disputes and source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported clause (i) claims that B = Gamma(Y,O_Y) is finite over every affine henselian base. Stacks 03H2 (more-morphisms.tex, theorem-stein-factorization-general) gives integral, not finite. The source's Noetherian finite version follows from 02O5/02O6. The suggested descent chain cannot justify the imported assertion: proper does not imply finite presentation over a non-Noetherian base, and H^0 of a structure sheaf does not in general commute with arbitrary nonflat base change. The cited etale section-continuity node concerns a different coefficient object.

Here is an explicit counterexample, including computation. Let k be a field, M an infinite-dimensional k-vector space, and A=k⊕M the square-zero ring, with I=M. A is local with nilpotent maximal ideal and (A,M) is henselian (idempotents lift uniquely modulo nilpotent ideals, also in every finite A-algebra). In P^1_A with homogeneous coordinates [X:Y], take the closed subscheme W defined by the homogeneous ideal generated by mX^2 for every m∈M. The morphism W→Spec A is proper: it is a closed immersion into the proper P^1_A. Its two usual charts have rings

    D_+(Y): k[t] ⊕ (M ⊗_k k[t]/(t^2)), t=X/Y,
    D_+(X): k[t^{-1}],
    overlap: k[t,t^{-1}].

The ideal kills all M on D_+(X); on D_+(Y) it kills exactly Mt^2k[t]. A pair of chart sections agrees on the overlap iff its reduced polynomial is constant. Therefore Gamma(W,O_W)=k⊕M⊕Mt, with square-zero ideal M⊕Mt. The map A→Gamma(W,O_W) embeds its M in the constant M summand. Multiplication by I on this ring has image exactly that summand, so B/IB=k⊕Mt has infinite k-dimension. A finite A-module would have finite-dimensional quotient modulo I, a contradiction. This tests the disputed clause within its actual henselian hypotheses, not just for an arbitrary non-Noetherian base.

Corrected clause (i): integral for every A, finite and coherent with A Noetherian. Clauses (ii) and (iii) retain the general henselian base, without adding a Noetherian hypothesis there. This is a correction of a false imported claim, explicitly authorized by the brief; no target statement or unit record was changed.

The imported final paragraph also says that local constancy is essential for spectral restriction and invokes the example T=A^1_k, T_0={0}, skyscraper at y≠0. That example does not satisfy the space hypothesis: the irreducible closed singleton {y} misses T_0. Stacks 09ZG, etale-cohomology.tex, lemma-h0-topological, has no local constancy assumption. Thus the third comparison obligation is true, and the existing general sheaf-of-sets node lem-weil-pbc-h0-spectral-closed-restriction supplies it. I did not edit the other worker's lemma-weil-pbc-spectral-restriction or lemma-weil-pbc-stein-connectedness. The latter still contains the same false general finiteness sentence and should receive the corresponding correction by its owner.
