Step 5 writes out the overring calculation: a negative pi-adic power in the dominating local subring would invert pi. Thus the stalk map is an isomorphism. The cited 09NH node asks only for finite type, reduced target and finitely many target components, all supplied in Steps 1 and 3. It does not require properness.
