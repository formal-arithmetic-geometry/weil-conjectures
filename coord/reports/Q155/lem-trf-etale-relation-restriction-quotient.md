## Argument allocation, with the returned text retained

The following is the original proof verbatim, not a replacement proof or a shortening. It records every argument transferred to the new nodes. ### Original Step 1: lem-trf-etale-pullback-relation-presheaf

By [def-pc-fibre-product], for every $S$-scheme $T$ a $T$-point of $R'$ is a triple $(a,r,b)$ with $a,b\in U'(T)$, $r\in R(T)$, $s(r)=g(a)$ and $t(r)=g(b)$. Thus the induced relation on $U'(T)$ is $a\sim' b$ if and only if $g(a)\sim g(b)$. Reflexivity, symmetry and transitivity follow respectively from these three properties of the relation on $U(T)$. Since $R(T)$ is an equivalence relation, its arrow with specified endpoints is unique, so the triple is determined by $(a,b)$; this also verifies the monomorphism into $U'\times_SU'$. These identifications are natural in $T$, giving the required equivalence relation over $S$.

### Original Step 2: lem-trf-etale-pullback-relation-presheaf

For the first projection put $A=U'\times_{g,U,s}R$, with maps $p:A\to U'$ and $v=t\circ\operatorname{pr}_R:A\to U$. Then $R'=A\times_{v,U,g}U'$, and $s':R'\to U'$ factors as $R'\to A\xrightarrow{p}U'$. The first map is a base change of $g$, and the second is a base change of $s$. Both are etale by [lem-weil-pbc-etale-scheme-base-change], so their composite is etale by clause (2) of [lem-weil-pbc-etale-composition-cancellation]. For $t'$ use $B=R\times_{t,U,g}U'$: the map $R'\to B$ is a base change of $g$ along $s\circ\operatorname{pr}_R$, and $B\to U'$ is a base change of $t$. The same two cited statements make $t'$ etale. Together with Step 1 this proves that $R'$ is an etale equivalence relation.

### Original Step 3: lem-trf-etale-pullback-relation-presheaf (presheaf construction and injection); lem-trf-fppf-generator-sheafification-comparison (canonical sheafification map)

Write $P(T)=U(T)/R(T)$ and $P'(T)=U'(T)/R'(T)$ for the presheaf quotients. The formula $[a]\mapsto[g\circ a]$ gives a natural map $v:P'\to P$ by the relation calculation in Step 1. Moreover it is injective on each $T$: if $[g\circ a]=[g\circ b]$, some $r:T\to R$ has endpoints $g\circ a,g\circ b$, so $(a,r,b):T\to R'$ witnesses $[a]=[b]$. Let $F'=P'^\#$ and $F=P^\#$. The universal property in [def-weil-pbc-sheafification] applied to $P'\xrightarrow{v}P\to F$ gives the canonical map $\alpha:F'\to F$, with $\alpha(\eta'[a])=\eta[g\circ a]$ for the sheafification units $\eta',\eta$.

### Original Step 4: lem-trf-fppf-generator-sheafification-comparison

To prove injectivity fix $\xi,\zeta\in F'(T)$ with $\alpha(\xi)=\alpha(\zeta)$. The local-representative assertion of [lem-weil-pbc-site-sheafification-sections-local], applied to $P'$, gives coverings representing each section by a quotient class. Pull back one covering along the other and compose, using the site axioms of [def-weil-pbc-sites-and-cohomology], to obtain a common covering $T_i\to T$ and representatives $a_i,b_i:T_i\to U'$ for $\xi|_{T_i},\zeta|_{T_i}$. Their images give classes $[g a_i],[g b_i]\in P(T_i)$ with equal images in $F(T_i)$. The local-equality assertion of [lem-weil-pbc-site-sheafification-sections-local], now applied to $P$, gives a further covering of each $T_i$ where these classes agree. Step 3's injectivity of $v$ then gives $[a_i]=[b_i]$ on that refinement. Hence $\xi$ and $\zeta$ agree on a covering of $T$, and the sheaf equalizer condition of [def-weil-pbc-sites-and-cohomology] gives $\xi=\zeta$. This proves sectionwise injectivity of $\alpha$ for every $T$.

### Original Step 5: lem-trf-fppf-generator-sheafification-comparison

Suppose now that $g$ is surjective. By [def-weil-etale-morphism], $g$ is flat and locally of finite presentation, so $\{g\}$ is an fppf covering by [def-pc-fppf-site]. For $z\in F(T)$, [lem-weil-pbc-site-sheafification-sections-local] applied to $P$ supplies an fppf covering $T_i\to T$ where $z$ is represented by a map $a_i:T_i\to U$. Pull back the singleton etale surjective family $\{g\}$ along $a_i$. By [lem-weil-pbc-etale-covering-base-change], the projection $W_i=T_i\times_{a_i,U,g}U'\to T_i$ is etale and surjective, hence again an fppf covering by the same two definitions. Its second projection $b_i:W_i\to U'$ satisfies $g b_i=a_i\operatorname{pr}_{T_i}$, so $\eta'[b_i]\in F'(W_i)$ maps to $z|_{W_i}$. The site composition axiom of [def-weil-pbc-sites-and-cohomology] makes $\{W_i\to T\}$ a covering. Thus every section of $F$ lifts locally through $\alpha$.

### Original Step 6: lem-trf-fppf-generator-sheafification-comparison

For the local lifts from Step 5, their two restrictions to each overlap have the same $\alpha$-image, namely the restriction of $z$. Step 4, applied to that overlap, makes the restrictions equal. The sheaf equalizer condition of [def-weil-pbc-sites-and-cohomology] therefore glues the lifts uniquely to $\widetilde z\in F'(T)$, and separation of $F$ gives $\alpha(\widetilde z)=z$. Step 4 gives uniqueness of this preimage. Restricting $\widetilde z$ along any test-scheme morphism gives the unique preimage of the restricted $z$, so these inverses are natural in $T$. They define an inverse of $\alpha$, proving the asserted isomorphism when $g$ is surjective.

For the sheafification child, local representatives of arbitrary P-sections are represented by maps into U using the assumed surjectivity of q; similarly for P' and q'. In the injection argument, Step 4's final use of presheaf quotient injectivity is precisely the hypothesis that v is injective. This introduces no additional geometric assumption. The pullback lift in old Step 5 becomes the stated section eta'(q'(b_i)), whose image is eta(q(g b_i)) = eta(q(a_i p_i)). Old Step 6 gives independence of all local choices and naturality by unique preimages. These are explicit conclusions of the new statement rather than unrecorded compatibility requirements.
