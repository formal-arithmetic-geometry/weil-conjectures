Even at r = 0, s = 1 and w the map from the empty scheme to Spec(k), `Lw* tau_{1,T}` starts at `Lw* G_T T_{1,T}`, while the second twist factor of the canonical datum starts at `G_{T'} Lw* T_{1,T}`.
