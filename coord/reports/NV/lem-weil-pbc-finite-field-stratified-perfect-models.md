The statement is true as written: finite-field coefficients remove the projective-dimension obstruction without any characteristic invertibility condition on X. The Noetherian hypothesis is retained, although the argument with the stated global constructibility convention needs only the finite partitions already provided by that convention. I did not substitute a completed integral category for the finite constant-ring category.

Step 1 uses `def-weil-pbc-bounded-derived-subcategories` to choose one finite interval and `def-weil-pbc-etale-constructible-sheaf` to choose one finite partition for each degree. `lem-weil-pbc-constructible-stratification-intersection` actually states both the scheme-theoretic fibre-product construction and constructibility of the intersections. Iterating it over the finite integer interval supplies the common partition. I checked that refinement projections exist as scheme morphisms; a mere inclusion of images would not suffice. The iteration has a starting partition even for a one-degree complex, and empty intersections cause no problem.

Step 2 uses part (3) of `prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact`, not just the definition of pullback. Preserving kernels, images and quotients gives the claimed cohomology identity and preserves the original interval. `lem-sfb-geometric-inverse-image-constant-module` explicitly states preservation of constant module sheaves with the same module and naturality in module maps. Base-changing the original trivializing families along the actual refinement projections, then along any etale object W, gives the local hypothesis on every object demanded in the next step. This checks the module structure as well as the underlying finite sheaf. The etale-site definition supplies the covering and fibre-product convention.

Step 3 applies `lem-weil-pbc-etale-site-bounded-constant-models` with precisely its finite commutative ring hypothesis, bounds a <= b, and local finite-generation condition on every etale object. Its statement gives a bounded constant complex of finite modules, with the stated interval; it is not merely a graded constant model. Finiteness as a set makes finite generation elementary by listing the elements, and finite generation over the field means finite-dimensionality. The source 094G proof was opened to verify that the differentials too are constant. No assertion about global splitting of etale extensions is needed.
