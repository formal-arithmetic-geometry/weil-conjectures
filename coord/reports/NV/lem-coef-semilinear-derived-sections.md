### STATEMENT-DISPUTED: tensor-Hom evaluation needs its input scope

The last paragraph's unqualified reference to simultaneous tensor evaluation could be read as asserting a fiberwise internal-Hom section and its evaluation for arbitrary lax inputs. That interpretation is false. On the point site with the constant coefficient field k, take N_n=k with identity transitions, M_1=0 and M_n=k for n>=2, and let M_2 -> M_1 be zero, with identity transitions above level 2. All objects are concentrated in degree zero. Fiberwise Hom gives H_1=0 and H_n=k for n>=2, so H_2 -> H_1 must be zero. If evaluation H tensor M -> N were a section map, along 2 -> 1 its top-side composite would be the nonzero evaluation k tensor k -> k followed by id_k, while the other side factors through H_1 tensor M_1=0. This forces id_k=0.

I clarified the item to assert fiberwise internal Hom/evaluation for a **cartesian first input** (and any second input). Step 8 constructs the transition by precomposing with the inverse cartesian comparison, followed by tensor evaluation and the second-input transition, and verifies the composite-arrow formula. This preserves the ancestor's intended application: lem-coef-derived-system-evaluation-calculus and lem-coef-finite-adjunction-diagram-realization use normalized systems. The source's desired coefficient-projection and exchange maps on arbitrary inputs still exist; their invertibility has not been added. No target statement, coefficient category, chapter or unit route is changed.
