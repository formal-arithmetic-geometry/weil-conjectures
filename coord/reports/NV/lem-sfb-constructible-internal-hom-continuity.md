I read Bhatt–Scholze 6.3.14, 6.8.12 and the formal internal-adjunction calculation in the proof of 6.7.13. For `lem-sfb-finite-model-stratum-filtration` I read Stacks Tag 09Y5, `lemma-noetherian-partition-refined-by-stratification`, and its Noetherian induction, as well as the preceding good-stratification construction. I use it only for a finite locally closed partition already supplied by the constructibility definition. A finite good stratification has an ordering with initial unions open (order strata oppositely to containment of closures). Its reduced induced strata factor into the original strata, so restrictions of the given local models are still models; this avoids inferring scheme-theoretic factorization merely from set-theoretic containment. For this fixed ordering, the exact open-closed sequence in the statement of `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-ringed-open-closed`, extended by the exact outer open extension, gives the successive quotient `v_!t_*L`. This is the reason for stating that factorization rather than appealing to independence of two locally closed factorizations. The new result packages the non-elementary finite good refinement, with its topological Noetherian hypothesis, and the sheaf filtration; it contains no continuity or derived internal Hom.
