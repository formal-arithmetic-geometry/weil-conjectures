## Proof of the new helper (retained for review)

Source: Stacks Tag 09BP, proetale.tex, lemma-j-shriek-limits. The new node is statement-only as required by the one-level contract. Its proof is:

1. Choose a closed immersion $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$ with image $X\setminus U$. Put $H=j_*F$. The direct-image formula and the slice identification give $j^{-1}H=F$: on $V\to U$, $V\times_XU=V$. Apply ringed open-closed exactness to $H$, using the quasi-compactness of $j$. Its first arrow identifies $j_!F$ naturally with $\ker(j_*F\to i_*i^{-1}j_*F)$. Uses: lem-weil-pbc-closed-subset-closed-subscheme, def-weil-proetale-inverse-image, lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization, lem-weil-pbc-proetale-ringed-open-closed.

2. The sectionwise direct-image formula and the sectionwise module-limit formula show that $j_*$ and $i_*$ preserve products, with coefficients restricted along their adjoint ring maps. Closed inverse image $i^{-1}:\operatorname{Mod}(R)\to\operatorname{Mod}(i^{-1}R)$ preserves products by the immersion-limit theorem. Thus both terms of $j_*F\to i_*i^{-1}j_*F$ preserve products, and naturality of the unit identifies the arrow for a product family with the product of its arrows. Uses: def-weil-proetale-inverse-image, lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-limits-sectionwise, lem-weil-pbc-proetale-immersion-limit-preservation.

3. On every test object, the kernel of a product of module maps consists exactly of tuples whose components lie in the respective kernels. Therefore the kernel of Step 1 for $\prod_sF_s$ is $\prod_sj_!F_s$. Its projections are $j_!$ of the original projections, proving the specified canonical comparison and its naturality. For $S=\varnothing$ both kernels are the zero module. Uses: lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-limits-sectionwise.
