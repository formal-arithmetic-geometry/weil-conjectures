## Statement corrected


**STATEMENT-DISPUTED:** originally (a) did not require the triviality locus of the divisorial log structure to be exactly the specified open complement. The original equivalence is false even over a perfect field.

Take any field F, X = Spec F[x,y], B = V(x,y), and U = X minus the origin. The divisorial log structure is the trivial one. Indeed, on a smooth etale neighbourhood all local rings are regular and hence factorial; a nonunit has a codimension-one prime divisor, which cannot be supported on the inverse image of the origin (codimension two). Thus any regular function invertible off that inverse image is already a unit. The trivial log structure is fs, is log smooth over the trivial log field because X/F is smooth, and has characteristic N^0 everywhere. Hence the original (a) holds. Condition (b) cannot hold at the origin: r = 0 has empty boundary, whereas any nonempty boundary pulled back under a smooth map from coordinate hyperplanes has pure codimension one. The etale-local origin still has codimension two.

The correction adds to (a) exactly: “The triviality locus of M is exactly U”. This is the missing pair condition in **ILO VI 1.4, printed p.69**, whose definition explicitly requires the closed subset to be the complement of the log structure's triviality locus. This does not alter the field hypothesis, the smooth comparison, the free characteristic condition, or either conclusion. The parent item `lem-sfb-equivariant-nodal-smooth-pair-resolution` works with actual boundary pairs; this correction makes that interface explicit. No other node was edited. No chapter or unit route was changed.
