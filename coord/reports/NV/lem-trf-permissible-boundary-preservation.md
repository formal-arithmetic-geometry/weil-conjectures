  Thus the center may use parameters absent from the boundary factors, including
  `B = V(x), M = V(x,z)` in affine three-space. This repairs H3/H12 while preserving

  `(M.comap M.blowupMap).radical` is E; `(B.comap M.blowupMap).radical` is B+.
  The ideal `H ⊓ M` defines the union H ∪ M, so
  `((H ⊓ M).comap M.blowupMap).radical` is H+.
- The first three conjuncts give full locally Noetherian regularity of the blowup,
  normal crossings of B+,

- The last two conjuncts are equalities of ideal sheaves defining reduced closed
  subschemes: B+ equals the strict transform of B union E, and H+ equals the strict
  transform of H union E. The meet `⊓` gives the scheme-theoretic union. Strict
  transforms of these reduced subschemes are reduced (they are scheme-theoretic
  closures of reduced opens), so these are the stated reduced identities.
