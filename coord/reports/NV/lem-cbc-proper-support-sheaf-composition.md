The latter is stated for fpqc covers: a general etale cover can be refined over each affine base open by affine etale source opens; their open images cover that affine, so finitely many suffice, giving an fpqc cover there. Properness is Zariski local on the target as part of the properness descent result. This is why the new node makes no global quasi-compactness assumption. Its base-change assertion is about the reduced closed subset, not an assertion that base change of a reduced scheme is reduced.
