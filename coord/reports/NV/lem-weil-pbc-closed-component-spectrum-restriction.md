## Statement dispute repaired

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the delivered `def-weil-pbc-ind-zariski-local-iso` identified “local isomorphism” with “all induced prime localizations are isomorphisms”. These are different predicates. The map Z to Q identifies its only local ring: the prime (0) of Q contracts to (0), and Z_(0)=Q. It is not a local isomorphism: the one-point image {(0)} is not open in Spec Z, since any basic open D(n) containing (0) contains primes not dividing n. Source: Stacks 096E, proetale.tex, definition-local-isomorphism. Stacks 09AP, examples.tex, lemma-not-ind-etale, moreover gives a map identifying local rings that is not even ind-etale, hence not ind-Zariski.
