## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the unconditional phrase “Ext is right exact” in clause 2 lacks the finite-colimit existence assumption required by Stacks tag 0034 (`categories.tex`, label `definition-exact`). Coequalizers alone do not imply that existence. The intended assertion is made explicit by the source's own `lem-sfb-modobj-extension-right-exact`: Ext preserves finite colimits.

For a small counterexample to the existence inference, take the category of nonempty finite sets (or a small skeleton), with cartesian tensor, and take both monoid objects to be the terminal singleton, with the identity morphism. This category is symmetric monoidal, has coequalizers, and tensoring with any object preserves all colimits that exist: for a nonempty finite set A, the exponential B^A remains nonempty and finite, so A × − has the right adjoint (−)^A and preserves every colimit that exists. There is no initial object, since every nonempty finite set has at least two maps to a two-element set. An action of the singleton monoid is unique, so the module category is again the category of nonempty finite sets. Thus the hypotheses of clause 2 hold while its source module category does not have all finite colimits. No claim is made that the identity functor fails to preserve existing colimits.

The corrected sentence asserts preservation of every existing finite colimit and consequently right exactness whenever the source has finite colimits. This changes no construction, drops no preservation conclusion, and imposes no new hypothesis on preservation or on either adjunction. In clause 4 both module categories are abelian, so finite limits and colimits exist and exactness has its usual unconditional meaning. Separately, the dangling bracket reference `[sk-prop-sfb-module-category]` was replaced by `[lem-sfb-modobj-abelian]`; the abelianity assertion itself is unchanged.
