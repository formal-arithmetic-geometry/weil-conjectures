## Source error

In sdga.tex, Tag 0FRY, the paragraph defining shifts on arbitrary homogeneous Hom maps says to keep the same components and claims differential compatibility. With unchanged components the differential on simultaneous k-shifts is (-1)^k times the old Hom differential, so that claim fails for odd k unless the differential vanishes or 2=0. For example take the one-point site with O=A=Z in degree zero, M=Z[0], and N the complex Z in degrees 0 and 1 with differential 1. The degree-zero map u:M→N selecting 1 in N^0 has nonzero Hom differential; after shifting both modules by 1, the same component map has differential -1, rather than +1. Step 2 corrects the dg-functor action to u[k]=(-1)^(k|u|)u. On closed degree-zero chain maps the factor is 1, so the item's stated shift is unchanged. This is the corrected ABC convention already recorded in def-coef-dg-abc-data and in the existing module-cone proof. The target statement is not weakened.
