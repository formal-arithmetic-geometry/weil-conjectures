## Mathematical argument for the new statement-only node

Read Stacks algebra.tex, Tag 04GH (lemma-finite-over-henselian), including its finite-product/local-map assertions, and Tag 00GN (lemma-integral-transitive). Also read the existing statement lem-trf-integral-component-henselian-permanence: it already supplies locality, henselianity, and algebraicity of the residue field for any nonzero integral algebra with no nontrivial idempotent. Apply it to B, a subring of a field. Its hypotheses do not require R to be a domain or its map to K to be injective. This is why the new lemma is stated for a general unital k_0-algebra map rather than only for a DVR.

Every monic polynomial over B has a root in Omega. That root is integral over B and hence over R by 00GN, and consequently lies in B. Lifting coefficients of a monic polynomial over B/n to B and reducing such a root proves that B/n is algebraically closed. Its extension of kappa is algebraic by the integral-local permanence result, so it is an algebraic closure, not an unspecified residue extension. A K-automorphism of Omega preserves integrality over R and the unique maximal ideal of B; it induces the stated quotient automorphism, fixing kappa. If a finite set in the residue closure is lifted to finitely many b_i in B, the subgroup fixing all b_i in Omega is Krull-open and maps into their residue stabilizer. This proves continuity.

Let k' be the elements of Omega algebraic over the finite k_0. It is algebraically closed: coefficients of any polynomial lie in a finite algebraic extension of k_0 and a root in Omega is again algebraic over k_0. Every element of k' is integral over the field k_0 and hence over R, so lies in B. Reduction on this subfield is injective (a unital map from a field); its image is algebraically closed. B/n is algebraic over k_0 because kappa is finite. Thus it is algebraic over this image too, and every one of its elements already lies in the image. This gives the claimed isomorphism t. The equality t(sigma b)=r(sigma)t(b) is the quotient action formula. The comparison and continuity, rather than mere locality or a residue-automorphism definition, are the new content. There are no curve, path or Weil groups in this algebra lemma, so it does not restate a clause of the item.
