## Preserved earlier proof, before the finer split

The following text is retained verbatim from the sandbox item. It is implementation guidance for the new statement-only nodes and evidence that no old argument was lost; it is not proof text inside a new node.

### Earlier Step 1

Work with alternating-face complexes in nonpositive degrees. The natural normalized-to-alternating homotopy equivalence of [lem-cbc-normalized-alternating-homotopy-equivalence] identifies the alternating-face complex with the normalized complex of the standard simplicial differential module. For a map of ring sheaves $\mathcal A\to\mathcal B$ on a space $T$, let $K_{\mathcal B/\mathcal A}$ be the sheaf standard-resolution complex in [lem-trf-affine-cotangent-comparison]. At each stalk its degree $-n$ term is $\Omega_{P_n/\mathcal A_t}\otimes_{P_n}\mathcal B_t$, free on the polynomial differentials by [lem-weil-pbc-polynomial-ring-kahler-basis-any-variables]. Thus every term is flat: by [lem-pc-stalk-tensor-complex], tensoring a short exact sequence with that term has stalks equal to tensoring with these free modules, hence is exact by [lem-sc-abelian-sheaf-exact-iff-stalk-exact], including injectivity and surjectivity by adjoining zero end terms. A free-module tensor is a direct sum of the original sequence. Since $K$ is zero in positive degrees, [lem-pc-bounded-above-flat-kflat-site] makes it K-flat. We can consequently compute all derived scalar extensions below using these models.

Cites: lem-cbc-normalized-alternating-homotopy-equivalence, lem-trf-affine-cotangent-comparison, lem-weil-pbc-polynomial-ring-kahler-basis-any-variables, lem-pc-stalk-tensor-complex, lem-sc-abelian-sheaf-exact-iff-stalk-exact, lem-pc-bounded-above-flat-kflat-site.

### Earlier Step 2

For a commutative square of ring sheaves, apply the standard-resolution maps sectionwise and sheafify. Explicitly $\mathcal A[\mathcal B]\to\mathcal A'[\mathcal B']$ sends coefficients by $\mathcal A\to\mathcal A'$ and $[b]$ to $[b']$; iterate this rule in every degree. The maps commute with evaluation and unit, hence with faces, degeneracies and augmentation, and induce $\mathrm dp\otimes r\mapsto\mathrm d(p')\otimes r'$ on differential complexes. The ring identities in [lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence] and the sectionwise sheaf model in [lem-trf-affine-cotangent-comparison] give identity and composition laws for these sheaf maps. Adjoining tensor coefficients gives $K_{\mathcal B/\mathcal A}\otimes_{\mathcal B}\mathcal C\to K_{\mathcal C/\mathcal A}$. By Step 1 and [lem-pur-derived-change-of-rings], this represents the canonical derived cotangent functoriality arrow.

Cites: lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence, lem-trf-affine-cotangent-comparison, lem-pur-derived-change-of-rings.

### Earlier Step 3

First suppose $\mathcal A\to\mathcal B\to\mathcal C$ are sheaves of rings on $T$ and $\mathcal B\to\mathcal C$ is injective. Form standard resolutions $P=P(\mathcal B/\mathcal A)$, $Q=P(\mathcal C/\mathcal A)$, $S=P(\mathcal B/\mathcal B)$ and $R=P(\mathcal C/\mathcal B)$, and set $\overline Q=Q\otimes_P\mathcal B$, $\overline R=R\otimes_S\mathcal B$. Construct these first as presheaves using the rings of sections and then sheafify. Since an injective sheaf map is injective on every open's sections, [lem-sfb-injective-standard-resolution-quotients] applies on every open, twice (the second time with base ring $\mathcal B(U)$). Thus the presheaf augmentations of $\overline Q$ and $\overline R$ are polynomial resolutions sectionwise. Exact module sheafification [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-site-module-sheafification-exact] carries their augmentation quasi-isomorphisms to quasi-isomorphisms. The sectionwise construction agrees with the sheaf standard resolution by [lem-trf-affine-cotangent-comparison]; the tensor identifications are those of [lem-pc-sites-module-sheafification-tensor].

Cites: lem-sfb-injective-standard-resolution-quotients, lem-weil-pbc-ringed-site-module-sheafification-exact, lem-trf-affine-cotangent-comparison, lem-pc-sites-module-sheafification-tensor.

### Earlier Step 4

There is a short exact sequence of simplicial $\mathcal C$-modules
$$0\to\Omega_{P/\mathcal A}\otimes_P\mathcal C\to\Omega_{Q/\mathcal A}\otimes_Q\mathcal C\to\Omega_{\overline Q/\mathcal B}\otimes_{\overline Q}\mathcal C\to0.$$
Indeed, sectionwise $P_n\to Q_n$ includes polynomial variables into a larger set: in degree zero this is the injection $\mathcal B(U)\hookrightarrow\mathcal C(U)$, and iteration remains injective on polynomial algebras. The basis in [lem-weil-pbc-polynomial-ring-kahler-basis-any-variables] splits the middle differential module into the old-variable and complementary-variable summands after tensoring with $\mathcal C(U)$. The relative differential module on the right is exactly the complementary summand: by the base-change isomorphism [lem-weil-pbc-kahler-differentials-base-change] for $P_n\to Q_n$ along $P_n\to\mathcal B(U)$, $\Omega_{Q_n/P_n}\otimes_{P_n}\mathcal B(U)\cong\Omega_{\overline Q_n/\mathcal B(U)}$, $\mathrm dq\otimes1\mapsto\mathrm d(q\otimes1)$, compatibly with the universal derivations of [def-weil-pbc-kahler-differentials]. These identifications are natural, so the maps commute with every simplicial operator, although the degreewise splittings need not. Sheafification is exact by [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-site-module-sheafification-exact] and commutes with these tensors by [lem-pc-sites-module-sheafification-tensor]. Taking alternating faces therefore gives a degreewise short exact sequence of cochain complexes.

### Earlier Step 5

Identify the quotient complex $T=s(\Omega_{\overline Q/\mathcal B}\otimes_{\overline Q}\mathcal C)$ with $L_{\mathcal C/\mathcal B}$ by the canonical roof
$$K_{\mathcal C/\mathcal B}\longrightarrow s(\Omega_{\overline R/\mathcal B}\otimes_{\overline R}\mathcal C)\longleftarrow T.$$
Both arrows are quasi-isomorphisms: sectionwise $R$, $\overline R$ and $\overline Q$ are polynomial resolutions by Step 3, so the comparisons natural for augmented resolution maps in [lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence] apply; exact sheafification [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-site-module-sheafification-exact] preserves them. The map $\overline Q\to\overline R$ comes from the commutative square $P\to S$, $Q\to R$, after tensoring with $\mathcal B$. Under this roof the quotient arrow from $K_{\mathcal C/\mathcal A}$ is the standard map to $K_{\mathcal C/\mathcal B}$: their composites into the common right-hand resolution are equal because $Q\to\overline Q\to\overline R$ equals $Q\to R\to\overline R$. This proves agreement of arrows, rather than just agreement of objects.

Cites: lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence, lem-weil-pbc-ringed-site-module-sheafification-exact.

### Earlier Step 6

Apply [lem-weil-pbc-complex-ses-triangle] to Step 4. Its first complex is $K_{\mathcal B/\mathcal A}\otimes_{\mathcal B}\mathcal C$, which computes $L_{\mathcal B/\mathcal A}\otimes^{\mathbf L}_{\mathcal B}\mathcal C$ by Step 1 and [lem-pur-derived-change-of-rings]; its middle complex is $K_{\mathcal C/\mathcal A}$; its quotient identifies with $L_{\mathcal C/\mathcal B}$ by Step 5. This gives the required sheaf-ring transitivity triangle for injective $\mathcal B\to\mathcal C$. The first arrow is Step 2's arrow by its polynomial-variable formula, and the second was checked in Step 5. The connecting arrow is the one from this specified short exact sequence and roof. Every restriction map respects the sequence and roof, and the cited short-exact-sequence triangle is natural, so the triangle commutes with open restriction.

### Earlier Step 10

For rings $A\to B$, the standard augmentation gives $K_{B/A}\to\Omega_{B/A}[0]$ by $\mathrm dp\otimes r\mapsto r\,\mathrm d(\epsilon p)$ in degree zero and zero in negative degrees. Its degree-zero cokernel has generators $\delta_b$ for $b\in B$. For $p\in A[B]$, the two faces on the degree-one generator $[p]$ impose
$$\delta_{\epsilon p}=\sum_b\epsilon(\partial p/\partial x_b)\delta_b.$$
By the polynomial differential formula in [lem-weil-pbc-polynomial-ring-kahler-basis-any-variables], the choices $p=x_b+x_{b'}$, $p=x_bx_{b'}$ and $p=a\in A$ give precisely additivity, Leibniz and vanishing on $A$; conversely those derivation rules imply the displayed identity for every polynomial by induction on sums and products. Thus the cokernel is exactly the generators-and-relations module [def-weil-pbc-kahler-differentials], not a larger quotient. This proves $H^0(K_{B/A})=\Omega_{B/A}$ through this augmentation. The formula is natural in ring squares.
