## What needed correction or expansion

The imported node was statement-only, with no proof. Its source names the smooth normal-form theorem, but that theorem first gives affine space of some dimension d; the proof must establish d=1. It must also prove that the valuation centre is closed and that the dominating valuation ring equals the local DVR, rather than assume either assertion. The generic residue-field theorem supplies finite separability of L/K(x), and its closed-point instance supplies the residue extension; these are two distinct applications.

The statement does not assume separatedness of C. Therefore the existing general curve-local-model lemmas that define a curve to be separated cannot be applied directly to C. The proof uses affine charts and prime chains instead. Nor is K assumed perfect: a closed point of a smooth curve need not have separable residue field over K. The final conclusion is the triviality of kappa(c)/kappa(y), not equality of these fields with K.
