The canonical strict-local morphism is formed from the projections to base-changed pointed neighbourhoods. Both sides of the displayed square send a pulled-back section germ to the same germ at the projected generic point. These give the additional naturality and constant-source clauses without a finite-stalk assumption. The statement introduces both schemes, both geometric points and the coefficient ring.
