## Review by Q120

The item agrees with 0ALS read through the cohomology criterion 0AJU(3): the base is Noetherian, the ideal is principal and may have zero divisors, the presentation has finitely many variables including zero, and the two continuous conormal cohomology modules are killed by one power. Its conclusion is a finite-type algebraization as an A-algebra, not a finite algebra and not a model with an unstated etaleness condition. I have preserved the statement verbatim. In particular no G-ring, excellence, regularity, flatness or non-zero-divisor hypothesis has been added.

For the lifting helper I checked 0AK7 hypothesis by hypothesis. Its source is complete and topologically of finite type because it is a complete quotient of a finite completed polynomial ring. The displayed two equations for H say precisely that multiplication by a^h on its completed two-term conormal complex is chain nullhomotopic. Thus it annihilates Ext^1 against every module, which is stronger than the source's assumption. The helper writes the source's target torsion-intersection condition, strict bound n > max(2h,h+t), and congruence n-h explicitly. Its conclusion is only a lifted homomorphism: it does not construct an algebraization. The target is not assumed torsion-free. For the reused transitivity theorem I checked its entire current statement against 0ALM, 0AJR and the presentation-independence assertion 0GAE. It permits the two chosen completed presentations, states the exact sequence, and includes the zero-variable relative presentation. Its hypotheses of completeness and finite-type reduction are verified explicitly in Step 8. The surjection has a zero-variable relative presentation, whose homology is K/K^2 and whose differential target is zero. This gives exactly the fragment used, without a claim that completion of arbitrary algebraic cotangent modules is harmless.

Step 1 uses the finite-type Noetherian permanence, Noetherian completion, and surjective-image Noetherian statements. I checked each statement applies to commutative rings and requires exactly the hypotheses supplied. This makes E Noetherian before its torsion ideal is used. Step 2 is an elementary calculation: choose preimages of a^c times the standard basis, compose with delta, and use the killed kernel to obtain both a^(2c) homotopy identities. The chosen f_i then have derivative matrix a^(2c) times the identity. I checked the exponents d=2c(r+1) and b=a^(2c); their product with Delta=a^(2cr) is a^d. This argument replaces the unneeded new Artin-condition node.

Step 3 uses the definition of Noetherian and the completion-mod-power statement. The torsion subset is an ideal, so a finite generating set has a common annihilating power; if a^t y is torsion, y is torsion, giving the required zero intersection directly. The completion projection supplies actual polynomial approximations. All remaining assertions are explicit polynomial construction and differentiation, not an approximation theorem cited without a node. Step 4 uses completion exactness on the finite modules L_0, Q and C, and the finite-module tensor formula to identify the completed ideal as generated by the same equations. Its completion Noetherianity and completeness are exactly the two cited statements. No arbitrary infinitely generated module is passed through completion.

Step 5 uses the existing adjugate-identity statement. I checked the row/column convention: the top-right column is z times the derivatives of Delta-prime, the bottom-left row is zero, and the bottom-right entry is Delta-prime, giving determinant (Delta-prime)^2. The map q is surjective because the completed ideal is generated by the equations, so checking H_0 delta on the q(e_j) checks it everywhere. Multiplication by z^2 gives a^(2d) on both terms. This bound depends on d, not the approximation precision n, so choosing n first in Step 3 is legitimate.

Step 6 uses completion-mod-power to define the map on the completed polynomial ring and the new principal lifting statement to improve it. I verified every polynomial relation at (x,b) modulo a^n, checked the target torsion bound, and used the strict inequality n > max(2h,h+t). The lifted map therefore agrees modulo a because n-h >= 1. Step 7 uses the same completion projection to establish generation of E/aE by the original x_i, and complete-source surjectivity with source ideal aD. The definition of completion guarantees the target's Hausdorffness. Neither surjectivity of the lift nor a complete-source hypothesis is being assumed implicitly.

Step 8 uses the existing lem-per-completed-cotangent-transitivity statement with D and E, both complete finite-variable quotients, and completion-mod-ideal to identify D/aD with C/aC. The relative zero-variable presentation gives (K/K^2 -> 0). Its first term is precisely the kernel from the item hypothesis; its third term is killed by a^h by the explicit homotopy and the formula on pure tensors. Exactness sends a^h times any kernel-conormal class into the image of the first term, and the additional a^c kills it. This is the written argument for the exponent h+c, not an uncited assertion about tensor preserving kernels. Step 9 uses the full statement of the existing conormal-torsion algebraization lemma, with base A, ideal (a), finite-type algebra C and kernel K. Its hypotheses match Steps 3, 7 and 8 exactly, and its A-algebra conclusion is the requested one. Each of these uses is quoted verbatim in citations.json.
