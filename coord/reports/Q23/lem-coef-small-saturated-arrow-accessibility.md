## Statement disputes found in the sandbox

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the initial common-refinement statement omitted compact values. Counterexample in the ordinary category of sets, viewed as an infinity category: κ=aleph_1, A_0=A_1=omega_1, p_0 the constant set omega_1 and p_1(alpha)=alpha with inclusions. Both total colimits are omega_1. Every nonempty countable filtered restriction of p_0 still has cardinality aleph_1, whereas a countable restriction of p_1 has countable colimit. The repaired node follows the compact-value hypothesis of HTT 5.5.4.13 and retains its actual conclusion.

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the initial diagram-presentation statement asserted the original accessibility rank for every κ-small shape. At κ=aleph_0, take C=sets and the finite simplicial circle K=Delta^1/partial Delta^1. A diagram is a set with an endomap. The endomap n↦n+1 of the natural numbers receives no map from a nonempty finite endomap diagram, since every endomap on a finite nonempty set has a periodic point and successor has none. It cannot be a filtered colimit of diagrams with finite underlying set. HTT 5.4.4.3 chooses a larger rank; the repaired statement writes precisely that rank condition. These are faults in the sandbox support statements, not in HTT and not in the item's unchanged statement.
