## Source error

In the online text of Rezk Lemma 11.11, the displayed unrestricted comparison uses `d^1H(k-1)`, but the proof immediately defines the subobject `d^0H(k-1)` and glues it to the initial edge `01` at vertex `1`. The latter is the subobject that the proof establishes. My steps 4-6 consistently use

\[
A(k)=d^0H(k-1)\cup F(\{0,1\}).
\]

The indexing matters before restricting to equivalences. For k=3 the face complex C(3) is the union of triangles 012 and 013. In the nerve of the fork poset with nonidentity arrows 0 to 1 and 0 to 2, a map from the star of edges 01,02,03 exists by assigning vertices (0,1,2,3) to (0,1,2,2). It cannot extend to C(3), since there is no arrow from object 1 to object 2. That star is `d^1H(2)` with the initial edge adjoined at vertex 0, whereas the proof's `d^0H(2)` uses edges 12 and 13 and the initial edge adjoined at vertex 1. Thus the unrestricted comparison cannot be justified by the printed d^1 formula interpreted as that restriction. The target statement itself has no error and was not changed or weakened.
