Every such preimage contains the nonzero f; prepending the domain prime (0) therefore adds one strict inclusion. Every finite chain then has length at most two since dim B = 3. The localization dimension/chains statement identifies the supremum of exactly these chains with dim (A_e)_p, and the affine-stalk node identifies this with the scheme stalk. This proves the bound for arbitrary primes, including nonmaximal primes; no Jacobson, catenary or closed-point-only argument was used.
