### lem-trf-affine-descent-saturation-quasi-affine-cover: old step 3

For every affine open $C\subset V$, let $r_C:C\times_B A\to V$ be the restriction of $\phi$ followed by the projection $A\times_B V\to V$, and put $W_C=r_C(C\times_B A)$. This is open: $r_C$ is an open immersion followed by the isomorphism $\phi$ and a base change of the flat finite-presentation morphism $p$, which is open by [lem-weil-pbc-flat-lfp-open]. It is quasi-compact: $C\times_B A$ is affine by [lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra], hence quasi-compact by [lem-weil-pbc-affine-scheme-qcqs], and its continuous image is quasi-compact (pull back any open cover and take a finite subcover). The restriction of $\phi$ to the diagonal of $A\times_B A$ is the identity: the cocycle on the triple diagonal says an invertible map equals its square. Therefore $C\subset W_C$. The affine opens cover $V$ by [lem-weil-pbc-affine-opens-basis], so the $W_C$ cover $V$.

### lem-trf-affine-descent-saturation-quasi-affine-cover: old step 5

Each $W_C\to A$ remains separated and locally quasi-finite: the diagonal is the restriction of the closed diagonal of $V\to A$ to $W_C\times_A W_C$, and [lem-weil-pbc-lqf-restriction-to-opens] gives local quasi-finiteness for its source-open restriction. Its source is quasi-compact by step 3 and its target is affine, hence quasi-separated by [lem-weil-pbc-affine-scheme-qcqs]; [lem-weil-pbc-qcqs-morphism-permanence](1) therefore makes $W_C\to A$ quasi-compact. It is locally of finite type by [lem-weil-pbc-lqf-locally-finite-type]. Consequently [lemma-nagata-quasi-affine-morphism-of-quasi-finite] applies with all four hypotheses and makes $W_C\to A$ quasi-affine. This uses no finite-presentation assertion for $V\to A$.

### lem-trf-descent-saturation-invariant-open: old step 4

The cocycle makes $W_C$ invariant, as follows. Regard $\phi$ as transport from one $A$-coordinate to another. For a test scheme $Z$, a map $v:Z\to W_C$, and a second map $b:Z\to A$ over $B$, pull back the surjection $r_C:C\times_B A\to W_C$ along $v$. Its projection $Z'\to Z$ is surjective by [lem-weil-pbc-surjective-base-change]. On $Z'$ the map $v$ is the transport of a map $c:Z'\to C$ from its $A$-coordinate to the current coordinate. Transporting once more to $b$ gives, by the cocycle, exactly the transport of $c$ directly to $b$, which lies in $W_C$ by its definition as the image of $r_C$. Since $Z'\to Z$ is surjective, the transported map on $Z$ also has image in $W_C$ and factors through this open. Interchanging the two coordinates gives the reverse inclusion; the cocycle with a repeated coordinate makes these transports inverse. Thus $\phi$ restricts to an isomorphism $W_C\times_B A\simeq A\times_B W_C$, satisfies the same cocycle, and the inclusion $W_C\to V$ intertwines every transport.
