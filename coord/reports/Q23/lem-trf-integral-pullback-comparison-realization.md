### Old step 2

The strict-tower homotopy-limit theorem [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-strict-tower-homotopy-limit] says that this construction is the total derived inverse limit, is functorial on tower maps, and has the Roos triangle. ### Old step 3

Towers of modules over the completed coefficient sheaf (resp. over the quotient rings A_{F,•}) are the modules over the corresponding sheaf of rings on the product of the site with the poset N^op, so [lem-pur-derived-change-of-rings] applies to the levelwise change of rings R→A_{F,•} on tower derived categories; a constant tower of a K-flat complex is K-flat there, since tensor products and acyclicity are levelwise. Apply ε to the reduction tower of F_{k,E}K and then apply h^*, with the levelwise change-of-rings counits and tensor associators; this gives η_{h,k,K}. Apply the same construction to the L-coefficient tower of F_{f,E}K and to the tower of T_VK after f^*; these give λ_{f,K} and μ_{f,K}. The finite pullback comparison is the canonical termwise map on K-flat reductions by [lem-weil-pbc-proetale-derived-pullback-composition]. Its levelwise reduction maps are the canonical isomorphisms supplied by [lem-pur-integral-operations-preserve-constructible] (1), while [lem-pur-coefficient-extension-reduction-model] identifies the scalarized reductions with the L-coefficient quotient tower and supplies their coherent counit. Hence η, λ and μ are maps of the whole strict towers with levelwise isomorphisms; functoriality and exactness of Rlim from [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-strict-tower-homotopy-limit] make them isomorphisms after realization.
