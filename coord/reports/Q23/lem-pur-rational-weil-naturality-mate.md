## New comparison lemma: mathematical argument and edge cases

This is an unsplit supporting statement, not an assertion that a Lean proof exists. Its full natural-language proof for the next worker is as follows.

First compare only the unit orientations at the base coefficient field Q_l. Let a=a^T_0 be the realized tower orientation, and let g=g_0 be the reconstructed unit map used by the trace construction. Both are isomorphisms from T_0=R_X(d)[2d] to G_0(R_Y), with exactly the finite diagonal purity reductions and the same exceptional-adjoint counit normalization. The automorphism h=a^{-1}g of T_0 becomes an ordinary sheaf endomorphism of the locally free rank-one sheaf R_X(d) after removing the common shift. The embedding of sheaves into their derived category is fully faithful, so there are no higher-Ext or tower-evaluation ambiguities for this endomorphism. Locally in a frame h is multiplication by a section of R_X; under a change of frame these multipliers agree since the ring is commutative. They glue to one global section s of R_X. The reduction of h is the identity for every n, so pi_n(s)=1 for every n. The projections of R_X=lim_n (Z_l/l^n)_X are jointly injective on sections, by the defining sheaf limit. Hence s=1, h=1 and g=a. This is a special calculation of the endomorphisms of a shifted line. It does not assert that separate finite evaluations detect all derived tower morphisms, or that arbitrary coherent lifts of a general finite family are unique.

Next extend coefficients. The canonical coefficient comparisons in field-extension-towers carry a^T_0 to a^T_E and preserve the counits. More explicitly let b:C_XG_0 -> G_EC_Y be the right comparison, c:C_YP_0 -> P_EC_X the compact-support comparison, and theta:C_XT_0 -> T_E the twist comparison. The orientation comparison is a^T_E theta=b_1 C_X(a^T_0). The counit comparison is epsilon_E P_E(b_1)=C_Y(epsilon_0)c_{G_0(1)}^{-1}. Substitution and naturality of c give

    epsilon_E P_E(a^T_E)
      = C_Y(epsilon_0 P_0(a^T_0)) c_{T_0}^{-1} P_E(theta^{-1})
      = C_Y(t_0) c_{T_0}^{-1} P_E(theta^{-1}) = t_E.

These are identities of actual integral maps with the chosen comparisons. Finite freeness, also for ramified E/Q_l, gives the ordinary-tensor coefficient comparisons on completed sheaves. The l-power and uniformizer-power systems are compared by their canonical cofinal limit comparisons, since (l^n)=(varpi_E^{en}); no flatness of quotient reduction is being asserted.

For a general K use tower-gamma's specified projection/counit definition of its tensor map. The naturality of the projection isomorphism in its oriented factor shows that the adjoint transpose of gamma^T_{E,K} is inverse projection followed by 1_K tensor (epsilon_E P_E(a^T_E)). The integral realization preserves the tensor, projection, compactification maps and units/counits before evaluation; use `lem-sf-integral-compactification-system-realization` and the actual Hom bijections of `det-sp-integral-adjunction-bijection`. Thus this transpose identity holds in the integral category, not merely at each quotient. By the trace equality just proved, the same transpose is exactly (1_K tensor t_E) Pi_{E,K}, the definition of gamma^tr_{E,K}. Injectivity of the specified adjunction Hom bijection gives gamma^T_{E,K}=gamma^tr_{E,K}. This is a comparison of actual constructions; uniqueness of a right adjoint alone would not identify these maps.

Finally rationalize this equality. The previously specified family in the rational-purity theorem uses these reconstructed-unit trace-transpose representatives. Applying Q_E gives precisely gamma^rat_{f,Q_E K}=Q_E(gamma^T_{E,K}); functoriality preserves the equality and the counit-preserving endpoint comparisons. The coefficient comparisons are the already specified ones in field-extension-towers, so applying common-stage descent gives equality of their common-stage images. No Frobenius, Weil structure or arbitrary-arrow naturality is part of this supporting conclusion; the original item still proves those clauses by its tower base-change and localization argument.

Hypotheses checked: the smooth separated pure-dimensional f has a compactification; the schemes are separated finite type over k, hence Noetherian and finite-dimensional; l is invertible. Constant completed coefficients and twists are in the integral constructible finite-Tor domain, and the twists are locally free rank one. Removing the same shift in both endomorphism endpoints is legitimate for every d, including d=0. The statement introduces the completed coefficient rings, all adjunctions and both families, and quantifies over every reconstructed unit choice. Its uniqueness assertion is deliberately restricted to the unit orientation. On the empty scheme the ring sheaf and the unique endomorphism have the same argument; no nonempty-frame assumption is used globally. On disconnected schemes multipliers are sections, not an assumed single constant scalar. Ramified finite extensions use their cofinal en levels. On a point an automorphism 1+l^n can agree at one quotient but not all of them, confirming the need for every n. For arbitrary K the argument compares transposes rather than detecting equality from reductions.
