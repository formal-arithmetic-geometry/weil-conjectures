### lem-sfb-finite-discrete-constructible-model

Statement source: literature/bhatt-scholze-pro-etale-topology_arXiv-1309.1198.pdf, §5.1 and §5.4, with explicit finite-discrete encoding; no direct-image finiteness is asserted.

1. The finite-discrete constant-ring theorem supplies the point identification needed by $E_Z$. The equivalence is exact since its underlying abelian sheaf is unchanged; its inverse is exact as well. Exact-functor cohomology therefore commutes with encoding and decoding. If (i) holds, decode $K$ termwise. On each cohomological stratum the finite-discrete local-piece theorem says its decoded cohomology is finite locally constant. Hence the decoded complex is bounded classically constructible. The scheme is qcqs: its Noetherian space makes every affine intersection quasi-compact. A finite ring is Noetherian because every ideal is a finite set and hence finitely generated. The constructible pro-etale/etale equivalence now supplies $L$ and $E_Z^{-1}K\cong\nu_Z^*L$, giving (ii).

   Uses: [lem-weil-pbc-finite-discrete-condensed-ring-constant], [def-weil-pbc-common-finite-discrete-encoding], [lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-complex-cohomology], [lem-weil-pbc-finite-discrete-constructible-local-piece], [sk-thm-pe-constructible-comparison]. Source: literature/bhatt-scholze-pro-etale-topology_arXiv-1309.1198.pdf, §5.1 and §5.4; ordinary constructible comparison followed by finite-discrete encoding.

2. Conversely take $L$ in (ii). Each of its finitely many nonzero cohomology sheaves has finite local partitions on a finite affine cover of $Z$. The stratification-gluing lemma gives a global finite stratification for each sheaf; the bounded-constructible definition permits different stratifications in different degrees. The finite-discrete derived-coefficient comparison commutes $E\nu^*$ with restrictions to strata and their covers. Thus on a cover of each stratum its cohomology is the encoding of a constant finite $T$-module $P$. Since $T$ is Noetherian, $P$ is finitely presented; choose $T^m\to T^n\to P\to0$. Encoding is exact and carries the regular $T$-module to $T_V^c$, so the image is the cokernel of $(T_V^c)^m\to(T_V^c)^n$. These are exactly the required local finite presentations. Encoding and $\nu^*$ are exact, so boundedness and this cohomology calculation prove (i), including $T=0$.

   Uses: [lem-weil-pbc-etale-stratification-local-gluing-core], [lem-weil-pbc-finite-discrete-derived-coefficient-data], [def-weil-pbc-common-finite-discrete-encoding], [lem-sc-noetherian-finite-module-finite-presentation], [lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-complex-cohomology], [sk-def-sfb-bounded-constructible], [def-sfb-finitely-presented-module], [def-weil-pbc-site-comparison-morphism]. Source: literature/bhatt-scholze-pro-etale-topology_arXiv-1309.1198.pdf, §5.1, §5.4; finite-presentation calculation for constant finite modules.

### lem-sfb-integral-ambient-tower-comparison

Statement source: literature/bhatt-scholze-pro-etale-topology_arXiv-1309.1198.pdf, Lemma 6.5.9(2),(3) and Lemma 5.4.3; agent specialization retaining the ordinary ambient coefficient action.

1. The valuation-ring theorem makes $A$ a principal ideal domain, hence Noetherian, with finite residue field and a nonzero uniformizer. Since $|\ell|=\ell^{-1}<1$, the valuation decomposition writes $\ell=u\varpi^e$ with $e>0$. A power of $\ell$ therefore kills $P_n$, and the finite-quotient theorem makes it finite. Multiplication by $\varpi^n$ is injective because $A$ is a domain, so $[A\xrightarrow{\varpi^n}A]$ resolves $P_n$ and proves perfectness. The coefficient-limit formula identifies $p_Z^*\underline A$ with the completed quotient sheaf, compatibly with all quotient maps and inverse images. The Cartesian coefficient theorem identifies $f^{-1}A_Y$ with $A_X$. Thus the adic datum is verified for the actual pulled-back coefficient ring.

   Uses: [lem-weil-l-adic-extension-integers-dvr], [lem-weil-valued-dvr-finite-quotients], [sk-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-x], [sk-prop-sfb-cartesian-coefficients], [def-weil-pbc-adic-coefficient-datum]. Source: literature/bhatt-scholze-pro-etale-topology_arXiv-1309.1198.pdf, §6.5 opening; Lemma 6.5.9.

2. Set $M=Rf_*K$ in the ordinary, uncompleted $A_Y$-module derived category. By [prop-weil-pbc-direct-image-limits], $M$ is derived complete and the limit comparison identifies it with $R\varprojlim_n\operatorname{Res}_n Rf_{*,n}K_n$. The finite-level comparison is also explicit: [lemma-weil-pbc-perfect-projection-formula], applied to the finite free resolution of $P_n$ from step 1, gives $M\otimes^L_{A_Y}(P_n)_Y\simeq Rf_{*,n}K_n$, naturally in $n$. Restriction of scalars along $A_T\to(P_n)_T$ in this formula is compatible with derived direct image by [lem-weil-pbc-ring-sheaf-change-adjunction], whose $d$ identifies the two composites of restriction and direct image. Thus the right side really is the direct image in the finite coefficient category, not merely an equality after forgetting the module action.

   Uses: [prop-weil-pbc-direct-image-limits], [lemma-weil-pbc-perfect-projection-formula], [lem-weil-pbc-ring-sheaf-change-adjunction]. Source: literature/bhatt-scholze-pro-etale-topology_arXiv-1309.1198.pdf, Lemma 6.5.9(2),(3); also Stacks Tag 099N (lemma-pushforward-Noetherian-case), Tag 0944 (lemma-projection-formula).

3. If $K_n\simeq\nu_X^*L_n$ is specified with $L_n$ bounded below, qcqs of $f$ is exactly the hypothesis of the étale/pro-étale direct-image comparison. Apply it to obtain $Rf_{*,P_n}K_n\simeq\nu_Y^*Rf_{\mathrm{ét},*,P_n}L_n$. The finite-discrete coefficients here are the prescribed $P_n$, so this is in their coefficient category. Neither finiteness of the cohomology nor properness is needed for this comparison. The inverse-limit and finite-level maps of the preceding step are the specified strict-tower maps, not an arbitrary collection of isomorphisms.

   Uses: [prop-weil-pbc-comparison-direct-image]. Source: literature/bhatt-scholze-pro-etale-topology_arXiv-1309.1198.pdf, Lemma 5.4.3; Lemma 6.5.9.

### lem-sfb-localized-direct-image-comparison

Statement source: literature/hemo-richarz-scholbach-constructible-sheaves_arXiv-2305.18131.pdf, Lemma 5.3(1), Lemma 5.6; Bhatt–Scholze Remark 6.8.15.

1. Choose a good truncation representing $C$ by a complex zero below some $a$. The coefficient localization formula identifies $E_Z$ with $A_Z[1/\ell]$ and makes it flat. Consequently, after restriction to $A_X$, $E_X\otimes^LC$ is represented by the termwise filtered colimit of $C\xrightarrow{\ell}C\xrightarrow{\ell}\cdots$. All terms are zero below $a$. Filtered colimits are exact for abelian sheaves, and the module forgetful functor creates these colimits and is exact; therefore their cohomology is the colimit of cohomology. The same assertions apply on $Y$.

   Uses: [det-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-x-invert-ell], [lem-weil-pbc-site-abelian-sheaves-filtered-colimits-exact], [lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-colimits-forgetful], [lem-weil-pbc-condensed-qcqs-pushforward-continuity]. ### lem-sfb-normed-coefficient-realization

Statement source: literature/bhatt-scholze-pro-etale-topology_arXiv-1309.1198.pdf, §6.8 coefficient conventions and Remark 6.8.15; agent expansion of returned proof step 1 using algebraic embedding and absolute-value uniqueness.

1. A finite extension is algebraic. Extend the fixed inclusion of $\mathbf Q_\ell$ into its algebraic closure to an embedding $\sigma:E\to\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, and let $E\prime$ be its finite image. The norm on $E$ and $x\mapsto|\sigma(x)|$ are absolute values extending the normalized norm of $\mathbf Q_\ell$. Uniqueness of the extended absolute value makes them equal.
