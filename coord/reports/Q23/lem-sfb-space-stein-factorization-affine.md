For C3/C4 I opened every cited node's statement. citations.json quotes the statements themselves, including all hypotheses, rather than phrases copied from proofs. Here is what each step actually takes from them and how I checked it.

1. lem-sfb-space-affine-target-global-functions is a bijection for arbitrary algebraic spaces and affine scheme targets, not merely full faithfulness on affine sources. Together with def-weil-affine-spectrum it constructs q from id_B and proves the composite equality from equality of ring maps. Keeping q-sharp literally the identity is essential to step 7.

2. lem-weil-pbc-affine-source-morphism-separated applies to the scheme map pi, whose source really is Spec B; it is not applied to X. lem-sfb-space-proper-cancellation allows a nonrepresentable algebraic-space u and needs only properness of the composite and separatedness of v. I added lem-sfb-space-closed-subspace-proper here so that the quasi-compactness of q's closed diagonal, hence the quasi-separatedness needed later, is supplied explicitly.

3. lem-weil-pbc-affine-scheme-qcqs supplies the base hypothesis for lem-sfb-space-proper-noetherian-approximation. Its statement supplies a small directed index, proper finitely presented stages, affine transitions on both systems and qcqs stages. lem-sfb-space-affine-limits-schematic-stages expressly includes eventual affineness when the limit is affine, so I do not assume the initial T_i are schemes. After taking a cofinal tail, lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian gives Noetherian schemes T_i, since finite presentation implies finite type and Spec Z is Noetherian. pqf-lem-affine-open-locnoeth then gives Noetherian rings A_i. This last citation avoids assuming that one affine Noetherian chart automatically makes the entire coordinate ring Noetherian by definition.

4. lem-sfb-space-noetherian-coherent-acc gives both Noetherianity of X_i (proper implies quasi-separated and finite type) and coherence of its finite free structure module. lem-sfb-proper-space-coherent-ext-finite applies to the actual Noetherian ring A_i and the proper space X_i, with two degree-zero coherent complexes. Both bounds in its statement are verified. lem-weil-pbc-derived-single-fully-faithful identifies their derived Hom with ordinary module-sheaf Hom. The further identification End(O)=Gamma(O) is written out by evaluation at 1 and its inverse multiplication, including A_i-linearity; no etale-to-Zariski coherence comparison is being assumed.

5. lem-sfb-space-limit-sections gives actual compatible ring-colimit identifications for qcqs spaces with affine transitions, not just eventual descent of points. lem-weil-pbc-finite-algebra-is-integral applies to the finite A_i-module B_i from step 4. The displayed monic equation is pulled back coefficient by coefficient along A_i -> A -> B. This neither assumes a finite module over the original possibly non-Noetherian A nor uses a Noetherian Stein theorem as a new name for a clause of this item.

6. def-weil-pbc-localization-at-prime gives the local ring and residue field at z. lem-weil-pbc-separable-closure-exists-galois gives the required separable closure, rather than silently assuming one. lem-weil-pbc-strict-henselization-triples specifies a local henselian H with residue field k^s and the correct preimage of the maximal ideal. lem-sc-strict-henselization-faithfully-flat supplies the flat local map, while lem-weil-pbc-localization-flat and lem-weil-pbc-flat-ring-map-comp give flatness over B. I added lem-weil-pbc-flat-ring-map-iff-flat-affine to justify conversion to flatness of the spectrum map. lem-sfb-space-proper-base-change works for arbitrary algebraic-space base changes without finite-presentation or Noetherian hypotheses. It applies both to Y and its closed fibre.

7. lem-sfb-space-flat-higher-image-base-change has an explicit affine cohomology clause. I use that clause at degree zero with a quasi-coherent structure module, a qcqs q from step 2, and a flat affine base change from step 6. I do not infer q_*O=O solely from equality of global sections. The canonical tensor map's formula, the identity q-sharp, and multiplication give the asserted ring isomorphism H -> Gamma(Y,O).

8. lem-sfb-space-global-idempotent-clopen applies without Noetherianity and includes all clopen subsets and the empty space. lem-weil-pbc-local-ring-idempotent-zero-one applies to the nonzero local ring H. Nonemptiness of Y is proved separately from the nonzero global-section ring; connectedness then follows from the correspondence, with U_0 empty and U_1 the whole space. I do not conflate absence of a partition with nonemptiness.

9. lem-sfb-space-proper-base-change gives the proper map used to take a closed image. The specialization of every prime of a local ring to its maximal ideal is the elementary Zariski closure calculation. lem-a1-space-point-open-calculus states surjectivity from fibre-product points to pairs of compatible points, so it supplies a point of the actual closed fibre. An arbitrary nonempty family over a local base would not suffice.

10. lem-sfb-space-global-idempotent-clopen realizes a hypothesized topological clopen as an open subspace. I wrote out the closed-immersion test for its inclusion using the zero/unit ideal sheaf on the two opens; this is an instance of def-weil-closed-immersion, not an extra unproved theorem about algebraic spaces. lem-sfb-space-closed-subspace-proper and lem-sfb-space-proper-composition give properness of that clopen over k^s. def-weil-pbc-henselian-terminology turns the henselian local H into the pair (H,m_H), exactly the ideal used for Y_0. lem-sfb-space-henselian-proper-clopen-lift requires separated finite type, which the proper Y has, and properness of the clopen, which was just checked. Its conclusion is equality after base change as subspaces, so W empty/all genuinely forces U empty/all. I am not using a mere set-theoretic lifting assertion.

11. lem-sfb-space-sepclosed-components-base-change explicitly includes arbitrary field extensions and nonempty connectedness; it is applied over the separably closed k^s. Factorization of B -> L through kappa(z) follows from localization and quotient, as explained. lem-trf-family-common-extension-field applies to the two extensions k^s and L with no algebraicity assumption on L. lem-pc-field-extension-surjective and lem-a1-space-point-open-calculus give a continuous surjection from the fibre over E to the fibre over L. The partition-pullback argument then proves nonempty connectedness over L. Thus the statement is checked for all extensions L/K, not only geometric points with algebraically closed field.
