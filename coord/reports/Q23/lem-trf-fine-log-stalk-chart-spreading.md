The finite-presentation child is an agent-formulated algebra consequence of the polynomial Noetherianity proved in Stacks Tag 00FN (algebra-lemma-Noetherian-permanence), not a claim that Stacks states the monoid theorem verbatim. Its future proof can proceed as follows. Fix the finite generating tuple and map the polynomial ring Z[x_1,...,x_n] onto the monoid algebra Z[R]. The kernel is generated by all binomials x^a-x^b with equal monoid images: group each polynomial's coefficients according to their images in R and use the free abelian basis indexed by R. Noetherianity supplies a finite subset of these binomials generating the kernel (choose finite polynomial generators, each a finite linear combination of binomials). Let C be the monoid congruence generated by the corresponding pairs. The kernel of Z[N^n] to Z[N^n/C] is precisely the ideal generated by these binomials, since adding a relation translates it by monomials and equivalence closure corresponds to adding and subtracting such differences. The two quotient rings therefore coincide compatibly with the monomials. Distinct C-classes are distinct basis elements of Z[N^n/C], so two monomials with equal image in R have the same C-class. This proves that the finite relations generate the full congruence. The universal property in the child is exactly the quotient-congruence property. This is a substantial algebra lemma, not a few-line step, and it does not assert chart spreading.

For the associated-log child, the given presheaf pushout is the specified construction, not data chosen from an unproved existence theorem. Sheafify its monoid operations. The pushout maps to O by [r,u] -> beta(r)u. In a local ring beta(r)u is a unit exactly when beta(r) is a unit; the pushout then identifies this element with that unit, which proves the log axiom on stalks and hence locally. Any beta-compatible R -> K extends uniquely by [r,u] -> v(r)u because K identifies the inverse image of units with O^times. These maps glue across sheafification. Taking a geometric stalk commutes with sheafification and the pushout. The unit stalk is A^times: lift an inverse and impose its product equality with 1 on a common neighbourhood. The same finite-equality argument identifies the inverse-image submonoid with E. This yields R plus_E A^times and the claimed restriction compatibility. No fineness, saturation, or finite generation is required for this foundational construction. Its proof is left for the next stage-1 item; no sketch is hidden in the child's statement.
