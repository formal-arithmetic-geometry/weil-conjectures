## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the import conflates two genuinely different categories. `def-weil-weil-sheaf-complex` defines fixed pairs over the geometric derived category. Its statement explicitly excludes calling this a derived category of Weil sheaves. In contrast, `WeilSheaf/Category.lean:145-182` defines native Weil complexes as the bounded constructible full subcategory of the derived category of the abelian category of pro-etale module sheaves with Frobenius structure. These categories cannot be identified just by notation. Over a geometric point, Hom from the coefficient unit to its shift is zero in the geometric derived category, hence in its fixed-pair category. In the native derived category the extension with Frobenius matrix [[1,1],[0,1]] is nonsplit, and gives a nonzero degree-one self-extension of the trivial Weil sheaf. A putative equivariant splitting would require a Frobenius-fixed lift of the quotient basis vector, which this matrix does not have.

The corrected item explicitly defines the native category, its coefficient ring sheaf, its lisse objects and its two Hom constructions. A route for its eventual proof is as follows (this is a report explanation, not a proof in the unsplit node). The forgetful functor U from Frobenius-equivariant R-modules to R-modules has an exact induction left adjoint, the direct sum over all integral Frobenius translates. The adjunction on Hom complexes shows that U sends K-injective complexes to K-injective complexes, since induction sends acyclic complexes to acyclic ones. The underlying precomposition map is consequently a quasi-isomorphism by Stacks 0A95 / `lem-pur-rhom-well-defined`; exact conservative U detects that it is a quasi-isomorphism of Weil complexes. For a degree-zero source, the Hom complex has just H(A,I^n) in degree n. Ordinary K-injective computation of a right derived functor then supplies the other model; the representative comparisons give the stated naturality. The induction/K-injective comparison is the nontrivial additional input missing from the existing nonequivariant ringed-site statement, and is kept for the new node's own future split.
