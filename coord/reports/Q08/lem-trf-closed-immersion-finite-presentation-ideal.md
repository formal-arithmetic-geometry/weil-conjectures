Edge cases checked before drafting:

- Identity immersion: the ideal is zero, with the empty generating family; all three
  morphism properties hold, over an arbitrary base.
- Empty closed subscheme: its ideal is the unit ideal, locally generated by 1, and
  Spec(R/R) → Spec R has a finite presentation. This works over a non-quasi-compact
  scheme as well; the morphism, rather than the target, is quasi-compact.
- Empty X (and hence empty Z): both sides are vacuous local conditions; no nonempty
  binder excludes this case.
- Affine quotient by an infinitely generated ideal, for example the ideal of all
  variables in a polynomial ring over a field with infinitely many variables:
  the ideal is not a finite module and the quotient is not finitely presented as an
  algebra. Neither finite type of the immersion nor finite presentation of I as a
  module was substituted for the node's conditions.
- The ring may be zero, have arbitrary characteristic, or be non-Noetherian. The
  universes and binders impose no restriction on these cases.
