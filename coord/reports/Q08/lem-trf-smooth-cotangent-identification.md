### Returned Step 1 -> lem-trf-constant-polynomial-resolution-retraction

Use the standard-resolution convention of [lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence]. Let $Q_\bullet$ be the constant simplicial $A$-algebra on $P=A[x_e\mid e\in E]$, augmented to $P$ by the identity. Each term is a polynomial $A$-algebra and its augmentation is surjective. All faces are identities, so on the alternating-face complex the differential from degree $-n$ to degree $-n+1$ is $\sum_{i=0}^n(-1)^i\mathrm{id}$: it is $0$ for odd $n$ and the identity for positive even $n$. Consequently $s(Q_\bullet)\to P[0]$ has identity on $H^0$ and zero negative cohomology (each negative degree belongs to a two-term identity pair). This verifies, directly, every condition for a polynomial resolution in the cited node, without assuming any finiteness of $E$.

### Returned Step 2 -> lem-trf-constant-polynomial-resolution-retraction

Apply the comparison for polynomial resolutions in [lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence] to $Q_\bullet\to P$. Put $M=\Omega^1_{P/A}$. The resulting differential complex is the alternating-face complex of the constant simplicial $P$-module $M$, since $\Omega^1_{Q_n/A}\otimes_{Q_n}P=M$ and all structure maps are identities. Its augmentation $a:s(M)\to M[0]$ is the identity in degree zero. More explicitly, let $j:M[0]\to s(M)$ be the identity in degree zero and let $h^{-n}:s(M)^{-n}\to s(M)^{-n-1}$ be the identity for odd $n\geq1$ and zero for even $n\geq0$. The differential formula in Step 1 gives $dh+hd=1-ja$, and $aj=1$. Thus $a$ is a homotopy equivalence. The degreewise evaluation maps from the standard resolution to the constant resolution send $\mathrm dq\otimes p$ to $p\,\mathrm d(\epsilon(q))$; composed with $a$ they are exactly the augmentation to universal differentials. The natural comparison for this morphism of polynomial resolutions therefore identifies the canonical augmentation $L_{P/A}\to M[0]$ with this isomorphism. This proves the first clause with its specified map.
