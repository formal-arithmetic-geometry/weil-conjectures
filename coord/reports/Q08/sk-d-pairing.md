## Source error — STATEMENT-DISPUTED

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the current sketch writes `Q=K boxtimes D_Y L` **on** `Z=Y times X`, with `K` on `X` and `D_Y L` on `Y`. The definition of an external product in `sk-d-six` puts its first argument on the first factor. With distinct schemes, for example `Y=Spec k` and `X=A^1_k`, the pullback `p_1^*K` is not typed: `p_1` has codomain `Y`, not `X`. The correct external product on the displayed `Z` is `D_Y L boxtimes K`. Equivalently, the reversed-product Künneth–Verdier target on `Z` is `p_2^*K tensor p_1^*D_Y L`, followed by the signed braiding. This repair is already corroborated by the detailed blueprint definition `def-trf-supported-pairing` and its reviewed DAG counterpart. I kept this sketch as the unit's key node and recorded that existing definition in `blueprint.same_as`, rather than creating a duplicate.

The shorthand also leaves the ring and operation domains implicit. I made the already inherited standing conventions of `sk-d-setup`, via `sk-d-six`, explicit. This is the finite ring `O_E/varpi^n` on the ordinary pro-étale derived categories, not an assertion for all coefficient categories or a completed unbounded category. The supports are in the geometric domain on which the given package acts, the displayed exceptional functors have compactifiable maps and supplied constructible adjunctions, and those adjunctions agree with the coefficient maps. This spells out where the recipe is defined; it asserts no existence of new unbounded adjoints. No target statement has been edited.
