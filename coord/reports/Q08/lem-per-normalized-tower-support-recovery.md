## Review by Q08

I read ILO VIII 2.1.12--2.1.20 and 2.2.1--2.2.12, including the proof of 2.1.18, and the proof of 3.4.15 on printed p.114, directly from the archived PDF text. The former identifies the center closure with the original center when all associated points are seen. The latter really uses restriction to X_Q, not a claim that X_Q is open, and then compares completed strict local schemes with synchronization retained. The new node is the generic geometric local-structure input stated in 2.1.12 and used again in 2.2.12. I checked the auxiliary ring epimorphism result at algebra.tex lemma-faithfully-flat-epimorphism, Tag 04VU. For its Noetherian consequence one may cover the source by affine opens over an affine Noetherian base: these affine maps are flat monomorphisms, and every ideal upstairs is the extension of its contraction (equivalently extend closed subschemes as in VIII 2.1.14). Hence each ascending chain stabilizes and each affine source ring is Noetherian. Quasi-compactness then gives a finite affine cover. The empty and unit-ideal cases satisfy this argument. This argument is explanatory material here, not a hidden proof inside the new statement.

Step 1 uses the statements of flat-morphism base change and quasi-compact-morphism base change to verify exactly those two hypotheses on j_i. The diagonal description makes preservation of monomorphisms an immediate fibre-product identity. The new local-structure statement supplies reducedness, Noetherianity and regularity; none of these is being inferred from an open immersion. I read the second paragraph of lem-per-normalized-tower-regular-pullback: it explicitly allows a regular morphism between arbitrary reduced Noetherian schemes, with Noetherian original stages, even though its first paragraph concerns quasi-excellent schemes and nowhere dense centers. Only the second paragraph is used here, and no quasi-excellence or nonempty-center assumption is imported. The regular pullback statement is earlier on the same independence route and does not depend on this item.

Step 2 uses lem-sfb-affine-associated-points-primes only for its forward implication, applying it to the actual quasi-coherent quotient O/J_i on a Noetherian stage. It really states this for arbitrary quasi-coherent modules, not merely for the structure sheaf; I read Tag 02OK, divisors.tex lemma-associated-affine-open. The hypothesis on Ass(B) is used at the image of every such associated point. The fibre and local-ring clauses of the new node give its lift and the precise quotient stalk identification. The final injectivity is exactly lem-weil-pbc-module-injects-localizations-associated-primes, whose statement allows M=A/I and whose source Tag 0311, algebra.tex lemma-zero-at-ass-zero, was read. Vanishing in every localization is the hypothesis it needs. The reverse kernel inclusion is the definition of the restricted center, not another lemma. Thus the proof recovers ideals with their nilpotents, not only closed subsets. The proof does not use the invalid claim that an associated point of the stage is automatically an associated point of the center.

Step 3 is a finite induction, beginning with X itself and using the recovered ideal to form the next normalized blowup. I read def-trf-normalized-blowup; it constructs precisely the normalization of the blowup of that ideal, and has no extra regularity hypothesis. Equality here means canonical equality of centered towers, as in ILO VIII 2.2.3--2.2.5, not an unrelated isomorphism of endpoints. Length equality comes from the uncontracted restricted datum, before induction. This step is elementary assembly from the previous argument and the definition; it creates no node for an item clause.

Step 4 reads the rational base change on an affine chart as localization by the image of all nonzero integers, allowing 0 in that image. The existing localization-flat statement applies also then (the source is empty on a positive-characteristic component). I read algebra.tex lemma-flat-localization. No finite presentation or openness is assumed.

Step 5 uses flat local maps, rather than asserting that an arbitrary affine open of an fpqc source still surjects. For every point downstairs one first chooses a point upstairs using surjectivity; the resulting stalk map is flat and local. The first sentence of lem-pc-local-flat-faithful-flat-reflects-surjective supplies faithful flatness (Tag 00HR, algebra.tex lemma-local-flat-ff, read). The ideal-contraction statement gives each ideal back from its extension (Tag 05CK, algebra.tex lemma-faithfully-flat-universally-injective, read); hence equality of extensions forces equality of the two original ideals. For each stage point z over x, choose a point v of the cover over x. The local-flat lemma makes O_X,x -> O_Y,v faithfully flat; faithful-flat base change and surjectivity on spectra applied to kappa(z) then give a point of the stage cover. I corrected the earlier phrase about applying a surjective-spectrum theorem on affine charts, since an individual affine chart of a surjective source need not surject onto the target chart.
