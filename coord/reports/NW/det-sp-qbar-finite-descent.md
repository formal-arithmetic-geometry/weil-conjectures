## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the latest imported wording “Let T be a scheme ... By [sk-thm-sfb-qlbar-two-colimit] ...” is not valid without its standing hypotheses. That theorem explicitly says “Let X be a standing scheme”; its scheme setting fixes characteristic p different from ell, separated finite-type schemes over k, and k-morphisms. The compact-support comparison also assumes standing schemes and a compactification in that setting. I restored precisely this scope, which the enclosing smooth-purity unit uses, rather than claiming the theorem for arbitrary schemes.

There is a concrete obstruction to the unqualified Hom formula, even with ell invertible. Take T to be the countable disjoint union of copies of Spec of an algebraically closed field of characteristic p different from ell, and take the rank-one constant E-object K=L in degree zero. The locally constructible convention on arbitrary schemes includes this object. Its scalar endomorphisms over Qbar include every sequence (alpha_n) in product_n Qbar. Choose alpha_n with degrees over E unbounded. An endomorphism descending to a finite F containing E would have every alpha_n in F, impossible since [E(alpha_n):E] would then be bounded by [F:E]. Thus union_F product_n F does not exhaust product_n Qbar. The old conditional wording excluded this failure by assuming the colimit description; the current blueprint instead invokes a theorem, so its standing scope must be explicit. No claim that this example satisfies the old colimit assumption is made.

The corrected hypotheses hold in every current use found by rg: sk-thm-purity-transport states the standing setting; lem-pur-transport-qbar-common-stage-counit explicitly fixes separated finite-type k-schemes; and lem-pod-rational-purity-pairing invokes this item only in its smooth-purity setting with separated finite-type schemes. No target statement or unit statement was edited.
