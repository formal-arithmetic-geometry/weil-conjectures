## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the unconditional equality H0(C,Z/d)=Z/d is false with the project's def-sc-curves convention. Take k algebraically closed of characteristic zero, d=2, and C the disjoint union of two affine lines. It is a smooth affine separated finite-type scheme of dimension one, but constant-sheaf global sections are (Z/2)^2, hence H0 is (Z/2)^2, a group with four elements rather than two. The empty curve gives H0=0 for d>1 as another counterexample. These follow from the locally constant sections description in Stacks 03P5, not from a guessed convention about the word curve.
