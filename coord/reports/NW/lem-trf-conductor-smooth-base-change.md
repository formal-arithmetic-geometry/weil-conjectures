## Returned proof, retained verbatim for the next workers

1. Because S is reduced and finite type over the field Q, the componentwise normalization theorem gives a finite normalization map and a dense open N of points whose local rings are integrally closed domains, over which the normalization is an isomorphism. On an affine chart A of S, reducedness says that the map A to the product of the fraction fields of its minimal components is injective (the kernel is the intersection of the minimal primes). The componentwise normalization is therefore finite and injective on structure sheaves, and has finitely many components by Noetherianity.

2. The finite pushforward node makes nu_* O_{S^nu} coherent, and the coherent-kernel/cokernel node makes its cokernel C_S coherent. The scheme-theoretic-support node identifies the sectionwise annihilator with a quasi-coherent ideal and gives its affine localization formula. On an affine chart Spec A, C_S is a finite A-module and A is Noetherian, so Ann_A(C_S) is a finitely generated ideal; hence this quasi-coherent ideal is coherent. Thus D_S is a coherent ideal sheaf.

3. For an affine chart, the support/annihilator formula gives V(D_S)=Supp(C_S). At a point x, the stalk of C_S vanishes exactly when the finite normalization map is an isomorphism after localizing. The componentwise affine description identifies the localized normalization algebra with the product of the integral closures of the reduced component domains; if it equals the local reduced ring, locality forces one factor and the local ring is an integrally closed domain, and the converse is the normal-locus isomorphism. Hence V(D_S) is exactly the nonnormal locus. The dense normal open from the normalization theorem misses every generic point, so this closed set is nowhere dense. Finally D_S=O_S iff C_S=0 iff every local ring is an integrally closed domain, i.e. S is normal.

4. Let h:T->S be smooth. Smoothness is locally of finite presentation, so after choosing affine U in S and an affine V in T above U, the induced smooth ring map A=Gamma(U,O_S)->B=Gamma(V,O_T) is of finite presentation; since A is a finite-type Q-algebra, V is finite type over Q. Apply the absolute comparison in the normalized-blowup calculus to U, V and the coherent ideal J=O_U (whose zero scheme is empty): its specialization B_J(U)=U^nu gives a canonical isomorphism V^nu ~= U^nu x_U V. These local isomorphisms glue by uniqueness of normalization, yielding T^nu ~= S^nu x_S T. On such affine charts, a finite morphism is represented by a finite algebra C over A and its pullback has algebra C tensor_A B, so h^*(nu_*O_{S^nu}) identifies with the pushforward of the pulled-back normalization. Smooth ring maps are flat, hence pulling back 0->O_S->nu_*O_{S^nu}->C_S->0 remains exact and its quotient is C_T.

5. On each affine V as above, apply the finite-module annihilator flat-base-change lemma to the finite A-module C_S(U) and the flat map A->B. It gives Ann_B(C_S(U) tensor_A B)=Ann_A(C_S(U))B. The affine localization formula for annihilators makes these equalities compatible on overlaps, so they glue to D_T=D_S O_T. Extension of ideals is associative, giving the identity and composition compatibilities.
