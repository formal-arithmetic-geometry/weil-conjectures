## Statement/source corrections

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the retained unlanded polynomial helper's claim that its splitting is natural for arbitrary maps of polynomial triples is false. Take A=Z, P=Z[x], Q=P[y], and the automorphism fixing x and sending y to y+x. It sends the coordinate complement dy to dy+dx. More generally no P-linear splitting of Ω_{Q/P} -> Ω_{Q/A} invariant under this automorphism exists: writing its image of dy as dy+f(x,y) dx would require f(x,y+x)+1=f(x,y); setting x=0 yields 1=0. The corrected fact is a canonical natural exact sequence which admits an objectwise splitting. Step 2 proves that fact directly from existing bases, and the proposed helper is omitted. The item's triangle statement is true and unchanged.

The retained source description claiming a polynomial construction in the proof of 031K was inaccurate: the local 031K proof cites the formal-smoothness characterization and the naive-cotangent exact sequence. The repaired elementary step is the explicit variable-basis proof, with 00RX as its actual basis source.

Source typo at sites-cohomology Tag 08Q8: the displayed order Lπ_! Lh* = Lf* Lπ'_! interchanges the coefficient-labelled π and π' and the proof says both are right adjoints. With the displayed structural maps, the well-typed identity is Lπ'^S_! Lh* = Lf* Lπ^R_!:D(underline R)->D(S). Both sides are left adjoint to the same functor N -> constant(Res_{R->S} N), since restriction commutes with the constant functor; the derived adjunction Hom calculation gives this identity. The new node states that typed identity explicitly, agreeing with its intended use in the cotangent source. No change of target strength is involved.
