## Source error

The blueprint proof begins by choosing a finite etale cover trivializing the sheaf with one finite fibre S. This is excessive for arbitrary T. For T the disjoint union of Spec(k) indexed by positive integers, the sheaf represented by the union of n copies over component n is finite locally constant and represented by a finite etale morphism (finiteness is local on the target), but cannot become constant with one fixed finite fibre S on any surjective cover: its geometric fibre cardinalities are unbounded. Even on disconnected bases, different components can have different ranks. Stacks Tag 03RV instead uses a covering family and finite local representatives. The split uses the existing nodes' arbitrary covering families and varying S_i; it does not add connectedness or quasi-compactness to the statement. This repairs the node-level proof without departing from the sk-thm-p1 unit route, which needs precisely the finite-etale machinery for torsor lifting. The item statement is true, so no STATEMENT-DISPUTED statement correction is required.
