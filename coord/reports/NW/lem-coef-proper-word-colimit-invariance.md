For completeness, the argument for the new statement (kept out of its statement-only node) is as follows. Properness makes each image E(v|S) an actual copy of its subword value; two such copies meet in E(v|(S intersect T)). Their maps to C_(n-1) agree on this common copy by the lower colimit cocone. They therefore define the asserted boundary map degreewise, and these maps respect every simplicial operator.

A cocone on words of length less than n, together with maps from the two alternating length-n values agreeing with it on their boundaries, extends uniquely to the full truncation. For a nonalternating length-n word w, collapse each maximal constant-sign run to obtain a shorter alternating word r(w), with quotient q_w; its forced cocone map is the lower map on r(w) composed with E(q_w). If a morphism has lower-length source and such a target, compatibility is the lower cocone equation for q_w composed with that morphism. A nonidentity morphism from a nonalternating length-n word has image a shorter word u: factor it as a surjection p followed by its image injection. The lower cocone equations or the specified boundary agreement handle the injection. The reduced sign-run word of u equals r(w), and q_u p = q_w, so the lower cocone equation for q_u handles the surjection. The identity case is immediate. An alternating length-n word admits no nonidentity outgoing arrow within the truncation, because sign preservation prohibits identifying any adjacent positions. These cases verify all cocone equations and uniqueness; they also prove naturality. This establishes exactly the repaired pushout universal property without assuming any unmentioned property of E.
