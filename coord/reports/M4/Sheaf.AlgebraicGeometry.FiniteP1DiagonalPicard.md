## What changed

Replaced the proof hole in `AlgebraicGeometry.Etale.finiteP1_diagonal_picard`
with the determinant-section argument. The proof constructs the global section
`x0 ⊗ y1 - x1 ⊗ y0` of the external tensor product of the two hyperplane
lines. It computes its coefficient on each product chart, identifies its zero
ideal with the diagonal ideal by equality on an affine cover, applies the
proved general Cartier divisor-line correspondence, and computes the pullback
through both canonical charts.

Added `Sheaf.AlgebraicGeometry.P1DiagonalLocalIdeal`, containing only the local
ideal calculation `AlgebraicGeometry.ProjectiveSpace.p1_diagonal_ideal_on_chart`.
Its conclusion is an equality of ideals of functions on an affine subopen;
it has no invertibility hypothesis, line-bundle comparison, Picard class,
projection chart, or cohomology assertion. The gluing, construction of the
section, passage from its zero ideal to its divisor line, and both chart
pullback calculations are written in the item module.

Added private, proved helpers for making a module morphism from a global
section, restricting coordinate sections, and computing pullback of the
Picard class of an invertible module. Existing signatures, definitions and
public imports are preserved. All added imports of the item are private proof
imports. Updated its node's proof, proof dependencies and proof origin.

The source-library search found
`Hom.nonempty_iso_dual_toModules_tensorObj` in `Modules.ZeroLocus`,
`pullbackMapTwistIso` and the free coordinate-generator API in
`ProjectiveSpace.Twist`, and the Picard tensor/pullback API in `Picard.Group`.
These existing proved results replace a fresh gluing of dual ideal frames.
The proved source declaration attached to `lem-sc-pic-group` is general for
schemes, and is used at that generality.
LeanSearch, LeanFinder, Loogle and source grep did not find the missing local
projective-line diagonal equation.

Validation: the new local-ideal module compiled cleanly in 3.6 seconds, and
the completed item module compiled cleanly in 10.4 seconds through `lcompile.py`.

The new lemma's four normalized determinants are `v-u`, `1-uv`, `uv-1`,

nonreduced rings and the zero ring, and uses polynomial quotient rings rather
than equality on geometric points. There are no new proof holes in definitions
or in private helpers.

## No longer needed

No modules or declarations are withdrawn. No original import is removed.

The separate construction and gluing of dual ideal-frame isomorphisms in the

been identified. The source Segre-twist theorem was inspected, but the proof
does not use or import it.

## Where the brief, the DAG or earlier work was wrong

The supplied proof dependencies state the projective-space charts and the
divisor-line definition, but none states the scheme-theoretic equality between
the diagonal ideal and its local normalized determinant. In particular,
standard coordinate generators alone do not identify this kernel ideal. That
missing local calculation is now isolated with its full ring-theoretic proof
in words; it is not replaced by another Picard or line-isomorphism assertion.

The current blueprint locates `lem-amb-p1-diagonal-integrals` under
`sec-six-functors-basics/sk-prop-geo-p1-adjunction`, rather than the historical
`sec-smooth-purity/sk-prop-sf-cbc` path in the original module's source comment.
The new node and revised proof origin cite the current determinant proof.

## New nodes

`lem-sp-p1-diagonal-local-ideal`, in
`dag/nodes/sec-smooth-purity/lem-sp-p1-diagonal-local-ideal.json`, records the
principal ideal generated by the normalized determinant on any affine subopen
of a standard product chart. It realizes only the local ideal-equation part of
step 1 of the item node. Its source is the first sentence of the current
blueprint proof of `lem-amb-p1-diagonal-integrals`; the docstring and node
explain the four polynomial quotient presentations and descent to arbitrary
bases. Searches cover LeanSearch, LeanFinder/Loogle and Mathlib/source grep.
There are no change records because no existing public interface is changed.
