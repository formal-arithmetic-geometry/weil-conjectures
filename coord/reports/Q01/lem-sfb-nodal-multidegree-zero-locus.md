For `lem-sfb-nodal-uniform-smooth-divisor` I read curves.tex Tag 0E3F, `lemma-global-generation`: on a proper reduced connected curve it assumes deg(M|B)>=2 dim H^1(B,O_B) for every reduced connected closed subcurve B, and concludes global generation. The new statement is a consequence, with the following full argument for its future worker. Normalization at the nodes gives g(C)=sum g(normalized components)+first Betti number of the connected component graph. For a connected union B of components, its induced graph has no more independent cycles and its component genera are a subsum, so g(B)<=g(C). Reduced closed one-dimensional subcurves are precisely such unions. By birational degree invariance and the component-sum formula, deg((L tensor H^m)|B)=m sum_{Z in B} h_Z>=m>=2g+1>2g(B). Tag 0E3F makes M=L tensor H^m globally generated. Choose all nodes and one smooth point on each component. Evaluation at each of this finite set of points is a nonzero functional on H^0(C,M). Over the infinite algebraically closed field, the union of their kernels does not fill H^0(C,M). A section outside the union is nonzero at every node and on every component. On the smooth locus its nonzero component restrictions cut an effective Cartier divisor; near each node it is a unit, so the local empty divisors glue with these divisors. Thus its zero divisor E is supported in the smooth locus and M=O(E). Tensor additivity, the component sum and the degree-of-an-effective-divisor formula give length(E)=deg M=mh. This last formula is explicitly included in existing `lem-pc-degree-base-change`. No characteristic-zero Bertini, reduced divisor, or choice of distinct zeros is needed.

For `lem-sfb-projective-smooth-divisor-parameter` I read moduli.tex Tags 0DPE (`lemma-hilb-open`) and 0DPI (`lemma-hilb-qc-over-base`), and pic.tex Tags 0B9A, 0B9B and 0B9D, including the Cartier implication for arbitrary base. The full construction is as follows. The existing projective fixed-polynomial representability theorem gives the projective finite-type Hilbert scheme H_d for the constant polynomial d and its universal flat finitely presented family Z. These are zero-dimensional length-d fibres; properness plus quasi-finiteness makes Z finite, and flat finite presentation makes it finite locally free of rank d. The closed subset Z intersect (C minus U) maps to a closed subset of H_d, since Z->H_d is proper. Its complement V_d is the open where the entire divisor lies in U. Conversely a finite flat rank-d subscheme of U_T is closed in C_T: its immersion has proper source over T and separated target over T, so it is proper and closed. It has Hilbert polynomial d, so this is exactly the open subfunctor, on arbitrary test schemes. V_d is finite type because it is an open of the Noetherian H_d. On U, Tag 0B9D makes the universal family a relative effective Cartier divisor. On the complement of its support its ideal is O; these local Cartier descriptions agree on overlap and give a relative effective Cartier divisor on C. The universal property and pullback of Cartier lines give all base-change assertions. This combines projective boundedness, support avoidance, finite flatness and the relative Cartier property; neither an existing smooth-field Hilbert theorem nor a representability-only assertion gives all these clauses by specialization.
