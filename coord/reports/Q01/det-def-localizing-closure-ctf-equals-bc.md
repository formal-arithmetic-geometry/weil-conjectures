## Source error

The imported Strata paragraph says to choose an open stratum whose closed complement is a union of strata and calls the induction Noetherian. Neither that choice nor a Noetherian hypothesis follows from the stated finite locally closed constructible partition on a qcqs scheme. A concrete finite partition with no open member is the union of three distinct affine lines L1,L2,L3 in the plane with pairwise distinct intersection points P12,P23,P13, partitioned into S1=L1 minus P13, S2=L2 minus P12, S3=L3 minus P23. These are disjoint locally closed constructible subsets covering the union; each includes one crossing with the next component, where it has no neighbourhood in the union contained in itself, so none is open. The corrected proof stays on the open--closed route: at each point choose an affine neighbourhood where its stratum is closed, use its quasi-compact complement, and induct on the number of strata of that complement. This is the existing `lem-sf-constructible-partition-local-closed-stratum` argument. The tensor cohomology is then detected by the ringed short exact sequence, so no separate derived projection-formula assertion is hidden.
