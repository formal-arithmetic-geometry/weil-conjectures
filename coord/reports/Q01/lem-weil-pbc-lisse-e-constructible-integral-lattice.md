### lem-weil-pbc-integral-constructible-hom-sums

Source: literature/bhatt-scholze-pro-etale-topology_arXiv-1309.1198.pdf, Lemma 6.8.12; passage from global derived Hom to its cohomology, expanded using the shifted internal-Hom and K-injective interfaces.

Use global derived Hom as defined in [def-weil-pbc-adic-global-derived-hom]. For any $M,r$, the natural shift comparison [lem-pur-derived-internal-hom-shift-right] identifies $R\mathcal Hom(K,M)[r]$ with $R\mathcal Hom(K,M[r])$. A shifted K-injective resolution is K-injective by [lem-weil-pbc-kinjective-shift]; its termwise sections are exactly the shifted sections complex. Thus $R\Gamma$ commutes with this shift. Taking degree zero and applying [lem-pur-rhom-global-sections] gives $H^rR\operatorname{Hom}(K,M)=\operatorname{Hom}_D(K,M[r])$, naturally in $M$.
Apply [lem-weil-pbc-integral-constructible-bounded-below-compactness] to the specified family. Its one common lower bound is precisely that node's hypothesis. Take $H^q$ of its isomorphism. By [lem-kun-derived-countable-sums-exact], countable derived sums of ordinary $A$-module complexes are termwise sums. Exactness of ordinary module sums shows that cycles, boundaries and their quotients sum, so their $q$th cohomology is the sum of the $q$th cohomologies. The preceding natural identification on source and target gives the claimed map, since each coprojection is carried to postcomposition by that coprojection. A shift preserves the displayed coproduct because it is an equivalence with inverse shift $[-q]$. The argument includes the empty coproduct.

### lem-weil-pbc-integral-models-extension-closed

Source: literature/bhatt-scholze-pro-etale-topology_arXiv-1309.1198.pdf, Lemma 6.8.13, last paragraph, clearing a denominator in an extension class; Stacks Tag 06XV (derived.tex, lemma-ext-1) for degree-one extension classification.

The fraction relation and the hypothesis give a class $\alpha\in\operatorname{Ext}^1_{A_X}(P,Q)$ and $m\geq0$ whose rationalized fraction is the class of $F$. By [lem-weil-pbc-ext-one-extension-classification], realize $\alpha$ by $0\to Q\xrightarrow{u}N\to P\to0$. By [lem-weil-pbc-complex-ses-triangle] this has its associated distinguished triangle. Both endpoint complexes lie in the integral constructible complete category by [lem-weil-pbc-integral-stratified-perfect-characterization]; its closure under shifts and cones puts $N[0]$ there, and its cohomology characterization says that $N$ is constructible integral.
The coefficient identification [lem-weil-pbc-continuous-coefficients-immersion-restriction] gives $E_X=A_X[1/\varpi]$. Underlying localization is the sequential colimit with transition $\varpi$: send stage $r$ to the fraction with denominator $\varpi^r$. The fraction relation proves this universal property after sheafification. Exactness follows from [lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-colimits-forgetful] and [lem-weil-pbc-site-abelian-sheaves-filtered-colimits-exact]. Exact scalar extension therefore sends the sequence to class $S_X\alpha$ by extension classification.
Keep its middle and quotient objects, but replace the inclusion $S_Xu$ by $S_Xu\circ\varpi^m$. This is still exact since $\varpi^m$ is an automorphism of $S_XQ$. There is a morphism from the original rational sequence to this new sequence with endpoint maps $(\varpi^{-m},1)$ and middle map $1$. Naturality of the connecting map in [lem-weil-pbc-complex-ses-triangle] makes its new class $\varpi^{-m}S_X\alpha$. It equals the given class of $F$. The bijection in extension classification now gives an isomorphism of middle objects $S_XN\cong F$.

### lem-weil-pbc-lisse-lattice-reduced-open

Source: literature/bhatt-scholze-pro-etale-topology_arXiv-1309.1198.pdf, Lemma 6.8.13, first paragraph, followed by extraction of a nonempty open from its finite stratification; Stacks Tag 03S0 (etale-cohomology.tex, lemma-etale-stratified-finite).

By [lem-weil-pbc-lisse-lattice-etale-local], an etale covering carries lisse integral models. On each member of a finite affine cover of $X$, [lem-weil-pbc-etale-covering-affine-refinement] gives finitely many affine members refining it. Their finite disjoint union $Y$ is a surjective etale cover of $X$. Define its integral sheaf componentwise from the supplied models: an object over the disjoint union decomposes over its open-and-closed components, so the sheaf values are their finite product and all maps and isomorphisms are componentwise. Both $X$ and $Y$ are qcqs ($X$ is quasi-separated by [lem-weil-pbc-locally-noetherian-quasi-separated], and $Y$ is a finite union of affine components). By [lem-weil-pbc-etale-stratified-finite], choose finitely many locally closed strata on which this cover is finite etale. Surjectivity persists after base change by [lem-weil-pbc-surjective-base-change]. On each stratum [lem-weil-pbc-lisse-lattice-finite-etale-descent] supplies a lisse integral model. The continuous coefficient identifications on immersions come from [lem-weil-pbc-continuous-coefficients-immersion-restriction].
There are finitely many irreducible components by [lem-sc-noetherian-finite-irreducible-components]; each has a generic point by [lem-sc-scheme-sober]. Choose one component $C$ with generic point $\eta$, and the stratum containing $\eta$. Factor its immersion into a closed immersion into an open $O\subseteq X$, using [def-weil-closed-immersion]. Its image is $O\cap F$ for some closed $F$ containing $\eta$, hence containing $C$. Set $U=O\setminus\bigcup_{C'\ne C}C'$. It contains $\eta$, is open, and lies in the stratum image. Since $U$ is reduced, [lem-weil-pbc-reduced-source-factors-through-closed-subscheme] factors $U\to O$ through the stratum. The factor is an open immersion: base change of the stratum to $U$ is the whole reduced $U$ (its ideal vanishes at every point). Restrict its lisse model to $U$. The open is quasi-compact by [lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-open-subscheme-noetherian].

### lem-weil-pbc-integral-model-open-closed-pieces

Source: literature/bhatt-scholze-pro-etale-topology_arXiv-1309.1198.pdf, Lemma 6.8.13, constructible filtration and extension assembly; Stacks Tags 09AH (proetale.tex, lemma-ses-associated-to-open), 09AA (lemma-closed-immersion), 09BN (lemma-open-immersion) and 03DI (sites-modules.tex, lemma-extension-by-zero).

Noetherianity makes the complementary open quasi-compact by [lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-open-subscheme-noetherian]; [lem-weil-pbc-locally-noetherian-quasi-separated] also makes $X$ qcqs. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-proetale-ringed-open-closed] with both coefficient rings, identified on $U,Z$ by [lem-weil-pbc-continuous-coefficients-immersion-restriction]. Its restriction identities say that $Q$ restricts to $(M_U,0)$ and $P$ to $(0,M_Z)$. Compose the given stratum immersions with $j,i$ by [lem-weil-pbc-proper-fp-permanence](e). Their finitely many images partition $X$; on them the indicated restrictions have exactly the given finite-presentation local models or zero models. This proves constructibility by [def-weil-pbc-continuous-constructible-module].
Localization is the sheaf colimit of multiplication by $\varpi$, using the fraction map at stage $r$, $m\mapsto m/\varpi^r$. The ringed open-closed node gives preservation of filtered colimits by $i_*$. It gives $j_!\dashv j^{-1}$, and its universal property gives preservation of that colimit: maps from $j_!\operatorname{colim}M_n$ to $F$ are compatible maps $M_n\to j^{-1}F$, equivalently compatible maps $j_!M_n\to F$. Both comparisons commute with multiplication by scalars, since the module actions are the induced coefficient actions. The coefficient restriction isomorphisms therefore identify these colimits with $S_XP$ and $S_XQ$, giving the two asserted scalar comparisons. Here module colimits are computed on underlying abelian sheaves by [lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-colimits-forgetful]. Substitute the specified rational identifications into the canonical short exact sequence from the ringed open-closed node to get the displayed sequence.
