## Worker report

The module [EdgeInvertingAnodyneExtension.lean](lean/Sheaf/CategoryTheory/EdgeInvertingAnodyneExtension.lean)
uses the existing `SSet.Enhanced.equivalenceDiagrams` full subcomplex from
`Sheaf.CategoryTheory.EnhancedDiagramsCones` as the Lean realization of `H(K,E)`.  It defines
`edgeInvertingRestriction` by lifting the internal-hom restriction map to the full subcomplex,
and `edgeInvertingPostcomposition` similarly by lifting postcomposition.  The helper
`map_isEquivalenceEdge` explicitly maps an inverse edge and its two witnessing 2-simplices.
The main theorem `SSet.Enhanced.edge_inverting_anodyne_extension` is postponed with `sorry`.

The theorem's first conjunct is
`modelCategoryQuillen.I.rlp (edgeInvertingRestriction (E := E) i)`.  Mathlib's `I` is the
morphism property generated by all boundary inclusions, so this is exactly the required right
lifting property against every `∂Δ^m ⟶ Δ^m`, including `m = 0`; it is the stated trivial Kan
fibration convention.  The hypothesis `hi : SSet.anodyneExtensions i` is Mathlib's Kan
anodyne closure (horn generators, pushouts, transfinite compositions, retracts, and isomorphism
closure).  The second conjunct gives strict compatibility under parameter restriction, with
`edgeInvertingRestriction (E := E) (p ≫ i)` equal to the composite of the two restrictions.
The third gives strict compatibility under postcomposition, with the target restriction using
`E := F` and the postcomposition maps on `K` and `K'` in the appropriate order.

2. Kan anodyne i: K -> K': `(i : K ⟶ K') (hi : SSet.anodyneExtensions i)`.
   I read Mathlib `AnodyneExtensions/Basic.lean`: `anodyneExtensions` is

   Isomorphism closure is an instance, and anodyne maps are monomorphisms.
   Verdict: the node's all-horn class, not merely inner or right anodyne maps.

   `equivalenceDiagrams` then tests every K-edge at those vertices. It does not

   indexed by all natural numbers. RLP quantifies every commuting square and
   asserts a diagonal with exact triangle equalities. Verdict: strict fillers,
   not an equivalence of functor categories or extension only up to equivalence.
6. Compatibility with parameter restriction: `edgeInvertingRestriction` is a
   simplicial map (a natural transformation of presheaves), whose naturality
   field supplies compatibility with every simplex operator. More generally,
   parameterizing by any P and restricting along v: P' -> P amounts to
   precomposition of maps into H; composing with this canonical restriction map
   commutes with that operation. No chosen fillers or natural choice of fillers
   is asserted by the node. Verdict: carried by the actual map and its naturality.
   The original theorem instead appended a formula for P -> K, which changes the
   diagram shape rather than the parameter. I removed that unrequested clause;
   the requested parameter compatibility remains part of the simplicial map.

   restriction followed by `edgeInvertingPostcomposition (K := K) q` equals
