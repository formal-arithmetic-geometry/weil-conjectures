## Source error

Dugger's proof of Lemma 6.3, printed p. 190, describes a cofibration followed by a trivial cofibration when constructing a Reedy cofibrant replacement. The second factor must be a trivial fibration; an arbitrary map does not factor as two cofibrations (their composite would again be a cofibration). Explicitly, in the Kan model structure the fold from the discrete two-point simplicial set to the point is not a monomorphism, so it cannot be a composite of two cofibrations. The bounded-replacement statement uses the correct Reedy cofibrant replacement, with an objectwise weak-equivalence augmentation. Also the final sentence of that proof reverses the displayed source and target of f_*. The evaluation functor gives hocolim(D/X) to hocolim(C/X), as the lemma statement and the final proof's triangle require. Neither typo changes the mathematical statement of our item. No STATEMENT-DISPUTED correction is needed for the item.
