## New lemma: source and mathematical check

For `lem-sfb-etale-tor-constant-test`, I read Stacks Tag 0DJJ, label `lemma-tor-amplitude-stalk`, in `literature/stacks-project/sites-cohomology.tex`, including both parts and their proof. The new statement is a corollary, not a claim that 0DJJ itself says finite-presentation testing. Here is the complete argument to guide its later split. The forward implication is the definition. Conversely, at each geometric point $x$, exact stalks and the monoidal derived pullback comparison imply that $B_x\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}M$ vanishes outside $[a,b]$ for every finitely presented $A$-module $M$. Every module $V$ is the filtered union of its finitely generated submodules. Since $A$ is finite, each such submodule is finite, hence admits a finite presentation: a finite generating list gives a surjection from a finite free module, whose finite kernel has a finite generating list. Choose a K-flat resolution with flat terms of $B_x$ by `lem-pc-kflat-resolution` on the one-point ringed site. Tensoring this resolution with that filtered system computes the derived tensors; tensor preserves the colimit termwise, and exact filtered colimits of modules commute with cohomology by `lem-pur-ringed-site-grothendieck-abelian`. Thus the same bound holds for $B_x\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}V$ for every $V$. For a module sheaf $N$, take $V=N_x$. The derived tensor stalk comparison identifies this with $(B\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}N)_x$. Conservativity of geometric stalks gives the required cohomology-sheaf vanishing, equivalently the converse part of 0DJJ. The exactness and conservativity inputs are `prop-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks-exact` and `lemma-weil-pbc-geometric-stalk-conservative`; the monoidal comparison is `lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-derived-tensor-pullback`.

The new node has a statement only, empty proof and proof dependencies, and explicit scheme, ring, derived-category, constant-sheaf, finite-presentation, tensor, and Tor-amplitude notions.
