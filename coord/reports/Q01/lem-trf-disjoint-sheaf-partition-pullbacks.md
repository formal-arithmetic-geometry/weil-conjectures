These are the exact assertions quoted in citations.json; no abelian-sheaf cohomology clause is used.

Step 2 is a direct application of that same equalizer definition to compatible sections. Distinct pieces have empty intersection, so Step 1 supplies compatibility there; diagonal compatibility is the identity restriction. This proves both existence and uniqueness of the glued section. It is elementary once the covering and the sheaf definition have been fixed, and its cited statement contains that equalizer, not merely a name for sheafification.

Step 3 compares intersections of the two partitions: the two images agree because both are the restriction of the same global section. The disjointness hypothesis applies to these actual site objects and forces every mixed intersection to be empty. The cover property then proves both set inclusions. The cited open-immersion definition identifies the structure sheaf with the restriction to the open subset, justifying equality of the open subschemes, after which sectionwise injectivity proves equality of sections. No assertion about morphisms into a locally closed stratum is substituted for factorization into an open.

Step 4 uses the genuine open-factorization statement, read in full. Its hypothesis is image containment in an open subscheme; for each inverse-image open that containment holds by its definition. After applying naturality of the given sheaf maps, the disjointness hypothesis kills each inverse-image piece of a different label. Since the pieces cover, the whole image lies in the desired open. Only at that point is the factorization lemma applied to the entire test scheme. The induced factor is over S by composition with the original structure maps, and injectivity proves the required equality of component sections. All schemes involved are open restrictions of site objects within the given size convention; no arbitrary colimit or larger indexing category is introduced.

Step 5 writes both directions of the bijection and the restriction identity for an arbitrary test morphism. Inverseness uses the factorization uniqueness already proved in Step 4 and the equality of component sections proved there. The computation with precomposition and pullback explicitly proves naturality. The transformation from a section to a representable presheaf is defined by its formula, so the proof does not use a fully faithful Yoneda lemma as a substitute for the general Yoneda correspondence. The presheaf isomorphism is the family of pointwise bijections with this proved naturality.

Step 6 uses inverse images of open and closed subsets to check the partition hypotheses after any map, the same open factorization for its component morphisms, and the displayed naturality equation for the new sections.
