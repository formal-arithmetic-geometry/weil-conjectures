The steps use scheme-theoretic subgroups throughout, with integer character weights rather than differentiated roots, which matters in characteristics 2 and 3.

1. The Borel-pair node explicitly says semisimple implies reductive and gives positive simple-root coordinates and the perfect pairing. The semisimple-roots-span node supplies rational spanning of both lattices; root-base-rational-integral-spans supplies the rational simple-root basis. Thus the dual rational cocharacter can be cleared into the actual lattice without assuming simply connectedness. Its zero-root calculation is the signed simple-coordinate sum, not a claim about differentials.
2. Character antiduality states the diagonalizable-group equivalence and the closed-immersion criterion. Largest-subtorus states existence and maximality. Applying them to the explicit quotient X/ZI shows that its maximal torsion-free quotient describes the largest subtorus. The root-base statement justifies replacing rational support by integral support. This handles nonreduced finite multiplicative factors: they are not mistaken for the largest torus.
3. Reductive-torus-fixed-subgroup says exactly that the centralizer of any torus in a reductive group is reductive and smooth connected. Reductive-rank-one-reflections gives the character weight decomposition and one-dimensional root spaces. Root-subgroup-parametrizations gives both the all-algebras conjugation identity and the uniqueness statement applied inside L. The tangent invariant formula is obtained directly by evaluating the centralizer definition on dual numbers. Maximality of T inside L is immediate from its maximality inside G.
4. The largest-subtorus node makes the inclusion T_I into T_gamma legitimate. The ambient and internal rank-one centralizers are literally identical, since C_G(T_gamma) is contained in L. The rank-one-reflections statement gives the same canonical coroot from that rank-one SL2 construction. I checked this clause because merely identifying the root set would not identify the root datum.
5. Torus-centralizer-borel-intersection applies with S=T_I contained in B_0 and states that the scheme intersection is Borel. Borel-opposite-root-dichotomy supplies its tangent positive-root convention. Intersecting tangent spaces and using the integral coordinates gives precisely the subsystem positives with simple base I. Simple-root-generation-mixed-commutation is cited for the generation conclusion needed later, not for a nonexistent assertion about parabolic quotients.
6. Cocharacter-limit-smooth-structure supplies smoothness, connectedness, the semidirect product and its zero-weight tangent formula. The ambient root decomposition identifies the zero-weight space. Smooth-subgroup-tangent-generation applies in the smooth connected group Z_lambda, to the finite family T and zero-pairing root subgroups, with closed subgroup L. Their tangent images span the entire tangent space. This proves equality of subgroup schemes, including possible infinitesimal tests.
7. Root-subgroup-parametrizations proves the explicit extension formula into U_lambda. Rank-one-reflections and the cocharacter tangent formula make the product differential an isomorphism. Positive-weight-equivariant-inverse applies: source and target are smooth, connected, affine and finite type, the chosen identity points are fixed, and every source tangent weight is a strictly positive integer. This is the generic existing inverse theorem, not an uncited assertion that unipotent root products work.
8. Root-subgroup-parametrizations and Borel-root-product-coordinates show B_0 is contained in P_lambda. Borel-pair-structure gives B=U semidirect T. Applying the root-product statement in G and again in reductive L gives the stated product scheme isomorphism with the outside roots ordered first. Cocharacter-limit-smooth-structure supplies normality, so the group law is actually the conjugation semidirect product, rather than just a product as varieties.
9. Simple-root-generation-mixed-commutation says the smallest closed subgroup containing T and both signs of the simple root groups is the entire reductive group. Apply it to L with base I, as established in step 5. Any closed K containing the generators of P_I therefore contains L and U_lambda; conversely P_lambda contains all the generators. The cocharacter semidirect product then proves equality, without assuming the generated subgroup smooth in advance.
10. Cocharacter-limit-smooth-structure gives the candidate smooth connected normal unipotent radical. Affine-homomorphism-flat-image gives the faithfully flat image factorization, and the split projection has full image. Unipotent-normal-quotient-image explicitly allows nonsmooth kernels and says the image of a smooth connected normal unipotent subgroup is again smooth connected normal and unipotent. Reductivity of L kills it. This supplies the needed maximality argument, which would not follow merely from the definition of unipotence.
11. Homogeneous-orbit-quotient-geometry says exactly that the fppf right-coset sheaf is represented, is separated finite type, and satisfies the displayed torsor identity. Algebraic-group-homogeneous-quotient supplies finite presentation of the projection and local lifts. I used the uniqueness of the quotient sheaf to identify its quotient with the preceding Q; no assertion that all algebra points lift globally is used.
12. Borel-pair-structure supplies properness of G/B_0. Proper-morphism defines properness as separated, finite type and universally closed. I proved universal closedness directly: the sheaf quotient map G/B_0 → G/P_I is universally surjective by local lifting, and the inverse image of any closed set has the same image under projection to any test S. This elementary two-map argument avoids an uncited proper-image theorem and does not assume G/B_0 → G/P_I is a projective bundle.
13. Cocharacter-limit-smooth-structure gives both smooth factors and complementary tangent spaces. The all-algebras intersection is proved explicitly by A[t] intersection A[t^-1] in A[t,t^-1], including rings with nilpotents; the value at infinity is the identity by the definition of U_-lambda. Transverse-subgroup-product-open has exactly these two hypotheses and gives the open multiplication map. I do not infer a scheme intersection from equality of F-points.
14. Homogeneous-orbit-quotient-geometry supplies the torsor fibre product. Its pullback along H → Q is H times P_I, with projection the already proved open multiplication. Representable-fppf-etale-open-descent states open-immersion descent for algebraic spaces along a representable surjective flat locally finitely presented cover; our quotient map is a scheme morphism satisfying the stronger finite-presentation condition. The inverse of this open immersion gives the section. This is why a separate local-section clause node is unnecessary.
15. Closed-point-residue-field-base requires algebraic closure and local finite type, both present. Homogeneous-orbit-quotient-geometry gives rational lifts, and lft-over-field-jacobson-space says the closed points are dense in each closed subset, ruling out a nonempty complement. The finite-type definition includes quasi-compactness, and quasi-compact-scheme defines exactly the finite-subcover property. Translated sections are written explicitly as g s(g^-1 x), so their regularity and section identity are explicit.
