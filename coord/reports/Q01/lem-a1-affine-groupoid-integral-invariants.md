For `lem-a1-affine-groupoid-polynomial-norm-invariant` I read 03BH, `lemma-determinant-trick`, and the polynomial-groupoid paragraph of 03BJ, as well as Exercises 02DU `exercise-trace-det` and 02DV `exercise-trace-det-rings`, particularly (1) and (3). In the source diagram, composition identifies the two pullbacks of the source map: on composable arrows it is translation `(h,g) -> (h composed with g,g)`, with inverse using g inverse. The target function has the same pullback through composition and the first projection. Determinant norms commute with those base changes, so their values have equal source and target pullbacks. Adjoining a common polynomial coordinate preserves the groupoid identities and finite local freeness, and the equalizer of the coefficientwise maps A[T] to B[T] is exactly C[T], by equality of polynomial coefficients.
