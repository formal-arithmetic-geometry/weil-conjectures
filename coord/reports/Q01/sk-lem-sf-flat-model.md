## Source error

The imported complement says that a locally closed dense open of a closed subscheme and its complement form a two-stratum partition of X. This is false in general. In X = affine 2-space, take W = V(y) and U = V(y) intersect D(x). Then X minus U is dense but not open: every neighbourhood of the origin meets U. A dense locally closed subset would be open, so X minus U is not locally closed. The statement of the item is still true. The repaired proof uses the actual finite ordered stratification of `det-sf-flat-model-generation`, then each stratum's closure. Equivalently a local induction step can use the three strata U, W minus U, X minus W. No statement weakening or new hypothesis is involved.

The source's cohomology warning is respected: for B = Z/ell² and the perfect complex [B --ell--> B], nonzero cohomology is F_ell and need not have finite Tor dimension. Thus the induction uses stupid tails of flat terms. It never replaces lisse generators by constant coefficients, and does not use averaging over a finite monodromy group.
