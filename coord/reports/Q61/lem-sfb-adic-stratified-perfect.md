Tensor with this tower commutes with shifts and mapping cones: in each degree its terms are finite sums of shifts of the input, its differentials are the given scalars, and the cone differential is unchanged by this termwise construction. It sends acyclic inputs to acyclic tower complexes since each Koszul complex is bounded and flat, hence K-flat. It thus gives an exact functor to the derived category of strict towers. Compose with the exact strict-tower $R\lim$. The augmentations commute with these cone and shift formulas, so their unit is compatible with shifts and maps of triangles.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-ringed-koszul-completion-reflector, lem-pc-bounded-above-flat-kflat-site, lem-weil-pbc-ringed-strict-tower-homotopy-limit.

2. The reflector lemma identifies local completeness with invertibility of $\eta$. Given a distinguished triangle, apply the natural map to its completed triangle. In the two long exact cohomology sequences, if two vertical unit maps are isomorphisms, exactness gives an isomorphism at the remaining vertex in every degree. Thus its unit is invertible as well. The same unit sends zero to zero, commutes with shifts and is natural for isomorphisms, proving all stated closure properties.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-ringed-koszul-completion-reflector, lem-sf-derived-homology-long-exact.


### lem-sfb-stratified-local-cohomological-bounds

1. Each affine chart of $X$ has Noetherian underlying space. Every subset of such a chart is quasi-compact; a finite union of these subsets remains quasi-compact. The image of each stratum, with its subspace topology, and hence the stratum itself is therefore quasi-compact. Choose finitely many affine opens covering each stratum. For each affine open the pro-étale covering axiom selects finitely many affine opens in covering members whose images cover it. Their composites give a finite covering family of the whole stratum. Restriction to an open of a slice is exact, so it retains its original cohomological interval.

Uses: lem-sc-affine-noetherian-underlying-space, lem-sfb-noetherian-space-compact-subsets, def-weil-pbc-proetale-site, lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization.

2. There are finitely many strata and finitely many selected members on each. Set $a$ to their minimum lower bound and $b$ to their maximum upper bound. Outside $[a,b]$ the exact restrictions of $H^q(K)$ all vanish. Finite-stratum detection forces $H^q(K)=0$. If the family is empty then the cover forces $X$ empty; detection gives $K=0$ and $[0,0]$ works.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-stratum-detection.


### lem-sfb-adic-stratum-completion-unit

1. Immersion inverse image is exact and preserves limits. Applied to the finite coefficient projections this gives $i^{-1}B_X=B_S$. The weakly étale localization identifies $U_{\mathrm{proét}}$ with $S_{\mathrm{proét}}/U$, where restriction is evaluation and is exact and limit preserving. Thus neither restriction changes the coefficient projections. The finite-reduction comparison of the constant/completed equivalence identifies their derived reductions.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-proetale-immersion-limit-preservation, lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization, lem-weil-pbc-constant-completion-equivalence.

2. Use the actual unit $M\to R\lim_m(M\otimes^L Q_m)$ from the constant/completed equivalence. The finite free Koszul complexes have transition $a_j$ in degree $-1$ and identity in degree zero. Exact restriction sends their finite sums, scalar differentials, transitions, and degree-zero augmentations to the identical formulas after restriction. Countable products of module sheaves are exact on each pro-étale site; hence termwise products represent derived products. The natural Roos triangle then computes the limit by the cone of $1-s$ on those products. Restriction preserves these products and this cone, and sends the compatible augmentations to the completion unit on the slice or stratum. This proves compatibility with the unit itself, rather than only an unspecified isomorphism of limit objects.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-constant-completion-equivalence, lem-weil-proetale-countable-products-exact, lem-weil-derived-countable-products-core, lem-weil-pbc-ringed-strict-tower-homotopy-limit.

3. Completeness is invertibility of that unit. Exact restriction commutes with its cone; finite-stratum detection on the chosen covering family detects the zero cone, giving both implications.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-constant-completion-equivalence, lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-stratum-detection.


### lem-sfb-adic-perfect-model-complete-reduction

1. For a term $P^q=eA^d$ with idempotent $e$, its realized term is $eB^d=\varprojlim_n e\underline{A/I^n}^{\,d}$: finite products and the equalizer expressing the idempotent image commute with limits. Every sheaf at level $n$ is killed by $I^n$. The torsion-limit completeness lemma applies to each term and to the bounded complex over constant $A$ coefficients. The constant/completed equivalence reflects local completeness, giving the assertion over $B$. This construction also works on each slice.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-torsion-limit-sheaf-completeness, lem-weil-pbc-constant-completion-equivalence.

2. Each $eB^d$ is flat, since its tensor functor is a direct summand of a finite sum of the identity. The bounded-above flat K-flat lemma and derived change of rings therefore compute the reduction termwise. The canonical quotient $B/IB=\underline k$ gives the constant complex $P^\bullet\otimes_A k$, with the same differentials and bounds.

Uses: lem-pc-bounded-above-flat-kflat-site, lem-pur-derived-change-of-rings, lem-weil-pbc-constant-completion-equivalence.

3. Constant realization is exact, so its cohomology is the constant realization of the algebraic cohomology. Each reduced term is a direct summand of $k^d$; its subquotient cohomology is finite-dimensional, hence finitely presented over the field. Both complexes are zero termwise outside $[a,b]$, so their cohomology vanishes there.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-constant-module-section-properties.
