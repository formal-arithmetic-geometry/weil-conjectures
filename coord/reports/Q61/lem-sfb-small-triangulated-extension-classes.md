All connecting maps for fixed endpoint representatives are counted, including zero maps; there is no assertion that a connecting map or its completion is uniquely chosen.

Step 2 cites the actual TR1 and TR2 clauses of `def-weil-triangulated-category`, whose statement I read in full. Completing the arrow C_j to B_i[1] and inverse-rotating twice puts B_i in the first position and leaves the specified connecting arrow in the third position. The only endpoint identification is the inverse-shift identification explicitly contained in the shift convention. This is a direct instance of those axioms, so it is elementary relative to the cited definition. The family of choices is indexed by the previously established small set S, not by all triangles. Each selected middle term is in the extension class because its two endpoints are actual members of the two given classes.

Step 3 again cites that definition's TR2 and TR3, not an unrelated exactness theorem. I checked the morphism types and wrote the conjugation explicitly: with a:B to B_i and c:C to C_j, delta=a[1] gamma c^{-1}, hence a[1] gamma=delta c. After two rotations, these are exactly the first-arrow square and the first two components required by TR3. The third objects are M[1] and M_s[1]. Both rotated triangles are distinguished by TR2. Thus this is the stated axiom applied to a displayed commuting square, not a hidden transport lemma.

Step 4 cites `lem-sf-triangle-morphism-iso`, whose statement says that a morphism of distinguished triangles with any two invertible components has invertible third component. The first two components here really are the isomorphisms c and a[1], so its hypotheses hold in the given pre-triangulated category; no octahedral axiom is required. This is the only non-elementary theorem used in the proof. The other cited definition supplies the inverse shift taking d to an isomorphism M to M_s. Finally composition of isomorphisms handles the iso-closure built into the item's definition. The cited lemma is an existing reviewed Mathlib leaf, so its use across units is an allowed generic tool automatically; no unit route is replaced.
