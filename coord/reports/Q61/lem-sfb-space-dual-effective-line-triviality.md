For lem-sfb-space-generic-line-section-injectivity, use the first part of the 0DMZ proof, which precedes and does not use properness. A separated finite-type space over a field is decent and has a sober Noetherian point space. Choose a trivializing affine etale chart on which a nonzero section is nonzero. In its reduced coordinate ring a nonzero function survives in the fraction field at some minimal prime; that point maps to the unique generic point of X by 0ABV. Vanishing in any chosen geometric generic stalk would, after changing the separably closed geometric lift, force vanishing at all such generic lifts. Geometric stalks above the same point detect the same zero-germ condition. This contradicts that nonzero generic value. The geometric local ring is a field because the etale local rings at these generic points are reduced and dimension zero; taking their strict henselizations remains a field. Etale-local triviality identifies the module stalk with one copy of that field. This argument includes singular integral spaces: regularity is deliberately absent. It also gives the statement for affine space, so it is strictly independent of the proper/global-field input, not the original conclusion under another name. These details belong to this report, not the statement-only node.
