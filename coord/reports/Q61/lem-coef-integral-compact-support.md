### Original Step 1

Write $B_{T,n}=(A_n)_T$ and $\widehat R_T=\varprojlim_n B_{T,n}$. By the definition of the integral category, each $K_n$ is intrinsically bounded constructible; by hypothesis its Tor interval is $[a,b]$. By [def-weil-integral-derived-reduction] and [lem-pur-derived-change-of-rings], $P_n=P\otimes_{\widehat R_X}B_{X,n}$ represents $K_n$, and the maps $P_m\to\operatorname{Res}_{m,n}P_n$ are the termwise quotient maps for $m\geq n$. These are exactly the input reduction maps in [def-weil-integral-rational-qbar-compact-support].

### Original Step 2

The comparisons $K_m\otimes^L_{B_{X,m}}B_{X,n}\to K_n$ are canonical isomorphisms with the quotient cocycle. Indeed $P_m$ is K-flat over $B_{X,m}$: for an acyclic $B_{X,m}$-complex $N$, its underlying $\widehat R_X$-complex is acyclic, and $P_m\otimes_{B_{X,m}}N=P\otimes_{\widehat R_X}N$ is acyclic by [def-pc-kflat-complex]. Thus [lem-pur-derived-change-of-rings] computes the comparison by $(P\otimes B_{X,m})\otimes_{B_{X,m}}B_{X,n}\to P\otimes B_{X,n}$. The tensor definition [def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product] gives its inverse on pure tensors: $(x\otimes u)\otimes v\mapsto x\otimes\overline u v$ and $x\otimes v\mapsto(x\otimes1)\otimes v$. These balanced maps commute with differentials and sheafification. For $r\geq m\geq n$ both composites send $((x\otimes u)\otimes v)\otimes w$ to $x\otimes\overline u\overline v w$, proving the cocycle and compatibility with the actual quotient transitions.

Cited nodes: def-pc-kflat-complex, lem-pur-derived-change-of-rings, def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product.

### Original Step 7

The normalized-tower definition also requires a coherent derived realization. Here it can be supplied explicitly. Choose a complex $C_1$ of $B_{Y,1}$-modules representing $M_1$. Inductively suppose $C_n$ has been chosen with an identification $[C_n]\cong M_n$. In the derived category of $B_{Y,n+1}$-modules represent $M_{n+1}\xrightarrow{\tau_{n+1,n}}\operatorname{Res}_{n+1,n}[C_n]$ by a roof $V_{n+1}\xleftarrow{\sim}C_{n+1}\to\operatorname{Res}_{n+1,n}C_n$, using [lem-pc-quasi-isomorphisms-multiplicative-system]; here $V_{n+1}$ is any representative of $M_{n+1}$, and a representative chain map is chosen for the right leg in the homotopy category. The left quasi-isomorphism identifies $[C_{n+1}]$ with $M_{n+1}$. Restrict all $C_n$ to $\widehat R_Y$ and use the chosen adjacent chain maps; define longer transition maps by composition. The resulting strict inverse sequence of complexes is a complex of towers, hence an object of the derived tower category, whose evaluations and transitions are the required $\operatorname{Res}_nM_n$ and $\operatorname{Res}_m\tau_{m,n}$. Thus the coherent datum required by [def-coef-normalized-dvr-tower] exists. There are no additional relations to lift: the sequential index category is generated by adjacent arrows, and Step 6 identifies every composite with its prescribed transition.
