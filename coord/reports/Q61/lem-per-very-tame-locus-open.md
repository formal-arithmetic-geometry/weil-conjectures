## STATEMENT-DISPUTED: missing separatedness

The imported openness statement is false for nonseparated schemes, even with trivial log structure. Let k be an algebraically closed field of characteristic 2, glue two copies of A^1_k along D(t) by the identity, and let C_2 exchange the copies. This is the doubled-origin affine line, a Noetherian regular scheme. With trivial log structure it is fs log regular. Each origin has trivial geometric inertia, and is very tame. On their common punctured affine line the involution is literally the identity, so every geometric inertia group has order 2, which is not invertible in k. Consequently T is exactly the two origins. Neither origin has an open neighbourhood contained in this set, so T is not open.

The repaired item assumes that Y is separated (its structural diagonal over Spec Z is closed). This agrees with ILO VIII Theorem 1.1, the standing hypotheses of VIII 5.1, and the separated schemes of the blueprint ancestor. VI 3.6 is used as a local inertia-group result, not as a licence to drop separatedness from the global action. No admissibility, generic freeness, excellentness, global tameness, or normal-crossings-boundary hypothesis has been added. The strict inert invariance clause requires no separatedness of the source Y'. The definition of very tame is unchanged. This is a faithful repair of an auxiliary imported lemma, not a change to a Challenge target.
