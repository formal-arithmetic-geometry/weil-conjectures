### Returned Step 1

Choose an affine open $U=\operatorname{Spec}A$ of $W$ containing $i(\bar a)$. By the affine characterization of a closed immersion, $i^{-1}(U)=\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)$ for an ideal $I$, and the image of $\bar a$ corresponds to a prime $\mathfrak p/I$ over $\mathfrak p$. The affine-neighbourhood/triples comparison [lem-weil-pbc-etale-local-ring-triples](2)-(3) lets us compute the chosen geometric colimits on these charts. Apply the affine strict-local-ring comparison [lem-exc-affine-strict-local-ring-comparison] to the open charts $U\hookrightarrow W$ and $\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)\hookrightarrow Z$. It identifies the two geometric strict-local rings with algebraic strict henselizations of $A_{\mathfrak p}$ and $(A/I)_{\mathfrak p/I}$, respectively, using the same separable closure $K\subset\Omega$ and the identity residue-field maps. The fibre-product maps are induced by the quotient $A\to A/I$, so the claimed scheme map can be checked on these affine rings.

Uses: def-weil-closed-immersion, def-pc-fibre-product, def-weil-affine-spectrum, lem-exc-affine-strict-local-ring-comparison, lem-weil-pbc-etale-local-ring-triples, def-weil-pbc-strict-henselization-limit.

### Returned Step 2

Put $C_W=\mathcal O^{\mathrm{sh}}_{W,i\bar a}$, $C_Z=\mathcal O^{\mathrm{sh}}_{Z,\bar a}$ and $R=(A/I)_{\mathfrak p/I}=A_{\mathfrak p}/IA_{\mathfrak p}$. By Step 1 the $A$-algebra map $A\to C_W$ is $A\to A_{\mathfrak p}\to C_W$, where $C_W$ is, as an $A_{\mathfrak p}$-algebra, an algebraic strict henselization of $A_{\mathfrak p}$ relative to $K$ [def-sc-ring-strict-henselization]; likewise $C_Z$ is an $R$-algebra strict henselization of $R$ relative to $K$. Both structure maps are local and both composites $A_{\mathfrak p}\to C_W\to K$, $R\to C_Z\to K$ are the canonical $\kappa(\mathfrak p)\subset K$, since the residue maps of [lem-exc-affine-strict-local-ring-comparison] are the geometric evaluations. The canonical local map $h^\#:C_W\to C_Z$ of [lem-weil-pbc-strict-henselization-functoriality](3) satisfies $j_W\circ\operatorname{Spec}h^\#=i\circ j_Z$; both sides factor through $U$, so by [def-weil-affine-spectrum] $A\to C_W\to C_Z$ equals $A\to A/I\to C_Z$. Two ring maps out of $A_{\mathfrak p}$ that agree on $A$ agree, so $h^\#$ is an $A_{\mathfrak p}$-algebra map and kills $IC_W$. Put $Q=C_W/IC_W$ with quotient map $q$; $Q$ is an $R$-algebra through $A_{\mathfrak p}\to C_W\to Q$, and $h^\#=\bar hq$ for a unique $R$-algebra map $\bar h:Q\to C_Z$. Since $q$ is surjective and $h^\#$ local, $\bar h^{-1}(\mathfrak m_{C_Z})=q(\mathfrak m_{C_W})=\mathfrak m_Q$. By (1)-(3) of the functoriality node and the identifications of Step 1, $h^\#$ induces $\mathrm{id}_K$ ($\kappa(i(a))=\kappa(a)=\kappa(\mathfrak p)$).

Uses: def-sc-ring-strict-henselization, lem-exc-affine-strict-local-ring-comparison, lem-weil-pbc-strict-henselization-functoriality, def-weil-affine-spectrum.

Source: literature/stacks-project/algebra.tex, Tag 04GU (lemma-strictly-henselian-functorial) and etale-cohomology.tex Tag 04HX (lemma-describe-etale-local-ring); the quotient Q of Tag 05WS.

### Returned Step 3

Since $A_{\mathfrak p}\to C_W$ is local, $IC_W\subseteq\mathfrak m_{C_W}$, and $C_W$ is strictly henselian (Step 1); so [lem-weil-pbc-strictly-henselian-quotient] makes $Q$ henselian local with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m_Q=q(\mathfrak m_{C_W})$ and residue field $K$ through $q$. By [def-sc-ring-strict-henselization] and [lem-sc-pointed-etale-filtered], $C_W$ is a filtered colimit of etale $A_{\mathfrak p}$-algebras and $C_Z$ a filtered colimit of etale $R$-algebras (as algebras with the structures of Step 2). All structure maps to $K$ are $\kappa(\mathfrak p)\subset K$, so $\mathrm{id}_K$ is a $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$-algebra map in each application below, and each maximal ideal lies over the maximal ideal of the base. (a) Apply [lem-weil-pbc-map-into-henselian-colimit] over $R$ with source $C_Z$, target $Q$, prime $\mathfrak m_{C_Z}$, $\tau=\mathrm{id}_K$: there is an $R$-algebra map $v:C_Z\to Q$ with $v^{-1}(\mathfrak m_Q)=\mathfrak m_{C_Z}$ inducing $\mathrm{id}_K$. (b) Over $R$ with source and target $C_Z$: $\bar hv$ and $\mathrm{id}$ are $R$-algebra maps with preimage $\mathfrak m_{C_Z}$ inducing $\mathrm{id}_K$, so $\bar hv=\mathrm{id}$. (c) Over $A_{\mathfrak p}$ with source $C_W$, target $Q$, prime $\mathfrak m_{C_W}$, $\tau=\mathrm{id}_K$: both $q$ and $vh^\#$ are such $A_{\mathfrak p}$-algebra maps ($vh^\#$ by Step 2 and (a)), so $vh^\#=q$, i.e. $v\bar hq=q$; as $q$ is surjective, $v\bar h=\mathrm{id}_Q$. Thus $\bar h$ is an isomorphism and $h^\#=\bar hq$ is surjective with kernel $IC_W$. This is the argument of Stacks 08HT from 08HR, with the bases written out.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-strictly-henselian-quotient, def-sc-ring-strict-henselization, lem-sc-pointed-etale-filtered, lem-weil-pbc-map-into-henselian-colimit.

### Returned Step 4

By Step 1, $j_W$ factors through the open $U$ [lem-exc-affine-strict-local-ring-comparison], and $i^{-1}(U)=\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)$ is an open subscheme of $Z$ [def-weil-open-immersion]. For any $a:T\to Z$, $b:T\to W_{(i\bar a)}$ with $ia=j_Wb$, the map $a$ lands in $i^{-1}(U)$ and so factors uniquely through it; hence by the universal property [def-pc-fibre-product] $Z\times_WW_{(i\bar a)}=\operatorname{Spec}(A/I)\times_{\operatorname{Spec}A}\operatorname{Spec}C_W$, with the same projections. By [lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra] this is $\operatorname{Spec}(A/I\otimes_AC_W)=\operatorname{Spec}Q$, with projections $\operatorname{Spec}$ of $A/I\to Q$ and of $q$. Under the isomorphism $\operatorname{Spec}\bar h:\operatorname{Spec}C_Z\to\operatorname{Spec}Q$ of Step 3 [def-weil-affine-spectrum], these become $\operatorname{Spec}$ of $A/I\to C_Z$ (as $\bar h$ is an $A/I$-algebra map), which is $j_Z$ on the chart, and $\operatorname{Spec}(\bar hq)=\operatorname{Spec}h^\#=h$. Thus $(j_Z,h)$ is an isomorphism, with no Noetherian or reducedness hypothesis and with nilpotents retained.
