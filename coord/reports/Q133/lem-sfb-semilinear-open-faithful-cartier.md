## Review by Q133

I checked each step against the statements of all its cited nodes and the sources above. In step 1, `def-weil-affine-spectrum` supplies contravariant functorial Spec, and `def-pc-fibre-product` supplies the unique map with its two projections. The order is essential: composing Spec(h inverse) after Spec(k inverse) gives Spec(k inverse composed with h inverse), which is Spec((hk) inverse), the left-action law. `def-weil-open-immersion` explicitly identifies the open's structure sheaf with the restriction; restricting both the continuous and sheaf maps constructs the morphism and its inverse. `def-weil-locally-ringed-space-morphism` gives the composition formula, and `def-weil-scheme-over-base` gives exactly the condition for being an e-morphism. Since H contains h inverse, forward stability gives equality of opens, not merely an inclusion. These are direct calculations from the definitions, not a separately hidden group-action lemma.

For step 2, `def-weil-residue-field` explicitly supplies the field map L to kappa(x) for a morphism to Spec L, and `def-weil-affine-spectrum` identifies the sole point's residue field with L. The locally ringed-space composition formula makes the equality of scheme maps give lambda = lambda composed with h inverse after passing to residue quotients. `def-weil-field` supplies the inverse of a nonzero element, and the written one-line contradiction proves injectivity; there is no unrecorded field-map theorem being assumed. Nonemptiness is used precisely to select x. Neither flatness nor integrality is used in this step. Faithfulness on L is the definition of a subgroup of its automorphism group.

Step 3 uses `lem-pc-flat-pullback-effective-cartier-ideal` with S=B, Y=X and g=u. I checked its statement, not its proof: it explicitly gives an effective Cartier ideal and the local formula `(t)A`. Its hypotheses are just a scheme, an effective Cartier ideal and a flat map, exactly as assumed here. This gives both effectiveness and the ideal identification, with no finiteness, normality, relative-divisor, properness or separability assumption. I record it as a generic tool in split_report; it is already Lean-closed.

In step 4 the same local formula reads off the support. The existing spectrum-map node's clause (1) explicitly says the image prime is the inverse-image prime. Thus phi(t) belongs to q exactly when t belongs to its inverse image, giving the required support equality without assuming anything about geometric fibres. This generic, Lean-closed tool is declared in split_report. The affine-spectrum definition supplies affine scheme notation; the effective-Cartier definition supplies the covering by principal generators, consistently with its current blueprint update. For invariance, u r_h = u ensures the inverse image of each chosen B-chart is stable. Pulling its ideal generator back yields exactly the same section by the cited locally ringed-space composition formula. Equality on this cover is equality of subsheaves, proving invariance of the ideal, rather than mere setwise preservation. This local generator argument is written out and is elementary.

I tested the statement on X=Spec L with B=Spec e, the trivial subgroup, a nontrivial automorphism of L, inseparable extensions (where the automorphism group can be trivial), disconnected or nonreduced nonempty opens, and the empty divisor with generator 1. In particular nonreducedness does not affect the residue-field injection, and a disconnected scheme still has the needed point.
