Use the canonical coefficient identification in [lem-per-rational-module-realization-functor]: the completed sheaf $B_X$ is the continuous-function sheaf $A_X$. ### Original step 2 — retained in lem-per-completed-continuous-hom-hypotheses

### Original step 3 — retained in lem-per-bounded-hom-localization-embedding

Apply [lem-weil-pbc-scalar-localization-derived-hom] on the small site $X_{\mathrm{proet}}$ with the commutative ring $A$, scalar $t=\varpi$, ring sheaf $R=B_X$ and $R_t=E_X$. The theorem therefore gives the natural isomorphism $\operatorname{Hom}_{D(B_X)}(K,L[q])[1/\varpi]\simeq\operatorname{Hom}_{D(E_X)}(F_XK,F_XL[q])$, with the specified image $f/\varpi^n\mapsto\varpi^{-n}F_X(f)$. In particular it is the actual scalar-extension comparison, with its shift comparison, rather than an unspecified isomorphism of the two modules.

Original cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-scalar-localization-derived-hom, lem-per-rational-module-realization-functor.

### Original step 4 — retained in lem-per-bounded-hom-localization-embedding

For completeness, the fraction formula gives precomposition and postcomposition by $a,b$ as $\varpi^{-n}F_X(f)F_X(a)$ and $F_X(b)\varpi^{-n}F_X(f)$, so gives naturality. Shifting gives $\varpi^{-n}F_X(f)[r]$ under the triangulated shift identification. The identity fraction maps to $F_X(1)=1$, and for composable fractions the formula gives $\varpi^{-(m+n)}F_X(gf)=(\varpi^{-m}F_X(g))(\varpi^{-n}F_X(f))$. These equations hold for every fraction. Finally [def-weil-pbc-bounded-constructible-complete] defines $\mathcal C_X$ as a full subcategory of $D(B_X)$, and [def-weil-pbc-rational-category] gives every object of $\mathcal R_X$ as $QK$ and its Hom modules as the localized integral Hom modules. The $q=0$ bijections of Step 3 are therefore exactly the Hom maps of $J_X$ specified in [lem-per-rational-module-realization-functor]. They prove that $J_X$ is fully faithful into the ambient $D(E_X)$.

Original cited statements: def-weil-pbc-bounded-constructible-complete, def-weil-pbc-rational-category, lem-per-rational-module-realization-functor.
