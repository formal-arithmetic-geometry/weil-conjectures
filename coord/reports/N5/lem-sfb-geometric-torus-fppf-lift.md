## STATEMENT-DISPUTED: global finite presentation over a non-quasi-separated base

The imported statement requires p to be of finite presentation over an arbitrary S. An affine restriction does not prove that: an affine open inclusion in a non-quasi-separated scheme need not be quasi-compact. In this problem the global finiteness assertion is actually false, even for a torus G of rank two.

Here is an explicit counterexample, including why a non-affine choice of S' cannot fix it. Let k be a field and A the ring of eventually constant sequences in k indexed by positive integers. It has the idempotents e_n supported at n. Spec A is the one-point compactification of the discrete points n: its additional point infinity is the kernel of the eventual-value map. Indeed, a prime either omits one e_n, giving evaluation at n, or contains all e_n, in which case it contains the finite-support ideal and is the prime of its quotient k. A principal open containing infinity contains all but finitely many n. Put W=union D(e_n), the non-quasi-compact open of discrete points. Glue two copies U,V of Spec A along W by the identity to form S, with distinct infinity_U and infinity_V.

Over each of U,V take the split torus of rank two. At the overlap point n glue these by the distinct integral automorphism matrices M_n=((1,n),(0,1)). Since W is the disjoint union of those open points, this is a genuine scheme gluing datum. It produces a torus G over S: smooth, affine and of finite presentation relative to S, all of which are local on the target. Take s=infinity_U and j_0 the standard geometric isomorphism of rank-two tori.

Suppose the imported conclusion held. Over P_U=p^{-1}(U) and P_V=p^{-1}(V), the closed immersion j from the split rank-two torus into G is an isomorphism and gives locally constant matrices A_U,A_V in GL_2(Z). These statements can be checked without a structure theorem: a group-like Laurent polynomial sum a_m x^m has pairwise orthogonal idempotent coefficients a_m, by comparing its comultiplication with its tensor square, and their sum is 1 by the counit. Thus locally each coordinate is one Laurent monomial. A closed immersion between split rank-two tori means the resulting lattice map is surjective, hence an invertible integral matrix. Since p is quasi-compact, P_U and P_V are quasi-compact; each locally constant matrix function therefore has finite image.

Flatness and local finite presentation make p open (the existing node lem-weil-pbc-flat-lfp-open). Its image contains infinity_U, so contains all but finitely many points n of U. At every preimage of such an n, the two frames satisfy A_V=M_n A_U, up to the harmless choice of the gluing convention. Consequently all these distinct M_n belong to the finite set of quotients of the two finite sets of frame matrices, a contradiction. This uses quasi-compactness of p, not affineness of S'.

Repair: over every S there is an affine open U containing s and an affine flat finitely presented S' over U with finite fibres, giving the prescribed j and residue embedding. The composite to S is flat, locally of finite presentation and locally quasi-finite, with finite fibres. If S is quasi-separated, U->S is quasi-compact, and the original finite-presentation and quasi-finiteness assertions hold over S as well. The corrected node states both conclusions. This keeps exactly the fppf-local content of Conrad's Proposition 3.1.9 proof. Its parent lem-sfb-reductive-torus-fibre-openness first restricts S to an affine neighborhood, so the conclusion used by that parent is preserved. No target statement was changed.
