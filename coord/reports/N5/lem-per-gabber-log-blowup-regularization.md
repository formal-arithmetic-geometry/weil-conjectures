Step 2 reads the statement of `lem-trf-strict-fan-chart-local-comparison`, which applies to arbitrary strict maps of Zariski log schemes and compares the actual chart maps after localization. Fan functoriality includes those localizations, isomorphisms and finite disjoint unions. The chart blowup node identifies pulled-back centers, and the universal property makes local identifications canonical. These check each of the independence, strict-etale functoriality, cocycle and finite-union hypotheses of `lem-trf-functorial-log-blowup-etale-descent`. Step 3 uses `lem-trf-coherent-log-ideal-chart-construction` exactly for its finite-type ring ideal, the saturated chart formula and the finite map to the fine log blowup. `lem-trf-log-regular-toric-refinement` applies because the saturated chart monoid contains P in P^gp and has the same group completion; its empty-ring case is allowed. Step 6 uses chart blowup's characteristic-stalk identification and the fan theorem's smooth endpoint. Smooth here means precisely free characteristic monoids. The free-characteristic regular-boundary statement applies because Step 3 has retained log regularity. It gives regularity of the scheme, rather than mistaking a smooth fan for a smooth scheme over a field.

Step 7 checks the stronger regular-locus clause. The regular-boundary criterion gives free characteristic at each regular point, and the sharp-chart node gives an etale neighborhood with that monoid as chart. `lem-trf-monoid-chart-characteristic-fan` identifies its sharpened chart with the fan S(N^r), including its stalks. From the definition of contraction, the fan rule on S(0) has length zero: every center there is whole, and the identity functoriality comparison identifies its tower with its contraction. Every stalk of S(N^r) is N^a, so its map to S(0) is smooth in the stated fan definition. The rule is therefore trivial on S(N^r) by functoriality. This proves that all centers are whole on the chart neighborhood; it is more than asserting resolution over the log-trivial locus. The same free-characteristic criterion makes the neighborhood lie over regular points, and the etale-open-map node makes its image open. These images cover exactly the regular locus; identity blowup stages glue there. No assertion that a nonexcellent regular locus is generally open was assumed.

Step 8 checks general strict, not merely strict-etale, functoriality. The local fan comparison's hypothesis is arbitrary strict and is applied after choosing strict-etale charts on both schemes. The fan rule compares the localized ordered centers and the chart pullback keeps them. Universal-property uniqueness gives the local comparison maps and their cocycle, so they glue as in the source's descent argument; deleted stages are exactly whole centers and their intervening identifications are identities. The same uniqueness verifies composition, including consecutive deletions, and covers automorphisms as a special case. This does not cite a statement giving only full faithfulness or only a terminal comparison to obtain tower data.

Step 9 has one genuine elementary tensor calculation, with the coherent-log-chart construction cited for the non-elementary chart existence and localization gluing. Under a strict chart map the two bases use the same monoid P and the same generators E. I wrote the maps a' tensor (a tensor z^q) to a'f(a) tensor z^q and its inverse, so the scheme pullback does not rest on the fs universal property alone. Both maps respect the monomial-localization overlap maps in the cited statement. They send alpha(e) to its pullback, proving equality of ordered ring centers as well. The normalized-blowup identification of Step 5 then gives the strict-transform statement of ILO VIII 3.4.6(ii), stage by stage.
