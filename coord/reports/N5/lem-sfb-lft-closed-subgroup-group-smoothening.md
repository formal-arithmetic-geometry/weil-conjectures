## Corrections and route

There is no statement dispute. The imported item had no proof, and its source had already distinguished universal smoothening from a Néron mapping property. The split preserves that distinction. BLR 7.1 Theorem 5 is a finite-type theorem over a DVR; applying it verbatim to the non-quasi-compact closed subgroup would be invalid. The proof instead exposes the actual local affine dilatation construction and the finite identity-defect calculation, then iterates them globally at the finite set of bad fibres.

The identity-section criterion proves a slightly more general auxiliary fact than the finite-type ambient-identity-component observation in BLR 10.1 Proposition 4. For a flat locally finite type group over a Noetherian base, the inverse image under the identity section of its open smooth locus is open; on a geometric fibre translations identify the behaviour at all geometric points. Consequently, if the generic fibre is smooth, the good base locus contains the generic point. On a Noetherian integral base of dimension one its complement is finite. This is a refinement of the source's group-translation argument, without changing the inherited route. It explains why the finite-type assumption on the ambient identity component is not needed a second time in the assembly. No hypothesis is removed from the item statement.
