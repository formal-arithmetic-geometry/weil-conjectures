### lem-trf-comonad-face-identities

Old step 1: Write $1_a$ for the identity transformation on $T^a$, including $1_0$ on the identity functor, and write $\star$ for horizontal composition. Thus $d_i^n=1_i\star e\star1_{n-i}$ and $s_i^n=1_i\star c\star1_{n-i}$. These are natural transformations because whiskering a naturality square by a functor again gives a naturality square. We use the interchange identity $(\alpha'\circ\alpha)\star(\beta'\circ\beta)=(\alpha'\star\beta')\circ(\alpha\star\beta)$. To verify it directly, define $(\alpha\star\beta)_x=\alpha_{G'x}\circ F(\beta_x)$ for $\alpha:F\to F'$ and $\beta:G\to G'$. Expand the right side at $x$, move $F'(\beta'_x)$ past $\alpha_{G'x}$ using naturality of $\alpha$ at $\beta'_x$, and use functoriality of $F$. The result is $(\alpha'\circ\alpha)_{G''x}\circ F((\beta'\circ\beta)_x)$, the left side. Horizontal composition is associative with these component formulas, and identities compose to identities; hence parentheses can be suppressed.

Old step 2: Face--face relations: for $n\geq1$ and $0\leq i<j\leq n+1$, expand both $d_i^n\circ d_j^{n+1}$ and $d_{j-1}^n\circ d_i^{n+1}$ and apply interchange from step 1. Each becomes $1_i\star e\star1_{j-i-1}\star e\star1_{n+1-j}$. Thus $d_i d_j=d_{j-1}d_i$. In the boundary case $j=i+1$ the middle identity is $1_0$, so the calculation still applies.
