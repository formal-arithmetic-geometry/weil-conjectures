## What was wrong

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the unqualified assertion that two isomorphic rational representatives have a canonical comparison omits the chosen identification. Already for f = identity on a point and K = K' = R in degree zero, the rational endomorphism alpha = c times identity, c in E^*, must induce c times identity, not identity. Two presentations a and a' of a common one-dimensional E-space give comparison (a')^{-1} a; changing a' changes it. Bare isomorphism classes do not fix that map. The corrected statement names alpha, or equivalently the two presentations, and says precisely what the canonical comparison is relative to them. If there is only existence of an identification, there is still existence of an output isomorphism. This makes explicit the source's intended representative-independence, without introducing a geometric or Tor hypothesis and without touching any target.

The original proof says that the derived limit is triangulated by its defining triangle. The statement of def-weil-derived-limit expressly defines a property of an object and triangle, not a functor. Neither functoriality nor an exact linear functor on coherent diagrams follows from that definition. The new prerequisite concerns the coherent finite-level compact-support tower, where the varying quotient coefficient rings must be handled before localization. Step 1 then uses the already existing strict-tower derived-limit theorem, so no functorial cone is asserted. Scalar linearity of a right derived construction is justified in Step 2 through the K-injective Hom bijection, not by assuming the chosen resolution functor is linear.

The imported proof_deps listed prop-weil-pbc-rational-transport without citing it in uses. Its first clause is phrased for the proper-base-change integral categories; those categories are not silently identified with this item's uniform-reduction-amplitude category. Step 4 writes the same generic tensor-Hom rational-transport calculation directly on the actual full subcategories, using their scalar-localization and module-base-change definitions. No off-unit theorem about geometric functor preservation or constructibility is substituted. The Roos definition and the finite-level triangulation lemma no longer stand in for facts they do not state.
