## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported sentence “Contraction deletes precisely the stages whose center is the whole monoid sheaf” is incorrect for arbitrary saturated towers on nonsaturated fine fans. Let $P=\langle2,3\rangle\subset\mathbf N$, $F=(\operatorname{Spec}_{\rm mon}P,\overline{\widetilde P})$, and take the whole ideal $I=P$, generated as an ideal by $0$. Then the sole unsaturated chart is $P[I-0]=P$, while its saturation in $P^{\rm gp}=\mathbf Z$ is $\mathbf N$. The saturated blowup is therefore $F^{\rm sat}\to F$, not an isomorphism: the closed sharp stalks are respectively $\mathbf N$ and $\langle2,3\rangle$, with one and two indecomposable nonzero elements. Deleting this stage would erase a genuine modification and could erase the smooth final fan.

The correction deletes whole-ideal stages only when their morphism is an isomorphism; it expressly retains a nontrivial initial saturation. This preserves the source's intended contraction of empty identity stages (ILO VIII 2.1.4 and 2.3.4–2.3.5), while distinguishing the saturated construction of 3.1.21 and 3.1.28. It does not delete every isomorphism: a principal proper ideal still records a center and is retained. Once the source of a stage is saturated, the extra condition on a whole-ideal stage is automatic. No hypotheses restricting all input fans to saturated ones were added. The finite-cover, torsion-free, smooth-final-fan and smooth-morphism functoriality clauses remain present. No target statement, other node, unit or chapter record was changed. Other changes make existing terminology explicit rather than changing it.

The current unit `sk-i-curve-zero` follows the contracting-correspondence route.
