For a point over a field every existing fibre point has dimension zero, so n=0 gives the full pullback. For the affine line over a field the dimension at every point is one (including its generic point), so the n=0 loci are empty and n>=1 gives the full spaces. Field extension preserves these values, whereas the generic-point stalk dimension would be zero; this checks the important distinction of notions. Nilpotents do not change the topological dimension.
