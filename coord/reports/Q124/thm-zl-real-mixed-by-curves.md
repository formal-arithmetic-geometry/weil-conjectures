### Returned Step 5; obligation: lem-zl-constant-extension-mixed-descent

Here is the constant-extension descent argument, valid on any separated finite-type base. Let $g:X_m\to X_0$ be a finite constant extension of degree $m$ and suppose $g^*\mathcal F$ is mixed for the $k_m$-structure. The comparison [lem-zl-constant-extension-weil-heart-equivalence] identifies this object with the corresponding $k_0$-Weil sheaf on the geometric disjoint union of $m$ copies of $X$, matching its subobjects, finite filtrations, local Frobenius operators and weight bounds. Thus the mixed filtration just constructed is an input in the $k_0$-constructible category used by the finite direct-image theorem. Choose an upper bound $w$ for its finitely many quotient weights (take $0$ for the zero object). The morphism $g$ is separated, quasi-finite and of finite type; its target is separated, hence quasi-separated, of finite type. The unit's quasi-finite lemma [sk-l-dl-general-quasifinite] therefore makes $g_!g^*\mathcal F=R^0g_!g^*\mathcal F$ mixed of weights at most $w$.
