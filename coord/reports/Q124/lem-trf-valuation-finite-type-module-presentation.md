## Full arguments assigned to the new workers

For lem-trf-generator-filtration-graded-module: choose the finite generator families specified in its statement. Every element of M is a finite sum of polynomials in b_i times m_j, so collecting their maximum monomial degree proves the union of F_d is M. Increasingness follows from the degree bound, and multiplication by b_i raises that bound by one. The coefficient-shift actions are A-linear and commute: two b_i shifts commute because B is commutative, and the plain shift commutes with each one. Extend them uniquely to the polynomial ring P. Finite formal support makes this an actual module, with disjoint coefficient degrees giving the internal direct sum and the indicated grading compatibility. For each spanning monomial b^alpha m_j t^d of degree d, with |alpha|≤d, the equality X_0^(d-|alpha|) X_1^alpha_1 ... X_n^alpha_n (m_j t^0)=b^alpha m_j t^d proves that the specified r elements generate. If 0≠a annihilates a finite sum in N, coefficient comparison says ax_d=0 in M for every d; torsion-freeness of M gives every x_d=0, proving torsion-freeness of N. This is the full construction and coefficientwise argument of old Steps 2–3, independent of the graded finite-presentation theorem.

For lem-trf-filtered-module-evaluation-presentation: the shift and endomorphism shifts commute and preserve the designated coefficient spaces by the hypotheses, giving the stated P-action. Evaluation of each generator action agrees with its action on M, hence epsilon is P-linear. For x∈M choose d with x∈F_d; epsilon(x t^d)=x proves surjectivity. If z=sum_(d=0)^D x_d t^d evaluates to zero, put y_d=-sum_(i=0)^d x_i for 0≤d<D. The inclusions F_i⊆F_d put y_d in F_d. Direct coefficient multiplication gives (t-1) sum_(d=0)^(D-1) y_d t^d=z; for D=0, z=0. Conversely evaluation kills (t-1)N. This proves the kernel assertion and the quotient identification, including each T_i action.

For its finite-presentation clause, set K=ker(pi) in a given P^u→P^v→N→0. It is generated by the u columns h_j of H. The preimage in P^v of (X_0-1)N is K+(X_0-1)P^v: one inclusion is direct; for the other, if pi(v)=(X_0-1)n, lift n to w using surjectivity and subtract (X_0-1)w. Thus M is P^v/(K+(X_0-1)P^v). ## Verbatim returned seven-step proof

Old Step 1: First $A$ is local. Indeed its fraction-field characterization [def-trf-valuation-ring] implies that any two nonzero elements $a,b\in A$ satisfy $a\mid b$ or $b\mid a$, by applying it to $a/b$. Thus its nonunits form a proper ideal: if $a,b$ are nonunits and $b=ac$, then $a+b=a(1+c)$ is a nonunit; the other divisibility and zero cases are identical, and multiplication preserves nonunits. Every proper ideal contains only nonunits, so this is the unique maximal ideal. Choose algebra generators $b_1,\ldots,b_n$ of $B$ over $A$ [def-weil-pbc-finite-type-algebra], and module generators $m_1,\ldots,m_r$ of $M$ over $B$ [def-weil-finitely-generated-module].

Old Step 2: For $d\geq0$ let $F_dM$ be the $A$-span of $b_1^{\alpha_1}\cdots b_n^{\alpha_n}m_j$ with $\sum_i\alpha_i\leq d$. Then $F_dM\subseteq F_{d+1}M$, $b_iF_dM\subseteq F_{d+1}M$, and $\bigcup_dF_dM=M$, by the chosen generators. Put $N=\bigoplus_{d\geq0}F_dM\,t^d$, viewed as the $A$-module of finite formal polynomials with degree-$d$ coefficient in $F_dM$. For the polynomial ring $P=A[X_0,X_1,\ldots,X_n]$ [def-weil-pbc-mv-polynomial-ring], let $X_0$ act by $t$ and $X_i$ by $b_it$. These commuting $A$-linear actions define a $P$-module, graded by polynomial degree. It is generated over $P$ by the $m_jt^0$: the spanning term $b^\alpha m_jt^d$ equals $X_0^{d-|\alpha|}X_1^{\alpha_1}\cdots X_n^{\alpha_n}(m_jt^0)$.

Old Step 3: The flat $A$-module $M$ is torsion-free by [lem-trf-flat-module-torsionfree-domain], since $A$ is a domain. If $0\ne a\in A$ and $a\sum_dx_dt^d=0$ in $N$, each $ax_d=0$ in $M$, hence every $x_d=0$. Thus $N$ is torsion-free as an $A$-module. Apply [lem-trf-valuation-torsionfree-flat] to deduce that $N$ is flat over $A$.

Old Step 4: Apply [lem-trf-local-graded-flat-module-finite-presentation] with coefficient ring $R=A$, polynomial ring $P$ with all $n+1$ variables of weight $1$, and graded module $L=N$ (zero in negative degrees). Step 1 gives locality of $A$, Step 2 gives the internal grading and finite generation, and Step 3 gives flatness over $A$. Hence $N$ is finitely presented over $P$.

Old Step 5: Evaluation $\epsilon:N\to M$, $\sum_dx_dt^d\mapsto\sum_dx_d$, is $P$-linear for the action $X_0\mapsto1$, $X_i\mapsto b_i$ on $M$, and is surjective because $\bigcup_dF_dM=M$. Its kernel is $(X_0-1)N$: if $\sum_{d=0}^Dx_d=0$, put $y_d=-\sum_{i=0}^dx_i\in F_dM$ for $0\leq d<D$. Direct multiplication gives $(t-1)\sum_{d=0}^{D-1}y_dt^d=\sum_{d=0}^Dx_dt^d$. For $D=0$ the kernel element is zero. Conversely evaluation kills $(t-1)N$. Therefore $N/(X_0-1)N\cong M$, with the stated action of every variable.

Old Step 6: Write the finite presentation from Step 4 as $N=P^v/\langle h_1,\ldots,h_u\rangle_P$, using [def-sc-finitely-presented-module]. Put $Q=A[X_1,\ldots,X_n]$. Evaluation $X_0=1$ identifies $P/(X_0-1)$ with $Q$ by the polynomial universal property [def-weil-pbc-mv-polynomial-ring]. Quotienting $N$ by $(X_0-1)N$ gives $P^v/(\langle h_1,\ldots,h_u\rangle_P+(X_0-1)P^v)$, which is $Q^v/\langle h_1(1,X),\ldots,h_u(1,X)\rangle_Q$: the coordinate quotient map has kernel $(X_0-1)P^v$, and sends the relation span onto exactly that evaluated span. Step 5 identifies this quotient with $M$. Thus $M$ is finitely presented over $Q$.

Old Step 7: The map $Q\to B$, $X_i\mapsto b_i$, is surjective by the algebra generators of Step 1. In particular $B$ is of finite type as a $Q$-algebra (no further generators). The $Q$-action on $M$ in Step 6 is restriction of its given $B$-action, as checked on each $X_i$ in Step 5. Apply [lem-trf-finitely-presented-over-subring] to $Q\to B$ and $M$: finite presentation over $Q$ from Step 6 implies finite presentation over $B$.
