## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported statement says that the chart maps' base change to S is the original morphism. If this means that the entire base-changed stage morphism IS f, it is false. Let k be a field, A=k[x_1,x_2,...], I=(x_1,x_2,...), S=Spec(A), and X=Spec(A/I)=Spec(k), with f the closed immersion. Both schemes are affine qcqs and f is of finite type, but f is not of finite presentation: I is not finitely generated. Indeed any finite list of polynomials involves only finitely many variables; after setting those variables to zero, a new variable survives, so it is not in the ideal they generate. On the other hand any morphism between schemes of finite type over Z is of finite presentation (locally finite type over a locally Noetherian target, with the qcqs properties), and its base change is finitely presented. Thus f cannot be the entire base change of a finite-stage morphism.

The faithful intended reading is given by `def-nagata-relative-stage-morphism-data`: the compatible square induces an IMMERSION into the base change, and composition with the projection to S is f. The corrected item spells out that equality and that comparison. It preserves every finite record and immersion clause, and explicitly prevents the false isomorphism reading. It also specifies that finite generator/relation presentations belong to the Noetherian stage charts, not to the arbitrary finite-type chart algebras of X/S and not to the immersion's kernel. This is a correction of a node's ambiguity/false literal reading, not a change to any target or unit statement.

The chapter and unit route are unchanged.
