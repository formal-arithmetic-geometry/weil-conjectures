## Takeover argument for the new node

For a degree-r closed graded A-linear map f:P→N, construct a degree-(r−1) h with δh=f, inductively along the split filtration. Start with h=0 on F_0=0. A graded splitting and the free shifted quotient give homogeneous lifts e of its basis, with de in F_n. Assume δh=f on F_n. Then w=f(e)+(-1)^(r−1)h(de) is closed: df(e)=(-1)^r f(de), while d h(de)=f(de), so the two terms cancel. Choose h(e) with d h(e)=w using acyclicity of N, and extend h graded A-linearly over the new free generators. The Leibniz rules imply δh=f on their full A-spans and preserve the previously chosen h on F_n. Induction produces compatible h on all F_n and exhaustivity gives h on P. Thus every degree-r cocycle is a boundary for every r. This is a constructive verification of the strengthened Hom-complex formulation of 09KM; the node itself remains statement-only for its own future split.
