## New nodes

`lem-sfb-truncated-coordinate-convolution-pairing`: statement; status `unsplit`; zero Mathlib leaves. It defines all objects and all bialgebra axioms explicitly, cites the field, tensor and convolution-algebra nodes, and assumes a prime characteristic and q=p^r. Its source is explicitly an agent algebra argument, using the distribution construction of Milne Chapter 10 as background rather than claiming Milne states this exact lemma.

Proof retained here for the next worker: write D=n(q-1), omega=product t_i^(q-1), and let delta_a be the coefficient dual of t^a for 0<=a_i<q. The counit identities imply Delta(t_i)=t_i tensor 1 + 1 tensor t_i + R_i, where every monomial of R_i has positive degree in both tensor factors. Therefore Delta(omega) has no terms of total degree below D. Its degree-D part is product (t_i tensor 1 + 1 tensor t_i)^(q-1). It follows that L(delta_a delta_b)=0 if |a|+|b|<D; when the sum equals D the value is zero unless a+b=(q-1,...,q-1), in which case it is product binomial(q-1,a_i). In characteristic p these binomial coefficients equal (-1)^a_i and are nonzero: multiply sum_{j=0}^{q-1}(-X)^j by 1+X and use (1+X)^q=1+X^q. For a nonzero h=sum c_b delta_b, choose a nonzero coefficient with MAXIMAL |b| and take a=(q-1,...,q-1)-b. Terms with smaller degree pair to zero, and among terms with the same degree only the selected b pairs nontrivially. Hence L(delta_a h) is nonzero. Switching the factors proves the other nondegeneracy. This uses only a finite coefficient expansion, not the theorem that finite Hopf algebras have one-dimensional integrals.
