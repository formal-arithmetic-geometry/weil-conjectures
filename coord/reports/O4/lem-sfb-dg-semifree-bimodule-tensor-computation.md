## Argument for the new categorical node

For each object Y of D choose P_Y in the class and an isomorphism u_Y:Q(P_Y) to Y, using essential surjectivity and (i). Set L(Y)=F(P_Y). For a:Y to Z there is a unique lift a~:P_Y to P_Z whose image under Q is u_Z inverse composed with a composed with u_Y, by (ii). Define L(a)=F(a~). Uniqueness of lifts proves identities and composition. For X in C let t_X:P_(QX) to X be the unique map with Q(t_X)=u_(QX). Define epsilon_X=F(t_X). The same lifting uniqueness gives t_X' composed with the lift of Q(f) equal to f composed with t_X, hence epsilon is natural. If X=P is in the projective class, lift u_(QP) inverse to v:P to P_(QP). Applying the two injective Hom maps in (ii) to t_P v and v t_P proves they are identities. Thus epsilon_P is an isomorphism.

For G:D to H and alpha:GQ to F, set beta_Y=alpha_(P_Y) composed with G(u_Y inverse). Naturality follows from Q(a~)=u_Z inverse a u_Y and naturality of alpha. Naturality of alpha at t_X gives epsilon_X beta_(QX)=alpha_X. Conversely, naturality of beta at u_Y, together with L(u_Y)=F(t_(P_Y)), forces this formula for beta_Y. Thus beta is unique and gives exactly the displayed bijection on natural transformations. This proves existence of the universal pair. Any two such pairs have a unique isomorphism compatible with epsilon (apply the bijection to their two counits and use uniqueness for the identity transformations). Consequently invertibility on the class also holds for every universal pair. No limit over a potentially large comma category is required. All choices range over sets after the stated universe enlargement.

This is an agent-written projective-representative variant of Stacks 06XN, not a claim that the literal statement of 06XN already says the new generality. I read its left-derived criterion in `derived.tex`, label `lemma-find-existence-computes`, and the explicit property-(P) application in 09LS. It is strictly smaller categorical infrastructure than the item: it mentions no DG algebra, tensor, bimodule, filtration, or B-action.
