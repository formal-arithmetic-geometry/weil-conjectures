The item requests det(d) on [E0→E1], so step 1 uses the sign-free degree-reindexing equivalence, which preserves quasi-isomorphisms and is inverse to backwards reindexing. This avoids silently replacing det(d) by (-1)^r det(d). On [k --1--> k] for characteristic different from 2 this distinction is already visible.
