## Mathematical argument

The completed primitive is used, not merely the fact that the cocycle has small norm. First reduce the pair (z,b) simultaneously to metrizable parameter and group quotients. For each parameter the Baire theorem gives one coset rectangle on which the homogeneous cocycle takes values in a finite subfield. Countability gives closed certificate sets A_j covering S. They need not be open, so choosing one certificate continuously is not legitimate.

Use finite clopen partitions to embed S in a countable product of finite discrete sets. The exact Mathlib retraction theorem yields a retraction rho_j:S -> A_j for each nonempty certificate set. The same partitions give locally constant finite-choice approximations k_i(s) which eventually stabilize, separately for each s, to its first valid certificate.

Finite-field contractions extend from Omega to its completion because their targets are complete. Choose a rapidly growing sequence of finite fields so that the projections approximate both compact images z and b faster than all the first i rectangle-operator norms grow. Write e_i for the projected cocycle differences and c_i for the projected primitive differences, so dc_i=e_i. Algebraicity gives pointwise eventual zero of e_i; no such claim is made for c_i.

For certificate j and sufficiently late i, define v=c_i-T_j e_i. On A_j its differential is zero. Therefore T_j e_i+v-v(rho_j(s)) is a primitive of e_i everywhere and equals T_j e_i on A_j. Use j=k_i(s). Its norm tends uniformly to zero. At each fixed parameter, j eventually stabilizes to a valid certificate, and the primitive terms become a fixed finite sum of evaluations of e_i. They eventually vanish at every fixed group tuple. The primitive series converges uniformly in the completion but is pointwise a finite sum in Omega; hence it is continuous into Omega. The finite cochain differential commutes with this limit and telescopes to z.

This separates the two indispensable properties: uniform convergence establishes continuity; pointwise finiteness puts each value in the original noncomplete field. No completion of Omega is silently identified with Omega, and no closure of coboundaries, vanishing theorem or derived-invariants comparison is substituted.
