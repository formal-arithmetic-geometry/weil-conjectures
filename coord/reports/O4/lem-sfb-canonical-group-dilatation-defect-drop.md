For the new closure node I read 3.3 Lemma 4 on p.67 and its schematic-density explanation immediately before it. I stated separately the field-theoretic extension used implicitly in 7.1: a Galois-stable family is dense after extension to the separable closure, not merely as a set of underlying points over k. Locally its ideal is the intersection of evaluation kernels. Coefficients of any finite collection of equations lie in a finite separable extension, where Galois saturation supplies all embeddings. Faithful flatness then identifies the base-changed vanishing ideal. Reducedness persists after extensions by the separability and coefficient-basis test; tensoring the injective evaluations on two factors proves the finite-product density, and iteration gives all m.
