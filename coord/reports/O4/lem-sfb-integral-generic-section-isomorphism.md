For y in W, s(f(y)) also lies in W and has the same f-image; injectivity of the restricted isomorphism implies s(f(y))=y. Thus the section image contains W. Step 3 uses the statement of lem-weil-pbc-reduced-source-factors-through-closed-subscheme, read in full: a morphism from a reduced scheme factors through a closed immersion exactly when its image is contained in the immersion's image. Reducedness follows from the explicit integral equivalence, and image containment is Step 2. The resulting t satisfies s∘t=id_Y, and the displayed associativity calculation t=(f∘s)∘t=f shows t=f as scheme morphisms, proving both inverse identities. Empty schemes are excluded by integrality and nonempty V, so density is never applied to an empty W. Dropping the open-isomorphism hypothesis would admit P1 over a field with a rational section of the structure map, which is not an isomorphism.
