Step-by-step citation audit:

1. The finite-type-model statement gives exactly the dominant integral model and the two generic-fibre hypotheses used. The original base is Noetherian, hence qcqs. This is the only reduction to finite-type arithmetic geometry.
2. The Riemann--Zariski statement supplies valuation rings with fraction field the chosen separable closure and finite height. Base-change permanence is applied to the already semistable generic pair, not to the unprepared special fibres. The valuation node then applies. Spreading supplies the neighbourhood and the model isomorphism; none of these inputs is supplied just by a definition.
3. Compactness and the common-refinement clause give the finite covering model. Base-change permanence supplies the geometric conditions, and proper-fp-permanence(b) supplies properness of both maps. Generic density is justified by the explicit flat-module localization injection. I corrected an initially too-strong claim here: the common refinement maps to the quasi-models can enlarge their generic fields, so they are dominant generically finite, not necessarily birational.
4. The normalization-in-residue-field-extension statement, applied at the generic point with the trivial extension, gives integral normal normalization. The finite-type-Z domain normalization node gives finiteness on every affine chart. The finite-proper node supplies properness, and proper-fp-permanence supplies composition/base change. The semistable base-change node applies on each preimage open. No finiteness theorem restricted to varieties over a field was used for an arithmetic scheme.
5. The uniqueness node applies on normal integral intersections, giving pair isomorphisms over the original curve. Uniqueness proves the cocycle, not merely existence of some identifications. The scheme-gluing statement gives the curve and marking separately. Their morphisms agree on overlaps and their quotient ideals glue, proving the compatible closed marking.
6. The base-change node expressly includes locality for an open cover. Properness is checked by the three actual conditions in def-weil-proper-morphism, including universal closedness after any base change. The local isomorphism opens glue and their density follows from the flatness calculation of Step 3. This is the global construction; it is not an application of the item's own conclusion.
7. The proper-finite-fibre-neighbourhood theorem is applied to N over T at the generic point; T is locally Noetherian and that fibre is a finite separable field. Thus its statement genuinely gives the finite dense open. After base change, the generic finite etale algebra has a field factor. Its reduced closed closure is integral, proper and finite over the indicated open. Finite-type-over-Noetherian and finite-type-localization-excellent give exactly the Noetherian and excellent output hypotheses. Proper permanence and semistable base change retain the pair; dominance retains both generic isomorphisms. The affine density calculation is written out.
8. The marked dualizing degree node gives the actual line bundle and positive degrees on every normalized one-dimensional component of a geometric b-fibre. The generic fibrewise-ampleness node applies because b is proper, both schemes are Noetherian, and its fibres have dimension at most one. Its embedding statement is precisely the projective-bundle convention in the cited definition. Thus projectivity is derived from degrees, not quoted from Temkin 1.1.
9. Pair compatibility and the marking's generic isomorphism have already been constructed; the final base-change statement supplies every clause for an arbitrary new base, without assuming that base is Noetherian.
