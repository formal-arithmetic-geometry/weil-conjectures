## New node and edge cases

lem-sfb-sequence-tensor-off-diagonal-kernel is the algebraic kernel claim inside 088I, specialized to a constant family K. It concerns arbitrary tensors in the algebra of all K-valued sequences, independently of A, prime ideals, spectra, closure or diagonal intersection. It therefore neither restates the item nor isolates its conclusion.

The kernel statement was checked by the following argument, recorded here for its next worker while the node itself remains statement-only. The full-coordinate evaluation map B tensor_K B -> K^(N x N) is injective: write a tensor as sum_{r=1}^d u_r tensor v_r with the u_r linearly independent. Their coordinate row vectors span K^d, since a linear relation annihilating every row is a relation among the u_r. Hence finitely many coordinate evaluations recover each coefficient, and vanishing of all entries forces every v_r=0. If only off-diagonal entries vanish, every nonzero column of the evaluated matrix is a nonzero scalar multiple of a distinct coordinate vector. Such columns are linearly independent, but all columns lie in span(u_1,...,u_d), so at most d are nonzero. The matrix is therefore a finite sum of its diagonal entries times e_n tensor e_n; full-coordinate injectivity gives that equality already in B tensor_K B. The converse inclusion follows by evaluating e_n tensor e_n at unequal coordinates. Finite linear combinations and the ideal generated by these tensors coincide, since multiplying a diagonal coordinate tensor by any tensor scales it by its (n,n) entry.

Checks included K=F_2, arbitrary characteristic, zero tensor, one coordinate tensor, and a finite sum of diagonal coordinate tensors. A constant nonzero diagonal on infinitely many coordinates is not an algebraic tensor matrix of finite rank, so it is not a counterexample. For the item, the infinite-support assertion uses the countably infinite index set; replacing it by a finite set would destroy the argument, as expected. A zero ring A or a ring with finitely many primes cannot satisfy the hypotheses. Comparable distinct primes, nilpotents in A, and transcendental residue extensions cause no problem: all maps use residue fields followed by compatible embeddings, not maximality or finite-dimensionality. Pairwise distinctness is preserved in the statement, although the refined argument does not need it once the indexed residue maps are supplied.
