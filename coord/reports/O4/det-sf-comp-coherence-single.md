## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: read literally for arbitrary qcqs Y, the imported statement is false. Let k be an algebraic closure of a finite field of characteristic different from ell, take R = k[x_1,x_2,...], I = (x_1,x_2,...), Y = Spec R, and X = Spec(R/I) = Spec k. The closed immersion f:X → Y is proper and of finite type (R/I is generated as an R-algebra by the empty set). Take the compactification j = id_X and p = f. The affine scheme Y is qcqs. Take Lambda = F_ell, an admissible residue-field coefficient, and K = the constant sheaf F_ell on X in degree zero, which is constructible and has Tor amplitude [0,0]. The zeroth direct image f_*K has nonzero stalk precisely on V(I).

The singleton V(I) is not a constructible subset of Spec R. If its open complement were quasi-compact, its cover by D(x_i) would have a finite subcover, giving V(I) = V(x_1,...,x_m) after enlarging the finite index set. But the prime ideal (x_1,...,x_m) belongs to the latter closed set and not to V(I). Hence the complement is not quasi-compact. A constructible closed subset of an affine spectral space has quasi-compact open complement, so V(I) is not constructible. A sheaf with finite constant local models on a finite constructible stratification has constructible support: on each stratum its rank is locally constant, and the nonzero locus is clopen, hence constructible on the qc strata. Thus f_*K is not constructible and Rp_*j_! cannot have the claimed Dctf-preservation clause. This counterexample already occurs in the finite coefficient case; no SGA or adic subtlety is involved.
