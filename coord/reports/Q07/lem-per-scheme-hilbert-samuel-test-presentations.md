## STATEMENT-DISPUTED: transform conventions

The imported “scheme always takes scheme-theoretic strict transform” is ill-typed at a line product: strict transform in def-nagata-strict-transform is defined relative to a blowup center. BMT §6.2 uses strict transform at scheme blowup tests. The existing def-trf-hilbert-samuel-presentation instead prescribes ordinary pullback for exceptional tests, for both X and N. These are not equal as arbitrary schemes: in A² with boundary coordinates u,v, blow up (u,v) and take X=V(u²). In the u-pivot chart total pullback is V(e²), whereas its saturation by e is the unit ideal and the strict transform is empty. One must compare retaining-level loci, not identify the two schemes. The new formal certificate input supplies coefficient-parameter saturation at equality points; Step 6 uses this together with the auxiliary-pullback bound to compare the two conventions through test prefixes. The item's primary geometric scheme convention remains BMT's strict transform. The correction makes line products explicit and explains rather than suppresses the discrepancy with the existing definition. That definition is read-only to Q07 and is not modified.
