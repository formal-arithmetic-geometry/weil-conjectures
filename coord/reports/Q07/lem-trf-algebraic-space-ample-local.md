## Findings and corrections

The item was statement-only, with source 0D3C/0D36, not a completed split. The statement is correct and was retained. I read the chapter record, sk-i-curve-zero unit, the current main-20261015 locality proof, and the existing polarized-scheme descent parent. This item is a supporting geometric lemma beneath that historical ancestry, not the key statement of the trace-formula unit; no unit realization or route was changed.

The proof of 0D36 says one may replace its etale cover by an affine open. A single affine open need not remain surjective over the entire affine target: over a disconnected two-point target, an open over one point misses the other. I did not copy that reduction or silently assume it. I used the direct section-algebra proof written in 0D3C for the whole covering family, with 0D2Z and 01VJ, rather than introducing an affine special case of the item as a purported split.

An arbitrary scheme test T -> Y need not be etale. The starting definition of a line module explicitly describes restriction for etale maps only. Step 1 therefore constructs an auxiliary line module N on F x_Y T by descending the ordinary scheme pullbacks of M_i. It uses the existing invertible-descent lemma, and identifies N with the restriction of M only when T -> Y is etale. Thus there is no unlicensed identification of site pullbacks. Self-review added a missing explicit input for fibre products of algebraic spaces and base change of etale covering families, and added the existing invertible-pullback theorem to justify that the local N_i really are invertible. I also replaced the scheme-property convention aggregator in new statements by the actual definitions of quasi-compact and separated morphisms.

The only off-unit proof tools declared in split_report.tool_uses are the existing relative-Proj existence theorem, scheme etale-cover base change, and invertible pullback/tensor theorem. Each supplies its stated generic construction. In particular lem-sc-ample-proj-open was read and not used: it gives an unspecified graded ring and open immersion, not the canonical section algebra and compatibility needed here. Likewise the scheme-only open-immersion descent lemma cannot be applied before the source is known to be a scheme; the new space lemma explicitly gives representability.

## Review by Q07

I checked the item against spaces-descent.tex, Tag 0D3C (lemma-descending-property-ample), and spaces-divisors.tex, Tags 0D31 (definition-relatively-ample), 0D35 (lemma-descend-relatively-ample), and 0D36 (lemma-relatively-ample-local). The scheme hypotheses in the item mean ampleness over all affine opens, as in morphisms.tex 01VH, rather than absolute ampleness on a possibly non-quasi-compact source. The conclusion asks representability on every scheme test, not merely on the given etale atlas. Both clauses are preserved. The proof follows the section-algebra embedding argument of 0D3C, using only its etale base-change case, so the much stronger flat-base-change theorem is unnecessary.

For the seven new nodes I read the following source statements, with their hypotheses. Scheme-relative-ample-base-change is morphisms.tex 0893 (lemma-ample-base-change): the base change is arbitrary and the line module is already relatively ample. The omitted explicit quasi-compactness assumption in its affine-open formulation follows directly because every ample inverse image is quasi-compact. Scheme-ample-canonical-proj-criterion is morphisms.tex 01VJ (lemma-characterize-relatively-ample), (1) iff (5), together with 01VI (lemma-relatively-ample-separated). I retain quasi-compactness and separation for the reverse implication; I do not claim that an arbitrary open subscheme carrying a map to a Proj is ample. Space-qc-separated-etale-local is spaces-morphisms.tex 03KG (lemma-quasi-compact-local) and 03KM (lemma-separated-local); it asserts no scheme descent. Space-section-algebra-etale-base-change uses spaces-morphisms.tex 03M9 (lemma-pushforward), its preceding representable comparison, and spaces-properties.tex 03LX (lemma-pushforward-etale-base-change-modules). Its assumptions are quasi-compact and separated; etale restriction is the comparison actually needed, and the node explicitly includes evaluation compatibility and the Zariski interpretation on schemes. Space-line-module-proj-map is spaces-divisors.tex 0D2Z (lemma-invertible-map-into-relative-proj), including the generation locus and comparison in (3), with scheme base and scheme-source etale tests. Space-open-immersion-etale-local is the open-immersion case of spaces-morphisms.tex 03M4 (lemma-closed-immersion-local), also confirmed in spaces-descent.tex 041X: the source may initially be an algebraic space, and the output includes representability. Space-fibre-products-etale-covers uses spaces.tex 02X2 (lemma-fibre-product-spaces) and its input 04T9, and spaces-topologies.tex 041F (lemma-etale), (3). I checked that these assert closure in algebraic spaces and preservation of the covering family, not just existence of fibre products of schemes.

For step 1 I opened def-trf-algebraic-space, def-pc-module-pullback, lem-weil-pbc-invertible-pullback-tensor and lem-trf-algebraic-space-invertible-descent, as well as the two new inputs for fibre products and scheme ampleness base change. The first defines representability by every scheme test; the fibre-product node supplies algebraic-space closure and cover stability; the invertible-pullback theorem supplies invertibility and composition comparisons for the N_i. The descent node's statement gives both existence and full faithfulness, exactly what the step uses to construct N and identify it for etale T. Triple-overlap cocycles are pulled back from the actual restriction cocycles of M. This avoids assuming a scheme conclusion in order to construct N.

Step 2 uses the forward part of the canonical criterion only on the schemes X_i, then uses the space qc/separated locality statement on the given etale cover T_i -> T. The hypotheses for the next step are therefore proved before pushforward is used. Step 3 uses the section-algebra node for quasi-coherence and its evaluation comparisons, lemma-nagata-relative-proj-exists for existence as a scheme, and the canonical-map node for U and r. I read the existing relative-Proj definition and existence statement: degree zero need not equal O_T, and the theorem supplies a scheme even for this general graded algebra. The identifications of evaluation maps are degreewise restriction of sections; they are explicitly part of the new algebra statement, not extracted from another node's proof.

Step 4 applies the canonical scheme criterion to N_i, uses the canonical-map comparison for the fibre squares and equality of generation loci, and uses the scheme etale-cover base-change theorem for P x_T T_i -> P. The elementary part is only that a complement whose inverse image under a surjective covering is empty is itself empty. The final open-immersion descent uses the new space statement, whose conclusion says that X is a scheme when P is. Step 5 uses the scheme interpretation from invertible descent, the algebra comparison, the canonical-map comparison, and the reverse scheme ampleness criterion with quasi-compactness and separation from step 2. Taking the arbitrary test proves representability; restricting to etale tests and then the identity of scheme Y gives exactly the other clauses. Every cited statement appears verbatim in citations.json.

I tested the statements on empty sources and empty bases, on identity covers, on an infinite covering family, on non-quasi-compact bases, and on an affine scheme with the trivial bundle. An empty cover is jointly surjective only over the empty space, and the empty Proj/source case gives the empty open immersion. No reduction to a finite cover or a single affine member is used; the construction is a quasi-coherent graded algebra over the entire scheme test. I also checked a nontrivial line bundle on projective space: the canonical criterion uses all tensor powers and does not assume degree-one generation. Nothing in the argument assumes Noetherian, finite type, projective, or proper. A general algebraic space with an etale scheme atlas would not suffice: its local arbitrary line bundle is not assumed ample, so the new open-immersion input alone is not a disguised scheme-descent assertion.
