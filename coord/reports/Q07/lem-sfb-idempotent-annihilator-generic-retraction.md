## Findings and changes

There is no statement dispute. The imported item was statement-only. I supplied the algebraic comparison which the stronger TIC discussion does not by itself prove under these hypotheses. Specifically, localization at a minimal prime shows that membership of a is complementary to membership of its annihilator idempotent. Membership of any idempotent is invariant along a prime inclusion.
