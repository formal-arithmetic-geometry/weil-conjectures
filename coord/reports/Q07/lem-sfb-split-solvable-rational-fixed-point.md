## Argument reserved for the new node's future split

This paragraph is explanatory report material, not a proof in its unsplit JSON. For n in N and g in G, n(g y)=g((g^{-1}ng)y)=g y on every extended test algebra when y is universally N-fixed. The representing property of X^N therefore restricts a to c:G×Y→Y. Since q has kernel N, G×_Q G is G×N by (g,n)↦(g,gn), inverse (g,g')↦(g,g^{-1}g'). Consequently c agrees on the two projections over Q×Y. The map q is an affine faithfully flat morphism (both groups are affine, and q is flat and surjective); it is an fpqc cover. Its base change q×id_Y is a cover. Representable descent, Stacks 023Q, gives b:Q×Y→Y. The unit law follows after composing the unit of G with q; the associativity law follows after the two successive base changes of q covering Q×Q×Y. Uniqueness of descended maps gives both the action identity and uniqueness of b. This argument never asserts that G(A)→Q(A) is surjective for the original algebra A. It works with infinitesimal kernels, nonreduced Y and empty Y. It is a genuine reusable lemma with no properness, splitting, or rational-point-existence hypothesis.
