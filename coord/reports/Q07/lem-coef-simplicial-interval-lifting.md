For the next worker on the pullback node, a proof route is as follows (this sketch is deliberately outside that unsplit node). For an arrow u=(a,c) whose two components are equivalences, precomposition gives weak equivalences on each of the three mapping sets in the pullback cospan, because an inverse vertex and the paths expressing the inverse equations give homotopy inverses to those composition maps. The mapping map A→B is a Kan fibration. Right properness therefore makes the comparison of the two mapping pullbacks a weak equivalence. Apply this for each target object to obtain bijections on π₀. Surjectivity for the source produces a left inverse to u; injectivity for the target, after precomposing, makes the opposite composite the identity. The converse follows by applying the projections. The comparison-of-pullbacks argument, rather than an assertion that π₀ preserves pullbacks, is the non-elementary part to split.
