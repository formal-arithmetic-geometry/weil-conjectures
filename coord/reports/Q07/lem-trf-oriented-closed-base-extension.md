## Sources and checks of the auxiliary statements

For the general closed-complement node I read the construction of the finer site in 08LY, its essential image H times U = U, the definition in 08LZ/08M0, and the exactness/full-faithfulness facts in 08M1. Here is the agent verification of the universal-property formulation, rather than a claim that ILO states it. If a^{-1}U is initial, then for every H on E the sheaf a_*H restricts to the terminal sheaf on U: sections over V times U are maps from a^{-1}(V times U)=0 into H, hence a singleton. Thus a_* lands in the closed full subcategory, defining a_{Z,*}; the adjunction gives a_Z^{-1}=a^{-1}i_*, which preserves finite limits. This is the required lifted geometric morphism. Conversely any factorization kills U, since its image in the closed complement is initial. Full faithfulness gives uniqueness and the statement on transformations. Applying the same characterization to any E -> T' identifies a pulled-back closed complement with the complement of a^{-1}U. For modules, 00YV transports the set-sheaf operations; full faithfulness uniquely lifts addition and the constant-ring action. The image is modules restricting to zero on U. Preservation of finite limits and epimorphisms in 08M1 gives exactness, and full faithfulness gives the actual counit and the supported-object unit. 08M3 is the compatible ringed-topos version. The scheme example is precisely the sets clause 04CA(1), not merely the abelian-sheaf clause in lem-kun-04ca. Small cases: U=0 gives Z=T and identity extension; U=1 gives the degenerate closed topos and zero module extension; a product topos gives literal extension by zero in one factor. No points or finiteness hypotheses are needed by this node.
