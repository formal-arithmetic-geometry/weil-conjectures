## Findings and corrections

The existing item was unsplit. Its statement is true as written, and every clause is retained; there is no STATEMENT-DISPUTED. The new node is the general relative construction of HA Proposition 2.2.4.4 with its concrete edge criterion from Lemma 2.2.4.15(4). It does not state concatenation, its preservation by maps, or stability of active cocartesian edges. Those arguments are in the item. Thus the split does not delegate an item clause to a new theorem.

The construction argument identifies the envelope color fiber with the wide active subcategory on simplices, calculates its block tensor and empty unit, and derives functoriality by the inert-source edge criterion. In the stability argument the inert arrows point OUT of a concatenation to its blocks. I corrected an early draft that described these arrows in the opposite direction and omitted the justification that they are q-cocartesian. Step 3 now proves that fact by mapping-square cancellation and proves the converse needed for the lift diagram. Steps 4-5 explicitly construct the vertical comparison through a cocartesian lift, and test it on inert color coordinates. They do not assume that p_E is globally cocartesian: transport is used only after pullback to an inert base edge, where the operad supplies all lifts. The zero fiber is terminal up to equivalence, not required to have literally one vertex; this is stated accurately in the proof.
