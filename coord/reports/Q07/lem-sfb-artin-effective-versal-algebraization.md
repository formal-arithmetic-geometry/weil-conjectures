lem-sfb-artin-object-graded-approximation is Stacks 07XB, artin.tex label lemma-approximate, specialized to an affine Noetherian base. It does not assume a versal formal object and does not conclude that the resulting family is versal. It supplies a prescribed finite jet of the object and a separate associated-graded isomorphism. lem-sfb-complete-local-cotangent-graded-rigidity is the ring-theoretic content of 06GZ plus the dimension argument used in 07XH. It is independent of fibred categories, effectivity, G-rings and finite type algebraization. It states surjectivity from the residue and cotangent conditions, and injectivity when the graded dimensions also agree. Its future split can use the following argument: by Nakayama, cotangent surjectivity gives n=f(m)D; completeness then lifts representatives successively to prove f surjective, as in 06GZ. The resulting maps on every graded piece are surjective between finite-dimensional spaces of equal dimension, hence injective. If c is in ker f, then c lies in m and, inductively, vanishing of its degree-j initial form implies c lies in m^(j+1). Separation of C makes c zero. This explains why the additional graded hypothesis is necessary; the complete proof belongs below this new unsplit node, not inside its statement.

Both new nodes have no proof, proof dependencies or proof origin. Their statements introduce all rings, fields, maximal ideals, maps and the relevant meanings of locality and completeness.
