## Review by Q48

I checked the item against both blueprint copies: the hypotheses are a dense open of the affine line and a punctually pure lisse Weil sheaf; the conclusions are the same weight, unramifiedness at the new infinity, a Frobenius-equivariant quotient in degree one, and preservation of geometric irreducibility. In particular I did not add geometric irreducibility as a hypothesis for the construction. I read the cited node statements, not just their titles or their proof dependency lists; `citations.json` copies the portions used by every step.

Step 1 uses `def-weil-finite-field-fiber` for the actual subfields F_{q^m} inside the fixed closure. The nonempty basic-open choice, the polynomial root bound and choosing q^m greater than the degree give a rational point without assuming U_0 already has an F_q-point. This is an elementary construction, and is written in the step rather than made into a node. Deleting a single rational closed point leaves the generic point, so neither W_0 nor V_0 becomes empty. The constant extension has positive finite degree.

Step 2 uses `def-weil-weil-sheaf-complex` for the direction of the Weil isomorphism, `def-weil-frobenius-detailed` for geometric rather than arithmetic Frobenius, `def-weil-local-frobenius-trace` for the iterated local operator, and `def-weil-punctual-weights` for the equality defining purity. I checked that the local residue-field degree is e=[kappa(y):kappa(x)], not the constant-extension degree m in general. The equality F_y=F_x^e follows by grouping the local return iterates, and the norms and residue cardinalities are raised to that same e. Triangularization gives the eigenvalues of the power even for Jordan blocks. This is a direct calculation from the definitions, not an appeal to semisimplicity or to a weight-bound theorem whose statement concerns a different object.

Step 3 gives tau and its inverse as matrices, verifies the determinant, and defines E by pullback along the inverse of the restricted isomorphism. `def-pod-lisse-l-adic-sheaves` supplies the locally constant finite systems transported by this isomorphism; `def-weil-weil-sheaf-complex` supplies the Weil structure; `def-weil-punctual-weights` applies because the isomorphism identifies residue fields and local stalk operators. `rem-dl-a1-conventions` expressly identifies being unramified at infinity with a lisse extension to V_0 union infinity. The displayed extension is on exactly tau(U'_0), so that conclusion is supplied by the cited statement, not by confusing middle extension with a lisse extension. All the maps are defined over the new constant field. No second finite extension is needed for this explicit matrix.

Step 4 uses part (4) of `lem-wf-dense-open-pi1-surjective`, whose statement expressly includes irreducibility for underlying geometric lisse Q_lbar-sheaves, and part (1) describes its geometric group mechanism. The source curve is connected normal Noetherian and the smaller open is dense and nonempty: both are opens of A^1_k. Changing constants does not change the geometric fibre or geometric sheaf. The final coordinate map is an isomorphism, hence has an inverse equivalence on sheaves. I checked that this establishes geometric irreducibility only; the false stronger claim that arithmetic irreducibility always survives a constant-field extension is not used.

Step 5 uses `prop-sf-open-closed-triangle-all-stages` at Q_lbar, including its final Weil-equivariance clause; `prop-functor-package` supplies the localization triangle after Rf_!, composition, exact field transport and equivariance. These are separated finite-type schemes over k, with complementary open and closed immersions, so the stated domain hypotheses hold. `def-weil-ordinary-compact-cohomology` supplies the point-fibre interpretation, and the Mathlib leaf `lem-sf-derived-homology-long-exact` supplies the displayed degree-one segment in the underlying derived coefficient category.
