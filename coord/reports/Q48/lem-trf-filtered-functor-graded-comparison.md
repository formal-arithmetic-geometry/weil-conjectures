## Source error

Varshavsky §5.1.1(c), p.39, prints that the subcomplex truncation `t_{>=a}` is left adjoint and the quotient truncation `t_{<=b}` is right adjoint to the respective embeddings. These directions are reversed. The canonical maps and the triangle of §5.1.2(c) have the correct orientation.

For an explicit check, work with complexes of vector spaces over `F_2`, concentrated in cochain degree zero. Let `P_0` and `P_1` be the one-dimensional space placed in filtration degrees zero and one. Then `t_{>=1} P_0=0`, but every linear map `P_0 -> P_1` is filtration-preserving. Thus `Hom(t_{>=1}P_0,P_1)` is a singleton while `Hom(P_0,P_1)` has two elements; the asserted left adjunction fails. Also `t_{<1}P_1=0`, while `Hom(P_0,P_1)` has two elements, disproving the asserted right adjunction for quotient truncation. The actual universal maps are `Hom(L,t_{>=r}M) -> Hom(L,M)` for high-supported `L`, and `Hom(t_{<r}M,R) -> Hom(M,R)` for low-supported `R`. The new node states these correct bijections. This repairs a source typo and does not alter the item's statement or route.
