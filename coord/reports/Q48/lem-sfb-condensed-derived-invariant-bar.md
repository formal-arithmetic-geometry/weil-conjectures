### Original step 1 → assembly step 1; existing Grothendieck and injective-resolution nodes

Write $\mathcal M_A$ for ordinary internal modules and $\mathcal E_A$ for internal $G$-equivariant modules. Apply [lem-sfb-condensed-product-action-inflation] with the second group trivial: both categories are Grothendieck abelian, and trivial-action inflation is exact with right adjoint $\Gamma_G$. Thus $\Gamma_G$ is left exact. By [lem-sp-grothendieck-enough-injectives], $\mathcal E_A$ has enough injectives. Construct a resolution $N\to I^\bullet$, zero in negative degrees, by embedding $N$ into an injective, then embedding each successive cokernel into an injective. This gives the exact augmented resolution in [def-sp-injective-resolutions]. All sites and parameter powers of $G$ lie in the coefficient universes fixed in the statement.

Cited statements: lem-sfb-condensed-product-action-inflation, lem-sp-grothendieck-enough-injectives, def-sp-injective-resolutions.

### Original step 2 → assembly step 2; existing free-flat, exact induction and injective-adjunction nodes

For every profinite $S$, the free internal module $A[S]$ is the sheafification of $V\mapsto\bigoplus_{V\to S}A(V)$: a map out of that presheaf sends $e_f$ to the restriction along $f$ of the selected section at $S$, which is precisely the free-module universal property. It is therefore $j_{S!}A_S$, flat by [lem-pc-sites-modules-j-shriek-flat]. The same statement applies to $S=G^n$. Use the tensor-Hom adjunction of [lem-pc-sheaf-modules-tensor-closed]. If $U:\mathcal E_A\to\mathcal M_A$ forgets the action, its left adjoint is $L(T)=A[\underline G]\otimes_A T$ with translation on the first factor: an equivariant map is uniquely $[g]\otimes t\mapsto g f(t)$ for a map $f:T\to UJ$. The functor $L$ is exact by the flatness just proved, because kernels and cokernels of actions are computed on underlying modules. Consequently $UI$ is injective whenever $I$ is equivariantly injective: extend a map into $UI$ across a monomorphism by transposing it to a map into $I$ across its image under the exact $L$.

Cited statements: lem-pc-sites-modules-j-shriek-flat, lem-pc-sheaf-modules-tensor-closed, def-sp-injective-resolutions.

### Original step 3 → lem-sfb-condensed-injective-bar-rows

Let $P_\bullet\to A$ be [lem-sfb-condensed-internal-bar-resolution]. For any equivariantly injective $I$, consider the augmented complex of internal equivariant Hom objects from $P_\bullet$ into $I$. Its sections at $S$ are $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal E_A}(A[S]\otimes_A P_\bullet,I)$; in degree $-1$ they are $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal E_A}(A[S],I)$ with trivial action on $A[S]$. The cited resolution states that the augmented tensor complex is exact. Applying $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal E_A}(-,I)$ is exact: restriction across any image subobject is surjective by the definition of injectivity, while the kernel condition is the universal property of the quotient. Thus these augmented section complexes are exact for every $S$, hence the augmented complex of sheaves is exact. Its augmentation object is $\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_G(A,I)=I^G$.

Cited statements: lem-sfb-condensed-internal-bar-resolution, def-sp-injective-resolutions.

### Original step 4 → lem-sfb-condensed-injective-bar-rows

Identify the degree-$p$ internal equivariant Hom just used with $\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_A(A[\underline G^p],UI)$. Indeed in the induced coordinates of [lem-sfb-condensed-internal-bar-resolution], a map is determined by its value on $[1]\otimes[g_1,\ldots,g_p]$; equivariance gives its value on every $[g_0]\otimes[g_1,\ldots,g_p]$ by multiplying that value by $g_0$. This formula is valid after every profinite base change, so identifies internal objects and their maps, using the closed structure of [lem-pc-sheaf-modules-tensor-closed]. Deleting the first homogeneous coordinate contributes $g_1c(g_2,\ldots,g_{p+1})$; deleting an interior coordinate multiplies the adjacent $g_i,g_{i+1}$, and deleting the last drops $g_{p+1}$. Thus the cochain differential is exactly the alternating inhomogeneous nerve differential in the statement. For $p=0$ it is $m\mapsto(g\mapsto gm-m)$ and its kernel is precisely the invariant inclusion.

Cited statements: lem-sfb-condensed-internal-bar-resolution, lem-pc-sheaf-modules-tensor-closed.

### Original step 5 → lem-sfb-condensed-bar-total-augmentation

Put $D^{p,q}=\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_A(A[\underline G^p],UI^q)$ for $p,q\geq0$ and zero otherwise, with the inhomogeneous horizontal differential of step 4 and the resolution differential vertically. Equivariance of $d_I$ makes these commute. By step 2, $UI^\bullet$ is an injective resolution of $UN$, so the $p$-th column represents $R\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_A(A[\underline G^p],N)$ by [lem-sp-derived-injective-resolution-naturality]. The cosimplicial maps are realized simultaneously on this one resolution; thus this is a compatible model, rather than unrelated resolutions for the individual columns. Give it the total differential $d_h+(-1)^p d_v$ and finite-diagonal sums of [def-sp-double-complex-total]. Step 3 says that every horizontal augmented row $\Gamma_G(I^q)\to D^{\bullet,q}$ is a quasi-isomorphism from the row concentrated at $p=0$.

Cited statements: lem-sp-derived-injective-resolution-naturality, def-sp-double-complex-total.

### Original step 6 → lem-sfb-condensed-bar-total-augmentation

Let $K^{p,q}$ be $\Gamma_G(I^q)$ for $p=0$ and zero otherwise. The invariant inclusions give a double-complex morphism $K\to D$. Apply [lem-sc-first-quadrant-total-column-comparison] with the two indices interchanged, so its quasi-isomorphism hypothesis is exactly the row assertion of step 5. This proves $\Gamma_G(I^\bullet)\to\operatorname{Tot}D$ is a quasi-isomorphism. The transposed total convention is related to the original convention by multiplication by $(-1)^{pq}$ on $D^{p,q}$; direct substitution in the two differentials verifies this identification. Both complexes are first-quadrant, so each diagonal has only $n+1$ potentially nonzero summands. By [lem-sp-derived-injective-resolution-naturality], the source represents $R\Gamma_G(N[0])$. Inverting the displayed quasi-isomorphism in the derived category proves the required derived-object isomorphism, with the invariant inclusion in degree zero.

Cited statements: lem-sc-first-quadrant-total-column-comparison, lem-sp-derived-injective-resolution-naturality.

### Original step 7 → lem-sfb-condensed-bar-total-naturality

For a map $N\to M$, lift it to chosen nonnegative equivariant injective resolutions by [lem-weil-pbc-injective-resolution-lift-exists]. Postcomposition gives a map of all $D^{p,q}$ commuting with faces and augmentation. The normalized comparisons of [lem-sp-derived-injective-resolution-naturality] identify this map with the appropriate derived-functor maps. Two lifts are chain-homotopic by [lem-weil-pbc-lift-to-injective-unique-up-to-homotopy]. Applying the internal Hom functors gives vertical homotopies compatible with all faces; on the total summand $D^{p,q}$ multiply that homotopy by $(-1)^p$. Then the horizontal cross terms cancel and $dH+Hd$ equals the difference of the total maps. Consequently they give the same derived map. Apply the same argument to identity maps between two resolutions: the maps in both directions compose up to homotopy to the identity. This proves resolution independence and functoriality of the total comparison, with the normalized $R\Gamma_G$ map on the other side of the augmentation square.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-injective-resolution-lift-exists, lem-sp-derived-injective-resolution-naturality, lem-weil-pbc-lift-to-injective-unique-up-to-homotopy.

### Original step 8 → lem-sfb-condensed-bar-scalar-resolution-comparison

Let $A\to A'$ and let $N'$ be equivariant over $A'$. For every profinite $S$, $A[S]$ is flat by [lem-pc-sites-modules-j-shriek-flat]. Placed in degree $0$ it is K-flat [def-pc-kflat-complex]: for an acyclic complex $F^\bullet$ the total tensor complex is $F^\bullet\otimes_AA[S]$ termwise, and the exact functor $-\otimes_AA[S]$ preserves acyclicity. Hence [lem-pur-derived-change-of-rings] computes $A'\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}A[S]$ by the underived $A'\otimes_AA[S]=A'[S]$: maps from the underived extension to an $A'$-module $M$ are $A$-maps $A[S]\to\operatorname{Res}M$, i.e. maps of condensed sets $S\to M$. The same free properties give the underived identification $\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_A(A[S],\operatorname{Res}M)=\operatorname{Res}\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_{A'}(A'[S],M)$. Apply [lem-sfb-dc-hom-scalar-adjunction] with $K=A[S]$ to obtain
$$R\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_A(A[S],\operatorname{Res}N')\cong\operatorname{Res}R\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_{A'}(A'[S],N').$$
The cited comparison is normalized by evaluation and natural in both variables. Hence it commutes with precomposition in the parameter $S$ and with the action on $N'$, and therefore with every face and degeneracy for $S=G^p$. This gives the scalar-restriction identification of the derived bar diagrams.

Cited statements: lem-pc-sites-modules-j-shriek-flat, def-pc-kflat-complex, lem-pur-derived-change-of-rings, lem-sfb-dc-hom-scalar-adjunction.

### Original step 9 → lem-sfb-condensed-bar-scalar-resolution-comparison

For scalar restriction take an equivariant $A'$-injective resolution $N'\to J^\bullet$ and an equivariant $A$-injective resolution $\operatorname{Res}N'\to K^\bullet$, both nonnegative as in step 1. The monic quasi-isomorphism $\alpha:(\operatorname{Res}N')[0]\to\operatorname{Res}J^\bullet$ admits a complex map $\beta:\operatorname{Res}J^\bullet\to K^\bullet$ extending the second augmentation, by [lem-weil-pbc-lift-along-injective-quasi-iso]. It is a quasi-isomorphism since both augmentations are. Form the underived bar double complex on $\operatorname{Res}J^\bullet$. By the underived identification of step 8 it is the restriction of the $A'$-bar complex of $J^\bullet$, so its augmented rows are exact by steps 3-4 over $A'$ and exactness of restriction. Columns: $UJ^\bullet$ is an $A'$-injective resolution (step 2), so by [lem-sp-derived-injective-resolution-naturality] its $A'$-column represents $R\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_{A'}(A'[G^p],N')$; composing the restriction of this comparison with the step-8 isomorphism gives a map with the same evaluation transpose as the canonical map from the $\operatorname{Res}J$ column to $R\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}_A(A[G^p],\operatorname{Res}N')$, so that canonical map is an isomorphism. By naturality of the comparison along $\beta$, the bar map induced by $\beta$ is a columnwise quasi-isomorphism. By [lem-sc-first-quadrant-total-column-comparison] it is a total quasi-isomorphism. The invariant-augmentation square commutes term by term and both horizontal augmentations are quasi-isomorphisms by the row argument of step 6. Therefore its map on derived invariants is exactly the scalar bar comparison in step 8. For $N\to\operatorname{Res}N'$, compose with step 7. The homotopy argument of step 7 makes the result independent of $\beta$. No injectivity of $\operatorname{Res}J^q$ over $A$ is assumed.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-lift-along-injective-quasi-iso, lem-sp-derived-injective-resolution-naturality, lem-sc-first-quadrant-total-column-comparison.
