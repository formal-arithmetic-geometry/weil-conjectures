## Returned proof preserved verbatim

### Returned step 1

Since C is of finite type over k, it has a finite cover by affine opens $\operatorname{Spec}A_1,\ldots,\operatorname{Spec}A_m$ with each $A_i$ a finitely generated $k$-algebra. Each $\operatorname{Spec}A_i$ is a Noetherian topological space by [lem-wf-affine-noetherian-components], so the finite union $|C|$ is Noetherian by [lem-kun-finite-union-noetherian]; passing to complements, ascending chains of open subsets of $|C|$ stabilize. Hence the connected component T of c (nonempty, as $c\in T$) is open by [lem-sfb-noetherian-connected-components-open], and T is quasi-compact because every subset of a Noetherian space is [lem-sfb-noetherian-space-compact-subsets]. In particular T, with its open subscheme structure, is a quasi-compact open subscheme of the locally finite type k-scheme C, hence of finite type over k.

Uses: lem-wf-affine-noetherian-components, lem-kun-finite-union-noetherian, lem-sfb-noetherian-connected-components-open, lem-sfb-noetherian-space-compact-subsets.

Source: literature/stacks-project/more-morphisms.tex, Tag 057K, lemma-local-local-structure-finite-type, proof, paragraph beginning 'Let $T$ be the connected component of $Y_s$ containing $y$': 'As $Y_s$ is Noetherian we see that $T$ is open.'

### Returned step 2

The field k is algebraically closed in $\kappa(c)\cong k(u_1,\ldots,u_r)$: let $\alpha\in k(u_1,\ldots,u_r)$ be algebraic over k and nonzero, with minimal polynomial $x^n+c_{n-1}x^{n-1}+\cdots+c_0$ over k, so $c_0\neq0$. As $k[u_1,\ldots,u_r]$ is a unique factorization domain, write $\alpha=p/q$ with $p,q$ coprime polynomials. Multiplying the relation by $q^n$ gives $p^n+c_{n-1}p^{n-1}q+\cdots+c_0q^n=0$, so $q\mid p^n$ and $p\mid c_0q^n$; coprimality makes $q$ a unit, and then $p$ divides the nonzero constant $c_0q^n$, so $p$ is a unit; thus $\alpha\in k$. (For r=0 there is nothing to prove.) Since T is connected and contains c, and $\kappa(c)$ is the same computed in T or in C (T is open), the connected-point criterion [lem-trf-connected-point-geometrically-connected] applied to T and c gives that T is geometrically connected over k: $T_K$ is connected for every field extension $K/k$.

Uses: lem-trf-connected-point-geometrically-connected.

Source: literature/stacks-project/more-morphisms.tex, Tag 057K, lemma-local-local-structure-finite-type, proof, paragraph beginning 'Let $T$ be the connected component of $Y_s$ containing $y$': 'We also see that $T$ is geometrically connected over $\kappa(s)$ by Varieties, Lemma 04KV'; the algebraic closedness of k in the purely transcendental $\kappa(c)$ is written out here (the source leaves it implicit).

### Returned step 3

The open subscheme T of the smooth k-scheme C is smooth over k: the inclusion is an open immersion and smoothness (locally of finite presentation and formally smooth) is checked on affine opens of the source, so it restricts to opens. Hence T is geometrically normal over k by [lem-trf-smooth-geometrically-normal]: for every field extension $K/k$ every local ring of $T_K$ is a normal domain.

Uses: lem-trf-smooth-geometrically-normal.

Source: literature/stacks-project/more-morphisms.tex, Tag 057K, lemma-local-local-structure-finite-type, proof, paragraph beginning 'Let $T$ be the connected component of $Y_s$ containing $y$': 'Since $T$ is also smooth over $\kappa(s)$ it is geometrically normal, see Varieties, Lemma 056T.'

### Returned step 4

Let $K/k$ be a field extension. By step 1, T is covered by finitely many affine opens $\operatorname{Spec}B_j$ with $B_j$ finitely generated over k, so $T_K$ is covered by the finitely many affine opens $\operatorname{Spec}(B_j\otimes_kK)$ with $B_j\otimes_kK$ finitely generated over K, hence Noetherian rings [lem-wf-affine-noetherian-components]; so $T_K$ is a Noetherian scheme (locally Noetherian and quasi-compact). By step 3 every local ring of $T_K$ is a normal domain, so $T_K$ is normal, and by [rem-pc-regular-normal] (2) its irreducible components are pairwise disjoint open and closed subsets which are integral with their open subscheme structures. By step 2, $T_K$ is connected, in particular nonempty, so it has exactly one irreducible component, equal to $T_K$; hence $T_K$ is integral. As K was arbitrary, T is geometrically integral over k. Together with step 1, this proves the assertion.

Uses: lem-wf-affine-noetherian-components, rem-pc-regular-normal.

Source: literature/stacks-project/more-morphisms.tex, Tag 057K, lemma-local-local-structure-finite-type, proof, paragraph beginning 'Let $T$ be the connected component of $Y_s$ containing $y$': 'We conclude that $T$ is geometrically irreducible over $\kappa(s)$ (as a connected Noetherian normal scheme is irreducible, see Properties, Lemma 033M)'; applied to every $T_K$, with reducedness from normality.
