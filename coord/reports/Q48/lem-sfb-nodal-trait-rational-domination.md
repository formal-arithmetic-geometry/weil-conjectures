### Returned Step 1

A DVR is Noetherian, and $C$ is Noetherian by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian]. For an affine open with coordinate ring $D$, flatness over $A$ makes $D\to D[1/\pi]$ injective by [lem-trf-flat-module-torsionfree-domain]. If this open is nonempty then $D[1/\pi]\ne0$ (otherwise injectivity forces $D=0$); it is the coordinate ring of a nonempty open of the integral generic fibre, so it is a domain. Thus $D$ is a domain. Every nonempty open meets the integral generic fibre, so this fibre is dense and $C$ is irreducible; hence $C$ is integral. At a nonsmooth point use [lem-trf-nodal-family-etale-local-model]. Localizing its etale base at the closed point gives a DVR whose uniformizer is still $\pi$, by [lem-sbc-etale-trait-stalk]. Its nonzero smoothing parameter is a unit times $\pi^n$ with $n\geq1$; absorb the unit into one coordinate. It is nonzero because otherwise the local model has a generic node, contradicting smoothness of the generic fibre. The local criterion of [lem-sfb-dvr-nodal-chart-blowup-calculus] gives normality at these points, and gives regularity and normality on the smooth locus as well. This proves the first assertion about $C$.

### Returned Step 2

By [lem-trf-projective-nodal-family-geometry], the relative nonsmooth locus is closed and has finite fibres. It lies in the special fibre, since the generic fibre is smooth. Consequently it consists of finitely many closed points. Step 1 and [lem-sfb-dvr-nodal-chart-blowup-calculus] identify the nonregular points among them: precisely the points of thickness $n\geq2$. Blow up their reduced union. This is intrinsic, so it requires no choice of a branch at a nonsplit node. Flat base change of blowups [thm-nagata-blowup-flat-base-change] and the chart calculus show that it stays a flat nodal family, leaves the generic fibre unchanged, and replaces each thickness $n\geq2$ by regular charts and at most one thickness $n-2$. Blowups at the finitely many disjoint centers can equivalently be performed successively: over an open missing all the other centers the two constructions are the same blowup, and over the complement all maps are identities. At each round every remaining thickness decreases by two. The initial finite set has a finite maximum thickness, so finitely many rounds give $r:Z\to C$ with $Z$ regular. Each stage is H-projective over the affine trait by [lem-sfb-projective-affine-base-blowup]; composition is H-projective by [lem-trf-h-projective-composition]. Thus $r$ is H-projective, is an isomorphism on the generic fibre, and $Z$ is integral regular Noetherian.

### Returned Step 9

The proper map $h:Y\to C$ has reduced integral source, normal integral target by Step 1, and its unique generic point dominates that of $C$. Its fibre over the generic point of $C$ is the spectrum of the common function field, so its global functions are precisely that field.
