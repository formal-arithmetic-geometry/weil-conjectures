## Source error

Stacks 07UT omits P(0). On X = Spec(k), the property “F is nonzero” is invariant under isomorphism, closed under extensions and positive-sum descent, and satisfies its full-support hypothesis using G = O_X and G' = I for every nonzero ideal I. It fails on F = 0. This was already repaired in the imported item; I preserved the repair. For the empty space, the original hypotheses are vacuous and also cannot force P(0).

The displayed stalk equation in spaces-morphisms.tex, Tag 0DK1 (lemma-stalk-push-closed), transposes the subscripts: i_*F is a sheaf on X, so its stalk is at the image x, whereas F is a sheaf on Z, so its stalk is at z. The new closed-coherent node states the resulting correctly typed support identity. No alteration of the item's mathematical statement is necessary.

## Proof details retained for the unsplit inputs

For the topology input I read spaces-properties.tex 04ZF, whose proof descends Noetherianity along the open surjective atlas point map; it gives precisely the induction on closed subsets. It is not the scheme-only Mathlib IsNoetherian.noetherianSpace.

For the ideal filtration I read all of spaces-cohomology.tex 07UR and the preceding 07UP/07UQ arguments. The proof chooses a minimal bad support, separates a reducible support using ideal powers, kills a power of the reduced-support ideal, then embeds a positive direct sum of a nonzero ideal on the reduced irreducible support. Its cokernel has smaller support. Splicing those filtrations terminates the contradiction; splitting the finite ideal sum gives the stated individual ideal factors. Omit any zero factor. Every factor is a subquotient of F, so its support is contained in Supp(F). A nonzero ideal on a reduced irreducible space has full support, as can be checked on the dense integral scheme open and reduced etale charts. This accounts for the support refinements in the new statement. The F = 0 case has the empty filtration.

The first dry run found the newly landed lem-sfb-space-closed-immersion-quasi-coherent-modules (04CJ). I read it in full, reused it for exactness, and removed exactness and full faithfulness from my new closed-coherent node so that it now supplies only Noetherianity, coherence preservation, and support.

For closed coherent modules I read spaces-morphisms.tex 04CJ, 0DK1 and spaces-cohomology.tex 08AM/07UH, including their proofs. Direct image on quasi-coherent modules is exact and fully faithful, coherent direct images are coherent, and on the closed image its geometric stalk is the source stalk; it vanishes on the open complement. These give the support formula. Additivity and finite direct sums are also included explicitly. The closed subspace is locally Noetherian by 06ED and 04ZK; its quasi-compactness and quasi-separatedness follow by restricting the closed immersion and diagonal on scheme charts. Hence it is Noetherian, as needed when applying the comparison lemma on Z.

For the generic comparison I read the last paragraph of 07UT and the statements and proofs of 07UQ, 07UM, 07UK and 07UL. On a common nonempty open the two full-support coherent modules have positive finite ranks r_H and r_G, so G^{r_H} and H^{r_G} are isomorphic there. Choose the ideal I of the complement and extend that isomorphism to I^n G^{r_H} using 07UM. Its coherent kernel K is supported on the complement and is killed by I^d by 07UK. Artin-Rees 07UL gives, for large m, K intersect I^m(I^n G^{r_H}) = 0. Restrict the map to I^{n+m}G^{r_H}; it is injective and still an isomorphism on the open. Set J = I^{n+m}, which is nonzero because its restriction to that nonempty open is the unit ideal. This lemma mentions no P, good subobject, or global extension argument; it is a genuine geometric input, not a restatement of the item.

For the coherent abelian input I read 07UC/07UB. Kernels and cokernels of coherent maps and extensions are coherent; finite sums are split extensions. The parent must cite this: the existing subquotient/support lemma by itself does not justify coherence of an arbitrary cokernel without first establishing quasi-coherence. I added this fifth input during self-review to close that gap.
