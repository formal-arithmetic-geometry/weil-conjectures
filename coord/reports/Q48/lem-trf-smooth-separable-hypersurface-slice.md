### Old step 1

Put $k=\kappa(s)$ and $L=\kappa(x)$. By [lem-weil-pbc-smooth-differentials-free-rank-fibre-dimension], choose affine opens $W=\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq X$ and $V=\operatorname{Spec}B\subseteq S$ about $x,s$, with $f(W)\subseteq V$, such that $\Omega_{A/B}$ is free of rank $d$. The map $W\to S$ is smooth by [lem-pc-open-immersion-smooth-composition]. It is smooth as a map to $V$ as well: local finite presentation uses the same target affine neighbourhoods, and a lifting square over $V$ is a lifting square over $S$, whose lift is still over $V$ because $V\to S$ is a monomorphism. Now [lem-weil-pbc-smooth-ring-map-iff-smooth-affine] makes $B\to A$ smooth. In particular $A$ is finitely presented over $B$. Set $C=k\otimes_B A$. By [lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra] and [lem-weil-pbc-open-fibre-product], $W_s=\operatorname{Spec}C$ is the open of $X_s$ above $W$. Let $\mathfrak q$ be its point corresponding to $x$, and put $R=C_{\mathfrak q}$, $\mathfrak m=\mathfrak qR$. By [lem-weil-pbc-affine-open-stalk-localization], $R=\mathcal O_{X_s,x}$. Its residue field is $L$: [lem-sbc-scheme-fibre-stalk-quotient] identifies $R$ with $\mathcal O_{X,x}/\mathfrak m_s\mathcal O_{X,x}$, and this ideal is inside $\mathfrak m_x$, so passage to the residue field gives $\mathcal O_{X,x}/\mathfrak m_x$. The algebra $C$ is finite type over $k$, since the images of any finite algebra-generating set of $A/B$ generate $C/k$.

### Old step 2

$$E\cong\Omega_{C/k}\otimes_C L\cong\Omega_{A/B}\otimes_A L\cong L^d.$$
For the first identification $(\omega/t)\otimes\lambda$ maps to $\omega\otimes\bar t^{-1}\lambda$, with inverse $\omega\otimes\lambda\mapsto(\omega/1)\otimes\lambda$; the middle identification cancels the $C$ tensor factor by $(c\otimes\omega)\otimes\lambda\mapsto\omega\otimes\bar c\lambda$. ### Old step 3

The extension $L/k$ is finite separable by hypothesis, so $\Omega_{L/k}=0$ by [lem-trf-separable-field-differentials-vanish]. Hence $\delta$ is surjective. This is the needed local equation, obtained from the full conormal exact sequence rather than merely from vanishing of $\Omega_{L/k}$.

### Old step 8

It remains to compute the dimension of this particular cut. The affine fibre description in step 5 and the final principal localization give
$$\mathcal O_{D_s,x}\cong R/(\bar h).$$

Further restriction to the open $U$ changes neither local ring. The ring $R$ is Noetherian local, and $\bar h\in\mathfrak m$ is a nonzerodivisor by steps 3--4. Therefore [lem-weil-pbc-dimension-quotient-nonzerodivisor] gives $\dim(R/(\bar h))+1=\dim R$. Its residue field is still $L$, since $(R/(\bar h))/(\mathfrak m/(\bar h))=R/\mathfrak m$. Apply [lem-kun-dimension-formula] to the finite-type $k$-schemes $(U_0)_s=\operatorname{Spec}C_0$ and $Z_s=\operatorname{Spec}(C_0/(a_s))$. The extension $L/k$ is finite algebraic, so its transcendence degree is zero: an algebraically independent nonempty set would contain a transcendental element. The open-fibre identification [lem-weil-pbc-open-fibre-product] and [lem-weil-pbc-dimension-at-point-open-subspace] identify these with $\dim_x(X_s)=d$ and $\dim_x(D_s)$.
