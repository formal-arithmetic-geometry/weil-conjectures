and `Mathlib.LinearAlgebra.TensorProduct.RightExactness`.

The intermediate quotient is explicit: `J_k` is generated by every original
`u_l=f_l-pi*z_l`, but only the chosen chart's `p_kl`. This differs from the existing
`PopescuLiftPartialChart.chartIdeal`, which retains only the original equations indexed
by `E_k`. We introduce no duplicate public definition: the ideal and its ULift quotient
are ordinary local `let` expressions in the statements and proof.

The proof extends the generator identities `hc` by polynomial extensionality and uses
`hs` to show that `s` and the canonical chart coefficient differ by a multiple of pi.
Their tensor images are equal because B kills pi. It constructs the quotient map from
the single-chart quotient to the full lifting algebra and proves it surjective.
The relations statement and ideal-span induction show that the full defining ideal
vanishes in the source localization. Tensor-product right exactness then identifies
and kills the entire kernel of the base-changed quotient map. The proved private
helper `Algebra.localized_surjection_bijective` converts kernel vanishing into
injectivity after localization and proves surjectivity there. Finally the proof
composes this equivalence with the single-chart polynomial equivalence.
No ring, algebra, map, or inverse is chosen from an unproved existence theorem in
a definition. The nonempty equivalence is eliminated only inside a theorem proof.

Updated the item's node proof and proof dependencies to this actual route. Added
one module and one node for each remaining calculation. No existing module other
