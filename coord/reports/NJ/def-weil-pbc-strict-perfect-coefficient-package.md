## Definition and elementary consequences

This is a definition, not a proposition: stages/1-dag.md §3(2) requires statement dependencies and no theorem proof. Its package data are exactly the displayed ranks, maps and splitting equations, with the bounded representative as input. There is no chosen unspecified object and no deferred existence axiom.

For every degree the identities are the elementary calculations (i p)(i p)=i(p i)p=i p, p(i p)=(p i)p=p, and (i p)i=i(p i)=i. They hold for sheaf morphisms and for ordinary linear maps. A sheaf term assumed a direct summand has a splitting by the meaning of direct summand; choose those data degreewise. The local perfectness paragraph spells out the local condition of the source, not a theorem extracting global projectivity from local data.

For a global perfect coefficient complex, def-pc-perfect-ring-complex supplies a bounded finitely generated projective representative. In each degree finite generators give a surjection p:Lambda^N -> F^r; the lifting characterization in def-weil-pbc-projective-module lifts id_{F^r} through p, giving i with p i=id. This is a two-line specialization of definitions, also verified by reading Mathlib/RingTheory/Finiteness/Projective.lean:30–35, Module.Finite.exists_comp_eq_id_of_projective. It needs no new lemma node. Outside the finite interval take N=0 and the unique maps. Sheafification as a functor preserves each displayed composition and identity. For the identification of the constant finite free module with a finite free constant sheaf, the existing lem-weil-pbc-sheafification-sets-exact explicitly states preservation of finite products and of the final object; repeated binary products give the N-fold product, including N=0. The identifications respect the module operations since these operations are maps of the underlying presheaves. Thus the constant differentials still square to zero and the constant maps still split.
