### lem-trf-normality-from-completed-monoid-chart

Let $\widehat A=\varprojlim_m A/\mathfrak m_A^m$ be the completion at its maximal ideal [def-weil-pbc-adic-completion]. Let $P$ be a finitely generated integral saturated commutative monoid [def-trf-fs-log-blowup], written additively and sharp (its only invertible element is $0$), and $n\geq0$. For a local coefficient ring $L$, define $B_L(P,n)$ as the completion of $L[P][t_1,\ldots,t_n]$ at $(\mathfrak m_L,z^q\ (q\neq0),t_1,\ldots,t_n)$; the monoid ring consists of finite sums with $z^qz^{q'}=z^{q+q'}$. Suppose $\widehat A$ is isomorphic as a ring either to $B_k(P,n)$ for a field $k$, or to $B_C(P,n)/(\theta)$, where $C$ is a Cohen ring of residue characteristic the prime $p$ [def-weil-pbc-cohen-ring] and $\theta-p\in(z^q\ (q\neq0),t_1,\ldots,t_n)B_C(P,n)$. Then the canonical maps $A_0\to A$ and $A\to\widehat A$ are faithfully flat [def-weil-pbc-faithfully-flat-ring-map], and $\widehat A$, $A$ and $A_0$ are normal domains [def-weil-pbc-normal-ring]. No log structure or log regularity is assumed on $X$ beyond the displayed completed-ring hypothesis.

### lem-trf-monoidal-chart-dense-injective

Let $(X,M,\alpha)$ be a locally Noetherian fs log scheme [def-trf-fs-log-blowup, pqf-def-locnoeth], without assuming log regularity. At every geometric point $\bar x$ [def-weil-geometric-point], let $A=\mathcal O^{\mathrm{sh}}_{X,\bar x}$ [def-weil-pbc-strict-henselization-limit], and suppose that $A$ is a domain. Suppose also that there is an affine pointed etale neighbourhood $W\to X$ [def-weil-etale-morphism] with a chart $P\to M|_W$ identifying $P$ with $M_{\bar x}/A^\times$, where $P$ is finitely generated, integral and sharp (only $0$ is invertible), written additively. Write $a_W:P\to\Gamma(W,\mathcal O_W)$ for the chart and $a:P\to A$ for its germ. Assume that for every monoid ideal $J\subseteq P$ (a subset with $J+P\subseteq J$, including $\varnothing$), one has $a^{-1}((a(j):j\in J)A)=J$. Let $V\subseteq X$ be the locus of zero characteristic monoid. For any such chart and any finite generating list $p_1,\ldots,p_r$ of $P$, put $s_W=\prod_i a_W(p_i)$ and $s=\prod_i a(p_i)$, allowing $r=0$ and empty product $1$. Then $a(p)\neq0$ for every $p\in P$, $s\neq0$, $V\times_X W=D(s_W)$, and its pullback to $\operatorname{Spec}A$ is $D(s)$. The locus $V$ is open dense in $X$. The chart identifies $M_{\bar x}=P\oplus A^\times$, and the structural map $(p,u)\mapsto u a(p)$ on this stalk is injective. Every section of $M$ restricts to a unit on $V$. Here $D(t)$ denotes the open set of prime ideals not containing $t$.

Source read: Agent-extracted chart criterion underlying Expose VI 1.4, printed p. 69, and VIII 3.4.1--3.4.3, printed p. 110; parent split, original steps 3--4. The stated contraction and domain hypotheses replace log regularity.

### lem-trf-faithfully-flat-monomial-unit-descent

Let $A$ be a nonzero commutative integral domain, $B$ a commutative ring, and $\varphi:A\to B$ a faithfully flat ring homomorphism [def-weil-pbc-faithfully-flat-ring-map]. Let $P$ be a finitely generated commutative monoid, written additively, and let $a:P\to(A,\cdot)$ satisfy $a(0)=1$, $a(p+q)=a(p)a(q)$ and $a(p)\neq0$ for every $p\in P$. Fix generators $p_1,\ldots,p_r$ of $P$ and put $s=\prod_i a(p_i)$, with empty product $1$. Suppose that for every $q\in P$ and $b,c\in B$ satisfying $bc=\varphi(a(q))$, there exist $p\in P$ and $v\in B^\times$ with $b=v\varphi(a(p))$. Let $\ell:A\to A[1/s]$ be localization at $1,s,s^2,\ldots$: its elements are fractions $f/s^m$, and $f/s^m=g/s^n$ means $s^h(s^nf-s^mg)=0$ for some $h\geq0$. Then
$$\ell^{-1}\bigl(A[1/s]^\times\bigr)=\{u a(p):u\in A^\times,\ p\in P\}.$$
This includes $P=\{0\}$, the empty generating list, $s=1$, and $B$ equal to a faithfully flat completion of $A$.

Source read: Agent-extracted faithfully flat algebra criterion for Expose VI 1.4 and 1.6, printed pp. 69--70; parent split, original steps 5--6. Ideal descent uses Stacks Tag 05CK (lemma-faithfully-flat-universally-injective). The complete-toric factor theorem is an explicit hypothesis here, not claimed as proved in this criterion.

### Original step 1

Fix a geometric point $\bar y$ over $y$, set $A_0=\mathcal O_{Y,y}$ and $A=\mathcal O^{\mathrm{sh}}_{Y,\bar y}$, and choose a sharp chart $P=\overline N_{\bar y}\to N$ on an affine pointed etale neighbourhood $W$ by the sharp-chart lemma. In particular $P$ is finitely generated, integral, saturated and sharp. The chart definition gives $N_{\bar y}=P\oplus A^\times$. Replacing that sharp chart and its affine neighbourhood $W$ if necessary, as permitted by the completed-chart lemma (from now on $W$ and $a:P\to A$ denote the replacement), it identifies $\widehat A$, respecting these monomials, with the complete monoid model $B_k(P,n)$ if $A$ contains a field, and with $B_C(P,n)/(\theta)$ otherwise. By normality of these saturated models, $\widehat A$ is a normal domain. This is only the fs case: no saturation of a non-saturated log scheme is introduced.

### Original step 2

By the affine strict-local comparison lemma, applied to an affine open $\operatorname{Spec}D\ni y$ (so $D_{\mathfrak p}=A_0$) and the separable closure $K$ of $\kappa(y)$ in $\Omega$, $A$ is isomorphic as an $A_0$-algebra to an algebraic strict henselization $A_0^{\mathrm{sh}}$ relative to $K$, by a local isomorphism. Its index pairs $(S,\chi:S\to K)$ are the pointed etale triples $(S,\ker\chi,\text{induced }\kappa(\ker\chi)\to K)$ over the closed point, with the same morphisms, so $A_0^{\mathrm{sh}}$ is the triples colimit of the two lemmas below. Hence $A_0\to A$ and $A\to\widehat A$ ($\widehat A$ the completion at the maximal ideal $\mathfrak m_{A_0}A$) are faithfully flat, by the strict-henselization and completed-strict-henselization lemmas (their Noetherian hypothesis holds because $Y$ is locally Noetherian). Descend normal-domainness along each map, as follows. For a faithfully flat $E\to F$ with $F$ a normal domain, ideal contraction for the zero ideal makes $E\to F$ injective, so $E$ is a domain. If $a/b\in\operatorname{Frac}E$ is integral over $E$, its image is integral over $F$ by the same monic equation, hence lies in $F$. Thus $a\in bF\cap E=bE$ by ideal contraction, so $a/b\in E$. Apply this argument first to $A\to\widehat A$ and then to $A_0\to A$. All local rings of $Y$ are therefore normal domains.

### Original step 3

On the chart $W$, choose generators $p_1,\ldots,p_r$ of $P$ and put $s=\prod_i a(p_i)$, with empty product $1$. The associated-log chart formula says that the characteristic is zero exactly where all these generators are units. Hence $V\times_Y W=D(s)$, an open subset of $W$. Etale maps are open, so their images show that $V$ is open in $Y$. Apply monoidal-ideal contraction at $\bar y$ to the empty monoid ideal: no $a(p)$ is zero in $A$, and therefore $s\neq0$ in the domain $A$ of step 2. Thus $D(s)\subset\operatorname{Spec}A$ is nonempty. Every open neighbourhood of the closed point of $\operatorname{Spec}A$ is the whole spectrum; the preimage of every neighbourhood of $y$ contains that closed point, and hence meets $D(s)$. Its image lies in $V$. Since $y$ was arbitrary, $V$ is dense. Every section of $N$ restricts to a unit on $V$, by the chart formula or the definition of the triviality locus.

Existing input nodes: def-trf-fs-log-blowup, lem-weil-pbc-etale-morphism-open-map, lem-trf-log-regular-monoidal-ideal-contraction.

### Original step 4

The structural map is injective on the geometric stalk at $\bar y$. Indeed, if $u a(p)=v a(q)$ with $u,v\in A^\times$, then $(a(p))=(a(q))$. Contraction of the monoid ideals $p+P$ and $q+P$ gives $p+P=q+P$. Thus $p=q+r$ and $q=p+t$ for $r,t\in P$; integrality gives $r+t=0$, and sharpness gives $r=t=0$, so $p=q$. Since $a(p)\neq0$ in the domain $A$, cancellation gives $u=v$. Together with $N_{\bar y}=P\oplus A^\times$, this proves injectivity.

Existing input nodes: lem-trf-log-regular-monoidal-ideal-contraction.

### Original step 5

Let a germ of $\mathcal O_Y\cap j_*\mathcal O_V^\times$ at $\bar y$ be represented by a regular function $f$ on a pointed etale neighbourhood, invertible on its intersection with $V$. Refine with the chart neighbourhood of step 1. Pullback of the inverse to $\operatorname{Spec}A[1/s]$ shows that $f$ is a unit in $A[1/s]$. Write an inverse as $g/s^m$. The localization equality gives $s^l(fg-s^m)=0$ for some $l\geq0$; since $A$ is a domain and $s\neq0$, $fg=s^m=a(m\sum_i p_i)$. Pass to the completed monoid model of step 1. The complete-toric monomial-factor lemma, applied to this equation, gives $f=\widehat u\,a(p)$ in $\widehat A$ for some $p\in P$ and $\widehat u\in\widehat A^\times$. The same argument covers $s=1$.

Existing input nodes: lem-trf-complete-toric-monomial-factors.

### Original step 6

We have $(f)\widehat A=(a(p))\widehat A$. By faithfully flat ideal contraction along $A\to\widehat A$, both inclusions descend: $f=a(p)u$ and $a(p)=fv$ for some $u,v\in A$. Substitution gives $a(p)=a(p)uv$. Since $a(p)\neq0$ and $A$ is a domain, $uv=1$; hence $u\in A^\times$. Consequently the germ $f$ is the image of $(p,u)\in P\oplus A^\times=N_{\bar y}$. This proves surjectivity onto the intersection at each geometric point; step 4 gives injectivity. The structural map factors through the intersection by step 3. Geometric-stalk detection of isomorphisms of etale sheaves of sets now identifies $N$ with $\mathcal O_Y\cap j_*\mathcal O_V^\times$, compatibly with multiplication and the structural maps.

Existing input nodes: lem-weil-pbc-faithfully-flat-ideal-contraction, lem-weil-pbc-sets-etale-iso-on-geometric-stalks.

### Original step 7

Let $\operatorname{Spec}C\subseteq Y$ be an integral affine open. For each maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ of $C$, the local ring $C_{\mathfrak m}=\mathcal O_{Y,y_{\mathfrak m}}$ is a normal domain by step 2. The local-normality criterion therefore makes $C$ a normal domain, equivalently integrally closed in its fraction field.
