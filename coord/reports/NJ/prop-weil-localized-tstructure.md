## Source error

Take T to be a geometric point of characteristic different from ell, A=O_E, and k=A/(varpi). Let K=k[-1], with its finite free two-term model A --varpi--> A in degrees 0 and 1. It is derived complete and has uniformly bounded constructible normalized reductions and compatible étale witnesses. K itself has cohomology only in degree 1, so its integral canonical truncation tau^{<=0} is zero. But K_n is the two-term complex P_n --varpi--> P_n, whose cohomology is k in degrees 0 and 1; hence tau^{<=0}K_n=k[0] for all n. At the transition P_2 to P_1=k, the infinite free resolution of k over P_2 has every differential varpi. After tensoring with k every differential is zero. Thus k tensor^L_{P_2} k has nonzero cohomology in every nonpositive degree and is not k[0]. The levelwise-truncated tower is not adic. It cannot be fed to the cited adic reconstruction theorem, even though QK is rationally zero. This is also the obstruction explicitly described in the existing node `prop-weil-pbc-levelwise-truncations`; no new obstruction lemma is needed.
