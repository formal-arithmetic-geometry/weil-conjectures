## Source error

In Tag 099D the claim “Since the topology in C_0 is chaotic, the value J^bullet(U) is a resolution of A by injective A-modules” does not hold for an arbitrary injective module presheaf. On the category of finite-type ring–ideal pairs take P=(F_p,0). Evaluation at P is exact. Its right adjoint sends the injective F_p-module F_p to the module diagram
R_P(F_p)(C,J)=prod_{a:(C,J)->(F_p,0)} F_p,
with the C-action through each a. The adjunction follows by projecting at the identity arrow at P; naturality determines every other component. Thus R_P(F_p) is injective in the diagram category. Its value at (Z,0) is F_p (there is exactly one ring map Z->F_p), which is not injective as a Z-module: the map pZ->F_p sending p to 1 cannot extend to Z. Both pairs are among the source's finite-type models. This is a counterexample to the inference, not to the lemma's statement. It also shows why merely citing ringed-site enough injectives cannot repair the argument.

The repair is explicit: K is the extended section complex of the actual Godement resolution on U=Spec(A) minus V(I), with A in degree zero. Pullback on stalk-products supplies strict ring-map composition. Finite principal opens give a natural augmented Cech roof, so the induced base-change map is proved using the bounded flat Cech complex. No arbitrary existence choice determines K's data, and no incorrectly evaluated injective resolution is used. The unit/chapter statements and routes are untouched.
