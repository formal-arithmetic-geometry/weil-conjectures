## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: read literally for noncommutative finite rings and left module sheaves, the displayed tensor of two left modules is ill-typed. For example, the finite ring $A=M_2(\mathbf F_2)$ has the left column module $V=\mathbf F_2^2$. Its commuting endomorphism ring is $\mathbf F_2$, so it admits no commuting unital right $A$-action: such an action would give a unital homomorphism $M_2(\mathbf F_2)^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathbf F_2$, impossible since the matrix ring is simple and has 16 elements. Thus general left sheaves cannot be used as both factors of this tensor over a noncommutative $A$.

I made explicit that $A$ is commutative, which is already the coefficient convention of every derived-category/tensor definition cited in the blueprint. In particular `def-weil-pbc-derived-categories` and even the original, wrong-site `def-weil-derived-tensor` both explicitly require a commutative ring. This repairs the missing ambient convention faithfully rather than replacing the intended theorem by a perfect-module case. The added scheme names, universe convention, unbounded direct-image interface and description of the actual canonical map spell out the existing objects. There is still no boundedness on $K$, no finite-projective-dimension hypothesis on $N$, no quasi-compactness or cohomological-dimension assumption on $j$, and no assumption that $A$ is a field. Finite as a set and finitely generated agree for modules over a finite ring, since a finite module is generated by its elements and a finite generating family presents it as a quotient of a finite power of $A$.
