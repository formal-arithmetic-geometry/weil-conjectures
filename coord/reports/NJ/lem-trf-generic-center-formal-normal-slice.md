## What remains

No unfinished argument is left in the item's proof relative to its cited statements. The sole new queued task is the general algebra lemma on completed parameter charts. Its natural proof, retained here for its next worker, is: first take the completion of R with the fixed section c and the prescribed parameters. At the coordinate origin, adjoining t and y and taking finite maximal-ideal-power quotients commutes with replacing R by its completion; localizing at the origin changes none of these finite quotients. Thus the completed chart is
`F[[U,t,y]]/(U_i-U_a*y_i)` (with v_i=t coordinates treated in exactly the same way). Eliminate the variables v_i for i in J except a, using their displayed relations. This gives a power series ring in exactly D variables, preserving the images of R and its fixed field section. The empty J case is just polynomial-origin completion. Noetherianness of the original polynomial quotient is immediate from R Noetherian; the regularity and dimension of B follow from the regular power-series completion, using the existing completion-regularity and completion-dimension statements. This is a general finite-quotient/completion comparison with an actual map, not a new restatement of the geometric generic-center lemma.
