## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported parenthetical equated “normal locally Noetherian” with “every local ring is a Noetherian integrally closed domain.” These are not equivalent: the Boolean ring A = product over the natural numbers of F_2 has every local ring a field F_2, but its spectrum is not locally Noetherian at a nonprincipal ultrafilter point. Every basic neighbourhood there is Spec(product over an infinite set of F_2); the ascending ideals generated by successive singleton idempotents show this ring is not Noetherian. Thus Noetherian stalks do not supply a Noetherian affine neighbourhood. The replacement explicitly requires an affine cover by spectra of Noetherian rings, as pqf-def-locnoeth states, and normal local domains. This repairs the gloss while retaining the already explicit normal locally Noetherian hypothesis and every original conclusion.
