## STATEMENT-DISPUTED

The returned `lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-derived-pullback` asserted a localization of ordinary termwise pullback on all complexes and a comparison from that localization to the derived functor. Neither assertion is valid for a nonflat structural ring map. On the one-point topos take R = Z and S = F₂. The acyclic complex Z --2--> Z --> F₂ in degrees -2,-1,0 becomes F₂ --0--> F₂ --1--> F₂ after termwise scalar extension; it has nonzero cohomology in degree -2. Thus termwise pullback does not preserve quasi-isomorphisms and cannot factor through D(Z). Also the free resolution Z --2--> Z of F₂ in degrees -1,0 gives derived pullback with F₂ in both degrees, whereas ordinary pullback of F₂[0] is only F₂[0]. The left-derived comparison is the augmentation from the derived object to the ordinary object, not an isomorphism on arbitrary representatives.

The corrected dependency states epsilon: Lu* Q_R -> Q_S u* on the homotopy category, with epsilon_P invertible only for K-flat complexes with flat terms. This follows the construction and comparison convention of Tag 06YY and Derived Categories' definition of an object computing LF. The item's tensor isomorphism itself remains the full bifunctorial assertion of Tag 07A4, with no flat-ring-map assumption.
