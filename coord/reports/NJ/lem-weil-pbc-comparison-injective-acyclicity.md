### lem-weil-pbc-finite-basis-cech-first-limit

1. By [lem-weil-pbc-finite-intersection-limit-compatibility], each ordered intersection is the affine inverse limit of its affine ind-étale stage intersections. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-proetale-classical-continuity-sets] to the underlying étale set sheaf of $F$. Its canonical restriction map $\varinjlim_jM(T_{\alpha,j})\to M(T_\alpha)$ is bijective.
