### Returned step 1

Write $P_L=P\otimes_R R'$, $K_L=B'\otimes_{\underline{R'}}\underline{P_L}$, and $J_L=B'\otimes_{\underline{R'}}\underline{\operatorname{Hom}_{R'}^\bullet(P_L,R')}$. Each term of $P_L$ is a summand of a finite free $R'$-module: tensor the splitting exhibiting the corresponding term of $P$ as a summand of $R^n$. Apply [lem-pod-point-completed-evaluation] over $E$ and over $L$. It identifies $H$ and $J_L$ with the respective internal Homs, gives the canonical sections identifications with $P^\vee$ and $\operatorname{Hom}_{R'}^\bullet(P_L,R')$, and identifies the actual completed evaluations with the signed module evaluations. Its constructibility and completeness conclusions apply also to $K\otimes_B^L H$ and to the analogous $L$-model. These finite-projective models have finite Tor amplitude as in [def-weil-finite-tor-amplitude]: tensoring a finite free term is exact, and tensoring its direct summand is a direct summand of that exact functor; each reduction is therefore represented by a bounded flat complex in the same degree interval. Thus the tensor comparison of [lem-sf-point-fibre-derived-tensor] applies to these objects.

Original uses: lem-pod-point-completed-evaluation, def-weil-finite-tor-amplitude, lem-sf-point-fibre-derived-tensor.

### Returned step 2

Define $\beta_P:P^\vee\otimes_R R'\to\operatorname{Hom}_{R'}^\bullet(P_L,R')$ by $\beta_P(f\otimes a)(x\otimes b)=f(x)ab$, with $R\to R'$ understood. This respects the balancing relations in both tensors and is $R'$-linear. For homogeneous $f$ of degree $j$, [def-pur-hom-complex] gives $df=-(-1)^j f d_P$, since the target ring is in degree zero. Consequently $d\beta_P(f\otimes a)=\beta_P(df\otimes a)$, so this is a cochain map in precisely that Hom convention. With factors ordered as $P\otimes P^\vee$, the evaluation sign specified by [lem-pod-point-derived-hom-evaluation] is $(-1)^{pj}$ for $x\in P^p$. Hence evaluating after $1\otimes\beta_P$ gives $(-1)^{pj}f(x)ab$, exactly scalar extension of evaluation. Degree-zero coefficients introduce no further sign.

Original uses: def-pur-hom-complex, lem-pod-point-derived-hom-evaluation.

### Returned step 3

Use $\chi$ of [lem-weil-finite-free-completed-scalar-comparison] to identify $T_{L/E}$ on these complete objects with $F=-\otimes_B^L B'$. Its naturality identifies $T_{L/E}(\operatorname{ev})$ with $F(\operatorname{ev})$. On the finite-projective models the ordinary tensor associator identifies $FK$ with $K_L$, by $(b\otimes x)\otimes b'\mapsto bb'\otimes(x\otimes1)$; its inverse sends $b'\otimes(x\otimes a)$ to $(1\otimes x)\otimes ab'$. The quotient-normalization stipulated in the item says that, at every finite quotient, the operation comparison is this canonical scalar-association map: it sends $((x\otimes\bar a)\otimes(y\otimes\bar b))$ to $(x\otimes y)\otimes\bar a\bar b$, and the formula commutes with every quotient transition. The completion map $\chi$ has exactly these quotient components and zero shifted-product component by the cited node. Hence the operation comparison and $\chi$ form a commuting map of strict quotient towers before taking the cone; equality survives the derived limit. The stipulated internal-Hom arrow is therefore the transpose of the explicitly extended evaluation, so after completion its uncurried formula is $(-1)^{pj}h(x)ab$ and its curried formula is $\gamma(h\otimes a)(x\otimes b)=h(x)ab$. Equality can be checked on local pure tensors, which generate the sheaf tensors; it is equality of chain maps and hence of derived morphisms. The completed evaluation used here is the one specified by [lem-pod-point-completed-evaluation], not a separately chosen map.

Original uses: lem-weil-finite-free-completed-scalar-comparison, lem-pur-integral-operations-scalar-extension, lem-pod-point-completed-evaluation.

### Returned step 4

Now take sections. Let $q_E:\Gamma(s,H)\xrightarrow{\sim}P^\vee$ and $q_L:\Gamma(s,R\mathcal Hom_{B'}(K_L,B'))\xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{Hom}_{R'}^\bullet(P_L,R')$ be the evaluations on $1\otimes x$ of [lem-pod-point-completed-evaluation]. Let $\delta:T_{L/E}H\to R\mathcal Hom_{B'}(T_{L/E}K,B')$ be the stipulated arrow, and interpret its target through $T_{L/E}K\cong K_L$. By [lem-weil-point-sections-scalar-extension], $\varphi_H^{-1}=\Gamma(s,\chi_H)\theta_H$, where $\theta_H(f\otimes a)$ is the section represented by $f\otimes a$. Thus Step 3 gives the explicit equality
$$q_L\Gamma(s,\delta)\varphi_H^{-1}(q_E^{-1}(f)\otimes a)(x\otimes b)=f(x)ab=\beta_P(f\otimes a)(x\otimes b).$$
Equivalently $q_L\Gamma(s,\delta)=\beta_P(q_E\otimes1)\varphi_H$, the required square. These are the prescribed vertical maps: $q_E,q_L$ evaluate on the unit, and $\varphi$ uses precisely $\chi$ followed by the sections-tensor map. The compatibility of the sections tensor constraint with scalar extension in [lem-sf-point-fibre-derived-tensor] identifies the uncurried square with this same calculation. The sign on both evaluations is the common $(-1)^{pj}$ from Step 2.

Original uses: lem-pod-point-completed-evaluation, lem-weil-point-sections-scalar-extension, lem-sf-point-fibre-derived-tensor.

### Returned step 5

For a cochain map $u:P\to Q$, the two naturality routes, evaluated on $f\otimes a$ and $x\otimes b$, are both $f(u(x))ab$. Hence $\beta_P(u^\vee\otimes1)=(u\otimes1)^\vee\beta_Q$. On the sheaf side the operation comparison is natural by [lem-pur-integral-operations-scalar-extension]; on sections $\varphi$ is natural by [lem-weil-point-sections-scalar-extension], and the internal-Hom sections comparison is natural by [lem-pod-point-derived-hom-evaluation]. These are comparisons of the derived functors themselves, so this also gives naturality on their derived morphisms, not only on the displayed chain representatives. Contravariance is precomposition in every occurrence.

Original uses: lem-pur-integral-operations-scalar-extension, lem-weil-point-sections-scalar-extension, lem-pod-point-derived-hom-evaluation.

### Returned step 6

For $L=E$, the multiplication identifications send $f\otimes a$ to $af$ and $x\otimes b$ to $bx$; then $\beta(f\otimes a)(x\otimes b)=abf(x)$ is the identity on the identified dual. The quotient-normalized operation comparison and $\varphi$ have this identity normalization by their stipulated quotient formulas and by [lem-weil-point-sections-scalar-extension]. For $E\subset L\subset M$, write $R''=\mathcal O_M$. Applying the two successive normalized Hom maps to $((f\otimes a)\otimes c)$ and evaluating on $((x\otimes b)\otimes d)$ gives $f(x)abcd$ in $R''$. Direct extension followed by the module associator gives the same value.
