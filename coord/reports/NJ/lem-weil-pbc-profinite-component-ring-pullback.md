Step 1 now cites affine-scheme-qcqs for both hypotheses of qcqs-scheme-spectral. I opened both statements. The 005F node explicitly identifies each component with its clopen-neighbourhood intersection, hence distinct components have a clopen separation. Every clopen is component-saturated, so it descends to a clopen in the quotient topology; these separate points of P. Quotient compactness and the w-local definition then prove profiniteness and the closed-point bijection. The compactness leaf's clause (iii) supplies compactness of X.
