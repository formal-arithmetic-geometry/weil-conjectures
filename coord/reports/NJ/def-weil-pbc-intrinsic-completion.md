The elementary checks establishing that this definition descends are recorded here, rather than being made new nodes. For an acyclic complex E, tensoring with [A_T -> A_T] gives a two-column total complex whose two columns are E and a shift of E; the degreewise split short exact sequence of columns makes it acyclic. Repeating for r factors proves that every Q_m preserves quasi-isomorphisms. Applying exact countable products to a quasi-isomorphism at each level gives a quasi-isomorphism on B(C), by the cited products-core statement. The fiber has a degreewise split short exact sequence 0 -> B(C)[-1] -> F(C) -> B(C) -> 0, so the induced map on F is also a quasi-isomorphism. All constructions act on a chain map h by tensoring h, taking its product, and applying that product to each summand of F; composition and identity are preserved. The localization property in def-weil-abstract-derived-category therefore gives the construction on D(Mod(A_T)) and independence of representatives, together with the natural map eta.
