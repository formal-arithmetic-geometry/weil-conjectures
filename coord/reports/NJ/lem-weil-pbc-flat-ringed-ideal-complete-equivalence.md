For the quotient clause, quotient sheaves are sheafifications of sectionwise quotients.
The kernel of their induced map is zero exactly when a section a whose image belongs
to IB locally belongs to I. Since I and IB are already ideal subsheaves, local membership
is equivalent to membership on U; this is the first conjunct of `IsQuotientIso`.
Surjectivity of the sheaf map is local surjectivity: a local section represented by b
lifts after a covering modulo IB. This is its second conjunct. Conversely, every quotient
section has local representatives, and transitivity of covering sieves supplies lifts
of arbitrary quotient sections. Thus the predicate is exactly that the induced quotient
ring-sheaf map is an isomorphism; it does not assert sectionwise quotient isomorphisms.
