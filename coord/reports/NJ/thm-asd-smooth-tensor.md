## STATEMENT-DISPUTED

The imported statement does not introduce A or require its torsion exponent to be invertible on S. Read literally for arbitrary A, it is false. Take S=Spec(k), k algebraically closed of characteristic p, A=F_p and f:P^1_k -> S (smooth, proper, relative dimension 1). The Artin-Schreier sequence, H^i(P^1,O)=0 for i>0, and surjectivity of x^p-x on k give RΓ(P^1,F_p)=F_p in degree 0. Since P^1 over an algebraically closed field has no nontrivial finite etale covers, a locally constant rank-one F_p sheaf L is constant. The claimed ω_f=L[2] would then imply, by adjunction,
Hom(A_X,L)=Hom(A_X,f^×A[-2])=Hom(RΓ(A_X),A[-2])=0,
although Hom(A_X,L)=F_p. Thus the invertibility hypothesis is essential. This is a report counterexample, not a new proof dependency.

The faithful correction explicitly fixes a finite commutative ring A annihilated by an integer N invertible on S. This matches the finite invertible-exponent setting of the source Lean docstring and the unit's finite-torsion stage. It retains all unbounded ordinary etale complexes, the canonical map, the Noetherian base, and the absence of a chosen orientation. It makes no statement about arbitrary pro-etale complexes or completed coefficients.
