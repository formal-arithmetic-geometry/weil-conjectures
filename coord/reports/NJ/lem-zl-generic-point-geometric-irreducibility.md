## Review by NJ

I checked the item against Tag 054Q, including that X need only be irreducible and may be nonreduced, non-Noetherian, not quasi-compact and not of finite type. I read Tag 038G (lemma-geometrically-irreducible-local) and its full proof for the first new node, and Tag 037N (lemma-subalgebra-geometrically-irreducible), all three clauses and its proof, for the second. The new 038G statement restricts to irreducible X, hence is nonvacuous and avoids any empty-scheme issue with a universal quantifier over nonempty affine opens. The 037N node explicitly requires a nonempty small directed index, allows noninjective transition maps, and states no unsupported reducedness or finite-type restriction. Its injective-map formulation is the subalgebra assertion applied to the image.

For step 1 I opened lem-weil-pbc-reduction-universal-homeomorphism: it expressly says the reduction is reduced, a surjective closed immersion, and has a homeomorphism after every base change. Thus it supplies all the geometric reduction properties actually used. I also opened lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-residue-field-iso, whose statement gives exactly kappa(x) -> kappa(z) as an isomorphism for every closed immersion. Applying it to i is legitimate because the first cited statement supplies closed immersion. The base k is respected by composing the fixed structure morphisms. The generic point is preserved by the actual homeomorphism, using def-sc-generic-point. Geometric irreducibility is then an immediate field-by-field reading of def-pc-geometrically-irreducible. This step is elementary relative to those cited statements; no unproved nil-invariance theorem is hidden.

For step 2 I opened def-pc-integral-scheme: its equivalence with reduced and irreducible gives integrality of Y, without any separatedness assumption. I opened lem-sc-integral-function-field-affine-fraction in full: it states that every nonempty affine contains eta, its ring is a domain, the germ map is injective, and the generic stalk is a field and a fraction field. It applies to arbitrary integral schemes, not just curves, so this use does not add dimension or finiteness assumptions. Def-weil-residue-field identifies the field stalk with its quotient by the zero maximal ideal. I checked def-weil-pbc-module-stalk: it supplies both the neighbourhood colimit and the elementwise germ/equality description. Restricting to affine representatives gives the displayed algebra colimit, since affine opens form a basis in the scheme definition. The step explicitly refines the intersection to an affine neighbourhood; it never assumes the intersection itself is affine. The neighbourhood poset is small in the fixed universe and nonempty since eta exists. These are elementary cofinality and field computations from the cited statements, not another substantive lemma being restated inline.

For step 3 the new affine-local node supplies geometric irreducibility of every affine coordinate ring. Clause (2) of the new algebra node supplies geometric irreducibility of their directed colimit; both the nonempty directed index and the actual colimit were verified in step 2. This is the forward implication's substantive use of 038G and 037N. For step 4 the existing fraction-field node supplies the injective germ map for every nonempty affine. Its k-linearity follows from the induced structure maps, not an arbitrary abstract ring isomorphism. Clause (1) of the algebra node supplies geometric irreducibility of these subalgebras; the affine-local node's converse then applies because Y remains irreducible. These cited statements say the exact facts used, rather than merely defining the notions. Step 5 just substitutes the two equivalences already proved; it has no additional external input. citations.json quotes the complete, verbatim statement of every cited node, so each claim can be checked without following an unrecorded source reference.

For small cases I checked X=Spec k and X=Spec k[t]. For X=Spec(k[t,epsilon]/epsilon^2), the generic residue field is k(t), and reduction rather than assuming its generic stalk is a field handles the nonreduced case correctly. Over an imperfect field of characteristic p, X=Spec(k(a^{1/p})) can remain geometrically irreducible while becoming nonreduced after base change; no step replaces irreducible by integral after extension. Conversely Spec C over R has reducible complex base change and its generic field C has the same failure, as the item requires. For the new affine criterion the hypothesis irreducible excludes the empty scheme; a pairwise-disjoint union cannot be mistaken for an irreducible input. For the algebra node a constant directed system k and the quotient diagram k[epsilon]/epsilon^2 -> k satisfy the claimed conclusions, including a noninjective transition. The zero algebra is never an input geometrically irreducible algebra since its spectrum is empty. Infinite directed systems and non-finite-generation are not excluded.
