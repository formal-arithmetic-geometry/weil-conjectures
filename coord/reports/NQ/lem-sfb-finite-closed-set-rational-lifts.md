## Argument retained for the new unsplit node

Milne, B.37 Step 2, printed p.605 explicitly chooses a normal extension receiving each closed source residue field and states that all points above those source points are rational. Its algebraic justification is as follows. A k-basis of E gives a K-spanning set of B=E tensor_k K. For a prime q, B/q is a finite-dimensional domain over K. Multiplication by a nonzero element is injective, hence surjective as a linear endomorphism, so B/q is a field. For each e in E, the minimal polynomial of u(e) over k splits over K by normality (Stacks 09HM). The image of e in B/q satisfies that same polynomial. A product of linear factors vanishing in a field has a vanishing factor, so that image is in the structural copy of K. Since these elements and K generate B/q, the structural map K to B/q is surjective, and it is injective because it is a nonzero map of fields. Compose the quotient's map from E with this inverse to obtain sigma:E to K. The quotient map then is ev_sigma, so q is its kernel. Conversely ev_sigma is surjective, hence its kernel is prime; its restriction to E recovers sigma, proving uniqueness. There are finitely many sigma by the existing lem-weil-pbc-finite-extension-finitely-many-embeddings. There is at least one, namely u. As q is maximal, its quotient is its residue field. This argument does not infer reducedness. It explains the queued node; no proof was put into that statement-only node.
