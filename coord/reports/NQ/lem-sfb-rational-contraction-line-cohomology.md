For the arbitrary-base formulation, the intended justification of that unsplit general input is as follows (this explanation is not placed in its statement or proof fields). Work locally with S affine. Descend the entire proper finite-presentation diagram, its module, and its S-flatness to a Noetherian finite-type Z-model; flatness and properness spread after increasing the stage. A morphism between these finite-presentation S-schemes is finitely presented by Stacks 02FV, morphisms.tex lemma-finite-presentation-permanence. The locus in V_i where f_i has fibres of dimension greater than one is closed. Its image in S_i is closed because V_i is proper over S_i. The map S->S_i misses that image, since every geometric f-fibre has dimension at most one. Restrict the model to its open complement. On that model, top cohomology in degree one commutes with arbitrary base change and R1 f_i* F_i is finite type: Stacks limits.tex Tags 0EX3 (lemma-top-cohomology-functor) and 0EX4 (lemma-proper-top-cohomology-finite-type), read in the local source. Thus the support of R1 f_i* F_i is closed and its image in S_i is closed. S misses that image by the assumed geometric H1 vanishing; shrink to its open complement as well. Knudsen's criterion now applies on the Noetherian model. Proper coherent direct image is coherent there, hence finitely presented. Its arbitrary S-base-change isomorphism identifies the desired direct image with the pullback of that finitely presented module, giving finite presentation and S-flatness over the original base. The criterion applied on every further S-base change gives the asserted canonical comparison. For generation, on the Noetherian acyclic model the evaluation cokernel is coherent; its support is closed and proper over S_i. Its pullback misses S because the fibre criterion and the already-established S-base change identify the geometric S-fibre evaluations, which are surjective by Knudsen's condition (2). Shrinking the model away from its image gives the global evaluation conclusion. This is a genuine additional theorem queued as unsplit; I have not claimed that local freeness of F makes f flat or that every proper pushforward is finitely presented without these hypotheses.
