## New nodes

lem-sfb-fibre-generated-subobject — statement, unsplit, zero Mathlib leaves. This is the existence of a least subobject containing an arbitrary specified subset of one fibre, in any essentially small linear abelian category with an exact faithful finite-dimensional functor. It makes no single-generator assumption and asserts no representing pair or global dimension bound. Its intended future proof chooses among subobjects containing S one with minimal fibre dimension. Intersection with any other such subobject is a pullback still containing S; preservation of the pullback and minimal dimension make the projection to the chosen object a fibre isomorphism, hence an isomorphism by faithfulness and abelianness. The resulting factorization is unique by the target monomorphism. Thus no arbitrary intersection limit or infinite completeness assumption is required. This argument is recorded here, not inside the unsplit node.
