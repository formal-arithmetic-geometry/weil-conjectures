## Intended argument and route

Let G be the subfield generated by F and t_2,...,t_r. Elements of G are quotients of polynomials in these last parameters: these quotients form a subfield, so this follows directly from the defining minimality of G. Given a polynomial relation on t_1 with coefficients in G, write each coefficient b_i/c_i and multiply by the product of the c_i. This gives a polynomial relation in all the original parameters. Algebraic independence makes its polynomial zero, coefficient by coefficient, and hence all the original coefficients vanish. Thus G[T] embeds with T sent to t_1. Fractions of its image are precisely K, again by the minimality of the generated subfields.

In G[T], the ideal (T) is prime because membership means zero constant coefficient. Its square is (T^2), and -T is outside that square by its coefficient of T. For f=X^m-T in (G[T])[X], the leading coefficient is 1, the constant is -T, and every other coefficient below degree m is zero. A constant divisor of f is a unit by comparison of leading coefficients, so f is primitive. The existing Eisenstein node now applies, and the monic Gauss leaf transfers its irreducibility to K[X], where f becomes exactly X^m-t_1. This is the obstruction used in Step 2 of lem-trf-algclosed-fg-field-finite. It stays below the inherited trace-formula unit route; it does not replace any geometric step or introduce an SGA route.
