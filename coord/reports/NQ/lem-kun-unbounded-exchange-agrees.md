## STATEMENT-DISPUTED

The imported sentence “in particular for sheaves ... it agrees with the exchange morphism of [def-weil-pbc-underived-exchange]” is ill-typed as an equality of entire morphisms. The derived source is `g^{-1} Rf_* F[0]` and its target is `Rf'_* g'^{-1} F[0]`, whereas the underived morphism has source `g^{-1} f_* F` and target `f'_* g'^{-1} F` in the category of sheaves. Placing F in degree zero does not make either derived direct image a sheaf in degree zero. This is an explicit type failure: without degree-zero cohomology the alleged equality does not even have the same source, target, or ambient category. Taking H^0, rather than assuming the derived images are concentrated in degree zero, is the source-compatible correction.

Correction: the first clause is an equality in the unbounded category after the natural comparisons on bounded-below objects, with abelian coefficients explicitly interpreted as Z coefficients. The module-sheaf clause now asserts equality after H^0, under the augmentation-induced identifications with the ordinary direct images. A Lambda-linear mate is compared with the abelian underived map only through the specified coefficient comparisons; module and abelian derived categories are not silently equated. This repairs the actual imported source sentence without assuming properness, torsion, finite coefficients, or vanishing of higher direct images.
