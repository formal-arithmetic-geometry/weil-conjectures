## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported phrase "local sections are killed by positive integers" is false if read as requiring an annihilator for each section on its entire etale domain. The standard intended condition is that each section is *locally* killed. Explicit counterexample: on a nonempty separably closed point T, take the constant sheaf Q/Z. On the etale object U equal to the disjoint union of copies of T indexed by j >= 1, choose the section whose j-th component is 1/2^j modulo Z. Every stalk element is torsion, and this section is killed locally on each component, but no positive integer kills the section on all of U. This is also exactly the distinction spelled out in `lem-weil-pbc-torsion-sheaf-characterizations`, whose complete statement I read.
