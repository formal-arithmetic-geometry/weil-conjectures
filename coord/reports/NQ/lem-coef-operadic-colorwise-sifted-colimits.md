## Source error

Gepner--Haugseng Corollary A.5.4(iv), read literally with its printed ambient over-functor-category codomain, omits the restriction to algebra cocones. Here is a singleton-indexed counterexample. Let M be the nerve of the subcategory of Delta^op containing all objects and only inert arrows; its fibers over [n] are points, so this is the trivial one-color nonsymmetric operad. Let V be spaces with cartesian tensor, and let A be a pointed discrete two-point space. Define an over-functor F by F([1])=*, and F([n])=(A,...,A) in the n-fold fiber for n>=2; F([0]) is the unique object of the zeroth fiber. On an inert arrow to [m] with m>=2, use coordinate selection; on an arrow to [1], use the unique maps to *, and on an arrow to [0], use the unique map. These assignments compose, since inert arrows only select consecutive blocks and decrease the degree. The terminal over-functor E has a transformation E -> F given by the chosen point of A in every coordinate. It is an equivalence on the unique color, but its component at [2] is (*,*) -> (A,A), which is not an equivalence. For K=Delta^0 its color cocone is therefore colimiting, while its ambient over-functor cocone is not. F is not an algebra: its inert projection to [1] is not cocartesian. The item's statement and Steps 3--4 use algebra cocones throughout, so no item correction is needed; the comparison argument proves precisely the valid restricted assertion. This makes explicit the source-precision note above and replaces an unqualified appeal to the printed clause.
