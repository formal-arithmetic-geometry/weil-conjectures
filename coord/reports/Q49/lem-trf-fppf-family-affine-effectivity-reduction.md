## Review by Q49

I checked the seven steps with the cited statements open, against 02W3 and its cited lemmas. The review below is my reading of the mathematics, not only the mechanical checks.

1. The coproduct node supplies actual disjoint open scheme components covering the sum. The open-immersion definition identifies their restricted sheaves with those on the components. The fibre-product universal property proves that inverse images of components are the indicated pullbacks, and the morphism-gluing node gives existence and uniqueness for the inverse component maps and for the transport. These facts give the family-to-singleton construction of 023X directly: pair components carry exactly the original phi_ij, triple components carry exactly its cocycle. This is an elementary construction from the cited statements, rather than a new equivalence node. It works for infinite families and for the empty sum. The singleton descent-datum definition is used only after this construction, not cited as if it defined family data.

2. I checked flatness on the local ring of each open component, using the definition of flat morphism. The new 021O leaf preserves the full fppf condition on restriction to an affine open U. The existing 021P node explicitly supplies finitely many affine opens inside the members with jointly covering images; it does not merely supply a family of affine maps. Those opens are also opens of the original coproduct, so they meet exactly the quantifiers of `def-weil-pbc-fpqc-covering`. This proves that the possibly infinite coproduct cover is an fpqc singleton. It uses no unjustified quasi-compactness of that infinite coproduct.

3. The affine-base restriction of the singleton datum is written out: the pair and triple pullbacks are the corresponding restrictions of those over T. Thus the cocycle and projection compatibility are equations of scheme morphisms, not an argument using just underlying topological points. The 021O and 021P nodes provide the finite W_j refinement. The fibre-product universal property and the component construction in step 1 identify the pullback with the finite coproduct of Q_j. Hypothesis (i) applies to Q_j over W_j because it is literally a base change of V_i over T_i. It is not necessary to descend the property P itself.

4. The affine-permanence node explicitly includes finite disjoint unions of affines. The finite-coproduct-lfp node explicitly supplies local finite presentation for the morphism to U. The morphisms-between-affines node makes p affine, and affine permanence supplies quasi-compactness and separatedness, hence quasi-separatedness. The cited finite-presentation convention is exactly lfp plus qc plus qs. Flatness is a local-ring computation on the disjoint components; surjectivity is their stated joint coverage. For the fpqc condition I wrote the affine preimage argument, rather than assuming every flat surjection is an fpqc singleton. There is no finite-type or Noetherian assumption in use.

5. The canonical-pullback-datum node supplies both the chosen cartesian square and the transport compatibility; its statement says this, not only that a datum exists. The hypothesis (ii) is applied to the finite Q_j coproduct, with invariance under isomorphism accounting for the chosen pullback. Then (iii) applies because all four qualifications on p (affine source and target, flat, surjective, finite presentation) were established in step 4. In the empty refinement case U is empty, and (ii) expressly includes m=0. P is never asserted for the original infinite coproduct.

6. The fpqc base-change node states that the singleton A_U/U is fpqc. Both coverings in the refinement-reflection node are singleton coverings, so regrouping the original family before applying it was essential. The map h is over the same affine base U; its pulled-back object is cartesian and its transports intertwine by step 5. These are all of that node's hypotheses. Its conclusion really is effectiveness of D_U, rather than only uniqueness of descended morphisms.

7. The open-local-effectivity node states exactly what is now available: a singleton fpqc cover, effective data over every affine open, and transport compatibility under projection to the original datum. This supplies the general-base gluing part of 02W3 as an existing tool. Restricting its effective isomorphism to each open component recovers V_i and phi_ij by the explicit construction of step 1. I do not assume that intersections of affine opens are affine; the open-local tool is stated for all affine opens and already handles this issue. The conclusion demands an effective model, not that its morphism has P, and no extra conclusion or assumption is introduced.

Small cases checked: an empty index family can cover only the empty base, where the sums and all data are empty; a nonempty family over the empty base has only empty components; a zero-term finite refinement likewise forces U empty. Repeated refinement indices and overlapping affine opens cause no difficulty because the disjoint coproduct components remain separate, with the original overlap transport. Infinite I never enters the finite-coproduct hypothesis. No separatedness or quasi-separatedness of the original T or T_i is assumed. The new 021O assertion handles empty bases, empty families and arbitrary base-change morphisms, exactly as its Mathlib specialization does.

I corrected a source-tag typo in the drafted step 4: the definition of a standard fppf covering is 021Q, not 021R. No mathematical statement correction or source dispute was needed.
