## 4. Source error and correction

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported item is false with matching objects and pullbacks formed by retaining only reduced components dominating the base. The fault is the discarded exceptional components, not the prime-to-ell coefficient condition.

Here is an explicit counterexample. Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic zero, R=k[[x,y]], X=Spec(R), U=D(xy), and X'=Spec(k[[t]]) with x and y both mapping to t. This is a local map of Noetherian strictly henselian regular local schemes. Blow up X at its closed point to obtain Y. In the category using dominant reduced products, the dominant reduced self-product of Y over X is just the diagonal Y: the ordinary self-product has an exceptional component supported over the closed point, and this is discarded. Thus the constant simplicial diagram Y is a hypercover in that sense, with proper birational degree-one augmentation, so it is prime-to-ell for every ell.

The dominant reduced pullback of Y to X' is the strict transform of the trait, isomorphic to X'. The entire exceptional P^1 in the ordinary pullback is discarded. The same assertion holds in every simplicial degree, giving the identity hypercover of X'. The lifted closed point has slope y/x=1. Near this point of Y the boundary has just the exceptional branch x=0; its pullback is t=0, with nonzero cotangent class. Thus all the imported regularity and matching-branch hypotheses hold in every degree. Nevertheless H^1(U,F_ell) has the independent Kummer classes of x and y as a basis, while H^1(U',F_ell) is one-dimensional, and both classes map to the class of t. Taking L=2 contradicts the imported conclusion.

ILO XII 2.1.1 explicitly calculates finite limits term by term using ordinary scheme products, and XII 2.2.4 constructs the base-changed hypercover using ordinary Cartesian products. XV 2.2.3 says the pullback hypercover is obtained by base change; XV 2.2.4 is exactly the branch-comparison consequence for those diagrams. I restored that intended formulation. The topology is spelled out inline on finite-type Noetherian schemes, generated by etale covering families and proper surjections. XII 2.3.1 reduces the descent theorem to Zariski covers and proper surjections. This removes any reliance on the imported prime-to-ell alteration category's different products. The coefficient, local hypotheses, and conclusion in degrees 0 <= q < L remain the intended ones.
