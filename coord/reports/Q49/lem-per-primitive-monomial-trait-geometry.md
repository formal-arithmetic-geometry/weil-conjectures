## New algebra input and its argument

The new lemma is genuinely about linear coordinates for an arbitrary coefficient ring and arbitrary scalar, not integrality, regularity, or smoothness of a trait model. Here is the reduction argument that justifies its statement and makes the unsplit task concrete. For each exponent vector v, let k(v) be the minimum of floor(v_i/a_i) over i with a_i positive. This minimum exists since a is nonzero. Write v=b+k(v)a. Then b is componentwise nonnegative and at least one positive-coordinate remainder is less than a_i, so b belongs to E_a. This representation is unique: k is precisely the largest number of copies of a subtractable componentwise. In particular replacing v by v+a increments k by one and fixes b.

Define an A-linear map N on the polynomial monomial basis by X^v mapping to c^k(v) times the formal basis vector indexed by b. For each monomial X^v, N(X^(v+a))=c N(X^v); consequently N kills every multiple of X^a-c. Conversely X^v-c^k X^b is X^b((X^a)^k-c^k), hence belongs to the principal ideal by the finite geometric-series identity. Summing this calculation shows every polynomial is congruent to its reduced normal combination. The induced map from the quotient to finitely supported coefficient families is inverse to sending a family to its reduced monomials: N is the identity on reduced monomials, and the just-established congruence proves the other composite. This establishes existence and uniqueness without any domain, flatness, or invertibility hypothesis. The new node contains only the assertion; the argument here is not installed as its proof.
