## Source error

In `literature/stacks-project/perfect.tex`, Tag 0CSF, label `lemma-pseudo-coherent-over-algebra`, the proof says to take the smallest index of nonzero cohomology after showing vanishing in high degrees. That choice is unsuitable for its downward induction and need not exist. For example, with A=B a field, the zero-differential complex with one copy of A in every nonpositive degree is pseudo-coherent but has no smallest nonzero degree. The zero complex has no such degree at all. The theorem itself is true. Step 1 instead fixes any upper term bound b of the pseudo-coherent A-model and starts the approximation at n=b+1 with zero. This is also the base-case convention in Tag 064U, label `lemma-pseudo-coherent`, in `more-algebra.tex`. No hypothesis or conclusion of the item was changed; this is not a statement dispute.
