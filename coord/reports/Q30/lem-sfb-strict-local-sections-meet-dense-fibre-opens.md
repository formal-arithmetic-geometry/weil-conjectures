## Review by Q30

I read BLR §2.3 Proposition 5 and §5.3 Lemma 7 and the field-extension paragraph of Lemma 6 from the primary scan. For the first new node I checked the general first assertion of Lemma 7, whose hypotheses are a Noetherian base, a finite type morphism, an arbitrary family of sections, and a specialization. Taking affine source and base is a specialization of exactly that assertion; neither flatness nor strict henselianity appears in that part of the source. Its later proof uses DVR reduction and fibre dimensions of the schematic closure. I retain that independent argument in this report for the next worker: pull the two base points to a DVR, take the schematic closure of the generic-fibre value closure, use torsion-freeness over the DVR to obtain flatness, and compare the dimension of its special fibre with its generic fibre; every section's special value lies in this special fibre. This is substantive geometry, so it is a new unsplit node, not an elementary assertion in the assembly.

For the second new node I read the actual French statement and proof of EGA IV_3 11.10.7 locally. It says that the reduced closure of a family of images from geometrically reduced k-schemes commutes with field extension. Here every member is Spec(k). If the rational points are dense, that reduced closure is X_red; after extension the nilpotent kernel of the reduction remains a nil ideal, so (X_red)_L and X_L have the same points and topology. Thus the new topological statement does not need reducedness, smoothness, finite type or separability. This comparison is a genuine independent geometric lemma and remains unsplit. It is not the statement that the inverse image of a dense subset under an arbitrary continuous map is dense.

I opened every node cited by the item and checked the following uses against its statement, with complete verbatim quotations in citations.json. Step 1 uses smoothness after base change from lem-pc-proper-base-change (its smooth clause, not its proper clause), finite-separable-point density from lem-pc-dense-separable-points, the locally constant dimension function and open strata from lem-sfb-smooth-space-dimension-strata, restriction of smoothness from lem-pc-open-immersion-smooth-composition, and the affine ring/scheme smoothness equivalence. Strict henselianity supplies precisely the separably closed residue field. The algebraic-space strata statement applies with the identity etale chart of a scheme and does not need its additional separated-finite-presentation hypothesis for the local dimension assertions used here. Choosing an affine open inside the stratum imposes no separation hypothesis on Y.

Step 2 applies lem-trf-henselian-smooth-algebra-lifting with base ring A, smooth algebra B, target algebra A, and the maximal ideal. Its full lifting conclusion is exactly what is needed; merely citing the definition of henselianity would be inadequate. The residue field is separably closed, so all finite-separable closed points of the smooth closed fibre are k-rational. This checks both density and existence, and yields actual sections of the original base rather than sections over a replacement base.

Step 3 uses the new dimension inequality only after fixing the family U(S), so it never assumes density on other components. All points of a local spectrum specialize to the closed point. The strata statement identifies the fibrewise local dimension with n, and lem-sbc-constant-local-dimension-components identifies each component dimension. Affine finite type fibres over fields satisfy the hypotheses of lem-wf-affine-noetherian-components. Taking the supremum of chains in the finitely many components gives dimension n whenever a fibre is nonempty; the new inequality itself guarantees that every required fibre is nonempty. Thus no unproved constancy across different strata is used.

Step 4 uses exactly the nowhere-dense dimension-drop statement of lem-sc-hilbert-nowhere-dense-dimension-drop: U_s is nonempty, Noetherian, and finite-dimensional, and C_s is closed. The contradiction gives a nonempty interior, not the stronger claim that C_s equals U_s. The density restriction to this open is written out using neighbourhoods. The open-factorization node permits applying the new field-extension lemma to actual rational points of V. No field extension hypothesis has been strengthened.

Step 5 uses the open-immersion base-change node for openness and the separate field-spectrum-surjectivity and surjective-base-change nodes for nonemptiness. A nonempty open of the ambient scheme meets its dense open O; density of the specified extended rational points then selects the required original section. The projection equalities verify that composition with U into Y gives the same induced L-point. All generic tools outside the inherited unit are declared individually in split_report.tool_uses and do not replace any unit-level proof route.

I tested the new statements with equal base points, one section, an empty family, the zero base ring (no points, hence vacuous), and the collapsing family x=c t in A=k[t], B=A[x]: generic closure dimension 1 can specialize to dimension 0, confirming the direction of the inequality.
