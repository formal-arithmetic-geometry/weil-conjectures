## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: a map `u:Y'' -> Y'` does not give maps from all the original corners to `Y'` (in particular there is generally no map `Y -> Y'`), so "pulling all four corners back along u" is undefined. Even on the successive-base-change reading, the pullback `u^{-1} Ex_square` has target `u^{-1} Rf'_* g'^{-1} K`. The outer exchange has target `Rf''_* (g'v)^{-1} K`. Pseudofunctoriality identifies successive inverse images; it does not turn the first target into the second. Their comparison is an exchange morphism for the extra square.

Here is an explicit failure if that comparison is treated as an isomorphism. Take `Y=Spec(C[t])`, `X=D(t)`, `f=j:X -> Y` the open immersion, the original square with `g=id_Y` and `g'=id_X`, and `u:Spec(C) -> Y` the origin. Let `K` be the constant Z/2 sheaf in degree zero on X. The original exchange is the identity, hence its pullback is the identity of `u^{-1} Rj_* K`. Its H^0 is nonzero: the constant section 1 on every punctured etale neighbourhood of 0 has a nonzero germ, since every etale neighbourhood of 0 meets D(t). The further fibre product is empty, so the outer exchange has zero target. Thus it cannot be the pulled-back identity under canonical isomorphisms. This is a failure already for bounded-below complexes and the prescribed abelian coefficients, with no change of coefficients involved.
