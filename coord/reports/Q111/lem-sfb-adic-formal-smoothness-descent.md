## Review by Q111

I checked the item against the full hypotheses of 0DZS's algebraic comparison, 07ED, 07PM and 07NT, and checked the new node against 07EL theorem-regular-fs at lines 9872 onward. In characteristic zero geometric regularity includes regularity by the trivial field extension, and 07EL's characteristic-zero (1) implies (2) applies. In positive characteristic its geometrically-regular (1) implies formally-smooth (2) applies directly. Thus the new statement covers all characteristics, with no perfectness or residue separability hypothesis, and contains only a statement, with empty proof and proof dependencies. Each nonbasic notion names its definition; locality and its maximal ideal are stated inline. The new node is a substantive field theorem, strictly below the regular-map descent argument, and is neither a restatement of ascent/descent nor a short corollary of a cited available statement.

For step 1 I opened def-weil-pbc-formally-smooth-adic: it gives exactly lifts for square-zero ideals and maps killing a positive ideal power. Restriction of g to C kills that power because C -> H is local. The first lift makes D a C-algebra, and the second lifts g compatibly with that same algebra structure. These are two applications of the definition, so this step is elementary relative to that citation; no discrete Mathlib composition theorem is substituted for the adic one.

For step 2 I opened all five cited statements. The completion definition supplies the inverse limit and the assumed bijections C -> C-hat and H -> H-hat. Adic-completion-noetherian gives only Noetherianity; local-completion-local-ring separately gives the maximal ideal m_R A, and adic-completion-mod-ideal separately gives completeness for that ideal. I did not attribute those conclusions to the Noetherianity node. Fs-adic-complete-source-target supplies formal smoothness of R-hat -> H-hat in the extended maximal-ideal topology, which becomes precisely the topology on H after its assumed completion isomorphism. Quotient coordinates define f and h, show locality on generators of m_R A, and show i f = h by separatedness; all maps are the induced ones, not arbitrary lifts.

For step 3 I opened formally-smooth-complete-local-regular, fs-adic-local-flat, flat-local-hom-faithfully-flat, and regular-permanence. The first requires both source and target complete; this is true for A and H after step 2. The second applies to C -> H without any extra completeness requirement. The third states surjectivity on spectra explicitly, so regular-permanence has both flatness and spectral surjectivity, not an unsupported invocation of descent. Its regular composite is exactly h = i f.

For step 4 I opened def-weil-pbc-g-ring, the new field node, and flat-formally-smooth-closed-fibre-adic. The regular-map definition supplies flatness and geometric regularity for every prime, in particular the maximal ideal of A. The fibre is the proper quotient C/m_A C; locality makes m_A C a subideal of m_C, so its maximal ideal is the image of m_C. The new node applies to this Noetherian local algebra over the field A/m_A. The existing criterion needs adic formal smoothness of this precise closed fibre, not merely its regularity; the new lemma supplies exactly that. It then gives formal smoothness of A -> C.

For step 5 I re-opened def-weil-pbc-formally-smooth-adic and adic-completion-mod-power. The latter gives surjectivity and the kernel for every quotient power, not just the residue field. If the test map kills m_C^n, the structural map from R sends m_R^n into J, hence kills m_R^(2n) since J squared is zero. This defines the structural map from A via its 2n-th coordinate. Both sides of the completed square kill m_A^(2n) and agree on the surjective image of R in that quotient, proving compatibility for all of A. This is a finite-quotient argument with the cited kernel formula, not an invocation of an uncited universal property or of the proof of 07ED. The lift over A restricts to the required R-algebra lift.

I tested the item on C = H (identity), and on field and power-series examples; the proof does not require a positive relative dimension or an injective R -> C. For the new field lemma k -> k and k[[x_1,...,x_r]] include zero variables. A purely inseparable extension k = F_p(t), L = k(t^(1/p)) has L tensor_k L with a nonzero nilpotent, so it is not geometrically regular and cannot be an illicit example of the new hypothesis; no separability of the final residue field was inferred. A singular local algebra such as k[epsilon]/(epsilon^2) also fails geometric regularity and is not claimed smooth. The zero ring has no unique maximal ideal, so is excluded by the stated notion of local ring. Nothing assumes the base completion map is regular, which would be false for general nonexcellent local rings.
