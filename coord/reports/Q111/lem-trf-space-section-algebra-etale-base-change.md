### Returned step 1

Put $F_d=L^{\otimes d}$, with $F_0=\mathcal O_X$. On an etale cover trivializing $L$ as in [def-trf-algebraic-space], every $F_d$ is identified with $\mathcal O$ by multiplying the tensor factors [def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product]. On that cover it therefore has the local presentation $0\to0\to\mathcal O\xrightarrow{\sim}F_d\to0$, where the displayed isomorphism is the chosen trivialization (and for $d=0$ the canonical one). This is a local free presentation, so each $F_d$ is quasi-coherent by [def-trf-space-quasi-coherent-module].

Cited nodes: def-trf-algebraic-space, def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product, def-trf-space-quasi-coherent-module.

### Returned step 2

Apply [lem-trf-space-qc-separated-quasi-coherent-pushforward] to $f$ and each $F_d$. The hypotheses are exactly the assumed quasi-compactness and separation of $f$; no representability of $X$ is used. It gives a quasi-coherent etale module $f_*F_d$. Apply the inverse equivalence in [lem-trf-scheme-zariski-etale-quasi-coherent-equivalence] on the scheme $T$, obtaining a quasi-coherent Zariski module $A_d$ whose associated etale module is canonically $f_*F_d$. Its sections on a Zariski open $V$ are the compatible sections of $F_d$ on $X\times_TV$.

Cited nodes: lem-trf-space-qc-separated-quasi-coherent-pushforward, lem-trf-scheme-zariski-etale-quasi-coherent-equivalence, lem-trf-space-etale-module-pushforward-restriction.

### Returned step 3

For each open $V\subseteq T$ and $s\in A_m(V)$, $t\in A_d(V)$, multiply their compatible local sections using $L^{\otimes m}\otimes L^{\otimes d}\to L^{\otimes(m+d)}$. This produces a section $st\in A_{m+d}(V)$ and commutes with restrictions, since tensor multiplication does so on every etale chart. Associativity, commutativity, bilinearity and the unit $\mathcal O_T\to A_0$ follow by multiplying local coefficients in a trivialization of $L$; the unit is pullback of functions. Hence these operations give the graded algebra of [def-weil-pbc-graded-sheaf-structures]. Let $\mathcal A=\bigoplus_{d\geq0}A_d$ in Zariski module sheaves. It is quasi-coherent by [lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-module-coproduct-closure], so is a quasi-coherent algebra by [def-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-algebra]. The equivalence of [lem-trf-scheme-zariski-etale-quasi-coherent-equivalence] identifies this sum with $\bigoplus_d f_*F_d$: restriction to the Zariski opens of that etale coproduct is the indicated sum, because sheaf tensor pullback defining the forward comparison preserves coproducts by its left-adjoint property [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-module-adjunction].

Cited nodes: def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product, def-weil-pbc-graded-sheaf-structures, lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-module-coproduct-closure, def-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-algebra, lem-trf-scheme-zariski-etale-quasi-coherent-equivalence, lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-module-adjunction.

### Returned step 4

For each degree, the adjunction [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-module-adjunction] gives the counit $\epsilon_d:f^*f_*F_d\to F_d$, corresponding to the identity of $f_*F_d$. In the tensor description it sends a local generator $a\otimes s$ to $a\,s|_W$ on a chart $W\to X$ lying over the domain of $s$. This formula follows by taking the identity map through the inverse-image and extension-of-scalars Hom bijections: the latter sends a map $v$ to $a\otimes s\mapsto a\,v(s)$. Form the direct sum of these counits. Pullback preserves coproducts: for every module $H$, the adjunction identifies maps out of $f^*(\bigoplus_d A_d)$ with $\prod_d\operatorname{Hom}(f^*A_d,H)$. This gives the displayed evaluation $\psi:f^*\mathcal A\to\bigoplus_dF_d$. On local tensors $\psi((a\otimes s)(b\otimes t))=ab\,(st)|_W=(a\,s|_W)(b\,t|_W)$, and the unit goes to the unit, so it is a homomorphism of graded algebras.

Cited nodes: lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-module-adjunction, def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product, def-weil-pbc-graded-sheaf-structures.

### Returned step 5

Write $h:X\prime\to X$ for the other projection. Since $g$ is etale, [lem-trf-space-etale-module-pushforward-restriction] identifies etale tensor pullback by $g$ and $h$ with restriction and gives $g^*(f_*F_d)\simeq f\prime_*(h^*F_d)$ canonically. The tensor comparison [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-module-pullback-tensor], iterated starting from $h^*\mathcal O_X=\mathcal O_{X\prime}$, identifies $h^*F_d$ with $(L\prime)^{\otimes d}$ for every $d$. By the pullback comparison in [lem-trf-scheme-zariski-etale-quasi-coherent-equivalence], the left side corresponds to ordinary scheme pullback $g^*A_d$. Thus the degreewise comparisons are isomorphisms $g^*A_d\simeq f\prime_* (L\prime)^{\otimes d}$, with the right side interpreted in the same Zariski equivalence. Pullback preserves the sum by the Hom argument of Step 4, so their sum is the required isomorphism of graded modules.
