## Argument handoff and preservation

The following are the five original proof texts from the returned current item, verbatim. They are preserved here so that the new workers have the actual argument, not only an abbreviated description.

### Returned step 1

Choose pointed affine etale normal-crossings neighborhoods on which $D$ is a product of a selected subset of the coordinate factors for $B$, and discard factors which are units at the chosen point by shrinking the neighborhood. The local branch-count lemma [lem-trf-nc-coordinate-max-stratum] identifies the geometric branch function on each neighborhood, makes it independent of the coordinates, and writes each locus $b_D\geq k$ as a finite union of closed coordinate intersections. Etale maps are flat and locally of finite presentation by [def-weil-etale-morphism], hence open by [lem-weil-pbc-flat-lfp-open]. Their open images cover the quasi-compact scheme $X$, so finitely many of these charts cover $X$ by their images. The finitely many numbers of factors bound every $b_D$, and nonempty $D$ supplies a positive value; thus a largest attained value $m\geq1$ exists. For any $k$, the complement of $\{b_D\geq k\}$ is the union of the open images of its open inverse images in these charts, so $\{b_D\geq k\}$ is closed. Consequently the nonempty locus $T=\{b_D=m\}$ is closed, and $b_D<m$ outside $T$.

### Returned step 2

Give $T$ its reduced induced closed structure $M$ using [lem-weil-pbc-reduced-induced-closed-subscheme]. At any geometric point of $T$, choose the chart in Step 1 centered at that point; its equation for $D$ has exactly $m$ nonunit factors. In that chart the inverse image of $T$ is $V(t_1,\ldots,t_m)$, regular and reduced by [lem-trf-nc-coordinate-max-stratum]. By the reduced-pullback assertion of [lem-trf-etale-reduced-cartier-descent], the pullback of $M$ is exactly this reduced coordinate intersection; this proves equality as closed subschemes, not merely as sets. The map from this pullback to $M$ is etale by [lem-weil-pbc-etale-scheme-base-change], hence induces flat local maps by [def-weil-etale-morphism, def-weil-flat-morphism]. Both local rings are Noetherian, since $M$ is closed in locally Noetherian $X$ and its etale charts are locally Noetherian. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-flat-under-regular] to deduce that the local ring of $M$ at the chosen point is regular. This proves regularity of $M$ and the asserted simultaneous local equations for $B$, $D$ and $M$.

### Returned step 3

Let $v:U\to X$ be one of the etale charts at a point of $M$. It is flat by [def-weil-etale-morphism], so the comparison of [def-nagata-blowup-flat-base-change-map] is an isomorphism by [thm-nagata-blowup-flat-base-change] and identifies the blowup along $M\times_XU$ with $U\times_XX^+$. We also identify strict transforms scheme-theoretically, as follows. Put $P=D\times_XX^+$, $S=D^+\subset P$, and $\Omega=D\setminus M$, viewed as the preserved open in $P$. By [lemma-nagata-strict-transform-closure-characterization], $S$ is the scheme-theoretic closure of $\Omega$. The open immersion $\Omega\to S$ is quasi-compact: the pulled-back finite-type center ideal has finitely many generators on any affine open, and its complement is the finite union of their principal opens. It is quasi-separated since it is an open immersion. Its scheme-theoretic image is $S$ by the closure definition [def-nagata-scheme-theoretic-closure]. The projection $S\times_XU\to S$ is flat by [lem-weil-pbc-flat-morphism-base-change]. Thus [lemma-nagata-dense-flat-pullback] makes $\Omega\times_XU$ scheme-theoretically dense in $S\times_XU$. The latter is closed in $P\times_XU$ by [lem-pc-closed-imm-base-change]; it is exactly the closure of that open: any closed subscheme containing the open has zero defining ideal after restriction to $S\times_XU$, by density. This is the strict transform of $D\times_XU$ by the same closure characterization. The identification respects the inclusions into $U\times_XX^+$.

### Returned step 4

Over $M$, Step 2 supplies pointed affine etale charts with $D_U=V(t_1\cdots t_m)$ and $M_U=V(t_1,\ldots,t_m)$. Apply [lem-trf-nc-coordinate-blowup-branch-drop] to these charts, using Step 3 to identify their blowups and strict transforms with the pullbacks from $X^+$. At each chosen point $y$ of the $i$-th blowup chart its equation is $\prod_{j\ne i}t_j'=0$; exactly the factors vanishing at $y$ are members of a regular system of parameters of the local ring there, and the remaining factors are units. This proves normal crossings pointwise and gives at most $m-1$ geometric branches. For $m=1$ the product is $1$, so the strict transform is empty on these charts. By [lem-weil-pbc-etale-scheme-base-change], the charts give etale neighborhoods of every geometric point of $X^+$ lying over $M$, which is precisely the local criterion for the asserted normal-crossings condition and branch bound.

### Returned step 5

Over $X\setminus M$, [thm-nagata-global-blowup-complement] identifies the blowup with $X\setminus M$, and [lemma-nagata-strict-transform-over-complement] identifies $D^+$ with $D$ there. Hence $D^+$ is normal crossings there and Step 1 gives $b_{D^+}=b_D<m$, or equivalently $b_{D^+}\leq m-1$. Together with Step 4 this covers every geometric point of $X^+$.
