### Original step 1

Use the canonical coefficient isomorphisms of [lem-weil-pbc-continuous-coefficients-immersion-restriction] to identify $A_T$ with the continuous integral ring and $E_T$ with its sheaf localization $A_T[1/\varpi]$. These identify the specified coefficient map, not merely the two rings. The comparison of [lem-weil-pbc-continuous-coefficient-realizations] intertwines the actual inclusion $A\subset E$ on the internal and section-ring sides. The encoding of [def-weil-pbc-continuous-coefficient-module-encoding] identifies internal module sheaves with sectionwise modules, sends the regular module and its finite sums to the regular sheaf and its finite sums, and [lem-weil-pbc-continuous-module-encoding-scalar-extension] identifies the actual internal scalar extension with the sheafified sectionwise scalar extension along this inclusion. We may therefore calculate with $E_T\otimes_{A_T}-$, retaining the specified internal functor when we return through the encoding.

### Original step 2

Apply [lem-weil-pbc-scalar-localization-sheaf-exact] on the fixed small site $T_{\mathrm{proet}}$, with $R=A_T$ and global section $s=\varpi$. Step 1 identifies its target ring and its actual change-of-rings functor with ours. Thus ordinary scalar extension is additive and exact on all module sheaves. By [lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-factorization] its termwise functor descends to an exact triangulated functor on unbounded derived categories, with its specified shift comparison. By [lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-functor] this functor computes the total left derived scalar extension $F_T$. In particular $H^i(F_TK)=E_T\otimes_{A_T}H^i(K)$: exactness preserves the kernels and cokernels defining cohomology. These assertions pass through the encoding because an equivalence of abelian categories preserves kernels and cokernels by their universal properties.

Cited nodes: lem-weil-pbc-scalar-localization-sheaf-exact, lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-factorization, lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-functor

### Original step 3

Apply [lem-pur-sheaf-ring-derived-coefficient-adjunction] to the identity site and the ring map $A_T\to E_T$. Its exact termwise restriction is right adjoint to its derived change of rings; by [lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-functor] that functor and the derived scalar extension of step 2 are both the left derived functor of the termwise scalar extension, hence canonically isomorphic, and on K-flat complexes both are computed termwise. We use the tensor symmetry to write this left adjoint with $E_T$ as the first factor. On a K-flat resolution $P\to K$ with flat terms the cited normalization gives the unit as the class of $p\mapsto1\otimes p$. This is also the derived image of the termwise unit on any representing complex $K$: naturality gives the commutative square between the units at $P$ and $K$, and both vertical maps are quasi-isomorphisms by exactness of scalar extension and restriction. Hence the square identifies their classes in the derived category. This fixes the asserted ambient unit and adjunction without imposing completeness, constructibility or boundedness on either ambient variable.

Cited nodes: lem-pur-sheaf-ring-derived-coefficient-adjunction, lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-functor

### Original step 4

For $K\in\mathcal I_T$, apply condition (iii) of [lem-weil-pbc-integral-stratified-perfect-characterization]. Its hypotheses hold because $T$ is Noetherian and finite dimensional and $\ell$ is invertible, as explicitly assumed. It says that $K$ is bounded and each cohomology sheaf is locally $A_Z\otimes_A N$ on pieces $Z$ of a finite stratification, for a finitely presented $A$-module $N$. A finite presentation $A^m\to A^n\to N\to0$, tensored with $A_Z$, presents this local module as a cokernel of a map of finite free $A_Z$-modules; right exactness follows directly from the tensor universal property. The immersion coefficient identifications of [lem-weil-pbc-continuous-coefficients-immersion-restriction] give exactly the restriction rings on $Z$. Under the encoding of [def-weil-pbc-continuous-coefficient-module-encoding], these are the same finite free presentations in internal modules: an equivalence preserves cokernels by their universal property. Thus $K$ is bounded constructible in the internal local-finite-presentation sense. Step 2 gives finite-limit preservation of internal scalar extension as well. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-bounded-constructible-exact-scalar-extension] with these integral and field coefficient ring objects. It yields $F_TK\in\mathcal C_T^c(E)$ with exactly the archived local-finite-presentation condition. No local-freeness characterization of that condition is required.

Cited nodes: lem-weil-pbc-integral-stratified-perfect-characterization, lem-weil-pbc-continuous-coefficients-immersion-restriction, def-weil-pbc-continuous-coefficient-module-encoding, lem-weil-pbc-bounded-constructible-exact-scalar-extension

### Original step 5

Use the raw Hom modules in [def-weil-pbc-rational-category] and [def-weil-linear-scalar-localization]. Define $J_T(K)=F_TK$ and $J_T(u/\varpi^n)=\varpi^{-n}F_T(u)$. Tensoring morphisms is $A$-linear, since $1\otimes(au)=a(1\otimes u)$ by balancing, and this equality persists on complexes and their localization. If $u/\varpi^n=v/\varpi^m$, there is $r\geq0$ with $\varpi^r(\varpi^m u-\varpi^n v)=0$. Applying $F_T$ and cancelling the invertible scalar $\varpi^{r+m+n}$ in the target proves equality of the two proposed images. At a common denominator, additivity is the additivity of $F_T$; for composable fractions the composition formula is $\varpi^{-m}F_T(v)\circ\varpi^{-n}F_T(u)=\varpi^{-(m+n)}F_T(vu)$. The image of $1_K/1$ is $1_{F_TK}$. For $a/\varpi^r\in E=A[1/\varpi]$, the same formula gives $J_T((a/\varpi^r)w)=(a/\varpi^r)J_T(w)$. Therefore $J_T$ is a well-defined $E$-linear functor with the stated formulas.

Cited nodes: def-weil-pbc-rational-category, def-weil-linear-scalar-localization

### Original step 6

The full integral category is closed under shifts and cones by [lem-weil-pbc-integral-stratified-perfect-characterization]. With its induced triangulated structure, apply [lem-weil-linear-scalar-localization-triangulated] to its $A$-linear shift. Every distinguished triangle in $\mathcal R_T$ is isomorphic to the image of an integral distinguished triangle. By step 5, $J_T$ on that image agrees with $F_T$; step 2 therefore makes its image a distinguished triangle in the ambient field derived category. Step 4 places its three terms in the full constructible subcategory. The shift comparison of $F_T$ descends to $J_T$: its naturality for integral maps extends to fractions by multiplication by $\varpi^{-n}$. The target is triangulated: since $T$ is noetherian, its underlying space is noetherian, so [lem-weil-pbc-condensed-constructible-serre] gives closure of the constructible $E_T^c$-module sheaves under kernels, cokernels and extensions, and [prop-weil-pbc-module-bounded-constructible-triangulated] makes $\mathcal C_T^c(E)$ a full triangulated subcategory of $D(\mathcal M_T^c(E))$. Hence $J_T$ is exact for this induced triangulated structure on $\mathcal C_T^c(E)$.
