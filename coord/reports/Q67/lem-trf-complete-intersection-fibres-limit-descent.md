### Old step 5

Fix $j\geq i$ and a triple, and abbreviate $k=i_{ab}$. There is a canonical isomorphism $C_j\cong B_j\otimes_{B_k}C_k$: the map sends $(r\otimes b)\otimes(s\otimes c)$ to $r\alpha_{jk}(s)\otimes\varphi_0(b)c$, and its inverse sends $r\otimes c$ to $(r\otimes1)\otimes(1\otimes c)$; these formulas respect the balanced tensor relations and are inverse on pure tensors. Thus [lem-weil-pbc-flat-module-base-change] and [lem-weil-pbc-finite-presentation-base-change-composition] (1) give flatness and finite presentation of $B_j\to C_j$. For a prime $\mathfrak q\subset B_j$, let $\mathfrak p\subset B_k$ be its inverse image. The induced field map $\kappa(\mathfrak p)\to\kappa(\mathfrak q)$ gives the fibre identity
$$C_j\otimes_{B_j}\kappa(\mathfrak q)\cong(C_k\otimes_{B_k}\kappa(\mathfrak p))\otimes_{\kappa(\mathfrak p)}\kappa(\mathfrak q).$$
Both identifications are the associativity and cancellation of balanced tensors. The $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$-algebra in parentheses is of finite type, by finite presentation and [lem-weil-pbc-finite-presentation-base-change-composition] (1), and is a local complete intersection by step 4. Therefore [lem-weil-pbc-lci-field-change] makes the fibre over $\mathfrak q$ a local complete intersection. This proves all three chart properties for every $j\geq i$.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-flat-module-base-change, lem-weil-pbc-finite-presentation-base-change-composition, def-weil-pbc-localization-at-prime, lem-weil-pbc-lci-field-change.
