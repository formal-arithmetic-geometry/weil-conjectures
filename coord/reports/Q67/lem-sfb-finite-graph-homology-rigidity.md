## Argument reserved for the new statement-only node

This paragraph is explanatory material for its next worker, not a proof field in the new node. Define the component-sum map Q^V -> Q^{pi_0}, sending each vertex basis vector to its component. It is surjective. The boundary image is its kernel: every boundary has component sum zero, and within a component the difference of a vertex and a chosen root is the boundary of a signed path. Subtracting these root differences expresses every vector of component sum zero as a boundary. Thus the equivariant sequence 0 -> Z_1 -> Q^E -> Q^V -> Q^{pi_0} -> 0 is exact. Taking dimensions gives dim Z_1 = |E|-|V|+c. For traces, choose bases of the kernel and image and extend to bases of their ambient vector spaces. The chain operators are upper triangular in these bases, and the image blocks identify equivariantly. Their equal diagonal sums cancel between Q^E and Q^V. The remaining quotient operator permutes components, so its trace is f_c; the vertex permutation trace is f_0, while the signed edge permutation trace is f_+ - f_-. This proves the stated sign convention. No rigidity or degree assumption is involved. These comparisons and the graph-component quotient calculation are why this is a real separate lemma, rather than an elementary step or a rewording of the item's conclusion.
