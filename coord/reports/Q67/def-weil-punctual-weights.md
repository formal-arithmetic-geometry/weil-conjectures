Hence the iterated-transport/pullback/Weil-map composite of the local-operator definition is meaningful at Qbar too. Its return is g_Y^r bar-y = bar-y g_s^r, not the false equality g_Y^r bar-y = bar-y.

I checked the use of lem-weil-local-frobenius-conjugacy-class carefully: its statement gives conjugacy at finite E, for arbitrary finite-type Y_0 and every cohomological degree. It is not cited as though it already asserted a Qbar theorem. For a Qbar fixed pair, represent its underlying object, its Weil isomorphism and inverse at a common finite stage, and enlarge the stage until the two inverse equations hold; this is exactly equality in the Hom colimits of def-weil-algebraically-closed-coefficients. Apply finite-stage conjugacy in degree zero there. Point-fiber functoriality and the explicitly cited transport compatibility extend its intertwining equation to Qbar. Finally an invertible intertwiner carries each nonzero eigenvector to a nonzero eigenvector with the same scalar; the inverse proves the reverse inclusion of spectra. This is the short common-stage specialization of the existing statements, not a new comparison lemma. It does not use the separated-only lem-wf-adic-local-frobenius-model-conjugacy or lem-per-adic-heart-pullback-fibres. Their separatedness hypotheses are why I did not cite them for this item.

For mixedness I read lem-a1-constructible-pullback-t-exact in full: it covers arbitrary finite-type k-schemes at E and Qbar, and says the restricted heart pullback is exact. Apply it to the geometric-Frobenius automorphism. A commuting inclusion u therefore induces an isomorphism coker(g_Y^*u) -> coker(u), since the two given Weil structure isomorphisms form an isomorphism of the two diagrams. Exactness identifies the first cokernel with g_Y^*coker(u). This is the induced Weil structure used by the quotient; no theorem that the definition of fixed pairs alone makes the category abelian is being presumed. The underlying heart is abelian by the statement of def-weil-constructible-heart. A monomorphism here is left cancellable, and the cokernel is the universal arrow annihilating u; these are the ordinary categorical meanings. The filtration consists of such inclusions, with its first and last terms identified with 0 and F, and with possibly zero successive quotients.

For the numerical expressions I read def-weil-complex-absolute-value and def-weil-real-power. The former is the complex norm, not the l-adic valuation, and the latter permits arbitrary real weights. Each q_y is the cardinality of a residue field, so the base is positive and the displayed real power is defined, also for negative w.

I tested the definition on the zero sheaf and the empty scheme: every purity condition is vacuous and a length-zero filtration witnesses mixedness for every bound. A rank-one sheaf on Spec k_0 with local scalar b satisfies purity precisely when |iota(b)| = q^(w/2). The identity scalar has weight zero. A nonzero direct sum of two distinct absolute-value eigencharacters need not be pure, although it is mixed when both weights are at most the bound; thus the filtration definition has not been replaced by a punctual eigenvalue inequality. A local operator with irreducible polynomial over E must still contribute all of its roots in Qbar, which the explicit scalar extension ensures. Repeated subobjects are allowed, as the zero-quotient cases require. No uniqueness of the weight of the zero object is asserted.
