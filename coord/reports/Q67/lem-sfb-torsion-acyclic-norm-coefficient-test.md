### Original step 1, assigned to packet 1

Work with section-ring sheaves via [def-weil-pbc-internal-abelian-sheaf-module-equivalence]; this preserves the underlying abelian sheaf and every section map. By [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-site-module-abelian-evaluation-comparison], degree-zero module cohomology agrees naturally in every nonnegative degree with cohomology of that underlying abelian sheaf, without a flatness assumption on the coefficient ring. For constant coefficients, [lem-pe-left-module-derived-evaluation-forget] makes the bounded-below comparison commute with derived evaluation restriction maps and geometric pullback, so the same actual restriction map is obtained in abelian sheaves. Site localization and its derived, unit-compatible form [lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization, lem-weil-pbc-ringed-derived-slice-localization] identify restriction to an affine pro-etale test with the corresponding base-changed open-unit calculation. Thus, for any affine weakly contractible $v:V\to X$, putting $T=V[1/f_V]$, the hypothesis gives the actual restriction isomorphisms
$$R\Gamma(V,(\mathbf Z/\ell^s)_V)\xrightarrow{\sim}R\Gamma(T,(\mathbf Z/\ell^s)_T).$$
Evaluation on $V$ is exact by [det-pe-sections-limits-colimits], so the source has no positive cohomology. Both $V,T$ are affine, hence qcqs [lem-weil-pbc-affine-scheme-qcqs].

### Original step 2, assigned to packet 1

Let $P(M)$ be the assertion that $R\Gamma(V,\underline M)\to R\Gamma(T,\underline M)$ is an isomorphism in $D(\mathbf{Ab})$. Constant module sheafification is exact by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-constant-exact]. Short exact sequences give distinguished triangles by [lem-weil-heart-short-exact-triangles]; derived sections are exact and shift-compatible by [lem-pc-right-derived-functor-triangulated]. Their restriction transformation comes from restriction of sections on resolutions, so commutes with shifts and gives a morphism of these triangles. Hence $P$ is extension-closed by [lem-sf-triangle-two-out-of-three], and is plainly isomorphism-invariant with $P(0)$. Step 1 gives $P(\mathbf Z/\ell)$. Apply [lem-sbc-finite-torsion-extension-induction] with annihilator $\ell^n$ to obtain $P(M)$ for every finite abelian group killed by $\ell^n$: its only possible prime divisor is $\ell$.

Original cited nodes: lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-constant-exact, lem-weil-heart-short-exact-triangles, lem-sf-triangle-two-out-of-three, lem-sbc-finite-torsion-extension-induction, lem-pc-right-derived-functor-triangulated.

### Original step 3, assigned to packet 1

Every abelian group $M$ killed by $\ell^n$ is the filtered union of its finitely generated subgroups; a subgroup generated by $d$ elements has at most $\ell^{nd}$ elements. Formation of constant sheaves commutes with this union: constant presheaves commute with colimits and the universal property of sheafification [def-weil-pbc-sheafification] makes sheafification a left adjoint. Choose the qcqs affine weakly contractible hypercovers supplied by [det-pe-hypercover-existence]. The degree-zero sheaves have common lower bound $0$, so [det-lem-bounded-continuity-sections](3) on $V$ and $T$ transports the restriction isomorphisms of step 2 through this filtered colimit. Therefore $P(M)$ holds for every such $M$, without finiteness of its cardinality.

Original cited nodes: def-weil-pbc-sheafification, det-pe-hypercover-existence, det-lem-bounded-continuity-sections.

### Original step 4, assigned to packet 2

Let $B$ be any complete nonarchimedean normed extension of $\mathbf Q_\ell$, let $O=\{b:|b|\leq1\}$, and put $O_n=O/\ell^nO$ with the discrete topology. On each of $V,T$ let $L$ be the subsheaf of $C_B$ consisting of continuous functions with values in $O$, using [lem-weil-pbc-condensed-field-continuous-realization]. Completeness gives $O=\varprojlim_n O_n$ as topological rings: compatible residues have Cauchy lifts, their limit exists in $O$, and the ideals $\ell^nO$ form a neighbourhood basis at zero. On qcqs affine tests, a continuous map to the discrete $O_n$ has finite image. Conversely constant $O_n$-sections are locally constant functions: use the finite-subgroup colimit of step 3 in degree zero and [lem-weil-pbc-proetale-finite-constant-representable] for its finite terms. Thus $L=\varprojlim_n\underline{O_n}$ as sheaves. Indeed a continuous function into a discrete quotient is locally constant, lifts locally by choosing representatives, and a compatible sequence is continuous into the inverse-limit topology. The quotient transitions are epimorphisms. Finally $C_B=\varinjlim(L\xrightarrow{\ell}L\xrightarrow{\ell}\cdots)$, with the $n$th insertion $x\mapsto\ell^{-n}x$: a continuous function on a qcqs affine has compact image, hence bounded norm, and the affines form a basis. These are explicit sheaf identifications, not finite-field descent or an assertion about incomplete $\Omega$.

Original cited nodes: lem-weil-pbc-condensed-field-continuous-realization, lem-weil-pbc-proetale-finite-constant-representable.

### Original step 5, assigned to packet 2

Apply [det-pe-milnor-epimorphic] in abelian sheaves to step 4's surjective quotient towers: $L[0]\cong R\varprojlim_n\underline{O_n}[0]$. By [lem-kun-site-rgamma-derived-limit], the two derived section functors preserve these derived limits, with the displayed projection maps. All transitions used are coefficient quotient maps or multiplication by $\ell$, so their restriction squares commute sectionwise; the resulting isomorphism is the restriction map itself. In particular $H^q(T,C_{B,T})=0$ for $q>0$, since evaluation on $V$ is exact.

### Original step 6, assigned to packet 3

Degree zero can be handled without completion. The constant finite sheaf representation [lem-weil-pbc-proetale-finite-constant-representable] identifies sections of $(\mathbf Z/\ell)_V$ and $(\mathbf Z/\ell)_T$ with locally constant functions. Their idempotents are exactly characteristic functions of clopen subsets, since the only idempotents in the field $\mathbf Z/\ell$ are $0,1$. Thus step 1 gives a bijection on clopens under $T\to V$. The spaces are spectral by [lem-weil-pbc-qcqs-scheme-spectral], so [lem-sfb-spectral-clopen-component-homeomorphism] gives $\pi_0(T)\cong\pi_0(V)$. A continuous map to the totally disconnected norm field $F$ is constant on each connected component and factors continuously through its quotient component space. Using [lem-weil-pbc-condensed-field-continuous-realization], restriction is therefore a bijection
$$\Gamma(V,C_{F,V})\xrightarrow{\sim}\Gamma(T,C_{F,T})$$
for both $F=E,\Omega$, including empty test schemes.

### Original step 7, assigned to packet 3

Together with step 6, and the absence of negative derived cohomology for degree-zero sheaves, this proves that $R\Gamma(V,C_{F,V})\to R\Gamma(T,C_{F,T})$ is a quasi-isomorphism for $F=E,\Omega$. ### Original step 8, assigned to packet 4

Return to $v:X'\to X$ and the supplied lisse module $N$, with its defining trivializing covering $\{U_i\to X'\}$ and isomorphisms $N|_{U_i}\cong C_{F,X'}|_{U_i}^{r_i}$ [def-sfb-lisse-coefficient-module]. Let $c$ be the cone of the actual unit $N[0]\to Rj'_*j'^*N[0]$. Restriction to a slice is exact, so $c|_{U_i}$ is the cone of the restricted unit, and its cohomology sheaves are the restrictions of those of $c$; a sheaf whose restrictions to a covering vanish is zero, so it suffices that each $c|_{U_i}$ is zero. By site localization [lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization] work on $(U_i)_{\mathrm{proet}}$. Let $V$ be any affine weakly contractible object of $(U_i)_{\mathrm{proet}}$. Restriction of sheaves of sets from $X_{\mathrm{proet}}$ to the slice at $V$ preserves epimorphisms and does not change sections over $V$, so $V\to X$ is also weakly contractible for the full site $X_{\mathrm{proet}}$ in the sense of step 1 and of the new node of step 7; put $T=V\times_XW$. By the unit-compatible derived slice comparison [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-derived-slice-localization], the derived evaluation of the restricted unit at $V$ is the direct sum of $r_i$ copies of the restriction map $R\Gamma(V,C_{F,V})\to R\Gamma(T,C_{F,T})$ of step 7, hence a quasi-isomorphism; finite direct sums commute with the additive derived functors. Since evaluation at $V$ is exact (step 1), $c|_{U_i}$ evaluates to an acyclic complex at every affine weakly contractible $V$ of $(U_i)_{\mathrm{proet}}$, and [det-pe-sections-conservative](2) on that site shows $c|_{U_i}=0$. This proves the actual unit for $N$. Rank zero gives the zero map of zero objects; for an identity open the unit is the identity, and an empty scheme contributes only zero sections. No global trivialization or finite coefficient field for $N$ was selected.

Original cited nodes: def-sfb-lisse-coefficient-module, lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization, lem-weil-pbc-ringed-derived-slice-localization, det-pe-sections-conservative.
