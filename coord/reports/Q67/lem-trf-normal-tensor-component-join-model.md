Here is the argument justifying the new statement, recorded here rather than inside its unsplit node: B is normal, hence reduced. Every minimal prime of B contracts to (0) in the domain A by flat going-down; otherwise going down to (0) would give a strictly smaller prime. Localization therefore injects the minimal primes into the primes of B tensor_A Frac(A). That generic algebra is a finite product of finite separable fields by 00U3, so there are finitely many such primes. The hypotheses of 030C now hold and give the finite product of normal domains. Giving each factor its projected A-structure makes the product isomorphism an A-algebra isomorphism. This argument neither assumes B finite as an A-module nor uses Noetherianity. The finite-factor auxiliary is a generic finite-presentation algebra theorem, not a relabelling of the item's henselian tensor component or embedding assertion.
