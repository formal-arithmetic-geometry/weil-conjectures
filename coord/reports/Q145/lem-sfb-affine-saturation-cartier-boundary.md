### Hypotheses and clauses

- Group scheme over k: `G : Grp (Over (Spec (.of k)))`. Its underlying `GrpObj`
  supplies multiplication, identity and inverse with their morphism identities.
- Smooth, connected, affine: `[Smooth G.X.hom]`, `[ConnectedSpace G.X.left]`,
  `[IsAffine G.X.left]`. ConnectedSpace includes nonemptiness, as the node requires.
- Finite type of G: `[LocallyOfFiniteType G.X.hom] [QuasiCompact G.X.hom]`.
  These are the two properties recorded by def-weil-finite-type-morphism.
- Scheme X over k and its action a: `X : CategoryTheory.Mod (Over (Spec (.of k))) G.X`.
  `X.X.left` is the scheme, `X.X.hom` its structure morphism, and
  `ModObj.smul (M := G.X) (X := X.X)` is the action as a morphism over k.
  `ModObj.one_smul` and `ModObj.mul_smul` are exactly the identity and associativity
  diagrams. In this Cartesian structure `(G.X ⊗ X.X).left` is definitionally
  `pullback G.X.hom X.X.hom`, so this is the specified fibre product action.
- Separated and finite type X/k: `[IsSeparated X.X.hom]`,
  `[LocallyOfFiniteType X.X.hom] [QuasiCompact X.X.hom]`.
- Normality: `hnormal : ∀ p : X.X.left, IsDomain (...) ∧ IsIntegrallyClosed (...)`,
  where both occurrences are the actual structure-sheaf stalk at p. No global
  integrality or irreducibility is required.
- Affine open subscheme U: `(U : X.X.left.Opens) (hU : IsAffineOpen U)`.
  The latter is definitionally affineness of the open subscheme U.toScheme.
- The output W with its open subscheme structure is represented by an internal
  module object W and an equivariant map `i : W ⟶ X` with
  `IsOpenImmersion i.hom.left`. The image equality specifies exactly the open
  subscheme, up to its canonical isomorphism over X; it adds no arbitrary choice
  of underlying subset.
- Rational points g are `𝟙_ (Over (Spec (.of k))) ⟶ G.X`. The tensor unit has
  underlying scheme Spec k and structure map the identity, so these are G(k).
- For such g, `((CartesianMonoidalCategory.toUnit X.X ≫ g) • 𝟙 X.X).left`
  is the morphism x ↦ a(g,x). Mathlib's Hom action is explicitly Cartesian lift
  followed by the action morphism. `toUnit` is the structure morphism to Spec k;
  therefore the first leg is the constant k-point g. Taking its image of U and
  the indexed set union over all g gives precisely the prescribed union gU,
  including all schematic points of U, rather than only U(k).
- Action factorization: `i` is a morphism of internal module objects. Its
  `IsModHom` law is `a_W ≫ i = (id_G × i) ≫ a_X` in schemes over k. This
  supplies the requested restricted action; the module structure on W includes
  its identity and associativity diagrams as well.
- Normality of W is the same universal domain-and-integral-closure condition
  on every stalk of `W.X.left`.
- Separatedness and finite type over k are `IsSeparated W.X.hom`,
  `LocallyOfFiniteType W.X.hom ∧ QuasiCompact W.X.hom`; quasi-compactness of
  W as a scheme is explicitly `CompactSpace W.X.left`. There is no Hausdorff
  condition. Relative separatedness agrees with absolute separatedness here
  because Spec k is affine (lem-sc-separated-over-affine-scheme-separated).
- The divisor is `∃ (D : Scheme.{u}) (d : D ⟶ W.X.left), IsClosedImmersion d`.
  Its actual quasi-coherent ideal is the recorded `d.ker`, and
  `d.ker.IsInvertible` says that every point has an affine neighbourhood where
  this ideal is generated by one non-zero-divisor. This is the recorded
  effective Cartier ideal predicate, not an abstract invertible module.
- `Set.range d = {w : W.X.left | i.hom.left w ∉ U}` is exactly the required
  support equality |D| = |W| minus |U| under the open embedding i. No condition
  requires the divisor to be nonempty or reduced, since the statement asks
  only for existence of an effective Cartier divisor with this support.

### Edge cases checked

U empty gives W empty by the image equality. Empty schemes are affine and the
zero ring is allowed in the Cartier generator condition; there is no nonempty
W or D binder. When U=W the boundary is empty and the unit ideal is regular
principal, with generator 1. The trivial group is allowed; then W=U and the
divisor is empty. If X is empty, normality is vacuous and all conclusions have
the expected empty interpretation. If U misses some normal components, the
image formula introduces exactly its translates, with no density or condition
on the omitted components. No smoothness, integrality, quasi-projectivity,
faithfulness, characteristic restriction, or chosen point of X is imposed.
The single universe u is arbitrary and shared by schemes and the base field,
as in the recorded group-scheme interface. There is no vacuous test-algebra
quantification or extra universe equality assumption. Auto-implicits and
relaxed auto-implicits are explicitly disabled.
