## Proposed arguments for the new statement-only nodes

### Quasi-compactness calculus

Work in an arbitrary small-site set-sheaf topos, with no coherence assumption. Sectionwise pullbacks and local surjectivity of sheaf epimorphisms show that an epi remains epi after pullback: locally lift the given section and pair it with the fixed section of the other factor. The sheafification adjunction realizes coproducts as sheafifications of objectwise disjoint unions, so every coproduct section locally lies in a summand. Thus a jointly epimorphic family stays jointly epimorphic under pullback, even for an infinite index set. Given an epi D to F with D quasi-compact, pull a covering family of F back to D and select finitely many members. Their images cover F since D to F is epic. For a finite coproduct of quasi-compact objects, pull any covering family back to each summand, select finitely many indices for each, and take their finite union. For the empty sum the initial object is strict, and its only subobject is itself.

For B mapping to a finite coproduct, extensivity decomposes it as the coproduct of B_i, its pullbacks to the summands. To test quasi-compactness of B_i, adjoin its complementary finite sum to an arbitrary covering family of B_i, obtaining a cover of B. A finite subcover exists; pull it back to B_i, where disjointness kills the complement. Each B_i is therefore quasi-compact. If the target summands A_i are coherent and both B and C are quasi-compact, apply that argument to both and identify B times over the coproduct C with the finite coproduct of B_i times over A_i C_i. Every summand is quasi-compact by quasi-separatedness of A_i. The first part gives quasi-compactness of the total pullback. This proves quasi-separatedness and coherence of the finite sum, including the empty sum. This is the full substance of old steps 4 and 8, not merely the extensivity interface.

### Finitary representable charts

Use the given finite-cover pretopology; every covering family already has finite index set, so identity refinement verifies condition (i) of the existing site-quasicompact-sheafification-criterion. Each H_U is quasi-compact. The sheafification adjunction followed by Yoneda gives Hom(H_U,F) = F(U): a section s sends an arrow f to F(f)(s), and evaluation at id_U is the inverse. Representable presheaves preserve finite limits by the sets of compatible arrows. Left exact sheafification therefore identifies the terminal, binary products, and pullbacks along site arrows. Apply the existing coequalizer-presentation node with all site objects and singleton identity covers. The coproduct arrow is epic by uniqueness in its coequalizer universal property. Its index is small in the containing universe. For any quasi-compact target its definition selects finitely many of those summands.

For arbitrary b:H_V to H_U and c:H_W to H_U, the displayed Hom bijection represents them by sections. The local-section lemma for sheafification represents these by actual site arrows after covers of V and W. Since the chosen pretopology has only finite covers, those local charts are finite. Coverings yield jointly epic maps on sheafified representables: two maps out of H_V correspond to sections, which agree globally if they agree on a covering family, by sheaf separatedness. Pullback stability of jointly epic families gives the finite paired family onto H_V times over H_U H_W. For each pair, left exact sheafification identifies its member with H_(V_a times over U W_d). Each such member is quasi-compact by the first paragraph. Finite-coproduct and epic-image quasi-compactness from the calculus node finish. This never assumes b,c are globally induced by site arrows, or that h_U is already a sheaf.

### Coherent subobject closure

In the abstract setting of the new node, quasi-compactness of any object B selects a finite subfamily of its generating epimorphism. For B,C quasi-compact mapping to G_i, use such finite generator covers. Pairwise pullbacks of the cover members over G_i are quasi-compact by the hypothesis for arbitrary arrows. Pullback stability gives a finite jointly epic family onto B times over G_i C. Finite sums and epic images are quasi-compact by the calculus node. Thus every G_i is quasi-separated and hence coherent.

A quasi-compact subobject T of G_i is quasi-separated because pullbacks over T equal the corresponding pullbacks over G_i: the inclusion is monic. The terminal generator is quasi-separated, hence products of quasi-compact objects are quasi-compact. Products of T in G_i and S in G_j embed into the product of their generators, which is a generator up to isomorphism; thus they stay in D. For arbitrary arrows f,g:T to S, their equalizer is T times over (S times S) S, along the diagonal of S. The product S times S is already a coherent member of D, so quasi-separatedness gives quasi-compactness of that pullback; its monomorphism into T embeds it into G_i. This proves product and equalizer closure, including terminal, hence finite-limit closure. Choose finite products and equalizers of all finite diagrams on the small starting full subcategory, then repeat in countably many stages. Each stage has a set of diagrams and of morphisms in the containing universe, and the union is small. Any finite diagram in the union appears at a stage, so its chosen limit appears at the next. Its objects lie in D, are coherent, and it is generating because it contains the original generating family. This retains both the arbitrary-arrow equalizer argument and the size argument of old step 7.

### Finite relations and transport

Quasi-compactness of A selects a finite generator cover p:P to A. The finite sum P is quasi-compact by the calculus lemma; because A is quasi-separated, R=P times over A P is quasi-compact. Select a finite generator cover q:Q to R in the same way. Empty finite families are allowed. No claim is made that R is representable or that an arbitrary subobject of a quasi-compact object is quasi-compact.

Write L right-adjoint to M. Hom(L(coproduct T_i), Z) is the product of Hom(T_i,MZ), equivalently the product of Hom(LT_i,Z), so L preserves coproducts. To see Lp epic, transpose two maps from LA to Z whose composites with Lp agree. Naturality of the adjunction makes their transposes agree after p, hence they agree since p is epic. The same applies to q. By hypothesis each LG_i is coherent. The calculus lemma makes LP, a finite sum of LG_i, coherent, and LQ quasi-compact. The epimorphism Lq then makes LR quasi-compact. Finite-limit preservation identifies LR with LP times over LA LP through the images of the two projections, so it is the kernel pair of Lp. This stops at exactly the interface needed by the existing quotient node, without presuming the conclusion that LA is coherent.
