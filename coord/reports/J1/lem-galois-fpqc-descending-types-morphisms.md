## Review by J1

I read Stacks Descent 02W3, including its proof, Descent 02W0 and Topologies 022E. The item's three hypotheses match 02W3 with tau = fpqc: arbitrary base change, finite disjoint unions and effectiveness for flat surjective affine covers. Its conclusion is the one-member case. The first new node takes exactly the finite affine-open refinement supplied by 022E, adds the finite coproduct and records the resulting flat surjective affine morphism; the second is 02W0 for two singleton fpqc coverings with a refinement between them. Both statements introduce their schemes, covering hypotheses and maps. Empty affine schemes and an empty finite refinement cause no exception: when the affine base is empty, the empty coproduct is affine and its map to the empty base is surjective. For a one-member refinement, 02W0 specializes directly. I also checked the identity refinement and the trivial cover as small cases of full faithfulness.

Step 1 uses the existing `lem-galois-fpqc-covering-base-change` (022D(3)) to restrict the singleton cover to each affine open; stability of the property under arbitrary base change is hypothesis (1). Step 2 uses the new 022E refinement node to produce a finite affine coproduct over that open. The datum pulled back to it is a datum for a flat surjective map of affines, so hypothesis (3) supplies its effective descent. For this singleton route, the property on the pulled-back object follows already from hypothesis (1); hypothesis (2) is needed in 02W3's general-family formulation but is redundant here. Step 3 uses the new 02W0 full-faithfulness node to descend the compatible isomorphism from the refinement to the original cover. Its inverse descends too, making the map an isomorphism. The cited `def-pd-descent-datum` supplies the compatibility equation.

Step 4 compares the two descents on each nested pair of affine opens. The existing `lem-galois-fpqc-morphisms-descend` says exactly that morphisms between objects pulled back from the base are uniquely determined by compatible morphisms on the singleton cover. Applying it to the comparison and its inverse gives the transition isomorphism, and uniqueness gives the cocycle. The existing `lem-weil-pbc-relative-glueing-affine-opens` (01LH) glues those transitions; `lem-weil-pbc-scheme-morphism-gluing` glues the local comparison maps. The required inverse-image condition for 01LH comes from the transition being an isomorphism over the smaller open. Restriction along an affine inclusion remains fpqc by the cited base-change node, so all applications of singleton morphism descent meet its covering hypothesis. This is also valid when an overlap is nonaffine, since the comparison is checked on its affine opens.

I searched the DAG for 022E and 02W0 and for affine refinement and fully faithful descent-data statements; `lem-weil-pbc-fpqc-local-on-base-criterion` contains a similar refinement in its proof, but its statement is a property-descent criterion and cannot supply this construction. `lem-galois-fpqc-morphisms-descend` gives full faithfulness only for objects already descended from the base, not for arbitrary descent data over a refinement. The blueprint search for these facts found no blueprint node stating either. The recorded LeanSearch, LeanFinder/Loogle and Mathlib source searches found related declarations but no exact leaf. I searched the Lean source by blueprint tag and descent terminology; it has no declaration stating this item or either proposed new node. The new nodes have statements only, and the item cites only statements that give the facts used. I corrected the draft's blueprint ancestry before delivery.
