## What was wrong, and the repair

The returned B3/C4 finding is correct. `def-cbc-enhanced-diagrams-cones` defines individual Kan complexes, right-pinched mapping spaces for pairs of objects, and cocones/colimits inside a specified quasicategory. It defines neither the infinity category of all spaces nor the coherent functor Map_C(c,-). The retained report wrongly attributed both to it; dropping the missing historical node did not resolve that gap.

I added `def-coef-infinity-category-of-spaces`, which defines S_W as the coherent nerve of the simplicial category of W-small Kan complexes with its full simplicial internal Homs. To specify the functor, it follows the actual construction at the start of HTT §5.1.3: rigidify C, form the locally Kan enriched morphism sets Sing|A(x,y)|, apply the enriched covariant Hom functor at c, and take its coherent-nerve adjoint. The node writes the simplex-colimit rigidification, subset-poset simplex categories, singular complex, enriched action, and adjunction-unit composite explicitly. Thus the higher coherence is supplied by an actual simplicial/enriched construction, rather than an unsupported claim that pairwise mapping spaces automatically form a functor. The action on any cocone is the simplicial composite with this functor.

The item now cites the new node and defines compactness by requiring each small kappa-filtered colimiting cocone to remain colimiting after applying that functor into the space category. No existence of a colimit in spaces or unnamed canonical comparison is assumed in order to type this predicate. The accessibility and presentability clauses, the infinite regular cardinal, the small diagram convention, the empty filteredness test, and the essentially small compact generating subcategory are retained.

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: interpreting the old unqualified target “spaces” as only U-small spaces is not well typed for a U'-small C not yet assumed locally U-small. For example the nerve of a one-object group G in U' whose cardinality is at least that of U has mapping space G, a discrete space not equivalent to a U-small space. HTT Definition 5.3.4.5 explicitly uses the larger space category (widehat S) here. I therefore write the target S_{U'} and permit a further ambient universe to contain it; U still governs small diagrams and local smallness. This corrects size bookkeeping and does not impose a local-smallness hypothesis on compactness or alter the intended predicate. The new definition works for each W and a W-small C, so it applies with W=U'.
