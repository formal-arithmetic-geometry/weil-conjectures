Geometric integrality of the residue fibres follows from the universally quantified field-extension definition, not from a theorem limited to curves. sbc-lem-smooth-geometrically-reduced says precisely that a smooth morphism has geometrically reduced fibres; applied over Spec k this gives reduced T. The finite-type-over-Noetherian node gives local Noetherianity too. A field is Noetherian since its only ideals are 0 and 1. Properness supplies quasi-compactness and separatedness. The closed-fibre-triviality statement explicitly assumes a section, which s_x supplies, and proper/flat/lfp geometrically integral fibres, supplied in step 1. The Jacobson node states density of the closed points in every closed subset; using T itself shows a closed set containing them is T. This justifies applying Grauert at all scheme points.
