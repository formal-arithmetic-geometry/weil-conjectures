## New lemma proof plan (not part of its statement)

For `lem-sfb-descent-data-refinement-hom`, let D=(A,alpha), E=(B,beta) be data over Y/S and v an arrow between their restrictions to U/S. On Z=Y times_S U define
h(y,u)=beta(y,a(u))^{-1} v(u) alpha(y,a(u)).
These are pullbacks of fibre arrows, not evaluations at geometric points. The notation denotes their common-base composites using the pseudofunctor comparisons.

The map t:Z to Y is a base change of c=ba, hence universally descends arrows. On Z times_Y Z, the cocycles of alpha and beta and the equation beta(a(u1),a(u2)) v(u1)=v(u2) alpha(a(u1),a(u2)) show h(y,u1)=h(y,u2). Explicitly replace alpha(y,a(u2)) by alpha(a(u1),a(u2)) alpha(y,a(u1)), replace beta(y,a(u2)) by beta(a(u1),a(u2)) beta(y,a(u1)), and cancel using the displayed equation. Hence h descends uniquely to w:A to B over Y. On U times_S U, the same equation says a*w equals v after pullback along the second base-change of c; faithfulness gives a*w=v. Over Y times_S Y times_S U, the formula for h and the two cocycles give beta(y1,y2) w(y1)=w(y2) alpha(y1,y2). This is a base change of c over Y times_S Y, so faithfulness proves the required equation on Y times_S Y. If two compatible w have the same restriction v, the formula recovers identical h; faithfulness along t identifies the two w. This proves full faithfulness. No descent property of a, no surjectivity, and no realization of D or E was used.
