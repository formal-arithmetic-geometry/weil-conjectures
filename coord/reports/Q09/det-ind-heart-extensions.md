There is a concrete warning against silently discarding the ambient condition in the imported continuity route. Take X to be an infinite disjoint union of algebraically closed points, take C to be the rank-one coefficient sheaf, and let D_F be its restriction to a finite set F of components, extended by zero. These sheaves are constructible under the local-on-affines definition. Their filtered colimit over finite F is C. Every map C to D_F vanishes outside F, so the image of colim Hom(C,D_F) in Hom(C,C) cannot contain the identity. Thus even the degree-zero continuity assertion used elsewhere in the unit is false on unrestricted X. The repair here follows the Noetherian setting of the existing corrected continuity node; it does not attempt a different route on arbitrary schemes.
