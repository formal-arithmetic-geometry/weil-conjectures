## Retained proof and task briefs

### Old step 1

Use the canonical coefficient isomorphisms of [lem-weil-pbc-continuous-coefficients-immersion-restriction] to write $B_T=A_T=C(|-|,A)$ and $E_T=C(|-|,E)$ on $X$ and its locally closed subschemes. These isomorphisms preserve constant sections, localization and immersion restriction. Nonarchimedean balls separate points and are clopen, so $E$ is totally disconnected. By [lem-weil-pbc-continuous-coefficient-realizations], the pulled condensed ring for $C(-,E)$ is this continuous coefficient ring, compatibly with the integral-to-field map. The internal/sectionwise module equivalence of [def-weil-pbc-internal-abelian-sheaf-module-equivalence] preserves the underlying sheaves. Thus a local finite free continuous model $E_T^a$ becomes precisely the finite constant model $p_T^*\underline E\otimes_E E^a$, and conversely. We use this identification solely to apply the finite-model filtration, then regard all its sheaves and functors as sectionwise module sheaves.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-continuous-coefficients-immersion-restriction, lem-weil-pbc-continuous-coefficient-realizations, def-weil-pbc-internal-abelian-sheaf-module-equivalence.

### Old step 2

The minimal-closed-subset property of [lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-scheme-closed-subsets-minimal] rules out an infinite strictly descending chain of closed subsets of $X$, so its underlying space is Noetherian. Apply [lem-sfb-finite-model-stratum-filtration] to $F$ through Step 1. It gives $\varnothing=V_0\subset\cdots\subset V_m=X$, reduced closed $t_k:S_k\hookrightarrow V_k$, qcqs $v_k:V_k\hookrightarrow X$, and the monic counit-image filtration $F_k=v_{k!}v_k^*F$ with $F_k/F_{k-1}\cong v_{k!}(t_k)_*L_k$, where $L_k=t_k^*(F|_{V_k})$ has finite constant local models. A finitely presented module over the field $E$ has a finite basis; its corresponding local model is $E_{S_k}^a$. Hence each $L_k$ is lisse in the required continuous sense.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-scheme-closed-subsets-minimal, lem-sfb-finite-model-stratum-filtration.

### Old step 3

Check the geometric hypotheses before taking lattices. The immersions $V_k\to X$ and $S_k\to X$ are locally of finite type by [lem-weil-pbc-immersion-base-change-lft]; [pqf-lem-locnoeth-lft] makes their sources locally Noetherian. Every subset of the Noetherian space $|X|$ is quasi-compact by [lem-sfb-noetherian-space-compact-subsets], so both sources are quasi-compact and therefore Noetherian schemes. For a strict chain of irreducible closed subsets of either locally closed source, taking closures in $X$ gives a strict chain of irreducible closed subsets: intersecting each closure with the source recovers the original subset. This bounds their dimensions by $\dim X$. Finally a local-ring homomorphism sends a unit to a unit, so invertibility of $\ell$ restricts to both sources. The open complements of $t_k$ are qcqs as already supplied by the filtration lemma.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-immersion-base-change-lft, pqf-lem-locnoeth-lft, lem-sfb-noetherian-space-compact-subsets.

### Old step 4

For each $k$, apply [lem-weil-pbc-lisse-e-constructible-integral-lattice] on the scheme $S_k$ verified in Step 3. It gives a continuous-constructible $B_{S_k}$-module $N_k$ with $E_{S_k}\otimes_{B_{S_k}}N_k\cong L_k$. Its finite-stratum models are exactly $B_S\otimes_A P$ with $P$ finitely presented; no global local-freeness of $N_k$ is asserted. The same cited lemma applied directly on $X$ proves the additional assertion about every lisse $E_X$-sheaf.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-lisse-e-constructible-integral-lattice.

### Old step 5

Set $P_k=(t_k)_*N_k$ and $M_k=v_{k!}P_k$, using the actual module functors. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-integral-model-open-closed-pieces] first on $V_k$, with closed part $S_k$, lattice $N_k$ there and zero on its complementary open. It proves that $P_k$ is continuous-constructible on $V_k$. Apply it again on $X$, with open part $V_k$, lattice $P_k$ there and zero on the closed complement. It proves that $M_k$ is continuous-constructible on $X$ and supplies the ordinary scalar-extension comparisons for both functors. Zero is an allowed integral model, represented by the finitely presented scalar module $0$; the zero choices impose no additional support assumption. The coefficient identifications are those fixed in Step 1, and the schemes satisfy the lemma's hypotheses by Step 3.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-integral-model-open-closed-pieces.

### Old step 6

The complex $M_k[0]$ is bounded, has $M_k$ as its sole possibly nonzero cohomology sheaf, and all its cohomology sheaves have the finite-stratum scalar models established in Step 5. Apply condition (iii) implies (i) of [lem-weil-pbc-integral-stratified-perfect-characterization] on $X$. Thus $M_k[0]\in\mathcal C_X$, including derived completeness, a uniformly bounded constructible reduction tower, and compatible etale-origin witnesses. In particular this is membership in the completed category of the statement, rather than just constructibility in the ordinary module category. This argument also puts each lattice $N_k[0]$ in the corresponding completed category on $S_k$, when desired.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-integral-stratified-perfect-characterization.

### Old step 7

Verify the normalization of the scalar comparison rather than choosing an abstract isomorphism. By [lem-weil-pbc-scalar-localization-module-colimit], for any $B_T$-module $H$ its ordinary localization is the colimit of $H\xrightarrow{\varpi}H\xrightarrow{\varpi}\cdots$, with the map from stage $n\geq0$ given by $h\mapsto\varpi^{-n}\otimes h$. By [lem-weil-pbc-proetale-ringed-open-closed], $(t_k)_*$ commutes with this small filtered colimit on the qcqs scheme $V_k$, and $v_{k!}$ commutes with it because it is a left adjoint. Their linearity sends the transition $\varpi$ to $\varpi$. Consequently the colimit comparisons give
$$E_X\otimes_{B_X}v_{k!}(t_k)_*N_k\ \cong\ v_{k!}(t_k)_*(E_{S_k}\otimes_{B_{S_k}}N_k).$$
On every stage both the composite comparison and the separate open/closed comparisons send a local section $h$ to the image of $\varpi^{-n}\otimes h$ under these same functors. The colimit universal property therefore makes them equal; the ring restrictions are precisely those of Step 1. Exactness of the actual open and closed module functors is part of the cited open-closed lemma, so these are the same functors as in the sheaf filtration. Compose with the lattice isomorphism from Step 4 to obtain $E_X\otimes_{B_X}M_k\cong v_{k!}(t_k)_*L_k$. Together with Steps 2 and 6 this proves every required clause.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-scalar-localization-module-colimit, lem-weil-pbc-proetale-ringed-open-closed.
