5. The finite support of an element of an algebraic direct sum places it in one F_J(W), where the retraction kills the possible kernel. The module-sheaf definition ensures all the maps are linear. 6. `def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category` explicitly constructs cokernels by sheafified sectionwise cokernels, and the sheafification definition and the general-site underlying-sets lemma justify application of the local-sections theorem.
