Here are the compatibility details needed beyond naturality. Inverse-image composition is termwise, with no signs. On total cochain tensor complexes, the comparison (P[1]) tensor Q -> (P tensor Q)[1] is identity on each summand, and P tensor Q[1] -> (P tensor Q)[1] multiplies first-factor degree i by (-1)^i; compare the differential in def-kun-derived-tensor. Inverse-image monoidality is induced by degree-preserving tensor comparisons, so it respects these signs. The counit at a K-injective representative is the degreewise sheaf counit; shifting that representative computes the shifted derived pushforward and commutes with its counit square. The unit has the same property: after applying the shifted adjunction bijection its two proposed composites are both identity.
