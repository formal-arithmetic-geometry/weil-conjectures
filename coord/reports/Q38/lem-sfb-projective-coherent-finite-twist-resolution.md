## Algebraic input source and future proof

For the new node I read algebra.tex Tag 00OQ, label proposition-finite-gl-dim-polynomial-ring, at line 27723: a polynomial algebra in m variables over a field has global dimension m. I also read Tag 0EKB, label lemma-graded-NAK, and the integer grading/shift conventions immediately before it. The tag does not literally assert the new graded theorem. Its intended proof, recorded here rather than in the unsplit statement, is: take m successive finite homogeneous free covers, retaining the induced gradings on their kernels. The kernels are finite by polynomial Noetherianity. Global dimension m and dimension shifting make the mth syzygy K projective as an ungraded module. Choose a homogeneous basis of the finite k-space K/S_+K and lift it homogeneously; graded Nakayama gives a finite shifted-free cover F onto K. For its finite graded kernel L, the vanishing of Tor_1(K,k), together with the isomorphism F/S_+F to K/S_+K, gives L/S_+L=0. Graded Nakayama gives L=0, so the final K is shifted-free. This supplies precisely the degree-preserving enhancement absent from the literal text of 00OQ. For m=0, finite graded k-vector spaces have a finite homogeneous basis, with no covers needed. This algebraic input has no Proj, coherence, sheafification or twisting-sheaf assertion, so the parent's geometric proof remains substantive.
