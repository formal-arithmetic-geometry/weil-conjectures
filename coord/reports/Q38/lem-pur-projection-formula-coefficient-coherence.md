## Original argument allocation (verbatim preservation)

### Original Step 1

Work first in the ambient ordinary constant-$A$ derived categories. Put $F=Lr^*$, $R=Rr_*$, $Q=Lj^*$ and $J=j_!$, so $P=RJ$ and $S=QF$ under the canonical composition comparison. [lem-pur-derived-pullback-tensor-iso] supplies the natural tensor comparisons for $F,Q,S$, including their unit, signed symmetry and composition coherence. Use the derived symmetric monoidal structures of [lem-sf-ringed-derived-closed-symmetric-monoidal]. Suppression of parentheses below always means the unique associator/unitor composite of [lem-coef-monoidal-structural-coherence], without permuting letters. Write $\delta^F,\delta^Q$ for the inverse tensor structure maps and $e_F,e_Q$ for the maps from the pulled-back units. Thus $\delta^S=\delta^Q\circ Q\delta^F$ and $e_S=e_Q\circ Qe_F$. For any such pullback $H$ set
$$h^H_N=\lambda_N(e_H\otimes1_N),\qquad a^H_{B,B',N}=\alpha_{HB,HB',N}(\delta^H_{B,B'}\otimes1_N).$$
All calculations below concern these fixed comparisons, in the ambient categories, before assuming any projection component invertible.

Uses: lem-pur-derived-pullback-tensor-iso, lem-sf-ringed-derived-closed-symmetric-monoidal, lem-coef-monoidal-structural-coherence.

### Original Step 2

Let $\rho_{N,B}:RN\otimes B\to R(N\otimes FB)$ and $\gamma_{M,C}:JM\otimes C\to J(M\otimes QC)$ be exactly the proper and open maps in [def-exchange-projection]. Define their left-coefficient versions
$$t_{B,N}=R(\beta_{N,FB})\rho_{N,B}\beta_{B,RN},\qquad v_{C,M}=J(\beta_{M,QC})\gamma_{M,C}\beta_{C,JM}.$$
The adjunction $F\dashv R$ and the counit definition of $\rho$ give, by [lem-weil-pbc-adjunction-of-natural-bijection],
$$\varepsilon_{FB\otimes N}F(t_{B,N})=(1_{FB}\otimes\varepsilon_N)\delta^F_{B,RN}.\tag{1}$$
Indeed transpose the displayed expression for $t$: naturality of $\varepsilon$ moves $R\beta$ outside the counit; naturality of symmetry moves $\varepsilon_N$ past $\beta$, and compatibility of $F$ with signed symmetry moves $F\beta$ past $\delta^F$. The adjacent symmetries cancel. For
$$T_{B,M}=P(\beta_{M,SB})\operatorname{PF}^c_{M,B}\beta_{B,PM},$$
substitution of the two definitions cancels $\beta_{FB,JM}\beta_{JM,FB}$ and gives
$$T_{B,M}=R(v_{FB,M})t_{B,JM}.$$
Here and below the target uses $QF\cong S$. This proves that the composite being studied is the specified PF map, conjugated by symmetry, rather than some other isomorphism.

Uses: def-exchange-projection, lem-weil-pbc-adjunction-of-natural-bijection, lem-pur-derived-pullback-tensor-iso, lem-sf-ringed-derived-closed-symmetric-monoidal.

### Original Step 3

The proper unit equation is
$$R(h^F_N)t_{\mathbf1,N}=\lambda_{RN}.\tag{2}$$
To check it, transpose under $F\dashv R$. Naturality of the counit and (1) make the transpose of the left side
$$h^F_N(1_{F\mathbf1}\otimes\varepsilon_N)\delta^F_{\mathbf1,RN}
=\varepsilon_N\lambda_{FRN}(e_F\otimes1)\delta^F_{\mathbf1,RN}
=\varepsilon_N F(\lambda_{RN}).$$
The middle equality is unitor naturality; the last is the unit identity for $F$ from [lem-pur-derived-pullback-tensor-iso]. The final expression is exactly the transpose of the right side. Injectivity of the adjunction bijection [lem-weil-pbc-adjunction-of-natural-bijection] proves (2). Nothing about $t$ being invertible is used.

Uses: lem-pur-derived-pullback-tensor-iso, lem-weil-pbc-adjunction-of-natural-bijection.

### Original Step 4

The proper coefficient-associativity equation is
$$R(a^F_{B,B',N})t_{B\otimes B',N}
=t_{B,FB'\otimes N}(1_B\otimes t_{B',N})\alpha_{B,B',RN}.\tag{3}$$
Transpose the right side. Formula (1) at $FB'\otimes N$, followed by naturality of $\delta^F$, gives
$$(1_{FB}\otimes[\varepsilon_{FB'\otimes N}F(t_{B',N})])\delta^F_{B,B'\otimes RN}F(\alpha_{B,B',RN}).$$
Insert (1) once more; this becomes
$$(1_{FB}\otimes[(1_{FB'}\otimes\varepsilon_N)\delta^F_{B',RN}])\delta^F_{B,B'\otimes RN}F(\alpha_{B,B',RN}).$$
By associativity coherence of the canonical pullback comparison [lem-pur-derived-pullback-tensor-iso], this equals
$$a^F_{B,B',N}(1_{F(B\otimes B')}\otimes\varepsilon_N)\delta^F_{B\otimes B',RN},$$
which is the transpose of the left side by counit naturality and (1). The bijection [lem-weil-pbc-adjunction-of-natural-bijection] proves (3). This is two substitutions of a defining counit equation, not an appeal to a new coherence theorem.

Uses: lem-pur-derived-pullback-tensor-iso, lem-weil-pbc-adjunction-of-natural-bijection.

### Original Step 5

For the open factor, [lem-sf-open-tensor-normalization] gives fully faithful $J$ and the invertible unit $M\to QJM$. Its canonical $\gamma$ restricts to the identity under that unit and the tensor comparisons. Consequently the same is true of its symmetry-conjugate $v_{C,M}$: naturality of symmetry and $\beta^2=1$ cancel the two swaps after applying $Q$. Thus
$$J(h^Q_M)v_{\mathbf1,M}=\lambda_{JM},\tag{4}$$
$$J(a^Q_{C,C',M})v_{C\otimes C',M}
=v_{C,QC'\otimes M}(1_C\otimes v_{C',M})\alpha_{C,C',JM}.\tag{5}$$
Here is the equality test, rather than a stalkwise argument: the source of (4) is isomorphic via $v$ to an object of $\operatorname{im}J$; the source of (5) is also, via $v_{C\otimes C',M}$; both targets are in $\operatorname{im}J$. On these sources and targets $Q$ is faithful, since $J$ is fully faithful and $QJ\cong1$. Applying $Q$ to (4) gives the unit constraint for $Q$ on $M$. Applying it to (5) gives the associativity constraint for $Q$ on $C,C',JM$; these hold by [lem-pur-derived-pullback-tensor-iso]. This proves both equalities of actual derived morphisms. No separation of the ambient schemes is required.

Uses: lem-sf-open-tensor-normalization, lem-pur-derived-pullback-tensor-iso, lem-sf-ringed-derived-closed-symmetric-monoidal.

### Original Step 6

Paste the unit equations. Since $e_S=e_Q\circ Qe_F$, naturality of $v$ in its coefficient gives
$$\begin{aligned}
P(h^S_M)T_{\mathbf1,M}
&=R\bigl(J(h^Q_M)J(Qe_F\otimes1)v_{F\mathbf1,M}\bigr)t_{\mathbf1,JM}\\
&=R\bigl(J(h^Q_M)v_{\mathbf1,M}(e_F\otimes1)\bigr)t_{\mathbf1,JM}\\
&=R(h^F_{JM})t_{\mathbf1,JM}=\lambda_{PM}.
\end{aligned}\tag{6}$$
The third equality is (4), and the last is (2). Naturality of $v$ follows from the specified natural $\gamma$ in [lem-sf-open-tensor-normalization] and tensor symmetry. The equation for $e_S$ and the identifications with $S=Lf^*$ are the composition/unit coherence of [lem-pur-derived-pullback-tensor-iso]. These are functorial substitutions inside the fixed compactification.

Uses: lem-sf-open-tensor-normalization, lem-pur-derived-pullback-tensor-iso.

### Original Step 7

Paste the coefficient equations. In the following display put $M'=SB'\otimes M$, and suppress only the comparisons fixed in Step 1. Expanding $T=R(v)t$ and using naturality of $t$ in $N$ at $v_{FB',M}$ yields
$$\begin{aligned}
&T_{B,M'}(1_B\otimes T_{B',M})\alpha_{B,B',PM}\\
&=R\bigl(v_{FB,M'}(1_{FB}\otimes v_{FB',M})\bigr)
 t_{B,FB'\otimes JM}(1_B\otimes t_{B',JM})\alpha_{B,B',RJM}\\
&=R\bigl(v_{FB,M'}(1_{FB}\otimes v_{FB',M})a^F_{B,B',JM}\bigr)t_{B\otimes B',JM}\\
&=P(a^S_{B,B',M})R(v_{F(B\otimes B'),M})t_{B\otimes B',JM}\\
&=P(a^S_{B,B',M})T_{B\otimes B',M}.
\end{aligned}\tag{7}$$
The second equality is (3). For the third, apply (5) to $C=FB,C'=FB'$ and then use naturality of $v$ at $\delta^F_{B,B'}:F(B\otimes B')\to FB\otimes FB'$. The resulting target map is $J(a^S_{B,B',M})$, since $\delta^S=\delta^Q\circ Q\delta^F$ by [lem-pur-derived-pullback-tensor-iso]. Naturality of $t$ is immediate from its counit-adjoint definition [def-exchange-projection]. This proves (7) without any right adjoint of $P$ or any change of compactification.

Uses: def-exchange-projection, lem-sf-open-tensor-normalization, lem-pur-derived-pullback-tensor-iso.

### Original Step 8

Now impose precisely the invertibility hypotheses in the item. By the definition in Step 2 and $\beta_{V,U}\beta_{U,V}=1$,
$$T_{B,M}^{-1}=\beta_{PM,B}(\operatorname{PF}^c_{M,B})^{-1}P(\beta_{SB,M})=\pi_{B,M}.$$
Right-compose (6) with $\pi_{\mathbf1,M}$ to obtain
$$\lambda_{PM}\pi_{\mathbf1,M}=P(h^S_M).$$
In (7), right-compose with $\pi_{B\otimes B',M}$ and left-compose first with $\pi_{B,SB'\otimes M}$ and then with $1_B\otimes\pi_{B',M}$. Cancellation gives
$$\alpha_{B,B',PM}\pi_{B\otimes B',M}
=(1_B\otimes\pi_{B',M})\pi_{B,SB'\otimes M}P(a^S_{B,B',M}).$$
Substitution of $h^S$ and $a^S$ from Step 1 is exactly the two equations of [def-sfb-projection-formula] displayed in the item. Only the components assumed invertible in those expressions were inverted; this proves no new invertibility assertion.

Uses: lem-sf-ringed-derived-closed-symmetric-monoidal, def-sfb-projection-formula.

Finally check the bounded constructible inputs. [lem-sf-pullback-tor-amplitude] applies to the given separated finite-type $f$ and puts $SB,SB'$ in $D^b_{ctf}(X,A)$. [lem-sf-tensor-ctf](ii) puts $B\otimes B'$ in $D^b_{ctf}(Y,A)$; clause (i) puts $SB'\otimes M$ and then $SB\otimes(SB'\otimes M)$ in $D^b_c(X,A)$, without finite Tor amplitude for $M$.
