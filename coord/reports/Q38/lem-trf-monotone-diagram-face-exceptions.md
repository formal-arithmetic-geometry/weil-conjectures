## Arguments retained for future workers

These are report material, not proof sketches hidden in the statement-only nodes. The original seven steps are also retained verbatim in retained-proof.json.

For lem-trf-monotone-vertex-prefix: every v in D dominates a listed vertex. The finite nonempty set {u in D : u<=v} contains v; choose u minimizing its coordinate sum. Any w in D with w<=u and w!=u would have strictly smaller sum, so u is minimal and equals a listed a_h. Conversely additive closure puts every vector above a vertex in D. This is old Step 1, with no appeal to Dickson finiteness.

For a_h,p>0 and p<t, monotonicity puts gamma=a_h-a_h,p e_p+a_h,p e_t in D. Choose a_l<=gamma by the preceding domination argument. Its degree is at most |gamma|=|a_h|. If strictly smaller, l<h. At equal degree, coordinatewise domination forces a_l=gamma. Coordinates before p agree with a_h and the p-coordinate is zero strictly below a_h,p, so gamma is strictly smaller in lex order and again l<h. This retains the entire ordering argument of old Steps 3--4.

For v in U_q choose h<=q with a_h<=v. Addition closure of U_q is immediate. To check a move p<t on v, if a_h,p=0 then a_h remains below the moved v: at p both are zero, at t the upper vector only increases, elsewhere nothing changes. If a_h,p>0 use the earlier a_l dominated by the moved a_h. Moving both vectors preserves their comparison: both p-coordinates become zero, their t-coordinates become the sums of the old p and t coordinates, and other coordinates stay ordered. Hence a_l is below the moved v, with l<h<=q. Thus the prefix is monotone. This exposes rather than hides the extension needed in the assembly.

For lem-trf-monotone-boundary-box: choose a in A below v+e_k. Since v is outside U, a is not below v. Only coordinate k changed; hence a_k=v_k+1>0 and a_t<=v_t for t!=k. Therefore v_k+1<=a_k<=|a|<=M, giving v_k<M. This is exactly the witness calculation of old Steps 2 and 5, now for any finite generating family.

If j>k and v_j>=M, whole-coordinate monotonicity puts gamma=a-a_k e_k+a_k e_j in U. Its k-coordinate is zero<=v_k; its j-coordinate is a_j+a_k<=|a|<=M<=v_j; each other coordinate is a_t<=v_t. Thus gamma<=v, and additive closure of a union of orthants forces v in U, a contradiction. This retains every coordinate inequality of old Steps 3 and 6. Hence v_j<M for all j>=k. No ordering is used by this boundary argument; the prefix lemma supplies the required monotone U in the vertex case. Old Step 4's contradiction is represented by prefix closure followed by this upper-set contradiction. Old Step 7's finite box and all its edge cases are retained explicitly in assembly Step 4.
