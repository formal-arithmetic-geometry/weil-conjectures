## Where the brief, the DAG or earlier work was wrong

The earlier DAG invokes the local-sections theorem in a universe containing the
objects and arrows, but the Lean statement specifies only a Type u-valued universal
property on the big site of schemes in universe u (whose objects live in Type (u+1)).
Applying that theorem directly does not elaborate, and enlarging F or hη would change
the statement. The new size argument resolves this: refine an fppf representative cover
by affine opens, use the essentially small category of affine schemes locally of
finite type over the test scheme, and encode sections by all their local representatives.
This gives small values for the usual large sheafification. Shrinking then permits the
given universal property to be used exactly as stated.

The edge cases require no exclusions: the affine refinement and sieve sheaf condition
include empty covers and the empty test scheme. Monicity is used only for uniqueness;
`hrel` supplies exactly the equivalence relation used by `Quotient.exact`. The unit
`sk-i-curve-zero` route and the kernel-pair assertion are preserved.
