## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported opening does not fix or introduce its geometric-Frobenius datum. The current blueprint update fixes this, and the retained split already incorporated that correction. The source's `u_K` notation is retained.

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the current cited `def-pur-scheme-geometric-frobenius` constructs its comparison on `D(T_proet,A_{E,n})`. It does not supply a comparison on completed, rationalized or common-stage categories by itself. The repaired definition specifies the integral comparison as a map of actual strict reduction towers, followed by functorial derived limit, then gives its rationalized and common-stage forms. The relative-Frobenius site identification acts on the site while the quotient acts on coefficients, so their two composites agree on sections; this explicitly supplies the tower map. The inverse is constructed from the inverse finite comparisons. No lift of an arbitrary diagram in an ordinary derived category is asserted.

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the exceptional formula previously applied `def-pur-smooth-pullback-exchange` outside its current stated domain. That definition is finite-coefficient and requires a Cartesian square of k-morphisms between finite-type k-schemes. The geometric Frobenius maps are semilinear: on `Spec k`, the k-morphism condition would make `Spec(gamma)` the identity, whereas gamma is nontrivial on the algebraic closure. I repaired the application by equipping the left copies of T with `a'_T = Spec(gamma) a_T = a_T g_T`. The horizontal maps then are k-morphisms; `a'_Y f = a'_X` checks the left vertical map. This is the same underlying scheme square and the same underlying exceptional functor. At integral and field coefficients the definition now specifies the analogous purity composite with the existing coefficient transports instead of claiming that the finite exchange definition itself applies there.

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: finite type of f_0 alone does not put X and Y in the domain of the cited purity maps. For example, let Y_0 be an infinite disjoint union of copies of `Spec k_0` and let f_0 be its identity. It is separated, finite type as a morphism and smooth of relative dimension zero, but its geometric fibre is not a finite-type k-scheme. The definitions of the purity map and smooth exchange do not cover it. For the Tate and smooth-purity constructions I have made the separated finite-type k-scheme convention explicit, as required by their existing definitions. The strict-datum definition and canonical finite Frobenius comparison are not asserted to need this extra condition. This is a domain correction to the intended purity setting, not a replacement of the lecture-notes route.

The scalar claim now cites `lem-pur-twist-frobenius-scalar` directly, rather than treating the twist-definition node as a proof of it, and explicitly concerns the coefficient line over `Spec k`.
