Original Step 3:

Original Step 4:

Check the inverse-image counit for these $\beta$ maps. Write $C_W=-\otimes^L_{R_{E,W}}R_{L,W}$ and $\chi_W:C_W\xrightarrow{\sim}T_W$ as in [lem-weil-finite-free-completed-scalar-comparison]. In the sectionwise model of [lem-weil-proetale-isomorphism-inverse-image], $b$ only reindexes the tensor: $x\otimes a\mapsto g^{-1}(x_0\otimes a_0)$ with the transported coefficient action. Reindexing along $g$ and then $\bar g$ returns both $x$ and $a$ to themselves. Hence on the same complexes $u_{L,C_YK}\,H_L(b^g_K)b^{\bar g}_{G_EK}=C_Y(u_{E,K})$. This equality persists under sheafification and localization because both maps are this identical sectionwise tensor map. Substitute the displayed formulas for $\beta$; the adjacent $H_L(\chi_X^{-1})H_L(\chi_X)$ cancel. Naturality of $u_L$ and then of $\chi_Y$ gives
$$u_{L,T_YK}H_L(\beta^g_K)\beta^{\bar g}_{G_EK}=\chi_{Y,K}C_Y(u_{E,K})\chi_{Y,H_EG_EK}^{-1}=T_Y(u_{E,K}).$$

Original Step 5:

$$u_L\,H_L(\beta^g_K)\,H_L(T_X\lambda^{g,E}_K)\,\beta^{\bar g}_{P_EK}\,T_Y(\lambda^{\bar g,E}_{P_EK}).$$

$$T_Y\bigl(u_{E,K}\circ H_E(\lambda^{g,E}_K)\circ\lambda^{\bar g,E}_{P_EK}\bigr)=T_Y(\epsilon^E_K).$$
The naturality of $\lambda$ is part of [lem-weil-isomorphism-derived-pullback]; that of $\beta$ follows directly from its natural $b,\chi$ formula in [lem-weil-isomorphism-comparison-scalar-extension]. All these arrows have the source and target displayed in clause (2).
