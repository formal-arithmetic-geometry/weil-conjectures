The locally free rank-one module admits a Zariski affine trivializing cover; [def-asd-vector-bundle-zero-section] identifies the projection and zero section on each member $U=\operatorname{Spec}R$ with $\operatorname{Spec}R[t]\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ and the quotient $R[t]\to R$, $t\mapsto0$. A field is Noetherian (its ideals are zero and the unit ideal), so [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian] makes $B$ Noetherian. Apply [lem-amb-vector-projective-completion] to this rank-one locally free module, transporting its construction through the chosen total-space isomorphism: it gives a projective completion of $E$, and asserts that $\pi$ is separated and finite type and $E$ Noetherian. These are the compactification and finiteness hypotheses needed below. To check the absolute hypotheses, choose a finite affine trivializing cover of $B$ with coordinate rings finitely generated over $k$. Then $E$ has the corresponding finite affine cover with rings $R[t]$, also finitely generated over $k$, so it is finite type by [def-weil-finite-type-morphism]. Its absolute diagonal factors through $E\times_BE$: the first arrow is the closed relative diagonal of $\pi$, and the second is the pullback of the closed diagonal of $B/k$. On affine charts these are quotients of rings; tensoring a surjection and composing surjections preserve surjectivity. Thus $E/k$ is separated by [def-weil-proper-morphism].
