## Where the brief, the DAG or earlier work was wrong

The earlier sandbox core theorem restated the original hypotheses and conclusion. It did
not isolate a part of the argument and has been removed, along with the earlier report's
second-reading sections. No previous review is represented as approval of this revision.

The original node's first two steps do not need the full polynomial height-one calculation:
the supplied integral effective Cartier centre plus regularity at its generic image point
already gives the DVR. The finite-free step in the third part is stronger than necessary;
Mathlib directly proves flatness of a torsion-free module over a Dedekind domain/DVR.

Edge cases checked mathematically: an empty divisor has no eta; a zero-dimensional ambient
regular stalk cannot admit the displayed nonzero principal Cartier maximal ideal. An empty
cover has no normalization point over s. After base change to a DVR, the open missing its
closed point is empty or the generic point. Disconnected generic etale algebras cause no
problem, since the argument uses a vector space over the fraction field rather than assuming
the generic algebra is a domain. The argument includes n=1 and imposes no ramification bound.
The existential domain/DVR witnesses are proofs about the specified stalk, not arbitrary
objects chosen from an unproved existence theorem.
