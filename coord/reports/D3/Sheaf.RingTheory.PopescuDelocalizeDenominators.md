Added the fully proved theorem `Algebra.exists_rescaled_integral_presentation` in the new module
`Sheaf.RingTheory.PopescuRescaledPresentation`. It constructs the rescaled generators using
localization fractions, fixes the original generators, transports the presentation kernel through
an explicitly constructed diagonal polynomial automorphism, and clears each equation's
coefficients. An induction proves the chain rule for that substitution. The evaluated Jacobian is
the original unit minor multiplied by finite products of units, using the two determinant scaling
lemmas. This theorem requires only a smooth presentation, the map to localized Lambda, and the
prescribed images of the fixed generators; it does not repeat the original theorem's certificate
or original-relation conclusions.

The remaining denominator choices are proved directly using Mathlib's ideal localization
criterion. In particular, membership of 1 in the localized ideal generated by the minor and the
equations yields an exact certificate before localization. This avoids separately clearing all
coefficients of an inverse-minor identity. The original relations vanish by compatibility of the
evaluation maps, and the evaluations of the smooth equations are annihilated using the
localization kernel criterion in Lambda. No injectivity assumption is used.
