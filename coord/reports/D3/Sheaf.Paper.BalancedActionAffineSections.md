## What changed

Proved `AlgebraicGeometry.balancedAction_affine_sections` without changing its signature,
visibility, or public imports. Added proof imports of
`Sheaf.Paper.BalancedActionAffineOpen` and
`Sheaf.Paper.AffineBaseChangeSectionsInduction`, two new fully proved modules.

The first constructs the single-open action in the commutative subring generated by scalar
multiplications and evaluated rho endomorphisms. Mathlib's affine section-ring pushout gives
the action, and generation proves uniqueness. The second proves generation using the subring
of sections satisfying the predicate and Mathlib pushout generation. The main theorem
proves restriction compatibility on generators, sums and products and assembles the unique
family. Its chosen data comes from a unique-existence theorem that is itself fully proved.

All three files compile with the project compiler. The new statements cover empty affine
opens and zero rings, and the action construction covers zero modules. No affine intersections,
flatness, finiteness, or localization of M is assumed. Source pages read: Stacks
[01LX](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01LX),
[01SB](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01SB), and
[0897](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0897).

## No longer needed

The original module had only its three public statement imports; none was removed.
The planned direct proof dependency `lem-weil-pbc-relative-spec-base-change`, module
`Sheaf.AlgebraicGeometry.RelativeSpecBaseChange`, is unnecessary for this proof and was
not imported. Mathlib's `isIso_pushoutSection_of_isAffineOpen` supplies the required
geometric section-ring interface directly. The descendant dependencies of relative-Spec
base change are not required through this edge: affine-morphism permanence,
affine pushforward base change, structure-sheaf pullback, structure-algebra pullback
transport, and affine-morphism relative Spec. This makes no claim that these modules
are globally unreachable; other users can still need them.

The two new helper modules use only Mathlib and the existing statement import of
`WeilConjectures.AlgebraicGeometry.Cohomology.Defs`, plus the proved generation helper.
Their proofs require no additional Sheaf theory modules. The original proof's module-sheaf
notion remains necessary for semilinearity and naturality. The unit's statement and
realized proof steps are preserved.
