To prove arrow compatibility for a:V' to V over T, let r_a:G_u to a_*G_{u'} be restriction on every open of V and transpose it to a^*G_u to G_{u'}. Compare a_{u'} with the composite of this transpose, a^*a_u, and the inverse normalized composition comparison u'^*M to a^*u^*M.
