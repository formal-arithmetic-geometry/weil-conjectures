Mathieu's Section 1 (pp.150--152) defines the distribution/hyperalgebra action and simple-root divided powers. Our new generator statement is the lowest-weight contragredient version: W=V(lambda)^* has generator of weight -lambda, and its positive-simple-root string length is <lambda,alpha_i^vee>. This avoids interchanging a Weyl module with an induced module or claiming the induced module is simple.
