## Source error

The target statement is unchanged. The blueprint proof's sentence “The fibrewise map is an isomorphism for every P: choose a bounded complex of finite free modules representing P” has an invalid justification for arbitrary P. For example, the module Z/ell over Z/ell² has infinite projective dimension: the periodic free resolution with differential multiplication by ell gives nonzero Tor with Z/ell in every nonnegative degree. It cannot have a bounded finite-free representative. This does not refute the source's stronger projection-formula assertion, but it refutes its asserted representative for arbitrary P. The argument needs only P equal to the stalk of the stipulated finite-Tor constructible L, which `fix-lem-finite-tor-perfect` covers explicitly on the small étale site. The unit route is retained with that proof correction.
