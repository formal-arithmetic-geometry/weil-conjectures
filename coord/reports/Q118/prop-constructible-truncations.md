## Proofs proposed for the two new statement-only nodes

For `lem-pod-texact-truncation-comparison`, apply F to the truncation triangle at m. Its end objects belong to D<=m and D>=m+1 because F is t-exact, and the triangle is distinguished because F is triangulated. Orthogonality gives the canonical factorization into tau<=m(FK) and out of tau>=m(FK), with the stated canonical-map equations. These maps are invertible by uniqueness of the truncation triangle. More explicitly, for X in D<=m the truncation map induces a bijection Hom(X,tau<=m Y) -> Hom(X,Y), since the adjacent Hom groups to tau>=m+1 Y and its shift [-1] vanish; dually Hom(tau>=m Y,Z) -> Hom(Y,Z) is bijective for Z in D>=m. These bijections prove uniqueness of the comparisons, naturality in K, and the displayed equation for any natural transformation eta by composing both sides with the canonical map. Applying uniqueness once more to the identity and composite functors gives the last two coherence assertions. No triangulatedness of truncation itself is used.

For `lem-pod-scalar-localization-transformations`, the R-linear map on Hom modules extends by tensor universality to u tensor a -> a F(u) in the S-module Hom_D(FK,FM). The tensor balancing relation is respected by R-linearity. Bilinearity of composition proves preservation of composition on pure tensors; distributivity gives it for finite sums; identities go to identities. This also proves the S-linearity and uniqueness of the extended functor. For eta:F=>G, on a pure tensor u tensor a the naturality equation is Gbar(u tensor a) eta_K = a G(u) eta_K = a eta_M F(u) = eta_M Fbar(u tensor a). Additivity covers every morphism, even in the presence of t-power torsion. Objects are unchanged, so the components determine the extension uniquely. Identity, composition, postcomposition and equations are checked on these same components, and applying this to eta and its inverse proves the isomorphism assertion. This lemma covers arbitrary linear targets where t is invertible, including coefficient-changing functors; it does not require either triangulatedness or an unproved categorical-localization universal property for Q.
