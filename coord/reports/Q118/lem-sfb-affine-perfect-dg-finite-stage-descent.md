## Argument for the new unsplit prerequisite

This is an agent refinement of the proof of 08S9, not a claim that 08S9 states a dg-algebra result. Free polynomial algebra on a set commutes with colimits; the underlying-set functor on algebras commutes with filtered colimits. Hence every fixed finite iterate (F_D U)^(n+1) commutes with filtered colimits. The evaluation faces and unit degeneracies commute with these comparisons by their functorial formulas. In degree n the quotient normalization is the cokernel of the map from the finite sum of the degeneracy sources to P_n. Colimits commute with this cokernel. Tensoring with fixed S commutes with the colimit as well. Thus the comparison is an isomorphism on every module degree. The alternating-face differential and each fixed shuffle product are finite sums of composites of those structural maps and multiplication, so the degreewise comparison respects both. This proves the dg-algebra assertion, including noninjective transitions. The degreewise disjoint-union/eventual-equality presentation gives homogeneous element lifting; after evaluation of each expression at a common stage, eventual equality in that degree gives its vanishing at a later stage. Directedness supplies one upper bound for the finitely many expressions. This lemma contains no perfect object, cell-module descent or retract descent, and is a separate functorial compatibility theorem.
