## What was wrong or incomplete

The earlier sandbox split was not ready. Its categorical-fibration support node omitted the colour-fibre equivalence hypothesis and simply asserted almost all of the item's conclusion. That assertion is false without a condition on colours: the fold map from two disjoint copies of an operad to that operad satisfies the stated lifting conditions, while restriction would send a section to two equal copies and cannot realize two inequivalent sections independently. For example, take the commutative operad as base and the cartesian monoidal operad of sets as target; the sections include inequivalent commutative monoids. The fold's colour functor is not an equivalence. The previous proof also treated marking/functor-category invariance and the factor's colour condition as automatic without justifying the relevant strict pullbacks.

I replaced that attempt rather than adding the missing hypothesis to an otherwise unchanged restatement. The new weak-equivalence input concerns a single marked map in the absolute symmetric operadic model, and the other input recognizes a relative target as a fibration. The actual six-step item proof constructs its slice enriched Hom, proves the Hom functor right Quillen by displaying its cotensor and corner map, and then identifies the resulting weak equivalence with restriction in every simplicial degree. Neither new node asserts the item's equivalence of section categories. No dispute or change to the repository item's statement was necessary.
