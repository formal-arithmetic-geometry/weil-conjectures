## Source error

HA Remark 4.2.2.26 factors a marked cross edge through a bottom edge and the final vertical edge, and asserts both factors inert. The first factor need not be inert in that order. For example take the cross edge `([1],0) -> ([0],1)` opposite to the map `[0] -> [1]` selecting 0. Its image under gamma has empty target tuple and is inert. Its bottom factor has LCut pointed map from the two inputs a,m to the single m output, and hence is not inert. The valid factorization is the vertical edge at the source followed by the top edge. Both of these are inert. The final prism node and step 5 state and use this valid factorization. This is a correction of a proof sentence in the source, not a change of the item's statement.
