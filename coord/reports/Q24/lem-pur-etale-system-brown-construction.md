## Problems found and corrections

The item was statement-only. Its existing searches were retained and five actual Q24 searches added, with the exact query strings from searches.jsonl. None states the full item. No statement correction or route departure is needed.

Two tempting shortcuts were avoided by writing the actual argument: an invertible family of evaluated projection maps does not itself construct a morphism of derived systems, and evaluations are not assumed faithful on morphisms. Steps 6–8 first construct unit-normalized conjugacy maps and adjunction mates in the derived system categories. Evaluation is used only to identify their canonical transpose and test invertibility. I did not use the overlapping `lem-pur-etale-tower-brown-adjunction`: its hypotheses impose separatedness on X and Y and a uniformizer, and using it would also hide the core construction of this item.

The source's gluing formalism is unnecessary here because the item fixes a compactification. The proof uses its open and proper factors, as Zheng's Construction 5.15 and Lemmas 5.13 and 5.16 do. It asserts no compactification-independence or normalization of the Brown output beyond the actual clauses of this item. The blueprint ancestor's adic-stage paragraph asks for compatible finite-level functors before inverse limit; this is that intermediate construction. The unit and chapter records are unchanged.
