## Source error

For $Z_0=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf F_q$, take the rank-one Weil sheaf with geometric Frobenius $-1$. Its geometric $H_c^0(\operatorname{Spec}k,\mathcal G)$ is its one-dimensional stalk. The invariant space in the blueprint's formula is zero, since $-1-1=-2$ is invertible over $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$ (also when $\ell=2$). This example even comes from a finite-order continuous arithmetic representation. Thus the displayed equality in the blueprint is false, while the lemma's weight conclusion holds: the eigenvalue has weight zero. For $Z_0=\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf F_{q^2}$ and the constant rank-one sheaf, geometric cohomology has dimension two; the proposed invariant formula has only one summand of dimension one. The new proof uses all geometric stalks and the local return operator; it never takes arithmetic invariants.
