Step 3 again uses that same exactness statement, at both K_i and Q_{i-1}. Step 4 is elementary relative to `def-weil-pbc-module-localization`, whose statement explicitly gives m/1=0 iff some element of the multiplicative subset annihilates m. Step 5 uses localization exactness at Q_{i-1}, with zero localized left term from step 4, so multiplication is injective. The quotient and multiplication identifications were proved in step 2. No flatness, normality, nonzero-divisor localization theorem, or stronger ring hypothesis is silently used.

Step 6 uses the second clause of `def-weil-pbc-regular-sequence`, not just weak regularity, and the explicit equality criterion of `def-weil-ring-localization-away`. A member x_i outside p would be invertible in R_p and would kill its final quotient. Thus all x_i lie in p. If the final ideal in R_g contained 1, a common power denominator and the localization equality criterion would put a power of g in p, contradicting primality and g outside p. The argument is elementary arithmetic with the cited definitions; it verifies precisely the condition omitted from the brief final sentence of 061L.
