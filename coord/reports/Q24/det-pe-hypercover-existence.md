## Source error

The blueprint proof's part 2 says that, for each selected affine chart B_j, the intersection H ∩ B_j is covered by finitely many opens standard in both affines. This need not hold when V is only quasi-compact. For a field k, take R = k[x_1,x_2,...] and glue two copies of Spec R along O = union_n D(x_n). The resulting scheme V is quasi-compact, while the intersection of its two affine charts is O, which is not quasi-compact. Take X = V and the identity object of X_proetale, so this example also meets the site-object hypothesis. No finite subfamily of the displayed principal opens covers O: after finitely many variables have been used, a prime containing those variables and avoiding a different variable is missed. More generally any finite union of principal opens is quasi-compact, so O has no finite principal-open cover. Taking H and B_j to be the two charts contradicts the quoted per-intersection finiteness assertion.

Step 3 repairs the proof: choose affine neighborhoods inside all the intersections, and extract one finite subcover of H collectively. Since each selected faithfully flat map has affine source and affine target, the inverse image of each chosen affine neighborhood is affine. This proves exactly the original clause 2 with no extra hypothesis. There is no statement dispute or statement change. This is a correction below the unit route, not a replacement of that route.
