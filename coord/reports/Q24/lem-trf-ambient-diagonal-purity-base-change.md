Choose etale coordinates about a smooth section and remove the other components of the inverse image of zero. In these coordinates the Rees algebra has x_j=t u_j, so the family is smooth with standard normal fibre and the section's conormal is free of rank d. This also proves that the Rees construction of this smooth pair commutes with arbitrary base change, rather than assuming arbitrary base-change compatibility for all blowups. The existing smooth-tensor theorem puts the section invariants in one degree with locally free rank-one sheaves. The finite-ring affine-space adjunction gives both local trivializations over A^1, with the same Kummer-normalized counit. Normalize their comparison at t=1. Geometric stalk detection and sheaf-Hom gluing give existence and uniqueness globally. Pullback and etale changes preserve the value at t=1, so uniqueness gives the stated compatibility. The normal-bundle chart is the differential; linear trace invariance identifies its integration. This argument works over A itself; it needs no flatness over Z/ell^n and no SGA trace-route purity.
