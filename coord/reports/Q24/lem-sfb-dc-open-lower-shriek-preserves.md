### lem-sfb-lfp-scalar-isomorphism

1. By clause 5 of [sk-def-sfb-change-of-scalars], restriction along $\varphi$ and along $\varphi^{-1}$ are inverse equivalences. For the regular modules the map $\varphi:R\to\operatorname{Res}_\varphi S$ is linear since $\varphi(rs)=\varphi(r)\varphi(s)$ and has linear inverse. On finite direct sums the componentwise maps give $R^n\cong\operatorname{Res}_\varphi(S^n)$: the biproduct equations in [def-sfb-finitely-presented-module] are unchanged under restriction, which is additive and the identity on underlying morphisms.

Uses: sk-def-sfb-change-of-scalars, def-sfb-finitely-presented-module

2. If $N$ is the cokernel of $d:S^m\to S^n$, the inverse equivalences give bijections on module morphisms that preserve composition and zero. The cokernel universal property of [def-sfb-finitely-presented-module] therefore makes $\operatorname{Res}_\varphi N$ the cokernel of $\operatorname{Res}_\varphi d$. Conjugating by the finite-free isomorphisms of step 1 gives a presentation $R^m\to R^n\to\operatorname{Res}_\varphi N\to0$. Applying the same argument to $\varphi^{-1}$ proves reflection. This uses actual arbitrary presentation maps, without any scalar-constant requirement.

Uses: sk-def-sfb-change-of-scalars, def-sfb-finitely-presented-module

3. Choose any pro-étale cover $\{u_a:X_a\to X\}$ as in [def-sfb-locally-finitely-presented]. The commutative-square comparison [def-sfb-comap-mapmod-square], with $F=u_a^*$, $\eta_R$ and $\eta_S$ identities, identifies $\operatorname{mapMod}(u_a^*)\operatorname{Res}_\varphi N$ with $\operatorname{Res}_{u_a^*\varphi}\operatorname{mapMod}(u_a^*)N$. The map $u_a^*\varphi$ is an isomorphism, its inverse being the image of $\varphi^{-1}$. Apply steps 1–2 on each $X_a$, preserving its ranks. This proves the equivalence on the same cover. Finally $\operatorname{Ext}_\varphi\cong\operatorname{Res}_{\varphi^{-1}}$ by [sk-def-sfb-change-of-scalars], so the extension assertion follows. Composing a cokernel presentation isomorphism with a module isomorphism preserves that presentation.

Uses: def-sfb-locally-finitely-presented, def-sfb-comap-mapmod-square, sk-def-sfb-change-of-scalars
