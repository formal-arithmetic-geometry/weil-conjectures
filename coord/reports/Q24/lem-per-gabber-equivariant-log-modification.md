The first gives the exact divisorial fs structure, its boundary and triviality locus; the second makes excellence imply quasi-excellence. Stabilizer orders divide the invertible group order, which directly verifies tameness; preserving the boundary preserves the divisorial subsheaf, so the action is by log automorphisms. Integrality, separatedness, Noetherianity and generic freeness are already the item's hypotheses.

For step 2 I opened the complete statement of `lem-per-gabber-absolute-equivariant-modification`. It accepts an integral quasi-excellent separated Noetherian fs log regular scheme with tame generically free action, with no admissibility or global quasi-projectivity hypothesis for its primary conclusion. Its Z and F are exactly this item's B and T. It supplies the integral source and the isomorphism over the full inverse image of the complement, so no birational inverse-image assertion is silently inferred. `def-pc-integral-scheme` supplies irreducibility for the dense intersection. I read `lem-trf-log-regular-divisorial-structure`: its statement identifies the supplied log structure with O intersect j_* O^times at the actual triviality open. That is exactly what turns the fs log scheme conclusion into the required pair; it is not a definition cited for a comparison theorem. The inertia actions remain the same under this canonical structural identification, hence very tameness is preserved.

For step 3 `lem-trf-projective-bundle-proper` says the specific projective-bundle predicate implies properness. `def-weil-proper-morphism` and `def-weil-finite-type-morphism` then give quasi-compactness. I wrote out the finite-union argument for quasi-compactness of the source. `def-trf-fs-log-blowup` explicitly assumes local Noetherianity when defining the log regular fs objects in use, so local Noetherianity is not inferred from an unstated normalization theorem. Together they give Noetherianity. I do not need finite dimensionality or a scheme of finite type over a field.

For step 4 I opened all four cited statements. `def-trf-projective-bundle-morphism` gives the finite-type quasi-coherent module and closed embedding. `lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-base-change` applies to any base morphism and gives the restricted closed embedding. `def-nagata-relative-proj` explicitly includes the identification over each affine open with Proj of the ring of sections. Clause (b) of `lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-symmetric-algebra` identifies that ring with the symmetric algebra on the module of sections. Thus the two restrictions are the same projective bundle on the same affine charts, with the same degree-one generators. Restriction of the local module presentations gives finite type and quasi-coherence on W. This is an elementary restriction argument from those interfaces, not a new general base-change theorem being assumed.

For step 5 I opened `def-weil-pbc-ample-invertible-module`, `lem-trf-projective-bundle-to-h-projective` and `def-kun-projective`. The definition of ample used here asks for a nonvanishing affine locus through every point on a quasi-compact scheme. On affine W the unit section of O_W has nonvanishing locus all of W and verifies it directly. The theorem then supplies an actual embedding into a finite projective space. No global embedding over C or descent of H-projectivity from an arbitrary etale cover is used. An arbitrary affine W need not be G-invariant, and this argument does not require that it be.

For step 6 I checked the integrality definition again. A nonempty intersection of dense opens in C is nonempty; its full inverse image is nonempty via the isomorphism, and is open and dense in integral C_1. This proves density for every dense open V. The proof uses the full-preimage isomorphism and does not merely identify an unspecified open of C_1 with an open of C.

Edge cases: C is nonempty by integrality. In dimension zero, with trivial group, or with empty boundary and free action, the absolute theorem allows the identity/length-zero tower and C^0=C. The empty affine open is harmless; its inverse image is empty and has a closed embedding into P^0 of the empty base. Nontrivial tame inertia is permitted: no hypothesis of a free action on all of C was introduced. On an affine open O_W is ample regardless of whether W is invariant. The intersections used are dense opens in an integral scheme, not intersections of affine opens assumed affine.
