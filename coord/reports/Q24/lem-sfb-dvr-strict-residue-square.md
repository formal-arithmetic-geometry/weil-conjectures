## Source error

Source read: `literature/stacks-project/more-algebra.tex`, Tag 09ET, label `lemma-solution-after-strict-henselization`, lines 34555--34605. I also compared the current primary source at https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09ET; it contains the same argument. Tag 09E0 is at `algebra.tex`, label `lemma-colimit-finite-etale-given-residue-field`, lines 44643 onward; the current page https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09E0 agrees.

The unsupported assertion in 09ET is that, after descending a finite extension to a finite-etale stage with fraction field F, all the factors of the upper tensor product over K are isomorphic to the one obtained using the chosen embedding of F. Splitting the finite-etale algebra only gives factors indexed by the different embeddings F into the upper field. The extension being tensored must be twisted along each embedding. The twists need not be isomorphic, and the same descended field need not be a weak solution over K.

An explicit test of that intermediate assertion is as follows. Work in characteristic 2. Let k=F_2, choose a in F_8 of degree 3 over F_2, and put

    A = F_2[[t]],  K = F_2((t)),
    D = F_8[[t]],  F = F_8((t)),
    N = F(x),     x^2-x = a/t.

Let B be the integral closure of D in N. It is a complete DVR with residue F_8 and ramification index 2 over D. Let A_s be the union of F_{2^n}[[t]] in compatible finite constant extensions (index by divisibility), put K_s=Frac(A_s), and take B_s to be the strict henselization of B. Its fraction field is L_s=K_s(x). The finite extension H=K_s(x) of K_s is a weak solution of A_s to B_s: the reduced tensor L_s tensor_{K_s} H is L_s times L_s and its two normalized local maps are isomorphisms. It descends to precisely the field N/F displayed above, with K_s tensor_F N=H.

For the embedding F into L_s sending a to a^2, however, a factor of L_s tensor_K N is

    L_s[y]/(y^2-y-a^2/t).

This is a field, not L_s times L_s. To see it explicitly, suppose a root z lies in L_s=K_s(x), and write z=u+v x, u,v in K_s. Comparing the x coefficient of z^2-z gives v^2-v=0, so v is 0 or 1. The constant equation would be u^2-u=(a^2-v a)/t. The coefficient is nonzero, since a^2 is neither 0 nor a. Every nonzero negative valuation of u^2-u is even, whereas the right side has valuation -1, a contradiction. Thus this conjugate factor is quadratic over L_s. It is also ramified of index 2: the two independent Artin--Schreier classes a/t and a^2/t give a degree-4 totally ramified extension of K_s (one can check at a finite constant-field complete stage). The corresponding lower normalization in N has index 2 over A, so this upper branch has index 2 over it. In particular the very field N/K selected by the published finite-stage argument is not a weak solution of A to B_s.

This counterexample concerns the particular N/K chosen in 09ET and its purported factor identification. Its lower residue field is already perfect, so it also shows why the corrected proof must allow a larger finite field. It does not contradict the corrected existential conclusion. The repaired argument uses upward permanence only after upgrading a weak solution to a solution; it never assumes that weak solutions generally go up.

## Argument for the corrected finite transfer

The source for the correction is more-algebra.tex: Tag 09EU (lemma-galois-relative, its transitive action on normalized upper branches), Tag 09EN (definition-solution), lemma-extension-dvrs-formally-smooth (index one plus separable residue is equivalent to adic formal smoothness), Tag 0GLR (lemma-solution-goes-up, with its reference to lemma-formally-smooth-goes-up), and the first sentence of the proof of proposition-epp-essentially-finite-type. That sentence explicitly says that a weak solution is a solution when the lower residue field is perfect. I read these statements and the relevant proofs; 09ET's false factor assertion is not used.

Here is the argument for the statement-only transfer node, whose notation is U -> D -> V, fractions K -> F -> M, and given H/F. Since D is finite etale local over U, its residue field is finite separable over the perfect residue field of U, and is perfect. Every lower normalized branch in a finite extension of F has residue finite over that field, hence perfect. Thus the index-one condition of the given weak solution H/F, together with separability of every extension of a perfect field, gives a solution in the sense of 09EN by lemma-extension-dvrs-formally-smooth. This upgrade does not assume that the upper residue extension is algebraic or perfect.

Take a finite normal extension N/K in an algebraic closure containing H (the splitting field of the finitely many minimal polynomials of generators suffices; inseparability is allowed). Then N/F is a finite extension of H/F, so 0GLR shows that N/F is still a solution for the original D -> V, in particular weak. This use of permanence is now legitimate. N is a single finite field, containing all the conjugates required below, rather than an unjustified compositum of merely weak solutions.

Let R be the integral closure of U in N, and C that of V in (M tensor_K N)_red. R is also the integral closure of D in N: D is integral over U, so one direction is transitivity of integrality and the other follows from U subset D. Splitting the finite etale V-algebra V tensor_U D gives a distinguished direct factor of M tensor_K N corresponding to the given map F -> M; this factor is M tensor_F N. Hence its integral closure is a direct factor of C, and the solution N/F just obtained supplies the weak-solution ideal equality for every maximal ideal in that distinguished factor. This factor is nonzero, and its normalization has at least one maximal ideal by 09EM. Choose one such ideal q0.

Now apply Tag 09EU (lemma-galois-relative) to the extension U -> V and the finite normal extension N/K. Its statement says precisely that Aut(N/K) acts on R and C and acts transitively on the maximal ideals of C. The action on C fixes V, and the action on R fixes U. Given any maximal q of C, choose g carrying q0 to q. If n0=q0 intersect R, it carries n0 to n=q intersect R. It induces isomorphisms R_n0 -> R_n and C_q0 -> C_q intertwining the local maps. The equality n0 C_q0=q0 C_q0 therefore gives n C_q=q C_q. Maximal contractions and the DVR conditions follow either by this transport or by the finite case of 09EM. Thus N/K is a weak solution for U -> V. This covers all coefficient embeddings and all upper branches simultaneously, without identifying the conjugate factors of the original field H or writing an unjustified balancing isomorphism over F. Tag 09EU applies to normal extensions, so no separability of H/F or N/K is required.

For comparison with the reviewer's proposed route, the normal closure of F/K is a finite Galois subextension of M; the transitive normal-extension action above performs the required transport of all coefficient branches. Taking N as a finite normal hull of H packages the compositum of those finitely many conjugates in one field. The use of 0GLR before this transport is exactly where perfect lower residue fixes the returned obstruction.
