Step 3: I opened def-weil-closed-immersion, which also explicitly defines an immersion as closed followed by open. I opened lem-pc-proper-monomorphism-closed: its forward implication says EVERY closed immersion is a monomorphism (and proper), without a Noetherian hypothesis. I opened lem-weil-pbc-open-immersion-monomorphism: it gives exactly left cancellation for j. Applying both gives cancellation for i in two lines. The coordinate-bijection node applies to the SAME S-structure on T in the two maps, since u,w are X-maps and a is fixed. Their V-projections are consequently S-maps. After cancelling i, the two projections agree, and def-pc-fibre-product states uniqueness for this exact pair. The representable-sheaf node also applies after base change. Finally, componentwise injectivity cancels any two sheaf transformations object by object, so no uncited conservative-family theorem is used. This step is elementary from the cited inputs.

Step 4: I opened lem-galois-inverse-image-representable, whose statement ensures P is an object of the small etale site and supplies an abstract comparison, but DOES NOT state the adjunct normalization. The proof therefore establishes normalization rather than taking it from that node. I opened lem-weil-pbc-etale-inverse-image-algebraic-structures: its clause (1) states the set-sheaf adjunction and the precise R on sections. Both h_V and h_P are site representables, so it applies on both sides; sheaf morphisms are their underlying natural transformations. I opened lem-weil-pbc-coyoneda-iso-of-natural-hom-bijection: it supplies the unique isomorphism realizing the natural Hom bijection. At F=h_P its inverse construction identifies the unit's image with id_P. Naturality at u:T->V then computes precisely id_X times u. This fills a real normalization issue, without creating a node restating part of the item.

Step 5: the algebraic-structures node explicitly states finite-limit preservation via the projections, the set/ring adjunction agreement and their units. The use of naturality of the adjunction is written out as an equation with eta. Product extensionality gives equality of maps, and cancellation through the isomorphism and e_a gives the final monomorphism.
