## Source error

The item faithfully states Stacks perfect.tex Tag 08ER (`lemma-induction-step`); there is **no statement dispute or theorem change**. Its proof needs these corrections:

- For T'=T minus U, the complement in V is `(V minus T) union (U intersect V)`, not the intersection printed in the source. Taking T=Y=U=V=Spec(k) gives T'=empty and nonempty left-hand side, whereas the printed right-hand side is empty. The corrected union is quasi-compact because each member is quasi-compact separately. No quasi-separatedness of Y or U is assumed.
- The printed base case t <= m-r' claims vanishing also in degree t. This is false at equality. For Y=U=Spec(k), V=empty, T=Y, E=k in degree zero, m=0, r_U=r'=0, t=0 satisfies that base case but H^0(E) is not supported on T'=empty. We use the strict base case h < m-s, which implies vanishing in all degrees >= m-s and works even when s=0.
- The source chooses an approximation on U at degree m but asserts it controls the current top degree t. If t<m, that does not follow; P can have cohomology between t and m and a surjection at m says nothing at t. We approximate at the induction upper bound h itself. Then P and P[1] have no cohomology above h, and the map in degree h is surjective.
- To apply A(U) at h, the needed inequality is h-r_U >= m-r. We take r=r_U+s (after enlarging r_U to be nonnegative). In the induction case h>=m-s this inequality holds, and the support and pseudo-coherence hypotheses both follow. The source's maximum bound does not give this implication in general: r_U=s=3, m=0, h=-2 gives h>m-s but h-r_U=-5 < m-max(r_U,s)=-3. This identifies a failing inference, not a counterexample to the theorem. The theorem asks only for some uniform r, so the additive bound changes no conclusion.
