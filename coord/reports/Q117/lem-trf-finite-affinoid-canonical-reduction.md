## Independent arguments for the two new obligations

These arguments explain why the statement-only nodes are legitimate smaller inputs and expose their independent source route; their node files intentionally remain unsplit.

For `lem-trf-tate-spectral-generic-reduction-rank`, start with the monic generator p_c of the evaluation kernel given by Bosch 1.4 Lemma 12(i), equivalently `lem-trf-tate-torsionfree-fibre-polynomial`. A finite domain C over T is T-torsion-free. Localizing at nonzero elements of T makes p_c the minimal polynomial over F: any smaller F-polynomial relation clears denominators to a smaller T-polynomial relation, and injectivity C -> C tensor F preserves the relation. For nonzero t, the minimal polynomial of tc is Z^r + sum(t^i a_i) Z^(r-i) when p_c=Z^r+sum a_i Z^(r-i). Its coefficients lie in T. The unique monic kernel generator for tc must equal it after localization and hence in T[Z]. Bosch Lemma 12(iii), obtained by taking suprema in the fibre formula, gives rho_C(c)=max_i ||a_i||G^(1/i). Gauss multiplicativity gives rho_C(tc)=||t||G rho_C(c). The zero cases hold separately. This identifies restriction to T with Gauss norm; it does not assert that rho_C is fully multiplicative on C. Annuli can have several boundary seminorms.

Coefficient reduction is `lem-trf-tate-unit-ball-polynomial-reduction`; in particular k is a field and T reduces to R. The homogeneity just proved shows that multiplying a nonzero class of S (represented by norm exactly 1) by a nonzero class of R (lifted with Gauss norm exactly 1) again has norm 1. Thus R -> S is injective and S is R-torsion-free. Power multiplicativity from the seminorm-calculus node shows S is reduced: if rho(c)<=1 and rho(c^n)<1, then rho(c)<1. For the generic bound, any finite Q-linearly independent family of fractions in S tensor_R Q can be multiplied individually by its nonzero R-denominators to give an independent family of classes s_i in S. Lift to c_i in C^circ. If those lifts were F-linearly dependent, clear the finitely many F-denominators to obtain sum t_i c_i=0 with t_i in T, not all zero. The maximum Gauss norm of these t_i lies in |K*|, since a nonzero restricted series attains its maximum coefficient norm. Divide all t_i by a scalar of that absolute value. All normalized coefficients lie in T^circ and one has norm 1. Reducing the relation gives a nonzero R-linear relation among the s_i, contradiction. Thus the lifts are F-linearly independent in E; there can be at most [E:F]. This argument needs no Shilov boundary theorem, complete residue extension, finiteness of S, or trace result for affinoid unit balls.

For `lem-trf-integral-generic-finite-algebra`, torsion-freeness makes S -> S_Q injective: a zero fraction means some nonzero R-scalar kills its numerator. S_Q is reduced: a nilpotent fraction s/t has u*s^n=0 for some nonzero u, whence s^n=0 by torsion-freeness and s=0 by reducedness. A finite-dimensional algebra over Q is Artinian (`lem-weil-pbc-finite-dim-algebra-artinian`, Tag 00J6). The existing `lem-sfb-reduced-artinian-residue-product` (00J7/00JB) identifies S_Q with a finite product of fields L_j. Each L_j is finite-dimensional over Q because it is a quotient of S_Q. An element of S projects to an element integral over R in every L_j, using the very same monic equation. Thus S embeds R-linearly into the product of the integral closures N_j of R in L_j. The existing `lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-field-integral-closure-finite` (0335) makes each N_j finite over R, without separability. R is Noetherian: present it as a quotient of a finite polynomial ring over k, use the Noetherian clause in `lem-weil-pbc-polynomial-ring-field-regular-ring`, and map ideal generators to the quotient. The finite product of the N_j is finite as a module (coordinate-supported spanning families). Now `lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-submodule-finite` (00IK) gives finiteness of S. S=0 is immediate; no field factor is required in that case.

This is the trace/Noetherian part at the correct level of reductions, not a disguised unit-ball theorem.
