## Source error

I read Glasman arXiv:1308.4940, Definition 2.8 and Lemma 2.9, in the online PDF. The definition selects vertices and edges by essential-image conditions and retains all simplices satisfying those conditions. Its subsequent claim about the fibre does not follow: decompositions up to equivalence do not retain compatible choices at a shared vertex. The proof of Lemma 2.9 takes products of separately chosen decompositions without the missing compatibility. I withdraw its use for this node; Glasman is cited here only to identify the error.

Here is the returned counterexample, checked against the actual withdrawn statement. Let A be the one-object groupoid BZ, with addition as its monoidal product, and B the cartesian category of spaces. The classifying space of A is S^1, so the classifying space of A^2 is T^2. Over the identity of [2], the essential-image condition accepts edges after changing the product decomposition by equivalence, including the coordinate-swap equivalence. Consequently the candidate fibre contains maps T^2 → S^1 with both winding numbers independently variable: its set of components is Z^2 between the tuples whose first functor is respectively constant * and constant S^1 and whose second functor is terminal. The actual product Fun(A,B)^2 has the single-variable mapping space Map(S^1,S^1), with components Z. Thus the asserted fibre equivalence is false.

The defect also obstructs inner horns. Compose the accepted edge represented by the second-coordinate projection T^2 → S^1 with the coordinatewise inclusion S^1 → S^1 ∨ S^1 into its first circle. The composite is (s,t) ↦ i_1(t). If this came from a product decomposition allowed by the discarded model, restriction to s = 0 would produce a Gottlieb element of the free group F_2. Such elements are central and the centre of F_2 is trivial; the loop i_1 is the nontrivial generator a. Thus that composite cannot be accepted, so the indicated two-horn has no filler in the essential-image subset. The mapping-space discrepancy alone already disproves the old node; the horn example explains why fixing only its claimed fibre would not repair it.

The replacement claims the space-presheaf construction that the item needs; no arbitrary-target exponential theorem survives under the old identifier. The replacement is the actual infinity-categorical pullback of RFib^times → Cat^times. A fibre over a fixed tuple of categories retains the specified identification of each base category and has mapping spaces in the product of the corresponding right-fibration categories. In the same BZ test the component set is Z, not Z^2. There is no closure operation that admits a coordinate swap of A^2 without the corresponding tuple/base data.
