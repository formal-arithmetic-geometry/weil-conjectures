For `lem-trf-complementary-pure-subcurve-components`, I checked the source's first-paragraph assertion without assuming nodality. An irreducible one-dimensional component of $Y$ lies in some component of $X$; if containment were proper, a length-one chain inside it followed by the larger component would give a length-two chain in a dimension-one component, contradiction. Thus $Y$ is a union of whole components. Noetherianity makes the component set finite. On every omitted component its complement of $Y$ is a nonempty open and hence dense; conversely all of $X\setminus Y$ lies in the omitted union. Its closure is exactly that union, whose maximal irreducible subsets are the omitted components. Repeating this for the other subset gives back $Y$. Reduced closed structures on those supports are unique. This verifies the statement independently of any nodal claim, including $Y=\varnothing$ and $Y=X$.

For `lem-trf-reduced-scheme-image`, 056B states exactly that reduced-source image equals reduced induced structure on the topological closure, with no quasi-compactness assumption; its source proof factors the map through the reduced structure. I checked empty source, a closed reduced immersion and a dense open immersion. Quasi-compactness is needed later for the existing restriction theorem, and is not inferred from this image lemma.
