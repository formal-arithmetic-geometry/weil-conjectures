I checked the node with 0AHR and 0BB6 open. The forward implication follows from the arbitrary affine-test definition, including every representable etale pair. For the converse, the proof of 0AHR only ever invokes the affine-test assumption for affine open subsets of its chosen etale atlas. I made these charts explicit and kept all products absolute over Spec Z. No quasi-compactness, Noetherian or separation assumption on X enters the argument.

Step 1 cites the exact statement of `def-sfb-space-absolute-quasi-separated`: it quantifies over all affine maps, so the maps in the question qualify. `def-trf-algebraic-space` asserts representability of the diagonal and defines that phrase by scheme tests. Thus the fibre product in the assertion is a scheme, and this step is just specialization of the definition, marked elementary. I did not claim that the predicate's Lean definition already proves the converse.

Step 2 uses `lem-a1-space-affine-etale-cover`, whose statement gives a scheme W that is a disjoint union of affine schemes and a representable surjective etale atlas. Its affine components are open by the meaning of disjoint union. `lem-weil-pbc-open-immersion-etale` explicitly makes these inclusions etale. `lem-trf-space-chartwise-etale-permanence` explicitly gives composition and the comparison with representable etaleness when the source is a scheme, so the composites meet the hypothesis of the item. The quasi-compactness of R_ij is now precisely the assumed test condition; it is not assumed for arbitrary maps.

Step 3 uses `lem-trf-space-fibre-products-etale-covers` for closure under products and pullback of the singleton surjective etale atlas, and `lem-trf-space-chartwise-etale-permanence` for composition of the two resulting covers. I checked the factorization: W×W -> X×W pulls back p along X×W -> X, and X×W -> X×X pulls back p along the second projection. `def-trf-algebraic-space` supplies the representable diagonal, whose base change over the scheme B is therefore a scheme. The identification of the base-changed diagonal with W×_X W is the compatible-pair universal property; no assertion about images factoring through a subscheme is used.

Step 4 is marked elementary, with its argument written out. `lem-weil-pbc-open-immersion-base-change` supplies each of the two open inclusions in the product-chart factorization; their composite identifies the open with the intersection of the two inverse images. `def-pc-fibre-product` supplies the universal property used both for that identification and for j^{-1}(Q_ij)=R_ij. `lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra` explicitly states that the product over an affine base is affine and gives the tensor product of rings; its base here is Spec Z. Coverage follows by placing each of the two projected points in a component of W. Thus I did not leave the affine-cover theorem of Tag 01JS as an uncited black box, and no separate product-cover node is warranted.

Step 5 uses the quasi-separated conclusion of `lem-weil-pbc-affine-scheme-qcqs` for Q_ij. Clause (1) of `lem-weil-pbc-qcqs-morphism-permanence` says exactly that a map with quasi-compact source and quasi-separated target is quasi-compact; R_ij has the first property by the hypothesis and Q_ij has the second by affineness. `lem-pc-quasi-compact-local-on-target` then applies to the displayed open cover of B and the restrictions of j.
