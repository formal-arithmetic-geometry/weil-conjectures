### Original step 2

For derived tensor choose flat-term K-flat representatives and use the comparison $t_{a,b}$ of [lem-pur-shifted-tensor-morphism-calculus], which multiplies the summand $M[a]^p\otimes N[b]^q$ by $(-1)^{bp}$. If the first input is shifted ($t_{r,0}$) the comparison is the identity on reindexed summands; if the second input is shifted ($t_{0,r}$) it multiplies the summand whose first factor has degree $p$ by $(-1)^{rp}$. In both cases the two total differentials agree after this factor, so it is a chain isomorphism (a factor $(-1)^{rp}$ in the first slot would fail for odd $r$). The signed tensor-morphism calculus states that this comparison is natural, associative and compatible with the Koszul symmetry. The completed integral tensor has the same signed finite-level comparison and the cone comparison on the reduction tower, and its quotient transitions commute with it: for the second slot this is [lem-sf-completed-tensor-shift-right]; for the first slot the finite-level maps are identities, which commute with transitions, and the cone map of Step 3 applies. Consequently the tensor operation and the tensor component of d_A have the required shift square in either slot.
