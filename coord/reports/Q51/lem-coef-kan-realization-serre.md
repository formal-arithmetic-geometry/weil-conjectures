## Maintainer review correction (cc reviewer, 2026-10-17)

Item 10 of the second reading ("`map_mul` preserves its existing multiplication") is
wrong as delivered, and it makes the theorem false. Mathlib's
`HomotopyGroup.mul_spec` says `⟦p⟧ * ⟦q⟧ = ⟦transAt i q p⟧`, so `q` is traversed first.
In degree one (Lean `n = 0`), `MulStruct a b c (Fin.last 0)` makes `a`, `b`, `c` the
edges `01`, `12`, `02` of a 2-simplex `w`. `realize` with `(1-z, z)` fixes
`equiv [c] = [|a| ⬝ |b|] = equiv [b] * equiv [a]`. The delivered `map_mul` required
`equiv [a] * equiv [b]`, so it forced `π₁(|K|)` to be abelian. That fails for the nerve
of any nonabelian group. Correction: `map_mul` now reads
`equiv (a·b) = equiv b.homotopyClass * equiv a.homotopyClass`. This is the node's
concatenation, `a` followed by `b` in the first coordinate, written in Mathlib's order.
The structure docstrings record the convention. In degrees at least two both sides are
abelian, so nothing else changes. The theorem signature and `lean.statement` are
unchanged. The fixed file compiles cleanly with `tools/nt_compile.py`.
