## Source error

Read curves.tex Tag 0E68 (`lemma-nodal-vector-fields`), including the ordinary-branch-stalk exact sequence in its proof. That sequence cannot in general be used as written before completion. For example over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field, the irreducible node
$A=(k[x,y]/(y^2-x^2(1+x)))_{(x,y)}$
has normalization the semilocal domain obtained from $k[t]$ at $t=1,-1$, with $x=t^2-1$ and $y=t(t^2-1)$. Both branch local rings have the same fraction field $K$. An asserted exact sequence $0\to A\to B_{(t-1)}\times B_{(t+1)}\to k\to0$ would, after localization at the non-zero-divisors of $A$, imply $0\to K\to K\times K\to0$, which is false. The given item already avoids this by assuming the correct completed product model. Its statement is true and was not corrected. The new proof uses the semilocal ring $B\subset Q(A)$ throughout and descends from its completed product by quotient-module injectivity. It makes no assumption that the uncompleted ring $B$ is a product.
