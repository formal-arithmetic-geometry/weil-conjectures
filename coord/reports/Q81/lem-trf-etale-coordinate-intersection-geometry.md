The affine-open stalk and Spec-map stalk lemmas identify the local rings and the actual localized ring map, not merely abstract isomorphic rings. lem-weil-pbc-etale-prime-generates-maximal applies to the affine etale map R to B and its contracted prime; its statement supplies exactly p B_q = q B_q. The definitions of regular local rings and parameter systems then say exactly that any d-element list generating its maximal ideal, with d = its dimension, is a regular parameter system. No independent minimal-generator or cotangent-space comparison theorem is hidden in this step.
