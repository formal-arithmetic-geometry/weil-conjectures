The proof implements all three existing DAG steps. Closure induction shows that a
face is generated by those ambient generators it contains. The localization is the
supremum of the image of Q and the inverse image of F, hence finitely generated by
Mathlib's finite-generation API. Its inclusion into Q^gp is injective on group
completions, and fractions of elements of Q give surjectivity. Finally, from
x^n = q/f one obtains (x*f)^n = q*f^(n-1); saturation of Q supplies x*f in Q,
and injectivity on completions gives the required witness in the localization.

power calculation uses n = k + 1, allowing k = 0.
