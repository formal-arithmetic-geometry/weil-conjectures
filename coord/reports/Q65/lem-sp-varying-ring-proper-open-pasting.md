### lem-sp-exact-derived-transformation-pasting

Source: literature/stacks-project/derived.tex, Tag 015F (lemma-right-derived-exact-functor); exact unbounded localization of possibly noninvertible square cells; agent proof using exact derived factorization and the localization universal property.

1. For each edge $F$, [lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-factorization] makes $C(F)$ preserve quasi-isomorphisms and identifies $D(F)Q$ with $QC(F)$. The natural-transformation part of [lem-weil-pbc-localization-universal-property] therefore defines $t^D$ uniquely. For a path $F_m\cdots F_1$, build its factorization by successively applying the edge factorizations. This is exactly the factorization used by the standard composition comparison, since both are the comparison to the same degreewise path.

2. Evaluate the proposed derived identity at $QK$ for an arbitrary cochain complex $K$. Conjugate its source and target by the path factorizations. By the defining equation of each localized cell, an unwhiskered cell becomes $Q(C(t)_K)$. For a whiskered cell use naturality of the adjacent edge factorization to move it past that factorization; then it becomes the localization of the corresponding whiskered chain map. At each internal path the adjacent comparison and its inverse cancel. Thus the two derived words become $Q$ of the two original degreewise words. They agree degree by degree by the given ordinary identity. Finally [lem-weil-pbc-localization-universal-property], clause (3), gives equality of natural transformations. Apply the same construction to $t^{-1}$ for an invertible cell; preservation of composition and identities makes it the inverse of $t^D$.

### lem-sp-varying-ring-open-unit-coherence
