## Proof retained for the new prerequisite

For preservation, apply curves.tex Tag 0CD8, lemma-nodal-family-postcompose-etale. Both flatness and local finite presentation compose with the etale map. Over an algebraically closed geometric point of S, the etale scheme T has discrete reduced geometric fibre with residue field the chosen algebraically closed field, hence is a disjoint union of copies of that point. The composite's geometric fibre is a disjoint union of geometric fibres of g. Those are pure one-dimensional with smooth points or ordinary nodes; empty components cause no exception. This argument is also the meaning of the source's reduction to finite separable residue fields and lemma-node-over-separable-extension.

For reflection, base change e g along e to get C times_S T -> T, which is nodal by Tag 0C5B. The map C -> C times_S T, c |-> (c,g(c)), is a section of an etale map, hence an open immersion (a pullback of the diagonal of e). The restriction of the base-changed family to this open graph is exactly g. Source locality (the existing nodal source-locality node, which includes preservation under etale source restriction) proves g nodal. The graph need not be closed: e is not assumed separated. This is why the reflection proof uses an open graph and does not impose separatedness. These arguments are report material only; the unsplit node has empty proof and proof_deps.
