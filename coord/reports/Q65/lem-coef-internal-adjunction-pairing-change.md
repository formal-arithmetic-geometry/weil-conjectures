## Argument handoff for the projection node

The source sentence in thm-coef-finite-hom, proof paragraph 4, is “Every comparison used is defined by the same evaluations and adjunction counits; transposing identifies their composite”. This node makes precise the projection part of that sentence. It is an agent formulation of the existing detailed proof, not a theorem falsely attributed to a Stacks tag. All operations here are on ordinary small etale module derived categories. The proof uses no finite-ring property, and so its natural domain is arbitrary commutative rings; no assertion about invertibility of the direct-image scalar mate is made.

For the open factor, compare the two maps from F_Z j_!(L tensor j^*T') to j_!F_SL tensor F_ZT'. The first is gamma_B after j_! of tensor/restriction identification after q_j; the other is q_{j,L} tensor identity after F_Z gamma_A. Precompose with the inverse open scalar comparison. Under j_! ⊣ j^*, both adjuncts are exactly the same restriction/tensor identification: gamma is normalized by that adjunct and q_j restricts to the identity. This is the full open-square argument of old Step 1. The necessary normalization and associativity statements are explicitly present in lem-coef-etale-open-tensor, whose statement was read.

For the closed factor put P=j_!L, a complex on Z. Define c_{g,P} by its ordinary counit transpose, for any derived P. Compare c_{g,g^*T tensor P} F_X pr_A(T,P) with pr_B(F_XT,F_ZP)(1 tensor c_{g,P}). Under g_B^* ⊣ Rg_{*,B}, the first adjunct is F_Z(1 tensor alpha_{g,A,P}); the second adjunct is (1 tensor alpha_{g,B,F_ZP})(1 tensor g_B^*c_{g,P}) = 1 tensor F_Z(alpha_{g,A,P}). They coincide by the canonical monoidal pullback/scalar identifications. This is old Step 2, using P=j_!L, not Q. The map pr is the tensor-symmetry reordering of def-kun-projection-formula-map. Generic ringed exchange/tensor coherence (lem-weil-pbc-ringed-exchange-tensor-coherence) also states the corresponding ordinary projection/mate square, but does not include the open factor.

Substitute q_s=Rg_{*,B}(q_j)c_g, and use the canonical tensor symmetry to get exactly the displayed Pi_B(1 tensor q_s)mu = s_{!,B}(iota)q_s F_XPi_A. ## Argument handoff for the evaluation node

This extracts the evaluation and ordinary-counit part of the same source sentence. It is an agent formulation; it does not invoke any pro-etale exceptional adjoint or purity theorem. For an arbitrary scheme morphism f, rings A to B, and a map b:F_YN to N', define u,c,w,h exactly as in its statement. No projection or exceptional adjunction is used, and b need not be invertible.

First expand f_B^*h = f_B^*Rf_{*,B}(w) f_B^*c. Naturality of the ordinary counit gives alpha_{B,H_B(F_YL,N')} f_B^*Rf_{*,B}(w) = w alpha_{B,F_YQ}. The defining transpose for c then gives alpha_{B,F_YQ} f_B^*c = F_Y(alpha_{A,Q}) under the scalar/pullback identification. Consequently the first factor entering the evaluation pairing is w F_Y(alpha_A).

Next naturality of evaluation in its target yields ev_{B,F_YL,N'}(H_B(1,b) tensor 1) = b ev_{B,F_YL,F_YN}. The defining tensor-Hom transpose for u says ev_B(u tensor 1) = F_Y(ev_A) mu_Y^{-1}. Substitute these two equalities and the previous geometric-counit formula into the displayed definition of p_h. Naturality of mu_Y and the scalar/pullback comparison combines F_Y(ev_A) and F_Y(alpha_A tensor 1) into F_Y(e_A), giving p_h=b F_Y(e_A)iota^{-1}. This is the full content of old Step 5 and the first half of old Step 6. It is a mixed comparison for two geometric adjunctions and two tensor-Hom adjunctions, not just uncurry_natural_right in one category.

### Original step 1

Notation: for $T\in D(X_{\mathrm{et}},A)$ and $L$ as in the statement, $\Pi_A(T,L):T\otimes_A^Ls_{!,A}L\xrightarrow{\sim}s_{!,A}(s^*T\otimes_A^LL)$ is the proper projection $T\otimes g_*j_!L\to g_*(g^*T\otimes j_!L)$ followed by $g_*(\gamma^{-1}_{L,g^*T})$, as in the statement; similarly $\Pi_B$. Open square: for $T'\in D(Z_{\mathrm{et}},A)$ the two maps $F_Zj_!(L\otimes j^*T')\to j_!F_SL\otimes_BF_ZT'$ given by $\gamma_B\circ j_!(\text{tensor identification})\circ q_{j,L\otimes j^*T'}$ and $(q_{j,L}\otimes1)\circ F_Z(\gamma_A)$ are equal. Indeed $q_j$ is invertible, so it suffices to compare them after precomposing with $q^{-1}$; both are then maps out of $j_!(\cdot)$, hence determined by their $j_!\dashv j^*$ adjuncts on $S$, and both adjuncts are the canonical restriction/tensor identification, because $\gamma$ is the adjunct of that identification and $q_{j,P}$ restricts to the identity. The associativity identifications are the canonical ones of [lem-coef-etale-open-tensor].

Cited nodes: lem-coef-etale-open-tensor.

### Original step 2

Proper square for $g$, applied to the pushed object $P=j_!L$ and $T\in D(X_{\mathrm{et}},A)$ (not to $Q$, which lives on $S$). Let $c_{g,P}:F_Xg_{*,A}P\to g_{*,B}F_ZP$ be the closed direct-image comparison that is the first factor of $q_{s,L}=g_{*,B}(q_{j,L})\circ c_{g,j_!L}$ [lem-coef-etale-locally-closed-shriek]; its $g^*\dashv g_{*}$ transpose over $B$ is $F_Z(\epsilon^{g}_{A,P})$ after $g^*F_X\cong F_Zg^*$ (definition in [lem-coef-finite-direct-image] and [lem-coef-etale-locally-closed-shriek]). The proper projection $\mathrm{pr}(T,P):T\otimes g_*P\to g_*(g^*T\otimes P)$ has transpose $1\otimes\epsilon^g_P$. Compare $c_{g,g^*T\otimes P}\circ F_X\mathrm{pr}_A(T,P)$ and $\mathrm{pr}_B(F_XT,F_ZP)\circ(1\otimes c_{g,P})$ through their $B$-transposes $g^*F_X(T\otimes g_*P)\to F_Z(g^*T\otimes P)$: the first is $F_Z(\epsilon^g)\circ g^*F_X\mathrm{pr}_A=F_Z(1\otimes\epsilon^g_P)$ by naturality of the counit transpose; the second is $(1\otimes\epsilon^g_{B})\circ(1\otimes g^*c_{g,P})=1\otimes F_Z(\epsilon^g_P)$. They agree under the monoidal identification $g^*F_X(T\otimes g_*P)\cong g^*F_XT\otimes F_Zg^*g_*P$, so the proper square commutes.

Cited nodes: lem-coef-finite-direct-image, lem-coef-etale-locally-closed-shriek.

### Original step 6

Put $h=Rs_{*,B}(w)\circ c_{s,Q}$ with $w=H_B(1,b_{s,M})\circ u$. Since $I_B$ is the internal adjunction whose transpose is the stated pairing, [lem-asd-internal-adjunction-evaluation] (for $s=gj$, finite ring $B$) gives the transpose of $I_B\circ h$ as $\epsilon_{s,B}\circ s_{!,B}(p)\circ\Pi_B(F_XRs_{*,A}Q,F_SL)$, where $p:s^*F_XRs_{*,A}Q\otimes F_SL\to s_B^!F_XM$ is the transpose of $h$, i.e. $p=\mathrm{ev}_B\circ((w\circ\alpha_B\circ s^*c_{s,Q})\otimes1)$. By the definition of $c_{s,Q}$ [lem-coef-finite-direct-image], $\alpha_B\circ s^*c_{s,Q}=F_S(\alpha_A)$ after $s^*F_X\cong F_Ss^*$. By Step 5 and naturality of evaluation in the target, $\mathrm{ev}_B\circ(w\otimes1)=b_{s,M}\circ\mathrm{ev}_u=b_{s,M}\circ F_S(\mathrm{ev}_A)$. Hence $p=b_{s,M}\circ F_S(e_A)\circ\iota^{-1}$ with $e_A=\mathrm{ev}_A\circ(\alpha_A\otimes1)$. The transpose of the left side is this map precomposed with $1\otimes q_{s,L}$. Step 3 with $T=Rs_{*,A}Q$ rewrites it as $\epsilon_{s,B}\circ s_{!,B}(b_{s,M})\circ s_{!,B}F_S(e_A)\circ q_{s,s^*T\otimes L}\circ F_X\Pi_A$; naturality of $q_s$ [lem-coef-etale-locally-closed-shriek] gives $s_{!,B}F_S(e_A)\circ q=q_{s,N}\circ F_Xs_{!,A}(e_A)$, and Step 4 turns $\epsilon_{s,B}\circ s_{!,B}(b_{s,M})\circ q_{s,N}$ into $F_X\epsilon_{s,A}$. So the transpose is $F_X(\epsilon_{s,A}\circ s_{!,A}(e_A)\circ\Pi_A)$, i.e. $F_X$ of the $A$ pairing defining $I_A$.

Cited nodes: lem-asd-internal-adjunction-evaluation, lem-sfb-dc-hom-scalar-adjunction, lem-coef-finite-direct-image, lem-coef-etale-locally-closed-shriek.
