### Returned step 2

For $u\in U$ let $\xi_u$ be the unique generic point of the integral fibre and set $r(u)=\dim_{\kappa(\xi_u)}(\mathcal F_{\xi_u}\otimes\kappa(\xi_u))$. At this point the fibre local ring is a field: on a domain affine chart it is the localization at the zero prime, its fraction field. Thus the fibre stalk is flat. The assumed relative flatness and finite presentation on $X_U$ allow [lem-trf-flat-fibre-free-neighbourhood] to give a neighbourhood $W\subset X_U$ of $\xi_u$ where $\mathcal F$ is free of rank $r(u)$. By [lem-weil-pbc-flat-lfp-open], $f(W)$ is open. For $v\in f(W)$ the nonempty open $W_v$ contains the generic point of the irreducible fibre $X_v$, so $r(v)=r(u)$. Hence $r$ is locally constant. Quasi-compactness of $U$ gives finitely many values and a finite partition into quasi-compact clopen rank strata $U_i$.

Uses: lem-trf-flat-fibre-free-neighbourhood, lem-weil-pbc-flat-lfp-open, def-pc-integral-scheme.

### Returned step 3

Choose a finite-type quasi-coherent ideal with zero set $S\setminus U$ using [lemma-nagata-qc-open-complement-ideal-exists], and blow it up. By [det-nagata-boundary-divisor-cartier] its exceptional ideal is invertible, its zero set is exactly the complement of $U$, and $U$ is scheme-theoretically dense. We retain the names $S,U,X,\mathcal F$ for the transformed data. The divisor and its invertibility persist under further admissible blowups by that same statement. This preliminary reduction is made before separating ranks, so that the existing separation-of-closures result suffices.

Uses: lemma-nagata-qc-open-complement-ideal-exists, det-nagata-boundary-divisor-cartier.

### Returned step 4

For one stratum $U_i$ and the union of the other strata, apply the construction [def-nagata-separate-constructible-closed] and its theorem [lemma-nagata-separate-constructible-closed], with open $U$. These subsets are disjoint, closed and constructible in $U$: each is clopen and quasi-compact, with quasi-compact complement. Their inverse-image closures in the new base are disjoint. By [lemma-nagata-admissible-blowup-preserves-density], their union is the whole base, since it is a finite closed union containing the dense open $U$. Thus each closure is open as well as closed. Repeat globally for each remaining stratum against the union of the others; preimages preserve the earlier clopen separations. There are only finitely many strata. We obtain a finite disjoint decomposition of the base into qcqs open-and-closed pieces, each meeting $U$ in a single rank stratum. If $U$ is empty, its scheme-theoretic density makes the base empty and the conclusion already holds.
