## Review by Q65

I read Tag 0C1C against the complete item. Its smooth curves are integral separated finite-type dimension-one schemes over an arbitrary field, precisely the convention `def-pc-curve`; smoothness is an extra assumption. No perfectness or algebraic closedness is needed. The assertion is about every module homomorphism, not just the differential of h. The scalar proof applies to that stronger quantification. I checked B through E of delivery-errors against the files and the sources, as follows.

Step 1 uses exactly the two conclusions of `lem-sc-dimension-locally-algebraic`: all nonempty opens have the total dimension, and every point has that local topological dimension. Its irreducibility and locally finite type hypotheses follow from integral finite-type curves. The only fibre over Spec k is X or Y itself, so `def-sbc-smooth-morphisms` converts the computed dimension into relative dimension one. I did not confuse local topological dimension with the Krull dimension of the local ring: at the generic point the latter is zero, but the former is one.

Step 2 applies the statement of `lem-sc-smooth-omega-finite-locally-free` with d=1 and the relative-dimension conclusion of step 1. It supplies constant-rank local trivializations, not just unspecified finite local freeness. The statement of `lem-weil-pbc-invertible-pullback-invertible` explicitly gives the pullback trivialization for arbitrary h. Thus this step does not assume h dominant, flat, nonconstant or smooth. I also read the project theorem recorded on the cotangent node; it covers this clause but not the equivalence or the affine-dimension claims.

Step 3 uses `def-sc-invertible-module` only to choose generators locally. The formula on all smaller opens is proved by linearity and restriction compatibility, so no Hom/global-sections identification is hidden. The function-field node explicitly states injectivity of sections into the generic stalk on every nonempty open. This matches the possibly nonaffine common trivializing opens used here. Every such open contains eta. If the generic map vanishes, its scalar coefficient is zero by that injection, and the sheaf uniqueness axiom glues the zero morphisms. These are two-line consequences of the cited statements and definitions, so the step is elementary; it does not hide the desired generic-isomorphism assertion in a dependency.

Step 4 uses the function-field node's field conclusion to make the nonzero generic coefficient a unit. `lem-weil-pbc-basic-open-function` explicitly supplies both openness and the section inverse on the unit locus. I checked this statement rather than citing an isomorphism-locus definition for an openness theorem. The scalar inverse gives both composite identities. To obtain the affine open the statement demands, I choose an affine chart around eta, use `lem-weil-pbc-spec-localization-away-basic-open` (i) to find a basic open inside the prescribed neighbourhood, and use only clause (0) of `lem-weil-pbc-affine-ideals-closed-subscheme` for its affineness. The node explicitly contains that clause, quoted separately in citations.json. No affine-intersection assertion is used. Eta lies in the resulting V, so its nonemptiness is checked, rather than inferred from an arbitrary principal open.

Step 5 again uses the function-field node's explicit containment of eta in every nonempty open and its nonzero-field assertion. Taking germs of the inverse identities gives an inverse to u_eta. Nonvanishing follows by applying the identity to a rank-one generator. This direction does not require an extra stalkwise-isomorphism detection theorem; it only applies the stalk functor to already given inverse identities.

Step 6 uses the actual domain assertion in the function-field node to certify that every structure stalk is nonzero. A local rank-one trivialization from step 2 yields a free stalk of rank one on W; intersecting a neighbourhood with the open W leaves the same germs. Thus the last clause applies at all points, including eta. It does not claim every nonzero map is invertible at every point.

I tested the criterion with u=0, u=identity, multiplication by t on the affine line (nonzero generically, not invertible at t=0), and the zero differential of Frobenius in positive characteristic. All give the stated equivalences; the zero map never becomes an isomorphism on a nonempty open because the stalk module is nonzero. A constant h still has an invertible pullback cotangent sheaf, so no missing dominance hypothesis was introduced. For a singular curve or a zero-dimensional smooth scheme the rank-one conclusion could fail, but both are excluded by the actual smooth dimension-one hypotheses. No new statement-only node was introduced for these routine arguments. Each proof citation has a verbatim statement quote in citations.json; the graph dependencies are exactly the union of step uses. All six steps are below 2000 characters; the longest is the explicit affine shrinking, with no hidden sublemma remaining.
