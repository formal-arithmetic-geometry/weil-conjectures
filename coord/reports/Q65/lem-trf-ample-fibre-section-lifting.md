### Original step 1: Parent local setup

Put $A=\mathcal O_{S,s}$, $\mathfrak m=\mathfrak m_s$, and $k=A/\mathfrak m=\kappa(s)$. Choose a Noetherian affine chart $\operatorname{Spec}R$ of the locally Noetherian cover containing $s$. The canonical-local-spectrum statement identifies $A=R_{\mathfrak p}$ for the prime corresponding to $s$. Here is the elementary Noetherianity check: for any ideal $K\subset R_{\mathfrak p}$, its contraction to $R$ has finite generators $a_i$; every $a/u\in K$ has $a$ in that contraction, so the $a_i/1$ generate $K$. Thus $A$ is Noetherian, and in particular $\mathfrak m$ has finite generators $a_1,\ldots,a_r$. The empty generating family is allowed when $A$ is a field.

Cited nodes: pqf-def-locnoeth, lem-weil-pbc-spec-local-ring-canonical-morphism, def-weil-pbc-noetherian-ring.

### Original step 2: Parent local setup

Form $X=Z\times_S\operatorname{Spec}A$, with projections $h:X\to Z$ and $g:X\to\operatorname{Spec}A$, and write $i:X_s=Z_s\hookrightarrow X$ for the closed fibre. Properness and closed immersions are preserved by base change. The single affine chart of $\operatorname{Spec}A$ makes it locally Noetherian, and the affine quasi-compactness theorem makes it Noetherian; likewise $\operatorname{Spec}k$ is Noetherian (a field has only two ideals). A proper map is of finite type, so finite-type preservation of Noetherianity shows that $Z$, $X$, and $X_s$ are Noetherian. Set $N=h^*L$ and $E_d=N^{\otimes d}$ for $d\ge0$. Pullback and tensor products preserve invertibility, so these are locally free of rank one, as are $L^{\otimes d}$ on $Z$. They are quasi-coherent of finite type by the definition of invertibility, and hence coherent by the locally Noetherian coherence criterion. Iterating pullback-tensor compatibility identifies $h^*(L^{\otimes d})=E_d$ and $i^*E_d=L_s^{\otimes d}$.

Cited nodes: lem-weil-pbc-proper-fp-permanence, def-weil-proper-morphism, pqf-def-locnoeth, lem-weil-pbc-affine-scheme-qcqs, lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian, lem-weil-pbc-invertible-pullback-tensor, def-sc-invertible-module, pqf-lem-coherent-ft-locnoeth.

### Original step 3: lem-trf-fibre-conormal-uniform-vanishing

Let $J=\mathfrak m\mathcal O_X$, the ideal generated by the global functions $g^\sharp(a_j)$. Each $J^n$ is the image of a finite free module mapping to $\mathcal O_X$, using all degree-$n$ monomials in those functions; $J^0=\mathcal O_X$. Coherence of kernels and cokernels makes these images and $J^n/J^{n+1}$ coherent. Multiplication by $J$ kills $J^n/J^{n+1}$, so closed-immersion coherent equivalence regards it as a coherent module $G_n$ on $X_s$, with $i_*G_n=J^n/J^{n+1}$ and $G_0=\mathcal O_{X_s}$. Put $P=k[T_1,\ldots,T_r]$, graded by total degree. Give $G=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}G_n$ the graded $g_s^*\widetilde P$-module structure in which $T_j$ acts by multiplication by $g^\sharp(a_j)$ modulo the next power of $J$. This is well defined: changing a representative in $J^n$ by $J^{n+1}$ changes its product by $J^{n+2}$; the coefficient action factors through $k$ because $\mathfrak m J^n\subset J^{n+1}$. Locally every element of $J^n$ is a sum of degree-$n$ monomials times local functions, and passage to its quotient has local lifts. Thus the one degree-zero section $1\in G_0$ generates $G$ over $g_s^*\widetilde P$. This verifies the finite-type graded-module hypothesis, including quasi-coherence of each piece, without an additional lemma.

Cited nodes: lem-weil-pbc-coherent-kernel-cokernel-locnoeth, lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-coherent-equivalence, pqf-lem-coherent-ft-locnoeth.

### Original step 4: lem-trf-fibre-conormal-uniform-vanishing

Apply graded Serre vanishing on the proper $k$-scheme $g_s:X_s\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ to the finite type graded $k$-algebra $P$, the finite type graded module $G$, and the ample invertible module $L_s$. It supplies one $d_0\ge0$ for which $H^1(X_s,G\otimes L_s^{\otimes d})=0$ for all $d\ge d_0$. Each $G_n$ is a retract of $G$: inclusion into the $n$th direct-sum component followed by projection is the identity. Tensoring and applying the cohomology functor preserve that identity. Hence $H^1(X_s,G_n\otimes L_s^{\otimes d})$ is a retract of zero and is zero, simultaneously for every $n\ge0$ and $d\ge d_0$. No exchange of an infinite sum and cohomology is needed.

Cited nodes: lem-weil-pbc-formal-systems-graded-vanishing, pqf-def-cohomology.

### Original step 5: lem-trf-fibre-conormal-uniform-vanishing for closed-immersion projection/cohomology comparison; lem-trf-infinitesimal-vanishing-section-lifting for the quotient sequence, long exact sequence, and composites

Check this on an open trivializing the invertible $E_d$: it is $0\to J^n/J^{n+1}\to\mathcal O_X/J^{n+1}\to\mathcal O_X/J^n\to0$, and the identifications agree on overlaps. By the closed-immersion projection formula, its kernel is $i_*(G_n\otimes L_s^{\otimes d})$. The closed-immersion cohomology comparison and step 4 give zero $H^1$ of this kernel. The long exact cohomology sequence therefore makes $H^0(X,Q_{n+1})\to H^0(X,Q_n)$ surjective. Their composites $H^0(X,Q_k)\to H^0(X,Q_1)$ are surjective for every $k\ge1$ (identity for $k=1$, and induction by composition). All sheaves here are coherent: locally tensoring by $E_d$ is the identity, and the untwisted kernels and quotients are coherent by step 3.

Write $\rho_1:H^0(X,E_d)\to H^0(X,Q_1)$ for quotient restriction. Its assertion (2) gives a $c\ge0$ such that, for $k\ge1+c$, the image of $H^0(X,Q_k)\to H^0(X,Q_1)$ equals $\operatorname{Im}\rho_1$. ### Original step 7: lem-trf-infinitesimal-vanishing-section-lifting

Apply the uncompleted local dictionary with $f:Z\to S$, $\mathcal F=L^{\otimes d}$ and $p=0$. Its stalk equivalence $a:(R^0f_*L^{\otimes d})_s\to H^0(X,E_d)$ and its finite-level comparison at index $0$ identify the map to $H^0(Z_s,L_s^{\otimes d})$ with $b_0\circ\rho_1\circ a$, where $b_0$ is an isomorphism. ### Original step 8: lem-trf-infinitesimal-vanishing-section-lifting
