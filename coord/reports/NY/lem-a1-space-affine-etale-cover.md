## Original proof retained verbatim

Old step 1:

Choose a representable surjective etale atlas $p:h_V\to E$ from [def-trf-algebraic-space]. For each point $v\in V$, choose an affine open $S_v\subseteq S$ containing its image and an affine open $U_v\subseteq V$ containing $v$ and contained in the inverse image of $S_v$, using [lem-weil-pbc-affine-opens-basis] twice. The maps $U_v\to S$ factor through $S_v$ by [lem-weil-pbc-morphism-factors-through-open-subscheme]. Thus these $U_v$ form a set-indexed open cover of $V$. Form $W=\coprod_v U_v$ by [lem-weil-pbc-scheme-coproduct-family], with its disjoint open components. The morphisms $U_v\to V$ give a morphism $r:W\to V$ by the coproduct property, and put $w=p r:h_W\to E$. Each affine component of $W$ retains its factorization through $S_v$.

Old step 2:

For any scheme test $T\to E$, the pullback $P=T\times_EV$ is a scheme and $P\to T$ is surjective etale by [def-trf-algebraic-space]. Set $P_v=P\times_VU_v$. These are open subschemes of $P$ by [lem-weil-pbc-open-immersion-base-change] and they cover $P$, since the $U_v$ cover $V$. Each composite $P_v\to P\to T$ is etale by clauses (1) and (2) of [lem-weil-pbc-etale-composition-cancellation]. By [lem-weil-pbc-scheme-coproduct-family], $Q=\coprod_vP_v$ is a scheme. It represents $T\times_EW$: a map to $W$ decomposes its source into inverse images of the disjoint open $U_v$, and maps of this decomposition into the corresponding $P_v$ glue uniquely. The projection $Q\to T$ is etale because its restrictions to the open components are etale, as can be checked directly on affine neighborhoods in [def-weil-etale-morphism]. It is surjective: every point of $T$ has a point above it in $P$, which belongs to some $P_v$ and therefore lifts to $Q$. This proves representable surjective etaleness of $w$. For a point of $|E|$ represented by $\operatorname{Spec}K\to E$, apply this surjectivity to that scheme test and choose a point of its nonempty pullback. Its residue-field map to $W$ represents the given point class, so $|w|$ is surjective. If $V$ is empty the index set and coproducts are empty, and the same assertions hold.

Old step 3:

Give $|E|$ the quotient topology for the surjection $|w|:|W|\to|E|$. Let $p:h_V\to E$ be any other representable surjective etale map from a scheme. By [lem-a1-space-fibre-product-point], the sheaf fibre product $Q=W\times_EV$ is represented by a scheme. Its projections $a:Q\to W$ and $b:Q\to V$ are surjective etale by the scheme-test clauses of [def-trf-algebraic-space], applied respectively to $p$ and $w$. They are continuous scheme morphisms and open by [lem-weil-pbc-etale-morphism-open-map]. If $A\subseteq|E|$ has open inverse image under $|p|$, then $D=|b|^{-1}(|p|^{-1}(A))$ is open in $|Q|$, so $|a|(D)$ is open in $|W|$. We have $|a|(D)=|w|^{-1}(A)$. One inclusion follows from $w a=p b$. For the reverse inclusion, take $x\in|W|$ with $|w|(x)\in A$. Surjectivity of $p$ on the scheme test $\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(x)\to E$ gives a point $y\in|V|$ with $|p|(y)=|w|(x)$; [lem-a1-space-fibre-product-point] then supplies a point of $Q$ projecting to both $x$ and $y$. This point lies in $D$. Thus $A$ is open in the quotient topology defined by $w$. Interchanging $w$ and $p$ proves the converse. This proves independence for every representable surjective etale scheme atlas.

Old step 4:

Let $t:h_T\to E$ be any morphism from a scheme and let $A\subseteq|E|$ be open. The sheaf fibre product $Q=T\times_EW$ is represented by a scheme by [lem-a1-space-fibre-product-point]. The projection $q:Q\to T$ is a surjective etale morphism of schemes by the representable-etale and surjective clauses of [def-trf-algebraic-space]. The inverse image in $Q$ of $|t|^{-1}(A)$ is the pullback of the open subset $|w|^{-1}(A)\subseteq|W|$, hence is open. Since $q$ is etale, its image is open by [lem-weil-pbc-etale-morphism-open-map]. The image is exactly $|t|^{-1}(A)$: a point of $T$ lies in it precisely when its image in $|E|$ lies in $A$, and the equivalence after a common field extension is represented by a point of the fibre product, as in [lem-a1-space-fibre-product-point]. Thus $|t|$ is continuous.

Old step 5:

Assume now that $t:h_T\to E$ is representable etale and let $B\subseteq|T|$ be open. The sheaf fibre product $Q=T\times_EW$ is represented by a scheme by [lem-a1-space-fibre-product-point]. In $Q$, the inverse image of $B$ is open, and the projection $Q\to W$ is etale, hence open by [lem-weil-pbc-etale-morphism-open-map]. Its image is $|w|^{-1}(|t|(B))$: a point in the image gives a common-field representative coming from $B$, while a point of the right-hand side gives such a representative by the fibre-product point criterion [lem-a1-space-fibre-product-point]. Therefore $|w|^{-1}(|t|(B))$ is open, so the quotient definition makes $|t|(B)$ open in $|E|$. Thus $|t|$ is open; taking $B=|T|$ shows that its image is open.
