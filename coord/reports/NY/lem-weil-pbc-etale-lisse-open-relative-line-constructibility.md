## Source gaps
Stacks 0GKY applies 0GJ0 after assuming only connectedness, but 0GJ0 explicitly assumes irreducibility and geometric unibranchness. For example the integer model R=Z[x,y]/(xy), U=A^1_R has intersecting components and is not irreducible. The finite-ring case needed here is nevertheless true: use 0DV5(3), which applies to every connected scheme and finite ring, for the finite monodromy/Sylow argument. This repairs the application without strengthening the item.
Also 0GKX assumes an affine morphism, whereas an arbitrary open of the relative affine line need not be affine. A^1_Z minus V(2,t) is an example. The final split uses a finite principal affine cover, applies the affine result to each intersection, then relative Mayer--Vietoris. These are gaps in the abbreviated source proof, not disputed conclusions.

## New-input argument and small cases
For the primary cover input, a Noetherian affine scheme has finitely many clopen connected components: its finitely many irreducible components form finitely many connected clusters, whose complements are finite unions of closed components. Each clopen is affine. On each connected component 0DV5(3) associates the finite module fibre and its finite monodromy group G. Restrict to an ell-Sylow H by the corresponding finite etale cover of degree [G:H], prime to ell. In the finite Lambda[H]-module M, the ideal generated by ell and the elements h-1 in (Z/ell^n)[H] is nilpotent and its action on M commutes with Lambda; its powers give Lambda-submodule quotients with trivial H-action. They therefore give constant finite Lambda-module sheaves on the connected cover. Normalize the trace by the inverse of the degree on each base component; 03SH proves that it retracts the adjunction unit. Assemble covers over the finitely many components and pad filtrations if necessary. This argument uses module subgroups, not merely additive subgroup filtrations. On the empty scheme choose the empty cover, and for the zero sheaf use the identity cover and length-zero filtration. For ell=2 and nontrivial 2-primary monodromy the Sylow cover may be the identity, and the augmentation filtration still works; no division by ell occurs.
The constant input is strictly smaller: a constant finite module on any affine smooth relative curve, rather than an arbitrary lisse sheaf on an arbitrary open. It is precisely the finite-ring, integer-model specialization of 0GKX. Empty fibres and zero modules give zero images. Neither input assumes the coefficient order is invertible on the base.
