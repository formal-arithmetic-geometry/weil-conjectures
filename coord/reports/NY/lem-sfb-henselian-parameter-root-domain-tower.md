- r=0: the quotient is the coefficient ring; the basis index has one empty function,
  the product is 1, local regular sequences are empty, and the full root construction
  has no generators. The residual condition is precisely connected nonempty Spec C.
- n=1: evaluating every X_i at t_i gives the inverse coefficient map. The power
  transition formulas still make sense and the monomial basis has one element.
- Characteristic dividing n: no etaleness is claimed; k[[t]][x]/(x^p-t) is k[[x]],
  so regularity/domain is consistent. No degree-invertibility condition was added.
- Partial ideal not maximal: R=k[[u,v]], tuple (u), is covered by the local theorem;
  the ideal (u) is not required to be the maximal ideal (u,v).
- Empty/zero rings: `ConnectedSpace (Spec (C/J))` excludes the zero residual ring;
  `IsRegularLocalRing` excludes the zero local ring. No zero-ring counterexample
  enters the domain assertions. The separate degenerate-construction theorem allows
  the zero ring because its conclusions concern actual isomorphisms only.
- Noninjective coefficient maps: for r=0 the unital evaluation k[x]→k at x=0 fits
  the hypotheses. The filtered theorem never assumes transition injectivity.
- Arbitrary small filtered categories, not just linearly ordered or directed-set
  diagrams: the coefficient diagram uses `SmallCategory` and `IsFiltered`.
- For p=1 the allowed orders are every positive integer; for prime p their products
  remain prime to p. Both index sets contain 1, so the full colimit is nonempty.
- Test-ring universes are `max u v`, and coefficient-index universe is u, matching
  the recorded size convention and avoiding vacuous universal properties.
