### Original step 1

Locally of finite presentation implies that on an affine open $\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq T$ over an affine open $\operatorname{Spec}R\subseteq B$ the $R$-algebra $A$ is finitely generated, so $\Omega_{A/R}$ is generated by the differentials of finitely many algebra generators; hence $\Omega^1_{T/B}$ is a finite-type quasi-coherent $\mathcal O_T$-module and $\operatorname{Fit}_d(\Omega^1_{T/B})$ is its $d$-th Fitting ideal sheaf in the sense of [lem-trf-fitting-ideal-generate-locally]. Clause (2) of that lemma, applied with $S=T$, $\mathcal F=\Omega^1_{T/B}$ and $r=d$, says that for every $x\in T$: $x\notin|Z_d|$ if and only if the stalk $\Omega^1_{T/B,x}$ is generated by at most $d$ elements over $\mathcal O_{T,x}$. The set $T\setminus|Z_d|$ is open because $Z_d$ is a closed subscheme.

Cited nodes: lem-trf-fitting-ideal-generate-locally.

### Original step 2

Fix $x\in T$, put $s=g(x)$, and let $\bar s:\operatorname{Spec}\Omega\to B$, $\Omega=\overline{\kappa(s)}$, be the geometric point of the geometric fibre $T_{\bar s}=T\times_B\operatorname{Spec}\Omega$. Choose affine opens $\operatorname{Spec}A\ni x$ of $T$ and $\operatorname{Spec}R\ni s$ of $B$ with $g(\operatorname{Spec}A)\subseteq\operatorname{Spec}R$; then $C=A\otimes_R\kappa(s)$ is a finite type $\kappa(s)$-algebra, $\operatorname{Spec}C$ is an open neighbourhood of $x$ in the fibre $T_s$, and $\operatorname{Spec}(C\otimes_{\kappa(s)}\Omega)$ is the open preimage of $\operatorname{Spec}A$ in $T_{\bar s}$. Since $\kappa(x)\otimes_{\kappa(s)}\Omega\neq0$ it has a prime ideal, giving a point $\bar x\in\operatorname{Spec}(C\otimes_{\kappa(s)}\Omega)$ over $x$; in particular $T_{\bar s}$ is nonempty, hence of pure dimension $d$ [def-weil-pure-dimension] by hypothesis. The morphism $T_{\bar s}\to\operatorname{Spec}\Omega$ is locally of finite presentation, being a base change of $g$ (clause (3) of [lem-weil-pbc-qcqs-morphism-permanence]), so locally of finite type, and [lem-sbc-equidimensional-open-local-dimension] (with $Y=O=T_{\bar s}$, $n=d$) gives $\dim_{\bar x}T_{\bar s}=d$. The dimension at a point is computed on any open neighbourhood (its defining minimum runs over arbitrarily small opens), so $\dim_{\bar x}\operatorname{Spec}(C\otimes_{\kappa(s)}\Omega)=d$; by [lem-weil-pbc-dimension-at-point-field-extension] (with $k=\kappa(s)$, $K=\Omega$, $S=C$) this equals $\dim_x\operatorname{Spec}C=\dim_x(T_s)$. Hence $\dim_x(T_{g(x)})=d$ for every $x\in T$.

Cited nodes: def-weil-pure-dimension, lem-weil-pbc-qcqs-morphism-permanence, lem-sbc-equidimensional-open-local-dimension, lem-weil-pbc-dimension-at-point-field-extension.
