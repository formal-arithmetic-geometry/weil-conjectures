## What remains

The new comparison lemma is deliberately unsplit. Its expected proof uses an integral basis of O_L over O_E and t O_L = v^e O_L: the v^(en) reductions are a cofinal subsystem; taking the inverse limit of the compatible finite-basis isomorphisms gives S as a finite free R-module. Tensoring a derived-complete input with this finite free module preserves its t-adic completeness (on underlying R-modules it is a finite direct sum). The two-term reduction complexes for t^n and v^m, and the cofinal relation t^n O_L = v^(en) O_L, then identify its v-adic completion with itself. The tower used here is precisely the one defining integral extension, by the ordinary tensor computation at each level. This explains why the new statement is true and materially smaller than the item's composition assertion; its complete split is a subsequent worker's task.
