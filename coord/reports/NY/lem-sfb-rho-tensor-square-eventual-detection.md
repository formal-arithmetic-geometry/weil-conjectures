On that orbit the fiber character is trivial because w_0 rho=-rho. An invariant trivialization therefore extends as the section with these effective boundary multiplicities. Torsor descent identifies its functions with right-T-invariant regular functions. These are the geometric tasks isolated in the unsplit node, rather than concealed inside an elementary step. The local source statements support the formulation; its eventual proof must assemble them.

For step 1 I opened the new node and checked that it expressly supplies every object and property taken from it: integrality, quasi-compact separated X, dense stable U, invariant d, and the equivariant function isomorphism. The character is minus rho in Milne's bundle convention, yielding positive rho in the regular-function transformation law. Confusing this inverse would make the section module wrong.

Step 2 cites no theorem: it writes the Laurent-monomial coaction expansion, compares coefficients after coassociativity, applies the counit and takes the zero-weight coefficient. This is the elementary proof of the needed special case of torus weight decomposition, including the coordinate functional with value 1. There is no appeal to semisimplicity of an S-representation in characteristic p. For step 3 the displayed matrix-coefficient formulas prove right-T-invariance and left-S-equivariance after all scalar extensions. Step 1 has already provided the descent isomorphism, so step 3 does not invoke uncited descent. Invariance of v gives the constant function 1, not merely a nonzero value at one point.

For step 4 I opened lem-sc-invert-s-cohomology and verified that its statement gives the degree-zero localization isomorphism for every quasi-compact quasi-separated scheme, not only an ample or projective case. Taking p=0 and the structure sheaf is legitimate. The finite basis of V is where finite dimensionality is used; increasing every denominator to m works for every m above the maximum, including m=N, with exponent zero allowed. The output vector v is nonzero, so dim V is positive and the maximum is well-defined.

Step 5 writes the injectivity argument on trivializing affine domains instead of relying on an undefined uniqueness-of-extension theorem. Density and integrality are explicit in the new node. Tensoring a vector-space injection with F[S] is injective by extending a basis; this is what permits equality of coactions globally from equality on U. This avoids replacing a group-scheme identity by an identity only on F-points. The nonzero value follows because the restriction of d^m is a generator on nonempty U.

For step 6 I opened lem-sfb-homogeneous-line-bundle-sections, lem-sc-external-global-sections-tensor-bijective and lem-sfb-reductive-borel-pair-structure. Tensor powers are handled by multiplying the line-bundle transition functions, not an additional comparison theorem. The pure-tensor description proves that theta_m respects translation, so its inverse preserves the coaction identity and the nonzero value. The two external-unit results are declared generic tool uses with specific reasons in split_report.
