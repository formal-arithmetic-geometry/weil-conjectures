Both directions preserve these monomial spans. This needs no new grading theorem.

lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-symmetric-algebra states both quasi-coherence of the symmetric algebra of a quasi-coherent module and its exact affine-section identification. Its hypothesis holds for the displayed free sheaf: on an affine open it is the tilde of the finite free module, with the usual componentwise restrictions. This is the same globally finite free sheaf on every open, not an assertion about arbitrary infinite locally free sums. def-nagata-relative-proj defines gluing over affine base opens; lemma-nagata-relative-proj-exists actually supplies the compatible restriction isomorphisms and structural maps needed here. def-nagata-affine-proj states the standard-open identification with degree-zero homogeneous localization, and def-nagata-homogeneous-localization-degree-zero defines its fractions and relation. Over a nonreduced affine base the fraction definition still applies without reducedness, integrality, or Noetherian hypotheses. All charts invert a homogeneous element of positive degree (one). The definition does not assume that intersections of arbitrary affine opens are affine, and adds no finite-type, separatedness or qcqs hypothesis to X.
