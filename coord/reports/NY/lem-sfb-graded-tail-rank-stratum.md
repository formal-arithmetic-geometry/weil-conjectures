### Edge cases checked before writing

- Empty base U: generation and quasi-coherence conditions do not assume a point;
  the representing stratum is empty. Arbitrary tests still have the right
  factorization property. No Nonempty constraint occurs in either module.
- Zero graded module: t=0 is allowed. Its generating span is the zero submodule.
  If all tail ranks are zero the stratum is U; if any tail rank is positive, it is
  empty. The rank-zero locally free definition is the existing free-on-Fin-0 case.
- Generators in positive degrees: a generator in degree a>0 is admitted by the
  new predicate. When n<a it contributes no generator to degree n. This is why
  the upstream degree-zero predicate would be an incorrect extra assumption.
- m=0: degree zero is tested; the structural unit must be invertible, and no
  implicit positive-degree cutoff is introduced.
- Constant zero or positive polynomials: floor reads the intended natural rank.
  A polynomial negative or nonintegral in the tail is excluded exactly by hP;
  such values below m impose no restriction.
- Algebra concentrated in degree zero: the degree-one generation condition
  correctly forces all positive pieces to vanish; its zero B 1 is coherent.
- Empty generating/index sets and empty chosen witness sets in the proof are
  allowed. The docstring explicitly takes V=U when there is no offending function.
- Nonreduced test schemes are included. This is not merely a locus of underlying
  points or fibre dimensions: the entire ordinary pullback sheaf must be locally
  free, retaining nilpotent rank equations (for example R/(epsilon) over the
  dual numbers with desired rank one is imposed by V(epsilon), not by all Spec R).
- Arbitrary non-Noetherian U' and T remain permitted in the final conjunct.
  The statement uses the actual fibre product and iterated tensor pullbacks.
