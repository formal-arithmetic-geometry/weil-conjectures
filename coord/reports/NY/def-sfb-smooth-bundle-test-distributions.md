## Agent argument notes retained for future splits

These notes are not proof steps inside new nodes, which remain statement-only.

For the density line, chain rule gives |det D(z/x)|=|det D(z/y)| |det D(y/x)|; determinants of coordinate changes never vanish. Thus the transition functions are positive smooth scalars, satisfy the cocycle relation, and define a real line bundle by the stated quotient. Tensor transitions satisfy their cocycle relation by functoriality of tensor products. These constructions have no orientation restriction and include the empty base and zero-rank factors.

For integral invariance, insert 1=sum sigma_j in each rho_i chart integral. Each rho_i sigma_j density has compact support inside the chart overlap: N is compact Hausdorff and both closed supports lie in their chart domains. The ordinary Euclidean change-of-variables formula and the absolute-Jacobian density coefficient rule identify its i-chart and j-chart integrals. Reorder the finite double sum and use sum rho_i=1. Real linearity follows from the integrability of every smooth compactly supported coefficient and finite additivity of these integrals. This is a genuine analytic theorem and is now a node, not an unproved clause of a definition.

For the smooth pairing, refine a finite cover to bundle-trivializing charts and use integral invariance. With K_i containing the compact coordinate support of rho_i, the absolute integral is bounded by a finite sum C_i p_{x_i,e_i,K_i,0}(phi); C_i may be chosen as a bound on the smooth section coefficients times the finite Lebesgue volume of a compact coordinate neighborhood. A chart lacking a bundle trivialization can be refined finitely before this estimate. This proves continuity using finitely many defining seminorms. It also shows why no metric or L² hypothesis is needed. The node itself asserts only continuity and linearity for every allowed choice, not an unproved universal norm estimate.

For smooth multiplication, Leibniz gives p_{x,e,K,k}(h phi) <= C_{h,K,k} p_{x,e,K,k}(phi), since the finitely many derivatives of h up to k are bounded on K. For chart extension, first fix a compact support K inside V. The extended section vanishes on a neighborhood of N minus x^{-1}(K), so it is smooth across the boundary of U. In each target chart/frame seminorm, only the compact intersection with x^{-1}(K) contributes. Finite compact neighborhoods within the overlap bound derivatives of the transition matrix, density Jacobian and chart changes. The chain and product rules bound that seminorm by finitely many fixed-support derivative seminorms of f, up to the requested order. Directional partial derivatives are evaluations of iterated Frechet derivatives on unit coordinate vectors, so those bounds are controlled by the actual Mathlib fixed-support seminorms. Finally the local test LF universal property gives continuity of J_{x,e}. These comparison estimates are the content of the unsplit operation theorem, not a claimed Mathlib leaf.

In the item, multiplication, local restriction, derivatives and transposes are simply continuous-linear composition. A multi-index derivative is a finite composition of coordinate-direction lineDerivCLM maps. The scalar sign (-1)^|alpha| preserves continuity. No theorem about invariant differential operators, smooth kernels or pseudodifferential calculus is imported implicitly.
