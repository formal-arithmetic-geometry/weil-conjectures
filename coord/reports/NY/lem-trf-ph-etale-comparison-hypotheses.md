## Review by NY

I read the chapter and sk-i-curve-zero unit, the item's exact statement, 0F0I with its whole proof, and the five displayed hypotheses in 0EZ3. They concern ordinary abelian sheaves and ordinary derived direct images on the two big sites; no condensed modules, constructibility restriction or invertibility of orders is inserted. I read the four new source places listed above, including the 0DDJ comparison proof and the affine standard-cover construction and composition argument in 0DBF. The four files introduce their own schemes, morphisms, sheaves, sites, coefficient and size conventions. Their `stmt_deps` define the notions named, their TeX is encoded once, and they have no proof fields beyond empty lists. I checked them against B1-B9, C1-C6, D1-D5 and E1-E7, rather than treating the source citation as a completed argument.

For step 1 I read def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaves (local, section-dependent annihilators) and lem-weil-pbc-abelian-sheaf-epi-locally-surjective (exactly local lifts of sections through an abelian sheaf epimorphism). Those statements justify the cokernel lifting. Kernel and extension annihilator arguments are written out: the extension uses n for the quotient and then m for ne in the subobject, hence mn for e. There is no assumed common exponent.

For step 2 I read lem-trf-big-small-etale-exact-retraction: it asserts exact restriction and the unit isomorphism on every small abelian sheaf. It does not assert the whole weak-Serre result. I also read lem-weil-pbc-site-pullback-abelian-exact, which explicitly states preservation of kernels, cokernels and short exact sequences for any morphism of sites. def-trf-geometric-morphism gives the adjunction. The displayed Hom bijection supplies full faithfulness, so the kernel/cokernel argument applies to every morphism between essential-image objects, not just a specially chosen morphism.

For step 3 those same cited statements give both exact rows and the counit. I read lem-weil-pbc-short-five-lemma: two exact rows and invertible endpoint verticals give an invertible middle vertical. I checked that the endpoint counits are invertible by the adjunction triangle identity and the unit from step 2. Exact R first makes RH a small torsion extension; exact L and the counit then recover H. This repairs the otherwise unproved identification in the source paragraph, rather than assuming it from exact R alone.

For step 4 I read def-pc-fibre-product, which states existence for every pair of scheme maps, and lem-weil-pbc-proper-base-change, which states properness of the arbitrary base change. Both have the hypotheses actually available; no qcqs condition is added to the arbitrary Y'->Y.

For step 5 I checked the new evaluation/square statement and lem-weil-pbc-inverse-image-stalk-germ, which gives the stalk isomorphism for every scheme map and every abelian sheaf. I also read def-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks: a germ is a colimit of pointed etale neighbourhood sections. That is exactly what permits shrinking an annihilating equality and obtaining local torsion, as the step spells out. There is no assumption that the scheme map is etale.

For step 6 the evaluation formula includes naturality under both projections, and the new section-equalizer statement includes the same pullback arrows; I compared the two formulas with U, V and V times_U V instantiated explicitly. The auxiliary sheaf is a_s^{-1}F on U, never an unrelated sheaf on V. Properness and surjectivity are hypotheses on p; neither flatness nor a torsion condition is used in that lemma.

For step 7 I checked the affine-basis statement against 0DBG and 0DBQ and the equalizer conclusion already proved in step 6. The only additional operations are the existing etale sheaf condition and its Zariski special case. Products of the open covering pieces cover the double fibre product. They need not be affine, and the proof does not say they are affine. Separatedness is proved on a chosen affine cover, then on standard refinements, then on their proper singleton; the same argument works after base change, so it proves compatibility on V_j times_U V_j' and equality with s_i on U_i. This fills the source's omitted gluing checks. The topology comparison comes from the basis tool's etale-cover assertion.

For step 8 I checked that K=F[0] is in D+ and has torsion cohomology, exactly the input of the new 0DDJ(2) tool. The inverse-image exactness lemma permits cohomology to pass through pi^{-1}; the argument via kernels, images and cokernels is written, not an uncited derived comparison. I read lem-pc-separated-quasi-separated and def-weil-finite-type-morphism, so a proper f is qcqs. I read the entire statement of lem-sbc-qcqs-higher-images-prime-torsion: choosing the set of all primes gives every stalk element of R^if_*F a positive annihilator. The earlier germ argument converts this to the local torsion convention. This is the precise 0DDD input of the source; the finite-coefficient or constructible variants elsewhere in the DAG would not suffice.

For step 9 I checked what the affine-basis lemma actually gives: membership of each W_jk->V_j in the pulled-back generated sieve. This yields a map to an original U_i, so the last sentence is an actual refinement, with the maps specified by membership, not just set-theoretic coverage. I read def-trf-ph-scheme-site: a proper surjection is a generator, hence its singleton is ph covering. The site axioms in def-weil-pbc-sites-and-cohomology then compose the three levels. Finiteness is claimed only for each W_j's affine covering; the first covering may be infinite. All these choices are small in the chosen presentations.
