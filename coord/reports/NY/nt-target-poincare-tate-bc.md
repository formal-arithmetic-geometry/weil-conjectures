## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the upstream lemma at Poincare.lean lines 78–81 quantifies over arbitrary schemes, without making X a scheme over the section's field k. Its proof is sorry. The unrestricted assertion fails for the following explicit characteristic-ell nil-thickening. This is not a missing Noetherianity condition: the counterexample's underlying space is one point and hence topologically Noetherian.

Fix an algebraic closure K of F_ell and the abelian group
G = direct sum over i >= 1 of (Q_ell/Z_ell).
Let A=K[G], the commutative group algebra, and let X=Spec A. The augmentation ideal J is nil: every one of its elements lies in the augmentation ideal of K[H] for a finite ell-primary subgroup H of G, and that ideal is nilpotent (for a cyclic factor of order ell^r it is generated by t-1 with (t-1)^(ell^r)=0; tensor finitely many such presentations). Thus A/J=K and Spec A has one point. The ring is henselian: each finite collection of coefficients and a candidate residue root lies in a finite-group subalgebra K[H], an Artinian local ring with residue K, so every simple residue root lifts there. The residue field is algebraically closed. Hence A is strictly henselian local.

The singleton component space is extremally disconnected, so X is affine weakly contractible. This is precisely the criterion of `det-pe-wlocal-strict-contractible` and `det-pe-weak-contractible-equivalences`; its projectivity means every pro-etale covering of X has a section in one covering member, since X is connected. Evaluation on X preserves limits and colimits by `det-pe-sections-limits-colimits`. The point-basis formula `lem-weil-pbc-condensed-point-basis-evaluation` gives R_X(X)=Z_ell and B_X(X)=Qbar_ell. It follows that the actual Tate construction has
L_X(X) = Qbar_ell tensor_(Z_ell) lim_n mu_(ell^n)(A).
Here the free-module levels evaluate to Z_ell tensor_Z mu_(ell^n)(A)=mu_(ell^n)(A), with exactly the root-power transitions. Evaluation computes these tensors because they are sheafified tensor coproduct/coequalizer constructions and evaluation on this connected affine weakly contractible object preserves those constructions. The level n=0 is zero and does not affect the limit.

For each summand i choose compatible standard generators g_(i,n) of its cyclic subgroups of orders ell^n, with g_(i,n+1)^ell=g_(i,n). Their group-ring basis elements give a compatible root sequence e_i in that limit. These e_i are Z_ell-linearly independent: a finite relation with coefficients a_i, at level n, says that the group basis element corresponding to sum_i (a_i mod ell^n)g_(i,n) is the identity basis element; independence of the summands implies every a_i is zero modulo ell^n, for every n. They remain independent after inverting ell and extending the field to Qbar_ell. Consequently L_X(X) is infinite-dimensional.

If L_X were locally finitely presented, a pro-etale finite-presentation cover would split over X, giving a finite presentation on X. Evaluation, which preserves cokernels, would then give a finite-dimensional Qbar_ell-vector space. This contradicts the preceding independence. Nor can finite stratification hide the problem: a nonempty stratum of this one-point X has surjective closed immersion into X. Local finite presentation reflects along that immersion by `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-module-lfp-closed-reflection` (equivalently the exact condensed equivalence `new-lem-weil-pbc-condensed-surjective-closed-equivalence`). Hence constructibility would again imply local finite presentation of L_X on X. Thus d=1,n=0 disproves the unrestricted target.

The corrected statement requires ell invertible on X. This is exactly the domain in the current blueprint `sk-def-sp-tate-twist`, not a new proof route. It retains arbitrary schemes within that domain: no finite type, quasi-compactness, Noetherianity, or finite field condition is added. The Poincare module's introduction and its intended geometric Poincare uses concern separated finite-type schemes over k with char(k) different from ell, so they satisfy this condition. However, the immediate wrapper boundedConstructibleTateTwist at lines 84–86 still quantifies over arbitrary schemes and calls this lemma.
