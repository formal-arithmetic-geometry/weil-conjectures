## Source error — STATEMENT-DISPUTED

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the former F=Omega clause of both this item and lem-sfb-normed-scalar-profinite-cech-exact is false when Omega has the norm topology. This is a correction of an agent-added input below the blueprint, not an error in the text of paragraph (3). I read main-20261015 sk-thm-sf-ctf-stability-direct-images.proof.md: paragraph (3) delegates the open-coefficient case to sk-prop-open-coefficients for O_E, E and Qlb, and says nothing about acyclicity of arbitrary profinite parameters for the sheaf C(-,Omega).

Here is the degree-one counterexample from the return, with its Baire step made explicit. Take an orthogonal Q_ell-basis (b_k) of Omega; its continuous coordinates lambda_k extend to its completion C_ell, and an element of C_ell belongs to Omega exactly when it has finitely many nonzero coordinates. Let S be the Cantor space. Choose distinct p_k such that every tail of (p_k) is dense. Decompose S minus p_k into clopen annuli C^k_n tending to p_k, and let phi_k be 1 on the even annuli and 0 on the odd annuli and at p_k. Choose nonzero eps_k in Q_ell with ||eps_k b_k|| tending to zero. The function w(s)=sum_k eps_k phi_k(s)b_k is C_ell-valued with relatively compact image. Each of its coordinates is continuous except at its own p_k.

The map w from the discrete underlying set of S extends to a continuous W:T=beta(S_disc) -> C_ell. Let q:T -> S be the continuous extension of the identity. If q(x)=s is not a p_k, all coordinates of W(x)-w(s) vanish. If s=p_k, only its k-th coordinate can be nonzero. Thus f(x,y)=W(y)-W(x), for q(x)=q(y), takes values in Omega. It is continuous into the subspace Omega and satisfies f(y,z)-f(x,z)+f(x,y)=0. It is a degree-one Cech cocycle for q.

If f=dg for a continuous g:T -> Omega, then W-g descends, by the quotient property of q, to a continuous h:S -> C_ell. Evaluating on the original discrete points gives h(s)-w(s) in Omega for every s. For each k put E_k={s: lambda_k h(s) != eps_k phi_k(s)}. Continuity of lambda_k h and the two alternating values of phi_k imply that the interior U_k of E_k meets every neighbourhood of p_k: if equality persisted on an entire punctured neighbourhood, continuity at p_k would give two different limiting values. Since every tail of (p_k) is dense, each union of the U_k for k>=N is dense and open. Baire gives a point in their intersection, hence lying in infinitely many E_k. At that point h-w has infinitely many nonzero coordinates, contradicting h-w in Omega. Therefore f is not a continuous Omega-valued boundary.

Consequently H^1(S,underline Omega) is nonzero. For A=underline Omega, evaluation of R^1 internal Hom_A(A[S],A) at the point is H^1(S,A_ab): the free-generator calculation gives I^n(S), and point evaluation is exact because covers of the point split. Thus the old item's asserted concentration in degree zero also fails. Empty sets, singleton parameters and finite surjections do not detect this obstruction; checking only those small cases was insufficient in the returned report.

The repair chooses the finite E branch. It does not relabel norm-topological Omega as another coefficient ring. Bhatt–Scholze Lemma 4.3.7 treats the sequential closed-colimit topology, not the norm-subspace topology on Omega. That explains why the literature's algebraic-closure example does not imply the rejected statement. A finite-subextension or completed-coefficient extension would require its own statement and proof; it is not asserted by this delivery.

For finite E the analytic input has a legitimate completeness argument. Write O_E=lim_m O_E/pi^m; the finite discrete transition maps admit continuous sections, hence are profinitely split. Lemma 4.3.9(2),(4) gives the relevant higher-evaluation vanishing, and (3) applies to E=colim_m pi^(-m)O_E along closed embeddings. The subspace stages are compact, and this colimit topology is the field topology: intersection of a set with each stage is open exactly when it is locally open in E, since the stages are also open and cover E. Unlike norm-topological Omega, E meets the hypotheses. The Cech consequence can also be checked by reducing an integral-valued cocycle modulo pi^m, taking compatible discrete primitives, and taking their limit in O_E. Compatibility is arranged by correcting differences by boundaries; in degree one a difference is a descended function on S, which lifts through the finite discrete quotient. Compactness bounds the original E-valued cocycle, so scalar multiplication makes it integral. This is a proof plan for the new unsplit node, not a proof inserted into its statement.
