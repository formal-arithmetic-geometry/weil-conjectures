Step 2 writes the unique kernel element g1*g2^(-1) on arbitrary test schemes, not only on field points. Exactness as fppf sheaves supplies local lifts of the identity section of B, and each lift gives an equivariant sheaf isomorphism t -> i(t)s with the displayed inverse. I checked this against the Stacks extension criterion: one may pass to a common extension and descend connectedness along the surjective projection. This avoids an unjustified rational-point choice over the original residue field.
