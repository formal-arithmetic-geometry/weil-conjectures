## Source error

In the online PDF's proof of Lemma A.3.4.15, the prose describing the second factorization calls its right leg an “injective trivial cofibration”. That cannot be the intended right class: it follows a projective cofibration, and Definition A.3.4.1(b) requires a trivial fibration. With the one-object indexing category and Kan simplicial sets, the literal two-cofibration assertion would factor the fold map from two discrete points to one point as two monomorphisms. Their composite would be monic, whereas the fold is not. I use a projective trivial fibration as the right leg. This is supplied by the ordinary enriched model factorization, agrees with the chunk definition, and is objectwise a weak equivalence as the essential-image argument needs. This repairs a label in the source proof, not the item's statement.
