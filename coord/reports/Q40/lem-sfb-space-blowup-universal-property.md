Edge cases checked mathematically before writing:

- K = O_X: its image is the unit ideal, locally generated by the regular element 1,
  and the blowup is X, so every map has its identity-comparison lift.
- K = 0: on a nonempty Y the Cartier condition fails, since 0 is not regular in a
  nonzero local ring. For empty Y the pointwise condition is vacuous and the unique
  empty-source map is the required lift.
- Empty schemes and empty charts are allowed; there is no Nonempty binder. Disconnected,
  nonreduced, non-quasi-compact and non-quasi-separated spaces are not excluded.
- A regular element may be a unit, so the empty effective Cartier divisor is retained.
  No characteristic assumption is made. The proof docstring explicitly retains n=0
  in the tensor-power multiplication argument.
- The Cartier condition is substantive: a map whose image ideal is zero on a nonempty
  reduced affine test does not satisfy it. It is not a tautological property of all maps.
