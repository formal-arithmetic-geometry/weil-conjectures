The cohomology sheaves are compared by exact inverse image (step 3), $\mathcal H^r(\nu_Y^*V)=\nu_Y^*\mathcal H^r(V)$, and the edge map sends a class to its local classes (step 10). Restriction and the comparison unit are natural: on an etale object $W\to Y$ the comparison takes the restricted etale class to the corresponding restricted pro-etale class, so the presheaf-to-sheaf maps commute with it. These local equalities give equality of the resulting sheaf sections. Thus the boundary, the edge formula and all displayed identifications agree with their ordinary etale counterparts.
