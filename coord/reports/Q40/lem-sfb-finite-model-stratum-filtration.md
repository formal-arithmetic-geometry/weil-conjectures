## Retained argument for the new node

The removed step follows 09Y5 by Noetherian induction on the closed remainder. A nonempty remainder has a component Z and a nonempty ambient open O in Z. From its finite closed cover by closures of the old parts, irreducibility yields a part P with Z contained in its closure. Consequently O meets P densely, and local closedness makes U=O intersect P a nonempty open. Split each old part of the closed remainder T minus U by the closed subset closure(U) minus U and its complement, and refine this partition inductively. Since U is dense in Z, closure(U)=Z. The refined remainder separates that boundary from its complement, so closure(U) is a union of refined strata; the other stratum closures are already good in the closed remainder. DCC provides the induction and the empty remainder gives the base case. This generic theorem asserts neither ordering by open prefixes nor anything about module sheaves; those are still the item's steps 3-7.
