## Argument for the new statement-only node

This is the argument assigned to its future worker, recorded here rather than inserted into its statement. Let h:X->Y satisfy the new node. The continuous image of quasi-compact X is quasi-compact, so finitely many affine opens of Y cover it. On a locally Noetherian Y these affine opens are Noetherian; also Y is quasi-separated, so the morphism from quasi-compact X is quasi-compact (existing generic permanence lemma). Hence the inverse image of each selected affine open is quasi-compact and admits a finite affine cover. It suffices to prove finiteness of the image of each such affine chart Spec A -> Spec B, where B is Noetherian. Put I=ker(B->A). The map factors through the affine scheme Z=Spec(B/I) and is dominant there: its image has closure V(I), as follows from contraction and the formula for closure of an affine morphism image. The map Spec A -> Z is quasi-compact. By Stacks 01RL every generic point of an irreducible component of Z lies in its image. Z is Noetherian and has finitely many irreducible components (0BA8). Every image point is a specialization of one of these generic points, so the antichain hypothesis forces equality. Thus this chart image is contained in a finite set. Take the two finite unions of chart images. This includes the empty source (empty finite unions) and does not require A Noetherian or the map of finite type. Using B/I avoids the erroneous claim that an arbitrary nonreduced source factors through the reduced closed image.
