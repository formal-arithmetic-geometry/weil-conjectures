The existing quasi-excellent-formal-affines statement gives quasi-excellence on every affine open of the completion of a quasi-excellent ring; completeness identifies that completion with the original chart. Step 3 uses the existing completed-regular-morphism theorem's full arbitrary-formal-affine conclusion. Step 4 is the full descent argument. I checked the common neighbourhood is principal in both target charts and then in both source charts; no affine intersection is assumed. Step 5 uses regular ascent with flatness and regular fibres exactly as stated by lem-weil-pbc-regular-map-ascent, and reduced ascent exactly as stated by lem-exc-regular-map-reduced-target. Reduced descent is written as the sheaf axiom for nilpotent sections after a common affine refinement. Regular descent uses a flat local map into a regular localized chart; the flat-under-regular node says precisely this. The regular-local-localization node then covers all primes below maximals. The definition of regular ring is only used after all localizations have been shown regular.

Step 6 checks singular *ideals*, not just loci. The two local regularity implications come from 031E and 00OF at the map supplied by 07C0. J-2 makes the nonregular loci closed. The quotient A/J_A is a finite type A-algebra, so 07C1 applies; 07QK then proves B/J_AB reduced with both rings Noetherian. This removes the possible gap of extending a radical ideal without showing its extension radical.

Step 7 checks completed ideal sheaves restrict to the extended ideals. Completion-exact-finite-modules states exactness of these actual maps, not merely existence of an abstract equivalence. Completing a finite presentation gives exactly the image ideal J_AC. The common-principal-refinement statement then verifies overlap equality, and the sheaf axiom explicitly constructs the gluing. Coherence is the formal coherence definition in the item: locally the completed ideal associated to a finite ideal.
