## Original nine-step proof, verbatim

### Original step 1

Put $B=L[Q]$ and $I=\mathfrak mA+(X_1,\ldots,X_r)A$. The basis map $j:B\to A$, $z^q\mapsto X^q$, preserves products because exponents add; distinct basis exponents give distinct series coefficients, so it is injective [def-weil-pbc-multivariate-power-series]. Write $|e|=\sum_i e_i$. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-power-series-combined-ideal-coefficients] with $\Lambda=L$, base ideal $\mathfrak m$ and $d=r$: $F\in I^n$ precisely when $c_e(F)\in\mathfrak m^{n-|e|}$ for every $|e|<n$. This statement assumes neither Noetherianity nor completeness, so it applies to the given $L$.

### Original step 2

Choose nonzero generators $q_1,\ldots,q_s$ of $Q$ and let $D=\max(1,|q_1|,\ldots,|q_s|)$; when $Q=\{0\}$ take the empty list and $D=1$. Put $E_n=j^{-1}(I^n)$. Since $j(J)\subseteq I$, $J^n\subseteq E_n$. Conversely take $f=\sum c_qz^q\in E_{Dn}$. Express $q$ with $h$ generator factors, so $|q|\leq Dh$. If $h\geq n$, its monomial already belongs to $J^n$. If $h<n$, the coefficient criterion gives $c_q\in\mathfrak m^{Dn-|q|}$, and $Dn-|q|\geq D(n-h)\geq n-h$; hence $c_qz^q\in J^{n-h}J^h=J^n$. Summing the finitely many terms proves $E_{Dn}\subseteq J^n$. These estimates include $q=0$, with $h=0$.

### Original step 3

For $x=(x_n)_n\in T$ choose a polynomial representative $f_n\in B$ of $x_n\in B/J^n$ [def-weil-pbc-adic-completion]. Compatibility gives $f_m-f_n\in J^n$ for $m\geq n$. By step 2 and the coefficient criterion, $c_e(jf_m)-c_e(jf_n)\in\mathfrak m^{n-|e|}$ whenever $n>|e|$. For each $k\geq1$ the residue of $c_e(jf_n)$ modulo $\mathfrak m^k$ is thus independent of $n\geq k+|e|$. These residues form a compatible system. The assumed bijection $L\to\varprojlim L/\mathfrak m^k$ supplies a unique $c_e(x)\in L$ with these residues. Changing representatives changes no stabilized residue. Thus $\iota(x)=\sum_e c_e(x)X^e$ is well defined as a series [def-weil-pbc-multivariate-power-series].

### Original step 4

The construction gives $c_e(\iota x)-c_e(jf_n)\in\mathfrak m^{n-|e|}$ for $|e|<n$, hence $\iota x-jf_n\in I^n$ by the coefficient criterion. Representatives for $x+y$, $xy$ are $f_n+g_n$, $f_ng_n$.
