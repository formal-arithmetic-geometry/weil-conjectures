Edge cases checked before and after drafting:

- Empty schemes are allowed everywhere. A generation set on an empty test is all
  of the test, so the empty scheme factors through the generation open as it should;
  the open is not incorrectly replaced by the bottom presheaf.
- With no positive-degree sections, the generation open has no nonempty scheme test.
  Its map to the empty Proj is still included. Zero rings and empty charts are allowed.
- Degree zero is used for the unit but is never admitted as a generator of the open.
  Arbitrarily large positive degrees are permitted. There is no degree-one hypothesis.
- The generation criterion is local on all open scheme tests, so it covers a
  non-quasi-compact source without requiring one global degree or a finite cover.
- A_0 need not equal O_T. The base-change maps are arbitrary, including non-flat,
  identity, and empty-source maps. No equality of independent universes is imposed:
  all scheme tests use the same universe as the schemes in the recorded space model.
- Coherence and cartesian naturality are equations of complete sheaf morphisms,
  not tests merely on global sections. This matters for bundles with no global sections.
