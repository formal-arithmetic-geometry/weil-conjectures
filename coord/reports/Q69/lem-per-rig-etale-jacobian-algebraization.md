## Review by Q69

I checked the item against 0AKG's hypothesis that E is complete and E/IE is finite type: the finite completed polynomial presentation supplies the latter, with the completion-mod-power leaf available. Condition (3) of 0AJU is exactly kernel and cokernel of the displayed two-term differential; it does not require the individual differential to be an isomorphism before inverting I. The item's conclusion includes both the finite TYPE condition and etaleness over the base complement. It does not assert finiteness as an A-module, and I have not used its parent theorem as a black box.

For the principal child I checked 0ALS together with 0AJU(3). The Noetherian assumption, principal generator, completed finite polynomial presentation and annihilation of both cohomology modules all occur in its statement. It permits a zero divisor a, a=0, a a unit, r=0, and the zero algebra. It does not silently impose the regular-generator hypotheses of some other approximation lemmas.

For the base-change child I checked 0GB2 and the quotient formula in remark-take-bar/0AL6: the quotient E/HE is already complete when one changes to an ideal with the same vanishing locus as IA_0. Noetherianity ensures the two ideal topologies are comparable by powers. Tags 0AJP/0AJQ supply the finite completed polynomial presentation, and 0AJU(3) supplies the kernel/cokernel formulation. I explicitly allow a new torsion exponent; tensoring the original kernel through a nonflat quotient was not used. The case H=0 and K=I is harmless, H=A produces the zero ring, and nilpotent images of a are allowed.

For the reduction child I read the entire compatibility paragraph and construction of B-prime in 0AKA. The input asks for models at every power a^m, as the proof requires, and explicitly does NOT assume these models have compatible transition maps. Its conclusion records the intermediate a-complete F, finite topological presentation, principal torsion, and the map whose I-completion is E. The construction of compatible models, the inverse limit, and its uniform principal torsion are precisely the non-elementary subclaim isolated here. It is not disguised as an elementary limit step in the item. In the source the uniform bound comes from the original rig-etale E and 0AJU's uniform truncation criterion, not from arbitrary exponent bounds of individually chosen C_m. The child retains that original torsion hypothesis. If a=0, the quotients are E and the statement reduces to an algebraic model already present in its hypothesis. If a is a unit, E and its complete models can be zero. These cases expose no missing regularity assumption.

For the shrinking child I checked 0ALQ(2) and its comparison with completed naive cotangent complexes (0GAF): C is already finite type over a Noetherian ring. The use of Nakayama in the source gives an element of 1+IC, not an arbitrary function cutting out a neighborhood. That distinction is needed for the elementary completion comparison in the item. Its given torsion presentation can be used by 0AJU's presentation-independent criterion. When I=0 one can take g=1 because the requested etale locus is empty; when I=A the completion is zero and g=0 is allowed, giving the zero localization. Neither edge case requires C to be nonzero.
