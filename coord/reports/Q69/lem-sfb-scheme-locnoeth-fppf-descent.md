I checked the unchanged item hypothesis by hypothesis against Tag 034C. The source's locality theorem applies to the singleton family because flatness, local finite presentation and topological surjectivity are exactly the three covering conditions in `def-pc-fppf-site`. It does not ask for quasi-compactness of that singleton morphism. The proof uses only the descent implication, not the finite-type ascent implication in the source, so the latter needs no dependency. For Step 1 I opened `def-pc-fppf-site`, `lem-trf-fppf-covering-is-fpqc` and `def-weil-pbc-fpqc-covering`. The first actually states joint surjectivity along with flatness and local finite presentation. The second says every such covering is fpqc and explicitly gives finitely many affine opens of its original source members covering an arbitrary affine target open. The third states the same finite-affine-open condition. Since the original family is a singleton, these opens are all opens of X, and equality of their union of images with V implies that each image lies in V. This obtains the necessary opens without using the proof of another node. No base-change or composition lemma is hidden here.

For Step 2 I opened `pqf-lem-affine-open-locnoeth`, `def-weil-affine-spectrum` and `lem-weil-pbc-flat-morphism-affine-criterion`. The first supplies Noetherianity of every chosen B_j from local Noetherianity of X, without any quasi-compactness of X. The spectrum node states the correspondence of all morphisms between affine spectra with ring homomorphisms. The flatness node explicitly applies to ANY source and target affine opens with image containment. We have exactly that containment from Step 1 and flatness of the original f, so its statement, not an unstated restriction-flatness theorem, proves each A to B_j flat. I checked the hypotheses even though the whole f inverse image of V need not be affine or quasi-compact.

For Step 3 I opened `lem-weil-pbc-finite-product-flat-modules` and `lem-weil-pbc-flat-surjective-spectrum-faithfully-flat`. The product lemma allows n=0 and gives flatness over A, not merely flatness over individual factors. The spectrum lemma states the implication flat plus surjective spectrum implies faithfully flat. Surjectivity is proved in the step: the refinement gives a prime in some B_j above each prime p of A, and contraction along the product projection gives the required prime of B; the composition of ring maps is precisely phi_j. Inverse images of prime ideals are prime because the quotient maps into the domain B_j/q_j, an elementary ring fact. Thus this citation is used only after both of its hypotheses have been verified.

For Step 4 I opened `def-weil-pbc-noetherian-ring`, whose statement is finite generation of every ideal. The finite-product Noetherian assertion is not silently quoted as a theorem and does not become a new node. The proof writes the coordinate-idempotent argument: projected ideals are ideals because the projection of a product ring is surjective; lift their finite generators, isolate each coordinate by its idempotent, and reconstruct every ideal element by the finite sum. This works for arbitrary commutative rings, zero factors, and the empty product, so elementary=true is appropriate for this explicit few-line calculation.

For Step 5 I opened `lem-weil-pbc-faithfully-flat-descent-noetherian` and checked its statement against Algebra 033E. It assumes only faithful flatness of A to B and Noetherianity of B. Both have been supplied, so it gives precisely Noetherianity of A; we have not assumed that result in constructing the covering. For Step 6 I opened `pqf-def-locnoeth`: it defines local Noetherianity by an affine cover with Noetherian section rings, which the arbitrary-affine argument has supplied. This last definitional assembly is elementary; its unrelated remark about locally finite type morphisms is not used. citations.json contains a verbatim statement quotation for each of the eleven step citations.

I tested the empty target (empty affine cover), an empty chosen affine open V (empty product ring), zero source factors, one affine member, a non-quasi-compact source with infinitely many available affine opens, and a non-separated target. The proof never takes intersections to be affine and never selects a global finite cover of the target.
