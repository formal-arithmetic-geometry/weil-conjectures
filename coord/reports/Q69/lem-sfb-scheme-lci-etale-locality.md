## Review by Q69

I checked each proof citation against the cited node's statement, copied it into citations.json, and checked its hypotheses in the step where it is used. Step 1 uses the definition of an immersion and of Koszul regularity, and the open-immersion/smooth-composition statement. Restriction to an open of a closed subscheme is a closed subscheme of a suitable ambient open; it does not assert that an arbitrary map factors through a non-open subscheme merely from an image inclusion. Step 1 is elementary local manipulation of the defining witnesses, with smooth restriction explicitly cited.

Step 2 uses the actual finite-generation and finite-presentation definitions to produce the polynomial surjection; this is not an appeal to an unstated embedding theorem. The polynomial ambient is smooth by the affine-space node. For a prescribed immersion, the new ambient-comparison lemma applies to its restriction and a witness immersion over the same base, and gives the Koszul property near each point. The smooth target-open composition is explicit. Step 3 uses clause (2) of proper-base-change, which really states smooth base-change stability, etale base-change stability, the definition's flatness clause, and the new flat-ascent statement. No flatness of f enters.

Step 4 applies the ambient lift only after making the ambient and the closed source affine and shrinking the etale chart to an affine open. The lifted square is cartesian by the existing node's statement, so the new flat-ascent lemma applies to the original closed immersion, along the etale ambient map. Etale smoothness and smooth composition give the new witness. Step 5 postcomposes the ambient smooth map with an etale, hence smooth, map; it makes no claim that arbitrary postcomposition preserves lci.

Step 6 first applies the existing source-locality statement for locally finite type, not a definition node cited as a theorem. Its family is jointly surjective because of the chosen points u_x. The covering-base-change node gives an etale family over the chosen affine source U; affine refinement preserves the point cover. The witnesses restrict to those source opens and to the target open V by step 1. Step 7 again checks the affine hypotheses of the ambient-lifting node. The prescribed immersion into W_alpha is Koszul-regular by step 2's ambient comparison. The open-map node says exactly that each image of W_alpha is open. Their union O contains j(U) because the chart sources cover U; the family is a genuine etale covering of O. Its pullbacks of U are precisely the closed immersions already checked. The descent lemma therefore applies with target O, and smooth restriction supplies the concluding factorization. This is the substantive correction that prevents an unjustified covering of the whole ambient affine space.

I tested the statements and steps on the empty source and empty covering, n=0, the identity immersion and its empty Koszul sequence, a hypersurface such as Spec(Z/p) over Spec(Z) (lci but not flat), and an ambient lift whose image is only a proper open. For the new comparison lemma, replacing an ambient scheme by an extra affine-line factor adds a regular coordinate equation and gives the same conclusion; the lemma imposes no regularity or nonzero-ring hypothesis. For the descent lemma, surjectivity is essential: an empty family on a nonempty target is excluded, and a cover of only a proper open is used only after restriction to that open. No separation, quasi-compactness, or Noetherian hypothesis is introduced. All non-elementary steps are under 2000 characters.
