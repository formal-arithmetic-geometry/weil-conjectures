## Proof in words for the extracted Mathlib leaf

For the two supplied universal pairs (R, epsilon) and (S, eta), apply the S universal property to epsilon to obtain u:R to S with eta composed with uQ equal to epsilon. Apply the R universal property to eta to obtain v:S to R with epsilon composed with vQ equal to eta. Substituting these equations, both vu and the identity of R have epsilon as counit composite, so R-uniqueness gives vu = identity. Similarly S-uniqueness gives uv = identity. Hence u is an isomorphism with inverse v. Any other counit-compatible map R to S equals u by S-uniqueness; in particular the compatible isomorphism is unique. This is exactly the Mathlib construction and requires neither invertibility of the counits nor a pointwise Kan formula. This proof is under 1000 characters and is already implemented by the leaf's recorded declarations.
