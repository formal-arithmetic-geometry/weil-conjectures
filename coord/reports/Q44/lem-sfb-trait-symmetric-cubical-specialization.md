## Review by Q44

I checked each B-to-E condition against the revised files. The item's statement and every new statement introduce their objects and their definition nodes. A regular scheme here is locally Noetherian with regular local rings; quasi-compactness is explicit in the new lemma. Separatedness is absolute there. This is a specialization of the affine-diagonal hypothesis of 0GML, because a closed-immersion diagonal is affine. No global quasi-projectivity is inferred from regularity. The family may vary with x, which is essential: arbitrary regular separated schemes need not have a single ample line.

Step 1 uses the DVR-dimension statement only for dimension one, and the regularity definition for the field and the DVR itself. The principal-ideal hypothesis gives Noetherianity directly; a one-dimensional local domain has only the zero and maximal primes, so the two localization checks prove that R is a regular ring. The smooth affine equivalence and regular-ring smooth ascent explicitly have the same ring maps and hypotheses needed on each affine chart. The definition of finite presentation includes quasi-compactness. Affine-scheme-qcqs supplies the base's quasi-compactness, permanence (2) supplies that of H, and separated-over-affine supplies absolute separatedness. These statements were opened; no assertion about properness or excellence was taken from their titles.

Step 2 uses the new node only after step 1 proves its regular, quasi-compact, separated hypotheses. The chosen A,t can depend on x. Pullback/tensor clause (3) says exactly that a section is nonzero after residue-field extension if and only if its original value is nonzero; this identifies the inverse-image open. Fibre-product universality identifies its restricted scheme with U times_T Spec(k), and the affine-spectrum tensor-product node explicitly makes that product affine. No theorem that an arbitrary open of an affine scheme is affine is used.

Step 3 keeps the accepted twist argument but applies it to this arbitrary A. The dual-frame description is precisely a rank-one locally free finite-type quasi-coherent module. The global-generation node requires ONLY B_K ample, plus that finite-type quasi-coherent twist. A_K is not required ample. Positivity of n is imposed explicitly. A generator survives at the identity fibre, a one-dimensional vector space. Tensor comparison and dual frames identify C_K with the restriction of the actual C on H.

Step 4 keeps the accepted fppf-quotient connectedness argument from the return. The semiabelian definition states the exact torus-kernel/abelian-quotient sequence used; clopen maps are constant on connected geometric torus cosets and descend to the connected quotient. Identity sections give nonemptiness. Those are exactly the primitive-section node's hypotheses together with the smooth, separated, finite-presentation hypotheses of the item. The primitive lemma's independent proof is retained: smoothness over the regular DVR makes the total space regular; the connected smooth geometric fibres are integral; the generic fibre makes the total space integral. There is one vertical height-one divisor, the special fibre with uniformizer order one. Adjust the rational section by its integer order on that divisor. All horizontal orders were already nonnegative from its generic regularity; Stacks 031T(2) extends the local coefficients over each normal affine chart, and their agreement in the function field glues them. Vertical order zero gives nonzero special restriction. This uses no existence of an ample line.

Step 5 checks the exact hypotheses of the field lemma by the three cited base-change nodes. It applies to C_k, which has a genuinely nonzero section from step 4; no properness is introduced. A generated rank-one module has a generating section nonzero at any specified point. The independent field argument is unchanged: over an algebraic closure, rigidify the line and use the semiabelian cube at (z,a,-a) to get t_a^*C tensor t_-a^*C isomorphic to C^2 up to a constant one-dimensional factor. For each z the two nonempty opens on the integral group allow a with both z+a and z-a outside the effective divisor. Their translated sections give a section of C^2 nonzero at z. Quasi-compactness reduces to a finite cover; faithful field extension descends generation, using flat base change of global sections for a qc separated scheme (finite affine-cover equalizer). The retained unsplit lemma carries this non-elementary group input, not the item's trait-specialization conclusion.

The existing tensor-section affine-open statement expressly gives both the intersection formula and affineness when one factor's section locus is affine. It applies to t_k^2 and u. This is why ampleness of A_k has been eliminated: its one affine t_k-locus suffices at the chosen x. Neither the cancellation nor the neighbourhood refinement is a new node.
