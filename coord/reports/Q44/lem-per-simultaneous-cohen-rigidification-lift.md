## New algebra node: why it is true

For the new node put I=(t_1,...,t_d) in R and n=(p,t_1,...,t_d). Evaluation induces an H-equivariant isomorphism I/nI -> m_A/m_A^2: both sides have the indicated d-element bases t_i and z_i. Write each z'_i as a linear combination of the z_j and lift the coefficients through f. This gives a lift u_i in I. Apply the chi'_i character projector to u_i, keeping u_i=t_i for i<=l. The projector stays in I, maps to z'_i and has the required eigencharacter. Its residue classes form a basis of I/nI because they map to the basis z'_i of m_A/m_A^2. Nakayama gives I=(t'_1,...,t'_d); therefore p,t'_1,...,t'_d generate n and form a regular parameter system in the (d+1)-dimensional regular local ring R. Stacks 07NM(3), already represented by the full-Cohen-coordinates node, gives the C-algebra isomorphism. The character formula on generators and constants proves equivariance of substitution. Common-prefix character values agree because distinct Nth roots differ by a unit and z_i is nonzero. This uses the actual semilinear projector, not a nonexistent coefficient-field linear structure on A.

This is a genuine lifting lemma with an arbitrary given equivariant surjection and parameter systems. It contains no boundary, quotient, strata or inertia-induction conclusion. It is smaller than the item. It is not the full-coordinate lemma: that lemma assumes the relative parameter list already exists, which is exactly what must be arranged here.
