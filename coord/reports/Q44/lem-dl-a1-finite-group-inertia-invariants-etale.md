## What was wrong or incomplete

The item had no proof. Its statement agrees with Stacks 0BST, including its lack of Noetherian, module-finiteness and tame hypotheses, so there is no statement dispute and no target change. The introductory sentence of the Stacks proof has the inclusion R -> R^I backwards; I use the actual inclusion R^Gamma -> R^I throughout. This is a prose typo, not a false assertion in the lemma.

The existing integral-fibre splitting node assumes finite-dimensional full residue extensions. The source 0BST only establishes algebraic normal residue extensions with finite automorphism groups, which imply finiteness of the separable part, not by that argument finiteness of the whole extension. I did not silently apply the existing stronger-hypothesis node. The new generic integral splitting statement records precisely the normal/finite-automorphism variant needed here.

The Stacks proof leaves equivariance of the product decomposition to the reader. Step 4 forms the Boolean atoms of all translated clopen partitions, removes the empty-fibre atoms by integral closedness, and reconstructs the product explicitly from its idempotents. Step 5 gives the inverse to first-coordinate projection on invariant tuples. These are written out, not replaced by clauses restating the item.
