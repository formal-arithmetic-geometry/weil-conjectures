### Returned Step 1 (verbatim)

For a scheme morphism $f:T\to X$, [sk-def-sfb-coefficient-morphisms] gives the ring isomorphism $\alpha_f:f^*\Lambda_X\xrightarrow{\sim}\Lambda_T$ (both rings commutative since $\Lambda$ is). With $c=\alpha_f$, [def-weil-pbc-proetale-operations] supplies the exact module inverse image $f^*$ and its unbounded derived functor $Lf^*$, computed termwise, which is triangulated, hence exact. The derived tensor comparison $Lf^*(F\otimes^L G)\cong Lf^*F\otimes^LLf^*G$ is [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-derived-tensor-pullback]; by [lem-sfb-ringed-derived-monoidal-comparisons] this comparison and the unit comparison $Lf^*\Lambda_X\cong\Lambda_T$ commute with associators, unitors and symmetries, so $Lf^*$ is strong symmetric monoidal for the closed symmetric monoidal structures of [lem-sf-ringed-derived-closed-symmetric-monoidal]. Scalar extension: by [def-zl-condensed-scalar-realization], $\Lambda_X\otimes^L_{\underline R}\underline P$ (constant-section structure map, constant realization of [def-sfb-site-derived-constant-module-functor]) is $D(p_X^*)(N_P)$, where $N_P=D(e^{-1})Lc^*\underline P\in D(\operatorname{Mod}_\Lambda)$ is formed on the point and does not depend on $X$; likewise for $T$ with the same $N_P$. For a $\Lambda$-module sheaf $M$ on $*_{\mathrm{proet}}$, the natural isomorphism $\varphi_M:f^*p_X^*M\cong p_T^*M$ of [sk-def-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp] is monoidal and its component at $\Lambda$ is $\alpha_f$ by definition of $\alpha_f$. Applying naturality and monoidality to the action $\Lambda\otimes M\to M$ shows that $\varphi_M$ carries the $f^{-1}\Lambda_X$-action on $f^{-1}p_X^*M$, transported along $\alpha_f$, to the $\Lambda_T$-action on $p_T^*M$. By the final paragraph of the module construction in [def-weil-pbc-proetale-operations], $f^*(p_X^*M)=f^{-1}p_X^*M\otimes_{f^{-1}\Lambda_X,\alpha_f}\Lambda_T$ is $f^{-1}p_X^*M$ with exactly this transported action, so $\varphi$ is a natural isomorphism $f^*\circ p_X^*\cong p_T^*$ of functors $\operatorname{Mod}_\Lambda\to\operatorname{Mod}(\Lambda_T)$. All functors involved are exact and act termwise on complexes, so $\varphi$ induces a natural isomorphism $Lf^*\circ D(p_X^*)\cong D(p_T^*)$, and evaluating it at $N_P$ gives $Lf^*(\Lambda_X\otimes^L_{\underline R}\underline P)\cong\Lambda_T\otimes^L_{\underline R}\underline P$, natural in $P$.
