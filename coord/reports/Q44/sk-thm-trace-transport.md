## Statement corrected

**STATEMENT-DISPUTED (integral trace notation, repaired):** “ordinary traces” at the integral stage must mean the trace on a perfect coefficient complex, not ordinary vector-space traces on its integral cohomology. For example, on `Spec F_q` the degree-zero object `O_E/varpi` with identity Weil map is represented by the perfect complex `[O_E --varpi--> O_E]`. Its integral cohomology is torsion, so ordinary finite-dimensional vector-space trace does not typecheck there. The perfect-complex trace of its identity is `1 - 1 = 0`. This agrees with the finite-level reductions. The repaired wording specifies `sk-d-trace` at `O_E` and alternating ordinary cohomology traces at the two field stages, as the source proof and the existing integral and field trace subnodes require. No hypothesis or conclusion is weakened.
