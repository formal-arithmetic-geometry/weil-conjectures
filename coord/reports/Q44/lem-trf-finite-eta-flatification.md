## Source error

Take a field k, S = Spec k[t], and X = S disjoint-union Spec k, where the second component maps to t = 0. This is a dominant finitely presented morphism with generic fibre Spec k(t), hence with geometrically reduced generic fibre. Suppose the original conclusion holds with an integral proper surjective b:S1 → S and a flat S1-scheme X1, and set Y = X ×S S1. Its vertical component C = S1 ×S Spec k = V(t) is nonempty by surjectivity of b and is open and closed as a component of the disjoint union Y = S1 disjoint-union C.

A schematically dense open U1 of Y must meet C: otherwise the nonzero section 1 of O_C restricts to zero on C ∩ U1, contradicting the defining sheaf injection. The isomorphism over U1 gives a nonempty open a⁻¹(U1 ∩ C) of X1 mapping into V(t). Choose a nonempty affine Spec B in this open whose image lies in a nonempty affine Spec A of S1. Since S1 is integral and dominates S, t is nonzero in the domain A, whereas tB = 0. Flatness tensors the injection A --t→ A to an injection B --t→ B. The latter map is zero, forcing B = 0, a contradiction. This also rules out repairing the old conclusion by a different choice of finite map or birational proper base.

Temkin's §3 convention on printed p. 13 imposes schematic density on the open subschemes in the definition of modification; Theorem 3.5.5 and its flattening Step 0 on pp. 21–22 use finite eta-modification language without excluding this vertical-component example. Thus the imported requirement is not justified in this generality by that wording. I do not reproduce it. The corrected strict-transform assertion follows from Stacks Tag 081R, whose conclusion concerns flatness and finite presentation of the strict transform and makes no target-density claim. The retained source-density assertion follows from flatness over an integral base.

The direct citer `lem-trf-reduced-fibre-modification` currently takes the false target-density conclusion in its Step 1 and uses it again in the assembly of Step 8; its own statement also requires schematic density in the original nonflat target. The same example refutes that clause even after an alteration: the nonempty vertical component persists over the surjective integral altered base. Its reduced-fibre construction after replacing X by the flat strict transform can continue, but its full target-density conclusion needs the corresponding explicit correction. I did not edit that other worker's node. Please record this source error and revisit that citer; it cannot be certified unchanged from the corrected item.
