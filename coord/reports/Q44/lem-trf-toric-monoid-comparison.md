## Source error

ILO VIII Lemma 3.2.9 is printed for a fine monoscheme without explicitly requiring torsion-freeness. Its normal-fibre argument fails in that unrestricted form: for the fine group monoid P=Z/2 and k=F_2, P is already saturated but k[P]=F_2[t]/((t-1)^2) is nonreduced, hence not normal. The item already requires torsion-free group completions, and the new lemma retains that restriction. No correction to the item's statement is needed.

The unit-ideal edge case also matters for nearby tower conventions: for a non-saturated monoid P, the saturated blowup along I=P is Spec_mon(P^sat), not necessarily an identity. For P=<2,3> in Z its toric realization is the normalization k[t^2,t^3] to k[t]. The present item handles this correctly and does not identify that map with the identity. The monoscheme/toric definition originally read says to contract unit-ideal stages, which needs the appropriate saturated/normal-source qualification. During this work the shared fan-definition node was updated to retain nontrivial unit-ideal saturation stages, resolving that issue for fans. No contraction convention is used in this split.
