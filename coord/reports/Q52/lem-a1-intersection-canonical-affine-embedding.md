## Original argument retained verbatim

### Original step 1

Fix one original quasi-compact open $U_0$ containing $E$. Any two quasi-compact opens $P,Q$ of $Y$ have quasi-compact intersection: $P\cup Q$ is quasi-compact, hence quasi-affine by hypothesis; its canonical open immersion into an affine scheme is supplied by [lem-trf-quasi-affine-canonical-affine-embedding]. The images of $P,Q$ are quasi-compact opens of that affine scheme, so their intersection is quasi-compact by [lem-weil-pbc-affine-scheme-qcqs]. Replace the given family by its finite intersections that include $U_0$, ordered by reverse inclusion, removing repetitions. This is a small nonempty directed poset, with intersection $E$; denote its opens by $U_i$. Each $U_i$ is quasi-affine, hence qcqs by the first cited node, so its space is spectral by [lem-weil-pbc-qcqs-scheme-spectral]. Inclusions $U_k\subseteq U_i$ are spectral maps: the inverse image of a quasi-compact open $Q\subseteq U_i$ is $Q\cap U_k$, which is quasi-compact by the same intersection argument. Finally $E=\varprojlim_i|U_i|$ in topological spaces: a compatible tuple consists of the same point in every open, and the subspace topology is already induced from $U_0$; this checks the universal property directly.

Cited nodes: lem-trf-quasi-affine-canonical-affine-embedding, lem-weil-pbc-affine-scheme-qcqs, lem-weil-pbc-qcqs-scheme-spectral.

### Original step 2

Put $A_i=\Gamma(U_i,\mathcal O_{U_i})$ with the restriction maps. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-spectral-limit-pullback-sections] to the spectral system from step 1, to the sheaf of sets underlying $\mathcal O_{U_0}$, and to the quasi-compact open $U_0$ of itself. Its hypotheses are precisely spectrality of the stages and transitions and the topological limit already checked. Open restriction gives $\mathcal O_{U_i}$; composition of inverse images is the canonical one in [lem-weil-pbc-sets-inverse-image-composition]. The ring inverse-image sheaf has this same underlying sheaf of sets: the presheaf inverse image is the neighbourhood colimit, the filtered neighbourhood colimit has exactly the same representatives and eventual-equality relation as its underlying set colimit by [lem-pc-filtered-colimit-commrings-elementwise], and its ring sheafification has the underlying set sheafification by [def-weil-pbc-topological-inverse-image] and [lem-weil-pbc-ring-sheafification-space-underlying-abelian]. We obtain that each element of $C$ comes from some $A_i$, and two representatives become equal after restriction to some common later $U_k$. Consequently $C=\varinjlim_i A_i$ as rings: a compatible family of ring homomorphisms out of the $A_i$ sends a represented section to its represented image, independently of the representative, and addition and multiplication can both be checked at a common stage.

Cited nodes: lem-weil-pbc-spectral-limit-pullback-sections, lem-weil-pbc-sets-inverse-image-composition, def-weil-pbc-topological-inverse-image, lem-weil-pbc-ring-sheafification-space-underlying-abelian, lem-pc-filtered-colimit-commrings-elementwise.

### Original step 3

For the inclusion $a:E\hookrightarrow Y$, [lem-weil-pbc-topological-inverse-image-stalk] identifies $(a^{-1}\mathcal O_Y)_e$ with $\mathcal O_{Y,e}$. These rings are local, so the restricted ringed space is locally ringed by [def-weil-locally-ringed-space-morphism]. Apply [lem-a1-locally-ringed-space-affine-adjunction] to $\mathrm{id}_C$ to obtain the unique $j_E$. Applied to the restriction $E\hookrightarrow U_i$, naturality of that bijection gives $p_i\circ j_E=j_i|_E$, where $p_i=\operatorname{Spec}(A_i\to C)$ and $j_i:U_i\to\operatorname{Spec}A_i$ is canonical. The same bijection shows that $j_E|_L$ corresponds to $C\to\Gamma(L,\mathcal O_Y)$ for each open $L\subseteq E$.

Cited nodes: lem-weil-pbc-topological-inverse-image-stalk, def-weil-locally-ringed-space-morphism, lem-a1-locally-ringed-space-affine-adjunction.

### Original step 4

By [lem-trf-quasi-affine-canonical-affine-embedding], each $j_i$ is an open immersion with quasi-compact image $Q_i$. By [lem-a1-filtered-spectrum-topological-limit] and step 2, $\operatorname{Spec}C$ is the topological limit of the $\operatorname{Spec}A_i$. Set $F=\bigcap_i p_i^{-1}(Q_i)$. A point of $F$ gives compatible points in the $Q_i$, hence unique compatible points in the $U_i$, and thus a unique point of $E$ by step 1. Conversely every point of $E$ gives such a tuple. The equality $p_i j_E=j_i|_E$ identifies this bijection with $j_E:E\to F$. It is a homeomorphism: on both sides the topology is the coarsest topology induced by the maps to $U_i\cong Q_i$ (subspaces of the coordinate spectra). Each $p_i^{-1}(Q_i)$ is quasi-compact open by the final assertion of the topological-limit node. Therefore $F$ has the required form.

Cited nodes: lem-trf-quasi-affine-canonical-affine-embedding, lem-a1-filtered-spectrum-topological-limit.

### Original step 5

Fix $e\in E$ and put $\mathfrak q=j_E(e)$ and $\mathfrak p_i=j_i(e)$. By [lem-weil-pbc-spec-domain-stalks-function-field](1) and the open immersion $j_i$ from [lem-trf-quasi-affine-canonical-affine-embedding], the germ map identifies $(A_i)_{\mathfrak p_i}$ with $\mathcal O_{Y,e}$. The stalk map of $j_E$ is a homomorphism $C_{\mathfrak q}\to\mathcal O_{Y,e}$ compatible with these maps, by the identity on sections in step 3. This map is surjective because already $(A_0)_{\mathfrak p_0}\to\mathcal O_{Y,e}$ is surjective. It is injective as follows. Represent a fraction in $C_{\mathfrak q}$ by $a/b$, $b\notin\mathfrak q$; step 2 puts both $a$ and $b$ in a common $A_i$. If its germ is zero, the germ of $a$ is zero, so $a/1=0$ in $(A_i)_{\mathfrak p_i}$. The fraction equality of [def-weil-pbc-localization-at-prime] supplies $t\in A_i\setminus\mathfrak p_i$ with $ta=0$. Since $p_i(\mathfrak q)=\mathfrak p_i$, the image of $t$ is outside $\mathfrak q$, and hence $a/b=0$ in $C_{\mathfrak q}$. The stalk map is therefore an isomorphism.

Cited nodes: lem-weil-pbc-spec-domain-stalks-function-field, lem-trf-quasi-affine-canonical-affine-embedding, def-weil-pbc-localization-at-prime.

### Original step 6

Use the homeomorphism of step 4 to regard $j_E$ as a morphism from $E$ to the restricted ringed space $(F,\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Spec}C}|_F)$. By [lem-weil-pbc-topological-inverse-image-stalk], its map of structure sheaves has at $e$ exactly the stalk map computed in step 5. It is an isomorphism by [lem-weil-pbc-sets-sheaf-iso-on-stalks] on the underlying set sheaves: their stalk elements and equality are the same germs as in the ring stalks by [lem-pc-filtered-colimit-commrings-elementwise] applied to the filtered neighbourhood systems. Its inverse is a morphism of rings sectionwise, since the inverse of a bijective ring homomorphism preserves the ring operations. Thus this is an isomorphism of locally ringed spaces, not merely a homeomorphism of their underlying spaces.

Cited nodes: lem-weil-pbc-topological-inverse-image-stalk, lem-weil-pbc-sets-sheaf-iso-on-stalks, lem-pc-filtered-colimit-commrings-elementwise.

### Original step 7

Let $W\subseteq Y$ be open and contained in $E$; it need not be quasi-compact. Fix a stage $i$. For every $k\geq i$, apply [lem-a1-quasi-affine-open-affinization-square] to $U_k\subseteq U_i$ and restrict the cartesian square over the open $W\subseteq U_k$. It says that the inverse image of $j_i(W)$ in $\operatorname{Spec}A_k$ is exactly $j_k(W)$, mapping isomorphically to $W$. By [lem-a1-filtered-spectrum-topological-limit], a point $z\in\operatorname{Spec}C$ over $j_i(W)$ has at every $k\ge i$ a unique coordinate $j_k(w)$, with the same $w\in W$ determined by its $i$th coordinate. Its remaining coordinates are determined by any common upper bound with $i$. Thus $z=j_E(w)$; conversely $j_E(w)$ lies over $j_i(W)$. Hence $j_E(W)=p_i^{-1}(j_i(W))$, which is open in $\operatorname{Spec}C$. Step 6 identifies the restricted sheaves, so [def-weil-open-immersion] makes $j_E|_W$ an open immersion.

Cited nodes: lem-a1-quasi-affine-open-affinization-square, lem-a1-filtered-spectrum-topological-limit, def-weil-open-immersion.

### Original step 8

If $h:Y\xrightarrow{\sim}\widetilde Y$ carries $E$ onto $\widetilde E$, its restriction is an isomorphism of the restricted locally ringed spaces and induces a ring isomorphism $\widetilde C\to C$ on sections. The two composites from $E$ to $\operatorname{Spec}\widetilde C$, namely $j_{\widetilde E}\circ h|_E$ and $\operatorname{Spec}(\widetilde C\to C)\circ j_E$, correspond to this same ring homomorphism. They are equal by [lem-a1-locally-ringed-space-affine-adjunction]. This proves naturality, independently of all choices of the family or of $U_0$.

Cited nodes: lem-a1-locally-ringed-space-affine-adjunction.
