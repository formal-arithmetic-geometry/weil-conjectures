- lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra: Let $R$ be a commutative ring and $A,B$ commutative $R$-algebras, and let $\operatorname{Spec}A\to\operatorname{Spec}R$, $\operatorname{Spec}B\to\operatorname{Spec}R$ be the morphisms induced by the structure maps [def-weil-affine-spectrum]. - def-pc-reduced-scheme: Let $X$ be a scheme [def-pc-scheme]. $X$ is *reduced* if every local ring $\mathcal{O}_{X,x}$, $x\in X$, is a reduced ring (has no nonzero nilpotent element); equivalently, $\mathcal{O}_X(U)$ is a reduced ring for every open $U\subseteq X$.

The *scheme-theoretic fibre* of $f$ over $s$, or simply the *fibre* $X_s$, is the fibre product $X_s=X\times_S\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)$ with its projections $X_s\to X$ and $X_s\to\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)$; it is regarded as a scheme over $\kappa(s)$ through the second projection. The first projection is a homeomorphism from the underlying space of $X_s$ onto the subspace $f^{-1}(s)$ of $X$.

- def-sc-discrete-valuation-ring: A *discrete valuation ring* is an integral domain $R$ which is a local ring, in which every ideal is principal, and which is not a field (its maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$ is nonzero). A *uniformizer* of $R$ is an element $t\in R$ with $\mathfrak m=tR$. (By Stacks Algebra Lemma 00PD this is equivalent to $R$ being a Noetherian local domain whose maximal ideal is generated by one nonzero element, and to $R$ being a valuation ring with value group $\mathbf Z$, Stacks Definition 00IE.)

- lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-scheme-closed-subsets-minimal: Let $X$ be a Noetherian scheme [def-pc-scheme], i.e. locally Noetherian [pqf-def-locnoeth] and quasi-compact [def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions]. Then every nonempty set $\mathcal S$ of closed subsets of the underlying topological space of $X$ has a minimal element for inclusion: there is $T_0\in\mathcal S$ such that no $T\in\mathcal S$ satisfies $T\subsetneq T_0$.

- lem-sfb-noetherian-connected-components-open: Let $X$ be a topological space in which every ascending chain of open subsets stabilizes. A connected component means a maximal nonempty connected subset, with connectedness as in [def-weil-connected-scheme]. Then the set of connected components of $X$ is finite, and each connected component is open in $X$. The empty space is allowed and has no connected components.

- def-weil-open-immersion: A morphism of schemes $f:X\to Y$ [def-pc-scheme] is an *open immersion* if $f$ is a homeomorphism of the underlying space of $X$ onto an open subset $U$ of $Y$ and the induced map $f^{-1}\mathcal O_Y\to\mathcal O_X$ is an isomorphism of sheaves of rings; equivalently, $f$ induces an isomorphism of $X$ with the open subscheme $(U,\mathcal O_Y|_U)$ of $Y$.

- def-weil-flat-morphism: Let $f:X\to S$ be a morphism of schemes [def-pc-scheme]. For $x\in X$, $f$ induces a local homomorphism of local rings $\mathcal O_{S,f(x)}\to\mathcal O_{X,x}$. The morphism $f$ is *flat at $x$* if $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ is a flat $\mathcal O_{S,f(x)}$-module, and *flat* if it is flat at every point of $X$.
