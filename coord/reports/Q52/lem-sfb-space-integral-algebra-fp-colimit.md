I read spaces-limits.tex at `lemma-integral-algebra-directed-colimit-finite` (082C), especially (2), and `lemma-finite-algebra-directed-colimit-finite-finitely-presented` (086Z), including the final finite list of monic equations. For the new Hom node I read spaces-cohomology.tex `lemma-finite-presentation-quasi-compact-colimit` (07U7): the target diagram is allowed to consist of arbitrary module sheaves and the source has finite presentation. For affine-etale calculus I read spaces-properties.tex `lemma-characterize-quasi-coherent-small-etale` (03LZ), `lemma-characterize-quasi-coherent` (03M0), `lemma-properties-quasi-coherent` (03M1), and `section-properties-modules`; the symmetric algebra and generated-ideal calculations are the constructions used in spaces-limits.tex 082A and 086Z. Those are operation/evaluation assertions and do not approximate integral algebras by themselves.

I opened every existing cited node and compared its statement with each use, rather than relying on its title or proof. In the following, C denotes the new affine-chart-calculus node, M the new module-approximation node, H the new Hom-continuity node.

1. M gives an actual module-sheaf colimit on the same qcqs X. The directed-cofinal node explicitly reindexes a colimit in every category, so it applies to the module diagram. C gives the symmetric-algebra universal property; composing with a linear transition produces its algebra transition, and uniqueness proves the two functoriality equations and compatibility with A. This needs no surjectivity of F_i to A.
2. C states the quasi-coherence and chart descriptions of kernel, image and quotient. The symmetric-algebra finite-presentation node explicitly says that a finite module generating list generates the symmetric algebra as an algebra. The finite-of-integral-generators node then gives module finiteness of the image algebra. The integrality hypothesis applies because its chart algebra is a subalgebra of A(U); every generator's monic relation holds in that subalgebra.
3. C evaluates the module colimit from step 1 on affine etale charts. The elementwise-module-colimit node says every section has a representative at some stage; this representative lies in the corresponding image algebra. Since later image algebras are literal subalgebras of A, equality is detected in a common later one. C's algebra-cocone criterion then supplies the sheaf-level universal property. I did not assume that arbitrary etale sheaf evaluation preserves all colimits.
4. J_i is quasi-coherent by step 2, so M applies to it on the original qcqs space. C identifies the ideal generated by E_ik with the ordinary ideal generated by its chart image. Its finite module generating set gives finitely many ideal generators. The symmetric-algebra finite-presentation node includes both finite presentation of Sym(F_i(U)) and preservation under quotient by a finitely generated ideal. It supplies exactly the finite algebra presentation required here, not finite module presentation of the quotient.
5. H applies with finitely presented source E_ik and colimit J_j of its E_jl diagram. Its factorization clause supplies a lift to one E_jl. Containment of generated ideals follows because an ideal containing the image of E_ik contains its generated ideal. The directedness proof explicitly chooses a common first coordinate, lifts both finite sources, then bounds the two second coordinates. Canonical quotient maps from C ensure functoriality without making noncanonical choices of module lifts into the transition maps.
6. The elementwise-module-colimit node and C imply that every section of J_i is in an image E_ik at some stage. The row ideals therefore have union J_i and the quotient universal property proves the row colimit. The directed-pairs-transitivity node has exactly the three conditions on P established in step 5 and the row/arrow compatibility equation written in step 6. Thus it gives the total colimit. Both iterated index sets and their disjoint union P are small; there is no large category of all presentations in the proof.
7. The same-unit affine-etale-atlas node supplies an affine surjective chart from quasi-compactness alone. C and the symmetric-algebra generating statement supply a finite list of chart generators. The rings-zero node detects vanishing of each monic expression at a later quotient stage, in the row colimit already proved in step 6. Directedness gives one bound for the finite list. The finite-of-integral-generators node proves module finiteness there, and C explicitly permits testing finite type after one surjective etale chart. Thus finiteness holds on all affine etale charts as the item requires.
8. Increasing a second coordinate both preserves finiteness and gives a cofinal subpreorder. The initial-full-subcategory node is used with the opposite preorder, as its statement requires; it says exactly that this restriction preserves the colimit. The directed-cofinal theorem can then replace a preorder by a directed set without changing the cocone or any termwise property.

The edge cases I checked were the empty space (affine atlas Spec(0), only zero module sections), the zero algebra, the identity integral algebra O_X, a finite non-finitely-presented quotient R/J with J infinitely generated, and noninjective transition maps. The last quotient is precisely why the approximations are not claimed to be subalgebras and why finite algebra presentation is kept distinct from finite module presentation. For M, zero modules and non-Noetherian affine rings are included; for H, zero source and arbitrary non-quasi-coherent targets are permitted by 07U7. For C, zero modules, zero maps and ideals 0 and the unit ideal give the stated chart constructions. The empty-space case can use Spec(0), and no step requires a nonempty set of generators.
