## Repair of the return

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the **former new supporting node**, not the item, incorrectly asserted finite Galois trivialization on an arbitrary connected Noetherian stratum. On the nodal cubic of ILO XVII Exercise 7.2.7, take coefficient ring F_l[u,u^-1] and the rank-one locally constant module sheaf descended along the infinite chain cover with monodromy u. On a connected finite n-cycle cover its monodromy is u^n; its invariant module is zero, whereas its stalk is the nonzero rank-one module. Thus it cannot be constant after such a cover. The old report's open-stabilizer argument assumed precisely the continuity that need not hold. ILO Definition 7.2.1 uses ind-finite-etale systems, and the paragraph after Lemma 7.2.6 explicitly distinguishes that condition from etale-local constancy.

I removed that false helper from this unmerged delivery and replaced Step 1's route, rather than adding a hypothesis to the item. The replacement expands the “évidemment” assertion in the proof of ILO XVII 7.4.1: above a generic point of a suitable open part of an original stratum, choose an affine trivializing etale chart, use the existing generic-finiteness theorem to make that chart finite after shrinking the base, dominate a nonempty connected component by a Galois cover, and repeat on the proper closed complement by Noetherian induction. This refines the strata before claiming finite monodromy. On the nodal-cubic example one can remove the node, and then treat the node as the next closed remainder. The item statement is unchanged.

I preserved the previously approved open-closed filtration argument (now Steps 5–7), with explicit cross-references to the expanded refinement, the zero stalks outside the closed subset, and the initial reduction. I also made reduction explicit so that a set-theoretic containment in an original stratum does not silently become a scheme factorization for a nonreduced source.
