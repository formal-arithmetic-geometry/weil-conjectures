## Argument retained for the statement-only child

The proof of Proposition (4.14) is deliberately not put into the new node, because this delivery splits only one level. The following explains the omitted inductive argument and the hypotheses for its later worker. For a closed S and a section c of C on S, choose local B lifts of c near each point of S. Epimorphism of abelian sheaves is locally surjective. A paracompact Hausdorff space admits a locally finite closed refinement (by shrinking a locally finite open refinement) subordinate to those lifting neighborhoods; restrict it to S and call the closed pieces K_alpha. Each local lift defines a section on K_alpha. Well-order the pieces. Suppose a lift has been chosen compatibly on the union F of earlier pieces. This union is closed in X because the family is locally finite. On the closed overlap F intersect K_alpha the difference between the previous lift and the local lift belongs to A: exactness can be checked on germs, and inverse image to the closed subset is exact. Since A is soft, extend that difference to a global A section. Adding its image to the local B lift makes the two lifts agree on the overlap. Glue them on the two closed subsets. At a limit stage compatible sections on the locally finite closed family glue: realize sections as continuous sections of the etale space and use the locally finite closed pasting property. Continue to obtain a lift on all of S. These refinement, exactness, and pasting inputs would need cited nodes when that child is split; none is silently used in the item's assembly, which cites the child's complete statement. Finally, when B is also soft, lift a C section on closed S to B on S, extend the B section globally, and apply p. This proves C soft without appealing to acyclicity and hence without circularity.
