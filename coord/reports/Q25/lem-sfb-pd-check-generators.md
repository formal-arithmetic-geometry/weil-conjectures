## Arithmetic proof retained for the new node

The new statement explicates the universal integer coefficient hidden in the last paragraph of 07GP. Here is a complete proof for the next worker, kept outside its statement-only node.

Fix n>=0, m>=1 and a+b=nm. Take disjoint labelled sets of sizes a and b, calling their elements red and blue. A partition of their union into n unordered m-element blocks has profile r_i equal to the number of blocks with i red elements. Its profile satisfies exactly the two conditions defining R. For a fixed profile, first label its r_i blocks within each type i. Put the a red labels into those specified red slots and the b blue labels into their specified blue slots. Within each block disregard the order of its i red slots and m-i blue slots. This gives a! b! / product_i (i!)^(r_i) ((m-i)!)^(r_i) assignments. Forgetting the artificial labels of blocks of the same type divides the count by product_i r_i!. This action is free: blocks are nonempty since m>=1, and disjoint distinct blocks cannot be equal. Thus the resulting count is precisely C_{a,b}(r), proving its nonnegative integrality. Counting all uncoloured partitions by a permutation of all nm labelled elements gives (nm)! / ((m!)^n n!): each partition has exactly (m!)^n n! presentations by ordering blocks and ordering their contents. Separating this set of partitions by its unique profile proves the sum formula. When n=0, a=b=0, the all-zero profile and empty partition each occur once; the formula gives 1.

An independent check of the coefficient equality is to take the coefficient of X^aY^b in (sum_{i=0}^m X^iY^(m-i)/(i!(m-i)!))^n/n! over Q. Expanding by profiles gives the left side divided by a!b!. The binomial formula identifies the expression with (X+Y)^(nm)/(n!(m!)^n), whose coefficient gives the right side divided by a!b!. This comparison occurs over Q solely for arithmetic coefficients, never by cancelling factorials in A.
