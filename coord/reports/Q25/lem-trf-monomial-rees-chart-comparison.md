I checked small cases: n=0, a singleton E, E containing 0 or a unit, P a group, and the zero ring/empty affine scheme. For a singleton, Q_h=P, L is a principal monomial ideal, and the constructions reduce to the identity chart. If E contains 0, its 0-chart is A and the other charts restrict to the appropriate localizations; no generator is required to be nonzero in A. The ordinary homogeneous chart theorems permit a zero denominator, yielding an empty chart when necessary. I did not impose A-flatness over Z[P], which would be an unjustified extra hypothesis. The local log-regular hypothesis is used precisely by the existing monomial injectivity theorem.
