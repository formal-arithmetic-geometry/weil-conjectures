## Corrections and source error

**STATEMENT-DISPUTED:** the opening “In the setting of thm-weil-pbc-integral-truncations” and unqualified identification of the full category's truncation functors with that theorem's tower construction hide an objectwise Tor hypothesis. That theorem explicitly assumes Tor compatibility at every level, described there as flat cohomology of local models. This is not a hypothesis on the full category. In particular the source proof's use of its clauses (ii)–(iii) for an arbitrary object does not prove unrestricted truncation closure.

Here is a small test detecting the missing hypothesis. Take a geometric point `T` of characteristic different from `ell`, `A = Z_ell`, and the completed constant realization of `K = (A/varpi)[0]`. Its local perfect model is `[A --varpi--> A]` in degrees `[-1,0]`, so it is in the integral category by the existing stratified-perfect characterization, with the completed coefficients and compatible etale origins of that statement. Its degree-zero cohomology is not flat over `A`. At levels 1 and 2 its upper truncations in degree zero are both the residue field `k`. But `k tensor^L_(A/varpi^2) k` has `Tor_1 = k`, so it is not `k[0]`. Thus the upper truncated levels are not an adic normalized tower. The source's unconditional reduction/normalization interpretation cannot apply to this torsion object. This example does not dispute existence of the ambient standard t-structure, or assert that every bare derived-limit formula must fail without Tor compatibility.

The repaired statement keeps the bounded t-structure on **all** integral objects and all original heart and torsion claims. It identifies its truncations with ambient canonical truncations on all objects, and with the particular normalized derived-limit construction only on the domain where the cited theorem states that construction. This follows the blueprint source note's intended standard t-structure and agrees with the adjacent already-repaired `prop-weil-localized-tstructure`, which has the same separation of hypotheses. No hypothesis is added to the t-structure, boundedness, heart, or torsion conclusion. The source's vague scheme and coefficient setting is now explicit, and power torsion means a common exponent killing the identity in the derived category, exactly as its proof uses.

The original proof also silently used: the triangulated structure of this full subcategory; the ambient standard t-structure; unrestricted truncation closure; and scalar compatibility of truncations. These are now either named statements of existing nodes or calculations in the steps. I removed the old dangling `lem-weil-pbc-reconstruction-uniform-tower` proof dependency: its current statement assumes finite type over a field, whereas the present statement permits any noetherian finite-dimensional scheme. Boundedness now comes directly from condition (iii) of the existing characterization, whose hypotheses match. Additivity is not treated as implying `A`-linearity: scalar centrality and the adjunction equations prove the final claim directly.

The item's maintainer-owned source and Lean metadata are unchanged. The repaired statement is compared with those metadata here rather than changing them. The unit is `sk-rem-rest`, a continuation remark with no prescribed proof steps; the item is not its key statement, so no unit realization record was edited. Chapter and unit routes are unchanged.
