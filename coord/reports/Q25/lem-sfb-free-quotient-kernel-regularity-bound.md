## Source error

In varieties.tex, Tag 08AG (lemma-bound-quotients-free), the final assertion that regularity is bounded by m' + dim H^1(K(m')) misses a shift. On P^1 let F be the structure sheaf of a length-two effective divisor, with 0 -> O(-2) -> O -> F -> 0. Its hyperplane quotient can be chosen zero. On the zero-dimensional hyperplane m'=0 is a valid induction bound, and dim H^1(O(-2))=1. The claimed closing estimate gives regularity 1, but H^1(K(1-1))=H^1(O(-2)) is nonzero; K is 2-regular. The same closing paragraph says H^0(O(m')) has dimension zero when m' is not positive, which is wrong at m'=0: H^0(O)=k. Neither error affects the lemma statement or the induction route. I choose b=max(1,m'), bound h_b by N=max(0,ceil P(b)), and take a=b+N+n+1. Every regularity test twist is then in the proved vanishing range. This enlarges the bound without changing the conclusion. The source definition's side comment about O(d) regularity also needs n>0, but the definition itself has the correct empty range when n=0; the split uses that definition, never the side comment.
