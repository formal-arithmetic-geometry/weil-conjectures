Original Step 2 (676 characters):

For any bounded constructible witness $(B_e,\alpha)$, $B_e$ is bounded below, so [thm-weil-pbc-site-comparison] gives the invertible unit $\eta_{B_e}:B_e\to R\nu_{T,*}\nu_T^*B_e$. Put $F=\nu_Z^*i_e^!$. By the finite defining formula in [def-weil-exceptional-inverse-image], the composite
$$F(B_e)\xrightarrow{F(\eta_{B_e})}F(R\nu_{T,*}\nu_T^*B_e)\xrightarrow{F(R\nu_{T,*}\alpha)}Ri^!B$$
is an isomorphism; its inverse is the claimed identification. Naturality of the unit and functoriality give naturality in morphisms of witnesses: for $\beta:B_e\to B'_e$ and $h:B\to B'$ satisfying $h\alpha=\alpha'\nu_T^*\beta$, the displayed composites intertwine $F(\beta)$ and $Ri^!(h)$.

Original Step 4 (595 characters):

For clause 2 choose any bounded constructible etale witness $B_e$ and invert its witness isomorphism to write $B\cong\nu_T^*B_e$. Apply [lem-sf-comparison-tor-amplitude](ii), with interval $[r,s]$. It follows that $B_e$ has etale Tor amplitude $[r,s]$, with the vanishing test for every degree-zero etale module sheaf $M_e$. The argument applies to every witness, not only the one chosen in Step 1.
