Steps 4–5 now separate its genuine use from the proof for twists: first identify coefficient limits, then construct the map to the twisted limit and test that map on a cover with compatible root generators. The uniformizer-power versus ell-power change is written out. The rational step previously attributed the twisted localization assertion to a lemma about untwisted coefficients and described a colimit as part of a definition that only specifies sections. The finite tensor comparison now cites the relative module tensor theorem, rather than taking a theorem about tensor over Z to directly state tensor over a coefficient ring.
