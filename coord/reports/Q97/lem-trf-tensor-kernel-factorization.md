## Preserved returned arguments and allocation

### Returned step 1 → quotient model

Put $C=V/f(U)$ and $\delta:U\to W\oplus V$, $\delta(u)=(g(u),-f(u))$. Define $P=(W\oplus V)/\delta(U)$, with quotient map $\rho$, and maps $a(w)=\rho(w,0)$, $b(v)=\rho(0,v)$. These are linear and $a\circ g=b\circ f$, since $\rho(g(u),-f(u))=0$. The map $p:P\to C$, $p(\rho(w,v))=[v]$, is well defined, linear and surjective: $-f(u)$ has zero class and every $[v]$ is $p(b(v))$. Moreover $\ker p=a(W)$. Indeed, $p(\rho(w,v))=0$ means $v=f(u)$ for some $u$, and the relation gives $\rho(w,v)=\rho(w+g(u),0)=a(w+g(u))$; conversely $pa=0$. Thus $W\xrightarrow{a}P\xrightarrow{p}C\to0$ is exact, with the given finitely presented module $C$ [def-sc-finitely-presented-module].

### Returned step 2 → tensor-kernel model

For any $R$-module $Q$, abbreviate $f_Q=f\otimes\mathrm{id}_Q$ and similarly for other maps. From the bilinear universal property [def-weil-pbc-module-tensor-product] there are inverse linear maps $D:(W\oplus V)\otimes_RQ\to(W\otimes_RQ)\oplus(V\otimes_RQ)$ and $E$ given by $D((w,v)\otimes q)=(w\otimes q,v\otimes q)$ and $E(x,y)=(\iota_W\otimes1)(x)+(\iota_V\otimes1)(y)$. Here $\iota_W(w)=(w,0)$ and $\iota_V(v)=(0,v)$. The identities $DE=1$ and $ED=1$ follow on pure tensors from $(w,v)=(w,0)+(0,v)$, and then on all elements because pure tensors generate. Under this isomorphism $\delta_Q(z)=(g_Q(z),-f_Q(z))$ and $\rho_Q(E(t,0))=a_Q(t)$: each identity holds on pure tensors, hence everywhere.

### Returned step 3 → tensor-kernel model

The quotient sequence $U\xrightarrow{\delta}W\oplus V\xrightarrow{\rho}P\to0$ is exact by construction. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-tensor-right-exact] with any finitely presented $Q$ to obtain $\ker\rho_Q=\operatorname{im}\delta_Q$. Hence $f_Q(z)=0$, and the hypothesis on this finitely presented $Q$ gives $g_Q(z)=0$. Thus $t=0$. This proves that $a_Q$ is injective for every finitely presented $Q$; no injectivity of $f$ or $\delta$ was assumed.
