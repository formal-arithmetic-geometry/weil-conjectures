Fix any compactification $X\xrightarrow{j}\overline X\xrightarrow{p}Y$ with $p$ proper. Apply [lemma-nagata-open-immersion-qc-over-separated-target] to the triangle $X\xrightarrow{j}\overline X\xrightarrow{p}Y$: $f$ is finite type, $p$ is separated, and $j$ is open, so $j$ is quasi-compact. Write $M=j_!K$. By the displayed definition in [def-exchange-projection], the projection map for this compactification is the ordered composite $Rp_*(\gamma_{K,L})\circ\pi_{p;M,L}$, with $\pi$ the counit-defined proper projection map and $\gamma$ the open-extension tensor comparison followed by $Lj^*Lp^*\simeq Lf^*$. We prove invertibility of this specified composite at each coefficient stage; the integral stage below is the cited integral theorem for this same composite, so invertibility of its two factors separately is not needed.
