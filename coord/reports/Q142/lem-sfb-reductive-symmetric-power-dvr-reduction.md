For the first prerequisite's local clause, the supporting argument is: for a fixed surjection, invariants are kernels of rho-minus-unit and commute with localization. Symmetric powers localize by their tensor/quotient construction. An invariant local lift can be represented as q/s^a; after multiplying q by a further power of s its coaction defect vanishes, and clearing the equation makes its image a power of s. Such s cover Spec A. A finite subcover suffices. Raise the finitely many homogeneous q to a common positive degree; their images are powers of the corresponding s. The ideal generated by those powers is the unit ideal, so an A-linear combination gives an invariant homogeneous lift of 1. The finite etale clause follows from faithfully flat descent along the product of the finite affine cover, or the same finite patching. This supports the local forms without assuming finite modules or a universal exponent for all surjections. The general base-change and exact-sequence inputs remain genuine lemmas to split, sourced exactly to Alper; they are not called elementary.
