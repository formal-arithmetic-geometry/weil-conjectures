### lem-weil-pbc-total-tensor-homotopy-cone-signs

Old Step 1: Write $H_A(B)=\operatorname{Tot}(A\otimes_R B)$ and $H^A(B)=\operatorname{Tot}(B\otimes_R A)$. The sheaf tensor is additive in each map, as follows from its balanced sectionwise construction. Its preservation of coproducts, supplied by [lem-weil-pbc-independent-sheaf-tensor-colimits] (and the pure-tensor swap for the other variable), allows distribution over the finite sums used below. The differential is that of [def-pc-tensor-complex-sheaves]. Both functors are additive on complexes and respect homotopy: if $u-v=dh+hd$ in the varying argument, use $h\otimes1$ for $H^A$, and use $(-1)^p1\otimes h$ on the summand of first-factor degree $p$ for $H_A$. In each case the cross terms from the fixed differential cancel, leaving respectively $(u-v)\otimes1$ and $1\otimes(u-v)$. Thus both define additive functors on $K(R)$. All formulas are maps on presheaf tensors, hence induce the stated maps on sheaf tensors.

Uses: def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product, lem-weil-pbc-independent-sheaf-tensor-colimits, def-pc-tensor-complex-sheaves, def-weil-abstract-derived-category. Source: literature/stacks-project/sites-cohomology.tex; Tag 06YM (lemma-derived-tor-exact); derived.tex remark-double-complex-as-tensor-product-of, explicit tensor homotopies.

Old Step 2: For a chain map $u:X\to Y$, write $C(u)^q=Y^q\oplus X^{q+1}$ with differential $(d_Y,u;0,-d_X)$. The cone comparison supplied by [lem-pc-tensor-mapping-cone-iso] can be taken as the following explicit map $\phi_L:H_A C(u)\to C(H_Au)$: identity on $A^p\otimes Y^q$, and multiplication by $(-1)^p$ on $A^p\otimes X^{q+1}$. Distribution is justified by Step 1. On this second summand both sides of the chain-map equation have $A$-differential coefficient $(-1)^{p+1}$ and $X$-differential coefficient $-1$; their off-diagonal coefficient is $(-1)^p(1\otimes u)$. For $\phi_R:H^A C(u)\to C(H^Au)$ take the identity on both summands: on $X^{q+1}\otimes A^p$ both differentials are $-d_X\otimes1+(-1)^q1\otimes d_A$, with off-diagonal $u\otimes1$. The displayed maps are invertible, natural in $u$, and preserve the inclusion of $Y$. If $p_u:C(u)\to X[1]$ and $p_{Hu}:C(Hu)\to H(X)[1]$ are the projections, their formulas give $p_{Hu}\phi_L=s^2_1H_A(p_u)$ and $p_{Hu}\phi_R=s^1_1H^A(p_u)$, with the shifts of [lem-weil-pbc-total-tensor-integer-shifts]. Negating these equalities proves compatibility with the connecting arrow $-p_u$, not just with the first two triangle maps.

Uses: lem-pc-tensor-mapping-cone-iso, def-weil-abstract-derived-category, def-pc-tensor-complex-sheaves, lem-weil-pbc-total-tensor-integer-shifts. Source: literature/stacks-project/sites-cohomology.tex; Tag 06YM (lemma-derived-tor-exact), expanded using derived.tex definition-cone and definition-distinguished-triangle; more-algebra.tex Tag 0FNH (item-shift-tensor).

Old Step 3: Let $u:X\to Y$ be any quasi-isomorphism, without a K-flatness assumption on $X$ or $Y$. Its cone is acyclic by [lem-pur-quasi-iso-cone-acyclic], so $C(u)\otimes_R A$ is acyclic by [def-pc-kflat-complex]. The signed interchange $x_i\otimes a_j\mapsto(-1)^{ij}a_j\otimes x_i$ identifies it with $A\otimes_R C(u)$. This is a chain isomorphism: the $d_X$ coefficient on either side is $(-1)^{ij+j}$ and the $d_A$ coefficient is $(-1)^{ij}$; the swap in each degree is the balanced swap on the presheaf tensor and passes to sheafification. Its inverse has the same sign. Step 2 now identifies these acyclic complexes with $C(H^Au)$ and $C(H_Au)$. Applying the cone criterion again proves that both tensor functors preserve every quasi-isomorphism.

The latter is acyclic by Step 3's signed interchange and the definition of K-flatness: shifting reindexes cohomology. Interchange again shows $E\otimes A[n]$ acyclic. Thus $A[n]$ is K-flat. ### lem-weil-pbc-derived-tensor-shift-descent

Old Step 8: To put shifts on the full bifunctor $T$, for each $n$ restrict the desired comparisons $T(M[n],N)\to T(M,N)[n]$ and $T(M,N[n])\to T(M,N)[n]$ to $\mathcal P_R\times\mathcal P_R$. Step 4 ensures shifted representatives remain there. Conjugate $Q_hs^1_n,Q_hs^2_n$ by the canonical representative isomorphisms of [lem-weil-pbc-site-derived-tensor-descent]. These are natural in both homotopy variables by [lem-weil-pbc-total-tensor-integer-shifts]. The arbitrary-transformation extension clause of tensor descent extends them uniquely, and also extends their inverses. Faithfulness proves the inverse, zero and addition identities, since they hold on representatives. For shifts $a,b$, the two extraction orders restrict to maps differing by $(-1)^{ab}$ by that shift leaf. The right side is again a natural transformation: multiplication by this scalar means identity or its negative, natural in the additive derived category. Faithfulness therefore gives exactly the same mixed-sign identity on $D(R)\times D(R)$. By uniqueness these restrictions at a fixed $Q_hA$ are precisely the comparisons constructed in Step 5 under Step 7's identifications.
