## Statement dispute / Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: The imported blueprint says that unpointed isomorphism classes correspond to individual open subgroups. The stabilizer depends on the chosen point and is only determined up to conjugacy. An explicit scheme counterexample is Y = Spec Q and P = Spec Q(cuberoot(2)). Its three geometric points are the three embeddings in an algebraic closure. The S3 Galois group of the splitting field acts transitively; their stabilizers map onto the three distinct order-two subgroups of S3. Thus one cover gives different open subgroups of the absolute Galois group. The same obstruction is visible in the finite S3-set S3/<(12)>: points H and (123)H have different, conjugate stabilizers. The repaired statement classifies unpointed covers by conjugacy classes and also records the pointed version, which classifies individual open subgroups. This is the usual source intent of the Galois-category equivalence, Stacks 0BND, not an added hypothesis on Y.

The final sentence of the imported proof also reverses the containment direction for morphisms: a G-map G/H -> G/H' taking H to aH' requires H contained in aH'a^{-1}. Taking H = 1 and H' = S3 gives a map to the singleton and disproves the printed reverse containment. The revised proof avoids the unneeded morphism-existence assertion and derives exactly the isomorphism criterion.
