HA's theorem is phrased for locally Cartesian categorical fibrations and relative left adjoints; the item's cocartesian version is its opposite dual. Cocartesian fibrations satisfy the stronger global lift condition needed in this split. The right adjoint need not preserve cocartesian edges. The proof uses the preservation assumption only for the given left adjoint F.

The previously empty proof needed a coherent construction over the base, not just an objectwise assignment. I first obtain total universal counits from the fiber adjunctions, then assemble them using the existing parametric universal-arrow theorem. Its initially assembled right adjoint and counit are not assumed to be strictly over S. The counit's projection is a natural equivalence; cocartesian transport normalizes the functor over q and its mapping-fiber property normalizes the counit over the identity. The unit and both triangle homotopies are then obtained in the degreewise functor-category fiber. This also proves actual base change of the chosen data rather than independent existence after base change.

During self-review I added the cocartesian mapping criterion in Step 1: the pointwise transport node assumes the intrinsic mapping-square characterization, while the item gives the horn definition. I also added homotopy-pullback invariance to Step 3, and made the inverse to postcomposition by the natural equivalence in Step 4 explicit, citing mapping composition. I removed an unnecessary blanket preservation claim about all cocartesian arrows after pullback; restriction of the already vertical adjunction data proves precisely the base-change clause.
