New-node proof (for its future splitter). Take the ordinary localization gamma at W in a larger universe. By Joyal E.1.8, every localized morphism from cofibrant T to fibrant Z is represented by a map T to Z, and equality is cylinder homotopy. These classes can be computed using any of the specified cylinders: given an arbitrary cylinder T -> J -> T and a homotopy H:J -> Z, factor J -> T as a trivial cofibration v:J -> K followed by a fibration q:K -> T. Since the cylinder projection and v are weak equivalences, q is a trivial fibration. Extend H along v using fibrancy of Z, then lift j_T against q with top map given by v on the two endpoints and bottom map p_T. Composing this lift with the extended H gives a homotopy using I_T with the original endpoints. Conversely a homotopy from I_T becomes equality in the localization because gamma(p_T) is invertible and p_T e_0=p_T e_1=1. Thus the quotient h_I(T,Z) is exactly Hom(gamma T,gamma Z), with precomposition represented by u. If u is weak, gamma(u) is invertible and these precomposition functions are bijective. For the converse, replace an arbitrary target Z by a fibrant replacement Z -> RZ; its invertible localized image identifies the corresponding target Hom-sets. Hence precomposition by gamma(u) is bijective for every target. Surjectivity for target gamma A gives v:gamma B -> gamma A with v gamma(u)=1; injectivity for target gamma B then gives gamma(u) v=1, since their composites with gamma(u) agree. Thus gamma(u) is invertible, and Joyal E.1.4 implies u is weak. This proves both directions for the fixed-cylinder quotient, with no appeal to model-structure uniqueness.
