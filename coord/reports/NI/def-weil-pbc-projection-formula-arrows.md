### Explicit elementary checks of the construction

For module sheaves M on the source and P on the target, ordinary pullback monoidality is given by the two displayed balanced-tensor maps in the repaired definition. Their composites are identity on local pure tensors, which generate the tensor presheaves and their sheafifications. Adjunction sends the resulting counit tensor to t_{M,P}. Naturality in M and P follows by composing the ordinary counit naturality square with the tensor of the coefficient map; the two pullback-tensor maps are natural by their formulas. In the free case, composition with each coordinate projection is the identity adjunct, by the adjunction triangle identity. Hence t is precisely the finite-product comparison and has the inverse q supplied by limit preservation.

Set A_M(u)=1_{h_*M} tensor u and B_M(u)=h_*(1_M tensor h^*u). Naturality gives B_M(i)t_P=t_N A_M(i) and B_M(p)t_N=t_P A_M(p). Therefore the explicit s_P=A_M(p)q_N B_M(i) satisfies s_P t_P=A_M(p)q_N t_N A_M(i)=A_M(pi)=1, and t_P s_P=B_M(p)t_N q_N B_M(i)=B_M(pi)=1. These are equations of ordinary module-sheaf maps. For any u:P→P', multiply B(u)t_P=t_{P'}A(u) on the left by s_{P'} and on the right by s_P: s_{P'}B(u)=A(u)s_P. For any w:M→M', multiplication of B(w)t_{M,P}=t_{M',P}A(w) gives s_{M',P}B(w)=A(w)s_{M,P}. Taking u=d_F and w=d_I gives both differential squares; preservation of a makes the coefficient (-1)^a identical on both sides. Adding the two squares and summing over a+r=n proves ds=sd. This checks the definition of the chain map without choosing unrelated representatives for derived maps.

Each retract of finite free sheaves is flat: its tensor functor is a retract of a finite sum of identity functors, hence exact. The pullback splitting proves the same for h^*F^r. Bounded-above flat K-flatness and preservation under pullback provide the models C and J. The exact statement of `lem-weil-pbc-split-perfect-tensor-kinjective` supplies K-injectivity of J for arbitrary unbounded K-injective I with bounded, degreewise split F; it does not require i and p to be chain maps. `lem-weil-pbc-kinjective-total-derived` therefore computes B by h_*J. The formal derived adjunction prerequisite is applied to the ordinary module adjunction with these existing total derived functors, just as Stacks 07A6 does. This is an application of its existence hypothesis, not an assumption that h_*I is K-injective when h^* is not exact.

For a single term P, the representative formula of `lem-pc-pre-derived-adjoint-functors-general` identifies the localization of t with the derived counit-tensor adjunct: resolve h_*I by a K-flat complex V, apply h^*V→h^*h_*I→I, and tensor with h^*P. The ordinary adjunct is t composed with V→h_*I tensor P. Since P is flat this replacement is a quasi-isomorphism, and the node's representative formula gives precisely the declared derived adjunct. Finite products identify the localized q with rho_N, and functoriality gives the displayed formula for rho_{F^r}. This verifies that the total chain map totalizes the specified term maps; the theorem identifying its total inverse with pi for the full complex remains downstream.
