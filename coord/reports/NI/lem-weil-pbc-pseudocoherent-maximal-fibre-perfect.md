## Corrections and scope

The statement is true and agrees exactly with Stacks 068V, implication (3) to (1); no added Noetherian, local, bounded-below or finite-cohomology assumption. Its original record had no proof. The source's first invocation of 068U is used separately for each integer outside the interval to prove ordinary cohomology vanishing.
