## Argument allocation

The old six-step docstring is covered as follows. Steps 1 (both differential matrices and their inverses) and 2 (chain-map naturality) belong to unit-endomorphism-cone-coordinates. Step 3 (degreewise split cone rows, the natural long exact sequences and five lemma) belongs to cone-square-quasi-isomorphism. Step 4 remains the short conjugation of cohomology maps in item step 2. Step 5 remains application of the existing either-argument K-flat tensor computation in item step 3. Step 6 remains localization plus the explicit derived right-tensor descent in item step 4. Thus no entire copy of the item was moved into a single new node, and neither new node assumes K-flatness or asserts the item's derived tensor/descent conclusion.

For unit-endomorphism-cone-coordinates, the intended proof starts from P_a^{-1}=1, P_a^0=1, d^{-1}=a. In right tensor degree n the off-diagonal is (-1)^(n+1)a^R and the second diagonal is +d; in left tensor it is a^L and -d. With s_n=(-1)^(n+1), s_(n+1)=-s_n, multiplication of the second summand by s_n changes the right matrix into [[d,a^R],[0,-d]]. Its square is identity. Left tensor already has the desired matrix. Unit naturality makes each induced map diagonal (f^n,f^(n+1)), which commutes with these signs; scalar linearity is not required for the generalized statement. Localizing these chain representatives gives the stated derived isomorphisms, not merely per-degree homology identifications. For the item, tensor linearity and naturality of unitors identify a^R=a^L=b.

For cone-square-quasi-isomorphism, the block differential and t u=u' s show c is a chain map and that it intertwines inclusion and projection. Hence (t,c,s[1]) maps the degreewise split short exact cone rows. Apply existing lem-weil-pbc-complex-ses-homology-long-exact and lem-weil-pbc-complex-ses-long-exact-naturality (Stacks 0117, homology.tex lemma-long-exact-sequence-cochain). In degree n the exact five-term row is H^(n-1)(K[1]) -> H^n(L) -> H^n(Cone u) -> H^n(K[1]) -> H^(n+1)(L). All four outer vertical maps are invertible because s and t are quasi-isomorphisms and the shift just reindexes kernels and images. Existing lem-weil-pbc-five-lemma (Stacks 05QB, homology.tex lemma-five-lemma) gives invertibility in the middle for every n. No exactness of tensoring or boundedness is smuggled in.

The alternative live R4 argument was also checked: naturality of the invertible lax comparison gives Q(f tensor 1)=mu_D (Qf tensor 1) mu_C^{-1}, so this map is invertible; reflection by Q gives a quasi-isomorphism. Braiding conjugates it to 1 tensor f. Naturality of theta similarly gives QF_b(f)=theta_D (Qf tensor 1) theta_C^{-1}. These are consistent checks on the same conclusions; the extracted coordinate and long-exact-sequence arguments retain the work of the longer proof that the repair specifically asks to distribute.
