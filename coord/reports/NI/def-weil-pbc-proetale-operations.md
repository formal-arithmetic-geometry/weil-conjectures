The blueprint's definition was schematic: it did not introduce schemes, the category of modules over an arbitrary coefficient sheaf, the size convention, the direct-image action, or the construction on morphisms. It also cited def-weil-pbc-kinjective-resolutions for both K-flat and K-injective resolutions. That existing node defines only K-injectivity on the etale site and supplies neither K-flat resolutions nor their existence. I replaced that dependency with the existing site-general definitions and existence statements, leaving the original node unchanged. def-weil-pbc-proetale-modules only introduces finite discrete constant coefficients; it does not define modules over this item's arbitrary R_V. I replaced it by def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category and def-weil-abstract-derived-category, keeping the original coefficient identification, the two functor types, and the adjunction.

The original assumption that R_U is identified with u^{-1}R_V makes u^* exact. Consequently its derived functor is termwise localization and can be evaluated on every K-flat resolution. The new text does not assume a functorial K-flat resolution: none is necessary for this construction, and lem-pc-kflat-resolution asserts individual existence, not functoriality. It retains functorial K-injective resolutions, which prop-pur-kinjective-resolutions-exist explicitly supplies. These are ordinary module categories on ringed pro-etale sites, not completed operations or a silent substitution of condensed-ring module objects. This is a clarification of construction, not a weakened derived-functor conclusion or an added geometric hypothesis.

Nothing remains unfinished in this one-level definition split. The new underived theorem is deliberately left unsplit. The unit's three-step proper-base-change route and its realization records were not changed; this item is an auxiliary definition, not the unit key statement.
