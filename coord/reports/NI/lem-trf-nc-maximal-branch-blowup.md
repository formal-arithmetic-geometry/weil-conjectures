## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: The returned coordinate blowup statement described the chart equations for an arbitrary etale R-algebra as products of distinct local parameters without specifying a local ring. For A=R x R, A and its chart algebras are generally not local. Even on a connected chart some displayed factors can be units at the chosen point. The replacement allows a regular Noetherian affine chart with explicit pointwise coordinate hypotheses. At every point y of a blowup chart, precisely the vanishing ratio factors extend to a regular system of parameters in the local ring at y, and the remaining factors are units. The equation is a unit times the product of those vanishing parameters. This is the pointwise interpretation of the coordinate calculation in ILO VIII Lemma 4.2.9, and it still gives every clause used by the item. The equations and bound are stable under etale base change. No global chart ring is called local.

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: The retained coordinate maximal-stratum helper also claimed that the value m is attained for every etale R-algebra. For R=k[x,y]_(x,y), m=2 and A=R[1/x] (a nonzero etale R-algebra), V(x,y) in Spec A is empty and there is at most one branch. The repaired helper says b <= s, with equality exactly on the coordinate intersection, which may be empty. The item's global maximum is instead obtained from a finite covering and the nonempty D hypothesis. The helper explicitly treats all threshold loci, not just the top coordinate intersection, so closedness of the global m-locus is actually proved.
