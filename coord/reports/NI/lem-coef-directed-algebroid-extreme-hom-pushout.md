## Repair of the return

The previous proof assigned Hom objects and binary composites and asserted that coherence was inherited. It also extended the formula for `V_S G_S P` to an amalgamated coproduct without justification, and described algebra mapping spaces as ordinary composition-preserving objectwise maps. All five previous steps have been replaced.

The first new lemma is precisely the smaller input requested in the return: `V_S(A ⨿ G_S P) ≃ V_S A ⨿ P` for extreme-supported `P`. It does not impose relations or compute an amalgamated pushout. Its canonical summand maps give the coherent substitution identity, including substitutions sending a generator into the old algebra. This is needed for the first bar face, and cannot be replaced by naturality only for separate maps `A → A'` and `P → P'`.

The second new lemma is independent of enriched algebroids: in any infinity category with finite coproducts and geometric realizations, the coproduct bar of a span realizes its pushout. The item itself applies it in the algebra category, rewrites each bar degree using the first lemma, identifies every operator, applies preservation of geometric realizations by `V_S`, and evaluates the resulting graph bar. Thus the item's pushout calculation is carried by its steps; neither new node restates it.

A discarded intermediate attempt put the entire relative pushout calculation into one new lemma. Although a dry run accepted it, I rejected that attempt in my mathematical review. Its files have been moved outside `dag/nodes/` as local history; it is not part of the proposed split.
