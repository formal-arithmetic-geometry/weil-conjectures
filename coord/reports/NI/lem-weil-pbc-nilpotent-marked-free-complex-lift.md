Step 5 first proves finite generation from lifted generators and the nilpotent cokernel argument. Step 6 defines both roof maps rather than assuming an isomorphism respects the marking. The derived-category definition supplies the actual cone triangle with connecting map -p, and `lem-sf-derived-homology-long-exact` proves its invertibility on cohomology when A is acyclic. The kernel of t has the displayed contracting homotopy; the cycle lifting and boundary argument proves t is a quasi-isomorphism directly. Thus beta is an honest invertible roof in the required direction.

Step 7 checks functorial reduction using the same K-flat and change-of-rings statements as step 1. Projectives are flat by the direct-summand characterization and the elementary retract argument, so it also applies to A, B and the cone. In the coordinates (x,y,z), the homotopy h = x has target Lbar[1], whose differential is -d. Hence dh+hd = delta y+z, giving -p = delta[1] rho t in the homotopy category. This explicitly accounts for the negative connecting-map convention and gives alpha beta_bar = Q(theta), rather than leaving a naturality assertion unstated. The identity is then shifted and composed with the invertible t.
