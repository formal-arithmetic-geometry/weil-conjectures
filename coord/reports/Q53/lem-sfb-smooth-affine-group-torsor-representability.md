## Statement dispute

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported hypothesis says the trivializations are equivariant for right translation, whereas its conclusion writes a left action $G\times_SP\to P$, $(g,p)\mapsto(gp,p)$. Ordinary right multiplication is not a left group action for a noncommutative group: for the constant smooth affine finite group $S_3$ over a field, take noncommuting $g,h$ and $x=e$. Writing $a(g,x)=xg$ gives $a(g,a(h,e))=hg$, while $a(gh,e)=gh$. Thus the unqualified action and right-translation wording cannot be used together with the displayed left-action convention.

I corrected the hypothesis to a left $h_G$-action and equivariant trivializations for left translation. This matches Stacks groupoids.tex Tag 0498 (definition-pseudo-torsor), its displayed $(g,x)\mapsto(a(g,x),x)$, and the actual downstream left-torsor usage (lem-sfb-action-groupoid-torsor-comparison). The proof uses $(x,y)\mapsto(xy^{-1},y)$, as required for left translation. The equivalent right-action version would instead use $(p,g)\mapsto(pg,p)$. No geometric hypothesis or conclusion was removed, strengthened or weakened. I added only the existing universe and fibre-product conventions as statement dependencies.
