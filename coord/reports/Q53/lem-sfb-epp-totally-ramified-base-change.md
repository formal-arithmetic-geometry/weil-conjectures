For step 1 I opened lem-per-complete-dvr-finite-normalization: it states that the actual integral closure A_1 is finite, complete, local, a DVR and has fraction field K_1. Thus the newly stated presentation lemma has its hypotheses before it is invoked. The original total-ramification assumptions provide precisely its residue and ideal-power hypotheses. def-sc-discrete-valuation-ring defines uniformizers as maximal-ideal generators, which is all that choosing pi and t uses.

For step 2 I checked that pi, not t, remains a uniformizer of B, by the given index-one ideal equality. A unit stays a unit under any unital homomorphism; cancellation proves pi times that unit is not divisible by pi squared. Hence the polynomial over B meets every hypothesis of the new quotient node.
