STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the sentence about identities in finite-quotient limit presentations can be falsely interpreted as asserting that every face is a map at a fixed finite quotient with base T. The first face is not such a map. For X=Spec(F_q), Pi is the profinite completion of Z, and its pointed limit is Spec(overline(F_q)). At the quotient N=Pi there is only one label, so C_(N,1)=T. Yet the restrictions of the first face along constant labels 1 and a nontrivial Frobenius are respectively id_T and the nontrivial Frobenius deck automorphism. Those two sections have the same map to C_(N,1), so that face cannot factor through C_(N,1). This also shows why verifying the formula merely on constant labels without constructing the scheme morphism is insufficient.

The corrected sentence asserts identities of morphisms between the inverse limits, checked after every finite-cover projection.
