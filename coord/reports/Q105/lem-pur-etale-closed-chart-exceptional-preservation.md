### Returned Step 1: assigned to lem-pur-etale-chart-compact-support-normalization

First check the domains of the operations. The coefficient datum makes $A$ finite. In the DVR $\mathcal O_E$, $|\ell|_E=\ell^{-1}<1$, so $\ell$ belongs to its maximal ideal $(t)$; hence $\ell^n\in(t^n)$ and $\ell^n A=0$. Since $\ell\ne p$, $\ell^n$ is invertible on every scheme under consideration. A field is Noetherian (its only ideals are zero and the unit ideal), so finite-type-over-Noetherian makes $X,Y$ Noetherian. Their open subschemes $U,V$ are Noetherian. Put $W=P\setminus i(U)$ and write $j:W\hookrightarrow P$. The affine presentation $V=\operatorname{Spec}B$, $P=\operatorname{Spec}B[x_1,\ldots,x_r]$ shows that $P\to V$ is finite type. The opens $U,V$ are quasi-compact and their inclusions are quasi-compact: intersect with an affine open of their Noetherian targets and use quasi-compactness of a Noetherian open. Thus the quasi-compact-open finite-type theorem and composition show that $U,V,P$, and then $W$, are finite type over $k$; $W$ is Noetherian and $j$ is quasi-compact by the same argument. Closed immersions are proper, so $i$ is compactifiable by $(\mathrm{id},i)$; the intermediate separated finite-type maps admit compactifications by Nagata. Every ambient right adjoint used below therefore exists on the whole ordinary etale derived category. None is being formed by restriction to bounded constructible objects.
