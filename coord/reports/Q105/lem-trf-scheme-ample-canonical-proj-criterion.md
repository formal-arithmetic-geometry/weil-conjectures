### Old step 2 → lem-trf-affine-local-ample-witnesses-separated

These affine restrictions imply that $f$ is separated: the opens $W\times_VW$ cover $X\times_TX$, and on each of them the diagonal of $f$ is the closed-immersion diagonal of $W\to V$. Closedness of its image, the identification with that image, and surjectivity of the structure-sheaf map glue on this open cover, exactly the closed-immersion definition [def-weil-closed-immersion]. Thus its diagonal is a closed immersion, the definition of separatedness [def-weil-proper-morphism]. Its inverse image of any affine open is affine, by the affine-quotient clause of [def-weil-closed-immersion], and is therefore quasi-compact. The diagonal is consequently quasi-compact, which is precisely quasi-separatedness [def-weil-quasi-separated-morphism].
