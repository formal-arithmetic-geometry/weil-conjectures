The faithful-flatness statement specifically covers maximal-ideal completions of Noetherian local rings and explicitly includes spectrum surjectivity. It is not merely a definition of flatness. Step 2 uses the polynomial differential basis node and its localization node to obtain the coordinate basis in P, and the regular-sequence conormal-free node twice, with the two regular sequences actually assumed in the item. This gives the indicated bases, not just abstract ranks. Scalar extension of each basis is written with inverse coordinate formulas. The rank of the formal middle module is part of its definition R^n; it does not require an assertion about ordinary Omega(T/k).

Step 3 uses the quotient differential map node, which states both exactness at the middle and surjectivity at the right, with exactly the maps used in this step. Tensor right exactness supplies exactly those same two properties after completion. No injectivity is inferred here. The quotient of the free coordinate module is generated by the images of its basis, so N is finite without any additional finiteness theorem.

Step 4 uses the completion comparison with residue field F=k. The structural section exists by the given k-algebra structure and identity residue identification; the identity field extension is finite separable. This statement actually asserts uniqueness and existence of the continuous derivation. The completed Jacobian theorem takes a continuous surjection and finitely many kernel generators; these are precisely the hypotheses supplied. It states the isomorphism of cokernels with the specified images of e_i. The conormal basis from Step 2 identifies its generator-domain with J/J^2. I explicitly checked the formula on any g and on J^2 using the formal partial-derivative definition, whose statement includes the Leibniz rule; that definition has been added to this step's citations. This addresses well-definedness, rather than silently identifying an arbitrary conormal map with the Jacobian matrix.

Step 5 uses the existing generic completed differential rank node under the identical hypotheses k perfect, S reduced, finite type and pure d, rational residue, and R reduced. It states both the field dimension for allowed maps and that each minimal-prime localization R_p is a field with smooth generic contraction and the same dimension. Their source dimensions are b and a, their common cokernel dimension is d, and b-a=d, forcing a zero-dimensional kernel. This proves, rather than assumes, field injectivity. All these vector spaces are finite-dimensional by the bases and the dimension assertion already supplied.
