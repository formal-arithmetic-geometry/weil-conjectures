### Edge cases checked

- Empty S and empty residual are permitted; all dimension conditions use `≤ 0`.
  The dependency gives the separate `r(empty) = 0` convention. The hypothesis
  `c < 0` rules out the trivial empty extremizer numerically, without a new binder.
- The empty X is excluded exactly by connectedness. An irreducible X has no
  nontrivial component decomposition, so `hTwoConnected` is correctly vacuous there.
- `R = 0` is allowed; no positivity of R is assumed. The formula for c is in Z,
  so negative integers cannot be lost to truncated natural subtraction.
- The union may equal S and the residual may be empty. Neither is required to be
  reduced; infinitesimal clusters are retained by ideal inf and ideal inequality.
- The conclusion direction is checked on affine ideals: residual inclusion is
  `I_S ⊆ I_residual`, and intersection of these ideals defines the larger union.
  At a smooth point, `(t^a)` and a residual `(t^(n-a))` have union ideal
  `(t^max(a,n-a))`, consistent with the chosen order.
- At an ordinary node the non-Cartier ideal `(u,v)` is allowed. The hypotheses and
  residual image characterization do not replace it by a local principal ideal.
- The field characteristic is unrestricted, as in the node. The single explicit
  universe `u` is used for the field and schemes consistently with the recorded
  dependency API. Every variable and universe is explicitly bound.
- No finite-dimensionality, residual splitting bound, intersection splitting bound,
  surjectivity of the section addition map, or generic-section assumption is added.
  Finite-dimensionality is a conclusion of the imported residual theorem.
