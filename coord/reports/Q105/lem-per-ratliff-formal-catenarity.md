### Hypotheses and conclusions, clause by clause

- A commutative ring A: `(A : Type u) [CommRing A]`, with `universe u` declared.
- Noetherian: `[IsNoetherianRing A]`, using the dependency's Mathlib definition
  `IsNoetherian A A`, i.e. finite generation of all ideals.
- Local with unique maximal ideal m: `[IsLocalRing A]` and
  `IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal A`. The existing local-ring class supplies
  nontriviality and the usual unique-maximal-ideal condition.
- The specified maximal-ideal completion B: every occurrence of B is the actual
  type `AdicCompletion (IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal A) A`. Its compatible sequence
  definition includes the n=0 quotient, which is the zero quotient; adding this
  uniquely determined coordinate does not change the inverse limit over n>=1.
  The ring and A-algebra structures come from Mathlib AdicCompletion.Algebra.
- Universal catenarity: the left side of the equivalence is the recorded
  `IsUniversallyCatenary A` from Sheaf.RingTheory.ExcellentRing. This includes
  Noetherianity and catenarity of every finite-type A-algebra, with the same
  prime-chain definition already used in the project.
- If and only if: `↔` asserts both implications, without choosing a direction.
- Every minimal prime p: `∀ p : Ideal A, p ∈ minimalPrimes A → ...`.
  Mathlib's `minimalPrimes A` is the set of primes minimal above the zero ideal,
  equivalently primes with no strictly smaller prime.
- The extended ideal pB: `Ideal.map (algebraMap A
  (AdicCompletion (IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal A) A)) p`. This is the ideal
  generated by the canonical images of elements of p, with no arbitrary map or
  algebra structure supplied as an additional hypothesis.
- The quotient spectrum: `AlgebraicGeometry.Spec (CommRingCat.of
  (AdicCompletion (IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal A) A ⧸ Ideal.map ... p))`.
  This uses the dependency's actual affine-scheme constructor, whose underlying
  topology is the Zariski prime spectrum.
- All irreducible components: `∀ Z ∈ irreducibleComponents (Spec ...), ...`.
  These are Mathlib's maximal irreducible subsets of that precise spectrum.
- Same finite Krull dimension: `∃ d : ℕ, ... topologicalKrullDim Z =
  (d : WithBot ℕ∞)`. The witness is shared by every component of a fixed
  quotient, and lies inside the quantifier over p, so different minimal-prime
  quotients may have different dimensions. A natural-number witness excludes
  both minus infinity and infinity, and includes zero. `Z` is given its
  subspace topology by the subtype instance. No equidimensionality predicate
  is recreated.

### Edge cases checked

- A field: m=0, the completion is A, p=0 is the sole minimal prime, and the
  one-point quotient spectrum has dimension zero; the statement permits d=0.
- A nonzero Artinian local ring, including rings with nilpotents: the completion
  and sole component are zero-dimensional. No reducedness/domain hypothesis is
  added.
- A DVR: the sole completed component has dimension one; d=1 is permitted.
- Several minimal primes: the separate d for each p is essential, and is exactly
  what the quantifier order expresses; no common dimension across different p
  is imposed.
- The zero ring is excluded by the ordinary source convention for local rings,
  matched by Mathlib's `IsLocalRing` extending `Nontrivial`.
- A completed quotient is nonzero for minimal p: completion is faithfully flat
  and its extended p is contained in the completed maximal ideal. Thus the
  equidimensionality condition is not silently about an empty spectrum. It is
  stated for all components, without adding a redundant nonemptiness clause.
- u is arbitrary; the completion, quotient and Spec stay in the ring's universe.
  No independent smaller universe or equality of unrelated universes is imposed.
