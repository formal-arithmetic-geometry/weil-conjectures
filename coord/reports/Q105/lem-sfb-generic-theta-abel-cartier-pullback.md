## Source inference repaired

Milne III 6.7's last paragraph asserts a bound on finite fibre ranks, mentioning normality of the target. Normality by itself does not imply this bound. For example take the normal domain A=k[x,y], char(k) not 2, and B=A+(x,y)t inside Frac(A)[t]/(t^2-x). B is a finite domain A-algebra of generic rank 2, but B/(x,y)B = k plus ((x,y)/(x,y)^2)t has dimension 3. Thus one cannot cite a general finite-normal-base fibre-rank bound from that sentence alone.
