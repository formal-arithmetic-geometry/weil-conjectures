## What was wrong and what changed

### STATEMENT-DISPUTED: omitted scheme hypotheses

Read literally for arbitrary schemes, the imported statement is false. Let k be a field of positive characteristic different from ell, Y = Spec(k[t_1,t_2,...]), I = (t_1,t_2,...), X = Spec(k), and let i:X → Y be the closed immersion defined by I. This is proper (closed immersions are finite type even when their defining ideals are not finitely generated). Set A = F_ell and C = A_X[0]. It is bounded constructible and has Tor amplitude [0,0]. Every geometric fibre of i is empty or a geometric point, so d = 0 is a uniform ell-cohomological bound. However i_*A_X has support {I}, which is not constructible in Y, so it cannot be a constructible sheaf under the project's finite constructible-stratification definition.

Here is an explicit check of nonconstructibility, including its local nature. The complement of {I} is the union of D(t_j), which has no finite subcover: after finitely many t_j have been chosen, the prime generated by those variables misses another variable. In any principal neighbourhood D(f) of I, f(0) is nonzero. Include all variables appearing in f among the finitely many variables already chosen; their generated prime still belongs to D(f) and misses another t_j. Thus the complement is not quasi-compact even in any such neighbourhood. A constructible open subset of an affine spectral space is quasi-compact, so the closed singleton is not locally constructible near I. Equivalently, no finitely generated ideal has radical I.

The correction restores the standing scheme setting explicitly: k has characteristic p > 0 with p != ell, X and Y are separated finite-type k-schemes and the proper map is a k-morphism. This is `sk-def-sfb-dc-setting`, the setting used throughout the same chapter, and exactly the finite-type setting of the imported first input `lem-fin-proper-bounded-constructible`. The blueprint's proper-image statements elsewhere say “between standing schemes.” Stacks 0GL0 itself requires finite presentation and qcqs hypotheses; it does not justify the unqualified arbitrary-scheme claim. The corrected statement also says commutative, defines ell-primary as killed by a power of ell, spells out the supplied fibre-cohomological bound, and writes out the etale Dctf subcategory from the existing etale constructibility and Tor definitions. It does not replace the finite ring by A_{E,n}, assume N is finite/flat, or change the claimed [a,b+d] interval. No target is edited. The counterexample and correction are also recorded in the node's `split_report.statement_dispute` for the maintainer.
