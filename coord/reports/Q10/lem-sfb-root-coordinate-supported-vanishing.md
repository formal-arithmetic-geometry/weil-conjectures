### Returned step 1

Write $B=\Gamma(V,\mathcal O_V)$ and first form the tower over $V$ itself. Fix $J,a$ as in the statement and set
$$B_n=B[x_{1,n},\ldots,x_{r,n}]/(x_{b,n}^n-t_b)_b,\quad X_n=\operatorname{Spec}(B_n[1/\prod_{b\in J}t_b]).$$
The index is the positive integers prime to $p$, ordered by divisibility, with least element $1$. For $n\mid m$, put $e=m/n$. As a $B_n$-algebra, $B_m$ is obtained by adjoining $y_b$ satisfying $y_b^e=x_{b,n}$; eliminate the old variables to check this presentation. Repeated monic division [lem-weil-pbc-monic-quotient-free] gives the basis $\prod_b y_b^{d_b}$, $0\leq d_b<e$, so the transition is finite free, also after the indicated localization; in the same way $B_n$ is free over $B$. Since $n$ is prime to $p$ it is a unit in the $k$-algebra $B$, so $B_n$ is regular [lem-sfb-snc-finite-root-regular-ring], and so is its localization $X_n$ [lem-weil-pbc-localization-of-regular-ring]. The ring of $D_n=V(x_{a,n})$ is the same localization of $B_n/(x_{a,n})$, which is regular by the face $J=\{a\}$ of [lem-sfb-snc-finite-root-face-regular]; hence $D_n$ is regular. The intersections $V(t_a,t_b)$ may be reducible or empty, so the root-ring lemma is not applied to $V(t_a)$ directly. The boundary ideal $(t_1\cdots t_r)$ is effective Cartier [def-trf-strict-normal-crossings-boundary], so the product, and hence its factor $t_a$, is a nonzerodivisor on $B$. By freeness $t_a$ stays a nonzerodivisor on $B_n$ and after localization, and so does $x_{a,n}$, which divides $t_a=x_{a,n}^n$. Thus $D_n$ is regular Cartier. The empty divisor case causes no exception.
