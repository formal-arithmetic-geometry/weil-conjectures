For clarity, the remaining general realization lemma has the following proof route (recorded here, not in its unsplit node). The n-skeleton of |A| attaches Aₙ⊗Δⁿ along (LₙA⊗Δⁿ) amalgamated with (Aₙ⊗∂Δⁿ); its attaching map is the pushout-product of the latching cofibration and ∂Δⁿ→Δⁿ. Tensor SM7 makes it a cofibration. Starting at the initial object and taking the sequential colimit proves that |A| is cofibrant, and proves the same assertion for B. A prism homotopy gives maps from Aₙ⊗Δⁿ⁺¹ to |B|, one for each prism simplex. The usual triangulation of Δⁿ×Δ¹ has n+1 simplices, with ordered vertices (0,0),…,(j,0),(j,1),…,(n,1); the common-face relations are precisely the prism identities. Since tensor preserves colimits, these maps glue on Aₙ⊗(Δⁿ×Δ¹), and the face/degeneracy prism identities identify them under the coend relations. They therefore give |A|⊗Δ¹→|B| with the required endpoints. For a cofibrant object C, C⊗∂Δ¹→C⊗Δ¹ is a cofibration by SM7; either endpoint inclusion C→C⊗Δ¹ is a trivial cofibration because Δ⁰→Δ¹ is anodyne. Two-out-of-three makes the projection to C a weak equivalence, so this is a good cylinder. The two realized homotopies consequently exhibit |g| as the inverse of |f| in the model homotopy category, and the model-category Whitehead/saturation criterion gives that |f| is a weak equivalence.
