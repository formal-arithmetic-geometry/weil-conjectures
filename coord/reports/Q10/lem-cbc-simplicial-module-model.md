For the horn helper, in P(n) the horn subcomplex K is spanned by the strings avoiding at least one vertex other than k. All strings except the top t and the missing face u satisfy this. The quotient therefore has only [t] in degree n and [u] in degree n-1, and its differential is (-1)^k. To see a chain splitting, use the rank-two subcomplex generated by t and its differential ∂t. The coefficient of u in ∂t is (-1)^k, a unit in every coefficient ring. Thus the new lower generator differs from ±u by an element of K, and replacing u by ∂t is an invertible change of basis in degree n-1. The upper generator t and lower generator ∂t form an identity-differential disk because ∂²t=0. This gives B = K ⊕ D^n as chain complexes, with the identity on K. Normalization identifies the horn with that subcomplex because it is an additive equivalence and the horn is the sum of its face submodules, whose images are the string spans under the natural q_m isomorphisms. This is the nontrivial comparison/splitting extracted from the original step, not an assertion that the raw omitted face alone has zero differential.

For the disk helper, a square consists of u:K→C and v:K⊕D^n→E. Let y=v(e_n). If g_n is surjective, choose x with g_n(x)=y and define h on K as u, on e_n as x and on e_{n-1} as ∂x. The chain conditions hold since ∂²=0; g∂x=∂y=v(e_{n-1}), so gh=v in both new degrees. Conversely, for any y in E_n take u=0, v|K=0, v(e_n)=y and v(e_{n-1})=∂y. This is a chain map for arbitrary y, not only for cycles. A filler gives the required preimage of y. This independent lifting calculation is extracted at the human's explicit request; it makes no model-category existence claim and does not restate a clause of the item.
