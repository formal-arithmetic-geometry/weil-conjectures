## Source error

Stacks 0605, proof (2), says that the two restrictions of c on W times_T W are equal. This is false: take X=Y=T=Spec(k), Z=Spec(k) disjoint-union Spec(k), p the fold map, and all thickenings the identities. They satisfy the hypotheses (etale maps are formally etale, so the identity thickening of Z is universal over X). Here W=Z, c is the identity, and on W times_T W the two projections to Z differ at the pair consisting of the two different components. Thus one cannot apply universality of Z+ to these two different reduction maps. The lemma's statement is true; there is no STATEMENT-DISPUTED change to it.

The repair is written in steps 7 and 8. For two proposed extensions b1,b2 into Y+, first use etale lifting into Z+ on the lifted test cover with the SAME chosen reduction c. Universality of Z+ equates these lifts, so b1 and b2 agree on the cover, hence everywhere. This uniqueness statement for arbitrary tests then equates the two composites into Y+ on the overlap. It never claims that the underlying maps into Z agree. Existence follows by sheaf descent. This retains the source's lifted-cover route and corrects the faulty comparison.
