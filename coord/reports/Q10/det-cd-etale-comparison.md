### STATEMENT-DISPUTED: literal global-section torsion wording

The imported phrase "each local section is killed by some power" must be read locally, as the already-reviewed `sk-def-cd` specifies. It is false as a general equivalence with that definition if interpreted as requiring an annihilator on every site object. Let S = Spec(k) for an algebraically closed field k, let A be the direct sum over n >= 1 of Z/ell^n, and take its constant etale sheaf. It is locally ell-power torsion. On the etale object U = the disjoint union of copies of S indexed by n >= 1, its section whose nth component is a generator of Z/ell^n has no ell-power annihilator. Likewise, direct image of local covers on the pro-etale site cannot simply be assumed to produce an etale cover. The repaired statement explicitly uses the sheaf-colimit definition from `sk-def-cd`, faithfully preserving the intended local notion and every comparison clause; Step 1 proves both directions without either false inference.
