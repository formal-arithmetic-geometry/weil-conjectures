I read lem-weil-pbc-morphism-affine-schemes-affine: it asserts that every map between affine schemes is affine, with no finite type assumption. On each affine etale B-chart both source and target of a transition are affine by the correspondence's chart formula. Their hypotheses apply to the pulled-back cover of Z_i. This proves affine transitions without incorrectly claiming that every map to an affine object is affine.

For step 3, I read lem-sfb-space-qcoh-affine-chart-calculus. Its statement explicitly creates filtered colimits of quasi-coherent algebra sheaves and computes them on every affine etale chart. Therefore the abstract QCAlg colimit from step 1 has the asserted ring colimit on charts, by uniqueness of colimits. The relative-Spec correspondence explicitly gives the Spec(A(U)) charts and the induced morphisms, so the ring colimit is the one belonging to this cone. I read lem-weil-pbc-limit-affine-transition: its last sentence supplies Spec of the ring colimit for an affine system. No schematicity conclusion for a general algebraic-space base is used. Yoneda preservation of this limit means exactly the existing universal property evaluated on each test scheme; it does not require a new approximation lemma. Limits of sheaves are calculated on sections. Pulling back the atlas to test schemes gives covers by the stated fibre-product theorem, and inverse maps agree on overlaps because a local inverse to a fixed comparison is unique. The sheaf axiom therefore glues them. This proves the comparison as an fppf sheaf over B, rather than only among affine objects.
