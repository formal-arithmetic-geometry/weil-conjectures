## Source issue in the rejected dependency

The inherited proof used `det-lem-int-integral-heart-local-model` to conclude constructibility on arbitrary affine schemes. Its statement promises finite locally closed strata, while the current definition additionally requires constructible subsets. A finite locally closed stratification need not have that property: in X=Spec k[x_1,x_2,...], U=the union of D(x_i) is open and not quasi-compact (the prime generated by all x_i except x_j lies only in D(x_j)). The dependency's imported proof also explicitly says “for our Noetherian schemes”, a hypothesis absent from its statement. This is a failing inference in the earlier sandbox, not grounds to add a hypothesis to this item. The repaired proof avoids that dependency entirely and uses the already constructible cohomology-sheaf strata. The item statement keeps arbitrary T.
