## Not duplicates

lem-sfb-ringed-site-dg-tensor-hom vs lem-sfb-bundle-endomorphism-derived-adjunction: the existing lemma treats a finite locally free bundle in degree zero on an A-scheme and modules over its ordinary endomorphism ring. The new lemma treats an arbitrary unbounded complex on a small ringed site, a genuinely graded endomorphism dg algebra, and right dg modules; it also specifies the K-injective regular-module unit. An arbitrary perfect generator need not be a bundle or have vanishing higher self-Ext.

lem-sfb-ringed-site-dg-tensor-hom vs lem-sfb-projective-space-tilting-equivalence: the existing lemma is a geometric tilting equivalence for a specific direct sum of projective-space twists. It gives neither the ringed-site construction for arbitrary complexes nor dg module coefficients with nonzero graded terms.

lem-sfb-ringed-site-dg-tensor-hom vs lem-sfb-space-generator-dg-equivalence: the item concludes an equivalence from a perfect detecting generator. The new lemma constructs the adjunction for every complex without compactness or generation, and does not assert full faithfulness or an equivalence.

lem-sfb-dg-adjunction-compact-unit vs lem-sfb-compact-bundle-self-ext-full-faithfulness: the existing lemma assumes a bundle in degree zero, its ordinary endomorphism ring, and vanishing self-Ext in every nonzero degree. The new criterion permits a nonconnective dg algebra and an arbitrary triangulated target, assuming the specified adjunction, compact regular-module image, and invertible unit at the regular module. Nonzero higher self-Ext is allowed and is essential in this item.

lem-sfb-dg-adjunction-compact-unit vs lem-sfb-projective-space-tilting-equivalence: the existing projective-space theorem concerns a particular tilting bundle and ordinary module algebra, and concludes a geometric equivalence. The new lemma is an abstract criterion for an already specified dg adjunction into any triangulated category; it permits higher dg cohomology and does not assert essential surjectivity.

lem-sfb-dg-adjunction-compact-unit vs lem-sfb-adjunction-generator-defect-criterion: the existing criterion concerns only the finite triangulated closure of generators and expressly requires no infinite sums. It does not extend the unit to the whole unbounded dg derived category. The new criterion uses all coproducts and dg resolutions; these hypotheses and conclusion are different.

lem-sfb-space-dg-tensor-quasicoherent vs lem-sfb-space-generator-dg-equivalence: the new lemma concerns only the image of an already constructed tensor functor for any quasi-coherent complex, with no compactness, perfection, generation, qcqs hypothesis, or equivalence conclusion. It is the strictly smaller auxiliary assertion 09MA used by 09MC, not a clause restating the item's result.
