### Original step 1 -> lem-pur-clopen-ringed-module-gluing

Work in the small skeleta and universe of [def-weil-pbc-proetale-site]. The empty scheme has only the empty object in its site, and the empty covering forces every module sheaf there to be zero. The family $X_i\to X$ is a cover by the covering definition: pull back any affine open and cover its finitely many clopen pieces by affines. The double overlaps for distinct indices are empty, while the diagonal overlaps are $X_i$.

### Original step 2 -> lem-pur-clopen-ringed-module-gluing

For any sheaf of commutative rings $R$ on this site, write $F_i$ for a module over its restriction to $X_i$. Use identity descent maps on diagonal overlaps and the unique maps on empty overlaps. They satisfy the cocycle identity. The module gluing statement gives a module sheaf restricting to the $F_i$. Morphism descent for underlying sets gives a unique glued map for any family of component maps; it is additive and $R$-linear because these equations can be checked after restriction to the cover. Thus restriction is fully faithful and essentially surjective. Explicitly, set $G((F_i))(U)=\prod_iF_i(U\times_X X_i)$, with $R(U)$ acting by its restriction in each factor. Matching sections for a cover of $U$ glue uniquely in each factor, so $G$ is a sheaf. The sheaf axiom on $\{U\times_X X_i\to U\}$ gives $F(U)\cong\prod_iF(U\times_X X_i)$, naturally in $U$ and $F$. Also $(G(F_i))|_{X_j}=F_j$, since all other factors are zero. These are the unit and counit of the required explicit equivalence.

### Original step 4 -> lem-pur-finite-product-derived-tensor-calculus

Let $r_i$ be component restriction and $e_i$ the exact functor inserting a module into coordinate $i$ and zero into every other coordinate, followed by $G$. The identities $r_ie_j=0$ for $i\ne j$, $r_ie_i\cong\mathrm{id}$, and $\bigoplus_i e_ir_i\cong\mathrm{id}$ hold on modules and degreewise on complexes.
