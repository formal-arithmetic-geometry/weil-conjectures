## Corrections and scope

The imported proof's sentence “of finite type, hence quasi-compact and quasi-separated” omits the actual reason for quasi-separatedness. Finite type alone does not imply it on arbitrary schemes: glue two copies of Spec k[x_1,x_2,...] along the non-quasi-compact open union of D(x_i); the morphism to Spec k[x_1,x_2,...] that is identity on each copy is finite type but not quasi-separated. In the item, f is separated, so its closed relative diagonal is proper, hence finite type and quasi-compact. Step 4 now spells this out. The same omitted inference occurs in cor-sf-direct-image-ctf-etale-origin; I report it without editing that other item. The item statement itself is correct and unchanged.
