For lem-sfb-continuous-integral-pivot-splitting the full intended argument is as follows. Write the block matrix as G = [[p,b],[c,B]]. The shell pi^e A^times is open: in E it is the set |x|=|pi|^e, which is open by the ultrametric inequality around any of its points. On its inverse image U under p, p never vanishes and p/pi^e is a unit u. Every entry is in pi^e A, so division of b and c by p takes values in A. These quotients are continuous in E because p is nowhere zero, and hence continuous into the subspace A. Put L0 = [[1,0],[-c/p,I]] and R0 = [[1,-b/p],[0,I]]. Their inverses replace the minus signs with plus signs, and multiplication gives L0 G R0 = diag(p,B-cb/p). Multiply the first row by u inverse to obtain pi^e. Multiplication by u inverse and its inverse u are continuous, as are all entries of L0, R0 and their inverses. The residual matrix B-cb/p is integral and continuous. This proves precisely one pivot reduction, with no assumption or conclusion about the residual module type, no induction and no Smith diagonalization. The same formulas make sense with an empty smaller row or column set.

For lem-sfb-dvr-cyclic-summand-cancellation the intended scalar argument is the invariant calculation previously written inline. The existing scalar Smith theorem applied to a finite free presentation gives Q = A^f direct sum a finite list of A/(pi^a) with a>0, omitting the zero summands a=0. For k>=1 put d_k(Q)=dim_(A/pi) pi^(k-1)Q/pi^k Q. Direct calculation gives d_k(Q)=f+#{a>=k}; the sequence is eventually f, and d_k-d_(k+1) counts summands of exponent exactly k. Therefore these dimensions determine Q's isomorphism class. They are additive under direct sums. An isomorphism T_e direct sum M = T_e direct sum N yields equal dimensions after subtracting the common finite contribution d_k(T_e), so M and N have identical free rank and positive cyclic multiplicities, and hence are isomorphic. A DVR is a PID by the cited definition; finite generation gives a finite free presentation since its relation submodule is finite free by the existing Smith leaf. For e=0, T_e=0 and cancellation is immediate. This is a scalar module lemma with no topology, no matrix family, and no local reduction conclusion.
