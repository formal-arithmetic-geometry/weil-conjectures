STATEMENT-DISPUTED (existing dependency, not this item's statement): `def-sf-fullcd-class` says that all sheaves of etale origin are also the constructible sheaves. This is false as worded. For T = Spec(k) with k algebraically closed and A a finite field (n = 1), the constant etale sheaf with stalk the infinite-dimensional A-module direct sum over N of A pulls back to a sheaf of etale origin, but its infinite stalk is not constructible under `def-weil-finite-torsion-constructibility`. The faithful correction is that constructible pro-etale sheaves come from constructible etale sheaves, and form a subclass of the etale-origin sheaves.
