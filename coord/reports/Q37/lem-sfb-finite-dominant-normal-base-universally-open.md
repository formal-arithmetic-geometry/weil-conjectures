## Preserved arguments for the new nodes

### Affine chart rings — old step 1

Choose an affine cover $B=\bigcup_i U_i$, $U_i=\operatorname{Spec}(A_i)$, with $A_i$ Noetherian, discarding empty opens. Finiteness gives $q^{-1}(U_i)=\operatorname{Spec}(C_i)$ with $C_i$ a finite $A_i$-module. Dominance makes this inverse image nonempty and its image dense in $U_i$, so integrality of $B,V$ makes $A_i,C_i$ domains. Write $\varphi_i:A_i\to C_i$. Its spectral image lies in the closed set $V(\ker\varphi_i)$; density makes this all of $\operatorname{Spec}(A_i)$. Evaluating at the prime $(0)$ shows $\ker\varphi_i=0$. Thus $A_i\subseteq C_i$. The affine-open stalk-localization lemma identifies every $(A_i)_{\mathfrak m}$ with a stalk of $B$, hence a normal domain by hypothesis. The local criterion for normal domains therefore makes $A_i$ a normal domain.

This future proof uses exactly the six cited statements of old step 1: the locally Noetherian cover definition, the finite affine morphism definition, the integral-scheme definition, the affine spectrum definition, the affine-open stalk-localization theorem, and the normal-domain-of-local-normal theorem. In particular the last two are theorem statements, not consequences assumed from a definition.

### Polynomial extension data — old steps 2 and 3

Fix $i$ and $n\geq0$ and abbreviate $A=A_i$, $C=C_i$, $A_n=A[t_1,\ldots,t_n]$, $C_n=C[t_1,\ldots,t_n]$. The induced map $A_n\to C_n$ is injective because equality of polynomials is coefficientwise and $A\to C$ is injective. If $c_1,\ldots,c_r$ generate $C$ as an $A$-module, they also generate $C_n$ as an $A_n$-module: in a polynomial $\sum_\alpha d_\alpha t^\alpha$, expand each $d_\alpha=\sum_j a_{j\alpha}c_j$ and regroup as $\sum_j(\sum_\alpha a_{j\alpha}t^\alpha)c_j$. These generators also generate $C_n$ as an $A_n$-algebra, since their linear span already contains every element.

The polynomial-domain lemma makes $C_n$ a domain. Apply the one-variable normal-polynomial lemma successively $n$ times, starting with the normal domain $A$, to make $A_n$ a normal domain (at $n=0$ it is $A$). The finite-type-algebra Noetherian theorem makes $A_n$ Noetherian, since its $n$ variables generate it over $A$. Step 2 makes $C_n$ a finite-type $A_n$-algebra, so the Noetherian finite-type theorem makes it finitely presented over $A_n$. Finally the finite-algebra theorem applied to the module generators from step 2 makes $C_n$ integral over $A_n$.

This future proof uses the polynomial-ring definition, the polynomial-domain theorem, the one-variable normal-polynomial theorem, the finite-type-algebra Noetherian theorem, finite type over Noetherian implies finite presentation, and finite algebra implies integral. These already exist in the DAG; this repair creates no replacement versions of them. The new statement includes the unchanged generators as well as all outputs needed for going down.
