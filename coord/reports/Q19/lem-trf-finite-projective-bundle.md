### Old Step 1: parent Step 1

Put $\mathcal E=h_*\mathcal O_T$, including its natural commutative $\mathcal O_S$-algebra structure. The finite-morphism definition [def-weil-finite-integral-morphism] gives that $\mathcal E$ is quasi-coherent of finite type, that $h$ is affine, and that over every affine $V=\operatorname{Spec}R\subseteq S$ the inverse image is $\operatorname{Spec}A$, where $A=\mathcal E(V)$ is a finite $R$-algebra. Cited statements: def-weil-finite-integral-morphism, def-trf-projective-bundle-morphism.

### Old Step 2: lem-trf-positive-copy-graded-quotient

Form a graded sheaf algebra $\mathcal B$ with $\mathcal B_0=\mathcal O_S$ and $\mathcal B_n=\mathcal E$ for each $n\ge1$. Write $a[n]$ for $a$ in its degree-$n$ copy. For positive $m,n$, define $a[m]b[n]=(ab)[m+n]$; let degree zero act through the given $\mathcal O_S$-algebra structure and take the unit in degree zero. Associativity, commutativity, unitality and restriction compatibility follow directly from those of $\mathcal E$, giving precisely [def-weil-pbc-graded-sheaf-structures]. Its underlying sheaf is the categorical sum $\mathcal O_S\oplus\bigoplus_{n\ge1}\mathcal E$, hence is quasi-coherent by [lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-module-coproduct-closure]. For an affine $V=\operatorname{Spec}R$, the tilde/global-sections equivalence and preservation of coproducts in [lem-weil-pbc-affine-tilde-gamma-adjunction] identify $\mathcal B(V)$ with $C=R\oplus\bigoplus_{n\ge1}A[n]$, with the displayed multiplication. Denote the global degree-one section $1_{\mathcal E}[1]$ by $t$.

Cited statements: def-weil-pbc-graded-sheaf-structures, lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-module-coproduct-closure, lem-weil-pbc-affine-tilde-gamma-adjunction.

### Old Step 3: lem-trf-positive-copy-graded-quotient (algebra construction) and parent Step 2 (apply Proj closed-immersion theorem)

The inclusion $\mathcal E\to\mathcal B_1$ induces a sheaf-algebra map $q:\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal E)\to\mathcal B$ by [lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-symmetric-algebra]. It is degree preserving for the symmetric-degree grading [lem-weil-pbc-symmetric-algebra-graded]: a product of $n$ degree-one generators goes to their product in $\mathcal B_n$. It is surjective in every degree. Degree zero is the identity on $\mathcal O_S$; for $n\ge1$ each local $a[n]$ is the image of $j(a)j(1)^{n-1}$. By Step 2, $\mathcal B$ and $\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal E)$ are graded $\mathcal O_S$-algebras whose underlying modules are quasi-coherent, i.e. quasi-coherent algebras [def-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-algebra], so both relative Proj schemes [def-nagata-relative-proj] are defined. Thus the surjective graded-map clause of [prop-nagata-relative-proj-functoriality] supplies a closed immersion $\iota:\operatorname{Proj}_S(\mathcal B)\hookrightarrow\operatorname{Proj}_S(\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal E))=\mathbf P_S(\mathcal E)$ over $S$.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-symmetric-algebra, lem-weil-pbc-symmetric-algebra-graded, prop-nagata-relative-proj-functoriality, def-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-algebra, def-nagata-relative-proj.

### Old Step 4: lem-trf-positive-copy-proj-affine-comparison

On an affine $V$ use $C=R\oplus\bigoplus_{n\ge1}A[n]$ from Step 2 and $t=1_A[1]$. Every positive-degree homogeneous element satisfies $a[n]^2=t\,(a^2)[2n-1]$. If a homogeneous prime contains $t$, primality therefore forces it to contain every positive-degree homogeneous element and hence $C_+$. Such a prime is excluded from $\operatorname{Proj}(C)$ by [def-pc-proj]. Consequently the entire Proj is the single standard open $D_+(t)$. By [prop-nagata-proj-standard-open] it is canonically $\operatorname{Spec}(C_{(t),0})$. This calculation uses no integrality, reducedness, or cancellation.

Cited statements: def-pc-proj, prop-nagata-proj-standard-open.

### Old Step 5: lem-trf-positive-copy-proj-affine-comparison

Compute $C_{(t),0}$ with the fraction convention of [def-nagata-homogeneous-localization-degree-zero]. Every fraction can be represented as $a[m]/t^m$ with $m\ge1$: for a degree-zero numerator $r\in R$, multiply numerator and denominator by $t$ to obtain $\eta(r)[1]/t$. The map $a[m]/t^m\mapsto a$ is well defined, since multiplication by $t$ on every positive-degree copy of $A$ is the identity on its coefficient, so the localization equality of two such fractions is exactly equality of their coefficients. Its inverse is $a\mapsto a[1]/t$. The product and sum formulas show these are inverse $R$-algebra maps, and the degree-zero structure map becomes $\eta:R\to A$. Thus the chart of Step 4 is canonically $\operatorname{Spec}A=h^{-1}(V)$ over $V$, using spectrum functoriality [def-weil-affine-spectrum]. The argument also applies when $A=0$: both localizations and both spectra are then zero or empty as appropriate.

Cited statements: def-nagata-homogeneous-localization-degree-zero, def-weil-affine-spectrum.

### Old Step 6: lem-trf-positive-copy-proj-affine-comparison (compatibility and gluing) and parent Step 3 (final composition)

Relative Proj restricts to the affine Proj of its section algebra, compatibly with further restriction and structure maps, by [prop-nagata-relative-proj-functoriality]. The local isomorphisms of Steps 4--5 commute with restriction: on every affine refinement of an overlap, the same formula sends $a[m]/t^m$ to the restricted section $a$, and $t$ restricts to the degree-one unit. The inverse formulas commute as well. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-scheme-morphism-gluing] to both families. Their two composites are identities on the affine-base open covers, hence are identities by uniqueness in that node. This gives an $S$-isomorphism $T\simeq\operatorname{Proj}_S(\mathcal B)$. Composing it with the closed immersion of Step 3 is a closed immersion (the homeomorphism onto a closed set and sheaf-surjection conditions of [def-weil-closed-immersion] are preserved under precomposition with an isomorphism). Since $\mathcal E$ is finite type quasi-coherent by Step 1, [def-trf-projective-bundle-morphism] now says exactly that $h$ is projective in the required convention.

Cited statements: prop-nagata-relative-proj-functoriality, lem-weil-pbc-scheme-morphism-gluing, def-weil-closed-immersion, def-trf-projective-bundle-morphism.
