## New nodes

`def-sfb-global-base-ideal-sheaf-quotient` defines the base-ideal-generated quotient sheaf
and its canonical map on global sections, in independent site object/morphism universes.
Its module is `Sheaf.CategoryTheory.GlobalBaseIdealSheafQuotient`. The statement includes
the compatible-family ring, evaluation, generated ideals, their restriction laws,
quotient presheaf, ring sheafification, quotient unit, induced global ring map, its
annihilation of IG, its unique quotient lift and the associated algebra structure.
The named theorem obligations in this module implement exactly those structural laws;
the node includes their statements, and each has a source and full proof plan.
Source is Alper Lemma 5.2.1 with the standard sheaf quotient construction. The three
actual search outputs are recorded in the node. This is not a new abstract ideal-
subsheaf definition: `RingedSiteIdeal` and its `ringQuotient` take a saturated ideal
subsheaf as input and require one universe for objects, morphisms and values. They
supply neither the structural base-ideal input nor the general universe interface needed
here. The new construction uses the existing Mathlib ring quotients and sheafification
and imposes the generated relations locally. It never picks an arbitrary map or object
from mere existence. Sheafification is a left adjoint uniquely determined up to the
relevant natural isomorphism, so its named existence obligation is not hollow data.
