The map $R^n\to B$, $(a_i)\mapsto\sum_{i=0}^{n-1}a_it^i$, is the unique map between singleton modules and is an isomorphism, so the displayed family is a basis in the sense of [def-weil-pbc-free-module]. Both spectra are empty because the zero ring has no proper prime ideal [def-weil-affine-spectrum]. Here is the faithful-flatness assertion used in Tag 03PL, written out from [def-weil-pbc-faithfully-flat-ring-map]. Let $b_i=t^i$ and let $c_i:B\to R$ be the coordinates of the basis from step 2.
