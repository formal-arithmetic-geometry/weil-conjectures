Step 1 uses `lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-base-change` exactly for closedness of D, `def-pc-fibre-product` exactly for the lifting equivalence, and `lem-pc-proper-monomorphism-closed` in its closed-immersion-implies-monomorphism direction, with no properness assumption on Y. `def-weil-finite-type-morphism` gives the finite affine cover of Y over Spec k. `lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra` gives the precise B_a tensor_k A ring and its projections. `lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-affine-cover-criterion` gives an affine inverse image and a surjective ring map; taking its kernel and first-isomorphism quotient is elementary. The source for the pullback is Stacks 01JU, the affine product is 01I4/01JQ and the quotient description is 01QO. All ring maps in these operations are k-compatible.

Step 2 uses `lem-weil-pbc-vector-space-free` for a possibly infinite basis of B_a, and `lem-weil-pbc-free-module-base-change-free` for that same basis after tensoring with A and with every R. Each expansion has finite support. This is Milne 1.102–1.103 in two explicit linear-algebra lines: a vector is zero iff its coordinates vanish, and a map kills a generated ideal iff it kills its generators. It is correctly marked elementary relative to those nodes. Finite-dimensionality of B_a and flatness of A→R are not needed.

Step 3 uses `def-weil-affine-spectrum` for the exact reversal between ring maps and affine scheme morphisms, and the quotient-ring universal property for factorization. The two other cited statements give monicity and gluing of compatible scheme maps. I corrected the coverage justification by explicitly citing `lem-weil-pbc-open-fibre-product`, whose statement identifies V_a times Spec R with the inverse image of V_a; these opens therefore cover. Overlaps need not be affine: uniqueness follows from monicity and gluing applies to arbitrary open covers. This proves both directions of the factorization criterion, not merely containment of underlying sets. It follows Milne 1.105 p.38.

Step 4 uses clause (0) of `lem-weil-pbc-affine-ideals-closed-subscheme` for U_h affine with ring A_h. The equality of ideals is proved, not assumed: the quotient tests by the two ideals give the two inclusions. Thus the compatibility demanded in clause (1) is established with no gluing of finite-type ideals or noetherian assumption on test rings. This explicates the uniqueness argument in Milne Example 1.100.

Step 5 applies precisely clause (1) of that same existing node: every affine U has an ideal, and Step 4 verifies principal-open extension compatibility. Its conclusion gives both a closed immersion L→S and the affine quotient descriptions, exactly what Step 5 takes from it. This is the Stacks 01QQ/01IN construction, not an uncited representability claim. No arbitrary-base-change assertion is taken from this node.

Step 6 extends to arbitrary T by an affine open cover subordinate to the inverse images of affine opens in S. I added `lem-weil-pbc-affine-opens-basis` for that refinement, and the open-fibre-product node for the resulting cover of Y times T. The exact gluing and equality clauses of `lem-weil-pbc-scheme-morphism-gluing`, together with monicity from `lem-pc-proper-monomorphism-closed`, supply both global implications. Nothing requires T to be separated, quasi-compact, reduced, or finite type.

Step 7 is an elementary argument from monicity: the represented maps inject, precomposition preserves the condition, and the lifts L→L′ and L′→L are inverse. The cited proper-monomorphism equivalence states the required monicity. This gives uniqueness up to the unique isomorphism over S, which is uniqueness of the closed subscheme, including its nilpotent structure.

Step 8 uses the general closed-base-change node, valid for arbitrary S′→S, and the exact universal mapping property of the fibre product definition. Writing b∘t in the test criterion shows explicitly why the two induced families agree, and gives the arbitrary-base-change clause without applying a finite-type-only existence theorem to S′. It is the elementary pullback-of-subfunctors argument of Milne 1.101.

I tested Y empty (all coefficient ideals zero and L=S), W=X (likewise), W empty with nonempty Y (a nonzero B_a over a field has faithful scalar extension, so the coefficient ideal is the unit ideal and L is empty), and S empty.
