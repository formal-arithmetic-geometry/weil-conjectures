1. **Induced pattern and size.** `def-coef-categorical-pattern-fibrancy` explicitly defines a small family of cones with every cone edge marked and a small quasicategory as base; `def-cbc-enhanced-diagrams-cones` gives the inner-horn and degreewise conventions. The step fills an inner horn in Y by first filling in C and then through the inner fibration. The induced cone indices form a small union of Hom subsets. These arguments check the actual hypotheses; they do not assume that arbitrary maps into Y are specified cones.

2. **Equivalence-edge calculus.** `lem-coef-infinity-cocartesian-mapping-criterion` states precisely the mapping-square criterion for an inner fibration, not merely a definition of cocartesian fibration. `lem-coef-infinity-mapping-composition` states the associative, unital homotopy composition, the criterion that an invertible component class gives inverse triangles, and dependence of precomposition on the component class. From these statements the step writes out the short inverse argument: an equivalence gives two invertible horizontal maps; cocartesian over an equivalence gives an invertible precomposition map; choose a left inverse at z=x and use injectivity at z=y for the other inverse. Thus this is an elementary consequence of cited theorems, rather than a new node for HTT 2.4.1.5 or an appeal to an uncited theorem.

3. **Fibration implies induced fibered.** I checked all seven generator types in the statement of `lem-coef-pattern-anodyne-generators`. After base composition, T maps into S, every induced cone projects to the original cone, and all triangles are scaled. The cited statement explicitly says these generators are trivial cofibrations and characterizes fibered objects by their lifting property. The model calculus supplies the pattern model, and `lem-coef-model-infinity-derived-adjunction` supplies its weak factorization systems. These statements together fill each square; no assertion that J generates all trivial cofibrations is used.

4. **Induced fibered implies X fibered over C.** The first lift exists because Y lifts every original generator; the second exists because X lifts the induced generators. I checked the target markings for A0, A1, B0 and C0, the unrestricted C1 case, and the all-marked cone for B1. For C2 the distinguished cone is the **last-vertex** cone, and it is sharp in the target of the displayed generator. Consequently the chosen extension to Y supplies precisely an induced specified cone, rather than just an arbitrary lift of its unmarked underlying diagram. The generator-recognition node gives full fiberedness, and the definition really states exact marking plus both the classifying-limit and section-relative-limit clauses. This avoids both a missing relative-limit theorem and a circular invocation of the item.

5. **Underlying isofibration.** The definition supplies f-cocartesian lifts only over T. Every equivalence of Y lies in T because its image in C is an equivalence in S and step 2 makes it pi-cocartesian. A chosen lift is an equivalence by step 2. The definition of isofibration in `lem-coef-infinity-isofibration-exponentiation` is exactly the resulting prescribed-source equivalence lift plus inner lifting. This step uses the hypothesis S contains equivalences at its necessary location; it does not use lifts outside T or surjectivity on vertices.

6. **First extension and marked cylinder.** The model calculus identifies X and Y with fibrant objects and explicitly supplies the full marked pushout-product axiom, trivial when either factor is trivial. The point-model statement makes the sharp boundary inclusion a cofibration. The model weak factorization systems therefore give h' and extend the cylinder into **Y**. I checked its endpoint order, the overlap condition on A, and that all maps lie over the fixed map B to C. This is not a claim that a homotopy is already an exact diagonal.

7. **Inverse witnesses with the boundary fixed.** `lem-coef-pattern-anodyne-lifting-products` explicitly states that restriction to any monomorphism is a Kan fibration on sharp mapping spaces and that these spaces are Kan. Lifting its horns over a constant simplex makes the specified fiber V Kan. `lem-coef-infinity-core-kan-fibration` explicitly says every edge of any Kan complex is an equivalence, with the two triangles interpreted by `def-cbc-enhanced-diagrams-cones`. The inverse witnesses therefore live in V and remain fixed on A. Their images prove that (1_a,G) is an equivalence in the corner target Q. In particular, I do not silently infer equivalence in a strict pullback solely from equivalence of its projections.

8. **Exact homotopy lift.** The exponential-corner node really states an isofibration for *any degreewise injective* A to B and the displayed strict fiber-product target. Step 5 verifies its isofibration hypothesis, and the model's cofibration description gives the monomorphism hypothesis. The equivalence supplied in step 7 begins at the actual image of h'. Its lift has exactly hi=a and fh=g. `lem-cbc-enhanced-functor-cone-calculus` supplies the pointwise criterion for equivalences, so the components H_b are equivalences. This is a generic off-unit tool use recorded only in `split_report.tool_uses`; it replaces no unit proof step.

9. **Marking check and conclusion.** `lem-coef-infinity-cocartesian-composition-initiality` says that in a 2-simplex with first edge cocartesian, the second edge is cocartesian iff the diagonal is. Applied to the two actual triangles of e times Delta1, this first makes the common diagonal cocartesian and then makes h(e) cocartesian. Step 2 supplies that the components H_b are cocartesian; step 4 supplies the exact marking of X. The base image of h(e) is in S because g and pi preserve marked edges. This checks all marked edges of B and finishes with the model's lifting characterization. No unsupported claim that markings transport under an arbitrary unmarked natural equivalence is used.
