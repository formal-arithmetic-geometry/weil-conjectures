## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported conditional statement is false for arbitrary algebraic P/K. The last paragraph of Stacks 09II, proposition-epp-essentially-finite-type, uses finite generation of a residue field extension and equality of composita to conclude eventual separability. That inference is false without additional control of the normalizations. This does not refute Epp's conclusion that SOME finite extension solves A -> B; the defect is in this extracted conditional and in the proposed descent argument for an arbitrarily chosen algebraic solution.

Here is an explicit example. Fix a prime p, let k=F_p(c_0,c_1,...) with the c_i algebraically independent, and set A=k[[t]], K=k((t)), u=sum_{j>=0} c_j t^{pj}. Let alpha be the unique pth root of u in k^perf[[t]], L=K(alpha), and let B be the integral closure of A in L. Since A is complete and excellent, B is a finite A-algebra and a DVR; A -> B is injective and local, so in particular essentially finite type. Let P be the union of E((t)) over finite purely inseparable constant extensions k subset E subset k^perf. It is an algebraic extension of K. Its normalization R_P is the union of E[[t]], a DVR with uniformizer t and residue field k^perf. The reduced tensor (L tensor_K P)_red is the field P(alpha): alpha is not in P, since its power-series coefficients c_j^(1/p) do not all lie in any finite E/k. Its normalization C_P is the union of the DVR normalizations of E[[t]] in E((t))(alpha). All these rings embed in k^perf[[t]], contain t and have value group Z. Their union is a valuation ring with uniformizer t, hence a Noetherian DVR; its residue field is k^perf. Thus R_P -> C_P has ramification index one and IDENTICAL residue fields, satisfying every original limit hypothesis, including finite generation of the residue extension.

For any finite constant extension E/k, there is a least j such that c_j^(1/p) is not in E. Subtract the finite initial series from alpha and divide by t^j. This is a unit integral over E[[t]], and its residue is c_j^(1/p). Therefore the normalized local extension above E((t)) has a nontrivial purely inseparable residue extension (indeed degree p), so is not a solution. Every finite K_0/K inside P is contained in some E((t)). If K_0 were a solution, Stacks 09EQ (the existing lem-sfb-dvr-index-one-separable-base-change) would make E((t)) a solution, contradicting this calculation. This verifies the counterexample for ALL finite subextensions, not just the constant ones.

The correction adds the explicit hypothesis that C_P is finite as a module over B tensor_A R_P, keeping every original conclusion. This is the finite-normalization condition needed to descend a finite algebra rather than chase an unbounded supply of residue generators. In the counterexample C_P is NOT finite over B tensor_A R_P: otherwise B finite over A would make C_P finite over R_P, and Nakayama applied to the common residue field and uniformizer would force C_P=R_P, contrary to alpha not in P.

## Argument for the new normalization-model lemma

Read algebra.tex 05N9 (lemma-colimit-category-fp-algebras), including the existence, maps and equality clauses, and 033G (lemma-descent-normal). Also read more-algebra.tex 09IH and 09EM for the normalized rings. The finite-module assumption makes C_P a finite, finitely presented algebra over T=B tensor_A R_P: T is a localization of a finite type algebra over the Noetherian ring R_P. All structure constants, integrality equations and relations descend to T_F=B tensor_A R_F at a finite stage, as does its generic-fibre identification with (L tensor_K P)_red after enlarging F to kill the finitely many nilpotence witnesses in a finite generic presentation. R_P is faithfully flat over each R_F: R_F is semilocal Dedekind, R_P is torsion-free, and integrality gives surjectivity on spectra. Hence the descended finite algebra D_F is normal by 033G. The generic identification and faithful flatness imply D_F injects into (L tensor_K F)_red with exactly that generic fibre. As it is finite integral over T_F, it is integral over B; conversely any element of that generic fibre integral over B is integral over the normal ring D_F, hence belongs to it. Thus D_F=C_F. Base change at later stages retains the same model and the same reasoning. This is a real finite-algebra descent input, without ramification or separability assumptions; it is strictly smaller than the item and does not restate a clause of it.

## Argument for the new field lemma

Read algebra.tex 030U (lemma-separable-extension-preserves-reducedness), 030W (lemma-characterize-separable-field-extensions), and 030X. For any extension H/k choose a common field Omega containing H and k'. If F tensor_k k' is separable over k', its tensor with Omega is reduced by 030U (using finitely generated separably generated subfields). The map F tensor_k H -> F tensor_k Omega is injective because field extension is faithfully flat, so F tensor_k H is reduced. In positive characteristic use 030W; in characteristic zero all extensions are separable in this sense. No finite generation or algebraicity of F/k was smuggled in. The Mathlib class Algebra.IsSeparable implies algebraicity, as explicitly checked in FieldTheory/Separable.lean:549-572, so it is not a match for this node.
