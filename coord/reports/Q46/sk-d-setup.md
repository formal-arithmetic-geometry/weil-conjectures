## Source error

`STATEMENT-DISPUTED:` main-20261015 `sec-trace-formula/sk-d-setup/_section.md`, order 2610, requires stability of the concretely defined bounded constructible finite-Tor category under pullback for every morphism and compact-support pushforward for every compactifiable morphism of arbitrary schemes. The compact-support part is false for the retrocompact constructibility notion required by `def-weil-finite-torsion-constructibility`, through `def-weil-scheme-morphism-properties` and `def-weil-constructible-subset` (Stacks Tag 005G, `topology-definition-constructible`). Declaring it fixed data cannot repair an inconsistent requirement.

Take $k=\overline{\mathbf F}_p$, $R=k[x_1,x_2,\ldots]$, $\mathfrak m=(x_1,x_2,\ldots)$, $Y=\operatorname{Spec}R$, $A=\mathbf Z/\ell$ with $\ell\ne p$, and $i:\operatorname{Spec}(R/\mathfrak m)\hookrightarrow Y$. This closed immersion is proper and finite type even though its ideal is not finitely generated. Thus it is compactifiable with $j=\mathrm{id}$ and $\bar f=i$. The constant $A$ on its one-point source is bounded constructible and has Tor amplitude $[0,0]$. However $H^0(Ri_!A)=i_*A$ is not constructible.

Here is the stratum obstruction explicitly. A constructible subset of the affine scheme $Y$ is a finite Boolean combination of principal opens, hence depends on finitely many polynomials. Choose $N$ containing the variables in all those polynomials. The primes $\mathfrak m$ and $\mathfrak p_N=(x_1,\ldots,x_N)$ have identical membership in those principal opens: a polynomial in those variables is in either prime exactly when its constant term is zero. Consequently every constructible stratum $S$ containing $\mathfrak m$ contains a distinct generization $\mathfrak p_N$. The sheaf $(i_*A)|_S$ has nonzero stalk at $\mathfrak m$ and zero stalk at $\mathfrak p_N$. If it were locally constant, a weakly étale cover trivializing it would have a member, with a point $u$ over $\mathfrak m$, on which the constant stalk module is nonzero. All points of that member would then map into $\{\mathfrak m\}$. The member is flat over $S$, so going-down lifts the generization $\mathfrak p_N$ to a generization of $u$, a contradiction. This proves the claimed failure, rather than merely assuming that constructibility of the support is automatic.

The correction required by the return is to impose the fixed triangulated/replete and preservation requirements only on geometric schemes (separated finite-type $k$-schemes) and $k$-maps. This is the scope of the inspected `GeometricCoefficients` fields and of `thm-sf-ctf-stability` (i), (iv). The latter node is a scope cross-check, not a new dependency proving an axiom of this definition. $D(X,A)$ and the full subcategory defined by bounded constructibility and the general finite-Tor predicate remain meaningful for arbitrary schemes. No preservation assertion is made for a non-qcqs scheme or an arbitrary morphism of schemes, so the returned pullback-scope issue is removed without asserting an unverified general pullback theorem.

There is a further scope correction against the **current** dependency statement: `def-compact-support-direct-image-detailed` defines a chosen-compactification composite on the ambient derived category, but restricts compactification independence to its bounded-below étale-origin subcategory. The retained sandbox supplied identifications with every compactification on the whole unbounded category. I replaced that stronger requirement by choosing a compactification for each compactifiable geometric map and taking the actual composite $R\bar f_*j_!$. Independence is mentioned only on the dependency's stated domain. This keeps the preservation requirement tied to a concrete operation and avoids smuggling in unrestricted independence.
