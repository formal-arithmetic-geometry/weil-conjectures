2. **Integral construction.** `def-weil-completed-integral-coefficients` explicitly says its ambient category is modules over the completed sheaf, not the discrete constant ring. `def-weil-derived-complete-integral-detailed` defines the full subcategory by complete objects and bounded constructible reductions with a uniform interval. `def-weil-integral-derived-reduction` gives the actual change-of-rings object over each quotient; this is the correct type for the finite formulas. `def-pur-integral-derived-operations` states the strict towers from reductions of K-flat representatives and the cone construction. `lem-pur-integral-operations-well-defined` states that these constructions define functors and are independent of resolutions. `def-weil-derived-limit` supplies only the triangle property and is used only for that property. `lem-pur-integral-operations-preserve-constructible` states both preservation assertions appearing in this item for the same general morphism of schemes; I did not silently add qcqs hypotheses or infer preservation from a definition. 3. **Rational construction.** `def-weil-rationalization-karoubi` explicitly gives the same integral objects and Hom tensor E, with no Karoubi completion. The scalar formulas now multiply both input scalars, and the first Hom variable is contravariant. The elementary balancing check is as follows: replacing $(r v)\otimes c$ by $v\otimes rc$ replaces the output $(rF(v,w))\otimes cd$ by $F(v,w)\otimes rcd$, which is the same element; the second variable is identical. Finite sums extend by additivity. Identity and composition follow from those of the integral functors and multiplication in E. No new lemma is needed for this two-line scalar calculation.

4. **Common-stage construction.** `def-weil-algebraically-closed-coefficients` explicitly states that two representative maps are equal when they agree at a larger common field. `def-weil-finite-subextension-directed-set` supplies such fields via the compositum. `lem-pur-integral-operations-scalar-extension` gives all three comparison isomorphisms, naturality in all variables, identity for L=E, and compatibility for towers E⊂L⊂L'. Those are precisely the clauses needed to take the class of the operation over a common field: moving to L' commutes with the operation, and the middle comparison/inverse cancel when composing. I did not replace this by an unsupported “transport”. For the two-variable operations I specified that both arrows are first represented over one field.
