### Original Step 1 → lem-cbc-ringed-open-scalar-limit-coherence

For any map of coefficient ring sheaves $R\to S$ on the ambient site, restriction of scalars commutes with open extension. Indeed the presheaf defining $j_!$ has the same summands and restriction maps on both sides, and the $R$-action is the $S$-action composed with $R\to S$. The unique extended action on its abelian sheafification is therefore the same. This gives the identity on underlying abelian sheaves, naturally in the module. It commutes with differentials and every transition, hence gives $\operatorname{Res}(j_!K_\bullet)\cong J_j\operatorname{Res}(K_\bullet)$ on strict tower complexes and their derived categories. The adjunction unit sends a section on the slice into the summand indexed by its structure map; this is the same map after scalar restriction. The counit evaluates that summand by the given slice map, again unchanged. Thus the comparison preserves the normalized open adjunction, not just its functors.
