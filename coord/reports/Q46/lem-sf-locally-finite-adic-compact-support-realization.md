## Review by Q46

I checked the statement and each cited statement, rather than borrowing constructions from their proofs. My starting source was Bhatt–Scholze Remark 6.8.15: the constructible operations restrict actual ambient module-derived operations; Definition 6.7.6 specifies the proper/open composite. The native locally-finite coefficient sheaf is not simply the all-continuous norm-topological algebraic-closure sheaf. I read its definition and the finite-dimensional-image lemmas in `lean/WeilConjectures/SixFunctors/ProEtale/Adic/ConstantSheaf.lean`, and its internal-module convention in `Adic/Module.lean`, to check the coefficient issue. This prompted the explicit local finite-field argument in step 2. I claim no equality of sections with a single finite field on non-quasi-compact objects.

Step 1 uses the compactification definition only for its proper/open factorization. `lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian` gives Noetherianity over the field; `lem-kun-dimension-finite-type` gives finite dimension; `lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-open-subscheme-noetherian` gives quasi-compactness of the open; and `lem-weil-pbc-closed-subset-closed-subscheme` provides its closed complement. Proper finite type makes the intermediate scheme finite type over the same field. None of these needs absolute separatedness. This is why I can apply the non-separated forms below.

Step 2 uses exactly the coefficient-sheaf identification in `lem-per-continuous-rational-derived-realization` (its Noetherian finite-dimensional hypotheses were checked in step 1), the three coefficient colimit identities in `sk-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-x`, and the internal convention in `def-sf-native-weil-derived-framework`. The extra local calculation is elementary: a finite-dimensional span is contained in the field generated by a finite basis of algebraic elements; conversely a finite field is finite dimensional. The module-action check is also explicit: restriction along constant scalars recovers the given vector-space action. I did not create a node for these few lines.

Step 3 cites three comparison statements that actually cover the presentation change: `lem-sfb-internal-section-scalar-adjunction` states derived scalar extension/restriction, generators, units and counits; `lem-pd-section-derived-pushpull-normalization` states the K-injective and ordinary derived-direct-image comparison; `lem-sfb-internal-module-operations-transport` states the open-extension comparison.
