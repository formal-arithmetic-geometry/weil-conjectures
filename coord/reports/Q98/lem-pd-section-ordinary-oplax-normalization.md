The comparisons are exactly those fixed in [def-pd-section-module-operations-data]. Thus $L(A)=S\otimes_{R'}f^{-1}A$ and $P$ is geometric direct image with scalar restriction along $c:R_Y\to f_*S$. The internal adjunction is the composite of the transported abelian adjunction, whose unit is the geometric insertion, with extension and restriction of scalars [def-sfb-module-upper-lower-adjunction]. The extension adjunction [def-sfb-modobj-extend-restrict-adjunction] says its unit is insertion of $1$; consequently the module unit $u_A:A\to PLA$ is geometric insertion followed by $a\mapsto1\otimes a$. No alternative choice of adjunction is being made.

### Original Step 2

Identify the actual direct-image pairing before taking mates. The abelian direct image is restriction along $V\mapsto V\times_YX$. The generator equation [lem-sfb-sheaf-pushforward-generator-comparison] identifies its actual lax tensor map with the restriction of the sheaf pure-tensor pairing. In [def-sfb-module-lower-star], scalar restriction changes only the action. Compose this ambient pairing with the balancing projection defining relative tensor [def-sfb-modobj-relative-tensor]. For $a,b$ local sections and $r\in R_Y$, the images of $(ra)\otimes b$ and $a\otimes(rb)$ agree: both equal $c(r)(a\otimes b)$. Hence the pairing factors through the $R_Y$-balanced tensor, sending $a\otimes b$ to $a\otimes b$. Its unit sends $r$ to $c(r)$. The pure-tensor identifications of Step 1 therefore identify this internal pairing with the unique section-module lax structure of [lem-pd-ringed-direct-image-pairing-lax]. These statements concern maps out of the sheafified tensor, not preservation of tensor products by evaluation.
