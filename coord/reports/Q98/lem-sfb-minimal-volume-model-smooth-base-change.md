## Statement dispute

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the final clause allowed removal of any closed subset merely nowhere dense in the special fibre. This need not preserve the entire generic fibre, which is required by the statement's definition of a model. Take an elliptic curve E over an algebraically closed field k, R=k[[pi]], and its constant smooth proper model E×Spec(R). It has good reduction and is a minimal-volume model for a nowhere vanishing invariant differential of order zero. Removing its identity section removes a single closed point from the irreducible special curve, hence a nowhere dense subset of that fibre, but also removes the identity point from the generic elliptic curve. The resulting generic fibre is punctured and is not the specified G. Thus the claimed resulting model is not even an object of the stated class. More generally every horizontal closed subset has this problem.

The corrected clause requires the removed closed subset to be **contained in** the special fibre and nowhere dense there. This is the shrinking of special fibres used in the first paragraph of BLR 4.3 Proposition 4, immediately following Lemma 3. It preserves the full G and all special generic local rings. No other hypothesis or conclusion has changed. The main classification and smooth-trait comparison remain exactly Proposition 2 and Lemma 3.
