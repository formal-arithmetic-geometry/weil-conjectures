Original step 2; owner: lem-sfb-product-interval-nullhomotopy.

Suppose $m_j\geq2$ and put $e=(0,\ldots,1,\ldots,0)$ with $1$ in coordinate $j$. This is a vertex of $T_m$. Define $u:T_m\to T_m$ by $u(v)_j=\max(v_j,1)$ and $u(v)_i=v_i$ for $i\ne j$. This is monotone and nonzero. If $u(v)=m$, then $\max(v_j,1)=m_j\geq2$ forces $v_j=m_j$; all other coordinates are also those of $m$, contradicting $v\ne m$. Thus $u$ stays in $T_m$. Pointwise $\mathrm{id}\leq u$ and $c_e\leq u$, where $c_e$ is the constant map at $e$. By [lem-sfb-poset-augmented-chain-prism], their induced maps on cycles differ by boundaries. Consequently $H_k(\mathrm{id})=H_k(u)=H_k(c_e)=0$, the last equality following from factorization through $\{e\}$ as in step 1. Hence all $H_k(A(m))$ vanish.
