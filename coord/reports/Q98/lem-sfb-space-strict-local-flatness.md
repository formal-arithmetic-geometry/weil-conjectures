The first equality is proved in the item, rather than outsourced to a child that restates it. The flatness proof keeps both scalars distinct. With A=O_(V,v) and B=O_(U,u), first use faithful flatness of B to Bsh to compare A-flatness of B and Bsh. The argument works for every compatible chart and its chosen point, so the existential chart convention is independent of choices.
