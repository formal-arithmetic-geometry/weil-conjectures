For the new auxiliary, the object called Pic is explicitly the group of locally free rank-one module classes. This removes any ambiguity about a more general meaning of tensor-invertible module on a ringed site. The generator-torsor proof of 040E works for that definition on any commutative ringed site: locally each object is the trivial line, so generators are a units torsor; conversely glue the free line using the units torsor. Tensor of generators gives the group law. Under a ringed-topos morphism, a trivialization pulls to a trivialization because B tensor over f^-1 A with f^-1 A is B. If two local generators differ by a unit a, their pulled-back generators differ by rho(a); the contracted torsor is therefore the generator torsor of the pulled-back line. The naturality of the class in H1 follows by the extension/pushout construction in 03AJ. This is a general Picard/cohomology tool with no square-zero ideal or obstruction map, not a relabelled clause of the item.
