For step 1 I opened `lem-weil-pbc-smooth-section-etale-lift.json`. Its statement explicitly gives the etale base extension, the *structural* isomorphism A/J to A'/JA', and the equation q tilde-sigma = alpha sigma. The proof uses all and only these conclusions. The composite pi_N w is an R-algebra map because coordinate projections and w respect the R-algebra structure. Taking J=m^N is permitted for every N>=1. In particular I do not use a henselian lifting theorem for the pair (R,m^N).

For step 2 I opened `def-weil-pbc-localization-at-prime.json`: it explicitly states that localization at a prime is local with maximal ideal extended from that prime, and defines its residue field as that quotient. The only other argument is elementary: alpha is structural and invertible, so it carries the ideal generated by m modulo m^N to the corresponding ideal on E/m^NE; quotienting yields R/m = E/mE via the structural map. This proves maximality and contraction, rather than assuming mE is prime. Fractions modulo q have nonzero denominators in the field E/q, so T/n and E/q are inverse-isomorphic by constant fractions and field inversion. This proves the actual structural residue-field assertion and locality of R to T.

For step 3 I opened `def-weil-pbc-adic-completion.json`. It defines the rings as compatible sequences and operations coordinatewise. Locality sends m^r into n^r, so lambda_r is a well-defined map on each quotient; reduction commutes on representatives. These elementary facts build the specified completed map, not an unspecified isomorphism of completions. The map beta is well-defined because n=mT implies n^N=m^NT. Testing beta alpha on a representative of R/m^N gives lambda_N exactly; testing beta q_N on e gives the class of e/1 exactly. These two computations turn the step-1 equality into equality of the actual Nth coordinates. No claimed identification is merely transported without its formula.

For step 4 I opened `def-weil-pbc-noetherian-ring.json` and `lem-weil-pbc-adic-completion-mod-power.json`. The former says every ideal is finitely generated. Images of generators of m generate n=mT. The latter explicitly identifies the kernel of the coordinate projection with I^N A-hat, for any finitely generated ideal. Substituting A=T and I=n proves the desired ideal membership from step 3. I deliberately use this stronger finite-generation formulation, so no theorem that T is Noetherian, etale flatness, or invariance of completions is silently needed.
