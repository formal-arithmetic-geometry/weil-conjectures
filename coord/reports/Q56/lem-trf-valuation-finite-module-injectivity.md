### Returned step 1

By [def-weil-pbc-essentially-finite-type-algebra] there are a finite type $A$-algebra $C$, a multiplicative subset $W\subseteq C$ and an isomorphism of $A$-algebras $B\cong W^{-1}C$, and by [def-weil-pbc-finite-type-algebra] a surjection of $A$-algebras $P=A[x_1,\ldots,x_n]\twoheadrightarrow C$. Let $\rho:P\to C\to W^{-1}C\cong B$ be the composite and $\mathfrak q=\rho^{-1}(\mathfrak m_B)$, a prime of $P$. Every $p\in P\setminus\mathfrak q$ maps outside $\mathfrak m_B$, hence to a unit of the local ring $B$, so $\rho$ factors through an $A$-algebra map $\pi:P_{\mathfrak q}\to B$. It is surjective: an element of $B$ is $c/w$ with $c=\bar p$, $w=\bar s$ for some $p,s\in P$; since $w/1$ is a unit of $W^{-1}C$, $\rho(s)\notin\mathfrak m_B$, i.e. $s\notin\mathfrak q$, and $c/w=\pi(p/s)$. Since $\pi$ is a surjection of local rings, $\pi^{-1}(\mathfrak m_B)$ is a proper ideal with residue field $B/\mathfrak m_B$, hence the maximal ideal of $P_{\mathfrak q}$; as $A\to B$ is local, the composite $A\to P_{\mathfrak q}\to B$ shows that $A\to P_{\mathfrak q}$ is local. The map $A\to P_{\mathfrak q}$ is essentially of finite presentation (a localization of a finitely presented $A$-algebra) because $P$ is a finitely presented $A$-algebra [def-weil-pbc-finitely-presented-algebra] (presentation with $m=0$) and $P_{\mathfrak q}$ is its localization at $P\setminus\mathfrak q$. Regard $N$, $M$, and $u$ as finite $P_{\mathfrak q}$-modules and a $P_{\mathfrak q}$-linear map through $\pi$; the hypotheses (finiteness, flatness of $M$ over $A$, injectivity of the closed-fibre map, which only involves $\mathfrak m_A$) and the conclusions are unchanged because the underlying $A$-modules and the map are unchanged, so it suffices to prove the result with $B$ replaced by $P_{\mathfrak q}$.
