## Argument behind the new statement-only node

This argument is recorded here, not as a proof in the unsplit helper. Let Spec B be an affine neighbourhood of the centre x, and identify B with a subring of R inside K. Write all the specified h_i as a_i/b with a common nonzero b in B. Let I be the radical ideal defining Z on this affine chart. It is finitely generated, nonzero, and contained in the centre prime. Every nonzero generator has positive integer valuation. Choose N with N times the least such valuation at least the valuation of b. For J=(b,a_1,...,a_r)+I^N, every generator has valuation at least that of b, and J is the unit ideal away from Z. On the b-chart of the blowup, B[J/b] is a subring of R; it contains every h_i. Thus Spec R lifts to this chart. Extend this admissible blowup from Spec B to X using Stacks 080M. All opens involved are quasi-compact because X is Noetherian, and X is quasi-separated. The global centre is disjoint from U, so its blowup is identical on U and birational. The Rees algebra embeds in B[t] on each affine chart, giving integral blowup charts and an integral global model. The blowup is proper by 02NS. The local lift extends to the entire valuation spectrum because an open containing its closed point is the entire spectrum. This proves exactly the helper's assertion. For an empty list take the identity model.
