## Review by Q56

This is my own mathematical self-review for direct delivery, not an independent second reading. I checked delivery-errors B--E with the sources and cited statements open.

Step 1 uses the new total-site definition, the definition of module sheaves (including its explicit kernel/cokernel description), the definition of the small etale site, and `lem-weil-pbc-etale-base-change-continuous`. The latter really says etale coverings remain coverings after arbitrary scheme base change. The displayed covering pullback is therefore legal. Sheaf compatibility is tested inside each degree, so sheafification of a cokernel commutes with restriction. This proves both exactness and detection without treating an arbitrary functor category as the total topos.

Step 2 uses the actual component geometric morphisms supplied by `lem-weil-pbc-sets-etale-inverse-image-exact`. I added this explicit citation during review: exactness for a geometric morphism would otherwise assume, without citing it, that scheme maps induce those morphisms. `lem-pe-constant-left-module-inverse-image` has arbitrary unital ring coefficients and explicitly states exactness, canonical constant-ring identification, unit compatibility and composition compatibility. Its hypothesis is met for every component and transition map. The total pushforward formula is proved through the displayed counit-defined transition and component adjunction, rather than borrowed from a definition node. Step 3 proves the augmentation adjunction by the equalizer and the vertex/edge argument. Degree-one compatibility forces agreement of every pair of vertex continuations; every order map preserves the continuations. Thus it does not assume descent of the coefficient system or invertibility of any transition.

Step 4 constructs the left adjoint to evaluation, denoted ell_n to distinguish it from the component of the input complex L. The summands are indexed by all order maps, including degenerate ones. There are finitely many at each fixed pair of degrees, so the exactness argument uses finite biproduct identities, not exactness of an infinite product. The cited injective-preservation lemma states precisely the right-adjoint-of-monomorphism-preserving-left-adjoint criterion. Its application to ell_n -| e_n is valid. The constant-coefficient exact-inverse-image lemma supplies each exact summand functor.

Step 5 cites `prop-pur-kinjective-resolutions-exist`, which is a theorem for any small ringed site with commutative ring sheaf, not just for schemes. Thus it applies to the constructed total site. The enough-injectives theorem and the bounded-below injective representative lemma give representatives zero below the actual cohomological bound c, not merely some unspecified lower bound. Component injectivity and component exactness were proved in steps 1 and 4. Step 6 applies those facts to the literal degreewise pushforward identity, and cites the K-injective comparison/computation statement for resolution independence. No unsupported general component base-change theorem is substituted.

Step 7 uses the generic right-derived-functor triangulated theorem and the existing `lem-sfb-derived-composition-adjunction-normalization`. I read the full latter statement: it assumes exact additive left adjoints on Grothendieck categories, says that the right adjoint of a K-injective stays K-injective, gives the representative of the composition isomorphism, and identifies it as the conjugate under the specified adjunctions. The exactness and Grothendieck hypotheses were proved in steps 2, 3 and 5. This yields the required unit/counit normalization. The naturality argument is written as equality of adjuncts of the two composites, with component inverse-image composition as the common adjunct; it does not assert an additional base-change isomorphism.

Steps 8 and 9 spell out the free simplicial augmentation complex.
