## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported statement asserted that any local intermediate diagram which is an isomorphism on the isomorphism locus of f automatically glues with the unchanged Y elsewhere. That condition does not identify its overlap with the unchanged source.

Here is an explicit obstruction within the allowed nonseparated setting. Let V have separably closed fraction field and rank two, with primes (0) < p < m. Such a V is obtained by extending the composed rank-two valuation of k((u))((v)) to its algebraic closure. Choose nonzero a in p and b in m minus p. In A1_V with coordinate t, put z=(p,t) and x=(m,t). The normalized blow-up of (a,t) is a normal proper eta-modification with smooth generic fibre; its two usual charts are the affine chart t=aT and the nodal chart tv=a. Their fibres are reduced, and the map is nontrivial above z as well as x. Its modification locus is the two-point section V(a,t).

Glue two copies of A1_V along the complement of x, producing distinct closed points x1,x2 but a common generalization z. The overlap is quasi-compact: its underlying open is D(b) union D(t). Glue the blow-ups by their common restriction. The resulting X and Y are normal finitely presented T-curves and Y -> X is a proper eta-modification. On the first affine chart choose the intermediate model to be that chart itself, so h_U=f_U and k_U=id. This diagram is an isomorphism wherever f is. But h_U contracts over z, whereas an h glued with the unchanged Y near x2 must be an isomorphism over z, since every neighbourhood of x2 contains z. The two restrictions cannot be identified on the overlap. Thus the blanket gluing claim is false, not merely unproved.

The repair states exactly the gluing datum: a closed support W contained in the retained open U, quasi-compact gluing opens, and h_U an isomorphism on U minus W. Then Z_U glues with Y outside W using that specified isomorphism. If W lies over generalizations of x, so does the support. This is the precise form of the observation following Lemma 4.2.1: a modification is determined by a neighbourhood of its entire modification locus. I do not attribute the stronger imported blanket assertion to Temkin.

The marked compactification clause now explicitly assumes an etale generic marking. This is the source's semistable-generic marking situation and is the hypothesis actually present in the citing exceptional-blow-down application. Over separably closed K these markings are rational points, so their closures in proper models are supplied by the valuative criterion. I do not assume that a separably closed field is perfect, nor that normalization makes an arbitrary generic boundary smooth.

The sole current citer is lem-trf-valuation-exceptional-blowdown. Its normalization and projective-preparation uses still hold. Its step 4 uses blanket gluing and infers that other closed-image components are outside the support; that inference must be repaired by proving the required overlap agreement. It is recorded holds:false in statement_change, and its file is untouched. Its generic marking is already etale, so the marking qualification does not introduce a further failure there.
