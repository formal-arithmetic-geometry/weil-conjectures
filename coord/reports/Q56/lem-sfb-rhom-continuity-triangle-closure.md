### Original step 1

Put $\mathcal M=\operatorname{Mod}(\mathcal O)$. Its small filtered colimits are exact by [lem-pur-ringed-site-grothendieck-abelian]. For a filtered complex diagram $K_i$, apply this exactness to $0\to Z_i^q\to K_i^q\to B_i^{q+1}\to0$ and $0\to B_i^q\to Z_i^q\to H^q(K_i)\to0$. The induced differential on $\varinjlim K_i$ has kernel $\varinjlim Z_i^q$ and image $\varinjlim B_i^{q+1}$, so $H^q(\varinjlim K_i)\cong\varinjlim H^q(K_i)$, naturally in the diagram. Thus termwise filtered colimits preserve quasi-isomorphisms and any common cohomological lower bound. In particular the prescribed complex models give well-defined comparisons in the derived category [def-weil-abstract-derived-category].

### Original step 2

Use the fixed functorial K-injective target models of [def-pur-derived-internal-hom]. The formula for the Hom complex gives $H(0,N)=0$: every component is a product of zero module sheaves. Hence its comparison is $0\to0$ and $d_0=0$ works. An isomorphism $A\cong A'$ induces natural isomorphisms $H(A',N)\cong H(A,N)$, compatible with each target cocone; taking their colimits identifies the two comparison maps by the colimit universal property [def-weil-pbc-category-colimit]. The same bound works for both sources.

### Original step 3

The source shift isomorphism of [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-internal-hom-exact-restriction] gives $H(A[n],N)\cong H(A,N)[-n]$, naturally in $N$. Termwise colimits commute with shifts: in degree $q$ both shifted-colimit complexes have $\varinjlim_i K_i^{q-n}$ and differential $(-1)^n\varinjlim_i d_i$. Naturality of the shift isomorphism identifies the comparison for $A[n]$ with the shift by $[-n]$ of that for $A$. Consequently continuity holds for $A[n]$ whenever it holds for $A$. Since $H^q(K[-n])=H^{q-n}(K)$ by the cochain convention [def-weil-abstract-derived-category], the lower bound $c+d_A$ becomes $c+d_A+n$ [def-sbc-standard-tstructure-derived-category]. This proves $P(A[n])$ with $d_{A[n]}=d_A+n$, for every integer $n$.

### Original step 4

Let $A\xrightarrow{u}B\xrightarrow{v}C\xrightarrow{w}A[1]$ be distinguished and assume $P(A),P(C)$. Fix a small filtered target diagram $N_i$ with common lower bound $c$ and put $N_\infty=\varinjlim_iN_i$. By [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-internal-hom-exact-restriction], each target $N$ gives the distinguished triangle $H(C,N)\to H(B,N)\to H(A,N)\to H(C,N)[1]$. The last arrow is induced by precomposition with $w$, using the signed source shift identification; all these arrows are natural in $N$. Apply [lem-sf-derived-homology-long-exact] at $N_i$ and at $N_\infty$. The cocone arrows $N_i\to N_\infty$ give commuting squares for precomposition with $u$ and $v$ by bifunctoriality. They also give a commuting square for precomposition with $w$; the shift identifications and their fixed signs are natural in $N$, so this is exactly the square for the last triangle arrow. Applying cohomology and then the colimit universal property gives a commuting comparison of the two long exact rows, including every connecting map. The vertical maps are $H^q(\alpha_X):\varinjlim_iH^q(H(X,N_i))\to H^q(H(X,N_\infty))$ under step 1, where $\alpha_X$ is the canonical comparison of the statement [def-pur-derived-internal-hom, def-weil-pbc-category-colimit].

### Original step 5

For every integer $q$, use the consecutive five terms $H^{q-1}(H(A,N))\to H^q(H(C,N))\to H^q(H(B,N))\to H^q(H(A,N))\to H^{q+1}(H(C,N))$ of those rows. Filtered colimits of the first row are exact by [lem-pur-ringed-site-grothendieck-abelian], and the second row is exact by [lem-sf-derived-homology-long-exact]. Its four outer vertical arrows are isomorphisms by $P(A),P(C)$ and step 1. The five lemma [lem-weil-pbc-five-lemma] makes the middle vertical arrow an isomorphism. This holds for every $q$, so the actual comparison chain map on the prescribed functorial complex models is a quasi-isomorphism, and therefore its image $\alpha_B$ in $D(\mathcal O)$ is an isomorphism [def-weil-abstract-derived-category]. This proves the continuity clause for $B$.

### Original step 6

For any $N\in D^{\ge c}$, exactness at the middle term of $H^q(H(C,N))\to H^q(H(B,N))\to H^q(H(A,N))$ [lem-sf-derived-homology-long-exact, lem-weil-pbc-ringed-internal-hom-exact-restriction] shows that $H^q(H(B,N))=0$ whenever both outer terms vanish. They vanish for every $q<c+\min(d_A,d_C)$. Thus $d_B=\min(d_A,d_C)$ gives the required bound, independent of $c,N$ and of any diagram [def-sbc-standard-tstructure-derived-category]. Together with step 5 this proves middle-vertex closure of the full property $P$, not just its continuity clause.

### Original step 7

Rotate the original triangle using [def-weil-triangulated-category]. If $P(B),P(C)$ hold, its inverse rotation is $C[-1]\to A\to B\to C$, and steps 3, 5 and 6 give $P(A)$, with $d_A=\min(d_C-1,d_B)$. If $P(A),P(B)$ hold, its rotation is $B\to C\to A[1]\to B[1]$, giving $P(C)$ with $d_C=\min(d_B,d_A+1)$. The rotation signs do not affect either the isomorphism or vanishing assertions. This proves two-out-of-three in every position.

### Original step 8

Induct on the length of a finite construction. Its initial sources have $P$ by assumption (and the zero source does by step 2). At the next operation use step 2 for an isomorphism, step 3 for an integer shift, or steps 5--7 for obtaining a vertex from two vertices of a distinguished triangle. Every operation preserves $P$, which proves the final assertion.
