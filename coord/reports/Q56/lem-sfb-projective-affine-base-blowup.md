### Returned Step 2

Form the quasi-coherent graded Rees algebra $\mathcal R=\bigoplus_{n\geq0}\mathcal I^n$ of [def-nagata-sheaf-rees-algebra]. For an affine open $V=\operatorname{Spec}R\subseteq X$ put $I_V=\Gamma(V,\mathcal I)$. By [def-sbc-ideal-sheaf-powers], $\mathcal I^n|_V=\widetilde{I_V^n}$. Clauses (2) and (4) of [lem-weil-pbc-affine-tilde-gamma-adjunction] identify the sheaf direct sum on $V$ with $\widetilde{\bigoplus_n I_V^n}$ and its sections with $\bigoplus_n I_V^n$. The products agree on homogeneous summands, so $\Gamma(V,\mathcal R)=\mathcal R_R(I_V)$ as graded $R$-algebras, with degree-zero identity, by [def-nagata-rees-algebra]. This also identifies restriction maps: restricting a homogeneous section in degree $n$ restricts its coefficient in $\mathcal I^n$.

Cited nodes: def-nagata-sheaf-rees-algebra, def-sbc-ideal-sheaf-powers, lem-weil-pbc-affine-tilde-gamma-adjunction, def-nagata-rees-algebra

### Returned Step 3

Apply clause (a) of [lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-symmetric-algebra] to the degree-one inclusion $\mathcal I\to\mathcal R$. It gives an algebra morphism $\varphi:\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal I)\to\mathcal R$. Clause (b) identifies it on each affine $V$ with $\operatorname{Sym}_R(I_V)\to\bigoplus_n I_V^n$, $x_1\cdots x_n\mapsto x_1\cdots x_n$ in degree $n$. In the symmetric grading of [lem-weil-pbc-symmetric-algebra-graded], which is the grading of the canonical affine-basis graded algebra $A_X(\mathcal I)$ of [def-trf-canonical-symmetric-graded-algebra] (the relative-Proj input for $\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal I)$, identified there with the affine sections of clause (b)), this map preserves degree. It is onto in every degree: an element of the ideal power $I_V^n$ is a finite sum of products of $n$ elements of $I_V$ with coefficients in $R$, each the image of the same symmetric monomial; in degree zero the map is the identity of $R$. Hence $\varphi$ is a surjective graded sheaf-algebra morphism. This is a direct calculation, not an extra lemma about the blowup.

Cited nodes: lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-symmetric-algebra, lem-weil-pbc-symmetric-algebra-graded, def-trf-canonical-symmetric-graded-algebra
