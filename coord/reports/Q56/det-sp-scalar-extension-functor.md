At level n its primitive root zeta_n trivializes both L_A,n and L_B,n. Define c^1 on root tensors by z tensor a mapping to z tensor f(a), c^0=f, and c^d by tensoring. The balanced relations prove these are well defined. In the resulting bases they are the coefficient map; extending scalars therefore gives an isomorphism. The inverse on degree-one tensor generators is z tensor b mapping to (z tensor 1) tensor b. Positive powers and the unit comparison are those of `lem-sp-invertible-tensor-scalar-extension`.

For a positive power, the coefficient-extension image of a local basis generates the B-line. Its evaluation pairing is nondegenerate. Consequently the formula c^-d(lambda)(c^d(x))=f(lambda(x)) determines the negative map uniquely. Existence is explicit in a basis: send the coefficient of the dual basis through f. On a change of basis by a unit u, the positive basis changes by u^d and the negative basis by u^-d, so the formula is unchanged. The local maps therefore glue, and their B-linear scalar extensions are isomorphisms. This is the evaluation-normalized generic dual comparison already stated by `lem-sp-invertible-tensor-scalar-extension`; the new work records its uniquely specified signed root family.

For n>=k, the actual Kummer map sends zeta_n to zeta_k. Clause 2 of the Tate-line node gives the positive-line isomorphism. For the coefficient/exponent square both degree-one paths send z tensor a to z^(ell^(n-k)) tensor f(a); degree zero is f on either path. Positive tensor powers retain equality. Evaluate both negative paths on an arbitrary positive section; the two evaluations are the same coefficient image of the original pairing.
