## Source error

The theorem of Tag 08HN remains unchanged; these are errors in its proof, also present in the scheme analogue 08ER. No target or unit statement is changed.

* The claimed formula for the open complement is false. For `W=V=Spec(k)`, `j=id`, `U=W` and `T=W`, the subset `T'=T\U` is empty, so `V\j^{-1}(T')=V`; the printed fibre product of `O=U×_W V` with `W\T` is empty. The correct formula is the union `(V\j^{-1}T) ∪ O`. Both summands are quasi-compact for the hypotheses actually present.
* The printed base case includes equality `t=m-s` and then asserts vanishing in degree `m-s`. For `U=W=V=Spec(k)`, `T=W` and a nonzero complex concentrated in that degree, this vanishing is false. Use an upper bound `h` and the strict base case `h<m-s`.
* Approximation at the fixed degree `m` does not imply surjectivity at a top nonzero degree `t<m`, or ensure the source vanishes above `t`. Instead approximate at the induction bound `h`. The inequality needed for applying A(U) is `h-r_U≥m-r`. With `h≥m-s`, taking `r=r_U+s` proves it; `max(r_U,s)` does not justify it. Enlarge `r_U` to be nonnegative first using monotonicity. This changes only a freely chosen witness in the proof.
* A largest nonzero degree does not exist when `E|_U=0`. Use an arbitrary upper bound and induction on `max(0,h-(m-s)+1)`. This also avoids induction on the integers without a lower bound.
