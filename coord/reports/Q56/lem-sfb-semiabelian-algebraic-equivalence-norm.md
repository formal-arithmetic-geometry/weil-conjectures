## New nodes

The multiplicative-torsor node constructs a commutative group structure on the frame torsor, compatible with the actual scalar action and a chosen identity frame. It asserts neither inversion nor ampleness. Pulling its multiplication along the anti-diagonal is the argument proving inversion in the item. Its stronger structural conclusion is the standard Picard-zero/extension input; it is not a new name for either clause of the item. A useful future proof route is split-torus Picard devissage, the abelian Weil--Barsotti theorem, and descent of the resulting multiplicative object. Those are not asserted to be proved by the present node.

The finite-locally-free node concerns arbitrary scheme morphisms and the supplied invertible module. Its source is Stacks divisors.tex: 0BD2, lemma-finite-locally-free-has-norm; 0BCY, lemma-norm-invertible; and the proof of 0BD0, lemma-norm-ample. For constant positive rank d the norm sends f*L to L^d and sends an ample module to an ample module. Thus L^d is ample, and the affine nonvanishing definition immediately reflects a positive tensor power. Rank pieces are open and closed; if f*L is ample the source and hence target are quasi-compact, so only finitely many nonempty ranks occur. This explains the absence of a constant-rank hypothesis. It is intentionally not split here.

I checked j=0,1,2 and the induction coefficient 2 binom(j,2)-binom(j-1,2)+1=binom(j+1,2).
