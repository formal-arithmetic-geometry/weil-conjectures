The surviving new statement follows 04MK as follows. Lift the given field point to an etale scheme chart after a field extension and take an affine neighbourhood of the lifted point. Take the reduced closure of that point in this affine scheme. It is nonempty, reduced and irreducible, hence connected, and remains locally of finite type over both fields (closed immersion into a scheme locally of finite type over either field). Proposition 04MK therefore identifies the two integral closures in its global ring. Every first-field scalar belongs to that common integral closure, hence satisfies a monic polynomial over the second field. Evaluate at the lifted point, then use injectivity of the extension of the given field to obtain the same polynomial relation in F. Interchange the fields for the symmetric clause. This argument checks the reducedness and connectedness hypotheses on the auxiliary scheme, rather than assuming them for E. Empty E has no field point, E=Spec of a finite common extension satisfies the claim, and infinite transcendental residue extensions cause no issue since only the scalar images are asserted algebraic.
