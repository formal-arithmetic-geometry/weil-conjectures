## Source errors

The first paragraph says that evaluation $M^\vee\otimes^L M\to R$ is an isomorphism. On the point, for $M=R^2$ in degree zero, evaluation is the contraction $R^4\to R$ and is not invertible. A duality pairing only requires the two triangle identities; the relevant invertible map is the canonical tensor–internal-Hom comparison. Steps 2–4 prove that comparison and construct its duality adjunction.

The assertion that equality of derived morphisms may be checked on a cover is also insufficient in this generality: positive-degree extension classes can be locally zero. The split checks only isomorphisms locally, through vanishing cohomology sheaves, and obtains the two duality identities globally from the closed adjunction.

The field-sheaf flatness argument must not assume that an arbitrary sheaf short exact sequence is exact on sections. Step 6 applies the precise presheaf-flatness/sheafification statement of Stacks 03ET instead. It then obtains right exactness from the tensor adjunction.
