For the new universal-immersion input I read 063V rather than extrapolating arbitrary base change from flat base change. Its hypothesis is exactly an H1-regular immersion with flat source over the base and ambient scheme locally of finite presentation. Its conclusion is a regular immersion after every base change, so the recorded Koszul consequence is valid. Ambient flatness is not a hidden hypothesis. The proof of 063V explicitly obtains ambient flatness near the source before using 063U, and does not require a Noetherian base. This is a substantive lemma, not a two-line restatement of one of our cited nodes.

For the new field-extension input I read the Koszul clause and the fpqc descent argument of 0694, and the use of universally open field extension in the proof of 06B8. For pointwise descent, choose a good affine ambient neighbourhood of the specified point after field extension. Its image in the original ambient scheme is open, since a field-extension projection is universally open. Inside that image choose a principal affine neighbourhood of the original point. The inverse image of this principal neighbourhood in the good affine ambient is affine, flat and surjective onto it, hence is an fpqc cover. The pulled-back immersion is Koszul-regular on this cover, so 0694 descends it there. Flat ascent proves the reverse implication. This explains why an etale-only descent node cannot supply the needed statement. Finite type of the ambient scheme over the field is harmless for the intended fibre application; no finite or separable extension hypothesis is needed.
