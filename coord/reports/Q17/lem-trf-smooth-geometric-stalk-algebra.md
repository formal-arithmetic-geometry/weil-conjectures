### Original step 1 → lem-trf-smooth-strict-local-base-model

### Original step 5 → parent assembly step 3, repeated in assembly step 4

For $I\subseteq\mathfrak m_A$, [lem-weil-pbc-flat-module-quotient-flat] applied to the flat $A$-module $B$ makes $B/IB$ flat over $A/I$. Since $A\to B$ is local, $IB\subseteq\mathfrak m_B$. ### Original step 8 → lem-trf-polynomial-strict-local-coordinate-dimension

Let $D=\kappa[t_i:i\in K]\subset P$ and $\mathfrak n=(t_i:i\in K)\subset D$. Since $\mathfrak q$ contains the selected variables, $\mathfrak q\cap D=\mathfrak n$: reduction of any $f\in D$ modulo those variables is its constant in $\kappa$, and a nonzero constant is a unit, so cannot belong to $\mathfrak q$. Both $D$ and $P$ are Noetherian by [lem-weil-pbc-polynomial-ring-field-regular-ring]. The map $D\to P$, adjoining the other variables, is smooth by [lem-weil-pbc-polynomial-ring-smooth] and flat by [lem-weil-pbc-smooth-ring-map-flat]. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-flat-height-formula] at $\mathfrak q$ and $\mathfrak n$:
$$\dim P_{\mathfrak q}=\dim D_{\mathfrak n}+\dim(P_{\mathfrak q}/\mathfrak nP_{\mathfrak q}).$$
The first summand is $k$ by [lem-weil-pbc-polynomial-ring-origin-regular-local]. The second ring is $(P/L)_{\mathfrak q'}$: the two maps send $f/g$ to $(f\bmod L)/(g\bmod L)$ and conversely, and denominators are invertible on both sides. By [lem-sc-strict-henselization-dimension] and the models in step 7, $\dim F=\dim P_{\mathfrak q}$ and $\dim F_K=\dim(P/L)_{\mathfrak q'}$. Hence $\dim F=\dim F_K+k$. The argument includes $K=\varnothing$ (then $D=\kappa$, $\mathfrak n=0$) and $K=\{1,\ldots,r\}$; it never assumes $\mathfrak q$ maximal.
