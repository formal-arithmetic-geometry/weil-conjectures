## Review by Q17

I checked B--E against the actual statements of all 11 distinct nodes cited in the six proof steps, and read the source passages above. Every `citations.json` quote is the exact full statement of the node cited by that step, with one entry per step/node pair. No proof of a cited node is being treated as part of its statement.

Step 1 uses the definition of the multivariable polynomial ring only. I checked the quotient maps in both directions by their values on coefficients and variables. The injectivity argument uses the highest nonzero coefficient in one variable; it works over zero-divisor rings, and does not assume Noetherianity. It is an elementary calculation, so it does not warrant a graph-regularity node.

Step 2 uses `lem-weil-pbc-regular-sequence-koszul-regular`, whose actual statement assumes successive injectivity (not a proper ideal and not a nonzero quotient). Step 1 verifies exactly that hypothesis with M=S. `lem-sc-koszul-conormal-basis` supplies precisely the classes forming the basis of I/I². `lem-sc-koszul-regular-sequence-dual-complex` gives the full derived dual, its vanishing off degree n, and its determinant-valued top cohomology over arbitrary commutative rings. `def-sc-koszul-dual-complex` specifies the signed differential; it is not cited for exactness. The determinant is identified with A by evaluation on the ordered conormal basis. The source's warning about signs is respected by retaining that order and the existing signed-dual convention.

Step 3 uses `lem-trf-derived-coinduction-adjunction` for the underlying RHom object, not just an adjunction slogan, and `lem-sc-derived-hom-projective-resolution` for the Hom-complex representative. The graph Koszul resolution is bounded and finite free, so meets its bounded-above-projective hypothesis; its target is the module S. The calculation of the A-action is written on the actual K-injective coinduction representative. In particular it is not inferred from an arbitrary isomorphism in D(S). `lem-sf-derived-concentrated-single` is a Mathlib leaf for any abelian category, so applies to A-modules and yields the first isomorphism in D(A) with the calculated top-cohomology map.

Step 4 cites `lem-weil-pbc-flat-tensor-exact`, a statement of exactness rather than merely a definition of flatness. Tensoring the cycle-boundary exact sequences verifies preservation of acyclic complexes; applying this to mapping cones verifies preservation of quasi-isomorphisms. Thus termwise tensor computes derived tensor by a resolving quasi-isomorphism. The identity N tensor_P S = N tensor_R A checks flatness of S/P without any finite-presentation assumption. The self-tensor identification uses the hypothesis that multiplication A tensor_R A -> A is an isomorphism, not an unstated surjectivity of R -> A. The associativity formula and multiplication inverse are written on pure tensors and apply degreewise to any complex of A-modules. This also records the equality v tensor b = bv tensor 1 needed to avoid an action ambiguity. No faithfully flat descent is used.

Step 5 applies `lem-pc-base-change-rhom-perfect`, whose statement explicitly allows arbitrary commutative rings and arbitrary target complex. Here its perfect source is A[0] over P, exactly the item's extra hypothesis, and its target is P[0]. Flatness, established in step 4, identifies the derived base-change factors with the ordinary ones, including A tensor_P S = A with the graph algebra structure. `lem-trf-derived-coinduction-adjunction` identifies the underlying D with the RHom to which this theorem applies. The result needed here is only a P-linear comparison on cohomology; no stronger A-valued base-change theorem is silently cited.

Step 6 checks A-linearity by choosing a P-lift of each b in A, possible by the stated polynomial presentation. On both coinduction objects the lift acts as the original precomposition action, by the explicit equation h(pa)=p h(a). Therefore the P-linear cohomology comparison is A-linear. Single-degree reconstruction is then applied in the category of A-modules, not lifted from D(P), which avoids the common false inference that restriction being conservative would lift isomorphisms. The final shift is [n], since the only cohomology sits in degree n. For n=0 the empty Koszul and determinant calculations give degree zero; for A=0 both coinductions are zero directly from their definition, so no regular-sequence convention for a proper ideal is being assumed.
