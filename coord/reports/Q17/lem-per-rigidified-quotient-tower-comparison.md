## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the old statement omitted strictness of the map of enlarged divisorial log pairs. Boundary inverse-image equality does not imply it. Take a field k of characteristic zero, trivial groups G=H=1, X=Spec k[t], Y=Spec k[x,y], phi given by t=xy, B_X=overline B_X=V(t), and B_Y=overline B_Y=V(xy). These are regular separated Noetherian schemes and (strict) normal crossings boundaries, with admissible generically free very tame trivial actions and identical trivial inertia. The scheme-theoretic pullback of V(t) is exactly V(xy), already reduced. At the origins the map of divisorial characteristics is N -> N^2, 1 |-> (1,1), which is not an isomorphism. The quotients are the schemes themselves, so the asserted strict quotient map is false even for trivial groups.

The correction explicitly assumes that (Y,overline B_Y) -> (X,overline B_X) is strict. This is the hypothesis of ILO VIII Proposition 5.4.2, and the opening sentence of section 5.4 explicitly specifies strict inert morphisms of quadruples. Corollary 5.4.4 writes the boundary equalities and then calls the morphism strict in its proof; reduced inverse images alone do not justify that sentence. The correction records the intended strict-combinatorial setting, rather than reproducing that inference. Boundaries in the statement are reduced; proper principal centers are retained even when the underlying blowup is the identity. I added the existing definition nodes for strict log morphisms, very tame actions and invariant log quotients, since the original def-trf-fs-log-blowup does not define very tameness.
