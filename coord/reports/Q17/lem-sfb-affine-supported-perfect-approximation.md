## Source detail: two different finite counts

The opening line of 08EQ's proof writes T=V(f_1,...,f_r), reusing the number r of affine opens in the hypothesis. It is unnecessary to assume an ideal defining T has that many generators. I did not use that inference. In 08E3's proof the number of affine opens is used solely to show H^j(U,G)=0 for j>=s for quasi-coherent G. Separately choose any finite principal-open covering of U, say with q members; the Koszul complex can use q generators. The Hom-colimit comparison in the proof of 08E3 remains available for that covering. Thus the class-lifting statement uses s for its vanishing window and imposes no bound on the length of a Koszul complex. This is a clarification of the proof, not a correction or weakening of the item's true statement.

For the new node I read perfect.tex 08E3, lemma-represent-cohomology-class-on-closed, its preceding I_e/O/K_e triangles and 08DD class-on-open comparison, and coherent.tex lemma-vanishing-nr-affines. Its derivation is: an open subset of an affine scheme is separated, so the s-affine cover gives cohomological dimension <s on quasi-coherent sheaves. The finite interval [-s+1,0] of zero cohomology implies H^0(U,E)=0 by the truncation and spectral-sequence argument printed in 08E3. On the affine Y, identify Gamma(Y,H^0(E)) with H^0(Y,E). The class restricts to zero and is killed after precomposition by one I_e -> O_Y, using the Hom-colimit comparison for any finite principal cover. The I_e -> O_Y -> K_e triangle then factors it through K_e. This K_e is the sheafified bounded finite-free Koszul complex in nonpositive degrees; where any generator is a unit it is contractible. It is therefore perfect, quasi-coherent, supported on T and has no positive cohomology. None of this uses pseudo-coherence of E. When U is empty use K=O_Y and the class's corresponding map; this also handles s=0. The future split must supply the truncation/colimit prerequisites as cited nodes; I have not embedded that proof into the new statement.
