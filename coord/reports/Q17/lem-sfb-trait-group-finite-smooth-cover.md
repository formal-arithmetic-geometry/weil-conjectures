For the second new node I read the entire proof of Corollary A.4, not just its conclusion, together with Stacks 035Q (`lemma-normalization-normal`) and the field-group smoothness statements 047N and 047P. Finite integrality then extends the generic multiplication and inverse; the generic identity extends over the integrally closed base DVR. The equalities extend by generic schematic density and separatedness. This argument works for nonaffine schemes: it uses normalization and finite maps locally, not a nonexistent global affine Hopf algebra.
