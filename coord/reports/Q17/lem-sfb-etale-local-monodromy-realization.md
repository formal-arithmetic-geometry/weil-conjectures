## What needed repair

There was no existing proof in the assigned node. Its exact statement already avoids the overly global equivalence with the profinite fundamental group suggested by the old blueprint: rational lisse objects are realized after etale localization, while J itself realizes representations globally on a connected chart. The current unit and parent use precisely that weaker, correct assertion, and no target statement was changed.

An initial sandbox draft was rejected in self-review. It inferred lisseness of a tower limit solely from finite-level freeness, treated finite-level full faithfulness as rational full faithfulness, and invoked an ordinary-module inverse-limit lemma for sheaves. The final split replaces those inferences by the integral reduction equivalence, the internal-Hom/global-section localization argument, and explicit sheaf product/Milnor lifting. It also chooses intersection/image lattices so that reduction of an exact representation sequence is exact, instead of claiming this for arbitrary lattices. Finally it proves that chart refinement preserves topological Noetherianity and that connected pieces are open; ring-theoretic Noetherianity is never substituted for topological Noetherianity.
