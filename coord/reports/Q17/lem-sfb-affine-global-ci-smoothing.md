## Source error

The last paragraph of 0E7V says the map from Spec(k[[t]]) maps into the good open. Only its generic point must do so: H(a) is nonzero but need not be a unit of k[[t]]. For example over a characteristic-zero field the special fibre of f=x^2 is singular; coefficients in tR force the closed parameter point to be the origin, where that special fibre stays singular. No arc with these special coefficients can factor entirely through a parameter open on which the universal family is smooth. The proof here uses the needed generic-point statement, and the item's statement requires no correction.

There is also an index typo in the displayed formula of 0E7U: after normalizing lambda_1=1 the relation is a_(i1) = - partial f_1/partial x_i - sum_(j=2)^c lambda_j(partial f_j/partial x_i + a_(ij)). The printed first derivative has mismatched j and 1. This does not affect its incidence count: n coordinates x_i, c-1 scalars lambda_j, and n(c-1) remaining linear coefficients total c(n+1)-1, less than the number of independent parameter coordinates. The new node states the actual full-rank consequence of this argument, not the misprinted formula. Its unsplit statement contains no proof sketch.
