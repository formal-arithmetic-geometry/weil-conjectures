- `d = 0`: `Fin 0` is empty. The basis forces C and T to be zero modules;
  the unital split equivalence then forces B and D to be zero rings.
  Empty bases and finite zero modules satisfy every clause. No `Nontrivial`
  instance or positive rank excludes this case.
- `K = ⊥`: J, KB and H are zero. The quotient comparison is the identity
  tensor comparison and the displayed γ formula reduces to θ on pure tensors.
- `K = ⊤`: its image contains 1 under each unital map, so all three ideals
  are top and all quotient modules are zero; D itself remains finite by the
  original basis and the product projection.
- B the zero ring: the B-algebra D is zero, and all tensor/quotient modules
  vanish. The statement still types and retains this case.
- Nonflat scalar extension and infinitely generated K: the quotient comparison
  is right exactness, not preservation of an injection. The span proof uses
  only finite sums representing individual elements and does not require a
  finite list of generators of K.
- All four ring universes remain independent; no size equality is imposed.
