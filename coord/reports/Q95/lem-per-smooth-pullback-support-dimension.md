## Review by Q95

I read the first paragraph of the current blueprint proof `lem-per-smooth-pullback-t-exact.proof.md` against the node's formula. Its shift is cohomological: H^n(C[d]) = H^{n+d}(C). At n=-i this is -i+d, not -i-d. For a constant sheaf on a point pulled back to affine d-space, the nonzero degree after [d] is -d and its support has dimension d; the formula gives equality. At d=0 it is the usual pullback support bound. I tested zero complexes and supports missed entirely by f: the left dimension is minus infinity. No surjectivity, connectedness, properness, smoothness of X over k, or smoothness of Y over k is used.

For `lem-per-adic-heart-pullback-fibres`, I read Bhatt–Scholze §6.8, especially Lemma 6.8.2 (completed and rational coefficient sheaves), Definitions 6.8.3 and 6.8.6 (lisse and constructible sheaves), Proposition 6.8.4 (finite-level local systems and rational lattices), Lemma 6.8.7 (abelian constructible heart), Definitions 6.8.8 and 6.8.10 and Proposition 6.8.11(3) (constructible complexes/cohomology), Proposition 6.8.14 (rationalization), and Remark 6.8.15 (module pullback). The new input records the needed consequences for the specified completed/raw-rational/common-stage categories; it does not claim that arbitrary naive pro-etale inverse image commutes with an infinite completion. Its hypotheses include finite-type separated k-schemes, ell invertible, and the compatible etale-origin data. For a lisse stratum, finite-level geometric fibres followed by inverse limit and inversion have the same vector space at x and h(x); a finite field extension preserves whether that vector space is zero. Rank zero, disconnected strata, empty schemes and changes of finite coefficient field are allowed. The degree-zero assertion is strictly weaker than the item's all-degree cohomology claim, which Step 1 derives by bounded truncations, and contains no dimension assertion.

For `lem-per-smooth-local-dimension`, I read `morphisms.tex` at `lemma-smoothness-dimension` and checked tag 0AFF in `tags/tags`. Its hypotheses are smoothness and local Noetherianity; the formula is about topological local dimensions, not local-ring dimensions. I kept those hypotheses exactly. Smoothness of relative dimension d means that the fibre has local topological dimension d at every source point, as the opened statement of `def-sbc-smooth-morphisms` says. Empty sources make the local formula vacuous. The identity on a singular reducible scheme has d=0 and satisfies it; no regularity of the target is needed.

For `lem-per-constructible-subset-closure-dimension`, I read Algebra Tag 00OT, `lemma-dimension-at-a-point-finite-type-over-field`, especially the proof that every nonempty open in a component has its component's dimension. Passing to a finite locally closed decomposition gives the constructible-subset consequence: the largest-dimensional components of the closure have dense locally closed pieces of S containing nonempty open subsets of them, whose dimensions are unchanged. Conversely subspace dimension is bounded by closure dimension. This is a genuine dimension lemma, not pure topology for arbitrary dense subsets. I checked S empty, finite sets of closed points, a dense open of an affine line, and a union of strata on components of different dimensions. The finite-type-over-a-field hypothesis matters: the generic open point of the spectrum of a DVR has dimension zero while its closure has dimension one, so that broader Noetherian statement would be false. The new node does not assert it.
