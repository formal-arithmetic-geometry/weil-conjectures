### Old step 1

Choose an affine neighbourhood $U=\operatorname{Spec}S$ of $x$. By [lem-weil-pbc-lft-affine-pairs], $S$ is a finitely generated $k$-algebra. Write $\mathfrak p$ for the maximal ideal representing $x$, $A=S_{\mathfrak p}$ and $\mathfrak m=\mathfrak pA$. By [lem-trf-geometric-field-stalk-descent], $F=S/\mathfrak p$ is finite over $k$, and every point of $U_{\bar k}$ over $x$ is closed with residue field $\bar k$. Write $B=(S\otimes_k\bar k)_{\mathfrak q}$ for the local ring of one such point, and $\mathfrak n$ for its maximal ideal. Let $J_A$ and $J_B$ be the respective first Fitting ideals of the differential modules. The definition of $Z$ identifies $\mathcal O_{Z,x}$ with $A/J_A$ when $x\in Z$. Thus $\mathcal O_{Z,x}=F$ via the residue map means exactly $J_A=\mathfrak m$; in particular it implies $x\in Z$.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-lft-affine-pairs, lem-trf-geometric-field-stalk-descent, def-trf-first-fitting-singular-subscheme.

### Old step 2

Suppose that $x$ is a node, and choose the point above $x$ whose completion is $\bar k[[u,v]]/(uv)$. Apply [lem-trf-separable-closed-point-formal-presentation] to the finite-type $\bar k$-algebra $S\otimes_k\bar k$ and its rational closed point. It gives a power-series presentation of $\widehat B$ and identifies its Jacobian cokernel with $\Omega_{B/\bar k}\otimes_B\widehat B$.
