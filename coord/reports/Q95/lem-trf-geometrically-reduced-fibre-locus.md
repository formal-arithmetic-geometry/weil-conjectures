### Original step 1

Let $f:\operatorname{Spec}B\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ be the morphism induced by $A\to B$ [def-weil-affine-spectrum]. At every point of $\operatorname{Spec}B$, take the whole source and the whole target as affine open neighbourhoods in [def-sbc-locally-finite-presentation]. Their induced ring map is the given finitely presented map [def-weil-pbc-finitely-presented-algebra]. Thus $f$ is locally of finite presentation. When the source is empty this condition is vacuous.

Cited nodes: def-weil-affine-spectrum, def-sbc-locally-finite-presentation, def-weil-pbc-finitely-presented-algebra.

### Original step 2

Apply [lem-weil-pbc-affine-scheme-qcqs] separately to $\operatorname{Spec}B$ and $\operatorname{Spec}A$. Both schemes are quasi-compact and quasi-separated. In particular the source is quasi-compact and the target is quasi-separated, so clause (1) of [lem-weil-pbc-qcqs-morphism-permanence] makes $f$ quasi-compact. These are precisely the morphism and base finiteness hypotheses needed below.

Cited nodes: lem-weil-pbc-affine-scheme-qcqs, lem-weil-pbc-qcqs-morphism-permanence.

### Original step 3

Fix $\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Spec}A$ and put $k=\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ [def-weil-residue-field] and $C=B\otimes_A k$. By [def-pc-scheme-fibre] and [lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra], $X_{\mathfrak p}\cong\operatorname{Spec}C$ as $k$-schemes: the projection to $\operatorname{Spec}k$ is induced by $c\mapsto1\otimes c$. By [def-weil-geometric-fibre], $X_{\bar{\mathfrak p}}$ is the base change of this fibre to a chosen algebraic closure of $k$. The criterion [lem-trf-geometric-reducedness-algebraic-closure-test], applied to $\operatorname{Spec}C/k$, therefore gives $X_{\bar{\mathfrak p}}$ reduced if and only if $\operatorname{Spec}C$ is geometrically reduced. The latter is exactly the meaning of geometric reducedness of $C$ in the item. Thus the reduced geometric-fibre locus of $f$ equals $E_{\mathrm{red}}(B/A)$. This comparison uses no finiteness assumption on the ring map.

Cited nodes: def-weil-residue-field, def-pc-scheme-fibre, lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra, def-weil-geometric-fibre, lem-trf-geometric-reducedness-algebraic-closure-test.

### Original step 4

Given any $a:A\to A\prime$, put $g=\operatorname{Spec}a$ and $B\prime=B\otimes_A A\prime$. The isomorphism [lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra] identifies the base change $\operatorname{Spec}B\times_{\operatorname{Spec}A}\operatorname{Spec}A\prime$ with $\operatorname{Spec}B\prime$, including its projection to $\operatorname{Spec}A\prime$. Apply [lem-trf-geometric-reduced-fibre-locus-base-change]: it says that the reduced geometric-fibre locus of this projection equals $g^{-1}$ of that of $f$. Apply the comparison in Step 3 to $A\to B$ and to $A\prime\to B\prime$; that comparison needed no finite presentation. The two loci are respectively $E_{\mathrm{red}}(B/A)$ and $E_{\mathrm{red}}(B\prime/A\prime)$. Substitution gives exactly the asserted equality for every $a$, including non-flat maps.

Cited nodes: lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra, lem-trf-geometric-reduced-fibre-locus-base-change.

### Original step 5

Choose a presentation $B\cong A[t_1,\ldots,t_n]/(F_1,\ldots,F_m)$ [def-weil-pbc-finitely-presented-algebra]. There are finitely many coefficients in the $F_j$. By [lemma-nagata-ring-finite-type-subalgebras] they lie in a finitely generated $\mathbf Z$-subalgebra $A_0\subseteq A$. The case $n=0$ of [lem-trf-mvpolynomial-noetherian] says that $\mathbf Z$ is Noetherian (a polynomial ring on no variables is its coefficient ring). Hence [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-algebra-noetherian], applied to $\mathbf Z\to A_0$, makes $A_0$ Noetherian. Write $F_j^0\in A_0[t_1,\ldots,t_n]$ for the same polynomials with coefficients in $A_0$.

Cited nodes: def-weil-pbc-finitely-presented-algebra, lemma-nagata-ring-finite-type-subalgebras, lem-trf-mvpolynomial-noetherian, lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-algebra-noetherian.

### Original step 6

Put $B_0=A_0[t_1,\ldots,t_n]/(F_1^0,\ldots,F_m^0)$, a finitely presented $A_0$-algebra [def-weil-pbc-finitely-presented-algebra]. There is an $A$-algebra isomorphism $B_0\otimes_{A_0}A\cong B$. Explicitly, $[P]\otimes a\mapsto aP(\bar t)$ is well-defined by bilinearity and the quotient relations [def-weil-pbc-module-tensor-product, def-weil-pbc-algebra-hom-quotient]. Its inverse is evaluation on the generators $a\mapsto1\otimes a$, $\bar t_i\mapsto[t_i]\otimes1$ [def-weil-pbc-mv-polynomial-ring]: each $F_j$ evaluates to $[F_j^0]\otimes1=0$, by tensor balancing, so evaluation factors through the quotient. Both maps preserve units and products, since pure tensors multiply by $(b\otimes a)(c\otimes e)=bc\otimes ae$; their composites fix the polynomial generators and all pure tensors, which generate the respective rings. This argument works for any base ring map in place of $A_0\to A$. Thus $f$ is the base change of $f_0:\operatorname{Spec}B_0\to T_0=\operatorname{Spec}A_0$, including its projection, by [lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra]. Applying Steps 1 and 2 to $A_0\to B_0$ makes $f_0$ quasi-compact and locally of finite presentation.

Cited nodes: def-weil-pbc-finitely-presented-algebra, def-weil-pbc-module-tensor-product, def-weil-pbc-algebra-hom-quotient, def-weil-pbc-mv-polynomial-ring, lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra.

### Original step 7

Let $E_0$ be the reduced geometric-fibre locus of $f_0$. Fix an irreducible closed subset $Z\subseteq T_0$. By [lem-weil-pbc-reduced-induced-closed-subscheme] it has a reduced closed subscheme structure $j:Z_{\mathrm{red}}\to T_0$; by [def-weil-closed-immersion] its underlying space is homeomorphic to $Z$, hence irreducible. By [lem-sc-scheme-sober], $Z_{\mathrm{red}}$ has a generic point $\xi$. The pullback $h:X_0\times_{T_0}Z_{\mathrm{red}}\to Z_{\mathrm{red}}$ is quasi-compact and locally of finite presentation by clause (3) of [lem-weil-pbc-qcqs-morphism-permanence]. Apply [lem-trf-generic-constancy-geometric-reducedness] to $h$: on a nonempty open $V$ every ordinary fibre is geometrically reduced, or every ordinary fibre is not. For each point, [def-weil-geometric-fibre] and [lem-trf-geometric-reducedness-algebraic-closure-test] identify this condition with reducedness of its geometric fibre. By [lem-trf-geometric-reduced-fibre-locus-base-change], the latter locus is $j^{-1}(E_0)$. Transporting $V$ through the homeomorphism onto $Z$ gives a nonempty relatively open subset either contained in $E_0$ or disjoint from it.

Cited nodes: lem-weil-pbc-reduced-induced-closed-subscheme, def-weil-closed-immersion, lem-sc-scheme-sober, lem-weil-pbc-qcqs-morphism-permanence, lem-trf-generic-constancy-geometric-reducedness, def-weil-geometric-fibre, lem-trf-geometric-reducedness-algebraic-closure-test, lem-trf-geometric-reduced-fibre-locus-base-change.

### Original step 8

The affine scheme $T_0$ has a one-member affine cover by a Noetherian ring, by Step 5. Step 7 verifies the hypothesis on every irreducible closed subset in [lem-trf-noetherian-constructible-subset-criterion]; hence $E_0$ is constructible. By Step 6, $f$ is the base change of $f_0$ along $g:\operatorname{Spec}A\to T_0$. The equality [lem-trf-geometric-reduced-fibre-locus-base-change] gives its reduced geometric-fibre locus as $g^{-1}(E_0)$, which is constructible by [lem-pur-scheme-constructible-preimage]. Step 3 identifies this locus with $E_{\mathrm{red}}(B/A)$, proving constructibility. Step 4 already proved the required arbitrary-base-change equality, so both assertions follow.

Cited nodes: lem-trf-noetherian-constructible-subset-criterion, lem-trf-geometric-reduced-fibre-locus-base-change, lem-pur-scheme-constructible-preimage.
