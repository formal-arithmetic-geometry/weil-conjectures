## Argument retained for the statement-only insertion node

I checked the stronger, explicitly exchange-based formulation against the proof of Björner--Brenti Lemma 2.2.1. Here is the argument for the next worker. Among the reduced embeddings of u in the fixed reduced word b_1...b_q, choose one whose largest omitted position r is minimal. The omitted set is nonempty because u differs from v. All positions after r are selected. Write the full word as A b_r B and the selected word as C B, so u=CB. Let t=B^{-1}b_rB. Inserting at r gives ut=C b_r B, a word of length ell(u)+1.

If that word is not reduced, ell(ut) is at most ell(u), so the stated strong exchange property deletes one selected position p from CB. If p is after r, write B=B_1 b_p B_2. The deletion equation gives t=B_2^{-1}b_pB_2. Consequently B t=B_1B_2 and also B t=b_rB, by the definition of t. Hence the full product v=A b_rB equals A B_1B_2, deleting positions r and p from a reduced word, a contradiction. If p is before r, write C=C_1 b_p C_2. The deletion equation gives ut=C_1C_2B. Since t is an involution, multiplying by t yields u=C_1C_2 b_rB. This is another reduced embedding of u, omitting p instead of r; its largest omitted position is strictly less than r, contradicting the choice. Thus the insertion is reduced. This proof uses just the hypothesis stated in the new node and group cancellation, not the parent equivalence or its conclusion.
