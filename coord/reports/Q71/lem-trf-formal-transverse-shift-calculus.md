- `transverseShiftVariables` sends W_i to W_i and Y_j to the embedded phi_j + Z_j.
  `transverseShift` is Mathlib substitution at these images.

  The main theorem supplies L-algebra equivalences `S` and `ST` whose entire
  underlying functions equal these explicit substitutions. Its next equalities
  specify both inverse functions by replacement of phi with -phi, namely
  Z_j ↦ Y_j - phi_j, fixing W and T. The extra S is exactly the no-T shift

  and its inverse, using the full variable ideal of each ring. For every N

  The common M for forward and inverse maps is equivalent to separate continuity
  by increasing to the maximum of their two bounds.

  `(Fin t →₀ ℕ) → MvPowerSeries (Fin m ⊕ Fin r) L`. The domain is the whole set
  of multi-indices, not finitely many indices. `transverseCoefficientFamily`

  or finite support of the family.
