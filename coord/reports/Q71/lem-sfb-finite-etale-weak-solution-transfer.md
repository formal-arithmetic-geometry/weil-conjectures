## Source error

I read More Algebra Tag 09ET, lemma-solution-after-strict-henselization, especially its final finite-etale reduction, together with Tags 09EN, 09EM, 09EU and 0GLR. Splitting M tensor_K F as copies of M does not by itself make the products after tensoring over F with H identical: the copies have different F-algebra structures. The invalid inference is treating isomorphism as M-algebras as isomorphism as F-algebras. As a small algebraic test of this inference, let K=Q, F=Q(sqrt(2)), M be a real closure of Q, and H=F(sqrt(1+sqrt(2))). The two embeddings F -> M give M tensor_F H respectively M x M and M(i), since 1+sqrt(2)>0 but 1-sqrt(2)<0. These are not isomorphic M-algebras. This test isolates the tensor inference; it is not asserted to be a counterexample satisfying the DVR hypotheses of the item.

The proof keeps the distinct embeddings, uses only the selected factor, and replaces H by a finite N normal over K. Perfectness of the lower residue makes the chosen index-one branch separable in residue, so the existing normalized base-change theorem preserves it after N/H. Normal coefficient conjugacy then transfers that one good branch to every branch. No changed statement is needed because the live item already asks only for existence of N.

Tag 09EU, lemma-galois-relative, uses K=N^G in its proof. For finite normal extensions this equality need not hold without separability: for K=F_p(t), N=K(t^(1/p)), G is trivial but N is not K. I therefore recorded the existing lem-sfb-normal-tensor-invariant-powers as the source correction for the new general conjugacy input. Invariant elements have a suitable power scalar, which suffices in the orbit-separation argument. This changes no assertion of the item or of the conjugacy statement.
