I read the statement and proof of 0CZT (`lemma-finite-free-open`), the statement and proof of 0CZS (`lemma-flat-and-free-at-point-fibre`), 05WY (`lemma-flat-on-fibres-at-point`), and 05VW (`spaces-morphisms.tex`, `lemma-base-change-module-flat`). Applied to id_X over Y, its fibre flatness is module flatness over the fibre space itself, precisely what the new criterion states. 05VW imposes no finiteness or support condition; flatness of the base morphism occurs only in its reflection clause, which the item does not use.

For step 1 I opened `lem-a1-space-affine-etale-cover`. Its statement explicitly says that a representable etale scheme map has open point image. The elementary remainder is that the same trivialization witnesses every point in that image, and that the union of the images is exactly W. This avoids using a definition of the topology as a substitute for a theorem about open images.

For step 2 I opened the statements of the fibre-product/cover node, the chartwise morphism-property definition, the tensor-pullback definition, the quasi-coherent-module definition, and the new 05VW node. The cover node supplies preservation of etale coverings under arbitrary base change. The local finite-presentation calculation retains the SAME finite list of polynomial generators and relations over the new ring; the module calculation retains the SAME finite row presentation, since tensor pullback is an adjoint and hence preserves its cokernel. These are calculations from the cited definitions, not further geometric flatness assertions. The 05VW statement then supplies relative flatness of F prime at each point. In particular no flatness of v or p, no finite-presentation condition on v, and no Noetherian base was inserted.

For step 3 the fibre-product node states the categorical universal property being used, not merely the existence of some charts. I wrote the inverse maps on test points and their action on the tautological point. I checked the tensor-pullback definition and wrote both directions of the tensor reassociation map. Thus the coefficient identification is the canonical pullback of the SAME F, and the distinguished points also agree. There is no separate hidden base-change theorem in this elementary step.

For step 4 the new criterion states exactly both pointwise equivalences required, including the use of an arbitrary representative Spec K. Step 2 establishes all its hypotheses for the primed family. Step 3 identifies the two fibre spaces, modules and points, so flatness in the two applications is literally the same condition. This proves both inclusions of the claimed equality.
