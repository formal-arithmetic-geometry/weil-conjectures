## Not duplicates

lem-per-pointwise-log-regular-completion vs lem-trf-log-regular-completed-chart: the existing statement assumes a locally Noetherian log regular scheme everywhere; the new statement assumes regularity only at the selected geometric point. The existing theorem cannot be applied under the present hypotheses. Both conclusions concern sharp charts and the same two complete models, and the pointwise version is exactly the hypothesis actually printed in ILO VI 1.6.

lem-per-pointwise-log-regular-completion vs lem-trf-log-regular-sharp-chart: the existing node assumes global log regularity and gives no completed-ring presentation. The new node supplies the pointwise presentation, with its chart compatibility, under the weaker local assumption.

lem-per-pointwise-log-regular-completion vs lem-trf-completed-chart-coefficient-surjection: the existing algebra lemma presupposes a sharp monoid map and produces a surjection onto the completion, not a sharp characteristic chart or an isomorphism with the toric models.

lem-per-complete-toric-face-strata vs lem-trf-complete-model-face-quotients: the existing node identifies the quotient by a monomial face ideal as another completed model and proves it normal. It does not state regularity after inverting all the face monomials at an arbitrary prime, nor the dimension difference at that prime. A normal quotient is insufficient for the regularity assertion here.

lem-per-complete-toric-face-strata vs lem-trf-complete-toric-facet-valuations: the existing node treats height-one boundary primes/facets and their valuations, not arbitrary primes and arbitrary-rank face strata.

lem-per-complete-toric-face-strata vs lem-trf-formal-chart-geometric-regularity: that statement concerns an isogroup chart base change at a point lying over the CLOSED point of the ring whose completion is controlled. The prime here can be any prime of the completed model, over a proper generization, so its hypothesis is not satisfied.

lem-per-complete-toric-face-strata vs lem-trf-formal-monoid-extension-model: likewise, the existing statement explicitly requires its chosen prime to contract to the maximal ideal of the source local ring; the new statement allows every prime of a fixed complete model.

lem-per-complete-toric-face-strata vs lem-per-rational-toric-completion-regular-map: that node assumes characteristic zero and gives a regular rational toric completion morphism, while the new statement includes arbitrary equal characteristic and mixed characteristic hypersurfaces, with a face-stratum dimension formula.

Neither new node restates a clause of the item: the first gives a presentation, not generization; the second is pure algebra for specific complete model rings, with no arbitrary log scheme or pointwise regularity hypothesis.
