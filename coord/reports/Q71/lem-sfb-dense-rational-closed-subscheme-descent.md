## Argument retained for the new statement-only lemma

For x in V tensor_F L, choose a finite expression x = sum_i v_i tensor lambda_i. Choose a basis b_1,...,b_r of the finite F-linear span in L of the lambda_i. After collecting coefficients, x = sum_j w_j tensor b_j with w_j in V. If every extended u_e kills x, then sum_j u_e(w_j)b_j = 0 for each e. Linear independence in the field L gives u_e(w_j)=0 for every e,j, so each w_j belongs to K. Hence x belongs to the L-span of K tensor 1. The converse follows immediately by linearity. This neither tensors an infinite product isomorphically nor interchanges a tensor product with an infinite intersection without proof. The inclusion of K tensor L need not be used to state the result: the statement asks for its span/image. Empty E gives K=V and the whole tensor product; V=0 and x=0 give empty sums. This is a genuinely uniform lemma for arbitrary linear functionals on arbitrary vector spaces, rather than the affine geometric clause of the item repackaged.
