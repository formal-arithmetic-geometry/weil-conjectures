The source attribution was too strong. Temkin Proposition 4.4.6 STATES existence of a stable modification. Its proof's final paragraph DOES construct the normalized image of a map to the relative projective line, after obtaining the special morphism used for one exceptional contraction. I now attribute only that construction to the proof paragraph and prove the general properness, connectedness and modification-locus clauses from explicitly cited Stacks statements. The displayed factorization was ambiguous: a graph map ends in the image I, not in X. I changed only that notation in the statement: ν:H→I is the normalization map, p:I→X is the projection, h=νq is the graph-image factorization, and g=pν:H→X. The hypotheses and every conclusion are preserved. The proof now maintains these codomains throughout; no statement-dispute weakening was needed.

A further false inference in the old proof was that a closed subscheme of a finitely presented scheme is finitely presented. For example, over a ring with a non-finitely generated ideal J, Spec(R/J)→Spec(R) is a closed immersion without finite presentation. Step 5 now applies 081N to the cyclic quasi-coherent module i_*O_I on P^1_X, flat over T, whose restriction over the retrocompact dense generic open is finitely presented. Thus the defining ideal is finitely generated locally. This is why the subsequent finite-normalization theorem applies to an actual T-curve.
