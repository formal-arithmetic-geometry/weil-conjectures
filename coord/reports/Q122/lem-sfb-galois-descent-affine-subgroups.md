## New lemma argument and edge cases

Milne A.64 proves descent for vector spaces with continuous semilinear action, including infinite Galois extensions. The canonical action on M tensor L is continuous with M tensor L discrete: every vector is a finite sum of pure tensors and the pointwise stabilizer of its finitely many algebraic coefficients is open. A stable subspace inherits that property. A.64 identifies W with L tensor its invariant vectors; coefficient uniqueness in any k-basis of M identifies those vectors with elements m tensor 1, hence with W_0. Extension of an injective map of vector spaces is injective (extend a basis of W_0 to a basis of M). This gives the new lemma. These arguments are source material for its later worker; the node itself carries no proof.

Checked M=0, W=0, W=M_L, L=k, and M infinite dimensional. Each vector has finite support, so no finite-dimensionality or finite-generation hypothesis is hidden. A nonreduced subgroup such as mu_p remains covered because equations, not geometric-point density, are used. N=J, the trivial subgroup, arbitrary imperfect k, arbitrary K/k (including inseparable and transcendental extensions), and the zero test algebra R=0 are covered. The subgroup ideal cannot be the unit ideal because V contains its identity; the construction therefore does not create an empty group.
