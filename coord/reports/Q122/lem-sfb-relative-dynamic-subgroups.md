## Proof plan for the new statement-only node

This is a general algebra lemma, not one of the dynamic-subgroup clauses: it concerns any smooth integer-graded commutative algebra and arbitrary graded square-zero targets, with no group structure, fixed locus, tangent formula, or open multiplication in its conclusion.

Choose a graded polynomial presentation P -> C on finitely many homogeneous algebra generators, with homogeneous kernel K. Smoothness gives that the conormal sequence K/K^2 -> C tensor_P Omega_(P/R) -> Omega_(C/R) -> 0 is split exact on the left. The first module embeds, and an ungraded C-linear retraction onto it exists. Taking the degree-preserving component of this retraction gives a graded retraction: for homogeneous v of degree n retain the degree-n component of its image; C-linearity is checked on homogeneous scalars, and the retraction identity survives. This degree-preserving projection is on a MODULE MAP, not on an algebra homomorphism.

Given h:C -> D/J, lift the finitely many generators homogeneously to D (the quotient is surjective in each degree), obtaining a graded polynomial map P -> D. Its restriction to K lands in J, kills K^2, and is a graded C-linear map K/K^2 -> J; the C-module structure on J is defined via h because J^2=0. Compose this map with the graded retraction to get a graded derivation P -> J extending the obstruction on K. Subtract this derivation from the polynomial map. The square-zero identity makes the result an algebra homomorphism; it still preserves degree and lifts h, and its values on K vanish. It therefore descends to the required graded lift C -> D. This is the argument assigned to the new node, not a claim that Stacks' ordinary lifting theorem alone has the graded conclusion.
