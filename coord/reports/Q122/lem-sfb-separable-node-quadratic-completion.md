## Retained nine-step proof and ownership map

The text below preserves the old arguments verbatim. These are implementation briefs for the new statement-only nodes, not proof fields of those nodes. Steps 1--3 belong to separable-curve-geometric-local-data. Steps 4--7 and 9 belong to split-completion-local-invariant-descent. Step 8's Jacobian identification belongs to the latter; its application of the already existing quadratic criterion and its residue/topology check remain the parent's short final assembly.

### Original step 1

Choose an affine open neighbourhood $U=\operatorname{Spec}S$ of $x$. By [lem-weil-pbc-lft-affine-pairs], $S$ is a finitely generated $k$-algebra. The closed point corresponds to a maximal ideal $\mathfrak p\subset S$ and its residue field is $S/\mathfrak p=\kappa$. By [lem-weil-pbc-affine-open-stalk-localization], the germ map identifies $A=\mathcal O_{C,x}$ with $S_{\mathfrak p}$ and $\mathfrak m$ with $\mathfrak pS_{\mathfrak p}$. Thus $A$ is essentially of finite type over $k$ by [def-weil-pbc-essentially-finite-type-algebra]: take the finite-type algebra $S$ and the multiplicative set $S\setminus\mathfrak p$. Put $K=\bar k$ and let $\mathrm{pr}:C_K=C\times_k\operatorname{Spec}K\to C$ be the first projection. By [lem-weil-pbc-open-fibre-product] (with $V=U$, $W=\operatorname{Spec}K$ over $\operatorname{Spec}k$), $U_K=U\times_k\operatorname{Spec}K$ is the open subscheme $\mathrm{pr}^{-1}(U)$ of $C_K$, compatibly with the projections, and it contains $\bar x$ since $\mathrm{pr}(\bar x)=x\in U$. By [lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra], $U_K\cong\operatorname{Spec}(S\otimes_kK)$ with first projection $\operatorname{Spec}$ of $s\mapsto s\otimes1$, so $\bar x$ is a prime $\mathfrak q$ of $S\otimes_kK$ over $\mathfrak p$. Clause (c) of [lem-weil-pbc-affine-open-stalk-localization] for the affine open $U_K$ of $C_K$ identifies $\mathcal O_{C_K,\bar x}$ with $(S\otimes_kK)_{\mathfrak q}$ through the germ map, hence also their maximal-ideal-adic completions.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-lft-affine-pairs, lem-weil-pbc-affine-open-stalk-localization, def-weil-pbc-essentially-finite-type-algebra, lem-weil-pbc-open-fibre-product, lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra.

### Original step 2

Let $\mathfrak q\subset S\otimes_kK$ be the prime of $\bar x$ from step 1. By [lem-trf-geometric-field-stalk-descent], this prime is maximal with structural residue field $K$. Its residue map gives a $k$-embedding $\iota:\kappa\hookrightarrow K$, and $\mathfrak q$ is the kernel of $s\otimes\lambda\mapsto\iota(\bar s)\lambda$. Let $B=(S\otimes_kK)_{\mathfrak q}=\mathcal O_{C_K,\bar x}$ and $\mathfrak n=\mathfrak m_B$. Apply [lem-trf-separable-closed-point-formal-presentation] to $(k,S,\mathfrak p)$ and $\iota$. Its hypotheses hold because $S$ is finite type and $\kappa/k$ is finite separable by assumption. It gives Noetherian local rings $A,\widehat A,B$, a faithfully flat local map $A\to B$ with $\mathfrak mB=\mathfrak n$ and closed fibre $K$, and a $k$-algebra residue section $\sigma:\kappa\to\widehat A$. Choose its continuous presentation $\widehat A=\kappa[[t_1,\ldots,t_n]]/(f_1,\ldots,f_s)$, $n\ge2$, with kernel in $(t_1,\ldots,t_n)$ and $\kappa$ acting through $\sigma$. Apply the same lemma to the finite-type $K$-algebra $S\otimes_kK$ at $\mathfrak q$, whose residue extension is $K/K$. It gives a presentation $\widehat B=K[[z_1,\ldots,z_N]]/(g_1,\ldots,g_r)$ with $N\ge2$. Both completions are Noetherian local, their maximal ideals are the extended maximal ideals, and their residue fields are $\kappa,K$, respectively.

Cited statements: lem-trf-geometric-field-stalk-descent, lem-trf-separable-closed-point-formal-presentation.

### Original step 3

The dimension assertion is independent of the singularity calculation. Apply [lem-sbc-equidimensional-open-local-dimension] with $Y=C$, $O=C$, and $n=1$: equidimensionality gives $\dim_x C=1$. By [lem-weil-pbc-dimension-at-point-open-subspace], $\dim_x U=\dim_x C$. Since $x$ is closed and $S$ is a finitely generated $k$-algebra, [lem-weil-pbc-closed-point-dimension-local-ring] identifies $\dim_x U$ with $\dim S_{\mathfrak p}$. Under step 1 this is $\dim A=1$.

Cited statements: lem-sbc-equidimensional-open-local-dimension, lem-weil-pbc-dimension-at-point-open-subspace, lem-weil-pbc-closed-point-dimension-local-ring.

### Original step 4

Write $M_A=\Omega_{A/k}$ and $M_B=\Omega_{B/K}$ using [def-weil-pbc-kahler-differentials]. They are finitely generated: for generators $s_1,\ldots,s_d$ of $S/k$, additivity and Leibniz express every differential of a polynomial in the $s_i$ as a combination of $\mathrm ds_i$; the symbols presentation in that definition proves generation. The same argument applies over $K$, and [lem-weil-pbc-kahler-differentials-localization] gives finiteness after localization. Define $J_A=\operatorname{Fit}_1(M_A)$ and $J_B=\operatorname{Fit}_1(M_B)$ by the presentation-minor ideals of [lem-trf-fitting-ideal-independence-base-change]. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-kahler-differentials-base-change] to $S/k$ and $K/k$, then [lem-weil-pbc-kahler-differentials-localization] to $\mathfrak p,\mathfrak q$: $M_B\cong M_A\otimes_AB$. Clause (2) of [lem-trf-fitting-ideal-independence-base-change] gives $J_B=J_AB$.

Cited statements: def-weil-pbc-kahler-differentials, lem-weil-pbc-kahler-differentials-localization, lem-weil-pbc-kahler-differentials-base-change, lem-trf-fitting-ideal-independence-base-change.

### Original step 5

For the presentation of $\widehat B$ in step 2, [lem-trf-separable-closed-point-formal-presentation] identifies $M_B\otimes_B\widehat B$ with the Jacobian cokernel of the $g_j$. The hypothesis gives a ring isomorphism $\widehat B\cong K[[u,v]]/(uv)$, hence condition (c) of [lem-trf-formal-jacobian-quadratic-criterion] with $a=c=0,b=1$ and discriminant $1$. Implication (c) to (a) gives $\operatorname{depth}(\widehat B)=1$ and says the ideal of the $(N-1)$-minors of this Jacobian is $\mathfrak n\widehat B$. By [lem-trf-fitting-ideal-independence-base-change], the same ideal is $J_B\widehat B$. To compare the finite relation rows with all kernel rows in that node, expand each minor of linear combinations of relation rows by multilinearity; conversely each relation row lies in the kernel. Thus $J_B\widehat B=\mathfrak n\widehat B$. Condition (c) explicitly permits a ring isomorphism without $K$-linearity, so the stated geometric hypothesis suffices.

Cited statements: lem-trf-separable-closed-point-formal-presentation, lem-trf-formal-jacobian-quadratic-criterion, lem-trf-fitting-ideal-independence-base-change.

### Original step 6

Since $B$ is Noetherian local, [lem-weil-pbc-local-completion-faithfully-flat] makes $B\to\widehat B$ faithfully flat. Contract the equality of step 5 by [lem-weil-pbc-faithfully-flat-ideal-contraction] to obtain $J_B=\mathfrak n$. The faithfully flat map $A\to B$ of step 2 satisfies $\mathfrak mB=\mathfrak n$, whereas step 4 gives $J_AB=J_B$. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-faithfully-flat-ideal-contraction] again to conclude $J_A=\mathfrak m$.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-local-completion-faithfully-flat, lem-weil-pbc-faithfully-flat-ideal-contraction.

### Original step 7

By [lem-weil-pbc-local-completion-faithfully-flat], both completion maps $A\to\widehat A$ and $B\to\widehat B$ are flat. The Noetherian local structures and extended-maximal-ideal equalities from [lem-trf-separable-closed-point-formal-presentation] show that the three flat local maps $B\to\widehat B$, $A\to B$, $A\to\widehat A$ have closed fibres $K,K,\kappa$, respectively. A field has depth zero by [def-weil-pbc-depth]: its maximal ideal is zero and the element zero cannot be regular on that nonzero ring. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-flat-local-depth-formula] successively to these three maps. Starting with $\operatorname{depth}(\widehat B)=1$ from step 5, one obtains $\operatorname{depth}(B)=1$, then $\operatorname{depth}(A)=1$, then $\operatorname{depth}(\widehat A)=1$.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-local-completion-faithfully-flat, lem-trf-separable-closed-point-formal-presentation, def-weil-pbc-depth, lem-weil-pbc-flat-local-depth-formula.

### Original step 8

Return to the presentation $\widehat A=\kappa[[t_1,\ldots,t_n]]/(f_1,\ldots,f_s)$ from [lem-trf-separable-closed-point-formal-presentation]. Its Jacobian cokernel is $M_A\otimes_A\widehat A$. As in step 5, [lem-trf-fitting-ideal-independence-base-change] identifies its $(n-1)$-minor ideal with $J_A\widehat A=\mathfrak m\widehat A$, using step 6. Together with step 7 this is condition (a) of [lem-trf-formal-jacobian-quadratic-criterion], now over $\kappa$. Implication (a) to (b) supplies $a,b,c\in\kappa$, $b^2-4ac\ne0$, and a complete local $\kappa$-algebra isomorphism $\widehat A\cong\kappa[[X,Y]]/(aX^2+bXY+cY^2)$. Its $\kappa$-structure on the left is precisely the chosen $k$-algebra section $\sigma$. A $\kappa$-algebra isomorphism fixes residue classes of constants, which exhaust the residue field, so its induced residue map is the identity. It sends the unique maximal ideal onto the unique maximal ideal and therefore sends every power onto the corresponding power. This proves continuity for the stated adic topologies (and continuity of its inverse).

Cited statements: lem-trf-separable-closed-point-formal-presentation, lem-trf-fitting-ideal-independence-base-change, lem-trf-formal-jacobian-quadratic-criterion.

### Original step 9

Finally $K[[u,v]]/(uv)$ is reduced: the substitution map $h\mapsto(h(u,0),h(0,v))$ into $K[[u]]\times K[[v]]$ has kernel $(uv)$, since vanishing of both substitutions says exactly that all nonzero coefficients have both exponents positive. The two one-variable power series rings are integral domains by [lem-weil-pbc-power-series-field-cohen-regular-complete] with $d=1$. Hence a nilpotent in the quotient maps to zero in both factors and is zero. Thus $\widehat B$ is reduced by the assumed ring isomorphism. By [lem-weil-pbc-faithfully-flat-ideal-contraction] with ideal zero, the faithfully flat maps $A\to B$ and $B\to\widehat B$ from steps 2 and 6 are injective. A nilpotent of $A$ maps to zero in $\widehat B$ and hence is zero. This proves the remaining reducedness assertion.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-power-series-field-cohen-regular-complete, lem-weil-pbc-faithfully-flat-ideal-contraction.
