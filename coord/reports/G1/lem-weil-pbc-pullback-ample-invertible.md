## Review by G1

I checked Stacks Morphisms Lemma Tag 0892(2) online: for a quasi-affine map, pullback of an ample invertible module is ample. An affine map is a special case, so the item's hypotheses and conclusion agree with that statement. I also read the statement of `def-weil-pbc-ample-invertible-module`, which uses Properties Definition Tag 01PS: quasi-compactness plus affine nonvanishing loci of sections of positive tensor powers. The node's absolute ampleness is this notion, not the source Lean repository's separate `IsAmple` notion over a field. The three recorded Mathlib searches for the item find no exact theorem; there is no new node to search. Searching the blueprint for “ample invertible pullback” returned no equivalent blueprint node.

Step 1 uses the stated clauses (1) through (3) of `lem-weil-pbc-invertible-pullback-tensor`: pullback preserves invertibility, commutes with tensor powers and identifies values of pulled-back sections with scalar extension of the original values. For a one-dimensional vector space, scalar extension along a field homomorphism preserves nonzero vectors. This proves the equality of nonvanishing loci, not merely a set inclusion. Step 2 uses the stated two conditions of `def-weil-pbc-ample-invertible-module` and clauses (1) and (2) of the Mathlib leaf `lem-weil-pbc-affine-morphism-permanence`: the restriction of `g` over each affine `X_s` is affine by base change, and its source is affine. The finitely many `X_s` covering quasi-compact `X` pull back to finitely many affine opens covering `Y`, giving both quasi-compactness of `Y` and the required pointwise affine nonvanishing loci. All cited nodes are in the item's unit except the affine-morphism definition in its statement, which is a notion already present there.

I tested the empty scheme case: the empty inverse image is quasi-compact, and the pointwise clause is vacuous. For `g` the identity, each equality of nonvanishing loci is literal. For a non-surjective affine map, the argument uses inverse images of a cover and does not require surjectivity.
