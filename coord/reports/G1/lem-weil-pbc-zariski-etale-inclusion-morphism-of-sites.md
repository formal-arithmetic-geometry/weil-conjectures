## Worker report

The module states that for a scheme `X`, with the Zariski topology on `X.Opens`,
there is a functor `i : X.Opens ⥤ X.Etale` sending every open `U` to the
étale object `Scheme.Etale.mk U.ι`. It asserts continuity, preservation of
finite limits (including the terminal object and fibre products), and exactness
of the induced sheaf pullback (finite limits and finite colimits), under the
standard right-adjoint instance for continuous sheaf pushforward.

The hypothesis `J` is the Grothendieck topology on `X.Opens`, and `hJ` identifies
it with `Opens.grothendieckTopology X.carrier`. The functor-object clause renders
`u(U)=U↪X`; `Functor.IsContinuous` renders clause (1), `PreservesFiniteLimits`
and `Nonempty (IsTerminal (i.obj ⊤))` render clause (2), and preservation of
finite limits and colimits by `i.sheafPullback` renders exactness in clause (3).
The pushforward is Mathlib's `sheafPushforwardContinuous`, i.e. sectionwise
precomposition with `i`.

Dependencies used: `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme` (Mathlib),
`Opens.grothendieckTopology` (Mathlib),
`AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.smallEtaleTopology` (Mathlib), and
`CategoryTheory.Functor.IsContinuous` / sheaf pullback constructions from
`Mathlib.CategoryTheory.Sites.Pullback`.

Edge cases checked: arbitrary schemes, empty opens and empty covering families,
terminal open `⊤`, and arbitrary universe `u`; the coefficient universe is
explicitly `Type v`.

Searches made: `msearch.py leanfinder` for a Zariski-to-small-etale inclusion
functor (no match); `msearch.py loogle` for continuous site functors and sheaf
pullback exactness (only generic Mathlib constructions); grep of Mathlib
`AlgebraicGeometry/Sites` and the project for an existing declaration (none).

Object/hypothesis/conclusion checklist, in the order of the node:

1. Arbitrary scheme X: `(X : Scheme.{u})`. Read the recorded dependency
   `def-pc-scheme`: Mathlib `Scheme extends LocallyRingedSpace`, with local
   affineness. No field, Noetherian, separation, connectedness or nonemptiness
   hypothesis. Verdict: exact.
2. Small Zariski site: `X.Opens` and
   `Opens.grothendieckTopology X.carrier`. Read the dependency
   `def-sc-zariski-site` and Mathlib Sites/Spaces: covering sieves cover every
   point of the target open; arrows are inclusions. This is the dependency's
   recorded Mathlib match, not the big Zariski topology on schemes. Verdict: exact.
3. Small étale site: `X.Etale`, `X.smallEtaleTopology`. Read the dependency
   `def-weil-pbc-etale-site` and Mathlib Morphisms/Etale and Sites/Etale:
   objects are étale morphisms into X, morphisms are X-morphisms; the
   `ofArrows_mem_smallEtaleTopology_iff` signature identifies covering families
   with jointly surjective families. Verdict: exact.
4. The inclusion functor, on both objects and arrows:
   `∃ (i : X.Opens ⥤ X.Etale), i ⋙ Scheme.Etale.forget X = X.restrictFunctor`.
   Read `restrictFunctor`: its object is `Over.mk U.ι`, its arrow is
   `Over.homMk (X.homOfLE i.le) ...`. Thus this equality specifies the actual
   open subschemes AND their inclusion morphisms. `Etale.forget` forgets only
   the property proof and is fully faithful. No alternative embedding is
   allowed. Verdict: exact.
5. Clause (1), preservation of covering families:
   `CoverPreserving (Opens.grothendieckTopology X.carrier) X.smallEtaleTopology i`.
   Read CoverPreserving's field: covering sieves have covering pushforward.
   Applied to the sieve generated by any open covering, its pushforward is
   the sieve generated by the image family; the small-étale covering criterion
   above gives precisely joint surjectivity. This explicitly supplies the
   covering condition missing from the first draft. Verdict: exact.
6. Clause (1), canonical pullback comparison is an isomorphism, and clause
   (2), terminal object and fibre products:
   `PreservesFiniteLimits i` and `Nonempty (IsTerminal (i.obj ⊤))`.
   Opens form the complete lattice of open subsets: top is the terminal open
   X and a pullback of V -> U <- W is their meet V ∩ W. These are existing
   categorical limits, not extra hypotheses or a new definition. Finite-limit
   preservation is equivalent to preservation of the terminal object and
   pullbacks (Stacks' remark immediately after 00X6), and entails that the
   canonical comparison is invertible. The functor equality identifies the
   image of top with the inclusion of the whole scheme, canonically isomorphic
   over X to id_X. Verdict: exact.
7. Clause (3), continuous pushforward and its section formula:
   the existential witness `_ : i.IsContinuous ...` enables Mathlib's
   `i.sheafPushforwardContinuous (Type (max (u + 1) v)) ...`. Read the recorded
   dependency `def-weil-pbc-sites-and-cohomology`, especially its note on
   continuity: Stacks continuity needs covering preservation and pullbacks,
   whereas Mathlib IsContinuous means precomposition preserves sheaves. We
   now state both. Read Sites/Continuous: pushforward is literally the lift of
   presheaf precomposition by i.op, hence its sections are F(U -> X), as in
   the node, without a redundant equality clause. Verdict: exact.
8. Clause (3), inverse image, adjunction and exactness:
   `∃ L : Sheaf ... ⥤ Sheaf ..., Nonempty (L ⊣ i.sheafPushforwardContinuous ...) ∧
   PreservesFiniteLimits L ∧ PreservesFiniteColimits L`.
   The left adjoint EXISTS as a conclusion, not an additional instance assumed
   for exactness. Read Sites/Pullback: `sheafPullback` is a chosen left adjoint,
   and `sheafPullbackConstruction.sheafPullback` is sheafified presheaf pullback.
   By uniqueness of left adjoints, the existential formulation is exactly
   the same inverse image up to its canonical isomorphism, with no private
   definition and no extra hypothesis. The definition-node's exactness means
   these two preservation properties. The item's clause (3) concerns sheaves
   of sets; the docstring follows its proof's abelian extension as motivation
   without adding an extra theorem conclusion. Verdict: exact.

Edge cases: X may be empty; bottom and top opens may coincide. Empty covering
families over the empty open are included by the topology, with no Nonempty
index assumption. Finite-limit preservation includes the empty diagram, so
terminal preservation is not restricted to nonempty limits. There is no
characteristic or numerical parameter. Universes u and v are bound explicitly;
sets live in `Type (max (u + 1) v)`, an arbitrary ambient universe large enough
for the u+1-sized category of étale schemes. This is the usual universe
placement for sheaves on this category, not a size restriction on X. Increasing
v permits arbitrarily large coefficient sets. No right-adjoint or sheafification
instance is imposed on X or on the inclusion.
