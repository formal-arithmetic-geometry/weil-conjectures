Reading its current statement revealed a substantive convention change: its definition now requires the generalized operad's zero fiber to be a Kan complex. That is suitable for the restricted algebra-presentability assertion but is not the generalized-operad notion of Gepner--Haugseng Definition 3.1.13 and cannot characterize all fibrant objects of their generalized model in Definition 3.2.9.

Explicit counterexample: take C to be the ordinary interval category [1], and M = N(C) times N(Delta opposite), projected to the second factor. Mark exactly the pairs whose first factor is an identity and whose second factor is inert. Every inert map has a cocartesian lift. The fiber diagram is constant N(C); each incidence category D_n is the edge/vertex subdivision of the finite interval and has contractible nerve, including D_0 a point. Thus its limit is N(C), and each mapping-fiber incidence limit is likewise the constant mapping space. Hence this is a generalized operad and is fibrant in the source's pattern model. Its zero fiber N([1]) is not Kan, because its nonidentity edge has no inverse. Importing the new Kan requirement into this item's fibrant-object clause would exclude this source fibrant object.
