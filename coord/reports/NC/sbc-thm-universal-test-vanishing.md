Its intended proof needs the isomorphism X_K≅X_L×_L K; for an arbitrary morphism Spec K→Spec A there is no such Cartesian identification. This is a missing relationship between existing parameters, not a change of the coefficient category or a new restriction to a nicer base. I made that relationship explicit, as in Stacks 0EYU's geometric generic test and the actual CurveTestDatum.isPullback field. A concrete failing type check for the old wording: take an algebraically closed valued field L extending k((t)), with k algebraically closed and A its valuation ring, and set K=L. Besides the inclusion A→L there is the residue map A→k→L, sending the nonzero element t to zero. The latter does not extend to Frac(A)=L, so one cannot form the asserted factorization through the specified Spec L. The revised statement uses the former map. Signed integers d remain allowed; step 1 replaces d by |d|, whose quotient group is identical.
