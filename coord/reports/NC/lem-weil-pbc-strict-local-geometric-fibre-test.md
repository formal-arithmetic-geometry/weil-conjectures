## Statement disputed and corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: The imported fixed-lift equivalence, also literally stated in Stacks Tag 0EZV, is false. Testing one fibre does not test all branches over the same geometric point. The corrected statement replaces the single second comparison by the comparisons for **every** lift v:Spec K→A of the fixed t:Spec K→S. It retains the first-to-selected-u implication used downstream and all coefficient and morphism generality. It is a fibrewise test, not a change to a target or to a unit's theorem.

Here is an explicit counterexample to the original statement. Work over an algebraically closed field k of characteristic zero. Let

S=Spec k[x,y]/(y²−x²(x+1)), s=(0,0),
X=Spec k[t], f given by x=t²−1, y=t(t²−1), and take xbar at t=1.

The curve is irreducible and has two normalization branches at s, t=1 and t=−1. Let A be its strict-local ring at s, and let B be the strict-local ring of X at t=1. In A there is the unique henselian root r of r²=1+x with r≡1 modulo the maximal ideal. The two branch quotients are A/(y−xr) and A/(y+xr); the first is B, with r mapping to t. This follows concretely from t²=1+x and xt=y: in the t=1 local factor t=r, and the remaining relation is y=xr; the quotient is the regular henselian local normalization branch. The other factor has t=−r.

Take K an algebraic closure of k(t). The fraction field of each strict-local normalization branch is separable algebraic over k(t), so both admit embeddings into K over the function field of S. Choose u:A→B→Frac(B)→K from the t=1 branch. For q=0 and the constant nonzero sheaf F=Z/2 on Spec K, B⊗_(A,u) K=K. The selected strict-local fibre map is thus the identity of F(K), an isomorphism.

On the other hand E=Spec(A⊗_R K), R=k[x,y]/(y²−x²(x+1)), has two nonempty clopen parts distinguished by r=t and r=−t. Indeed r²=t² in this tensor product and 2t is invertible, so e+=(r+t)/(2t) and e−=(t−r)/(2t) are complementary idempotents. Both parts are nonempty by the two branch embeddings just described. The map Y=Spec(B⊗_R K)→E has image only in e+=1, because in B the element r equals t=y/x after passing to the generic field. The section of F_E equal to 1 on e−=1 and 0 on e+=1 is a nonzero section killed by restriction to Y. Hence the first comparison is not injective. The second comparison is an isomorphism and the first is not, contradicting the imported equivalence.
