## Argument for the new obligation (not a split of that node)

The new lemma is the algebraic-closure counterpart of the already-existing finite-E local-triviality lemma. It is deliberately kept as a statement-only node. A way to split it is to refine presentation objects by the weakly contractible affine covering family and use the existing condensed point-basis evaluation statement. On such an affine U the coefficient sections are exactly bounded-image continuous functions on pi_0(U); finitely many matrix entries lie in one finite E, and their pullbacks to |U| are genuine continuous functions. The finite-field bound is local to a matrix, never assumed uniform on an arbitrary infinite covering.

Define the cokernel dimension at a geometric field by exact evaluation there, as in the existing geometric-field-proetale-evaluation node. Flat going-down lifts a generalization of a base point to a presentation object; the values of continuous functions to the Hausdorff field are equal along specialization. Hence the intrinsic dimension is constant under specialization on X. Finitely many irreducible components of a noetherian scheme, each with its generic point, show that every rank level is clopen. On a presentation object over a fixed rank level, cover by nonvanishing maximal minors and perform the block calculation B^{-1}, H-DB^{-1}C. Its entries lie in the same finite E as the local matrix. The constant-rank hypothesis makes the Schur complement pointwise zero; the faithful function description on the weakly contractible basis makes it the zero section. The cokernel is then finite free on these opens. This is not an invocation of global finite-field descent, and in particular does not use any of the nodes which already depend on the current item. A future split must cite the existing basis evaluation, geometric-field evaluation, flat going-down, noetherian finite-component, and sobriety nodes at the respective steps.
