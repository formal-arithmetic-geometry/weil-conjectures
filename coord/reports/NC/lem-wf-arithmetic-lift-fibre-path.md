For the one new node I checked every condition of 0BTV's second assertion. A separated finite-type scheme over a field is qcqs, its constant-field base changes are again separated finite type, and their transition maps are affine. The index is the set of finite intermediate fields, directed by compositum. The coordinate algebras on a base-changed affine chart are A tensor_K E; their union is A tensor_K L, and these identifications commute with restriction, giving the required limit X_L. The new statement assumes connectedness both at the limit and at every stage, and specifies the composite geometric points and all transition maps. Thus it is a specialization of 0BTV, without a hidden Noetherian, finite-field, normal-extension or smallness hypothesis. It has only a statement and searched metadata, not a proof sketch inside its statement or a second-level split.
