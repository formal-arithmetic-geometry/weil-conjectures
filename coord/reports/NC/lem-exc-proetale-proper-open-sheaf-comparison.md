## Mathematical argument and sources

The proof uses the actual small pro-etale site and its fpqc covering condition, not ordinary geometric stalks. It proves the chosen adjunction transpose monic by exact sheafification. On an affine base with quasi-compact open, use the finite principal-open/closed-specialization localization cover of Stacks 09AH. Its localization member has every point specializing into the complement. Proper closedness lifts that specialization in the source by taking the image of the closure of a point. Affine specialization-localization charts then form a genuine finite pro-etale cover of that source, and Stacks 09AG annihilates the zero-extension's sections on them. Both sides have zero sections on the base localization member; lifts on the principal opens and that member glue because the comparison is monic.

For an arbitrary open, a section on the proper inverse image has finitely many local zero-extension presheaf representatives after finite affine refinement. The images in the affine base of those representative objects that factor through the open are quasi-compact. Finitely many principal opens contained in the original open contain all those images. The local representatives lift to extension from this smaller quasi-compact open and glue using the monic inclusion. Apply the quasi-compact-open case and check compatibility with the original map by its open-adjunction transpose. This avoids both an unjustified all-sheaf proper-base-change theorem and any claim about arbitrary derived direct image preserving coproducts.
