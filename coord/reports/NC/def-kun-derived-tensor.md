Stacks and def-pc-kflat-complex place the K-flat factor on the right whereas the item places it on the left. I checked the explicit sign swap rather than assuming a coefficient/site comparison: in bidegree (p,q) send x tensor y to (-1)^(pq) y tensor x. The coefficient of y tensor dx after the swap is (-1)^((p+1)q), agreeing with (-1)^(pq+q) in the swapped differential; the coefficient of dy tensor x is (-1)^(p+p(q+1))=(-1)^(pq), agreeing with the other differential term. Swapping twice multiplies by (-1)^(2pq)=1. Thus it is a chain isomorphism and the two predicates are the same elementary convention. This needs no new lemma node and is not an additional theorem smuggled into the definition.

I checked the small cases Lambda=0, the empty scheme, the zero complex, and complexes supported in degree zero: the direct-sum totalization and sign convention still make sense, and the formula reduces to ordinary tensor for a flat degree-zero representative. For arbitrary unbounded inputs I retained direct sums, not products, in each degree. Tensor is over the constant sheaf Lambda_T, never over O_T or a pro-etale completion.
