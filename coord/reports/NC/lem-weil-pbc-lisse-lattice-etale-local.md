Its section conclusion uses classicality and the filtered-colimit evaluation formula of Lemmas 5.1.1--5.1.2: locally refine the pro-etale covering by affine inverse limits of etale schemes, descend each chosen section to a finite etale stage, and use that the images of these stages contain the images of the original covering members. These stage maps cover X. No assertion that an arbitrarily specified pro-etale cover itself is etale is made.
Small cases: on a geometric point and rank one, cosets are E^times/A^times and distinguish the lattices varpi^m A, so the discrete set is generally infinite; on rank zero the coset set is a singleton and the lattice is zero; on the empty scheme the empty covering suffices. Over a field the theorem asks only etale-local existence and adds no global monodromy condition. A sheaf with an empty local value cannot satisfy the comparison node's hypothesis of sections on every covering member.
