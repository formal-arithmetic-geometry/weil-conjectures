1. The regular local ring is `(R : Type u) [CommRing R] [IsRegularLocalRing R]`.
   Its regular parameters are `t : Fin d → R` and
   `ht : IsLocalRing.IsRegularSystemOfParameters (List.ofFn t)`.
   Lean's zero-based indices `i.val < r` correspond to the node's indices 1 through r.
   `hr : 1 ≤ r` and `hrd : r ≤ d` give precisely its numerical bounds.
2. `J I : Finset (Fin d)` give subsets of all parameter indices. `hJ : J.Nonempty`
   states nonemptiness, `hJB` states that J meets the first r indices, and `hI` restricts
   I to those indices. `j : Fin d`, `hj : j ∈ J` specify the chart. No restriction on
   the size or ordering of J or I is added.
3. `Chart t J j` is literally
   `MvPolynomial (↥(J.erase j)) R ⧸ relations t J j`; `relations` is generated by
   `C (t i) - C (t j) * X i`. The first conjunct gives an R-algebra equivalence to
   the existing `AffineBlowup.chartRing (center t J) (t j)` and identifies every ratio
   variable with the actual fraction `t_i/t_j` in `Localization.Away (t j)`.
   Thus the displayed quotient is a presentation of the existing blowup chart, not
   an arbitrary replacement. The algebra equivalence also fixes all base coefficients.
4. `IsRegularRing (Chart t J j)` states chart regularity, with Mathlib's Noetherian
   and every-prime-localization definition.
5. `equation ... none` is `t_j`. For `some i`, `strictEquation` is `u_i` when
   i belongs to `J.erase j`, and the image of `t_i` when i is outside J; for i=j
   it is 1. `labels B j` is `{none} ∪ some '' (B.erase j)` with B the first r indices.
   These are exactly the three lists of hyperplanes in the node, without duplicating
   the exceptional hyperplane even when j itself is a boundary index.
   The third conjunct equates the radical of the extended principal product ideal
   to `unionIdeal`, the intersection of these hyperplane ideals. On Spec a quotient's
   reduction is obtained by replacing its defining ideal by its radical. Thus this is
   equality of reduced closed subschemes, stronger than merely equal supports only
   in syntax, and mathematically precisely the stated reduced inverse image.
6. For every `p : PrimeSpectrum (Chart t J j)`, the next conjunct supplies a list s
   forming a recorded regular system of parameters of `Localization.AtPrime p.asIdeal`.
   It supplies an injective map from the labels whose equations vanish at p to the
   positions in s, and `s.get (f k)` is exactly that localized equation. Hyperplanes
   not passing through p have unit equations there and need no parameter position.
   This is the exact local-parameter assertion, without claiming all ratio coordinates
   generate the maximal ideal at an arbitrary non-coordinate point.
7. For every finite subset S of boundary labels, `intersection` is the ideal generated
   by the chosen equations, hence its quotient is the scheme-theoretic intersection.
   `Subsingleton` means its Spec is empty; otherwise the quotient is `IsRegularRing`.
   At each prime containing the intersection ideal, the displayed `ringKrullDim`
   equality gives ambient local dimension = quotient local dimension + `S.card`.
   This is pure codimension equal to the number of selected hyperplanes, component
   by component, including all their specializations. No empty intersection is
   assigned a spurious dimension or nonempty point.
8. `Ideal.span {∏ i ∈ I, t i} ⊓ center t J` is the ideal of H union M.
   Its map along the chart algebra map is the inverse-image ideal. Taking its radical
   and equating it to `unionIdeal ... (labels I j)` states precisely the reduced
   preimage as exceptional plus the strict transforms indexed by I. Products over
   empty I are 1, as in the node.
9. For every `i ∈ I`, the final conjunct uses the recorded

   closed immersion of the existing strict-transform construction. Computing it
