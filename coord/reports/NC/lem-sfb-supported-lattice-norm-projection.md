## Intended arguments for the statement-only auxiliaries

These arguments explain the interfaces and the small-case tests; the new node files themselves have no proof steps or proof dependencies.

**Bounded algebraic coordinates.** Choose a countable spanning sequence starting with the prescribed nonzero vector. Inductively keep an orthogonal basis of the finite-dimensional subspace already treated. For a new vector outside that space, its distance to that subspace has a minimum: outside a sufficiently large closed ball the distance is larger than its value at zero, and closed balls in that finite-dimensional space are compact over the local field E. Subtract a best approximation. The resulting new vector is orthogonal to the old subspace. Indeed when the old and new norms are unequal the ultrametric inequality gives equality with their maximum; when equal, a smaller norm would contradict best approximation. Induction exhausts the algebraic spanning sequence; it never takes a limit in V. Rescale each new basis vector by an E power of a uniformizer to put its norm in one common bounded interval, and keep b_0 unchanged. The orthogonality identity gives the asserted uniform two-sided bound. Coordinate subset projections have norm at most one in the weighted coordinates. Basis injections and their inverses are bounded by the fixed ratio of the upper and lower weights. The one-dimensional case is the chosen vector alone. Infinite-dimensional incomplete finite-support sequence space is a model example, not excluded by the theorem.

**Rank-one corner reconstruction.** Write I for the ideal of continuous finite-rank operators in D. First show H=IH. In finite dimension I=D. In infinite dimension suppose H/IH is nonzero. There is an uncountable almost-disjoint family of infinite subsets of N: label finite binary strings by natural numbers and take the initial segments along each infinite binary branch. The coordinate projection p_A onto any member is continuous. Coordinate enumeration gives continuous u_A,v_A with v_A u_A=1 and u_A v_A=p_A. For distinct members, p_A p_B has finite rank, hence acts as zero on H/IH. Each p_A has nonzero image on a nonzero D/I-module, because v_A u_A=1; the uncountably many pairwise orthogonal nonzero images supply an uncountable linearly independent family, impossible in a countable-dimensional E-space. Thus H=IH.

Every continuous finite-rank operator is a finite sum of rank-one operators v times lambda with lambda continuous: choose coordinates on its finite-dimensional image, or finite coordinate functionals on the finite union of supports of a basis of that image. Each rank-one operator factors as theta_v followed by w maps to lambda(w)b_0, and the second factor has image in eH. Hence the displayed map V tensor_E eH to H is onto. For injectivity, write a finite tensor with independent v_i; choose continuous finite-coordinate functionals mu_i with mu_i(v_j)=delta_ij. Apply the operator w maps to mu_i(w)b_0 to an asserted zero image. The result is h_i=0. This also proves D-linearity and naturality, and treats H=0 and finite V without a topological assumption on H. This is why a mere finite matrix Morita citation would not establish the infinite case.

**Geometric fibres.** At an algebraically closed scheme point every pro-etale covering of the final point has a section: a nonempty weakly etale affine object has a rational point over the algebraically closed field. Thus that final object is weakly contractible, its evaluation is exact, and sheafification does not change evaluation there. Combine with exact Cartesian coefficient inverse image. Evaluation of a sheafified balanced tensor is ordinary tensor over F, which proves the ordinary symmetric monoidal assertion. On a flat-term K-flat representative this becomes the stated derived comparison, since all complexes of vector spaces over a field are K-flat; insertion of one is preserved. Coefficient restriction changes none of the underlying evaluated groups.

For constructible detection, restrict to each locally closed stratum and trivialize the finite constant local models. A finite presentation over the scalar field is finite-dimensional, hence gives a finite free module. Morphisms between such local models are matrices of continuous F-valued coefficient sections (continuous-function realization). Each geometric point lifts to the trivializing pro-etale cover, since its pulled-back covering splits. The map is invertible precisely where the fibre matrix is invertible; if its fibre matrices are all invertible, the determinant never vanishes and the pointwise matrix inverse is continuous, so is an inverse section. This gives sheaf-level detection on each stratum, and open/closed localization gives detection on Z. Exactness applies the same argument to every cohomology map, proving the bounded derived assertion. There is no claim of conservativity on arbitrary pro-etale sheaves. In particular the familiar nonzero condensed quotient with zero point value is outside this finite-local-model domain. Empty schemes and zero-rank local models cause no exception.
