## Corrections and scope

The item's mathematical statement agrees with 09AG and has not changed. Doubled TeX escapes were normalized. A tempting reuse of lem-weil-pbc-wcontractible-closed-localization would impose finite definability of the closed subset; it is not used. The item assumes neither that Z is finitely presented nor that its complement is retrocompact. Ordinary geometric stalks are not used to test pro-etale sheaf vanishing.

The source's final sentence that there are no morphisms into U needs an empty-object qualification: the empty scheme always has a morphism to U. The proof instead handles it by G(empty)=0 using the empty covering. This is a correction to the proof, not a statement dispute. For a nonempty W, use an actual point specializing into the closed fiber and the fiber-product universal property, rather than asserting that a nonempty scheme cannot map into a disjoint locus without identifying the point.
