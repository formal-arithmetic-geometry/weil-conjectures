## Source error

Read Milne, Algebraic Groups, Theorem 3.31, printed pp.73--74 (PDF pp.85--86). The assertion that its map c is injective because the augmentation ideal is a direct summand of A does not establish injectivity over A. For A=k[t]/(t^2), the ideal (t) is not an A-module direct summand: an A-linear projection p:A to (t) must satisfy p(t)=t p(1)=0, so cannot restrict to the identity. In characteristic 2 this A itself has the additive Hopf structure, so the objection occurs inside the allowed Hopf setting. The vector-space splitting does not justify tensoring over A.

The conclusion is nevertheless true. The repair first proves A finite-dimensional by monomials, uses its self-injectivity to obtain an A-linear retraction of A into B, and then uses the split coideal criterion. Only after flatness has been obtained independently does the original tensor-kernel argument become valid. There is no invocation of lem-sfb-hopf-subalgebra-faithfully-flat, its geometric image factorization, or any descendant that would close a cycle.
