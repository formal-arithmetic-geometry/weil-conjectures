## Source proof completion

The source begins with density and says it suffices to construct neighbourhoods of separable closed points. Density plus openness alone does not imply covering: for example the complement of the closed inseparable point u^p=t in A^1 over F_p(t) is a proper open containing every separable closed point. This is a missing justification, not a dispute with the lemma's statement. The unit and the existing project proof supply the justification: a component above an etale slice maps onto the entire original fibre component. Steps 2, 4 and 9 write that argument explicitly.

Here the existing relative-section-component node explicitly supplies geometrically irreducible fibres. Step 8 uses exactly that statement, and spells out the elementary open-subset argument. The second slice is used to retain coverage of an arbitrary original point without assuming that one affine open contains both that point and the original section point.
