## Statement correction — STATEMENT-DISPUTED

The imported last sentence attributes the counits for arbitrary finite commutative rings A and B to `sk-lem-sp-finite-existence`. That node's statement quantifies only over the coefficient ring Z/ell^n. For example, A = F_ell[epsilon]/(epsilon^2) is a finite ell-primary commutative ring but is not any Z/ell^n: its characteristic is ell and its cardinality is ell^2. Instantiating that cited node at A is therefore a coefficient-type error. This is a scope error in the reference, not a counterexample to the coefficient-comparison theorem.

I replaced that reference with `sk-prop-sf-rf-shriek-preservation`, whose statement explicitly supplies the constructible adjunction for every finite commutative ell-primary A. Both displayed comparisons and the counit equation are retained. I made explicit the standing scheme setting from `sk-def-sfb-dc-setting` (positive-characteristic field, ell invertible, separated finite-type schemes), the meaning of smooth relative dimension, and Dctf for a general finite ring using the general pro-etale constructibility and Tor-amplitude definitions. These are the existing source's standing hypotheses and notions, not an added flatness, perfectness, DVR-coefficient or finite-projective-dimension restriction. The definition of the canonical b is made explicit to ensure that the exceptional comparison uses the same map.
