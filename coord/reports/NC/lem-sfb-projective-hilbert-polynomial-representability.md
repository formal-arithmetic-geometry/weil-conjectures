### Original step 2 → lem-sfb-bounded-hilbert-grassmann-chart

Choose $m\geq1$ from [lem-sfb-hilbert-uniform-section-bound]; thus every flat finitely presented closed subscheme of $\mathbf P^n_T$ with polynomial $P$ is recovered from its degree-$m$ ideal sections, and all higher ideal sections are their multiples. Write $V_d=\mathcal O_S[x_0,\ldots,x_n]_d$ and $N=\binom{n+m}{n}$. The degree-$m$ quotient has rank $r=P(m)$. If this is not an integer in $[0,N]$, no nonempty test scheme admits a family, and the empty $S$-scheme represents this polynomial functor. Otherwise [lem-sfb-free-quotient-grassmann-projective] gives the H-projective Grassmannian $G=G_S(N,r)$ and its universal exact sequence $0\to R_m\to V_{m,G}\to Q_m\to0$. This uses the free module of all ambient projective coordinates, so it works without any flatness of $E/S$.

Uses: lem-sfb-hilbert-uniform-section-bound, lem-sfb-free-quotient-grassmann-projective.

### Original step 3 → lem-sfb-bounded-hilbert-grassmann-chart

Work first over an affine open $U=\operatorname{Spec}A$ of $S$. Let $I_E\subset A[x_0,\ldots,x_n]$ be the homogeneous ideal supplied by [lem-sfb-projective-closed-subscheme-homogeneous-ideal]. On $G_U$ form the graded quotient
$$\mathcal B=\mathcal O_{G_U}[x_0,\ldots,x_n]/(I_E,R_m),$$
where $(R_m)$ is the ideal generated by the universal degree-$m$ kernel and $I_E$ means its coefficientwise image. These ideals are locally finitely generated: $A$ is Noetherian and $R_m$ is finite locally free. Apply [lem-sfb-graded-tail-rank-stratum] to the polynomial algebra and its finite graded module $\mathcal B$, imposing rank $P(d)$ for every $d\geq m$. This yields a finite-type immersion $Y_U\to G_U$, with that universal condition for arbitrary test schemes. If some $P(d)$ for $d\geq m$ is negative or nonintegral, there can be no family by [lem-sfb-hilbert-uniform-section-bound], and use $Y_U=\varnothing$.

Uses: lem-sfb-projective-closed-subscheme-homogeneous-ideal, lem-sfb-graded-tail-rank-stratum, lem-sfb-hilbert-uniform-section-bound.

### Original step 4 → lem-sfb-bounded-hilbert-grassmann-chart

On $Y_U$ the graded pieces $\mathcal B_d$ have rank $P(d)$ for $d\geq m$. By [lem-sfb-flat-graded-tail-proj], $Z=\operatorname{Proj}_{Y_U}\mathcal B$ is a flat finitely presented closed subscheme of $\mathbf P^n_{Y_U}$ with polynomial $P$, and its equations contain $I_E$, so it lies in $E_{Y_U}$. The same holds after any pullback. Conversely a family $Z_T\subset E_T$ with polynomial $P$ gives, by [lem-sfb-hilbert-uniform-section-bound], a quotient $V_{m,T}\twoheadrightarrow p_*\mathcal O_{Z_T}(m)$ and hence a map to $G_U$. Its kernel generates the whole ideal sheaf and all its degree-$d$ ideal sections for $d\geq m$. Thus the quotient algebra $\mathcal B_T$ has degree-$d$ piece $p_*\mathcal O_{Z_T}(d)$, of rank $P(d)$, and the map factors uniquely through $Y_U$.

Uses: lem-sfb-flat-graded-tail-proj, lem-sfb-hilbert-uniform-section-bound, lem-sfb-graded-tail-rank-stratum.

### Original step 5 → lem-sfb-bounded-hilbert-grassmann-chart

These two operations are inverse, including over nonreduced and non-Noetherian test schemes. For a map $T\to Y_U$, the composite
$$V_{m,T}\twoheadrightarrow Q_{m,T}\twoheadrightarrow (\mathcal B_T)_m\longrightarrow p_*\mathcal O_{Z_T}(m)$$
is the ordinary restriction of degree-$m$ polynomials. The last composite is surjective by [lem-sfb-hilbert-uniform-section-bound]. Its target, $Q_{m,T}$, and $(\mathcal B_T)_m$ are all locally free of rank $P(m)$, so the two surjections between equal-rank free modules are isomorphisms (locally, a surjective square matrix has invertible determinant). Consequently one recovers exactly the original Grassmannian quotient. Starting with $Z_T$, generation of its ideal in [lem-sfb-hilbert-uniform-section-bound] gives back its closed subscheme, not just its point set. All arrows used are the restriction and multiplication maps, and their arbitrary-base-change compatibility is part of [lem-sfb-hilbert-uniform-section-bound] and [lem-sfb-flat-graded-tail-proj]. This proves the natural bijection $\operatorname{Hom}_U(T,Y_U)=\operatorname{Hilb}^P_{E_U/U}(T)$.

Uses: lem-sfb-hilbert-uniform-section-bound, lem-sfb-flat-graded-tail-proj.

### Original step 6 → lem-sfb-hilbert-chart-gluing-projectivity

For affine opens $V\subset U\subset S$, the inverse image of $V$ in $Y_U$ and the constructed $Y_V$ represent the same family functor. By [lem-pc-representable-unique] they have a unique isomorphism preserving the family; these isomorphisms satisfy the identity and triple-overlap identities by uniqueness. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-relative-glueing-affine-opens] to glue them to $Y\to S$, representing the constant-$P$ functor. The degree-$m$ quotients agree on overlaps, so they give $j:Y\to G$. On each target open $G_U$, it is the immersion above; hence $j$ is an immersion. Also $Y\to S$ is of finite type. Indeed $Y_U\to G_U$ is of finite type by [lem-sfb-graded-tail-rank-stratum], and $G_U\to U$ is proper by the Grassmannian lemma and [lem-kun-projective-implies-proper]. Local finite type is closed under composition by clause (e) of [lem-weil-pbc-proper-fp-permanence]; quasi-compactness follows by clause (2) of [lem-weil-pbc-qcqs-morphism-permanence]. A finite affine cover of $S$ suffices. No separate separatedness argument is needed for the full DVR criterion below. The universal closed families agree as subschemes on overlaps, so their ideals glue to the universal $Z_Y\subset E_Y$.

Uses: lem-pc-representable-unique, lem-weil-pbc-relative-glueing-affine-opens, lem-sfb-graded-tail-rank-stratum, lem-sfb-free-quotient-grassmann-projective, lem-kun-projective-implies-proper, lem-weil-pbc-proper-fp-permanence, lem-weil-pbc-qcqs-morphism-permanence.

### Original step 7 → lem-sfb-projective-dvr-flat-closure

We verify the DVR criterion directly. Let $R$ be any DVR over $S$, $K$ its fraction field, and $Z_K\subset E_K$ the family given by a map $\operatorname{Spec}K\to Y$. On each affine chart $\operatorname{Spec}C$ of $E_R$, let $J_K\subset C\otimes_RK$ be its ideal and put $J=C\cap J_K$, the inverse image under $C\to C\otimes_RK$. Contraction commutes with localizing $C$, so these ideals glue. They are finite because $E_R$ is Noetherian. Moreover $C/J\hookrightarrow(C\otimes_RK)/J_K$ is $R$-torsion-free, hence flat by [lem-trf-valuation-torsionfree-flat]. The resulting closed subscheme $Z_R\subset E_R\subset\mathbf P^n_R$ is finitely presented and flat. Its Hilbert polynomial is everywhere $P$ by [lem-sfb-flat-projective-hilbert-polynomial-locality] and connectedness of $\operatorname{Spec}R$. Thus it gives a lift to $Y$. Uniqueness follows because for any flat quotient $C/J'$ the injection into its localization at $K$ forces $J'=C\cap J_K$: if $c$ vanishes after localization, a nonzero scalar kills its class, which flatness forbids: tensoring the injective multiplication map $R\xrightarrow{a}R$ with $C/J\prime$ keeps it injective for every nonzero $a\in R$. No excellence or finite-type assumption on $R/S$ is used.

Uses: lem-trf-valuation-torsionfree-flat, lem-sfb-flat-projective-hilbert-polynomial-locality.

### Original step 8 → lem-sfb-hilbert-chart-gluing-projectivity

By [lem-sfb-noetherian-dvr-properness], the finite-type map $Y\to S$ is proper. Since $G\to S$ is separated (it is projective by [lem-sfb-free-quotient-grassmann-projective] and [lem-kun-projective-implies-proper]), [lem-weil-pbc-proper-to-separated] shows that $j:Y\to G$ is proper. It is an immersion, so [lem-weil-pbc-proper-immersion-closed] makes it a closed immersion. Compose with the Plücker closed immersion of [lem-sfb-free-quotient-grassmann-projective] using [lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-comp]. Thus $Y=H_P$ is H-projective in the specified finite-projective-space convention, including the Grassmannian endpoint cases. In particular it is separated and of finite type by [lem-kun-projective-implies-proper] and the definition of properness.

Uses: lem-sfb-noetherian-dvr-properness, lem-sfb-free-quotient-grassmann-projective, lem-kun-projective-implies-proper, lem-weil-pbc-proper-to-separated, lem-weil-pbc-proper-immersion-closed, lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-comp.

### Original step 9 → Parent assembly step 4

Set $H=\coprod_PH_P$ and take the disjoint union of their universal closed families in $E_H=\coprod_PE_{H_P}$. A family on $T$ has the canonical open-and-closed decomposition $T=\coprod_PT_P$ from [lem-sfb-flat-projective-hilbert-polynomial-locality]; on $T_P$ it gives the unique classifying map to $H_P$, and these maps glue to $T\to H$. Conversely the inverse images of the components under $T\to H$ recover those pieces and their families. This proves representability of the full functor, allowing nonconstant fibre polynomials on disconnected bases. Finally for any $S'\to S$ and any $S'$-scheme $T$, the identification $(E\times_SS')\times_{S'}T=E\times_ST$ identifies its families and their pullbacks exactly. Hence $H\times_SS'$ and its pulled-back universal family represent the base-changed functor, with pieces $H_P\times_SS'$. The natural bijections already constructed give this identification; [lem-pc-representable-unique] makes it canonical. The zero-polynomial piece is $S$, since each of its geometric fibres must be empty by [lem-sfb-flat-projective-hilbert-polynomial-locality], and a scheme with every fibre empty is empty.

Uses: lem-sfb-flat-projective-hilbert-polynomial-locality, lem-pc-representable-unique.
