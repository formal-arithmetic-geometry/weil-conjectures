### Returned Step 1

Step 1 (domains and twists). Write $A=A_{E,n}$, $B=A_{E,m}$ and $Q_T=(-)\otimes_A^LB$. These are positive finite stages, on the finite-type schemes required by [def-weil-exceptional-inverse-image]. If $K$ has Tor amplitude $[a,b]$, [lem-sf-flat-model] and [lem-sf-reduction-flat-model] give a flat model of $Q_TK$ in the same interval. The finite-reduction assertion of [lem-fix-operation-reduction] supplies the bounded constructible comparisons for pullback, tensor and compact supports on these finite-Tor domains. Use [lem-coef-finite-exceptional] with its larger index equal to our $n$ and smaller index equal to our $m$ to obtain $Q_XRf_n^!K\cong Rf_m^!Q_YK$, with the unit, counit and quotient-cocycle compatibilities. No flatness of $A\to B$ is asserted. By [lem-pur-twist-sheaf-locally-free], $A_T(1)$ is a locally free rank-one sheaf and is the specified reduction of the integral line. Tensor associativity gives $A_T(1)\otimes_A B=B_T(1)$. Tensor powers give this identity for positive twists; the zero twist is $A_T$, and for negative twists the dual of a rank-one free module reduces to its dual (locally, both maps are $\operatorname{Hom}_A(A,A)\otimes_A B=B$). Thus the definition in [def-coefficient-twists-detailed] gives $Q_T(K(r))\cong (Q_TK)(r)$ for every integer $r$. Derived tensor commutes with shifts, giving the left vertical comparison in the square; the exceptional comparison gives the right vertical one.
