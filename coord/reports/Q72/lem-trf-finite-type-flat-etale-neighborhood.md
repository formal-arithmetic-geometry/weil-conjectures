For step 1 I checked the actual statements of the existing Jacobson-Nakayama and surjective-on-stalk nodes. The finite stalk comes directly from a local finite generating surjection. The ideal generated by the base maximal ideal lies inside the source local maximal ideal, so Nakayama applies, without assuming the base Noetherian. The finite-dimension node is for finite-type schemes over a field, precisely the affine closed fibre in use. Restricting a chain of closed subsets bounds support dimension, using the dimension definition.

For step 2 I read `lem-weil-pbc-local-ring-spec-limit-affine-neighbourhoods` and the object clause of `lem-weil-pbc-etale-finite-data-descent`.
