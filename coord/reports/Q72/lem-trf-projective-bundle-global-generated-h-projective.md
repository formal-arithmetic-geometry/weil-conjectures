Returned Step 1; assigned to lem-trf-symmetric-sheaf-map-degreewise-surjective:

Choose a finite generating family $s_0,\ldots,s_r\in\Gamma(S,\mathcal E)$ ($r\geq0$; for $\mathcal E=0$ take $r=0$, $s_0=0$). By [def-weil-pbc-generating-sections], its evaluation map $\varepsilon:\mathcal O_S^{\oplus(r+1)}\twoheadrightarrow\mathcal E$ is surjective as a map of sheaves. Clause (a) of [lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-symmetric-algebra] gives the induced algebra map $\operatorname{Sym}(\varepsilon):\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal O_S^{\oplus(r+1)})\to\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal E)$ with $\operatorname{Sym}(\varepsilon)\circ j=j\circ\varepsilon$; on an affine open $U$ with $R=\mathcal O_S(U)$, clause (b) identifies it with the $R$-algebra map $\operatorname{Sym}_R(R^{\oplus(r+1)})\to\operatorname{Sym}_R(\mathcal E(U))$ sending $\iota(e_i)$ to $\iota(s_i|_U)$. Since $\operatorname{Sym}^n_R$ is spanned by products of $n$ degree-one elements and is the degree-$n$ summand [lem-weil-pbc-symmetric-algebra-graded], this map is degree-preserving and is the identity of $R$ in degree $0$. Surjectivity as a map of sheaves in each degree $n$: a section of $\operatorname{Sym}^n(\mathcal E)$ over an affine $U$ is a finite sum of products $\iota(t_1)\cdots\iota(t_n)$ with $t_k\in\mathcal E(U)$; by the definition of generation each $t_k$ is, on the members of an open cover, an $\mathcal O$-linear combination of the $s_i$, and on a common refinement by affine opens (finitely many $t_k$) each product, hence the sum, is the image of a polynomial expression in the $\iota(e_i)$. Thus $\operatorname{Sym}(\varepsilon)$ is a degree-preserving graded algebra map, surjective as a map of sheaves.

Returned Step 3; assigned to lem-trf-free-projective-bundle-base-change-space (comparison); the last closed-immersion composition and projectivity test remain in parent Step 3:

The relative-Proj $\mathbf P^r_S$ is identified over $S$ with $\mathbf P(\{0,\ldots,r\};S)=S\times_{\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf Z}\operatorname{Proj}\mathbf Z[T_0,\ldots,T_r]$ of [def-nagata-projective-space-base-change], the projective space used in [def-kun-projective]. Let $\mathcal A_{\mathbf Z}=\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal O^{\oplus(r+1)})$ on $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf Z$. By clause (b) of [lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-symmetric-algebra] and [lem-weil-pbc-symmetric-algebra-free-module-polynomial] (index the basis $e_0,\ldots,e_r$), $\Gamma(\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf Z,\mathcal A_{\mathbf Z})\cong\mathbf Z[T_0,\ldots,T_r]$, $\iota(e_i)\mapsto T_i$; it preserves degree since both degree-$n$ pieces are spanned by products of $n$ degree-one elements [lem-weil-pbc-symmetric-algebra-graded]. By [lemma-nagata-relative-proj-exists] applied to the affine open $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf Z$ itself, $\operatorname{Proj}_{\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf Z}(\mathcal A_{\mathbf Z})\cong\operatorname{Proj}\mathbf Z[T_0,\ldots,T_r]$ over $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf Z$ (an isomorphism of graded rings induces one of the Mathlib Proj schemes [def-nagata-affine-proj]). Let $g:S\to\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf Z$ be the unique map. The algebra $\mathcal B=\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal O_S^{\oplus(r+1)})$ is identified with the coefficientwise pullback $g^*\mathcal A_{\mathbf Z}$ in the sense of [def-nagata-graded-algebra-pullback-data]: for an affine $U'=\operatorname{Spec}R\subseteq S$ take $\alpha:\mathbf Z[T_0,\ldots,T_r]\to\mathcal B(U')\cong R[T_0,\ldots,T_r]$ (clause (b) and the polynomial leaf over $R$), $T_i\mapsto T_i$; in degree $n$, $R\otimes_{\mathbf Z}\mathbf Z[T]_n\to R[T]_n$ is an isomorphism of free $R$-modules on the degree-$n$ monomials, and these maps commute with restriction because restriction is coefficientwise and fixes each $j(e_i)$. Hence [lemma-nagata-relative-proj-base-change-arbitrary] gives an isomorphism $\mathbf P^r_S=\operatorname{Proj}_S(\mathcal B)\cong S\times_{\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf Z}\operatorname{Proj}_{\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf Z}(\mathcal A_{\mathbf Z})\cong\mathbf P(\{0,\ldots,r\};S)$ under which $p$ becomes the first projection [def-pc-fibre-product]. Composing $\iota$ of Step 2 with this isomorphism gives a closed immersion $i:\mathbf P_S(\mathcal E)\hookrightarrow\mathbf P(\{0,\ldots,r\};S)$ (a closed immersion followed by an isomorphism, directly from [def-weil-closed-immersion]) with $\pi=\mathrm{pr}_1\circ i$. This is exactly the data of [def-kun-projective] with $N=r$, so $\pi$ is H-projective.
