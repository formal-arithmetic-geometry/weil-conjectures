   the identity stratification is still available.
2. All-zero list: zero scalar models on the identity covering have zero finite
   projective terms and all reductions are zero; [0,0] satisfies every Tor test.
3. Empty scheme: a single empty stratum is permitted, as are empty covers of it;
   its module categories are zero. No Nonempty chart-index hypothesis is added.
4. At the first positive reduction n=0, the coefficient is R/(t), not the zero ring.
   For [R --t--> R] in degrees [-1,0], reduction has both possible cohomology
   degrees and correctly retains Tor interval [-1,0]. A cohomological interval
   alone was not used to infer Tor amplitude.
5. Shifts and finite sums: the interval is chosen after the finite list and
   before any quotient level or test sheaf, and can contain all term supports.
6. Torsion and arbitrary nonflat test sheaves are included: Tor amplitude tests
   arbitrary degree-zero sheaves, while flatness belongs only to the local models.

   live in u+1 without forcing those site universes equal. E is permitted in u+1;
