Step 1 uses the statement of def-sc-general-linear-group, which really defines SL as Spec(P/(D-1)), gives its group structure and its closed inclusion in GL. It also gives the determinant-one R-points, so a basis of E identifies exactly the functor in the item. def-weil-finite-type-morphism allows a finite affine cover by finitely generated algebras; here one affine with r² generators suffices. def-sbc-locally-finite-presentation explicitly uses finitely many variables and relations; here there is one relation. These are applications of definitions, not hidden geometric lemmas.

Step 2 uses polynomial evaluation from def-weil-pbc-mv-polynomial-ring, the ordinary quotient from def-weil-pbc-algebra-hom-quotient, and the contravariant affine ring/scheme correspondence from def-weil-affine-spectrum. The relation check is written: every determinant term contains exactly one first-column factor, so scaling that column by D^{-1} makes the determinant 1. The restriction back to SL sends D^{-1} to 1, proving qj=id on every coordinate. This proves the scheme map and its retraction property, not just a set map on field-points. No group-homomorphism property of q is used.

Step 3 uses only the smoothness clause of lem-sfb-general-linear-reductive and the lifting definition in def-sbc-smooth-morphisms. For an arbitrary affine first order thickening, the GL lift b exists by that clause; qb extends the original SL map because qj=id. Together with step 1's finite presentation this is the definition of smoothness. I verified Stacks Tag 02H0 in tags/tags: more-morphisms, definition-formally-smooth. This is a short explicit proof of a retract's lifting property, so creating a retract-smoothness node would be an elementary-node error B/C. There is no unstated smoothness-descent theorem.

Step 4 uses the geometric connectedness clause, not the reductivity clause, of the same GL theorem. It applies over the original field k and gives connectedness after the specified extension to its algebraic closure. The base-changed retraction is surjective because it has a section. The inverse images of a hypothetical nontrivial clopen partition give a nontrivial clopen partition of H_K, exactly contradicting def-weil-connected-scheme. Identity supplies nonemptiness, as that definition requires. This argument is written out instead of silently assuming that surjective images preserve connectedness.

Step 5 uses exactly the affine fibre-product formula of lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra and the existence/uniqueness of algebra maps out of a tensor product from lem-weil-pbc-algebra-tensor-pushout-universal-property. Step 6 uses def-weil-closed-immersion for an affine quotient presentation of U. Therefore U is affine and finite type, as required by lem-sfb-unipotent-rational-representation-fixed-vector. That node's statement assumes a faithful representation (injective for every K-algebra R), not a faithful closed immersion, and gives a fixed vector in every nonzero rational representation. The item provides exactly such a faithful representation with unipotent geometric matrices. The inclusion U→SL→GL is the representation to which the conclusion is applied; its unipotence is not assumed. Algebraic closure supplies algebraically closed K; r≥1 supplies the nonzero dimension. Smoothness of U is explicitly part of the reductivity test. This avoids the C4 fault of using a fixed-vector theorem only for the original faithful representation.
