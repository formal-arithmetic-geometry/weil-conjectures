For step 2, I checked the exactness node's stated chart normalization and finite freeness against the completed-system comparison's actual quotient/reconstruction maps. The latter gives equivalence with etale finite free systems and compatibility with pullback. A local integral trivialization of rank s rationalizes coordinatewise to rank s, so the existing rank theorem's covering-independent characterization forces s=r. The constant-section clause (1) of the lisse tensor/scalar node identifies finite quotient constant modules with their etale stalks; I do not substitute an unnormalized stalk map. This uses dimension of a finite free vector space, not an unjustified interchange of arbitrary inverse limits and tensor products. It proves the conclusion on every specified chart, rather than merely on one chosen trivialization.
