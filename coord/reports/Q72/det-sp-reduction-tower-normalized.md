Here is the returned counterexample explicitly. Let k be algebraically closed of characteristic different from ell and let T be the disjoint union of affine spaces A^d_k for d>=0. In each A^d choose a closed linear flag X_0 subset ... subset X_d=A^d and put M_d=direct sum_{j=0}^d (i_{X_j})_* R_{X_j}; form M on the disjoint union by these restrictions. Each affine open of T meets finitely many components and M is bounded and affine-locally constructible. On X_k minus X_{k-1}, reduction modulo ell has rank d-k+1. A locally closed stratum on which this sheaf is locally constant cannot contain two generic flag points eta_a, eta_b with a<b: a pro-etale neighborhood of eta_a carrying the locally constant model must, by flat going-down, also contain a point above eta_b whenever both lie in that stratum, contrary to the different ranks. Thus a partition into such strata needs at least d+1 pieces on A^d. No finite global partition works on T. The old arbitrary-scheme constructible conclusion is false.
