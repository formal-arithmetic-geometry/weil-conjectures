## Errors, gaps and corrections

The item was an unsplit import of an upstream sorry. Its statement is retained. The surrounding derived-category definitions and the upstream CommRing variable make the commutative coefficient convention explicit. The original same_as pointing to lem-kun-0f1j was inaccurate: that theorem has Z/n coefficients, whereas this item allows any finite commutative ring. I cleared same_as; I did not change the target to Z/n.

The ordinary 0F1J proof reduces the first complex to a flat sheaf before changing coefficients. This order matters even more here: restricting a flat Lambda-module to Z/N need not preserve its Tor lower bound. For example, for Lambda=Z/4 x Z/2, its free module has a Z/2 summand after restriction to Z/4. I instead use an existing finite ideal composition series, identify its simple quotients explicitly, and filter F. Over a residue field k I reduce to the existing prime-field theorem using the separability retract. This neither assumes that the finite ring is a product of fields nor divides by the extension degree.

The existing prime-torsion coefficient reduction is too specialized for both arbitrary Lambda to k and the non-surjective prime-field inclusion into k. The only new lemma is a general coefficient comparison including the equation for the actual tensor-exchange maps. Its statement does not assert that either exchange map is invertible and does not restate the item or its finite-field case.

No assertion about a general bounded-below flat complex being K-flat is used. Step 2 derives K-flatness of the tails from the chosen K-flat model and bounded flat quotient by a degreewise split tensor sequence. Both reductions use finite truncations and explicit lower bounds, not an uncited infinite-filtration convergence result.
