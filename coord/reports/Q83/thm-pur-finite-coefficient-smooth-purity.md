### lem-pur-vector-integration-chart-comparison: retained Step 5

For the vector-bundle model clause of the unit, let $Z$ be a separated finite-type $k$-scheme and let $\pi:E\to Z$ be a vector bundle of constant rank $d$. The field $k$ is Noetherian (its ideals are $0$ and $k$), and $Z$ is Noetherian by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian]. Write $c_\pi:\Lambda_E(d)[2d]\xrightarrow{\sim}R\pi^!\Lambda_Z$ for the inverse of [cor-pur-vector-bundle-purity]. Its normalization is determined by integration: [thm-amb-vector-bundle-purity] identifies the ordinary etale map with the adjoint of $t_\pi$, and [def-weil-exceptional-inverse-image] defines the constructible pro-etale adjoint by this etale/pro-etale transport. By [def-amb-vector-integration], on a trivializing open of $Z$ the trace is affine-space integration. More precisely, [lem-amb-affine-space-top-trace] specifies that integration as the counit of the ordered composite affine-line adjunctions, with the rank-one adjunction normalized by the restricted projective-line trace and rank zero by the identity adjunction. Its coefficient hypotheses hold: choose $N=\ell^r$ annihilating the finite ring $A_{E,n}$; $N$ is invertible on every $k$-scheme because $\ell\ne p$. The ordered affine-line comparison of [def-pur-affine-space-comparison] has this same adjoint trace: [def-pur-affine-line-purity-comparison] defines each factor as the adjoint of the restricted projective-line trace, and the counit of a composite $F_2F_1\dashv G_1G_2$ is $\epsilon_{2,K}\circ F_2(\epsilon_{1,G_2K})$. Iterating this formula shows that the adjoint of the ordered comparison is exactly the ordered trace specified in [lem-amb-affine-space-top-trace]; the two maps therefore agree by injectivity of the adjunction bijection. Thus on a trivializing open $c_\pi$ is the affine-space comparison, with its specified normalization.

Additional precision for its future worker: all coefficient levels are intended. The current affine-line comparison definition has a narrower named-datum domain; use the explicit extension of the ordered comparisons for every n >= 1 in `lem-pur-projection-purity-agreement`, rather than imposing ramification-index divisibility. `lem-pur-vector-integration-site-comparison` supplies the equality of actual transported trace transposes, not just an equivalence of categories. The equality of the ordered transposes with the transpose of the composite trace is the comparison argument assigned here; its general counit calculation is available as `lem-pur-composite-integration-counit`. No scalar-basis choice of a Tate line changes these maps.

### lem-pur-vector-model-chart-descent: retained Step 6

Choose a Zariski open cover $\{Z_i\}$ trivializing $E$, and let $E_i=E\times_Z Z_i$. The base-change compatibility clause of [cor-pur-dualizing-object-purity] applies to the canonical composite of the diagonal orientation with the tensor formula, which is exactly the map identified in Step 4. Apply this clause to each open base change $Z_i\hookrightarrow Z$; the schemes $Z_i,E_i$ remain separated and of finite type over $k$, and $\Lambda_{Z_i}$ has Tor amplitude zero. Consequently the restriction of $\gamma_{\pi,\Lambda_Z}$ to $E_i$ is the smooth purity map for $\mathbf A^d_{Z_i}\to Z_i$. By [lem-pur-projection-purity-agreement], it is the ordered affine-space comparison, which is the restriction of $c_\pi$ by the preceding step and the base-change compatibility of [cor-pur-vector-bundle-purity]. To deduce global equality, form $h=c_\pi^{-1}\circ\gamma_{\pi,\Lambda_Z}$, an endomorphism of $\Lambda_E(d)[2d]$. By [lem-pur-twist-sheaf-locally-free] and the tensor-power definition of [def-coefficient-twists-detailed], $\Lambda_E(d)$ is a rank-one locally free sheaf in degree zero. Cancel the common shift. By [lem-weil-pbc-derived-single-fully-faithful], $h$ then comes from a unique morphism of the degree-zero sheaf $\Lambda_E(d)$, hence from a section of its endomorphism sheaf. This endomorphism sheaf is canonically $\Lambda_E$: on any frame $e$ a linear endomorphism has the form $e\mapsto ae$, and replacing $e$ by $ue$ for a unit $u$ leaves $a$ unchanged. Thus the scalar descriptions glue independently of the frame. That section restricts to $1$ on every $E_i$, so the sheaf uniqueness axiom gives $h=1$ and $\gamma_{\pi,\Lambda_Z}=c_\pi$. Inverting gives precisely the vector-bundle model identification required by the unit, including rank zero.

In the new conditional statement c is any specified isomorphism with these local affine comparison restrictions, not only c_pi. The same proof forms h = c^{-1} gamma. Open base change identifies gamma locally using the *compatibility clause* of `cor-pur-dualizing-object-purity`, and `lem-pur-projection-purity-agreement` identifies it with Gamma at every positive coefficient level. The hypothesis identifies c with the same map. Cancel the common shift, use degree-zero full faithfulness, and compute the endomorphism sheaf of the rank-one Tate sheaf in frames exactly as above. Existing `lem-pur-degree-zero-derived-map-gluing` and `lem-pur-lisse-rank-one-aut` can supply the general sheaf-map gluing and scalar calculation when this task is split; this task also has to establish the compatibility of the particular diagonal construction with these actual chart restrictions.
