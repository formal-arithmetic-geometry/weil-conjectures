Its hypotheses allow the given absolute value extending Q_ell. Principal ideals are finitely generated by their single generator, giving exactly def-weil-pbc-noetherian-ring; there is no extra finiteness theorem hidden in this deduction.

Step 2 uses the explicit absolute value formula from def-weil-l-adic-numbers. Restriction of the given extended value gives |ell|=ell^{-1}; the maximal ideal was identified in step 1. Writing ell=varpi c proves ell^n=varpi^n c^n, so ell^n kills the entire quotient, including in ramified extensions. Since the residue field is nonzero and ell is prime, its characteristic is ell. This is an elementary calculation, not a cited definition standing in for an arithmetic theorem.

Step 3 checks all hypotheses of lem-weil-valued-dvr-finite-quotients: a nonarchimedean field, its actual valuation ring, DVR property, finite residue field and a generator of the maximal ideal. Its clauses give both the ideal equality and the specified surjective transitions. The statement of lem-weil-pbc-standard-quotients-perfect explicitly gives the finite-extension two-term resolution in degrees -1,0, its quotient augmentation and perfection. Thus the step takes this from its statement, not its proof. Stacks 0657 agrees with the ring-perfect notion used here.

Step 4 reads off every ring hypothesis of def-weil-pbc-adic-coefficient-datum, including finite-set and perfect-module requirements. It uses the actual compatible-family limit sheaf defined there. Step 5 reads def-weil-pbc-bounded-constructible-complete with its scheme hypotheses and compatible etale-origin witnesses; it does not extend that category to arbitrary schemes. The order bijection m to m+1 identifies every quotient and transition, so the index assertion needs no comparison lemma. Neither step identifies the completed sheaf with a discrete constant sheaf.

Small-case checks: n=1 gives a nonzero finite residue field and an injective uniformizer map in the resolution; E=Q_ell gives the usual Z_ell/ell^n case. For ramification index greater than one, ell is divisible by varpi but need not be a uniformizer, and the claimed ell^n annihilation remains valid. The zero scheme causes no issue for the limit-sheaf construction. The zero ring cannot occur as R or its residue field. There are no new statements to test and no long proof steps hiding new lemmas.
