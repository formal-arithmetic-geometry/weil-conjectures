### Old Step 2 → triangle child

The termwise functor $K(V):K(\mathcal A)\to K(\mathcal B)$ is exact as a triangulated functor. Indeed applying $V$ to $f-g=d h+h d$ preserves the homotopy equation, applying it to the signed shift differential gives the signed shift of $V(d)$, and the finite-biproduct comparison identifies $V(Y^n\oplus X^{n+1})$ with $V(Y^n)\oplus V(X^{n+1})$. Under this comparison the cone differential matrix $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}d_Y&f\\0&-d_X\end{smallmatrix}\right)$ becomes $\left(\begin{smallmatrix}V(d_Y)&V(f)\\0&-V(d_X)\end{smallmatrix}\right)$, with the same inclusion and negative projection. Thus $K(V)$ preserves the cone triangles defining the homotopy triangulation [lem-trf-additive-homotopy-category-triangulated]. By [lem-pur-quasi-isos-saturated-triangulated], $Q_{\mathcal B}$ is exact and quasi-isomorphisms in $K(\mathcal A)$ form a saturated triangulated-compatible multiplicative system. Consequently $F=Q_{\mathcal B}K(V)$ and this system satisfy Situation 05S8.
