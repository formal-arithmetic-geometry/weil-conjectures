## Argument behind the new prerequisite
Read groupoids.tex Tags 0EKK and 0EKL in full: the Laurent-coefficient coaction argument gives unique weight projections. Read descent.tex proposition-fpqc-descent-quasi-coherent (Tag 023T) for descent of the character line. Existing split-torus-torsor-frame-geometry gives the underlying frame torsors, but not section extraction. Locally trivialize the r frame lines; q_*O_P is the direct sum of Laurent monomial lines indexed by Z^r, with weight zero O_X. Transition changes multiply each monomial by an invertible function, so the weight lines N_chi glue, and their tautological monomials trivialize q^*N_chi. Tensoring by L gives q_*q^*L = direct sum of L tensor N_chi. On a finite affine cover of the quasi-compact base any section has finite support; component projection is compatible with restrictions. A nonzero global section therefore has a nonzero weight component, which is the required downstairs section. An inverse character changes N_chi to its dual and is harmless since all weights are allowed. This construction involves neither a group extension nor positivity, so it is strictly below the item and not a clause of it. It is not labelled elementary because it requires torsor descent and weight-sheaf gluing.
