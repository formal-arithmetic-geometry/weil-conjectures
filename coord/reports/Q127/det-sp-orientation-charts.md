### lem-pur-chart-cartesian-domain

Inherited Step 1:

Domain and target localization. Cover $Y$ by affine opens $V$ and put $X_V=f^{-1}V$, $U_V=U\cap X_V$ and $A_V=\mathbf A^d_V$. These are the actual restrictions of $f,j,e$. Finite-type schemes over $k$ are Noetherian by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian]; their open subschemes are Noetherian by [lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-open-subscheme-noetherian]. Hence all these open inclusions are quasi-compact and finite type by [lem-weil-pbc-qc-open-immersion-finite-type]. Finite type for their structure maps follows by composing finite sets of algebra generators on affine charts [def-weil-finite-type-morphism]. The affine $V$ is separated over $k$ by [lem-weil-pbc-affine-morphism-permanence](3). Separatedness is preserved by base change [lem-pod-separated-finite-type-base-change] and composition [lem-weil-pbc-proper-fp-permanence](e); thus $X_V,U_V,A_V$ and their fibre products used below are separated finite-type $k$-schemes. The affine-space projection is smooth of dimension $d$ by [sbc-lem-affine-space-smooth]; the other projections have dimension $d$ by [lem-sbc-smooth-relative-dimension-permanence], and restricting their sources to the displayed opens preserves smoothness and the pointwise fibre dimension by [def-sbc-smooth-morphisms]. We first compare the maps on $U_V$, writing $Y,X,U,A$ for these restricted schemes. The final step checks that this suffices without absolute separatedness of the original $Y$.

Inherited Step 2:

The chart is separated. Put $h=f|_U=p_de$. The diagonal of $h$ is the pullback of the closed immersion $\Delta_f:X\to X\times_YX$ along $U\times_YU\to X\times_YX$, so $h$ is separated by [def-weil-proper-morphism] and [lem-weil-pbc-proper-fp-permanence](b); this makes explicit the relative separatedness inherited from $f$. The map $\Delta_e:U\to U\times_AU$ is the pullback of $\Delta_h:U\to U\times_YU$ along $U\times_AU\to U\times_YU$: the fibre-product equation says the two $U$-components coincide. Since $h$ is separated, $\Delta_h$ is a closed immersion by [def-weil-proper-morphism], and its pullback is closed by [lem-weil-pbc-proper-fp-permanence](b). Thus $e$ is separated by that same definition. This proves the separatedness used in the blueprint instead of assuming that an arbitrary étale map is separated. Also $U$ is quasi-compact and $A$ quasi-separated, so $e$ is quasi-compact by [lem-weil-pbc-qcqs-morphism-permanence](1); its local finite presentation, part of [def-weil-etale-morphism], makes it finite type.

Inherited Step 3:

Cartesian centres on a neighbourhood. Write $W=U\times_YU$, $W_0=A\times_YA$, and $q=e\times e:W\to W_0$. Each factor of $q$ is a base change of $e$, hence étale by [lem-weil-pbc-etale-scheme-base-change]; their composite is smooth of relative dimension $0+0=0$, hence étale, by [lem-sbc-smooth-relative-dimension-permanence](2), including its explicit dimension-zero étale case. The scheme-theoretic inverse image of $\Delta_A$ is $Z=U\times_AU$, a closed subscheme of $W$, since $\Delta_A$ is closed. The projection $Z\to U$ is the base change of $e$ along $e$, hence étale by [lem-weil-pbc-etale-scheme-base-change] and separated of finite type by [lem-pod-separated-finite-type-base-change] (Step 2), with section $\Delta_U$. By [lem-weil-pbc-etale-section-clopen-immersion], that section is open and closed in $Z$. Thus $Z\setminus\Delta_U(U)$ is closed in $W$, and its complement $W^\circ$ is an open neighbourhood of $\Delta_U$. On $W^\circ$ the inverse image of $\Delta_A$ is exactly $\Delta_U$ as a closed subscheme. The square of centres with top map $q^\circ:W^\circ\to W_0$ and base map $e:U\to A$ is therefore Cartesian. The restricted first projection $W^\circ\to U$ remains smooth of dimension $d$ and has closed section $\Delta_U$. All objects are separated finite type by Step 1.

### lem-pur-chart-normal-differential

Inherited Step 4:

Normal bundles in that square. Both diagonals are regular closed immersions of codimension $d$ by [lem-pur-diagonal-regular-immersion]; restricting the ambient scheme near the closed image does not change its conormal algebra, which lives on the closed image. Since the Cartesian ambient map $q^\circ$ is étale, it is flat by [def-weil-etale-morphism]. By Step 3 the square with closed immersions $\Delta_U:U\hookrightarrow W^\circ$, $\Delta_A:A\hookrightarrow W_0$, ambient map $q^\circ$ and base map $e$ is a fibre product, so [lem-pur-conormal-algebra-flat-base-change] gives an isomorphism of graded $\mathcal O_U$-algebras $c:e^*\mathcal G_{\Delta_A}\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathcal G_{\Delta_U}$ between the conormal algebras of [lem-pur-conormal-graded-algebra], given on affine charts by $[a]\otimes r\mapsto[ar]$.
