This is the source's truncation/flat-model/uniformizer-filtration argument with the actual variables of 0F1J. It does not cite a proof of a node as though it were that node's statement. The argument never assumes Rf_* preserves coefficient-flatness and never identifies an A-derived tensor with a residue-field tensor just by forgetting coefficients.
