## Extracted arguments for the next workers

The essential-image node isolates the full-subcategory argument formerly embedded in step 4. Form the full simplicial subcategory E of L_W C on objects isomorphic in pi_0 to images of C. Factor q through E. Fullness reflects invertibility, so the factor through E inverts W. The universal property gives a class L_W C → E whose composite with inclusion restricts to q. Injectivity of restriction gives that composite equal to the identity. Applying pi_0 up to natural isomorphism shows that every object belongs to the prescribed essential image. This is an argument about the enriched derived localization and does not assume the ordinary localization property of pi_0 q.

The marked-cylinder node isolates successive inversion with its required endpoint compatibility. First use the existing product-parameter comparison to invert horizontal copies of V in A × [1]. Then invert vertical arrows. The essential-surjectivity hypothesis is explicit: an object d of L_V A is isomorphic to q(a), and the naturality square for that isomorphism shows that a vertical arrow at d is inverted if and only if the vertical arrow at q(a) is inverted. Thus the image vertical family and the whole vertical family give the same restriction images. Injectivity and the image description in the universal-property node identify successive inversion with inversion of their union. Apply the product-parameter comparison in the other variable to [1] → J. Localization of [1] at its arrow is J by its defining derived pushout with an identity leg. The comparisons are induced by the original functors, so their composites with either localized endpoint map are the corresponding endpoint inclusions. These arguments belong to this smaller cylinder lemma; its statement does not contain the item's fibrant-subcategory, model-existence or homotopy-limit conclusions.
