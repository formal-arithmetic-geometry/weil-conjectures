## New nodes

`lem-sfb-algclosed-integral-product` is a genuine general geometric fact, not an elementary identity or a clause of the item. Its statement is the integral part of Stacks 05P3, varieties.tex, lemma-product-varieties. The affine argument specializes one factor at a rational closed point where a chosen nonzero coefficient product does not vanish; linear independence of the coefficients in the other factor contradicts a zero product. This needs the Nullstellensatz and does not follow from integrality by definition. Searches of DAG titles/statements and tags, and blueprint searches `product varieties`, `05P3`, `integral product` and `product integral`, found no node stating it. I also opened lem-sc-geometrically-irreducible-product: it assumes geometric irreducibility and concludes only that property, not reducedness. lem-wf-affine-product-reduced is affine and supplies only reducedness. Both are partial ingredients, not the same statement or a generalization.

`lem-sfb-prime-divisor-image-extraction` has no group, group action, invariant divisor or involution. It supplies only the general birational extraction fact behind the middle paragraph of Milne's argument, so applying it does not finish any invariant-action conclusion. I searched the DAG for prime divisors, divisorial valuations, Lemma 4.4, and prime-divisor image/birational phrases; the latest blueprint searches `divisor birational`, `divisor image` and `valuation divisor` had no matching node. The valuation-curve normalized-graph nodes impose curves over a valuation base and have a different conclusion. cor-nagata-conrad-proper-target-extension modifies the source of a rational map, whereas this lemma modifies its target to extract a divisor. Neither states this fact.

For the extraction node, the supported form requires care beyond the literal graph construction in Milne. At the generic point of T, normality gives a DVR with valuation v on k(V). Its restriction w to k(P) is nontrivial, since its center lies in the proper closed subset Z. The residue transcendence-degree inequality makes its residue transcendence degree dim(P)-1. Choose residues of that many rational functions of w-value zero which are algebraically independent. They can be made regular at the center on a proper modification supported on Z: on an affine neighborhood of the center, clear their common denominator a and blow up the ideal generated by a and its products with the chosen functions, together with a sufficiently high power of the ideal of Z. The power has w-value strictly greater than w(a), so the valuation chooses the a-chart, where these ratios are regular; the extra ideal makes the blowup an isomorphism off Z. Coherent ideals extending this local construction give a global modification with this support. Normalize the resulting graph/blowup model; finite normalization over a field preserves properness and is unchanged over the normal complement of Z. The center of w has residue transcendence degree at least dim(P)-1, hence is a divisor and lies over Z. Properness extends the original map at the DVR of T; its image closure is exactly this center closure. These are the arguments to be split on the new node's own turn; they are recorded here, not hidden as proof steps inside its statement. The supported refinement is agent-written and is explicitly identified in its source field. The item's proof_origin is correspondingly `agent`.
