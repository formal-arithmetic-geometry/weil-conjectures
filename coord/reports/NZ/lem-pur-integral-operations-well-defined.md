## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: unqualified “unique isomorphism of functors” is false; uniqueness requires compatibility with the specified identifications on K-flat representatives. For example take E=Q_3, f=id and P the completed coefficient ring sheaf in degree zero on Spec of an algebraically closed field. The tower has A_{E,n} in degree zero. H^0 of its shifted cone is the kernel of 1−s and contains the compatible tuple of units. Multiplication by −1 and the identity are distinct natural automorphisms of the pullback construction: already at n=1 the units 1 and −1 differ in F_3. Thus a functor cannot have just one arbitrary self-isomorphism. The corrected statement requires the comparison to respect the natural model identifications after localization, which is exactly the uniqueness supplied by the localization universal property. No scheme, coefficient, boundedness or completeness hypothesis was added, and the finite-reduction operations and invariance conclusions were kept. This repairs the new auxiliary node, not a target or unit statement.

Tag 08TC explicitly warns of nonunique abstract derived limits; the proof therefore uses strict complex towers and a concrete shifted-cone model, rather than claiming functoriality of arbitrary choices of cones in a triangulated category.
