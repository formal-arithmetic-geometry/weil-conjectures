No closed subscheme structure or assertion that the restriction to Z_d is proper is needed. This step is an elementary application of the definition and gives its argument explicitly.

In step 3, def-pc-scheme-fibre explicitly gives the homeomorphism of the fibre with f^{-1}(s), so the points and their local dimensions in the new node are exactly those in the fibre of this step. The existing 0B7I node, lem-weil-pbc-dimension-supremum-local-dimensions, states dim X = sup_x dim_x X for every topological space, with the empty-space convention; it applies directly to the underlying space of X_s. An image witness gives a summand greater than d. Conversely, absence of an image witness bounds every summand by d and hence bounds their supremum by d. This avoids an unwritten claim that a maximal-dimension point exists. Infinite dimensions are allowed as well. The 0B7I citation is declared as a generic tool use across units, without altering the blueprint route.

Step 4 takes the complement of C_d and uses the proved equivalence. It uses only elementary set logic and the definition of closedness, and has no uncited geometric input. Properness is used in step 1 for finite type and in step 2 for closedness; neither separatedness, flatness nor a Noetherian hypothesis is introduced along the way.

I checked small cases. For X empty, U_d and Z_d are empty, C_d is empty and the conclusion is all of S; the supremum is minus infinity. For the identity morphism every fibre has dimension zero, U_d = X for d >= 0 and the conclusion is S. Over a field, a positive-dimensional proper scheme gives the empty locus if d is below its dimension, and the full singleton otherwise. The new node also applies to an arbitrary open immersion (local fibre dimension zero at all source points); it does not assert any closedness of its image. A disjoint union of affine spaces of unbounded dimensions over a field is locally finite type without quasi-compactness: U_n is the union of components of dimension at most n and is open, showing that the new statement does not accidentally impose a uniform bound. A point of the new node is always in a nonempty fibre and its point-dimension is nonnegative. These tests reveal no statement error.
