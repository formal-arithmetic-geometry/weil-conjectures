I checked cancellation in that pushout explicitly: first localize the integral monoid by the face sent to units, then quotient its product with the ring-unit group by translations by the group completion of that face. Equalities are single translations, so a common summand cancels. This avoids silently assuming that chart pushouts preserve integrality. It works without saturation and without a Noetherian ring.

Step 2 uses the log unit axiom of def-trf-fs-log-blowup and the free-transitive locally trivial action of def-sc-gm-torsor.
