## Review by NZ

I reconstructed this interface from ILO XVI §2.1 (printed pp. 228–229), Lemma 2.2.2.4 (p. 232), Proposition 2.2.3.2 (p. 233) and the transverse-operator calculation in the proof of Theorem 2.3.2 (pp. 236–237). The source explicitly constructs the relative Kummer class, describes Gys by composition, warns that the ideal-line normalization is opposite to the effective divisor class, uses the canonical action pairing, and states the transverse operator identity. The detailed tensor-Hom argument here is agent-written, not represented as a verbatim proof from ILO. I also read Stacks `etale-cohomology.tex` at the open-closed, supported triangle and closed-pushforward statements, `sites-cohomology.tex` at the associative/symmetric cup and projection-map statements, and `cohomology.tex` at the supported pairings. The latter are ringed-space statements and are not applied as if they were etale-site theorems; the actual proof cites ringed-site and ordinary-etale DAG statements.

Step 1 uses the exact sequence in `lem-weil-pbc-open-closed-short-exact`, the precise derived Hom/cone formula of `lem-amb-closed-support-hom`, and `lem-pur-support-restriction-triangle` with first support all of X. The definition node specifies which derived supported sections are meant. Applying Hom to the constant open-closed sequence fixes the evaluation map and identifies forgetting support with precomposition by constant restriction; this prevents an unspecified automorphism of supported cohomology. I checked that the objects used are bounded below, as both supported interfaces require. The closed-constant tensor statement supplies the specified exchange used for restriction.

Step 2 uses `lem-pur-etale-closed-constant-tensor` for the flatness and associative symmetric multiplication on actual supported constants, `lem-asd-etale-finite-twist-pullback` for rank-one twist lines, `lem-pur-signed-line-scalar-tensor-tower` for multiplication of signed powers, and `lem-pur-supported-source-system-cup-calculus` for the resulting operation on all derived morphisms. I checked its hypotheses individually: closed supports form the meet set, source sheaves are flat in degree zero, the coefficient lines are invertible, and all prescribed multiplication maps agree. This cites the stronger source-system statement rather than asking a generic Yoneda or definition node for associativity it does not state.

Step 3 uses closed-finite and finite-direct-image exactness, direct-image composition, the canonical projection mate, locally free twists and the shifted tensor calculus. Only projection against a shifted rank-one line is inverted in defining the action: locally the line is the tensor unit, where the cited projection map is the identity; its cited shift law handles all integer degrees. I made the grading explicit: with the specified total-complex comparison, the tensor action is (-1)^(a a') times the unadorned shifted composite. Associativity has the same exponent a a' + a a'' + a' a'' on both sides. This is the homogeneous composition convention used throughout; every Cartier factor has degree two, so its sign is one. This detail avoids silently assuming all general input degrees are even.

Step 4 uses only naturality and the unit/shift laws of the same actual projection mate, existence of the ordinary closed right adjoint in `lem-exc-closed-ambient`, and the precise Hom-bijection formula in `def-weil-pbc-composite-adjunction`. It gives both the action projection formula and exceptional composition. No constructibility or purity of an exceptional inverse image is asserted. The closed maps and the coefficients meet the right-adjoint statement's hypotheses.

Step 5 uses exact ordinary-etale inverse image, the particular constant-support exchanges, the twist comparisons, the exact-pullback source-system assertion, finite exchange and the defined projection mate. I checked that finite exchange can be applied because every immersion in use is closed and therefore finite. In the nested action the naturality square is obtained by taking the mate of the identical tensor/counit map, not by postulating exceptional base change. This applies to arbitrary pair pullback; open restriction is its special case.

Step 6 uses the actual derived change-of-rings functor, `lem-geo-cartier-coeff-extension` for its normalized source identification, signed-line scalar comparisons, the ringed-topos tensor-pullback theorem and the shifted tensor/projection formulas. I checked the nonflat case explicitly: reduction from Z/ell^N to Z/ell^n is never assumed exact on sheaves, and all tensors here are derived. The balanced K-flat tensor formula commutes with the actual multiplication and signed shift maps. Primitive-root bases verify that the power map on roots gives the required scalar identification for positive and negative twists.

Step 7 uses the full statement of `lem-amb-relative-torsor-cohomology`, exact Kummer and the tensor/contracted-product clause of `lem-sc-line-bundle-torsor`. The missing relative group-law argument is written out using the product coefficient sheaf, its projection maps and its multiplication map. Finite products of injective resolutions and restriction cones compute these products, so this is a two-line additive argument, not an uncited relative-torsor theorem. Evaluation then gives the dual sign. Enlarging support is restriction of the specified sections and the corresponding cone map.

Step 8 uses the naturality and forgetting clauses of that relative torsor statement, exact Kummer, `lem-asd-ordinary-supported-kummer` and the precise dual-ideal definition in `def-pc-divisor-line-bundle`. I wrote the coefficient-reduction Kummer diagram explicitly, including which of its two Gm maps is a power and which is identity. The supported Cartier theorem is applied only when the pulled-back divisor remains Cartier. It identifies the positive O(D) class, whereas Step 7 supplies the negative ideal class; these signs agree with ILO's warning.

Step 9 is substitution into the constructed action, with the ordinary closed counit isomorphism from `prop-openclosed-lowerstar-fully-faithful`. The relative torsor theorem supplies the forgotten first Chern class, and the supported tensor multiplication fixes forgetting of the product. The argument works for every W contained in C, so it does not ask a class on the intersection to extend to either divisor. Restriction of the operator and the ambient-class projection formula are both proved with their stated supports.

Step 10 uses the actual projection-mate naturality square and the normalized constant multiplication; their precise statements were open during the check. The source comparison in that square is an equality of degree-zero sheaf maps (restriction followed by multiplication), so no conservativity-on-derived-Hom assertion is made. Transversality is used exactly to identify the restricted divisor as Cartier in each D_i. The local equation remains a non-zero-divisor there, hence the pulled-back ideal line and its off-divisor section are the required pair. Naturality of the supported Kummer theorem supplies the restricted class. The displayed square identifies the two composites with the two orders of the degree-two product. Their interchange sign is +1. Step 11 is the elementary final composition of this equality with the same represented class on I, giving exactly the common degree, twist and support in the item.
