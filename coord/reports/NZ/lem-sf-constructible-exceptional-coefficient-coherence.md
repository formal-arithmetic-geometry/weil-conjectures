## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the old universal statement `CoefficientConstruction ... -> CoefficientCoherence ...` does not follow from its defined input record. At closed coefficients `common_normalized` fixes the left comparison on each separate finite stage to an identity, but there is no normalization of the closed compact-support functor on morphisms BETWEEN stage-labelled objects. Naturality of that comparison tests arrows within a single stage only. A canonical action on those remaining arrows cannot be inferred from such tests.

Here is an explicit small counterexample to the missing inference, already for the identity morphism of a point. In the literal finite-stage pair category of bounded constructible coefficient complexes on `Spec(k)`, assign a nonzero rational scalar `c_i` to each finite field label: take `c_Q_l=1` and `c_j=2` for one proper finite extension `j/Q_l`. Define an identity-on-objects functor H by

`H(v:A -> B) = (c_(stage B)/c_(stage A)) v`.

This is additive, preserves identity and composition, commutes with shifts, and is naturally isomorphic to the identity: the isomorphism has component `c_(stage A) id_A`. It is therefore exact. Its inverse divides by the same ratios. Within the inclusion of any one field H is literally the identity on objects and arrows, so all separately normalized common left comparisons can remain identity maps. Take the closed left operation to be H, the closed right operation to be H inverse, and the strict inverse adjunction; its unit and counit are identities. Then the common right comparisons also remain identities and satisfy their individual counit equations. Keep the canonical data at finite, integral and rational stages. For `f=id_Spec(k)`, apply the same closed scalar twist at EVERY compactification: replace each canonical closed left operation by H composed with that operation, and use the corresponding composite adjunction on the right. Put `ρ_Ω = H(ρ_Ω^can)` at every refinement. These refinement components have unchanged field label at both ends, so the prescribed components and refinementCommon equations remain unchanged. Restricting the twist to only one compactification would not check the refinement fields and is not the counterexample used here. None of the other input fields links different field labels of the closed functor.

Let K be the nonzero rank-one complex at field i=Q_l, and let `t: I_j(T_ij K) -> I_i K` be the canonical pair transition. It is invertible and nonzero. The proposed common left law would say, after the standard tensor identifications, `1 = t H(t inverse)`. Its right side is `(c_j/c_i) id = 2 id`. This fails in characteristic zero, including at ell=2. Thus fixed-stage naturality, exactness and individual adjunction compatibility do not supply the missing transition law.
