For exact comparison, the original six steps copied from the read-clone node follow verbatim:

1. Assume first that $M$ is Mittag-Leffler. Since $M$ is flat, the auxiliary least-support lemma [lem-trf-flat-ml-minimal-support] applies to the finite free module $F$ and the element $x$. It gives a smallest submodule $F_0\subseteq F$ whose tensor image contains $x$, exactly the property required in the statement.

2. Conversely, assume the least-submodule property for every finite free $F$. The flat-tensor exactness statement [lem-weil-pbc-flat-tensor-exact], applied at each term of a short exact sequence including its zero endpoints, shows that $-\otimes_R M$ preserves short exact sequences. Thus [lem-kun-lazard-flat-module] gives a small nonempty directed partially ordered set $I$, finite free modules $(M_i,f_{ij})$, and canonical maps $f_i:M_i\to M$ exhibiting $M$ as their colimit [def-weil-pbc-category-colimit].

3. Fix $i$. For $j\ge i$ let $Q_j$ be the image of precomposition $\operatorname{Hom}_R(M_j,R)\to\operatorname{Hom}_R(M_i,R)$. Put $H=\operatorname{Hom}_R(M_i,R)$. For a basis $(e_a)$ of $M_i$, the inverse to $H\otimes_R N\to\operatorname{Hom}_R(M_i,N)$, $h\otimes n\mapsto(v\mapsto h(v)n)$, sends $g$ to $\sum_a e_a^*\otimes g(e_a)$, for any $N$. Choose also a basis $(b_s)$ of $M_j$ and write $f_{ij}=\sum_s h_s\otimes b_s$. The $h_s=b_s^*f_{ij}$ generate $Q_j$. The identification $H\otimes_R M_j\simeq\bigoplus_s H$ identifies the image of $G\otimes_R M_j$ with $\bigoplus_s G$ for every submodule $G\subseteq H$. Consequently that image contains $f_{ij}$ exactly when every $h_s$ lies in $G$, equivalently $Q_j\subseteq G$. This proves the least-submodule characterization of $Q_j$.

4. The same finite-free identification puts the canonical map $f_i:M_i\to M$ in $\operatorname{Hom}_R(M_i,R)\otimes_R M$. By the assumed least-submodule property there is a smallest submodule $Q\subseteq\operatorname{Hom}_R(M_i,R)$ with $f_i\in Q\otimes_R M$. Compatibility $f_i=f_jf_{ij}$ shows that $f_i$ lies in $Q_j\otimes_R M$ for every $j\ge i$, so minimality gives $Q\subseteq Q_j$. Thus the decreasing family $(Q_j)_{j\ge i}$ all contains $Q$.

5. Write $H=\operatorname{Hom}_R(M_i,R)$. The tensor-colimit comparison [lem-weil-pbc-tensor-commutes-with-colimits], using the elementary symmetry $h\otimes m\leftrightarrow m\otimes h$, gives $H\otimes_R M=\operatorname{colim}_j(H\otimes_R M_j)$ and $Q\otimes_R M=\operatorname{colim}_j(Q\otimes_R M_j)$. Finite free modules are flat: under $N\otimes_R R^r\simeq N^r$, tensoring an injection is a componentwise injection. Thus [lem-weil-pbc-flat-tensor-exact] makes each $Q\otimes_R M_j\to H\otimes_R M_j$ injective. By [lem-weil-pbc-filtered-colimit-modules-elementwise], choose $t\in Q\otimes_R M_l$ mapping to $f_i$. Its image and $f_{ii}\in H\otimes_R M_i$ have equal colimit images, so the same cited equality criterion gives a common stage $j\ge l,i$ where their images agree. Hence $f_{ij}$ belongs to the image of $Q\otimes_R M_j$. Step 3 gives $Q_j\subseteq Q$, while Step 4 gives $Q\subseteq Q_j$, so $Q_j=Q$. For every $k\ge j$, composition of precomposition maps gives $Q_k\subseteq Q_j$, and Step 4 gives $Q\subseteq Q_k$. Therefore $Q_k=Q$ for every $k\ge j$.

6. Therefore, for every $i$, the inverse system of duals $(\operatorname{Hom}_R(M_i,R),\operatorname{Hom}_R(f_{ij},R))$ is Mittag-Leffler. The dual criterion [lem-trf-flat-ml-dual-criterion] then implies that $M$ is Mittag-Leffler in the tensor-product sense.
