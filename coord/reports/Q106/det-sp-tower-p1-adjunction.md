## Source error: STATEMENT-DISPUTED

The final sentence of the retained item asserted preservation of the full uniformly bounded constructible finite-Tor tower subcategories over every scheme S. Its cited bound `sk-lem-sp-tower-bound` requires the standing finite-type field setting. This is not just an absent citation: the unrestricted sentence is false with the global finite-stratification definition in `def-weil-proetale-constructible-sheaf`.

Here is a counterexample. Fix an algebraically closed field k of positive characteristic different from ell and let S be the disjoint union, for n at least 1, of S_n = affine n-space over k with coordinates x_1,...,x_n. In P^1 over S_n take D_n to be the union of the n sections t=x_j, defined homogeneously by the product of (y_1-x_j y_0). It is a closed effective Cartier divisor, finite over S_n, disjoint from infinity. Let D be their disjoint union and i:D into P^1_S. Choose the nonzero finite ring B=O_E/ell. The sheaf H=i_*B_D is constructible with two global strata D and its complement, and is flat over B: its geometric stalks are B or zero. Both strata are constructible, since their complementary opens are retrocompact; a quasi-compact open meets finitely many of the Noetherian components. Regard its pro-etale image as a tower concentrated at level 1, with zero transitions and every other level zero. This has uniform cohomological and Tor intervals [0,0].

Since D to S is finite, q_*H has, at a geometric point of S_n, the module B^r, where r is the number of distinct coordinate values x_j. For j=1,...,n, take the generic point of the irreducible locus x_1=...=x_j, requiring the remaining coordinates generically distinct. These points form a specialization chain, and the stalk ranks of q_*H are n,n-1,...,1. Two such points cannot lie in the same stratum on which q_*H is locally constant: the specialization relation remains in that stratum, and a locally constant finite module has the same rank along specialization. Thus any such stratification of S_n needs at least n strata. There is no finite global stratification of S on which q_*H is locally constant. Already H^0 of the direct image is not constructible in the node's global sense.

The correction is confined to the last restriction clause: it is asserted in `sk-def-sfb-dc-setting`, with S separated and finite type over a field of positive characteristic different from ell. This is exactly the standing scheme setting used by the parent unit's uniform-bound node. The arbitrary-base adjunction on C^+ is retained. No target statement or coefficient category was changed.

A separate C4 repair concerns the projection formula: `det-sp-tower-shriek-levels` clause 2(a) has field, constructibility and two finite-Tor hypotheses, so it cannot justify the retained argument on all C^+. Step 3 instead uses the existing arbitrary-base torsion-ring etale projection theorem `lem-kun-0f0g` (Stacks Tag 0F0G, etale-cohomology.tex, lemma-projection-formula-proper-mod-n), transports its canonical maps by the normalized comparison, and detects invertibility by tower evaluation. ## Retained proof plan for the new finite interface

The following is the detailed arbitrary-base argument extracted from the retained split. It is kept here for the future worker of the statement-only node.

For any scheme T over S define b_T by the shifted twisted unit and h_T. Kummer is valid on arbitrary T by lem-amb-kummer-any-base, Stacks 03PL. For each geometric point x:Spec Omega to T, proper exchange lem-kun-proper-base-change-torsion-ring, Stacks 0F0C, identifies the fibre of r_T with P^1_Omega by lemma-nagata-projective-space-base-change-pullback. Twist pullback, the adjunction unit and Kummer naturality identify x^{-1}b_T with b_Omega. The latter is invertible by the decomposition clause of lem-asd-ordinary-finite-p1-adjunction, used only over the Noetherian field base and with the same finite A killed by N. The cone of b_T has zero cohomology on every geometric stalk, by prop-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks-exact. Lemma-weil-pbc-geometric-stalk-conservative applied to each cohomology sheaf makes that cone acyclic. Thus b_T is invertible. Naturality of the two components proves that arbitrary proper exchange carries b_T to b_T', hence carries its inverse and second projection to the corresponding trace. For a general change of base, properness still holds, so this uses exactly the same exchange isomorphism.

Write beta_i=Rp_{i*}(delta_A) alpha_i. The supported Kummer lemma gives delta_A u_Delta=c_1(O_W(Delta)). The composite-adjunction unit formula in def-weil-pbc-composite-adjunction gives alpha_i=Rp_{i*}u_Delta eta_{p_i}, because p_i Delta=1. Thus beta_i is the adjoint of the ordinary Chern class. At a geometric point x of P, the pulled-back diagonal is a single rational Cartier point of P^1_Omega, with equation a_0 y_1-a_1 y_0. Its associated divisor bundle is O(1), including at infinity, by trivializing on the two standard charts. The supported Kummer lemma therefore identifies the pullback class with h_Omega.

The source identification is canonical: def-kun-unbounded-exchange defines exchange as the counit mate. If f=p_i, v is the fibre inclusion, r is the geometric-fibre projection and theta:r^{-1}x^{-1} isomorphic to v^{-1}f^{-1}, then exchange on f^{-1}L composed with x^{-1}eta_f is the r-direct image of (v^{-1}epsilon_f composed with theta composed with r^{-1}x^{-1}eta_f), followed by eta_r. Naturality of theta and the f-adjunction triangle reduce the parenthesized composite to theta_L. This leaves eta_r under the pullback identification. Hence proper exchange takes x^{-1}beta_i to h_Omega with the correct source, not merely to an abstract class. The geometric pullback of t_P beta_i is therefore t_Omega h_Omega=pr_2 b_Omega^{-1} b_Omega in_2=1.

Finally t_P beta_i is an endomorphism of the degree-zero constant sheaf A_P. Degree-zero full faithfulness lem-weil-pbc-derived-single-fully-faithful identifies it with a sheaf endomorphism. The image of its difference from identity has zero geometric stalks by exactness, and is zero by geometric-stalk conservativity. Thus both finite scalar identities hold. This argument proves no equality of arbitrary higher derived morphisms by stalks and makes no finite-type assumption on S.
