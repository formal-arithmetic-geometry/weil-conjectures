## New nodes

`lem-sfb-isogeny-postcomposition-degree` states a geometric fact about arbitrary equal-dimensional abelian varieties A,B,C, an arbitrary h:A→B and an isogeny u:B→C. It includes preservation and reflection of being an isogeny and the kernel-order degree formula extended by zero. It contains neither a Tate module nor a determinant nor a rational inverse, so it is a strictly smaller input, not the item or a clause relabelled. This fact is not a two-line consequence of the cited endomorphism multiplicativity node: the maps have three different sources/targets, and the nonisogeny case needs the finite-image dimension argument.

For its later worker, the source's argument is as follows (this is a report sketch, not proof fields on the unsplit node). If h is an isogeny, h and u are finite flat surjections by Milne 7.1, their composite is an isogeny, and its function-field degree is the product by Stacks 02NZ. Milne p.32 identifies these degrees with kernel lengths. If h is not an isogeny, its proper image I is closed and has dimension <g by Milne 7.1 and its fibre-dimension proof. The image u(I) under the finite map u has dimension at most dim I, so it cannot be all of C. Thus uh is not an isogeny; both sides of the degree formula are zero. Conversely, the isogeny case already shows that h being an isogeny forces uh to be one. The finite-map image-dimension input is a genuine part of this geometric lemma to split later.
