## Original proof retained for subsequent workers

### Original Step 1

Put $h=g\circ f$. By [lem-sfb-finite-type-composition-base-change](1), $h$ is of finite type. In particular $h$ is quasi-compact by [def-weil-finite-type-morphism]. This proves the finite-type clause and supplies the quasi-compactness needed by the affine-cover criterion below.

Cited nodes: lem-sfb-finite-type-composition-base-change, def-weil-finite-type-morphism.

### Original Step 2

Choose an affine open cover of $S$ and a finite subcover $S=\bigcup_{i=1}^r V_i$, using [def-pc-scheme] and quasi-compactness [def-weil-pbc-quasi-compact-scheme]. Set $Y_i=g^{-1}(V_i)$, $X_i=h^{-1}(V_i)$, and let $f_i:X_i\to Y_i$ be the restriction of $f$. Put $L_i=L|_{X_i}$ and $M_i=M|_{Y_i}$. The morphism $f_i$ is the base change of $f$ by $Y_i\hookrightarrow Y$, hence is of finite type by [lem-sfb-finite-type-composition-base-change](2), and is quasi-compact by [def-weil-finite-type-morphism]. Every affine open $W$ of $Y_i$ is an affine open of $Y$, and $f_i^{-1}(W)=f^{-1}(W)$. Thus $L_i$ is $f_i$-relatively ample directly from the hypothesis on $L$. The hypothesis on $M$, applied to the affine open $V_i$, says that $M_i$ is ample. Restrictions of the two invertible modules remain locally free of rank one by restricting their local trivializations [def-sc-invertible-module].

Cited nodes: def-pc-scheme, def-weil-pbc-quasi-compact-scheme, lem-sfb-finite-type-composition-base-change, def-weil-finite-type-morphism, def-sc-invertible-module.

### Original Step 3

Apply [lem-sfb-relative-ample-absolute-twist] to $f_i,L_i,M_i$ for each $i$. All its hypotheses were checked in Step 2: quasi-compactness of $f_i$, relative ampleness of $L_i$, and absolute ampleness of $M_i$. Obtain $a_i\geq0$ such that $L_i\otimes f_i^*(M_i^{\otimes a})$ is ample on $X_i$ for every $a\geq a_i$. Choose $a=\max(\{0\}\cup\{a_i:1\leq i\leq r\})$. Then the same exponent works for every member of the finite cover. This also defines $a=0$ when the cover is empty; no bound over an infinite family is asserted.

Cited nodes: lem-sfb-relative-ample-absolute-twist.

### Original Step 4

Define $N=L\otimes_{\mathcal O_X}f^*(M^{\otimes a})$, using [def-pc-module-pullback] and [def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product]. The tensor power $M^{\otimes a}$ is invertible by [def-weil-pbc-ample-invertible-module]; its pullback and its tensor product with $L$ are invertible by [lem-weil-pbc-invertible-pullback-tensor](1). If $j_i:X_i\hookrightarrow X$ and $k_i:Y_i\hookrightarrow Y$ are the inclusions, then $f\circ j_i=k_i\circ f_i$. Parts (2) of that same node, applied to these maps and iterated over the $a$ factors, give $j_i^*N\cong L_i\otimes f_i^*(M_i^{\otimes a})$. For $a=0$, the comparison of units is the defining map $\mathcal O_{X_i}\otimes_{\mathcal O_{Y_i}}\mathcal O_{Y_i}\cong\mathcal O_{X_i}$, $b\otimes c\mapsto b f_i^{\sharp}(c)$, with inverse $b\mapsto b\otimes1$. Pullback by an open inclusion is restriction, since $j_i^{-1}\mathcal O_X=\mathcal O_{X_i}$ in the pullback formula of [def-pc-module-pullback]. Consequently $N|_{X_i}$ is ample by Step 3; isomorphisms preserve the affine nonvanishing loci in the definition of ampleness [def-weil-pbc-ample-invertible-module].

Cited nodes: def-pc-module-pullback, def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product, def-weil-pbc-ample-invertible-module, lem-weil-pbc-invertible-pullback-tensor.

### Original Step 5

Apply [lem-trf-scheme-relative-ample-affine-cover] to $h:X\to S$, the invertible module $N$, and the affine open covering $(V_i)$. Step 1 supplies quasi-compactness of $h$, and Step 4 supplies ampleness of $N|_{h^{-1}(V_i)}$. The cited statement gives ampleness of $N|_{h^{-1}(V)}$ for every affine open $V\subseteq S$. Together with quasi-compactness of $h$, this is precisely $h$-relative ampleness as defined in the item. The already established finite-type clause finishes the conclusion, including the empty-base case, where any morphism to the empty scheme has empty source.

Cited nodes: lem-trf-scheme-relative-ample-affine-cover.
