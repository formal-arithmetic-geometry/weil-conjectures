### New-node mathematics and small cases

For `lem-coef-infinity-parameterized-evaluation-naturality`, use the same family argument as returned step 4. Apply the right-pinch comparison in the functor category to the universal transformation parameter T, then internally evaluate on C and on its arrow prism M_x times Delta^1. This gives one map from M_x times T times the square into the space category, with boundary functors F and G in the transformation direction, and component transformations at a and x in the arrow direction. The two triangles of the square compare the two composite paths. Precompose the starting vertex with eta and pass through the point-family equivalence to get the two displayed maps into G(x). This produces a homotopy on the full product, not a collection of homotopies on vertices. Restriction commutes with internal evaluation, so every parameter face, degeneracy, and higher C-simplex retains its compatibility. A later split of this new lemma must keep that family argument; it cannot use this item, coherent Yoneda, classification, or a model comparison that reaches this item. Tested C=Delta^0 (unit action), M_x empty (unique empty maps), F constant, and arbitrary disconnected T. No connectedness or nonemptiness is needed; eta is required only in F(a), just as stated. This is a genuinely smaller generic naturality lemma: e need not be invertible and no evaluation equivalence is concluded.

For `lem-coef-kan-weak-equivalence-space-edge`, the existing Kan Whitehead lemma gives g and homotopies gf to 1_A, fg to 1_B. The function complexes are Kan by `lem-cbc-kan-mapping-homotopy-invariance` applied to identity maps, so outer horn filling reverses those homotopy edges. In the subset-poset thickening of Delta^2, the (0,2) Hom is Delta^1: its initial vertex {0,2} is the long edge and its final vertex {0,1,2} is the composite of the short edges. The reversed homotopy from the identity to the composite is therefore exactly the enriched map for a triangle with long edge the identity and short edges f,g (respectively g,f). All other Hom data are fixed by these edges. These are the two triangles requested by the node, in the actual nerve. The same enriched maps remain valid in a containing universe. Tested f=1, both complexes empty, disconnected Kan complexes, and the impossible case of a weak equivalence from empty to nonempty; the weak-equivalence hypothesis excludes the last case. This conversion is not stated by the existing Whitehead node or by the general composition node, which begins with arrows and triangle data already in a quasicategory.
