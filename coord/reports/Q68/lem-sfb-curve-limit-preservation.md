## Source error

In quot.tex, Tag 0D55 (lemma-curves-limits), the line asserting the eventual dimension bound prints T_{i'} times_{T_i} X_i to T_i. The relative curve over the new base is the projection to T_{i'}, not its composite to T_i. This distinction matters: for a constant family P^1 over Spec k, base change to Spec k[t] remains relative dimension one over k[t], while its composite to Spec k has dimension two. This example diagnoses the malformed arrow, not the conclusion of limit preservation. The cited 0D4K and the source's use of a base-changed family fix the intended projection. Neither item statement nor unit statement needs modification.
