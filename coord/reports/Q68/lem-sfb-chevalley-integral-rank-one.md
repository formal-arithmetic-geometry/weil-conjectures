## What remains

The three non-Mathlib new statements remain unsplit by design. They contain no proof or proof dependencies; their intended next arguments are recorded below for a later worker. The item itself has a complete assembly argument relative to their statements.

Integral integration: decompose the rational sl2 representation into highest-weight modules (symmetric powers of the standard representation), obtaining its rational SL2 representation. On the chart a invertible, send a matrix (a b; c d) to X_-(c/a) D(a) X_+(b/a). On the chart c invertible use n X_-(-a/c) D(-c) X_+(d/c), where n=X_+(1)X_-(-1)X_+(1). Their entries are integral Laurent polynomials by the exact divided-power hypotheses. They agree rationally, and their overlap ring is torsion-free over Z, so they glue. The charts cover because (a,c) is unimodular. The same torsion-free coordinate-ring argument on SL2 times SL2 proves multiplication. This also proves the asserted arbitrary base change and uniqueness; no assertion that arbitrary A-points are globally generated by elementary matrices is necessary.

Adjoint signs: on each alpha root string, use the sl2 raising/lowering operators with the normalized Chevalley coefficients plus or minus (p+1), and [e_alpha,e_-alpha]=h_alpha. The finite Weyl operator reverses the string with coefficient plus or minus one on its Chevalley endpoint-normalized vectors. Treat beta=plus or minus alpha inside the three-dimensional adjoint sl2 module separately; on Cartan the formula is h-alpha(h)h_alpha. This is a Lie-algebra lemma over Q only; integral identities for the given representation are derived in item steps 10--11, not assumed in this new node.

Normal-kernel detection: Lie(N) is a D-stable Lie ideal in sl2. The torus characters 0, plus 2 and minus 2 remain distinct as characters in characteristic two. Trivial N intersect D excludes the diagonal tangent vector. Either off-diagonal tangent vector forces the diagonal vector through its bracket with the opposite matrix unit, so Lie(N)=0. A finite-type group scheme over an algebraically closed field with zero tangent space at the identity is finite etale. A finite etale normal subgroup of connected SL2 is central, since the conjugation action on its finite set of points is constant. The scalar centre is contained in D, so N=1. The standard faithfully-flat closed-image factorization then makes a representation with trivial scheme kernel a closed immersion. In characteristic two the diagonal tangent vector is the scalar matrix I, still nonzero, so this proof does not discard infinitesimal kernels.
