### lem-sp-etale-ctf-chart-domain

Source: `Charts: passage from ordinary etale finite-Tor categories to pro-etale charts; retained Steps 3 and the domain check of Step 4` in the current exceptional blueprint companion proof, with the cited existing source statements. Complete proof text length: 1995 characters.

1. Every finite coefficient ring is Noetherian, since an ascending chain of ideals stabilizes in its finite underlying set. Every standing scheme is Noetherian, hence qcqs, by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian]. Apply [det-pe-finite-tor-comparison-noetherian] to obtain the equivalences and reflection of each Tor interval.

2. For $L\in D^b_{ctf}(U_{et},R)$, $F^{et}_{h,R}L$ is bounded below: the open extension is exact [lem-weil-pbc-extension-by-zero-exact] and bounded-below right derived direct image takes values in $D^+$ [def-weil-pbc-direct-image]; its compatibility with the unbounded functor is [src-right-derived-functor-plus-compatibility]. By [lem-sp-et-proet-coefficient-compatibility], its comparison pullback is $F_{h,R}\nu_U^*L$, which belongs to $D^b_{ctf}$ by [sk-prop-sf-rf-shriek-preservation]. Choose a bounded constructible etale origin $M$ of this pro-etale value by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-ring-constructible-derived-origin]. The full faithfulness on $D^+$ in [thm-weil-pbc-site-comparison] lifts the identification with $\nu_V^*M$ to $F^{et}_{h,R}L\simeq M$. Tor reflection in the preceding step now applies.

3. The comparison pullback is exact and preserves the constant coefficient ring by [lem-sf-comparison-pullback-exact]. Its tensor identification is [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-derived-tensor-pullback], and its geometric pullback comparison is [lem-weil-pbc-comparison-pullback]. The Tate sheaves are inverse images of the same root sheaves, with geometric pullbacks given by [lem-asd-etale-finite-twist-pullback]. Thus it intertwines the listed functors. On the pro-etale side scalar extension preserves $D^b_{ctf}$ by [det-sp-scalar-extension-functor], and geometric pullback does so by [det-proper-base-change-unbounded-pullback]. A Tate line is locally the free rank-one module [lem-asd-finite-etale-twist-line]; tensoring with it preserves Tor bounds, while shifts translate them. Reflect membership through the equivalence to obtain the ordinary-etale assertions.


### lem-sp-p1-splitting-scalar

Source: `Charts: Kummer classes come from Z/ell^n, and the splitting into two free rank-one summands; retained Steps 5 and 6` in the current exceptional blueprint companion proof, with the cited existing source statements. Complete proof text length: 1456 characters.

1. The Tate line is flat by [lem-asd-finite-etale-twist-line]. Its derived extension is ordinary tensor; balancing gives $(\mu_N\otimes_{\mathbf Z/N}A)\otimes_AB\simeq\mu_N\otimes_{\mathbf Z/N}B$ with the displayed formula, inverse $z\otimes b\mapsto(z\otimes1)\otimes b$. These maps agree on overlaps, including the zero ring. Use the normalized models supplied by [lem-pur-system-scalar-model]; their derived scalar associativity is [lem-pur-system-scalar-coherence]. Thus if $H_0:(\mathbf Z/N)_P\to(\mathbf Z/N)_P(1)[2]$ is the Kummer morphism, the classes $H_R=H_0\otimes^L_{\mathbf Z/N}R$ specified in [lem-asd-ordinary-finite-p1-adjunction] satisfy $T_P(H_A)=H_B$.

2. The proper map $\beta_q$ is the counit transpose specified in [lem-sp-et-proet-coefficient-compatibility]. Transpose $\beta_q(L_A)T_S(h_A)$ under $q_B^*\dashv Rq_{*,B}$. The defining counit equation for $\beta_q$ and naturality give $T_P(\epsilon_{q,*,A}\,q_A^*h_A)=T_P(H_A)=H_B$. This is also the transpose of $h_B$, so the two maps agree. For the other splitting component the same equation with the pullback/direct-image unit reduces to that unit by the triangle identity. Consequently both components of $\beta_q(L_A)T_S(s_A)$ equal those of $s_B$.

3. By [def-amb-p1-trace], $t_R=\operatorname{pr}_2s_R^{-1}$. Both $t_B\beta_q(L_A)$ and $T_S(t_A)$ become the second-summand projection after precomposition with $T_S(s_A)$. This map is invertible, so cancellation proves the trace equation.

### lem-sp-etale-sum-counit-scalar

1. Use the presheaf sum of [def-weil-pbc-etale-sum-extension]. ### lem-sp-affine-counit-scalar

Source: `Charts: iterated projective-line integration over the ordered coordinate tower; retained Steps 9 and 10` in the current exceptional blueprint companion proof, with the cited existing source statements. Complete proof text length: 1554 characters.

2. Induct on $d$. At $d=0$ both maps and the counit are identities. At $d+1$ apply the same mate-pasting clause to the $d$-coordinate composite and one more affine-line factor. Its refinement square gives this equality for the chosen compactification as well. The counit is exactly the ordered composite counit, so the induction proves the stated equation.
