This is ill-typed at the rational/common-stage coefficients of the already repaired `def-coefficient-twists-detailed`: only their rationalized/common-stage derived categories are defined, not an ambient category of all discrete coefficient sheaves. At integral coefficients the unit is the completed sheaf, not the constant sheaf of the discrete ring. I corrected the sentence to locally free rank one over the completed unit, finite locally constant rank one at each finite reduction, and invertible images at the field stages. This is a correction to the source's coefficient interpretation, not a new geometric hypothesis or a weakened finiteness conclusion.

An explicit check of the integral distinction is obtained with `E = Q_l` over an algebraically closed field of characteristic different from l. On the pro-étale object corresponding to the profinite set `Z_l`, the completed coefficient sheaf has sections `lim_n LC(Z_l,Z/l^n)`, which include the compatible reductions of the identity map `Z_l -> Z_l`. The discrete constant `Z_l` sheaf has locally constant functions, and the identity is not locally constant. Already the twist with `m = 0` therefore cannot be identified with the discrete constant integral unit. The corrected statement uses the completed coefficient sheaf that its definition actually supplies.

The old single proof paragraph cited only definitions for rank-one existence and a tensor spectral sequence. The new proof instead cites the existing rank-one construction node and the exact-functor cohomology theorem, gives an explicit K-flat representative calculation, and proves product preservation before applying the Roos definition of completeness. Rationalization is the raw scalar localization; no idempotent completion or unstated ambient category is introduced.
