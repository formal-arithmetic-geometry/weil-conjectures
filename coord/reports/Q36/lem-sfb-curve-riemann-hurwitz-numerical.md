## Original argument retained for the two new tasks

The following are the exact six original proof texts, retained without summarizing their calculations. They identify all the arguments transferred to the new statements; only the new nodes' later workers write their proof fields.

### Original Step 1

The function-field extension in the hypotheses is induced by $f$, so $f$ is dominant. It is finite by hypothesis. Its dimension $d$ is a natural number and is positive: the singleton consisting of $1\in k(X)$ is linearly independent over $k(Y)$, since $a\cdot1=0$ implies $a=0$. Thus a basis has at least one element.

### Original Step 2

Set $B=\Omega_{X/k}$ and $A=f^*\Omega_{Y/k}$. The integral curves are nonempty connected curves of dimension one. Here $\Omega_{Z/k}$ is the relative differential sheaf of [def-sc-relative-differential-sheaf] for $Z\to\operatorname{Spec}k$, which on every affine open $\operatorname{Spec}A$ is the sheaf associated with $\Omega_{A/k}$; by the affine-local description in [def-sc-kahler-differentials] it is canonically the sheaf $\Omega^1_{Z/k}$ of that definition. By [lem-sc-canonical-sheaf] these differential sheaves are invertible, and [lem-weil-pbc-invertible-pullback-tensor] (1) makes $A$ invertible. Apply [lem-sfb-separable-curve-differential-nonzero] to the given finite separable function-field extension to obtain $\mathrm df:A\to B$ nonzero.

### Original Step 3

Use the dual line bundle of [def-sc-invertible-module] and put $L=B\otimes A^\vee$, with tensor product as in [def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product]. Locally choose generators $e_A,e_B$. If $\mathrm df(e_A)=h e_B$, associate to this map the section $h e_B\otimes e_A^\vee$ of $L$. On changing generators $e_A'=u e_A$, $e_B'=v e_B$, its coefficient becomes $uv^{-1}h$, and $e_B'\otimes(e_A')^\vee=vu^{-1}(e_B\otimes e_A^\vee)$, so the sections agree on overlaps. They glue to a section $s\in\Gamma(X,L)$, nonzero since $\mathrm df$ is nonzero. Local dual generators show $A^\vee$ and $L$ are locally free of rank one. Since $X$ is integral and proper of dimension one, [lem-trf-curve-section-degree-positivity] applies to $(L,s)$. Set $r=\deg_k L\in\mathbf Z$; then $r\geq0$.

### Original Step 4

All degrees from now on are $\deg_k N=\chi(Z,N)-\chi(Z,\mathcal O_Z)$, as in [def-pc-degree-line-bundle]. For either $Z=X,Y$, [lem-sc-riemann-roch] (1) says $\chi(Z,N)=\deg_{\mathrm{Pic}}N+1-g(Z)$ and $\chi(Z,\mathcal O_Z)=1-g(Z)$. Subtract to get $\deg_k N=\deg_{\mathrm{Pic}}N$. By [lem-sc-canonical-degree] there is a canonical divisor $K_Z$ with $\Omega_{Z/k}\simeq\mathcal O_Z(K_Z)$ and $\deg K_Z=2g(Z)-2$. By [def-sc-pic0] the degree of a line bundle is $\deg[\mathcal O_Z(D)]=\deg D$ on isomorphism classes, so $\deg_{\mathrm{Pic}}\Omega_{Z/k}=\deg K_Z$. Thus $\deg_k\Omega_{Z/k}=2g(Z)-2$. This verifies the agreement of the two degree conventions before using canonical degree.

### Original Step 5

Evaluation gives $A\otimes A^\vee\simeq\mathcal O_X$: on a local generator it is $a e_A\otimes b e_A^\vee\mapsto ab$, whose inverse sends $c$ to $c e_A\otimes e_A^\vee$; these formulas agree on overlaps. Since $\deg_k\mathcal O_X=0$ by [def-pc-degree-line-bundle], tensor additivity [lem-trf-curve-degree-tensor-additive] applied to $A,A^\vee$ gives $\deg_k A^\vee=-\deg_k A$. Apply it again to $B,A^\vee$ to obtain $r=\deg_k L=\deg_k B-\deg_k A$. Degree is invariant under isomorphism because its defining cohomology dimensions are.

### Original Step 6

Apply [lem-sfb-proper-curve-bundle-pullback-degree] with $\mathcal M=\Omega_{Y/k}$. The curves are proper integral of dimension one, $f$ is dominant, its function-field extension is finite, and $\mathcal M$ is invertible by Step 2; hence $\deg_k A=d\deg_k\Omega_{Y/k}$. Substituting this and Step 4 into Step 5 gives $r=(2g(X)-2)-d(2g(Y)-2)$, or $2g(X)-2=d(2g(Y)-2)+r$. Step 1 gives $d>0$ and Step 3 gives $r\geq0$. No step assumes tame ramification or invertibility of $d$ in $k$.
