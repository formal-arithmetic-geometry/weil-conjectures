The current source's path-independence argument is as follows. Changing gamma to gamma' gives theta_2 = Ad(g) theta_1 with g=gamma' gamma^(-1) in pi_1(U_0). With e_s=[k(s):k_0]>=1 and D the image of D_s, the source has deg(D)=e_s Zhat. Choose d in D with deg(g)-deg(d) an integer; this is possible since Zhat=Z+e_s Zhat. Then w=g d^(-1) is in W(U_0), so rho(w) exists even when rho(g) and rho(d) do not. If d=theta_1(delta), normality of I_s in D_s gives d theta_1(I_s) d^(-1)=theta_1(I_s), and the abelian residue group gives delta Phi delta^(-1) Phi^(-1) in I_s. Thus the invariant space for theta_2 is rho(w) times the invariant space for theta_1, and the residue Frobenius operators intertwine under rho(w); replacing a residue lift has no effect by the quotient-invariants leaf. This preserves the old report's degree/conjugation route while leaving the new path-independence node statement-only.
