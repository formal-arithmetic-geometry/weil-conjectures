## Source error

I verified the previously reported defect in Zheng's alternative injective-evaluation argument on printed p. 24; the item itself is not disputed. In the punctual topos take O=k[[t]], lambda=t, and the injective A_1-module k. The right adjoint e_(1,*) of exact evaluation produces an injective system whose second evaluation is k as an A_2=k[t]/(t^2)-module. That module is not injective: the linear map (t) to k sending t to 1 cannot extend to A_2, since every A_2-linear map A_2 to k kills t. Hence evaluation does not in general preserve injectives in this varying-ring system category. The unbounded proof immediately preceding Lemma 4.10, which uses the left adjoint LE_n, remains valid and is precisely the proof used here. No source error requires changing the item's statement.
