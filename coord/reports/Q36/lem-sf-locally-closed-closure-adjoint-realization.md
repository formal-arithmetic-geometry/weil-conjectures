1. **Noetherian input and quasi-compactness.** `lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian` says a finite-type scheme over a Noetherian scheme is Noetherian. It applies to both given finite-type structure maps after the elementary two-ideals check for the field. `lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-open-subscheme-noetherian` actually asserts quasi-compactness of every open subscheme, not just local Noetherianity. Thus it applies to V and to the inverse images of affine opens under s, c and v. The argument proves the morphism quasi-compactness needed later. No finite-type assumption on an arbitrary open was inserted.

2. **Canonical closure.** `def-nagata-scheme-theoretic-image` explicitly gives the largest quasi-coherent kernel ideal, closed image and canonical factor, and identifies the raw kernel when s is quasi-compact. `lemma-nagata-immersion-open-in-scheme-image` states that its constructed factor is a quasi-compact open immersion. Its quasi-compact hypothesis was checked in Step 1. These are statements of the nodes themselves; the proof does not claim that mere topological image containment gives an arbitrary scheme factorization. In particular the closure is not reduced by fiat.

3. **Finite type, separatedness and compactification.** `lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-proper` includes properness and finite type of c and i. `lem-weil-pbc-qc-open-immersion-finite-type` applies to v and u, whose quasi-compactness is already available. `lem-sfb-finite-type-composition-base-change` supplies the required finite-type compositions. `lem-weil-pbc-separated-cancellation` states separatedness of immersions; clause (e) of `lem-weil-pbc-proper-fp-permanence` states separated composition. Thus the ambient schemes V and the closure are separated over k. `def-weil-compactifications-refinements` really defines a compactification as open then proper, so the established maps qualify without assuming T proper over k.

4. **Closure Noetherianity, complements and dimension.** Finite type of i allows `lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian` to apply over T. The open-subscheme node then supplies quasi-compactness of every relevant open complement in V and the closure, the exact hypothesis needed by pro-etale closed exactness. `lem-kun-dimension-finite-type` states finiteness of the scheme's chain dimension, and I compared `def-kun-dimension` and `def-kun-topological-krull-dimension`: both use the same strict chains of nonempty irreducible closed subsets. Passing from finite dimension to a nonnegative integer bound is elementary; the empty case has bound zero.

5. **Exact sheaf functors and lower bounds.** `lem-per-etale-closed-sheaf-internal-adjunction` explicitly asserts exactness of i_* for constant ring module sheaves on small etale sites, so it is a correct citation here; a topological/Zariski pushforward exactness node would not be. `lem-weil-pbc-extension-by-zero-exact` is the etale open result, `lem-weil-extension-by-zero-exact` the pro-etale open result, and `lem-exc-proetale-closed-exact` the pro-etale closed result with the checked complement hypothesis.
