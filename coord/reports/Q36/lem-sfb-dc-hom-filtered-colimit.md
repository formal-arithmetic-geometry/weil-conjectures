## Research handoff for the new statement

The future proof has a distinct task, not the internal-Hom conclusion. First tensor-colimit preservation gives kappa degreewise using the specified underlying-module colimit of S. The differential squares follow from functoriality. Its legs are the tensor maps of q_i; sheafification-unit pure tensors determine them locally, so no invalid claim that all sections of a tensor sheaf are global pure tensors is needed.

For a short exact sequence of R-module sheaves, each S_i tensor sequence is exact because its underlying module is a finite power of R. Taking the assumed exact I-colimit and transporting through kappa gives exactness of U E. Restriction retains the underlying abelian sheaves and reflects exactness, proving E exact as an S-module functor. This works for arbitrary small filtered categories, not just a chosen directed poset. In every degree, exactness identifies the kernel and image of the extended differential, hence cohomology. It follows that E preserves every unbounded quasi-isomorphism without a boundedness or Tor-amplitude assumption.

For any K-flat resolution p:P->A, derived change of rings gives E^L(Q_R A) represented by EP. The actual derived comparison to Q_S(EA) is Q_S(Ep), now invertible because E preserves all quasi-isomorphisms. Its identity on this fixed representative establishes the canonical comparison, not merely existence of some isomorphism. Independence of p and naturality follow from the comparison of K-flat resolutions in derived extension and the universal property of localization; compatibility with inverses follows by applying the commuting comparison square to a quasi-isomorphism. Compatible coefficient-diagram maps give commuting tensor-cocone squares on their pure-tensor legs and the same commuting derived comparison. These arguments are research notes, not a split of the new node: it is delivered statement-only for another worker.
