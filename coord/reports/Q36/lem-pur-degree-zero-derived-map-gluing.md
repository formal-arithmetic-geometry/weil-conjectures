### Inherited Step 3 → lem-pur-open-cover-cohomology-detection

Fix $a\ne0$ and set $F=\mathcal H^a(M)$ or $\mathcal H^a(N)$. Step 2 and the hypotheses give $F|_{U_i}=0$ for every $i$. For any object $V$ of $T_\tau$, the sections of $F$ over $V\times_T U_i$ are therefore zero, by the sectionwise formula in [def-pur-localized-ringed-site].
