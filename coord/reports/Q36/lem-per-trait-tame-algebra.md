Unfinished work: the new node is deliberately unsplit under the one-level brief. Its argument for the future worker is the following complete finite-order calculation. Write the maximal ideal as (t). For a residue-trivial automorphism a with a(t)/t congruent to 1, expansion of a(t^i c) shows (a-id)(t^i C) is contained in t^(i+1) C, for every i>=0. If a is nonidentity choose c with x=a(c)-c nonzero and let i be the finite valuation of x. Its residue-triviality gives i>=1. Each a^j(x) is congruent to x modulo t^(i+1). If a^N=id, telescoping gives 0=sum_{j=0}^{N-1} a^j(x), so N*x belongs to t^(i+1). Divide by t^i and reduce to obtain N times a nonzero residue equal to zero. Residue characteristic zero contradicts N>0. Thus a=id. This is precisely the telescoping argument in Stacks 09EE, without the unnecessary Galois-extension hypotheses. No part of this proof has been put inside the new node's statement.
