- `I = ⊤`: torsion is the zero functor; both dimensions are bottom (`-1`).
- `I = ⊥`: torsion is the identity functor; the dimension is zero for a nonzero
  ring, and bottom for the zero ring. Extension still gives the zero ideal.
  This includes an empty generating list.
- Zero target: all target modules vanish, so its dimension is bottom. A unital
  map from the zero source forces the target to be zero as well.
- Images of individual generators may vanish, or all images may generate the

- Identity maps give the reflexive inequality. Non-flat quotient maps remain
  admissible; no exactness of tensoring is asserted.
- Distinct ring universes are admitted explicitly. Arbitrary modules are not
  restricted to finite or finitely generated modules.
