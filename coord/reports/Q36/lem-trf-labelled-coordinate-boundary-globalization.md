Closed subschemes are represented by Mathlib `Y.IdealSheafData`: these carry the actual
quasi-coherent ideal, its canonical closed immersion `subschemeι`, and its associated scheme
`subscheme`. Every closed immersion is represented by its kernel ideal, up to the canonical
isomorphism over Y. Thus this encoding does not restrict the given closed subschemes.
Infimum is scheme-theoretic union, supremum is scheme-theoretic intersection, and `.radical`
is reduction. In particular containment of closed subschemes reverses ideal inclusion.
