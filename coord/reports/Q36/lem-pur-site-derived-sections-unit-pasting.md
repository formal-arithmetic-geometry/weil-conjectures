## Review by Q36

I checked the statement against the source and all eight steps against their cited node statements, not merely the dependency names. The hypothesis on alpha is essential: a geometric inverse-image comparison comes from set sheaves, so its component at the terminal sheaf is the unique terminal comparison. An arbitrary R-linear automorphism can multiply a section by a unit and would invalidate the asserted invariance. The proof retains, uses and explains that restriction.

Step 1 uses lem-pur-ringed-site-grothendieck-abelian for arbitrary small ringed sites, not the etale-scheme-only Grothendieck node. Its statement explicitly gives the Grothendieck property. Lem-sp-grothendieck-enough-injectives gives enough injectives, and lem-weil-pbc-complex-injective-resolution-mono gives degreewise monomorphic replacements with the same lower bound. Lem-weil-pbc-bounded-below-injectives-kinjective and lem-sc-kinjective-quasi-iso-comparison give respectively K-injectivity and the localization Hom bijection. Lem-weil-pbc-right-derived-plus-injective explicitly gives natural comparison maps and their invertibility on injective complexes. Def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category explicitly says kernels are sectionwise, which justifies left exactness of sections. All categories here are module categories on the given small sites; no scheme or finite coefficient hypothesis is smuggled in.

Steps 2 and 3 use def-trf-geometric-morphism for the actual adjunction, finite-limit preservation, composite inverse image and the set-sheaf 2-morphism convention. The additional identities are elementary calculations, not assertions extracted from that definition: the sections bijection is the adjunction at the terminal object; its R-linearity follows by applying naturality to addition and constant scalar endomorphisms. The triangle identity identifies sections of the ordinary unit with inverse image of a section. Naturality at x:1 to F then proves the composition and alpha equations, since their components at 1 are the unique terminal identifications. These arguments are written in the steps and require no new lemma node.

Step 4 uses lem-sp-right-adjoint-preserves-injectives with the exact inverse image as left adjoint. This gives injectivity of f_*I, not of f^{-1}I. Lem-weil-pbc-right-derived-plus-injective therefore computes both sides of lambda on the same source resolution; the explicit s-map gives lambda. Lem-sc-kinjective-quasi-iso-comparison supplies passage from homotopy maps to derived maps. Equation (*) is checked using naturality of the comparisons at P to I, even though f_*P to f_*I need not be a quasi-isomorphism. In particular the proof never incorrectly calls f_*I a resolution of f_*P. This resolves the main possible comparison gap.

Step 5 invokes exactly the unit formula in lem-trf-bounded-below-derived-adjunction, whose statement includes the right-derived comparison a on a noninjective pulled-back complex. Lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-factorization gives the termwise model for the exact inverse image. The monomorphic injective resolution lemma supplies b. Applying (*) and comparison naturality writes the unit/Leray map as (**). The middle section complex is deliberately accompanied by beta; it is never identified with derived sections without a resolution. The source has enough injectives by Step 1, so the hypotheses of the derived adjunction are all present.

Step 6 uses the same monomorphic resolution lemma for g^{-1}J and exact-functor factorization for preservation of quasi-isomorphisms. It then multiplies the chain maps from Step 5. The only interchange is the specific naturality equation already proved in Step 3, and the displayed c inverse cancels c in the explicit compositor equation. This checks the order and direction of every arrow. There is no appeal to an unquoted derived-composition coherence theorem.

Step 7 localizes the given geometric alpha using exact-functor factorization. With delta=alpha c inverse, the resolution on the h side is literally r delta inverse, and its chain map cancels delta from p_h. This proves transport before homology, and the target identification is precisely RΓ(delta), giving RΓ(alpha) after Step 6. The argument is a short substitution in already established formulas, so it is marked elementary.

Step 8 uses the monomorphic replacement theorem in lower bound zero. Lem-weil-pbc-lift-along-injective-quasi-iso explicitly requires a degreewise monomorphic quasi-isomorphism; here it is f^{-1}K to f^{-1}I, which is such because inverse image is exact. The target J is bounded below and termwise injective. The unique-up-to-homotopy node explicitly applies to any quasi-isomorphism into such a target, so it verifies independence from every allowed comparison, not just a chosen replacement. Sections preserve the homotopy equation because they are additive. The source and target section complexes have no negative terms, proving the negative-degree clause too.
