## Source error

The conclusion of 0ADV(2) and of 04U0 is correct, but the point-indexed refinement in the proof of 04U0 is not enough for the displayed size estimate. It bounds the number of cover members by the number of points of the base, then concludes a bound by size of the base. These are different cardinals. For example let R = F_2[x_0,x_1,...]. R is countable; the size of Spec R as defined in sets.tex is aleph_0 (000P). But every binary sequence defines a different maximal ideal (x_n-a_n)_n, so Spec R has at least 2^aleph_0 points. Thus the inference from a point-indexed cover to |I| <= size(S) is invalid.

Step 2 repairs just this inference: affine source pieces of the fppf cover have open images, and affine opens of the base form a basis. Choose at most one source piece for each affine basis open lying inside one of those images. This still covers the base and has index set bounded by its set of affine opens. Steps 4–6 then apply the true size estimates of 04T7/000Q and the bounded-size closure of the fixed scheme category in 000J/000R. The item statement needs no correction and is not STATEMENT-DISPUTED.

The last sentence of the proof of 03H6 says the constructed atlas is finite; the construction actually supplies an etale surjection from the finite disjoint union of affine chart sources. The new atlas node states exactly the lemma's etale conclusion, and the item never uses finiteness of the atlas morphism.
