### Returned step 1: lem-trf-smooth-stalk-artinian-criterion

Put $A=\mathcal O_{T,t}$, $B=\mathcal O_{Z,z}$ and $\varphi=f_z^\sharp:A\to B$. These are Noetherian local rings: choose a Noetherian affine neighbourhood of $t$ and a finite-type affine chart of $f$ containing $z$. Its source coordinate ring is Noetherian by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-algebra-noetherian]. Both stalks are localizations of these affine coordinate rings by [lem-weil-pbc-affine-open-stalk-localization], hence Noetherian by [lem-weil-pbc-localization-noetherian]. In particular their maximal ideals are finitely generated by [def-weil-pbc-noetherian-ring]. Write $i_A:A\to\widehat A$, $i_B:B\to\widehat B$ and $h=\widehat\varphi$, so $h i_A=i_B\varphi$ and $h$ is the given isomorphism.

Original cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-algebra-noetherian, lem-weil-pbc-affine-open-stalk-localization, lem-weil-pbc-localization-noetherian, def-weil-pbc-noetherian-ring.

### Returned step 2: lem-trf-completion-isomorphism-local-invariants

By [lem-weil-pbc-local-completion-local-ring], the maximal ideals of $\widehat A$ and $\widehat B$ are $\mathfrak m_A\widehat A$ and $\mathfrak m_B\widehat B$, and the canonical completion maps identify their residue fields with those of $A$ and $B$. A ring isomorphism preserves units and therefore carries the unique maximal ideal onto the unique maximal ideal, as well as its square onto its square. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-adic-completion-mod-power] with $N=1,2$ on both sides. It follows that $\varphi$ induces isomorphisms $A/\mathfrak m_A^N\to B/\mathfrak m_B^N$ for $N=1,2$: these maps are the quotient maps induced by $h$, since $h i_A=i_B\varphi$. In particular the actual residue-field map $\kappa(t)\to\kappa(z)$ is an isomorphism, proving the second conclusion already.

Original cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-local-completion-local-ring, lem-weil-pbc-adic-completion-mod-power.

### Returned step 3: lem-trf-completion-isomorphism-local-invariants

Let $J=\varphi(\mathfrak m_A)B\subseteq\mathfrak m_B$. For $b\in\mathfrak m_B$, the isomorphism modulo squares of step 2 supplies $a\in A$ with $b-\varphi(a)\in\mathfrak m_B^2$. Its reduction modulo $\mathfrak m_B$ is zero, so the injectivity of the residue-field map implies $a\in\mathfrak m_A$. Thus $\mathfrak m_B=J+\mathfrak m_B^2$. The finite $B$-module $M=\mathfrak m_B/J$ satisfies $\mathfrak m_BM=M$. The Jacobson radical of a local ring is its unique maximal ideal, so [lem-weil-pbc-nakayama-jacobson] gives $M=0$, that is $J=\mathfrak m_B$.

Original cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-nakayama-jacobson.

### Returned step 4: lem-trf-smooth-stalk-artinian-criterion

To prove smoothness at $z$, apply the small-Artinian-extension criterion of [lem-weil-pbc-smooth-artinian-lifting-at-point]. Fix one of its diagrams, with $q:C'\twoheadrightarrow C$ a small extension and $\alpha:\operatorname{Spec}C\to Z$, $\beta:\operatorname{Spec}C'\to T$. By [lem-weil-pbc-artinian-local-unique-prime] the spectra have one point; the point of $\alpha$ is $z$, and the point of $\beta$ is consequently $t$. By [lem-weil-pbc-morphism-from-spec-local-ring] these morphisms correspond to local homomorphisms $b:B\to C$ and $a:A\to C'$. Their compatibility is $qa=b\varphi$, by [lem-weil-pbc-scheme-stalk-map-composition]. It remains to construct a local $c:B\to C'$ with $qc=b$ and $c\varphi=a$.

### Returned step 5: lem-trf-completion-isomorphism-artinian-lifts

By [lem-weil-pbc-artinian-local-noetherian-nilpotent] choose $r,s\ge1$ such that $\mathfrak m_{C'}^{r}=0$ and $\mathfrak m_C^s=0$. Locality gives $a(\mathfrak m_A^r)=0$ and $b(\mathfrak m_B^s)=0$. Factor $a$ through $\bar a:A/\mathfrak m_A^r\to C'$ and $b$ through $\bar b:B/\mathfrak m_B^s\to C$, and define $\widehat a=\bar a\pi_{A,r}:\widehat A\to C'$ and $\widehat b=\bar b\pi_{B,s}:\widehat B\to C$ using the projections of [lem-weil-pbc-adic-completion-mod-power]. Then $\widehat a i_A=a$, $\widehat b i_B=b$, and these maps kill $(\mathfrak m_A\widehat A)^r$ and $(\mathfrak m_B\widehat B)^s$ respectively. Here extension of an ideal commutes with powers: both ideals are generated by the same products of $r$, respectively $s$, elements of the original maximal ideal.

Original cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-artinian-local-noetherian-nilpotent, lem-weil-pbc-adic-completion-mod-power.

### Returned step 6: lem-trf-completion-isomorphism-artinian-lifts

The homomorphisms $q\widehat a$ and $\widehat b h$ from $\widehat A$ to $C$ agree on $i_A(A)$ because $qa=b\varphi$ and $hi_A=i_B\varphi$. They both kill $(\mathfrak m_A\widehat A)^n$ for $n\ge r,s$, since $h$ carries the extended maximal ideal of $A$ onto that of $B$ (step 2). For any $u\in\widehat A$, choose $a_0\in A$ representing $\pi_{A,n}(u)\in A/\mathfrak m_A^n$. By [lem-weil-pbc-adic-completion-mod-power], $u-i_A(a_0)$ belongs to that killed ideal. Consequently $q\widehat a(u)=qa(a_0)=b\varphi(a_0)=\widehat b h(u)$, proving $q\widehat a=\widehat b h$. Set $c=\widehat a h^{-1}i_B$. Then $qc=\widehat b i_B=b$ and $c\varphi=\widehat a h^{-1}i_B\varphi=\widehat a i_A=a$.

Original cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-adic-completion-mod-power.

Step 3 gives the required maximal-ideal equality, and step 2 gives a residue extension of degree one, hence finite separable (every element has a linear minimal polynomial). We obtain an open $W\subseteq U$ containing $z$ with $f|_W$ unramified. By [lem-pc-open-immersion-smooth-composition], $f|_W$ is smooth. By [sbc-lem-smooth-criterion] it is flat and locally of finite presentation; together with unramifiedness this is precisely etaleness by [def-weil-etale-morphism]. Since $W$ is open in $U$ and $U$ is open in $Z$, it is the required open subscheme of $Z$.
