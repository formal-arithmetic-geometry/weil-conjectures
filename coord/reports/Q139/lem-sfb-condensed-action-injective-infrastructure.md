## Statement dispute

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the phrase “restriction along every continuous u:H→J” is false for arbitrary continuous maps. Let H=J=C₂ with their discrete topologies, let R be the discrete condensed integer ring, and let J act on its regular underlying integer module by the sign character. The continuous constant map sending both elements of H to the nonidentity element of J sends the identity to that element. Pulling back the action along this map makes the identity of H act by -1 on the nonzero integer section over the point, violating the identity law of an action. This is already a failure to define the functor, not a failure of exactness.

Corrected the item to “every continuous group homomorphism u:H→J”. This is the conventional meaning of restriction of group actions and is exactly the morphism of group sheaves to which the existing inflation theorem applies. The parent restriction-calculus node uses “continuous homomorphism”, so the correction agrees with its use and removes no intended case. All other clauses of the statement are retained verbatim.
