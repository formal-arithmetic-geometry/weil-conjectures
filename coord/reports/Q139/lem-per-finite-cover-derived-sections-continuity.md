Proof retained for the future worker: write E_i for evaluation. Exactness of f_a^{-1} makes kernels and cokernels of systems componentwise, so E_i is exact and the system category is abelian. For an injective A on C_k, the right adjoint R_k to E_k has component (R_k A)_i = product over b:k→i of f_{b,*}A, with the adjoint transition maps. The adjunction is checked by taking the identity-arrow component; all other components are forced by system compatibility. Thus R_k(A) is injective in the SYSTEM category, because E_k is exact. Given F, choose F_k→A_k injective and map F→product_k R_k(A_k). At component i the identity-arrow coordinate F_i→A_i proves this is monic. This is the 0EXZ construction, with its system-level injectivity made explicit. Evaluation E_i also has the left adjoint (L_i A)_j = coproduct over a:j→i of f_a^{-1}A. This left adjoint is exact since inverse images and direct sums of abelian sheaves are exact, so E_i preserves injectives. Apply 013K(3) INSIDE the system category to obtain a monomorphic bounded-below injective resolution, then evaluate. All products and sums range over sets by smallness. This is a real additional lemma, not a three-line consequence of the existing componentwise-injective embedding statement; notably that statement does not assert that its targets are injective as SYSTEMS.
