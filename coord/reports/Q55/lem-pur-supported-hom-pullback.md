### Old step 4 → lem-pur-supported-hom-cocycle-normalization

For a module sheaf $M$, precomposition with $\rho$ identifies $\operatorname{Hom}(P,M)$ with the kernel of $\operatorname{Hom}(A_X,M)\to\operatorname{Hom}(j_!A_U,M)$. The etale open adjunction [lem-weil-pbc-extension-by-zero-adjunction] and its pro-etale counterpart [lem-cbc-proetale-extension-zero] identify the latter Hom with $M(U)$. A module map $A_X\to M$ is determined by the image of the global section $1$; its inverse sends $s\in M(X)$ to the sectionwise map $a\mapsto as|_V$ on each object $V$. Under these identifications precomposition with $\epsilon$ is restriction: $(\varphi\rho\epsilon)_U(1)=0$, and for an arbitrary map $\beta:A_X\to M$ its restriction is $(\beta\epsilon)_U(1)=\beta_X(1)|_U$. Thus
$$\theta_M:\operatorname{Hom}(P,M)\xrightarrow{\sim}\Gamma_Z(X,M),\qquad\varphi\longmapsto(\varphi\rho)_X(1)$$
is an additive natural isomorphism, where the kernel is exactly the definition in [def-pur-support-cohomology]. The same formula defines $\theta'$ on the target site.

### Old step 6 → lem-pur-supported-hom-cocycle-normalization

All sites are taken with the small skeleta in the conventions of the statement. The categories of ordinary module sheaves are Grothendieck by [prop-pur-kinjective-resolutions-exist]. ### Old step 7 → lem-pur-derived-representable-transport

### Old step 8 → lem-pur-derived-representable-transport

For a cocycle $f:P\to I^a$ representing $\alpha$, step 6 represents $\chi(\alpha)$ by $e[a]^{-1}\circ f$, and step 7 represents the pulled-back supported class by $t[a]\circ Lf\circ d^{-1}$. Consequently its target $\chi'$ is
$$e'[a]^{-1}\circ t[a]\circ Lf\circ d^{-1}=(Le)[a]^{-1}\circ Lf\circ d^{-1}=L(\chi(\alpha))\circ d^{-1}.$$
Here $tLe=e'$ in the homotopy category by step 7, and hence also in the derived category. The canonical shift comparison is the one obtained by applying the additive exact functor termwise. Homotopic choices of $t$ induce homotopic maps of both complexes, and boundaries in $f$ give null-homotopic maps out of $P[0]$, as in [lem-sc-p1-rhom-hom-derived-roof]. Thus the equality is independent of resolutions and holds in every integer degree. Specializing $L$ and $d$ gives the etale formula, the pro-etale formula (in particular for etale-origin $F$), and the formula for $\nu^*$ and $\gamma$.
