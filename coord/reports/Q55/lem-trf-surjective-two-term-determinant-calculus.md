The source's final paragraph of 0FJK leaves the simultaneous-complement construction over the residue field and its lifting to a local ring as “Some details omitted”. This is an independent linear-algebra lemma, now a statement-only child. It does not state homotopy invariance or any determinant calculus. For checking its truth, here is the omitted argument: for two equal-dimensional kernels A,B in a finite-dimensional vector space choose I=A∩B and decompositions A=I⊕A', B=I⊕B'. Their remaining dimensions agree. If e_j,f_j are bases of A',B', the span of e_j+f_j is disjoint from both A and B in A+B. Add a complement of A+B to get a common complement of both kernels. This works also over F_2. Lift a basis of this complement to R^n. Each of its two m×m image matrices has nonzero determinant modulo the maximal ideal, hence unit determinant over the local ring; the adjugate formula gives inverses. The resulting span U maps isomorphically under both surjections. Thus no infinite-residue-field hypothesis, Noetherian condition or unproved Nakayama step has been imported into the statement.

The source's composition proof is omitted except for hints to 0FJB–0FJD. Steps 9–11 give the exact sequence of kernels, the compatibility of its differential trivializations, and the three-block wedge calculation. The determinant-line construction itself is a genuine module construction (rank variation, gluing, lift independence and invertibility) and stays as a lower-level input.
