## Source error

The Harvard PDF's printed Lemma 5.5.2.1, pp. 462-463, has the wrong variance: it describes the functor corepresented by a presheaf and claims its restriction is that presheaf. Already for the one-object terminal indexing category and the empty presheaf, this would identify the space of maps from the empty space to the point (a point) with the empty space. The corrected assertion needed in Proposition 5.5.2.2 is instead

$$\operatorname{Map}_{\mathcal P(D)}(j(d),F)\simeq F(d).$$

The new Yoneda-evaluation node states this formula, with coherent naturality. I checked it directly against HTT Lemma 5.1.5.2 and Kerodon 8.3.1.3/8.3.1.5 applied to the opposite category. This corrects an auxiliary source error; the assigned node's statement is true and needs no new hypothesis. The item also expands the source's unexplained assertion that its representing presheaf is local: step 5 writes both inverse equations for the reflector's unit.
