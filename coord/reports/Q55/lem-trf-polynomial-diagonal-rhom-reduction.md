## New nodes

`lem-trf-derived-bimodule-tensor-hom` is an ordinary-module specialization, with S-linear internal-Hom enhancement, of Stacks dga.tex Tags 09LN (lemma-tensor-hom-adjunction) and 09LT (lemma-tensor-hom-adjoint). For its eventual proof, take a K-projective B-complex P representing X and a K-injective S-complex I representing V. Hom_B(P,Hom_S(E,I)) is the same S-linear complex as Hom_S(Tot(P tensor_B E),I), by currying. P is K-flat over B, so the inner tensor computes the derived tensor. K-projectivity and K-injectivity compute the two derived Homs. Hom_S(-,I) carries quasi-isomorphisms of B-complexes to quasi-isomorphisms after restriction, hence also as B-complexes with precomposition action. This explains representative independence without any S-flatness of B. This proof explanation is in the report only; the new node contains a statement and no proof.
