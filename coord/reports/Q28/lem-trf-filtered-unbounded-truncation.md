## Source error

Varshavsky §5.1.1(c) calls the subcomplex cutoff a left adjoint and the quotient cutoff a right adjoint. The directions must be reversed. For a nonzero vector space V in cochain degree zero, let J_s(V) have filtration V for indices at most s and zero above s. The identity of V defines a filtered map J_{r-1}(V) → J_r(V); it remains nonzero after localization, since forgetting sends it to the identity. But t_{≥r}J_{r-1}(V)=0 and t_{<r}J_r(V)=0. These give direct counterexamples to both adjunctions as printed. The two natural Hom bijections proved in Step 9 give the right directions. This was already corrected in the item's statement and source field; there is no further statement change or new dispute.
