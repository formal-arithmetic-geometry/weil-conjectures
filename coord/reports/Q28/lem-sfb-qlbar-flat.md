## Proof in words: lem-sfb-qlbar-tail-module-colimit

Source: the first sentence of `literature/blueprint/main-20261015/blueprint/sec-six-functors-basics/sk-prop-sfb-finite-extension-compatibility/lem-sfb-qlbar-flat.proof.md`, expanded from the returned steps 2-3. The generic ring-to-module lemma follows Stacks Tag 03DB (`sites-modules.tex`, `lemma-limits-colimits`); I read that source and its module-limit and colimit paragraphs. Each of the following three paragraphs is one proof step.

1. By [det-def-sfb-qlbar-condensed-subextensions], $I_E$ contains $E$ and any two of its fields have finite compositum. For each finite subextension $H$ of $\Omega$, $EH$ belongs to $I_E$. A ring-sheaf cocone $(t_F:F_X\to A)_{F\in I_E}$ extends to all finite subextensions by $t'_H=t_{EH}\circ(H_X\to(EH)_X)$. For $H\subseteq H'$, both compatibility composites factor through $(EH')_X$, and agree by functoriality of coefficient inclusions [def-sfb-subfield-coefficient-map, sk-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients]. The extension restricts to the original cocone because $EH=H$ when $E\subseteq H$. Conversely every extended cocone is forced by the arrow $H\subseteq EH$. These constructions are inverse, also on morphisms of cocones. Hence [sk-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-x] exhibits the specified $q_F:F_X\to B$ as a ring-sheaf colimit over $I_E$.

2. Here the internal ring colimit may be read in section-ring sheaves: multiplication is $r s=\mu(r\otimes s)$ and the unit is $\eta(1)$, the reconstruction in [def-weil-pbc-internal-abelian-sheaf-module-equivalence]. A map commutes with these operations exactly when it commutes with $\mu,\eta$, since maps out of sheafified tensor are precisely compatible bilinear section maps [sk-def-sfb-ring-object]. This identifies the ring morphisms and cocones. All coefficient rings are commutative by [sk-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients], so the colimit universal property also holds among commutative ring sheaves.

3. Give $F_X$ the $R$-action via $s_F:E_X\to F_X$, and $B$ its specified action via $s:E_X\to B$. For $F\subseteq G$, the equality $a_{FG}s_F=s_G$ follows from functoriality of pullback of the inclusions $E\subseteq F\subseteq G$ [def-sfb-subfield-coefficient-map, sk-def-sfb-pulled-back-coefficients]. The equality $q_Fs_F=s$ is [lem-sfb-subfield-coefficient-map-comp]. Apply [lem-sfb-directed-ring-sheaf-module-colimit] with these rings, maps and the preceding ring colimit. Its nonempty-directed and size hypotheses hold by the first paragraph and the chosen coefficient universe. Its conclusion is exactly the required $R$-module colimit with these same $q_F$.
