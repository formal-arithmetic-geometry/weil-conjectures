For the eventual worker, the retained mathematical argument is: positive-degree induction from the basis modulo m^2 gives the surjection from the weighted polynomial ring. Localizing at the origin gives a surjection from a d-dimensional regular local polynomial ring to the regular local ring B_m, also of dimension d by its cotangent basis. A nonzero homogeneous element cannot disappear on localization at the homogeneous maximal ideal: an annihilator outside it has nonzero constant term, and comparison of the least graded component gives a contradiction. Therefore the global kernel is zero. A finite homogeneous cotangent basis exists by finite generation and grading. This argument is deliberately retained here, not inserted as a proof in the new node.
