## Source error

Varshavsky §5.1.1(c), p.39, interchanges “left” and “right” for the two filtration adjoints. The natural inclusion and quotient, and §5.1.2(c)'s triangle, determine the correct directions. Over the field $k=\mathbf F_2$, let $P_a$ be $k[0]$ with pure filtration grade $a$ ($F^rP_a=k$ for $r\le a$, zero otherwise). Then $\operatorname{Hom}(P_0,P_1)=k$, but $t_{\ge1}P_0=0$ and $t_{\le0}P_1=0$. If $t_{\ge1}$ were left adjoint to inclusion, its adjunction with target $P_1$ would identify $0$ with $k$. If $t_{\le0}$ were right adjoint, its adjunction with source $P_0$ would likewise identify $k$ with $0$. These Hom computations persist after localization: forgetting detects the nonzero identity of $k$, and no chain homotopies exist between distinct degree-zero maps. The new node now writes both Hom bijections in full: $t_{\ge r}$ is right adjoint and $t_{<r}$ is left adjoint. This agrees with the already reviewed bounded-below truncation node. The item's statement is unchanged; the false intermediate assertion of the first split is corrected below.
