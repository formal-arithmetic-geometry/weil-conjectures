## Source error

Stacks Tag 07PC says that the image S' of S tensor_R R' in the auxiliary field K' has fraction field K'. Its preceding field diagram does not ensure that equality. For example take R=L=F_p, S=F_p[x], K=F_p(x), and K'=F_p(y) with x mapped to y^p. Then L/R is trivial finite purely inseparable, K'/K is finite purely inseparable, and K'/L is separably generated. Choose the allowed model R'=L=F_p. The image S' is F_p[y^p], so its fraction field is F_p(y^p), strictly smaller than K'=F_p(y).

Step 4 corrects the inference: the fraction field is the compositum KL inside K'. This field is separable over L because the separability of K'/L restricts to subextensions directly from the universal quantifier of def-weil-pbc-separable-field-extension. Thus the application of Tag 07PB is valid with KL/L, and the statement of the item is unchanged. This is a source-proof error, not a STATEMENT-DISPUTED claim about the item. It does not change the unit route.
