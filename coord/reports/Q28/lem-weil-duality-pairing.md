## Preserved cone calculation from the preceding split

The preceding split's step 4 contained the following argument, now separated as the new helper's working material. Its statement is deliberately unsplit in the JSON.
We make the double-dual shift comparison explicit. With cochain shifts as in [def-weil-abstract-derived-category], there is a chain isomorphism $s_{C,m}:T(C[m])\to T(C)[-m]$ sending a degree-$q$ map $f$ to $(-1)^{mq}f$, after reindexing its components. Substitution into $d(f)=d_If-(-1)^qfd_C$ checks it is a chain map. Put $\chi_C=-s_{T(C),-1}\circ T(s_{C,1})^{-1}:T^2(C[1])\to T^2(C)[1]$. Concretely, on a degree-$p$ map $g\in T^2(C[1])^p$ and $y\in T(C)^r$, this is $(\chi_Cg)(y)=(-1)^{p+r}g(y)$, where the argument on the right is reindexed into $T(C[1])^{r+1}$. We check directly that this is an exact comparison, rather than deducing the sign from exactness alone. For a chain map $u:A\to B$, let $E=\operatorname{Cone}(u)$, with maps $i:B\to E$ and $\pi:E\to A[1]$; its cone triangle ends in $-\pi$ by [def-weil-abstract-derived-category]. Put $X=T(B)$, $Y=T(A)$, and $h=T(u)$. A degree-$q$ element of $T(E)$ is $(x,y)\in X^q\oplus Y^{q-1}$, and [def-pur-hom-complex] gives differential $(d_Xx,d_Yy+(-1)^{q+1}h(x))$. Write a degree-$p$ element of $T^2(E)$ as $(\alpha,\beta)$, where $\alpha:X^q\to I^{q+p}$ and $\beta:Y^r\to I^{r+p+1}$. Set $\gamma(y)=(-1)^{p+r}\beta(y)$ for $y\in Y^r$. Then $\theta_E(\alpha,\beta)=(\alpha,\gamma)$ identifies $T^2(E)$ with $\operatorname{Cone}(T^2u)$. Indeed, the first differential component on $x\in X^q$ is $d_{T^2B}\alpha(x)+(-1)^{p+q}\beta(h(x))=d_{T^2B}\alpha(x)+(T^2u)(\gamma)(x)$, and the second raw component is $\beta'(y)=d_I\beta(y)-(-1)^p\beta(d_Yy)$; multiplying it by $(-1)^{p+1+r}$ gives $\gamma'=-d_{T^2A}\gamma$. These are exactly the cone differential components. Under $\theta_E$, $T^2(i)$ is inclusion into the first component. Also $T^2(\pi)$ is the raw second component $\beta$, so $\chi_A\circ T^2(-\pi)=-\operatorname{pr}_2\circ\theta_E$, exactly the connecting map of the cone of $T^2u$. Hence $T^2$, with $\chi$, sends each cone triangle to a cone triangle; localization and invariance under triangle isomorphisms give the assertion for all distinguished triangles. This makes the shift sign in the exactness assertion of [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-internal-hom-exact-restriction] explicit. Finally, for $x\in C[1]^p=C^{p+1}$ and $\psi\in T(C)^t$, the three signs in $\chi_C b_{C[1]}(x)(\psi)$ have exponent $1+p+p(t+1)+(t+1)\equiv(p+1)t\pmod2$. Therefore $\chi_C\circ b_{C[1]}=b_C[1]$. Localization gives $\chi_K\circ\delta_{K[1]}=\delta_K[1]$. Iterating this identity and its inverse gives compatibility with every integer shift.
