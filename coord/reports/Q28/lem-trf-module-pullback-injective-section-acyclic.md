Step 2 uses exactly the module-sheafification universal property and the explicit free-presheaf Yoneda bijection in the cited statements. The inverse sends r e_a to r times the restriction of s along a. Additivity is immediate from this formula, and naturality for a map b is the equality theta(b alpha)=b_U theta(alpha). This is the promised natural Hom/evaluation identification and hence its derived interpretation. No separate generator-identification node is needed.

The deletion-map identity follows by comparing each remaining projection. This is one routine universal-property argument, correctly marked elementary.
