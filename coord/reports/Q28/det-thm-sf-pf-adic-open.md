Here is an actual counterexample to dropping ell != char(k). Take k algebraically closed of characteristic ell, X=A^1_k, U=G_m,k, j the usual quasi-compact open immersion, E=Q_ell, and K the completed constant integral coefficient sheaf on U in degree zero. This K is bounded constructible and derived complete. At the origin the stalk of R^1 j_* F_ell is H^1(Spec F,F_ell), where F is the fraction field of the strict henselization of k[t]_(t). Artin-Schreier identifies it with F/(a^ell-a). The classes of t^(-m), for positive m prime to ell, are linearly independent over F_ell: a nonzero finite combination has a pole of maximal order prime to ell, whereas if v(a)<0 then a^ell-a has pole order divisible by ell, and if v(a)>=0 it has no pole. Hence this stalk is infinite-dimensional and not constructible. Reduction is the cone of ell and commutes with Rj_*, so the claimed integral constructibility would force this nonconstructible residue direct image to be bounded constructible, a contradiction. This also exhibits exactly the missing hypothesis required by the proof's finite-coefficient theorem.
