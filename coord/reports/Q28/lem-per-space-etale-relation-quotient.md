Step 1 uses `lem-weil-pbc-site-sheafification-sections-local` for local equality of original presheaf sections after sheafification. That statement really includes the equality clause, which the mere definition of sheafification does not. `def-trf-algebraic-space` supplies that R is an fppf sheaf. Monomorphy then makes the local relation sections agree on overlaps, so sheaf gluing gives the global section. The inverse is natural by uniqueness. This establishes the kernel pair without presupposing the fibre product over F is an algebraic space.

Step 2 uses `def-trf-algebraic-space` to choose a representable surjective etale scheme atlas of A. `lem-trf-space-fibre-products-etale-covers` supplies algebraic-space fibre products over the already algebraic space A. `lem-trf-space-representable-etale-scheme-tests` supplies base change of the representable atlas, and `lem-trf-space-chartwise-etale-permanence` supplies arbitrary base change and composition for chartwise maps. Thus both projections of D are chartwise etale without asserting that s or t is representable. The triple formula shows that its image is exactly the inverse image of the given equivalence relation; it is monic by monomorphy of R. It is therefore a genuine equivalence relation on every test scheme, including nonreduced ones.

Step 3 checks the crucial schemehood hypotheses. Every etale chart W of D has etale composite to V. `def-weil-etale-morphism` and `def-sbc-locally-finite-presentation` give a finite presentation, whose finite list of algebra generators satisfies `def-weil-finite-type-morphism`. Clause (a) of `lemma-nagata-finite-type-to-separated-target` requires only locally finite type of the composite, not separatedness of the second arrow. Apply it to W -> V x_S V -> V. `lem-sfb-space-lft-monomorphism-separated-lqf` accepts exactly this chartwise locally finite type monomorphism to a scheme and gives both the required separated diagonal and local quasi-finiteness. `lem-a1-separated-locally-qf-space-representable` then applies with scheme target V x_S V and no quasi-compactness hypothesis. Finally the scheme-test clause of `lem-trf-space-representable-etale-scheme-tests` identifies the projections' etaleness with scheme etaleness. This avoids applying the scheme quotient theorem to an unschematic relation.

Step 4 uses `lem-trf-etale-relation-quotient` only for D and V, now schemes, with both projections etale and the full pointwise equivalence relation proved. That node states algebraicity, a representable surjective etale presentation, the kernel identity, the local-lifting clause, and the universal property used to construct c.
