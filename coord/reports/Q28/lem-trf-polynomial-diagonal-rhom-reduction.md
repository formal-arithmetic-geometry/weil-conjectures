### Original step 2: lem-trf-polynomial-diagonal-dual-base-change

The rings $P$ and $S$ are Noetherian by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-algebra-noetherian], applied respectively to $R$ and $A_1$ and their polynomial algebras. The presentation makes $R\to A$ finite type. By [lem-trf-flat-polynomial-quotient-perfect], $A_2$ has a bounded finite-projective $P$-resolution $L$. Each term is finite projective over $S$: a finite-free retraction presenting a finite-projective term remains a retraction after scalar extension. Hence $B$ is perfect over $S$. The same exactness calculation, using $N\otimes_PS=N\otimes_RA_1$, proves $P\to S$ flat.
