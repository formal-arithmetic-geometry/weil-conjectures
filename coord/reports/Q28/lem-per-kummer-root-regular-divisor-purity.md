## New node: mathematical argument and small cases

For A=F_ell, let P=A[Q], Q=C_ell, and choose the equivariant identification P with Map(Q,A) sending group basis vectors to delta functions. The complex [P --(sigma-1)--> P] in degrees 0,1 has kernel the constant line and cokernel the augmentation line. Its derived truncation obstruction is the two-extension

0 -> A -> P -> P -> A -> 0,

where the first map sends 1 to the norm sum, the middle map is sigma-1, and the last is augmentation. This is the initial two-extension of the periodic cyclic resolution. Inflate along C_(ell^2) -> C_ell. In the periodic resolutions the chain comparison has multiplication by ell in degree two: the norm on the larger group maps to ell times the smaller norm. Thus its degree-two extension class is multiplied by ell, hence is zero over A. This is also exactly the s=2, m=r=ell case of det-base-field-cd-inflation (read its entire statement). Vanishing of this truncation obstruction splits the triangle A -> K -> A[-1] -> A[1], giving K isomorphic to A plus A[-1] in the derived category. A concrete check uses A[w]/w^(ell^2) -> A[z]/z^ell, w maps to z: the larger norm w^(ell^2-1) maps to zero, so the degree-two comparison can be zero while the degree-zero and degree-one comparisons are the quotient map. This proves the same vanishing directly.

This new node is genuinely a derived splitting, as required by the derived-object rule, not a list of cohomology groups. It is false without the lift to C_(ell^2): over C_ell the periodic extension class can be nonzero; that is why the second Kummer root level is essential. At ell=2 the comparison is still zero since the larger norm maps to z^3=0 in A[z]/z^2. At ell=3 it maps to z^8=0 in A[z]/z^3. The two constant cohomology lines and their degrees 0 and 1 agree with A[0] plus A[-1], not A[1]. No canonical splitting or chain-level splitting is asserted. All objects, action, topology, differential and shifts are stated inline with their definition dependencies. The node carries no proof or proof dependencies, and is not marked Mathlib.
