Hypotheses and clauses, read against the complete node:

- Fields are `Field`, or the inherited field of `NontriviallyNormedField`.
  `NontriviallyNormedField` is exactly a nontrivial real multiplicative absolute
  value with its metric; `IsUltrametricDist` states the strong triangle inequality.
  `CompleteSpace` is completeness for that metric. `NormedAlgebra` records the given
  field inclusion and compatibility of the norms (`norm_algebraMap'`). Each field
  has its own explicit universe. `FiniteDimensional` is the finite-extension
  hypothesis. Compositum fields and fields in towers are complete as stipulated
  in the node; the separate completeness theorem supplies this condition for finite
  extensions, so none is an extra mathematical assumption.
- `integers K` is literally `NormedField.valuation.valuationSubring`, hence the
  elements of norm at most one. `residue K` is Mathlib's quotient by its maximal
  ideal. `Valuation.mem_maximalIdeal_iff` identifies that ideal with norm less
  than one. `values K` is the image of K-units under the norm valuation into
  units of the nonnegative reals: exactly the positive real value group.
- `ramificationIndex K L` is the subgroup relative index. Compatibility places
  the smaller group inside the larger, so this is the stated coset index.
  `residueDegree K L` is `Module.finrank` for the canonical residue scalar action.
  `defect K L` is the rational quotient `[L:K]/(ef)`, without rounding or an
  assumed integrality theorem. `integerMap`, `residueMap` and `residueAlgebra`
  use the given embedding, never an arbitrary residue embedding.
- Immediacy is equality of these actual real subgroups and surjectivity of the
  canonical residue embedding (`IsImmediate`). `IsUnramified` is e=d=1 and
  separable residue extension. `IsTame` is d=1, separable residue extension, and
  either residue characteristic zero or `Nat.Coprime e p`. Thus it imposes no
  index restriction in characteristic zero. `HasSeparableResidue` uses
  `Algebra.IsSeparable`, whose `IsSeparable` definition is separability of the
  minimal polynomial, equivalently distinct roots. No new separability notion
  replaces Mathlib's definition. The node only applies it to finite extensions.
- `exists_unique_extending_absoluteValue` gives an extending nonarchimedean
  `AbsoluteValue L ℝ` and uniqueness among all extending absolute values.
  `finite_extension_complete` proves `CompleteSpace L` from a compatible norm,
  without assuming this conclusion.
- `finite_ramification_invariants` asserts actual finiteness of the value quotient
  and `Module.Finite` of the residue extension, positivity of both natural-valued
  invariants, defect one in residue characteristic zero and `∃ n : ℕ, d=p^n`
  in positive residue characteristic. Thus the zero junk values of index and
  finrank do not weaken any finite-extension clause.
- `finite_ramification_tower` states multiplicativity of e,f,d and the equivalence
  between tameness of the composite and tameness of both tower steps. This
  includes composition, lower intermediate fields, and the upper extensions.
  `IsScalarTower` says these are the given compatible field inclusions.
- `tame_compositum` encodes a field compositum by `hcomp`: the intermediate field
  generated by the two actual field images is top. It asserts finiteness and
  tameness over the new finite base. It does not quantify over arbitrary
  unrelated maps. Separate universes for K,L,M,D impose no same-size restriction.
- `exists_unramified_residue_lift` gives `U : IntermediateField K L`, so both
  required inclusions are built in; it is numerically unramified over K and its
  actual inclusion into L induces a surjective residue-field map. Injectivity
  of a field homomorphism makes this precisely equality of residue fields.
- `immediate_tame_compositum` uses the same precise field-compositum condition,
  assumes the lower extension immediate and the new base tame, and concludes
  immediacy and the exact degree equality `[D:M]=[L:K]`.
- `finite_extension_relative_invariants` takes an arbitrary complete extension K/k
  followed by finite L/K. It states equality of the two residue transcendence
  degrees with `Cardinal.lift` to compare independent universes; equality of the
  two rational ranks is already in a common universe because value groups lie
  in the positive reals. Both are full cardinal invariants, with no finite
  transcendence degree or rank hypothesis. The final implication uses degree
  zero and `IsAlgClosed (residue k)` and gives surjectivity of all three canonical
  residue maps, exactly `residue L = residue K = residue k` as embedded fields.
- `finite_index_divisible_values` treats any positive-real subgroups G≤H. The
  divisibility hypothesis gives roots for every positive integer and the finite
  quotient hypothesis gives finite index. Its conclusion is actual G=H. It
  therefore applies in particular to the divisible ground value group Γ_k.
