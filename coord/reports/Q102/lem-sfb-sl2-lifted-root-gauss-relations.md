## New-node proof plans (not proofs stored in the nodes)

For lem-sfb-fppf-local-unit-square-root, read Stacks Tag 040N, etale-cohomology.tex, lemma-kummer-sequence-syntomic. On an affine open Spec A of T with unit s, take Spec B where B=A[r]/(r^2-s). The monic quotient basis is (1,r), so B is faithfully flat over A, and its one-generator one-relation presentation is finite. The induced scheme map is flat, surjective and locally of finite presentation, hence an fppf cover. The root r is a unit, with inverse r/s. These affine covers together cover T. This is the n=2 instance of the source proof and includes characteristic two and nonreduced A. This node asserts the scheme-level local existence, whereas the existing monic-quotient tensor-coordinate node supplies only its algebraic input; passing between those statements requires affine scheme flatness/surjectivity and finite-presentation results, not a direct specialization of its statement.

For lem-sfb-fppf-local-two-unit-decomposition, read Conrad's paragraph after (4.3.1), p.129. On Spec A take the open W=Spec(A[z,1/(z(u-z)(1+zv))]) in the affine line. Its projection is flat and finitely presented: polynomial extension is flat, localization is flat, and the displayed algebra has finitely many generators and relations. Each residue-field fibre is nonempty because z(u-z)(1+zv) is a nonzero polynomial over that field: if v=0 the last factor is 1, otherwise it is a nonzero linear factor; z and u-z are nonzero polynomials regardless of u. The localization of the polynomial ring over the field is therefore nonzero and has a prime. Thus W maps surjectively to Spec A and is an fppf cover, on which b=z and a=u-z have the claimed properties. Apply on all affine opens. This also shows that the auxiliary condition 1+uv invertible in the surrounding application is unnecessary for the local decomposition itself. This is a geometric covering/existence result, not a ring identity. There is no proof sketch in either new node's statement or proof fields.
