## Proofs behind the new statement-only inputs

These explanations establish why the proposed inputs are sound and how their sources apply; their node proof fields remain empty as required for a one-level split.

For `lem-sfb-flat-fp-presentation-noetherian-model`, finite presentation of A as an R-algebra implies that the kernel of the specified finite polynomial surjection is generated by finitely many polynomials f1,...,fm. Choose the finitely generated integer subring R1 of R containing all their coefficients. For every finitely generated integer subring Ri containing R1 set Ai=Ri[x1,...,xn]/(f1,...,fm). This is a small directed system (subrings form a set), R is its ring colimit and A its indicated scalar extension. All transition squares are tensor base-change squares, all Ri -> Ai are finitely presented, and Mi=Ai is a finitely presented Ai-module. Apply 02JO(3) to these data and M=A, which is R-flat by hypothesis, to obtain a later index 0 for which A0 is R0-flat. The quotient presentation is unchanged and hence its base extension is exactly the given pi, not merely an unspecified presentation. Finite type over the Noetherian integers makes R0, P0 and A0 Noetherian by 00FN. The construction also works when m or n is zero and when A or R is zero. The existing arbitrary integer-model theorem alone omits preservation of this specified presentation; the existing eventual-flatness theorem starts with the system which this construction must provide.

For `lem-sfb-noetherian-full-support-localizing-generation`, put L=Loc_B(E). It is a tensor ideal: the class of V with E tensor^L V in L is localizing, contains B, and hence is all D(B) by the existing free-generation theorem (09PB). Exactness and coproduct preservation of derived tensor follow from its derived adjunction. For each prime p, the bounded coherent complex E_p is nonzero. If b is its highest nonzero cohomological degree, H^b(E_p) is finite and nonzero; Nakayama gives H^b(E_p) tensor k(p) nonzero, and right t-exactness of derived tensor gives the same nonzero top cohomology in E tensor^L k(p). Over a field every complex splits into its cohomology and contractible pairs (choose complements to boundaries in cycles and to cycles degreewise). Thus a nonzero cohomology vector, together with a linear functional taking it to 1, exhibits a shift of k(p) as a retract of E tensor^L k(p). It follows that k(p) belongs to L.

Now prove B/p belongs to L by Noetherian induction on the closed subset V(p). Assume B/q belongs to L for every strictly larger prime q. In the domain D=B/p let K=Frac(D)=k(p) and Q=K/D. Every finite B-submodule N of Q is killed by p and by a nonzero element of D (take a product of the finitely many denominators). Its finite prime filtration, 00L0, has factors B/q supported on V(p) intersect V(s), using 00L4. Hence each q strictly contains p, and the induction gives N in L. The poset of finite submodules is a small filtered set, with colimit Q. To justify passing to Q using only localizing operations, use its augmented simplicial replacement: each degree is a coproduct of the N indexed by finite chains in this poset. Its augmented complex is exact because every finite collection of indices has an upper bound; the usual extra-vertex contraction at such a bound kills each finite cycle. Its direct-sum total complex is the sequential union of its finite brutal truncations, so the telescope triangle (1-shift on the sum of the truncations) puts its total in L and identifies it with Q[0]. Thus Q lies in L. The triangle D -> K -> Q -> D[1] now gives D in L since K=k(p) is already there. The induction is legitimate because a Noetherian spectrum has no infinite strictly descending closed subsets. Finally apply the prime filtration 00L0 to the finite module B itself: its factors B/p all belong to L, so B belongs to L. For B=0 this conclusion is immediate. This is an agent corollary of the quoted ingredients, not an assertion that any single Stacks tag states the whole theorem.
