## Source error

The statement of the item is not disputed and is unchanged. The normality inference in the first paragraph of the online proof of EGA IV 21.14.1 is invalid for a general inseparable residue extension: it asserts that a finite free local coefficient extension C with C/mC = k' is normal, from IV 7.5.1, 7.5.3 and 6.5.4(ii). The hypothesis for 7.5.1 is geometric normality of the closed fibre, not ordinary normality of the field k'. I read 7.5.0 (definition of P), 7.5.1, 7.5.3(vi), and 6.5.4(ii); the last requires normality of all fibres. A finite inseparable field extension does not satisfy the required geometric condition. This is a defect of the asserted inference, not a claim that no specially chosen normal coefficient lift exists.

An explicit test: let k=F_2(a), with a transcendental, and
A=k[[x,y,z]]/(y^2+a*x^2+z^3).
This is a complete normal local domain. It is an irreducible hypersurface, hence Cohen--Macaulay. Away from z its defining equation has nonzero derivative in z. The only height-one prime containing z is (z), since y^2+a*x^2 remains irreducible over k; its localization has principal maximal ideal generated by z and is a DVR. Thus the hypersurface satisfies R_1 and S_2. But for k'=k(b), b^2=a, the finite free local algebra C=A tensor_k k' has field closed fibre k' and is
k'[[x,u,z]]/(u^2+z^3), u=y+b*x.
It is not normal: u/z is integral, with (u/z)^2=z, and is not in C. For example, the normalization parametrization z=t^2, u=t^3 shows that t has no power-series expression in x,t^2,t^3. This also demonstrates why replacing finite by finite separable without notice would lose cases of the item.

There is a second, harmless wording error in that paragraph: for inseparable k'/k, k' tensor_k k' need not have a local factor ISOMORPHIC to k'. It has a quotient/residue field k' by multiplication, which suffices for selecting a local factor. For k'=k(b), b^2=a in characteristic two the tensor square is k'[epsilon]/epsilon^2. Our completed tensor construction uses the kernel of multiplication to the residue field explicitly.

Repair: set C=completed B, prove C normal through the existing theorem that a formally smooth complete local map is regular, and use the completed LOCAL tensor square of C over completed A at the multiplication residue point. It is symmetric in the two factors, and has a multiplication retraction. The second factor is the coefficient ring for the existing split power-series criterion; the first gives the desired faithfully flat map from B. The closed-fibre map gives the required contraction. This preserves the inherited completion/tensor-local-factor/power-series route, the chapter and unit, and every hypothesis and conclusion of the item. No target, definition or unit statement changed.
