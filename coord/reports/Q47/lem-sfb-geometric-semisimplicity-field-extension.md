## Proof planned for the statement-only prerequisite

For lem-sfb-endomorphism-minpoly-scalar-extension: choose a finite K-basis v_1,...,v_n of V. The maps sending lambda tensor v to (lambda times the coordinates of v), and (lambda_i) to sum lambda_i tensor v_i, are inverse L-linear maps between L tensor_K V and L^n, by the tensor universal property and the coordinate identities. Thus scalar extension preserves finite dimension and the matrix A of T maps coefficientwise to the matrix of T_L. Let d = deg m_T. If n = 0 the conclusion is 1 maps to 1. Otherwise I,A,...,A^(d-1) are linearly independent over K: a nontrivial relation, normalized by its highest coefficient, contradicts minimality of m_T. Regard their entries as the columns of an n^2 by d matrix. Gaussian elimination over K gives d pivot rows, so some d by d minor is nonzero. A field homomorphism is injective, and sends this determinant to the corresponding nonzero determinant over L. Therefore the same powers of the extended matrix are L-linearly independent. The coefficient image of m_T annihilates the extended matrix and is monic of degree d, so the extended minimum polynomial has degree at most d and, by this independence, at least d. Subtracting the two monic degree-d annihilators gives a relation of smaller degree, hence zero. This proves equality. No algebraicity or perfectness of either field is used. This Gaussian-elimination comparison is a genuine independent lemma; it is not hidden inside an elementary step of the item.
