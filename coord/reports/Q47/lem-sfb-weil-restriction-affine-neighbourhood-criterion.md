For the new affine input, over V=Spec A the scheme S prime restricted to V is affine finite locally free, say Spec B. On an affine cover of V where B has basis e_1,...,e_n, present the B-algebra C of U by arbitrary variables and relations C=B[x_i]/I. Use A-coefficient variables x_{i,j}, substitute x_i=sum_j x_{i,j}e_j into each relation, and quotient A[x_{i,j}] by all basis coefficients of these evaluations. The universal B-algebra map C to B tensor this quotient represents exactly B-algebra maps C to B tensor D, not arbitrary A-algebra maps. This explicitly enforces the S prime condition. Variables and relations need not be finite. The local schemes agree on overlaps by their universal B-map property and glue; affineness is local on the target V. The affine-test bijections extend to all tests by morphism gluing. This is why the new input is a real algebraic construction and not an elementary new node or a relabelled copy of the global criterion.
