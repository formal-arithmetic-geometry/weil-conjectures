On an overlap a sheet can change its index only on a clopen piece, and permutation invariance of addition makes the local maps agree; the sheaf axiom glues them. For step 5 I opened `lem-weil-pbc-lattice-sheaf-locally-constant`. It gives exactly the classification of embedded lattice pairs in a free rational module by the constant coset sheaf GL_r(E)/GL_r(O_E), including rank zero. On a free rational chart each M_j determines such a class. The sheafification definition says a section is locally represented by an actual coset, and there are only finitely many sheets; successive refinement makes all these classes fixed simultaneously. This is a covering refinement, not a claim that a section of a constant sheaf is globally constant on a disconnected base. A matrix representing a coset identifies the embedded integral module itself, not merely its rationalization.
