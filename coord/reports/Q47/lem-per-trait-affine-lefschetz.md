I reviewed the source and the current statements, with delivery-errors B--E in view. Both the item and the new node have exact blueprint.from.place, the complete inherited via chain, and same_as = []. No unit or chapter was edited.

Step 1 uses the strict-localization definition's inverse-limit construction, the affine-pairs criterion's global finite-type ring map, complete-local excellence, finite-type/localization excellence, the geometric algebraic strict-henselization presentation, and strict-henselization Noetherianness. I read each statement and checked that the complete DVR is complete and Noetherian, that the affine chart is of finite type over it, and that the algebraic presentation really permits application of the Noetherian theorem. The cofinality argument for replacing B by the chosen affine neighbourhood is written out.

Step 2 uses the affine-chart ind-etale theorem and the explicit sentence 'An ind-etale map is weakly etale' in ind-etale stability. Weakly-etale excellence ascent assumes a Noetherian target, which Step 1 supplies; that condition is not discarded. Finite-type excellence and the excellent affine-cover theorem then give quasi-excellence on every affine open of each P_j. I checked the affine qcqs theorem and affine-morphism permanence separately: qcqs of the schemes alone is not used as a proof that the projection is a finitely presented morphism. Its polynomial algebra presentation gives local finite presentation and its affineness gives the other two properties. These generic geometry inputs serve the unit's route; no trace-formula proof is being substituted by using the generic affine-cover lemma filed in that chapter.

Step 3 uses strict-henselization functoriality clause 1, which actually says the ring is strictly henselian local, not merely that a limit exists. Exactness/positive-degree vanishing comes from the strict-local acyclicity theorem; degree zero at a negative support bound comes from the distinct sections-to-closed-stalk theorem. The closed point has delta zero. The induction quantifies over every integer c from the outset, which makes c-r legitimate later.

Step 4 cites the quasi-excellent finite-coefficient direct-image finiteness statement for constructibility and the new one-coordinate node for the support inequality.
