Small cases: for A=B any nonzero essentially finite-type local k-algebra with the identity, both relative degrees and the dimension difference vanish. For A=k and B=k[x]_(x), the difference is 1=1-0; for A=k and B=k(x), it is 0=1-1, testing the residue correction and preventing the false formula dim B=trdeg_k Frac B. The faithfully flat map k -> (k[x,y]/(xy))_(x,y) has two minimal primes over the single downstairs prime, so a claimed bijection would fail; both components give 1=1-0 as required. Nilpotents cause no failure, e.g. the identity of k[e]/(e^2), whose unique minimal-prime quotient is k. Nonzero hypotheses exclude the empty-spectrum zero-ring pathology.
