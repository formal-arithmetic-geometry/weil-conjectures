### Returned Step 1

Put $Y=X\times_SS^b$, with projections $p:Y\to X$ and $q:Y\to S^b$, and $H_i=p^*\mathcal F_i$. The universal hypothesis applied to $T=S^b$ makes $0\to H_1\to H_2\to H_3\to0$ exact. These modules are quasi-coherent by [lem-pc-ringed-space-tensor-pullback-qcoh]. By [thm-nagata-blowup-principalizes-center], the exceptional ideal is locally principal on $S^b$. Choose affine opens $V=\operatorname{Spec}A$ covering $S^b$ on which it is generated by $a\in A$. By [lem-weil-pbc-affine-opens-basis], cover $q^{-1}V$ by affine opens $Z=\operatorname{Spec}C$. On $Z$ the exceptional closed set is $V(a)$, where $a$ now acts through $A\to C$. Put $N_i=\Gamma(Z,H_i)$. The counits of [lem-weil-pbc-affine-tilde-gamma-adjunction](3) identify $H_i|_Z$ with $\widetilde N_i$, naturally in the two sequence maps. It suffices to prove the assertion on these opens $Z$.

Uses: `lem-pc-ringed-space-tensor-pullback-qcoh`, `thm-nagata-blowup-principalizes-center`, `lem-weil-pbc-affine-opens-basis`, `lem-weil-pbc-affine-tilde-gamma-adjunction`.

### Returned Step 2

Write $i:N_1\to N_2$ and $j:N_2\to N_3$ for the induced $C$-linear maps. They form a short exact sequence. Indeed, the sheaf injection gives injectivity on sections; the sectionwise kernel formula of [lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-module-kernel] gives $\ker j=N_1$ inside $N_2$. The epimorphism $H_2|_Z\to H_3|_Z$ identifies $H_3|_Z$ with the cokernel of the inclusion of $H_1|_Z$. By [lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-module-cokernel-image](3), its map on the affine open $Z$ is surjective and has kernel $i(N_1)$. Thus $i$ is injective, $j$ is surjective, and $\ker j=\operatorname{im}i$.

Uses: `lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-module-kernel`, `lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-module-cokernel-image`.

### Returned Step 3

For every $n\geq0$, let $T_n=\operatorname{Spec}(A/(a^n))\to V\to S^b\to S$. The universal hypothesis makes the pullback of the original sequence to $X\times_ST_n$ short exact. The fibre-product universal property [def-pc-fibre-product] identifies this scheme with $Y\times_{S^b}T_n$. Since $Z\hookrightarrow Y$ is open, [lem-weil-pbc-open-immersion-base-change] makes $Z_n=Z\times_VT_n$ an open subscheme of it. Restrict the sequence to $Z_n$. By [lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra], $Z_n=\operatorname{Spec}(C\otimes_A A/(a^n))=\operatorname{Spec}(C/a^nC)$: the ring isomorphism sends $c\otimes\bar r$ to $\overline{cr}$ and its inverse sends $\bar c$ to $c\otimes1$. The composition comparisons of [def-pc-module-pullback] identify its terms with the pullbacks of $H_i|_Z$. By [lem-weil-pbc-tilde-pullback-base-change] these are $\widetilde{N_i/a^nN_i}$, naturally in $i,j$. Here $(C/a^nC)\otimes_CN_i\cong N_i/a^nN_i$ sends $\bar c\otimes m$ to $\overline{cm}$ with inverse $\bar m\mapsto1\otimes m$; both maps are well defined and inverse by the tensor relations. Taking sections of the injection on this affine scheme and using [lem-weil-pbc-affine-tilde-gamma-adjunction](2) shows that $N_1/a^nN_1\to N_2/a^nN_2$ is injective. Equivalently, $i(y)\in a^nN_2$ implies $y\in a^nN_1$.

Uses: `def-pc-fibre-product`, `lem-weil-pbc-open-immersion-base-change`, `lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra`, `def-pc-module-pullback`, `lem-weil-pbc-tilde-pullback-base-change`, `lem-weil-pbc-affine-tilde-gamma-adjunction`.

### Returned Step 4

Put $T_i=\Gamma_{(a)}(N_i)$, the power-torsion submodule [def-nagata-power-torsion, lemma-nagata-power-torsion-submodule]. On $Z$, the supported-section subsheaf $K_i$ in [def-trf-module-strict-transform] is $\widetilde T_i$. Take a principal open $D(s)\subset Z$. By [lem-weil-pbc-spec-module-tilde-sections], its sections of $H_i$ are $(N_i)_s$, and restriction to $D(s)\cap D(a)$ is localization at $a$. Its kernel is $\Gamma_{(a)}((N_i)_s)$ by [lemma-nagata-power-torsion-kernel-localization]. This is exactly the image of $(T_i)_s$ in $(N_i)_s$: if $a^n(m/s^r)=0$, the fraction equality in [def-weil-pbc-module-localization-away] gives $s^ka^nm=0$ for some $k$, so $s^km\in T_i$ and $m/s^r=(s^km)/s^{r+k}$ comes from $(T_i)_s$. Conversely a fraction with numerator in $T_i$ is killed by a power of $a$. The same fraction equality shows $(T_i)_s\to(N_i)_s$ is injective, since $T_i\subseteq N_i$. Thus $K_i$ and $\widetilde T_i$ have the same sections as submodules on the principal-open basis, and $K_i=\widetilde T_i$. The colimit-preservation clause of [lem-weil-pbc-affine-tilde-gamma-adjunction](4) identifies $H_i|_Z/K_i$ with $\widetilde{N_i/T_i}$, the strict transform on $Z$. A linear map carries elements killed by $a^n$ to elements killed by $a^n$, so the strict-transform maps are precisely the sheafifications of the induced maps on torsion quotients.

Uses: `def-nagata-power-torsion`, `lemma-nagata-power-torsion-submodule`, `def-trf-module-strict-transform`, `lem-weil-pbc-spec-module-tilde-sections`, `lemma-nagata-power-torsion-kernel-localization`, `def-weil-pbc-module-localization-away`, `lem-weil-pbc-affine-tilde-gamma-adjunction`.

### Returned Step 5

The induced map $\bar i:N_1/T_1\to N_2/T_2$ is injective: if $i(y)\in T_2$, choose $n$ with $a^ni(y)=0$; injectivity of $i$ gives $a^ny=0$, hence $y\in T_1$ by [def-nagata-power-torsion]. The induced map $\bar j:N_2/T_2\to N_3/T_3$ is surjective: choose a representative in $N_3$ and lift it using surjectivity of $j$. Their composite is zero because $ji=0$.

Uses: `def-nagata-power-torsion`.

### Returned Step 6

Suppose $\bar j([x])=0$. By [def-nagata-power-torsion], choose $n$ with $a^nj(x)=0$. Exactness in Step 2 supplies $y\in N_1$ with $i(y)=a^nx$. Its image modulo $a^nN_2$ is zero, so Step 3 gives $y=a^nz$ for some $z\in N_1$. Therefore $a^n(x-i(z))=a^nx-i(a^nz)=i(y)-i(y)=0$. Thus $x-i(z)\in T_2$ and $[x]=\bar i([z])$. This proves $\ker\bar j\subseteq\operatorname{im}\bar i$; the reverse inclusion follows from $\bar j\bar i=0$. Together with Step 5 the torsion-quotient sequence is short exact. This uses no regularity of $a$ on any $N_i$ or on $C$.

Uses: `def-nagata-power-torsion`.

### Returned Step 7

Apply associated sheafification on $\operatorname{Spec}C$ to the sequence just proved. It preserves kernels and monomorphisms by [lem-sc-tilde-preserves-finite-limits], and preserves cokernels by [lem-weil-pbc-affine-tilde-gamma-adjunction](4). More explicitly, $N_1/T_1$ is the kernel of $\bar j$, while $N_3/T_3$ is the cokernel of $\bar i$; these two identifications remain kernel and cokernel identifications after sheafification. Step 4 identifies the resulting sequence with the required strict-transform sequence restricted to $Z$. The $Z$ cover $Y$, and the quotient maps used here are the original induced sheaf maps. Their composite vanishes on every member of the cover, hence globally. A section in the kernel has a local lift from the first term on each member, and a section of the last term has a local lift from the middle term there; injectivity is also checked by restriction to the cover. These are exactly sheaf exactness and the endpoint monomorphism and epimorphism. Consequently the strict-transform sequence on $Y$ is short exact.

Uses: `lem-sc-tilde-preserves-finite-limits`, `lem-weil-pbc-affine-tilde-gamma-adjunction`.
