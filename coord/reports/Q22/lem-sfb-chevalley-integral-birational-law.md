## Source error

The paragraph preceding Conrad Lemma 6.3.9, printed p.222 (PDF page index 221), identifies a product of the simple reflections, each used once, with the longest Weyl element. In type A2, `s1 s2` has length 2 and order 3, whereas the longest element is `s1 s2 s1`, with length 3 and order 2. Moreover `s1 s2(alpha1)=alpha2` is positive, so that product does not interchange all positive and negative roots. The new partial-conjugation lemma uses an actual longest element and its reduced expression. The word induction is retained as the non-elementary supporting input; no false Coxeter-element assertion is imported. The inverse partial map is conjugation by `n^{-1}`: the source's inverse-composition clause cannot in general follow from two copies of conjugation by `n`.

Conrad Proposition 6.3.13's brief tangent discussion also does not explain why the derivative of translation at the identity is invertible. Step 7 supplies the explanation using inverse translations on one-sign subgroup squares and the factorization `x=a t b`; it does not infer it merely from the identity axiom. These are corrections to the supporting proof, with no changed target or unit statement.
