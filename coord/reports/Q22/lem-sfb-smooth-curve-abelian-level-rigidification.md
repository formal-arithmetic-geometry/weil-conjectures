For the local-system node I read more-etale.tex, Tag 0GLI, proposition-loc-cst-torsion, including its reduction to a Noetherian base; the base itself need not be Noetherian. The coefficient ring F_ell is Noetherian, its finite-dimensional modules satisfy the finite-type hypothesis, and ell is a unit in every residue field of the base. This node does not silently include arbitrary base change; Step 1 cites the existing prime proper-base-change theorem separately. For the general DM criterion I read algebraic.tex, Tag 04SZ, proposition-algebraic-stack-no-automorphisms, the DM/unramified-diagonal convention in stacks-morphisms.tex, and spaces-morphisms.tex, Tag 05W1, lemma-diagonal-unramified-morphism. The geometric-stabilizer corollary works because inertia over a DM stack is unramified: its identity section is an open immersion and is surjective on geometric points when the geometric stabilizers are trivial. It is therefore an isomorphism; 04SZ then gives an algebraic space. The DM assumption prevents the false assertion that trivial geometric groups alone kill infinitesimal inertia in an arbitrary Artin stack.

Step 1 cites four statements which I opened in full. The general local-system node gives exactly finite-dimensional local models; projective-proper gives properness of the family; lemma-weil-pbc-prime-proper-base-change states the specified derived exchange isomorphism for an arbitrary base-change morphism, rather than only properness of the new morphism; thm-sc-proper-cohomology supplies the geometric H1 group of rank 2g. Taking degree one gives the canonical base-change map, and scalar multiplication by an element of the prime field is repeated addition, so the group computation gives the vector-space rank. The geometric fibres are smooth proper nonempty connected curves and ell is prime to their characteristics. Neither algebraic closedness nor Noetherianity of T is introduced.

Step 2 cites lem-galois-flc-representable for existence, lem-galois-stalk-representable for geometric fibre identification, and lem-galois-inverse-image-representable for arbitrary scheme pullback. I read all three statements: the last applies to P because P is etale over T, not to an arbitrary non-etale representing scheme. In a local trivialization a frame is exactly an invertible finite matrix. This gives finite local constancy and nonempty stalks without a new lemma. Nonempty fibres imply surjectivity. To avoid proving only representability on the small etale site, the step explicitly pulls the represented sheaf to an arbitrary T-prime and evaluates at its terminal object; the proper-base-change isomorphism identifies the resulting local system with the one actually defined from X over T-prime.
