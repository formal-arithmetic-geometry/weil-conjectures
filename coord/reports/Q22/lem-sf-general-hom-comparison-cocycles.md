Step 1 uses `lem-sf-ringed-derived-closed-symmetric-monoidal` for the natural Hom-set bijection, evaluation and symmetry. Applying it to identity topoi with coefficient ring maps is legitimate on the small proetale sites in the datum. `lem-fix-internal-hom-comparison-composition` explicitly states equality of the actual transposed maps for composite tensorators, and does not assume those maps are invertible. I checked the opposite evaluation order in that node: the symmetric monoidal structure and symmetric coefficient functors convert it to the item's H tensor K order. No missing braiding hypothesis is being silently used.

Step 2 uses finite-Hom's unique-transpose characterization, the monoidal identity normalization and the closed counit formula. It proves the unital equation by the Hom-set bijection. It never assumes evaluation is a monomorphism. The cases n=1, n=m and zero K or M are covered.

Step 3 uses integral reduction's definition of c_n and c_m, finite-Hom's definition of v_nm, and the abstract composition lemma. The two functors are exactly reduction from R_X to (A_n)_X and then from A_n to A_m. The ringed monoidal theorem identifies their composite with reduction to (A_m)_X. Both K_n and M_n have the finite-Tor and constructibility hypotheses of finite-Hom by the item's explicit assumption. The composition theorem applies on ambient categories, so no unproved preservation assertion for all ambient objects is inserted. This also checks the source and target of c_n tensor A_m: they are H_R(K,M) tensor A_m and H_An(K_n,M_n) tensor A_m before the last comparison.

Step 4 reads `lem-fix-site-perfect-scalar-hom` for the *specified evaluation-transpose* definition of the elementary finite map, not merely its invertibility. I retained site-perfectness only on the finite elementary overlap and retained both finite-Tor inputs needed by v_nm; I did not replace finite Tor by site-perfectness in the general theorem.
