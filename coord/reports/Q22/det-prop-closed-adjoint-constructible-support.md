An explicit check showing why the omitted inference is invalid is X = Spec k[t_1,t_2,...], Z = V(t_1,t_2,...), and U = union D(t_n). This is an allowed closed immersion under the original words. The source's assertion that j is quasi-compact because X is Noetherian cannot be deduced for this X. Thus it cannot be left as an unstated hypothesis.
