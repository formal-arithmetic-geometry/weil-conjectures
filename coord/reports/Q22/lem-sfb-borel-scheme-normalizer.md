## Not duplicates

lem-sfb-borel-maximal-torus-conjugacy vs lem-sfb-torus-normalizer-borel-transitivity: the existing node conjugates Borels through a fixed ambient maximal torus; the new node conjugates maximal tori in an arbitrary connected smooth group and places such a torus in a prescribed Borel. Neither assertion is the other's specialization.

lem-sfb-borel-maximal-torus-conjugacy vs lem-sfb-general-borel-flag-variety: that node conjugates Borels and puts a torus in some Borel; it does not state conjugacy of maximal tori or existence of an ambient maximal torus in each prescribed Borel.

lem-sfb-borel-maximal-torus-conjugacy vs lem-sfb-triangular-quotient-section-conjugacy: the latter only conjugates two given sections of a specified triangular-group quotient. It does not give general maximal-torus conjugacy or the required ambient maximality/existence.

lem-sfb-torus-centralizer-borel-intersection vs lem-sfb-maximal-torus-centralizer-borel: the existing node contains C_G(T) in B for an ambient maximal torus; the new one treats arbitrary smaller S and proves maximal solvability of C_G(S) intersect B inside C_G(S).

lem-sfb-torus-centralizer-borel-intersection vs lem-sfb-chevalley-borel-intersection: the latter computes the reduced identity component of the intersection of all Borels through a maximal torus; this node concerns the unreduced scheme intersection of one Borel with the centralizer of an arbitrary torus.

lem-sfb-cartan-containing-normalizer-smooth vs lem-sfb-torus-normalizer-components: the existing node concerns normalizers of torus subgroups. The new lemma concerns any smooth H containing C_G(T), including non-torus and disconnected H. Its conclusion is smoothness and equality of identity components, not equality N=H.

lem-sfb-cartan-containing-normalizer-smooth vs lem-sfb-borel-scheme-normalizer: the new lemma does not prove self-normalization, even on F-points. For example in a reductive group of positive rank, H=N_G(T) contains C_G(T) but is disconnected. The item must still eliminate the components of N_G(B) by its own dimension induction and representation argument.

lem-sfb-torus-endomorphism-kernel-dichotomy vs lem-sfb-multiplicative-type-largest-subtorus: that node constructs a largest subtorus but does not assert its dimension is positive for a non-surjective endomorphism, or faithful flatness in the full-rank case. The new lemma also distinguishes inseparable finite kernels from positive-dimensional torus kernels.

lem-sfb-torus-endomorphism-kernel-dichotomy vs lem-sfb-multiplicative-character-antiduality: the existing antiequivalence gives character objects and detects closed immersions, but its statement contains neither the exact kernel correspondence nor this rank/faithful-flatness alternative. This is a genuine supporting torus lemma, proved later from character lattices and diagonalizable kernels, not a new clause of the Borel theorem.

lem-sfb-torus-endomorphism-kernel-dichotomy vs lem-sfb-split-torus-hom-matrices: the existing statement identifies homomorphisms with integer matrices; it does not assert faithful flatness, dimension of the kernel, or existence of a positive-dimensional closed subtorus. These need the exactness and diagonalizable-kernel theory cited in the torus alternative.
