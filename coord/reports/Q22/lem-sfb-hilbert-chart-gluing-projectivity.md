## Review by Q22

I checked the proof against delivery-errors B–E with the node statements and the sources open. My main concern was not the final properness criterion but the preceding descent and finite-type hypotheses. The report below specifies what each step actually takes from its cited statements.

1. **Affine restriction data.** `def-pc-fibre-product` supplies the Hom universal property; `lem-weil-pbc-open-fibre-product` identifies the base restriction with the open preimage. I use `lem-galois-yoneda-fully-faithful` only between two representable functors in the category of schemes over the smaller affine open. Both represent the stipulated functor on *all* those schemes, so it supplies the unique isomorphism and its identity/cocycle equations. The affine opens form a set; no proper-class gluing index is used.
2. **Scheme and ambient map.** `lem-weil-pbc-relative-glueing-affine-opens` asks for nested-affine transitions with open-preimage images and composition; step 1 verifies exactly these. Its statement supplies compatible charts, not representability on arbitrary test schemes. The latter is proved later. Agreement of maps to G is checked over affine refinements of intersections using Yoneda full faithfulness, then `lem-weil-pbc-scheme-morphism-gluing` makes those equalities global and glues j. Intersections of affine opens are never assumed affine.
3. **Embedded family descent.** I use `lem-weil-pbc-glue-schemes` with the open restrictions of the given closed families; equality as embedded closed subschemes supplies canonical cocycles. `lem-weil-pbc-open-fibre-product` identifies ambient restrictions with opens. The morphism-gluing node gives the inclusion map, and `lem-weil-pbc-closed-finite-etale-zariski-local` says exactly that closed immersion can be checked on an arbitrary target-open cover. Finally `def-sbc-closed-immersion-ideal-sheaf` supplies the actual kernel, affine quotient description and quasi-coherence. The displayed formula proves the ideals glue and proves uniqueness, so I have not cited an unrelated definition as a descent theorem.
4. **Family conditions.** The statement of `lem-trf-structural-fibrewise-open-locality` explicitly allows any chosen subset of its conditions and arbitrary covers/bases. I specialize its diagram to identities as written in the proof and choose just flatness and finite presentation of p. Thus there is no illicit dimension, smoothness, etale, Noetherian or quasi-compact-cover assumption. The fibre-polynomial check is a literal identity of the same embedded geometric fibre, with the same O(1). `def-sfb-proper-hilbert-presheaf` supplies the stipulated pullback maps. No claim that the ambient E is flat occurs.
5. **Representability for all T.** The inverse-image opens T_U cover any S-scheme T. On them the hypothesis applies, even when T_U is not affine or Noetherian. The earlier family descent gives one direction, and the cited morphism-gluing node gives the other. Compatibility on overlaps is checked on affine refinements in S using the transitions of step 1. Uniqueness makes the constructions inverse and natural. Yoneda full faithfulness is used only for uniqueness of representing chart isomorphisms, which its statement actually says.
6. **Universal ideals.** The cited `lem-galois-yoneda-hom-bijection` really states evaluation at the identity and its pullback naturality formula. That is the general Yoneda statement needed here. Together with step 3 and the cited kernel/ideal description it identifies the glued family with the identity element and every pullback with the represented family. Equality of ideals on overlaps is checked, not inferred from abstract isomorphism of unembedded schemes. Arbitrary base-change ideals are understood as their image in the new structure sheaf; no false assertion about injectivity of a nonflat tensor pullback is made.
7. **Chart finite type.** `lem-kun-projective-implies-proper` applies to G because `def-kun-projective` uses the H-projective finite-projective-space convention. `def-weil-proper-morphism` explicitly includes separatedness and finite type. `lem-sfb-finite-type-composition-base-change` states both arbitrary base change and composition, exactly what is needed for G_U/U and Y_U/U. The theorem about Y's properness is not being used prematurely.
8. **Noetherian source.** `lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-open-subscheme-noetherian` applies to affine opens of S because S is both locally Noetherian and quasi-compact. `lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian` then applies to each chart from step 7. The finite affine cover of S gives a finite cover of Y by quasi-compact opens, whose finite-cover argument is written out. `pqf-def-locnoeth` permits combining the chart affine covers to prove local Noetherianness. This works without separatedness of S or Y.
9. **Global finite type.** `lem-weil-pbc-lft-pointwise-criterion` allows the local affine source/target neighbourhoods obtained from each finite-type chart to establish local finite type globally, for both f and j. Its statement really is pointwise, so no unproved shrinking of an affine neighbourhood is hidden. Every affine-target preimage is an open in the Noetherian Y and hence quasi-compact by the cited open-subscheme node. `def-weil-finite-type-morphism` now gives finite type. This is where Noetherianness avoids the invalid inference that a globally quasi-compact source alone makes every morphism quasi-compact.
10. **Immersion.** `lem-zl-immersion-near-image-locality` states immersion descent on opens covering the image, including arbitrary and empty index sets. The G_U cover G itself, and their preimages and restrictions were identified in step 2, so it applies directly.
11. **DVR criterion.** The given F_P bijection is transported using the natural global representing bijection, so both existence and uniqueness of the actual map over S are proved. `lem-sfb-noetherian-dvr-properness`, checked against Limits 0208, assumes only a locally Noetherian base and finite type of f; these have already been proved. It requires all DVRs, exactly as the hypothesis provides, with no restriction on their image, field or finite-type nature. Properness then supplies separatedness by its definition.
12. **Closed immersion.** `lem-weil-pbc-proper-over-separated` requires Y/S proper, G/S separated and an S-map, all now established. It makes j itself proper. `lem-weil-pbc-proper-immersion-closed` then has precisely its two hypotheses and gives a closed immersion. I did not infer that an immersion is closed merely from source properness without the separated ambient hypothesis.
13. **H-projectivity.** `def-kun-projective` gives the fixed finite N and G embedding. The first clause of `lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-products` is exactly composition of closed immersions; I do not use its product or open-immersion clauses. It gives the required finite-projective-space embedding of Y, not a different convention of projectivity.

I tested the limiting cases: the empty base and empty test scheme cause no missing-cover problem; the only closed subscheme of an empty ambient scheme is empty; P=0 allows the empty family, while a polynomial realized by no family forces empty representing charts and empty Y. Nothing uses positivity of P, n>0, nonempty fibres, reducedness or flatness of E. For n=0 the same chart argument applies. A nonflat E can still be handled, provided the explicitly assumed family DVR bijection holds; the proof never tries to derive it from ambient flatness. All 13 steps are below 2,000 characters.
