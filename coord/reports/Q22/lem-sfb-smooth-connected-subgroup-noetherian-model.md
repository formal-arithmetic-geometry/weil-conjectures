### Source and edge cases

Read Conrad's linked PDF online, printed p.57, direct-limit reduction in Proposition 2.1.2,
and printed p.62, the noetherian reduction in Proposition 2.1.6 citing EGA IV 8.3.3, 9.7.9,
17.8.7. These passages give the simultaneous spreading and the eventual smoothness and connected
fibre conditions. The blueprint's current `latest` unit statement is the six-functor stability
statement; this item is its supporting input and no unit or chapter is modified.

Checked before drafting: A = 0 is allowed (no `Nontrivial A` or nonempty spectrum assumption),
and in this case all fibre conditions over the empty base are vacuous as in the source.
The trivial subgroup and H = G both satisfy the form of the statement. A disconnected finite
subgroup does not satisfy `GeometricallyConnected`; an empty geometric fibre also fails because
`ConnectedSpace` extends `Nonempty`. The base ring need not be a field, reduced or Noetherian,
and its extension from A₀ need not be flat or faithfully flat. G itself is not assumed connected.
One explicit universe u holds the ring and schemes; no smaller-universe testing restriction is
introduced. Models can be taken in that universe by finite-presentation descent.
