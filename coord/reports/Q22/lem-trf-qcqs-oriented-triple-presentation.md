I read Stacks topologies.tex Tag 021D (lemma-fibre-products-etale), Tag 04HR (lemma-alternative), and the adjacent proof of lemma-affine-big-site-etale, plus the finite affine covering refinement lemma-etale-affine. The new node is an assembled ordinary-site formulation of these sources, not a claim that 021D alone contains a comparison theorem.

The argument supporting its truth is as follows (recorded here, not as a proof in the unsplit node). An object finite-presented over a qcqs base is qcqs. A morphism A to B between these objects is etale by etale cancellation, separated by separated cancellation, and finite-presented by qcqs permanence and local finite presentation. Thus pullbacks over arbitrary objects remain in the category, using base change and composition; the identity is terminal. Separatedness and finite presentation survive arbitrary change of base. Every etale object is covered by affine open subschemes; an affine source over the quasi-separated base is quasi-compact, etale and separated, hence of finite presentation. This makes the subcategory cover-dense. Any cover of one of its objects has a finite affine refinement, since its target is quasi-compact and etale images are open. Those affine sources are again in the subcategory. Therefore finite jointly surjective covers induce exactly the restricted topology, and the comparison argument of 04HR applies to this intermediate full subcategory. This argument requires several scheme permanence and site comparison results; it is not a two-line elementary identity or a one-line specialization of a single Mathlib declaration.
