No assertion of sectionwise cokernel exactness is used.

Step 2 cites only the triple-arrow definition and proves faithfulness directly: once the X-arrow is fixed, its base arrow is fixed, and the arrow in Sch/V is unique. I did not assume h faithful or representable. This short uniqueness argument is elementary.

Step 3 cites the scheme fibre-product existence and universal property in def-pc-fibre-product, the cartesian-arrow axiom in the fibred-groupoid definition, and the full pullback universal property in cartesian-lift-pullback-square. Those statements supply the required total-category pullbacks, and applying the same theorem to their images proves preservation. No unproved assertion that total categories have terminal objects or binary products is made.

Step 4 uses only the new total-equalizer theorem on the algebraic stack X, never on an unproved algebraic test-fibre category. I verified that the equalizer in X lifts to a triple by composing its base map with the original map to V and pulling back alpha. The arrow factorization into that triple is explicitly checked on the map to V and on alpha. Faithfulness from step 2 gives uniqueness and equality. Thus the source C has equalizers and the functor preserves them, even for parallel arrows with different base maps. The new equalizer statement follows precisely 06WY(3), which needs algebraicity but neither a separated nor finite-type diagonal.

Step 5 is the cartesian lifting axiom written as a slice equivalence: every arrow into z is uniquely isomorphic over z to the cartesian lift of its base arrow; morphisms are exactly base triangles. The two cited definition statements supply this uniqueness and the ring Gamma(W,O_W). I checked that the induced slice functor is the identity under both equivalences and that covers, as well as rings, coincide. This is an elementary argument from those definitions, not an invocation of an uncited site-equivalence theorem.

Step 6 cites the new exact-lower-shriek statement after the continuity, limits and ringed-slice hypotheses have all been established. In 0798 equivalence on slices makes the localized abelian lower shriek carry the restricted ring to the restricted ring. Consequently its comparison with module lower shriek is an isomorphism, and 04BH supplies exactness. This explains why a plain continuous/cocontinuous functor alone would not suffice. The second citation, right-adjoint-preserves-injectives, states precisely the mono-preserving-left-adjoint criterion, and exact L preserves monomorphisms. The extension and transpose argument is also written out so its direction is auditable.

Small cases checked: h the identity gives restriction to a big scheme slice; V empty leaves the usual empty-scheme objects, and the categorical proofs still apply; zero modules cause no exception. Nontrivial stabilizers do not destroy faithfulness of the fibre projection: they are retained in X-arrows, while the Sch/V component is forced.
