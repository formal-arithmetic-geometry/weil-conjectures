## Corrections and omissions in the incoming node

The incoming statement agrees with the first proof paragraph of Stacks 05FT (flat.tex, lemma-fibres-irreducible-flat-projective-nonnoetherian), and is unchanged. Its proof was empty. The four approximation references in its source are not themselves proof steps. I replaced that gap by a common directed-system construction, using existing algebra descent and eventual-flatness nodes and the source's constructible-locus argument. I did not create a separate approximation node for each desired clause, or a single new node restating the item.

The source needs geometric reducedness as well as geometric irreducibility. Keeping only the latter would allow nonreduced fibres. The new reduced-locus node explicitly states that equality. For irreducibility I reused the more general existing lem-sbc-radicial-preparation-fibre-comparison with h the identity universal homeomorphism, rather than creating a duplicate of its base-change case.

The current blueprint main-20261015 ancestor lem-trf-local-term-locality and its blueprint_update concern contracting correspondences. The current item is an already recorded algebraic helper deep in the flattening/projectivity branch of that ancestor. This delivery expands that helper alone, preserving its ancestry, chapter and unit; it does not change the ancestor or its current contracting-correspondence statement, and cites no SGA source.

## Review by Q04

I read the item against flat.tex, Tag 05FT, lemma-fibres-irreducible-flat-projective-nonnoetherian, including its four explicit approximation citations. I then read more-morphisms.tex: 05FE (lemma-Noetherian-approximation-flat), 05FG (lemma-Noetherian-approximation-geometrically-reduced), 05FH (lemma-Noetherian-approximation-geometrically-irreducible), and 05FL (lemma-Noetherian-approximation-combine). The source's construction uses flatness descent and then refines a model so its base maps into the good constructible loci. The six steps implement this common-stage argument; a mere fibre product of three independent models would not suffice without identifying their algebra models. The proof retains finite presentation of every algebra by existing base-change permanence, so there is no unnoticed appeal to finite type over a non-Noetherian ring implying finite presentation.

For the reduced-locus node I read the statements and proofs of 0579, lemma-geometrically-reduced-constructible, and 0576, lemma-base-change-fibres-geometrically-reduced, in more-morphisms.tex. The first assumes quasi-compact and locally finitely presented, satisfied by a finite-presentation ring map; the second allows any base morphism and gives an equality, not only inclusion. I also read properties.tex 054C, lemma-locally-constructible: on an affine base the locally constructible locus is constructible. The node includes the base-change equality because step 3 genuinely needs its reverse implication. It does not impose flatness or Noetherian hypotheses absent from those sources.

For the irreducible-locus node I read 055B, lemma-nr-geom-irreducible-components-constructible, together with its use in the proof of 05FH. Its n=1 locus is exactly geometric irreducibility for finite-type fibres, including nonemptiness. The node asserts only constructibility; I deliberately did not add a new irreducibility base-change lemma. The arbitrary-base-change equivalence is already the statement of lem-sbc-radicial-preparation-fibre-comparison, read in full and used with h the identity. I checked the corresponding source statement 0555, lemma-base-change-fibres-geometrically-irreducible. The existing node's source tag is a section tag, but its actual statement has the required stronger arbitrary-base-change equivalence; this delivery depends on that statement, not on a hidden construction in its proof.

I opened both node statements. The former allows adjoining the finitely many generators of the chosen stage to any finite subset of R, so the tail really has colimit R. Source checks: algebra.tex 00R0 (lemma-limit-finite-presentation) and 05N9 (lemma-colimit-category-fp-algebras).

Step 6 is marked elementary: it collects earlier conclusions and writes the tensor-associativity maps and their inverses explicitly on pure tensors, including the two ring maps giving the cocartesian diagram. All steps have fewer than 2000 characters (lengths 666, 561, 1374, 761, 873, 1080), so there is no long step concealing a new comparison lemma.

I tested the new statements on A=B=k, polynomial algebras, A=0, and B=0. For B=0 the reduced locus is all of Spec A but the irreducible locus is empty: the distinction is essential and the proof's geometric integrality excludes empty fibres.
