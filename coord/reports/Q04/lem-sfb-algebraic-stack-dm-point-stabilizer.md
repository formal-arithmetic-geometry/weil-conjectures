The group operations are composition and inversion in the fibre groupoid. Step 2 cites `lem-sfb-group-space-unramified-base-locus`. Its hypothesis of local finite type was established in step 1. Conjugation identifies the two pulled-back groups, and the corresponding maximal opens agree by their defining property. This is an elementary uniqueness argument from the cited theorem.

Step 3 cites `lem-sfb-invariant-open-substack-descent` for the invariant open from step 2; its statement gives precisely W and P times_X W=V. Maximality of U supplies W contained in U.

Step 4 uses the same atlas criterion in its reverse direction on P_U to U. The automorphism space is exactly G restricted to P_U, because U is full. The maximality clause of `lem-sfb-group-space-unramified-base-locus` says P_U is contained in V; the inclusion-preserving clause of `lem-sfb-invariant-open-substack-descent` then gives U contained in W. It reduces the question to the equality of opens already obtained in step 5. A field has the zero prime as its unique point, so its spectrum has only two opens. The two implications between a full group base locus and global unramifiedness use the existence/unramifiedness and maximality clauses of the group-locus node, respectively.
