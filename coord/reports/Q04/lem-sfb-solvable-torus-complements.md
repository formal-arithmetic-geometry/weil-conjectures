## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED (repaired): Milne iAG Theorem 16.27(a), printed p.333, omits the smoothness hypothesis on the diagonal quotient. The old new-node statement explicitly allowed non-smooth D and was false. Summary 16.29, printed p.334, explicitly requires D to be a smooth group of multiplicative type and states the needed conjugacy in case (b), over an algebraically closed field. The corrected node follows that statement, not the unrestricted printed 16.27(a).

In characteristic p let J consist of matrices [[t,x],[0,1]] with t^p=1 and x^p=0. Then J is alpha_p semidirect mu_p, its unitriangular kernel V is alpha_p, and its diagonal quotient D is mu_p. For a nonzero a in F the maps s1(t)=[[t,0],[0,1]] and s2(t)=[[t,a(1-t)],[0,1]] are group-scheme sections: a(1-tt')=a(1-t)+t*a(1-t') and (a(1-t))^p=a^p(1-t^p)=0. They are distinct as scheme morphisms since 1-t is nonzero in F[t]/(t^p-1). But V(F)={1}, so no element of V(F) conjugates s1 to s2. The conjugating matrix [[1,a],[0,1]] is in the ambient U_2(F), but not in V(F). Thus checking rational points of D alone would conceal the failure.

The vanishing H^1(D,N)=0 asserted in the proof of 16.27(a) cannot be used in this generality: the character group of mu_p has p-torsion. The future split of the corrected input must retain smoothness when justifying that vanishing, using the smooth-quotient conclusion of Summary 16.29. No restricted source is used or cited for the repaired route.
