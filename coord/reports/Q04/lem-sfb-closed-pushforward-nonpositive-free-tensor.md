## Source error

Stacks `literature/stacks-project/more-morphisms.tex`, Tag 09US, `lemma-pull-relative-pseudo-coherent`, tensor-filtration paragraph, says that the sufficiently negative columns have zero cohomology in degrees less than or equal to m. This inequality is reversed. On Spec(k), take E=k[0], m=0, and F with F^q=k in every nonpositive degree and zero differentials. The q-th column has nonzero H^q=k for every negative q, directly contradicting the printed claim. For an E bounded above by a, the correct quotient-tail estimate is n <= a+c-1; choosing c=min(0,m-a) makes the tail vanish in every degree n >= m. Our item's source already requests this repair, so its statement is unchanged. The proof uses one finite truncation and a single quotient tail, never an infinite-extension closure assertion.
