## STATEMENT-DISPUTED: arbitrary filtered diagrams need more than quasi-compactness

Stacks `sites.tex`, Tag 0738 (`lemma-directed-colimits-sections`), distinguishes precisely the missing hypotheses: (2) gives injectivity on a quasi-compact object; (3) gives bijectivity there for injective transitions; (4) gives bijectivity for arbitrary transitions with a cofinal system of finite covers having quasi-compact overlaps. The blueprint's sentence “the fibre products of these are again quasi-compact when computed over affines” does not prove that the actual products over a non-quasi-separated target are quasi-compact.

Here is an explicit counterexample to the imported set-valued clause (1). Let R be the Boolean ring of eventually constant sequences in the field with two elements, and let A = Spec(R). Its space is the convergent sequence N union {infinity}: each n is an isolated open-and-closed point, and every neighborhood of infinity contains all but finitely many n. The open W = N is not quasi-compact. Glue two copies A_+, A_- along W to obtain the quasi-compact scheme V with two limit points infinity_+, infinity_-. It is not quasi-separated because A_+ intersects A_- in W.

For each finite subset S of N let V_S be two copies of A glued only along S. The identity maps on the two charts give an etale (hence weakly etale) map V_S -> V, so h_(V_S) is a representable sheaf on V_proet. For S contained in T there is the chartwise identity V_S -> V_T. The sheaf colimit of this filtered diagram is h_V: the two chartwise local lifts cover V; their disagreements at n are identified at the stage S union {n}. More explicitly, every test morphism to V lifts on the inverse images of A_+ and A_-, and two lifts with the same image agree locally after enlarging S, since any mixed-chart comparison factors through the discrete open W and can be covered by its points. Thus the map from the presheaf colimit to h_V is locally surjective and locally injective, giving h_V as its sheafification.

There is no section V -> V_S over V. The point infinity_+ must map to the unique point over it in the positive chart of V_S, and likewise infinity_- to the negative chart. By continuity the inverse image of each of these open charts contains a cofinite neighborhood of the corresponding limit point. Their intersection therefore contains an n outside S. A global section would have to send that same point n of V into both charts of V_S, whose intersection is S, a contradiction. Consequently every h_(V_S)(V) is empty, whereas (colim_S h_(V_S))(V) = h_V(V) contains the identity. This directly disproves surjectivity in the imported clause.

Correction: clause (1) now says injectivity for quasi-compact V, and bijectivity if V is quasi-separated OR the transitions are sectionwise injective. Clause (2), the actual coefficient-descent conclusion, is preserved for every quasi-compact V. Its transitions are injective, so the second alternative is sufficient. No quasi-separatedness assumption is added to coefficient descent. The new statement also introduces the prime, coefficient convention and smallness, and makes explicit that the matrices have finitely many entries.

The only other nodes citing this item are `det-lem-coefficient-finite-descent-lisse` and `sk-thm-sfb-qlbar-two-colimit`. The lisse proof uses clause (2), which is unchanged and still applies to its quasi-compact overlaps. The two-colimit proof's first step uses the finite affine refinements, now explicitly proved in step 2. Neither needs the disproved unrestricted claim. Those nodes were read, not edited. The chapter route and the unit route through the coefficient colimit are unchanged.
