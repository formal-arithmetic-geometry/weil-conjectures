## Review by Q130

I checked the statement against 09LT and 09LN with commutative rings concentrated in degree zero, tensor over B and Hom over S. In Stacks' bimodule notation the source algebra is B, the target algebra is S, and the fixed bimodule is Q. The B-action on Hom is precomposition by multiplication on Q. The item assumes Q K-flat over B and I K-injective over S; it assumes neither B/S flat nor UQ K-flat over S. None of the steps introduces those extra assumptions. The ordinary module interpretation of the sheaf-complex conventions is the one-point ringed-site specialization.

Step 1 uses the actual statement of `lem-trf-bimodule-resolved-hom-tensor-models`, which explicitly asserts the B-action, B-K-injectivity of J(Q,I), its comparison bifunctor and both tensor quasi-isomorphisms. Taking T=Q and q the identity is permitted because Q is assumed K-flat. The second citation, `def-pc-module-kflat-complex`, explicitly includes the equivalent quasi-isomorphism-preservation criterion, so it really supports descent in C. Restriction preserves a quasi-isomorphism here because its cohomology has the same underlying groups. This step does not reprove the model lemma or rely on its unpublished proof.

Step 2 uses `def-sc-module-total-hom-complex`, whose statement gives the product and differential. Substituting degrees 0 and -1 proves that cycles are chain maps and boundaries are null homotopies. The operation is natural under composition. This is an elementary definition calculation, not an uncited claim that arbitrary unresolved Hom computes derived Hom.

Step 3 uses `lem-weil-pbc-kinjective-hom-derived`: its statement is for any abelian category and gives exactly the localization bijection into a K-injective target, so it applies once to B-modules and once to S-modules. The first target is K-injective by step 1, the second by hypothesis. `lem-trf-bimodule-chain-currying-descent` states the entire-complex isomorphism with the exact forward and inverse formulas, and explicitly asserts naturality for arbitrary derived morphisms under canonical resolved-model identifications. It therefore supplies both the H0 formula and the derived, rather than merely chain-level, naturality. I did not substitute a fully faithful Yoneda or a definition-only node for any of these facts.

Step 4 uses `lem-weil-pbc-adjunction-of-natural-bijection`, a Mathlib leaf. Its statement supplies the unit and counit as transposes of identities, both triangles, and the requisite naturality. The orientation is correct: its Psi is the inverse of the displayed bijection in this item. Substitution of 1_J gives chain evaluation, while substitution of the resolution representative r of the identity gives the unit formula. Thus the abstract triangles belong to the same specified maps, rather than to an unrelated choice of adjunction. A resolution to N is used only over S, as the statement stipulates.

Step 5 uses the localization definition `def-weil-abstract-derived-category` and the S-complex isomorphism/naturality of `lem-trf-bimodule-chain-currying-descent`. I checked scalar linearity by the explicit evaluation equation. Restriction changes no underlying groups or differentials and therefore changes neither kernels nor images; comparison quasi-isomorphisms survive. It carries the already established commutative squares and identities to the same equations. This elementary check does not assert preservation of K-injectivity, does not tensor down to K, and does not claim fullness on K-linear derived Hom sets. It avoids the common invalid substitution of a larger Hom complex.

For small cases I checked Q=0 (both functors are zero, both Hom sets are singleton sets, and the zero-object triangles hold), Q=B in degree zero (restriction/coextension of scalars, with counit evaluation at the regular module and the displayed unit), and S=B with Q=B (the usual identity normalization).
