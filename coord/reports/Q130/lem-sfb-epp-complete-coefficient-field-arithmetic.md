is the identity up to its canonical identification, and N = K suffices. In equal
characteristic take S = top, r = 0, and z the image of t for any separable E/K.
The purely inseparable case allows S = bottom and arbitrary r; no separability
hypothesis is imposed on E/K. Mixed characteristic is retained because only the final
clause has `CharP K p` as premise. All uniformizers, including unit multiples of a
chosen one, are quantified, as required by the complete-field expansion argument.

uniformizer zero cannot generate the nonzero maximal ideal of a DVR. Universes u, v, w
are explicitly bound and independent for A, K, E. The existential overfield lives in
max v w, large enough to contain E and K and a finite normal closure (or a transported
copy thereof); there is no site or index-universe restriction.
