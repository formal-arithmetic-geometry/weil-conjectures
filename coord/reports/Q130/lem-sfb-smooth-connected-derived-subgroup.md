For lem-sfb-pointed-word-kernel-stabilization I checked Milne 6.20(a) and generalized its prime-kernel argument to a pointed map G^r to J. Products of a smooth connected group over an algebraically closed field are geometrically integral. Appending identity tuples makes I_(n+1) a subideal of I_n; integral coordinate rings make every kernel prime. The chain lies below I_1, whose finite height in the finite-type Noetherian coordinate ring bounds strict descents, so it stabilizes. Its limiting prime gives the integral quotient, and the injective coordinate map gives the dense image. This is not an appeal to Noetherian descending chains of closed sets. The scheme-image definitions found in Mathlib do not give this result. The node is statement-only, and does not assert the image is a group.
