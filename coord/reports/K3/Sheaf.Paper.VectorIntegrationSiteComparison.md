## Where the brief, the DAG or earlier work was wrong

The node's planned `p1KummerTraceSiteTransport` and
`integrationExponentTransposeTransport` declarations have a finite-type-over-fixed-finite-
field domain and coefficient level `P.level - 1`. The corresponding item theorems have
arbitrary Noetherian bases and arbitrary coefficient indices. They cannot simply be
applied at those general types. The projective reduction retains the original broader
statement; the other two original holes are left unchanged rather than specialized.

The new class comparison is the explicit infinity-divisor step of the existing plan,
not a replacement of that unit's route. It is true over arbitrary base rings: on the
chart containing infinity its ideal is generated by the coordinate, and on the other
chart it is the unit ideal. Dualizing gives O(1). The empty base and zero coefficient
ring give the same identity, and no flatness over the Kummer coefficient ring is used.
Root-tensor extensionality, rather than an unconstrained choice of a Tate isomorphism,
identifies the normalized comparison. No arbitrary data are chosen from unproved existence.
