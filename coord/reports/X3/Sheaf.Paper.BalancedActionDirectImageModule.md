are unchanged. Added proof-only imports of `Sheaf.Paper.BalancedActionAffineSections`
and `Sheaf.Paper.AffineBasisRingActionGluing`.

The new gluing theorem extends the action as a natural map of presheaves of sets
`R(U) × F(U) → F(U)` using Mathlib's `TopCat.Sheaf.restrictHomEquivHom`.
Ring and additive identities and uniqueness are proved by separatedness on the
affine basis. It needs neither affine intersections nor compactness.

The main proof extends the scalar and B-generator identities to arbitrary opens,
then constructs the new module sheaf with `PresheafOfModules.ofPresheaf` on exactly
M's underlying abelian sections and restrictions. Its sheaf proof is M's original
sheaf proof. The restriction-of-scalars isomorphism and its inverse are the
identity on sections; linearity is proved from the scalar identity. Thus no data
is selected from an undetermined existence theorem. Both action families are
specified by unique-existence statements, and gluing is now fully proved.

two new nodes. No statement or definition changes, forced adaptations, or changes
