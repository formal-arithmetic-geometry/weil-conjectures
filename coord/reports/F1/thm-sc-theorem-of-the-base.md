## Review by F1

I read Stacks Tag 0BA0 (Lemma 44.6.7), its cited Tag 0B9T for local constancy of Euler characteristic, Tag 0B9I for the smooth proper Hilbert scheme, Tags 0B9X and 0B9Z for the open Hilbert chart and representability, Tag 03GN for surjective proper images, Tag 047L (Lemma 39.7.3) for group-scheme separatedness, and Tag 056T for reducedness of smooth schemes over fields. The item has the hypotheses of 0BA0: a separably closed field and a geometrically integral smooth projective curve. The new Tag 047L node assumes only a field and a group scheme, exactly as the source does. Its statement introduces both objects, uses the actual definition nodes for separatedness and closed immersion, has no proof or proof dependencies, and carries the precise blueprint proof clause it serves. Whole-DAG searches for 047L, group-scheme separatedness and the proposed id, plus whole-blueprint searches for 047L and "group scheme over a field", found no duplicate. The one blueprint result was a definition of translation, not separatedness. For the ring node, whole-blueprint searches for "smooth algebra over a field is reduced" found no match; the Tag 056T hit is the stronger scheme-level fact in another unit. The ring statement introduces K, A and the smoothness hypothesis, cites the smooth-ring definition, and is the exact ring calculation used by the Lean source. It has no proof steps or proof dependencies.

Step 1 takes representability from thm-pc-picard-representable, the affine comparison from lem-pc-picard-functor-agrees-sc, a rational point from lem-pc-rational-points-dense, and the locally constant degree, decomposition, and zero subgroup from clauses (1)--(3) of prop-pd-picard-degree-decomposition. The last node states all three facts, so Tag 0B9T is background to that node rather than an uncited proof input.

Step 2 uses lem-pc-f-open-subfunctor to place the functor F openly in Pic, thm-pc-f-representable to identify it with an open W of Hilb^g, and cor-picard-degree-g to put W inside Pic^g. Lem-pc-twist supplies, for each residue-field point, a line bundle on C whose twist lies in F; prop-pd-picard-degree-decomposition supplies addition of degrees and translation by k-points. I checked that the required twist has degree zero when the original point has degree g, so its translate remains in Pic^g. Step 3 takes smoothness and dimension g of Hilb^g from clause (1) of thm-pc-hilb-curves; the open charts and their translates cover every point, and def-sbc-smooth-morphisms supplies the local smooth-relative-dimension notion.

Step 4 gives the Mathlib leaf lem-weil-pbc-image-universally-closed every hypothesis: Hilb^g is proper by thm-pc-hilb-curves, Pic^g is separated by the new Tag 047L node and open-and-closed restriction, and Pic^g is locally finite type because Step 3 made it smooth. Lem-sc-hilb-to-pic-morphism supplies gamma_g and lem-sc-hilb-to-pic-surjective supplies surjectivity at d=g. The leaf's second clause states exactly that these hypotheses make Pic^g proper. The first dry run rejected the earlier route through the generic smooth-geometrically-reduced node; I replaced the scheme-theoretic-image argument with this direct proper-image result.

Step 5 uses lem-sc-hilb-geometrically-integral for the source after every field extension and lem-weil-pbc-surjective-base-change for the map after extension. The continuous-image node then gives irreducibility of Pic^g after extension. Lem-pc-proper-base-change (2) keeps Pic^g smooth over every field extension K/k; the affine-local criterion of def-sbc-smooth-morphisms makes each affine chart a smooth K-algebra, and the new ring node makes it reduced. Localizations of reduced rings remain reduced, so def-pc-integral-scheme combines reducedness and irreducibility. I checked the Hilbert integrality lemma's nonnegative-degree hypothesis at d=g, and the curve's geometric irreducibility follows from its geometric integrality. Step 6 uses clause (4) of the degree-decomposition node to identify every P_d with P_g via P_0. Lem-pc-rational-hyp verifies the section and pushforward assumptions of thm-pc-pic-split; over Spec k the Picard group of the base is zero, so P_d(k) is exactly the degree-d line bundles. The zero piece is already a subgroup by Step 1.

For edge cases, g=0 makes Hilb^0 and Pic^0 zero-dimensional and the same chart argument applies; d below g, including negative d, is handled by translation rather than an impossible Hilbert scheme of negative degree. No algebraic-closedness assumption was slipped into a field extension K: the twist node explicitly allows arbitrary K/k, while the rational point on C uses only separable closedness. I corrected the new node's initial reference to def-pc-morphism-conventions, which only forwards the definition, to def-weil-proper-morphism and def-weil-closed-immersion, which state the notions.
