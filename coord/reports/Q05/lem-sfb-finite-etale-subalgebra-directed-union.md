For each factor, `lem-weil-pbc-finite-separable-extension-etale` gives etaleness; `lem-weil-pbc-etale-algebra-finite-product` gives it for the remaining product, including the zero ring. The kernel calculation is written explicitly using coordinate idempotents and inverses inside the fields, so no uncited general quotient-preserves-etale assertion is assumed (that assertion would be false over an arbitrary base). The map from the image to the surviving coordinates is described and both its injectivity and surjectivity are proved. `def-weil-finite-integral-morphism` says that finiteness on an affine spectrum is module finiteness, obtained here by a finite spanning family. All hypotheses are met: the base is a field and each L_i is finite separable; A need not be reduced, finite type or nonzero.
