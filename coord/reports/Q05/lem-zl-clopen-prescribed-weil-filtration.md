Step 1 uses the geometry and abelian conclusions actually stated by `lem-zl-weil-heart-localization-exact`: each open/closed piece is separated finite type, its geometric base is finite type over k, and the Weil heart is abelian. Thus the finite-type and coefficient hypotheses of `lem-pur-finite-clopen-derived-decomposition` hold, including invertibility of ell. That node states its equivalence at Qbar, not merely at finite coefficients, and componentwise cohomology makes it restrict to the standard hearts. Every component is over k0, so Frobenius preserves it. The open-unit node supplies precisely the natural comparison alpha: G_V h* -> h* G_S. In the transport node its b must be alpha inverse, so the transported structure J(theta)b inverse is h*(theta) alpha, the actual restriction structure. I checked this direction rather than merely asserting equivariance. Fixed pairs in a product are coordinatewise by their defining equation. Kernel and cokernel universal properties pass through a fully faithful essentially surjective functor; that supplies exactness of the resulting heart equivalence, with no triangulated structure asserted on Weil pairs.

Step 2 uses the open adjunction of the open-unit node on the underlying Qbar categories. In a product category insertion of L in coordinate b has Hom into r(M) equal to Hom(L,h_b* M); other coordinates contribute singleton Hom sets from zero. The identity arrows under the two natural Hom bijections give inverse arrows, explicitly explaining the identification instead of hiding another lemma. The b-coordinate is the very unit that the node states is a Weil isomorphism; all other coordinates vanish and have unique structure maps. Consequently this identifies the actual induced Weil h_! with coordinate insertion, not an arbitrarily selected zero extension. The immersion local-behavior node states precisely that Rj_! restricts to an exact functor of constructible hearts. Each h_b is an open immersion of separated finite-type models, as required. Its closedness is used for the finite component decomposition, not as an unmentioned properness assumption in the open adjunction.

Full faithfulness lifts their arrows and tests equality, so they are subobjects of the given H, with compatible inclusions. This calculation uses every given G and works without strict inclusions; it neither asserts a direct-sum decomposition of a nonsplit component filtration nor applies a lemma merely stating mixedness.
