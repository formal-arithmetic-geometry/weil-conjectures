For the child I checked that the connected-component map is meaningful for every continuous f: the image of a connected set is connected, and the quotient identity gives continuity. A proof plan, kept outside the statement-only node, is as follows. The two quotient component spaces are profinite by 0906. Clopens on either original space are precisely the inverse images of clopens of its quotient, since a clopen is a union of connected components and the quotient topology detects both it and its complement. Thus the induced map on profinite quotients has bijective inverse image on clopens. It is injective: separate two source quotient points by a clopen and lift that clopen from the target. Its compact image is closed in the Hausdorff target. If this image missed a point, use the profinite clopen basis to find a nonempty clopen disjoint from the image; its inverse image equals that of the empty clopen, contrary to injectivity on clopens. It is therefore surjective. A continuous bijection from compact to Hausdorff is a homeomorphism, since images of closed subsets are compact and hence closed. This is a real general topology lemma with its own hypotheses and surjectivity argument, rather than an elementary restatement of the item.
