It therefore induces $\psi:IR\otimes_RA_a\to IR\otimes_RA_{a'}$, with inverse given by $u(q)$ in place of $u(q)^{-1}$, and $\mu_{a'}\psi(z^q\otimes x)=a'(q)u(q)^{-1}x=a(q)x=\mu_a(z^q\otimes x)$. Hence $\psi$ maps $\ker\mu_a$ isomorphically onto $\ker\mu_{a'}=0$ (Step 5), and $\operatorname{Tor}^R_1(R/IR,A)=0$ for the structure of the statement, for every monoid ideal $I$; for $I=\varnothing$ the source $IR\otimes_RA$ is $0$, and for $I=P$ the quotient $R/IR$ is $0$.
