## Review by Q05

I read the source statements and proof listed above with the item and the new node open. I retained the item's DG-algebra, differential, action and shift conventions, its complete decreasing filtration starting at F_0 I=I, the graded A-linear sections, and products of arbitrarily many shifts. Nothing is assumed bounded below, commutative or finite. The new node introduces the DG algebra, modules, character dual and differential, cohomology, chain maps and the quotient by actual degree -1 A-linear homotopies; it uses no unexplained coinduction or K-injective terminology.

Step 1 uses exactly the general extension assertion in lem-per-divisible-group-hom-extension, whose statement I opened. Its additional algebraically-closed-field specialization is irrelevant. I checked divisibility of Q/Z explicitly by q/m modulo Z; the subgroup inclusion is injective. The cocycle vanishes on im d, which equals ker d by the given acyclicity, so beta is well-defined. For a dual degree r-1 extension mu, d(mu)=(-1)^r mu d equals lambda with the chosen sign. Thus the argument proves the exactness needed, rather than citing a definition of injectivity or assuming duality is exact.

Step 2 uses exactly the new node's Hom in K(A) isomorphism with H^k(C dual). The new statement includes what equality to zero in K(A) means, so this supplies a genuine A-linear null-homotopy. A separate theorem about products of injectives is unnecessary: the product homotopy is explicitly the tuple of the component homotopies, chosen over a set in the stated universes. I checked that conjugation along a DG layer isomorphism preserves the homotopy equation. The new node is strictly smaller and useful for nonacyclic M as well; it does not state the complete-filtration result.

Steps 3 and 4 use only Step 2, the supplied graded sections and explicit algebra, so they are elementary and cite no additional nodes. I checked the invariant, base case Q_0=0, and induction step. A graded section need not be a chain map, so the proof does not claim its lift is already a homotopy. Instead it computes the defect e, shows q(e)=0 and de=ed, factors it through the kernel L_p, then subtracts the defect by adding j_p k_p. Right A-linearity of d h+h d follows from cancellation of the Leibniz terms (-1)^(n-1)h(c)d_A(a) and (-1)^n h(c)d_A(a). Kernel inclusion and quotient are the actual DG-module maps. The correction preserves the previously chosen homotopy under q_p, which is the essential compatibility for the limit.

Step 5 uses the exact canonical limit isomorphism assumed in the item. Each tuple of homotopy values has degree n-1 and is compatible by Step 4. Linearity and the homotopy equation are checked under every quotient projection, and injectivity of the canonical limit map reflects the final equality. This is an explicit algebraic inverse-limit argument, not an appeal to exactness of inverse limit. It does not require convergence of a sum or stabilization in a cochain degree.

For the new 09K4 statement, I checked the signs directly against the source: a cocycle lambda on M^(-k) gives f(m)(a)=lambda(ma) for homogeneous m and a of the complementary degree. Associativity gives A-linearity. The shifted target differential has sign (-1)^(deg(m)+1), and the equation lambda d=0 gives the chain-map equation by the DG Leibniz rule. Evaluation at 1 is inverse to that formula, since d_A(1)=0. A dual boundary d(mu)=(-1)^k mu d corresponds to the homotopy h(m)(a)=(-1)^k mu(ma): its d_A(a) terms cancel, and d h+h d has the required lambda(ma). Conversely evaluation of a homotopy gives a dual boundary after the same sign. These calculations are review notes, not proof material inserted into the statement-only node. Naturality is precomposition, exactly as in 09K4.

I tested zero algebra/module, zero target, empty layer products, zero layers, a filtration constant after one layer, positive and negative shifts, and A=Z in degree zero with M a single abelian group in any degree. The latter gives Hom_Z(M,Q/Z) exactly when the degrees match. The character-dual argument was also checked on the exact complex 0 -> Z -> Q -> Q/Z -> 0, where acyclicity does not give a contracting homotopy before dualizing; the extension step, rather than a false splitting assumption on C, is what is used. For an infinite tower there is no finite-stage stabilization assumption. The DG Leibniz sign calculations do not use commutativity. I found no counterexample or missing hypothesis.
