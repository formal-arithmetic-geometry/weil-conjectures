Step 1 reads the statements of lem-exc-quasi-excellent-nagata, def-exc-excellent-ring, def-weil-pbc-g-ring and lem-weil-pbc-regular-ring-normal. They respectively supply Nagata of Q, the G-ring part of quasi-excellence, geometrically regular Noetherian formal fibres at every prime localization, and normality of each regular ring. Thus every fibre after a finite purely inseparable extension has precisely the normality demanded by N(Q). No statement about fibres of A is assumed at this step. Step 2 reads the whole statement of lem-per-nagata-normal-formal-fibres-lifting. I corrected the prospective wording to emphasize that no lifting theorem is applied to A_p as a complete pair. Step 3 reads all five cited statements in full. lem-weil-pbc-localization-noetherian applies with the multiplicative set A minus p; lem-weil-pbc-localization-normal-domain applies because A is a normal domain and 0 is not in that set. The node's conclusion is normality as a ring, not yet a normal domain. All five hypotheses are explicitly matched in the proof.

Step 5 uses only def-weil-pbc-normal-ring and elementary localization. In a local ring every denominator outside its maximal ideal is a unit, and c/s maps back to c times the inverse of s. Consequently localization at that ideal is the ring itself; the definition then gives a normal domain.
