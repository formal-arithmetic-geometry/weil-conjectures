## Review by Q05

I checked B–E with the item and cited statements open. The item's first paragraph requires an ordinary Fun(BD,M_R), whereas the existing injective infrastructure is phrased for internal actions by C(S,D). Treating these as the same category without an argument would be C4. I corrected this by extracting the general discrete-action comparison as the sole new node. Its statement introduces its ring, group, universe, site, categories and both directions; it contains no proof or proof dependencies. Its proposed proof belongs here: continuity to a discrete group makes a map from compact S have finite image and clopen fibers. Glue rho(a)(m|S_a) across that cover. Restriction pulls back the fiber cover; uniqueness of gluing proves naturality, addition and scalar linearity. Intersecting the finite fiber covers for g and h proves (gh)m=g(hm) by the ordinary group law on each intersection. Constant maps recover rho, and gluing uniqueness shows restriction and extension are inverse on objects and maps. This is a comparison with its own general hypotheses and descent argument, rather than an unexplained identification hidden in a proof step. The exact external reference is requested above.
