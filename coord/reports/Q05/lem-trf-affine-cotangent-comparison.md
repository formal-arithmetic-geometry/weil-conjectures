### Original step 1

First record the elementary free-algebra comparison used below. If $F\dashv G$ is a geometric inverse image and $R$ a ring sheaf, give $GQ$ its $R$-algebra structure through the adjunction unit for any $FR$-algebra $Q$. The set-adjunction restricts to algebra homomorphisms: the algebra equations on an adjunct are exactly the adjuncts of the original equations, since $F$ preserves finite products and the unit is natural. Consequently
$\operatorname{Hom}_{FR\text{-alg}}(F(R[E]),Q)=\operatorname{Hom}_{R\text{-alg}}(R[E],GQ)=\operatorname{Hom}(E,GQ)=\operatorname{Hom}(FE,Q)$.
Thus $F(R[E])\cong(FR)[FE]$ by the free-algebra universal property, with the comparison sending the coefficient and generator maps to their inverse images. Evaluation and the free-algebra unit are preserved because both sides send each generator to that same section. This applies also when the source topos is a presheaf category.

Uses: def-trf-geometric-morphism

### Original step 2

Let $a$ be sheafification from presheaves on the open-set site of $T$ to sheaves. It is a left adjoint preserving finite limits by [lem-weil-pbc-sheafification-sets-exact], hence is the inverse image of a geometric morphism. Form the standard resolution sectionwise: $P_n(V)=(F_{\mathcal A(V)}U)^{n+1}\mathcal B(V)$. Its restriction maps and all simplicial maps are the standard functorial maps of [lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence]. Iterating step 1 identifies $aP_\bullet$ with $\mathcal P_\bullet$, including evaluation faces, unit degeneracies and augmentation. Apply [lem-trf-topos-inverse-image-differentials] to $a$ and then [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-module-pullback-tensor] with identity coefficient map to obtain, degree by degree,
$a(\Omega_{P_n/\mathcal A}\otimes_{P_n}\mathcal B)\cong\Omega_{\mathcal P_n/\mathcal A}\otimes_{\mathcal P_n}\mathcal B$.
Here on the presheaf side differentials and tensors are sectionwise; on the sheaf side they are their sheafifications. All comparisons commute with faces, so they commute with their alternating sums. This proves the asserted sheafification identity as complexes. Its presheaf-to-sheaf unit evaluated at $V$ is the asserted section map, and its naturality in $V$ proves restriction compatibility.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-sheafification-sets-exact, lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence, lem-trf-topos-inverse-image-differentials, lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-module-pullback-tensor

Step 1 identifies $u^{-1}\mathcal P_\bullet$ with the standard resolution of $u^{-1}\mathcal B$ over $u^{-1}\mathcal A$, including every face and degeneracy. The differential comparison [lem-trf-topos-inverse-image-differentials] and the tensor comparison [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-module-pullback-tensor], with coefficient map the identity, now identify the simplicial modules defining the two cotangent complexes. Taking the same alternating sum of faces gives a canonical isomorphism of complexes $u^{-1}L_{\mathcal B/\mathcal A}\cong L_{u^{-1}\mathcal B/u^{-1}\mathcal A}$. Exactness ensures that this is also the asserted identification in the derived categories.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-left-exact-site-inverse-image-tensor, lem-trf-topos-inverse-image-differentials, lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-module-pullback-tensor

### Original step 4

These comparisons are natural in ring maps and coherent for composites. Indeed the free-algebra comparison is determined by its coefficient map and by $[b]\mapsto[Fb]$ on generators. The differential comparison carries $\mathrm db$ to $\mathrm d(Fb)$, and the tensor comparison carries a pure tensor to the tensor of its images. For two successive inverse images, both candidate comparisons therefore have the same values on coefficients, generators, and the generating expressions $\mathrm dp\otimes b$ in each degree. The universal properties of free algebras, differentials and tensors force equality. The identity functor gives the identity by the same calculation. The calculation also commutes with the standard maps for squares of rings, which compose strictly by [lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence].

Uses: lem-trf-topos-inverse-image-differentials, lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence

### Original step 5

Take the continuous inclusion $i:\{t\}\to T$. By [lem-weil-pbc-topological-inverse-image-stalk], evaluation of $i^{-1}\mathcal F$ on the singleton is canonically $\mathcal F_t$, with the section $s$ mapped to its germ $s_t$. On a singleton a sheaf is determined by this value, its value on the empty open being terminal. Hence step 3 for $i$ is precisely $(L_{\mathcal B/\mathcal A})_t\cong L_{\mathcal B_t/\mathcal A_t}$. This identifies the actual complexes and their ring actions. Stalk exactness [lem-weil-pbc-module-stalk-exact] also permits passage to derived objects. The germ formula and step 4 show naturality in sheaf-ring maps. Restricting first to an open neighbourhood of $t$ gives the same germs, so the identification is compatible with open restrictions.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-topological-inverse-image-stalk, lem-weil-pbc-module-stalk-exact

### Original step 6

Put $C=W^{-1}B$ and $D=S^{-1}A$. The hypothesis on $S$ gives the unique map $D\to C$, $a/s\mapsto f(a)/f(s)$. By [lem-weil-pbc-localization-flat], $C$ is flat over $B$. By [lem-weil-pbc-flat-tensor-exact], tensoring the sequences of cycles and boundaries of any acyclic complex by $C$ preserves their exactness. Thus $C[0]$ is K-flat by [def-pc-module-kflat-complex]. The ordinary-module K-flat tensor computation [lem-pc-source-kflat-module-tensor-model], using the symmetry of ordinary tensor, consequently identifies $K\otimes_B^{\mathbf L}C$ with the termwise complex $K\otimes_B C$ for every $K$. In particular this applies to $K=L_{B/A}$.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-localization-flat, lem-weil-pbc-flat-tensor-exact, def-pc-module-kflat-complex, lem-pc-source-kflat-module-tensor-model

### Original step 7

Apply the transitivity triangle [lem-trf-cotangent-transitivity-triangle] to $A\to B\to C$. The third term $L_{C/B}$ is zero by [lem-trf-localization-cotangent-vanishing]. By [lem-kun-distinguished-cone-zero], its first arrow is an isomorphism $L_{B/A}\otimes_B^{\mathbf L}C\xrightarrow{\sim}L_{C/A}$. It is the arrow induced by the standard ring functoriality, as specified in the transitivity statement. Use step 6 to replace the source by the ordinary localized complex.

Uses: lem-trf-cotangent-transitivity-triangle, lem-trf-localization-cotangent-vanishing, lem-kun-distinguished-cone-zero

### Original step 8

Apply the transitivity triangle [lem-trf-cotangent-transitivity-triangle] to $A\to D\to C$. By [lem-trf-localization-cotangent-vanishing], $L_{D/A}=0$. Its derived tensor with $C$ is zero as well, since it can be computed using the zero K-flat representative. Rotate the triangle: the third term is now this zero object shifted once. The zero-cone criterion [lem-kun-distinguished-cone-zero] gives an isomorphism $L_{C/A}\xrightarrow{\sim}L_{C/D}$. Compose it with step 7. The result is the canonical map $L_{B/A}\otimes_B C\to L_{C/D}$: both arrows come from the corresponding squares of ring maps, and [lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence] says their composite is the standard map for $A\to D$, $B\to C$. This proves the localization assertion.

Uses: lem-trf-cotangent-transitivity-triangle, lem-trf-localization-cotangent-vanishing, lem-kun-distinguished-cone-zero, lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence, def-pc-module-kflat-complex, lem-pc-source-kflat-module-tensor-model

### Original step 9

For successive localizations $B\to C\to C'$ and the matching successive localizations of $A$, extend the two comparison maps to $C'$. The tensor associativity identification sends $(x\otimes c)\otimes c'$ to $x\otimes cc'$. On the standard simplicial resolution, both the resulting composite and the direct comparison are induced by the same composite square of ring maps. Their equality is the strict composition assertion of [lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence], degreewise on $\mathrm dp\otimes b$. Hence they give the same map after localization of complexes. These are the quasi-isomorphisms proved in steps 7--8, so this equality is also the required compatibility of the localization identifications in derived categories.

Uses: lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence
