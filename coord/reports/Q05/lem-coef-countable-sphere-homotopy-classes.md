Its statement gives the actual Ex construction, the sequential colimit, Kan fibrancy and weak equivalence of the unit; these are exactly what the step takes. It does not assert countability, which the step proves: a finite poset has only finitely many strict chains, every weak chain is obtained by repeating entries in one of those chains, and a natural transformation is determined by the strict-chain values. This is a finite tuple of simplices, not an arbitrary countable product. Countability is then preserved by each Ex, induction in the iteration number, and the quotient of a countable union defining the colimit. Only the definition is used for counting here: factor a monotone map into its surjection to its image followed by the ordered-image injection. This proves, rather than assumes as an uncited theorem, that a map out of the boundary is determined by finitely many proper faces. All target values belong to the countable degreewise sets proved in step 1. No additional topology assertion is hidden in this elementary step.

For step 3 I opened the full statement of `lem-cbc-kan-realization-mapping-classes` and checked Kerodon 0148 and 0149.
