## Arguments preserved for the new obligations

These paragraphs are handoff evidence, not proof steps in the new node files.

For global reduction control, use the finite free resolution `[A --varpi^n--> A]` of `P_n` in degrees `[-1,0]` from standard-quotients-perfect. Completed-constant-sheaf-reduction identifies its completed extension with a two-term flat resolution of `R_n`, also on each covering slice. Independent-bounded-flat-kflat allows this resolution to compute tensor with the possibly nonflat local complex L. Its total complex is zero outside `[d0-1,d1]`. Exact restriction and noetherian-stratum-detection carry the local cohomological vanishing to T. A descending closed chain of |T| stabilizes on each member of a finite Noetherian affine open cover, hence globally. Every subset is then quasi-compact by noetherian-space-compact-subsets; every open is retrocompact. A locally closed stratum is a difference of two such opens and so constructible by constructible-subset. The local finite-lisse cohomology supplied by the uniform tower descends along the given covers to finite-lisse cohomology on the strata (combine their covering sieves); exact restriction identifies it with the restriction of H^q(M_n). These same constructible strata give the intrinsic-constructibility predicate. No flatness of L, no perfect integral L, and no etale witnesses enter this argument.

For generic tower lifting, exactness gives H^q(DF_n C)=F_n(H^q C), and faithfulness reflects zero objects: if F(X)=0 then F(id_X)=0, hence id_X=0. Thus DF_n reflects every lower and upper bound. Apply this first to alpha_n to bound L_n and then to `C_mn=L_m tensor^L R_n`, whose image is identified with M_n by the canonical scalar comparison followed by alpha_m and rho_mn. This puts every needed source in D+ before full faithfulness is used; infinite Tor dimension of R_m -> R_n is not assumed away. The target L_n is in D+ as well. Fullness lifts the displayed conjugated normalization arrow and its inverse; faithfulness proves both inverse identities and uniqueness. For equal indices, the canonical scalar unit comparison and rho_nn identify the lift with the unit. For three indices the inverse-image coefficient comparison commutes with scalar associativity: on K-flat models both balanced maps send a tensor `((x tensor r) tensor s)` to the same tensor, since the supplied coefficient isomorphisms commute with the ring maps; these maps commute with differentials and descend through quasi-isomorphisms. Substitution of the two lift equations and the rho cocycle therefore makes the images of the two beta composites identical. The iterated source is canonically `L_k tensor^L R_n`, already in D+ by reflection. Faithfulness proves the full cocycle. This is a separate argument about derived comparison and coherence, not just an application of preimageIso.
