Step 1 uses `lem-sc-divisor-module-local` for the actual local generator t^(q n_P), not merely the definition of a divisor. It uses `lem-pc-tensor-dual-invertible` for tensor invertibility, `def-sc-rational-section-divisor` for the normalized order in a local basis, and `def-sc-ringed-space-module-cohomology` for H^0. The local membership test identifies the imposed vanishing subsheaf and includes the zero section; the nonzero-rational-section convention is never applied to zero. At the generic point the divisor is absent and the twist is the original line bundle, so the closed-point description covers the sheaf everywhere.

Step 2 uses the statement of `lem-trf-curve-degree-tensor-additive` on a proper scheme of dimension one. `lem-pc-degree-agrees-sc` is used only for the effective divisor qB, exactly within its scope; negative twists are handled using `lem-sc-divisor-module-tensor-add` and tensor additivity, not by incorrectly applying effective-divisor degree agreement to a negative divisor. `lem-sc-divisor-module-local` supplies O(0)=O and `def-pc-degree-line-bundle` makes its degree zero. The induction has an explicit zero base and successor calculation. No characteristic or positivity condition on A is used.

Step 3 uses `lem-trf-proper-field-invertible-coherent` to justify coherence before applying `lem-pc-coherent-hi`. The base is Spec k with k Noetherian; properness is already a hypothesis. Thus this really gives finite-dimensional H^0 for the proper curve, without importing a theorem that assumes projectivity.

Step 4 uses the integrality clause of `lem-sc-smooth-connected-curve-local-rings` before applying `lem-pc-nonzero-section-integral-cartier`. The latter explicitly gives the nonzerodivisor local coordinate, which the exact-sequence argument later needs. `lem-pc-zero-scheme-divisor-iso` gives L isomorphic to O(D), not an identification of noncanonical local bases. `lem-pc-cartier-divisor-finite-over-field` applies because D is now Cartier, C is proper and dim C=1. I did not take finiteness of D from the definition of a zero scheme.

Step 5 uses `lem-sfb-finite-trivialize-invertible-upstairs` with the actual finite map D to Spec k. Every neighborhood of the unique base point is the whole base, so its conclusion is a global trivialization, not just unrelated local frames. Restriction of a local free rank-one basis verifies that L|D is invertible. The effective-divisor clause of `lem-pc-degree-base-change` explicitly says dim Gamma(D,O_D)=deg O(D); its field-extension clause is not used. This supplies both the finite-dimensional target and its dimension d, including D empty.

Step 6 locally writes the three-term module sequence R --a--> Re --> (R/(a))e. The nonzerodivisor assertion from Step 4 proves injectivity and ordinary quotient arithmetic proves the remaining exactness. There is consequently no hidden general tensor-exactness theorem in this step. `lem-sc-global-sections-left-exact` preserves its kernel, while `lem-sc-global-functions` gives Gamma(C,O_C)=k under the exact smooth proper connected hypotheses here. The proof asserts no surjectivity onto restricted sections. Lifting a basis of the actual image and adjoining a basis of the one-dimensional kernel is the elementary dimension estimate. Step 7 then handles zero global-section space and negative d explicitly; it uses no additional theorem.

I tested B=0, q=0 and m=0: the twist is respectively absent or the tensor unit, and the displayed degree formula still holds. For m=q=0 it gives h^0(O_C)=1 and the bound is equality. On P^1 with A=O(e), the result becomes h^0(O(me-qb))=max(me-qb+1,0), so the estimate is sharp, including negative degree. A nontrivial degree-zero line bundle may have no section, which is allowed; a trivial one has a nowhere-vanishing section with D empty and d=0. Positive genus also imposes no problem because the proof uses h^0(O_C)=1, not a genus-zero equality. A disconnected counterexample O(1) disjoint-union O(-3) explains why connectedness is retained, not silently dropped. Every statement citation has the needed hypotheses, and no SGA source is used.
