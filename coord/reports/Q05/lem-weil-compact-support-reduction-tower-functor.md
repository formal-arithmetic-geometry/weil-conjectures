### lem-pur-ringed-derived-pushforward-linear (retained argument, not a delivered split)

1. On module sheaves the $A$-action on $u_*M$ is induced by $c$. Therefore $u_*$ sends multiplication by $s_B(r)$ to multiplication by $s_A(r)$: under the sheaf adjunction the two maps send a generalized element $x$ to $c(u^{-1}s_A(r))x=s_B(r)x$. The same holds degreewise on complexes. The induced scalar action on the homotopy and derived categories is the one carried by localization.

Uses: def-weil-pbc-ringed-topos-morphism, lem-weil-linear-abelian-derived-category.

2. Small ringed-site module categories admit K-injective resolutions. Take $K\to I$ such a resolution. The natural comparison $Q(u_*I)\xrightarrow{\sim}Ru_*(QI)$ computes the derived functor. Apply its naturality to the actual chain endomorphism $r\,\mathrm{id}_I$. The first step gives $Ru_*(r\,\mathrm{id}_{QI})=r\,\mathrm{id}_{Ru_*(QI)}$. Conjugate by the resolution isomorphism to obtain this at $K$; resolution comparisons need not have been chosen linearly. The K-injective-derived functor is triangulated, hence additive. For $a:K\to L$, functoriality and $ra=(r\,\mathrm{id}_L)\circ a$ now imply $Ru_*(ra)=rRu_*(a)$. This is the required Hom-module linearity.

Uses: prop-pur-kinjective-resolutions-exist, lem-weil-pbc-kinjective-total-derived, lem-pc-right-derived-functor-triangulated, lem-weil-linear-abelian-derived-category, def-weil-linear-functor.

### lem-pur-ringed-derived-pullback-linear (retained argument, not a delivered split)

1. Module pullback is $B\otimes_{u^{-1}A}u^{-1}(-)$. For each $r$, the identity $b\otimes s_A(r)x=s_B(r)b\otimes x$ proves that it carries the scalar endomorphism to the indicated scalar endomorphism. This equality holds on the presheaf tensors, hence on their sheafification and on complexes. The module-sheaf structures give $R$-linear abelian categories; their derived categories and localization functors have the indicated induced scalar actions.

Uses: def-weil-pbc-ringed-topos-morphism, def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product, lem-weil-linear-abelian-derived-category.

2. Choose a flat-term K-flat resolution $q:P\to K$. The canonical computation $Lu^*(K)\simeq Q(u^*P)$ is obtained from $Lu^*(Qq)^{-1}$ and the derived-pullback comparison. Naturality of that comparison at the actual chain map $r\,\mathrm{id}_P$ gives $Lu^*(r\,\mathrm{id}_{QP})=r\,\mathrm{id}_{Lu^*(QP)}$. Naturality of scalar multiplication with $Qq$ gives the same identity at $K$. Thus no linear choice of resolutions is required. Derived pullback is additive since it is triangulated. For any $a:K\to L$, apply the scalar-identity formula to $ra=(r\,\mathrm{id}_L)\circ a$ to get $Lu^*(ra)=rLu^*(a)$; additivity supplies the other linearity equation.
