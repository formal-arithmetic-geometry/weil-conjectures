## Reconstruction for the new unsplit algebra node

For polynomial E, or the nodal model with a!=0, E is a normal domain (polynomial normality and the already existing binomial Laurent normal-form theorem). An etale algebra C has finitely many normal-domain factors, by the existing lem-trf-etale-normal-domain-finite-factors, whose source is 030C and etale normal ascent. Every nonzero factor is A-torsion-free by etale/model flatness, so its generic localization is a nonzero domain. Thus a chosen generic component subset is exactly a chosen subset of these clopen factors; locally the two contracted ideals are 0 and 1 in one order.

For a=0, put C_u=C/(u) and C_v=C/(v); both are etale over the normal polynomial domains A[v] and A[u]. Their domain factors are finite and clopen. At any chosen prime q one may shrink a principal neighbourhood to retain only the factor passing through q on each axis, if that axis passes through q, and to remove every other factor. If an axis does not pass through q, make its coordinate invertible. Each retained domain factor has a nonzero integral generic localization. Hence on each retained axis the chosen subset selects all or none. The four possibilities select neither, both, just the u-axis, or just the v-axis. The injection C→C_u×C_v and intersection uC∩vC=0 follow by tensoring the corresponding exact sequence for A[u,v]/(uv) with the flat etale algebra C. Contracting the chosen generic minimal-prime intersection now gives respectively 1, 0, (u), or (v). Applying the same argument to the complementary subset gives the ordered pairs in the statement. All quotients involved are reduced (etale over reduced models), so these ideals are radical. The principal neighbourhoods make them locally finitely generated. This argument uses no separable-closedness, valuation ordering or finiteness of the base prime chain; those occur only in the item's etale-base reduction. This reconstruction is retained here for the next worker; the node itself is statement only.
