## Review by Q05

I reviewed the item and the new leaf against the sources above with every cited statement open. The item is exactly the affine-first-order unique-lifting version of the converse of 02HM. In particular it needs locally finite presentation, not finite type; it imposes neither Noetherianity, separation, quasi-compactness, nor a choice of base field. The leaf is exactly the pinned Mathlib equivalence, with R and A arbitrary commutative rings and all R-algebra tests. It introduces D, I, u, v and q itself and cites the definitions of both modules. As a Mathlib leaf it is statement-only with empty proof/proof_deps. Its source includes the fixed Stacks tag/label and the exact Mathlib declaration and lines.

Step 1 uses def-pc-scheme for the scheme/affine-open language and the standard affine basis, then lem-weil-pbc-lfp-affine-opens-finite-presentation for the actual non-elementary conclusion: every affine pair U,V with c(U) inside V has a finitely presented section ring map. The existing statement says exactly this for arbitrary schemes; c satisfies its locally finite presentation hypothesis. There is no claim that intersections of affine opens are affine: U is chosen as an affine neighbourhood inside the open inverse image of V.

Step 2 uses def-sbc-first-order-thickening, whose statement explicitly proves equality of underlying spaces in the square-zero case, and def-weil-open-immersion, whose statement identifies U with the open subscheme carrying the restricted structure sheaf. The underlying image containment and restricted continuous/sheaf maps are written out. This is why factorization through U is valid here; there is no attempted factorization through an arbitrary closed immersion based only on topological image. Uniqueness is inherited from the hypothesis and equality of the restricted sheaf maps. This is a short argument from the cited definitions, marked elementary, agreeing with 02HK.

Step 3 uses def-weil-closed-immersion for the affine quotient closed immersion, def-sbc-first-order-thickening for its square-zero interpretation, and def-weil-affine-spectrum for the contravariant functorial and bijective correspondence on ring maps. That last node really does say that every map between spectra comes from exactly one ring map; it is not just the construction of Spec. Thus the lift into U corresponds to v:A to D, and commutative triangles become q composed with v equals u and R-linearity. These elementary deductions realize 02HL. All test schemes remain affine, exactly as required by the item's hypothesis.

Step 4 uses the full statement of the new leaf to get both module vanishings. It then uses lem-weil-pbc-etale-ring-map-iff-etale-affine, whose statement explicitly lists finite presentation, H_1=0, and Omega=0 as the three conditions equivalent to etaleness of Spec A to Spec R. Finite presentation comes from step 1, not from the lifting property alone. Thus this cited comparison says exactly what this step uses; neither a conormal-presentation theorem nor a mere definition of formal smoothness is being used as a substitute.

Step 5 uses the explicit affine-local formulation recorded in the statement of def-weil-etale-morphism: etale means covered by affine opens whose section-ring maps are etale (Stacks 02GI). We have proved this at each point, so this final application is elementary. The reference is present in that definition node's statement; I am not taking an unrecorded theorem from its proof. Step 4's comparison already supplies the ring etaleness in this formulation. Each uses entry has a verbatim quote in citations.json, and proof_deps is exactly their union. No step exceeds 2000 characters or contains a separately asserted standard comparison.

Small cases checked: W empty makes the pointwise conclusion vacuous; a zero target ring forces the compatible algebras and test maps to be the zero-ring ones, with unique lifts.
