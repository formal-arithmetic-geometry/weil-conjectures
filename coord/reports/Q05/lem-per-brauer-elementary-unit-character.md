My independent check is: every eigenvalue of a finite-order operator is an Nth root of unity in K and is integral. For the induction-formula node I read the local Induced.lean construction/adjunction and the existing induction-calculus statement, and checked the convention against quotient induction. In the quotient model a basis indexed by left cosets, each with a copy of W, makes the trace a sum over fixed cosets. Each fixed coset contributes chi_W(x^(-1) g x). Summing over its |H| representatives gives the first formula; each element of H intersect g^G has |C_G(g)| conjugators, giving the second. Induction/transitivity or reciprocity alone do not state this formula. H=G yields chi_W, H=1 yields the regular-character formula and W=0 yields zero.
