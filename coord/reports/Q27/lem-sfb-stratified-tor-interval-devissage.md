## Source error

The blueprint's Strata paragraph says to choose an open original stratum with complementary union of strata. A finite locally closed constructible partition need not have an open member. For example take the disjoint union of two affine lines over a field. Let S1 be the punctured first line together with the origin of the second, and S2 the origin of the first together with the punctured second. Each is a constructible locally closed subscheme (closed in the open consisting of its punctured component and the whole other component); they partition X, but neither is open in X. Thus that choice is false even for a Noetherian qcqs scheme. The replacement refines by actual complementary open/closed scheme pullbacks using `lem-fix-constructible-open-closed-refinement`. This changes the node-level proof only; the unit has no proof steps, and the item's statement was already formulated without an open-stratum assumption. There is no statement dispute.
