### Original step 1

Retained in assembly step 1 (finite-free/K-flat reduction).

Choose an $E$-basis of $W$, of size $d\geq0$. Its coordinate maps and their inverse are continuous by [lem-sfb-finite-dimensional-linear-map-continuous], since $E$ is a complete nontrivially normed field and $W$ has its finite-dimensional norm topology. Hence $\underline W\cong A^d$. A finite free module in degree zero is K-flat by [def-pc-kflat-complex]: tensoring an acyclic complex with $A^d$ is a finite direct sum of that complex and is acyclic. Consequently $R\otimes_A^L\underline W$ is represented by the actual action module $N_R=R^d$ in degree zero, for $R=C,B$. Its action is the matrix $\rho(g)\in\mathrm{GL}_d(E)$ extended to $R$; its coefficient map is entrywise $c$. These basis choices are used only to prove invertibility of the specified morphism. For $d=0$ both sides and the comparison are zero.

Original uses: lem-sfb-finite-dimensional-linear-map-continuous, def-pc-kflat-complex.

### Original step 6

Allocated to the cochain quasi-isomorphism node (injectivity, continuous finite-field primitive).

Let $n\geq1$ and let $z\in C^n_{\mathrm{fin}}(G,V_\Omega)$ be a cocycle which is $db$ for a continuous $V_\Omega$-valued $(n-1)$-cochain. Choose $F\supseteq E$ with $z(G^n)\subseteq V_F$. By [lem-sfb-finite-subfield-continuous-linear-retraction] choose a continuous $E$-linear $p_F:\Omega\to F$ restricting to the identity on $F$. Apply it coordinatewise to obtain $p:V_\Omega\to V_F$. Every $\rho(g)$ has entries in $E$, so $p(\rho(g)v)=\rho(g)p(v)$. Consequently $p$ commutes with every summand of the displayed differential, and $d(pb)=p(db)=pz=z$. The cochain $pb$ is continuous and takes all its values in this one $V_F$. Hence $z$ is already a boundary in $C^\bullet_{\mathrm{fin}}$, proving injectivity of $H^n(i)$. For $n=1$, $b$ is a single vector and the same argument applies.

Original uses: lem-sfb-finite-subfield-continuous-linear-retraction.

### Original step 7

Allocated to the cochain quasi-isomorphism node (surjectivity, descent and transfer).

Let $n\geq1$ and let $z\in C^n_{\mathrm{cts}}(G,V_\Omega)$ be a cocycle. Apply [lem-sfb-local-norm-algcl-cocycle-descent] to obtain an open $H$, a finite $F\supseteq E$, and $u\in Z^n_{\mathrm{cts}}(H,V_F)$ with $[u]=\mathrm{res}[z]$ after inclusion. Put $m=[G:H]$, a positive finite integer. Apply [lem-sfb-continuous-cochain-finite-index-transfer] simultaneously to $V_F\hookrightarrow V_\Omega$, using the same coset representatives. The continuous cochain $\mathrm{cor}(u)$ takes values in $V_F$; its class therefore comes from $C^\bullet_{\mathrm{fin}}(G,V_\Omega)$. Naturality and the stated homotopy give
$$i_*[\mathrm{cor}(u)]=\mathrm{cor}\,\mathrm{res}[z]=m[z].$$
Since $E$ has characteristic zero, $m^{-1}\in E\subseteq F$. The finite-field cocycle $m^{-1}\mathrm{cor}(u)$ maps to $[z]$. This proves surjectivity of $H^n(i)$ without any bound on $n$ or any restriction on $G$.
