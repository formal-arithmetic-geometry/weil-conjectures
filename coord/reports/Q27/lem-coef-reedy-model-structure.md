## Source error

The online rendering of Remark 5.2.3 labels its two factors in the opposite order to Definition 5.2.1 and to its own displayed matching-to-latching composite. The construction must use an inverse map followed by a direct map, exactly as the item already states. I used that convention consistently. Definition 5.2.1 also calls the Reedy degree a functor from B to an ordinal; it must be a function on objects for a general Reedy category. For example Delta has maps [1] -> [0] and [0] -> [1], so an ordinal-valued functor could not assign degrees 1 and 0. The item's object function is already correct. In addition, the full degree subcategory is not generally discrete at its top degree: mixed nonidentity arrows can join objects of equal degree. Step 2 defines them through the unique lower intermediate object and checks their composition, rather than asserting that no such arrows exist.
