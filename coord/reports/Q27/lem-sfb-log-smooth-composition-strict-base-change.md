For arbitrary fine test schemes, the formula $[z,u]\mapsto a^\flat(z)u$ directly verifies the relations, uniqueness and overlap agreement; it does not apply the locally Noetherian associated-log lemma to a non-Noetherian test scheme. In particular the proof does not assert that an arbitrary product of locally Noetherian schemes is locally Noetherian: finite presentation of $f$ is essential at this point.
