## Mathematical construction behind the new lemma

This is a bounded construction for a single extension, not the item's Ext-detection assertion or its complex version. Choose an infinite cardinal mu dominating all arrows of the small site and all sections of R. Let kappa >= mu with kappa^mu = kappa. Pull back B -> C along C_0 -> C; this is an epimorphism with kernel A. For every section of C_0 choose a covering and lifts. Covering sieves have at most mu arrows; altogether there are at most kappa chosen lifts. Generate their R-subpremodule D of the pullback. It has size at most kappa and D -> C_0 is locally onto. Its kernel K is a subpremodule of A with size at most kappa.

Apply the pure subpremodule enlargement of Proposition 2.5.31 to contain K, obtaining a subpremodule H of A of size at most kappa, sectionwise pure. For a seed at all site objects rather than one, the same construction starts from the whole subpremodule K: visit every object, enlarge its current sections to a pure submodule using the twisted endomorphism ring, close under restrictions and scalar operations, and repeat the sweep countably often. There are at most mu objects, each operation uses at most kappa elements, so the union still has size at most kappa. Each sectionwise inclusion becomes pure in the filtered union. This avoids assuming the presheaf of sections of A is sectionwise flat.

The subpremodule D+H of B maps locally onto C_0 and its kernel is exactly H: q(d+h)=0 forces d in K contained in H. Sheafify. Exactness of sheafification gives a short exact row with A_0=H^# and B_0=(D+H)^#. Lemma 2.5.12 makes A_0 -> A tensor-pure. Since A is tensor-flat, Lemma 2.5.25 gives both A_0 and A/A_0 flat. All sheafifications embed into their ambient sheaves.

The cardinal bound does not grow at successive downward steps. A separated subpremodule with at most kappa sections has at most 2^mu choices of covering sieve and at most kappa^mu matching families per sieve. Its sheafification therefore has size at most mu times 2^mu times kappa^mu = kappa. A subpremodule of a sheaf is separated, so this estimate applies to H and D+H. Thus |B_0| <= kappa, exactly as stated. No points and no global lifts of sections are used. A suitable kappa always exists: if nu >= mu is infinite, kappa=2^nu satisfies kappa^mu=kappa. The report records this reasoning for review; the new node itself contains only its self-contained statement.

## Source error

In the proof of Proposition 2.5.37 the reference to Lemma 2.5.26 is made for enlarging a subpremodule in a cycle sheaf. That numbered lemma is for a module over a single ring. The applicable ringed-category enlargement is Proposition 2.5.31 (built from Lemmas 2.5.26 and 2.5.30). The finite-row formulation above uses the latter and keeps local epimorphisms distinct from sectionwise surjections. This is a source-reference correction, not a dispute of the item's statement.


A second source error concerns cardinal bounds: the proof of Corollary 1.4.7 bounds a product of at most mu sets of size at most i by mu times i. That arithmetic is false in general. For a concrete failure of its general estimate, take the countable site consisting of a terminal object, countably many disjoint singleton objects covering it, and the empty object. Put an abelian group of cardinal beth_omega at each singleton and zero at the terminal and empty objects. This separated presheaf has size beth_omega, while its plus construction at the terminal object has cardinal beth_omega^aleph_0 > beth_omega (Koenig). The claimed bound 2^aleph_0 times beth_omega is only beth_omega. Our finite-row statement and proof deliberately use kappa^mu = kappa instead: matching families have size at most kappa^mu, there are at most 2^mu sieves, and the whole sheafification has size at most mu times 2^mu times kappa^mu = kappa. Thus this source error does not dispute or change the item or the new lemma.
