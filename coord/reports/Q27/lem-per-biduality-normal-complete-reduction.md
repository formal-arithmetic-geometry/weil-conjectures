1. Dimension functions: subtract their immediate-specialization equations along chains on each irreducible component. The differences agree where components meet. There are finitely many components; after removing those not containing a given point, the difference is constant near it. Thus the change is locally constant. lem-per-potential-dualizing-uniqueness gives uniqueness for the same pinnings/dimension function after twist-shift; lem-asd-finite-etale-twist-line gives the rank-one line (use its dual for negative powers). lem-sf-etale-twisted-bidual-transport states both cancellation in the double dual and compatibility with the evaluation transpose. Its morphism is the identity here, so the two Hom comparison hypotheses are identities. Pinning uniqueness glues the local identifications. This proves the statement's extra independence clause, not just invariance of vanishing.

2. Closure: lem-weil-heart-short-exact-triangles supplies the triangle of a short exact sequence in the ordinary sheaf heart. lem-per-relative-bidual-triangles explicitly states contravariant triangulatedness and naturality of the evaluation transpose, including connecting maps; lem-sf-derived-homology-long-exact supplies the exact cohomology segment. For q>1 its preceding term is zero by (ii); for q=1 its preceding arrow is the epimorphism B to C by the assumed degree-zero natural isomorphisms. Hence 0 to T(A) to T(B) to T(C) is exact. Subobjects, extensions and summands now require only elementary exactness/additivity. This does not claim closure under quotients.

3. Finite maps: lem-per-potential-finite-bidual-comparison states exactly p_*T_Y(N) = T_X(p_*N), exactness/conservativity and the inherited pinned p^!K. Preservation of bounded constructibility is a standing hypothesis on both schemes. lem-exc-finite-type-localization-excellent makes finite sources excellent, hence Noetherian by the definition of excellence. lem-weil-pbc-integral-incomparability shows strict prime chains contract strictly, giving the required dimension bound. Thus step 2 is applied only within dimension at most d.

4. Devissage: lem-per-constructible-finite-open-constant-generation is explicitly for a finite coefficient field, has integral finite sources, and asserts finitely many extensions, finite sums and summands. Apply it to F_ell sheaves viewed as Lambda modules, using step 2's closures. lem-per-excellent-normalization-preparation applies because these integral sources are reduced and Noetherian excellent; it supplies finite surjective normalization with normal excellent source. The injection F_ell into Lambda and lem-weil-pbc-open-closed-short-exact embed j!F_ell in Lambda_Y. The surjective-unit clause of lem-per-potential-finite-bidual-comparison embeds Lambda_Y in nu_*Lambda_{Y^nu}. Step 3 and closure under subobjects therefore apply. Finally the ell-power filtration has finite locally constant factors after restriction to each constructibility stratum and an etale trivialization.
