The inverse-image-open formulation of modification agrees with the
isomorphic-dense-opens formulation in the node. In one direction it directly
supplies those opens. Conversely, for a proper map and an isomorphism V → U
between schematically dense opens, V defines a section of the separated proper
map over U. Its image is both open and closed in the inverse image of U;
schematic density excludes any complementary open subscheme. Thus V is the
whole inverse image of U. This does not add a birational-integral or Noetherian
hypothesis.
