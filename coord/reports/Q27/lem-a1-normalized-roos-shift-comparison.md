Write $P_X=\prod_nX_n$, $h_X=1-s_X$ and $a=(-1)^r$. Termwise products identify $P_{X[r]}=P_X[r]$ and $h_{X[r]}=h_X[r]$. Applying $(y,x)\mapsto(a y,x)$ before or after these differentials gives $(-d y-a h_Xx,a d x)$ in either order, since $a^2=1$. Thus the displayed map is a chain map and its inverse is the same coordinate map.
