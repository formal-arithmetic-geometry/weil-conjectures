  structure of an additive commutative group is used. Module.Free is the
  dependency's notion of having a basis; Module.Finite makes that basis finite.
- An arbitrary abelian B, subgroup D, and homomorphism φ are
  `(B : Type v) [AddCommGroup B] (D : AddSubgroup B) (φ : L →+ B)`.
  The universes of L and B are independent. No injectivity of φ is assumed.
- D is n-divisible means `hD : ∀ d : D, ∃ e : D, n • e = d`;
  elements of the subgroup are used, so the preimage remains inside D.

- The cokernel is killed by n exactly when

  Choosing G in L's universe loses no case: the source's construction realizes

- Rank-zero L: the finite torsion-free quotient B/D must be zero, and rank-zero
