## Problems found and corrections

The imported item had no proof. Its source already warns correctly that 00T7 and 00ON alone do not give the tangent-cone comparison. Neither is misrepresented here as supplying it. I separated the genuinely additional complete-ring structure assertion and wrote the finite-jet calculation in the item. I did not turn the two clauses of the item into two new nodes.

The distinction between local-ring dimension s and local topological fibre dimension r is essential: r=s+trdeg_K L. For example Q -> Q(t) has s=0 and r=1 in the one-dimensional smooth model, so identifying r with s would be false. The proof retains the transcendence correction until the last subtraction.

I also avoided applying flat dimension addition to B/q: B/q need not be flat over A/p simply because B is flat over A. Instead the domain dimension identities are applied to their finite type Q-models, and smooth local constancy computes the transcendence degree of each generic component. No minimal-prime bijection or residue-field equality is introduced. The statement needs no correction; there is no STATEMENT-DISPUTED issue.

A locally constant function has the same value along a specialization pair, since every open neighbourhood of the specialization contains its generization. Applying 00P1 to the finite type K-fibre gives exactly r=s+t; no closed-point assumption is used.
7. The going-down node states precisely the lifting below a prescribed prime that proves contraction preserves minimality. 8. Every prime below Q is also below N and hence survives localization at N. Therefore minimality of q really implies minimality of Q in P, and then in the fibre at p. Applying it to the two specified towers and substituting steps 5, 6 and 8 gives the dimension shift.
