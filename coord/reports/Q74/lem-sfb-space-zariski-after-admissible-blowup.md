## Source error and corrections

Stacks 087F omits separatedness in its enumerated hypotheses although its final invocation of 0879 requires it. The scheme version 081S states it. The inherited item had already corrected this and included the doubled-origin counterexample; I retained that correction and the explicit absence of density, properness and finite-presentation assumptions. For X equal to the doubled-origin affine line over T=A^1_k and U=D(t), every admissible finite-type ideal on T is a nonzero principal ideal, hence invertible, and remains invertible on X. The blowups are identities and the map is not injective. This checks that the missing source hypothesis cannot be ignored. No further change of mathematical statement is made here.

I normalized doubled backslashes in the copied item, without editing any upstream dependency. I expanded the source's casual use of successive blowups into a support-controlled composition result. It is insufficient to cite merely the existing assertion that the base composite is some admissible blowup: 0868 explicitly requires the union-of-supports formula. I also avoided treating the map between arbitrary compatible etale charts of an isomorphism as an isomorphism; identity charts after identifying the two spaces witness the flat and locally finitely presented identity map.
