## Source caution

Milne's abbreviated paragraph on p.508 must be read with the opposite-root normalization, not merely with the displayed involution compatibility. In sl3 with theta equal to negative transpose, replace the nonsimple pair E13,E31 by 2E13,2E31 and keep the simple generators unchanged. Involution compatibility remains, but their bracket becomes 4(E11−E33), and [E12,E23] has coefficient 1/2 in that rescaled root vector. Thus compatibility alone cannot imply Theorem 23.71's integral coefficients.
