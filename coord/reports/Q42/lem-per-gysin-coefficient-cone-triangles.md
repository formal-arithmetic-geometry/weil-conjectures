The source does not require smoothness or regularity of the ambient schemes for this coefficient construction beyond the hypotheses needed to define the supplied regular-immersion classes. I retained those hypotheses exactly. The new lemma's inputs and construction were checked against the first paragraph of Stacks 0DD7 (sites-cohomology.tex, lemma-torsion: injective abelian sheaves are divisible on sections) and 09XQ (etale-cohomology.tex, lemma-sections-with-support-acyclic and its proof: support is right adjoint to exact closed direct image, so preserves injectives). This is an agent refinement of those source arguments, not an attribution of the full new statement to either tag.

For step 1 I opened the statement of lem-asd-etale-finite-twist-pullback. It covers arbitrary schemes with the finite exponent invertible, gives locally free lines for all tensor powers, and explicitly gives the root power comparison for changing exponent. Thus local bases at N and r use the same root. I opened lem-weil-pbc-site-abelian-sheaves-filtered-colimits-exact: it provides existence by sheafification and filtered exactness on any small site, so the torsion-kernel colimit calculation is legitimate. The index is the set of positive integers, not a large category. I checked the lift formula is independent of a representative and that t composes; both are scalar calculations modulo ell^N. The step is elementary relative to these two statements and defines a real colimit, not data chosen from a bare existence claim.

For step 2 I opened lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-enough-injectives. It explicitly includes abelian etale sheaves and makes no torsion-ring or field restriction; the familiar successive-cokernel construction is written in the step. I then checked every clause taken from the new node: nonnegative complexes, finite-ring injectivity, their derived support comparison, cocycle normalization, and exactness on both supported sheaves and global cochains. The input sheaf M satisfies its epimorphism hypothesis by step 1. Its torsion layers really are the Tate sheaves, not their derived reductions. No tensor-flatness assumption on A_r over A_N occurs.

For step 3 I opened lem-per-regular-immersion-class-calculus. Its coefficient-reduction sentence says exactly that reduction carries eta_N to eta_r; this is not inferred from flat base change or from a definition of the class. The new node's finite scalar comparison identifies this with multiplication on E. I checked the correction sign: if ell*w - z = d(v), replacing w by w-d(v_tilde) makes the difference zero and changes no class. Degreewise global surjectivity, explicitly part of the new statement, is what lifts v. At c=0 there is no negative-degree cochain, so the difference vanishes outright. Countable induction fixes all representatives before any pair (a,b) is used.

For step 4 the same new statement supplies the target coefficient maps, and I verified the two squares on the generator 1: inclusion(z_a)=ell^b*z_N and ell^a*z_N=z_b. This checks injection as well as reduction; tensor powers of a root inclusion would be incorrect. I opened def-weil-abstract-derived-category: its statement explicitly gives the mapping-cone differential, the shift convention and the cone triangles generating the derived triangulation. Thus the displayed cones represent the actual class maps, with no functorial-cone assertion for an arbitrary triangulated category hidden in the argument. The shift m=2c is even, so its differential sign causes no extra ambiguity.

For step 5 I opened lem-weil-pbc-complex-ses-triangle. It accepts any abelian category and every degreewise short exact cochain sequence, and gives a distinguished triangle with exactly those first two arrows. No degreewise splitting is required. Exactness of the cone sequence follows summand by summand from the two written rows; this is an elementary finite biproduct calculation, so I did not create a node for it. I checked the A_N actions on both summands and the bounds: C_r has no terms below min(-2c,-1). The statement of def-weil-pbc-derived-categories makes the bounded-below derived category a strictly full triangulated subcategory, so the triangle remains there.

I checked small cases before writing the new statement. On a point, take M=Q_ell/Z_ell with its length-zero injective resolution: J_r=K_r=M[ell^r], the inclusion and ell^a sequence is the usual exact cyclic-group sequence, and E_r is degreewise exact. For M=0 or Z empty the supported complexes vanish. For the identity regular immersion c=0, the actual class is 1 and the cone is acyclic; the compatible choices reduce to the degree-zero case of step 3. The a=b=1 test gives source injection ell and target inclusion, not ell^c. The proof uses no quasi-compact-support finiteness or purity on a singular ambient scheme.

The new lemma is the only non-elementary construction extracted. Its intended proof is not the old Godement argument. If I is an injective abelian sheaf, multiplication by every positive integer is surjective on sections by the free-abelian-sheaf extension argument in 0DD7. Taking ell^r-torsion is right adjoint to exact forgetting of A_r-actions, so I[ell^r] is injective over A_r. The kernel of ell^r on the injective resolution of an ell-divisible M resolves M[ell^r], by the long exact sequence of the short exact complex sequence 0 -> I[ell^r] -> I -> I -> 0. Closed support commutes with these kernels. The supported sheaf of I is again injective abelian by 09XQ, so its section groups are divisible as well. Consequently inclusion and ell^a give exact sequences of supported sheaves and of their global sections. The normalization of the finite-scalar derived comparisons is part of the new lemma, not assumed from these underived identities. It can be checked using the closed-support localization triangle and cohomology of module sheaves after forgetting coefficients (sites-cohomology.tex, lemma-cohomology-modules-abelian-agree): coefficient restriction leaves the ordinary section complexes and support inclusions unchanged, so the resulting comparison respects the support counits. This is precisely why the new statement explicitly includes those comparisons; the item does not cite the proof of an old excluded lemma for them.
