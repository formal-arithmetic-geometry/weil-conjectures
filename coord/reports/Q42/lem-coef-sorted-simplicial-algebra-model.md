## Source error

Bergner's proof of Theorem 4.7 ends by claiming that every simplicial-set fibration can occur as the evaluation of an algebra map. For the one-sort theory of groups, the Kan fibration from the empty simplicial set to the point cannot occur: every simplicial group has an identity vertex. More basically, a lift known to exist for algebra maps does not by evaluation prove lifting against every simplicial-set fibration. Thus the printed final inference cannot justify acyclicity. This is a proof error, not a false statement of the item. Final steps 6–8 give an explicit replacement without that assertion: product-preserving Kan replacement, algebraic path objects, two lifts, and cancellation on components and homotopy groups. The blueprint's fixed-object-model input and all item hypotheses/conclusions are retained. This report brings the source error upward without changing maintainer-owned files.
