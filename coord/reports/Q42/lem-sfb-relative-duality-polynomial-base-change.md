## Algebraic arguments retained for the new statement-only nodes

For lem-sfb-perfect-coinduction-tor-independent-exchange, 0661 identifies restriction of derived A-scalar extension with P-scalar extension under Tor independence. Adjunction then defines gamma by its counit, for arbitrary unbounded M. Stacks 0BZP proves invertibility when A is perfect over P, without Noetherianity or flatness of P to T. A bounded finite projective resolution F of A computes the underlying derived Hom. For finite projective L, its dual Hom complex is again bounded projective; extending it is therefore a valid derived extension model. Tor independence makes F tensor T resolve A_T. The degreewise finite projective Hom comparison is invertible: it is immediate for a finite free module from its basis and descends to direct summands. Its formula is (h tensor t)(z tensor s)=ts(h(z) tensor 1). The differential is preserved because the scalar variables have degree zero. The adjunction counit is evaluation at one, so that formula is the underlying mate, with the A_T action retained by the mate construction, not by equipping an arbitrary P-resolution with an artificial strict A action. Resolution comparisons give the same derived map since it is specified by its counit. For P to T to T', direct and successive mates have the same extended evaluation counit; adjunction injectivity identifies them. Alternatively the resolution formula multiplies the same three scalar factors in either order. This lemma is genuinely more general than the polynomial-family clause of the item.

For lem-sfb-polynomial-diagonal-adjunction-scalar-naturality, the input is the natural polynomial-diagonal reduction for arbitrary relative-perfect targets T, not a new node asserting the item's beta. The existing reduction uses S=A_1 tensor_R P, q:S to Q=A_1 tensor_R A_2, the perfect dual E=C_q(S), evaluation duality for E, the bimodule tensor-Hom adjunction, and the ordered graph Koszul resolution of S to A. Those objects and maps are all introduced in the existing lem-trf-polynomial-diagonal-rhom-reduction and its dependencies. Extend this diagram by R'. The perfect polynomial quotient resolution remains a resolution because A is R-flat. The new coinduction exchange is the mate of evaluation, so the square for E and evaluation commutes with exactly that evaluation. Tensor-Hom currying is the formula h(x)(e)=h(x tensor e), and therefore its base-change square commutes on pure tensors. The graph resolution has the ordered relations x_i-a_i; under extension these are x_i-a'_i and its basis wedges and differential signs are unchanged. Its final evaluation sends the ordered dual volume to 1 before and after extension. Thus the graph contraction square also commutes. Composing these squares gives the asserted rho square, with c inverse on its source. This is an equality of derived morphism adjunctions, not just of cohomology dimensions. The relative-perfect hypotheses on both T and T' allow precisely the existing reduction on both sides. With T=C, source extension is isomorphic to C' by the already established perfect exchange; consequently it has the target relative-perfect properties. Under the evaluation normalization, the polynomial diagonal class is the adjunct of the identity, so rho_C(xi_C)=1_C. The square and target naturality show that c sends the pulled class to xi_C'. This last specialization is carried out in the item's Step 4, rather than stated as a separate clause-sized new node.

The second argument is a genuine compatibility lemma: it compares an entire morphism adjunction for arbitrary T with scalar extension. It does not assert existence or uniqueness of the global beta, sheaf gluing, polynomial refinements between two presentations, proper adjunction, or passage to a dualizing module. Neither new node restates the item, and neither is a two-line consequence of the cited existing statements; in particular the old polynomial-diagonal reduction only asserts naturality in T over a fixed base ring, not this scalar-naturality square. Future splitters have the argument above while the node files themselves contain statements only.
