The homotopy-category model is the existing Mathlib construction, not a new
category. Its objects are wrappers of vertices (`HomotopyCategory.mk` is a
bijection), and its arrows are paths of edges modulo degenerate-edge and
2-simplex composition relations. In a quasicategory, inner 2-horn filling
reduces each such path to one edge. Equality of these arrows is homotopy of
edges relative to endpoints: the triangle relations reduce to these homotopies
by inner horn filling, and homotopic edges are equal by their degenerate
triangle. The 0-simplices of the specified right-pinched `mappingSpace` are
exactly those edges; its 1-simplices are the right homotopy triangles. Components
of its realization are the equivalence relation generated by these 1-simplices.
Thus its components are exactly these homotopy-category hom sets. This is a
canonical identification carrying each `[q(f)]` to `homMk` of its actual edge,
and preserving composition via the 2-simplex relation. It changes neither the
objects nor the mathematical meaning of the node's `hL`.
