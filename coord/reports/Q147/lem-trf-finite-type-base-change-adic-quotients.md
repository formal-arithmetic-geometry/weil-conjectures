## Preserved proof handoff: lem-trf-tensor-base-change-ideal-power-quotients

Specialize its general rings to B=R-hat, I=m, J=q. Conversely the same argument only needs the finite-level projection and kernel hypotheses now stated in the new node. The argument is as follows, with no omitted calculation.

Old Step 2: Fix $n\geq1$ and put $E_n=C/\mathfrak q^n$. Because $\mathfrak mC\subseteq\mathfrak q$, products of $n$ elements of $\mathfrak m$ act as zero on $E_n$; hence the structural map $R\to E_n$ factors through $R/\mathfrak m^n$. Compose this factor with $\pi_n$ to obtain $\theta_n:\widehat R\to E_n$. The $R$-balanced bilinear map $(c,a)\mapsto\bar c\theta_n(a)$ defines an $R$-linear map $b_n:D\to E_n$ by the tensor-product universal property. It is a ring map: on pure tensors multiplicativity is $\overline{cc'}\theta_n(aa')=(\bar c\theta_n(a))(\bar c'\theta_n(a'))$, and distributivity gives the assertion for finite sums; it preserves the unit as well. Since $b_n(f(c))=\bar c$, it kills $\mathfrak q^nD$ and induces $\beta_n:D/\mathfrak q^nD\to E_n$. Conversely $f$ induces $\alpha_n:E_n\to D/\mathfrak q^nD$, and $\beta_n\alpha_n=1$.

Old Step 3: For $a\in\widehat R$, choose $r\in R$ representing $\pi_n(a)$. Step 1 gives $a-\iota(r)\in\mathfrak m^n\widehat R$. Write this difference as a finite sum $\sum_j\iota(t_j)a_j$ with $t_j\in\mathfrak m^n$ and $a_j\in\widehat R$. For every $c\in C$, balancing gives $c\otimes(a-\iota(r))=\sum_j ct_j\otimes a_j\in\mathfrak q^nD$, since $t_jC\subseteq\mathfrak q^n$. Therefore in $D/\mathfrak q^nD$ the class of $c\otimes a$ equals that of $cr\otimes1$, which is $\alpha_n\beta_n$ of the former class. Pure tensors generate $D$ additively, so $\alpha_n\beta_n=1$. Thus $\alpha_n$ is a ring isomorphism for every $n$, induced by $f$.
