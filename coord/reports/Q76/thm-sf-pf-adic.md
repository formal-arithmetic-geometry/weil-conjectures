## Source error

The last sentence of both versions of the detailed blueprint says that the reconstructed map equals the mate map because their reductions agree and reduction is conservative. Conservativity detects whether a morphism is an isomorphism; it does not, by itself, identify morphisms. For example total cohomology is conservative on bounded derived categories, but on `D^b(Z_p)` the nonzero extension class `F_p -> F_p[1]` induces zero on every cohomology group, just as the zero morphism does. This exhibits the invalid general inference, without claiming that the particular reduction family here is nonfaithful.

The repair is the explicit identity `u_T alpha = L(R(alpha)) u_S` for the specified coherent reduction functor and its adjunction unit. After proving both unit components invertible by reconstruction, the counit triangle identity, and conservativity, this gives exactly the realized map. The theorem's mathematical hypotheses and conclusion and the unit's reconstruction route are unchanged; the self-contained expansion below corrects its imported presentation.
