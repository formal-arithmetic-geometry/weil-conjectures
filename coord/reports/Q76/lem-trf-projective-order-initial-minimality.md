Edge cases checked before writing:

- b=0: every indexed hypothesis is vacuous, both ideals are zero, and the
  homogeneous-ideal conclusion is true. No index is selected by the definition.
- r=0: the affine polynomial ring has no variables and its prime is zero;
  every nonzero degree-e form in one projective variable dehomogenizes to a
  nonzero constant. For e≥1 this cannot belong to N^e=0. Consequently only
  the empty family is admissible, as the node requires.
- A rational prime (t_1,...,t_r): φ_i=X_i gives order-one independent initial
  forms; repeated degrees are allowed by `Monotone`, whereas duplicate forms
  fail `hminimal` as they should.
- A nonmaximal prime (e.g. (t_1) in two variables) is admitted by the type.
  Taking X_1 gives a valid singleton, with exact order one.
- Nonrational/inseparable primes remain admitted. For p=(t^2+1) over ℝ, a
  nonzero degree-e dehomogenization has degree at most e and cannot be
  divisible by (t^2+1)^e for positive e. Likewise for p=(t^p-u) over a field
  with u not a pth power. These restrictive examples do not justify imposing
  a residue-field assumption. In several variables, nonempty families may
  still arise from independent rational directions; those cases are retained.
- Zero forms are expressly excluded. The initial-form helper itself accepts
  every member of U_d, including zero, and makes no false assertion about its
  order; the theorem requires nonzero inputs.
- Positive characteristic, finite fields, and arbitrary universes are all
  included; the residue-field-diagonal argument does not require perfectness.
