## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: The imported statement treats composition by the semilinear scheme automorphism as a periodic action on actual geometric-point morphisms. Its own definition has `g_gamma = id × Spec(gamma)` with `gamma = sigma^{-1}`. Take `X_0 = Spec F_q`, `X = Spec k`, `z_0 = id`, and `d = 1`. Then `g_gamma ∘ z_0 = Spec(gamma) ≠ id` (choose an element of `F_{q^2}` outside `F_q`), although the statement says that the sole lift is fixed. If arbitrary morphisms `Spec k → X` are meant, there are many parametrizations; if k-points are meant, this composition is not a k-morphism.

The faithful correction is to distinguish normalized k-valued lifts from their parametrizations. Define `G(z) = g_gamma ∘ z ∘ Spec(sigma)`. This is a k-point and induces the same action of g on the underlying geometric-fibre points. On an affine evaluation `a ⊗ b ↦ e(a)b`, this gives `a ⊗ b ↦ e(a)^q b`, not the inverse-power formula claimed in the original proof. The d lifts, their transitive orbit, their fixed-point property for d dividing m, and the claimed conjugacy all survive. The transport is the original pullback-stalk transport with the now-explicit canonical reparametrization of its source. The revised statement gives its complete formula. It does not change the definition of the semilinear scheme automorphism or the Weil structure.

Two further source defects are repaired. The statement uses `varphi^[0]` although `def-trf-iterated-weil-map` only defines positive iterates; the item now explicitly extends it by the identity. The original proof writes `T_i F_0 T_i^{-1}` as an endomorphism of `K_{z_i}`, although `T_i:K_{z_i}→K_{z_0}`. Step 7 instead proves the typed equation `T_i F_i = F_0 T_i`, then cancels on the left. Transports remain indexed by nonnegative integers: `T_d` need not be the identity even though `z_d=z_0`.
