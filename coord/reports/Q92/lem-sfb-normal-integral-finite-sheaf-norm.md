## Original arguments retained for the three new work items

The following six paragraphs are the original proof text and citation lists from the current repository's returned version, reproduced without summarizing them. This is proof material for the future workers, not proof steps added to the unsplit children.

### Original step 1 -> lem-sfb-finite-normal-cover-affine-field-data

For a nonempty affine open $U=\operatorname{Spec}B\subseteq Z$, write $p^{-1}U=\operatorname{Spec}A$. Finiteness supplies this affine inverse image and makes $A$ a finite $B$-module. Surjectivity makes it nonempty, and integrality of the schemes makes $A,B$ domains. The map $B\to A$ is injective: a point above the prime $(0)\subset B$ exists by surjectivity, its prime in $A$ contracts to zero, and the kernel is contained in that contraction. Thus the fraction fields form a finite extension by the cited finite-domain lemma. These fields identify with the generic stalks $K=k(Y)$ and $L=k(Z)$ by the affine-fraction-field lemma. The map $p$ sends the generic point of $Y$ to that of $Z$: its image is dense since $p$ is continuous and surjective. The fraction-field embeddings on every affine are therefore the same map $L\hookrightarrow K$ on generic stalks, because they extend the same section maps and fractions have a unique extension. Fix $d=\dim_L K\geq1$; it is independent of the affine open.

Original cited nodes: def-weil-finite-integral-morphism, def-weil-surjective-morphism, def-weil-affine-spectrum, lem-weil-pbc-spec-map-residue-field-naturality, lem-sc-integral-function-field-affine-fraction, lem-trf-finite-domain-fraction-degree.

Source: literature/stacks-project/divisors.tex, Tag 0BD3 (divisors-lemma-norm-in-normal-case), first paragraph, affine rings and their fraction fields.

### Original step 2 -> lem-sfb-finite-normal-cover-affine-field-data

The affine ring $B$ is integrally closed in $L$. Indeed, if $c\in L$ satisfies a monic polynomial over $B$, that polynomial maps to a monic polynomial over each $\mathcal O_{Z,z}$, $z\in U$. The stalk embeds in $L$ and has $L$ as fraction field, so normality of that stalk puts $c$ in its image. The cited section-gluing statement then supplies $b\in\Gamma(U,\mathcal O_Z)=B$ with generic germ $c$. Hence $B$ is integrally closed, without any Noetherian hypothesis.

Original cited nodes: def-weil-pbc-normal-ring, lem-sc-integral-scheme-stalks-in-function-field, lem-sc-function-field-sections-glue.

Source: literature/stacks-project/divisors.tex, Tag 0BD3 (divisors-lemma-norm-in-normal-case), normality needed for the application of the minimal-polynomial lemma.

### Original step 3 -> lem-sfb-finite-normal-domain-norm-prime

Let $N=N_{K/L}$ be the field norm. Every $a\in A$ is integral over $B$ by finite-algebra integrality. The minimal-polynomial descent lemma gives a monic $Q_a\in B[T]$, annihilating $a$ in $A$, whose image in $L[T]$ is its minimal polynomial. If $m=\deg Q_a$ and $e=\dim_{L(a)}K\geq1$, the field-norm formula says $N(a)=((-1)^mQ_a(0))^e\in B$. Define $\nu_U(a)$ to be this element of $B$, equivalently the unique element with image $N(a)$ in $L$. Since the field norm preserves multiplication and one, injectivity of $B\to L$ proves the same for $\nu_U$. Its scalar formula gives $\nu_U(p^\sharp b)=b^d$ for $b\in B$.

Original cited nodes: lem-weil-pbc-finite-algebra-is-integral, lem-sfb-integral-minpoly-fraction-descent, lem-sfb-finite-field-norm-minpoly, lem-sc-integral-function-field-affine-fraction.

Source: literature/stacks-project/divisors.tex, Tag 0BD3 (divisors-lemma-norm-in-normal-case), first paragraph, minimal polynomial and construction on affine sections.

### Original step 4 -> lem-sfb-generic-multiplicative-affine-gluing

If $W\subseteq U$ is another nonempty affine open, the sections $a$ and $a|_{p^{-1}W}$ have the same generic germ in $K$. Both $\nu_U(a)|_W$ and $\nu_W(a|_{p^{-1}W})$ consequently have germ $N(a)$ in $L$, so they are equal by injectivity of the germ map on $W$. For two affine opens $U,U^{\prime}$, cover $U\cap U^{\prime}$ by affine opens $W$ and apply this equality twice. Thus the affine norms agree on overlaps. This uses no assumption that the intersection itself is affine.

Original cited nodes: lem-sc-integral-scheme-stalks-in-function-field, def-pc-scheme.

Source: literature/stacks-project/divisors.tex, Tag 0BD3 (divisors-lemma-norm-in-normal-case), compatible system of maps on affine sections.

### Original step 5 -> lem-sfb-generic-multiplicative-affine-gluing

For any nonempty open $V\subseteq Z$ and $a\in\Gamma(p^{-1}V,\mathcal O_Y)$, choose an affine cover $(U_i)$ of $V$. The sections $\nu_{U_i}(a|_{p^{-1}U_i})$ agree on overlaps by the preceding step, so the structure-sheaf axiom glues them uniquely to $\nu_V(a)$. Uniqueness of gluing proves compatibility with restriction to every open subset. Multiplicativity, preservation of one, and $\nu_V(p^\sharp b)=b^d$ hold after restricting to every $U_i$, hence hold on $V$ by the uniqueness part of the same sheaf axiom. For $V=\varnothing$ both section rings have one element; use the unique map. This also verifies restrictions to the empty open.

Original cited nodes: def-pc-scheme.

Source: literature/stacks-project/divisors.tex, Tag 0BD3 (divisors-lemma-norm-in-normal-case), global norm map and property (1).

### Original step 6 -> lem-sfb-finite-normal-domain-norm-prime

For property (2), take $y\in p^{-1}V$ with $a(y)=0$, and choose an affine neighbourhood $U=\operatorname{Spec}B\subseteq V$ of $p(y)$. Write $p^{-1}U=\operatorname{Spec}A$ and $a_U=a|_{p^{-1}U}$. The point $y$ corresponds to a prime $\mathfrak q\subset A$, and $a_U\in\mathfrak q$ by the affine vanishing criterion. Its image point has prime $\mathfrak r=\mathfrak q\cap B$. From step 3 write $Q_{a_U}(T)=T^m+\sum_{i<m}b_iT^i$ in $B[T]$, with $Q_{a_U}(a_U)=0$. Thus $b_0=-a_U(a_U^{m-1}+\sum_{1\leq i<m}b_i a_U^{i-1})\in\mathfrak q$, so $b_0\in\mathfrak r$. The signed norm formula gives $\nu_U(a_U)=((-1)^mb_0)^e\in\mathfrak r$ since $e\geq1$. The affine vanishing criterion says this section vanishes at $p(y)$, and restriction compatibility gives $\nu_V(a)(p(y))=0$.

Original cited nodes: lem-weil-pbc-affine-open-section-vanishing-prime, lem-weil-pbc-spec-map-residue-field-naturality, lem-sfb-integral-minpoly-fraction-descent, lem-sfb-finite-field-norm-minpoly.

Source: literature/stacks-project/divisors.tex, Tag 0BD3 (divisors-lemma-norm-in-normal-case), final paragraph, vanishing at the image point; constant coefficient is b_0 and the sign is retained.
