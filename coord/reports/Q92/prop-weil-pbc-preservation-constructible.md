## Proof guidance for the new frontier

This paragraph is guidance in the report; the new node itself has no proof or proof dependencies. Given alpha_m, alpha_n and rho_mn, exact inverse image and K-flat pullback give
nu^*(L_m tensor^L P_n) = (nu^* L_m) tensor^L P_n.
At the complex level this comparison is the balancing map nu^-1(x tensor r) -> nu^-1(x) tensor r. Its iterates agree for three quotients by the tensor balancing relation. The inverse image is isomorphic, via rho_mn(alpha_m tensor 1), to M_n. Exact faithful inverse image reflects zero cohomology: exactness identifies cohomology with inverse image of cohomology, and the isomorphism of the sheaf unit reflects its vanishing. Consequently L_m tensor^L P_n is bounded below, even though a derived quotient tensor need not preserve boundedness in general. Only now apply the bounded-below full faithfulness at P_n. Lift alpha_n^-1 rho_mn(alpha_m tensor 1), and its inverse, uniquely to obtain beta_mn and its inverse. The compositions are identities by faithfulness. For k >= m >= n, apply inverse image to beta_mn(beta_km tensor 1); insert the two defining equations and cancel alpha_m^-1 alpha_m. Tensor coherence and the rho cocycle give the defining equation for beta_kn. Faithfulness proves their equality. The identity case is the same calculation. This argument needs no common bound and no finite Tor dimension; boundedness of each reduced etale source was proved before using D+ full faithfulness.
