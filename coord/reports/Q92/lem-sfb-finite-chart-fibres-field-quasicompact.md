For step 1, I opened `lem-sfb-space-change-base-to-integers`. Its statement gives the absolute algebraic space, the explicit sheaf formula, preservation of the fibre products of diagrams over S, the equivalence with spaces equipped with a map to S, and identification of etale scheme objects. The explicit pairs formula also identifies field-valued point classes: equality forces the same structure map after field extension. This ensures that all original finite-fibre assumptions remain the same. Applying the final absolute assertion to an original S-test gives precisely the original fibre product. I did not treat preservation of products over an unrelated terminal object as part of that lemma.

For step 2, the new 03JS node says exactly that a jointly surjective scheme-etale family with finite point fibres at x gives finite fibres for every affine etale chart at x. In its source proof the affine test is covered by finitely many images of quasi-compact opens in its base changes; these opens have finite fibres over the charts by local quasi-finiteness. This is where affineness of the test is used, not affineness or quasi-compactness of the given U_i. The node is strictly smaller than the item and has no condition on self-relations. It remains statement-only as required.

For step 3, surjectivity gives one chart meeting x and a chosen scheme point u above it. The relation points with both projections u form a subset of the assumed finite relation fibre over x. The new 03JU input applies to exactly this subset, not to a possibly larger fibre product guessed from a point set. I checked its statement against 03JU's proof: the residual product has the underlying pair fibre; it maps monomorphically into Spec kappa(u) times_X U, an etale disjoint union of finite separable field spectra. The monomorphism-into-field-spectra lemma in that source restricts it to components; finite point set makes the selected union finite and hence both projections etale and quasi-compact (interchange the two factors for the second). This proves the stated residual-relation result without assuming all chart fibres finite. Next I opened `lem-sfb-finite-point-self-relation-field-monomorphism`: it assumes precisely these two properties of the self-relation and supplies a field monomorphism in the same point class. Its statement does not assert quasi-compactness, and the proof never takes that from it.

For step 4, I opened `lem-trf-space-fibre-products-etale-covers`. Its statement gives base change of the etale chart and representability of the resulting scheme D. Monomorphy of D to V is proved directly from its pair-valued universal property and the supplied monomorphism j. I opened `def-weil-radicial-morphism`: besides definitions, its statement explicitly asserts the field-valued-point criterion equivalent to universal injectivity and radiciality. Cancellation on field tests therefore implies injectivity on ordinary points. No theorem was inferred from a definition that did not state it. D's image lies in the finite fibre F_V by composition to X; injectivity into this finite set proves finiteness of |D|. I did not assume that a point-class fibre automatically equals a scheme fibre as sets.

For step 5, I opened `lem-weil-pbc-etale-fibre-discrete-separable-points`. It states the complete disjoint-union decomposition and finite separability of each field, and applies here at the sole point of Spec k. I also opened `lem-weil-pbc-spec-finite-product-fields`: its component open immersions and their images give the affine finite-product realization of this finite union. `def-weil-finite-integral-morphism` states finiteness in terms of a module-finite coordinate algebra.
