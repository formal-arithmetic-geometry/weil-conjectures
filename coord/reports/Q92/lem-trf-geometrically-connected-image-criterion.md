Former step 4:

The group $G$ is finite by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-galois-fixed-field]. Set $P=\bigcap_{\sigma\in G}\sigma_X(A)$ and $Q=X_L\setminus P$. Thus both $P$ and $Q$ are $G$-invariant.
