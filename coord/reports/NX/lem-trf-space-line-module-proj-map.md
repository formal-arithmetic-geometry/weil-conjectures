### Returned step 1

Choose a representable jointly surjective etale scheme cover $V\to X$ from the definition of an algebraic space, then refine $V$ by affine opens over affine opens of $T$ and by etale trivializations of the line module $L$. On a chart $W=\operatorname{Spec}(B)\to X$ over $T_0=\operatorname{Spec}(R)$, write the trivialized line module as $\mathcal O_W$. For every positive-degree local section $a$ of $f^*\mathcal A_d$, its image $\psi_d(a)$ is a function on $W$, and the locus where $a$ generates the target is the principal open $D(\psi_d(a))$: a map to an invertible module is surjective exactly where one local image is a unit. These principal opens cover the locus where $\psi_d$ is surjective, and their union over $d\geq1$ is therefore open. On $V\times_XV$ the two pullbacks of each such principal open agree, because both are defined by the pullback of the same section and surjectivity of a map of sheaves is local on the etale site. Thus the chartwise opens carry a canonical descent datum; the sheaf definition of the algebraic space gives the descended open subspace $U(\psi)$, and its pullback to every scheme over $X$ is the same union of principal opens. On each such principal open, the affine relative-Proj construction (Constructions, Tag 01O9) gives the canonical morphism to $\operatorname{Proj}(A)$; the affine-chart identifications in [lemma-nagata-relative-proj-exists] identify these targets with the restriction of $\operatorname{Proj}_T(\mathcal A)$.

### Returned step 2

On a refinement of two affine and trivializing charts, the two local morphisms just constructed have the same homogeneous-fraction formulas on every standard Proj chart. The uniqueness clause in the affine construction therefore identifies them on overlaps, and the same equality holds after pullback to $V\times_XV$. To descend the map, let $W\to U(\psi)$ be any scheme and choose an etale cover $W_i\to W$ factoring through the presentation $V\to X$. The local maps $W_i\to\operatorname{Proj}_T(\mathcal A)$ agree on every $W_i\times_WW_j$ by the overlap equality, so the sheaf condition for the target algebraic space glues them uniquely to a map $W\to\operatorname{Proj}_T(\mathcal A)$. This construction is functorial in $W$, hence defines the unique morphism $r_{L,\psi}$; the structural map to $T$ agrees locally and therefore globally. Pullback to the jointly surjective presentation detects equality, so both the map and its homogeneous-algebra compatibility are unique.

### Returned step 3

For an arbitrary commutative square with $X'$ and $T'$ schemes, pullback of the local charts, the graded algebra, the line module, and $\psi$ gives precisely $(\mathcal A',L',\psi')$. Surjectivity of a sheaf map and invertibility of a local generator are preserved and reflected after pullback, so the principal-open description in Step 1 gives $U(\psi')=(g')^{-1}(U(\psi))$. The unrestricted comparison [lemma-nagata-relative-proj-base-change-arbitrary] gives the canonical isomorphism $\operatorname{Proj}_{T'}(\mathcal A')\simeq T'\times_T\operatorname{Proj}_T(\mathcal A)$ for this arbitrary $g$, with no flatness assumption. On every pulled-back affine chart, the homogeneous-fraction formula is the pullback of the original formula; by the uniqueness and descent argument of Step 2 these local equalities identify $r_{L',\psi'}$ with the base change of $r_{L,\psi}$.

### Returned step 4

When $X$ is a scheme, the small-etale line module is the usual invertible $\mathcal O_X$-module [def-sc-invertible-module], and the tensor pullback in the statement agrees with the ordinary module pullback [def-pc-module-pullback]. The preceding affine construction is then exactly the usual canonical morphism obtained from graded maps into tensor powers of an invertible module.
