### Retained argument 1

Write $Y=\operatorname{Spec}A$ and let $g:P\hookrightarrow Q$ be the inclusion. Since $Q$ is a submonoid of the group $P^{\mathrm{gp}}$, it is integral; together with its assumed finite generation and saturation this makes it fs [def-trf-fs-log-blowup]. The homomorphism $Q^{\mathrm{gp}}\to P^{\mathrm{gp}}$ induced by the inclusion is inverse to $g^{\mathrm{gp}}$: every $q\in Q$ is a difference of two elements of $P$, and these differences already generate $P^{\mathrm{gp}}$. Hence $g^{\mathrm{gp}}$ is injective with zero, in particular torsion-free, cokernel.

### Retained argument 2

Apply [pqf-lem-affine-open-locnoeth] to the locally Noetherian scheme $Y$ and its affine open subscheme $Y=\operatorname{Spec}A$ itself, whose ring of sections is $A$ [def-weil-affine-spectrum]: $A$ is Noetherian. Choose monoid generators $q_1,\ldots,q_r$ of $Q$, allowing $r=0$. Then $C$ is generated as an $A$-algebra by the finitely many elements $1\otimes z^{q_i}$. Indeed a tensor is a sum of pure tensors [def-weil-pbc-module-tensor-product], each second factor is a finite integral linear combination of monomials $z^q$, and each $q$ is a sum of the $q_i$. The multiplication $(a\otimes u)(b\otimes v)=ab\otimes uv$ therefore expresses each pure tensor as an $A$-linear combination of products of these generators.
