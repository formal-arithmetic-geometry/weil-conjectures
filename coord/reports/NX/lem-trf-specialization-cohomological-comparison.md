## Source error

In Varshavsky, Definition 4.1.3(VI), the two proper maps are the arrows of the two fibre diagrams. In 4.1.4(b) he claims (VI) by proper base change, while 1.3.2(c) states the actual properness hypothesis on the morphism of lifts. These hypotheses differ.

Take nonzero allowed finite coefficients A and c the identity correspondence of X = Spec(k) disjoint union Spec(k). Let the identity-correspondence lift have Xtilde = Ctilde = D disjoint union eta, with the evident morphism to X times D, identity on its generic fibre. Its special fibre is a single point. All its fibre structure maps to the point are proper and all operations here are admissible. Specialization on X is the projection (M1,M2) to M1. For the structure arrow g, its ordinary direct-image mate at (A,A) is

    f_s Rg_*(A,A) = A direct-sum A  -->  Rg_0* f_X(A,A) = A.

It is the first projection, hence not invertible. The total structure map D disjoint union eta -> D is not proper. Thus the assertion is false even with nonempty special fibre. The still simpler example X = Spec(k), Xtilde = eta gives A -> 0. To avoid any coincidence of labelled diagram objects one can instead take X = P^1_k and Xtilde = P^1_eta; the structure mate is RΓ(P^1_k,A) -> 0, noninvertible already in degree zero.

A proper refinement cannot automatically fix the open-fibre-proper condition in the first example. Proper nearby base change would identify its special global cohomology of specialized coefficients with A, while its generic global cohomology is still A direct-sum A. The first factor of the claimed compactifiable factorization would still fail (VI). On the proper compactified lift diagrams the total arrows really are proper, so the extension assertion remains valid.

That invocation is not supplied by the corrected item without checking the extra condition or repairing the stronger abstract comparison definition/calculus. The underlying trace-specialization theorem is not refuted by this example; its canonical maps and the geometric factorization are retained.
