## Mathematical plan for the new statement-only node

I read Stacks `divisors.tex`, Tag 0BUT (`lemma-finite-trivialize-invertible-upstairs`), Tag 0BCY (`lemma-norm-invertible`), including its transition-function construction, and Tag 0BD2 (`lemma-finite-locally-free-has-norm`), including its arbitrary-base-change assertion and determinant proof. A basis of K over k makes R tensor_k K free of rank d on every affine R of Z. Thus p_Z is finite locally free of degree d. On a base open covering whose inverse images trivialize F (0BUT), write frame changes u_ij and glue trivial lines using det(m_uij), as in 0BCY. Multiplicativity gives the cocycle. Replacing frames changes these functions by a norm coboundary, and refining covers changes nothing on isomorphism classes; the same calculation treats isomorphisms of F. Tensor products multiply their transition functions. For p_Z^*A these functions come from functions a_ij on Z, whose scalar multiplication matrices are a_ij I_d, so their determinants are a_ij^d. For an arbitrary h:W -> Z, pull back the covering and its frames. The multiplication matrix becomes its scalar extension, and its determinant is the pullback determinant (0BD2). Hence its norm transition functions agree with those of h^*Nm(F). These equalities establish the two stated identities without requiring flatness of h or separability of K/k. The new node contains only the statement; this plan is retained here for its next worker.
