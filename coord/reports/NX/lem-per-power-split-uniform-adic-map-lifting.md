## Mathematical justification of the two auxiliary statements

For lem-per-graded-power-split-linear-factorization, read local-cohomology.tex section-uniform (Tag 0GA6), definitions of d(S), q(S), lemma-factor-hom (Tag 0GAC), and lemma-bound-two-term-complex (Tag 0GAD), including the proofs. Set S=gr_K(R). The dimension d(S) of fibres of Proj(S) over Spec(S_0), with the source's empty convention, and the Serre tail bound q(S), depend only on the graded ring S. Choose e=(d+2)c and N=max(q+e,2e+1). These choices depend only on the reference graded class and c. Every a in K^c acts on M through the finite free module R_n^r, so M is an (R,n,c)-module in the inline definition before 0GAC. The hypotheses n>=q+(d+1)c, n>=(d+2)c, and, when d=0, n>=q+2c all follow from n>=N. Apply 0GAC to u. The image of u in K^(n-(d+1)c)/K^(2n-(d+1)c) is a finite sum a_j w_j, where a_j is in K^c and w_j maps M to K^(n-e)/K^(2n-e). For each a_j choose h_j with h_j d=a_j id_M. Compose each w_j with h_j and reduce its target modulo K^(2n-2e); their sum defines v. Equality of 0GAC after inclusion and reduction gives v d=rho u. The reductions exist since 2n-e>=2n-2e and n-(d+1)c>=n-e. Thus this is a linear module lemma, strictly below the nonlinear algebra lifting problem. There is no map B->C in its statement, and no completeness hypothesis. No argument is hidden in its statement; this paragraph is explanatory material for the later split of that unsplit node.

For lem-per-completed-polynomial-taylor-evaluation, read restricted.tex Tag 0AK3 (remark-linear-approximation), formal-spaces.tex Tag 0AJM (remark-universal-property), the immediately following remark-I-adic-completion-and-restricted-power-series, and algebra.tex Tag 05GG (lemma-hathat-finitely-generated). For noncomplete A, construct evaluation from the compatible polynomial evaluations at every quotient A/I^m and pass to the complete target. The source is the completion for the ideal generated by coefficients I, not the full variable-adic completion. Arbitrary y_i are allowed. Differentiation preserves I^m in the polynomial ring and therefore extends continuously, with the Leibniz rule checked at each finite level. Reducing f modulo I^(2s) reduces evaluation to an actual polynomial over A/I^(2s). Polynomial monomial expansion gives the constant and linear terms, and each remaining term has at least two factors from the correction tuple, hence lies in I^(2s)T. This formulation needs only a congruence modulo I^(2s), not an assertion that an arbitrary nonclosed finitely generated ideal in a non-Noetherian target is closed. The output node is statement-only, retaining that argument here for its future worker.
