Step 2 uses only finite-group representatives and Euclidean division: the nonzero class has order d at least two, and d divides n because n kills the cokernel. Step 3 checks the often implicit hypothesis needed to divide by d: writing n=de and nv=u gives d(ev)=u inside D.
