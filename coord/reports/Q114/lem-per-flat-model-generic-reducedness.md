## Proof plan for the new statement-only lemma

Sources read: algebra.tex Tag 00HS, label lemma-flat-going-down (lines 9265ff); Tag 00I4, label lemma-perfect-reduced (10581ff); and the faithfully flat local-ring argument in 00HS, using 00HQ (lemma-ff-rings) and 00HR (lemma-local-flat-ff, 9164ff). I also read varieties.tex Tag 0384 (lemma-geometrically-reduced-upstairs, 491ff), which explains the general pointwise geometric-reducedness comparison, and algebra.tex Tag 033F (lemma-descent-reduced, 46668ff). The local files, not a forbidden SGA route, are the proof sources.

For a field extension E/F, the map A to E tensor_F A is faithfully flat: choose an F-basis of E containing 1, so it is a nonzero free A-module with the copy of A corresponding to 1. Going down contracts minimal primes to minimal primes. Conversely every minimal prime p of A is the contraction of a minimal prime upstairs: faithful flatness supplies a prime above p, a minimal prime contained in it exists, and contraction must equal p by its minimality. The local map A_p to (E tensor_F A)_q at primes over one another is faithfully flat, as in 00HS, so reducedness upstairs implies reducedness downstairs (or directly use injectivity and nilpotents).

For ascent, at a minimal q upstairs with contraction p, generic reducedness says A_p is reduced. Characteristic zero makes F perfect, so 00I4 says E tensor_F A_p is reduced; its localization at the prime induced by q is (E tensor_F A)_q, which is reduced. Localization preserves reducedness by the fraction argument: if (a/s)^m=0, some allowed denominator t has t a^m=0, hence (ta)^m=0, hence ta=0, so a/s=0. This proves the equivalence. This lemma is a real local commutative-algebra input, not the domain-model item or one of its clauses under renamed variables.
