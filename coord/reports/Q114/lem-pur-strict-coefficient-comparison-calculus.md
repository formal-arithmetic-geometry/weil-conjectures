### Original step 2 → lem-pur-coefficient-comparisons-balanced-construction

Spell out the scalar association in Step 1. For $r\in u^{-1}R_Y^a$, balancing $a\phi^a(r)\otimes x=a\otimes rx$ in the source of $\kappa$ gives the same image, since $i_X^{ab}\phi^a(r)=\phi^b i_Y^{ab}(r)$ and the latter scalar can move into $1\otimes rx$. Balancing its outer tensor over $R_X^a$ is multiplication by $i_X^{ab}(a)$. For its proposed inverse, balancing $d i_Y^{ab}(r)\otimes x=d\otimes rx$ has the same image by that same square, and balancing the outer tensor over $u^{-1}R_Y^b$ multiplies by $\phi^b(d)$. These give precisely the two displayed formulas.
