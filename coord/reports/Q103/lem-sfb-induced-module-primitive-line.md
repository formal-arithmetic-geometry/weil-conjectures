## Review by Q103

I reviewed the node files with the sources open and checked delivery-errors B--E as follows.

Step 1 uses the actual statement of `lem-sfb-reductive-borel-pair-structure` for semisimple implies reductive and B=U semidirect T; both clauses are explicitly present. The new geometric node introduces every object itself, works for all reductive groups, and supplies exactly the longest-element and dense open immersion used here. Its roots and positive system are those of the existing structure node, not roots of an unrelated Lie algebra. Multiplication in the reverse order T times U follows by writing ut=t(t^{-1}ut); this elementary rewriting is not a new node. The induced space is stable under left translation by its defining identity. The explanation using the kernel of right equivariance and commuting left/right translations justifies rationality even when E is not finite-dimensional; the tensor-kernel assertion here is explicit basis-wise vector-space linear algebra. It is not an appeal to an uncited flatness theorem over a general ring.

Step 2 uses `sbc-lem-smooth-geometrically-reduced`, whose statement concerns every smooth morphism and therefore applies to the structure maps of both S and U; their fibre over Spec F is the same scheme. U is the smooth connected unipotent radical from the hypotheses. The source and target of j are affine; on spectra the morphism is quasi-compact (principal affine opens pull back to principal affine opens) and separated, hence quasi-separated. Thus every hypothesis of `lemma-nagata-reduced-dense-image-schematic` holds: reduced target, quasi-compact/quasi-separated map, dense topological image from step 1. Its conclusion is an isomorphism of scheme-theoretic image with S, not directly a coordinate restriction assertion. The exact definition `def-nagata-scheme-theoretic-image` states that for a quasi-compact morphism the actual structure-sheaf kernel defines that image. This is why the proof obtains injectivity; it does not take an unstated consequence from a definition node. The formula j^*f(x,b)=lambda(b)^{-1}r(f)(x) then gives injectivity of r in one step. I did not create a separate lemma for either this elementary consequence or the substitution giving the translation identity.

Step 3 uses `lem-sfb-comodule-finite-subrepresentation` for a finite singleton in an arbitrary rational U-module. Its statement does not require E finite-dimensional. It produces M containing a nonzero h, so its basis has d at least one. `lem-sfb-unipotent-rational-representation-fixed-vector` assumes precisely smooth affine finite-type U with a faithful unipotent matrix representation, all supplied by the radical's definition here; its conclusion only fixes every F-point. I explicitly avoid treating this as the stronger coaction conclusion. In coordinates the difference rho(v)-v tensor 1 has d coordinate functions vanishing on U(F). The vanishing principle in clause 2 of `lem-wf-affine-nullstellensatz-vanishing` is exactly what is used: F algebraically closed, F[U] finitely generated and reduced by step 2. Each coefficient is zero, giving the full coaction identity. This elementary coefficient argument does not require a new point/coaction comparison node. It would fail for a nonreduced group, which is why smoothness and reducedness were checked before the upgrade.

Step 4 uses only the translation identity and injectivity already proved, and substitutes the same universal point into u and x. Taking A=F[U] proves equality of regular functions, not equality just on rational points. Constants form a copy of F because U has its identity point; injectivity and the nonzero invariant from step 3 then give exactly dimension one. No existence of an extension of an arbitrarily prescribed constant from the open cell is assumed: it is the nonzero invariant already obtained that spans the image.

Step 5 writes the full multiplication and character calculation over every commutative F-algebra A. The crucial right factor is n_0^{-1}t^{-1}n_0, and its inverse character is lambda(n_0^{-1}tn_0); hence the claimed weight is w_0 lambda, with no missing sign. T normalizes U since U is normal in B. Injectivity after scalar extension is justified by an actual F-linear splitting of r, so no reducedness of A or inference from A-points over fields is made. The final equality is therefore the group-scheme weight identity on the already constructed line. These are elementary substitutions and vector-space operations within the item, not hidden independent lemmas.
