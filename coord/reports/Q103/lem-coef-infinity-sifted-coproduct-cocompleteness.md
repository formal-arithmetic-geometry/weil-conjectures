Take finite discrete colimit shapes. One can first take a small inner-horn fibrant replacement C of K; functors from K to a quasicategory are unchanged up to coherent equivalence. The full finite-coproduct closure of representables in P(C) has a small model: objects are finite lists of vertices of C and mapping spaces have the finite-family formula, a product over source summands of a disjoint union over target summands of Map_C(c,d). This is U-small, even though P(C) itself is larger. Extension sends a finite list to the finite coproduct of the F(c)'s. The coproduct universal property supplies its left Kan extension at each list (including the empty list), so the mapping equivalence applies to arbitrary H, retaining parameter simplices.
