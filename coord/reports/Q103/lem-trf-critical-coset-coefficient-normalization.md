## Intended argument for the unsplit child

Let G=Gal(E/F), with cardinal n. The field trace is the sum of the conjugates (Mathlib's Algebra.trace_eq_sum_automorphisms is the relevant algebraic identity). Completeness of F ensures uniqueness of the extending absolute value, so every sigma in G is isometric. Because p does not divide n, its residue in the prime field of the residue field is nonzero; hence |n|=1. The average T=n^{-1} sum_sigma sigma is F-linear, fixes F, and satisfies |T(x)|<=|x|.

Put x_sigma=sigma(x). In E,

n^p (T(x^p)-T(x)^p) = n^{p-1} sum_sigma x_sigma^p - (sum_sigma x_sigma)^p.

For prime p, every mixed multinomial coefficient in the last power is divisible by p; the coefficient of a pure pth power is n^{p-1}-1, also divisible by p since p does not divide n. Every resulting monomial has total degree p, so its norm is at most |x|^p and its integer coefficient has norm at most |p|. The ultrametric inequality and |n|=1 give the claimed bound. This also proves exact commutation in characteristic p. This argument is not placed in the child's statement or proof field: it remains a statement-only next item.
