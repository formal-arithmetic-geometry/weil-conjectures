### lem-sbc-koszul-fibre-linear-descent

Read algebraization.tex, Tag 0A0E, lemma-derived-completion-koszul, together with proetale.tex Tag 099M, lemma-naive-completion; the explicit mapping fibre and linearity refinement is agent supplied. For the application the exact-product statement is lem-weil-proetale-countable-products-exact, and def-weil-pbc-intrinsic-completion specifies the same differential and zero-homotopy coaugmentation. The broader new statement treats any small ringed site with exact countable products.

Each Q_m is a bounded complex of finite free modules. Tensoring with Q_m therefore preserves quasi-isomorphisms: filter by its finite stupid truncations, whose successive quotients are shifts of finite free sheaves. For a quasi-isomorphism a, every a tensor Q_m is a quasi-isomorphism. Exactness of the countable product then makes the product map a quasi-isomorphism, by applying exact products to cycles/boundaries or the acyclic cones. The fibre map is a quasi-isomorphism because a map of the two product complexes gives a map of their fibre triangles and both product maps are quasi-isomorphisms. This is the point at which exact products, not only existence of products, is required.

The maps B(a) commute with 1-s because a tensor identity commutes with every transition. The formula F(a)(x,y)=(B(a)x,B(a)y) commutes with the stated differential, respects identity and composition, and is additive and A-linear. If h is a chain homotopy between a and a', its tensor homotopies form a product homotopy H commuting with s. On the fibre the homotopy is (x,y) mapped to (Hx,-Hy). The minus sign in the second component cancels the two cross terms involving 1-s. Consequently F preserves homotopies as well as quasi-isomorphisms, so it descends through the homotopy quotient and then localization. Linear localization is supplied by lem-weil-linear-abelian-derived-category; this descent does not assume a chosen injective-resolution functor is linear.

The sequential derived-limit product/fibre theorem identifies this very fibre with Rlim of the strict Koszul tower; exact products identify its product complexes with derived products. The inclusion in degree zero gives compatible maps M to M tensor Q_m. Their product u satisfies (1-s)u=0, so (u,0) is a chain map and is exactly the completion unit. Tensor and products commute with the finite two-term biproduct: in every degree the canonical product of (M_m direct-sum N_m) is product M_m direct-sum product N_m. This identification commutes with 1-s, with the fibre differential and with (u,0). Hence F preserves zero and finite biproducts, and the complete locus is closed under these operations. All scalar identities hold on representatives and survive localization.

For the application's coefficient preparation, A is a DVR with nonzero uniformizer, hence Noetherian. The exact sequence A --varpi^n--> A to P_n has injective first map; it gives the perfect two-term free resolution, even for n=1. Finite P_n is additive torsion. After passage to A_W the same perfect model is valid: lem-weil-pbc-constant-completion-equivalence says A_W is flat over the constant A sheaf and A_W/varpi^n A_W is (P_n)_W. No perfectness over an unrelated ring is assumed.
