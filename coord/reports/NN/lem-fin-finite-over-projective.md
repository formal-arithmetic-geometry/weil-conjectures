## STATEMENT-DISPUTED

The imported conclusion says global projectivity under def-pc-projective-morphism, whose statement explicitly requires one closed embedding into a finite-dimensional projective space over S. The proof ends only with an embedding locally on S. Stacks Morphisms 01W8 (definition-projective) distinguishes H-projective, projective-bundle embeddings, and locally projective; 01WB (lemma-characterize-locally-projective) characterizes the last by local H-projective embeddings. These cannot be identified.

Here is an explicit counterexample to the global H-projective assertion. Let k be a field and let S be the affine line with doubled origin: glue S_1=Spec k[t] and S_2=Spec k[t] along D(t) by the identity. It is quasi-compact and locally Noetherian, hence Noetherian. Let L be the line bundle with frames e_1,e_2 and transition e_2=t e_1. A global section has coefficients (a,b) with a=t b. In particular every global section of L vanishes at the first origin. Form the square-zero algebra B=O_S direct-sum L with multiplication (a,l)(b,m)=(ab,am+bl), and let Y=Spec_S B. Its morphism h:Y->S is finite (B is locally free of rank two). Set T=S, with its identity embedding in P^0_S. All hypotheses of the imported statement hold.

Suppose j:Y->P^n_S were a closed immersion. Its pulled-back O(1), M, is an invertible sheaf generated by its coordinate sections. Restrict along the canonical closed section S->Y. Every line bundle on S has transition c*t^d: both affine charts have trivial Picard group and the units on the overlap are exactly c*t^d. If d is positive all global sections vanish at one origin, and if d is negative they vanish at the other. Thus the only globally generated line bundle on S is O_S, so M|_S is trivial. In that trivialization the restricted coordinate sections are polynomials f_i in k[t] generating the unit ideal (otherwise a maximal ideal gives a point at which none generates). Choose polynomials g_i with sum g_i*f_i=1. Pull the g_i to Y along h. The section sum g_i*s_i of M restricts to 1 on S; since Y is a square-zero thickening of S, it generates at every point of Y. Hence M itself is trivial. Now the coordinates of j are global elements of B. Their nilpotent parts are global sections of L, which vanish at the first origin. Consequently on the fibre Spec k[epsilon]/(epsilon^2) over that origin, every coordinate is constant, and j factors through Spec k. Such a morphism is not a closed immersion: on the nonempty affine coordinate chart its ring map misses epsilon. This contradicts j being a closed immersion.

The corrected statement retains the original hypotheses and asserts explicitly the local H-projective conclusion actually constructed by the source, for every affine open of S. It also states the full original conclusion when S is affine. It does not silently change the project's definition of projective. This counterexample explains why a global gluing step cannot repair the original proof. The Noetherian hypothesis is retained, although the local proof does not need it.
