## Corrections and scope

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported statement writes A=A_{E,n} without binding E or n (and without binding the prime or chosen uniformizer needed to interpret that expression). Reading it as a closed statement leaves E,n free and A_{E,n} uninterpreted. I repaired this notation/type-completeness defect by explicitly quantifying a prime ell, a finite extension E/Q_ell, a chosen uniformizer, and n>=1, and giving A=O_E/(varpi_E^n). This is the finite coefficient datum intended by the source, not a new geometric hypothesis. I replaced the generic scheme-property collector by the actual finite-type, separated/proper, and scheme definition nodes. The other statement additions name the existing definitions of the derived module categories, witness, and operations. The source's geometric hypotheses and conclusion are preserved.

The original proof called extension by zero exact while citing only its definition nodes. The split now cites lem-weil-pbc-extension-by-zero-exact and lem-weil-extension-by-zero-exact. The original phrase “p is of finite type, hence quasi-compact and quasi-separated” was not a valid general implication: a finite-type morphism need not be quasi-separated. The proof now uses the actual hypothesis that p is proper, via pqf-lem-proper-basic. It also checks that the schemes to which def-amb-etale-compact-support is applied are Noetherian.

The imported proper-direct-image citation supplies a natural isomorphism but does not specify the counit-mate formula in its statement. The split additionally cites the existing lem-weil-pbc-comparison-direct-image-canonical-mate, whose statement fixes and proves invertibility of exactly that map. Its inverse has the direction needed here. No fact from a dependency's proof is used as if it were part of its statement.

The ordinary etale compact-support definition now uses an unbounded K-injective construction, whereas the imported comparison is stated on D+. Step 3 explicitly computes both etale interpretations on the same bounded-below injective complexes and on the same homotopy classes of maps. This preserves the ordinary unbounded functor's restriction, rather than silently choosing another functor.

The narrower definitions def-compact-support-direct-image-detailed and def-weil-derived-direct-image include an ell-invertibility hypothesis on the geometric base. This item itself defines its fixed pro-etale candidate by Rp_* j_!, and neither constituent comparison needs that hypothesis. I therefore did not invoke those narrower definitions as theorems on the item's arbitrary K_0, and did not add an invertibility hypothesis. Its Rp_* is the ordinary constant-coefficient operation of def-weil-pbc-proetale-operations. There is no compactification-independence claim here.
