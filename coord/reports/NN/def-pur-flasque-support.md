## Source error — STATEMENT-DISPUTED

The previous delivery established only that Stacks 09AH could not be applied without quasi-compactness. That was insufficient to establish the source error, exactly as the return says. This repair proves the unrestricted comparison false on an explicit allowed pair, rather than treating the hypothesis of a sufficient theorem as necessary.

Take a field $k$ (for example $\overline{\mathbf F}_p$), a nonzero finite coefficient ring $\Lambda$ (including $A_{E,1}$, with $\ell\ne p$), and

$$
 R=\prod_{n\ge1}k,\qquad J=\bigoplus_{n\ge1}k,\qquad
 X=\operatorname{Spec}R,\quad Z=\operatorname{Spec}(R/J).
$$

The coordinate idempotents $e_n$ cut out pairwise disjoint clopen points $X_n=D(e_n)\cong\operatorname{Spec}k$. A prime omitting $J$ omits some $e_n$ and hence contains $1-e_n$, so the open complement of $Z$ is exactly $U=\coprod_nX_n$. It is not quasi-compact: its covering by the $X_n$ has no finite subcover, whereas $X$ is affine.

Let $H_n$ be the sheaf on $X_{\mathrm{proet}}$ with

$$
 H_n(V)=\Lambda_{X_n}(V\times_XX_n),\quad
 P=\prod_nH_n,\quad S=\bigoplus_nH_n,\quad F=\operatorname{coker}(S\longrightarrow P).
$$

The arrow is a monomorphism of sheaves. To see the coproduct and the monomorphism concretely, define $S$ as the submodule sheaf of $P$ consisting of tuples which become finite-support tuples on a covering. The sheaf condition follows by gluing in $P$ and composing covers. Maps from this sheaf to any sheaf $T$ are exactly families of maps $H_n\to T$: define the map on local finite sums, check equality on overlaps coordinatewise, and glue. This proves its coproduct universal property; it also avoids assuming that infinite sheaf coproducts are objectwise direct sums.

For **affine** $V$, the finite affine refinement built into a pro-etale covering makes every section of $S(V)$ globally finite-support. Indeed, choose finitely many affine opens in members of its witnessing cover whose images cover $V$. Their finite supports have a finite union, and coordinates outside that union vanish by the sheaf condition. Thus $t=(1,1,\ldots)\in P(X)$ is not in $S(X)$. The abelian-category identity $S=\ker(P\to F)$ is sectionwise, so the image of $t$ in $F(X)$ is nonzero. This argument does **not** assume that the cokernel is computed sectionwise or that evaluation at $X$ is exact.

Each $s^{-1}H_n$ is zero. In the actual inverse-image colimit, refine any index $V$ to $V\times_X(X\setminus X_n)$; its pullback to $Z$ is unchanged, while the restriction of $H_n$ to that refinement is zero. Hence every generator of the colimit dies. Stacks **09AA**, clause (1), and the existing `lem-weil-pbc-proetale-immersion-limit-preservation` say that this closed inverse image preserves countable products **without** quasi-compactness of the complement. Therefore $s^{-1}P=0$. It preserves the coproduct $S$ by the module adjunction, and it preserves the quotient by exact finite constant-coefficient inverse image, so $s^{-1}F=0$. The constant-coefficient ring map is an isomorphism, as required by the immersion lemma's module clause.

On the localized site over $X_n$, both $S$ and $P$ reduce to $H_n$ and their map is identity. Thus $F|_{X_n}=0$ for every $n$, and the open covering of $U$ gives $j^*F=0$. Consequently

$$
 F\ne0,\qquad \mathcal G_ZF=F,\qquad s^!F=s^{-1}F=0,
 \qquad\mathcal G_ZF\longrightarrow s_*s^!F=(0)
$$

is not an isomorphism. This disproves the master's arbitrary-pair assertion, including its finite-coefficient scope. It does not assert that all non-quasi-compact complements fail. The counterexample is an affine, non-Noetherian $k$-scheme; the item itself imposes no finite-type hypothesis. The ordinary finite-type applications remain within the valid quasi-compact scope.

The repaired statement retains the kernel, closed inverse image, left exactness and pair-flasque predicate for **every** pair. It gives the canonical closed-support comparison under quasi-compactness and explicitly records that it can fail in general. This is now an established E7 correction, not merely an E1 obstruction in one proof attempt.

The other wording correction remains valid: “distinct from ordinary restriction” cannot mean unequal for each pair, because $Z=X$ gives $s^!=s^{-1}=\mathrm{id}$. The definition distinguishes the constructions and allows coincidence. No target statement, unit route, coefficient category or base-change map is altered.

## Downstream scope and concrete interface repair

The source correction has the following precise consequence: for arbitrary pairs, “supported on $Z$” as $j^*F=0$ describes a full subcategory of sheaves on **X**, which need not be equivalent to sheaves on the scheme **Z**. The kernel functor coreflects onto that subcategory by its elementary universal property. The replacement $\mathcal G_ZM=s_*s^!M$ requires quasi-compactness (or a separately proved condition guaranteeing that comparison). It is not licensed for arbitrary pairs or just because the sheaf is injective.

For the injective issue, embed the counterexample $F$ into an injective $I$ in the module-sheaf category (enough injectives on a small ringed site, Stacks 01DU). Since $j^*F=0$, its embedding factors through $\mathcal G_ZI$. If the latter were a closed direct image $s_*N$, the composite $F\hookrightarrow\mathcal G_ZI\cong s_*N$ would be zero: adjunction gives $\operatorname{Hom}_X(F,s_*N)=\operatorname{Hom}_Z(s^{-1}F,N)=0$. This contradicts monicity and $F\ne0$. Thus restricting the source's missing comparison to injective inputs does not fix it.
