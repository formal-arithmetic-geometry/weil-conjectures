## Sources and the statements left unsplit

I read Bhatt–Scholze 1309.1198 printed pp.34–35 (Definitions 5.1.3, Lemmas 5.1.1–5.1.4), p.39 (5.4.1), and pp.66–68 (Definitions/remarks 7.3.1–7.3.4, 7.3.6–7.3.9 and 7.3.11). The item statement is precisely the forward Loc_X to Cov_X assertion, specialized to locally Noetherian schemes, and is unchanged.

For the realization node I read Stacks `spaces-properties.tex`, Tag 0GF6, label `lemma-sheaf-gives-space`, including the construction immediately preceding it, the set-theoretic condition and the etale presentation proof following it. The condition is not dropped: the source category is restricted to spaces with section sets in the chosen sheaf universe, and the node explicitly permits enlarging the scheme universe to contain the set-indexed presentation, as is available for the item's small site and set-valued sheaf. The functor constructed there is natural in the sheaf; an etale space recovers its sections sheaf and conversely the construction recovers it, giving full faithfulness as well as existence. For comparison with the pro-etale site, apply BS 5.1.1 to the etale scheme charts and their overlap, using that inverse image preserves their quotient presentation; BS 5.4.1 gives the restriction/base-change compatibility. These are recorded in the general realization lemma, which is deliberately not split further in this delivery. This lemma has no separatedness or valuation conclusion and does not restate the item.

For the descent node I read the descent assertion in BS 7.3.9 and Stacks `spaces-descent.tex` Tags 0421 (`lemma-descending-property-separated`) and 04P2 (`lemma-fpqc-universal-effective-epimorphisms`). The node is the generic property-transfer lemma left for later splitting. The valuation argument underlying the source's descent assertion is as follows. First descend the closed diagonal by 0421. Given a valuation diagram over R, choose a point in a base-cover member above the closed point of Spec R, and a valuation ring R' dominating a local-domain quotient at that point and dominating R. Flatness prevents killing a nonzero element of R, and valuation domination makes R to R' faithfully flat. This gives Spec R' to the chosen cover member. Its fraction field extends K, so local valuation lifting gives a lift after R'. On Spec(R' tensor_R R') the two lifts agree on the generic fibre by the prescribed original K-map. That generic fibre is schematically dense, since this tensor product is R-flat; separatedness then makes the two lifts equal. The fpqc morphism sheaf condition 04P2 descends the lift to R, and separatedness also proves uniqueness. In particular this reasoning does not require q quasi-compact, does not equate valuation existence with universal closedness without a quasi-compactness hypothesis, and applies to general algebraic-space q. These non-elementary extension and descent facts belong to this unsplit generic lemma, not to an uncited step of the item.

For schemehood I read `spaces-morphisms.tex` Tag 03XU (`lemma-etale-locally-quasi-finite`) and Tag 03XX (`proposition-locally-quasi-finite-separated-over-scheme`), not just the Section 0417 heading. The source starts with an algebraic space, a scheme target, local quasi-finiteness and separatedness, and has no quasi-compactness hypothesis. Etaleness supplies local quasi-finiteness by 03XU. The new node is their standard separated-etale specialization, and does not assert representability for an arbitrary nonseparated etale space.
