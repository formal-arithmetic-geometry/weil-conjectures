1. On a local model $A[0]\oplus E[-1]$, tensor pullback is ordinary tensor in both degrees: finite free tensor is a finite sum of the identity and is exact. The truncation map is identity on the degree-one summand. Hence $\gamma_g$ is invertible. The assumed $\delta_g$ identifies the higher images with $g^*A,g^*E,0$ in degrees $0,1,>1$. The cocycle-to-cohomology-sheaf formula for that edge is linear: a cycle $az$ maps to $a[z]$. The other factors in $e_g$ are maps of modules, so $e_g$ is linear.

2. Use lem-pc-derived-base-change-horizontal-pasting for $g,h$. Append the derived-to-ordinary comparisons of def-pc-derived-module-base-change-map. Their composition identity follows by transposing the composite ordinary pullback unit: $(gh)_*(c)\,g_*(\eta_h)\,\eta_g=\eta_{gh}$, the unit equation in lem-weil-pbc-module-cohomology-fmap-functorial (2). Thus the composite module base-change maps equal $\delta_{gh}$ under the pullback-composition isomorphisms. Truncation is natural, so taking degree one gives the same identity for the $\beta$ maps.

Independence of resolutions is lem-weil-pbc-module-cohomology-fmap-functorial (1). Substituting the base-change identity from 2 yields $e_{gh}h_X^*=\rho_he_g$.
