Identify E^gp with fractions e/e' inside P^gp: equality of such fractions gives a cross equation in E by injectivity into P^gp, so every homomorphism E to a group extends on those fractions. Equality of pairs in H then gives exactly pe'=p'e and g f(e)=g'f(e'). Conversely those equations equate [p,g]=[pe',g/f(e')] and [p',g']=[p'e,g'/f(e)] in M. Thus M embeds into H. The embedding factors through M^gp by its presentation, proving integrality and cancellation. Applying the criterion to [1,g]=[1,g'] proves injection of G; applying it to [p,hg]=[p,g], cancelling p in P and then f(e)g in G, proves h=1.
