## Source error

The second reader opened both the arXiv v4 PDF (printed pp. 13-15) and the HTML, and Muro, *Homotopy theory of nonsymmetric operads*, Definitions 6.1 and 9.1 and Section 10 (printed pp. 1591, 1595-1597). Haugseng Definition 3.11 restricts the auxiliary objects of its nonsymmetric monoid axiom to cofibrant ones. That restriction makes the condition automatic by the pushout-product axiom (as Remark 3.12 itself observes), whereas the proof of Corollary 3.14 uses the map from the initial object to an arbitrary algebra. For instance the one-object dg algebra with mapping complex Z/2 in degree zero is not cofibrant as a complex (its degree-zero group is not projective), so its initial map is not among those allowed by the printed definition. This is an explicit failure of the hypothesis used in that proof, not a counterexample to the intended transfer theorem. Muro Definition 9.1 has arbitrary auxiliary objects, and Proposition 10.3 verifies precisely that condition for matrix graphs. The two new associative-algebra nodes use this correct condition explicitly. The projective-chain input already supplies the corresponding strong symmetric monoid axiom, and Step 3 checks its matrix version. The item's statement is unchanged; no target statement or definition has been changed.
