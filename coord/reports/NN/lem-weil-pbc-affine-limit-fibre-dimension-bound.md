## Source error

In limits.tex, Tag 05M5 (lemma-limit-dimension), the final displayed diagram asserts a closed immersion from a stage fibre after field extension into the limit fibre X_s. The available closed immersions point from later stages into earlier stages, and from the limit into earlier stages. This reversal is not a consequence of assumption (4). For example over a fixed field k, take the two-stage system X_0 = Spec k[t], X_1 = Spec k with the origin inclusion and constant base Spec k. The limit is Spec k, and the natural reverse map Spec k[t] to Spec k is not a closed immersion. This example diagnoses the inference from (4), not the bad-locus contradiction hypotheses. Also local dimension greater than d at a point says that some component through that point has dimension greater than d, not that all components do or that all components contain the point. The theorem itself is correct; no statement correction or STATEMENT-DISPUTED is needed. The proof now uses the whole base-extended stage fibres inside a fixed finite-type fibre, not an asserted stage-to-limit closed immersion.
