Hypothesis and clause correspondence:

- The commutative local ring A is `(A : Type u) [CommRing A] [IsRegularLocalRing A]`.
  Mathlib's IsRegularLocalRing extends both IsLocalRing and IsNoetherianRing; no
  separate Noetherian, domain, finite-type, excellence or characteristic hypothesis
  is added. The arbitrary universe u is explicitly declared and autoImplicit is false.
- Krull dimension two is `hdim : ringKrullDim A = (2 : WithBot ℕ∞)`.
- The field k is literally `IsLocalRing.ResidueField A = A ⧸ maximalIdeal A`.
  `IsLocalRing.residue A` is the quotient map, so its Spec map is the reduced closed
  point. This is the very local-ring residue-field construction used by the recorded
  Scheme.residueField declaration, not a private copy or an arbitrary field.
- `localSurfaceMaximalBlowupCenter A` is the kernel ideal sheaf of that Spec map.
  `(...Center A).blowup` and `.blowupMap` are the recorded global blowup and map,
  giving exactly Y = Bl_m(Spec A) and b. The centre is neither a chosen ideal nor a
  strengthened blowup datum.
- `localSurfaceMaximalBlowupExceptional A` is the categorical scheme pullback of b
  along `Spec(A/𝔪) → Spec A`. Thus it is the scheme-theoretic fibre E, with its
  structure sheaf, rather than just the set-theoretic preimage or its reduction.
  `localSurfaceMaximalBlowupExceptionalι A` is precisely `pullback.fst` into Y.
- Y Noetherian is `IsNoetherian (...Center A).blowup`. In Mathlib this means locally
  Noetherian AND quasi-compact, so the global conclusion is not weakened to local
  Noetherianity alone.
- Y regular is `IsRegularScheme (...Center A).blowup`, using the recorded predicate
  `IsLocallyNoetherian Y ∧ IsRegular Y`. The upstream IsRegular means all stalks
  are regular local. The local Noetherian condition is retained.
- E effective Cartier is `(...Exceptionalι A).ker.IsInvertible`. The inclusion is
  the base change of the residue quotient's closed immersion. Its kernel is the
  defining quasi-coherent ideal of this actual E. The recorded IsInvertible means
  each point has an affine neighbourhood on which this ideal is generated by a
  nonzerodivisor; it does not mean merely abstract invertibility of a line bundle.
- E isomorphic to P^1_k is the existential scheme isomorphism e to
  `ProjectiveSpace (ULift.{u} (Fin 2)) (Spec (.of (IsLocalRing.ResidueField A)))`.
  The base-change model indexes homogeneous coordinates, hence TWO coordinates,
  not Fin 1. The equality after e says its composite with the projective structure
  map is the canonical `pullback.snd`. Thus this is an isomorphism over k, as in
  source Tag 0AGQ, and not just an isomorphism of underlying spaces or schemes.
- Every closed q of E is `∀ q : ...Exceptional A, IsClosed ({q} : Set _) → ...`.
  This is closedness in E, not rationality over k and not just closedness in Y.
- O_{Y,q} is the structure-sheaf stalk of the actual blowup at the image of q under
  Exceptionalι. Both `IsRegularLocalRing` and `ringKrullDim ... = 2` refer to that
  ambient stalk. They do not mistakenly describe O_{E,q}, whose dimension is one.
