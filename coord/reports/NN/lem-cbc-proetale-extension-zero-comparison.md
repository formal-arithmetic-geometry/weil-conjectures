## Source error

The statement of Stacks 09AF is correct, but its one-line proof writes the common right adjoint as $j_{\mathrm{proet}}^{-1}\epsilon_* = \epsilon_*j_{\mathrm{et}}^{-1}$. Read literally with $M$ on $X_{\mathrm{proet}}$, the leftmost restriction is ill-typed: $\epsilon_{X*}M$ is on $X_{\mathrm{et}}$, so it must be restricted by $j_{\mathrm{et}}^{-1}$. The correctly typed identity is $j_{\mathrm{et}}^{-1}\epsilon_{X*}=\epsilon_{U*}j_{\mathrm{proet}}^{-1}$, proved sectionwise in step 3. This is a subscript correction in the source proof, not a change to the item's proposition or unit route. Definition 09AE (1) also prints the set-valued shriek arrow in the restriction direction; its module clause (3), which the proof uses, has the correct extension direction. Neither typo is reproduced in the split.
