Here is the proof obligation checked for truth: let the finitely many minimal primes of R be p_i. The map R[t,t^-1] to the product of (R/p_i)[t,t^-1] has nilpotent kernel, since the nilradical of a Noetherian ring is nilpotent. Its restriction to B therefore has nilpotent kernel. The kernels K_i on B are prime, and their intersection is nilpotent. Every minimal prime of B is one of the K_i (a prime containing the product of the finitely many K_i contains one, then use minimality). Each B/K_i embeds into (R/p_i)[t,t^-1], contains R/p_i, and has fraction-field transcendence degree at most one over Frac(R/p_i). Apply 0BAG to Spec B -> Spec R: every component's generic residue degree is at most one, giving dim B <= dim R + 1. This handles nonreduced R and avoids any catenarity hypothesis. The eventual worker can split this general algebraic bound using the existing generic-dimension theorem and minimal-prime lemmas.
