Step 2 cites `def-weil-pbc-localization-at-prime`, whose statement gives the fraction equality criterion and the local maximal ideal. I checked that the residue map really is x/s -> epsilon(x)/epsilon(s), with nonzero denominator. For a numerator in m, replacing s by its constant term changes the fraction by an element of n^2. Conversely, clearing a finite sum of fractions in n^2, including the equality witness, gives tx in m^2 with epsilon(t) nonzero; subtracting (t-epsilon(t))x proves x in m^2. These two formulas establish the cotangent isomorphism without assuming injectivity of B -> B_m or silently appealing to an uncited localization-comparison theorem.
