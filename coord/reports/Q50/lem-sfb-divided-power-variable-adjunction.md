## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the parenthetical clause asserted unique graded algebra isomorphisms fixing A and the variables, and then asserted they preserve divided powers. In positive characteristic, fixing T does not determine the divided symbols as an algebra map. Over F_3 with A=F_3 in degree zero and e=2, let a_1 be the digit at 3 in the base-3 expansion of a. The F_3-linear map T^[a] -> 2^(a_1) T^[a] is a degree-preserving algebra automorphism, fixes T, and sends T^[3] to 2T^[3]. Indeed a binomial coefficient is zero mod 3 when addition carries, while in the no-carry case the base-3 digits add and the scaling respects multiplication. Its square is the identity. It does not preserve gamma_3(T)=T^[3]. Thus the claimed underlying algebra uniqueness is false even for one even variable. The corrected parenthesis requires that the isomorphisms preserve divided powers; they then automatically fix every divided symbol. This is exactly the intended canonical identification in Stacks 0F4I. No adjunction, existence, uniqueness of the divided-power structures, or union clause is removed.

Stacks 09PI itself also incorrectly writes gamma_n(T^(i))=T^(ni). The given item's correction is necessary and was retained: gamma_2(T^[2])=3T^[4] over Q, since T^[2]=T^2/2! and gamma_2(z)=z^2/2!. Axiom 09PG(7) gives (na)!/(n!(a!)^n), an integer counting partitions into n unlabeled a-element blocks. The proof never divides in R.
