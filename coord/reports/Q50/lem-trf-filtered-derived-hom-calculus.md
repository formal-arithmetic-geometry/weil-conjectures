## Source error

Varshavsky §5.1.1(c) reverses the two adjunction directions: F^a is right adjoint to inclusion of the high-filtration subcategory, and quotient by F^{b+1} is left adjoint to inclusion of the low-filtration subcategory. For example, take the nonzero one-dimensional vector space k concentrated in cochain degree zero, first in filtration grade 0 and then in grade 1. There are nonzero filtered maps from grade 0 to grade 1, but F^1 of the grade-0 source is zero; thus F^1 cannot be the asserted left adjoint. Dually the quotient onto grade 0 cannot be the asserted right adjoint. This affects the source's explanatory sentence, not its truncation-triangle conclusion or this item's statement. Step 2 proves the required universal property in the correct direction using orthogonality and TR3; it never uses that erroneous sentence.
