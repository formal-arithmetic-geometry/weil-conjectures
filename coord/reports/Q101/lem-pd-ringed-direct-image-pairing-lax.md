## Proof plans retained for the new workers (not node proofs)

For bilinear transfer, read Stacks 03D6, definition-pushforward, and 03EK, section-tensor-product including the displayed Hom=Bilin identity. On C the pairing beta_U(x,y)=b_(vU)(x,y) commutes with restrictions by the sheaf-map property of b. For r in R(U), beta_U(c_U(r)x,y)=c_U(r)beta_U(x,y)=beta_U(x,c_U(r)y). The existing lem-pc-sites-sheaf-tensor-bilinear-universal therefore produces exactly one bar(b). For f,g,h satisfying the stated compatibility, both P(h) bar(b) and bar(b') (Pf tensor Pg) evaluate on x tensor y as h_(vU)(b_(vU)(x,y))=b'_(vU)(f_(vU)x,g_(vU)y). Injectivity of that same bijection proves equality. The R-linear restriction maps and functor P are checked from the scalar action as in original Step 1. No left adjoint, geometric inverse image, flatness, exactness, or monoidal structure on P is assumed.

For the trilinear helper, read 03EK and 03EO, lemma-internal-hom-adjoint-tensor, together with the all-slice internal Hom in 04TT, section-internal-hom. For a trilinear sheaf map t, send x,y over U to the section of Hom_R(T,H) whose component on V→U sends z to t_V(x|V,y|V,z). The restriction compatibility of t makes this a morphism on the slice, and separate linearity makes the resulting A×B→Hom_R(T,H) bilinear. The existing bilinear universal property gives A tensor B→Hom_R(T,H). Symmetry and the canonical closed adjunction uncurry it to (A tensor B) tensor T→H with the claimed value. Conversely any such morphism yields the stated trilinear sheaf map by composition with the canonical tensor pairing. On currying, the two operations are inverse because evaluation on every V→U determines an internal-Hom section. The bilinear universal property then determines the map to that internal Hom; adjunction determines the original map. Precomposition in the three inputs and postcomposition in H give the displayed naturality on every triple, and injectivity gives the naturality identities. This retains the original all-slices argument and also states its inverse construction so that the helper is stronger than an existing scalar-extension equality test. Relevant existing tools are lem-pc-sheaf-modules-tensor-closed, lem-pc-sites-sheaf-tensor-bilinear-universal and def-pur-sheaf-hom; they are proof-plan references here, not proof dependencies of the statement-only node.
