## Verbatim returned proof (retained argument, before re-splitting)

### Returned step 1

Restrict to the cofinal directed tail $I_{\ge i_0}$ and set $Y_i=Y_0\times_{S_{i_0}}S_i$, $Z_i=Z_0\times_{S_{i_0}}S_i$. The scheme-over-limit lemma gives affine transition maps and limits $Y,Z$. By qcqs permanence, the morphisms $Y_0,Z_0\to S_{i_0}$ are quasi-compact and quasi-separated; their base changes have these properties, so $Y_i,Z_i$ are qcqs. Similarly $f_0$ is quasi-compact, and base change preserves this and local finite presentation, so each $f_i$ is of finite type. The comparison $Y_j\to Y_i\times_{Z_i}Z_j$ is an isomorphism by the universal property of fibre products, hence a closed immersion. Thus the finite-stage bound applies with bases $Z_i$ and sources $Y_i$.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-scheme-over-limit, lem-weil-pbc-qcqs-morphism-permanence, lem-trf-limit-fibre-dimension-bound.

### Returned step 2

Applying the finite-stage fibre-dimension bound to the limit morphism $f:Y\to Z$, whose fibres have dimension at most $d$ because they are pure of dimension $d$, gives an index $i_1\ge i_0$ such that every fibre of $f_{i_1}$ has dimension at most $d$. Replacing $i_0$ by $i_1$ and reindexing the upper tail, assume from now on that every fibre of $f_0$ has dimension at most $d$.

Uses: lem-trf-limit-fibre-dimension-bound, def-weil-pure-dimension.

### Returned step 3

If $d>0$, let $U_0=\{y\in Y_0\mid\dim_y((Y_0)_{f_0(y)})\le d-1\}$. Since $d-1\ge0$ and $f_0$ is locally of finite presentation, the bounded local fibre-dimension locus is open and retrocompact by Tag 02G0. If $d=0$, use the same displayed set: it is empty because the local topological dimension at a point is nonnegative, directly from its definition. The empty open is retrocompact. In either case put $E=Y_0\setminus U_0$. It is constructible by the constructible-subset definition (take the retrocompact open $Y_0$ minus $U_0$).

Uses: lem-trf-retrocompact-bounded-fibre-dimension, def-weil-pbc-scheme-conventions, def-weil-constructible-subset, def-kun-topological-dimension-at-point.

### Returned step 4

For a scheme $F$ of finite type over a field, local dimension at a point is the maximum of the dimensions of the components through that point. Indeed choose an affine neighbourhood $V$: the affine dimension-at-point-components lemma gives the maximum for $V$, its components are the intersections with the components of $F$ that meet $V$, and the irreducible locally algebraic dimension lemma identifies their dimensions with those of the corresponding components of $F$. Thus a pure $d$-dimensional fibre has local dimension $d$ at every point. For $y\in Y$, let $y_0\in Y_0$ be its image. Tag 02FY identifies the local fibre dimensions at $y$ and $y_0$. If $y_0\in U_0$, this contradicts the local dimension $d$ at $y$. Hence the image of $Y\to Y_0$ lies in $E$. The fibres here are of finite type over their residue fields, since $f$ is a base change of the finite-type morphism $f_0$.

Uses: lem-sc-fibre-dimension-after-base-change, def-weil-pure-dimension, def-pc-scheme-fibre, def-weil-constructible-subset, lem-weil-pbc-dimension-at-point-components, lem-sc-dimension-locally-algebraic, lem-weil-pbc-qcqs-morphism-permanence.

### Returned step 5

Apply the constructible-containment lemma to the system $(Y_i)_{i\ge i_0}$, its limit $Y$, the stage $Y_0$, and $E$. It gives $i\ge i_0$ such that for every $j\ge i$ the image of $Y_j\to Y_0$ is contained in $E$.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-limit-contained-in-constructible, def-weil-constructible-subset, lem-weil-pbc-scheme-over-limit.

### Returned step 6

At the index $i$ obtained in Step 5, take $y_i\in Y_i$ with image $y_0\in E$. The upper bound for $f_0$ from Step 2 and Tag 02FY give local fibre dimension at most $d$ at $y_i$. Membership in $E$ says the local fibre dimension at $y_0$ is greater than $d-1$; Tag 02FY gives the same lower bound at $y_i$. Thus every point of every nonempty fibre of $f_i$ has local dimension $d$. To obtain purity, take the generic point of each component of such a fibre. In an affine neighbourhood of that point only that component passes through it; the affine dimension-at-point-components lemma and invariance of dimension on nonempty opens of an irreducible locally algebraic scheme identify its component dimension with the local dimension $d$. Hence $f_i$ has pure $d$-dimensional nonempty fibres. For every $j\ge i$, the morphism $f_j$ is the base change of $f_i$ along $Z_j\to Z_i$, so the pure-relative-dimension base-change lemma gives the required purity at every later stage.

Uses: lem-sc-fibre-dimension-after-base-change, def-weil-pure-dimension, def-pc-scheme-fibre, lem-weil-pbc-dimension-at-point-components, lem-sc-dimension-locally-algebraic, lem-trf-relative-dimension-base-change.
