### Old step 1: parent step 1 (decomposition/well-order), exhaustion task (filtration specification)

Put $N=B\otimes_A M$. Apply [lem-trf-projective-countable-decomposition] over $B$ to obtain $N\cong\bigoplus_{i\in I}Q_i$, with every $Q_i$ countably generated and projective. Regard this as a fixed coordinate decomposition of $N$. By [lem-weil-pbc-well-ordering-theorem], well-order the underlying set of $M$, and write its elements as $(e_\alpha)_{\alpha<\kappa}$ in that order, where $\kappa$ is the order type. We construct a continuous increasing filtration $(M_\alpha)_{\alpha\le\kappa}$ of $M$ such that $B\otimes_A M_\alpha$, embedded in $N$, is a coordinate sum $\bigoplus_{i\in I_\alpha}Q_i$. Every successor quotient will be countably generated projective. The construction and its verification are given in Steps 3--8.

Original cited statements: lem-trf-projective-countable-decomposition, lem-weil-pbc-well-ordering-theorem.

### Old step 2: lem-trf-coordinate-projective-successor

We record the tensor identifications used in the construction. Faithful flatness makes $B$ flat over $A$ by [def-weil-pbc-faithfully-flat-ring-map]. Thus [lem-weil-pbc-flat-tensor-exact] identifies $B\otimes_A U$ with a submodule of $N$ for every $A$-submodule $U\subseteq M$. Applying [lem-weil-pbc-tensor-right-exact] to $U\to M\to M/U\to0$ identifies $B\otimes_A(M/U)$ with $N/(B\otimes_A U)$, by $b\otimes(m+U)\mapsto(b\otimes m)+(B\otimes_A U)$. The same two results apply to any submodule of $M/U$ and to $0\to U\to V\to V/U\to0$ for $U\subseteq V\subseteq M$. In particular, if $B\otimes_A U=\bigoplus_{i\in H}Q_i$, projection to the remaining coordinates gives $B\otimes_A(M/U)\cong\bigoplus_{i\notin H}Q_i$. Its kernel consists exactly of families supported in $H$, and every remaining-coordinate family lifts by inserting zeros.

Original cited statements: def-weil-pbc-faithfully-flat-ring-map, lem-weil-pbc-flat-tensor-exact, lem-weil-pbc-tensor-right-exact.

### Old step 3: lem-trf-coordinate-projective-exhaustion

Start with $M_0=0$ and $I_0=\varnothing$. At a limit ordinal $\lambda\le\kappa$ put $M_\lambda=\bigcup_{\alpha<\lambda}M_\alpha$. At a successor stage where $M_\alpha=M$, keep $M_{\alpha+1}=M$ and $I_{\alpha+1}=I_\alpha$, and take the successor quotient to be zero. Otherwise choose the least element $x\in M\setminus M_\alpha$ in the fixed well-order. Steps 5--7 give the successor construction. These are choices from sets of submodules and linear maps, so transfinite recursion on the set ordinal $\kappa+1$ constructs the filtration once the successor and limit assertions are verified.

Original cited statements: (none).

### Old step 4: lem-trf-coordinate-projective-exhaustion

At a limit $\lambda$, each element of $B\otimes_A M_\lambda$ is a finite sum $\sum_r b_r\otimes m_r$, with all the finitely many $m_r$ in some common $M_\alpha$, $\alpha<\lambda$. This uses the finite-tensor-expression assertion of [def-weil-pbc-module-tensor-product]. Therefore its image in $N$ is exactly the union of the earlier tensor images. The coordinate submodules are increasing. We may discard any indices with $Q_i=0$ at the start, so their indexing subsets are increasing as well. Their union is exactly the coordinate sum indexed by $I_\lambda=\bigcup_{\alpha<\lambda}I_\alpha$: a family has finite support, so all its support indices lie in one earlier $I_\alpha$. This verifies the required invariant at limits, without an additional theorem on tensor products and colimits.

Original cited statements: def-weil-pbc-module-tensor-product, def-weil-pbc-direct-sum-modules.

### Old step 5: lem-trf-coordinate-projective-successor

At a nonconstant successor stage, put $L=M/M_\alpha$ and let $\bar x\ne0$ be the image of the least missing element. Step 2 gives $B\otimes_A L\cong\bigoplus_{i\notin I_\alpha}Q_i$. The cyclic submodule $C=A\bar x\subseteq L$ is countably generated. Apply [lem-trf-countable-submodule-adapted-to-base-change-sum] to this decomposition and $C$. It gives a countably generated $P\subseteq L$ containing $\bar x$ and $J\subseteq I\setminus I_\alpha$, whose tensor image is $\bigoplus_{j\in J}Q_j$. Flat tensor exactness in Step 2 makes $B\otimes_A P\to B\otimes_A L$ injective, so $B\otimes_A P$ itself is identified with that coordinate sum, not merely a quotient of it.

Original cited statements: lem-trf-countable-submodule-adapted-to-base-change-sum.

### Old step 6: lem-trf-coordinate-projective-successor

The module $B\otimes_A P$ is projective: its inclusion in $N$ and the coordinate projection back to the $J$-coordinates are split maps, so [lem-weil-pbc-projective-direct-summand] applies to the projective $B$-module $N$. If $(p_n)$ is a countable generating family of $P$, the elements $1\otimes p_n$ generate $B\otimes_A P$ over $B$: expand each pure tensor $b\otimes p$ after writing $p$ as a finite $A$-linear combination of the $p_n$. The tensor-product presentation [def-weil-pbc-module-tensor-product] justifies this expansion and that pure tensors generate. Consequently [lem-trf-countable-projective-faithfully-flat-descent], for this same faithfully flat map $A\to B$ and the module $P$, gives that $P$ is projective over $A$.

Original cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-projective-direct-summand, def-weil-pbc-module-tensor-product, lem-trf-countable-projective-faithfully-flat-descent.

### Old step 7: lem-trf-coordinate-projective-successor (preimage/invariant), parent step 4 and existing lem-trf-split-transfinite-module-filtration (sections/complements)

Define $M_{\alpha+1}$ as the inverse image of $P$ under $M\to L$. Thus $M_{\alpha+1}/M_\alpha\cong P$ and $x\in M_{\alpha+1}$. The quotient map restricts to a surjection $M_{\alpha+1}\to P$ with kernel $M_\alpha$. Step 2, applied to this sequence, says that the image of $B\otimes_A M_{\alpha+1}$ in $N$ maps onto the $J$-coordinate sum in $N/(B\otimes_A M_\alpha)$, and has kernel $B\otimes_A M_\alpha$. It is therefore precisely the inverse image of that sum, namely $\bigoplus_{i\in I_\alpha\cup J}Q_i$: lift a desired quotient element to the image and subtract; the difference lies in the already contained kernel. This establishes the successor invariant. Because $P$ is projective by Step 6, [lem-weil-pbc-ses-projective-quotient-splits] gives a section $s_\alpha:P\to M_{\alpha+1}$. Put $F_\alpha=\operatorname{im}(s_\alpha)$; then $M_{\alpha+1}=M_\alpha\oplus F_\alpha$, and $F_\alpha\cong P$ is projective by [lem-weil-pbc-projective-direct-summand]. At a constant stage set $F_\alpha=0$, also projective by the lifting definition.

Original cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-ses-projective-quotient-splits, lem-weil-pbc-projective-direct-summand, def-weil-pbc-projective-module.

### Old step 8: lem-trf-coordinate-projective-exhaustion

The filtration exhausts $M$ at stage $\kappa$. Indeed, transfinite induction proves that $e_\beta\in M_\alpha$ whenever $\beta<\alpha\le\kappa$. At a successor stage all earlier elements are already present. If $e_\alpha$ is missing, it is the least missing element and Step 7 adds it; if it is present there is nothing to prove for it. At a limit stage the claim follows from the union definition. Thus every element $e_\beta$ is in $M_{\beta+1}\subseteq M_\kappa$, and $M_\kappa=M$. This also shows that no recursion over a proper class of ordinals is needed.

Original cited statements: (none).

### Old step 9: existing lem-trf-split-transfinite-module-filtration

The chosen complements give $M\cong\bigoplus_{\alpha<\kappa}F_\alpha$. To verify this, use transfinite induction on $\gamma\le\kappa$ for the assertion that the addition map $\bigoplus_{\alpha<\gamma}F_\alpha\to M_\gamma$ is an isomorphism. It holds at $0$. At a successor it follows by adjoining the split complement from Step 7. At a limit, every element belongs to an earlier $M_\delta$, hence has a finite expansion in the earlier complements; a finite relation among complements has all its indices below a common $\delta<\gamma$, so its coefficients vanish by the earlier-stage injectivity. These are exactly surjectivity and injectivity for the direct sum of [def-weil-pbc-direct-sum-modules]. Apply the assertion at $\gamma=\kappa$ and use Step 8.

Original cited statements: def-weil-pbc-direct-sum-modules.

### Old step 10: parent step 5

Each $F_\alpha$ is projective by Step 7, so [lem-weil-pbc-projective-direct-sum-family] makes $\bigoplus_{\alpha<\kappa}F_\alpha$ projective. Transport projectivity along the isomorphism of Step 9 using clause (2) of [lem-weil-pbc-projective-direct-summand]. Hence $M$ is projective over $A$, as asserted.

Original cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-projective-direct-sum-family, lem-weil-pbc-projective-direct-summand.
