## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the original third clause is false if a common refinement requires strictly commuting diagrams of chain maps. Take the abelian category of abelian groups, $K=\mathbf Z[0]$, and $L=(\mathbf Z\xrightarrow{1}\mathbf Z)$ in degrees -1 and 0, the contractible cone of the identity of $\mathbf Z[0]$. Let $h:K\to L$ be identity in degree 0. It is a chain map, null homotopic via the identity $K^0\to L^{-1}$. The roofs $(K,1,h)$ and $(K,1,0)$ give the same zero derived morphism. A strict common refinement would have $t_1=t_2=t:P\to K$ and $ht=0$. In degree 0 this forces $t^0=0$, so $H^0(t)=0$ and $t$ cannot be a quasi-isomorphism to $K$.

The corrected third clause asks for a bounded $P$ and quasi-isomorphisms $v_i:P\to M_i$ with $s_1v_1\simeq s_2v_2$ and $g_1v_1\simeq g_2v_2$. The statement explicitly defines chain homotopy and says that roof equivalence is in $K(R)$. This is the intended localization route in Stacks 05RT/05RU/05RW. Existence of bounded objects and bounded chain-map roofs is unchanged. No ring hypothesis is added. Transport between representative isomorphisms is made explicit.

Thus the homotopies in our conclusion become equality in its filtered homotopy category.
