Step 2 uses the affine-pairs statement for finite algebra generation, the affine fibre-product formula for the actual tensor fibre (not the quotient A/pA), the finite-type-over-a-field dimension theorem, both pullback-preservation statements, the finite-type support-closed theorem, and the definition of dimension as chains and the minimum over neighbourhoods.
