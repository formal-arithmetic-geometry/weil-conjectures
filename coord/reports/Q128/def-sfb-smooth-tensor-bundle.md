2. def-weil-pbc-module-tensor-product states the quotient construction, universal property and generation by pure tensors. I read its entire statement. For fixed x the bilinear map (v,w) to phi_i(v) tensor psi_i(w) therefore defines tau_i. Applying the same universal property to the two inverse fibre maps gives its inverse: their composites fix every pure tensor, hence every element. This is a two-line consequence of the cited definition, not a new lemma node. For the model identification, the bilinear map (u,z) to the array (u_a z_d) induces a linear map to R^(Fin b times Fin c). Its inverse sends an array t to the finite sum of t_(a,d) e_a tensor f_d. One composite fixes coordinate vectors; the other fixes every pure tensor by bilinear expansion. Thus the model really has rank bc. This check does not assume a basis theorem for a different tensor construction.

3. On a common overlap, apply tau_j tau_i^{-1} to a pure tensor and obtain (g_ji u) tensor (h_ji z). Generation by pure tensors makes this the asserted tensor operator on every vector. Its row (a,d), column (k,l) entry is exactly g_ji,ak h_ji,dl. Products of smooth scalar functions are smooth by the ordinary product rule in the base charts; there is no functional-analytic tensor completion or infinite-dimensional smoothness claim. The reverse transition is g_ij tensor h_ij: applying both to a pure tensor gives u tensor z. On triple overlaps the product has coefficient sum over k,l, which separates as (sum_k g_kj,ak g_ji,kr)(sum_l h_kj,dl h_ji,ls); it equals the coefficient of g_ki tensor h_ki. Equivalently both compositions agree on pure tensors. These are routine identities from the universal property and the supplied original bundle transitions, not a hidden existence theorem.

4. For each x choose one of the common trivializations containing x and give its fibre tensor space the topology pulled back from R^bc. Every other such fibre coordinate map differs by an invertible finite matrix, with inverse the reverse transition, so it induces exactly the same topology. This supplies the inducing hypothesis in lem-sfb-smooth-vector-prebundle-realization, rather than assuming it for an untopologized tensor product. The common cover and chosen indices give the covering fields; tau_i is linear and bijective by (2); the transition maps in (3) are continuous and smooth on the overlaps. They can be extended by zero outside the overlap when the prebundle fields request globally defined maps, since regularity is required only on the open overlap. All hypotheses of the new node are therefore present. Its statement says precisely that the displayed final topology makes these maps bundle trivializations and the bundle smooth, so this non-elementary realization is an actual cited dependency, not a claim taken from the proof of a definition node. The input data and quotient are concrete; no data is selected from a sorried existence theorem.

5. Changing the starting atlases does not alter the defined smooth bundle structure. A chart from either construction overlaps a chart from the other; their transition is again the same tensor product of the two original bundle chart changes, hence is smooth in both directions by (3). The chart criterion for openness then makes the two topologies equal: restrict to each overlap, where both coordinate descriptions are related by a homeomorphism, and use the covering property. The maximal compatible smooth bundle atlases agree by their defining compatibility condition.
