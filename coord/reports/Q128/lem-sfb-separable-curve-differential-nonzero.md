### Original proof, retained verbatim

Original step 1:

Let $\eta_X,\eta_Y$ be the generic points and put $L=k(X)$ and $K=k(Y)$ [def-sc-function-field]. Dominance gives $f(\eta_X)=\eta_Y$: continuity gives $f(X)\subseteq\overline{\{f(\eta_X)\}}$, and density of $f(X)$ makes this closure all of $Y$, whose generic point is unique. The given function-field extension is the resulting local-ring map $K\to L$. For each $Z\in\{X,Y\}$, every nonempty affine $W=\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq Z$ contains $\eta_Z$ and has $A$ a domain with $k(Z)=\operatorname{Frac}(A)$ via the germ map, by [lem-sc-integral-function-field-affine-fraction].

Original step 2:

The structure morphism $X\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ is smooth of relative dimension one. Indeed its sole fibre is $X$, which is integral of dimension one [def-pc-curve]; as $X$ is irreducible and of finite type over $k$, $\dim_x(X)=\dim X=1$ for every $x\in X$ by [lem-sc-dimension-locally-algebraic]; this is the fibre characterization of smooth relative dimension in [def-sbc-smooth-morphisms]. Thus [lem-sc-smooth-omega-finite-locally-free], with $d=1$, makes $\Omega_{X/k}$ locally free of rank one. In particular, its stalk at $\eta_X$ is a free $L$-module of rank one: a local trivialization [def-amb-finite-locally-free-module] gives a basis germ. It is nonzero, since $L$ is a field and its basis vector cannot be zero.

Original step 3:

For $Z=X$ or $Y$, choose a nonempty affine $W=\operatorname{Spec}A\subseteq Z$. By [def-sc-relative-differential-sheaf], $\Omega_{Z/k}|_W$ is associated to $\Omega_{A/k}$, compatibly with universal derivations. Its generic stalk is $(A\setminus\{0\})^{-1}\Omega_{A/k}$ by [lem-weil-pbc-spec-tilde-stalk-localization], using the generic prime $(0)$. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-kahler-differentials-localization] to $k\to A$ with $S=A\setminus\{0\}$ and the fraction field of Step 1. We obtain $$(\Omega_{Y/k})_{\eta_Y}\cong\Omega_{K/k},\qquad (\Omega_{X/k})_{\eta_X}\cong\Omega_{L/k},$$ with the germ of $da$ mapping to $d(a/1)$. The second module is nonzero by Step 2.

Original step 4:

Apply [lem-weil-pbc-module-stalk-pullback] at $\eta_X$ to $\mathcal G=\Omega_{Y/k}$. With Step 3 and the flip of tensor factors this identifies $$(f^*\Omega_{Y/k})_{\eta_X}\cong L\otimes_K\Omega_{K/k}.$$ Under these coordinates $(df)_{\eta_X}$ sends $a\otimes db$ to $a\,db$ in $\Omega_{L/k}$. Here is the map check: choose an affine neighbourhood $V$ of $\eta_Y$ and an affine neighbourhood $U\subseteq f^{-1}V$ of $\eta_X$. Every $b\in K=\mathcal O_{Y,\eta_Y}$ is represented by a regular function on a possibly smaller neighbourhood of $\eta_Y$. For such a representative, [def-a1-relative-differentials-pullback-map] sends the pullback of $db$ to the differential of its image, and the stalk comparison sends a pulled-back germ to $db\otimes1$. Multiplying by $a\in L$ gives the asserted formula. The symbols $db$ generate $\Omega_{K/k}$ by its defining presentation [def-weil-pbc-kahler-differentials], so this determines the entire map.

Original step 5:

The tower $k\to K\to L$ consists of fields, and $L/K$ is finite separable by hypothesis, exactly the hypotheses of [lem-weil-pbc-separable-algebraic-differentials-base-change]. That node makes the map $L\otimes_K\Omega_{K/k}\to\Omega_{L/k}$, $a\otimes db\mapsto a\,db$, an isomorphism. By Step 4 this is $(df)_{\eta_X}$. Its target is nonzero by Steps 2--3, so this stalk map cannot be zero: a zero map cannot carry the inverse image of a nonzero vector to that vector.

Original step 6:

If $df$ were zero as a morphism of sheaves, its induced map on germs at every point would send every germ to zero, in particular at $\eta_X$. This contradicts Step 5. Therefore $df\ne0$.
