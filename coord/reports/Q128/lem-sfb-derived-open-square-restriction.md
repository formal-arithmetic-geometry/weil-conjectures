## Original eight-step handoff (verbatim)

### Original step 1

For any map $f$ in the square, [sk-prop-sfb-cartesian-coefficients] identifies $f^{-1}\Lambda_{\mathrm{target}}$ with $\Lambda_{\mathrm{source}}$ and makes the module pullback exact. For $h=i,j$, [sk-def-sfb-module-lower-shriek] gives the exact module functor $h_!$ and its adjunction with $h^*$. Its underlying sheaf adjunction is the abelian open adjunction. By [lem-sfb-finite-extension-by-zero-fully-faithful], applied with $\mathcal A=\Ab$, the abelian unit is invertible. By [def-sfb-module-open-lower-shriek-adjunction] the module adjunction $h_!\dashv h^*$ is the composite of $\operatorname{Res}_{\alpha_h}\dashv\operatorname{Coext}_{\alpha_h}\cong\operatorname{Ext}_{\alpha_h}$, whose unit is invertible because $\alpha_h$ is a ring isomorphism, with the linearized adjunction $h^{\mathrm{lin}}_!\dashv\operatorname{mapMod}(h^*_{\Ab})$, whose unit has as underlying map the abelian unit. The latter is therefore an invertible module-linear map, and its inverse is module-linear (multiply the linearity equation on the two sides by the inverse). So the module unit is a composite of two isomorphisms. Consequently the actual units $1\to i^*i_!$ and $1\to j^*j_!$ are isomorphisms.

Cited statements: `sk-prop-sfb-cartesian-coefficients`, `sk-def-sfb-module-lower-shriek`, `lem-sfb-finite-extension-by-zero-fully-faithful`, `def-sfb-module-open-lower-shriek-adjunction`.

### Original step 2

Apply [lem-weil-pbc-termwise-adjunction-homotopy] to each $h_!\dashv h^*$, $h=i,j$. Its unit and counit on complexes have degree-$q$ components the sheaf unit and counit. Both functors are exact, so [lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-factorization] gives their unbounded derived functors and their factorization through localization at quasi-isomorphisms. Apply [lem-sfb-adjunction-localization] to these two factorizations. This produces $D(h_!)\dashv D(h^*)$, with unit and counit the localized termwise maps, under the specified composition identifications. In particular the units $\eta_i,\eta_j$ are invertible, because their termwise representatives from step 1 are invertible in every degree.

Cited statements: `lem-weil-pbc-termwise-adjunction-homotopy`, `lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-factorization`, `lem-sfb-adjunction-localization`.

### Original step 3

Use [lem-sf-ring-sheaf-base-change-mates] with ambient scheme $\overline Y$, ring object $B=\Lambda_{\overline Y}$, open immersion $i$, the reduced closed complement of $Y$ as closed immersion, and $g=r$; the qcqs hypotheses hold for the finite-type separated schemes here. Its open comparison is an isomorphism $r^{-1}i^{\mathrm{sf}}_!N\cong j^{\mathrm{sf}}_!p^{\mathrm{sq}}N$ for $i^{-1}B$-modules $N$, where, by [def-sf-proetale-ring-sheaf-operations], $r^{-1}$ and $p^{-1}$ are mapMod of the abelian inverse images, $h^{\mathrm{sf}}_!$ is mapModLeft of the abelian open adjunction, and $p^{\mathrm{sq}}$ is $p^{-1}$ followed by restriction of scalars through the square coefficient isomorphism $c:j^{-1}r^{-1}B\cong p^{-1}i^{-1}B$, the component at $B$ of the inverse-image composition comparison for $rj=ip$. On the other side, [sk-def-sfb-module-pushforward-pullback] gives $r^*=\operatorname{Ext}_{\alpha_r}\circ r^{-1}$ and $p^*=\operatorname{Ext}_{\alpha_p}\circ p^{-1}$, and [sk-def-sfb-module-lower-shriek] gives $h_!=h^{\mathrm{lin}}_!\circ\operatorname{Res}_{\alpha_h}$, where $h^{\mathrm{lin}}_!$ is the same linearization of $h^{\Ab}_!$ as $h^{\mathrm{sf}}_!$. All $\alpha$'s are ring isomorphisms by [sk-prop-sfb-cartesian-coefficients], and $\operatorname{Ext}_{\alpha}\cong\operatorname{Res}_{\alpha^{-1}}$ by [def-sfb-modobj-extension-along-iso]. Restriction of scalars along a ring isomorphism commutes with $h^{\mathrm{lin}}_!$: both have underlying sheaf $h^{\Ab}_!M$, and the actions agree since the projection-formula isomorphism of [sk-def-sfb-module-lower-shriek] is natural in the ring factor.

### Original step 4

By step 3, for $T\in\Mod_{\Lambda_Y}$ both $r^*i_!T$ and $j_!p^*T$ are identified, through the comparison of [lem-sf-ring-sheaf-base-change-mates], with $j^{\Ab}_!p^{-1}T$, the $\Lambda_U$-action on $p^{-1}T$ being obtained through the ring isomorphisms $\varphi_1=\alpha_j\circ j^{-1}(\alpha_r)\circ c^{-1}$, resp. $\varphi_2=\alpha_p\circ p^{-1}(\alpha_i)$, from $p^{-1}i^{-1}\Lambda_{\overline Y}$ to $\Lambda_U$.
