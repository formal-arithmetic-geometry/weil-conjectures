### Original step 1

Put $R=\mathcal O_E$ and $A_m=R/\varpi_E^m$. The valuation ring is a DVR with finite residue field, hence is Noetherian, and each $A_m$ is finite. Finite-type schemes over the field $k$ are Noetherian. All opens in question are quasi-compact since their source schemes are Noetherian. Base change and composition of proper morphisms show that the proper projections and the product structural map $c$ used below are proper. These check the coefficient, scheme and properness hypotheses of the tower proper/open comparison; they do not impose any additional condition on the compactifications. Completeness of $E$ also gives $R\cong\lim_m R/\varpi_E^m$: representatives of compatible residue classes are Cauchy since successive differences lie in $\varpi_E^mR$; their limit has absolute value at most one and represents every residue class. Injectivity follows since $|\varpi_E|^m\to0$. Thus $\varpi_E$ is a non-zero-divisor and the complete-ring hypothesis of the coefficient-system comparison is satisfied.
