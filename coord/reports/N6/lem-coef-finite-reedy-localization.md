## Problems found and changes

The item's statement is consistent with the sources and has not been weakened or restricted. No STATEMENT-DISPUTED correction is needed. The inherited proof was empty. The ancestry describes infrastructure below the second proof paragraph of prop-coef-integral-exceptional-adjoint; that paragraph does not itself prove the general model-localization foundation. The new proof supplies an actual finite induction rather than creating separate children asserting the direct comparison, inverse comparison, and (co)limit clauses.

The diagram comparison is not deduced from HTT 4.2.4.4 or from the existing dg Quillen-section rectification node: those have extra combinatorial/enrichment hypotheses. The current proof applies to every model category in the item. Likewise Mathlib's Adjunction.derived is an ordinary-category adjunction, not an infinity-categorical one.

The induction must be on maximal chain length, not on the number of objects. A category with two parallel arrows s → j has two objects in its outgoing boundary at s although it has only two original objects; larger parallel families make the boundary larger than the original category. Each boundary still has shorter chain length. This was checked and built into step 4. The right adjoints used for frame restriction/evaluation were spelled out, so no uncited right-Kan-extension existence theorem is hidden in step 6. Actual cones and cocones are tracked by the derived counit and unit, rather than by an isomorphism in the ordinary homotopy category.
