## Source error

In `literature/stacks-project/spaces-properties.tex`, Tag 03H6 (`lemma-quasi-compact-affine-cover`), the last sentence of the proof calls the finite union's map a “finite surjective morphism”. The proof establishes **etale surjective**, as the lemma states, and does not establish a finite morphism. For example over a field the map
$\mathbb A^1\amalg\mathbb G_m\to\mathbb A^1$
which is the identity on the first summand and the standard open immersion on the second is etale surjective with affine source. It is not finite: its second open-and-closed summand would then be finite over $\mathbb A^1$, whereas $k[t,t^{-1}]$ is not finite over $k[t]$. This is a typo in the source proof, not a disputed item statement. The corrected proof takes representable surjective etaleness from the finite-subatlas node. It never asserts finiteness of the atlas morphism.
