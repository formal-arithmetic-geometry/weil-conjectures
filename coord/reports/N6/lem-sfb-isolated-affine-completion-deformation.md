## Source arguments retained for the new inputs

For lem-sfb-adic-completion-flat-deformation-functor I read examples-defos.tex Tag 0DYV, algebra.tex Tag 0DYC and the surrounding Noetherian completion results. A deformation Q has nilpotent finitely generated ideal m_A Q with Noetherian quotient B. The nilpotent-ideal adic filtration makes Q Noetherian (equivalently apply completion-Noetherian with that nilpotent ideal, whose completion is Q). Its J_Q-adic completion is flat over Q, hence A, and finite-module exact completion identifies its special fibre with B-hat. For base change to A_1, the Artinian k-algebra A_1 is a finite A-module. Complete a finite presentation after tensoring with the A-flat Q: finite-module completion gives Q-hat tensor_A A_1 as the completion of Q tensor_A A_1 as a Q-module. The extended ideal J_Q(Q tensor_A A_1) and the inverse image of J differ only by a nilpotent ideal coming from the maximal ideal of A_1; their power filtrations are cofinal. This gives the stated ring isomorphism and its naturality. If B is already complete, apply 0DYC to Q with I=m_A Q and the inverse image of J: Q is I-adically complete since I is nilpotent. If B is local and J its maximal ideal, nilpotence of the special-fibre kernel shows Q is local with the claimed maximal ideal.

For lem-sfb-completion-reflects-flat-liftability I read 0DZ3's statement and full polynomial-presentation/module-lifting proof. Its hypotheses are Noetherian bases and nilpotent kernel, finite-type flat Q, and syntomicity away from the completion ideal. For the specialized input stated here, Artinian bases provide the first hypotheses. A finite-type algebra over a Noetherian ring is finitely presented. Outside J_Q the only base fibre is an open of P smooth over k, so algebra.tex lemma-flat-fibre-smooth makes Q smooth there, hence syntomic. Thus 0DZ3 applies. The source converts cyclic module lifts over a polynomial presentation and its completion into algebra lifts, using Nakayama, and compares module obstruction/liftability; it does not provide prescribed-marking realization. That stronger task is left to the criterion, not silently included in this input.

For lem-sfb-exal-completion-smooth-complement I read the Exal definition immediately before 0DZ4 and all of its proof. Positive naive-cotangent Ext has support in V(J); comparison with quotients N/J^n N proves injectivity. For surjectivity pull an extension of B-hat back to B, identify Ext with completed coefficients using finite-module completion and pseudo-coherence, then descend its class from N-hat to N. The difference extension becomes split over B, and that splitting extends continuously to B-hat because the extension ring is Noetherian and complete modulo its nilpotent kernel. This forces the difference to vanish. Completion is natural in coefficients and extensions, so the resulting bijection respects Baer sum and k scalars. The new statement is general in finite N, with no isolated-point or tangent assertion.

For lem-sfb-ring-deformation-smoothness-test I read examples-defos.tex 0DY2 (RS), 0DY3 (tangent calculation), formal-defos.tex 0DYP and its cited small-extension and free-transitive-action proofs. Flat dual-number deformations give exactly square-zero extensions via epsilon multiplication. RS supplies the tangent action on isomorphism classes of marked lifts. Across a small extension the existence hypothesis supplies one source lift; the difference between its image and the specified target lift is a target tangent vector tensored with the one-dimensional kernel. Tangent surjectivity lifts that difference and adjusts the source lift. Factor a general Artinian surjection into small extensions and keep the reduction isomorphisms at every stage. This proves marked smoothness, not an assertion of full faithfulness on automorphisms. This general input concerns arbitrary ring deformation groupoids and has no completion hypothesis.
