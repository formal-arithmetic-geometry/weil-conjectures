## Source error

The proof of 0GS1 says to choose a lower target index `b'' <= min(b,b')`, establish equality after mapping into `S_b''`, and then concludes the triple order is directed. Its displayed order requires an upper target index. Equality at a lower target alone gives no such upper bound; moreover, a general directed poset need not have common lower bounds. For example, the poset of nonempty finite subsets of `{1,2}` ordered by inclusion is directed, but `{1}` and `{2}` have no common lower bound. Thus the printed choice itself is not available in general. This is a proof error, not a false theorem or a statement dispute.

Step 5 repairs the argument without changing its route. Choose an upper target index `d >= b,b'`, independently factor the map into `S_d` through a source stage, and then descend the two compatibility equalities into `S_b` and `S_b'` at successive later source stages using 01ZC injectivity. The resulting triple lies above both original triples. This also supplies the actual common-upper-bound witnesses used to check initiality in step 6.
