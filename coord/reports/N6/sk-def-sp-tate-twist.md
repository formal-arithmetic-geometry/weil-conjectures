## STATEMENT-DISPUTED: implicit coefficient convention

Without the surrounding convention, “finite ring A” includes noncommutative rings. For example A = M_2(F_ell) is finite and ell A = 0, but two arbitrary left A-modules cannot be balanced-tensored over A without a right action, and Hom_A(M,A) naturally has a right action. The intended symmetric module-sheaf tensor therefore needs commutative coefficients. The current `sec-proetale-coefficients/sk-def-localizing-closure/_section.md` explicitly says that admissible A is a finite commutative ring killed by a power of ell. I made that existing convention explicit here, together with primality of ell, finite intermediate E, positive annihilating exponent and the zero-fold tensor unit. This is a source-faithful clarification; no target is changed.
