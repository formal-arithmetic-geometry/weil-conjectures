For the graded-function node I read `more-morphisms.tex`, `lemma-generic-flatness-stratification` (0ASY), with an actual finitely presented sheaf, not only its structure-sheaf specialization 0H3Z. On a Noetherian affine chart the graded algebra is finite type and Noetherian, and the finite graded module is finitely presented over it. Apply 0ASY to that module on its affine spectrum. After pulling to each stratum the graded pieces are direct summands of a flat module, hence flat; each is finite presented over the stratum by `algebra.tex`, `lemma-graded-module-fg` (00K0) and Noetherianity. It is therefore finite locally free. On each connected component its rank is constant, simultaneously in all degrees: connectedness applies to every one of the locally constant rank functions separately. Each Noetherian stratum has finitely many connected components, and a finite affine cover and finite stratifications give finitely many entire functions. This also explains why reduced/nonreduced bases cause no issue: every point lies on a stratum with the same residue field. This argument is recorded here rather than put into the new unsplit node.
