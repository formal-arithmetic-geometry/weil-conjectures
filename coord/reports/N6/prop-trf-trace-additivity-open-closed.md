### Returned Step 1

Restricted structures and iterates. Since $g_X=\operatorname{id}_{X_0}\times\operatorname{Spec}(\gamma)$, the equalities $hg_W=g_Xh$ for $h=i,j$ are equalities of maps of fibre products. The open squares for all powers are Cartesian by [lem-trf-frobenius-open-square]; the closed Galois square is also Cartesian: base change of the subscheme $Z_0\times_{k_0}k$ by the coefficient-field automorphism gives this same subscheme, with its same defining ideal. The finite-type scheme $X$ has a finite affine cover by finitely generated $k$-algebras. By [lem-sfb-finite-type-noetherian-spectrum] these affine spaces are Noetherian topologically, since $\operatorname{Spec}k$ is a singleton. Restricting an ascending chain of opens to each of the finitely many affine opens shows that it stabilizes on $X$. Thus every open of $X$, in particular $U$, is quasi-compact: any open cover admits a maximal finite union by the ascending-chain condition, and maximality forces that union to be the covered open. Thus the open immersion $j$ is finite type (it is locally the identity and is quasi-compact); the closed immersion $i$ is finite type, locally being given by a ring quotient. Both are separated. By [thm-sf-ctf-stability](i), $Lh^*K\in D^b_{ctf}(W,A)$. The displayed map $h^*\varphi$ is a composite of isomorphisms, hence gives the strict Weil structure of [def-trf-strict-weil-complex]. Under the pullback-composition identifications [lem-weil-pbc-proetale-derived-pullback-composition], the product defining $(h^*\varphi)^{[m]}$ in [def-trf-iterated-weil-map] is
$Lh^*(\varphi\circ Lg_X^*\varphi\circ\cdots\circ Lg_X^{(m-1)*}\varphi)\circ c_{h,m}$.
Indeed the case $m=1$ is its definition; multiplying by the next pulled-back factor replaces $c_{h,m}$ by $c_{h,m+1}$. This identity of comparisons is checked termwise on inverse-image sheaves, where both bracketings identify the inverse image along the same composite map, and descends to complexes. This proves clause 1. The same multiplication, using the recursion of [def-trf-frobenius-coefficient-morphism], proves the iterate formula for $h^*\psi$, since naturality of the $q$-power scheme Frobenius [def-trf-scheme-frobenius] gives $h\Phi_W=\Phi_Xh$.

### Returned Step 2

$0\to j_!j^*C\to C\to i_*i^*C\to0$.
