## Source error

Tag 09F1, lemma-characteristic-p-case, paragraph beginning “Suppose that j prime, j in J”: its displayed replacement subtracts the telescoping sum h, but its following formula changes xi by wp(h). Those signs disagree in odd characteristic. For p=3 and h=a*pi^(-j), wp(z-h)=xi-a^3*pi^(-3j)+a*pi^(-j); a pre-existing lower coefficient a becomes 2a rather than zero. The correct replacement is z+h, which gives xi-a*pi^(-j)+a^3*pi^(-3j) and removes it. Step 4 uses this corrected sign. In characteristic two both signs agree. The source's definition of J also has lambda_i in its predicate for an index named j; the split writes the intended predicate lambda_j not in k. Neither typographical correction changes the item's statement. The item's finite separable conclusion is intentionally retained: the last source construction only promises separable, whereas its lemma statement says Galois. I do not claim an unproved Galois strengthening.
