Strict fullness in a discrete stack identifies the descended object with a set subfunctor, and its stack axiom becomes the etale sheaf axiom. 4. For a section of F over a scheme V, the compatible-pair identity gives `V ×_F D = V ×_X U`. The latter is a scheme surjective etale over V by the atlas definition. The closed-substack result of step 3 already gives its representable closed-immersion scheme tests. `etale-proposition-etale-reduced` makes the reduced scheme D reduced on every etale chart, and `lem-trf-space-representable-etale-scheme-tests` plus `lem-weil-pbc-etale-smooth-zero-dimensional-fibres` make D→Z smooth on scheme charts. The exact surjective reduced-source clause of `lem-sfb-space-smooth-reduced-locality` now makes Z reduced. The point-calculus image and surjectivity clauses give image T.
5. A competing reduced closed subspace has a scheme pullback to U because it is representable, and this is an etale chart because the atlas base changes. The relevant cited scheme-test node says exactly this. Its reducedness is the definition assumed in the item. The point-calculus node gives support T_U; the scheme reduced-structure node identifies the pullback with D; the bijection in the invariant closed-substack node is injective, so equality of pullbacks gives equality as subfunctors.
