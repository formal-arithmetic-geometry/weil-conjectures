### lem-trf-etale-completion-congruence

For $U$ use affine charts of the etale map $p$; finite presentation implies finite type. The map $p_u^{\sharp}:B\to C$ is flat by [def-weil-etale-morphism, def-weil-flat-morphism]. By [lem-weil-pbc-etale-prime-generates-maximal] on these etale affine charts it satisfies $\mathfrak m_B C=\mathfrak m_C$, and Step 1 supplies the residue-field isomorphism. Consequently [lem-weil-pbc-flat-local-completion-iso] gives an isomorphism $\widehat p^{\sharp}:\widehat B\xrightarrow{\sim}\widehat C$. Put $\alpha=\widehat p^{\sharp}\circ\varphi:\widehat A\xrightarrow{\sim}\widehat C$ and let $g=\widehat f^{\sharp}:\widehat A\to\widehat C$ be the actual map induced by $f$.

Former step 3: Let $\mathfrak n_A=\mathfrak m_A\widehat A$ and $\mathfrak n_C=\mathfrak m_C\widehat C$. These are the maximal ideals by [lem-weil-pbc-local-completion-local-ring]. ### lem-trf-completion-near-identity-automorphism

Former step 4: Identify $\widehat A$ with $D=\widehat C$ using $\alpha$ and set $h=g\circ\alpha^{-1}:D\to D$. Thus $h(a)-a\in\mathfrak n^2$ for every $a\in D$, where $\mathfrak n=\mathfrak n_C$, because $N\geq2$. It follows that $h$ is the identity on $D/\mathfrak n$ and on $\mathfrak n/\mathfrak n^2$. More explicitly, for $a_1,\ldots,a_r\in\mathfrak n$, the telescoping expansion of $\prod_i h(a_i)-\prod_i a_i$ has in each term one factor in $\mathfrak n^2$ and $r-1$ factors in $\mathfrak n$, so it belongs to $\mathfrak n^{r+1}$. Every element of $\mathfrak n^r$ is a finite sum of such products: scalar coefficients can be absorbed into one factor. Hence $h$ acts as the identity on every $\mathfrak n^r/\mathfrak n^{r+1}$. This is the generation-in-degree-one argument for the associated graded ring, with the degree-zero case stated separately.
