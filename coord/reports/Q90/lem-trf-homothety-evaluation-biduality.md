## Review by Q90

I checked the item against 0A7C's evaluation argument, and the three inputs of 0A69 against the actual hypothesis list. The item does not assume that L has finite cohomology, and the proof does not borrow that extra dualizing-complex hypothesis from 0A7C. It never replaces an unbounded K by a single bounded-above approximation without explanation.

Step 1 cites `lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-pseudocoherent-cohomology-criterion`, whose statement is the equivalence between pseudo-coherence and bounded-above finite cohomology over a commutative Noetherian ring. Each good truncation has that boundedness individually. The kernel construction written in the step gives the claimed cohomology groups; hence all the criterion's hypotheses hold. No theorem about arbitrary unbounded complexes being pseudo-coherent is used.

Step 2 cites `lem-sf-ringed-derived-closed-symmetric-monoidal`. Its statement supplies the tensor bifunctor and unit R in degree zero for unbounded derived categories on a small ringed site. The single-object site with only the identity and trivial topology is small, and its module sheaves are R-modules, so the statement applies. Tensoring h with N[0] and using the unitor gives precisely N[0], whose cohomology is zero outside degree zero. This proves the finite Tor dimension required by 0A69 without presuming that arbitrary bounded complexes have finite Tor dimension. Finite injective dimension for both L arguments is the given bounded injective representative, not finite generation of its terms.

Step 3 cites `lem-trf-derived-evaluation-finite-injective`. Its statement explicitly allows unbounded K, asks for every good truncation to be pseudo-coherent, and describes the evaluation morphism by the same transpose as the item. Steps 1 and 2 verify respectively its third, second, and first conditions. Thus it proves invertibility of exactly e_{K,L,L}, with no missing boundedness or Noetherian assumptions beyond those already used in Step 1.

Step 4 cites `lem-trf-derived-evaluation-canonical-map` and `lem-sf-ringed-derived-closed-symmetric-monoidal`. The former states the evaluation/composition transpose and its naturality, not merely existence of some morphism. The latter gives a natural adjunction bijection with evaluation as counit, together with the unit and graded symmetry. Uncurrying the proposed composite inserts h into the outer evaluation. The defining adjunction of h turns that outer evaluation into the unit action; naturality of the unitor then cancels the remaining unit insertion. The resulting map is evaluation B tensor K to L. Injectivity of the adjunction bijection identifies the composite with the canonical delta, so this proves the claimed equality of maps rather than only an abstract isomorphism of objects. Invertibility follows by composition, and naturality follows from the cited natural family e, tensor functoriality for the fixed h, and naturality of the unitor. This is a routine single calculation, with no separately assumed comparison lemma. Each proof step is under 1000 characters, and the full proof is under 3000 characters.
