## Supporting arguments for the new unsplit nodes

For primitive-weight logarithmic rigidity, form K=F0 F1^{-1}. The dual-number product rule gives delta K=delta F0-Ad(K)delta F1. If delta F0=delta F1 this is not immediately zero: instead use K=F0^{-1}F1, for which delta K=Ad(F0^{-1})(delta F1-delta F0)=0. This order is essential. Translation by K^{-1} is invertible on tangent spaces, so both ordinary coordinate derivatives of K vanish. Equivariance of the coordinate isomorphism and linear independence of r,s force its jth coordinate to be C_j x^{i_j} y^{k_j}: comparing characters in A[M] works even over nonreduced A, since its monomials are an A-basis. Its two derivatives therefore give i_j C_j=k_j C_j=0. Bezout's identity for gcd(i_j,k_j)=1 gives C_j=0. All coordinates vanish, so K=1. Without coprimality the statement would be false: in characteristic p a map x -> x^p into Ga has zero derivative. This supporting argument is here rather than in the statement-only new node.

A mere generic-fibre comparison is insufficient over nonreduced bases and is explicitly not claimed here.
