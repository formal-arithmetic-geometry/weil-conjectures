Tag 00P6 gives dim S=deg P+1 for the degree-piece polynomial P, taking deg(0)=-1. If Q is the given cumulative polynomial then Q(n+1)-Q(n)=P(n) for all sufficiently large n. Equality at infinitely many integers makes this a polynomial identity. If P is nonzero, the leading term of the difference of a polynomial of degree e is e times its leading coefficient, of degree e-1, so deg Q=deg P+1. If P=0, Q is constant and is positive since S_0=k contributes 1. Thus dim S=0 in this case. This is the proof to carry into the new node's eventual split; it is deliberately kept out of its statement and proof fields.
