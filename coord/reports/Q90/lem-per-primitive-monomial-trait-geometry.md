Edge cases checked before writing:

- n = 1, a_1 = 1: B is R itself and the generic fibre is Spec K over itself.
- n = 1, a_1 > 1 invertible in K: the Eisenstein trait extension has a smooth
  zero-dimensional generic fibre; no geometric integrality is asserted.
- n > 1 with some zero exponents: those variables remain polynomial factors.
  The existential condition needs only one positive exponent invertible in K.
- n = 0 and the all-zero tuple are excluded exactly as the node says. Dropping
  this restriction would instead allow the constant relation 1 - pi.
- Equal characteristic p with every positive exponent divisible by p does not
  satisfy ha; it must not acquire a false generic smoothness conclusion.
- Mixed characteristic allows an exponent divisible by the residue characteristic
  when it is nonzero in K. Requiring invertibility in R would incorrectly exclude
  this case; the delivered statement uses K.
- π = 0 is incompatible with the given domain, dimension-one, maximal-ideal
  equality hypotheses. The zero ring and a field cannot be trait inputs.
- No finite generation of B as an R-module, reduced special fibre, coprimality of
  the entire exponent tuple, excellence, or henselianity is assumed.
- The theorem is universe-polymorphic in R; its canonical fraction field and
  displayed schemes remain in that universe, without unrelated test universes.
