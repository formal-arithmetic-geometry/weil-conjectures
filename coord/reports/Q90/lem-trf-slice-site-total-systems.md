## Corrections and statement dispute

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported formulation assumes only continuous functors preserving covering pullbacks, but then calls their induced adjunctions geometric morphisms. Continuity alone is insufficient. Let I have one arrow i -> j, let C_j be the terminal category, let C_i be the discrete category with two objects a,b, and equip both with the topology of identity coverings. Let u_alpha select a. It is continuous and preserves all covering pullbacks. Its sheaf direct image is evaluation at a, from Set x Set to Set. The left adjoint is A |-> (A, emptyset), which fails to preserve the terminal object: (singleton, emptyset) is not terminal in Set x Set. Hence this adjunction is not a geometric morphism with the prescribed direct image. The corrected item explicitly assumes that each u_alpha defines a morphism of sites, equivalently that its sheaf left adjoint preserves finite limits. This is exactly the assumption in Stacks 09WC/09WF Case A and in the parent lem-trf-slice-site-simplicial-augmentation, where base change of localized sites already defines a morphism of sites. The equivalence construction itself works for the adjunctions even without this additional exactness assumption; the correction makes the geometric-morphism wording legitimate rather than asserting extra exactness from continuity.

The original blueprint.from.place said transition isomorphisms. Replaced this with arbitrary transition maps, as in the actual Stacks discussion; no invertibility of the transition maps is assumed or proved. Unit and compositor isomorphisms are structural comparisons only. Named the comparisons as the ones compatible with the supplied adjunctions. No target, chapter, unit record or maintainer metadata was edited.

The existing pretopology-sheaf-condition node assumes every fibre product in the category exists. That hypothesis is unavailable for a general indexing category or total category. I did not cite it under weakened hypotheses. Instead I added the strictly more general, already-proved Mathlib precoverage criterion and applied its pullback-sieve formulation. No new node restates any clause of this total-site equivalence.
