## What was wrong

The unsplit item had no proof. Its source already correctly warned that Stacks 019G supplies only normalization; that warning matters. The normalization equivalence does not by itself assert a topological homotopy-group computation or a chain diagonal, and the Mathlib chain homotopy equivalence between normalized and alternating-face complexes does not supply that computation either. Those facts now have separate, smaller inputs. The proof writes the good truncation and the lax-monoidal/enriched-category assembly itself.

There is a convention difference to record, not a false statement: Stacks and Choudhury–Gallauer use the kernel of the first faces and a signed last-face differential; Mathlib and Higher Algebra use the kernel of positive faces and differential d_0. The normalization leaf is stated exactly in the Mathlib convention. The simplex input explicitly gives the natural comparison from that kernel model to normalized free simplex chains. The Moore input uses the same positive-face convention; Higher Algebra Remark 1.2.3.14 uses it as well. Thus no silent change of normalization is needed in the proof.

The phrase “underlying infinity category” is realized here in the simplicial-realization model: apply the displayed truncation/Dold–Kan construction to the dg Hom complexes and use the locally Kan coherent-nerve presentation theorem. This is the model called the underlying simplicial category in Higher Algebra Construction 1.3.1.13. The item does not specify a separate differential graded nerve or demand a particular comparison map to it. In particular I have not added a node restating Higher Algebra Proposition 1.3.1.17 as a black box for the item's last clause. The mapping spaces, closed degree-zero edges, composition, and dg-functor naturality are all retained explicitly in steps 2 and 5–7. A reader wishing to choose Lurie's separate small dg nerve as a fixed presentation should also record the additional comparison construction; the present item states the realization independently of such a choice.
