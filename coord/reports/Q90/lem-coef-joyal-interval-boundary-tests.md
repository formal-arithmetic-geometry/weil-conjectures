## New child: proof guidance and small cases

For lem-coef-joyal-small-test-universe-invariance the implication (ii) to (i) is restriction of tests. For (i) to (ii), a U-small family of simplices in a V-small quasicategory Q lies in a simplicial subset generated by that family, with U-small total cardinality. For every inner horn into it choose one filler in Q and adjoin its image. There are U-smallly many such horns because each horn has finitely many nondegenerate simplices. Repeat countably and take the union: every horn in the union is already in one finite stage, so the union Q_0 is a quasicategory. It need not be full, so the argument does not presume small mapping spaces. Relabel its degree sets by U-small copies if needed.

For essential surjectivity of hFun(B,Q) to hFun(A,Q), put the simplicial image of a given A-diagram in one such Q_0 and use (i) there; the resulting isomorphism remains an isomorphism in Q. For fullness, put the images of the two B-diagrams and the finitely many edges representing the specified morphism in hFun(A,Q) in a common Q_0 and apply fullness there. For faithfulness, represent the two morphisms by finite words of edges in Fun(B,Q); a claimed equality of their restrictions in hFun(A,Q) is a finite derivation of the defining identity and triangle relations, including finite contexts for each relation. Put the images of these finitely many parameterized diagrams and triangles in a common Q_0, apply faithfulness there, and include back into Q. All their images are U-small because A and B and their products with finite simplices are U-small. This proves the claim for every V, including empty inputs. When Q is empty, the empty subquasicategory suffices; there are no horn data forcing a vertex. For an ordinary nerve with large Hom sets the same argument uses a non-full small subcategory, confirming that no local-smallness condition is required.
