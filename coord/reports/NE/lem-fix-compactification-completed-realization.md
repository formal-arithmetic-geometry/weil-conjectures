For every step I opened every cited node and read its statement. These are the precise uses and hypothesis checks:

1. `lem-weil-l-adic-extension-integers-dvr` says the valuation ring is a PID and has finite residue field; the principal-ideal argument supplies Noetherianity. `lem-weil-pbc-standard-quotients-perfect` supplies the finite-set and perfect-resolution clauses for this very valuation ring, not just for Z_ell. `lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian` applies over Spec(k), which is Noetherian directly because k is a field. This checks the inputs to the later ringed adic and open-constructibility results.
2. `lem-sf-integral-open-reduction-comparison` explicitly asserts the isomorphism of derived semilinear systems, its augmentation/transition compatibility and completeness of ordinary localized j_!K. The quasi-compact hypothesis is present in the item. `lem-sf-open-finite-constructibility-interval` requires a Noetherian ambient scheme and invertible ell, both verified. It preserves the same cohomological interval and does not ask for Tor amplitude. `def-weil-derived-complete-integral-detailed` then gives exactly the category membership asserted.
3. `prop-weil-pbc-direct-image-limits` asserts the total derived strict-tower comparison, its augmented normalization and preservation of completeness. `lem-pur-completed-coefficient-pushforward-comparison` asserts the completed/finite restriction comparison as a derived strict-diagram functor, and specifies the actual limit projections. Its auxiliary distinct primes impose no characteristic restriction; an auxiliary prime different from ell exists independently of k. Both nodes concern ordinary completed-ring images, so no category encoding is switched here.
4. `lem-sf-compact-support-proper-image-tower` states exactly beta_n t^N_n = t^M_n beta_(n+1) for the chosen finite composite. `lem-sf-integral-open-reduction-comparison` and `lem-pur-completed-coefficient-pushforward-comparison` ensure the maps are obtained on actual coefficient diagrams, not chosen lifts of evaluated arrows. `lem-weil-pbc-ringed-strict-tower-homotopy-limit` supplies a functor on derived strict towers. The tower and its restriction transitions are those written in `def-weil-integral-rational-qbar-compact-support`. This verifies the actual target of d_c, rather than only its isomorphism type.
5. `lem-sf-integral-direct-image-constructible` is stated over any field and for the bounded finite-stalk category, with a uniform bound. Applied to H, it gives the tower-defined image. `def-weil-derived-direct-image` identifies that precise defining tower; Step 3 already identifies it with the ordinary completed-ring image. No arbitrary-field cohomological-dimension bound for absolute Galois cohomology is assumed.
6. `lemma-weil-pbc-perfect-projection-formula` explicitly identifies the specified projection map with the perfect-quotient isomorphism and gives quotient compatibility. `prop-weil-pbc-direct-image-limits` identifies completion with this augmented projection map before total limit. `lem-sf-integral-open-reduction-comparison` supplies the second, open-tensor arrow. `lem-coef-derived-system-evaluation-calculus` says the evaluated counit is the scalar-extension adjoint of the actual total-limit projection. These statements determine the ordered canonical comparison required by the item.
7. `lem-weil-pbc-constant-completion-equivalence` provides the equivalence only on complete objects, with common-quotient reduction, pullback and unit-compatible limit comparisons. `lem-pur-integral-tensor-reduction-tower-comparison` provides the strict tensor comparison; it is not used to identify an underived tensor with a derived tensor. `def-pur-integral-derived-operations` defines the tensors and pullbacks by those same quotient diagrams, and `lem-pur-completed-operation-tower-substitution` supplies their natural substitution comparisons and structural maps. Thus this is a direct application of those formulas to two presentations of the same total limit, not an uncited general monoidal-equivalence assertion.
8. `lem-pur-ringed-coefficient-restriction-counit` gives the displayed ordinary counit equation with the actual scalar-unit map tau. `lem-sbc-reflector-adjunction-unit-formulas` gives the ordinary/completed counit equation. `lem-weil-pbc-ringed-koszul-completion-reflector` supplies its universal-property injectivity, and `lem-weil-pbc-constant-completion-equivalence` identifies this unit with the quotient-tower unit. `lem-pur-completed-coefficient-pushforward-comparison` fixes kappa. Precomposing with these units therefore proves the completed counit identity; it is not inferred from evaluated equations.
9. `lem-pur-compact-support-completed-projection-coherence` is applied in its constant-A setting, to complete H and L, with Step 1's exact ring hypotheses. Its statement gives equality with the strict-system projection and includes the counit normalization. The comparisons proved in Steps 7–8 identify all maps in its defining transpose. `lem-weil-pbc-constant-completion-equivalence` is fully faithful on the complete target, so the equality returns to the completed-ring category. There is no use of faithfulness of finite reductions.
10. `lem-sf-open-tensor-normalization` is valid on unbounded ringed diagram sites without coefficient flatness and normalizes the restricted map to the identity. `lem-sf-integral-open-reduction-comparison` fixes the realized open adjunction and extension. `lem-pur-completed-operation-tower-substitution` fixes the completed tensor and pullback identifications.
