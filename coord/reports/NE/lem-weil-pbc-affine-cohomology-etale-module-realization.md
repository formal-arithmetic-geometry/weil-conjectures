The site module-sheafification interface applies to an arbitrary site and a varying presheaf of rings, unlike similarly titled open-space or fixed-ring special cases. No injective module resolution is confused with an injective abelian resolution.
4. The simultaneous comparison explicitly supplies both scheme-pullback naturality and multiplication compatibility. For the sieve dense-subsite theorem I checked all four hypotheses: image density by affine open covers, local fullness and faithfulness by fullness/faithfulness of the inclusion, and topology detection by the definition of induced topology.
