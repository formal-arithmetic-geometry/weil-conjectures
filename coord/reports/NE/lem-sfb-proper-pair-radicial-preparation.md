Finite type of both proper schemes is already part of `IsProper`: it supplies locally
finite type, and universal closedness supplies quasi-compactness. For closed sets,
`interior Z = ∅` is exactly nowhere density. The finite open cover is pairwise disjoint;
the complement of each member is the finite union of the other opens. Thus each member
is clopen and B is their scheme-theoretic disjoint union. `IsNormalScheme U` includes
`IsIntegral U`, hence U is nonempty, and says every local ring is both a domain and
integrally closed. It is applied to the summands, not to B, which may be disconnected.
No integrality, smoothness or geometric irreducibility of B is imposed.

The generic-point sets use the recorded `irreducibleComponents` and `IsGenericPoint`;
`Set.BijOn` includes preservation, injectivity and surjectivity on exactly those sets.
Both residue-field predicates use the local map induced by r, not an arbitrary algebra
structure. An injective field homomorphism is trivial as a field extension exactly when
it is surjective. `IsPurelyInseparable` agrees with the p-power formulation by Mathlib's
`isPurelyInseparable_iff_pow_mem`; exponential characteristic is 1 in characteristic 0,
so that case says precisely that the field map is surjective.

### Edge cases checked before writing

- Z empty: no nonemptiness hypothesis on Z or C; D can be the unit ideal with empty
  support. The blowup of the unit ideal is the identity.
- X empty: the node excludes this case, and `[Nonempty X]` retains that exclusion.
  The open-cover encoding permits n = 0 syntactically; surjectivity onto the nonempty
  compactification rules it out for a witness, without adding a stronger hypothesis.
- Zero-dimensional X: the empty marking and finite normalization clauses still apply.
- Characteristic zero: e/k is required to be trivial as the given field extension,
  expressed by surjectivity of its structure map, rather than literal type equality.
- Imperfect positive-characteristic k: no perfection assumption is added. A genuinely
  nontrivial finite purely inseparable extension is permitted.
- Reducible or nonreduced X: no reducedness, integrality or normality hypothesis is
  placed on X or Xbar. Generic points refer to all components of their underlying spaces.
- Several intersecting components: only the summands of B are integral normal;
  B itself may be disconnected, as required.
- Universes: k, X and the finite extensions/models use the common arbitrary universe u.
  Finite extensions admit a model in k's universe; this introduces no restriction.
  No independently smaller test-field universe is introduced: geometric reducedness
  uses the recorded Mathlib predicate and its base-change equivalence.
