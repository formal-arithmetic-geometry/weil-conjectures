## STATEMENT-DISPUTED: unqualified contractible uniqueness

The original assertion said the transformation p_j with inert components over q_j was unique up to a contractible space of choices. This is false without fixing its comparison with the chosen Segal inverse / envelope universal data. Take the ordinary colored operad with one color, with unary operations an abelian group G = C_2, no operations of other arities, and the usual identities. Its operadic nerve is a symmetric infinity-operad. Objects in its active category are finite lists of the color; active morphisms are permutations decorated by elements of G, so list length is preserved. In the strict ordered unary concatenation s = 1, F is the identity. For each central g in G, apply g to every entry of every list. This defines a natural automorphism of the inclusion of the active category into the whole operad: it commutes with every decorated permutation because G is abelian. Each component is inert over the identity pointed map. The identity choice and the nontrivial g choice are distinct; at the one-entry object they are distinct morphisms in an ordinary nerve, whose fixed-endpoint mapping space is discrete. There is no path between these transformations. Thus the asserted choice space is not contractible. It also shows why componentwise uniqueness cannot repair the claim with targets fixed.
