STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the new node originally asserted flatness of ker(d^n) from tensor exactness in degree n-1. On the one-point site with R=k[epsilon]/(epsilon^2), put P^n=P^{n+1}=R, d^n=multiplication by epsilon, and all other terms zero. Every term is flat and every tensor complex is exact in degree n-1 (its middle term is zero). But ker(d^n)=(epsilon) is not flat: tensoring the injection (epsilon) -> R with R/(epsilon) gives the zero map from a nonzero copy of k. This works for n=0 or n=1. The corrected conclusion is the opcycle cokernel P^n/im(d^{n-1}), which is R in this example. The item's imported statement remains correct.

The repaired criterion does not require boundedness: here is its direct argument, expanding the generic categorical proof rather than assuming a flat resolution exists. Fix a short exact sequence of sheaves 0 -> N' -> N -> N'' -> 0 and put Q=coker(P^{a-1} -> P^a). Right exactness of tensor gives P^{a-1} tensor N' -> P^a tensor N' -> Q tensor N' -> 0 and the corresponding sequences for N and N''. To show Q tensor N' -> Q tensor N is mono, take a local section x in its kernel. Locally lift x to y in P^a tensor N'. Its image in P^a tensor N is d(z) for a local section z of P^{a-1} tensor N. The image of z in P^{a-1} tensor N'' is a cycle, since d(z) came from N'. Universal exactness in degree a-1 makes it locally d(w) for w in P^{a-2} tensor N''. Right exactness of tensor lifts w locally to P^{a-2} tensor N. Subtract its differential from z; the new z has zero image in P^{a-1} tensor N'' and still has differential equal to the image of y. Flatness of P^{a-1} makes it locally the image of z' in P^{a-1} tensor N'. Then y-d(z') maps to zero in P^a tensor N. Flatness of P^a makes this map mono, so y=d(z'), and x=0. All lifts and exactness assertions are local for the topology, so the successive covering refinements prove the kernel sheaf is zero; no points or stalk criterion are assumed. Tensor with Q is right exact and now preserves every injection, hence is exact, which is the stated flatness. This is the non-elementary input isolated by the new node; no separate node is needed for the elementary cokernel-cycle identification in the item.
