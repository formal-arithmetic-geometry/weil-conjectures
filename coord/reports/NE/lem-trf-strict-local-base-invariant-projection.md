## Generation lemma: source and argument for the next worker

The principal source is Stacks Tag 05K0, `more-morphisms.tex`, `lemma-quasi-finite-separated-pass-through-finite`, read at lines 12456 onward. Its target need only be qcqs. The auxiliary sheaf facts were read in `etale-cohomology.tex`: Tag 03QP, `proposition-finite-higher-direct-image-zero`, lines 8245 onward, including its finite-over-henselian decomposition proof; Tags 03S3 and 03S5, `definition-extension-zero` and `proposition-describe-jshriek`, lines 11360 and 11421 onward; and Tag 0F70, `lemma-jshriek-open`. These show that finite direct image is exact and that etale sum-extension is the free module sheaf on an etale object, with the displayed geometric-fibre stalks. The lemma is an agent-derived consequence, not a claim that 05K0 alone states a derived-category theorem.

Here is a proof to guide its later split. On an affine S, affine etale objects a:W→S form a basis. Each such a is separated and quasi-finite of finite type, so 05K0 factors it as W open in T finite over S. Let j:W→T and let i:Z→T be a closed complement; the choice of closed subscheme structure does not affect the stalk calculation. The exact sequence

    0 → j_!Λ_W → Λ_T → i_*Λ_Z → 0

is checked on geometric stalks: on W it is 0→Λ→Λ→0, and on Z it is 0→0→Λ→Λ→0. Finite p_* is exact, so it gives a triangle with the last two terms Rp_*Λ_T and R(pi)_*Λ_Z. The first term p_*j_!Λ_W is a_!Λ_W: the natural compact-support comparison identifies at each geometric point the summands indexed by the same finite set of W-points (03QP and 03S5). Hence every etale free sheaf a_!Λ_W belongs to the localizing hull of finite constant direct images.

These etale free sheaves generate the abelian sheaf category: take the sum over sections on a small affine etale basis. Its evaluation map is stalkwise surjective. Repeating this for kernels yields a bounded-above exact augmented resolution of every module sheaf by sums of those free sheaves; projectivity is not asserted or needed. Such a resolution is the sequential homotopy colimit of its finite brutal truncations from below. Coproducts of module sheaves are exact, so the telescope has the original complex's cohomology. Thus all sheaves belong to the hull. A bounded-below complex is in the hull by telescoping its bounded canonical truncations from above (their terms, including the top kernel, are sheaves). Any complex is then the telescope of its brutal truncations from below, which are bounded below. This proves generation of the ordinary unbounded derived category. None of this uses a Noetherian coefficient condition or finite Tor dimension.
