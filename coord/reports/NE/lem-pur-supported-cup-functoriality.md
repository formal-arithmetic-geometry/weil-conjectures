### Original Step 2

First work on the etale site. For $i:C\hookrightarrow X$ write $P_C=i_*A_C$. For $v:X'\to X$ there is a sheaf map $P_C\to v_*P_{C'}$: on an etale $T\to X$ pull a locally constant section on $C\times_XT$ back to $C'\times_XT$. Let $b_{v,C}:v^{-1}P_C\to P_{C'}$ be its adjoint. Closed immersions are finite by [lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-finite]. Thus [lemma-weil-pbc-finite-stalk-formula] and [lem-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf-geometric-stalk] give stalk $A$ at a point on $C$, and $0$ off $C$. By [lem-weil-pbc-inverse-image-stalk-germ], the stalk of the source of $b_{v,C}$ at $\bar x'$ is the stalk of $P_C$ at $v\bar x'$, with the stated germ normalization. The stalk map is therefore $1_A$ on $C'$ and the unique map $0\to0$ off $C'$. [lemma-weil-pbc-geometric-stalk-conservative] makes $b_{v,C}$ an isomorphism. This includes empty supports and nonreduced closed subschemes. If $v,w$ are composable, pulling a section back twice equals pulling it back along $vw$, so the $b$ maps satisfy the composition and identity equations under the canonical inverse-image composition comparisons.
For two supports put $I=C\times_XD$. We now construct the etale auxiliary coefficient map, since the current [def-pur-supported-cup] specifies its pro-etale counterpart only. On every etale test object restrict sections of $P_C$ and $P_D$ to $I$ and multiply: $(s,t)\mapsto s|_I t|_I$. This is balanced and commutes with restrictions, so the sheaf tensor universal property in [lem-pc-sheaf-modules-tensor-closed] gives $\mu^{\mathrm{et}}_{C,D}:P_C\otimes_{A_X}P_D\to P_I$. The scalar-ring tensor here agrees with the sheafification of the tensor over the constant ring $A$: every section of $A_X$ is locally a constant section, and therefore every $A_X$-balancing relation is locally an $A$-balancing relation, and conversely. Thus [lem-pod-etale-stalk-tensor-scalar] applies. At a geometric point on $I$, the map on stalks is $A\otimes_A A\to A$, multiplication; elsewhere one source factor and the target are zero. It is an isomorphism by stalk conservativity. The stalk identifications respect $A$-action because each germ map commutes with multiplication by constants. Each $P_C$ is flat: by [prop-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks-exact] and the tensor-stalk formula, tensoring an exact sequence with it has exact stalks, since its stalk is $0$ or $A$. Exactness is detected on stalks: the homology of the sequence has zero stalks by exactness of the stalk functors and is zero by conservativity applied to its map to zero. The bounded-above flat-complex assertion of [lem-pc-kflat-closure] makes these degree-zero models K-flat. Hence [lem-weil-pbc-site-derived-tensor-descent] gives the induced isomorphism $\mu^{\mathrm{et}}_{C,D}:P_C\otimes_A^L P_D\xrightarrow{\sim}P_I$. Write it as $\mu_{C,D}$ until site comparison in Step 6. Consequently the following identities hold: triple multiplication is independent of parentheses; exchanging the factors gives multiplication after the interchange $C\times_XD\cong D\times_XC$; and $b_{v,I}\,v^{-1}\mu_{C,D}=\mu_{C',D'}(b_{v,C}\otimes b_{v,D})$, with the pullback tensor comparison understood. Each equality is an equality on local tensor generators, hence an equality of sheaf maps by the tensor universal property. These equalities pass to derived tensors: the etale $P_C$ are flat by the preceding stalk argument, so they are already degree-zero flat tensor models.

### Original Step 4

$$v^*\alpha=\tau_{v,r}[a]\circ v^{-1}(\alpha)\circ b_{v,C}^{-1}:P_{C'}\longrightarrow A_{X'}(r)[a],$$
where $\tau_{v,r}:v^{-1}A_X(r)\xrightarrow{\sim}A_{X'}(r)$ is Step 1 and the exact functor's shift identification is understood. This is the usual supported pullback, not an exceptional base-change isomorphism. ### Original Step 7

By [thm-weil-pbc-site-comparison] the functor $\nu_X^*$ is fully faithful on $D^+$, for every scheme $X$ and finite ring $A$.
