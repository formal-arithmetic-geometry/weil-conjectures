## Source and intended proof of the new node

`lem-sfb-fixed-point-action-finite-jets` is the intermediate assertion in Milne Proposition 8.9's proof, printed p.150, before using faithfulness; compare Corollary 8.10 on the same page and the fixed-subscheme theorem 7.1, printed pp.138--139. I read all of those passages. The new statement assumes that X is smooth connected over the algebraically closed field so X is reduced and irreducible. This deliberately avoids relying on the questionable sentence “closed and irreducible implies equal” for a nonreduced ambient scheme. It imposes no smoothness or affineness on H. There is no Stacks tag stating this algebraic-action theorem, so its source is the actual available human-authored book, rather than an unrelated tag.

The proof to be split later is as follows. The fixed section gives algebraic actions on the finite infinitesimal neighbourhoods and thus representations on their finite-dimensional coordinate algebras. Their kernels descend and stabilize because H is Noetherian. The stable kernel acts trivially on every infinitesimal neighbourhood. Its fixed subscheme of X is closed by 7.1; its defining ideal has zero stalk at x by the Krull intersection theorem. Coherence makes it vanish on an open neighbourhood of x. The defining ideal is therefore zero everywhere because X is integral: each local regular function vanishing on a dense open is zero. Hence the stable kernel acts trivially on all of X as a scheme, and the reverse containment is immediate. This is a scheme-theoretic argument and survives arbitrary algebra-valued points; it is not a comparison just of k-points. The proof sketch belongs in this report, not in the new statement-only node.
