The node takes the ideal/subspace bijection missing from the existing forward quasi-coherence node. The proof to implement later is precisely the source construction: the functor of test maps killing I is a closed subspace because on a scheme test U it is the subscheme for the image of I on U. For relative flatness I read the small argument omitted in quot.tex 0D00 together with spaces-morphisms.tex Tag 0DK1 (`lemma-stalk-push-closed`) and the relative flatness convention of `def-pc-scheme-module-flat-over`. On an etale scheme chart U of X the closed inverse image V is a scheme, and the restricted direct image is the direct image from V. On a Zariski open of U it has sections G on the inverse image open of V. At a point in V the Zariski stalk is exactly the original stalk, with the same action of the base local ring; at a point outside V it is zero. Thus the relative flatness tests are identical, with zero stalks adding no condition. Applied to G=O_Z, the chart test is the definition of flatness of Z over T. This is a genuine comparison lemma on etale modules and is therefore a child, rather than an uncited elementary step in the item.
