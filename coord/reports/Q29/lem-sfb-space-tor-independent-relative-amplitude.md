## Source error

Stacks spaces-perfect.tex Tag 0DKD (`lemma-tor-independence-and-tor-amplitude`) has the already corrected inverse-image typo. More significantly, its proof says that the product-stalk derived tensor equals the base-stalk extension in D(B), without displaying the flat extension from D=A tensor_R B to C=O_(X',x'). Those are different rings in general. For example over an algebraically closed field k, take X=Y'=A1_k and Y=Spec(k), and the geometric origin in the product. The tensor product of the two strict local rings is not the strict local ring of the product: in the tensor product there is a map to a field sending the two coordinates to t and 1/t, so 1-xy maps to zero, whereas in the strict local ring at (0,0), 1-xy is a unit. The individual strict local rings map to an algebraic closure of k(t) by extending the coordinate embeddings. This shows why deleting the D-to-C extension is unjustified. The theorem remains true: the existing geometric-stalk tensor-calculus node states flatness of that extension, and steps 5 and 6 explicitly preserve the Tor interval through it. No hypothesis or conclusion is changed.
