### Original step 1

Write $F_E=Lf^*_{\mathrm{comp},E}Rg_{*,\mathrm{comp},E}$ and $G_E=Rh_{*,\mathrm{comp},E}Le^*_{\mathrm{comp},E}$. The square has $g:T\to S$, $f:X\to S$, $e:Y\to T$, $h:Y\to X$ [sbc-def-base-change-square], so both functors take their values on $X$. The integral completed operations are additive and $\mathcal O_E$-linear, preserve the derived-complete categories, and have the canonical natural integral completed exchange of [lem-sbc-completed-operations-linear-natural-exchange]. For fixed $E\subset M$, write $P_E^u=Lu^*_{\mathrm{comp},\mathcal O_E}$ and $D_E^u=Ru_{*,\mathcal O_E}$ on the ambient complete integral categories. The comparisons in [prop-weil-pbc-scalar-extension] are $p^f:C_XP_E^f\xrightarrow{\sim}P_M^fC_S$, $p^e:C_YP_E^e\xrightarrow{\sim}P_M^eC_T$, $d^g:C_SD_E^g\xrightarrow{\sim}D_M^gC_T$, and $d^h:C_XD_E^h\xrightarrow{\sim}D_M^hC_Y$, where $C_W=C_{M/E,W}$. Their composites $(P_M^f d^g)\circ(p^fD_E^g)$ and $(D_M^h p^e)\circ(d^hP_E^e)$ compare the integral $F$ and $G$ composites, respectively. After rationalization and Karoubi extension these give coherent isomorphisms $b^F_{M/E}:T^X_{M/E}F_E\xrightarrow{\sim}F_MT^T_{M/E}$ and $b^G_{M/E}:T^X_{M/E}G_E\xrightarrow{\sim}G_MT^T_{M/E}$. They pass to the ambient rational and Karoubi categories [def-sbc-ambient-complete-coefficient-categories] using the formula for each integral linear operation $H$: $u\otimes c\mapsto H(u)\otimes c$ and $(A,p)\mapsto(HA,H(p))$, and extending the components of the comparisons by the same formulas. For a natural comparison $b:H\Rightarrow J$, the arrow on $(A,p)$ is $J(p)b_AH(p)$ and its inverse is $H(p)b_A^{-1}J(p)$. Naturality and $p^2=p$ make their two composites $H(p)$ and $J(p)$, the identities of the corresponding Karoubi objects. The unit, tower and exchange equations survive these operations since both operations preserve composition and identities. This uses the ambient part of the scalar-extension statement, without asserting a constructible direct-image functor.
