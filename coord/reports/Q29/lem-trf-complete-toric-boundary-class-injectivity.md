## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the withdrawn `lem-trf-toric-boundary-exactness` is false for the arbitrary normal chart domains it allowed, even with its stated prime and valuation hypotheses.

For a field k of characteristic different from 2 take the sharp saturated monoid
P = {(a,b) in N^2 : a+b is even}, whose group G is the parity sublattice of Z^2. Write x=z^(2,0), z=z^(1,1), y=z^(0,2), so its monoid ring is A=k[x,y,z]/(xy-z^2). Put H=x+y-2z and R=A[1/H]. This is a normal Noetherian domain: A is the invariant ring of k[s,t] under simultaneous sign change (x=s^2,z=st,y=t^2), and localization preserves normality. The facet primes are (x,z)R and (y,z)R. They remain distinct height-one primes, since their quotients are k[y,y^-1] and k[x,x^-1]. Their normalized monomial valuations are the primitive forms a and b on G; all height-one primes meeting monomials are these two (z^2=xy). But
(x-z)(y-z)=-zH,
so z belongs to (x-z)R and x=(x-z)+z also does. Thus (x,z)R=(x-z)R is principal, and similarly (y,z)R=(y-z)R. The nonzero class of (1,0) in Z^2/G therefore maps to zero. This also explains why retaining the complete-model hypotheses, and using the supported-series projection, matters. The repaired target has those hypotheses and is not disputed or weakened.
