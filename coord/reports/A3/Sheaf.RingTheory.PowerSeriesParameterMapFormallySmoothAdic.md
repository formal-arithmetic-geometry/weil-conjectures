## What changed

Replaced the whole proof of
`MvPowerSeries.formallySmoothAdic_of_parameters_closedFiber` without changing its
signature or existing docstring. Its three new proof imports are
`FormallySmoothAdicSmallKernelCriterion`, `PowerSeriesParameterPreservingRingLift`,
and `ClosedFiberDefectDerivationExtension` under `Sheaf.RingTheory`.

The small-kernel criterion is proved: for a continuous map g into D/J, take the
preimage a of the ideal generated by g(n), filter by I_e = a^e J, and inductively
lift through D/I_(e+1) -> D/I_e. The proof identifies surjections with their kernel
quotients, verifies annihilation and square-zero conditions, and doubles continuity
bounds. It treats n = 0, J = 0 and arbitrary permitted universes.

The parameter-preserving ring-lift theorem is also proved, from the existing
field/Cohen lifting theorem, parameter-quotient theorem (with empty quotient list),
and complete power-series universal property. It retains the coefficient lift,
changes parameter coordinates to their required base images, and evaluates in a
nilpotent ideal. Uniqueness of evaluation proves agreement modulo J. In the Cohen
case it omits p as a variable and verifies that ring maps already preserve p.

The remaining extension theorem is a generic differential lemma: extend the
parameter-vanishing base defect to a derivation on T, when the closed fibre is
formally smooth and the kernel is killed by m_T. The assigned module constructs
f - delta itself, proves multiplicativity using J^2 = 0, proves base linearity,
and verifies the quotient equation. This is a decomposition with one remaining
hole, not an axiom-free proof of the target closure.

All four modules compile together without warnings. Source checked: the final
