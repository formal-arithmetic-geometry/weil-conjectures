Give these images their reduced structures for the dimension calculation. If p|D_i has one-dimensional image E_i, then dim D_i=dim E_i=1. The generic fibre has dimension zero by the computation in the proof of 0B2L; over each closed point of E_i, clause (2)(d) gives dimension zero as well. A positive-dimensional fibre of p, covered by finitely many closed fibres of the D_i, must therefore contain a positive-dimensional D_i for which p(D_i) is a singleton. Properness of p makes this singleton closed in Y. Its dimension is one, and the original component Z_i gives exactly the stated conclusion.
