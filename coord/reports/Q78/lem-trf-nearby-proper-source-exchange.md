## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported wording asserted a canonical identification of arbitrary triangulated cones, compatible with all further base changes, without specifying any functorial cone model. Orgogozo section 4.1, printed p. 11, chooses a cone and asserts that its proper direct image is isomorphic to the other cone. It does not assert uniqueness of that extending cone map.

The issue can already be tested in D(k), for a field k: the zero arrow k[0] -> k[1] has split cone k[1] direct-sum k[1]. With its inclusion from k[1] and projection to k[1], every matrix [[1,t],[0,1]] is an automorphism of that cone triangle fixing the first two vertices and their maps. Thus the comparison of the first two vertices does not determine a unique comparison of chosen cones.

The corrected statement asserts a canonical natural identification of the two comparison ARROWS, compatible with further base change, and an extending isomorphism of distinguished cone triangles. It explicitly disclaims uniqueness only of the third arrow for independently chosen cones. This is faithful to the source and preserves its proper-source cone isomorphism and its finite-source P_r consequence, with no added geometric or coefficient hypothesis. The precise strict-localization maps defining j_b are now stated. Rh_*L is only required to lie in D^+ for the proper-source part; boundedness is proved for the finite-source part, where P_r uses it. No unproved uniform cohomological-dimension bound is being assumed.
