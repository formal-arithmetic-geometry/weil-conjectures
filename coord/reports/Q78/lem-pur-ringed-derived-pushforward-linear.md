## Statement dispute

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: a homomorphism from a commutative ring into a possibly noncommutative ring sheaf does not necessarily give the asserted scalar operation on module morphisms. The ringed-topos-morphism definition does not assume its rings commutative.

Counterexample: take both topoi to be the topos of a point, the identity geometric and ringed morphism, A=B=M_2(Q), R=Q[t], and both scalar maps given by evaluation t -> d=diag(1,0). Compatibility is automatic. For the regular left A-module and f=id, the proposed scalar map t f is left multiplication by d. But d E_12=E_12, while E_12 d=0, so (t f)(E_12 * 1) differs from E_12 * (t f)(1). Thus t f is not A-linear, even before deriving; the claimed R-linear category is undefined. This is a failure of the imported prerequisite, not an error in Stacks 070K.

Correction: require s_A(r)|_U and s_B(r)|_V to be central in A(U) and B(V) for all site objects and every r. This makes explicit the title's “compatible central scalars” and the intended integral-coefficient application. Requiring centrality only in the global-section ring would not suffice on a general site. Commutative ring sheaves and the coefficient-system application already satisfy the correction. No change was made to any target, chapter or unit record.
