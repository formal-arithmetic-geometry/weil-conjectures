Old step 2 -> lem-trf-source-hom-double-complex-model

Construct a nonnegative injective resolution $N[0]\to I^\bullet$ [def-sp-injective-resolutions] from [lem-weil-pbc-ring-modules-enough-injectives]. Set $C^0=N$ and, for $r\geq0$, choose an injection $e_r:C^r\hookrightarrow I^r$ into an injective module and set $C^{r+1}=\operatorname{coker}(e_r)$. Define $d_I^r$ as $I^r\twoheadrightarrow C^{r+1}\xrightarrow{e_{r+1}}I^{r+1}$, and set $I^r=0$ for $r<0$. Then $\ker d_I^0=e_0(N)$ and $\ker d_I^r=e_r(C^r)=\operatorname{im}d_I^{r-1}$ for $r>0$, proving that this is the required resolution. This is the elementary recursive construction in Tag 013K, lemma-injective-resolutions-exist (1).

Cited statements: def-sp-injective-resolutions, lem-weil-pbc-ring-modules-enough-injectives.

Old step 4 -> lem-trf-source-hom-double-complex-model

Form the first-quadrant double complex [def-sp-double-complex-total] $D^{i,b}=\operatorname{Hom}_A(M^{u-b},I^i)$ for $i,b\geq0$, zero otherwise. Set $d_I(f)=d_I^i\circ f$ (raising $i$), and $d_{II}(f)=(-1)^{b-u+1}f\circ d_M^{u-b-1}$ (raising $b$). Their squares vanish because $d_M^2=d_I^2=0$, and they commute because precomposition and postcomposition commute and the displayed sign depends only on $b$. In degree $t$, only $-t\leq a\leq u$ can contribute to $T^t=\prod_a\operatorname{Hom}_A(M^a,I^{a+t})$, so this product is a finite direct sum. Writing $a=u-b$ and $i=a+t$ identifies $T^t$ with $\operatorname{Tot}(D)^{t+u}$. The total differential $d_I+(-1)^i d_{II}$ has source-precomposition coefficient $(-1)^{i+b-u+1}=(-1)^{t+1}$, exactly the coefficient $-(-1)^t$ in [def-pur-hom-complex]. Hence this degree translation identifies differentials as well, and $H^t(T)=H^{t+u}(\operatorname{Tot}D)$. This assertion uses degree translation with the same differential, not a convention for signed shift functors.

Cited statements: def-sp-double-complex-total, def-pur-hom-complex.

Old step 5 -> lem-trf-injective-hom-double-cohomology

For fixed $i$, compute the cohomology in $b$ directly from injectivity [def-sp-injective-resolutions]. Put $a=u-b$, $Z^a=\ker d_M^a$ and $B^a=\operatorname{im}d_M^{a-1}$. A cocycle $f:M^a\to I^i$ kills $B^a$, so restriction induces a map to $\operatorname{Hom}_A(Z^a/B^a,I^i)$. Every map $Z^a/B^a\to I^i$ extends across $Z^a/B^a\hookrightarrow M^a/B^a$, proving surjectivity. A cocycle restricting to zero on $Z^a/B^a$ factors through $M^a/Z^a\cong B^{a+1}$; extending that map across $B^{a+1}\hookrightarrow M^{a+1}$ expresses it as a boundary (up to the invertible sign in $d_{II}$). Therefore $H^b_{II}(D^{i,\bullet})=\operatorname{Hom}_A(H^{u-b}(K),I^i)$. This also covers $b=0$: $M^{u+1}=0$ forces $Z^u=M^u$, so the restriction map has zero kernel. Restriction, quotient factorization and postcomposition commute, so this identification is $A$-linear and compatible with $d_I$.

Cited statements: def-sp-injective-resolutions.

Old step 6 -> lem-trf-injective-hom-double-cohomology

Apply the first spectral sequence of [thm-sp-double-complex-spectral-sequences] in the abelian category of $A$-modules (its abelianity is part of [lem-weil-pbc-ring-modules-enough-injectives]). Step 5 gives $\widetilde E_1^{i,b}=\operatorname{Hom}_A(H^{u-b}(K),I^i)$, with differential induced by postcomposition with $d_I$. Hence $\widetilde E_2^{i,b}=H^i\operatorname{Hom}_A(H^{u-b}(K),I^\bullet)$. Since $I^\bullet$ is the K-injective resolution fixed in step 3, [def-pur-derived-internal-hom] identifies this with $H^iR\operatorname{Hom}_A(H^{u-b}(K)[0],N[0])$, and [lem-sc-rhom-cohomology-ext-ring] identifies it with $\operatorname{Ext}^i_A(H^{u-b}(K),N)$ for $i\geq0$. These are $A$-linear identifications: the maps send a cocycle to its localized shifted map, and multiplication by $a\in A$ is postcomposition with $a\operatorname{id}_{I^\bullet}$ throughout. All pages and filtration maps are in $\operatorname{Mod}(A)$, so their kernels, images and quotients are $A$-submodules. The spectral-sequence page isomorphisms [def-sp-spectral-sequence] give $\widetilde E_{s+1}=H(\widetilde E_s,d_s)$.
