I reviewed `lem-sfb-dedekind-action-square-power` against BLR 6.3 Theorem 1 and its preceding functorial definition. The generic fibre must be nonempty and geometrically connected; connectedness of X alone over an arbitrary Dedekind base would not justify this hypothesis. The new statement retains this hypothesis explicitly. It allows dimension zero because the local tests include fields, precisely the field case treated in BLR's reduction. For every B the square discrepancy is pulled back from B, as required by the existing criterion; it is not merely an assertion about rational points, numerical equivalence or a chosen trivialization. The general base is spelled out as a Noetherian integrally closed domain of dimension at most one. No commutativity is needed.

I reviewed `lem-sfb-dedekind-invariant-component-ample-test` against BLR's initial local, strict-henselian and connected-component reductions, supplemented by the three Stacks lemmas read above. Its statement tests arbitrary line modules, without any affine divisor complement. Here is the argument retained for its future splitter. Localize at each closed point and then strictly henselize. A smooth finite-type scheme over the resulting Noetherian regular local base is regular with finitely many disjoint open-and-closed integral connected components. Components meeting the closed fibre are surjective over the trait; each has a rational point in its smooth closed fibre over the separably closed residue field, hence a section. Normality and the section imply geometrically connected generic fibre (the geometric generic components cannot be permuted nontrivially while containing this rational section; this is BLR's EGA IV 4.5.13.1 input). Geometrically connected smooth group fibres preserve these components. Components missing the closed fibre are schemes over the fraction field; extend that field to a separable closure, where their smooth connected components are geometrically connected, and apply the field tests. Descend the ampleness of this specified line along the field extension by fpqc descent, not existence-of-an-ample-line descent. Glue on the finite clopen decomposition by extending a section as zero on the other components; positive tensor powers absorb the finitely many exponents. Descend from the strict henselization by fpqc descent, then spread ampleness from the local base to an open neighbourhood using 09MT, and glue relative ampleness on S. Equivalently one can spread directly from the affine etale inverse-limit presentations. Over an affine base relative ampleness is absolute. This avoids treating the family of all strict localizations as an fpqc cover. Generic-only components are essential: they are included via the separably closed field tests, not silently discarded. For a field base, only the field-extension part is needed; for empty X the conclusion is vacuous. The new node defers this genuine geometric reduction, rather than hiding it in an elementary step.
