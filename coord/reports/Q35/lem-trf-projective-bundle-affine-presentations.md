For step 5 I opened lem-trf-free-projective-bundle-base-change-space, def-weil-closed-immersion and def-kun-projective. Composing with this isomorphism preserves the closed image and the surjection of structure sheaves, exactly the two conditions in the definition of a closed immersion. The equality of projections follows by composition, and the definition of H-projectivity then applies with N=n.
