## Argument retained for the new tower worker

For any abelian category with the stated limits, maps V→lim T are precisely compatible families V→T_n, hence tower maps ΔV→T. The correspondence and inverse are given by coordinates, proving the ordinary adjunction with its actual projections. Δ is additive and exact because exactness is levelwise. The K-injective tower hypothesis computes the everywhere-defined total right derived limit; the exact-functor factorization computes the left derived Δ. Apply Stacks 09T5. On a K-injective tower I the 0FND fraction formula, using the identity left resolution and the chosen right resolution, sends the identity of lim I to Δlim I→I with components π_n. Thus evaluation of the derived counit is precisely the resolution-normalized total-limit projection, independently of the chosen resolution. For a map f:j→j′, put b=B(f), c=C(f). Naturality gives RL(b)λ_j=λ_j′c and bε_Bj=ε_Bj′ΔRL(b). Then bε_BjΔλ_j=ε_Bj′ΔRL(b)Δλ_j=ε_Bj′Δλ_j′Δc. This is the equality of tower morphisms required by the conclusion; no faithfulness of evaluation is invoked.

## Argument retained for the new exact-sections worker
