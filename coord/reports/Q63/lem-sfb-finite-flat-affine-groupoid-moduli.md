Step 2 uses the actual exactness statement `lem-weil-pbc-flat-tensor-exact`, including preservation of the initial injection, not merely the definition of flatness. Step 3 uses the finite flat surjective atlas and kernel-pair assertions of the presentation node. A fibre over an existing object is nonempty even when the original scheme is empty in other cases. Its coordinate algebra over an algebraically closed field is finite-dimensional; a maximal-ideal residue is finite algebraic and therefore the field itself, giving the required point. Step 4 takes integrality from `lem-a1-affine-groupoid-integral-invariants` and universal closedness from `pqf-lem-integral-univclosed`. Geometric-point bijectivity is supplied by step 3 over common algebraically closed extensions.

Step 5 proves quasi-compactness from the continuous image of each affine $U_T$: the finite-subcover argument is explicit and does not need a new node. The diagonal is representable for an algebraic stack. The pulled-back map is between the affine schemes R and U times_M U. Affine-morphism permanence gives quasi-compactness and separatedness of the diagonal.
