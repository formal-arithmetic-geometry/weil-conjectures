## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported summary and its identical unit conflate constant-coefficient ordinary derived categories with the categories in the definitions they name. The unqualified claim that all small coefficient-stage categories are full subcategories of the same ordinary constant-coefficient bounded category is not supplied by those definitions. `def-weil-finite-torsion-constructibility` and `def-weil-constructible-intervals` define only finite-level pro-etale categories, while the last clause invokes field stages.

The explicit type check is in the current blueprint itself: `sec-proetale-coefficients/sk-prop-integral-criterion/def-weil-completed-integral-coefficients.md` says its ring sheaf is the inverse limit of finite constant sheaves and explicitly says it is not the discrete constant integral sheaf. `def-weil-derived-complete-integral-detailed.md` in that same folder imposes derived completeness and one interval for every reduction. In `sec-proetale-coefficients/sk-def-sf-operations/`, `def-weil-rationalization-karoubi.md` has the integral objects with Hom tensored with E; `def-weil-algebraically-closed-coefficients.md` has pairs (E,K) and common-stage Hom groups. Neither of the latter two constructions is literally the subtype of ordinary constant-field complexes asserted by the summary; such a realization would require a separately specified functor and theorem.

For a concrete warning against identifying the integral ring sheaves, take T to be the spectrum of an algebraically closed field of characteristic different from ell, and the pro-etale object indexed by the profinite set S = Z_ell (the inverse limit of the finite disjoint unions indexed by Z/ell^j). Sections of the discrete constant Z_ell sheaf there are locally constant functions S -> Z_ell. Sections of the inverse limit of the finite constant sheaves include the compatible functions s -> s mod ell^j. Their inverse-limit function is the identity S -> Z_ell, which is not locally constant. Thus the two ring-sheaf data really differ. A second small check is that an integral object killed by a power of the uniformizer becomes zero after scalar localization, because its identity becomes zero in Hom tensor E; simply retaining its integral Hom group is not that category.

The repair keeps ordinary ambient categories for an explicitly chosen ring sheaf, all four boundedness notations, and the allowable-torsion condition. It defines the three finite-level small categories on each named site separately, uses the completed pro-etale integral convention, and retains exactly the rationalization/common-stage and field finite-Tor conventions of the cited source. Higher-level intervals are made meaningful by the existing standard-pair definition. No preservation, comparison, t-structure-existence, or integral finite-Tor theorem is made part of the definition. This resolves the unit's ambiguity by its own referenced coefficient definitions; its record's text and route are untouched. The extra invertibility proviso is exactly the scope of its cited finite-Tor and interval definitions, and is not imposed on the general ordinary ambient categories.
