### Old step 5 → lem-trf-bimodule-chain-currying-descent

The isomorphism is canonical and natural. On complexes its component is the formula of Step 2; precomposition in $C$ or $Q$ and postcomposition in $I$ commute with this formula on every $a\otimes t$.
