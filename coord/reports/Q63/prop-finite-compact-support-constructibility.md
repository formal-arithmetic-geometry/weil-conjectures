## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the final sentence, "The restriction and its amplitude bound commute with finite coefficient reduction", does not define a square on all the displayed bounded constructible categories if reduction means ordinary derived extension of scalars. For E=Q_ell, n=2, m=1 and X a geometric point of characteristic different from ell, set A=Z/ell^2, B=F_ell and K=B[0]. The free resolution of B over A has every differential multiplication by ell. Its exactness follows from ker(ell:A→A)=ell A. Tensoring it with B gives zero differentials and Tor_i^A(B,B)=B for every i≥0. Thus K is bounded constructible but K tensor_A^L B has nonzero cohomology in every degree ≤0. Even the identity map of the point cannot make an unconditional Dbc reduction square well typed. Restriction of scalars is a different functor and does not repair this claim.

I retained the unconditional constructibility, triangulated restriction and uniform amplitude assertions. I made the last clause explicit on its bounded domain: when K_m=K tensor_A^L A_{E,m} is bounded constructible, the canonical derived coefficient comparison is an isomorphism in Dbc(Y,A_{E,m}); the bound uses the actual interval [c,d] of K_m and is [c,d+2e]. This expresses the source's intended coefficient compatibility without asserting that a nonflat quotient preserves the bounded domain or the original interval. It follows from Stacks Tag 0GL6 (`more-etale.tex`, `remark-Rf-lower-shriek-change-of-rings`), which states scalar compatibility on the ambient unbounded etale categories, together with the bounded site comparison. No hypothesis was added to the main constructibility or amplitude theorem.

The other wording correction is that comparisons are identities for *identity* refinements, and satisfy the cocycle law; they are canonical isomorphisms for general refinements. This matches the actual statement of `prop-compactification-coherence-detailed`, rather than equating differently presented candidate functors literally. Inherited `blueprint_update` text was preserved so both discrepancies remain visible to the next reader.
