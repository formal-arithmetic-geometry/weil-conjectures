## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the auxiliary sentence in the proof of HRS Theorem 4.13 saying that the closed subschemes U_i of a w-contractible affine U are w-contractible by BS Lemma 2.2.15. The cited lemma proves only w-strict locality of quotients. The general closed-subscheme assertion is false, and its unrestricted use is not justified by that citation. This disputes a sentence of the proof, not the statement of this item or its unit.

Explicit counterexample to the general assertion: let k be an algebraically closed field and let A be the ring of locally constant k-valued functions on the Stone space beta(N), with k discrete. Its spectrum is beta(N); its local rings are k and its component space is extremally disconnected, so it is w-contractible. Quotient by the ideal of finitely supported functions. The quotient spectrum is beta(N) minus N, whose Boolean algebra of clopens is P(N)/Fin and is not complete; hence this compact Stone space is not extremally disconnected. For example, partition N into infinitely many infinite blocks A_n. Their classes in P(N)/Fin have no least upper bound: from any upper bound, delete one of its points in each A_n to obtain a strictly smaller upper bound modulo finite sets. Thus the quotient is w-strictly local, but not w-contractible.

Repair: for each reduced irreducible closed component X_a, first take an affine faithfully flat ind-etale w-contractible cover W_a. Prove its perfect scalar model by the irreducible case. Lift W_a to V_a by the existing closed-lift construction; the added henselian-pair lemma and HRS Corollary 4.4 make V_a w-contractible and lift the isomorphism. The finitely many V_a are a covering of X. This is the same component/henselian-neighborhood route as the source, with the necessary refinement inserted. Since the item allows different P_a, no uniform scalar value or specialization zig-zag is required. I marked proof_origin.by as agent to make this expanded repair explicit. The item's statement and all hypotheses remain unchanged.
