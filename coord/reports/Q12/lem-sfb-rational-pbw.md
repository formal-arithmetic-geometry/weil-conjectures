## Source error

Milne's printed Theorem 10.36 is the correct statement, but its displayed proof cannot be followed literally. On p.201 the sentence using skew-symmetry has dropped a minus sign. Step 1 on p.202 prints a strict inequality in the wrong direction: for an abelian Lie algebra with ordered letters a>b, the rule sends ab to ba, which is strictly smaller in the stated inversion measure, not larger. More substantially, Step 3's assertion that a nonzero ideal element cannot be reduced is PBW independence itself; the argument by maximum prefix degree does not justify cancellation between different contextual relations. For example, in an abelian algebra with a>b>c, r(a,b)c and a r(b,c) share the leading word abc, so those leading terms cancel in their difference. That does not refute PBW, but it shows why the assertion needs a genuine normal-form argument. Step 4's congruence merely modulo the entire ideal is automatic for reductions and cannot repair Step 3.

I retain the source's adjacent-interchange/Jacobi route and replace the gap by a general triangular linear-relations criterion, verifying the stronger condition modulo relations with strictly smaller leading words. The item's statement is true and unchanged; there is no STATEMENT-DISPUTED claim about the item and no change to target semantics or unit route.

## Proof plan for the new unsplit criterion

This argument belongs in the report, not in the statement-only node. Define a map from coordinate vectors e_w to the free vector space on the irreducible indices by well-founded recursion. For an irreducible w send e_w to its corresponding coordinate vector; otherwise choose a rule at w and send e_w to the already defined finite linear combination for its right side. Simultaneously, induction proves that the resulting linear map kills every relation whose leading index has already been treated. At stage w, the difference between any two right sides belongs to the span of relations with leading index below w, which the map kills by the induction hypothesis. Thus the definition is independent of the chosen rule and kills every rule at w. Finally e_w minus its normal form belongs to the full relation span, by induction and finite summation. The map annihilates R and fixes the irreducible coordinate vectors, while every vector differs from its normal form by R. It therefore induces an inverse to the map from the free space on irreducible indices into F/R. This proves both spanning and independence without assuming PBW or any algebra structure.
