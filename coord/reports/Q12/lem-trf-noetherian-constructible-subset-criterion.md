Hypotheses and conclusion, clause by clause:

* The scheme Y is `{Y : Scheme.{u}}`, with arbitrary explicit universe u.
  Its underlying topological space is the standard coercion of Y to a type.
* The finite affine open family is `(n : ℕ) (U : Fin n → Y.affineOpens)`.
  Mathlib's `Scheme.affineOpens` consists of open subsets with `IsAffineOpen`;
  the latter means the associated open subscheme is affine. Its canonical
  `isoSpec` identifies it with the spectrum of its ring of sections. This is
  precisely an affine chart by a spectrum, not merely a topological chart.
  Any finite family can be enumerated by some Fin n; no nonempty-index
  hypothesis or distinguished chart is imposed.
* That the family covers Y is `(hU : (⨆ i, (U i : Y.Opens)) = ⊤)`.
* The Noetherian chart rings are
  `(hN : ∀ i, IsNoetherianRing Γ(Y, U i))`. These are the chart's own
  coordinate rings, using the spectrum/global-sections identification from
  `def-weil-affine-spectrum`. Replacing a chosen chart ring by this canonically
  isomorphic ring changes neither the cover nor Noetherianity.
* The arbitrary subset is `(E : Set Y)`.
* The local test runs over **every** `(Z : Set Y)`, with hypotheses
  `IsClosed Z → IsIrreducible Z →`; it is not limited to irreducible components
  or to closures of closed points. `IsIrreducible` includes nonemptiness, as
  the dependency definition requires.
* The witness is `∃ (V : Set Y)`. It satisfies `V.Nonempty` and `V ⊆ Z`.
  Relative openness is the explicit ambient-open description
  `∃ O : Set Y, IsOpen O ∧ V = Z ∩ O`. This is exactly the subspace-topology
  definition and allows V to equal Z.
* The two alternatives are `V ⊆ E ∨ V ∩ E = ∅`.
* The sole conclusion is `Topology.IsConstructible E` in the original
  topology of Y, exactly the recorded constructible-subset notion. No
  separation, finite-type, base-field, dimension, or irreducibility assumption

  The only E is empty and is constructible; the irreducible-closed test is
