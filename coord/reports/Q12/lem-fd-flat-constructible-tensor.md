### Returned step 1, assigned to lem-fd-ringed-flat-single-tensor-cohomology

Put $R=\Lambda_X$ and $F(M)=M\otimes_R L$. By [def-pc-flat-sheaf-modules], $F$ is exact. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-complex-cohomology] to this functor on the abelian category of $R$-module sheaves. For every cochain complex $C$ this gives canonical isomorphisms $H^i(F(C))\cong H^i(C)\otimes_R L$, natural in $C$. The total complex $C\otimes_R L[0]$ is exactly $F(C)$: the only nonzero summand in degree $n$ has bidegree $(n,0)$ and its differential is $d_C\otimes1$, by [def-kun-derived-tensor]. In particular this tensor is acyclic whenever $C$ is acyclic. Thus $L[0]$ is K-flat in the sense of [def-pc-kflat-complex], and has flat terms (the other terms are zero).

Cited nodes: def-pc-flat-sheaf-modules, lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-complex-cohomology, def-kun-derived-tensor, def-pc-kflat-complex.

### Returned step 2, assigned to lem-fd-ringed-flat-single-tensor-cohomology

Represent $K$ by $C$, and choose a flat-term K-flat resolution $P\to C$ using [lem-pc-kflat-resolution]. The derived tensor of [def-kun-derived-tensor] is represented by $P\otimes_R L[0]$. Step 1 and the quasi-isomorphism give
$$\mathcal H^i(K\otimes^L_\Lambda L[0])\cong H^i(P\otimes_R L[0])\cong H^i(P)\otimes_R L\cong\mathcal H^i(K)\otimes_R L.$$
Here cohomology of a localized complex is the cohomology of that complex by [def-weil-pbc-derived-categories]. These identifications are natural on flat-term K-flat models: [lem-weil-pbc-site-derived-tensor-descent] supplies the natural tensor comparison and the equivalence between their localization and the derived category; the cohomology comparison is natural by [lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-complex-cohomology]. Consequently the displayed comparison descends to the derived category and is natural in $K$. For a map $L\to L'$ between flat sheaves, the maps $C^n\otimes_R L\to C^n\otimes_R L'$ commute with the differentials and carry cycles to cycles and boundaries to boundaries. The comparisons induced on their cycle quotients therefore commute with this map as well, proving naturality in the flat factor.

Cited nodes: lem-pc-kflat-resolution, def-kun-derived-tensor, def-weil-pbc-derived-categories, lem-weil-pbc-site-derived-tensor-descent, lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-complex-cohomology.
