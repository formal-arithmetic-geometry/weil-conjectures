## Proof in words: lem-sfb-ring-isomorphism-tensor-transport

Source read: literature/stacks-project/sites-cohomology.tex, Tag 06YP, lemma-K-flat-quasi-isomorphism, lines 3011 onwards; literature/stacks-project/sites-modules.tex, Tag 03EK, section-tensor-product, lines 3551 onwards. This is an agent-written ring-isomorphism application of those constructions, not a claim that Stacks states this compound comparison verbatim.

1. Restricting first along $c$ and then $c^{-1}$ restores every action and every map, since $cc^{-1}$ and $c^{-1}c$ are identities. Both restrictions are additive. Their inverse-equivalence Hom bijections preserve the kernel and cokernel universal properties; hence both are exact and preserve all existing coproducts.
2. For an object $U$, the balancing relation over $R(U)$ is $(c(r)m)\otimes n=m\otimes c(r)n$. Since every $s\in S(U)$ is uniquely $c(r)$, these are precisely the balancing relations over $S(U)$. Thus the two maps on pure tensors are inverse balanced additive maps. They commute with restriction, and sheafification gives the asserted inverse module-sheaf maps, using [def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product]. The unit map is $c^{-1}:C_cS\to R$. Associativity, unit and symmetry diagrams agree on the same pure tensors and therefore agree after sheafification.
3. Apply these maps on every summand of $(P\otimes Q)^n=\bigoplus_{p+q=n}P^p\otimes Q^q$ [def-pc-tensor-complex-sheaves]. They commute with both differential terms $d_P\otimes1+(-1)^p1\otimes d_Q$. Exactness preserves and reflects acyclicity. The tensor comparison and essential surjectivity identify every short-exact tensor test for flatness and every acyclic-complex tensor test for K-flatness [def-pc-flat-sheaf-modules, def-pc-kflat-complex].
4. An acyclic-test K-flat complex tensors quasi-isomorphisms to quasi-isomorphisms by [lem-pur-kflat-tensor-quasiiso-independent] (Stacks 06YP); symmetry gives the other tensor variable. Conversely apply quasi-isomorphism preservation to $0\to Z$ for each acyclic $Z$. This also identifies the two-sided convention [def-sfb-preserves-kflat-quasi-iso].
5. Corresponding K-flat resolutions now compute the two derived tensors. Exact termwise descent is [lem-sf-exact-functor-derived-triangulated]. Naturality and independence of representatives, and descent of the associator, unit and signed-symmetry equations, follow from the K-flat localization uniqueness in [lem-weil-pbc-site-derived-tensor-descent]. Flat-term K-flat representatives remain flat-term K-flat by step 3. This proves the stated coherent derived comparison without boundedness.
