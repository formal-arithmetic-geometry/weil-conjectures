### Old step 1 → lem-a1-representable-etale-kernel-relation

Work on the big fppf site fixed in the statement. The hypothesis on $p$ is a representable map of sheaves, with the meaning of [def-trf-algebraic-space]. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-site-sheaf-sets-limits-sectionwise] to the finite pullback diagram: for every $S$-scheme $T$, $R(T)=\{(a,b)\in U(T)^2:p_T(a)=p_T(b)\}$. The scheme fibre-product property [def-pc-fibre-product] identifies $(U\times_SU)(T)$ with $U(T)^2$. Thus $(s,t)$ identifies $R(T)$ with this subset. Equality in $X(T)$ is reflexive, symmetric and transitive, so these subsets are equivalence relations, compatible with restriction in $T$. This proves precisely the equivalence-relation assertion.

### Old step 2 → lem-a1-representable-etale-kernel-relation

By the definition of representable surjective etale in [def-trf-algebraic-space], the resulting projection $U\times_XU\to U$ is a surjective etale scheme morphism. This is one of $s,t$. This uses the scheme-test definition directly, so no extra separation, finiteness or quasi-compactness hypothesis is required.
