  "quote": "A *pre-triangulated category* is an additive category $\\mathcal T$ with an additive automorphism $[1]:\\mathcal T\\to\\mathcal T$ (the *shift*; write $[n]$ for its $n$-th power, $n\\in\\mathbf Z$) and a class of *distinguished triangles* $X\\xrightarrow{u}Y\\xrightarrow{v}Z\\xrightarrow{w}X[1]$ satisfying: (TR1) every triangle isomorphic to a distinguished one is distinguished, $X\\xrightarrow{\\mathrm{id}}X\\to0\\to X[1]$ is distinguished, and every morphism $u:X\\to Y$ is the first arrow of some distinguished triangle $X\\xrightarrow{u}Y\\to Z\\to X[1]$ (a *cone triangle* of $u$, $Z$ a *cone* of $u$); (TR2) $X\\xrightarrow{u}Y\\xrightarrow{v}Z\\xrightarrow{w}X[1]$ is distinguished if and only if its rotation $Y\\xrightarrow{v}Z\\xrightarrow{w}X[1]\\xrightarrow{-u[1]}Y[1]$ is; (TR3) given two distinguished triangles $(X,Y,Z,u,v,w)$, $(X',Y',Z',u',v',w')$ and morphisms $a:X\\to X'$, $b:Y\\to Y'$ with $b\\circ u=u'\\circ a$, there is $c:Z\\to Z'$ with $c\\circ v=v'\\circ b$ and $a[1]\\circ w=w'\\circ c$ (a *morphism of triangles*). A *triangulated category* is a pre-triangulated category satisfying moreover (TR4, the octahedral axiom): for composable $u:X\\to Y$, $v:Y\\to Z$ and distinguished cone triangles of $u$, $v$ and $v\\circ u$ with cones $C_u$, $C_v$, $C_{vu}$, there are morphisms $C_u\\to C_{vu}\\to C_v$ making the evident diagram commute and forming, with the composite $C_v\\to Y[1]\\to C_u[1]$, a distinguished triangle $C_u\\to C_{vu}\\to C_v\\to C_u[1]$. An additive functor $F:\\mathcal T\\to\\mathcal T'$ between pre-triangulated categories equipped with a natural isomorphism $F(X[1])\\cong F(X)[1]$ is *exact* (triangulated) if it carries distinguished triangles to distinguished triangles."
