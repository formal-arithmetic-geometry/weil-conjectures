The structure map is smooth separated finite type of pure dimension zero. Compactly supported degree-zero cohomology is A direct sum A and the counit is (a,b) |-> a+b: pullback is the diagonal functor on this disjoint union and its left adjoint is direct sum. The class (1,1) obtained by taking the unit generator on both components goes to 2, not 1. Each individual component generator goes to 1, but that does not select a single generator for the whole fiber. For the empty smooth scheme there is no such generator at all.

No connectedness, properness, descent to a finite field, ambient-admissibility datum, or coefficient-field restriction was added. I am not asserting that componentwise integration on a general connected smooth X is false; that different top-cohomology theorem is not established by the normalization sentence cited here.
