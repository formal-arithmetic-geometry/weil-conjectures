## Supporting arguments for the new statement-only nodes

For the weight decomposition I read Milne 12.12 and its coefficient-comparison proof, pp.234--235, and Stacks groupoids.tex 0EKJ/0EKK/0EKL in full. On an affine open of a trivially acted scheme, expand the module coaction in the Laurent basis. Counit says the coefficient projections sum to the identity; coassociativity says that they are pairwise orthogonal projections onto the character components. Since the base ring has degree zero, the projections are module-linear. Finite generators have finitely many coefficients, hence only finitely many weights occur. Restriction localizes those projections, so they glue; a finite affine cover gives global finiteness. A direct summand of a vector bundle is locally finite free. Exactness follows by lifting an element and projecting it to its specified weight. Exterior powers of a direct sum have their usual multi-index wedge decomposition, and local wedge bases calculate their ranks. These give the stated tensor/exterior and G0 comparisons. Ranks of vector bundles are locally constant; finitely many bundles on a quasi-compact scheme therefore admit a finite clopen common rank partition. A locally free sheaf nonzero at some point is nonzero on an open neighbourhood of constant positive rank, and that open contains a closed point on a Noetherian scheme. Thus vanishing on all closed fibres suffices. These are prerequisites about arbitrary trivial torus actions, not assertions of fixed-immersion smoothness or Euler invertibility.

For the support filtration I read chow.tex 02S8/02S9 including the generic-point filtration and the two ideal-sheaf exact sequences at the end of the proof, and homology.tex 02MX. In the quotient by sheaves of support dimension at most k-1, the class of a sheaf is its cycle with coefficients the lengths at dimension-k generic points. Tensor by a rank-n bundle multiplies each such length by n, since the bundle is free at the corresponding local ring. Thus tensor minus rank has zero graded class. The exact K0 sequence of the Serre quotient puts its image in the image of the smaller support category. This proves the lowering assertion even when the map from that category's K0 is not injective. After coefficient extension use the image filtration, with no flatness assertion required. Any sum of lowering operators lowers one step, so d+1 iterations are zero. This is an ordinary coherent-group fact, with no fixed locus, torus, or invertibility conclusion hidden in the new node.
