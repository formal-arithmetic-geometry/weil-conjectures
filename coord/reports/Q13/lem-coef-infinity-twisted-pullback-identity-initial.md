When i'<i, adjoining i' produces an earlier prioritized subset (of lower priority) containing the deletion. When i'=i, the deletion itself is prioritized of smaller cardinality and is earlier. Thus every facet except deletion of i is present, that facet and the full simplex are absent, and the intersection is exactly the stated horn.
