Old step 2 -> lem-trf-space-atlas-base-change-cech-geometry:

Fix $V\to Y$ etale from a scheme and put $W=V\times_YX$, $Z=V\times_YU$ and $Q=V\times_YR$. By [lem-trf-space-fibre-products-etale-covers], $W$ is an algebraic space and $Z\to W$ is the pullback of the singleton surjective etale covering $U\to X$, hence is a covering. Old step 3 -> lem-trf-space-atlas-base-change-cech-geometry:

The overlap of this covering is $Z\times_WZ\cong Q$. Indeed a map from a test scheme $T$ into $Z\times_WZ$ is a triple $(v,u_1,u_2)$ with $v:T\to V$, $u_i:T\to U$, $a u_1=a u_2$ equal to the image of $v$ in $Y$, and $p u_1=p u_2$ in $X$. The last equality gives a unique map $r:T\to R$ with projections $u_1,u_2$, and $b r$ is the image of $v$. Thus the triple is exactly a map $T\to V\times_YR$, with the two projections given by $p_1,p_2$. This construction and its inverse commute with test maps, so the fibre-product universal property in [lem-trf-space-fibre-products-etale-covers] gives the asserted canonical isomorphism. The base of the overlap here is $W$, not $X$.

Old step 4 -> lem-trf-space-atlas-direct-image-section-diagram:

Old step 5 -> lem-trf-space-enlarged-cover-module-kernel:

The equalizer sheaf axiom in [def-weil-pbc-sites-and-cohomology](1), applied to the singleton cover $Z\to W$ and the underlying abelian sheaf of $\widetilde F$, gives injectivity of $\widetilde F(W)\to\widetilde F(Z)$ and a unique antecedent for each $s\in\widetilde F(Z)$ whose two restrictions to $Q$ agree. For abelian groups this condition is exactly that their difference is zero. All maps are $\mathcal O_Y(V)$-linear: restrictions are semilinear over the structure-sheaf restrictions by [def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category], and the scalar actions here are induced from the same map to $V$. Thus the sequence of sections is exact at the first two terms as a sequence of $\mathcal O_Y(V)$-modules.

Old step 6 -> lem-trf-space-enlarged-cover-module-kernel:

For a map $V'\to V$ of etale schemes over $Y$, all the maps just constructed are restrictions along the induced fibre-product maps, so functoriality of restriction makes the square for every arrow commute.
