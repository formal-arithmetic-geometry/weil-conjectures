Step 1 uses precisely the faithful exact forgetful functor in clause (1) of `lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-colimits-forgetful`, with the ring sheaf equal to the constant ring sheaf on the small etale site, where abelian sheafification exists. I checked `lem-sp-exact-functor-preserves-homology` (a), (b), (c): they respectively provide kernels/quotients, images, and natural homology comparison for an exact functor. The step does not use that lemma to establish exactness of forgetting: that comes from the former node. The ordinary-module kernel/image/coset descriptions are written out.

Step 2 uses the actual colimit statement of `def-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks`, the constant-presheaf sheafification statement of `def-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf`, and the semilinear restriction identity in `def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category`. The formula `(a_V m)|_W = a_W(m|_W)` is why scalar multiplication respects the colimit relations. The displayed equation for a morphism is why all stalk maps are A-linear. Thus forgetting the stalk action really gives the same underlying abelian stalk; no comparison between two different fibre constructions is being assumed.

Step 3 cites `prop-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks-exact` only for what it says: exactness on abelian sheaves. The passage to modules uses the linearity established in step 2 and the explicit ordinary-module exactness criterion from step 1. There is no substitution of a Zariski stalk lemma for an etale stalk lemma and no finiteness assumption.

Step 4 applies `lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-complex-cohomology` between the two abelian module categories. Its statement explicitly covers integer-indexed complexes, naturality, and compatibility with cycles and quotient projections. The comparison is therefore an isomorphism in Mod(A). The argument for quasi-isomorphisms checks each degree, so it needs no boundedness or spectral-sequence convergence.

Step 5 uses the full unbounded factorization and representative formula of `lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-factorization`, not a D-plus-only theorem. The definitions `def-weil-derived-cohomology-sheaves` and `def-weil-abstract-derived-category` state the representative cohomology and the localization, respectively. I checked that the former is for an arbitrary ringed site, even though its final example is pro-etale. Naturality for inverted quasi-isomorphisms follows by composing the already established naturality square with inverses; the step writes this out, rather than asserting an unspecified transport.

Step 6 applies the exact statement of `lemma-weil-pbc-geometric-stalk-conservative` to a morphism of abelian etale sheaves, namely zero to the underlying cohomology sheaf. It never applies that statement directly to unbounded module complexes. The same faithful-forgetful clause used in step 1 then detects the zero identity. I checked that this works for the empty scheme: there are no geometric points and conservativity still implies every abelian sheaf there is zero.
