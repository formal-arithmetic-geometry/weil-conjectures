### lem-sf-bounded-below-hom-test

1. Complete $\alpha$ to a distinguished triangle $B\to G\to C\to B[1]$. Exactness of covariant Hom, also on rotations, and the assumed bijections imply $\operatorname{Hom}(H[n],C)=0$ for all bounded-below $H$ and all $n$. Indeed exactness makes every map to $C$ lift from $G$ after its connecting image vanishes by injectivity, and surjectivity then makes that lift zero in $C$. Inputs: def-weil-triangulated-category, lem-weil-pbc-hom-into-triangle-exact.

2. Choose a representing cochain complex $E$ of $C$. Its brutal lower tails $E_{\ge-r}$, zero in degrees below $-r$, are bounded-below subcomplexes with inclusion maps and degreewise colimit $E$ (each fixed degree eventually stabilizes). The complex-colimit/homotopy-colimit theorem gives the distinguished telescope triangle $\bigoplus_{r\ge0}E_{\ge-r}\xrightarrow{1-\mathrm{shift}}\bigoplus_{r\ge0}E_{\ge-r}\to C\to(\bigoplus_{r\ge0}E_{\ge-r})[1]$ in the derived category. Inputs: lem-kun-complex-colimit-hocolim.

3. For every $n$, the coproduct universal property gives $\operatorname{Hom}(\bigoplus_rE_{\ge-r}[n],C)=\prod_r\operatorname{Hom}(E_{\ge-r}[n],C)=0$. Contravariant Hom exactness on the telescope therefore gives $\operatorname{Hom}(C,C)=0$. Thus $1_C=0$ and $C$ is zero. For every object $W$, covariant Hom exactness on the first triangle now makes $\operatorname{Hom}(W,\alpha)$ bijective. Taking $W=G$ gives a right inverse and taking $W=B$ gives its left-inverse identity, so $\alpha$ is invertible. Inputs: lem-weil-pbc-hom-out-of-triangle-exact, lem-weil-pbc-hom-into-triangle-exact.

### lem-sf-etale-chart-adjoint-comparison

For a closed immersion, $i_*$ is exact on abelian sheaves, hence on module sheaves by forgetting the action, and $Ri_!=i_*$ using its identity/proper compactification. Closed immersions are finite and hence proper. Every compact-support composite $Rr_*j_!$ sends $D^+$ to $D^+$: after exact $j_!$, choose a bounded-below injective resolution and apply $r_*$ degreewise. Thus all inputs below meet the bounded-below composition theorem's hypothesis. ### lem-sf-etale-stalk-tor-test

1. Exact geometric stalks commute with cohomology. Their module versions are exact too: kernels and cokernels carry the induced actions and forgetting the action detects zero objects. The derived tensor/pullback theorem, applied to the geometric point as a ringed-topos point with constant coefficient ring $A$, gives $(K\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}F)_{\bar t}\simeq K_{\bar t}\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}F_{\bar t}$. Inputs: prop-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks-exact, lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-derived-tensor-pullback.

2. For an arbitrary module $M$, the constant module sheaf $M_T$ has stalk $M$: the germs of its locally constant sections are determined by their value at the point. Applying the preceding comparison to $M_T$ proves necessity. Conversely, apply the assumed stalk bounds to $M=F_{\bar t}$ for an arbitrary module sheaf $F$; conservativity of geometric stalks makes every cohomology sheaf outside $[a,b]$ zero. Inputs: def-kun-constant-sheaf, lemma-weil-pbc-geometric-stalk-conservative.

3. At a geometric point $\bar u$ of $U$, the stalk of $u^*K$ is $K_{u\bar u}$: composing the point inverse image with $u^*$ gives the inverse image at $u\bar u$. For any source module stalk $M$, the first step identifies the tensor test with $K_{u\bar u}\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}M$. Apply the already proved criterion. This uses the same ring $A$ on both sites, rather than an arbitrary nonflat change of rings. Inputs: prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact, lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-derived-tensor-pullback.

### lem-sf-localizing-hom-test

1. Complete $\alpha$ to a triangle with cone $C$. The localizing closure contains $C$. Covariant Hom exactness on this triangle and its rotations gives $\operatorname{Hom}(S[n],C)=0$: the two adjacent maps induced by $\alpha$ are respectively surjective and injective. This is valid for every integer $n$. Inputs: lem-weil-pbc-hom-into-triangle-exact, def-weil-triangulated-category.

2. Let $\mathcal V$ consist of the objects $T$ for which $\operatorname{Hom}(T[n],C)=0$ for every $n$. It is replete and shift-stable. Contravariant Hom exactness on any triangle shows it contains its third vertex whenever it contains the other two. It is closed under small sums: a morphism from a coproduct is exactly a family of morphisms from its summands, and shifting is an equivalence so preserves coproducts. Hence $\mathcal V$ is a localizing subcategory containing $\mathcal S$. Inputs: lem-weil-pbc-hom-out-of-triangle-exact.

3. The definition of the intersection gives $\operatorname{Loc}(\mathcal S)\subseteq\mathcal V$. In particular $C\in\mathcal V$, so $1_C=0$ and $C$ is zero. Covariant Hom exactness on the cone triangle then gives bijective $\operatorname{Hom}(W,\alpha)$ for every $W$. The preimage of $1_G$ is a right inverse; injectivity for $W=B$ makes it a left inverse too. Thus $\alpha$ is an isomorphism. Inputs: lem-weil-pbc-hom-into-triangle-exact.
