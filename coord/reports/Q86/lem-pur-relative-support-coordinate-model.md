### Original Step 1 → lem-pur-finite-type-coordinate-support-orientation

First identify the two meanings of closed support on the ordinary etale site. For $i:C\hookrightarrow P$ put $Q(F)=\ker(F\to j_*j^*F)$, with $j:P\setminus C\hookrightarrow P$. By [lem-kun-04ca], $Q(F)=i_*i^{-1}Q(F)$; every map from a sheaf supported on $C$ to $F$ factors uniquely through this kernel. Thus $i^!F=i^{-1}Q(F)$ is right adjoint to $i_*$. Closed direct image is exact by [lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-finite] and [lemma-weil-pbc-finite-direct-image-exact]; these assertions apply to constant-$A$ modules by forgetting the action. Also $i^{-1}$ is exact by [prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact]. Hence $i^!$ and $i_*$ preserve injectives, by their adjunctions to exact functors. Applying them to an injective resolution gives $RQ=i_*Ri^!$, where the right adjoint is the one of [lem-exc-closed-ambient], and $R\Gamma_C(P,F)=R\Gamma(C,Ri^!F)$. In the latter equality the same complex computes both sides: $\Gamma(P,Q(I^\bullet))=\Gamma(C,i^!I^\bullet)$. The natural triangle of [lem-exc-closed-support-triangle] consequently gives the localization triangle for the precise sheaf support functor in the item.

In an affine open of $S$ meeting that image choose a point in the image and a minimal prime $s$ contained in its prime; the minimal prime still lies in the image, since open subsets are stable under generalization. Existence here follows from Zorn: primes contained in the chosen prime are nonempty, and the intersection of a descending chain is prime (if a factor of a product is absent from one chain member, the other factor belongs to all smaller members and hence to every member). At this $s$, the image of $(Y_T)_s\to\mathbf A^a_{\kappa(s)}$ is a nonempty open, so it contains the generic point $\eta$. Choose $t'\in(Y_T)_s$ over $\eta$ and let $y\in Y$ be its image.

### Original Step 9 → lem-pur-coordinate-chart-finite-dimension-witness

Compute the local dimensions at this chosen point. Let $R$ be the affine base ring and replace it by $R_s$. The only prime of this local ring is its maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, since $s$ is minimal. Every element of $\mathfrak m$ is therefore nilpotent. Its image generates a nil ideal in any $R_s$-algebra: every element of that generated ideal is a finite sum of nilpotents. All primes contain this ideal, so quotienting by it gives an inclusion-preserving bijection on primes, also after localization. Thus local dimensions in the chart and in its support equal their local dimensions in the fibre over $\kappa(s)$. Localizing the base from $R$ to $R_s$ does not change any local ring at a point over $s$, because the inverted elements are already units there. By [lem-sc-etale-local-dimension], the local ring of $(Y_T)_s$ at $t'$ has dimension zero, that of $\mathbf A^a_{\kappa(s)}$ at its generic point. The ambient image in $\mathbf A^{a+b}_{\kappa(s)}$ is the prime $(v_1,\ldots,v_b)$ over this generic point. Its local ring is $\kappa(s)(u_1,\ldots,u_a)[v_1,\ldots,v_b]_{(v_1,\ldots,v_b)}$, whose dimension is $b$ by [lem-weil-pbc-mv-polynomial-maximal-height]. The etale chart and the etale maps $T\to X$, $Y_T\to Y$ preserve local dimensions by [lem-sc-etale-local-dimension]. Hence $\dim\mathcal O_{X,y}=b$ and $\dim\mathcal O_{Y,y}=0$. The hypothesis at this $y$ now reads $b=0+c$, proving $b=c$. This uses finite integers at the selected point and never cancels an infinite dimension.
