### Returned step 1

Allocation: Parent step 1 retains the initialization; the finite correction child includes the polar-basis premise used below.

Write $f=Q+\sum_{d\ge3}H_d$, where each $H_d$ is homogeneous of degree $d$. The polar linear forms of $Q$ are $L_1=2au+bv$ and $L_2=bu+2cv$; their coefficient matrix has determinant $4ac-b^2\ne0$, so they form a basis of the degree-one forms. Thus $E_2:=f-Q\in\mathfrak p^3$, and we start with $x_2=u$, $y_2=v$ and $f=Q(x_2,y_2)+E_2$.

### Returned step 2

Allocation: Finite correction child: homogeneous polar decomposition. This remains part of that child, not a separate elementary child.

For each $r\ge2$, the invertibility of the $L_1,L_2$ coefficient matrix means that every homogeneous form of degree $r+1$ can be written $L_1A_r+L_2B_r$ with $A_r,B_r$ homogeneous of degree $r$: this is obtained by expressing one degree-one factor in the basis $L_1,L_2$ and collecting the remaining degree-$r$ factor. This is the only finite-dimensional correction fact used below.

### Returned step 3

Allocation: Finite correction child: exact quadratic expansion, evaluated-order estimates, and the improved residual. Parent step 1 applies its assertion recursively.

Assume inductively that $x_r-u,y_r-v\in\mathfrak p^2$ and that the fixed series $f$ satisfies $f=Q(x_r,y_r)+E_r$ with $E_r\in\mathfrak p^{r+1}$. Let $H_{r+1}$ be the homogeneous degree-$(r+1)$ part of $E_r$, so $E_r-H_{r+1}\in\mathfrak p^{r+2}$. By the preceding basis calculation choose homogeneous $A_r,B_r$ of degree $r$ with $H_{r+1}=L_1A_r+L_2B_r$, and define $x_{r+1}=x_r+A_r(x_r,y_r)$ and $y_{r+1}=y_r+B_r(x_r,y_r)$. For any $x,y,A,B\in S$ one has the exact identity $Q(x+A,y+B)=Q(x,y)+L_1(x,y)A+L_2(x,y)B+Q(A,B)$, by expanding $a(x+A)^2+b(x+A)(y+B)+c(y+B)^2$. Since $x_r\equiv u$, $y_r\equiv v$ modulo $\mathfrak p^2$ and $A_r,B_r$ are homogeneous of degree $r$, we have $A_r(x_r,y_r)-A_r(u,v),B_r(x_r,y_r)-B_r(u,v)\in\mathfrak p^{r+1}$ and $L_i(x_r,y_r)-L_i(u,v)\in\mathfrak p^2$; in particular $A_r(x_r,y_r),B_r(x_r,y_r)\in\mathfrak p^r$. Hence $L_1(x_r,y_r)A_r(x_r,y_r)+L_2(x_r,y_r)B_r(x_r,y_r)\equiv L_1A_r+L_2B_r=H_{r+1}\pmod{\mathfrak p^{r+2}}$, and $Q(A_r(x_r,y_r),B_r(x_r,y_r))\in\mathfrak p^{2r}\subseteq\mathfrak p^{r+2}$ because $r\ge2$. Therefore $Q(x_{r+1},y_{r+1})\equiv Q(x_r,y_r)+H_{r+1}\pmod{\mathfrak p^{r+2}}$, and $E_{r+1}:=f-Q(x_{r+1},y_{r+1})\equiv E_r-H_{r+1}\equiv0\pmod{\mathfrak p^{r+2}}$. The correction lies in $\mathfrak p^r\subseteq\mathfrak p^2$, so $x_{r+1}-u,y_{r+1}-v\in\mathfrak p^2$, completing the induction from the base case of step 1.

### Returned step 4

Allocation: Limit child: all of this step, including coefficient stabilization, tail bounds, Noetherian/Nakayama/parameter-coordinate construction, and continuity in both directions.

The increments $x_{r+1}-x_r,\,y_{r+1}-y_r$ lie in $\mathfrak p^r$ for $r\ge2$ (step 3), and $\mathfrak p^r$ consists of the series with no monomial of degree $<r$ [def-weil-pbc-multivariate-power-series]. Hence for every degree $d$ the coefficients of degree $d$ of $x_r,y_r$ are constant for $r>d$; let $x_\infty,y_\infty\in S$ be the coefficientwise limits. Then $x_\infty-x_r,\,y_\infty-y_r\in\mathfrak p^r$ for every $r\ge2$, and $x_\infty-u,\,y_\infty-v\in\mathfrak p^2$. By [lem-weil-pbc-power-series-field-cohen-regular-complete] (1) and (4) with $\Lambda=F$, $d=2$, $S$ is a Noetherian local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak p$ and Krull dimension $2$.
