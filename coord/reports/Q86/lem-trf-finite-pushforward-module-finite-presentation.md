### Returned Step 1

For any open neighbourhood $W$ of $t$, the set $C=T\setminus W$ is closed and $p(C)$ is closed by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-closed-map-finite-fibres](1). Since the entire fibre over $u$ is $\{t\}$, we have $u\notin p(C)$. Thus $V_0=U\setminus p(C)$ is an open neighbourhood of $u$ with $p^{-1}V_0\subseteq W$. Choose an affine open $u\in V\subseteq V_0$ by [lem-weil-pbc-affine-opens-basis]. Consequently inverse images of affine neighbourhoods of $u$ are cofinal among all neighbourhoods of $t$.

Allocation: Existing lem-trf-finite-singleton-fibre-localization supplies the closed-map shrinking conclusion, refined by the affine basis in new Step 1.

### Returned Step 2

Let $V=\operatorname{Spec}R$ be any affine neighbourhood of $u$. Finiteness of $p$ means $p^{-1}V=\operatorname{Spec}S$ is affine and $R\to S$ is finite [def-weil-finite-integral-morphism]. Since $p$ is locally of finite presentation, [lem-weil-pbc-lfp-affine-opens-finite-presentation] applied to these two affine opens makes $R\to S$ finitely presented as an algebra. Put $N=\Gamma(p^{-1}V,\mathcal M)$. The direct image is quasi-coherent by [lem-weil-pbc-affine-pushforward-base-change](1), since $p$ is affine and $\mathcal M$ is quasi-coherent. Its module of sections on $V$ is $N$ regarded as an $R$-module: [lem-weil-pbc-pushforward-spec-global-sections] identifies the scalar action with restriction of scalars along $R\to S$.

Allocation: Both new global helpers carry this complete affine reduction and scalar identification, in their independent directions; see the two full argument allocations above.

### Returned Step 3

Apply [lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-finite-type-affine](b) on $p^{-1}V$ and on $V$. It identifies finite presentation of $\mathcal M|_{p^{-1}V}$ with finite presentation of $N$ over $S$, and finite presentation of $(p_*\mathcal M)|_V$ with finite presentation of the same $N$ over $R$. $$\mathcal M|_{p^{-1}V}\text{ is of finite presentation}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad(p_*\mathcal M)|_V\text{ is of finite presentation}.$$

### Returned Step 5

The map $\theta:(p_*\mathcal M)_u\to\mathcal M_t$ sends the germ at $u$ of a section $s\in\mathcal M(p^{-1}V)$ to its germ at $t$. This is bijective directly from [def-weil-pbc-module-stalk] and Step 1: every germ at $t$ is represented on some $W\ni t$ and can be restricted to $p^{-1}V\subseteq W$, proving surjectivity.
