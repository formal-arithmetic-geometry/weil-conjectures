### Old step 1

Algebraize the ideal. On a strict etale chart with generators $E$, every section of $\mathcal H$ is locally $e+p+u$ with $e\in E$, $p\in P$ and $u\in\mathcal O^\times$, so its image is $\alpha(e)\alpha(p)u$. Conversely each $e$ lies in the ideal. Hence its image generates exactly $J_E=(\alpha(e):e\in E)$. This ideal and its inclusions into the structure sheaf agree on chart overlaps, since they are both the ideal generated by the same image sheaf $\alpha(\mathcal H)$. Etale covers are fpqc covers by [lem-weil-pbc-fpqc-finer-than-etale]; effective descent and full faithfulness for quasi-coherent modules in [lem-pc-fpqc-descent] descend these ideals and inclusions to a quasi-coherent ideal $K\subseteq\mathcal O_Y$. Faithful flatness reflects the injectivity of these inclusions, so its image is precisely the stipulated image ideal. Finite type is local for this cover: over an affine base choose a finite affine fpqc refinement, use its faithfully flat product algebra, and apply [lem-weil-pbc-faithfully-flat-descent-finite-module] to the module of the descended ideal. The generators on this refinement are the finitely many images of the corresponding $E$. Thus $K$ is of finite type in [pqf-def-coherent], with the asserted chart formula.

### Old step 4

For $e,f\in E$, inside $P^{\mathrm{gp}}$ one has
$$Q_e+\mathbf N(e-f)=Q_f+\mathbf N(f-e).$$
Indeed $h-f=(h-e)+(e-f)$ and $h-e=(h-f)+(f-e)$, while the adjoined elements are mutually inverse. Thus localizing $\mathbf Z[Q_e]$ at $z^{f-e}$ and $\mathbf Z[Q_f]$ at $z^{e-f}$ gives the same monoid algebra. Tensoring with $A$ preserves this equality of the presentations (both are obtained by adjoining a variable inverse to the same monomial). These identify the corresponding principal open subschemes and their chart log structures. The same construction works with saturated monoids by localization compatibility in [lem-trf-fine-monoid-finite-saturation]. On a triple overlap all three identifications are the identity in the common localization inside $P^{\mathrm{gp}}$, so the cocycle condition holds. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-glue-schemes] to obtain schemes $U_0$ and $U_s$, and glue their log sheaves by these log isomorphisms. The associated-log pushouts agree on overlaps, so the glued sheaves satisfy the log-structure condition and have the displayed fine or fs charts. Their maps to $U$ and their principal pulled-back ideals agree on overlaps as well.

### Old step 5

The group homomorphism $P^{\mathrm{gp}}\to M_T^{\mathrm{gp}}$ induced by $g$ therefore restricts to $Q_e\to M_T$.
