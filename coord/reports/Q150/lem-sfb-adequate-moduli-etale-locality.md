Step 1 uses def-sfb-adequate-moduli-space for necessity and for the tests to be proved. For sufficiency, the new affine-algebra-direct-image statement gives both affineness of the projection from an affine scalar extension and the exact quasi-coherent algebra direct image, with a natural global-section ring identification. Applying the assumed power-lifting condition to this direct-image algebra surjection therefore proves the test for every algebra on the extended stack. I checked that this is the quasi-coherent right adjoint, not the naive big-site direct image. For the structure sheaf, lem-weil-pbc-etale-algebra-flat supplies flatness of D over C, the new flat-scalar-sections statement applies to the original qcqs morphism, and its unit formula identifies the tensor comparison with the canonical structural map. Tensoring the assumed C-algebra isomorphism gives the required map on every affine etale test. This is independent of the existing global-power-lifting-adequacy node.

Step 2 uses lem-sfb-algebraic-stack-fibre-products for algebraicity and the arbitrary-base-change clause of lem-sfb-stack-etale-local-qcqs-homeomorphism for qcqs. I opened both statements: they concern general algebraic stacks, not only DM stacks. The composition clause of lem-trf-space-chartwise-etale-permanence makes each affine test over Y_i a test over Y. The triple construction of def-sfb-stable-pointed-moduli-groupoids gives the reassociation of test stacks. The global-section direct-image formula in def-sfb-stack-quasi-coherent-algebra identifies the structural maps. Adequacy supplies all the test algebras on that very stack, so no descent of algebras is being assumed.

Step 3 uses the cover-base-change clause of the chartwise-etale statement and the same direct-image formula. Under the project's representable-etale convention, the pullbacks of the Y_i to a scheme W are schemes. Adequacy gives a structural-map isomorphism on each covering member. The inverse maps agree on overlaps because they invert the same map; sheaf gluing then gives a global inverse. This elementary inverse-gluing argument needs no separate descent lemma and does not claim that sections of an algebra epimorphism lift globally.

Step 4 applies the existing finite-affine covering-sieve refinement with base scheme W, final etale object W, and the sieve generated by the pulled-back covering family. I checked that the theorem requires compactness of this object, which holds because W is affine; it does not require a finite original covering family. Its selected arrows belong to the generated sieve and hence factor through individual covering members. Cancellation in lem-trf-space-chartwise-etale-permanence makes their maps to Y_i etale. The finite-affine-coproduct-spectrum statement identifies the coproduct with Spec of the finite product ring. The etale-algebra-flat, finite-product-flat-modules, and flat-surjective-spectrum-faithfully-flat statements then give exactly flatness and faithful flatness of R to S. Algebraicity and relative qcqs of the test stack come from the same two stack statements as in step 2.

Step 5 uses flat-scalar-sections first to preserve the algebra surjections and then to identify the map on global sections with h tensor S, naturally in the algebra map. Adequacy of the selected f_i supplies a power lift on each affine test. Taking the product of the finitely many positive exponents, raising the lifts accordingly, and using the sheaf axiom on disjoint components produces a lift over Spec S. With Q the additive module cokernel of h, tensor-right-exact says its class is zero in Q tensor S. Faithfully-flat-universally-injective states injectivity for every module Q, with no finite-generation restriction, and makes that class zero already over R. Thus the step descends image membership, which requires no compatibility between the separate lifts. Step 6 only assembles these tests and the structural isomorphism through the adequate-moduli definition.
