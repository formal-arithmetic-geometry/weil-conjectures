The new node is true for the following reason, recorded here rather than as a proof in its statement-only JSON. Flatness of A→B preserves the injections given by multiplication by nonzerodivisors, so Q(A) tensor B is a localization of B at nonzerodivisors. By presenting B ind-etale, this algebra is a filtered colimit of etale algebras over Q(A). Since Q(A) is a finite product of fields, those algebras are finite products of finite separable fields. Their colimit is reduced and has dimension zero: in any prime quotient every element is algebraic over the relevant base field, so a nonzero element has its inverse as a polynomial in that element. It is also Noetherian, being a localization of B. All minimal primes of B survive the localization because its denominators are nonzerodivisors. Dimension zero then shows that the surviving primes are exactly those minimal primes. Hence every nonzerodivisor of B becomes a unit, which gives the asserted canonical isomorphism. This explains why a genuine algebra lemma remains, rather than hiding the generic comparison inside an item step.
