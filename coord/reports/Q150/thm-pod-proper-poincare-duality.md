## What was wrong and what changed

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the item binds `A=A_{E,n}` but uses a tensor over `A` with target `A` while allowing coefficients `Lambda=O_E,E,Qbar_l`. This is an overloaded-symbol error. On `X=Spec k` with `K=E_X`, the intended pairing is `E tensor_E E -> E`. It cannot be an `A_{E,n}`-module pairing with the intended scalar action: a unital map `O_E/(varpi_E^n) -> E` extending the coefficient action would send a nilpotent uniformizer to an invertible element, hence would send `0=varpi_E^n` to a nonzero element. For `E=Q_l`, already `Z_l/l^n -> Q_l` has no unital ring map, since `l^n*1` is zero in the domain and nonzero in the codomain. The same finite-ring notation cannot be literal at the integral stage.

The conventions also say to write `A` for the selected point coefficient ring, which explains the source's intent, but the item's explicit finite binding makes that abbreviation ambiguous. I replaced the displayed tensor subscript and target by `Lambda` and stated that point duality is `RHom_Lambda(-,Lambda)`. This agrees with the already split definition of the derived Poincare pairing and the derived-duality theorem. At the finite stage it is identical to the old formula. No geometric or perfectness hypotheses or conclusions were changed.
