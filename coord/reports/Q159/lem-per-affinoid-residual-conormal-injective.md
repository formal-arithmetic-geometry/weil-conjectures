New rational-local-coordinate node: recentering is permitted because every coordinate has absolute value at most one. At the origin a restricted series decomposes into its constant, linear and degree-at-least-two parts; the last part lies in the algebraic ideal (x)^2 by grouping the monomials into finitely many pairs of variables, with restricted remaining coefficients. Thus first-order classes are the linear coefficients. Inverting u with u(a) nonzero changes the class of h/u to the linear coefficients of h divided by u(a), since h(a)=0. This verifies the displayed formula and its inverse rather than using a vague transport.
