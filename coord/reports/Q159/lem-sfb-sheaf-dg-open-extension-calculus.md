## Source error

In `literature/stacks-project/sdga.tex`, section-localize-dg (0FRV), the prose immediately before lemma-extension-by-zero-dg says that the displayed extension functor is “right adjoint to” the direct image. This has the wrong adjunction direction and objects. The formula in 0FRW and the graded construction in 0FRA give extension by zero left adjoint to restriction. For example, on the identity open this is harmless, but for a proper nonclosed open, direct image and extension by zero have different boundary stalks, so the two functors cannot be substituted. The item already states the correct formula. No correction to its statement was necessary; the split follows the displayed Hom formula, not that prose sentence.
