### Returned Step 1, now carried by lem-trf-proj-product-chart-twist-frame

Use the union characterization of $W_d$ in the hypothesis. Its covering charts are $V=D_+(ab)$ with $\alpha=\deg(a)$, $\beta=\deg(b)$, $\alpha=\beta+d$; put $h=ab$ and $k=\alpha+\beta>0$. By [def-weil-pbc-proj-twisting-sheaf], sections of $\mathcal O_P(nd)$ on $V$ are the degree-$nd$ fractions in $A_h$. Both $a$ and $b$ are units in $A_h$, since their product is inverted. Thus $e=(a/b)^n=a^{2n}/h^n$ has degree $nd$, its inverse has degree $-nd$, and multiplication by $e$ identifies $(A_h)_0$ with $(A_h)_{nd}$: the inverse sends $t$ to $e^{-1}t$. These formulas commute with localization restrictions, so they give an isomorphism of sheaves $\mathcal O_V\simeq\mathcal O_P(nd)|_V$. The canonical section restricts to $f/1=c e$, with $c=b^nf/a^n\in(A_h)_0$. This is a direct fraction calculation from the cited definition, including when $V$ is empty.

### Returned Step 2, now carried by lem-trf-proj-product-chart-coefficient-locus

Fix a chart from Step 1 and a point $x\in U\cap V$. Nonvanishing means $s_{f,x}\notin\mathfrak m_x\mathcal O_U(nd)_x$, by [def-weil-pbc-ample-invertible-module]. In the basis $e$ this is $c_x\notin\mathfrak m_x$, equivalently $c_x$ is a unit. The degree-zero fraction $u=b^{2n\alpha}/a^{2n\beta}$ is a unit of $(A_h)_0$, with inverse $a^{2n\beta}/b^{2n\alpha}$. Direct multiplication, using $d=\alpha-\beta$, gives $c^k=u f^k/h^{nd}$. In any commutative local ring a positive power is a unit exactly when its base is a unit; multiplication by a unit does not change this. Therefore $c_x$ is a unit exactly when $(f^k/h^{nd})_x$ is. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-proj-basic-open-intersection] with its chart element $h\in A_k$ and second element $f\in A_{nd}$: this unit locus is precisely $V\cap D_+(f)$. Both degrees are positive. Consequently $U_{s_f}\cap V=U\cap V\cap D_+(f)$. The charts cover $U$, so equality of these sets on every chart gives $U_{s_f}=U\cap D_+(f)$.

### Returned Step 3, now carried by lem-trf-proj-degree-multiple-affine-refinement

Let $x\in U$ and let $N\subseteq U$ be an open neighbourhood. Since $U$ is open in $P$, $N$ is also open in $P$. The positive-degree basis clause of [lem-weil-pbc-proj-standard-open-inclusion] gives a homogeneous $g\in A_q$, $q>0$, with $x\in D_+(g)\subseteq N$. Put $f=g^d\in A_{qd}$, so its degree is divisible by $d$. For every homogeneous prime $\mathfrak p$, primality gives $g^d\notin\mathfrak p$ if and only if $g\notin\mathfrak p$ because $d\geq1$. Thus $D_+(f)=D_+(g)\subseteq U$, and Step 2 gives $U_{s_f}=D_+(g)$. Every prescribed neighbourhood therefore contains one of the stated loci through its point; all these loci are open since $D_+(f)$ is open. This proves the basis assertion without quasi-compactness of $U$.

### Returned Step 4, now carried by lem-trf-proj-degree-multiple-affine-refinement

The loci chosen in Step 3 equal whole standard opens $D_+(g)\subseteq U$. By the affine-chart clause of [def-pc-proj], each is the affine scheme $\operatorname{Spec}(A_{(g)})$. Hence for every point and neighbourhood in $U$ there is an affine member of the stated family inside that neighbourhood. This proves that its affine members form an affine-open basis. We do not assert that an arbitrary intersection $U\cap D_+(f)$ is affine. When $U$ is empty all the equalities and basis conditions are vacuous; when $f=0$ or is nilpotent, the chart calculation yields the empty nonvanishing locus as required.
