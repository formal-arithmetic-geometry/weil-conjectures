## Where the brief, the DAG or earlier work was wrong
The draft `chart_overlap_scalar` quantified over an arbitrary additive equivalence without requiring compatibility with restriction or the A-action. This is false. For A = Q(√2), Y = Spec A, h = identity, t = 1 and f = 1, compose the canonical additive section identification with field conjugation. On a = √2 and m = 1, the two sides of the claimed scalar equation differ by sign. Its proposed proof by the localization universal property therefore could not work. The draft nodes also lacked self-contained statements, verbatim Lean signatures and recorded Mathlib searches; the target node did not describe the draft proof.

The replacement avoids this fault by obtaining an A-linear equivalence directly from localization. Source read: [Stacks 0BLH](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BLH), first proof paragraph (two chart localizations represent the same overlap), and [Stacks 05EL](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/05EL), item (1) (module glueing transitions).
