## Review by E1

I read Stacks `cohomology.tex`, Proposition Tag 02UZ (`proposition-vanishing-Noetherian`) and its proof, including the reduction to irreducible spaces and the dimension-zero case, against the item's statement. It assumes a Noetherian topological space and an abelian sheaf, with vanishing in degrees strictly above an upper bound for its dimension. A Noetherian scheme has a Noetherian underlying space by Properties Lemma Tag 01OZ, exactly the fact included in the Mathlib leaf `lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-scheme-closed-subsets-minimal`; the source of that leaf and Mathlib `IsNoetherian.noetherianSpace` at `Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Noetherian.lean:318-322` were checked. The item assumes the stronger property that the abelian sheaf comes from a quasi-coherent module. No separatedness or finite-type hypothesis is needed. If `q` is a finite natural number above the dimension and `X` is nonempty, the dimension is a finite integer `d<q`; this is exactly the case stated by the existing blueprint node `lem-sc-zariski-dimension-bound`, whose statement I read. Its proof is not used.

For step 1, `lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-scheme-closed-subsets-minimal` supplies Noetherian topology, `def-kun-topological-krull-dimension` supplies the dimension comparison, `lem-sc-zariski-dimension-bound` supplies vanishing for the underlying abelian sheaf, and the new `lem-sc-module-abelian-zariski-cohomology` supplies the canonical passage back to module cohomology. The remaining substitution of `d<q` is elementary. I checked the hypotheses at this step: nonempty scheme, finite degree, Noetherian topology, finite dimension, and an abelian underlying sheaf.

For step 2, the only open of the empty space is empty and the sheaf axiom for its empty cover makes every abelian sheaf's section group there zero. Thus its restrictions are surjective by `def-sc-zariski-flasque`; `lem-sc-zariski-flasque-acyclic` supplies positive-degree vanishing, while degree zero is the zero section group. The comparison node gives the corresponding module groups. I checked Stacks 02UZ at dimension bound `d=0` and `def-kun-topological-krull-dimension` at the bottom dimension of the empty space. In particular the edge case `q=0` is covered, not only `q>0`.
