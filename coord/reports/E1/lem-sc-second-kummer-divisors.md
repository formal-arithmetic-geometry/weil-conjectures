In step 1, the hypotheses that `C` is a curve over algebraically closed `k` match `lem-sc-brauer-zero-all-curves`; the hypothesis that `n` is invertible in `k` makes it invertible on `C`. `lem-sc-kummer-exact` supplies exactness of the sequence defined in `def-sc-kummer-sequence`, repairing return point 2 (C4). This node states the connecting map and the exact segment needed here, repairing return point 1 (C4/C3). The power map on `G_m` is the sum of `n` identities as a map of abelian sheaves, so its induced map on `H¹` is multiplication by `n`; the group isomorphism of `lem-sc-h1-gm-pic` carries it to multiplication by `n` on `Pic(C)`. `lem-sc-brauer-zero-all-curves` kills the next group. Ordinary exactness then gives the boundary kernel, its surjectivity, and the induced quotient isomorphism. This proves both the isomorphism with its specified map and the item's explicit Brauer-vanishing sentence. `proof_deps` is exactly the union of the two steps' citations. The long-exact-ladder node is an off-unit generic tool: it supplies long exact cohomology and connecting maps for any étale sheaf short exact sequence, which this unit's own nodes do not state.
