Complete-node plan: use the conormal factorization associated to the evaluated obstruction bound to perform the principal Newton correction in the parameter-regular quotient. Iteration improves the relation error quadratically with a fixed loss controlled by h, and completeness gives an exact solution. To account for zero divisors, let T be the t-power torsion of A. Noetherianity stabilizes Ann(t^k), so T is killed by a fixed power t^c; the induced t on A/T is regular, and A/T is complete. For k at least c one has T intersect t^k A=0: if t^k x lies in T, then x is t-power torsion and hence t^c x=0. Reduce the presentation and approximate tuple modulo T; the base-change inclusion for obstruction ideals preserves the bound. Lift the resulting correction from t^{n-r}(A/T) to t^{n-r}A. Its residual equations belong both to T and to a sufficiently deep power of t, so are zero. This gives constants independent of the approximate tuple once h and the presentation are fixed. This is the agent's conormal formulation of Elkik I Lemma 1, with GR (5.4.4)/5.4.8 supplying the regular-parameter Newton mechanism; the node does not pretend to quote Elkik's classical Jacobian conductor verbatim.
