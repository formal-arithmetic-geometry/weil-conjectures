It uses the affine spectrum and algebra tensor pushout statements to express group diagrams as Hopf identities. The new lattice statement really supplies a finite basis and stable coaction containing the chosen generators and 1, rather than only a finite-dimensional generic-fibre representation. Its torsion-free hypothesis follows by tensoring multiplication by a nonzero a with the already flat B. Smooth affine finite-type H is quasi-compact and quasi-separated, so its locally finitely presented structure morphism is finitely presented. I verified that including 1 forces n positive through the counit, even when H is trivial.

The cited closed-immersion definition expressly includes the affine surjective-ring-map characterization; the affine-spectrum statement supplies contravariance and the morphism dictionary.
