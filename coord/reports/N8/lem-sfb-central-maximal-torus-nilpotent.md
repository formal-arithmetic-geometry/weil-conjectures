  No characteristic restriction is imposed.
- H and S are `Grp (Over (Spec (.of k)))`; their underlying schemes with group laws
  are `H.X`, `S.X`. The group-scheme homomorphism is `iS : S ⟶ H`.
- Affineness is `haff : IsAffine H.X.left`. Finite type is the pair
  `hft : LocallyOfFiniteType H.X.hom`, `hqc : QuasiCompact H.X.hom`.
  The closed subgroup S automatically is affine and finite type, so these are not
  imposed as independent additional hypotheses on S.
- Smoothness is `hsm : Smooth H.X.hom`; connectedness (including nonemptiness) is
  `hconn : ConnectedSpace H.X.left`.
- `hmax : ReductiveTorusCentralizer.IsMaximalTorus iS` includes the closed subgroup
  condition on iS, that S is a torus, and that any factorization through another
  closed torus has an isomorphism as its factor. Its torus predicate uses a finite
  product of the recorded G_m after algebraic closure. Since k is already
  algebraically closed this is equivalent to being a split torus over k, as in the
  item; rank zero is included. This uses the existing torus notion, not a new copy.
- `hcentral` quantifies over every `(A : Type u) [CommRing A] [Algebra k A]`, every
  `s : StandardGroupSchemes.affine k A ⟶ S.X`, and every corresponding H-point h.
  `Commute (s ≫ iS.hom.hom) h` is precisely centrality of the inclusion on A-points.
- Nilpotence is the first conjunct: an integer `m : ℕ`, family
  `C : Fin (m + 1) → Grp (Over (Spec (.of k))))`, and inclusions `i j : C j ⟶ H`.
  Every inclusion is `IsClosedSubgroupScheme (i j).hom.hom`. `IsIso (i 0)` says that
  the first subgroup is all of H; `Nonempty (C (Fin.last m) ≅ Grp.trivial _)` says
  the last subgroup is the trivial group scheme. These encode equality of subgroups
  independently of their chosen representing objects.
- For every `j : Fin m`, the morphism `l : C j.succ ⟶ C j.castSucc` with
  `l ≫ i j.castSucc = i j.succ` expresses the descending inclusion.
- For every such j and every algebra A, h in H(A), and c in C_j(A), existence of z
  in C_(j+1)(A) with
  `z ≫ (i j.succ).hom.hom = h * (c ≫ (i j.castSucc).hom.hom) * h⁻¹ *
  (c ≫ (i j.castSucc).hom.hom)⁻¹` is the relative commutator containment.
  A single series works for all A, including nonreduced algebras. Since its terms
  are closed, containing all these commutators is equivalent to containing the
  closed subgroup generated by them, including after scalar extension. Morphisms
  from affine schemes detect the scheme-theoretic condition, not just k-points.
- The second conjunct `IsSolvableGroupScheme H.X` uses the recorded recursive
  scheme-theoretic derived subgroup definition: the smallest closed subgroup
  through which the commutator morphism factors, iterated until terminality.
  Thus this is termination of the actual algebraic derived series.
