## What was wrong or incomplete

In particular, I did not claim duality for every bounded constructible complex over a singular coefficient algebra, nor assume that every coefficient object is individually extended from the prime field.

The item already uses that convention and needs no correction. The proof also uses the evaluation-transpose biduality, not an arbitrary choice of a double-dual isomorphism.

The proof follows ILO XVII 7.1.3's residue-field reduction. For the arbitrary residue-field case I spell out 7.5.3's generator argument directly using the canonical internal-Hom comparison, rather than introducing another theorem that restates the field-coefficient clause. I realize the vanishing test of Lemma 7.6.2 by the existing general Stacks 066E and 068V nodes. The original imported ancestor place mentioned an obsolete Step 3. I filled it with the current ancestor's Step 1, identifying exactly the relative Verdier duality and biduality input along the preserved `via` chain.
