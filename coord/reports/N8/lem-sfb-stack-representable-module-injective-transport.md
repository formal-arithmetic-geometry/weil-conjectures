## Proofs retained for the new prerequisites

For lem-sfb-fibred-limit-calculus I read stacks-sheaves.tex 06WY and 06WZ, including their proofs. Fibre products lift from schemes via the cartesian-arrow property. To equalize two arrows, first restrict their source to the equalizer of the two base maps. They then differ by an automorphism of the same pulled-back object. Its equality with the identity is represented by the pullback of the identity section against this automorphism in the algebraic-space Isom sheaf. This is a scheme, since a section of an algebraic space over a scheme is representable; restriction to it is the desired universal equalizer. Algebraic stacks have representable Isom sheaves. A functor over schemes preserves the cartesian-lift construction of fibre products. For equalizers, given y -> F(x) equalizing F(r),F(s), identify y with F of the pullback of x along its base map. Faithfulness makes that pullback arrow equalize r,s; the unique factorization in C, followed by F, supplies the required unique factorization in D. This is precisely 06WZ, not the false assertion that arbitrary faithful functors preserve equalizers.

For lem-sfb-local-slice-module-shriek-exact I read sites-modules.tex 0797, 0798 and 04BH in full. The module left adjoint exists by 0797. In 0798 the comparison from abelian lower shriek of the restricted structure sheaf on C/U to the restricted structure sheaf on D/u(U) is an isomorphism because u_U is a ringed-site equivalence. Hence module lower shriek agrees on underlying abelian sheaves with abelian lower shriek. The latter is exact by 04BH, whose hypotheses are continuity, cocontinuity, fibre products, equalizers and preservation of both. Forgetting the module action is exact and faithful, so exactness follows for modules. This is a general site criterion, not the representable-stack injective-transport clause restated. The proof does not assume nonempty comma categories or a terminal object: disjoint/empty components give zero contributions.
