## Problems found and corrections

The imported item is not false and has not been weakened. Its references require care: Theorem 7.2.1 does not grant universality for every algebraic-space target. Using it directly for the conclusion would be a gap. The split first produces a finite diagonal and a separated Keel--Mori coarse algebraic space. It uses 7.2.1 only to build the two inverse maps between that separated space and the affine invariant spectrum. Arbitrary target spaces then come from the coarse universal property, not 7.2.1.

A second possible gap is confusing zero-dimensional geometric stabilizers with finite inertia as a morphism over the whole stack. Step 1 proves only the quasi-finite separated diagonal; step 3 cites the adequate finite-diagonal theorem for the global finiteness conclusion. A third is silently passing from finite-type algebra power lifting to arbitrary quasi-coherent algebra sheaves. The new power-lifting criterion states that passage explicitly; it is supported by Alper's finite-type equivalences in Lemma 9.2.5 and Theorem 9.1.4, rather than hidden inside the item's step.
