## Review by N8

I read more-morphisms.tex, Tags 05GT and 05GU, statements and full proofs. The displayed 05GU statement allows an arbitrary closed subscheme, but its proof expressly chooses the reduced closure of the selected prime's image. This gives exactly the strengthened integral/generic-point formulation in our item. I also read schemes.tex at Tags 01J3/01J4 (the reduced induced structure and its affine ideal), 01J6/01J7 (the canonical local-spectrum map and its image), properties.tex at 02IM (punctured local spectrum), morphisms.tex at 01TH in full, and algebra.tex at 00E3, 00FN and 00PK. The new node is precisely 01TH's implication (3) to (1); it needs locally finite type but neither Noetherianity nor separatedness. Its source and four definition dependencies introduce every object.

Step 1 uses the exact existing statement pqf-lem-locnoeth-lft to deduce local Noetherianity of X, not a definition standing in for that theorem. The covering clause of pqf-def-locnoeth permits choosing a Noetherian affine chart at x; the canonical-local-spectrum node explicitly identifies its stalk with R_p; localization-noetherian explicitly proves the required Noetherianity of B. Properness of p ensures B is nonzero. I did not use the faulty old proof text of pqf-lem-affine-open-locnoeth; that node is unnecessary here.

Step 2 uses the actual bijection of morphisms from Spec of a local ring to identify the composite and its stalk map, and the explicit image/chart description in spec-local-ring-canonical-morphism. Primes-of-localization states the bijection and contraction inverse that show only m_A maps to s. The rest is elementary: a prime containing a maximal ideal equals it; the preimage of the closed base point is V(m_A B); the given generalization lifts, so the open W avoiding that fibre is nonempty. I checked that c need not be an open immersion, and used only its continuity and its stated chart description.

Step 3 applies lem-per-punctured-local-closed-points with B nonzero, local and Noetherian, all verified in step 1. Its statement supplies closedness in the entire punctured spectrum, not merely closedness in W. This distinction is essential: it makes the primes containing q exactly q and m_B. Since W avoids the closed fibre, q is strictly below m_B and its image u lies in V away from s. No algebraic quasi-section lemma duplicating the item is introduced.

Step 4 cites the full existence and reducedness assertion of reduced-induced-closed-subscheme. The supplied affine description also explains the intended closed structure, although its stalk computation is not needed in our route. I verified nonemptiness and irreducibility directly from the closure of u and used precisely the reduced-plus-irreducible equivalence in def-pc-integral-scheme. The closed-immersion definition supplies its homeomorphism onto T. Thus the generic point of the actual constructed Z maps to u, not an arbitrary different point.

Step 5 uses primes-of-localization's inclusion-preserving bijection, not an unproved statement about points of a local spectrum. On the affine chart, q corresponds to r and the generalizations of a in the closure correspond exactly to r subset l subset p. By the two-prime conclusion, there are only a and z. The scheme-fibre definition explicitly identifies fibre topology with the subspace topology, and the closed-immersion definition supplies the embedding, so fibre-closedness descends from the hypothesis on x. A specialization in the fibre is a specialization in Z. The only competing generalization z maps away from s. These are elementary inclusion and subspace arguments, written out, not an invocation of an uncited quasi-finiteness theorem.

Step 6 uses the exact finite-type-on-every-affine-pair clause of lft-affine-pairs and the affine quotient description in the closed-immersion definition. Images of a finite list of algebra generators generate a quotient, so these charts prove local finite type of f composed with i without an uncited composition theorem. The new node then applies with precisely the two fibre properties established in step 5, giving quasi-finiteness at a. The reduced closure already gives every other clause, including closure equality and specialization.
