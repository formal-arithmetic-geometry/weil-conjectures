In the supported Yoneda extension with that section sent to zero, the frame maps to the coefficient `f`; the Kummer Yoneda product therefore gives `partial kappa(f)` for the ideal-line pseudodivisor class, and its negative for the Gysin class. This explains the precise sign asserted in the new child. It is a comparison lemma with an explicit convention, not an appeal to the abbreviated proof of XVII 2.4.2.1(a).

This yields the unique continuous linear map on the completed ideal. Coboundaries correspond to the maps `a -> a m`.
