## Not duplicates

For an ordinary equivalence of categories the corresponding object simplicial sets need not be bijective, so its degree-zero column need not be a weak equivalence. The new lemma supplies exactly the additional criterion required in Proposition 3.2.

lem-coef-segal-dk-diagonal-equivalence vs lem-coef-algebroid-ff-simplicial-realization: the existing lemma concerns a simplicial diagram of enriched algebroids whose structure functors are fully faithful; it does not concern a Dwyer--Kan equivalence between two Segal simplicial spaces or diagonals of bisimplicial sets.
