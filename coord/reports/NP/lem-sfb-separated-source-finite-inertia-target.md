## Source error

In stacks-morphisms.tex, Tag 050M (lemma-compose-after-separated), proof of (3), the sentence after the displayed A -> B -> T diagram says that A -> T is proper because gf is "quasi-separated". The standing hypothesis of (3) is separated, which means its diagonal is proper. Quasi-separatedness alone gives only quasi-compact/quasi-separated diagonals: for example B G_m over a field is quasi-separated but its diagonal has fibre G_m and is not proper. I use the separated hypothesis already present in the source statement and in the item. This is a proof typo, not a change to either statement.
