Hypothesis and clause correspondence:

- A w-contractible affine scheme T is `X : Scheme.{t}` with `[IsAffine X]` and

  scheme, not a chosen presentation or a stronger condition on its points. Its site
  is exactly `Scheme.ProEt.topology X` and its terminal object is `ProEt.mk (𝟙 X)`.
- The second alternative is `T : Profinite.{t}` with `[ExtremallyDisconnected T]`.
  Profinite already means compact Hausdorff and totally disconnected. Its site is
  `(coherentTopology Profinite).over T`, evaluated at `Over.mk (𝟙 T)`. The coherent
  topology is generated by finite effective-epimorphic families; Profinite's
  `effectiveEpiFamily_tfae` identifies these with finite jointly surjective families.
  `GrothendieckTopology.over` is the slice topology, so this is the stated site of
  profinite sets over T.

  it does not assume both kinds of geometry on the same T.
- The arbitrary coefficient sheaf is `R : Sheaf J CommRingCat`. There is no condensed
  origin, constant-ring, discreteness, field, flatness, or nonzero-ring assumption.
