Restrictions to smaller affine opens give equality on base overlaps. This explains the gluing without an unjustified density or uniqueness assertion on a possibly nonreduced base.
9. The cofactor-naturality statement is about a derived isomorphism of two-term projective complexes and explicitly does not require the conormal differential to be injective. This is crucial at nodes. Its formula gives the Jacobian with the last differential row. The signed volume convention is the one specified by the Noetherian fundamental-class and adjunction comparison statements, and agrees in every presentation through the intrinsic determinant line.

The partial-local-flatness and openness-of-flatness nodes allow the Noetherian charts to be shrunk with all prefix quotients flat. The flat-quotient tensor-exactness theorem, specialized to ordinary modules on the one-object site, then preserves the multiplication injections under every coefficient change. The explicit extra equation gt-1 converts a principal localization into a polynomial presentation and its injectivity is proved coefficient by coefficient in the step. The nodal-Fitting theorem identifies the entire smooth locus under base change, so the proof does not merely cover the inverse image of an arbitrarily chosen smaller smooth open.
12. The base-change/family-isomorphism compatibility is checked using the actual cotangent, determinant and dualizing comparison laws and the finite Jacobian determinant formula. Cofactor naturality ensures independence of changed presentations. This supplies the composition clauses and avoids the unsupported phrase 'the same calculation transports'.

Closed-point finiteness makes all branch residue fields Omega; degree additivity is cited separately.
