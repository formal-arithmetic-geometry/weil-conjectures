### Statement dispute and source error

`STATEMENT-DISPUTED`: the original final assertion that `cor-sf-ambient-adjoint-discharge` supplies an unconditional all-object ambient right adjoint is false. The archived unit explicitly says its ambient pro-etale clause (b) is conditional and false in general, and the cited discharge node only supplies the bounded-below etale-origin class adjunction. The archived `sec-six-functors/sk-rem-sf-fullcd-scope/_section.md`, “The counterexample and the repair boundary”, gives the explicit example: an elliptic curve $C/\overline{\mathbf F}_p$, a prime $r\ne p$, and $A=\mathbf F_r$. A quotient of the geometric generic Galois group onto $\mathbf Z_r$ provides an abstract subgroup $H\cong\bigoplus_{\mathbf N}\mathbf Z$; the exact right adjoint $V_H$ of generic evaluation gives $\mathcal F=V_H(A)$ with $H^q(C_{\mathrm{proet}},\mathcal F)\cong H^q(H,A)\ne0$ for every $q\geq1$. The retract $\mathbf Z^q\subset H$ already gives nonvanishing in degree $q$. Thus no uniform all-sheaf FullCD bound exists. The same source gives the failure of coproduct preservation of $R\Gamma$ using $\mathcal F[q]$, and hence failure of an ambient right adjoint. The corrected item retains the conditional formal reduction and asserts no all-object adjunction on this example.
