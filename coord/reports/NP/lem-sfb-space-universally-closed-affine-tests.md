In particular P_i need not be a scheme. The isomorphism of the two fibre-product descriptions gives a homeomorphism by continuity of its two inverse maps, which is the same cited continuity clause.

This is the principal correction to leaving the source's closedness-descent inference implicit.

Applying that definition to the atlas gives one direction of equality of topologies; the continuity clause gives the other. No unstated descent theorem for closed maps is hidden in this step.
