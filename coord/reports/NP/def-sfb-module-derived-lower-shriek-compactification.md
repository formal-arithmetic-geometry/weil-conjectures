## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the final sentence, “It is not the total right derived functor of g_* composed with i_!”, would be false if interpreted as asserting non-isomorphism for every compactification. Take X = Y = Spec(F_2), Lambda the constant condensed ring F_2, and c the identity compactification, with i and g both identities. Extension by zero and pushforward are then identities. Both the displayed composite and the total right derived functor of the underived composite are the identity on the same unbounded derived category. More generally, i an isomorphism already prevents a universal non-isomorphism assertion.

The source's intended distinction is a construction convention, also stated in the upstream docstring: first descend the exact i_! levelwise, then apply Rg_*. I rewrote the final sentence to say that the definition uses this composite and does not apply the right-derived-functor construction to the underived g_* composed with i_!. I assert no comparison theorem or universal inequality. This changes no functor, hypothesis, or defining formula and preserves the prescribed route. The unit's stronger explanatory assertion about failure of acyclicity is not used or reproduced as a theorem here.
