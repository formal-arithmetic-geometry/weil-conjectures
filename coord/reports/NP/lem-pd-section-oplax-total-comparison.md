## Review by NP

I checked the delivery-errors B–E conditions against the written nodes and sources. Both new statements introduce four categories, functors, the natural isomorphism, all monoidal maps, the signed total and every conjugating map. The natural isomorphism a is deliberately arbitrary: the new nodes assert path expansions, not equality with the independently specified section pullback tensorator. The parent supplies that equality by the ordinary normalization theorem. This keeps the tasks strictly smaller than the item and avoids moving the item into a renamed lemma. They contain no proof steps or proof_dependencies; the proof roadmap belongs here. Strong-monoidal and total-constraint notions reference their existing nodes. No hollow object is introduced and no Lean file is written or compiled.

Parent Step 1 uses the first new statement for the expansion and small coproduct cocones; lem-pe-internal-section-tensor-comparison says E is exact, strong monoidal, preserves the stated constraints and identifies direct-sum totals; def-pd-proetale-section-module-functors fixes the actual oplax adjunction mates and the summand-defined total; lem-weil-pbc-left-adjoint-preserves-colimits says precisely that the image cocone is a colimit for a small indexing category. Here I_n is countable in the module universe. lem-pd-section-ordinary-oplax-normalization explicitly gives the forward pure-tensor formula, and lem-pur-cochain-pullback-tensor-coherence gives exactly the same formula for its cochain map on arbitrary unbounded inputs. Coproduct uniqueness therefore proves equality of the maps, not merely existence of an isomorphism. No result about finite coproducts alone is substituted.

Parent Step 2 uses the second new statement for the actual reverse summand map and inverse equations, ordinary normalization for the displayed st formula, and the cochain statement for the independent inverse with that formula. Each cited statement explicitly gives the assertion used. Inverting and conjugating a fixed isomorphism introduces no choice of inverse.

Parent Step 3 uses the second new statement's degree-zero unit and full path restrictions, ordinary normalization's scalar multiplication and ordinary constraints, the section tensor comparison's explicit compatibility with associators and unitors, and the cochain statement's compatibility with triple reassociation and both ring action unitors. Thus both paths are checked against cited statements rather than against their proofs. The internal/section categories are closed module categories, so tensoring preserves the coproducts needed for nested triple injections; the universe remains that of sections, independent of the site-morphism universe. The generic single-functor totalization theorem is existing infrastructure; the new task concerns a natural square of two composite functors.

Parent Step 4 cites only the definition of the homotopy/derived localization, whose statement gives precisely these localization functors. Preserving equalities and inverse equations is the elementary functor calculation stated in the step. This step localizes b on total complexes and does not conflate this operation with derived tensor of arbitrary representatives. All proof citations are backed by verbatim excerpts in citations.json. The non-elementary parent steps are each shorter than 2000 characters; no hidden long argument remains there.

I tested both new statements on identity functors/a, zero objects and zero ring coefficients, complexes concentrated in one degree (including negative/odd degrees), the unit complex, and complexes nonzero in all integer degrees. The double-sum index stays countable; the triple index is a countable nested sum and no infinite sum of elements is used. The sign is (-1)^p in both models, additivity preserves it, and no reassociation permutes homogeneous factors, so no extra Koszul sign appears. With arbitrary nonmonoidal a the comparison need not equal F_s's chosen tensorator; the new statements do not claim that. Parent ordinary normalization supplies exactly the missing premise. Left adjoints rather than exactness supply coproduct preservation; no flatness or boundedness is added. The two-sided inverse and zero-summand assertions include the zero ring and empty geometric cases. No SGA source is used. The item statement was retained verbatim, so no statement dispute is opened.
