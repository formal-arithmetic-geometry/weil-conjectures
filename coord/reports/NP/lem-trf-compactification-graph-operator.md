## Argument extracted into the new node

`lem-trf-witnessed-open-pullback` isolates passage of the specified sheaf comparisons to the derived categories, with explicit witness maps. With finite constant coefficients, pro-etale module inverse image is exact by its operations construction, and site-comparison pullback is exact by `lem-sf-comparison-pullback-exact`. Etale inverse image is exact, etale open extension is exact by `lem-weil-pbc-extension-by-zero-exact`, and pro-etale open extension is exact by `lem-cbc-proetale-extension-zero`. Apply the two given sheaf natural isomorphisms termwise to a complex. Naturality makes their components commute with differentials; their inverse components do so as well. Exactness makes every functor invert quasi-isomorphisms, and preserves a lower cohomological bound. The natural-transformation part of the localization universal property gives the specified natural derived isomorphisms, independent of representatives. Applied to `(L, alpha)`, kappa followed by `Lu^* alpha` has source `nu_U^*(u_et^{-1} L)` and target `Lu^*K`. Applying delta for each open immersion and then open extension of alpha or alpha_u yields the other two displayed isomorphisms. Thus each pair has exactly the type required by the witness definition. This argument supplies the derived comparisons and their normalization, not just existence of unnamed etale models. It is extracted at the human's explicit request from the former long witness/domain verification; only the final applications/formulas are left in the parent.
