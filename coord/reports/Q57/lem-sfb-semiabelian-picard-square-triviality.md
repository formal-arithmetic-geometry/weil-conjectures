## What was wrong or missing

The item had a statement but no proof. No statement dispute or correction is required. Its ground-field conclusion is stronger than geometric triviality and must not be obtained merely by declaring that trivial line bundles descend. In particular, over an imperfect field, the tensor square of an algebraic closure can have nilpotents; normalized units on two axes need not be trivial over a nonreduced base. The proof deliberately obtains its trivialization over a finite Galois splitting field using the abelian quotient. All uses of the unit-factorization input then have reduced field base. Compatibility, not just existence of a geometric trivialization, is established before applying descent.
