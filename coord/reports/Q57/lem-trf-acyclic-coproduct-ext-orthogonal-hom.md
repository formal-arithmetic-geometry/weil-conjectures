### lem-trf-coproduct-ext-resolution-cohomology

Old step 1:

Work in $\mathcal A=\operatorname{Mod}(R)$. Since the site is small in the module universe, [lem-weil-pbc-ring-sheaves-grothendieck] makes $\mathcal A$ Grothendieck abelian. By [lem-sp-grothendieck-enough-injectives] it has enough injectives, and clause (1) of [lem-weil-pbc-left-exact-injective-resolution-degree-zero] gives an exact augmented injective resolution $0\to F\xrightarrow{\iota}J^0\to J^1\to\cdots$, extended by zero for negative degrees. Fix this resolution for every Ext calculation. This is legitimate: by [lem-sp-derived-well-defined], applied to the left exact additive functor $\operatorname{Hom}_R(E_i,-):\mathcal A\to\operatorname{Ab}$ (and to $\operatorname{Hom}_R(M,-)$ for the coproducts $M$ of step 2), the groups $H^p\operatorname{Hom}_R(E_i,J^\bullet)$ do not depend on the chosen injective resolution of $F$, so the hypothesis $\operatorname{Ext}^p_R(E_i,F)=0$ for $p>0$ holds for this $J^\bullet$.

Supporting statements for the future split: lem-weil-pbc-ring-sheaves-grothendieck, lem-sp-grothendieck-enough-injectives, lem-weil-pbc-left-exact-injective-resolution-degree-zero, lem-sp-derived-well-defined.

Old step 2:

If $M\simeq\bigoplus_{a\in A}E_{i(a)}$, restriction to summands gives a degreewise isomorphism of complexes $\operatorname{Hom}_R(M,J^\bullet)\simeq\prod_{a\in A}\operatorname{Hom}_R(E_{i(a)},J^\bullet)$: postcomposition by $d_J$ commutes with restriction. For complexes $L_a$, apply the exact-product statement [lem-pc-abelian-groups-exact-products] to $0\to Z^q(L_a)\to L_a^q\to B^{q+1}(L_a)\to0$ and $0\to B^q(L_a)\to Z^q(L_a)\to H^q(L_a)\to0$. Kernels are computed componentwise, so these two exact product sequences identify $H^q(\prod_aL_a)$ with $\prod_aH^q(L_a)$. Thus $\operatorname{Ext}_R^q(M,F)=0$ for $q>0$. The argument includes an empty coproduct and arbitrary repetitions.

Supporting statements for the future split: lem-pc-abelian-groups-exact-products.

Old step 4:

The $p$-th column is $\operatorname{Hom}_R(K^{b-p},J^\bullet)$. By step 2 and the coproduct hypothesis its positive cohomology vanishes. In degree zero, the exact augmentation identifies $F$ with $\ker(d_J^0)$, so the kernel universal property identifies $\ker(\operatorname{Hom}_R(K^{b-p},d_J^0))$ with $\operatorname{Hom}_R(K^{b-p},F)$; there are no negative-degree boundaries. These identifications commute with precomposition by $d_K$. Therefore the degree-zero column cohomology, with its horizontal differential, is the complex $C^p=\operatorname{Hom}_R(K^{b-p},F)$, $d_C(h)=h\circ d_K^{b-p-1}$ for $p\ge0$.

Supporting statements for the future split: def-sp-injective-resolutions.


### lem-trf-acyclic-injective-hom-bicomplex

Old step 3:

Choose an integer $b$ such that $K^r=0$ for $r>b$. Define the first-quadrant double complex $D^{p,q}=\operatorname{Hom}_R(K^{b-p},J^q)$ for $p,q\ge0$, with zero terms elsewhere, horizontal differential $d_I(h)=h\circ d_K^{b-p-1}$ and vertical differential $d_{II}(h)=d_J^q\circ h$. Both differentials square to zero and they commute, since postcomposition and precomposition commute. The boundary at $p=0$ is compatible with extension by zero because $K^{b+1}=0$. This is the commuting convention of [def-sp-double-complex-total], whose total differential is $d_I+(-1)^pd_{II}$. Each total diagonal is finite: $0\le p,q\le p+q$.

Supporting statements for the future split: def-sp-double-complex-total.

Old step 5:

Every row $D^{\bullet,q}$ is exact, directly from acyclicity and injectivity [def-pc-acyclic-complex-modules, def-sp-injective-resolutions]. Indeed, write $r=b-p$. A horizontal cocycle $h:K^r\to J^q$ kills $\operatorname{im}d_K^{r-1}=\ker d_K^r$, so it factors through $K^r/\ker d_K^r=\operatorname{im}d_K^r$. If $p>0$, extend this factored map across the monomorphism $\operatorname{im}d_K^r\hookrightarrow K^{r+1}$ using injectivity of $J^q$; the extension $g$ satisfies $h=g\circ d_K^r$, so $h$ is a horizontal boundary. If $p=0$, then $r=b$ and $d_K^b=0$, hence the factorization already gives $h=0$. This proves exactness also at the left endpoint, without any assertion about sectionwise exactness of $K$.

Supporting statements for the future split: def-pc-acyclic-complex-modules, def-sp-injective-resolutions.

Old step 6:

Apply the second spectral sequence of [thm-sp-double-complex-spectral-sequences] to $D$. Its first page is ${}''E_1^{q,p}=H_I^p(D^{\bullet,q})=0$ by step 5. All later pages and all stable terms are consequently zero. For each $n\ge0$, the cited theorem gives a finite filtration $H^n(\operatorname{Tot}D)=F^0\supseteq\cdots\supseteq F^{n+1}=0$ whose successive quotients are these stable terms. Descending induction, from $F^{n+1}=0$ and $F^i/F^{i+1}=0$, gives $F^i=0$. Hence $H^n(\operatorname{Tot}D)=0$ in every degree (negative total degrees are already zero).

Supporting statements for the future split: thm-sp-double-complex-spectral-sequences.
