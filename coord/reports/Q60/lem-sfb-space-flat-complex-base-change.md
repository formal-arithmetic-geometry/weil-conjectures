## Arguments retained for new-node workers

The stalk-calculus node reduces on pointed etale charts to localizations of tensor products of ordinary local rings (0FLS); compatible strict henselizations give flat extension to the geometric local rings using 08HV and the 0DKG proof. In those charts choose K-flat representatives and use the transitive derived tensor identity (the source cites more-algebra lemma-triple-tensor-product). For the single-complex theorem use 0DKG to reduce scheme charts to the scheme single-complex theorem.
