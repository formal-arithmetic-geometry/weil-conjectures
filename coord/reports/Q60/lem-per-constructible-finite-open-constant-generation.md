For the new geometric node I read Stacks more-morphisms.tex, 05K0, lemma-quasi-finite-separated-pass-through-finite, including its factorization conclusion; also etale.tex 025O (etale preserves reducedness) and schemes.tex 01J3 (reduced closed subschemes). The intended proof, kept here rather than in the unsplit node, is: factor T -> U -> C by Zariski main as T open in a finite C-scheme, and replace that scheme by the reduced closure of T. This retains T because T is reduced and its finite map over U is proper: the open immersion into the finite compactification restricted to U is proper by the graph criterion, hence closed, so its closure over U is exactly T. The closure is Noetherian with finitely many irreducible components. The generic fibre is a disjoint union of spectra of finite separable fields. Consequently all dominating components are disjoint over the generic point; delete the closed images of their pairwise intersections, and delete images of any components not meeting the generic fibre. Choose a nonempty affine open W inside the surviving part of U. Each reduced component Y_a is integral and finite over C, its inverse image of W is nonempty open integral, and these inverse images are disjoint and give all of T_W. This is genuinely a geometric component/compactification lemma, strictly smaller than the sheaf-generation theorem. It does not restate any clause of that theorem.
