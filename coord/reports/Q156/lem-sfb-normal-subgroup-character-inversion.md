The literal tensor-power containment in the general-case paragraph of Milne Lemma 5.16 is not valid for arbitrary lines. For example in characteristic 2 let G = G_a act on k e1 + k e2 by g(t)e1=e1 and g(t)e2=e2+t e1; take the trivial normal subgroup and L=k e2. The first Frobenius image is smooth. Over A=k[t]/(t^2), its Frobenius kernel acts on e2 tensor e2 with additional nonzero term t(e1 tensor e2 + e2 tensor e1). Thus L tensor L is not contained in the invariants of that kernel. This concerns that proof assertion, not the conclusion of Lemma 5.16. The split makes the intended Frobenius reduction through V^(q), the base change of V, and the restricted base-changed representation on the smooth image I. The formula b tensor c maps to c b^q proves that the pulled-back character is chi^q. No tensor-power kernel-invariance claim is used. Because this repairs the source proof, proof_origin.by is agent and describes the change.
