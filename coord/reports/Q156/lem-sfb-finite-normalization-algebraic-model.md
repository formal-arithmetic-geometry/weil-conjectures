## Supporting arguments for the statement-only nodes

These arguments are recorded here for the later workers; the new nodes themselves have no proof or proof dependencies.

For the transition-system lemma, the set of finite intermediate fields is directed: the compositum of two finite subextensions is finite, and any finite list of elements of the algebraic extension lies in one such field. The maps L tensor F -> L tensor P are injective since they are scalar extensions of injective vector-space maps. If an element of the smaller algebra becomes nilpotent, injectivity implies it was already nilpotent. This proves injectivity after reduction. Every element of E_P has a representative using finitely many coefficients in P, hence lies in E_F for some F. If it is integral over B, its monic equation is already zero at that stage, by injectivity E_F -> E_P. Thus C_P is the union of the C_F, and R_P is the union of the R_F by the same argument. Tensoring B with the union gives the T colimit: tensor expressions and their balancing relations each use finitely many coefficients.

For finite F, Krull-Akizuki (algebra.tex, Tag 00PG, lemma-krull-akizuki) makes R_F Noetherian; it is an integrally closed one-dimensional domain, hence Dedekind. R_P is a torsion-free integral R_F-algebra inside the field P. More-algebra.tex Tag 0AUW (lemma-dedekind-torsion-free-flat (1)) makes it flat; integral injective lying-over makes its spectrum surjective, and algebra.tex Tag 00HQ (lemma-ff-rings) makes it faithfully flat. This proof does not require R_P Noetherian. Every x in F can be multiplied by a sufficiently large power of the A-uniformizer a to be integral over A: clear the finitely many coefficients in a monic equation over K, choosing a power large enough for every coefficient of the rescaled polynomial. Hence R_F[1/a]=F. Since a maps to a unit times a positive power of the B-uniformizer b, localizing T_F at b gives L tensor_K F, whose reduction quotient is E_F. This gives the claimed surjectivity. Elements of C_F have monic B-equations and so remain integral over T_F.

For the image-model lemma, choose finite S-module generators of D and use them as algebra generators, giving S[X_1,...,X_n] -> D. Its kernel is finitely generated because S is Noetherian (algebra.tex Tag 00FN, lemma-Noetherian-permanence). Lift these generators of D to some D_i, lift the coefficients of finitely many polynomial relations to S_i, and enlarge i until every relation vanishes in D_i. This is the finite-data argument of Tag 05N9 (lemma-colimit-category-fp-algebras); the general finite-data node and the general fp-algebra descent node were also checked for duplicates. Let P_i be the resulting presented S_i-algebra, and H its image in D_i. Then P_i tensor S is D by construction; the composite P_i tensor S -> H tensor S -> D is that isomorphism. The first arrow is surjective, and an isomorphic composite forces both arrows to be isomorphisms (its kernel is zero, then apply surjectivity). Finally H is generated by finitely many elements integral over S_i, so the bounded monomials in them span it, exactly as in Tag 00GM (lemma-characterize-integral). Thus H is module-finite. This argument tolerates noninjective transitions and zero rings. It constructs a subalgebra of a stage, not just an abstract presentation.
