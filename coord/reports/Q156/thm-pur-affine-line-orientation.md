### Returned step 1 → lem-pur-affine-line-boundary-realization

Use the affine-line datum [def-pur-affine-line-datum]: write $X=\mathbf A^1_Y$, $Z=s(Y)$, $U=\mathbf G_{m,Y}$ and $F=A_X(1)$. Retain the datum's hypothesis $e(E/\mathbf Q_\ell)\mid n$ and write $N=n/e\geq1$, $m=\ell^N$; the coefficient map is $\varphi(u)=\theta^{-1}(u\otimes1_A)$, and $\kappa(t)$ is the pro-etale comparison image of $\kappa_{\mathrm{et}}(t)=H^1(\varphi_U)(\delta_U(t))$. The zero section is a closed immersion, and its complement is quasi-compact (locally it is the principal open $D(t)$). The base is the algebraic closure of a finite field, $X$ is finite type, hence has a finite dimension bound $d\geq0$ by [lem-kun-dimension-finite-type] (choose $d=0$ if $X$ is empty), and the finite commutative ring $A=\mathcal O_E/\varpi_E^n$ is finite and killed by a power of $\ell\ne p$. Thus [lem-exc-open-complement-known] supplies the open-complement condition of [def-exc-open-complement-bound], with $c=2d$. For the coefficient input to these bounds, [lem-pur-twist-sheaf-locally-free](1),(3) and the datum's etale/pro-etale twist identification give an etale cover on which $F$ is the constant free rank-one $A$-module in degree zero. Thus $F$ is constructible, and for each $A$-module sheaf $M$, $F\otimes_A^L M$ is locally $M$ in degree zero; its Tor amplitude is $[0,0]$. The ambient etale right adjoint exists by [lem-exc-closed-ambient]; [lem-exc-closed-amplitude] and [prop-exc-closed-tor] then give respectively the bounded constructible and finite-Tor assertions in the statement. On the stated pro-etale coefficient category, $Rs^!$ is the transported etale adjoint, exactly as defined in [def-weil-exceptional-inverse-image]. In particular the ambient etale adjoint is not being asserted to be an adjoint on all pro-etale complexes.

### Returned step 6 → lem-pur-closed-support-exceptional-mate
