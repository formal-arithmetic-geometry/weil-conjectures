## Returned proof retained verbatim

The six original steps are retained here for comparison and to make the work transferred to the two new nodes explicit. The first step's base-localization issue is corrected above.

1. The assertion is local on X, Y, and S. Choose affine neighbourhoods S=Spec(R), X=Spec(A), and Y=Spec(B) containing s, x, and y, and write x and y as primes p of A and q of B. Put r=p intersected with R and replace R,A,B by their localizations at R\r; then R=O_{S,s} is a G-ring, A and B are finite type R-algebras, and A_p and B_q are the local rings at x and y. The finite-type G-ring transfer of Stacks Tag 07PV, recorded in [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-g-ring], makes A a G-ring, and its localization clause makes A_p a G-ring. The given map is a local map B_q to the p-adic completion of A_p.

2. Choose a presentation B=R[x_1,...,x_n]/(f_1,...,f_m). Let a_i in the completion of A_p be the images of x_i under B to B_q to the completion of A_p. The images in A[x_1,...,x_n] of the f_j vanish at the a_i. Apply [lem-trf-artin-approximation-etale-local-map] with base ring A, prime p, these A-coefficient polynomials, the completed solution (a_i), the G-ring A_p, and N. It gives an etale map A to A', a prime p' over p with kappa(p) to kappa(p') an isomorphism, and b_i in A' solving the same equations with a_i-b_i in (p')^N times the completion of A'_{p'}.

3. The b_i define an R-algebra map phi:B to A'. For every polynomial h in R[x_1,...,x_n], the difference h(b_1,...,b_n)-h(a_1,...,a_n), after mapping the second term to the completion of A'_{p'}, lies in (p')^N times that completion: expand h into monomials and use that each b_i-a_i lies in this ideal. This gives the congruence for the image under phi of every element represented by a polynomial.

4. The induced map on residue fields from the local map B_q to the completion of A_p is injective from kappa(q) into kappa(p). If c is in q, its image under the given map has zero residue; the congruence of Step 3 therefore makes phi(c) have zero residue at p', so phi(c) is in p'. Conversely, if phi(c) is in p', the same congruence makes the image of c under the given local map have zero residue in kappa(p); injectivity on residue fields gives c in q. Hence phi^{-1}(p')=q. This is the required inverse-image equality, not merely the inclusion q subseteq phi^{-1}(p').

5. Because phi^{-1}(p')=q, phi localizes to a local map B_q to A'_{p'}. Its contravariant map is an S-morphism f:Spec(A') to Spec(B), and f(p')=q. For a fraction h/s in B_q with s outside q, both evaluations of s are units in the two local rings. The identity (h(b)/s(b))-(h(a)/s(a)) = (h(b)s(a)-h(a)s(b))/(s(b)s(a)), together with the polynomial congruences and the analogous congruence for s, shows that the difference belongs to (p')^N in the completed local ring. Thus the congruence holds for every element of B_q.

6. The etale ring map A to A' induces an etale morphism p:U=Spec(A') to X=Spec(A), with u=p' mapping to x=p; the residue-field isomorphism is the one supplied in Step 2. The localized map B_q to A'_{p'} gives an S-morphism f:U to Y with f(u)=y. Under the affine identifications, the congruence of Step 5 is exactly that the two completion maps differ by an element of m_u^N times the completed local ring, where m_u=p'A'_{p'}. Shrinking back through the chosen affine opens transports these data to the original schemes and proves the stated conclusion for the arbitrary N.
