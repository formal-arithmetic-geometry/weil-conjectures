### Returned Step 1

Fix $s$, write $K=\kappa(s)$, and let $\overline K$ be the algebraic closure of $K$ used for the geometric fibre $Z_{\bar s}=Z_s\times_{\operatorname{Spec}K}\operatorname{Spec}\overline K$ [def-weil-geometric-fibre]. Charts: for affine opens $\operatorname{Spec}A\subset Z$ and $\operatorname{Spec}R\subset S$ with $p(\operatorname{Spec}A)\subset\operatorname{Spec}R$ and $A\cong R[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/(f_1,\ldots,f_m)$, the fibre $Z_s$ is covered by the affine opens $\operatorname{Spec}C$ with $C=A\otimes_RK\cong K[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/(\bar f_1,\ldots,\bar f_m)$, the $\bar f_j$ being the images of the $f_j$; so $Z_s\to\operatorname{Spec}K$ is locally of finite presentation [def-sbc-locally-finite-presentation]. The corresponding affine opens of $Z_{\bar s}$ are $\operatorname{Spec}(C\otimes_K\overline K)$, and $C\otimes_K\overline K$ is reduced, since a nilpotent element has nilpotent, hence zero, germs at every point. Let $E/K$ be a finite purely inseparable extension [def-weil-pbc-finite-purely-inseparable-extension]. It is generated by finitely many algebraic elements, and sending them one at a time to roots in $\overline K$ of their minimal polynomials gives a $K$-embedding $E\hookrightarrow\overline K$ [def-weil-algebraic-closure]. Extending $1$ to an $E$-basis of $\overline K$ shows that $C\otimes_KE\to(C\otimes_KE)\otimes_E\overline K=C\otimes_K\overline K$ is injective, so $C\otimes_KE$ is reduced. By [lem-trf-geometric-reducedness-finite-inseparable-test], (1)$\Rightarrow$(2), $C\otimes_KL$ is reduced for every field extension $L/K$. The affine opens $\operatorname{Spec}(C\otimes_KL)$ cover $(Z_s)_L$ and localizations of reduced rings are reduced, so $(Z_s)_L$ is reduced [def-pc-reduced-scheme]: $Z_s$ is geometrically reduced over $K$ [def-pc-geometrically-irreducible]. Therefore [lem-pur-geometrically-reduced-dense-smooth-open], applied to $Z_s\to\operatorname{Spec}K$, says that its smooth locus $D$ is dense in $Z_s$; it is open, being a union of opens.

### Returned Step 2

Let $C$ be the irreducible component of $Z_s$ with generic point $z$, and choose an affine open $U=\operatorname{Spec}B\ni z$ of $Z_s$ as in Step 1, with $B$ a finitely presented $K$-algebra. Then $U\to\operatorname{Spec}K$ is of finite type over the Noetherian scheme $\operatorname{Spec}K$, so $U$ is Noetherian [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian] and has finitely many irreducible components $U_1,\ldots,U_n$ [lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-finitely-many-components]. The closure of $\{z\}$ in $U$ is $C\cap U$; it is irreducible, so it lies in some component, say $U_1$. If $C\cap U\subsetneq U_1$, the closure of $U_1$ in $Z_s$ would be irreducible and would contain $C=\overline{C\cap U}$ strictly, contradicting the maximality of $C$; so $U_1=C\cap U$, with generic point $z$. Also $z\notin U_i$ for $i\geq2$, since otherwise $U_1\subseteq U_i$ and the two components would coincide. Thus $V=U\setminus(U_2\cup\cdots\cup U_n)$ is an open neighbourhood of $z$, and $V\subseteq U_1$. The dense open $D$ of Step 1 meets $V$, so $D\cap V$ is a nonempty open subset of the irreducible space $U_1$. It contains $z$, for otherwise $U_1\setminus D$ would be a closed subset of $U_1$ containing $z$, hence all of $U_1=\overline{\{z\}}$. So $z\in D$, i.e. the fibre morphism $Z_s\to\operatorname{Spec}K$ is smooth at $z$. The finiteness of the components near $z$ is essential: in a space with infinitely many components (for example the spectrum of an infinite product of fields) a dense open can miss the generic point of a component.

Allocation: Noetherianity of the affine fibre chart is an output of lem-trf-geometric-fibre-affine-preparation. The rest of the finite-component and closure argument belongs to lem-trf-locnoeth-component-generic-open, whose statement gives the explicit V and the dense-open conclusion. The final interpretation as fibre smoothness remains in parent Step 2. Sources read: topology.tex, Tag 0052 (lemma-Noetherian), (2)--(3) and the full proof removing the other components; properties.tex, Tag 0BA8 (lemma-Noetherian-irreducible-components); varieties.tex, Tag 056V (lemma-geometrically-reduced-dense-smooth-open), its finite-component reduction and proof. The statement uses local Noetherianity only to choose Noetherian affine neighbourhoods; it does not infer that a dense open meets each component densely in an arbitrary space.
