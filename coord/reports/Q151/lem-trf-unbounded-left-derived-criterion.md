### Original step 1

Destination: First new node: isomorphism closure and its generating-class properties. Second new node: transfer of the acyclicity hypothesis to that closure.

Replace $\mathcal P$ by its isomorphism closure $\overline{\mathcal P}$. It contains every zero object, is closed under finite biproducts, and still covers every object by epimorphisms. Cited inputs: def-weil-abstract-derived-category.

### Original step 2

Destination: Second new node: bounded-stage quasi-isomorphism preservation, used for the existing bounded-stage-roof lemma.

If $s:E\to E'$ is a quasi-isomorphism between bounded above $\overline{\mathcal P}$-complexes, its cone is acyclic by [lem-pur-quasi-iso-cone-acyclic]. Its terms are $E'^i\oplus E^{i+1}$, hence belong to $\overline{\mathcal P}$, and it is bounded above. The hypothesis makes $F(C(s))$ acyclic. Additivity identifies its differential matrix with the cone differential of $F(s)$, including the minus sign on the shifted summand. The same cone criterion in $\mathcal B$ makes $F(s)$ a quasi-isomorphism. This supplies precisely the preservation hypothesis of [lem-trf-bounded-stage-roof-comparison].

Cited inputs: lem-pur-quasi-iso-cone-acyclic, def-weil-abstract-derived-category.

### Original step 3

Destination: First new node: compatible truncation resolutions and the augmented colimit quasi-isomorphism.

For any complex $K$, apply [lem-sfb-subcategory-truncation-resolution-system] to $\overline{\mathcal P}$. It gives bounded above $E_n$, strict compatible quasi-isomorphisms $E_n\to T_n=\tau_{\le n}K$, and transition maps. Form the termwise sequential colimit $E=\varinjlim E_n$ and its induced map $a:E\to K$. Exactness of sequential colimits in $\mathcal A$ and [src-weil-homology-preserves-exact-colimits] identify $H^i(E)$ with $\varinjlim H^i(E_n)$. The explicit good truncation has $H^i(T_n)=H^i(K)$ for $i\le n$ and zero for $i>n$ (kernel/image at the cutoff gives the same quotient). The transition maps act as the identity on $H^i(K)$ once $n\ge\max(1,i)$. The colimit of this eventually constant system is $H^i(K)$, with comparison $H^i(a)$: a compatible cocone is uniquely determined on that constant tail. Hence $a$ is a quasi-isomorphism.

Cited inputs: lem-sfb-subcategory-truncation-resolution-system, src-weil-homology-preserves-exact-colimits, def-weil-pbc-category-colimit, def-weil-abstract-derived-category.
