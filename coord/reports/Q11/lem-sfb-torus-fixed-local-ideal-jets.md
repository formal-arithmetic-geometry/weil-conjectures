### Exact affine-coordinate encoding

This is the coordinate presentation of the same objects, without a source correction.
`Coordinates k r = AddMonoidAlgebra k (Fin r → ℤ)` is the Laurent algebra k[Z^r]. Its
Mathlib Hopf structure has Δ(z^a)=z^a⊗z^a, ε(z^a)=1, S(z^a)=z^(-a).
Thus Spec of it is the product of r copies of the recorded Spec(k[t,t⁻¹]), including
the tensor unit when r=0. The affine product/tensor equivalence identifies scheme
actions with the recorded `HopfAction.AlgebraCoaction`. This encodes an algebraic
action, not merely an action of the abstract group T(k).

Every affine closed immersion has the coordinate presentation Spec(B/K) → Spec B.
An input `x : (B ⧸ K) →ₐ[k] k` is precisely a rational point of that closed subscheme;
`quotientPoint K x` is its ambient rational point. `LocalRing` is the actual
`Localization.AtPrime` of its kernel, the canonical model of the stalk of Spec.
The isomorphism in the conclusion is normalized on every b∈B, so it is the map
induced by the closed immersion, rather than a bare abstract isomorphism.

  action is trivial, hfixed forces K=0, and the local difference ideal is zero.
- n=1: order n+1 is 2 and the required reduction is the identity, including when
  the maximal ideal/cotangent space is zero. n=0 is correctly outside the stated
  jet assertion; it is not silently assumed positive by an unbound variable.
- X empty/zero coordinate ring: there is no algebra map B/K→k, so the rational-point
  hypothesis excludes exactly the impossible input, without imposing a nonempty
  scheme hypothesis on the statement as a whole.
- Nonreduced B and nonreduced test algebras are allowed. K is an ideal, not its radical;
  the fixed-functor condition quantifies over all commutative algebras and all maps.
- Arbitrary characteristic is allowed; there is no characteristic-zero hypothesis.
- B=k and a zero maximal ideal give finite jets k and zero ideal representations;
  the inclusions, coactions and surjectivity remain well-typed.
- The universes are explicit (`universe u`, every algebra and field in Type u),
  with test algebras in the same universe as B,k, so the universal Laurent algebra
