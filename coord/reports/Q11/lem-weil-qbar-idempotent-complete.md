## Problems found and corrected

The original proof's `uses` named only two definitions while its text invoked finite-stage Karoubianity and filtered retract descent. Its final inference also omitted the non-elementary triangulated-envelope theorem needed for exactness. The six steps now expose those arguments and cite every non-elementary input.

The proper-base-change finite-stage theorem is written for the category of `def-weil-pbc-rational-category`, whose underlying integral category `def-weil-pbc-bounded-constructible-complete` requires a noetherian finite-dimensional scheme with ell invertible, and compatible etale-origin witnesses. The present item and its rationalized category permit an arbitrary scheme Y and do not impose the same witness datum. Neither global finite-field datum nor ell != p alone makes an arbitrary Y satisfy those conditions: Y = Spec(F_ell) with independently fixed p != ell already fails invertibility, and an infinite disjoint union of Spec(k)'s fails noetherianity. I therefore did not identify these different coefficient models or add a scheme hypothesis to this item.

The existing same-unit `lem-weil-rationalization-idempotent-complete` states exactly the required finite-stage completeness for `def-weil-rationalization-karoubi` and arbitrary Y. It is used instead. It remains an open dependency, with its own eventual proof obligation; this delivery does not certify that dependency as Lean-closed. The scoped `prop-weil-filtered-retract-descent` is not cited as a more general theorem than it states. Its elementary representative/equality/splitting argument is written out directly in steps 2–3 using the common-stage category definition. This preserves the source's finite-stage followed by retract-descent route. The unit record has no proof steps to replace, and no unit or chapter statement changes.

The statement is unchanged, so there is no STATEMENT-DISPUTED correction in this delivery. The issue was an unjustified proof citation, repaired using an existing exact statement.
