## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the nilpotent-kernel version of Stacks Tag 0DZ2 (examples-defos.tex, lemma-lift-equivalence-module, statement and proof around lines 1995–2073) is false even for finite M. Its proof correctly uses a square-zero obstruction class from defos.tex, lemma-canonical-class-algebra, but the first sentence's nilpotent induction does not preserve its hypotheses. An arbitrary intermediate module lift need not admit the next local lifts, or a compatible lift of its scalar extension. The returned step 7 made precisely that unjustified application. The new module node now states only I²=0. Its other hypotheses and its id are retained; it is still a new, unlanded node. Finiteness of M is also essential to the displayed Ext base-change argument, which explicitly invokes it although the source statement omits it.

The maintainer's counterexample, checked against that exact statement, is as follows. Over k=C put R=C[x,y]/(y²−x²(x+1)), with normalization inside C[t] given by x=t²−1, y=t(t²−1). Put

A'=C[u,v,s]/(uv,us,vs,u²−v²,u²−s²,(u,v,s)³), I=(u,u²), A=A'/I=C[v,s]/(v,s)².

Here I²=(u²) is nonzero. Take S'=R[z]/(z²) tensor_C A', K'=(x,y)S', and T' its K'-adic completion; S=S'/IS' and T=T'/IT'. These are Noetherian rings, both horizontal maps are flat, and completion gives the required isomorphism modulo K'. Let M=R tensor_C A, with z acting by bv+es, b=−x/2 and e=(x+2)/(2i). It is finite cyclic over S and flat over A. The relations give b²+e²=−(x+1). A flat S'-module lift is necessarily rank one over R tensor_C A' (lift its cyclic generator and use flatness and nilpotent Nakayama on the kernel). Writing the lifted z-action as au+bv+es plus a multiple of u², its square is (a²+b²+e²)u²; thus liftability is exactly the equation a²=x+1 in R. There is no such a: in C[t] it must be t or −t, neither in R, since elements of R take equal values at t=1 and t=−1. On the complement of V(x,y), x is invertible because y²=x²(x+1); a=y/x is a solution there and hence on every D(g) for g in K'. In the completion, the formal binomial series sqrt(1+x) is a solution. This verifies the local-lift and completed-lift hypotheses but disproves global liftability.
