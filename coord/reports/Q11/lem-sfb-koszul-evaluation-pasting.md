## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported sentence says that the canonical pasted evaluation with coefficient comparison t^Ch_(r,s) is tau_(f,g). With the total-Hom differential and tensor symmetry fixed in the item this is false when rs is odd and 2 is nonzero in the final quotient. I changed that sentence to say it is (-1)^(rs) tau_(f,g), and replaced the incorrect cancellation explanation by the three-sign calculation. Equivalently, the comparison (-1)^(rs) t^Ch_(r,s), which sends the raw ordered shifted tensor generator to the ordered concatenated generator without a sign, gives precisely tau_(f,g). Every existence, augmentation, Tor-independent naturality and generator-change assertion is retained; no hypothesis was added. In the final compatibility clause a base change of a two-stage formula naturally means a change for which the participating quotient resolutions are still resolutions; the proof states this domain explicitly rather than inferring Tor independence for D from that for B alone.

A small counterexample is S = Q[x,y], f=(x), g=(y), B=Q[y], D=Q. Let a:K(x)->S[1] and b:K(y)->S[1] take the negative-degree basis to 1. Use the raw coefficient S[1] tensor S[1] first. The signed block flip sends e_x tensor e_y to -e_y tensor e_x. Insertion of the right coefficient of degree -1 into Hom(K(x),S[1]) gives the sign (-1)^((-1)(-1))=-1. These cancel, so the two evaluations on the raw coefficient tensor send its top functional to +1. The coefficient map t^Ch_(1,1), with formula (-1)^(bp), sends 1 tensor 1 to -1 in S[2]; its inverse has the same sign. Thus the actual pasted evaluation on the functional sending e_x wedge e_y to 1 in S[2] is -1 in Q, whereas the individually specified tau_(x,y) sends it to +1. This is a difference in H^0, so the maps differ in D(Q), not merely at the level of a chosen chain representative.

The independent source of this convention is the existing `lem-pur-total-tensor-signed-coherence` (the exact bp formula), `lem-pod-projective-hom-evaluation-model` (signed projective evaluation), and `lem-trf-coinduction-tensor-comparison-model` (unsigned left-coefficient insertion). Stacks 0BQZ and 0C0F do not state the disputed extra sign and are not being accused of this error: the item explicitly calls its pasting an agent refinement.

The only live citing node found was `lem-sfb-polynomial-koszul-diagonal-class`, including proof steps 2, 5, 6, 7 and 8. ## New nodes

`lem-sfb-perfect-resolution-coinduction-comparison` states the complete derived comparison on an arbitrary double bounded finite projective resolution P->B and B tensor Q->D, with arbitrary unbounded coefficients N. It specifies the quasi-isomorphism square, currying and signed block flip, and identifies their localization with the actual composite coinduction counit, including its derived D-action. This is a strictly smaller, general model-comparison input, without a Koszul complex, top wedge, normalized quotient evaluation, change of generators or Tor-independent base change; the item carries all those arguments itself.

Source read: dualizing.tex 0C0F, lemma-composition-right-adjoints, with the preceding 0A70 right-adjoint construction and 0A71 scalar-restriction statement; more-algebra.tex 0A5Y, lemma-compose, including its three differential terms; and 0917, lemma-hom-K-injective. The square is an agent refinement, not a verbatim assertion of those tags. Proof plan for the new node: Q is K-flat, so P tensor Q -> B tensor Q is a quasi-isomorphism. The coinduced J=Hom_S(B,I) is K-injective over B. The two vertical arrows are therefore quasi-isomorphisms; Hom(Q,a*) is one because I is K-injective and Q is bounded projective. Evaluation-at-one is the ordinary coinduction composition isomorphism and localizes to the unique right-adjoint comparison with pasted counit. Currying followed by the signed flip gives kappa. The square commutes termwise: after precomposition with a, only degree zero of P contributes, where the flip sign is 1, and B-linearity moves a(p) into b. Finally Hom(W,j) is a quasi-isomorphism because W is bounded projective. These facts supply the whole comparison and its naturality without imposing a strict D-action on P or Q. The new node deliberately states an isomorphism in derived form through the specified quasi-isomorphism models, rather than only a per-degree consequence.
