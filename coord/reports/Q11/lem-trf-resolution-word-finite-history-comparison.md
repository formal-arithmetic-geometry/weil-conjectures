## STATEMENT-DISPUTED: domain of ambient pullbacks

Taken literally without the domain already implicit in “such histories”, an arbitrary smooth Q -> P need not have separated finite-type source. For example P=X=Spec(Q), a length-zero history, and Q the infinite disjoint union of Spec(Q) is an etale, hence smooth, source which is not quasi-compact and not of finite type. For separatedness, take X=P=A^1_Q and Q the affine line with doubled origin mapping etale to P; Q is not separated. Thus the asserted geometry of the pulled-back ambient is false for unrestricted smooth sources. I made explicit that Q is separated and finite type over the ground field and Y=X times_P Q. Smoothness of Q follows by composition; reducedness of Y is proved. This is the category of embedded rational pairs already fixed in the item and admissible-history definition. The counterexamples are outside that category, and no constant relative dimension or surjectivity restriction is added. This faithfully realizes ILO VIII 2.3.13's algorithm on reduced varieties over Q and its arbitrary smooth morphisms between objects of that category.
