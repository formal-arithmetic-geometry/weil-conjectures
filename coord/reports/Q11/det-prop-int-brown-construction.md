## Statement and proof errors corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported statement omits the surrounding standing-scheme setting. Its proof explicitly uses “our schemes being Noetherian”, and its preservation inputs concern the standing separated finite-type schemes over a field of positive characteristic different from ell. A literal assertion over all schemes is false.

An explicit counterexample outside that setting is as follows. Take a prime p different from ell, let R be the product of countably many copies of F_p, let m be the maximal ideal associated to a nonprincipal ultrafilter, and put Y = Spec R and X = Spec(R/m). The quotient is F_p, and the closed immersion f:X -> Y is proper and of finite type. Take j = id_X and the proper factor p = f. The rank-one constant integral coefficient sheaf on X is constructible. Its closed direct image is supported exactly on the singleton {m}. Every basic open of Spec R is clopen (R is absolutely flat), so every constructible subset is clopen. The nonprincipal ultrafilter point is not isolated, hence {m} is closed but not clopen and not constructible. Consequently its direct image is not constructible. This disproves the unqualified preservation clause. The corrected statement restores the source-intended standing setting and leaves every conclusion intact. Base change and composition are explicitly typed within that setting, and coefficients remain the condensed ring pulled back from the point, not discrete non-torsion etale coefficients or a completed ambient category. No target is changed.

The imported proof has a second, logical citation error. The current statement of `sk-prop-sf-existence` proves existence of an adjoint for a chosen compactification; it does not assert comparison, composition, or base-change isomorphisms. Nor does `sk-lem-brown-hypotheses` state the preservation hypotheses attributed to it. The missing inference cannot be repaired by citing those statements. The cited umbrella ids `sk-prop-int-compactness`, `sk-cor-int-compactness-ii`, and `sk-thm-sfb-proper-base-change` have no individual DAG files in this import. I replaced these with existing precise component statements. For comparison and composition I expanded the compactification construction the blueprint was referring to, using its own generic comparison/interchange/base-change nodes. This repairs the failed inference in the unit's shorthand “case of sf-existence”; it does not use a different geometric route. The node's split_report records this and declares the generic off-unit tools.

The blueprint's final sentence also jumps from isomorphisms on generators to all objects. That argument only tests an already supplied natural transformation; it does not construct the transformations. The split instead uses existing nodes which explicitly supply normalized transformations on the localizing categories. Thus it does not assume a general principle that a natural transformation on the small subcategory automatically extends.
