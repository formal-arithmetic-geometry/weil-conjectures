The ring is a quotient of a formal power-series ring over a field, not a polynomial smoothness assertion. The ideal is contained in the maximal ideal, and depth is exactly one. The restriction n>=2 just avoids negative minors; any presentation needed here can be padded with variables and their linear equations.
