## Corrections and scope

The item's statement is correct and is unchanged. HAGII states its surrounding discussion for a category of weak equivalences in a model category; its proof of A.0.3 only uses the small category, the simplicial replacement, and arrow-inverting presheaves. The written argument verifies this generality explicitly. There is no STATEMENT-DISPUTED claim.

HAGII abbreviates three arguments: the localized Quillen condition, the homotopy-fiber theorem for the simplicial replacement, and conservativity of the derived right adjoint. They are expanded here. In particular, NC need not be Kan (C=[1] already shows this); therefore a theorem assuming a Kan base cannot simply be cited for the conservativity step. No step assumes all projective presheaves are cofibrant.
