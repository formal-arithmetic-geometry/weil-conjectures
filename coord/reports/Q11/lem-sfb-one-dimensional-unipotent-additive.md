## Review by Q11

I checked the four steps with every cited node's statement open, against delivery-errors B--E. In step 1, `def-weil-algebraic-closure` really gives a root of each nonconstant polynomial, so X^p-b has a root for every b in positive characteristic. `def-sc-scheme-tangent-space` defines vectors as dual-number maps over the base field, so the trivial group has the zero tangent space. Thus both hypotheses needed next, perfectness and nontriviality, are verified rather than assumed.

In step 2, I checked the new node's statement against Milne 14.55 in positive characteristic and the central-series sentence in 14.32 in characteristic zero. Its conclusion actually gives a smooth connected closed subgroup N and a group-scheme isomorphism Ga to N, exactly what the proof takes. All six geometric hypotheses on U and its nontriviality have been supplied. This does not cite a definition of unipotence as a substitute for the existence theorem.

In step 3, `def-sc-gm-ga` gives Spec F[t] and the identity t=0. The dual-number definition and its explicitly stated linear operations in `def-sc-scheme-tangent-space` give t maps to c epsilon and identify the tangent space with F. The same definition states that the tangent map is linear postcomposition. Applying postcomposition to a and its inverse gives inverse tangent maps. Finally `def-sc-group-scheme-subgroup` states injectivity on R-points for EVERY algebra R, including the nonreduced dual numbers; restricting that injection proves tangent injectivity. I did not infer tangent injectivity from injectivity on field points, which would fail for Frobenius. The remaining dimension argument is elementary linear algebra and stays in the proof, rather than becoming another node.

In step 4, I read the full statement of `lem-sfb-smooth-subgroup-tangent-generation`. It applies in arbitrary characteristic to a smooth connected ambient affine finite-type group, with a finite family of smooth connected closed subgroups, and concludes equality for every containing closed subgroup K, with no smoothness assumed on K. Here the singleton family is N and K=N, the factorization is the identity, and the tangent-span hypothesis is precisely step 3. Thus its literal conclusion gives N=U as schemes, not just equality on F-points, and composing with a gives the requested isomorphism. There is no circular citation: the new node has only definition dependencies and the existing generation node does not depend on the item.

Small cases checked: Ga satisfies the item and the new lemma in every characteristic.
