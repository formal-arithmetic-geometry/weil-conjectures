### Retained step 1

Owner: The initial finite-type-over-field application remains assembly step 1; all subscheme Noetherian, topological, quasi-compact-open and qcqs checks belong to lem-sfb-coefficient-heart-locally-closed-extension. Coefficient identifications remain the existing sk-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-x.

The standing finite-type-over-a-field setting is [sk-def-sfb-dc-setting]. Its schemes are Noetherian by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian], applied to the structure morphism to the spectrum of a field (a field is Noetherian since its only ideals are zero and itself). Open subschemes are Noetherian by [lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-open-subscheme-noetherian]. Closed subschemes are locally Noetherian because their affine rings are quotients of Noetherian rings: an ascending chain of quotient ideals lifts to one in the original ring. They are quasi-compact as closed subsets of a quasi-compact space. Factoring a locally closed immersion as closed into an open therefore shows that its source is Noetherian. By [lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-scheme-closed-subsets-minimal], the underlying spaces are topologically Noetherian. Every open subset is quasi-compact: otherwise a cover without a finite subcover gives, by successively adding a member, a strictly increasing chain of opens, contradicting the descending condition on their closed complements. Applying this inside each open subset proves that open immersions are quasi-compact. We use coefficient module sheaves, not sheaves over the discrete constant field: [sk-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-x] identifies $E_X$ with $\cO_{E,X}[1/\ell]$ and $\QbarX$ with $\varinjlim_E E_X$. The scheme is quasi-separated by [lem-weil-pbc-locally-noetherian-quasi-separated], so the qcqs hypothesis for the constructibility and Tor-amplitude nodes is satisfied.

### Retained step 2

The finite constant local models defining constructibility are those in [sk-def-sfb-constructible]. Over the scalar field $\Lambda(*)$, a finitely presented module is a finite-dimensional vector space, hence isomorphic to $\Lambda(*)^r$; extending scalars gives $\Lambda_U^r$. Conversely $\Lambda_U^r$ comes from the finite free scalar module. Therefore constructible means lisse on a finite stratification, as asserted also in [det-lem-coefficient-heart-closure]. The preliminary equality $\Dctf(X,\Lambda)=\Dbc(X,\Lambda)$ is the field case of [det-def-localizing-closure-ctf-equals-bc]: every bounded constructible complex has finite Tor amplitude. Standard truncations have the same cohomology sheaves in a smaller finite range and zero elsewhere, so remain bounded constructible.

### Retained step 3

Owner: lem-sfb-coefficient-heart-locally-closed-extension

Let $a\colon S\hookrightarrow X$ be a locally closed immersion, so $a=w\circ c$ with $c\colon S\to W$ a closed immersion and $w\colon W\hookrightarrow X$ an open immersion. By step 1, $S$ is Noetherian, so $a^{-1}(V)$ is quasi-compact for every affine open $V\subseteq X$; and $a$ is a monomorphism, so its diagonal is an isomorphism. Thus $a$ is quasi-compact and quasi-separated. Let $\overline S=\operatorname{SIm}(a)$ be its scheme-theoretic closure [def-nagata-scheme-theoretic-closure], with closed immersion $i\colon\overline S\hookrightarrow X$ and factor map $u=a^{\mathrm{im}}\colon S\to\overline S$, $a=i\circ u$ [def-nagata-scheme-image-factor]. By [lemma-nagata-scheme-image-ideal-qc] the underlying set of $\overline S$ is the closure of $a(S)$. Since $S\times_XW=S$ and the base change of $a$ to $W$ is $c$, [lemma-nagata-scheme-image-restrict-open] gives $\overline S\times_XW\cong\operatorname{SIm}(c)$ over $W$, and [lemma-nagata-scheme-image-of-closed-immersion] identifies $\operatorname{SIm}(c)$ with $S$ through $c^{\mathrm{im}}$. Hence $u$ is an isomorphism of $S$ onto the open subscheme $\overline S\cap W$ of $\overline S$, i.e. an open immersion. This is the factorization through the closure used in the statement. Use the module extension by zero $u_!$ of [def-sfb-module-open-lower-shriek]; that definition records that $u_!$ is exact. The coefficient identifications $i^{-1}\Lambda_X\cong\Lambda_{\overline S}$, $u^{-1}\Lambda_{\overline S}\cong\Lambda_S$ and exact inverse image are [sk-prop-sfb-cartesian-coefficients]. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-proetale-ringed-open-closed] with ring sheaf $\Lambda_X$ to $i$ and its complementary open: $i_*$ is exact, since that open is quasi-compact by step 1. Consequently $a_!=i_*u_!$ is exact. Applied to a complex $K$, exactness gives $H^n(a_!K)=a_!H^n(K)$, by preserving the kernel and image of each differential. It therefore preserves quasi-isomorphisms and descends to the localization $D(S,\Lambda)\to D(X,\Lambda)$, without taking a resolution.

### Retained step 8

Owner: lem-sfb-coefficient-heart-curve-generic-neighborhood

For the curve assertion, choose an adapted stratification of $F$. There are finitely many irreducible components by [lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-finitely-many-components]. Each component $C$ has a unique generic point $\eta$ by [lem-sc-scheme-sober]. If its adapted stratum has image $S=O\cap D$, with $O$ open and $D$ closed, then $\eta\in D$ implies $C\subseteq D$. Remove the other components from $O$ to get an open neighborhood $W_\eta$ of $\eta$ contained set-theoretically in $S$. This does not assert a scheme factorization through $S$. Instead form $T=W_\eta\times_X S$. Its projection $t:T\to W_\eta$ is an immersion by [lem-weil-pbc-immersion-base-change-lft]. It is surjective by [lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-points]: the residue field of an immersion at a point is that of its image (localization for an open immersion, quotient for a closed immersion), so the residue-field tensor product above each $w\in W_\eta$ is $\kappa(w)$ and has a prime. A surjective immersion is closed, since its factorization as closed into an open has that open equal to the entire target. The projection $T\to S$ is an open immersion, and $F|_T$ is lisse by [det-lem-coefficient-heart-common-stratification](2).

### Retained step 9

By [new-lem-weil-pbc-proetale-surjective-closed-cover-refinement] there is a pro-étale cover $\{B_b\to W_\eta\}$ such that $T_b=B_b\times_{W_\eta}T$ maps over $T$ to a member of $C$. Hence $F|_{T_b}$ is finite free. The map $t_b:T_b\to B_b$ is a surjective closed immersion by [lem-weil-pbc-surjective-closed-immersion-universal-homeomorphism]. ### Retained step 10

Owner: lem-sfb-coefficient-heart-curve-complement
