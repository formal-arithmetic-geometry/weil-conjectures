Use the existing split root-decomposition theorem for the integral evaluation root/coroot pairing, simple-root generation and normalized rank-one brackets. The existing root-base rational/integral-spans theorem makes the simple coroots a basis of h. For i different from j, alpha_j-alpha_i has mixed simple coordinates and is not a root. Reflection in alpha_i maps it to alpha_j+(1-a_ij)alpha_i, also not a root. Moreover s_i(alpha_j)=alpha_j-a_ij alpha_i has j-coordinate 1, so its sign-coherent coordinates imply a_ij<=0. Jacobi shows that a bracket of root-weight vectors has the sum weight: [h,[u,v]]=[[h,u],v]+[u,[h,v]]. Iterating this identity shows (ad e_i)^(1-a_ij)e_j has the forbidden weight alpha_j+(1-a_ij)alpha_i and is zero by the root decomposition. The negative relation has the negative of that forbidden weight. The other relations follow from the normalized triples, the root-space eigenvector equations, abelianness of h and the absence of alpha_i-alpha_j. Thus free evaluation kills every relator. Its kernel is an ideal and therefore contains their generated ideal, so evaluation descends to the displayed quotient.

The descended homomorphism is surjective: its image contains all e_i and f_i, hence the positive and negative nilpotent algebras by simple-root generation, and contains all h_i=[e_i,f_i], which span h.
