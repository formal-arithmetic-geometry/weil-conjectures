## Extracted argument for future workers

Use the projective model on unbounded $\Lambda$-module cochain complexes from [lem-coef-projective-cochain-monoidal-model]. Verify the dg model axiom directly from its cofibration description. For a cofibration $i:A\to B$ and a fibration $p:X\to Y$, the Hom comparison is degreewise surjective: split $i$ degreewise, then lift the remaining graded map from its projective cokernel through the degreewise surjection $p$. Its kernel is $\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(\operatorname{coker}i,\ker p)$. If $p$ is trivial, $\ker p$ is acyclic and dg-projectivity of $\operatorname{coker}i$ makes this kernel acyclic. If $i$ is trivial, its cokernel $Q$ is acyclic and dg-projective; its identity is a boundary in $\underline{\operatorname{Hom}}(Q,Q)$, hence $Q$ is contractible and so is its Hom into any complex. Thus the comparison is a trivial fibration in either case. This also proves the axiom over $\mathbf Z$: the same Hom comparison has the underlying abelian-group complex, with unchanged surjectivity and acyclicity; tensoring and cotensoring by abelian-group complexes have their usual Koszul-sign formulas. To apply [lem-coef-quillen-section-rectification], verify stability with [lem-coef-pointed-model-stability]. For a cofibrant complex $Q$, put $C=\operatorname{Cone}(1_Q)$ with differential $(b,a)\mapsto(db+a,-da)$. It is contractible, and $Q\to C$, $b\mapsto(b,0)$, is a cofibration: it is split in each degree with dg-projective cokernel $Q[1]$. The pushout $C\amalg_Q C$ therefore computes $0\amalg_Q^h0$. In coordinates $(b,a_1,a_2)$ its differential is $(db+a_1+a_2,-da_1,-da_2)$; projection to $a_2$ is a chain map to $Q[1]$ with contractible kernel $C$. A contraction of that kernel is $(b,a_1)\mapsto(0,b)$; the projection is a quasi-isomorphism by lifting cycles and boundaries across this acyclic kernel. Thus suspension is $[1]$ on the homotopy category, hence an equivalence, as required by the stability criterion. Shifts preserve dg-projectivity since shifting the target Hom complex only reindexes its cohomology. Now apply section rectification to the constant system of these model categories indexed by the small category $\Delta$, with all transition adjunctions the identity. It gives the objectwise projective model on $\operatorname{Ch}(\mathcal P)$, where $\mathcal P=\operatorname{Fun}(\Delta,\operatorname{Mod}_\Lambda)$, and identifies its enhanced localization, over evaluation, with $\operatorname{Fun}(N\Delta,\mathbf D(\Lambda))$. Every object of either projective model is fibrant because maps to zero are degreewise surjective.
