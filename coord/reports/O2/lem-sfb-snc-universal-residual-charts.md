## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the original root-algebra paragraph did not explicitly bind its index n to integers prime to p, despite asserting every punctured level was etale. On the literal all-positive-integers reading this is false: take k of characteristic p, Y=Spec k[t], D=V(t), I={D}, and n=p. On the puncture the map k[t,t^-1] to k[x,x^-1], t mapped to x^p, has nonzero relative differential dx and is inseparable, hence not etale. The same obstruction persists after an ind-etale base change: t remains a regular parameter at its divisor and cannot acquire a p-th root by a separable algebraic local extension.

I inserted explicitly that all root constructions range over positive n with (n,p)=1 ordered by divisibility, as the preceding roots-of-unity sentence, the parent tame-root-tower node, and ILO IX Proposition 2.1 intend. No coefficient or functor hypothesis is changed. These are now constructed in steps 19–20. All clauses of the original geometric conclusion under that intended indexing are retained.
