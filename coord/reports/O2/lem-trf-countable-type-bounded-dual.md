## Proof of the new auxiliary for its next worker

For a closed linear subspace U and x outside U, the set of distances ||x-u|| is nonempty (u=0) and bounded below. Since the complement of U is open at x, some positive epsilon is a lower bound. Thus its real infimum delta is positive. For 0<r<1, delta<delta/r, so choose u0 in U with ||x-u0||<delta/r and put y=x-u0. Then y is outside U, U+ky=U+kx, and for all u in U one has ||y-u||>=delta>r||y||. Every w in U+kx has unique coordinates w=u+a y; uniqueness follows because a nonzero difference of a-coordinates would put y in U. Define P(w)=u. This is linear and fixes U. If a is nonzero, homogeneity gives ||w||=|a| ||y+a^{-1}u||>=|a|delta>=r|a| ||y||. Hence |a| ||y||<=||w||/r; it is also true for a=0. Ultrametricity now gives ||P(w)||=||w-a y||<=max(||w||,|a| ||y||)<=||w||/r. This proves every conclusion, using no completeness or compactness. Infimum approximation and the bounded projection estimate are the comparison to formalize; the new node itself carries no proof.
