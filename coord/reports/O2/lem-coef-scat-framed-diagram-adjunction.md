For the new lemma I tested J empty, J a point, and a group as a one-object indexing category. The empty coend is initial and the empty target diagram terminal; the point case is the ordinary cosimplicial-frame tensor. The projective condition on the entire diagram cannot be omitted: for a nontrivial group G, an objectwise standard frame on a trivially acted-on point would make the coend the orbit functor, which sends EG to BG and the point to a point; EG→* is an objectwise weak equivalence but BG→* need not be. This failure is excluded by requiring projective Reedy cofibrancy, rather than just objectwise Reedy cofibrancy. A free projective resolution gives the homotopy-orbit construction instead. These tests also confirm that no local Kan hypothesis on the index or nonemptiness hypothesis is being silently used.
