Step 1 uses lem-sfb-tensor-word-basis exactly for its arbitrary-index word basis and concatenation algebra, and def-wf-lie-algebra for bilinearity and alternation. Finite basis expansions prove the relation-span identification without any finite-dimensionality assumption. The canonical quotient in the item is already explicitly defined; its statement dependency lem-sfb-enveloping-universal-property matches that same quotient.

Step 2 is a definition and finite counting argument, with no black-box lemma: minimize the two natural-number ranks to obtain a minimal word. Swapping an adjacent inverted pair removes exactly its own inversion; contributions involving any third position have unchanged total. Shortened bracket terms are lower regardless of their new basis letters. It is essential that the order is only a strict partial order, exactly as permitted by the cited normal-form criterion.

Step 3 compares disjoint reductions with the written common polynomial ApCqD. Each difference expands into contextual defining relations strictly below the original word. No global confluence theorem is silently used. Step 4 lists both cubic expansions and every intermediate leading word. Contexts preserve their inversion comparison because the same three letters are permuted; contexts need not preserve rank comparisons between arbitrary words.

Step 5 uses def-wf-lie-algebra precisely for skew-symmetry (from alternation) and Jacobi. I expanded the three r terms: their bracket corrections are the three displayed nested brackets; the latter sum is the negative cyclic Jacobi expression. Their contextual leading lengths drop by one, including bracket expansions with indices outside the original finite support. This proves membership in the smaller-relation span, not just in the whole defining ideal.

Step 6 uses the complete statement of lem-sfb-triangular-linear-relations. Checked every hypothesis: a set of words, a well-founded strict partial order, finite right-side supports, strict triangularity, and compatibility for same-leading rules. Same-position, disjoint and single-overlap exhaust the possibilities. The conclusion is exactly independence and spanning of irreducible words in the vector-space quotient by the relation span, identified with U(L) in Step 1. Nondecreasing lists are exactly those irreducibles.

Step 7 uses lem-weil-pbc-vector-space-free only for existence of a vector-space basis, which its statement asserts. For any chosen basis its one-letter words are members of the proved basis of U(L), so the finite coefficients of a kernel vector vanish individually. Classical choice permits ordering a basis; no assumption on a supplied order is added. The empty alphabet has one empty word, so the quotient is Q and j from zero is injective.
