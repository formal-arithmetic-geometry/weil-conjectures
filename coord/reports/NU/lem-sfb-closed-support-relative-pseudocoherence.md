## Source error

The parent delivery already corrected the source's generality before this assignment. This delivery retains that correction, without a new statement change. Stacks 09VG's topological-embedding hypothesis does not imply its claimed surjection on reduced rings. To recall the explicit counterexample recorded in coord/reports/Q23/lem-sfb-flat-ambient-relative-pseudocoherence.md: over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field k, take A=k[t²−1,t³−t], B=k[t,1/(t−1)], S=Spec k. The partial normalization Spec B→Spec A is bijective, hence a homeomorphism of these irreducible curves, since their proper closed subsets are finite sets of closed points. It is not finite: A is contained in the valuation ring k[t]_(t−1), whereas 1/(t−1) is not integral there, so B is not finite over A. O_X[0] is relatively pseudo-coherent over k on the smooth finite-presentation source, but its image B is not even relatively 0-pseudo-coherent on Y. This example is excluded by the present closed-immersion hypothesis. For a closed immersion A→B is actually surjective; a polynomial presentation of A is also a presentation of B after composition, and both chart conditions test the same derived object. That is the proof used here. No erroneous nilpotent-generator argument from the broader source is used. Separately, 09UU's printed appeal to 09UK requires quasi-coherent cohomology; this item's arbitrary-E formulation is precisely why the parent uses this valid closed-immersion argument.
