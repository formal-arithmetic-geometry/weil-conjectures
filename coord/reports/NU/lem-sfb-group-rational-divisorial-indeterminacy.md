The statement explicitly quantifies scheme points and does not require a rational u. Here is the intended proof of that leaf, recorded here rather than in its statement. Smoothness makes X and its products geometrically reduced; the dense open U stays schematically dense after extending the ground field (flat base change of its dense open immersion). All representatives of the difference map glue by reduced-source, separated-target equality. If f extends near x, its product with its inverse extends on the product of that neighbourhood with itself, hence at Delta(x), and its diagonal restriction is e. Conversely suppose a representative b is defined on an open N around Delta(x). Set M=N intersect (X times U). The first projection M to X is smooth and open, and its image V is an open neighbourhood of x: in the fibre over x, N contains the diagonal point and U over kappa(x) is dense, so the fibre meets M. On M define a=b times f(second coordinate). On M over U this is f(first coordinate). On M times_V M the two resulting maps agree over U; this open is schematically dense because the projection is smooth (flat) and U is schematically dense in the reduced integral V. The triple product is smooth over k, hence reduced, so separatedness gives equality everywhere. Shrinking V around x to an affine and choosing finitely many affine pieces of M with images covering V gives an fpqc cover. Representable fpqc descent (Stacks 023Q, descent-lemma-fpqc-universal-effective-epimorphisms, read in descent.tex) descends a to V to G; it equals f over V intersect U. Glue to the original U if needed. This proves the criterion and the identity on the diagonal. For lem-sfb-factorial-affine-rational-poles, I read AV pp.17–18 and Stacks divisors.tex Tag 0AGA, lemma-weil-divisor-is-cartier-UFD; also the principal-divisor definitions and Tag 02RL, lemma-divisor-locally-finite. This is a general affine-target lemma; it has no group or diagonal. Choose algebra generators a_1,...,a_m of A and their pullbacks r_i in the function field of W. At a local UFD stalk R write each nonzero r_i=c_i/d_i in coprime form; zero r_i has denominator 1. After shrinking, these are regular sections with nonzero denominators and no common prime-divisor component between numerator and denominator. A rational function is regular at a point of this neighbourhood precisely when its reduced denominator is a unit in that local ring: unique factorization and cancellation give this in both directions. The union of the prime components of the denominators therefore describes the complement of the simultaneous regularity locus. These are the negative prime-divisor supports, independent of local presentations; by Noetherianity and the finite family there are finitely many globally. Each prime divisor is Cartier by 0AGA. The sum of the reduced polar prime divisors is the required P. Equivalently, their local product equations differ by units on overlaps and define an invertible ideal. On the common regularity locus the r_i define a map to Spec A: every relation vanishes in the function field and hence in the integral section ring. Conversely any extension to Spec A makes every r_i regular. Thus the locus equals E. This is the missing pole-support/regularity interface; divisor-line-bundle dictionaries alone do not state it. No proof has been hidden in the statement-only JSON.
