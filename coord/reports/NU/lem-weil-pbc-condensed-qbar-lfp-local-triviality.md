### Proof and dependencies

The proof refines every finite presentation to the weakly contractible affine basis. Basis evaluation and finite directed composita put each finite matrix in a single finite intermediate field, without claiming that arbitrary sections on non-quasi-compact objects have a global finite field. Intrinsic geometric fibre rank is defined for the original sheaf, not merely for an unrelated finite-E model. Flat going-down makes rank constant along specializations. Finitely many irreducible components and their generic points make the rank levels clopen on X. On the inverse images of these levels the finite-E matrix has constant rank. Nonvanishing minors, continuous inverses in the same E and explicit block operations give the desired free cokernels after an open refinement. No coefficient-module descent theorem or Serre-closure theorem is used, which avoids a cycle through the item's parent.

The only added proof dependencies are existing general inputs.
