  together with `∀ i : ℤ, Function.Injective (ι.hom.f i)`.
  Here the morphism type is Mathlib's `Mon.Hom` for the category of complexes:
  its underlying chain map is linear in every degree and preserves both the
  multiplication and the unit. Thus the image of each component is an additive
  subgroup of `A.X.X i`; chain compatibility makes these images stable under
  differential, and preservation of multiplication and unit gives exactly the
  remaining closure clauses in the node. Degreewise injectivity identifies
  `A₀` with these images, rather than an arbitrary DG algebra mapping to `A`.
  Conversely, the node's actual subgroups with restricted operations and their
  inclusion give precisely this witness. This is an equivalent representation
  of a subalgebra, with no new notion, assumption or statement correction.
- Countability of the disjoint union of homogeneous pieces, retaining degrees,
  is `Countable (Σ i : ℤ, A₀.X.X i)`. Degreewise injectivity identifies this
  degree-tagged type with the degree-tagged image subalgebra.
- The isomorphisms on cohomology are `QuasiIso ι.hom`. Mathlib defines this by
  the isomorphism of the induced `HomologicalComplex.homologyMap` in every
  integer degree; these are the maps of the inclusion, not arbitrary isomorphisms.

nontriviality assumption); ordinary rings in degree zero are included; all
positive and negative degrees and unbounded complexes are allowed. Acyclic

the subalgebra generated by cohomology representatives. No countability of the
ambient algebra is assumed. The degree-tagged zeros are retained, including in
the zero algebra, so the sigma type is the required disjoint union. An empty

explicitly includes unit and zeros. Ambient and subalgebra share universe `u`,
