## Proof of the new analytic input: guidance for its future worker

The weighted coefficient model is a separate analytic lemma, not a disguised division theorem. Scaling each coefficient c_alpha by rho^alpha identifies its vector space isometrically with ordinary l1(N^d,C), proving completeness; convolution obeys the norm bound by summing |c_beta|rho^beta |b_gamma|rho^gamma. This space includes every coefficient family with finite weighted sum, not merely functions extending past the closed polydisc. On strictly smaller polydiscs the majorant gives uniform absolute convergence. Binomial recentering, dominated by the original absolute sum whenever |x_i|+|y_i|<rho_i, gives a local power series at each interior point. Coefficients are recovered by iterated coordinate derivatives, so the germ map is injective. Conversely the defining convergent expansion of an analytic germ has finite absolute majorant at some smaller positive radii. Absolute multiplication of the two sums proves the algebra-map property. Decreasing radii is termwise norm domination, and setting coordinates to zero deletes precisely the coefficients with a positive corresponding exponent. These observations explain why the lemma is true but require a real coefficient/multilinear-series comparison to formalize; they are not one-line specializations of Mathlib's generic lp or analytic APIs.
