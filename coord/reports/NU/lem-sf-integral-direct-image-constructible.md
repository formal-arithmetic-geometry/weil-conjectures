### Retained argument for the nilpotent-coefficient node

For any R-module sheaf M set M_j=I^j M, the subsheaf locally generated by products. Then M_0=M, M_t=0, and Q_j=M_j/M_{j+1} is killed by I. Its R-action factors uniquely through R/I. The existing lem-sbc-coefficient-higher-direct-image-zero applies to the qcqs morphism and the ring map R to R/I: the vanishing hypothesis for the derived image of Q_j over R/I gives the same vanishing after regarding it as an R-module sheaf. No flatness of R/I is used. Module direct image is left exact, the module-sheaf category has enough injectives, and lem-weil-pbc-derived-functor-long-exact supplies the exact segment R^q f_* M_{j+1} to R^q f_* M_j to R^q f_* Q_j, including degree zero. Descend from j=t. Both outside terms vanish for each fixed nonnegative q>N, so the middle term vanishes, ending at M. This retains the earlier three-step proof in retained-nilpotent-proof.md. The integer N never grows with t, and negative N is allowed because the segment includes q=0.

### Argument assigned to the finite-proetale constructibility node

Finite-type schemes over a field are Noetherian by lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian. Every open subset of a Noetherian scheme is quasi-compact; inverse images of affine opens under f and its diagonal therefore show f is qcqs, without separation. The existing lem-weil-pbc-finite-ring-constructible-derived-origin applies to the arbitrary finite commutative ring B: choose bounded constructible etale L with nu_X^* L isomorphic to C. Its statement explicitly preserves and reflects cohomological intervals, so no boundedness is lost in choosing L. Apply thm-dg-finite-type-constructibility to L, with mB=0 and m invertible on the field, to obtain bounded constructible etale Rf_*L. The qcqs bounded-below comparison prop-weil-pbc-comparison-direct-image gives nu_Y^* Rf_*L isomorphic to the ordinary pro-etale direct image of C. Use the finite-ring origin equivalence on Y to identify this with the intrinsic bounded constructible class. Stacks 09AL proves the origin equivalence for Noetherian coefficients, which includes B, and 09A6 proves the direct-image comparison. This does not impose finite projective dimension on any B-stalk: for example a nonperfect residue module over Z/l^2 is allowed. This retains all four operations and all hypothesis checks in old step 4, and uses the existing etale theorem for its nonseparated case.

### Argument assigned to the etale-origin amplitude node

Exact, fully faithful comparison pullback identifies nu_X^* H^r(L) with H^r(C) and reflects zero objects. Hence L has the given interval [a,b]. For a sheaf M in degree zero, the right-derived etale image vanishes in negative degrees by lem-weil-pbc-derived-functor-negative-vanishing and in degrees above N by hypothesis. For N>=0, apply lem-pur-bounded-heart-amplitude to the etale triangulated derived functor, with the class of all B-module sheaves and heart interval [0,N]. Concretely this is the old finite truncation argument: the successive triangles tau_{<=r-1}L to tau_{<=r}L to H^r(L)[-r] have third term with image supported in [r,r+N]. Start with zero below a; the long exact sequence shows each image truncation through r has range [a,r+N]. At r=b obtain [a,b+N]. For N<0, every heart image has zero cohomology in all degrees, hence is zero; the same finite sequence of triangles then gives zero image for L. Finally the bounded-below qcqs direct-image comparison and exact nu_Y^* transfer the interval, or zero image, to the pro-etale image. This retains the lower bound, the shift signs, finite induction and negative-N case, while making no assertion about all pro-etale sheaves or any unbounded convergence.
