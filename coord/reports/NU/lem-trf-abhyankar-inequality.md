## Proof and route

The argument writes out the valued independence calculation rather than replacing the inequality by a new clause-copy node. Choose arbitrary finite rationally independent value images and algebraically independent residue elements. Normalize each nonzero coefficient polynomial by a coefficient of minimal value. Its reduction is nonzero, so its evaluation differs from that base-field coefficient by a unit. Distinct monomials in the value elements then have distinct values modulo base values. Normalize the finite sum by a term dividing every other term; all the other normalized terms lie in the maximal ideal, so the sum has residue one. Thus the combined family is algebraically independent. Its size is at most the field transcendence degree. A finite greedy spanning argument and the bounded supremum of finite independent residue families finish the dimension bound without any advance finiteness assumption on the two left-hand terms.
