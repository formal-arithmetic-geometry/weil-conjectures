Reduced Noetherian pure one-dimensional schemes are CM; the CM dualizing module has no associated point at a closed point. Consequently its coherent generic-zero submodules vanish. Those are explicit properties in the new statement, not an unmentioned consequence borrowed from a definition. Its uniqueness is uniqueness of a representing object with its specified natural isomorphism. The Euler identity is included because 0BS5 states it for CM pure-dimensional curves, independently of connectedness or the field of constants.
