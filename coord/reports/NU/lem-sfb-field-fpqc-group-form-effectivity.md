  of schemes over the field, including nonaffine, nonreduced and nonquasicompact
  schemes. It is not a restriction to field-valued or affine test objects.
- Its underlying set-valued functor is `G ⋙ forget GrpCat`. The hypothesis `hG`
  is `Presheaf.IsSheaf (Scheme.fpqcTopology.over (Spec (.of k)))` of precisely
  that functor. The topology is generated by the recorded `fpqcPrecoverage`;
  its sheaf condition is the covering equalizer condition. The slice topology
  tests the underlying fpqc coverings of each test scheme, so the base is fixed.
- The fpqc morphism is `t : T ⟶ Spec (.of k)` with `[Flat t] [Surjective t]`
  and `[QuasiCompact t]`. These are exactly a flat, surjective, quasi-compact
  morphism. No local finite presentation or finite type condition on `t` occurs.
- Restriction of G to T-schemes is `(Over.map t).op ⋙ G`: `Over.map t` composes
  a T-scheme structure map with t. This is restriction of the given group functor,
  not an independently supplied group functor or a sheafification.
- `hlocal` says there is `P : Grp (Over T)` with `IsSeparated P.X.hom`,
  `LocallyOfFinitePresentation P.X.hom`, and
  `Nonempty (yonedaGrp.obj P ≅ (Over.map t).op ⋙ G)`.
  `Grp` bundles the scheme and `GrpObj` structure. `yonedaGrp` is the actual
  group-valued functor of points, and the natural isomorphism is in group-valued
  functors. In particular it preserves multiplication, identity and inverse.
- Separatedness is Mathlib `IsSeparated`, whose defining field is
  `IsClosedImmersion (pullback.diagonal f)`, exactly the closed-diagonal clause.
- The conclusion existentially supplies `P : Grp (Over (Spec (.of k)))` with
  `IsSeparated P.X.hom`, `LocallyOfFiniteType P.X.hom`, and
  `Nonempty (yonedaGrp.obj P ≅ G)`. Thus it represents the original G with all
  its operations, and says locally finite type rather than finite type.
- No smoothness, reducedness, commutativity, quasi-compactness of P, or finite
  type of T is assumed. Those absent assumptions remain absent in the signature.
