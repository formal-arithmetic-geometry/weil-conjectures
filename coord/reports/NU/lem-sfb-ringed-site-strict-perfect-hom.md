## Original proof retained for the newly exposed workers

### Original step 1

For any term $T=E^i$, choose its splitting $T\xrightarrow{i}\mathcal O^{\oplus r}\xrightarrow{p}T$. With the standard sections $u_j$ and coordinate functionals $u_j^*$ put $t_j=p(u_j)$ and $\lambda_j=u_j^*i$, so $\sum_jt_j\lambda_j=1_T$. The sheaf Hom and tensor definitions [def-pur-sheaf-hom, def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product] give, for every module sheaf $B$, mutually inverse maps
$$B\otimes T^\vee\longrightarrow\mathcal Hom(T,B),\quad b\otimes\phi\longmapsto(t\mapsto b\phi(t)),\qquad h\longmapsto\sum_jh(t_j)\otimes\lambda_j.$$
Indeed their composites send $h$ to $h\sum_jt_j\lambda_j=h$ and $b\otimes\phi$ to $b\otimes\sum_j\phi(t_j)\lambda_j=b\otimes\phi$. All formulas are compatible with further restriction. The dual maps $p^\vee,i^\vee$ exhibit $T^\vee$ as a summand of $(\mathcal O^{\oplus r})^\vee=\mathcal O^{\oplus r}$. The double-dual map $t\mapsto(\phi\mapsto\phi(t))$ has inverse $\alpha\mapsto\sum_j\alpha(\lambda_j)t_j$. Finally $\mathcal Hom(T,-)$ is a retract, naturally in its argument, of $\mathcal Hom(\mathcal O^{\oplus r},-)=(-)^{\oplus r}$. The latter is exact by [lem-sc-finite-free-internal-hom-exact]. Applying the retraction to any short exact sequence splits its kernels, images and cokernels, so its retract sequence is exact too.

Uses: def-pur-sheaf-hom, def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product, lem-sc-finite-free-internal-hom-exact.

### Original step 2

For the fixed K-injective augmentation F to I(F), Hom(E^i,-) is exact by step 1. The bounded exact-source Hom calculation [lem-pc-bounded-exact-term-hom-quasi-iso] therefore gives a quasi-isomorphism Hom-complex(E,F) to Hom-complex(E,I(F)). Equivalently, filter by the finitely many source degrees of E: the successive quotients are shifted exact termwise Hom functors, so induction and the cohomology long exact sequence apply to arbitrary unbounded F. This finite filtration proof works with independent site and coefficient universes; no exactness of infinite products is used.

Uses: def-pur-derived-internal-hom, lem-pc-bounded-exact-term-hom-quasi-iso, def-pur-hom-complex.

### Original step 3

The Hom-product description [def-pur-hom-complex] and the fact that O is concentrated in degree zero identify D^q with Hom(E^(-q),O), and d_D^q with (-1)^(q+1) times the dual of d_E^(-q-1). If E is supported in [a,b], then D is supported in [-b,-a]. Step 1 dualizes the chosen finite-free retractions, producing a finite-free retract package for D. Step 2 applied to D gives its augmentation comparison at O.

Uses: def-pur-hom-complex, def-weil-pbc-strict-perfect-coefficient-package.

### Original step 4

For the ordinary signed bidual map characterized in [lem-sfb-ringed-site-duality-maps], the Hom-product projections identify its degree-i component with (-1)^i times termwise double-dual evaluation. The dual basis identity of step 1 gives the inverse, including for odd i and in characteristic two. This proves invertibility as an actual chain map, not merely of its localization.

Uses: lem-sfb-ringed-site-duality-maps, def-pur-hom-complex.

### Original step 5

In degree n, the ordinary evaluation of [lem-sfb-ringed-site-duality-maps] is the finite sum, over p+q=n, of F^p tensor (E^(-q))^dual to Hom(E^(-q),F^p), y tensor phi mapping to (x mapping to phi(x)y). The dual basis inverse of step 1 inverts every summand; both sides have the same finite indexing because E is bounded. Expanding the differentials gives d_F y tensor phi + (-1)^(p+q+1)y tensor (phi d_E) on the tensor side, exactly d_F f-(-1)^n f d_E on the Hom side. Thus the componentwise inverse is an inverse chain map. The formula also covers zero ranks, the zero ring and unbounded F.

Uses: lem-sfb-ringed-site-duality-maps, def-pc-tensor-complex-sheaves, def-pur-hom-complex.
