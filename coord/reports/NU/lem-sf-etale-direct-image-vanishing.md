## What was wrong and what changed

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the original “that is UDV in the sense of def-sf-uniform-direct-image-vanishing” is outside that definition's domain. That definition fixes k as an algebraic closure of a finite field, fixes A=A_{E,n}, and requires a separated finite-type morphism. The item explicitly allows arbitrary algebraically closed k and nonseparated f. For example k=C, X=Y=Spec C, f=id and A=F_2 satisfy the item but do not supply that definition's global finite-field datum. I kept the full higher-direct-image vanishing conclusion and made the UDV identification conditional on the setting in which the existing definition is defined. This adds no condition to the vanishing theorem. The bound d is explicitly a natural number, as intended by a cohomological-dimension bound; the coefficient ring is explicitly commutative, as required by the project's stated module-valued direct-image definition (whose module clause begins “Let R be a commutative ring”). This resolves the otherwise unspecified ring convention and does not change coefficient categories.

The original proof takes objectwise equality from `lem-weil-pbc-qcqs-higher-direct-image`, whereas its statement gives sheafification of the cohomology presheaf. Tag 03Q8 agrees with the existing node. In general this distinction matters even for f=id: higher direct images vanish in positive degree, while the cohomology presheaf on etale objects need not vanish. It also says every etale W over Y is finite type: for Y=Spec k, an infinite disjoint union of Spec k is etale but not quasi-compact and not finite type. One must restrict to affine pointed neighbourhoods or use a cover-dense affine basis. The given dimension step additionally uses equality of dimensions of etale local rings and separable residue extensions without citing statements for those facts. Its constructible-coefficient citation does not cover arbitrary module sheaves. All these defective proof steps are removed rather than reproduced.

A whole-DAG search found `lem-kun-relative-torsion-direct-image-dimension-bound`, already split and reviewed on the shared live DAG. It states the result for separably closed k, any commutative ring with torsion additive group, and quasi-compact locally finite-type f, with Y merely locally finite type over k. Its statement asserts the module higher-direct-image conclusion itself. It therefore covers this item and eliminates the need for new geometry or comparison nodes. No new node restates the item or one of its clauses.
