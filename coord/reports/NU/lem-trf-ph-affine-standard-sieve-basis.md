## Review by NU

I reviewed the final three node files against the sources above, their actual dependency statements, the unit `sk-i-curve-zero` and the trace-formula chapter. This remains on the recorded ph-descent branch of the normal-cone argument. The ten steps supply the basis needed by the parent's 0F0I refinement and gluing argument. The source is not substituted for a proof, and the source's non-elementary standard-composite argument and finite-fppf-domination argument are isolated as strictly smaller nodes. Every step is under 1200 characters; no long step conceals a new lemma.

Step 1 uses `lem-pc-fppf-affine-open-cover` exactly for affine covers of each open subscheme, `lem-weil-pbc-affine-scheme-qcqs` for compactness of affine targets, and the definitions of quasi-compact scheme, open immersion and properness. Taking affine covers of the given opens and then a finite subcover proves the subordinate refinement; it does not assume a finite subcover of a non-quasi-compact scheme. The identity proper map is checked from its diagonal, ring maps and base changes. These are elementary consequences of the quoted statements.

Step 2 uses `lem-weil-pbc-proper-base-change` and `lem-weil-pbc-surjective-base-change` with arbitrary affine base change, exactly as their statements allow. `lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra` gives affineness after writing all three affine schemes as spectra by `def-weil-affine-spectrum`. The open restriction identification is explained by the fibre-product universal property and the open-immersion definition, both cited. No intersections of affine opens in an arbitrary scheme are assumed affine.

Step 3 cites the actual three sieve-topology axioms in `def-weil-pbc-topos` and the identity case of properness. It constructs an auxiliary topology using *all affine tests*, so stability under pullback is the equality of iterated sieve pullbacks; it does not silently assume the desired basis theorem. Step 4 verifies the transitivity axiom from the newly stated 0DBF refinement theorem and those sieve definitions. The tested domains are affine and both levels finite, exactly the child's hypotheses. Refinement arrows really factor through the selected second-level maps, so sieve closure gives membership in the original sieve. This is the only non-elementary input in that transitivity argument.

Step 5 uses the precise generators in `def-trf-ph-scheme-site`, proper/surjective base-change statements and the proper/finite-type definitions. A proper source over an affine target is quasi-compact because the preimage of that affine target is quasi-compact. Pullbacks of opens are described by the cited open-immersion and fibre-product definitions. Together with Step 1 this puts both sorts of generator in the auxiliary topology, so its inclusion follows from leastness. Step 6 derives upward closure and covering of a standard composite directly from the quoted topology axioms and the two sorts of ph generators; it needs no unquoted “comparison of topologies” lemma.

Step 7 cites the general affine-open-cover node and the topology/ph definitions to get the other inclusion by local character. A non-affine target need not have a finite affine open covering. The same transitivity argument proves locality on affine opens. Affine pullbacks and refinements proved earlier give a sieve basis on affine objects without asserting that standard families themselves form a composition-closed pretopology.

Step 8 uses `lem-weil-pbc-etale-covering-base-change` exactly for preservation of both étaleness and joint surjectivity. The statements of `def-weil-etale-morphism` and `def-pc-fppf-site` then give the fppf hypotheses. `lem-weil-pbc-affine-scheme-qcqs` verifies *both* target hypotheses of the 0CP0 child. That child supplies an actual finite surjection and open local lifts, not merely an integral morphism or a sheaf condition. `lem-weil-pbc-finite-proper` then gives properness, and the proper/finite-type definitions give the compactness needed for the finite subordinate affine cover. The resulting maps factor through the original family, as required for sieve membership. The missing base-change citation in my draft was repaired here before validation.

Step 9 uses `lem-weil-pbc-open-immersion-etale`, whose statement actually asserts étaleness of every open immersion, together with the ph definition's description of a Zariski family as jointly surjective open immersions. Step 10 uses the fixed universe convention and ph generators for the smallness and empty cases. The children also explicitly use this size convention and carry full definition dependencies; no new primitive notion is left undefined.
