The valuation-ring condition in `hvA` is the meaning of the valuation attached to A:
`0 ≤ vA x ↔ x ∈ Set.range (algebraMap A F)`. It also includes x=0. Merely asking for
an arbitrary additive valuation with value one on π would omit the requirement
that it be the valuation of this DVR. No new valuation definition is introduced.
`IsLeast` avoids an inappropriate infimum in `WithTop ℤ`, which has no bottom and
is not a complete lattice. Its set is finite and nonempty mathematically because q
is the row rank, and its least value is part of the conclusion, not an assumption.

Edge cases checked before drafting:

- Zero matrix, including s=0 or N=0: q=0, free quotient, zero torsion, empty minor
  equal to 1, so the least value and torsion length are zero.
- A single unit entry: the quotient has no torsion and the least valuation is zero.
- A single entry π^e, including e=0: the cyclic quotient has length e and its only
  nonzero maximal-rank minor has value e.
- Rectangular diagonal examples with a free quotient part, e.g. a 1 by 2 matrix
  `(π^e, 0)`: torsion length e, values e and infinity.
- A 2 by 3 diagonal block with pivots 1 and π^3: rank 2, quotient torsion length 3,
  one nonzero 2-minor of value 3 and zero minors of infinite value.
- Duplicate/dependent rows do not alter q or exclude the zero determinants among
  q-minors. All-zero q-minors for q>0 would contradict the specified field rank;
  nonemptiness/nonvanishing is not added as a hypothesis.
- Independent universes u and v for A and its fraction field are retained. There
  is no artificial universe equality or cardinality assumption.
