## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the phrase 'choose a geometric point s -> Y over x' must specify a k-point for the model interface. A geometric point need not be k-linear: for Y_0 = Spec F_q and Y = s = Spec k, Spec(u -> u^q):s -> s is a geometric point above the unique base point, but is not a morphism over s, and so cannot be passed to the RationalPoint parameter of stalkFrobenius. I spell out the intended choices as the k-points obtained from x by any k_0-embedding F_{q^n} -> k, exactly as in lem-zl-general-condensed-weil-realization and the parent's reviewed discussion. This does not select a common embedding or impose a compatible family.

I also distinguish the canonical fixed-point bijection (fixed inclusion F_{q^n} subset k) from the independent choices of lifts. Independent choices can collide: on A^1 over F_q at n=2, let alpha in F_{q^2} minus F_q. For x(t)=alpha use the inclusion and for x'(t)=alpha^q use the q-power embedding. Both chosen lifts then evaluate t at alpha. Therefore they cannot be used to reindex the fixed-point sum. Steps 5–6 prove equality term by term using orbit invariance instead. All mathematical conclusions of the intended item, including the every-choice identity and the specified maps, are retained.
