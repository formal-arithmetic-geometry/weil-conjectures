## Review by ND

I checked the seven steps against the source and opened the statement of every cited node. The quotes in citations.json are the complete verbatim statements, so the second reader can inspect each interface independently of its proof.

Step 1 uses the affine spectrum definition's explicit field clause: Spec k has one point and residue field k. The w-local definition asks that closed points be closed and that each component have one closed point; both are immediate for this space. Extremal disconnectedness is checked by its two opens. The henselian definition is the finite-algebra idempotent criterion, so maximal ideal zero makes the lifting map the identity for every finite k-algebra. Algebraic closedness eliminates non-linear irreducible polynomials and hence finite separable residue extensions. These verify precisely all three conditions of 0982. This is an elementary specialization, not a new lemma about algebraically closed points.

Step 2 cites only `lem-weil-pbc-wcontractible-geometric-criterion`. Its statement concludes a retraction for every faithfully flat weakly etale map, exactly what this step takes, rather than only ind-etale maps or finite-presentation maps. I checked the 0982 equivalence and its use of the w-contractible-ring definition. No retraction is inferred merely from faithful flatness.

Step 3 cites `lem-weil-pbc-wcontractible-affine-weakly-contractible`. Its statement assumes exactly the retractions supplied by step 2 and concludes surjectivity for every epimorphism of set sheaves on the full small pro-etale site. Here its base X and its affine object T are both s, and the object arrow is the identity. I read 098H's big-site statement and covering/gluing proof to check the issue explicitly; the existing node supplies the small-site version, so there is no unjustified extension of small-site sheaves to the big site. This is the main inherited ambiguity corrected by the split.

I take only this exactness clause; its stronger colimit assertion is not used. Step 5 supplies the abelian-group clause by the integer action. I checked that `def-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf` defines the sheafification of the constant presheaf, `def-weil-pbc-sheafification` states the universal property actually used to extend the integer action, and `def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category` defines precisely compatible sectionwise actions and additive linear morphisms. Compatible endomorphisms of restrictions glue uniquely, allowing the universal property to be applied to a sheaf of rings. Forgetting a module structure preserves the underlying kernel/image equation, which is the elementary reason exactness of step 4 gives exactness of groups. No claim that the entire pro-etale topos is the category of sets is used, and it is unnecessary to identify the ring of sections of the constant integer sheaf with Z.
