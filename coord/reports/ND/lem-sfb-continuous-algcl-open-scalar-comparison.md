Step 1 uses lem-weil-pbc-ring-sheaf-change-adjunction for exact conservative scalar restriction and its explicitly counit-compatible direct-image comparison. It allows arbitrary commutative coefficient ring maps with Cartesian inverse-image identifications, so E to B is allowed; admissibility or finite freeness is not a hypothesis. The coefficient sheaves pulled back from the point have those identifications. The K-flat description and the explicit x tensor a maps in lem-pur-sheaf-ring-derived-coefficient-adjunction identify the restricted arrow with the ordinary projection arrow. This is an identification of the defining adjuncts, not an assumed projection-formula isomorphism. Lem-sfb-ringed-projection-first-shift states exactness and shift compatibility in the INPUT K variable, including connecting maps, for arbitrary fixed P; I take P=B_X as an A_E module.
