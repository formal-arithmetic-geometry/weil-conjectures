For the broadened comparison assertion I checked why it is true even in the unbounded target category. A finite filtered projective module is a degreewise split sum of its graded projectives. The differential E^(n-1) onto the cycles in E^n is therefore surjective on every filtration step. Any filtered map from a split sum of graded projectives lifts along that differential, separately in each summand and its specified filtration step. Starting above the upper bound of Q and descending in degree builds a filtration-preserving null homotopy for any map Q to E.
