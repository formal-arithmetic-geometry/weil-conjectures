## Source corrections and scope

There is no dispute with the current item's statement. There is a false inference in the suggested route, not a justification for changing the target: an etale ordinary pushforward of a supported finite scalar model cannot in general be identified with the same model supported along the composed open chain. Take j:G_m -> A^1 over an algebraically closed field and the constant F_l sheaf. The geometric stalk of j_*F_l at 0 is F_l (the punctured strictly henselian local trait is connected), while the stalk of j_!F_l is zero. Ordinary direct image must retain this boundary. The etale child states hull membership and does not claim equality with extension by zero. HRS Lemma 5.3 and Bhatt--Scholze Lemma 5.4.3 are not cited for that equality.

Also, no unqualified formula expressing an adic local model as the filtered colimit of its values at finite etale stages is asserted. Even C(Z_l,Z_l) has non-locally-constant continuous functions, such as the identity function. The conditional continuity child leaves the adic limit/product argument explicit as a proof obligation. For rational coefficients a global lattice is not assumed: BS Proposition 6.8.4(6) gives an etale-local lattice. Existing lem-sfb-supported-rational-lattice-generation and lem-sfb-qcqs-lisse-stratified-integral-lattices provide the more relevant project interfaces. They are mentioned here as future child-proof input, not used without citation in the item's assembly.
