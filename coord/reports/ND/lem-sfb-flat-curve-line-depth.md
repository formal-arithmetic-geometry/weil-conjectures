## Review by ND

I read every statement cited in the six proof steps from its live JSON, not just its name or proof. citations.json copies the full statement for each occurrence, so the specific formulas and hypotheses can be checked directly. I checked delivery-errors B--E against these files and sources; the following is my mathematical self-review.

Step 1 uses the DVR definition as a nonfield local PID. The union-of-ideals argument explicitly proves Noetherianity, rather than invoking the DVR characterization without a node. The finite-type-over-Noetherian node says precisely that X is Noetherian when S is; the affine-open Noetherian node applies to arbitrary affine opens of that X. The affine stalk node and localization-Noetherian node provide Noetherianity of every stalk. The base is the single affine Spec R, hence already quasi-compact. No separatedness assumption enters.

Step 2 uses the invertible-module definition only for local rank-one generators and their tensor powers; its m=0 generator is the tensor unit. The flat-morphism definition supplies flat local stalk maps. The flat-module torsionfree-domain node says exactly that nonzero base scalars act injectively, so it applies over the closed point where the base local ring is R. Over the generic point the uniformizer is invertible, making the assertion immediate. The existing DVR unit-times-uniformizer-power statement shows all nonzero primes are the maximal one, so these two cases exhaust S. It does not assert this for an arbitrary valuation ring.

Step 3 uses the generic-fibre affine-ring node's actual restriction equation epsilon(i* a)=a/1. Its reduced closed fibre hypothesis holds; it does not need connectedness or flatness for that identification. The localization-injective node applies only after step 2 and the sheaf axiom explicitly show that pi is a nonzerodivisor on the affine section ring. A vanishing restricted section therefore has zero coefficient, and sheaf separatedness gives the global conclusion. A trivializing cover for M also trivializes each nonnegative tensor power. Pullback of its generator gives exactly the target module appearing in the statement. This checks the restriction map itself, not an unspecified injection between isomorphic groups.

Step 4 uses 0HA1 in exactly its stated stalk form: quotient of O_X,x by the extended maximal ideal of R, which is pi O_X,x. It does not identify a nonlocalized global quotient with a stalk. Reducedness is stalkwise by the cited definition, and Noetherianity passes to this quotient by the elementary ideal correspondence. Locally finite type survives the field base change by the existing node. The equidimensional-open local-dimension statement applies to O=X_k, n=1, using precisely the assumed pure dimension, and a finite-type affine chart of this locally finite type k-scheme exists by definition. Closedness of x makes its chart prime maximal. The closed-point dimension node then applies to that chart algebra; the affine-stalk node identifies its localization with B. Thus B has dimension one and has a nonzero maximal ideal. No rationality of x is assumed.

Step 5 rules out ann(b)=m_B explicitly. In a local ring b is either a unit or lies in m_B. A unit has zero annihilator; a nonunit with that annihilator has square zero and is zero by reducedness, giving annihilator B. This argument uses m_B nonzero and proper. The cited 00LL node has exactly the hypothesis that the maximal ideal is not associated, expressed by the absence of such an element. It applies to the finite B-module B and yields a nonzerodivisor t in m_B. Because t is a nonunit its quotient is nonzero, satisfying both parts of the cited regular-sequence definition. The depth definition therefore gives depth(B)>=1. This is a short in-place argument, not a new lemma concealed in a long step.

Step 6 verifies both rings Noetherian, the stalk map local and flat, and identifies its closed fibre with B before using 0337. A length-one regular sequence pi in the domain R proves positive base depth directly from the cited regular-sequence and depth definitions. Thus the exact stated equality depth(A_x)=depth(R)+depth(B) yields at least two. A rank-one basis identifies each successive regular-sequence quotient and preserves whether it is zero, so module depth equals ring depth. This last transport is written out and needs no theorem about arbitrary projective modules.

Edge cases: m=0 uses 1 and O_X throughout; m>0 uses the same local basis calculation. X empty gives injectivity between zero section groups and no stalk claim. Empty special fibre makes the depth assertion vacuous and makes pi a unit at every remaining point. X=Spec R[t] has closed-special depth two but depth one at the generic point of its special fibre, confirming the scope. A reducible nodal fibre (uv=pi) is reduced and the argument remains valid; no domain hypothesis is made on B. If the reduced-fibre assumption is removed, R[u,v]/(u^2,uv,pi*u) would not be flat, so it is not a counterexample to this theorem; the appropriate flat example R[u,v]/(u^2,uv) has pure one-dimensional nonreduced special fibre and local depth one at the special origin (u is annihilated by both u and v, while pi is regular). This confirms that reducedness has real content. A zero-dimensional special fibre, as for X=Spec R, gives depth only one, confirming the pure dimension requirement.
