The local-triviality input is strictly about a SHEAF and its presentations, and says nothing about Ext, derived Hom or filtered diagrams. Here is the argument intended for its later split. HRS Example 3.1 identifies the pulled-back topological coefficient sheaf with continuous functions on underlying spaces. After local presentation on a cover V, pull back to geometric points; a cover of an algebraically closed point has a point section, so the resulting presented module is a finite-dimensional E-vector space and has a well-defined rank independent of the lift. For a specialization x' to x in X and a lift v over x in the presentation cover, flatness lifts the generalization x' to a generalization v' of v. Each entry of the presenting continuous matrix has the same value at v and v', since E is Hausdorff. Thus the dimension of the cokernel is invariant under specialization. A connected noetherian space has finitely many irreducible components joined through their intersections; specialization invariance therefore makes this dimension constant on each connected component. Those components are finitely many clopen subsets. On each corresponding part of V the presenting matrix has constant rank. Its nonzero maximal minors give an open cover, on which Gaussian elimination is continuous (invert a nowhere-zero continuous determinant); all higher minors vanish as functions. The cokernel there is free of the fixed finite rank. Open coverings refine pro-etale coverings and give the asserted local trivializations. This argument is NOT taken from HRS Theorem 6.2: that theorem describes the lisse heart but starts from a different notion. Establishing the comparison between the archived geometric inverse image and Example 3.1, the lifting of generalizations and the rank argument are the substantive work of the new unsplit input.

The pushforward input is HRS Lemma 5.3(1), with its hypercover proof and its global-sections explanation. It is stated for arbitrary condensed ring sheaves and qcqs morphisms, in terms of cohomology sheaves, with functorial bounded-below strict models. There is no claim that a termwise colimit is the categorical colimit in an ordinary triangulated category. Exact filtered colimits identify quasi-isomorphic strict models. This distinction also permits the inductive second arguments Ri^!K_i, which have a cohomological common bound even though the initial diagram has a termwise common bound.

The recollement input is the ringed condensed version of HRS (3.3), (3.4), Lemma 3.4 and its use in Lemma 5.3(2). I also read Stacks proetale.tex labels lemma-closed-immersion (09AA), lemma-closed-immersion-complement-retrocompact-exact (09BM), lemma-open-immersion (09BN), and lemma-ses-associated-to-open (09AH). This node does not claim any continuity of Ri^!: the item proves that from the triangle and pushforward continuity.
