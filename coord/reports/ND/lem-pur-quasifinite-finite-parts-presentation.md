3. **Clopen pieces and disjointness.** I checked separatedness survives base change using the exact existing statement. Finite-proper, proper-over-separated, and proper-closed-image give closedness of each finite open in the ambient pullback; an open inclusion then identifies it with the induced open-and-closed subscheme. After deleting those images the pieces are disjoint and exhaust the fibre; their complement is a clopen remainder. The radicial definition node explicitly includes equivalence with universal injectivity, so it supplies exactly the geometric-singleton assertion for a one-point fibre with purely inseparable residue field, including nonreduced fibres. The residue quotient and the unique purely inseparable embedding into the algebraically closed field give nonemptiness. No unramifiedness or flatness is assumed here.

4. **Generators.** The module inverse-image node explicitly states the module adjunction and that its unit is the underlying abelian-sheaf unit. The sheaf definition supplies gluing on the disjoint open cover. Finite permanence and finite-proper imply the selected piece is proper after restriction. The closed-support property node says a closed subset of a proper closed support is proper; its local-finite-type hypothesis holds for the separated quasi-finite map and all its base changes. The support-sheaf node actually defines the subsheaf by these sections. Thus each extension-by-zero section belongs to that subsheaf. Linearity and restrictions follow from these concrete maps.

5. **Relations and common refinements.** I checked each coordinate, including pieces carved out of the old remainder: those receive zero, not a repeated arbitrary coordinate. The sheaf equalizer condition detects equality on the open cover. For two decompositions, every intersection other than the intersection of both remainders is closed in a finite piece; the closed-immersion-finite and finite-composition statements say exactly why it is finite. These are two elementary arguments from the cited statements and definitions, so this step is marked elementary. There is no new node for them.

6. **Stalks and actual maps.** Finite base-change and binary finite coproduct, iterated, make the special geometric fibre finite; the zero-summand case is Spec(0). It is proper by finite-proper, and every closed support is proper by the closed-support node. The geometric fibre is a disjoint union of singletons, hence has dimension at most zero. Thus `lem-weil-pbc-zero-dimensional-sections-stalks` has all of its hypotheses: algebraically closed field, separated finite-type scheme and dimension at most zero. Its statement names the global-section-to-germs map, rather than an unspecified isomorphism. Likewise `lem-cbc-proper-support-geometric-stalk` names restriction to the geometric fibre, and `lem-weil-pbc-inverse-image-stalk-germ` explicitly identifies germs of unit sections, not just stalk objects. Together they give the stated zero/diagonal matrix for every decomposition. Stalks commuting with colimits gives the finite sum source stalk. This verifies the last clause of the item for arbitrary B, including non-torsion modules.

7. **Special common refinements.** The cofiltered-neighbourhood node gives an actual common pointed neighbourhood; finite permanence preserves the special decomposition there. I checked the removed intersections all have a finite parent piece, even those meeting one remainder. Their geometric fibres are empty, hence their ordinary fibres are empty, and proper closed-image removes them without deleting the marked point. The point of each good finite piece determines its unique label in each older decomposition, and the remainder maps to zero. The resulting containment is containment of opens, so it gives actual refinement morphisms. The proof does not use a general immersion factoring through an arbitrary subset.

8. **Descent and universal property.** I added the previously implicit cofiltered-neighbourhood citation: it is needed both to put the finitely many tuple coordinates on one neighbourhood and to make equality of filtered-colimit classes hold after one further restriction. Special decompositions over an arbitrary etale T are obtained by pulling back the original ones over S, so no Noetherian assumption on a non-quasi-compact T has been smuggled in. At each overlap, the special common refinement has an injective generator on the chosen stalk, so equal images force equal refined tuple germs. The family relations then force equal G-germs. Addition, scalars, restrictions, the generator identity and uniqueness are all checked on these actual local representatives.
