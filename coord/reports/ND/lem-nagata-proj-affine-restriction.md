I read constructions.tex at lemma-proj-inclusion (01NO) and lemma-transitive-proj (01NP), their base-change input lemma-base-change-map-proj (01N2), and the full chart characterization in lemma-morphism-proj (01MY). For the new node I checked 01N2's ring-map hypotheses: R maps into A_0, S is any R-algebra, and B is graded-isomorphic to S tensor A. Neither flatness nor generation in degree one is assumed. Its chart domain is all of Proj B because the images of positive homogeneous elements cover, as asserted by 01N2(1); 01MY supplies the preimage and fraction formulas. These formulas characterize maps of schemes, including their structure sheaves, rather than just maps of spaces.

For step 1 I read def-weil-pbc-graded-sheaf-structures and lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-module-coproduct-closure in full. The former identifies the underlying sheaf with the module coproduct; the latter asserts exactly that a small coproduct of quasi-coherent modules is quasi-coherent. The index set here is N, which is small. I checked Schemes 01ID and the three direct-sum/colimit/locality sentences in section 01LA. This repairs the potential gap between componentwise quasi-coherence in the item and total-module quasi-coherence in the affine algebra theorem.

For step 2 I read lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-algebra-affine-inclusion, especially clause (i), which explicitly gives the tensor isomorphism and formula. Its hypotheses are only a scheme, a quasi-coherent algebra sheaf and two nested affine opens; step 1 gives exactly the missing total-module condition. The graded structure definition says the operations commute with restriction. Thus the comparison sends each component to the same component; injectivity proves its inverse is graded. The tensor swap is given on pure tensors and respects the displayed multiplication.

For step 3 I read every clause of the new scalar-extension node and def-weil-affine-spectrum. The restriction map equals beta(1 tensor -), and the vertical maps use the units R to C_0 and S to D_0; I checked their directions, with no assumption C_0=R or D_0=S. Spectrum contravariance gives V to U. The new node supplies both the entire source domain and the cartesian square, so arbitrary affine opens, not only principal ones, are handled.

For step 4 I read def-weil-open-immersion and def-pc-fibre-product. The former identifies an open immersion with an open subscheme; the latter supplies the universal property and uniqueness of fibre products. I wrote the factorization property showing that the inverse-image open is the pullback rather than citing a stability theorem that neither definition states.

For step 5 I checked the scalar-extension chart formulas, the explicit homogeneous fractions in def-nagata-homogeneous-localization-degree-zero, spectrum functoriality in def-weil-affine-spectrum and the restriction compatibility in def-weil-pbc-graded-sheaf-structures. The two compositions send a/f^m to exactly (a restricted to W)/(f restricted to W)^m on the same inverse-image opens, which cover the source. This verifies equality of the scheme morphisms. No affine intersection or generic Proj functoriality theorem is silently used.

Small cases tested: V=U yields identity on all charts; the empty affine open has zero sections and empty Proj, so all restriction and pullback assertions hold. An algebra concentrated in degree zero has empty Proj on every affine open. For A=C[T] with C an arbitrary quasi-coherent degree-zero algebra, the claim reduces to the affine spectrum square, confirming that A_0 need not be O_X. Weighted polynomial rings are allowed, and non-flat R to S remains valid for the new ring-level statement. For a zero/nilpotent homogeneous f the standard open and its source chart are empty, so the fraction formula causes no extra invertibility hypothesis.

I read Mathlib ProjectiveSpectrum/Functor.lean's map, away-chart compatibility, map_comp and map_id, and Basic.lean/Proper.lean at the pullback/tensor hits. Those concern maps satisfying an irrelevant-ideal condition, chart intersections or separatedness; they do not prove the scalar-extension square. The semantic hits pullbackSpecIso and pullbackAwayιIso are affine spectrum pullbacks and intersections within a single Proj. Thus the new node remains unsplit, rather than a mislabelled Mathlib leaf.
