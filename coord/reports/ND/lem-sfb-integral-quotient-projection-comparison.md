The scalar tensorator here is the specified $b$: both normalized maps send $(u\otimes v)\otimes s$ to $(u\otimes1)\otimes(v\otimes s)$. Thus (*) specializes the monoidal composition equation of [lem-sfb-ringed-derived-monoidal-comparisons] with these tensorators. There is no permutation of the homogeneous variables and hence no extra Koszul sign.

### Returned step 4

Put $C=F_RK$, $W=C\otimes^L_{R_Y}M$, $H=K\otimes^L_{R_X}L_RM$, and

This follows by applying the Hom bijection to its own inverse; it does not use invertibility of $\rho$.
