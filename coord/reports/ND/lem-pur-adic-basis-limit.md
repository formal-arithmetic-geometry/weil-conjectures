Conclusions, clause by clause:

- `∃ f : adicBasisCompletion J A t ⟶ adicBasisCompletion J B v` is a genuine
  ring-sheaf morphism. `AdicBasisScalarCondition ... f` is, for every level n,
  `f ≫ projection_B n = projection_A n ≫ adicBasisScalar ... n`.
  `adicBasisScalar` is constant-sheafification of `Ideal.quotientMap` induced
  by `algebraMap A B`; its class-of-a formula is Mathlib's quotient map formula.
- `(∀ g, AdicBasisScalarCondition ... g → g = f)` proves uniqueness among
  **all** ring-sheaf morphisms satisfying the projection condition, independently
  of whether a basis isomorphism has been chosen for g.
- `adicBasisFree J R d` is the categorical finite product of d copies of
  `SheafOfModules.unit R` in the dependency's `ringedSiteModules R`.
  In this preadditive category the finite product is the finite direct sum, with
  the same coordinates, including the empty direct sum. The conclusion contains
  an actual categorical isomorphism `Φ` from this free module sheaf to
  `(SheafOfModules.restrictScalars f).obj (SheafOfModules.unit S)`.
  Thus linearity and naturality are part of its type, not unverified properties
  of a bare map of sets. Its target has the R-action through exactly f.
- The first formula for every n, object U, and source section x is
  `π_B,n (Φ x) = ∑ i, scalar_n (π_A,n (coordinate_i x)) * residue_n (b i)`.
  Here `adicBasisCoordinate` is the categorical product projection and
  `adicBasisConstant` is `Ideal.Quotient.mk` followed by the actual
  sheafification unit `toSheafify`. Taking the coordinate unit vectors gives
  exactly the compatible residues of b_i as the columns of Φ. Conversely,
  those columns and R-linearity give this same formula, so the formula is an
  equivalent explicit rendering of the specified basis map.
- At every positive exponent n+1, the next existential `β` is a module-sheaf
  isomorphism from the finite free module over the constant A/t^(n+1) sheaf
  to the constant B/v^(e(n+1)) sheaf, with its scalar action through
  `adicBasisCofinalScalar`. Since `ht` gives
  `t^(n+1) B = v^(e(n+1)) B`, this target is precisely the node's
  `B/t^(n+1) B = B/v^(e(n+1)) B`. Its displayed section formula
  `β y = ∑ i, cofinalScalar_n (coordinate_i y) * residue_(e(n+1)) (b i)`
  specifies the quotient basis isomorphism, rather than merely asserting that
  some two abstract module sheaves are isomorphic.
- The final formula is
  `π_B,e(n+1) (Φ x) = ∑ i, cofinalScalar_n (π_A,n+1 (coordinate_i x)) *
  residue_e(n+1) (b i)`.
  The right side is β's preceding formula evaluated at the coordinatewise
  projection of x. This is exactly the composite in the last sentence of the
  node, with the coordinatewise-product map expanded on sections. It does not
  assert that the projection from the completion to a quotient is invertible.
  Level `e * (n+1) - 1` has exponent `e * (n+1) - 1 + 1 = e * (n+1)`:
  positivity follows from `he`. Every positive n is covered by the shift.
