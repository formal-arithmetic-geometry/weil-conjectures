For lem-a1-normal-geometric-cover-components I read 0BQ3 and the opening and final paragraphs of the proof of 7.4.10. Normal local rings give the geometrically unibranch hypothesis; irreducible components of a locally topologically Noetherian geometrically unibranch scheme are clopen, hence its connected components are open as used in the new statement. The source proves that a connected geometric covering over a connected quasi-compact base is quasi-compact and then finite etale. The valuative criterion restricts to a clopen component: Spec of a valuation domain is connected, and a map with its generic point in that component has all its image there. The image of a nonempty finite etale component is open (etale) and closed (finite), hence the whole connected base. This verifies the added surjectivity wording. These explanations check the statement; they are not proof fields on the unsplit child. Normality is not replaced by irreducibility or reducedness.

Step 1 cites the new representability statement, which explicitly gives both the bijections and valuation lifting. The item's hypotheses imply exactly its locally Noetherian and pro-etale local-constancy assumptions. No extra connectedness or global section is needed for this application.

Step 2 cites the same statement for F(V_j) = Hom_X(V_j,Z), and the actual covering clause in def-weil-pbc-proetale-site. The latter says finitely many affine pieces cover each affine open of the target, and therefore the covering is jointly surjective. Taking one point over x, mapping it by the morphism associated to the given section, is a direct few-line argument. This is elementary, not a new existence lemma, and it never assumes a geometric point lifts to an arbitrary pro-etale cover.
