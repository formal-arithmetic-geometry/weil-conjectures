## Statement dispute repaired

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the previous delivered finite-limit replacement does not supply the parent's stated exact module inverse image and countable products. Its rationale confused two coefficient categories. Pure inverse image goes from `Mod(R)` to `Mod(u^{-1}R)` and has the same underlying abelian inverse image. A quotient example instead concerns extension of scalars to a *different* ring. For example on a one-point ringed topos, tensoring `0 -> Z --2--> Z -> Z/2 -> 0` with `Z/2` makes the injection the zero endomorphism of a nonzero group. Thus exactness for an arbitrary structure map is false. This counterexample says nothing against pure inverse image, for which the structure map is the identity. The repaired statement names the coefficient comparison and requires it to be an isomorphism for the tensor pullback assertion.

The source's intended assertion is Stacks Tag 09AA: closed pro-etale inverse image commutes with limits. Open inverse image is slice restriction. These apply on sets, and algebraic structures give the module result. This retains the original intended route; no target or unit statement is weakened.
