## Source error — STATEMENT-DISPUTED

The source is `literature/blueprint/main-20261015/blueprint/sec-six-functors-basics/sk-prop-a1-kummer/lem-pur-support-localization-triangle.md` and its `.proof.md`; I also read its original master path recorded in the node. Both snapshots omit quasi-compactness of the complementary open. The current reviewed `def-pur-flasque-support` and `lem-pur-support-exact` have already repaired the unrestricted pro-etale support identification. The imported proof therefore applies those interfaces outside their hypotheses. Stacks proetale.tex Tag 09AH (`lemma-ses-associated-to-open`) assumes a quasi-compact open immersion, and Tag 09BM (`lemma-closed-immersion-complement-retrocompact-exact`) supplies exact closed direct image with that hypothesis.

This is a genuine false case, not simply a theorem whose available citation is weaker. The explicit example in the reviewed support definition has
`R = product_{n>=1} k`, the finite-support ideal `J`, `X = Spec R`, `Z = Spec(R/J)`, and `U = union_n D(e_n)`. The open complement is not quasi-compact. For nonzero finite commutative coefficients, let `H_n` be the direct image of the rank-one constant sheaf on the clopen coordinate component. The sheaf `F = (product H_n)/(coproduct H_n)` is nonzero: the all-ones tuple over the affine object X has infinite support, whereas sections of the coproduct over an affine object have finite support (the pro-etale covering definition admits a finite affine refinement). Its open restriction vanishes, and its closed inverse image vanishes, since closed inverse image preserves products and each coordinate component misses Z. Thus `G_Z F = F` but `s^! F = 0`. This already disproves the unrestricted kernel identification needed by the specified construction. There cannot even be a derived triangle of the displayed form for this F if the closed first term is interpreted as Rs_* of an object on Z: j^*F=0 would make that first term isomorphic to F. But s^*Rs_*L is isomorphic to L for every bounded below L: resolve L by injectives, use that s_* preserves injectives because its left adjoint s^* is exact, and apply the sheaf counit s^*s_*=id termwise. Applying the exact s^* to the putative isomorphism F=Rs_*L therefore gives L=0, hence F=0, a contradiction. This uses the derived direct image for the arbitrary pair, so it does not assume the exactness of underived s_* that is unavailable without quasi-compactness. The proof in the reviewed support definition gives the full sheaf construction and nonvanishing argument; I read its steps 5–7, rather than inferring a counterexample from the scope of Tag 09AH.

Correction delivered: explicitly require the complementary j to be quasi-compact, specify all finite constant-coefficient pro-etale module sheaves and ordinary D+, and derive the claimed triangle on that domain. This includes the intended finite-type/Noetherian Kummer applications. No constructibility hypothesis is added. This correction is to this supporting node, not to a target declaration or a unit/chapter statement.

A separate typing issue is the original proof's identification with `def-weil-exceptional-inverse-image` by uniqueness. That definition now transports an etale adjoint through etale/pro-etale comparison. An adjoint on etale-origin or constructible objects is not automatically the same as an adjoint on all pro-etale complexes. The delivered statement proves s_* ⊣ Rs^! directly on D+ and makes the exceptional comparison only for a right adjoint to this same s_* on these same categories, preserving its counit. It does not assert that the transported functor has that property for arbitrary pro-etale K. The old citations of `def-weil-open-closed-recollement` and `def-weil-pbc-kinjective-resolutions` likewise stated definitions, not the injective-preservation or comparison theorems the imported proof used; they are replaced by actual existing theorem interfaces.
