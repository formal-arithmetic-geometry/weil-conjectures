For step 1 I opened each cited statement. lem-sc-dedekind-localization-dvr gives D_s a DVR under exactly this item's dimension-one Noetherian normal-domain hypotheses. def-weil-pbc-strict-henselization-limit actually constructs the pointed-neighbourhood colimit, and lem-sc-strict-henselization-dvr says that it is a DVR when the local ring is Noetherian and a DVR. I used the construction at the closed point of Spec(D_s) in both the criterion and the step, rather than silently identifying the ring and scheme strict-henselization encodings. lem-sc-abelian-variety-projective gives the projective embedding needed for the coordinate computation. The local-criterion statement defines boundedness by finite affine generators, and the step provides precisely these: coordinate ratios on the closed affine charts, each with lower valuation bound zero. The smooth finite-type group required by the criterion is A, as in the existing abelian-variety definition. No properness-to-boundedness theorem is taken without proof: the relevant projective case is computed in the step.

For step 2 I compared the two new statements directly. The spreading lemma gives a smooth proper group with connected fibres, exactly the hypothesis of the abelian-scheme mapping lemma, over a nonempty open of Spec D with the correct generic identification. def-weil-proper-morphism explicitly includes separatedness and finite type, so those hypotheses of the patching input are justified and not inferred from a definition of smoothness. Arbitrary smooth test schemes, not only finite-type ones, appear in both mapping statements.

For step 3 I read lem-sc-noetherian-closed-points-finite: it needs a Noetherian scheme and a closed subset whose every point is closed. Spec D is Noetherian by the hypothesis on D, and any prime outside U is nonzero and therefore maximal by dim D=1. Thus every hypothesis is verified; I did not substitute the existing curve-over-an-algebraically-closed-field finite-complement lemma, which would have the wrong base. The patching statement assumes exactly the open model supplied by step 2 and the local models supplied by step 1. It concludes finite type and the complete mapping property, not merely a presheaf gluing. Its overlap-spreading and local mapping-property issues are the substance of that unsplit node, not an uncited assertion in the assembly.

For step 4 I checked lem-weil-pbc-smooth-ring-map-base-change-composition: its two clauses give smoothness of iterated tensor products by base change followed by composition. def-sbc-smooth-morphisms includes the agreement with affine-local smooth ring maps. Hence the finite products used as test schemes are smooth. Extending multiplication, inverse and identity uses the surjectivity obtained in step 3; every axiom is equality of two morphisms from a smooth finite product, so the injectivity there proves each equality. Finally lem-sc-av-commutative says exactly that m composed with swap equals m on the generic fibre. This proves commutativity on the model by the same injectivity, rather than assuming it from the definition of an abelian variety.
