## What needed correction or clarification

The item is true; its statement is preserved. ILO X 2.3's induced diagram must be made semilinearly meaningful when enlarging the acting group. A larger abstract group does not automatically act on the old constant field. Here a common normal K/e receives all finite constants and compatible-pair subgroups act diagonally before induction. In particular no separate Sylow or prime-degree field is chosen for different components.

The marked resolved divisor is generally larger than the inverse image of C_B. Step 4 extracts the latter by the local factorization c = u product(t_i^a_i), rather than silently identifying the two divisors. The one-curve resolution is formulated with an already supplied birational base modification: it states both the resolution of the fibre product and birationality of the composite back to the original curve. Thus Step 3 does not assume that an arbitrary fibre product is integral.

Quotient existence is not taken from lem-sfb-finite-quotient-normal-proper or lem-sfb-finite-quotient-generic-fields: both assume an already supplied quotient. The new quotient theorem applies to locally finite-type separated schemes with admissible action, without tameness or generic freeness. It does not claim projectivity of the quotient. The projective quotient/envelope and tame quotient nodes in the old perverse route are listed in coord/route-pivot-dropped.txt and are not cited.

The invariant full quotient is proved radicial on a dense open, using actual generators of a finite algebra and common p-power equations after one localization. This verifies a finite universal homeomorphism, beyond a generic degree statement. A preliminary source-tag transcription in the scratch draft was corrected to Stacks 03JE, lemma-find-invariant-affine, after checking tags/tags and groupoids.tex.
