For Step 2 the chart-ring definition is used only to identify the localized fraction rings. Clearing denominators gives both maps of this identification. If one t_k is a unit at the base point, the chart relation forces a to be a unit at the chart point, hence also at its contracted base prime. The chart local ring is then exactly A_p. For Step 3 I read the complete statement of the reviewed `lem-trf-permissible-coordinate-chart`. For Step 5 the statement of `lem-trf-regular-local-ufd-boundary-factorization` says both that the regular local ring is a UFD domain and that every listed parameter is prime. I read Stacks 0AG0 (more-algebra.tex, lemma-regular-local-UFD), including its first paragraph deriving primality of a parameter from 00NQ. Step 3 ensures that a and each nonunit factor occupy distinct parameter positions, so their primes are nonassociate. Prime factor cancellation shows a^n h in (a^m P) implies P divides h, and multiplying by a^m proves the converse. This is an elementary finite product argument after the cited theorem. It proves the scheme structure, not merely equality of supports; equality of all stalk ideals proves equality of ideal sheaves.

For Step 6 I checked the effective Cartier and normal-crossings definition statements and the full statement of `lem-trf-nc-coordinate-max-stratum`.
