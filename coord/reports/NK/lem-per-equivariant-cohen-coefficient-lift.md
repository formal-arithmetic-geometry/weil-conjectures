For later workers on the tensor input, the reason the ordinary tensor is complete is as follows (this is explanatory material here, not a split of the new node). A -> W with the same perfect residue is a coefficient subring; W is finite free over A: if p is a unit times pi^e, W/pW has dimension e over k, and successive p-adic approximation gives a finite generating list lifting a basis; torsion-freeness over the DVR A makes it free. Thus W tensor_A B is finite free over the p-adically complete ring B and is p-adically complete. In this tensor p is still a unit times pi^e, so the pi-adic and p-adic topologies agree. Its quotient by pi is L, and pi is a nonzerodivisor by flatness of the torsion-free A-module B. Every nonzero element is a unit times a power of pi, by separatedness and the largest-exponent argument; least exponents then show every nonzero ideal is principal. Flatness of W -> W tensor_A B is base change of A -> B; the nonzero closed fibre supplies locality and faithfulness, or injectivity follows directly from the nonzero powers of pi. This explains why one cannot silently assert completeness of an arbitrary tensor product.
