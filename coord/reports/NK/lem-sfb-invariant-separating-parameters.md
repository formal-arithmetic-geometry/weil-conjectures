I opened and read the statement of every cited node. Here is what each numbered step actually takes from those statements, including the hypotheses checked:

1. `lem-wf-finite-field-action-fixed-degree` supplies finite Galois extensions over fixed fields for faithful finite actions. The two finite images in Aut(k) and Aut(E) are faithful; their fixed fields are exactly k^H and E^H. I do not apply its faithfulness hypothesis to H itself. Compatibility on k ensures k^H is a subfield of E^H. The statement really includes both finiteness and separable algebraicity.
2. `lem-weil-pbc-algebraic-generators-finite-extension` supplies finiteness of a field generated by finitely many algebraic elements. I construct the intermediate coefficient field C inside F=E^H, prove invariance by permutation of orbit factors, and exhibit a monic annihilating polynomial for every generator. E=C(a_i) is checked explicitly. This is a short construction from the stated finite-generation theorem, not an unproved invariant-basis lemma.
3. `def-weil-finite-dimensional-subspace` says finite-dimensional means a finite spanning family. The dimension bound in E stops successive independent choices in its subspace F. A finite C-spanning family generates F as a field, so the coefficients from step 2 together with these vectors are explicit finite field generators over k^H. I do not assume the fixed field is finitely generated merely because the overfield is. This is the elementary finite-dimensional-subspace argument, written out in the step.
4. `lem-trf-geometric-reducedness-separable-scalar-restriction` says reducedness after every field base extension is equivalent across a separable algebraic extension of base fields. Step 1 supplies precisely k/k^H separable algebraic. The hypothesis supplies domains after k-base change and hence reduced rings. The node applies to an arbitrary algebra E, without needing E finite as a k-algebra. I declare it as a generic tool from another unit.
5. `lem-trf-flat-tensor-preserves-injection` supplies injectivity after tensoring an injective linear map by a flat module. Here the map is F into E and the flat module is an arbitrary extension field L of k^H. Every vector space over a field is free, and `Module.Flat.of_free` is the relevant inferred instance (Mathlib/RingTheory/Flat/Basic.lean:231); I read the generic injection declaration there at line 131. Tensor flip gives the indicated ring map. A nilpotent in its source maps to zero in the reduced target, so reducedness descends by injectivity. No geometric-integrality descent is silently assumed.
6. `lem-kun-transcendence-degree-tower` gives both the tower equality and zero transcendence degree of algebraic extensions. Apply it to the two actual towers inside E, with algebraicity supplied by step 1. `def-kun-transcendence-degree` explicitly says that the size of any transcendence basis is that degree. Thus the basis will have exactly d elements, not an unspecified number.
7. `lem-weil-pbc-geometrically-reduced-extension-separable` is explicitly a positive-characteristic statement; I use it only when the characteristic is a prime p. Its conclusion concerns finitely generated intermediate fields, and step 3 permits taking the whole field F. In characteristic zero I use `lem-weil-pbc-perfect-field-separating-basis`, whose statement explicitly includes that characteristic. `def-weil-pbc-transcendence-basis` identifies the generated parameter subfield and independence. Both branches therefore yield precisely the separating basis in F used below. The positive-characteristic criterion is declared as a generic tool from another unit.
8. `lem-zl-separable-permanence` is separable algebraic transitivity, not transitivity of arbitrary nonalgebraic separability. Both extensions here are separable algebraic. The finite algebraic-generators node then gives finite dimension over B=k^H(t): the original generators also generate E over B and have already been proved algebraic over B.
9. `lem-zl-independence-algebraic-base` really states independence equivalence for an algebraic base extension in an ambient field. k/k^H is algebraic and E contains the full tower, so this applies to the chosen family. For finite separability over D=k(t), I write out the finite spanning argument and the polynomial Bezout identity. `def-weil-pbc-separable-algebraic-extension` supplies the minimal-polynomial divisibility and coprimality meanings used here. The identity 1=Q(UR+VR')+Q'VR proves coprimality over D directly. This final elementary calculation avoids an uncited separability transport theorem.
