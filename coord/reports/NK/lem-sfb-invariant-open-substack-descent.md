## What needed correction

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported wording said only “full substack”. For a literal bijection of subcategories this must be “strictly full substack (closed under isomorphism)”, as required by Stacks Tag 04YM and used explicitly in the proof of Tag 0507. I made exactly that clarification, retaining every hypothesis and conclusion.

Explicit counterexample to a non-replete reading: let an algebraic stack equivalent to the terminal stack over a nonempty scheme S have, over each T, two isomorphic copies of its unique object, with exactly one arrow between any two copies. Its full substack consisting only of the first copy and its whole category both have scheme tests equivalent to all of T, and both pull back to all of the atlas P=S. They are different full subcategories. Strict fullness excludes the first as a non-replete subcategory. This is an ambiguity in the imported wording, not an error in the Stacks statement.

The original item was statement-only. Its proof is now explicit. In particular, neither “the open descends” nor “factorization is local” is left as a black box. Tag 0507 proves the open case using smooth groupoid quotients; I instead wrote a direct scheme-test proof from the existing general openness and algebraic-space nodes.
