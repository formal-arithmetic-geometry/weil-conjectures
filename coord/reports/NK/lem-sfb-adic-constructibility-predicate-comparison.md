## Proof in words

Cartesian coefficient pullback identifies the rings on every stratum and on its covers. The hypotheses make X qcqs. They also give a Noetherian underlying topological space, and the descending-closed-chain calculation in step 2 gives Noetherian topology on every stratum.

A finite constant scalar model gives a coefficient-sheaf presentation by the same scalar matrix. Thus the finite-constant predicate always implies the stratified local-presentation predicate here. For integral coefficients the existing integral comparison gives the other implication, allowing torsion scalar modules and a changed stratification. For finite rational coefficients, restrict a witnessing local-presentation stratification to one stratum and apply the existing finite-field local-triviality theorem there. Its local models are finite free; taking the scalar free module of the same rank gives precisely the required finite constant model. Do the same for the finite-subextension-valued algebraic-closure ring using the existing qbar theorem. Finally, the two bounded derived object properties impose the same vanishing interval and the two now-equivalent predicates on each cohomology module. This identifies them in the same ordinary ambient derived category.
