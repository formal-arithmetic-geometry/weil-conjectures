## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the sentence “Strong monoidal left Quillen functors induce coherent strong monoidal derived functors” is false without a derived-unit hypothesis when the source unit is not cofibrant.

Here is an explicit counterexample within the imported scope. On the category of unbounded cochain complexes of abelian groups take the model structure M with every map a weak equivalence and a fibration, and only isomorphisms cofibrations. Both weak factorization systems are `(isomorphisms, all maps)` and are generated by the empty set; factor a map as its source identity followed by itself. The category is locally presentable, is enriched/tensored/cotensored over complexes, and satisfies the stated dg compatibility axiom because a cofibration is an isomorphism. Its localization is zero, hence stable. The usual closed tensor is a monoidal model structure: every pushout product of cofibrations is an isomorphism, the only cofibrant object is zero, and the unit axiom for `0 -> Z[0]` holds on that only cofibrant input. Take N to be complexes with their usual projective dg model structure. The identity adjunction M to N has a strong symmetric monoidal enriched left adjoint preserving both sorts of cofibrations. Its left derived functor is nevertheless the zero functor from the zero localization to D(Z). It sends the source derived unit to zero, which is not the target derived unit Z[0] (`H^0` distinguishes them). Thus its derived functor is not strong unital monoidal.

I also made the conventional completeness/cocompleteness of a model category explicit (already implied by local presentability), and spelled out that the already-homotopical functors under discussion are enriched, as required to induce the specified chain-enriched enhancements. Neither clarification restricts the intended enriched combinatorial model setting.

The correction requires the actual map `F(Q1_M) -> F(1_M) = 1_N` to be a weak equivalence, and writes the usual monoidal model unit axiom explicitly. It changes no conclusion under that necessary hypothesis. It does not demand cofibrancy of every unit: the proof treats noncofibrant units. Cofibrancy of the source unit, or preservation of all weak equivalences by F, is given as a sufficient condition and proved in the item. This matches the distinction in [Shipley, *Monoidal Uniqueness of Stable Homotopy Theory*, Definitions 2.1–2.3](https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~bshipley/ideal.pdf), inspected online; that document is also absent from local literature and no copy was downloaded.
