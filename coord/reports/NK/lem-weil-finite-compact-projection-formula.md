## Problems found and changes

The item's statement agrees mathematically with `literature/blueprint/main-20261015/blueprint/sec-six-functors-basics/sk-thm-sf-pf-finite/lem-weil-finite-compact-projection-formula.md`. I added precise notion citations and an explicit description of the projection composite; I changed no hypotheses or conclusion. In particular the schemes need not be separated over the field: only the morphism is separated. There is no Tor-amplitude assumption on K and no finite-type assumption on the geometric-point base change. There is no statement dispute.

I removed the earlier attempt's malformed JSON draft `lem-weil-pbc-site-comparison-monoidal`. It duplicated existing ringed tensor, pullback-comparison and canonical-mate tools, while bundling extra unit/counit/projection compatibility without an argument. Its unrestricted D+ formulation also failed to specify the domain of tensor products: over A=Z/4, two degree-zero Z/2 modules have derived tensor with nonzero Tor in arbitrarily negative cohomological degrees, so D+ is not closed under this operation. The item avoids that problem by its finite-Tor hypothesis on L and the bounded flat model. The discarded draft is not a new node in this delivery; the imported item's mathematical statement needs no correction.

Several imported citations no longer say what the proof needs. `def-compact-support-direct-image-detailed`, `def-exchange-projection`, and `def-weil-derived-pullback-tensor-hom` cannot just be used as definitions of the etale operations: the relevant current versions use pro-etale coefficient categories. I replaced those etale uses by `def-amb-etale-compact-support`, `def-kun-derived-tensor`, `def-kun-projection-formula-map`, and the exact constant-coefficient etale pullback statement. The pro-etale definition `def-exchange-projection` is used only for the final pro-etale map.

The imported proof suppressed the existence/hypotheses of the compactification, boundedness before both compact-base-change applications, the actual exchange/projection-map compatibility, exactness and connecting-arrow compatibility in the finite-free devissage, and the normalization needed to compare projection maps across sites. These are now arguments with the appropriate existing statements, rather than stronger properties attributed to definition or full-faithfulness nodes. In particular site-comparison full faithfulness alone is not used to assert tensor compatibility or proper-map compatibility. The bounded flat model proves the needed tensor boundedness without requiring K to be flat. Independence of compact-support functors is not silently taken to assert equality of their projection transformations; invertibility is proved for an arbitrary chosen compactification.
