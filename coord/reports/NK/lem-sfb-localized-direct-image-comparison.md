Thus proving invertibility of that specified mate in sectionwise modules proves the original assertion; no change of coefficient category or completion is involved.

Original step 2: For $Z=X,Y$, [det-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-x-invert-ell] gives the actual coefficient map $A_Z\to E_Z$ and identifies $E_Z$ with $A_Z[1/\ell]$, with $n$th coefficient leg multiplication by $\ell^{-n}$. Put $L_Z=E_Z\otimes^L_{A_Z}-$, $U_Z=\operatorname{Res}_{A_Z\to E_Z}$, and denote the scalar unit by $\eta$. Localization extension is exact by [lem-weil-pbc-scalar-localization-sheaf-exact], and [lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-functor] identifies $L_Z$ with termwise ordinary extension on every complex. By [sk-prop-sfb-cartesian-coefficients], the two coefficient systems have compatible inverse-image ring isomorphisms along $f$. Their compatibility with $A\to E$ follows from functorial pullback of this one map. Consequently [lem-weil-pbc-ring-sheaf-change-adjunction] supplies exact conservative restrictions and the adjunction-compatible isomorphism $d:U_YRf_{*,E}\xrightarrow{\sim}Rf_{*,A}U_X$.

Original step 3: Choose a complex representing $C$ and $a\in\mathbf Z$ with $H^r(C)=0$ for $r<a$. Replace the representative by its good truncation $K=\tau_{\geq a}K$: it is zero below $a$, has $\operatorname{coker}(d^{a-1})$ in degree $a$, and the replacement is a quasi-isomorphism since exactly the already-zero cohomology below $a$ is removed. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-scalar-localization-complex-colimit] with the integer scalar $\ell$. Its sequence $K\xrightarrow{\ell}K\xrightarrow{\ell}\cdots$ has termwise colimit $U_XL_XK$ and its $n$th leg represents $u_n=\ell^{-n}\eta_C:C\to U_XL_XC$. The exactness inputs are those of step 2. This is a small sequential diagram with a common termwise lower bound $a$.

Original step 4: Apply [lem-weil-pbc-condensed-qcqs-pushforward-continuity] to the sequence of step 3, with condensed ring $\underline A$. Its hypotheses hold: $f$ is qcqs, the index is the small filtered category $\mathbf N$, and every term vanishes below $a$. The good truncation in its statement leaves this representative unchanged (up to the canonical identity comparison). For each $q\in\mathbf Z$ it gives the isomorphism $v_q:\varinjlim_nH^q(Rf_{*,A}C)\xrightarrow{\sim}H^q(Rf_{*,A}U_XL_XC)$ with legs $H^q(Rf_{*,A}u_n)$. Put $M=Rf_{*,A}C$. Exactness of localization and restriction identifies $H^q(U_YL_YM)$ with $U_YL_YH^q(M)$: an exact termwise functor preserves the kernels and cokernels defining cohomology. By [lem-weil-pbc-scalar-localization-module-colimit] this latter object is the colimit of $H^q(M)\xrightarrow{\ell}H^q(M)\to\cdots$, with legs $H^q(\ell^{-n}\eta_M)$. Denote the resulting isomorphism by $w_q:\varinjlim_nH^q(M)\xrightarrow{\sim}H^q(U_YL_YM)$.

Original step 5: Let $\beta:L_YM\to Rf_{*,E}L_XC$ be precisely the morphism in the statement and set $t=d_{L_XC}\circ U_Y\beta$. We check $t\circ\eta_M=Rf_{*,A}(\eta_C)$. Under the $A$-coefficient pullback/direct-image adjunction, the right-hand side transposes to $\eta_C\circ\epsilon_C:Lf_A^*M\to U_XL_XC$, where $\epsilon$ is its counit. For the left-hand side, adjunction compatibility in [lem-weil-pbc-ring-sheaf-change-adjunction] permits transposing $U_Y\beta$ before restriction. By the definition of $\beta$, this gives $U_XL_X(\epsilon_C)$ after the scalar-unit map $Lf_A^*M\to U_XL_XLf_A^*M$. The canonical pullback/tensor identification [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-derived-tensor-pullback] carries that unit to $\eta_{Lf_A^*M}$: on K-flat tensor representatives both maps insert $1$ in the $E$ factor. Naturality of $\eta$ makes this transpose $\eta_C\circ\epsilon_C$. The two adjuncts therefore agree. The scalar units and the chosen tensor map have the sectionwise normalization fixed in steps 1–2.

Original step 6: Naturality of $d$ in the $E_X$-module argument, and $E_Y$-linearity of $\beta$, show that $t$ intertwines inverse multiplication by $\ell$ on $U_YL_YM$ with $Rf_{*,A}$ of inverse multiplication by $\ell$ on $U_XL_XC$. Hence step 5 gives $t\circ(\ell^{-n}\eta_M)=Rf_{*,A}(\ell^{-n}\eta_C)=Rf_{*,A}(u_n)$ for every $n$. Taking $H^q$ shows that $H^q(t)\circ w_q$ and $v_q$ agree on every colimit leg, so they are equal by the colimit universal property. Thus $H^q(t)=v_q\circ w_q^{-1}$ is an isomorphism for every $q$. This is only the equality of the normalized legs, using the naturality of the previously specified comparison.

Original step 7: By [lem-weil-pbc-unbounded-derived-iso-cohomology], $t$ is an isomorphism. Since $d$ is an isomorphism, $U_Y\beta$ is one as well, and conservativity of $U_Y$ from [lem-weil-pbc-ring-sheaf-change-adjunction] implies that $\beta$ is an isomorphism. Finally the equivalences and normalized adjunction comparisons of step 1 give exactly the original ambient-module morphism. The proof used only bounded-below continuity and scalar localization, so it imposes no completeness, constructibility or finite Tor-amplitude hypothesis.
