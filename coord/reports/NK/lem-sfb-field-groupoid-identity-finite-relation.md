The tensor quotient is nonzero because it has its diagonal, so it has one point. def-weil-unramified-morphism supplies an open diagonal and def-weil-open-immersion says a surjective open immersion is an isomorphism. The proof then spells out how the diagonal isomorphism gives injectivity on all test-scheme Hom sets. No generic monomorphism theorem or unjustified universal-injectivity assertion is hidden here.

It uses the separated-scheme assertion of the new structure node for the absolute diagonal of R and writes the relative diagonal as its pullback. The last sentence derives each equivalence-relation axiom from the restricted groupoid maps, rather than treating geometric-point injectivity as sufficient.
