## Proof ideas for the new unsplit statements

These notes are not proofs hidden inside their statement-only node files. For nerve contractibility, a finite simplicial subset of the ordinary nerve contains only finitely many relevant objects and arrows. Choose a common predecessor of its vertices and successively equalize the finitely many arrow-compatibility equations. This gives a cone on that finite simplicial shape. Finite boundary tests in a Kan replacement commuting with filtered unions then show weak contractibility; the successor/coequalizer version is dual. The finiteness is of the prescribed simplices, not of the subcategory they generate, which may have infinitely many composites.

For localization the general simplicial theorem is essential: replacing K by its ordinary fundamental groupoid is invalid for arbitrary K. The universal property is taken on full enhanced functor categories. A proof via cores alone must be upgraded for all parameter categories, as in the verified Kerodon Proposition 6.3.1.13; an equivalence only on cores is insufficient.

The calculus node isolates standard, non-elementary foundational facts. Its cone mapping-space formula says precisely that a cone morphism is an apex map together with its compatibility homotopy, retaining higher homotopies. The item uses the homotopy fiber of an equivalence, hence a contractible space, rather than an underived equalizer of morphisms.
