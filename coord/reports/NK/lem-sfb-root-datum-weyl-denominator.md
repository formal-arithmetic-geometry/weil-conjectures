Step 1 spells out the entire change to real coordinates and the invariant form: integer-basis injection, preservation of rank and reducedness, a finite sum of coordinate dot products, and the coroot identity obtained by substitution. These are elementary finite-dimensional calculations, not an invocation of an unrecorded realization theorem. The form is positive because its identity summand is positive. The empty root case is resolved before any positive-rank argument.

Step 2 proves the simple-reflection permutation by the root's other simple coordinates, using reducedness precisely to rule out a positive multiple of the simple root. It then sums the roots to get the rho pairing 1, and proves positivity by the same simple-coordinate expansion. It assumes no group-scheme realization and makes no claim that rho is always integral: its integrality is the item's explicit hypothesis.

Step 3 cites only the statement of lem-sfb-simple-root-reflection-generation, which I opened: it asserts generation by simple reflections for a finite spanning reduced real crystallographic root system with the specified base and positive definite form. Step 1 verifies every one of these hypotheses, and the perfect integral pairing supplies crystallographic integrality. The actual action on the product is displayed, and determinants multiply along the word. No Coxeter presentation or sign-of-length formula is silently assumed.

Step 4 compares integer coefficients to kill every reflection-fixed weight; torsion-freeness here is the elementary implication 2c=0 implies c=0 in Z. Finite expansion supplies subset support, not a claim of uniqueness of subset sums.

Step 5 cites lem-sfb-root-chamber-simple-transitivity, whose statement I opened in full. It explicitly identifies chambers as the components of the root-hyperplane complement and asserts transitivity on them. Regularity from step 4 permits its application, and step 1 supplies its real inner-product hypotheses. The support comparison is written out: a dominant integral weight has each simple coroot pairing at least 1, so eta=mu-rho pairs nonnegatively with each simple root; eta is also the negative sum of a subset of positive roots. Pairing this equality with eta forces its squared norm to be nonpositive, hence eta=0. There is no unstated inverse-Cartan positivity lemma or hidden assertion that an arbitrary real dominant weight is at least rho.

Step 6 uses exactly the freeness clause of that same chamber node: an element fixing the regular rho fixes its chamber and hence is the identity. This proves that coefficients indexed by W are not counted repeatedly. The coefficient at rho is normalized by the total simple-coordinate sum, which is strictly positive on every positive root. The expansion therefore equals A(rho) coefficient by coefficient.
