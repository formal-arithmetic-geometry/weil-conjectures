## Statement error: STATEMENT-DISPUTED

The imported demand that each base-point stabilizer act trivially on the fibre dual graph *including marked legs* is false. Take an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, X=P^1, H=C2 acting by x -> -x, and Z={1,-1}. This is a normal proper geometrically integral example satisfying every input hypothesis. Every finite extension F of k equals k, so the sole base point of the length-one tower is fixed by all of J. A trivial action on the marked legs means that every marked section is fixed by J. Its image in X must then be fixed by H, by equivariance and the surjection J -> H. But surjectivity of a and a^{-1}Z contained in the section union require marked points over both 1 and -1, neither of which is fixed by H. This is a contradiction independent of ramification or degree.

The correction replaces the imported entire split/trivial-stabilizer description by de Jong's quasi-split condition: nodes and their two normalization branches are rational over the residue field. No stabilizer-triviality assertion is retained. The source explicitly permits sections to be permuted (Situation 5.8, p.616). All other hypotheses and conclusions, especially F^N/k purely inseparable and the geometric N-orbit condition on a dense open, are preserved. No coefficient category or locked Lean target is changed.

Upward report: `lem-sfb-equivariant-nodal-smooth-pair-resolution` currently assumes the same stronger marked-graph condition, and its parent `lem-sfb-equivariant-smooth-galois-model` uses this item to supply that hypothesis. The corrected output here does not supply it. The resolution statement and that parent need independent review/correction to the source's equivariant quasi-split formulation.
