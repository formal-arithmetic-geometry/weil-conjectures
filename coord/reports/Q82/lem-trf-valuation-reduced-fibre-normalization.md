## Review by Q82

I read Temkin’s entire valuation argument in Theorem 3.5.5 Steps 2–5, the finite-height union corollary and the eta-normality proposition, then reopened each cited DAG statement. The source is absent locally, so proof_origin.by is agent. I checked delivery-errors B–E against the following concrete arguments, including exact coefficient, generic-fibre and geometric-fibre hypotheses.

1. Flatness embeds A in A_K by tensoring multiplication injections. The torsion-free-flat node applies to every intermediate algebra, not just local algebras. The finite-type module presentation node takes a finite module over any finite-type V-algebra; its hypotheses hold for the cyclic module over a polynomial algebra and for a finite intermediate module over A. I wrote the finite kernel calculation from the finite free presentation, rather than invoking an uncited change-of-presentation theorem. Zero algebras and V=K are dealt with here.

2. The localization node actually identifies the localized relative integral closure in the localized ambient algebra; its multiplicative set is V minus the fibre prime, mapped into A. The first clause of the geometric-components node actually says every residue field is separably closed, without a height restriction. It does not claim algebraic closedness, so I prove perfection explicitly by the perturbed polynomial in equal characteristic and by algebraic closedness of characteristic-zero K in mixed characteristic. The valuation test for monic roots is written out.

3. The divisible-value-group node applies to V localized at any prime, whose fraction field is still K. A finite sum witnessing a^n in mE supplies a single nonzero scalar divisor by valuation comparability. Transitivity of integrality, not merely its definition, justifies a/y belonging to the relative closure. The algebraic-closure test is used only after the residue field was proved algebraically closed. The two affine comparison nodes identify the scheme fibre with the tensor ring and reducedness with no nonzero nilpotents. This proves all fibres, not only the closed fibre in finite height.

4. The formal comparison gives the required algebraically closed complete rank-one field and reduced affinoid algebra from the geometrically reduced generic fibre. The analytic finiteness node explicitly assumes a bounded topologically finitely generated model contained in C^circ, as Temkin does. Its conclusion is module finiteness; the finite-algebra-integral node turns that into integrality. Conversely a monic recurrence over the bounded model gives bounded powers, so I did not assume power-bounded means integral by definition.

5. Each analytic generator is approximated modulo its scalar denominator. Approximation of the finitely many monic coefficients preserves the cleared-denominator membership, and the scalar saturation clause promotes that membership to A. The resulting honest monic equations imply finite generation of D by bounded-exponent monomials; step 1 supplies precisely the flatness and both presentation hypotheses needed for the next completion comparison.

6. The new formal comparison states the tensor-completion isomorphism and the injection of the completed finite intermediate algebra into C. Thus its image is exactly the algebra generated by the approximated elements, C^circ. Writing b=a/s and applying saturation for D, rather than assuming injectivity of A -> its completion, gives B=D. This also covers A=V[1/pi], for which the completion is zero.

7. Finite-height induction chooses the minimal nonzero prime, with localization height one and residue valuation height one less. Localization of integral closure yields B_T, and a finite list of cleared denominator generators creates a finite intermediate A prime. The residue field L is separably closed by the actual residue clause. Transitivity, with A prime integral over A, keeps its relative integral closure equal to B.

8. The residue-comparison helper has the precise extra hypothesis A prime_T is relatively integrally closed; step 7 establishes it. It yields both equality of the p ideals and identification of the quotient closure, not just a definition. The quotient of A prime is flat by the displayed tensor-injection argument and finitely presented by reducing a polynomial presentation. Its generic fibre is geometrically reduced by steps 2–3, so the induction hypothesis applies. Lifting module generators and including 1 spans B because pB=pA prime; finite generation over A follows by multiplying finite spanning lists. No value-group lemma was falsely substituted for this residue comparison.

9. The finite-height descent helper constructs an actual V0 -> V model, allowing a smaller separably closed fraction field. I checked its intent against Corollary 2.1.3: finitely many presentation coefficients lie in a finitely generated subfield; take its relative separable closure inside K, whose valuation height remains finite by Abhyankar and algebraic-height invariance. V0 -> V is local torsion-free hence faithfully flat; flatness and geometric generic reducedness descend. The step applies the proved finite-height case, tensors a finite spanning list and presentation, and proves flatness by the tensor criterion. The base-change fibre-locus node says exactly that geometric reducedness pulls back for arbitrary morphisms; no surjectivity is smuggled in. Finite-algebra integrality gives D subset B. Integral closure is not asserted to commute with arbitrary base change.

10. The eta-normality node is an existing independent theorem, not a descendant of this item (I traversed its dependencies to check). Its base is normal integral qcqs, and the constructed D is flat finitely presented with every geometric fibre reduced. Flat affine equivalence and the presentation definition supply the scheme hypotheses. For b integral over A, D[b] is finite over D by its same monic polynomial; the finite morphism definition and finite-proper theorem apply. Its generic fibre and its canonical D(s) restriction are the actual induced isomorphisms. Torsion-free localization gives schematic density on both schemes. Thus it is an eta-modification of exactly the kind the node says is an isomorphism. This proves D=B instead of hiding a normalization/base-change comparison.

11. Finite module generation, the step-1 presentation argument, localization sandwich and the already proved fibres give every clause of the item. This final step is elementary bookkeeping and invokes no fresh lemma.

For each new helper I checked separate hypotheses and small cases: analytic C=0 and the model V in K; formal A=V, A=V[X], and A=V[1/pi] (completion maps can fail to inject); residue comparison p maximal, zero A, and already relatively closed A; input descent A=0, A=V[X], height zero and nontrivial varying fraction fields.
