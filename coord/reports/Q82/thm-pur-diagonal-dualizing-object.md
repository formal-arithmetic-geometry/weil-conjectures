### Returned Step 5

Here is the explicit reduction to sheaf maps on the possibly nonseparated scheme $X$. Put $K=\omega_f$, $r=-2d$, $M=\mathcal H^r(K)$ and $G=\Lambda_X(d)$, using [def-weil-derived-cohomology-sheaves] and [def-coefficient-twists-detailed]. By [lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization](1)--(3), sheaves on each open are exactly sheaves on the localized site with the restricted constant coefficient ring. Exact restriction [lem-pur-site-restriction-module-adjunction-exact] commutes with kernels, images and quotients, hence with cohomology. The local orientations of Step 3 therefore give $\mathcal H^q(K)|_{X_i}=0$ for $q\ne r$. A sheaf which restricts to zero on a covering family is zero: restrict any section to the induced cover of its domain and apply uniqueness in the sheaf axiom. Thus $\mathcal H^q(K)=0$ for $q\ne r$ globally. For a representative $P$ of $K$, the good truncation of [def-kun-truncations] gives the explicit roof $P\xleftarrow{\sim}\tau_{\le r}P\xrightarrow{\sim}M[-r]$. The left arrow induces an identity on cohomology in degrees at most $r$, and the groups above $r$ vanish. The right arrow is the quotient $\ker d^r\to\ker d^r/\operatorname{im}d^{r-1}$ in degree $r$ and zero in lower degrees; it induces the identity in degree $r$, and the other cohomology groups vanish. Both arrows are therefore quasi-isomorphisms. Inverting the left arrow in [def-weil-abstract-derived-category] gives the canonical isomorphism $\epsilon:K\xrightarrow{\sim}M[2d]$. Both truncation and cohomology preserve quasi-isomorphisms by their displayed cohomology computations, so the natural roof descends through the localization universal property and is independent of the representative. The roof is natural in $P$; exact restriction preserves its kernels and quotient, so it gives the same isomorphism on every open. This calculation uses no absolute separatedness and does not apply the separated-scheme concentration lemma to $X$.
