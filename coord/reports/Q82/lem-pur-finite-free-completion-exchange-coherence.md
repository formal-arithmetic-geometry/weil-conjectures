## Mathematical argument for the supporting statements

For the ideal-quotient tower lemma, form extension of the quotient presentation R←I_n by sheafified tensor. Its cokernel is S/J_n: the image ideal consists locally of finite sums a(i)s, and tensoring the quotient presentation is right exact. Equivalently, construct its mutually inverse balanced maps on local generators: (R/I_n)⊗_R S→S/J_n sends rbar⊗s to a(r)s modulo J_n, and the inverse sends sbar to 1bar⊗s. Their well-definedness uses exactly the locally generated ideal, rather than a false assertion of objectwise surjectivity of a quotient sheaf. Apply tensor reassociation with P to obtain β_a, and do the same for b and ba. Both iterated maps on ((p⊗rbar)⊗s)⊗q have image (p⊗b(s)q)⊗overline{ba(r)}. The inverse constructions, naturality in every differential and reduction of ideal representatives give actual isomorphisms of complexes of whole towers. Quotient units are the r=1 case. This construction requires arbitrary ideal-sheaf presentations and sheafification, not only an equality of ordinary ring tensors.

For horizontal Roos coherence, first construct c_F using all product projections and show it commutes with both differentials and the shift endomorphism s. The additive biproduct comparison followed by the two c_F maps is consequently a cochain map for the signed differential (-dy-(1-s)x,dx). This uses preservation of addition and negatives, not exactness. Project the proposed horizontal equation to a tower factor in each of its two product blocks. Naturality of α identifies either route with α_{X_n}GF(π_n); functoriality gives the same equations in both degrees. The canonical biproduct comparison is essential here: it rules out an arbitrary completion of the square to a cone map. Products and biproducts determine equality of the complete cochain maps, including the shifted coordinate. If c_F is invertible, these same coordinates make κ_F invertible; the inverse chain map is provided by inverse block maps. The identity and augmentation formulas follow from the same coordinates. This lemma isolates the general construction and horizontal law, whereas the existing additive Roos node supplies arithmetic compatibility for one product-preserving functor.
