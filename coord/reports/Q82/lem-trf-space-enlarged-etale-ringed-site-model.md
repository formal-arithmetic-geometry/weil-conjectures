I opened spaces-properties.tex at definition-etale, Tag 03FR (around lines 2093–2110), as well as the imported definition def-trf-algebraic-space. The latter defines etale for a representable sheaf map by testing every scheme mapping into the TARGET, and the original model did not override that convention. Stacks defines etale for an arbitrary map X -> Y of spaces by testing every representable etale scheme chart U -> X: the composite U -> Y must be representable etale. These are different predicates with different quantifiers. The paragraph after 03FR explicitly compares them when the map is representable; it does not require representability in its general definition. Thus using the former predicate for every arrow does not give the category of ALL etale algebraic spaces in 03G0.
