## Retained fibre corollaries

The following is the retained Q45 proof, including steps 2-4 accepted as correct specializations by the return review. It is preserved as a corollary record, not another live node duplicating the survivor.

1. Apply [lem-sfb-flat-space-smooth-locus] with its base scheme $T$ equal to the present $S$ and its morphism equal to $f$. Its flatness and local finite presentation hypotheses are precisely the hypotheses here, with the same etale-chart convention. Set $U_f=W$, where $W$ is its largest open subspace with smooth restriction. Largestness means that any open subspace on which $f$ is smooth is contained in $W$, so this is also the required largest open here. For a given $g:T\to S$, substitute the present $T$ for its $T'$ and $g$ for its $h$. Its equality of smooth loci gives exactly the inverse image of $U_f$. This assertion quantifies over every scheme morphism $h$ and imposes no flatness condition on $g$.

2. For $s\in S$, specialize the same arbitrary-base-change assertion of [lem-sfb-flat-space-smooth-locus] to $\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)\to S$. By the fibre convention in the statement its base-changed morphism is $X_s\to\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)$, and its inverse-image open is $(U_f)_s$. This proves exactly the stated fibrewise equality.

3. Let $k=\kappa(s)$, let $K/k$ be any field extension, and write $a:\operatorname{Spec}K\to\operatorname{Spec}k$ for its map. Apply [lem-sfb-flat-space-smooth-locus] to the original $f$ and $\operatorname{Spec}K\to S$: its smooth locus is $U_f\times_S\operatorname{Spec}K$. To compare with the iterated fibre, put $A=(X\times_S\operatorname{Spec}k)\times_{\operatorname{Spec}k}\operatorname{Spec}K$ and $B=X\times_S\operatorname{Spec}K$. The universal property of [lem-trf-space-fibre-products-etale-covers] gives $A\to B$ from the projections to $X$ and $\operatorname{Spec}K$. Conversely, the projections $B\to X$ and $B\to\operatorname{Spec}K\xrightarrow{a}\operatorname{Spec}k$ give $B\to X\times_S\operatorname{Spec}k$; together with $B\to\operatorname{Spec}K$ this gives $B\to A$. The composites preserve both projections at each fibre-product stage, so uniqueness makes them identities. Apply the same construction with $U_f$ in place of $X$. Its maps commute with the inclusions into $A$ and $B$, again by their projections. More explicitly, for every test scheme $Z$ over $S$, a map $Z\to A$ factors through the iterated pullback of $U_f$ exactly when its projection $Z\to X$ factors through $U_f$; the same condition characterizes maps $Z\to B$ factoring through $U_f\times_S\operatorname{Spec}K$. Thus the inverse maps identify these open subfunctors, not only their underlying point sets. Hence the smooth open is precisely $(U_f)_s\times_kK$ under this identification. No separability or algebraicity of $K/k$ is assumed.

4. If a fibre in steps 2 or 3 is empty, an open subspace $V$ of that fibre is empty: use the identity map from the empty scheme to the empty algebraic space as the scheme test in [def-a1-space-open-subspace]. The pullback is $V$ itself and is represented by an open subscheme of the empty scheme. Its underlying open subset must be empty, so it is the empty scheme. In particular the smooth open and the pullback of $U_f$ agree in this case as well. The preceding base-change equalities never required a point of the fibre.
