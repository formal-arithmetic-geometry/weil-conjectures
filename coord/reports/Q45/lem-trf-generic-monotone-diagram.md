- Zero stalk/Taylor ideal: the diagram is empty, s=0, the regular ideal is zero after shrinking, and the formal generation sum is empty. The prefix division family consists solely of remainder monomials and gives the identity remainder.
- n=0: there is still one monomial (the constant), but a point of X forces the proper stalk ideal in the zero-dimensional smooth local ring to be zero. The vertex list is empty; this case is not excluded by a positive-dimension or nonempty-variable assumption.
- Empty degree prefix and homogeneous degree t=0: both are included by the two natural-number quantifiers. A vertex whose degree exceeds t contributes no quotient index. Zero homogeneous input has the unique zero coefficient family.
- Nonclosed a: the coefficient field is the residue field, and all n relative chart variables are retained. No identification of the original stalk's completion with an n-variable ring is assumed; the centered diagonal Taylor map is the existing one. The pointed etale chart explicitly preserves the original residue field.
