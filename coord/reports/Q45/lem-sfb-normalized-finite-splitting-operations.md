## Supporting lemma argument for the next worker

Read Stacks sites-modules.tex Tag 03CZ (`lemma-adjointness-tensor-restrict`), including its sectionwise scalar adjunction and sheafification proof. For the geometric comparison, a morphism out of either side is equivalently a separately additive map out of the inverse images of the coefficient and module sheaves satisfying the same balancing and S-linearity diagrams. Transpose those maps by the geometric adjunction; finite-product preservation transports all these diagrams, giving inverse hom-set bijections.
