## Original proof retained for the supporting tasks

### Original step 1

Let $P$ denote exactly the quasi-affine-open condition in the statement. By [lem-a1-ind-quasi-affine-permanence], $P$ is stable under arbitrary base change and can be tested on an affine open cover of the target. It is preserved by finite disjoint unions: cover the disjoint target by affine opens lying in single components; on such an open its inverse image is the inverse image for that component, so it has $P$. The coproducts and their disjoint open components exist by [lem-weil-pbc-scheme-coproduct-family]; the empty coproduct case is vacuous. Thus [lem-galois-fpqc-descending-types-morphisms] reduces the assertion to proving effectivity for a flat surjective morphism $p:X\to S$ of affines and a $P$-morphism $V\to X$. We prove that case in the following steps; this is a reduction, not an induction or an invocation of the result being proved.

Uses: lem-a1-ind-quasi-affine-permanence, lem-weil-pbc-scheme-coproduct-family, lem-galois-fpqc-descending-types-morphisms.

### Original step 2

Write $S=\operatorname{Spec}R$ and $X=\operatorname{Spec}A$. By [lem-a1-ind-quasi-affine-permanence](3), $V$ is ind-quasi-affine as a scheme and is quasi-separated. The cover $p$ is fpqc: it is affine by [lem-weil-pbc-morphism-affine-schemes-affine], so the inverse image of each affine open $H\subset S$ is affine; that single affine open has image $H$ by surjectivity, which verifies [def-weil-pbc-fpqc-covering]. Fix $v\in V$. Apply [lem-a1-fpqc-invariant-intersection-neighbourhoods] to obtain $v\in W\subset E\subset V$, with $W$ open, $E$ an intersection of a nonempty family of quasi-compact opens, and both invariant under the datum. We do not assume $E$ open, or give it a scheme structure.

Uses: lem-a1-ind-quasi-affine-permanence, lem-weil-pbc-morphism-affine-schemes-affine, def-weil-pbc-fpqc-covering, lem-a1-fpqc-invariant-intersection-neighbourhoods.

### Original step 3

Put $C=\Gamma(E,\mathcal O_V|_E)$, an $A$-algebra by restriction of the structure map. Write $E_0\subset V\times_S X$ and $E_1\subset X\times_S V$ for the inverse images of $E$. Invariance gives an isomorphism of their restricted locally ringed spaces. By [lem-a1-intersection-sections-flat-base-change], their section rings are $C\otimes_R A$ and $A\otimes_R C$, respectively, and the comorphism of $\varphi|_{E_0}$ therefore gives an $A\otimes_R A$-algebra isomorphism $\psi:A\otimes_R C\xrightarrow{\sim}C\otimes_R A$. These identifications also apply after one further base change by $X\to S$: flatness survives base change by [lem-weil-pbc-flat-morphism-base-change]. Naturality and iterated compatibility in the sections lemma identify the three pulled-back comorphisms with $\psi_{01},\psi_{12},\psi_{02}$ on the triple tensor products. Restricting $\varphi_{02}=\varphi_{12}\circ\varphi_{01}$ to the corresponding inverse images of $E$ and taking sections reverses composition, hence $\psi_{02}=\psi_{01}\circ\psi_{12}$. This supplies the cocycle omitted in the source; no scheme structure on $E$ is used.

Uses: lem-a1-intersection-sections-flat-base-change, lem-weil-pbc-flat-morphism-base-change, def-pd-descent-datum.

### Original step 4

Let $Z=\operatorname{Spec}C$ over $X$. By [lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra], its two pullbacks to $X\times_S X$ are the spectra of $C\otimes_R A$ and $A\otimes_R C$. Contravariant functoriality of [def-weil-affine-spectrum] turns $\psi$ into an isomorphism $\theta$ between them, and turns the equality just proved into $\theta_{02}=\theta_{12}\circ\theta_{01}$. Thus $(Z,\theta)$ is a scheme descent datum [def-pd-descent-datum]. Its structural morphism to $X$ is affine by [lem-weil-pbc-morphism-affine-schemes-affine]. Apply [lem-galois-fpqc-affine-descent-effective] to obtain a scheme $Z_0\to S$ and a datum-compatible isomorphism $Z_0\times_S X\cong Z$. We need no affineness assertion about $Z_0$.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra, def-weil-affine-spectrum, def-pd-descent-datum, lem-weil-pbc-morphism-affine-schemes-affine, lem-galois-fpqc-affine-descent-effective.

### Original step 5

By [lem-a1-intersection-canonical-affine-embedding], the canonical map $j_E$ identifies $W$ with an open $J\subset Z$. Its open-image comparison under flat affine base change can be checked on affine opens $L\subset W$. Write $D=\Gamma(L,\mathcal O_L)$. The map $L\to Z$ is the morphism corresponding under [lem-weil-pbc-morphism-into-affine-scheme] to restriction $C\to D$, by the restriction clause of the embedding lemma. After base change by $X\to S$, [lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra] identifies its square with the spectra of the tensor square $C\to D$, $C\otimes_R A\to D\otimes_R A$; it is cartesian. By naturality of [lem-a1-intersection-sections-flat-base-change], the canonical map from $L\times_S X$ induced by restriction of sections on $E_0$ has precisely this ring map: $c\otimes a$ maps to $(c|_L)\otimes a$; since $L\times_SX$ is affine, [lem-weil-pbc-morphism-into-affine-scheme] makes the two morphisms equal. The affine opens $L$ cover $W$, so these squares give an isomorphism $W\times_S X\cong J\times_S X$ as opens of $\operatorname{Spec}(C\otimes_R A)$; the same argument gives $X\times_S W\cong X\times_S J$ in $\operatorname{Spec}(A\otimes_R C)$. The definition of $\psi$ as the comorphism of $\varphi|_{E_0}$ makes the restrictions to $W\times_S X$ commute with these canonical maps. Since $\varphi$ carries this open onto $X\times_S W$, $\theta=\operatorname{Spec}\psi$ carries the first entire open inverse image of $J$ onto the second. Under $Z\cong Z_0\times_S X$, these are the two inverse images of $J$ on $Z\times_{Z_0}Z$.

Uses: lem-a1-intersection-canonical-affine-embedding, lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra, lem-a1-intersection-sections-flat-base-change, lem-weil-pbc-morphism-into-affine-scheme.

### Original step 6

The morphism $Z\cong Z_0\times_S X\to Z_0$ is an fpqc cover by [lem-galois-fpqc-covering-base-change]. The equality of inverse images just proved therefore permits [lem-weil-pbc-open-subscheme-fpqc-descent] to descend $J$ to an open subscheme $W_0\subset Z_0$, with $W_0\times_S X\cong J\cong W$. This isomorphism respects descent data: the datum on $J$ is the restriction of $\theta$, since the preimages of $J$ agree; the canonical embedding intertwines its restriction with $\varphi|_W$ by the restriction-of-sections calculation in step 5. Thus the restricted datum on $W$ is effective, not merely its underlying open scheme.

Uses: lem-galois-fpqc-covering-base-change, lem-weil-pbc-open-subscheme-fpqc-descent.

### Original step 7

Do this for every $v\in V$. The resulting invariant opens $W$ cover $V$, and their inclusions intertwine their restricted data with the datum on $V$, since both pullbacks are the same open restriction of $\varphi$. By [lem-pd-effective-invariant-open-cover] their effective models glue, so $(V,\varphi)$ is effective over $X/S$. This proves the affine case required in step 1; that reduction now gives the asserted $B$-scheme $V_0$ and datum-compatible isomorphism $V_0\times_B A\cong V$ for the original singleton fpqc cover. At no stage was $V\to A$ assumed quasi-compact or locally of finite presentation. If $V$ is empty the empty invariant open cover is allowed and its model is the empty scheme.

Uses: lem-pd-effective-invariant-open-cover.
