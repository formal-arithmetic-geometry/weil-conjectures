## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the literal phrase “a specified map E to A” does not require the base triangle to commute. The construction needs a D-algebra map. For D = Z[t], E = A = D with both structural maps identities, the ring map E to A sending t to 0 is not D-linear. With Y = Spec D, p_E is the identity, whereas p_E composed with Spec(E to A) is Spec(t to 0), different from p_D = id. Thus Rp_E* Rpi_E* K cannot be identified with Rpi_D* K as the original counit formula requires. The item now explicitly says “D-algebra map”; this repairs the missing compatibility in the literal wording and preserves the intended diagram D to E to A. No flatness, finite type, separatedness, boundedness or coherence hypothesis was added.

The original definition also left the left-module shift sign implicit. It now specifies that b acts on M[k] by (-1)^(k|b|) b, using the new generic sheaf-dg definition. ## Construction audit and prerequisite arguments

This is a definition split under stages/1-dag.md section 3(2), not a statement with an assembly proof. Accordingly proof, proof_deps and proof_origin remain empty/empty/null. The following paragraphs explain the construction, rather than hiding new proof steps inside the item.

1. The fiber products use def-pc-scheme and def-pc-fibre-product, with the corrected commuting base triangle. The standard simplicial polynomial resolution is the one defined in def-trf-cotangent-resolution-data. That definition explicitly does NOT assert existence of its coherence datum, so it was insufficient for the old claim that the standard augmentation is a quasi-isomorphism. The existing theorem lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence explicitly says that the standard augmentation is a resolution and gives its functorial augmented ring-square maps; it is now cited. I read cotangent.tex definition-standard-resolution (08PM), and the proof of lemma-pi-shriek-standard explaining the augmentation homotopy equivalence via 08ND and 08P2. I did not create another polynomial-resolution theorem.

2. lem-sfb-simplicial-algebra-shuffle-normalization gives the generic normalization construction, its explicit shuffle product, and the additive normalization comparison. Apply it to that polynomial resolution. Each polynomial algebra is free as a D-module on its monomials. A direct summand of a free module is flat: tensor an exact sequence with the free module, decompose this tensor sequence by the projector, and exactness passes to each summand. The quotient normalization therefore has flat terms and no positive terms. The already existing lem-pc-module-bounded-flat-kflat says exactly that a bounded-above termwise flat module complex is K-flat. No new K-flat theorem is needed. For E=D use the given constant resolution, whose normalization is D[0], with the unit map to R_A (or the augmentation to D when A=D). No strict functoriality for all intermediate maps through this replacement is asserted.

3. The ambient dg category is defined by def-sfb-sheaf-dg-module-localization, specialized to the Zariski ringed space (Y,O_Y) and C = B = O_Y tensor_D R_A. The equation d(bx)=d(b)x+(-1)^|b| b d(x) is exactly its left-module equation. Underlying quasi-isomorphisms allow the forgetful functor to descend by localization. Restricting to objects with quasi-coherent underlying cohomology uses precisely the quasi-coherent clause of pqf-def-coherent and def-pc-derived-category. The definition of local perfection is explicit closure under finite sums, shifts, cones and retracts; no equivalence with a separate compactness notion is claimed. I read sdga.tex definition-dga (0FRF), definition-dgm (0FRI), section-shift, definition-cone (0FS5) and definition-derived-category (0FT5). The source uses right modules by default; the left signs were checked directly, including the (-1)^|b| twist on the shifted cone summand.

4. Apply lem-sfb-sheaf-dg-relative-flat-bar-extension with S=O_Y, P=R_E and Q=R_A. Both P and Q are D-K-flat by paragraph 2. Rp_E*G is taken with its E-action, restricted through R_E to a dg action, and retains its O_Y-action. The new lemma supplies extension in this category, not just tensoring after erasing O_Y. Its generality in unbounded M is necessary because G is not assumed bounded. I read sdga.tex lemma-derived-tensor-product (0FTF), definition-pullback, and lemma-derived-adjoint-push-pull (0FTR). They supply derived extension and adjunction in sheaves; the explicit D-relative bar model is an additional agent-stated lemma. In its prospective proof, B(Q,P,P) augments to Q by the unit extra degeneracy. Its increasing bar-length filtration has successive right-P-induced pieces with D-K-flat coefficient Q tensor_D P^n; their tensors preserve acyclicity. Finite stages and their exact filtered union give right-P homotopical flatness. Tensoring this resolution with M gives the displayed bar and respects the external S-action. Equivalently apply the same argument over S to S tensor_D P and S tensor_D Q: their tensors with an acyclic S-complex are Q or P tensored over D with its underlying acyclic D-complex. Thus no S/D flatness is used. This is a real generic derived construction, strictly below the scheme realization, not a restatement of Phi_E or its scheme comparison.

5. At E=D, relative tensor reduces to R_A tensor_D^L G; balancing the external O_Y factor identifies it with B tensor_O_Y^L G, as stated in the last clause of the bar prerequisite. For K, restriction through R_A to A equips Rpi_D*K with its intended action. The statement of lem-pc-derived-direct-image-composition is the canonical isomorphism R(f composed with p)_* = Rf_* Rp_* for arbitrary scheme maps; apply it to pi_E followed by p_E. The base triangle now commutes. Extension of Rp_E*gamma followed by the bar lemma's counit is exactly nu_E. The latter lemma specifies multiplication in bar length zero and zero in positive bar length; the two faces in length one have the same action and opposite signs. This verifies that the chosen chain-level augmentation represents the action counit, rather than merely a map agreeing on cohomology. No scheme projection-formula or quasi-coherence comparison theorem is used here; those belong to the existing comparison node and would create a cycle if cited back into the definition.
