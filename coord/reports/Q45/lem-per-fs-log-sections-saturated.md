## Review by Q45

I read the source places above and opened the statement of every cited node. There are no new nodes whose sources need a separate check. I retained the exact item statement because its local-to-global claim is true without torsion freeness and without finite generation of global sections.

Step 1 uses `def-trf-fs-log-blowup` for both etale fs charts and their actual stalk-pushout definition. It uses `lem-per-integral-monoid-unit-pushout` for the injection of that pushout into the displayed quotient group, exactly as its statement asserts. P is integral because it is fs; E is a submonoid as the inverse image of the units; G is an abelian group. The lemma does not require f to be injective or G to be torsion free. The switch from multiplicative to additive notation changes no hypothesis.

Step 2 uses the same embedding statement and the saturation definition in `def-trf-fs-log-blowup`. I checked the group-completion identification by its universal property, rather than assuming that the embedding lemma also states it. For a representative (v,u) with n times its class in the monoid, quotient equality produces e_1-e_2 in E^gp and gives n(v+e_2) in P. Saturation of P gives a monoid representative (v+e_2,u-f(e_2)) of the original class. The sign is correct because adding (e_2,-f(e_2)) is exactly an element of the subgroup being divided out. This is a short calculation from the cited statement and definitions, so is elementary. It neither cancels an n nor assumes a unique n-th root. It does not hide an existence theorem for group-completion sheaves.

Step 3 uses the last paragraph of `lem-trf-finite-monoid-stalk-data-spreading`: finitely many equalities of germs hold on a common refinement, and restriction to a pointed etale object has the same stalk. The node really states both facts for sheaves of sets, with no cancellation or finite-generation condition on their sections. Applying the equalities clause at one geometric point over each underlying point gives a covering; `def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaf-of-sets` gives separation by its equalizer. The injection into a product of the integral stalks' groups then factors through the group completion, proving injectivity of the section monoid's canonical map. Cancellation is pulled back from the target group. This proves the result for every etale object, which is what the later overlap argument requires, not just U itself.

Step 4 uses `def-trf-fs-log-blowup` for the generators-and-relations group completion and step 2 for stalk saturation. An arbitrary group-completion element is a difference a-b because every group word is finite. The representative-and-equality clauses of `lem-trf-finite-monoid-stalk-data-spreading` lift a stalk element t and the equality t+b=a to the same pointed refinement. Its local-Noetherian hypotheses hold on the original X, and its statement explicitly allows any etale starting object. No quasi-compactness or finite cover is needed.

Step 5 uses the equalizer statement of `def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaf-of-sets`, which expressly includes the fibre-product overlaps. On each overlap, both candidates satisfy t+b=a, so cancellation from step 3 proves equality. The sheaf gives the glued section and separation gives its equation with a and b. This equation, rather than the weaker n t=c, identifies its group-completion image with the original g. All the content here is the explicit two-line cancellation and sheaf-gluing application.

Step 6 checks the identity etale object and empty covering using the same sheaf definition. For the empty scheme the monoid is a singleton and its group completion is zero, so no argument by nonexistent points is needed.

I tested the reasoning on a trivial chart P=0, on P=N with E={0} and G containing torsion, and on a chart whose entire P maps to units (the pushout becomes a group). For example the trivial log structure over F_3 has section group F_3^times of order two: squaring is not injective, but saturation still holds and the gluing argument still uses cancellation with b. The trivial log structure over Q is fs via P=0 while Q^times is not finitely generated, confirming why the conclusion must not assert fineness. I checked n=1 and disconnected etale objects as well as the empty object.
