### Conclusions, clause by clause

Yoneda identifies sections a of F(T) with natural transformations
`a : yoneda.obj T ⟶ F.obj`. This is an equivalent encoding of the node's sections,
not a restriction of them. The theorem existentially quantifies a simultaneous family
`V T a : T.left.Opens` and isomorphisms `e T a` from the open's Yoneda functor to
`pullback a α`. Both are theorem witnesses, not definitions obtained from an arbitrary
existence axiom.

1. Each `e T a` is a natural isomorphism, and the first conjunct makes it an
   isomorphism over h_T by identifying its first projection with the open inclusion.
2. The second conjunct says that any other such isomorphism over h_T equals `e T a`.
   This records independence of the isomorphism from every choice. Its uniqueness
   follows from sectionwise injectivity of alpha, which makes each fibre have at most
   one G-section. No uniqueness of arbitrary scheme presentations is asserted.
3. The third conjunct quantifies every covering family `(I : Type v) (Ti : I → Over S)`
   with arrows φ to T and membership of the underlying family in `fppfPrecoverage`.
   No index nonemptiness or finiteness hypothesis is imposed. It then quantifies all
   lifts `ai : ∀ i, Ti i ⟶ U`. Their equations are
   `yoneda.map (ai i) ≫ q = yoneda.map (φ i) ≫ a`, exactly q(ai) = a|Ti by Yoneda.
4. Its first subclause is the equality of the two inverse-image opens on the scheme
   pullback of `(φ i).left` and `(φ j).left`. The local open Wi is written directly

5. Its second subclause is `(φ i).left ⁻¹ᵁ V T a = (ai i).left ⁻¹ᵁ W` for every i.
   Its third subclause says every open O satisfying these equations equals V T a.
   Together these give existence and uniqueness of the requested descended open.
   Since the clauses hold for every cover and every system of lifts, V is independent
   of the cover and of the lifts, without selecting one cover as extra data.
6. The fourth conjunct, for every scheme map `b : Z ⟶ T`, is the exact open equality

### Edge cases

- T empty, including the empty cover: the local clauses allow any index type,
  including Empty; the sheaf condition on the empty scheme supplies a unique section,
  and there is exactly one open. No `[Nonempty I]` was introduced.
- W empty and W = U: the pullback hypothesis forces respectively the empty open
  subfunctor and the full open subfunctor; the same representing formulas apply.
- Alpha an isomorphism: the representing open is all of T. Alpha need only be
  sectionwise injective in the theorem, not surjective.
- A noninjective q or two different lifts of the same section: every lift is
  quantified; only alpha, not q, is assumed injective.
- A single-member cover, identity cover, disconnected schemes, arbitrary open
  subschemes and non-quasi-compact covering arrows: none is excluded. Fppf-to-fpqc
  uses finite affine refinements over affine opens, not quasi-compactness of each arrow.
