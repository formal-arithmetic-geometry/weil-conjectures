Simply completing the trait of a finite-type model would require regularity of base change that the original arbitrary DVR need not have. ## Mathematical content of the new algebraic input

The simultaneous approximation node concerns algebra, not purity. Its conclusion supplies an actual small directed system of regular pairs with prescribed parameters, independently of whether any of them is pure. The intended lower-level argument starts with a Cohen presentation C[[x]] onto the complete regular ring; the kernel is generated by part of a regular system of parameters of the regular source. Express the images of the chosen parameters and the relation p in their ideal in the complete source. Popescu approximation of the power-series ring over the localized polynomial ring descends this finite data to smooth polynomial stages. At the image of the closed point, independence of the chosen equations in the cotangent space is forced by their independent images in the complete source. Shrink each stage at that point so its ambient quotient and parameter quotient are regular and the given sequence is regular; retain the integral component through the point. The image of p in the ambient quotient is nonzero because its image in the target regular ring is nonzero; torsion-freeness over the DVR gives flatness. Descending the equation p=sum a_j t_j makes the parameter quotient an F_p-scheme. Further affine localizations and finite-data refinements give the directed system and the stated pair limit. This paragraph explains why the unsplit algebraic input is the required fact, rather than claiming that ILO states its exact wording.
