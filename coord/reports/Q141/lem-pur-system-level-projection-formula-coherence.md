### Original Step 1

Work on the diagram site $\mathcal D_W=J^{\mathrm{op}}\times W_{\mathrm{proet}}$, $J=\mathbf N_{\ge1}^{\mathrm{op}}$, with coverings in the site direction as in [lem-weil-pbc-diagram-site-realization]. On it use the varying ring sheaf $\mathcal B_W(n,U)=B_{n,W}(U)$ with its quotient maps. The sheaf condition is checked for each $n$; giving a module action on a sheaf on $\mathcal D_W$ is exactly giving the component $B_{n,W}$-actions and semilinear transitions, because the action's naturality in $J$ is precisely that semilinearity equation. This identifies $\operatorname{Mod}(\mathcal B_W)$ exactly and additively with $\mathscr S_W$, not with towers tensored over the constant ring $\mathcal O_E$. Apply the simultaneous-model clause of [lem-coef-semilinear-derived-sections]: inverse image, derived direct image, tensor, open extension, and the specified units, counits and mates are realized together on these ringed diagram sites. These are the same constructions used to define $\mathcal F_h$ in [lem-coef-finite-adjunction-diagram-realization] and $\operatorname{PF}^{\mathrm{sys}}$ in the statement. For a geometric map $u$ write $P_u=u^*$ and $R_u=Ru_*$ on the diagram sites. By [def-exchange-projection] and [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-projection-mate-exact], its direct-image projection map $\pi_{u;H,Q}$ is the $P_u\dashv R_u$ transpose of $(\varepsilon_{u,H}\otimes1)\mu_u$, where $\mu_u$ is the tensor-pullback comparison. This construction is natural in both inputs; composing it with the natural open tensor map proves the required naturality of $\operatorname{PF}^{\mathrm{sys}}$.

### Original Step 2

First calculate for two direct images on these diagram sites. Under the canonical derived compositor of [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-derived-pushforward-composition], prove
$$\pi_{vu;H,Q}=R_v(\pi_{u;H,P_vQ})\,\pi_{v;R_uH,Q}.$$
Transpose both sides under $P_uP_v\dashv R_vR_u$. The composite counit is $\varepsilon_u\circ P_u\varepsilon_vR_u$ by [lem-weil-pbc-mate-pasting]. Expanding the defining transposes, both become
$$P_uP_v(R_vR_uH\otimes Q)\longrightarrow P_uP_vR_vR_uH\otimes P_uP_vQ\xrightarrow{P_u\varepsilon_{v,R_uH}\otimes1}P_uR_uH\otimes P_uP_vQ\xrightarrow{\varepsilon_{u,H}\otimes1}H\otimes P_uP_vQ.$$
Here naturality of [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-derived-tensor-pullback] moves the intermediate counit through the tensor comparison. The iterated tensor comparisons coincide on flat-term K-flat representatives supplied by [lem-pc-kflat-resolution]: both send each local pure tensor to that same pure tensor, with the identical total-complex signs. Thus these are equal transposes, and the adjunction bijection gives the displayed equality in the derived category of systems itself. This argument does not require a Tor bound or invertibility of $\pi_u$.

### Original Step 3

Next let $k:A\hookrightarrow B$, $i:C\hookrightarrow D$ be quasi-compact opens, $r:A\to C$, $p:B\to D$ proper, and $pk=ir$. Use the geometric normalization of [lem-cbc-strict-tower-proper-open-calculus]: put $B_C=B\times_D C$, let $s:A\hookrightarrow B_C$ be its clopen component, and form $a:P_pi_!\to k_!P_r$ by open base change followed by the projection which is identity on $s(A)$ and zero on the complement. On the varying-ring diagram sites these maps exist by [lem-coef-semilinear-derived-sections] and exact open extension [lem-pur-site-restriction-module-adjunction-exact]; all are $\mathcal B$-linear since restriction and component projection preserve the scalar action. Define their proper-adjunction mate
$$\gamma=R_p(k_!\varepsilon_r)\,R_p(aR_r)\,\eta_pi_!R_r:i_!R_r\longrightarrow R_pk_!.$$
This uses the formula and geometric clopen description of the cited tower lemma, not an application of its constant-ring tensor product to varying-ring modules. Write $\delta_j:j_!H\otimes Q\to j_!(H\otimes P_jQ)$ for the open tensor comparison obtained from [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-slice-extension-tensor] and K-flat resolutions [lem-pc-kflat-resolution]. The proper/open tensor-mate identity is the statement of [lem-pur-system-clopen-tensor-compatibility], applied to this square; its formula uses exactly the same $a$, $\gamma$, $\pi$ and $\delta$. Thus the required identity holds in the derived system category before evaluation.

### Original Step 5

Choose compatible compactifications. The schemes are Noetherian by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian] over the field $k$, hence qcqs by [lem-weil-pbc-locally-noetherian-quasi-separated]. Choose $g=si$ and apply [lem-weil-pbc-compatible-compactification-triangle] to obtain $f=qj$ and $gf=(sp)(bj)$, with $i:Y\hookrightarrow\overline Y$, $j:X\hookrightarrow V$, $b:V\hookrightarrow\overline X$ open, $s:\overline Y\to Z$, $p:\overline X\to\overline Y$, $q:V\to Y$ proper, and $pb=iq$ Cartesian. Proper compactifications are again finite type over $k$, so all opens involved are quasi-compact. In the fixed simultaneous models the geometric composition comparison is
$$R_si_!R_qj_!\xrightarrow{R_s\gamma j_!}R_sR_pb_!j_!\xrightarrow{\sim}R_{sp}(bj)_!.$$
The last arrow uses the derived direct-image compositor of [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-derived-pushforward-composition] and open composition [lem-cbc-extension-zero-composition], realized together by [lem-coef-semilinear-derived-sections]. The proper/open map has exactly the mate normalization in Step 3; its finite normalization is specified by [lem-cbc-strict-tower-proper-open-calculus] and [thm-sf-comp-composition]. This is the geometric $\alpha_{g,f}$ assumed in the statement, constructed in the same section models; it is not identified with that map merely by matching finite evaluations.

### Original Step 6

Expand $\operatorname{PF}^{\mathrm{sys}}$ through these compactifications using [def-exchange-projection]. Put $T=M\otimes P_fP_gN$. The left side of the asserted identity first applies the following four maps, then $\alpha_{g,f,T}$:
$$R_si_!R_qj_!M\otimes N\xrightarrow{\pi_s}R_s(i_!R_qj_!M\otimes P_sN)\xrightarrow{R_s\delta_i}R_si_!(R_qj_!M\otimes P_iP_sN)\xrightarrow{R_si_!\pi_q}R_si_!R_q(j_!M\otimes P_qP_iP_sN)\xrightarrow{R_si_!R_q\delta_j}R_si_!R_qj_!T.$$
Insert the formula for $\alpha$ from Step 5. Move its proper/open factor $R_s\gamma$ to the left of $R_s\delta_i$ and $R_si_!\pi_q$ by the equality of Step 3 with $(r,p,k,i)=(q,p,b,i)$ and $Q=P_sN$; its movement past $\delta_j$ is the naturality of $\gamma$ in its input. Move it past $\pi_s$ by naturality of $\pi_s$. The two proper projection maps now combine to $\pi_{sp}$ by Step 2. The remaining two open tensor maps combine to $\delta_{bj}$: under the canonical open composition of [lem-cbc-extension-zero-composition], both are the identity on a local tensor supported on $X$ and zero off $X$, using the extension/tensor map of [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-slice-extension-tensor]. The resulting composite is precisely $\operatorname{PF}^{\mathrm{sys}}_{gf,M,N}(\alpha_{g,f,M}\otimes1_N)$, with pullback identifications $P_jP_qP_iP_s=P_{gf}$. Thus the required equality holds in $D(\mathscr S_Z)$ for these compactifications.

### Original Step 8

Check domains and finite evaluations. Open extensions of the finite-Tor constructible levels remain finite-Tor constructible, with the same cohomological range, by [lem-trf-open-ctf]. Thus the proper/open comparisons used on $j_!M$ and in refinements have bounded constructible levels. The realization [lem-coef-finite-adjunction-diagram-realization] carries $M$ to $\mathcal F_fM\in\mathscr N_Y$. Pullback preserves each bounded constructible finite-Tor level by [lem-sf-pullback-tor-amplitude], and tensor preserves it by [lem-sf-tensor-ctf](ii), adding the two finite Tor intervals separately at each $n$. Apply [lem-pur-system-normalized-tensor-closure] to the quotient-ring systems and the morphisms of their pro-etale topoi. It identifies the actual transition-adjoint maps for pullback and tensor with the invertible reduction comparisons. Thus these systems are normalized without flat coefficient transitions or a uniform bound in $n$. Hence every occurrence of $\mathcal F_h$ in the desired identity has the domain required in the statement. At each $n\ge1$, evaluation preserves the actual units, counits, tensor comparisons and mates by [lem-coef-semilinear-derived-sections]. Therefore it takes the system equality of Steps 6–7 to the composition identity for the specified finite maps of [def-exchange-projection] and the finite compositor of [thm-sf-comp-composition]. The constructible levels have etale-origin witnesses by [lem-sf-proetale-constructible-origin], so the proper/open and compactification comparisons have their stated witnessed domains. The finite maps in particular agree with the pro-etale realization in [lem-weil-finite-compact-projection-formula]. This checks which finite identity has been obtained; it is not used to infer the system equality.
