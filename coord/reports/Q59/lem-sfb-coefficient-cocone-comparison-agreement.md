## Preservation of the previous argument

The previous first step identified the actual finite-coefficient and cocone functors, not just isomorphic functors: both are total left derived `Mod.extendScalars` for precisely the same coefficient map. Assembly step 1 retains this unfolding; the derived-normal-form node also states the identities of all three functors on objects and arrows, and forbids inserting an extra automorphism. The finite-subfield-valued closure coefficient ring is specified both in the unchanged item and in that node.

The previous second step had two different arguments. Its underived argument is now the internal-tensor-unit node: the double relative-tensor coequalizer admits the multiplication map and the insertion-of-one inverse, and multiplication carries the composite adjunction unit to the direct unit. For the future proof, on symbolic tensors the composite unit sends x to 1 tensor (1 tensor x); multiplication sends this to 1 psi(1) tensor x = 1 tensor x. The balancing relations give the two factorizations and inverse identities, and naturality follows from the functorial coequalizer construction. The equation is an internal coequalizer equation; no global sections or concreteness assumption is used. The uniqueness statement already recorded in `lem-weil-pbc-conjugate-iso-unit-equation` then identifies multiplication with the specified unit-compatible isomorphism. The new statement includes all these obligations, rather than only the last equality.

The derived part of the previous second step is now the derived-normal-form node. In its notation A is extension E_X to H_X, B is extension H_X to Omega_X, D is extension along the composite map, and C is extension along the direct map. The canonical coefficient-map comparison is e:D to C, while u:BA to D is oriented from iterated extension to direct extension. The exact-derived comparison c points from L(BA) to (LB)(LA). Consequently the cocone formula is L(e o u) o c^{-1}; the fixed finite-tensor formula is L(e o m_0) o c^{-1}. This is a theorem about the chosen construction, not an inference from the existence of an isomorphism in the older definition node's statement. The source code uses `(Mod.extendScalarsComp _ _).symm` and then `extendScalarsIsoOfEq`, confirming these orientations. A future proof expands this construction, using the exact-derived composition and natural-isomorphism normalization already present in the DAG.

The previous third step, projection of an equality of natural isomorphisms to both component maps, remains a literal substitution in assembly step 2. The previous fourth step is supplied by the cancellation leaf: the full inclusion maps the preimage of the forward ambient arrow back to that arrow and does the same for the inverse. Assembly step 3 specializes those formulas and substitutes the ambient equality. No argument is replaced by a vague transport claim, and no new preservation theorem is assumed.
