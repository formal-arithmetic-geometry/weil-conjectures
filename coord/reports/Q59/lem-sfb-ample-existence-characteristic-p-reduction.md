For the new node, the 0BDZ construction really gives ring homomorphisms, a stronger property than the multiplicative norms used later: on a finite Noetherian affine cover, take a common nilpotence bound N and choose e>=1 with p^e>=N. Frobenius kills the nilradical, so the ring map A -> A, a |-> a^(p^e), factors through A/sqrt(0). On arbitrary overlaps work locally with lifts from O_X to its quotient sheaf. Two lifts differ by a nilpotent killed by this same Frobenius, so their p^e-powers agree. The resulting local sections glue uniquely and commute with restrictions; addition and multiplication can be checked locally on lifts. This constructs the maps on arbitrary opens without incorrectly assuming surjectivity on global sections of the quotient. The scalar-power equation is the formula on a lift. The reduction has identical residue fields at corresponding points, so if b(y)=0 then any local lift has zero residue there and its p^e-power has zero residue too. These are precisely the extra properties used by the item. This sheaf-level construction and its uniform exponent are a genuine supporting lemma, not an elementary repackaging of ampleness.
