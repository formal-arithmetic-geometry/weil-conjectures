### Old step 1 → lem-trf-derived-square-polynomial-resolution

Take the standard polynomial resolution $P_\bullet\to C$ of [lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence], and write $S=s(P_\bullet)$, $q:S\to C[0]$. It is a quasi-isomorphism and $S^{-n}=P_n$, with no positive terms. Choose polynomial coordinates $P_n=A[x_i\mid i\in I_n]$. By [def-weil-pbc-mv-polynomial-ring], its monomials form an $A$-module basis. Tensoring a free module with a module $M$ identifies it with finitely supported families in $M$: the mutually inverse maps send $x^\alpha\otimes m$ to the family with entry $m$ at $\alpha$, and a family $(m_\alpha)$ to $\sum_\alpha x^\alpha\otimes m_\alpha$ [def-weil-pbc-module-tensor-product]. Exactness is coordinatewise, and a preimage needs only the finitely many nonzero coordinates. Thus the $P_n$ are flat. The ordinary-module bounded-above flat criterion [lem-pc-module-bounded-flat-kflat] makes $S$ K-flat, so $q$ is a K-flat resolution of $C[0]$.

Cited nodes: lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence, def-weil-pbc-mv-polynomial-ring, def-weil-pbc-module-tensor-product, lem-pc-module-bounded-flat-kflat.

### Old step 2 → lem-trf-derived-square-polynomial-resolution

Use the exact tensor equivalence of [lem-trf-one-point-derived-ring-identification] to interpret [lem-pur-derived-change-of-rings] and the normalized adjunction of [lem-pur-sheaf-ring-derived-coefficient-adjunction] in ordinary modules. For $A\to A'$, computation on the K-flat resolution $q$ identifies $C\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}A'$ with $S\otimes_A A'$. The canonical map $\mu$ is represented by $\epsilon'(p\otimes a')=f(\epsilon(p))a'$ in degree zero and zero in negative degrees, where $f:C\to C'$ is the given map: this is exactly the adjunct formula $x\otimes a'\mapsto a'g(x)$ applied to $g=fq$. Consequently $\epsilon'$ is a quasi-isomorphism by the hypothesis. Its degree-zero cohomology is the canonical ring homomorphism $\alpha:C\otimes_A A'\to C'$, $c\otimes a'\mapsto f(c)a'$. Indeed $H^0(S\otimes_A A')=\operatorname{coker}(d_S^{-1}\otimes1)=C\otimes_A A'$: a bilinear map from $S^0\times A'$ kills $\operatorname{im}d_S^{-1}$ exactly when it factors through $C\times A'$, which proves this cokernel identity from [def-weil-pbc-module-tensor-product]. Thus $\alpha$ is bijective, hence an isomorphism of rings; its multiplicativity follows directly from the displayed formula. No flatness of either ring map has been inferred.

Cited nodes: lem-trf-one-point-derived-ring-identification, lem-pur-derived-change-of-rings, lem-pur-sheaf-ring-derived-coefficient-adjunction, def-weil-pbc-module-tensor-product.
