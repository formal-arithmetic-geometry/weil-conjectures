## Source error

The item is true and is not STATEMENT-DISPUTED. In 06N0's first paragraph the sentence describing the coordinate ring of U/P calls it the ring of R-invariant functions. It must be the P-invariants. For an explicit check, take a nontrivial finite separable Galois extension L/k and its pair groupoid U=Spec L, R=U times_S U, S=Spec k. The identity component P is the diagonal U (the diagonal is clopen for this etale cover), so U/P=U and its coordinate ring is L; the full R-invariants are k. These differ. Step 3 uses the correct equalizer of the two P-projections and cites the existing affine quotient theorem for it. The inverse of a nonzero invariant is invariant, so this equalizer is a field even over an imperfect base field.

I also expanded the sentence saying s,t are base changes of the quotient projections. The restriction formula involves both factors of q. Step 4 descends flatness and local finite presentation using a target cover and a further source cover, citing the statements that expressly provide that locality. This avoids concealing a source-descent theorem behind the word 'base change'. No change in the source's route is needed.
