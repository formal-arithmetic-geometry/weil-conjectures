### Returned step 1

*Step 1 (restriction is a functor $\Mod(\mathcal O_X)\to\Mod(\mathcal O_U)$).* Sheaves on $X$ and on $U$ are sheaves on the sites of open subsets with open coverings [def-weil-pbc-sites-and-cohomology]; the open subsets of $U$ are exactly the open subsets of $X$ contained in $U$. Let $\mathcal M\in\Mod(\mathcal O_X)$ [def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category]. The rule $W\mapsto\mathcal M(W)$ ($W\subseteq U$ open) with the restriction maps of $\mathcal M$ is a presheaf of abelian groups on $U$ (composition and identity laws are those of $\mathcal M$). A covering $\{W_i\to W\}$ of an open $W\subseteq U$ in the site of $U$ is a family of opens $W_i\subseteq W$ with $\bigcup_iW_i=W$, i.e. a covering of $W$ in the site of $X$, and the sheaf condition for $\mathcal M|_U$ on it is word for word the sheaf condition for $\mathcal M$ on it; so $\mathcal M|_U$ is a sheaf. In the same way $\mathcal O_U$ is a sheaf of rings on $U$. For $W\subseteq U$, $\mathcal M(W)$ is an $\mathcal O_X(W)=\mathcal O_U(W)$-module and for $W'\subseteq W\subseteq U$ the restriction $\mathcal M(W)\to\mathcal M(W')$ is semilinear over $\mathcal O_U(W)\to\mathcal O_U(W')$, which is the restriction of $\mathcal O_X$; so $\mathcal M|_U$ is a sheaf of $\mathcal O_U$-modules [def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category], the restriction of [def-weil-pbc-module-finite-type-presentation]. For a morphism $\mu:\mathcal M\to\mathcal N$ of $\mathcal O_X$-modules the components $\mu_W$, $W\subseteq U$, are $\mathcal O_X(W)=\mathcal O_U(W)$-linear and commute with the restriction maps $W'\subseteq W$, so $\mu|_U$ is a morphism of $\mathcal O_U$-modules; $(\nu\circ\mu)|_U=\nu|_U\circ\mu|_U$ and $\mathrm{id}_{\mathcal M}|_U=\mathrm{id}_{\mathcal M|_U}$ hold componentwise. Hence $(-)|_U$ is a functor.

### Returned step 2

*Step 2 ($j_*$ is a functor $\Mod(\mathcal O_U)\to\Mod(\mathcal O_X)$).* Let $\mathcal G\in\Mod(\mathcal O_U)$ and write $j:U\to X$ for the inclusion, so $j^{-1}(V)=V\cap U$. As a presheaf of abelian groups, $j_*\mathcal G$, $V\mapsto\mathcal G(V\cap U)$, with restriction maps $\mathcal G(V\cap U)\to\mathcal G(V'\cap U)$ for $V'\subseteq V$, is the direct image of the abelian sheaf $\mathcal G$ along the continuous map $j$, hence a sheaf of abelian groups on $X$ [def-weil-pbc-topological-inverse-image] (1) (directly: an open covering $V=\bigcup_iV_i$ gives the open covering $V\cap U=\bigcup_i(V_i\cap U)$ in $U$, and the sheaf condition of $j_*\mathcal G$ for the first is that of $\mathcal G$ for the second, with $(V_i\cap U)\cap(V_k\cap U)=(V_i\cap V_k)\cap U$). Module structure: for $V\subseteq X$ open let $\rho_V:\mathcal O_X(V)\to\mathcal O_X(V\cap U)=\mathcal O_U(V\cap U)$ be the restriction, a ring homomorphism; $a\cdot t:=\rho_V(a)t$ for $a\in\mathcal O_X(V)$, $t\in\mathcal G(V\cap U)$ makes $\mathcal G(V\cap U)$ an $\mathcal O_X(V)$-module (restriction of scalars along $\rho_V$), extending its group structure. ### Returned step 3

*Step 3 (the map $\Phi:\alpha\mapsto\beta$).* Let $\mathcal M\in\Mod(\mathcal O_X)$, $\mathcal G\in\Mod(\mathcal O_U)$ and $\alpha:\mathcal M|_U\to\mathcal G$ a morphism of $\mathcal O_U$-modules.
