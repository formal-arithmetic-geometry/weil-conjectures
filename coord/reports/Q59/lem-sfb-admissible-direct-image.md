Original step 1:

Let $j:U\hookrightarrow X$ be open and $A$ finite; $j$ is quasi-compact and quasi-separated because the schemes are Noetherian (Step 1). Descend $K\in\Dctf(U,A)$ of Tor interval $[a,b]$ to a bounded constructible etale complex $C$ of the same Tor interval by [det-pe-finite-tor-comparison-noetherian]. Its etale direct image $B=Rj_*C$ is bounded constructible by [cor-fin-deligne-open-finite-coefficients], since a power of $\ell$ kills $A$ and is invertible in $k$. Put $d=\operatorname{cd}(j)<\infty$ by [lem-kun-0f10](1); clause (1) of [det-geo-open-finiteness-ii-amplitude] states $R^qj_*F=0$ for every sheaf $F$ of $A$-modules and every $q>d$. A finite $A$-module $M$ is finitely presented: it is a quotient of some $A^n$, and the kernel is a finite set, hence finitely generated. Since $C$ is bounded, [lem-sf-projection-finite-constant] with $N=d$ shows that the projection map $B\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}\underline M_X\to Rj_*(C\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}\underline M_U)$ is an isomorphism for every finite $A$-module $M$. These are exactly the hypotheses of [lem-sf-open-tor-bound-arbitrary-field] for $L=C\in D^+(U_{\mathrm{\acute et}},A)$ and $e=d$, over the arbitrary standing field; it gives that $B$ has Tor amplitude in $[a,b+d]$ against every $A_X$-module sheaf. Finally [prop-weil-pbc-comparison-direct-image] identifies $\nu_X^*B$ with the pro-etale $Rj_*K$, [lem-exc-comparison-tor-interval] keeps the interval $[a,b+d]$ against every pro-etale module sheaf, and the equivalence of [det-pe-finite-tor-comparison-noetherian] places $\nu_X^*B$ in $\Dctf(X,A)$. This proves the finite open direct-image case over the arbitrary standing field.
