## Audit findings and corrections

The item was statement-only, with earlier searches but no proof or report. Its boundedness definition agrees with BLR 1.1/2: finite generating coordinate bounds in a finite affine cover. BLR 1.3/1 imposes no completeness, excellence or residue-field perfection hypothesis, so no statement correction or dispute was necessary.

A weak Néron model is not yet a group and does not have the required mapping property. Also, keeping minimal components can lose the extension property for unramified points. I therefore did not infer that the minimal model or its group envelope is a Néron model merely from its construction. The last three steps instead prove the mapping property using local translations and the identity section, following BLR 4.4/4. Left translation preserves the first coordinate, right translation preserves the second; the two universal translations are treated separately. The local rational spreading lemma explicitly supplies the passage from local rings at generic special-fibre points to fibrewise dense opens; it is not concealed under “spreading out”. Its base-change clause supplies density of the open model after extension of those local rings.

The local-spreading node is a genuine lemma, not an elementary new node: at each generic point of V_k, smoothness makes its image generic in the appropriate component of Z_k. Passing to the local ring of that image gives the hypothesized map. Finite presentation spreads a neighborhood and its finitely many equations and denominators to a neighborhood on V. The resulting opens contain the generic points of V_k; together with V_E they are fibrewise dense. Morphisms agree because smoothness implies flatness over R, so on any affine open the ring maps into its localization at the uniformizer; the separated diagonal makes the equalizer closed, and its ideal vanishes after localization, hence vanishes. Local isomorphisms spread together with their inverses, giving the birational clause. Fibrewise-density is stable under base change because each fibre is smooth and reduced: a dense open is schematically dense and field extensions are flat. This is the argument to retain when this new statement-only node is later split, not an extra proof hidden in its statement.
