## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: read literally, the imported definition required only the object set to be small and allowed morphism complexes of unrestricted modules. That does not imply the asserted small Hom sets. For an explicit counterexample, take R = Z and a set S in the larger universe whose cardinal is not small in the smaller universe. The one-object category with degree-zero endomorphism algebra Z[x_s | s in S] has at least |S| distinct Z-linear dg endofunctors: for each s send every variable to x_s. Thus the unrestricted version has a non-small endofunctor set. The repaired statement explicitly requires the morphism complexes to consist of small modules, which is the intended category of small dg categories in Tabuada. No coefficient-ring, flatness, degree or object-injectivity hypothesis was added.

'Finitely presented complex' now explicitly means preservation of filtered colimits by its Hom functor in Ch(R); termwise finite presentation alone is insufficient for an unbounded complex. Tensor products now cite their existing definition node.
