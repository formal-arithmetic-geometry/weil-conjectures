This is visible already for the inclusion of a singleton in a two-point discrete profinite set: the free map inserts its basis vector in the rank-two free module, in the covariant direction. I corrected the wording to make E(R[P])≅L_P covariant in P and generator-normalized, while EH_P≅G_PE is contravariant in P, covariant in modules, and normalized by its transported evaluation pairing. The theorem's objects, assumptions and conclusions are otherwise retained. This repairs a typing error without weakening the intended comparison.
