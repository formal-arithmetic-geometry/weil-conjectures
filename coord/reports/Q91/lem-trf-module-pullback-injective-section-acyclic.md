### Original step 2

Write $P_U(V)=\bigoplus_{a:V\to U}R'(V)$, so $E_U=P_U^\#$. For any $R'$-module sheaf $N$, the sheafification adjunction [def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification] and [lem-weil-pbc-free-yoneda-presheaf-module] give $\operatorname{Hom}_{R'}(E_U,N)\cong N(U)$. Explicitly, a sheaf map is sent to the value on $e_{\mathrm{id}_U}$ of its composite with $P_U\to E_U$; the inverse associated to $s\in N(U)$ sends $r e_a$ on $V$ to $rN(a)(s)$ and factors uniquely through sheafification. These maps are additive: both evaluation and the displayed inverse preserve sums, and uniqueness of sheafification factorization preserves this equality. For $b:N\to N'$ they satisfy $\theta_{N'}(b\alpha)=b_U\theta_N(\alpha)$. Thus for an injective resolution $N\to J^\bullet$, $\theta$ is an isomorphism of complexes $\operatorname{Hom}_{R'}(E_U,J^\bullet)\cong J^\bullet(U)$: it intertwines each differential $d_J$ by this formula. Taking kernels modulo images gives $\operatorname{Ext}_{R'}^p(E_U,N)\cong R^p\Gamma_{\mathrm{Mod},U}(N)$, with the derived evaluation computed in $R'$-modules, exactly as specified in the statement.

Uses: def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification, lem-weil-pbc-free-yoneda-presheaf-module.

Source: literature/stacks-project/sites-cohomology.tex, Tag 07AC, Ext display and the equality Hom(j_{U!}O_U,H)=H(U); sites-modules.tex Tags 03EJ (remark-localize-presheaves) and 03DI, equation-map-lower-shriek-OU-into-module.

### Original step 3

Put $F=g^{-1}I$ as an $R'$-module, and write $F_{\mathrm{ab}}$ for its underlying abelian sheaf. By [lem-weil-pbc-continuous-cocontinuous-topos-adjunction], $g^{-1}$ on underlying set sheaves is precomposition with $u$ and preserves finite products. Apply this formula to the addition, zero, negation and scalar-action maps of $I$. Since products in the formula are sectionwise, it gives $F(V)=I(u(V))$ as modules over $R'(V)=R(u(V))$, with restriction $F(h)=I(u(h))$. In particular it identifies the underlying abelian presheaves, including their restrictions. Only continuity and cocontinuity, not the extra clauses of special cocontinuity, are used.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-continuous-cocontinuous-topos-adjunction.

Source: literature/stacks-project/sites-cohomology.tex, Tag 0D6X, lemma-pullback-injective-pre-limp, proof: g^{-1}=u^p by Sites Tag 00XR (lemma-when-shriek).

### Original step 4

Fix a covering $\mathcal U=\{U_t\to U\}_{t\in T}$ in $\mathcal C$. Continuity [def-weil-pbc-sites-and-cohomology](3) makes $u(\mathcal U)$ a covering. For a tuple $\mathbf t=(t_0,\ldots,t_n)$ let $W_{\mathbf t}=U_{t_0}\times_U\cdots\times_U U_{t_n}$ and $P_{\mathbf t}=u(U_{t_0})\times_{u(U)}\cdots\times_{u(U)}u(U_{t_n})$, with the choices of [def-weil-pbc-site-cech-complex]. The canonical comparison $c_{\mathbf t}:u(W_{\mathbf t})\to P_{\mathbf t}$ is an isomorphism: for $n=0$ it is the identity, and appending a factor uses continuity (3)(b) with the original covering and the map from the preceding fibre product to $U$, followed by the preceding comparison. For the projection $\delta_j$ deleting factor $j$, one has $c_{\mathbf t\setminus t_j}\,u(\delta_j)=\delta_j\,c_{\mathbf t}$, since both maps have the same projection to each remaining factor and the fibre product universal property makes such a map unique. This constructs all the nerve comparisons and their compatibility, without assuming that $u$ preserves arbitrary fibre products.

Uses: def-weil-pbc-sites-and-cohomology, def-weil-pbc-site-cech-complex.

Source: literature/stacks-project/sites-cohomology.tex, Tag 0D6X, proof: images of the covering and its iterated fibre products; Sites Tag 00WV (definition-continuous).
