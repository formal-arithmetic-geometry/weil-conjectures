The item had an empty proof. It was created as supporting infrastructure for the first paragraph of Tag 0794, not as a verbatim restatement of that unbounded proposition. In particular this item assumes preservation of quasi-isomorphisms on the resolving class directly and claims descent only on D-minus; the right exactness and countable-colimit hypotheses of the full proposition are unnecessary here. I retained the item's statement exactly and wrote the bounded argument explicitly.

The important distinction is between the full homotopy subcategory C of complexes with bounded-above COHOMOLOGY and the full subcategory R of bounded-above COMPLEXES with terms in P. They cannot be identified. The proof first compares the localization of C with the full cohomologically bounded-above derived subcategory, then applies the existing right-fraction density result to R inside C. It does not assume that R is dense in the entire unbounded homotopy category, or that resolutions can be chosen functorially.
