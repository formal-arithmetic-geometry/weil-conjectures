## Argument available for the new node

This is supporting reasoning for later splitting, not a proof field of the new unsplit node. A chain retraction writes the target as X direct sum E, where E is isomorphic to its cokernel. Identity maps correspond to E=0. A small coproduct of such arrows is the inclusion of the coproduct of their sources with complement the direct sum of the E's, with the direct sum contracting homotopy. A pushout along any X to X' is X' into X' direct sum E, so no flatness or exactness assumption on that map is needed.

For a continuous chain indexed by an ordinal theta, choose a chain complement E_alpha to each successor map. Inductively construct compatible isomorphisms X_beta = X_0 direct sum (direct sum over alpha<beta of E_alpha). At a successor extend the previous isomorphism by the chosen splitting. At a limit use precisely the stated continuity and the universal property of direct sums; every vector has finite support, so the colimit has the stated direct sum. The cokernel of X_0 to X_theta is the direct sum of the contractible E_alpha and is contracted componentwise. This is an induction on beta, including beta=0; it makes no claim about noncontinuous systems or nonsplit inclusions.

For an arrow retract of i:X to Y through j:A to B, let the section square have maps s_X,s_Y and the retraction square t_X,t_Y. If r_j is a chain retraction of j, then t_X r_j s_Y retracts i. The cokernel construction applied to these two squares makes coker(i) a retract of coker(j). If u,v are the induced section/retraction and h contracts coker(j), the degree-minus-one map v h u contracts coker(i), since d commutes with u,v and vu=1.

In X direct sum E coordinates, projection onto X is a chain inverse to the inclusion up to the homotopy 0 direct sum h on Y; the difference between the identity and inclusion followed by projection is d(0 direct sum h)+(0 direct sum h)d. This also shows directly on cycles that the inclusion induces cohomology isomorphisms. Tensoring with any K preserves the chain direct sum decomposition, and contracts K tensor E by H(k tensor e)=(-1)^{degree(k)}k tensor h(e). In dH+Hd the two terms involving dk cancel and the remaining terms equal k tensor (dh+hd)e. These calculations prove every clause of the new statement without a projectivity or flatness assumption.
