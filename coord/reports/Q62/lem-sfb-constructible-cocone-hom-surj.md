Uses: def-weil-pbc-hom-colimit-property, lem-weil-pbc-condensed-sheaf-ext-continuity.

2. The comparison for $P(A[t])$ at $n$ identifies with the comparison for $P(A)$ at $n-t$ by precomposition with the shift equivalence; naturality makes the identification commute with every cocone leg. Hence $P$ is shift invariant. Precomposition by an isomorphism similarly preserves $P$, and $P(0)$ holds since both groups vanish. Uses: def-weil-pbc-hom-colimit-property.

3. Apply [lem-sfb-bounded-cohomology-cone-witness] to the bounded object $K$, obtaining an ordered finite list ending in $K$. Induct over the list. The two outer terms have $P$ by induction and step 2, so [lem-weil-pbc-hom-colimit-extension] gives $P(F_r[t])$, hence $P(F_r)$. At the last term this is the asserted comparison for every $n$.
