- Degree zero is included without a positive-degree assumption.
- A constant simplicial scheme gives the degree-indexed total category of its small
  étale site, with the expected order-map arrows and component coverings.
- No simplicial structural map is assumed étale. Comma squares and base-change arrows
  are valid for an arbitrary simplicial scheme, including a Čech nerve.
- An empty component is allowed. Its empty covering is a generator precisely because
  the component étale topology accepts it. The quantifier `I : Type (u + 1)` allows
  `I` empty as well as infinite, and imposes no `Nonempty` or finiteness condition.
- The zero ring and arbitrary characteristic are allowed. Commutativity is the only
  ring condition. Module sections need not be finite or constructible.
- An augmentation is arbitrary (including the identity augmentation of a constant
  simplicial scheme), and only its compatibility equations are required.
- The site retains different object and morphism universe levels; coefficients are
  in the containing universe, so sheafification is available without a hollow or
  extra mathematical hypothesis.
