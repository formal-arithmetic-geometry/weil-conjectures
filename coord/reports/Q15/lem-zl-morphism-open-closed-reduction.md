The immersion lemma applies to both complementary source immersions. In the complex case the actual outer Weil complexes have bound w by the source hypotheses, and complex-extension applies to an underlying distinguished triangle with equivariant first arrows, exactly its statement. For step 3 I checked the statements of immersion-base-change-lft, separated-finite-type-base-change, package clause (4), Weil operations, the new immersion weight node and the retained higher-shriek comparison. The first two are the distinct geometric checks needed for the two restricted morphisms and source inclusions. For step 4 I read extension-by-zero-punctual (exactness and purity), the new open-shriek node (mixed and derived preservation), closed-immersion-finite (finiteness), finite-pushforward (Rg_* = Rg_! and weight bound), and the new target reassembly statement. For G[0] the restriction functors are exact on the constructible heart, as in the immersion behavior and retained higher-shriek statements. At degree zero the definition of the complex bound is exactly the sheaf bound; every other cohomology object is zero. No arbitrary closed-point residue-field extension is treated as an immersion.

For step 5 I reread the retained coefficient long-exact statement: it gives an exact Weil segment with the very three higher compact-support images in the coefficient clause, so exact-middle applies at w+r for each r. In the filtration induction the previous filtration term and its successive quotient are the endpoints of each exact coefficient sequence; the zero base has the empty mixed filtration. Step 6 is just induction on the finite binary decomposition tree: restrictions at each vertex are separated finite-type base changes, and steps 3--4 combine its two children. Thus it covers precisely stratifications built by successive open/closed decompositions, not an arbitrary unproved stratification criterion.

Small cases checked: with zero endpoints the exact-middle hypothesis forces a zero middle; the identity immersion preserves a filtration; an immersion disjoint from support can collapse all its terms, permitted by the non-strict definition. For an empty open, Rj_! is zero; for the identity open it is the identity. Zero stalks have no eigenvalues and hence are pure of every real weight. Source localization with empty open reduces to the closed identity and conversely with empty closed. For reassembly either empty piece contributes zero and the remaining restriction is the whole object; nonempty nonreduced closed complements are allowed. The statements do not depend on strict filtration inclusions, smoothness, or properness that were not given. Negative cohomological degrees are harmless: the source segment is indexed by all integers in the bounded derived category. All seven new nodes are true on these cases and self-contained.
