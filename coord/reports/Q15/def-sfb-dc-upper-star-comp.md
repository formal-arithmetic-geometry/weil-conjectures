Second paste the three inclusion comparisons with this isomorphism, using the explicitly displayed formula for A. Third use the new postcomposition Mathlib leaf to pass from a fully faithful inclusion to a fully faithful functor on functor categories, and use `lem-cbc-fully-faithful-isomorphism-lift` there. This produces a unique isomorphism of functors with image A, including naturality and invertibility. It does not merely lift unrelated component maps. The definition is the resulting unique preimage. No step exceeds 2000 characters; the entire proof is well below 3000.

Proof in words for the new leaf (already implemented in Mathlib, so its node has no proof obligation): for a natural transformation between HF and HG, take the unique inverse image under H of each component. To check naturality, apply the injective Hom map of H to the proposed equality; preservation of composition reduces it to the given naturality equality. This gives surjectivity on natural transformations. If two transformations have the same postcomposition, injectivity of H on each Hom set gives equality of every component, hence equality of transformations. This is exactly the construction read at Whiskering.lean:116–125; it is separate from the existing iso-lifting theorem on objects of an arbitrary category.
