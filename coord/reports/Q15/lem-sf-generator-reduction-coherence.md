### Original step 2

For one basic unary operation \(A\), and for tensor in its two input variables, write \(h_A=d_A^{-1}:A_{\rm int}(K_i)\to\mathcal L A(\mathcal R K_i)\). Under \(\mathcal R\dashv\mathcal L\) of [lem-coef-derived-system-evaluation-calculus] the system map whose adjunct is \(h_A\) is \(e_{A(\mathcal R K_i)}\circ\mathcal R(h_A)\), by the triangle identities stated there. For pullback, tensor and proper direct image, \(d_A\) is the identity of models: [def-pur-integral-derived-operations] defines \(Lf^*K=R\lim_n\operatorname{Res}_nf^*(P_n)\) and the completed tensor as \(R\lim_n\operatorname{Res}_n(P_n\otimes Q_n)\) on flat-term K-flat representatives with \(P_n=P\otimes(A_n)_W\), and [def-weil-derived-direct-image] defines \(Rp_*K=R\lim_n\operatorname{Res}_nRp_{*,n}(K_n)\); by [lem-coef-derived-system-evaluation-calculus] \(\mathcal R K\) is represented by the same tower \((P\otimes(A_n)_W)_n\) and \(\mathcal L=R\lim\circ U\) uses the same total-limit model, while the system operations evaluate to \(f^*\), \(\otimes\) and \(Rp_{*,n}\) [lem-coef-semilinear-derived-sections]. by naturality of \(e\) and the triangle identity \(e_{\mathcal RX}\circ\mathcal R(u_X)=1\). The two-input formula for tensor is the same argument in both variables. These equalities hold in the derived semilinear-system category and are natural in all inputs.
