## Review by Q15

I checked the six proof steps against both their sources and the actual statements of every node in `uses`, and copied those statements verbatim to citations.json. The proof keeps the 0CCX/0CCY route. Over an algebraically closed base, the source's comparison through the kernel of differentials simplifies to a direct calculation with pth powers; this removes the need for a separate differential or degree-p classification lemma.

Step 1 uses exactly the finite-generation/transcendence-degree and induced-embedding assertions of `lem-sfb-proper-curve-function-field-morphisms`, the complete root tower of `lem-weil-pbc-purely-inseparable-pth-root-tower`, and the degree product of `lem-weil-pbc-field-degree-multiplicativity`. The tower's base field is E, not k: E has characteristic p, and the assumed finite pure inseparability is L/E. Every adjacent extension is finite of degree p, so the degree product applies repeatedly, including the empty product. No separable/purely inseparable factorization of a general field extension is silently assumed.

Step 2 uses the existence clause, not the proof, of `lem-trf-algclosed-curve-model`. The preceding sentences verify its hypotheses separately for each intermediate field: it is finite over E, finitely generated over k, and has transcendence degree one. Algebraic closedness is already a hypothesis. The unused valuation-ring clause of that node is not invoked. The models are smooth integral proper finite-type dimension-one schemes and consequently are curves in the statement's sense.

Step 3 uses the new node exactly for its twist geometry, nonconstancy, and degree p^n. The tensor base-change algebra and the two Frobenius projections are unpacked directly from `def-pc-base-change` and `def-weil-absolute-frobenius-scheme`; the quotient-field description is stated in `def-sc-function-field`, which is why that definition is an explicit use. The image calculation is written rather than hidden behind an identification: a tensor sum maps to the p^n power of a sum after choosing roots of its scalar coefficients. Every power is conversely an image. Passing to fractions produces exactly the power subfield. This does not confuse absolute Frobenius (usually not k-linear) with relative Frobenius (which is k-linear), and it uses the second projection as the twist's k-structure.

Step 4 is an elementary subfield argument, with the degree product explicitly cited. Writing a rational expression in alpha_j and taking its pth power proves K_j^p is contained in K_{j-1}. Both indices equal p. The intermediate extension is finite because it is a linear subspace of K_j over K_j^p, so the cited degree law has all its hypotheses. Degree one then forces equality. The successive equalities give E=L^{p^r}. This step is not a new lemma node: the rational-expression calculation and the degree-p cancellation take only a few lines from previously stated facts.

Step 5 applies the new degree node to X with n=r, verifying the curve type needed to invoke `lem-sfb-proper-curve-function-field-morphisms` on its twist. Equality of image subfields defines a k-field isomorphism in the correct contravariant direction. The existing correspondence supplies morphisms for that isomorphism and its inverse, and its composition/identity and injectivity clauses prove that they are inverse scheme morphisms and that h composed with F equals f. This is not an appeal to the proof of the correspondence or to an unstated extension theorem.

Step 6 uses the same correspondence for composition and faithfulness and the new degree node for any competing exponent m. A k-isomorphism induces an isomorphism of function fields, so the degree of f equals that of its Frobenius factor. Comparing p^r and p^m proves the exponents agree since p>=2. Cancellation of the injective field embedding F^* and faithfulness then prove uniqueness of h. Both uniqueness clauses are thus accounted for separately.

I tested n=0 and f an isomorphism (the empty tower, degree 1, identity twist), the projective line with t mapping to t^{p^r} (the expected exponent and power-subfield image), a nontrivial k-isomorphism composed with Frobenius (the isomorphism is recovered from the field map), and positive-genus curves (no rational-coordinate assumption is used). Dimension one is essential for degree p in step 3; in dimension two distinct intermediate index-p fields can occur and the argument would fail. Constant maps are excluded before any induced function-field embedding is used. No imperfect-base claim is made: the scalar-root calculation explicitly uses algebraic closedness. No new definition, elementary lemma node, duplicate, or restatement of an item's clause was introduced. The sole new node is statement-only with empty proof and proof dependencies.
