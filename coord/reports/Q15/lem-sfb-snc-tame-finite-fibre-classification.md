### Returned Step 2 -> lem-sfb-snc-finite-tame-root-model

Choose a finite locally constant set sheaf $F_0$ on $V^\circ$ whose pullback is $F$. Form its sheaf of all finite frames
$$\mathcal P=\coprod_{d\geq0}\operatorname{Isom}(\underline{\{1,\ldots,d\}},F_0).$$
On a member of a pro-etale trivializing cover with value $A$, this is the constant finite set of bijections $\{1,\ldots,|A|\}\to A$: all other summands are empty. Here a map between finite constant objects is locally a choice of the finitely many images of their elements; choosing these simultaneously describes the frame sheaf, including $A=\varnothing$. Hence $\mathcal P$ is locally constant and has sections on every member of that cover. The locally-classical etale-section lemma gives an etale covering with sections of $\mathcal P$. Refining by the summands selected by each section gives actual etale finite frames for $F_0$. Thus $F_0$ comes from an etale finite locally constant sheaf, represented by a finite etale $P\to V^\circ$ by the finite-cover equivalence. Full faithfulness of classical comparison identifies maps and fibres. The stipulated strict-local tower triviality is exactly triviality of this represented finite cover.

### Returned Step 5 -> lem-sfb-universal-residual-root-finite-etale-splitting

The algebra $H_n$ is finite over $H$: successive monic division gives the spanning basis $\prod_i x_i^{a_i}$ with $0\leq a_i<n$. In particular it is integral, since the $x_i$ satisfy their monic equations. The integral-over-henselian-pair lemma gives the henselian pair $(H_n,JH_n)$. Its quotient is $C=R[x_1,\ldots,x_r]/(x_i^n)_i$. Put $K=(x_1,\ldots,x_r)\subset C$. Then $C/K=R$ and $K^{r(n-1)+1}=0$ by counting monomial exponents (for $r=0$, $K=0$). The nilpotent base-change idempotent lemma, applied also to every finite $C$-algebra, gives unique idempotent lifting modulo $K$. Thus $(C,K)$ is henselian in the stated idempotent-lifting definition.

### Returned Step 8 -> lem-sfb-compatible-kummer-tower-cech

Put $\Gamma_n=(\mathbf Z/n\mathbf Z)^r$. Over $X_n$ the second root of each parameter satisfies
$$y_i^n-x_i^n=\prod_{a\bmod n}(y_i-\zeta_n^a x_i).$$
The ideals for distinct $a$ are comaximal, because their root differences are units: $x_i$ is invertible on $X_n$ and $\zeta_n^a-\zeta_n^b$ is a unit by hypothesis. Repeated Chinese remainders therefore identify
$$X_n\times_{U^\circ}X_n\simeq\coprod_{g\in\Gamma_n}X_n,$$
with comparison $(x,g)\mapsto(x,gx)$. The identification sends the third vertex $h(gx)$ to $(hg)x$, and is compatible with every divisibility transition by the chosen compatible roots. Inverse limits of these actual affine scheme diagrams give
$$X\times_{U^\circ}X\simeq X[\varprojlim_n\Gamma_n].$$
The roots-product lemma evaluated in the algebraically closed field of $\bar x$ identifies $\varprojlim_n\Gamma_n$ with $G$: the chosen primitive roots identify each $\mathbf Z/n\mathbf Z$ with its root group there. This also fixes the same coordinate labels as the rotations on $H_n$. The meaning of $X[G]$ is precisely the indicated inverse limit of finite disjoint unions.

### Returned Step 9 -> lem-sfb-connected-abelian-tower-normalized-descent
