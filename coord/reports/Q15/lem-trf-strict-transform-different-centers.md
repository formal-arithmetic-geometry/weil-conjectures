## Returned proof preserved verbatim

### Returned step 1

By [lemma-nagata-effective-cartier-is-invertible] the effective Cartier ideal $\mathcal I_D$ is invertible, so [lemma-nagata-blowup-invertible-factor] applies to $\mathcal L=\mathcal I_D$, $\mathcal J=\mathcal I_Z$ and gives the fixed isomorphism $\varphi:S'_D\cong S'$ over $S$, i.e. $b\circ\varphi=b_D$. Hence $\mathrm{id}_X\times_S\varphi:X\times_SS'_D\to X\times_SS'$ is an isomorphism commuting with the projections to $X$ [def-pc-fibre-product], and it carries the pullback of $\mathcal F$ on the one side to the pullback on the other. The centre ideal $\mathcal I_Z\mathcal I_D$ is of finite type (locally generated by the products of finitely many generators of $\mathcal I_Z$ with one generator of $\mathcal I_D$), so both module strict transforms are defined [def-trf-module-strict-transform]. Because $\varphi$ is over $S$, it maps $b_D^{-1}V(\mathcal I_Z\mathcal I_D)=b_D^{-1}(Z\cup D)$ onto $b^{-1}(Z\cup D)$. So, by the definition [def-trf-module-strict-transform], after transport by $\mathrm{id}_X\times_S\varphi$ the two kernels are, inside $p^*\mathcal F$ on $X\times_SS'$: $\mathcal K$, the local sections vanishing off $q^{-1}b^{-1}(Z)$, and $\mathcal K_D$, the local sections vanishing off $q^{-1}b^{-1}(Z\cup D)$. It remains to show $\mathcal K=\mathcal K_D$. Both are quasi-coherent submodules of $p^*\mathcal F$ by [lem-trf-strict-transform-sheaf-kernel] (applied to the centres $Z$ and $Z_D$).

Uses: lemma-nagata-effective-cartier-is-invertible, lemma-nagata-blowup-invertible-factor, def-pc-fibre-product, def-trf-module-strict-transform, lem-trf-strict-transform-sheaf-kernel

### Returned step 2

Equality of two quasi-coherent submodules can be checked on an affine open cover, so we compute on charts. Cover $S$ by affine opens $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ on which $\mathcal I_D=(x)$ with $x\in A$ a nonzerodivisor [def-nagata-effective-cartier-ideal] and $\mathcal I_Z$ corresponds to a finitely generated ideal $I=(f_0,\ldots,f_r)$ (finite type, [pqf-def-coherent]). Over $\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ the blow-up $S'$ is covered by the charts $\operatorname{Spec}(A[I/f_i])$ [prop-nagata-affine-blowup-chart-cover, def-nagata-blowup-chart-ring]. Fix $a=f_i$ and an affine open $\operatorname{Spec}(R)\subseteq f^{-1}\operatorname{Spec}(A)$ with $\mathcal F|_{\operatorname{Spec}R}=\widetilde M$. Then $W=\operatorname{Spec}(R)\times_{\operatorname{Spec}A}\operatorname{Spec}(A[I/a])=\operatorname{Spec}(C_a(A,R,I))$ [def-nagata-strict-transform-basechange-chart-ring], and these $W$ cover $X\times_SS'$; on $W$ the pullback is $p^*\mathcal F|_W=\widetilde N$ with $N=M\otimes_R(R\otimes_AA[I/a])=M\otimes_AA[I/a]$ [def-pc-module-pullback]. Elementary: since $f=a\cdot(f/a)$ for $f\in I$ and $a\in I$, $IA[I/a]=aA[I/a]$, so $W\cap q^{-1}b^{-1}(Z)=V(a)$ and $W\cap q^{-1}b^{-1}(Z\cup D)=V(xa)$; and since $\widetilde N(D(r))=N_r$, a section $s\in N$ restricts to zero on $D(r)$ iff $r^ns=0$ for some $n$ (Tag 01I8). Hence the global sections over $W$ of the quasi-coherent submodules $\mathcal K|_W$ and $\mathcal K_D|_W$ are $\Gamma_{(a)}(N)$ and $\Gamma_{(xa)}(N)$ [def-nagata-power-torsion], and $\mathcal K|_W=\Gamma_{(a)}(N)^\sim$, $\mathcal K_D|_W=\Gamma_{(xa)}(N)^\sim$.

Uses: def-nagata-effective-cartier-ideal, pqf-def-coherent, prop-nagata-affine-blowup-chart-cover, def-nagata-blowup-chart-ring, def-nagata-strict-transform-basechange-chart-ring, def-pc-module-pullback, def-nagata-power-torsion

### Returned step 3
