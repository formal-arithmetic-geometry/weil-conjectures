### Proposed argument for the quotient child (not a split of that child)

In a coherent topos choose a small finite-limit-closed coherent generating category G as in the definition. Every quasi-compact object has a finite jointly epic family from G. Pullback stability of jointly epic families follows from local surjectivity of sheaf maps, with coproduct sections locally in a summand. Finite sums and epic images of quasi-compact objects are quasi-compact by pulling back a covering family and selecting finite indices. Testing quasi-separatedness on pairs of objects of G suffices: first take finite G-covers of the two arbitrary quasi-compact test objects and pull them back.

For clarity, coherent objects in this topos have coherent products. The terminal object is coherent: products of members of G are members of G, hence quasi-compact, and the preceding test proves quasi-separatedness of the terminal object. Thus products of quasi-compact objects are quasi-compact. To test quasi-separatedness of X times Y for coherent X,Y, use D,E in G. D times E is coherent. Each of D times_X E and D times_Y E is a quasi-compact subobject of D times E. Their intersection is quasi-compact by quasi-separatedness of D times E and equals D times_(X times Y) E. The test on G proves the assertion. A quasi-compact subobject of a quasi-separated object is coherent because pullbacks over that subobject equal pullbacks over the ambient object.

Now let p:P to A be epic with P coherent and R=P times_A P quasi-compact. A is quasi-compact as an epic image. To test quasi-separatedness choose D,E in G mapping to A. Pull p back over each; cover those pullbacks by G and compose to D,E. Because D,E are quasi-compact, retain finitely many components covering each. These components D_i,E_j are coherent G-objects equipped with lifts to P. Their products D_i times E_j are coherent. Since P times P is quasi-separated, the pullback (D_i times E_j) times_(P times P) R is quasi-compact; it is D_i times_A E_j. These finitely many objects jointly cover D times_A E, making that pullback quasi-compact. The test on G proves A quasi-separated. This establishes the quotient criterion without a generator-preservation assertion and without assuming a quasi-compact subobject is automatically quasi-compact.
