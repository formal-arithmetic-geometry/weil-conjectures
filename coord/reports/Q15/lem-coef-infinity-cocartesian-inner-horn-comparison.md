## Statement dispute repaired

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the predecessor version of `lem-coef-infinity-relative-joyal-factorization` asserted, for every map $C\to\Delta^3$, a quasicategory $C'$ equivalent to the inverse horn and a map $g:C'\to C$ restricting to that horn. Take $C=\Lambda^3_2$ with its inclusion into $\Delta^3$. A quasicategory $C'$ contains a filler of its embedded identity horn; composing with $g$ would extend the identity of $\Lambda^3_2$ to $\Delta^3\to\Lambda^3_2$. No such simplex exists: the image would have vertices $0,1,2,3$, but the horn lacks that nondegenerate 3-simplex. Thus the predecessor statement is false.

The corrected auxiliary statement is exactly the extension part of HA Lemma 3.1.2.4: an inner fibration $E\to\Lambda^3_2$ with cocartesian lifts over $1\to2$ extends by a categorical-equivalence monomorphism to a quasicategory $E'$ over $\Delta^3$, with the inverse horn fixed. It asserts no map back to a given total space. Step 6 obtains that map from the Joyal lifting property of the original inner fibration, with its hypotheses verified. The item's own statement has not changed. The earlier citation to HTT Lemma 3.1.2.4 was incorrect; this is a lemma of HA.
