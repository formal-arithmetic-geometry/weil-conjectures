## Source error

HA p.331 first tests whether the HORN base map factors through H, but what its easy case needs is whether its unique SIMPLEX extension factors through H. The n=2 horn with vertex labels 0,1,3 is an explicit counterexample to that inference.

More seriously, let the ordered inserted-vertex simplex have vertices (a,x,y,b,c), with base labels (0,1,2,2,3). The printed K0 is generated by {a,x,y,b} and {x,y,b,c}; K1 adds the original Lambda^3_1 on {a,x,b,c}; and V is {a,y,b,c}. The actual K1 intersection V is the printed union U_y=(a*y*b) union (y*b*c) TOGETHER WITH the extra edge {a,c}. Thus delta chosen only on U_y need not agree with alpha there. The alleged pushout K1 to K2 is not the claimed pushout along U_y.

Finite-face enumeration gives cell counts by dimension K0=(5,9,7,2), K1=(5,10,8,2), K2=(5,10,10,3), and Delta^4=(5,10,10,5,1). Their Euler characteristics are respectively 1,1,2,1. Therefore the printed inclusion K2 into Delta^4 is not even a weak homotopy equivalence, so that asserted categorical-equivalence step cannot be repaired merely by choosing delta more carefully. This is an error in the printed proof, not a statement counterexample. The replacement avoids K0,K1,K2 altogether and makes the needed face-localization comparison a separate, honestly unsplit lemma. The second reader should retain this source-error report for upward reporting.
