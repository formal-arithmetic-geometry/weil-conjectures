1. All categories and slice sites below use the common universe in the statement. The category $\operatorname{Mod}(R)$ is Grothendieck abelian by [lem-weil-pbc-ring-sheaves-grothendieck], and hence has enough injectives by [lem-sp-grothendieck-enough-injectives]. Abelian sheaves have enough injectives by [lem-weil-pbc-site-sheaves-enough-injectives]. The same theorem applied to $\mathcal C$ with only isomorphism coverings gives enough injectives for $\operatorname{PAb}(\mathcal C)$: the sheaf condition for an isomorphism covering imposes no condition on a presheaf. Choose a degreewise monomorphic quasi-isomorphism $r_M:M[0]\to I_M^\bullet$, with injective terms and $I_M^n=0$ for $n<0$, using [lem-weil-pbc-complex-injective-resolution-mono].

2. Fix $V\in\mathcal C$. The forgetful functor $j_V:\mathcal C/V\to\mathcal C$ is continuous and cocontinuous: coverings in the slice are exactly the underlying coverings, and a covering of an object $W$ lifts to the slice by composing every member with $W\to V$; covering pullbacks have the same underlying objects. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-module-sheafification-restriction]. The restriction $R[P]|_V$ is the sheafification, as an $R|_V$-module, of the presheaf $W/V\mapsto\bigoplus_{b:W\to P}R(W)$. For each fixed $a:W\to V$, pairing $b$ with $a$ identifies this presheaf with the free $R|_V$-module presheaf on the representable object $(V\times P\to V)$ of $\mathcal C/V$. This identification sends $e_b$ to $e_{(a,b)}$ and commutes with every restriction; the sheafification comparison preserves these generator images.

3. The definition of sheaf Hom [def-pur-sheaf-hom], the module sheafification adjunction [def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification], and [lem-weil-pbc-free-yoneda-presheaf-module] on $\mathcal C/V$ now give an additive bijection
$$\theta_{P,M,V}:H_P(M)(V)\xrightarrow{\sim}M(V\times P).$$
Explicitly, evaluate a morphism $R[P]|_V\to M|_V$ at $e_{\mathrm{pr}_P}$ over $(V\times P\to V)$. Conversely $s\in M(V\times P)$ sends $e_b$ over $(W\xrightarrow{a}V)$ to $s|_{(a,b)}$, and extends uniquely through sheafification. These formulas are inverse, preserve addition, and show that restriction along $c:V'\to V$ corresponds to restriction along $c\times1_P$. A module map $f:M\to N$ acts on $s$ by $f_{V\times P}$, and $u:P'\to P$ acts by restriction along $1_V\times u$. Thus $\theta$ is an isomorphism of abelian presheaves $\iota U H_P(M)\cong t_P\iota UM$, natural in both variables. Applying $a$ and its counit $a\iota\cong\mathrm{id}$ gives $UH_P\cong a t_P\iota U$.

4. The functor $U$ is exact by [lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-colimits-forgetful], and $a$ is exact while $\iota$ is left exact by [lem-weil-pbc-sheafification-exact]. Exact evaluation of abelian presheaves [lem-weil-pbc-abelian-presheaves-evaluation-exact] shows that $t_P$ is exact: exactness of its value at $V$ is just exactness at $V\times P$. Hence $U,a,t_P$ induce their termwise functors on derived categories by [lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-factorization]. Also $H_P$ is additive and left exact: the slice Hom description sends a kernel to a kernel, and kernels of module sheaves are sectionwise.
