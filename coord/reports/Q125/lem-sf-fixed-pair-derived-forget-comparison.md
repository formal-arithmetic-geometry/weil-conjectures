### lem-sf-fixed-pair-created-abelian-structure: original steps 1-2

Original step 1 (verbatim):

Work in the same chosen universe for all small diagrams. By [lem-sfb-grothendieck-filtered-finite-limits], $\mathcal C$ has all small limits and colimits. By [lem-galois-equivalence-preserves-limits], $F$ and its chosen quasi-inverse preserve these. For any small diagram $(X_i,\theta_i)$ of fixed pairs, put $V=\lim_i X_i$. Give $V$ the isomorphism $FV\cong\lim_i FX_i\xrightarrow{\lim\theta_i}\lim_i X_i=V$. The projections are fixed-pair morphisms. If a cone has fixed-pair morphisms as legs, its unique underlying mediating map commutes with the structure isomorphisms: check this after every projection, which determines a map into $V$. Thus $U_F$ creates these limits. The same construction with colimits, using coprojections to check the commuting equation, shows that $U_F$ creates small colimits. We use the category and its commuting equation from [def-weil-endofunctor-fixed-pair-category].

Uses: lem-sfb-grothendieck-filtered-finite-limits, lem-galois-equivalence-preserves-limits, def-weil-endofunctor-fixed-pair-category.

Original step 2 (verbatim):

The fixed-pair commuting equation is closed under sums and differences of morphisms because $F$ is additive; hence the Hom groups and composition are inherited from $\mathcal C$. Step 1 gives a zero object, finite biproducts, kernels and cokernels with their underlying constructions in $\mathcal C$. Also $U_F$ reflects zero objects: the only structure map on a zero object is its unique isomorphism. A morphism in a category with kernels is monic exactly when its kernel is zero: if the kernel is zero and $fa=fb$, the map $a-b$ factors through that kernel; the converse follows from the kernel equation. Dually, an epimorphism is characterized by zero cokernel. Thus $U_F$ preserves and reflects monomorphisms and epimorphisms. If $f$ is monic in the fixed-pair category, $U_Ff$ is the kernel of its cokernel in $\mathcal C$; the created kernel of the created cokernel identifies $f$ with that kernel, since the underlying comparison is invertible. Its inverse commutes with the structure maps by multiplying the commuting equation for the comparison by its inverse. The dual argument treats epimorphisms. This proves all clauses of [def-sp-abelian]. Consequently the fixed-pair category is abelian and $U_F$ is exact. More generally, an underlying invertible morphism has a commuting inverse by the same multiplication, so $U_F$ is conservative.

Uses: def-weil-endofunctor-fixed-pair-category, def-sp-abelian.

### lem-sf-fixed-pair-free-integer-adjunction: original steps 3-4

Original step 3 (verbatim):

Write $P_nM=F^nM$, using iterates of the chosen quasi-inverse for $n<0$. The unit and counit isomorphisms provide $c_n:F(P_nM)\xrightarrow{\sim}P_{n+1}M$, with $P_0M=M$. Put $A(M)=\bigoplus_{n\in\mathbf Z}P_nM$. Coproduct preservation by $F$ identifies $FA(M)$ with $\bigoplus_n F(P_nM)$; use $\bigoplus_n c_n$ and reindex by $n\mapsto n+1$ to obtain $\sigma_M:FA(M)\xrightarrow{\sim}A(M)$. In particular $\sigma_M F(\iota_n)=\iota_{n+1}c_n$, where $\iota_n$ is the coprojection. For $f:M\to N$, take $A(f)=\bigoplus_nP_n(f)$; naturality of $c_n$ proves $A(f)\sigma_M=\sigma_NF(A(f))$ on each coprojection. Identities and composition hold on each coprojection. This constructs the additive free-pair functor $A:\mathcal C\to\mathcal C^F$ as an actual coproduct construction.

Uses: lem-galois-equivalence-preserves-limits, def-weil-endofunctor-fixed-pair-category.

Original step 4 (verbatim):

For a fixed pair $(X,\theta)$ and $a_0:M\to X$, there is a unique family $a_n:P_nM\to X$ satisfying $a_{n+1}c_n=\theta F(a_n)$. For $n\geq0$ this recursively defines $a_{n+1}$. For $n<0$, full faithfulness of the equivalence $F$ defines $a_n$ uniquely by $F(a_n)=\theta^{-1}a_{n+1}c_n$. Its coproduct map $a:A(M)\to X$ satisfies the fixed-pair equation, precisely because these equations hold for every $n$. Conversely any fixed-pair map out of $A(M)$ satisfies this recursion on coprojections, so is determined by its zeroth component. Therefore restriction to $\iota_0$ gives a natural bijection $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal C^F}(A(M),(X,\theta))\cong\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal C}(M,X)$ with inverse the displayed recursion. Naturality follows by precomposing $a_n$ with $P_n(f)$ or postcomposing it with a commuting map of pairs; both operations satisfy the same recursion and zeroth component. This proves $A\dashv U_F$.

Uses: def-weil-endofunctor-fixed-pair-category.

### lem-sf-created-grothendieck-exact-integer-sum: original steps 5-6

Original step 5 (verbatim):

Filtered colimits in $\mathcal C^F$ are exact: forget a filtered system of short exact sequences, take its exact colimit in $\mathcal C$, and use step 1 and the created kernels and cokernels to read the resulting sequence back in $\mathcal C^F$. Choose a generator $G$ of $\mathcal C$. Given a proper subobject $N\subsetneq X$ of fixed pairs, $U_FN\subsetneq U_FX$ is proper, since $U_F$ preserves monomorphisms and reflects isomorphisms. The generator property supplies a map $G\to U_FX$ not factoring through $U_FN$. Its adjoint $A(G)\to X$ cannot factor through $N$, by naturality of the bijection of step 4. Hence $A(G)$ is a generator. Small colimits, abelianness and exact filtered colimits have already been verified, so [def-sp-grothendieck-abelian] proves $\mathcal C^F$ is Grothendieck abelian. The same arguments with $(\mathcal D,H)$ prove this for $\mathcal D^H$ and prove exactness and conservativity of $U_H$.

Uses: def-sp-grothendieck-abelian, lem-sfb-grothendieck-filtered-finite-limits.

Original step 6 (verbatim):

Direct sums of short exact sequences in $\mathcal C$ are exact. Indeed, first take the finite subsums, which are short exact: their kernels and cokernels are the finite biproducts of the kernels and cokernels, as follows by testing the component projections and injections. Their filtered colimit over finite subsets of the index set is the full direct sum. To verify this identification, a compatible family of maps from finite subsums to $T$ is precisely one map from each summand to $T$, the coproduct universal property. Filtered colimits preserve kernels by [lem-sfb-grothendieck-filtered-finite-limits] and preserve cokernels by their colimit universal property. Thus this filtered colimit remains short exact. Every $P_n$ is an additive equivalence and is exact by [lem-galois-equivalence-preserves-limits]. Consequently $U_FA=\bigoplus_nP_n$ takes short exact sequences to short exact sequences. Since step 2 computes kernels and cokernels under $U_F$ and detects zero objects, it reflects exactness, proving that $A$ is exact.

Uses: lem-sfb-grothendieck-filtered-finite-limits, lem-galois-equivalence-preserves-limits.

### lem-sf-fixed-pair-common-derived-resolution: original steps 9-10

Original step 9 (verbatim):

For a commuting morphism $a$, naturality $e_Y H L(a)=L F(a)e_X$ and $L(a)L(\theta_X)=L(\theta_Y)L F(a)$ show that $L(a)$ commutes with the new structures. These formulas give an additive functor and the equality $U_H\widetilde L=LU_F$ on objects and maps, hence on complexes and homotopies. It is left exact: $U_H$ creates finite limits, $L$ and $U_F$ preserve them, and the underlying comparison for a finite-limit cone is invertible. Its inverse is a fixed-pair morphism by step 2, so the cone is limiting. This establishes the transported left exact functor without adding a hypothesis on $L$.
