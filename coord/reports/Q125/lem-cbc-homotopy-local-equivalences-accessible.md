## What could not be finished

The one new prerequisite is deliberately unsplit as required. Its future split must construct one Reedy-cofibrant resolution of each member of S, take the boundary corner maps in every degree (including degree zero), and adjoin a small original generating set J of trivial cofibrations. Fixed chosen resolutions suffice by Theorem 17.8.4/Proposition 17.8.8. Lemma 3.3.11 supplies the local-object criterion. Proposition 17.8.14 gives orthogonality of each generalized horn to every local fibrant terminal map. J consists of original weak equivalences. For pushouts the current object is cofibrant, and the horns have cofibrant endpoints, so Proposition 17.8.12 applies without properness. Attaching coproducts can be well-ordered. At limits, use right mapping complexes into a simplicial fibrant resolution of Z; restriction maps along core cofibrations are trivial fibrations, and inverse limits of a continuous tower of trivial fibrations are trivial fibrations (solve each boundary lift successively, taking the compatible maps at limit stages). For a member of the full weak saturation whose domain is cofibrant, the small-object/retract argument factors that map from its own fixed cofibrant domain, making it a retract of a core cellular map. This avoids assuming cofibrancy of the domain of an unrelated retract presentation. This argument is report material, not a proof embedded in the new statement.
