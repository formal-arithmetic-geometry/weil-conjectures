For the partial-derived-adjoint assertion, fix cofibrant A. Restrict the specified comparison of B to (A,D) for cofibrant D. The left derived functor of F(A,-) is presented by applying F(A,-) to cofibrant replacements. Brown preservation identifies its restriction to cofibrant D with qF(A,D), including natural transformations. Restriction along the cofibrant localization is fully faithful and essentially surjective, by the preceding inclusion comparison and localization universal property. The comparison therefore extends to an equivalence of the two coherent functors on the whole localized category. Repeat in the other variable. Each derived partial functor has its derived right adjoint; every localized object is equivalent to a cofibrant representative, so evaluation of B at that equivalence transfers the adjunction to every partial functor.
