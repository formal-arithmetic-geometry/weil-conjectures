For `lem-cbc-complete-proetale-open-limit-comparison`, use the natural normalized strict Roos isomorphisms. The product comparison identifies the two components of the signed cone and intertwines the transition operator because its projections are the functor's images of the original projections. Exactness carries the cone to the cone, so conjugating that chain isomorphism by the two Roos comparisons gives the stated derived isomorphism. A strict augmentation is represented in the cone by its compatible product family and zero shifted-product component; the diagonal cone map carries it to the image family's augmentation. Naturality and independence follow from descent of those actual chain maps. For open extension, the product of extended modules restricts to the original product on the open and to zero on the closed complement, because both inverse images preserve products. The ringed localization sequence then identifies it with extension by zero of that product. This uses no qcqs assumption on the ambient scheme.

For `lem-cbc-complete-proetale-strict-tower-realization`, compute restriction of scalars and open extension using the same localized presheaf of sections. Restricting the scalar action and extending the presheaf commute, and their canonical sheafification comparisons preserve the units. The constant/completed equivalence identifies reduction towers, their actual quotient transitions and completion units. The proper factor is the strict normalized mate from the existing normalization lemma; the open factor is the specified derived limit of beta. Substitution in the ordered composite, with the direct-image/limit comparison, yields precisely the image of the composite tower morphism. Functoriality of derived limit combines its two factors. This is a comparison of those specified maps, not an inference from equality of evaluations.

For `lem-cbc-complete-proetale-refinement-exchange-coherence`, apply the general strict-tower proper/open calculus to the refinement square. Its finite-origin criterion gives invertibility. Its base-change interchange, whiskered by the target proper direct image and combined with proper mate pasting, gives the equation between the refinement and the two compact-support exchanges. Its vertical pasting law gives identity and composition of refinement maps. Cofilteredness supplies a common dominating compactification and a further equalizer of the maps to each original compactification. Cancel the common invertible refinement factors to obtain independence and then the cocycle. The sources of these maps are strict derived towers throughout.

For `lem-cbc-complete-proetale-composition-pasting-coherence`, the cartesian proper/open square of the intermediate open scheme gives D. Whisker these two identities by the outer proper direct image and the open extension of X, move disjoint transformations by naturality, and use the three proper composition identifications.
