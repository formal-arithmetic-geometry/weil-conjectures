For each coefficient chain map, the section maps commute with both differentials. ### Old step 2 → lem-sfb-hypercover-normalized-single-site-descent

### Old step 6 → lem-sfb-hypercover-derived-unit-chain-squares

Naturality of the ordinary unit and $b\,h^*i\simeq j$ give $ai\simeq h_*j\circ\eta_{M[0]}$. Thus $Qa\circ(Qi)^{-1}$ is precisely the actual derived adjunction unit, under the resolution identification $Rh_*h^*M[0]\cong Qh_*J$. This fixes the normalization, rather than choosing an adjunction on isomorphic functors.
