Edge cases checked mathematically before writing:

- For q=(0), the polynomial ring is a domain and its local extension of zero
  is zero, so all positive U_d vanish; U=k and T contains only zero. Its
  adjoin is k. This includes N=1, where the only relevant homogeneous prime
  is (0).
- For a coordinate linear prime q=(Z_1) with N>=2, U_d=k Z_1^d and
  U=k[Z_1]. The form Z_1 is additive, so the claimed generation holds.
- In characteristic p, over the imperfect field F_p(a), the relevant prime
  `(Z_1^p-a Z_2^p)` includes the additive homogeneous form of degree p.
  The statement includes this generator and does not replace additive forms
  by linear forms or divide by factorials.
- The zero polynomial is homogeneous in every degree and passes the positive
  degree and identity conditions; constants of nonzero value do not pass
  the positive homogeneous condition. The d=0 membership imposes no extra
  restriction because the zeroth ideal power is top.
- The irrelevant maximal ideal is explicitly excluded, as the node requires;
  N=0 is excluded by exactly the node's hypothesis, not by a universe choice.
- On a finite field, polynomial functions can agree while polynomials differ;
  the two-tuple `MvPolynomial` equality avoids that weakening.
