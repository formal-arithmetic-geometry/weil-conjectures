### STATEMENT-DISPUTED: a literal reading of “return”

A return is not a literal equality `g_Y^n ∘ xbar = xbar` of morphisms from `Spec k`. Already take `Y_0 = Spec k_0`, `Y = Spec k`, `xbar = id` and `n = 1`. The two sides would be `Spec(gamma)` and `id`, which differ since geometric Frobenius is nontrivial on the algebraic closure. The correct equation is `g_Y^n ∘ xbar = xbar ∘ Spec(gamma^n)`. The new text supplies this equation and the canonical point transport before applying the iterated Weil structure. This makes the original source's intended operator explicit and changes no hypothesis, coefficient field or trace convention.

The derived stalk `K_xbar = L xbar^* M` remains a point-category object. I separately name `C_xbar = Fib_s(K_xbar)` as its E-module realization for cohomology and ordinary traces, rather than identifying the entire pro-etale point category with modules. This is important for the completed integral coefficient convention inherited at the rational stage.
