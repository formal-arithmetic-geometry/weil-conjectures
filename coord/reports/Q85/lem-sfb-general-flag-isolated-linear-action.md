Its scheme-theoretic form spells out the standard projective-chart calculation omitted by the eigenvector wording of 13.51. On an eigenbasis chart z_i != 0 the ratio z_j/z_i has character chi_j/chi_i, so the universal fixed ideal is generated precisely by ratios with unequal characters; Laurent monomials are independent over arbitrary coefficient algebras, including nonreduced algebras. These ideals glue to the disjoint projective character spaces. The same coordinate ideals appear for any cocharacter separating the characters. Such a cocharacter exists because integer vectors (1,N,...,N^{r-1}) avoid the zeros of the finitely many nonzero difference polynomials; for rank zero take the trivial map. For a closed invariant X, monicity of X -> P(E) makes the fixed condition equivalent to the ambient one, so the stated fibre product and its invariant-chart restrictions have the required universal property. This argument is recorded here, not put into the statement-only node as a proof.
