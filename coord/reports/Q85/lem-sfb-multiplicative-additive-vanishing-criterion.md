## New nodes

lem-sfb-finite-algebra-split-or-dual-quotient: a finite-dimensional commutative unital F-algebra over an algebraically closed field is either F^n or has a surjective F-algebra map onto F[epsilon]. This is the finite-algebra assertion in Milne's proof of 12.18(b) => (c), p.237, not a group-scheme vanishing criterion or a restatement of either direction of the item. Its intended later proof uses the finite product decomposition into local Artinian factors; their residue fields are F. In a nonfield local factor B, the maximal ideal m is nonzero and m/m^2 is nonzero by Nakayama. A nonzero linear functional m/m^2 -> F gives the surjection B -> F[epsilon], c+u -> c+lambda(u)epsilon. Compose with the projection onto B. If all factors are fields they are copies of F. This argument is recorded here, not in the unsplit node's statement or proof fields.
