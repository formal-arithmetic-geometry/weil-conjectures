## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED — correction of ordinary compact-support completion.

The original item sets H=Rp_Y* c!K, claims it complete, and asserts c!Rlim ≅ Rlim J_c. The current blueprint proof similarly infers completeness from exactness. Exactness only handles finite kernels, cokernels and finite tensor constructions; it does not imply this countable-limit assertion. The upstream project explicitly warns of the problem at lean/WeilConjectures/SixFunctors/ProEtale/FiniteCoefficient/Derived/CompletedExchange.lean:266–293 and defines compact-support exchange with completed target.

Here is a counterexample retaining bounded-below compatible finite etale origins. Let R be a strictly henselian discrete valuation ring with fraction field k and uniformizer t; choose a prime l different from the residue characteristic, let A=Z_l, I=(l), and j:eta=Spec(k)→T=Spec(R). The coefficient sheaf K=A_eta=lim (Z/l^n)_eta is intrinsically complete with the compatible origins L_n=(Z/l^n)_eta in degree zero. Compactify j by (j,id_T), and take h=id_T. This is a supplied compatible tower with proper p_Y=id_T and quasi-compact open c=j.

To see failure of completeness of j!K on the pro-etale site, use the affine pro-etale object V=Spec(B), where B is the filtered colimit of B_m=R×k^m. The transition replaces the R factor by R×k via r↦(r,r) and retains the existing k factors. These are etale transition maps and R→B is ind-etale. Equivalently B consists of sequences in k eventually equal to an element r∈R, with finitely many exceptional k entries. Its spectrum contains isolated clopen generic points v_1,v_2,… and a limiting copy of Spec(R), with closed point s_infinity. Every neighbourhood of s_infinity contains all but finitely many v_m. In the generic open V_eta the v_m accumulate at the limiting generic point.

For every a≥1, put the value l^m at v_m for m<a and zero elsewhere, as a section with Z/l^a coefficients. Its support is a finite union of clopen points, so it gives s_a∈(j!(Z/l^a)_eta)(V). These sections are compatible. They define a section of lim_a j!(Z/l^a)_eta on V. A section of j!K mapping to this family would have value l^m≠0 at every v_m and zero at the limiting generic point. Its nonzero support accumulates at s_infinity, outside V_eta, so it is not a section of extension by zero. (The support criterion here is the usual open-extension support criterion: a section must be locally zero near the complementary closed locus.) Thus j!K→lim_a j!K_a is not surjective on V. Countable products are exact on pro-etale module sheaves, so for these degree-zero towers H^0(Rlim)=lim, and the quotient completion map cannot be an isomorphism. The strict open tensor comparison identifies its reductions with j!K_a. Hence the original H is not complete even with the item's finite origins and lower bounds.

The same example disproves the blanket product comparison used in lem-cbc-ringed-open-extension-products: take sections supported at v_m in the m-th factor. The family defines a section of the product of the extended sheaves, but cannot be a section of the extension of the product, because its union of supports meets s_infinity in its closure. The proof of that node relies on closed restriction preserving arbitrary products, which is precisely the unsupported inference. Its downstream lem-cbc-completed-extension-zero-comparison and lem-cbc-complete-proetale-strict-tower-realization cannot be used to justify the imported ordinary endpoints.

I corrected the item to H=the completed compact-support output, with constant-coordinate Hbar=C_Y(Rp_Y* J_c Kbar). This is canonically Rp_Y* C_U J_c Kbar and L_Y F_f M. The exchange target is completed too. The replacement mu is a comparison on completed support, not the false arbitrary-tower comparison Rp_Y* J_c L_X→L_YF_f. The finite reductions, retained witnesses, actual strict maps, quotient augmentations and the two successive-exchange substitutions are retained with their proper types. The proof still follows the unit's finite proper/open then completion route. No hypothesis is silently added to rescue ordinary extension.
