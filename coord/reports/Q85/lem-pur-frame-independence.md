## Source error

The item's statement is retained. Its imported proof needs corrections. I read the current main-20261015 sk-prop-an/lem-pur-frame-independence.proof.md and its statement, rather than treating the older unit binding as the current proof text.

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the intermediate assertion in the imported proof that the supported group is the constant sheaf Lambda_T is false. Take U=A^1_k and Z={0}. The local supported sheaf restricts to zero on T minus Z_T, whereas a nonzero constant A_T restricts nontrivially there; a single global cohomology group is not a sheaf on T in the first place. The supported Kunneth statement supplies a derived exterior-product isomorphism for separated finite-type schemes, not this sheaf identification. It is replaced by the specific projection isomorphism on supported cohomology and the fact that both section pullbacks invert it. This is the source's interpolation route, with its missing homotopy argument made an auxiliary lemma.

The source's assertion that the frame class is a section of the local cohomology sheaf also requires justification: global H^{2c} with support is not defined to be sections of that sheaf. The new bottom-degree lemma supplies lower vanishing and the edge isomorphism. It is deliberately not a purity isomorphism, not an orientation, and not a claim that the sheaf is constant. Its proof can proceed independently by local coordinate zero-section lower vanishing and the local-to-global spectral sequence; it does not use frame independence. Milne 16.7/16.8 alone only treats pairs smooth over a field; the relative-base geometry is Stacks 0FUE. No finite-type theorem is applied to a strict henselization or another out-of-domain scheme.

The Smith-normal-form wording is invalid for general local rings: not all local rings are principal ideal domains (for example k[x,y]_(x,y), whose maximal ideal needs two generators). Only elimination of invertible matrices by unit pivots is needed. There is also an unstated assertion that equal generating tuples are GL-equivalent. The local-generating-tuples lemma states precisely the needed fact, including redundant tuples. One way to prove it is to select a basis subtuple of M/mM, lift generation by Nakayama, eliminate redundant entries by shears, compare the two minimal generating subtuples by a matrix invertible modulo m, then use unit-pivot elimination on that invertible matrix. This argument is substantially more than an elementary one-line step and uses no Smith form.

Finally, Spec of a strict henselization is not generally an etale neighborhood. The proof now works with ordinary local rings only to obtain finite algebraic changes, and spreads the finitely many coefficients, inverse equations and tuple equalities to honest open neighborhoods. Also A^1_U -> V is not etale. Step 5 constructs the supported class by the explicit boundary/product formula on T without calling it an etale frame.
