### Original step 1 → lem-trf-flattening-ambient-module-preparation

Because $g:W\to T$ is of finite type, every affine open of $T$ has inverse image covered by finitely many affine opens of $W$; quasi-compactness of $T$ therefore gives a finite affine open cover $W=\bigcup_{i=1}^r W_i$.
