### Former step 1 -> lem-trf-coordinate-chart-localization-parameters (chart cover also remains in assembly step 1)

Put $I=(t_1,\ldots,t_m)$. By [prop-nagata-affine-blowup-chart-cover] and [def-nagata-blowup-chart-ring], the standard charts cover the blowup and have rings $C=A[I/t_i]=A[t_j/t_i:j\ne i]\subset A[1/t_i]$. Write $a=t_i$, $z_j=t_j/t_i$ for $j\ne i$, and $P=\prod_{j\ne i}z_j$. The relations are $t_j=az_j$, and hence $f=\prod_jt_j=a^mP$. By [lemma-nagata-chart-extended-ideal-principal], $IC=(a)$, so the complement of the center on this chart is $D(a)$. Each $C$ is finitely generated over the Noetherian ring $A$, and thus Noetherian by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-algebra-noetherian].

### Former step 2 -> lem-trf-coordinate-chart-localization-parameters

Fix a prime $y\subset C$, put $p=y\cap A$, $R=A_p$ and $S=C_y$. Localizing the generators and their relations identifies $C\otimes_A R$ with $R[IR/a]\subset R[1/a]$, and $S$ with its localization at the prime induced by $y$: both maps send every ratio to $t_j/a$, and the inverse follows by inverting the elements of $A\setminus p$. If $p\not\supset I$, choose $t_k\notin p$. The equation $t_k=az_k$ (or $t_k=a$ for $k=i$) forces $a\notin y$, and hence $a\notin p$. Thus $R[IR/a]=R$ and $S=R$. Each nonunit $z_j$ is then a unit multiple of $t_j$, and the pointwise hypothesis gives its extension to a regular system of parameters; multiplying selected parameter generators by units preserves their generating ideal. This proves regularity and the parameter assertion away from the center.

Cited nodes: def-nagata-blowup-chart-ring, def-weil-regular-scheme, def-weil-pbc-regular-system-of-parameters.

### Former step 4 -> lem-trf-parameter-product-closure-kernel

At $y$ this becomes $KS=\{h\in S:\exists n,\ a^nh\in fS\}$: clearing the localization denominators shows that $a^n h/s\in fS$ iff some $v\notin y$ satisfies $va^nh\in(f)$. Thus the closure is computed by saturation of $(f)$ by $a$, including all scheme structure.

### Former step 5 -> lem-trf-parameter-product-closure-kernel

We compute this saturation in $S$. Away from the center $a$ is a unit by Step 2, so $(f):a^\infty=(P)$. Over the center, Step 3 and [lem-trf-regular-local-ufd-boundary-factorization] say that $S$ is a domain and that $a$ and the nonunit factors of $P$ are pairwise nonassociate prime elements. Nonassociation follows also from their distinct cotangent-basis positions. If $a^nh\in(a^mP)$, each such prime factor of $P$ divides $h$, since it does not divide $a$; unique factorization therefore gives $P\mid h$. Conversely $h=Pq$ satisfies $a^mh=fq$. Hence again $(f):a^\infty=(P)$. All stalks of the closure ideal thus agree with those of $(P)$, so the chart strict transform is exactly $V(P)$. When $m=1$, $P=1$ and this closed subscheme is empty.

### Former step 6 -> lem-trf-etale-coordinate-divisor-parameters

Steps 2 and 3 prove the pointwise coordinate condition for the $m-1$ functions $z_j$ on the regular Noetherian chart $\operatorname{Spec}C$. In each local ring $P$ is a unit times the product of exactly the nonunit $z_j$. It is a nonzerodivisor, because that regular local ring is a domain by [lem-trf-regular-local-ufd-boundary-factorization]. A function whose multiplication is injective on all local rings has injective multiplication on the ring (a nonzero kernel element survives at some prime). Thus $V(P)$ is an effective Cartier divisor in the sense of [def-nagata-effective-cartier-ideal], and the equations give normal crossings by [def-trf-normal-crossings-divisor]. For $m\ge2$, apply [lem-trf-nc-coordinate-max-stratum] with $s=m-1$ and functions $(z_j)_{j\ne i}$: its geometric-branch conclusion is exactly $\#\{j\ne i:z_j\in y\}\le m-1$. For $m=1$ the empty divisor has zero branches. In particular an ordinary parameter hyperplane is not being assumed geometrically irreducible without the cited geometric-branch theorem.

### Former step 7 -> lem-trf-parameter-product-closure-kernel (the blowup comparison also remains in assembly step 4)

Let $v:W\to U$ be etale. Its flatness is part of [def-weil-etale-morphism]. The comparison of [def-nagata-blowup-flat-base-change-map] is an isomorphism by [thm-nagata-blowup-flat-base-change]. Localization and quotient commute with this tensor product by their defining universal properties. Thus the kernel is precisely the closure kernel for the base-changed product divisor, and Step 5 identifies it with the ideal generated by the pulled-back $P$. ### Former step 8 -> lem-trf-etale-coordinate-divisor-parameters

The projection $W\times_UU^+\to U^+$ is etale by [lem-weil-pbc-etale-scheme-base-change].
