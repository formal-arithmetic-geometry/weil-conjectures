## Review by N0

I read the node files against the sources listed above and checked delivery-errors B--E. Every new non-leaf has a self-contained statement, empty proof and proof_deps, the item's chapter and unit, created.item naming this item, and a filled blueprint origin. None is a clause of the equivalence merely renamed: completed Jacobian torsion is a local algebra criterion; its algebraization theorem applies to arbitrary complete quotients and gives only a finite-TYPE model; hom lifting allows a finite-type source and a target that need not be finite or etale; clopen lifting allows arbitrary separated finite-type schemes and a proper piece of the fibre. Finiteness and the equivalence are proved by assembling these inputs in the item.

Step 1 uses exactly the etale base-change statement of `lem-weil-pbc-etale-scheme-base-change` and the canonical natural tensor-to-completion comparison in `lem-weil-pbc-completion-finite-module-tensor`. The latter assumes a Noetherian base and a finite module, both supplied by the item. Its formula on pure tensors checks that its linear isomorphism preserves algebra multiplication and the unit; no extra ring-comparison theorem is silently used.

Step 2 uses `lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-algebra-noetherian` for C2, `lem-weil-pbc-finite-algebra-is-integral` and `lem-weil-pbc-integral-over-henselian-pair` to make (C2,IC2) henselian, and the new hom-lifting node with A=B, D=C1, C=C2. The source's finite-type assumption follows from a finite module generating set. Its etaleness is exactly the object's hypothesis. The lifting node gives uniqueness into C2, not just an approximation or a lift into some unrelated algebra. Continuity of all base-linear maps of completions follows from preservation of each power of I, and coordinatewise reduction gives B-hat linearity of completed maps. Thus the step establishes the actual Hom bijection required by the equivalence.

Step 3 uses `lem-weil-pbc-adic-completion-noetherian`, `lem-weil-pbc-module-completion-complete` with M=B, the new completed-Jacobian node with A=B-hat, and `lem-weil-pbc-adic-completion-mod-power`. All ideals used are finitely generated: B is Noetherian, as is B-hat. The last node explicitly identifies B-hat/I^n B-hat with B/I^n. Consequently the polynomial quotients over the two bases agree at every level. The coordinatewise inverse-limit identification respects partial differentiation, so the same conormal differential and its annihilation bound can be used over B. This is an identification of the displayed presentation, not an unsupported invariance assertion about an unspecified cotangent complex.

Step 4 applies the new algebraization node to that presentation, obtaining a finite-type D. It does not incorrectly call D finite. The completion-complete node, applied over D to the extended finitely generated ideal ID, explicitly identifies reduction of D-hat with D/ID. Its algebra compatibility is visible on residues. Combining it with D-hat isomorphic to E gives D/ID finite over B/I by reducing the finite B-hat-module generators of E. The Noetherianity of D needed in the text is provided by the finite-type/Noetherian node.

Step 5 uses affine-morphism permanence for separatedness of Spec D, and 01WN's exact proper/affine equivalence first to see that the finite closed fibre is proper. It then applies 0CT9 to the WHOLE closed fibre, which is trivially open and closed in itself. The ambient X need only be separated and finite type; no properness is assumed before this application. The new node's statement gives W proper over B and W0=X0. The closed-embedding-affine node applies because W is clopen, hence its inclusion is a homeomorphism onto a closed subspace. Affine-morphism permanence therefore gives W affine. The second use of the 01WN leaf now yields finiteness of W over B. Finally the added existing etale-composition node says precisely that an open immersion is etale and that a composite of etale maps is etale; these are the two assertions used to retain etaleness away from V(I).

Step 6 cites no new theorem. V(I^nD)=V(ID) follows primewise, so the inverse image of W on every infinitesimal closed fibre is the whole scheme, including its restricted structure sheaf. Thus the quotient ring isomorphisms are compatible, and their inverse limit gives C-hat=D-hat. With the earlier natural tensor comparison this produces an isomorphism over B-hat to E. The final quasi-inverse is obtained directly by choosing these representatives and lifting arrows through the Hom bijections; uniqueness proves identities and composition. This is elementary category bookkeeping after the two substantive properties have been proved. No non-elementary step exceeds 2000 characters.

Small-case checks: I=0 makes completion the identity, with no etale condition off the empty complement; nilpotent I likewise imposes only a bounded-power torsion condition, consistent with the two Jacobian nodes. For r=0, E is a complete quotient and the conormal map has zero target, so the statements still have their literal meaning. The zero algebra and empty Spec are allowed throughout. For clopen lifting Y may be empty (choose W empty); the item instead uses the whole finite closed fibre. No step assumes a field, a local ring, a DVR, flatness of E, excellence of B, a principal ideal or a nonempty fibre. The proper/affine leaf has no field restriction. The whole-fibre construction is necessary because a finite-type algebra with finite special fibre need not itself be finite before splitting off the proper part.
