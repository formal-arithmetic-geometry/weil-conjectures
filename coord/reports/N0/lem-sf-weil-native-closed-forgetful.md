The intended later proof of that foundational node is to use its displayed crossed-arrow category: a sheaf restricts to a base sheaf and its restrictions along the degree-one isomorphisms supply the equivariance map. The composition law gives all integer iterates. Conversely the iterated action defines restrictions along every crossed arrow, and sheaf gluing is the base gluing because the topology is generated by base covers. Limits and colimits are formed on the underlying sheaves and inherit the action. On sheaves of sets the right adjoint to forgetting is the orbit product, so forgetting is a geometric inverse image. On modules the orbit sum is its LEFT adjoint, determined by the zero summand, and is exact since the summands are images under exact autoequivalences and sheaf-module direct sums are exact. Diagonal tensor and conjugate Hom are checked by the ordinary tensor–Hom adjunction. This argument is recorded here, not inserted as a proof sketch into the unsplit node.
