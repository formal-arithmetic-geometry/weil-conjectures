## Allocation of all seven original arguments

Original Step 1 is the E=A, r=0 specialization of the existing pullback-line model, after the same bounded-flat existence/Noetherian checks (new Step 1). Original Steps 2 and 5 belong to lem-sp-etale-counit-scalar-model. Original Steps 3 and 4 belong to lem-sp-etale-sheaf-fold-scalar. Original Step 6 is new Step 3, and original Step 7 is new Step 4. Original Step 1

First check the input of $\sigma_e$. The schemes $U,V$ are Noetherian by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian], since a field is Noetherian. By [lem-sf-etale-flat-constructible], $K$ has a bounded flat constructible model $R$ supported in some finite interval $[a,b]$. This model is K-flat by [lem-weil-pbc-independent-bounded-flat-kflat]; its pullback is flat termwise by [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-derived-tensor-pullback] and supported in $[a,b]$. Tensoring that bounded flat model with any degree-zero module sheaf computes derived tensor and is still supported in $[a,b]$. Thus $e_A^*K$ has finite Tor amplitude. Inverse image is exact [prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact], so $\mathcal H^i(e_A^*K)=e^{-1}\mathcal H^i(K)$ and $e_A^*K$ is bounded. Each $F=\mathcal H^i(K)$ is constructible [def-weil-pbc-etale-constructible-sheaf]: there are a stratification $(\iota_k:Z_k\to V)$ with constructible strata $V_k$ and etale coverings $\{W_{kj}\to Z_k\}$ trivializing $\iota_k^{-1}F$ with finite values $M_{kj}$. The base changes $\iota'_k:Z'_k=Z_k\times_VU\to U$ form a stratification with strata $e^{-1}(V_k)$ [lemma-constructible-pullback-stratification], and these are constructible [lem-pur-scheme-constructible-preimage]. With $h_k:Z'_k\to Z_k$, one has $\iota_k'^{-1}e^{-1}F\cong h_k^{-1}\iota_k^{-1}F$, as both are inverse images along $e\iota'_k=\iota_kh_k$. The base-changed covering $\{W_{kj}\times_{Z_k}Z'_k\to Z'_k\}$ trivializes it, since inverse image sends the constant sheaf $M_{kj}$ to the constant sheaf $M_{kj}$ [lemma-constructible-pullback-constant]. Hence $e_A^*K$ has constructible cohomology and is an allowed input for $\sigma_e$ and its compact-support coefficient square.

Original Step 2

Now choose a complex $K_0$ representing $K$ and a quasi-isomorphism $P\to K_0$ with $P$ K-flat and flat termwise [lem-pc-kflat-resolution]. Exact geometric inverse image computes $e_A^*$ [prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact]; by [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-derived-tensor-pullback], $e_A^*P$ is K-flat with flat terms. By [lem-pur-etale-sum-flat-kflat], $e_{\Sigma!,A}e_A^*P$ is also K-flat with flat terms. Thus [lem-pur-derived-change-of-rings] computes the scalar extensions of $K$, $e_A^*K$ and $E_Ae_A^*K$ by tensoring respectively $P$, $e_A^*P$ and $e_{\Sigma!,A}e_A^*P$ termwise with the appropriate constant $B$-sheaf. The exact sum-extension adjunction descends degreewise to the whole derived categories [lem-amb-etale-adjoint-locality], so its counits on these representatives are the degreewise sheaf counits. This supplies a common domain and codomain of actual $B$-linear chain maps for the proposed identity, even if $B$ is nonflat over $A$.

Original Step 3

For a coefficient ring $R$ and an $R_V$-module sheaf $F$, identify $(e_R^*F)(W,s)$ with $F(W)$ for each lift $s:W\to U$ over an etale test object $W\to V$ [lem-weil-pbc-etale-inverse-image-restriction]. The module action is the same sectionwise action under the canonical constant-ring identification [prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact]. By [def-weil-pbc-etale-sum-extension], the presheaf presenting $e_{\Sigma!,R}e_R^*F$ is $W\mapsto\bigoplus_s F(W)$. Its map to $F$ sends each summand inclusion $i_s(x)$ to $x$, hence sends a finite family to its sum. It commutes with restrictions and induces a unique sheaf map by [def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification]. This is the counit: a map from the sum presheaf for a sheaf $H$ to $F$ is a compatible family of maps $H(W,s)\to F(W)$, exactly a map $H\to e_R^*F$; the displayed map corresponds to the identity when $H=e_R^*F$. This proves the fold formula directly, without identifying all sections of the sheafification with presheaf sums.

Original Step 4

On the representatives of Step 2 the comparison $\sigma_e$ is the summandwise tensor map specified in [lem-sp-et-proet-coefficient-compatibility]: $i_s(x)\otimes b\mapsto i_s(x\otimes b)$. The canonical comparison $a_e$ is represented by the inverse of the ordinary scalar/pullback map normalized in [def-pur-etale-scalar-pullback-iso]; on the restriction description of Step 3 it takes $x\otimes b$ to that same tensor on $(W,s)$. The tensor on sheaves is the sheafification of the sectionwise tensor [def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product]. For every degree $r$, lift $s$ and pure generator $i_s(x)\otimes b$, with $x\in P^r(W)$ and $b\in B_V(W)$, the three maps on the left therefore give
$$i_s(x)\otimes b\longmapsto i_s(x\otimes b)\longmapsto i_s(x\otimes b)\longmapsto x\otimes b.$$
The right side, tensoring the fold map of Step 3, gives exactly $x\otimes b$. Linearity gives equality on finite sums of generators, and the tensor and sum sheafification universal properties [def-weil-presheaf-modules-sheafification] give equality of sheaf maps. The maps are $B$-linear and commute with the differentials (the coefficient factor is in degree zero), so these are equal $B$-linear chain maps.

Original Step 5

Apply localization to the equality of chain maps in Step 4. The localization description in [def-weil-pbc-derived-categories], the K-flat computation and resolution independence in [lem-pur-derived-change-of-rings], and the degreewise descended adjunction in [lem-amb-etale-adjoint-locality] identify its two sides with the two specified derived composites at the image of $P$. The normalizations of $\sigma_e$ in [lem-sp-et-proet-coefficient-compatibility] and of $a_e$ in [def-pur-etale-scalar-pullback-iso] are precisely the representative maps used in Step 4. Finally use the isomorphism from the image of $P$ to $K$. Naturality of these comparisons and of the counits conjugates the equality to
$$v_{B,T_VK}\circ E_B(a_e(K))\circ\sigma_e(e_A^*K)=T_V(v_{A,K}).$$
This proves equality in $D(V_{\mathrm{et}},B)$ itself. In particular it applies to the stated $D^b_{ctf}$ inputs; no flatness or finite projective dimension of $B$ over $A$ has entered.

Original Step 6

Precompose the equality of Step 5 with $\sigma_e(e_A^*K)^{-1}$. The resulting equation is
$$v_{B,T_VK}\circ E_B(a_e(K))=T_V(v_{A,K})\circ\sigma_e(e_A^*K)^{-1}.$$
In [def-weil-pbc-adjunction-mate] take $L_1=E_A$, $R_1=e_A^*$, $L_2=E_B$, $R_2=e_B^*$, $G=T_U$, $H=T_V$ and $\alpha=\sigma_e^{-1}$. The right side is exactly the transpose that defines $\alpha^\flat$. Injectivity of the adjunction bijection therefore gives $a_e=(\sigma_e^{-1})^\flat$, with the required orientation.

Original Step 7

For the chosen compactification write $F_R=Re_!^c$ and $C_R:F_R\xrightarrow{\sim}E_R$. The schemes $U,V$ are Noetherian by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian], since they are finite type over the spectrum of a field. By [lem-asd-etale-compact-support-normalization], after its right-adjoint identification the compact-support counit is $\epsilon_R^c=v_R\circ C_R(e_R^*(-))$. Let $\beta:T_VF_A\to F_BT_U$ be the specified compact-support coefficient map. The etale clause of [lem-sp-et-proet-coefficient-compatibility] states
$$C_B(T_UL)\circ\beta(L)=\sigma_e(L)\circ T_V(C_A(L)).$$
Set $L=e_A^*K$. Naturality of $C_B$ at $a_e(K)$, then this equation, then Step 5 give
$$\begin{aligned}
\epsilon_B^c(T_VK)\circ F_B(a_e(K))\circ\beta(L)
&=v_{B,T_VK}\circ E_B(a_e(K))\circ C_B(T_UL)\circ\beta(L)\\
&=v_{B,T_VK}\circ E_B(a_e(K))\circ\sigma_e(L)\circ T_V(C_A(L))\\
&=T_V(v_{A,K})\circ T_V(C_A(L))=T_V(\epsilon_A^c(K)).
\end{aligned}$$
This is the compact-support assertion for the actual coefficient map and the counit-preserving normalization, for every chosen compactification.
