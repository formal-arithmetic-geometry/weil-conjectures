The other two new statements are precisely the connected-group torus tangent criterion and ambient normal-subgroup criterion used by Conrad on p.102 (and the latter on p.100). They keep nonsmooth subgroup schemes, full torus characters and all characteristics.

Step 1 uses lem-sfb-smooth-affine-adjoint-kernel-calculus for representability and finite presentation of K and for its arbitrary base change, and lem-sfb-relative-centre-multiplicative-base-change for the closed, flat, finitely presented Z and its arbitrary base change. Centrality implies trivial conjugation on dual-number points directly, so the inclusion Z -> K does not borrow the conclusion from a cited centre theorem.

Step 2 uses lem-sfb-locally-algebraic-group-identity-component for the full open and closed connected component, and lem-sfb-reductive-normal-subgroup-central-neutral-test for its normality in the ambient G. It uses the new calculus node's field tangent-kernel formula. The field is algebraically closed at this stage, as required by the component and normality statements. The open-subscheme tangent equality is local at the identity and elementary. Crucially, H is not replaced by H_red.

Step 3 uses lem-sfb-reductive-rank-one-reflections for the root line and its semisimple-rank-one centralizer, and lem-sfb-split-rank-one-classification for its three matrix models with specified maximal torus. Maximality of T in the smaller group follows from maximality in G, not from an unquoted stronger assertion. lem-sfb-affine-group-lie-tangent-calculus states the matrix bracket and preservation of brackets. lem-sfb-affine-group-dual-number-calculus states injectivity for closed subgroup tangent maps. lem-sfb-projective-frames-pgl states the determinant-nonzero projective-matrix model and scalar quotient for PGL2; normalizing the bottom-right entry near the identity shows directly that the upper-right tangent direction survives.
