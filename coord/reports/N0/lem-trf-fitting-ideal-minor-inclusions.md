## Worker report

The delivery adds `Matrix.minorIdeal` and states
`Matrix.minorIdeal_inclusions`.  `minorIdeal` is the span of determinants of
all square submatrices selected by a pair of finite row and column subsets of
the same cardinality.  The increasing `Fin` order embeddings make the chosen
orders explicit.

The theorem binds a commutative ring `R`, natural numbers `p`, `n`, `r`, and
`d`, and a matrix
`A : Matrix (Fin p) (Fin n) R`.  Its first conjunct is
`minorIdeal p n (r + 1) A ≤ minorIdeal p n r A`, rendering clause (1).  Its
second conjunct quantifies
`B : Matrix (Fin p) (Fin (n + d)) R`, assumes
`∀ i j, B i (Fin.castAdd d j) = A i j` (the first `n` columns are `A`), and
concludes
`minorIdeal p (n + d) (r + d) B ≤ minorIdeal p n r A`, rendering clause (2).
Its third conjunct quantifies
`C : Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) R` and concludes
`minorIdeal p n r (A * C) ≤ minorIdeal p n r A`, rendering clause (3), with
matrix multiplication giving the stated entrywise sum.  Natural numbers cover
all `r ≥ 0`, `d ≥ 0`, and all empty dimensions.

The definition uses the recorded Mathlib declarations `Matrix`,
`Matrix.det`, and `Matrix.submatrix`; the theorem imports
`Sheaf.LinearAlgebra.Matrix.MinorIdeal` and the recorded Cauchy--Binet module
`WeilConjectures.LinearAlgebra.Matrix.CauchyBinet` (the `lean.module` of
lem-weil-pbc-cauchy-binet; corrected by the maintainer's reviewer from the
duplicate `Sheaf.LinearAlgebra.Matrix.CauchyBinet`).  The adjugate identity used by the
proof plan is Mathlib's `Matrix.adjugate_mul` (the proof remains `sorry` at
stage 2).

Edge cases checked: `r = 0` has the unique empty row and column subsets and
the empty determinant `1`, hence the span is `⊤`; `r > p` or `r > n` gives an
empty indexing type and the span is `⊥`; `p = 0` and/or `n = 0` are therefore
included.  The induction parameter `d = 0` gives `Fin.castAdd 0` and the
same-size matrix.  No field, domain, nonzero, characteristic, or finiteness
hypothesis beyond the finite `Fin` dimensions and `CommRing R` is present.
