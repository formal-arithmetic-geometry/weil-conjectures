Minimal leading exponents of nonzero encoded elements form an antichain, so the existing Dickson leaf gives a finite set. Normalize one representative at each such exponent by an actual coefficient of maximal magnitude. Divide an arbitrary encoded element by these scalar series using the existing bounded convergent division statement. Extract the terms of Y-degree one: the quotients reduce to their Y-constant slices and the remainder becomes an element z of L. Its support avoids every selected leading exponent, whereas a nonzero z must have a leading exponent above one of them. Hence z=0, with the desired coefficient bounds.
