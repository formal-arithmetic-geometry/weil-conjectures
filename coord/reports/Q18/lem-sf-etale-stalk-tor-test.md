## Argument preservation and work briefs

Nothing from the returned proof is discarded. The following is the original argument, retained verbatim, followed by where it is now assigned. These are work briefs in this report, NOT proof steps attached to the unsplit nodes.

### Original step 1

Kernels and images of maps of module sheaves inherit the action of $A_T$, and their quotient has the induced action, so their underlying abelian sheaves compute the same kernels, images and cohomology. For any representing complex $C$ of $A_T$-module sheaves, apply exactness of geometric stalks to its underlying abelian sheaves. The exact-functor homology comparison, applied in degrees $i-1,i,i+1$, gives $H^i(C)_{\bar t}\cong H^i(C_{\bar t})$. In particular termwise stalks send quasi-isomorphisms to quasi-isomorphisms, so this identity is valid for every $E\in D(T_{\mathrm{et}},A)$. A module sheaf is zero exactly when its underlying abelian sheaf is zero. Apply geometric-stalk conservativity to the map from that underlying abelian sheaf to zero: $H^i(E)=0$ if and only if $H^i(E_{\bar t})=0$ for every $\bar t$. This uses no bound on $E$.

Assigned to lem-sf-etale-module-stalk-cohomology-test.

### Original step 2

Assume $K$ has Tor amplitude $[a,b]$. Fix a geometric point $\bar t$ and an arbitrary $A$-module $M$. In the defining quantifier for Tor amplitude take the degree-zero constant sheaf $\underline M$. Then $H^i(K\otimes_{A_T}^{\mathbf L}\underline M)=0$ for $i\notin[a,b]$. By step 1 the same vanishing holds after taking the stalk at $\bar t$. The constant-module tensor/stalk comparison identifies this stalk complex with $K_{\bar t}\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}M[0]$. Hence its cohomology vanishes outside $[a,b]$, for every $M$ and $\bar t$.

Assigned to parent step 1.

### Original step 3

Conversely assume the stated condition for all geometric points and all ordinary $A$-modules. Let $\mathcal F$ be any $A_T$-module sheaf, placed in degree zero, and fix $i\notin[a,b]$. The unbounded derived tensor/stalk comparison gives $(K\otimes_{A_T}^{\mathbf L}\mathcal F[0])_{\bar t}\cong K_{\bar t}\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}\mathcal F_{\bar t}[0]$. Its right-hand side has zero $i$-th cohomology by the assumption with $M=\mathcal F_{\bar t}$. Step 1 therefore gives zero for every stalk of $H^i(K\otimes_{A_T}^{\mathbf L}\mathcal F[0])$. Geometric-stalk conservativity applied to its underlying abelian sheaf makes this cohomology sheaf zero. Since $\mathcal F$ and $i$ were arbitrary, this is precisely Tor amplitude $[a,b]$.

Assigned to parent step 2.

### Original step 4

Let $u:U\to T$ be any scheme morphism and suppose $K$ has Tor amplitude $[a,b]$. The constant-coefficient module inverse image is exact, agrees with inverse image on underlying abelian sheaves, and identifies $u^{-1}A_T$ with $A_U$. Thus $u^*K$ is represented by applying $u^*$ termwise. For a geometric point $\bar v$ of $U$, the natural inverse-image stalk comparison gives $(u^*C^n)_{\bar v}\cong C^n_{u\circ\bar v}$ in every degree. These are additive and $A$-linear: the canonical comparison respects products and addition, and its naturality for each scalar-multiplication map $m_c:C^n\to C^n$ gives $c\,x\mapsto c\,x$ for all $c\in A$. Naturality for $d^n$ makes them an isomorphism of complexes. By exactness of stalks it induces $(u^*K)_{\bar v}\cong K_{u\circ\bar v}$ in $D(A)$. Step 2 says that this complex tensored with any $A$-module has cohomology in $[a,b]$. Applying step 3 on $U$ proves that $u^*K$ has the same Tor interval.

Assigned to lem-sf-etale-derived-pullback-stalk for the comparison and parent step 3 for the Tor consequence.
