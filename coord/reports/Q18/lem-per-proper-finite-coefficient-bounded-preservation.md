## Review by Q18

I checked B to E against the item, the source passages above, and the statement of every node cited by each step. Every use has a verbatim statement in citations.json; no step uses the internal proof of a cited node. The five steps have respectively 752, 839, 360, 999 and 1110 characters, so none hides a lemma in a step exceeding 2000 characters.

Step 1 uses affine-scheme-qcqs only to obtain compactness of the finitely many affine pieces; their finite union is compact. pqf-def-locnoeth applies to the affine covers in the hypothesis. Locally-Noetherian-quasi-separated states quasi-separatedness of each scheme. Proper-morphism and finite-type-morphism provide separatedness, local finite type and quasi-compactness of f. pqf-lem-lft-fp-locnoeth assumes only a locally Noetherian target, which S is. Separated-quasi-separated concerns the morphism (not just its source), giving the remaining hypothesis of finite presentation in scheme-conventions. Thus both schemes and the morphism have exactly the hypotheses needed in Step 2.

Step 2 reads boundedness and constructible cohomology from constructible-complexes. Projection-compactification explicitly permits the identity compactification for a proper map. Extension-by-zero gives its presheaf formula, so for the identity all objects factor uniquely and sheafification returns the original sheaf. No compactification independence theorem or Nagata existence theorem is needed for this chosen candidate. Etale-compactification-constructibility states constructibility of every cohomology sheaf for a finite commutative ring, a bounded-below constructible input, a separated finite-presentation map, and qcqs schemes. All these conditions have been verified; in particular its statement has no invertibility or finite-dimensional-base condition.

Step 3 uses finite-type-fibre-dimension-bound exactly as stated, with Y of that lemma renamed to S here. Its target need only be quasi-compact. The resulting d bounds ordinary scheme-theoretic fibres, precisely the fibres used in proper-torsion-cd-bound; there is no need to compare geometric and ordinary dimensions. In particular a Noetherian scheme with infinite Krull dimension creates no gap.

Step 4 uses the torsion bound for the underlying abelian sheaf, whose torsion is proved directly by repetition of multiples of 1 in the finite ring. The node higher-direct-image-forget-comparison applies to every commutative ring and every scheme morphism and identifies the module higher direct images after forgetting, including degree zero and the zero ring. Vanishing of the underlying sheaf implies vanishing of the module sheaf by its definition. Module-sheaves-grothendieck-abelian and module-direct-image-left-exact supply the hypotheses of derived-functor-negative-vanishing, which explicitly applies on D+ and gives the other end of the interval. I did not assume that forgetting sends injectives to injectives.

Step 5 uses enough-injectives for the module categories, not merely for abelian sheaves. Derived-direct-image-triangulated is stated for a general additive left exact functor between abelian categories with enough injectives, so it applies to module coefficients and gives shift compatibility. Truncation-triangle explicitly supplies successive cohomology triangles and says they remain bounded below; cohomology-shift gives the sign convention H^i(M[-r])=H^{i-r}(M). Derived-homology-long-exact makes the vanishing interval stable under these extensions. The induction starts from the acyclic truncation below a and ends at the truncation above which L has no cohomology. It is finite (r=a,...,b), not an assertion about an infinite limit. The final definition of constructible-complexes has exactly constructibility plus a finite cohomological interval. I wrote this induction explicitly rather than create a redundant amplitude lemma or silently apply an unbounded-functor theorem to a functor only specified on D+.

Edge cases checked: L=0; a=b; f=identity; finite proper morphisms with d=0; empty source or empty fibres (choose d=0 when all fibres are empty); the zero coefficient ring (take a positive annihilator as in Step 4); and p-primary coefficients on characteristic-p proper fibres. The last case is allowed because 095U has no invertibility condition. No finite Tor-amplitude hypothesis, exact scalar-reduction assertion, field-base hypothesis, or finite-dimensionality assumption enters the proof. No SGA source is used. All non-elementary geometric, comparison and derived facts are existing cited nodes; the finite-cover union, identity extension by zero, finite-ring annihilator and interval arithmetic are the short elementary arguments written in the steps, not new nodes.
