### Old step 2 → lem-trf-local-quotient-evaluation

Put $F=g_*N$. Invertibility makes $N$ quasi-coherent [def-sc-invertible-module], so the quasi-compactness of $g$ and quasi-separatedness from Step 1 give quasi-coherence of $F$ by [pqf-lem-pushforward-qcoh].
