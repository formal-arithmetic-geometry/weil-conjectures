For the statement-only weight lemma, I checked the claim independently: each element of the finite image of A has finite order, so over the stated field its operator is diagonalizable; commuting operators split into common eigenspaces. A common eigenvalue assignment is a group homomorphism because rho is a representation. Normality of A makes G permute those spaces, and an orbit sum is G-stable, so irreducibility gives transitivity. For a T-stable proper subspace of one weight space, its G-translates lie in distinct weight spaces indexed by G/T; their direct sum would be a proper nonzero G-subrepresentation. Thus the T-action is irreducible. The canonical induction map is well-defined by the balancing relation and identifies coset summands with the direct-sum weight spaces. This substantiates the new statement but is not recorded as a completed split of it.
