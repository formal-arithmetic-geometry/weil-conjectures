## Source error

The current blueprint says that the absolute cohomological bounds needed to apply Tag 0F12 on V and X_V hold for the standing schemes. This inference is false without a hypothesis on k. For example take k to be the rational function field in countably many independent variables over an algebraic closure of F_p, with ell different from p. The n-fold Kummer symbol of the first n variables is nonzero in H^n(k, mu_ell tensor ... tensor mu_ell): successive residues at those variables give the nonzero degree-zero class 1. Thus cd_ell(k) is infinite. Already X=Y=Spec k and f=id satisfy the standing geometric assumptions; cd(f)=0, while the claimed absolute bound on V=Spec k fails. This identifies the incorrect inference, without asserting that the final comparison is false. The item already asks for this repair, so no hypothesis or conclusion of the item is changed. Steps 4–5 use Tag 0F10(1) and the relative finite-constant projection theorem instead, retaining the blueprint's classical-etale then pro-etale route.
