## Source error

For $n=1$, $\sigma_1$ is the vertical edge $(0,0)\to(0,1)$; its initial endpoint belongs to the bottom boundary and its final endpoint belongs to the top $\Delta^1$. Thus its intersection is $\partial\Delta^1$, already a counterexample to the asserted horn pushout. In general the zeroth face is in the top, the first face is in the preceding attachment, and every other face is in the horn prism. The corrected proof uses the trivial Kan fibration in Definition 3.1.1.2 to fill the entire boundary with first vertex $c$ and all remaining apexes colors, exactly as Step 4 spells out. This repairs the proof without changing the item's statement or hypotheses.
