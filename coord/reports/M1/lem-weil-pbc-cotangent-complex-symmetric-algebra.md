## Reviewer fix (R-ccs1001, stage 2)

The delivered statement quantified existentially over arbitrary maps `lam`, `delta`
(A-linear, with only a balancing law), so it did not say that the node's specific maps
`λ : k ⊗ c ↦ c·[ℓ(k)]` and `δ : [u] ↦ 1 ⊗ du` have the stated properties (H-weakening);
`Set.range delta = Set.range (delta ∘ lam)` was then trivial from surjectivity of `lam`.
Clause (4) was a bare `Nonempty` of an `A`-linear isomorphism, not the `C`-linear one with
`d(ι x) ↦ x ⊗ 1`. The fix restates with Mathlib's naive cotangent complex: the presentation
is `Algebra.Generators.ofSurjective a hα` (surjectivity `hα` is part of the conclusion),
`J/J²` is `G.toExtension.Cotangent`, `δ` is `G.toExtension.cotangentComplex`, `λ` is a
`C`-linear map `C ⊗[A] K → J/J²` pinned by `λ(c ⊗ k) = c • Cotangent.mk ⟨ℓ k, _⟩`, the
composite formula is written against `1 ⊗ D(X j)`, and (4) is `∃ e : Ω[C⁄A] ≃ₗ[C] C ⊗[A] M`
with `e (D (ι x)) = 1 ⊗ₜ x`. The redundant `iotaM`/`hiotaM` binders were replaced by
`SymmetricAlgebra.ι`, the two inclusions by `LinearMap.ker π = K`, `J` by `RingHom.ker`,
the ideal by `Ideal.span (ℓ '' K)`. `K ⊗_A C` is written `C ⊗[A] K` (Mathlib's form
carrying the `C`-module structure).
