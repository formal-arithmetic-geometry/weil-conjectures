In Step 3, the image equality gives surjectivity as defined by `def-weil-surjective-morphism`; the open-immersion and flat-morphism definitions identify the local ring map of g with that of f at each component point. In Step 4, `lem-weil-pbc-morphism-affine-schemes-affine` says that g is affine because both ends are affine, and `pqf-def-affine-morphism` says the inverse image of each affine open is affine. Surjectivity makes that inverse image map onto the open; together with Step 3 this is exactly 022B's fpqc condition. Every non-elementary fact in these steps is the statement of its cited node, rather than an argument hidden in another node's proof.
