## Source error

Take any field k, R=k[u,v], B=R[T], and the smooth (hence flat) finitely presented projection Spec B → Spec R. Set q=(u,v)B and q'=(uT-v). The point q is the generic point of the fibre over p=(u,v), since that fibre is Spec k[T] and its prime is (0). The ideal q' is prime, since B/q' ≅ k[u,T] by v=uT. It is contained strictly in q, so its point generalizes q. Its contraction to R is (0): under k[u,v] → k[u,T], u maps to u and v to uT; distinct monomials u^i v^j map to distinct monomials u^(i+j)T^j, so this map is injective. Over the generic point of Spec R the corresponding prime is (T-v/u) in k(u,v)[T], a nonzero maximal prime. It is not the generic point of that fibre. Thus the conclusion fails even over a regular universally catenary Noetherian base for a smooth morphism of pure relative dimension one.

The offending inference in Temkin Lemma 3.5.3 is that going down supplies a source chain between a prescribed point and a prescribed generalization whose length dominates the corresponding base chain. Going down supplies some generalization over each earlier base point, and does not force it to be the chosen one. Here ht(q)=2, ht(q')=1, ht(p)=2 and ht(p')=0: the claimed source drop is 1 and the base drop is 2. Merely adding regularity, universal catenarity or pure relative dimension would not repair this.
