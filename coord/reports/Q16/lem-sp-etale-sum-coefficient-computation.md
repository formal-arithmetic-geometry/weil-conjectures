### Old step 1: assigned to lem-sp-etale-compactification-bounded-models

The compactification has $r$ proper, hence finite type, so $W$ is Noetherian by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian]. For either finite coefficient ring $R=A,B$ and any $R$-module sheaf $M$ on $U$, apply [lem-asd-etale-compact-support-normalization] to $M[0]$. It identifies $Rr_*j_!M[0]$ with $e_{\Sigma!}M[0]$. Taking cohomology gives $R^ar_*j_!M=0$ for $a>0$ and the canonical degree-zero isomorphism $r_*j_!M\cong e_{\Sigma!}M$, with exactly the sheet components specified there. Apply this first to $M=P^n$ over $A$, then to $M=P^n\otimes_A B$ over $B$. The second application requires no flatness of that sheaf over $A$. This proves both acyclicity assertions and gives natural degreewise sheet identifications.

### Old step 4: assigned to lem-sp-etale-compactification-sheaf-scalar-map

Compute that ordinary map before passing to the derived category. For an etale test scheme $S\to V$, direct image is evaluation on $W_S=W\times_VS$ [def-weil-pbc-direct-image]. Thus the adjunct defining $\pi_C^n$ sends a local pure tensor $z\otimes b$ to the section $z\otimes r^{-1}b$ on $W_S$: the ordinary counit evaluates the pulled-back section $z$. Here $b$ is a local section of the constant ring sheaf $B_V$, so the formula also covers locally constant, rather than globally constant, coefficients. The open tensor isomorphism is the identity on $U$ and zero on its complement [lem-coef-etale-open-tensor]. Under the degree-zero normalization of [lem-asd-etale-compact-support-normalization], $z$ is sent to its sheet components $(x_s)_s$. Taking the component of $z\otimes r^{-1}b$ restricts to the same sheet and hence gives $x_s\otimes b$; tensor restriction acts on each factor. Consequently the composite of step 3 is $(x_s)_s\otimes b\mapsto(x_s\otimes b)_s$. This is a calculation on local generators: by [def-weil-pbc-etale-sum-extension], sum extension is the sheafification of a presheaf of direct sums, so its sections locally come from finite sums of sheet generators; ordinary sheaf tensor is likewise generated locally by pure tensors. The calculation is additive and balanced, since $(a x_s)\otimes b=x_s\otimes\varphi(a)b$. It therefore proves equality of the sheaf morphisms, without assuming that a test scheme globally splits into sheets.
