## Review by Q16

I read the HTT places named above against the item and each new tool. The cardinal construction is the successor-of-a-power observation after Definition A.2.6.3, made explicit with pinned Mathlib identities. The filtered-subset tool is Lemma 5.4.2.8 with the lower and upper ranks renamed alpha and beta; it includes the empty subset, so the resulting poset is nonempty. The cofinal-poset tool is Proposition 5.3.1.18, with the cofinality consequence written into the statement. The size-calculus tool combines Proposition 5.4.1.7 with the bounded-diagram count of Proposition 5.4.2.9 and the retract-envelope argument of Lemma 5.4.2.4. Its additional clauses are general consequences rather than a quotation of one numbered statement: small models of G and bounded-size shapes provide a small family of diagrams; each colimit is unique up to equivalence; homotopy retracts are bounded by split idempotents in the small Hom sets of h(E), whose two splittings are uniquely isomorphic. Proposition 5.4.1.7 turns these small representative families into small equivalent full quasicategories. The compact-stage tool is the identity-factorization argument in Lemma 5.4.2.4, applied to an arbitrary supplied filtered colimit; for a general map use that the compactness comparison is an equivalence and that a component of a colimit of spaces has a representative in some vertex space (equivalently the colimit's left-fibration realization, Proposition 3.3.4.5). It asserts a homotopy factorization, not a strict equation.

For Step 1 I opened the entire statement of `def-coef-presentable-infinity-category`. Its accessibility clause gives the essentially small compact generator subcategory and the *small* filtered index for every chosen endpoint. Only the small endpoint family is chosen simultaneously; closure of the universe under small disjoint unions gives theta. This is a choice and size step from the definition, not a hidden uniform bound for every object of C.

For Step 2 I read the two arithmetic leaves against the actual Mathlib signatures. Infinite eta satisfies `isRegular_succ`'s aleph0 bound. Cantor puts theta strictly below eta, and the power identity gives eta^theta=eta without GCH or an inaccessible-cardinal assumption. The function-set injections in the step justify the inequalities for every mu and nu, including zero. A mere arbitrary larger regular rank would not suffice; that invalid shortcut was explicitly avoided.

For Step 3 the same definition node says compactness is preservation by the mapping-space functor of small filtered colimits. The inclusion of the kappa-filtered diagrams among lambda-filtered diagrams proves monotonicity directly. I reopened the statement of `lem-coef-compact-small-colimits`: it requires a kappa-small simplicial shape, locally small ambient quasicategory, all small kappa-filtered colimits, compact vertex objects, and an existing colimiting cocone. Here Step 1 bounds the endpoint shapes, presentability supplies local smallness and the colimits, and the chosen presentation supplies the cocone. Thus it really proves compactness of both endpoints of every arrow in S, not just one selected object.

For Step 4 I compared the new size node's bounded-apex clause and the existing compact-colimit node with H's definition. H uses a single layer of kappa-small diagrams in G; there is no assertion here about an unproved iterated closure. G is essentially small and C locally small. Presentability supplies precisely the partial colimits the size node leaves conditional. All vertices are compact by Step 3, so the existing node proves every apex compact. Equivalence of represented mapping-space functors preserves this condition directly.

For Step 5 the new cofinal-poset statement supplies the same colimit cocone after restriction, rather than merely asserting that a poset exists. Its smallness assumption is met by the accessible presentation. The new filtered-subset node takes exactly lambda<kappa, their regularity, and the numerical bound from Step 2. It supplies the small poset of subsets, its cover, and its kappa upper-bound property. The passage to a kappa-filtered nerve is written out using unique arrows in a poset, and the finite-chain bound uses infinite squaring plus uncountability. Thus a kappa-small *poset* is not silently identified with a kappa-small *nerve* when kappa=omega; the latter rank is expressly uncountable.

For Step 6 I reopened the entire statement of `lem-coef-directed-cover-colimit-assembly`. It assumes a small nonempty directed index poset, an increasing cover by simplicial subsets, all small ambient colimits, and specified partial-colimit cocones. Each of these is checked in the step. The finite-chain argument shows the cover of the whole nerve, not just its vertices. The statement actually supplies coherent assembly of the chosen apices and the colimiting comparison, so the proof does not extract a construction from that node's proof. The accessibility definition then applies to H exactly; its generation clause does not require H to contain *all* compact objects or to be closed under all colimits.

For Step 7 I checked both clauses of the compact-stage tool and the retract clause of the size node. The presentation has small kappa-filtered index and C admits the relevant colimits, so the identity factors up to homotopy and gives a retract of one object of H. No compactness of the cocone arrows is assumed. The full compact subcategory has representatives among the small set of retract-equivalence classes. Local smallness is inherited from C and the size node's first clause applies. This is the missing transition from a small generating family to essential smallness of **all** compact objects; it is not taken from the compactness definition.
