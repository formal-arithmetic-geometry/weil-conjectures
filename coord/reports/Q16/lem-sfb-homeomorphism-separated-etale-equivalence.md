## Review by Q16

I read Rydh's actual Theorem (5.21), printed page 24, and its proof: the diagonal is a nil-immersion, etale objects have unique data, and effectiveness follows by descent and gluing. The printed statement includes the full etale category and its separated subcategory, precisely the two clauses retained here. Printed page 4 assumes all algebraic spaces quasi-separated; this is explicitly retained for the bases and objects. Notation (5.1) describes the fibered category, and Remark (5.2) permits algebraic-space bases. I separately read Theorem (5.19)(i), printed page 23: universally open surjections give effective descent for that entire category. The new node states exactly this input with the cocycle and arrow compatibility written out, including all objects and base maps, and contains no postponed proof or hidden proof sketch. The absence of flatness or quasi-compactness assumptions agrees with that theorem; they were not accidentally dropped from an fpqc theorem.

Step 1 uses only the new effective-descent statement: after every base change a homeomorphism is open and surjective. The quasi-separated base hypotheses are those of the item. It does not claim the forgetful functor is an equivalence until steps 2–4 prove that every object has unique data.

Step 2 uses lem-trf-space-fibre-products-etale-covers for the existence of X_2, lem-a1-space-point-open-calculus for continuity of the diagonal's point map, and the equation p_1 d = id to identify it as the inverse homeomorphism. Separatedness, explicitly assumed for r, makes this diagonal representable and a closed immersion. Thus it satisfies exactly the definition of a thickening in lem-sfb-space-thickening-base-change; that node gives universal persistence. On scheme tests lem-trf-universally-homeomorph-integral supplies integral, universally injective, surjective. The full statement of lem-sfb-space-integral-radicial-etale-invariance then applies. I opened its source, Stacks Tag 05ZH, spaces-more-morphisms.tex, theorem-topological-invariance: it is indeed an equivalence on etale algebraic spaces, not merely scheme objects. I also read Tag 09ZX, lemma-base-change-thickening. The representability needed for 05ZH belongs to d and its base changes, not to r. Only full faithfulness between existing quasi-separated etale objects is used subsequently, so no preservation of quasi-separatedness by an arbitrary inverse to the unrestricted 05ZH category is assumed.

Step 3 uses the statement of lem-trf-space-chartwise-etale-permanence for base-change etaleness and the fibre-product statement for the double/triple overlaps. It then uses precisely the full faithfulness proved in step 2 to lift the identity, its inverse, and their composites. The cocycle is checked explicitly after e:(x,z) -> (x,x,z); this map is the base change of d along p_12:X_3 -> X_2. The restriction of theta_12 is identity, and theta_23 and theta_13 both restrict to theta. Faithfulness gives the equation before restriction. Normalization plus faithfulness along d gives uniqueness. These computations are in the item's proof, not hidden in a new restatement node.

Step 4 uses those same morphism bijections: both compatibility composites pull back to a. It is marked elementary because it is just that uniqueness calculation and composition of the already established equivalences; no additional geometric fact is introduced. It proves all morphisms, not only isomorphisms, satisfy descent compatibility, which is necessary for full faithfulness of the desired ordinary category equivalence.

Step 5 uses lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-base-change on scheme tests to preserve separatedness under pullback. For reflection it forms D -> T as a test of the correct relative diagonal E -> E times_Y E. Its pullback D' -> T' is a base change of the separated diagonal of E_X -> X and therefore a closed immersion. I read lem-sfb-space-closed-subspace-proper and Tag 04XZ, lemma-closed-immersion-proper: its stated definition of properness includes universal closedness, exactly what is used. Both vertical maps are base changes of r and hence homeomorphisms. The identity between the two resulting continuous squares therefore makes |D| -> |T| closed, for every T. I read lem-sfb-space-separated-diagonal-closed and Tag 04Y0, lemma-separated-diagonal-proper: universal closedness of the diagonal is equivalent to separatedness for any algebraic-space morphism. This is a statement-level citation, not use of a definition as an unproved descent theorem. The last restriction to full subcategories and the existence assertion are categorical consequences of preservation and reflection.
