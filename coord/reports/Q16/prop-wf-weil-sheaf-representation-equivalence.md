## Source error and statement correction

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported statement calls its target the “representations of def-wf-weil-group”, but that definition defines the group, not a representation category. Read literally as all representations, this is false: the infinite-dimensional trivial representation with vector space a countable direct sum of copies of Qlbar cannot be the fibre of a finite-rank lisse sheaf. Finite-dimensional representations also require the finite-coefficient-field continuity convention, rather than arbitrary abstract actions. The current main-20261015 unit explicitly specifies finite-dimensional lisse representations. Weil II (1.1.6), (1.1.10) and (1.1.12), pp.150-152, specify the finite field of definition and the topology with the geometric group open. I corrected the statement to that exact intended category, with restriction to P an l-adic representation in def-pod-l-adic-representations(3). Step 6 proves the equivalent whole-Weil finite-field model by adjoining the matrix entries of the degree-one operator. This adds no unintended lattice condition: that operator need not preserve an integral lattice.

The imported proof also said degree-one elements g gamma act through the same operator Phi. The correct formula is rho(g gamma)=rho_geom(g) Phi; nontrivial geometric monodromy distinguishes these operators. Step 4 records the correction. The existing repaired fundamental-representation definition already uses the correct formula and supplies the s-inverse path convention. The imported Hom-tower Mittag-Leffler discussion is unnecessary: the geometric dictionary now states the full integral/rational equivalence. I do not assume surjective transition maps on equivariant Hom groups, and do not claim that integral reduction of a rational short exact sequence is always exact.
