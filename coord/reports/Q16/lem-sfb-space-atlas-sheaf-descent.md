Step 2 uses the model node's restriction equivalence at all four bases, its postcomposition identification with a localized site, its assertion that inverse image is restriction for etale maps, and its compatibility with composition. I checked the hypotheses: H/W/R/T are all algebraic spaces over the same B, and the relevant maps are etale. The equivalence between C/H and C uses only terminality and is written explicitly. On the overlap the two composites to H coincide; the identity comparison therefore gives the canonical alpha, and on the triple overlap it gives its cocycle. This avoids citing a definition of localization as though it proved a comparison theorem.

Step 3 uses the same model statement to extend G and lift alpha by full faithfulness. It uses faithfulness on T to detect the cocycle, with the composition comparisons from step 2. Lifting alpha's inverse shows the lifted map is an isomorphism. These are elementary categorical consequences of the cited equivalences, rather than new lemmas. I checked the repeated-index case: for a singleton covering, its self-overlap is W times_H W. There is no need to require alpha to be the identity on all R. Its pullback to the diagonal is the identity because the cocycle gives an idempotent automorphism; this cancellation is written out.

Step 4 uses precisely the existence and intertwining-isomorphism clause of `lem-pe-set-sheaf-cover-descent`. The sheaf produced lives on C/H; step 2's explicit terminal-object identification makes it a sheaf on C, and E_H returns the desired sheaf on the original scheme site. Neither quasi-compactness nor any finiteness property enters that statement or this application.

Step 5 uses the model's full faithfulness to lift the local map and detect its compatibility, then the map-gluing clause of the general descent node to obtain a unique global map. Uniqueness forces functoriality and the two inverse natural comparisons for the chosen realizations. To check the isomorphism clause I followed both the map and its inverse: the inverse respects the overlap square by cancellation, and their glued composites are identities by uniqueness. Thus I did not assume that mere essential surjectivity also gives uniqueness of arrows.
