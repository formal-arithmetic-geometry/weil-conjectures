## What needed repair or clarification

The imported item had a statement and search evidence but no proof. The statement agrees with the intended mapping-cylinder comparison of Remark A.4.2 and is retained. This item is not the free-algebra theorem and must not assume that its transformation is a Kan extension.

The pre-existing relative-nerve statement gives a cocartesian fibration, fibers, transition edges and a map from the strict mapping simplex. It does **not** assert that an objectwise categorical fibration of diagrams induces a categorical fibration on total relative nerves. That missing general result is the first new input; it verifies the joint projection to the operadic base times the interval, not just its endpoint projections.

The existing mapping-simplex presentation statement asserts a weak equivalence in the relative Cartesian model. This is **not** a statement that its underlying map is a categorical equivalence, and that implication is false for arbitrary relative-model weak equivalences. The second new input is precisely the stronger, independently sourced conclusion of HTT 3.2.2.7(2) under the mapping-simplex hypotheses. We use its covariant opposite for the actual cylinder.

The source projection of an active slice is a right fibration over the **active** source category; it is not asserted to be a right fibration over all of the operad. Step 4 keeps this distinction. Likewise equivalence of total functor categories was not simply asserted to imply equivalence of strict fibers over the operadic base: Step 6 checks the base functor map and uses the target isofibration.
