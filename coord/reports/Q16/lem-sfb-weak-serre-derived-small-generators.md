For the new extension lemma I checked the construction in A.3, not just its wording. Write Y^j=ker d_Z^j and X^j=im d_Z^{j-1}, and K=ker(N^i to N^{i+1}). Take L=K intersect X^i. It belongs to S by subobject closure and K/L is the already-controlled image in H^i(Z). Lift L monomorphically to a small subobject V of Z^{i-1}/X^{i-1} using the epi to X^i. The kernel T=V intersect H^{i-1}(Z) belongs to S. Presentability of T factors its map to H^{i-1}(Z) through a small subobject P in A intersect S. Form Q=V amalgamated_T P. Both maps from T are monic, so Q is an extension of L by P and belongs to S; the map Q to Z^{i-1}/X^{i-1} is monic. One way to check the last claim is that both short exact sequences have quotient L and kernel P, with identity on L and the inclusion P into H^{i-1}(Z). Lift Q through Z^{i-1} epi onto the quotient, again monomorphically, obtaining N^{i-1} in S surjecting to Q. Its differential has image L; therefore H^i of the extended partial complex equals K/L in A intersect S, and its next cycle-image equals P. This verifies the omitted final invariant check and corrects the source index typo. These construction details stay in this report because the new node is statement-only, as required.
