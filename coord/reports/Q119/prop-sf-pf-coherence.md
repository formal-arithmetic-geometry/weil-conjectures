### Preserved returned step 4

For the proper factor let $M=j_!K$, $Q=M\otimes FL$, and set $e_M:\,GRM\to R'HM$. By [def-exchange-projection] its transpose is $\tau_M=H(\varepsilon_M)\circ\theta_{RM}:F'GRM\to HM$. The projection $\pi_p$ is the mate of $(\varepsilon_M\otimes1)\mu_F$, so
$$\varepsilon_Q\,F(\pi_{p;M,L})=(\varepsilon_M\otimes1)\mu_{F;RM,L}.$$
Transpose both routes under $F'\dashv R'$, to the common target $HM\otimes F'GL$. The route using $\pi_{p'}\circ(e_M\otimes1)\circ\mu_G$ transposes, by naturality of $\mu_{F'}$, to
$$(\varepsilon'_{HM}F'e_M\otimes1)\mu_{F';GRM,GL}F'\mu_{G;RM,L}=(\tau_M\otimes1)\mu_{F';GRM,GL}F'\mu_{G;RM,L}.$$
For the other route the transpose, including its target identification, is
$$(1\otimes\theta_L^{-1})\mu_{H;M,FL}\,H\varepsilon_Q\,\theta_{RQ}\,F'G(\pi_{p;M,L}).$$
Naturality of $\theta$ moves $F'G\pi_p$ to $HF\pi_p$; insert the preceding counit equation, then use naturality of $\mu_H$ and the monoidality equation in step 2. The result is
$$(1\otimes\theta_L^{-1})(H\varepsilon_M\otimes1)(\theta_{RM}\otimes\theta_L)\mu_{F';GRM,GL}F'\mu_{G;RM,L}=(\tau_M\otimes1)\mu_{F';GRM,GL}F'\mu_{G;RM,L}.$$
Thus the transposes coincide, and the adjunction bijection gives equality of the original maps. The mate and triangle identities used here are exactly [fix-prop-adjunctions](1); no exceptional adjunction is involved. The same algebra is valid in the completed integral categories using the completed adjunction and the monoidality already supplied in step 2. It proves integral commutativity directly, without testing equality on finite evaluations.
