## Argument retained for the new statement-only node

For a smooth finite-type F-scheme X with relative SNC divisor D, field extension is flat, so the pullback remains Cartier. On affine charts the ideal of the reduced union D is the intersection of its finitely many component ideals. Flat tensor product preserves this finite intersection; each base-changed component is smooth and geometrically reduced. Thus the intersection of the new radical ideals is radical, and D_L is reduced. Smooth finite-type schemes over a field are regular/normal Noetherian, so their irreducible components are finite and pairwise disjoint open and closed integral subschemes. Splitting a base-changed D_a therefore produces disjoint smooth components. Components over different a cannot coincide: their intersection has codimension two, whereas each individual component has codimension one. For distinct original indices J, their scheme-theoretic intersection base changes to (D_J)_L; selecting one open-and-closed component of each (D_a)_L cuts out an open-and-closed subscheme of this smooth base-changed intersection. Smooth relative dimensions are unchanged by field extension (02FY(1)), so the difference between the local dimension of X and that of D_J is still |J|. This gives pure codimension |J| in every smooth component of X_L. Two chosen components over the same a are disjoint. For J empty the assertion is X_L smooth and codimension zero. This argument requires no separability and works independently on components of different dimensions. It is retained here as working material; the new node itself has no proof or proof dependencies.
