  Here c is written as the reduction of an arbitrary tensor representative z with

  quotient surjectivity. Its value is precisely reduction((id tensor F.val)(z))

  the B-action as well. Since B tensor R_P is generated by these images, the
  ring equivalence is an isomorphism of the stated coefficient algebras. Writing
