The helper's mixed identity is checked on sections: an element of R_a together with its second coordinate lifted to U_b determines x_b via that lift. The first coordinate at level a and this same x_b determine a unique first coordinate in U_b = U_a ×_{Z_a} Z_b. The two coordinates then have equal image x_b, giving an element of R_b. Conversely project a relation at level b to its level-a relation and keep its second coordinate. These operations are inverse and commute with restrictions and all transition maps. They identify h_{a,b} with the indicated base change of j_a. Taking the limit gives the displayed R identity. The limits of mixed products follow on sections from compatible pairs with the fixed first coordinate. This is a coherent atlas-construction lemma, not a separatedness-descent theorem or a renamed clause of the item.
