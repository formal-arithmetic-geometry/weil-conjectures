## Review by Q119

I checked each step against the statements of all nodes in its uses list; citations.json copies those actual statements, not facts taken from their proofs. The original statement and fixed-first-model clause agree with the 0AKA claim. I do not assert that a transition C_m to C_(m-1) is etale.

Step 1 sets up induction with a genuinely fixed earlier finite tower. The finite-type-Noetherian statement applies to the quotient base rings and their finite type algebras over A. The key nilpotence is b^2=0 in A_m, since 2(m-1)>=m for m>=2. The independent D_m comes from the item hypothesis, without assuming any prior compatibility.

Step 2 uses the exact completion node on finite modules over D, not on the generally nonfinite A_m-module D. The surjection onto bD and the inclusion of bD into D identify its completed image with b times the completion; this proves the actual completion-quotient comparison. The etale-base-change node gives precisely the etaleness of D_0 on the old complement. This avoids assuming a general completion commutes with arbitrary quotients theorem without its hypotheses.

Step 3 applies the new 0AKJ refinement to A_(m-1), D_0, R and the specified map of their completions. R is Noetherian. The resulting R' has identical closed fibre; the existing augmented-etale node 0H1D gives all its K-power quotient isomorphisms and hence the completion isomorphism. This argument is written inline, not made into an unnecessary new node.

Step 4 uses composition/cancellation only on the open complement where both objects are etale over the same base. At the closed subset, the module-completion node identifies power quotients on both sides of the completed isomorphism, and 0A43 applies to a finite type morphism between locally Noetherian affine schemes. The finite-type-Noetherian node justifies Noetherianity of R'. Thus u is etale everywhere, not merely formally etale at the closed fibre.

Step 5 uses the affine-etale finite-presentation node to check the exact hypothesis of lem-exc-etale-algebra-lift. That lemma gives D' with its specified reduction equal to u. Composition/cancellation gives etaleness of D'/T away from H; finite generation over T is the elementary concatenation of two finite generator lists.

Step 6 checks the source's omitted comparison. Etaleness survives reduction modulo H by the base-change node. The image of b has square zero and the reduction modulo b is an isomorphism because completed u is an isomorphism. In 0H1D the kernel of the map to the quotient is exactly the source ideal (b); n=2 makes both its squares zero, giving an isomorphism before reduction modulo b. A second application with H gives all H-power quotients. The inverse limit is the original completed map, and its reduction modulo b is the specified completed u, so the isomorphism with E_m has the required reduction normalization.

Step 7 checks all three hypotheses of 0ALT with base T, nilpotent ideal (b), C=D', and fixed B_0=R. In particular the direction of the etale map is R to R', and its isomorphism modulo K is known. The lemma provides an actual isomorphism B/bB=R and the commuting reduction diagram; no flatness of the transition is used.

Step 8 repeats the two short 0H1D applications for B to D', now using the specified reduction j beta. This preserves the completion and constructs v_m. The equation v_(m-1)t_m=red(v_m) follows because the completed j in j beta is cancelled by its inverse in the reduction of D' to E_m. The earlier tower is untouched, so ordinary recursion gives the infinite tower. This writes out the compatibility rather than using an unspecified transport.
