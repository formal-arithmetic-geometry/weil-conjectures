## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported preamble says that the whole statement holds verbatim for Weil complexes. In this project these are pairs in an already triangulated category, not a triangulated category themselves. I read the full statement of `def-weil-weil-sheaf-complex`, including its final paragraph, and the analogous warning in `def-trf-strict-weil-complex`. The type error is specific: clause (2) applies `def-weil-t-structure` to a category for which no triangulated structure is provided.

There is a concrete obstruction to conflating that pair category with the derived category of Weil sheaves. At a geometric point a nontrivial two-dimensional unipotent Frobenius Jordan block is a nonsplit extension of the trivial Weil representation by itself. Nevertheless Hom of the corresponding geometric pairs from the trivial object to its shift by one is zero: geometric point cohomology has no degree-one group. Thus one cannot infer Weil derived Ext groups or triangles from the pair category's Hom groups. On a curve the same example is supported at a rational closed point. This is exactly the counterexample recorded in the existing definition node.

The repaired statement keeps all five geometric clauses, over every perfect field with ell invertible. It retains clauses (1), (3), (4), (5) and abelianness for the category of Weil pairs in the geometric perverse heart. Only the ill-typed assertion of a t-structure on a bare pair category is qualified. Step 13 proves the equivariant assertions by kernels/cokernels in an abelian category; it never completes a cone there.

I also made the rational-coefficient meaning explicit. `def-per-support-condition` is written with qbar coefficients but points to `def-trf-dualizing-complex`, which at present defines finite A-coefficient duality over an algebraic closure of a finite field. The imported theorem, however, allows any perfect k, including characteristic zero and k0 itself. I retained that scope and explicitly defined its rational dual as RHom(-,Ra_C^!qbar), with the needed comparisons stated in the new lemma. I did not apply `thm-coef-qbar-verdier`, `prop-constructible-truncations`, or the purity-conventions corollary outside their finite-field hypotheses. No target declaration is edited.

Finally the blueprint Step 0(i) trivializes a rational lisse sheaf on an étale cover. An arbitrary l-adic local system need not have such an étale trivialization (finite discrete reductions do). Step 2 instead uses the existing dense-open unit theorem on a normal curve and adjunction. This preserves the unit's route through the maximal finite-support subsheaf and ordinary truncation.
