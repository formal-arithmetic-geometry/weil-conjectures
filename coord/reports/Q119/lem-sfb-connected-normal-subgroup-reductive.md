## Source error

Milne Corollary 21.53's proof says that R_u(G) is contained in N intersect R(G), then concludes R_u(G)=e. Both occurrences must refer to R_u(N): triviality of R_u(G) is already a hypothesis and cannot establish reductivity of N. The correction is visible by taking G=GL_2 and N=SL_2: G's radical being trivial in the unipotent sense says nothing new about N without an argument. This is a proof-variable typo, not a false statement. I do not change the item's correct statement. The present proof instead uses 6.46 and 1.85 directly and is marked proof_origin.by=agent to distinguish it from Milne's displayed semisimple-quotient proof.
