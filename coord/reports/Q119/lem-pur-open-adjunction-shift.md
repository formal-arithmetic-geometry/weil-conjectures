## Argument preservation and future proof briefs

The first new node is a general form of the old proof's system assembly. For a section X its LX transition is alpha composed with L of the X transition, and PY uses beta composed with P of the Y transition. To check the unit is a section map, expand the transition of PLX, commute P(alpha) past the ordinary unit by naturality, and use the displayed beta-P(alpha)-eta identity; this gives r(eta) composed with the original transition. For the counit use the second displayed compatibility and counit naturality. Both triangles then hold componentwise, and section morphisms are exactly compatible families, so the triangles are equalities of section maps. Componentwise transposition and its inverse preserve the transition equations by these same identities. Kernels and cokernels of section morphisms are computed in the fibers: exactness of r and s supplies the induced transitions on these kernels and cokernels, with the universal properties componentwise. Thus exactness of L and P is checked in every fiber. There is no assumption that section transitions are invertible, and no normalized-system hypothesis.

For its application to the old open-extension argument, j is etale and U is an object of W_proet, with localization the site U_proet. Apply the existing exact localized-module adjunction separately to (W_proet,(A_n)_W). There is no object localizing all levels simultaneously in the naive product site. The open extension presheaf is the direct sum over maps V to U of the sections on (V to U); restricting coefficient scalars changes the action but neither these groups nor the restrictions or summand maps. Module sheafification is the underlying abelian sheafification, so the comparison with coefficient restriction is identity on underlying sheaves. Restriction itself has the same compatibility. The construction of the open transpose on each summand commutes with the scalar action, so these comparisons identify the ordinary units and counits. These facts are already stated by `lem-pur-sequence-site-open-extension`, which the assembly cites instead of creating another open-extension node. The gluing node now assembles those compatible fiber adjunctions. The given system realization names exactly this exact termwise adjunction, and `def-weil-exact-derived-adjunction` fixes its localization and the actual unit/counit.

The second new node retains the old proof's entire shift calculation and descent. In degree m the two sides of the left shift comparison have term L(X^(m+a)), and their differentials coincide because L is additive: L((-1)^a d)=(-1)^a L(d). The right functor has the same property. The identity term maps are natural and satisfy the zero and addition coherence, including the standard cochain shift identifications. The unit of the complex adjunction at X[a] in degree m is eta at X^(m+a); thus the unit equation is already an equality of cochain maps.
