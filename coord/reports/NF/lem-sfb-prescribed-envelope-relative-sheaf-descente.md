## What changed and what was wrong

The item was statement-only in both the sandbox and the repository. I retained its full statement, including its explicit adjunction normalization, arbitrary nonflat coefficient maps, the prescribed ambient scheme, and the sheaf-level cone bound. I did not replace a sheaf bound by a bound after global sections. The Stacks paragraph after 0CSD only asserts Kiehl finiteness and cites the article; it does not give this approximation theorem. Accordingly all assembly steps and both new formulations are marked as agent material, with that paragraph used as context rather than falsely cited as a numbered proof.

The split uses the allowable choice Z=Y_{B'} and k=id. Closed-support invariance first passes from E on X to Rj_*E on Y_A. The two real existence difficulties are then separated into an affine module problem and a conditional two-open gluing problem. The finite affine-cover induction is in the item, not hidden in either new node. In particular the locality lemma cannot prove the item without the affine input, and the affine input cannot construct objects on a nonaffine envelope.

During self-review I caught a real citation mismatch: lem-pc-affine-finite-type-algebra assumes a FIELD. It has been removed and replaced with the existing general lemma lem-weil-pbc-lft-affine-pairs. Searching its declaration and Tag 01T2 found the existing node, so no generalization node was created. The Mathlib searches confirm Scheme.Hom.finiteType_appLE (FiniteType.lean lines 44–48). I also expanded the affine pushforward dictionary into its own proof step, using the principal-open section formula and the unbounded affine-pushforward resolution lemma, rather than taking restriction-of-scalars compatibility from a theorem that says only t-exactness.

## What remains below this split

The two new lemmas are deliberately unsplit, as required for a one-level delivery. Their difficult content is genuine: the affine lift must retain the R-action and control the DERIVED scalar tensor without assuming flatness, and the locality lemma must reconcile finite-window approximations and their maps on an overlap. Ordinary descent of finitely presented modules, descent of perfect objects, and the gluing theorem for objects with an already-given exact overlap isomorphism do not supply either assertion. These issues are not asserted elementary in the item. A future split of the locality lemma will have to supply the overlap comparison and necessary low-degree corrections; merely citing the ordinary derived gluing lemma would not suffice.

Step 5: opened pqf-lem-coherent-ft-locnoeth, finite-type Noetherian permanence, affine equivalence, the affine-pushforward resolution lemma, the associated-sheaf localization formula, affine derived pullback model, tensor structure and derived adjunction. S'=S tensor_D D' is finite type over D', hence Noetherian. Tilde of a finitely generated module is quasi-coherent and of finite type (its generators give a sheaf surjection); the coherent criterion thus applies to every H^i(L). To check the scalar identification one may take a K-flat D'-resolution P→A: S' tensor_D' P is K-flat over S' because tensoring an acyclic S'-complex with it is tensoring its underlying D'-complex with P. Then (S' tensor_D' P) tensor_S' L=P tensor_D' L. This computes the claimed source even when S' is not flat over D'. The transpose acts by the A-action after gamma, exactly the map specified in the affine node. Exact tilde and the affine equivalence transfer the cone bound, retaining quasi-coherence. No boundedness of the derived scalar tensor was asserted.
