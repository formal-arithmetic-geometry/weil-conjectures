## STATEMENT-DISPUTED: norm topology versus colimit topology

The source document of the imported item is the compactification refinement proof (2), not a proof of its subsequently added coefficient-generation claim. I read Hemo–Richarz–Scholbach, printed pp. 14–15 and 21–22: Theorem 7.7 treats finite E on qcqs schemes, whereas Lemma 7.9 explicitly equips the algebraic closure with the colimit topology. Neither licenses replacing its condensed ring by all norm-continuous functions. Here is the obstruction to that replacement, including why it obstructs a *localizing* hull rather than only finite-field descent.

Take l=3, k a finite field of characteristic different from 3, S=beta(N), and X=Spec C(S,k), with k discrete. This is affine qcqs, with underlying Stone space S, all residue fields k, and no nontrivial specializations. Choose primes d_n tending to infinity and elements u_n in the unramified extension of Q_3 of degree d_n, integral, with residue of degree d_n. Put a_n=1+3^n u_n. These are units tending uniformly to 1, with [Q_3(a_n):Q_3]=d_n. Extend n -> a_n to a continuous function a:S -> Omega by setting it equal to 1 at every nonprincipal ultrafilter. The family of characters

    rho_n: Zhat -> Omega^times, z -> a_n^z

factors through Z_3, and is jointly continuous in (s,z): on the complement of finitely many n its values are uniformly 3^n-close to 1. Define rho(s,z)=1 for nonprincipal s. Descent along the constant geometric-field pro-etale Zhat-torsor over X gives a rank-one locally free module L for the all-norm-continuous coefficient ring B. It is a finite constant local model on the single stratum X and has Tor amplitude [0,0]. Arithmetic Frobenius is the B-linear endomorphism multiplication by a on L.

For clarity, the following test can be implemented on a weakly contractible affine cover W -> X obtained by adjoining the algebraic closure of k and then an extremally disconnected cover of the profinite component space. Such covers are provided by Stacks proetale.tex, proposition-find-w-contractible and lemma-w-local-strictly-henselian-extremally-disconnected. Choose one point w_n above each principal n. Let R=Gamma(W,B). Evaluation at w_n and a nonprincipal ultrafilter U on N give a unital ring map R -> K=prod_U Omega to an ultraproduct field. Central arithmetic Frobenius acts as a B-linear endomorphism on this zero-dimensional constant finite-field family (equivalently use its description as sheaves with continuous Zhat-action over S); it acts trivially on B. It therefore makes Gamma(W,M) an R[t,t^-1]-complex for every coefficient module complex M. Form the exact derived functor

    Q(M) = K(t) tensor^L_{R[t,t^-1]} Gamma(W,M).

Here R -> K -> K(t) is as above, and t maps to the indeterminate t. More generally first localize K[t,t^-1] at all polynomials represented by bounded-degree nonzero F-polynomials, for all finite F/Q_3; this suffices and does not invert t-a. To avoid that last mistake, use this latter localization, call it D, in place of K(t) in the displayed formula. Include all sequences of monic polynomials of a fixed finite degree with coefficients in a fixed finite F, viewed in K[t,t^-1]. Each has nonzero value at alpha=[a_n] in K: for cofinally many n, d_n exceeds its degree times [F:Q_3]. Thus evaluation t -> alpha extends to D -> K, and t-alpha is not inverted.

The functor Gamma(W,-) is exact because W is weakly contractible; it preserves small coproducts because W is qcqs and pro-etale coverings admit finite qc refinements (sections commute with filtered colimits on coherent objects). Derived tensor with D also preserves coproducts and triangles. Hence the kernel of Q is localizing. This construction deliberately does not apply an ultraproduct functor to module vector spaces: that functor would fail to preserve arbitrary coproducts. The ultraproduct here only specifies a coefficient-ring map; the functor is derived scalar extension along it.

For any proposed generator B tensor^L_{O_F} J, the bounded constructible integral cohomology has uniformly bounded scalar ranks. On this zero-dimensional Stone base a finite constructible stratification is clopen; after the cover W its integral models are finite scalar modules over O_F. Frobenius on their rationalizations has at w_n a characteristic polynomial over F of uniformly bounded degree. Cayley–Hamilton kills its rationalized cohomology by a monic polynomial sequence included among the denominators defining D. A finite product deals with the finitely many cohomology degrees and strata; torsion dies upon extension to B. Thus Q kills each proposed generator. But Q(L) is nonzero: its t-action becomes alpha, and the map D -> K with t -> alpha exhibits a nonzero quotient, so its degree-zero tensor is K. Therefore L is outside the localizing hull. The obstruction uses unbounded coefficient degrees over a non-Noetherian qcqs Stone family, not a claim about a single finite-type connected scheme.

Correction: the algebraic-closure coefficient is Omega^fin of sk-def-sfb-qlbar-condensed, the finite-subextension-valued ring. The family rho above is not defined over that condensed ring: the compact family a(S) lies in no finite extension. This is exactly the source distinction in HRS Lemma 7.9. The corrected statement explicitly says which coefficient changed and no longer invokes the norm-topological localizing-closure definition for this case.
