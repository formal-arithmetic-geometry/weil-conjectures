## Statement clarification

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the original sentence “The complement of W has regular local rings of dimension at least two” is ambiguous. It must mean the ambient local rings O_{X,x} for x outside W, precisely the hypothesis of Stacks 0BMA and of the cited extension node. If interpreted as local rings of a closed subscheme on the complement, it is false. For example take B=k[x,y], f=x and C=B x B/(x,y), with the diagonal B-algebra structure. Over D(x) the second component is empty and the first component is the identity. At each height-one boundary prime the only source points are on the first component, where identity neighbourhoods are etale. Taking O to be that component gives W=X minus the origin. The reduced complement is Spec k, of dimension zero, whereas the ambient stalk at the origin is regular of dimension two. I corrected the sentence to explicitly name O_{X,x}; no hypothesis or intended conclusion is weakened. Added the dimension definition to statement dependencies.
