## Source error

Zheng's alternative bounded-below justification after Lemma 4.10 claims that a product of e_(n,*)I_(n), with injective A_n-modules I_(n), has injective evaluation at every index. That last assertion fails for changing quotient rings. In the punctual topos take O=k[[t]], lambda=t, I_(1)=k over A_1=k and I_(n)=0 for n>1. The system e_(1,*)k is injective because e_1 is exact and e_(1,*) is its right adjoint. Its second evaluation is k over A_2=k[t]/(t^2), which is not injective: the A_2-linear map (t) -> k taking t to 1 cannot extend to A_2, since any A_2-linear map A_2 -> k kills t. (Use n=0 and n=1 for Zheng's indexing.) The item itself is not disputed: its comparison follows from the preceding, valid unbounded proof using Le_n!, which this split follows. No statement is weakened. In particular no constant-ring evaluation-preserves-K-injectives lemma is used on a changing-ring system.
