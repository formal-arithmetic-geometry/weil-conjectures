The product-site argument underlying the new realization lemma is as follows. Give J^op x C coverings in the C direction with identity J component, including their isomorphic variants. Pullback of a generating cover along (a,V -> U) is the same C-cover pulled back to V, with fixed source J-object; composites are composites of C-covers. Thus these covers generate the stated topology. Currying a presheaf gives a covariant J-diagram of presheaves on C. Its sheaf condition for these covers is precisely the sheaf condition at each fixed j. The same formulas identify module structures, linear maps, kernels and sheafified cokernels. For evaluation at j, define R_j A(k)=product_{k -> j} A. For b:k -> l, its component at alpha:l -> j is projection onto alpha b:k -> j. A map F(j)->A gives the natural map F(k)->R_j A whose alpha component is F(k)->F(j)->A; conversely project its j component onto id_j. These constructions are inverse. Finite limits of diagrams are computed componentwise, so evaluation is a left exact left adjoint, hence inverse image of a geometric morphism. On modules over the constant ring diagram its inverse image is exact evaluation. This proof uses no exactness claim about infinite products of sheaves.
