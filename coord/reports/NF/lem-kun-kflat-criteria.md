## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported clause (3) claims every bounded-below complex of flat sheaves is K-flat. This is false for cochain complexes with direct-sum totalization. Clause (2) must refer to the homotopy category, as Stacks 07A3 explicitly does, rather than allow arbitrary distinguished triangles in the derived category. The repaired statement specifies the homotopy category and replaces the false bounded-below corollary with the actual consequence: a small filtered degreewise colimit of bounded-above flat complexes is K-flat. It retains the valid filtered-colimit assertion, without asserting that upper stupid truncations supply such a diagram. It is the source's valid K-flat-resolution/closure intent, not a proof of the false imported sentence.

Explicit counterexample for clause (3): let R = F_2[e]/(e^2) and T = Spec of an algebraically closed field, so constant R-module sheaves on the small etale topos are R-modules. Let F^i=R for i>=0, zero otherwise, with every retained differential multiplication by e. Every term is free, and F is bounded below. Let A^j=R for all integers j, with every differential multiplication by e. A is acyclic since ker(e)=eR=im(e). In every total degree of F tensor A the module is V=direct sum over i>=0 of R; its differential is eB, where B=1+S and S takes basis vector v_i to v_(i+1). Signs are all + in characteristic 2. Modulo e, B is injective on finite-support vectors but has cokernel F_2: sum of coefficients vanishes on its image, and every finite vector with coefficient sum zero is in its image (solve the successive coefficient equations). Therefore ker(eB)=eV, im(eB)=eB(V), and H^n(F tensor A)=F_2 for every n. F is not K-flat.

The derived-category ambiguity has an even shorter counterexample: the same acyclic A is isomorphic to zero in D(R), so 0 -> 0 -> A -> 0 is a distinguished triangle there. The first two terms are K-flat, but A is not: A tensor_R A has in every degree the finite-support module direct sum over i in Z of R, with differential e(1+S). On finite-support bilateral vectors over F_2, 1+S is injective and has cokernel F_2 (sum of coefficients again identifies the cokernel). Thus the same kernel/image calculation gives nonzero F_2 cohomology in every degree, although the testing factor A is acyclic. Thus K-flatness is not a property of arbitrary representatives of a derived object. This failure does not occur for homotopy-category isomorphisms, and step 3 explicitly verifies that invariance.

The blueprint's proof (1) uses descending induction on the upper bound without a base case for an infinitely left-unbounded complex. Its proof (3) uses inclusions sigma_(<=n) F -> sigma_(<=n+1) F that are not chain maps when d^n is nonzero: the source has zero outgoing differential in degree n, and the proposed target has d^n. These are quotient truncations, not subcomplexes (confirmed by the repaired def-kun-truncations statement). We replace these invalid arguments with the existing bounded-above ringed-site result and the exact filtered-colimit argument.
