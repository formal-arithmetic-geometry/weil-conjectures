For the retained node, the source argument is: the covering definition/098D gives a finite affine open refinement $V_j\to T$. The finite coproduct is affine, surjective and weakly etale; its corresponding ring map $A\to\prod_j\Gamma(V_j,\mathcal O)$ is faithfully flat and weakly etale. The ring hypothesis gives a retraction, hence a section into this finite coproduct. Composing with the morphism induced by $V_j\to T_{i_j}$ gives a section into the original coproduct, including for repeated indices. These standard affine/flat/product facts are the nontrivial content left in that smaller node, not hidden in an elementary step of the item.

Edge cases: for $A=0$, $T$ is empty and the section into any coproduct is unique; the empty covering causes no excluded case. A one-member cover gives an actual section and its single pullback lift. For a separably closed field satisfying the ring hypothesis, the nonempty clopen partition has one piece; an arbitrary non-separably-closed field need not satisfy the splitting hypothesis (a nontrivial finite separable extension has no retraction), so no false unconditional field assertion is made. No positivity assumption on the number of covering members is imposed.
