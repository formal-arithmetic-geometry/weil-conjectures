## What changed and why

This is the deliberate re-split requested by the human, not a correction of the item statement. The old proof combined the independent quotient evaluation argument and a relative affine classification/base-change argument with the final assembly. Those are now distinct nodes. The generic relative statement involves no roots, exponent, polynomial or sheaf: it cannot imply the item by itself. The algebraic leaf involves no scheme and cannot imply representability by itself. Their combination, together with the unit conversion and the sheaf comparison, is the item's proof.

The old non-elementary kernel step named Mathlib comparisons but cited only the roots definition. The existing `def-pur-abelian-sheaf-kernel` already states the needed sectionwise kernel and inclusion comparison; I now cite it, instead of creating a duplicate. The roots definition itself already specifies the actual restriction maps. No invertibility assumption on n is used for this identification.

The final assembly is 2293 characters, and its longest step is less than 700 characters. There is no non-elementary step over 2000 characters.
