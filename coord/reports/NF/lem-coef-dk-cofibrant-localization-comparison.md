## Findings and corrections

The statement agrees with the cited papers and needs no mathematical correction or STATEMENT-DISPUTED marker. Its phrase “weak equivalences on mapping sets” is interpreted as the maps of mapping simplicial sets to the corresponding discrete sets, as the free resolution and source specify.

The two different weak-equivalence arguments must be distinguished: augmentation is not a degreewise equivalence of ordinary categories, and ordinary localization does not preserve arbitrary mappingwise weak equivalences. The left comparison applies the general hammock homotopy lemma to the mappingwise augmentation equivalences. The right comparison first uses the actual free-factor decomposition in each resolution degree, then the existing bisimplicial diagonal theorem. Both new nodes are needed for non-elementary source facts; neither is an application of ordinary localization uniqueness.

Cofibrancy in the paper is originally expressed in the fixed-object model structure. The item asks for the Bergner structure. Step 4 supplies a direct Bergner lifting argument, including object lifts, rather than silently identifying those model structures. Step 3 proves the relative nondegenerate-generator property needed to retain the prescribed lifts on the subcategory.

I rejected the tempting Mathlib `ExtraDegeneracy.homotopyEquiv` leaf: its statement is for alternating face-map complexes in a preadditive category. The source inspection also found the general `ExtraDegeneracy.homotopy`, but the item instead gives the concrete realization contraction, so no extra node is needed. Likewise the free-factor decomposition, the generator lifting construction, and the strict naturality calculation are proved inside the item instead of becoming clauses under new names.
