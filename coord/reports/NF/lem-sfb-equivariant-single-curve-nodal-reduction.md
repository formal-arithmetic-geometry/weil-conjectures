## Review by NF

I read the retained report and proof, the current shared item, its chapter and unit, GOAL.md, the three levels in SETUP.md and both complete B–E lists in delivery-errors.md. I did not use any SGA source.

For the four replacement statements, I checked their hypotheses and their strict separation of tasks. The auxiliary cover is the Kummer construction of §§3.4–3.5, after the finite separable extensions of §§3.2 and 3.6. The fibre-product group projects onto the original group even if its action is nonfaithful; its kernel fixes exactly the preceding total field. This is stronger information than equality of full-group invariants and is explicitly stated in the node. The cover gives no semistability, so it cannot replace the whole item by itself. Over a field the cover is the smooth genus-raising covering of §3.3, followed by rationalization of the marking; no positive-dimensional-base pencil is used in that case. Empty markings are allowed.

Stable pointed generic extension is Lemma 3.7: its curve already has genus at least two, so zero, one or two markings do not violate stability. It gives a separable alteration and an extended stable pointed curve, without a map to the old curve. This avoids restating the dropped target-relative theorem. Finite-Isom rigidity is the unordered-marking statement used at the end of §3.8. Its finiteness is what allows a generic section to extend over a normal integral base, and separatedness gives uniqueness. Generic maps may be nonidentity, as needed to lift the action.

For Hom closure I read §§3.9–3.13 and its valuative argument. The node isolates representability and properness of the closure, not the existence of the source. The source must already be projective semistable with smooth generic fibre. The target is projective and has no nonconstant rational fibre curve. This last property is the only use of the target's high genus in Lemma 3.13: after resolving the rational map from the nodal trait surface, rational exceptional curves have constant image, so the map descends. It therefore applies also to the raw pullback Q over S_2; no unproved integrality or flatness of that pullback is required. The closure of a single K-point is integral after taking its reduced structure. Naturality gives the action on R. This modest target generalization is stated as agent-formulated, rather than attributed verbatim to the source.

Step 1 uses exactly the retained preparation and the excellence/Noetherian permanence statements; it no longer claims a stable modification can be applied. Step 2 takes the high-genus covering, rational generic markings and both kernel fixed fields from the new cover statement. I checked all input hypotheses against preparation, in particular pure one-dimensional geometric fibres, projectivity, smooth geometric generic integrality and etale generic marking. Step 3 applies only stable pointed extension to that smooth genus-at-least-two generic curve; its alteration has an integral source T. Domination gives a normal projective base and the separable fixed-field equality. Pullback preserves stable pointed curves by the explicit extension-node clause.

Step 4 uses finite-Isom extension for h^*W and W, with the group law proved by equality on the generic fibre and uniqueness.
