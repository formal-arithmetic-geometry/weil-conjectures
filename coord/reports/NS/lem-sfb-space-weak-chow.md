The intended arguments for the three unsplit inputs, retained here for the next workers, are as follows. For maximum degree, separatedness gives quasi-separatedness, so Tag 03JX bounds the fibres of a quasi-compact atlas. Tag 07S8 produces the degree filtration; its top nonempty stratum is open because no higher degree occurs. Over that stratum the atlas is finite locally free, and etaleness gives finite etale. The closed complement pulls back to a closed subscheme of the affine atlas, and its geometric fibres have smaller cardinality.

For the configuration input, each repeated-coordinate diagonal is open and closed since the atlas is etale and separated. Thus the distinct-tuple complement in the affine fibre product is quasi-compact. The map to the product of compactifications is a quasi-compact immersion: the equal-image condition is a closed diagonal test of the separated space. Tag 01RG makes its image a schematically dense open in its closure. The coordinate projections are proper by projectivity and the closed graph over the separated projective base. Their composites with the atlas agree on the dense configuration; the closed equalizer for the separated target makes them agree on each overlap, so the sheaf property glues them. The configuration fibre over a geometric point is exactly the set of enumerations of the d-point atlas fibre. A field-valued enumeration splits the finite etale fibre into d copies of that field, which proves the coordinate-completeness assertion under every subsequent field extension. These are the inputs to properness, not its proof; the valuation argument and induction remain in the item.

For the refined criterion, Tag 089G applies to the specified dense quasi-compact open. Finite type gives quasi-compactness of p. Separatedness supplies condition (3): the equalizer of two valuation lifts is closed in the spectrum of a valuation domain and contains its generic point, hence is all of that spectrum. The stated valuation-extension hypothesis is condition (4). Universal closedness then combines with the already assumed separatedness and finite type to give properness.
