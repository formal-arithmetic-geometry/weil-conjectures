## Corrections and choices

The imported statement is correct and was kept exactly. Its proof did not discharge the auxiliary exceptional-admissibility assumptions of the existing trace-map lemma; those are now checked for the fixed-square maps and structure maps. The current definition `def-trf-admissibility` means constructible exceptional adjoints, not the stronger ambient adjoint datum. Step 3 invokes a theorem, `sk-thm-sf-package`, for existence and preservation rather than deriving those assertions from the definition of admissibility.

The imported phrase identifying the *target object* with a Hom set is imprecise: global-sections adjunction identifies morphisms from the constant coefficient object to that target with morphisms from the constant sheaf on the fixed scheme to its dualizing complex. Steps 4 and 6 state this correctly. This is a correction of the proof's typing description, not a change of the theorem or of the trace class.

The imported citations to external duality, Kunneth–Verdier, and the supported pairing merely re-explain the existing trace-map lemma's proof. The split uses the lemma's actual stated existence assertion, and leaves those arguments below that lemma. The pairing unit is not keyed by this proposition: its key statement is point normalization. I therefore did not change its `realized` fields. The current `main-20261015` proposition has the same statement and proof as the older imported node; I followed its current separate proof file. The two-arrow route is preserved.
