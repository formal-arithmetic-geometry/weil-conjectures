## Source error

The unit's Coordinates proof says that eliminating x makes the general chart affine space over the base. This is false for an etale coordinate algebra R. For B=Spec k, V=G_m, and the section z=1, set x=z-1. Then R=k[x,(1+x)^{-1}] and the actual deformation chart is k[t,u,(1+tu)^{-1}], with x=tu. It is not k[t,u]: 1+tu is a nonconstant unit in the former, whereas the units of the latter are constants. The chart is etale over affine space, which suffices for smoothness. Steps 1-2 use the existing `lem-pur-dnc-etale-rees-chart` to make exactly this correction. The affine-space identification is retained only for the standard pair, where it is true. This repairs a failing inference inside the unit's route without changing its statement or the chapter route. The item itself says etale-local coordinates and is not statement-disputed.
