### Old step 1

Choose a regular affine neighbourhood $V=\operatorname{Spec}R$ of $x$; its existence and the Noetherianity of $R$ are part of the affine definition of regularity. Write $\mathfrak p\subset R$ for the maximal ideal of the reduced closed point $x$. By the glued local-model construction of the blowup, $Y=b^{-1}V$ is the blowup of $\mathfrak p$ in $\operatorname{Spec}R$, with its coefficient structure map. One can include $V$ in the gluing cover; cover independence gives the same morphism over $T$. Every ideal of the Noetherian ring $R$ is finitely generated, so this blowup is proper. In particular $f:Y\to V$ is separated and of finite type, hence quasi-compact; the separated-implies-quasi-separated lemma makes $f$ quasi-separated. Put $M=\Gamma(Y,\mathcal O_Y)$, an $R$-algebra via pullback.

### Old step 2

For any multiplicative subset $S\subset R$, the localization $R\to S^{-1}R$ is flat, so the induced affine-scheme map is flat. Apply flat base change with $i=0$ and $\mathcal F=\mathcal O_Y$ to obtain the canonical isomorphism
$$M\otimes_R S^{-1}R\longrightarrow\Gamma(Y\times_V\operatorname{Spec}(S^{-1}R),\mathcal O),\qquad m\otimes a/s\longmapsto (a/s)\,m|.$$
The pullback of the structure module is the structure module: locally the identification is $B\otimes_B C\to C$, $b\otimes c\mapsto bc$, with inverse $c\mapsto1\otimes c$. Thus the displayed $i=0$ comparison is a ring homomorphism, not just a module map, and is an isomorphism of rings. Identify its source with $S^{-1}M$ by $m\otimes a/s\mapsto am/s$, whose inverse sends $m/s$ to $m\otimes1/s$. This formula also shows compatibility with further localization and with the coefficient map from $S^{-1}R$. The hypotheses on $f$ were checked in step 1; the structure module is quasi-coherent, being the free rank-one module on each affine chart.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-localization-flat, lem-weil-pbc-flat-ring-map-iff-flat-affine, lem-pc-flat-base-change-cohom, pqf-def-coherent.

### Old step 3

$$\Gamma(b^{-1}D(h),\mathcal O_W)=M_h.$$
These identifications commute with restrictions. The $D(h)$ containing $x$ form a neighbourhood basis, so the stalk definition gives
$$(b_*\mathcal O_W)_x=\varinjlim_{h\notin\mathfrak p}M_h\cong M_{\mathfrak p}.$$

### Old step 5

### Old step 6

### Old step 8

The charts in step 7 are the standard opens $D_+(sT)$ and $D_+(tT)$ in the Proj of the Rees algebra. On $U_s$ the second homogeneous coordinate divided by the first is $t/s$; consequently $U_s\cap U_t$ is the basic open where $t/s$ is invertible.
