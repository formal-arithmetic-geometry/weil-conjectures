## Faults and corrections

The existing item was sound but unsplit. Its Stacks citation, Categories Tag 04AZ (`definition-codirected`), only defines cofilteredness; it cannot by itself justify the homotopy conclusion. I preserved the source's explicit distinction, kept the statement unchanged, and supplied the actual finite-piece argument.

I did not replace the item with a new node asserting that filtered quasicategories are weakly contractible. Instead, the common-target/coequalization induction and the actual contraction of the finite piece are carried by the item's steps. The only unsplit new theorem concerns realizations of arbitrary simplicial sets, without filteredness or any contractibility conclusion.

The initial draft of the Mathlib tool described local compactness as existence of one compact neighborhood per point. Reading `Topology/Defs/Filter.lean:293-311` showed that Mathlib distinguishes that weak condition from the neighborhood-basis condition used by `continuous_lift_prod_left`. I corrected the new statement to the latter. The interval application meets the stronger condition. I also avoided the false shortcuts that a finite nerve piece is itself contractible, that the nerve of a filtered category is Kan, or that reversing an ordinary nerve gives a simplicial isomorphism to the opposite nerve.
