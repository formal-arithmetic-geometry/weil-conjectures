## Source error

The item's statement agrees with dualizing.tex, Definition 0E2C (definition-relative-dualizing-complex) and Lemma 0E2D (lemma-uniqueness-relative-dualizing). No statement dispute or correction is needed. However, the printed proof invokes more-algebra.tex 0ATK(3) twice without checking its Tor hypothesis over B=A tensor_R A. R-perfectness gives a Tor bound over R, not over B or the opposite copy of A. This implication is false: over a field k take A=k[x,y]/(x,y)^2 and its ordinary dualizing module Hom_k(A,k). It is k-perfect and satisfies the diagonal condition, but has infinite projective dimension over A (a finite module of finite projective dimension over this depth-zero local ring would be free, and the dualizing module is not free). Its induction from either copy consequently cannot be asserted to have finite Tor dimension over B. Thus the cited case of 0ATK cannot be applied merely with the hypotheses printed in 0E2D. I have not claimed the displayed isomorphisms themselves are false.

The split uses a polynomial-duality argument below the unit level. It does not assume any Noetherian hypothesis, replace a unit-level step, use smooth purity, or cite SGA. proof_origin.by is agent because the arbitrary-base polynomial detection route is not a verbatim source proof.

## Supporting arguments and remaining proof obligations

The three new nodes are statement-only obligations, not claims that their proofs have already been formalized or split.

For polynomial-dual properties, read dualizing.tex 0BZW, lemma-relative-dualizing-algebraic, its complete proof, and more-algebra.tex 067J (lemma-flat-finite-presentation-perfect), 068X (lemma-perfect-over-polynomial-ring), 07VI (lemma-dual-perfect-complex), and 0E1T (lemma-more-relative-pseudo-coherent-is-moot). Flat finite presentation makes A perfect over every finite polynomial presentation. Dualizing a finite projective polynomial resolution yields a P-perfect restriction of C and a finite R-Tor interval. Pseudo-coherence of its A-object is obtained by descending the finite presentation and its perfect polynomial resolution to a Noetherian model, taking the usual polynomial dual there, and using perfect coinduction base change; the base change of the A-pseudo-coherent object stays pseudo-coherent. Homothety is polynomial perfect biduality under coinduction adjunction, exactly the scalar compatibility already isolated in lem-trf-algebraic-shriek-derived-hom-adjunction. This argument uses no existence or uniqueness of the relative dualizing object being proved here.

For the diagonal comparison, read the existing lem-trf-polynomial-diagonal-rhom-reduction and its five proof steps, and the statements lem-trf-perfect-algebra-dual-evaluation, lem-trf-derived-bimodule-tensor-hom, lem-trf-polynomial-graph-coinduction and lem-trf-algebraic-shriek-derived-hom-adjunction. Its only Noetherian role is establishing perfectness of the polynomial quotient. Over arbitrary R, 067J gives the same perfectness. With S=A_1 tensor_R P, B=A_1 tensor_R A_2 and q:S to B, the perfect dual E=C_q(S) is B tensor_{A_2}^L C_{P to A_2}(P). Perfect base change gives A tensor_B^L E the graph action on that polynomial dual. Perfect evaluation identifies B tensor_S^L V with derived S-Hom from E into V. Mixed-ring tensor-Hom adjunction and graph Koszul coinduction then give the stated comparison for V=S tensor_{A_1}^L T. The graph computation is valid for arbitrary T, so the new node deliberately does not add the unnecessary relative-perfect restriction on T. All isomorphisms are in D(A), and restriction is along p_1 throughout.

The detection obligation is specifically for the polynomial coinduced C and R-perfect test objects, not arbitrary semidualizing objects over arbitrary rings. Read 0A7C (lemma-dualizing), 0E1U (lemma-compute-RHom-relatively-perfect), and the existing Noetherian lem-trf-semidualizing-rhom-conservative. Relative-perfect objects admit bounded R-flat representatives and are perfect on the polynomial ambient ring; fibrewise polynomial duals are the usual dualizing complexes. These provide the natural route through relative duality and residue-field detection. One must prove the requisite derived comparison/detection, rather than infer that tensoring an unbounded below-resolution Hom model preserves quasi-isomorphisms. This comparison is deliberately left inside the new unsplit detection node, not used as an uncited assertion of the item's assembly proof. It is a generic detection statement about maps without any diagonal trivialization hypotheses and is strictly smaller than the two-object uniqueness theorem.
