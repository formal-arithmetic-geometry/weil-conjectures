## What was wrong and what was corrected

## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported text says only “Let Lambda=E or Qlb”, leaving X and all size hypotheses unbound. Read as a statement for arbitrary schemes, clause 1 is false. Let X be the countable disjoint union of spectra of an algebraically closed field and take Lambda=underline E. The regular rank-one sheaf Lambda_X is constructible under the affine-local convention in `sk-def-sfb-constructible`. For each finite subset F of components, let M_F be its extension by zero on those components. These form a small filtered diagram with colimit Lambda_X. A map Lambda_X -> M_F is a tuple of E-elements supported in F. Thus colim_F Hom(Lambda_X,M_F) is the direct sum of countably many copies of E, whereas Hom(Lambda_X,Lambda_X) is their product. The identity (the tuple constantly 1) does not occur in that direct sum. These Hom computations may equivalently be made componentwise by evaluating the regular internal module on each point; its scalar sections there are E. The same failure prevents fullness of the proposed realization on this example.

The corrected statement explicitly assumes X Noetherian (locally Noetherian and quasi-compact), exactly as the existing `sk-lem-constructible-ext-continuity` and `det-lem-coefficient-heart-small` require. This is the setting required by the unit's cited Ext-continuity input; it does not change a Challenge target. The unit proof uses Ext continuity in this same setting, so it does not supply the asserted result for a non-quasi-compact X. Coefficients are now the condensed rings, including the project's finite-subextension Qlb construction, pulled back to internal module objects; no discrete coefficient category or completed derived category is substituted.

The original proof silently identified derived degree-zero Hom with ordinary Hom and took actual colimits without recording existence or size. Step 1 supplies colimits in the actual internal-module category using the internal/sectionwise equivalence, and step 2 cites the general abelian derived-single fully faithful node. The proof now gives the universal-property bijection and verifies that it is the claimed functor on morphisms. The imported `elementary: null` fields have been replaced by definite judgements and every step has a source.
