Step 2 takes precisely two assertions from `lem-per-regular-immersion-class-calculus`: composition of the specified Kummer maps under exceptional transitivity, and their localization comparison through canonical supported-cohomology identifications. This is used only for the constant-coefficient maps in that statement. The shifts add because of step 1's dimension equality; the map, rather than an unnamed isomorphism of its endpoints, is what is composed.

Step 3 is elementary cancellation of an invertible map in that diagram: a functor preserves an inverse by applying it to the two inverse identities. It proves both implications involving (a). Step 4 uses the two outside inverses to solve for the middle map, then removes the invertible Tate line and the shift. The statement of `lem-asd-etale-finite-twist-pullback` supplies a locally free rank-one line and the dual negative line; evaluation becomes the identity on a trivializing cover and hence is invertible. The bare positive-twist definition does not state this fact, so the first draft's definition citation was corrected to this theorem before delivery. I deliberately did not identify a supported restriction with an ordinary stalk here; that identification is only available after the support reduction.

Thus zero localized cones imply zero restriction on the punctured local scheme. No finite or closed support of an arbitrary cohomology sheaf is presupposed.
