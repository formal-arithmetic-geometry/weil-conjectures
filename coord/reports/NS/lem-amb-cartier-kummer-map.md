A proof plan for the retained relative node, to justify that its refinement is true: take an injective resolution G to I^bullet. Restriction to V preserves injectives, since its left adjoint j_! is exact. A relative degree-one cocycle is (c,b), with c in I^1(T), dc=0, b in I^0(V), and c restricted to V=db; coboundaries are (da,a restricted to V). Let P be the sheaf of local lifts x in I^0 of c under d. Exactness of the resolution makes it locally nonempty, and differences of lifts are in G, so it is a G-torsor; b gives its prescribed section. The two constructions are inverse, including on section-preserving isomorphisms. Forgetting b is the absolute construction. Pushout and inverse image preserve the displayed torsor/extension maps; maps of resolutions induce the same classes independently of choices.
