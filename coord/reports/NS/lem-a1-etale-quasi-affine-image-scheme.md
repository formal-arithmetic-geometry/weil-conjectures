## Review by NS

I read spaces-morphisms.tex, lemma-neighbourhood-scheme (03XW), from its diagram and four assumptions through the end of the proof. Its affine schemes T,T′, etale a, separated locally quasi-finite X/T and quasi-affine open V′ are exactly the item's hypotheses. No assumption on the base S or Noetherian hypothesis is needed. I kept every clause of the statement. I also read the cited statements, and citations.json contains their actual statement text, indexed by proof step.

For the new image tool I read spaces-properties.tex: lemma-topology-points (03BX), lemma-open-subspaces (03BZ), lemma-etale-open (03IR), lemma-points-cartesian (03H4), lemma-characterize-surjective (03H5), and lemma-quasi-compact-space (03E4). These give the topology, open-subspace correspondence, openness, and the surjectivity needed for arbitrary-base-change equality of image subsets. Compactness of the image is ordinary compactness of a continuous image; the final atlas characterization is exactly 03E4. This is a statement-only node, not a proof hidden in its statement. Empty E yields the empty open, the identity yields all of F, and a non-surjective open inclusion yields that same open. Its quasi-compactness assertion is about the space, not quasi-compactness of its inclusion into F.

For the new permanence tool I read spaces-morphisms.tex: lemma-base-change-separated (03KL), lemma-base-change-quasi-finite (03XL), lemma-composition-separated (03KQ), lemma-composition-quasi-finite (03XK), and the chartwise definition of local quasi-finiteness (03XJ). These apply to arbitrary scheme base changes. Composing the pullback structure map with the open inclusion preserves both properties. When the resulting open is a scheme, the identity chart gives the scheme local-quasi-finiteness condition, and the scheme diagonal is the same diagonal. Tests include an empty open, the whole pullback, the identity base change and nonreduced base changes. No finiteness condition was added to the base.

Step 1 uses the two existing space fibre-product/etale-permanence statements exactly for their etale conclusions; the scheme etale-composition node explicitly includes open immersions. Affine-morphism-permanence says affine over affine implies affine, and affine-scheme-qcqs supplies compactness. The new image tool then gives the actual open W and its compactness, leaving its schemehood unproved until the induction.

Step 2 uses etale-composition-cancellation and etale-space-locally-quasi-finite to check the hypothesis of lqf-qc-source-bounded-fibres. The latter statement requires quasi-compact SOURCE, which is affine here, not a quasi-compact morphism. fibre-degree-bound defines precisely the integer used for strong induction. The claim quantifies over every diagram, permitting the change of target in step 6. Bound zero is handled by empty fibres, and does not get lost in the source's n=1 wording.

Step 3 uses affine fibre-product spectra for T″, affine-morphism-permanence for the closed diagonal, and the etale/unramified definition nodes for its open diagonal. These definition nodes really state these diagonal conditions. The idempotent and principal-localization statements identify the complementary open as affine. Etale base change and open-immersion composition give the restricted etale map. None of these conclusions was taken from a node that merely defines an unrelated property.

Step 4 uses fibre-degree-base-change to keep the same n and both directions of fibre-degree-bound-field-points to pass to arbitrary field fibres and back. The rest is the elementary direct-product identity for finite-dimensional vector spaces. The diagonal contributes one copy of K. This avoids an unrecorded geometric-point counting theorem and works over nonsplit residue fields.

Step 5 treats n=1 separately: vanishing complementary fibres force the diagonal to be an isomorphism, hence the map is mono by the fibre-product universal property. The flat-lfp-monomorphism-open statement supplies exactly the needed open immersion from the flat and lfp clauses of the etale definition. I corrected the first draft to cite open-immersion-base-change for the scheme tests of r; stability was not silently inferred from a definition.

Step 6 checks each hypothesis of the induction. In Z=T*×_{T′,pi_0}V′ the quasi-affine map is base-changed along pi_0 by quasi-affine-base-change, whereas the target X′ comes from pi_1. The new permanence tool supplies separation and chartwise local quasi-finiteness of X′/T′. Both T* and T′ are affine, b is etale, and its bound is strictly smaller. Thus the induction gives H as a quasi-compact open SCHEME, not just an algebraic space.

Step 7 uses the image tool's arbitrary-base-change identity to identify the saturation P, and its open-subspace correspondence to identify P with V′ union H. The algebraic-space definition supplies the fppf (hence Zariski) sheaf condition. schemes-criterion explicitly concludes representation by a scheme from open representable subfunctors; its covering condition was checked after every scheme test. Compactness of a finite union is elementary.

Step 8 applies the new permanence tool to the now-schematic P, so no scheme-only lemma is being applied to a space. qcqs-morphism-permanence requires a quasi-separated target; affine-scheme-qcqs supplies it for T′. The existing lqf-locally-finite-type statement supplies the finite-type hypothesis of the Nagata quasi-affine theorem, whose other three hypotheses were all checked. I also read the source quasi-finite-separated-quasi-affine in more-morphisms.tex. This is the fourth paragraph of 03XW, not a replacement proof of 03XX.

Step 9 uses the image tool on a itself, and affine-morphism-permanence on its factor p:T′→B, which is the base change to B. The open-image correspondence shows W really factors through B. Etale gives flatness and lfp; affine inverse images of affine opens of B and surjectivity check exactly the stated fpqc and fppf definitions. B was not assumed affine or separated over S.

Step 10 uses fppf-sheaf-representability-descent-data, whose statement explicitly constructs the datum and says representable iff effective. Its covering is fppf, as verified in step 9; fpqc is deliberately not treated as a site. The overlap comparison and triple-overlap cocycle retain the same W-section, so this is the canonical datum of W, not some unrelated descent datum. The sheaf property is the actual algebraic-space definition and is preserved on the slice over B.

Step 11 uses quasi-affine-fpqc-effective-descent with the fpqc cover from step 9 and the quasi-affine scheme P/T′ from step 8.
