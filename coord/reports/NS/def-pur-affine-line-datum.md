STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported source identifies `G_m(Y)` with `H^0(G_{m,Y},G_m)`. These are units on different schemes. Take `Y=Spec(overline(F_p))`: every element of `G_m(Y)=overline(F_p)^*` has finite order, whereas the coordinate `t` in `overline(F_p)[t,t^{-1}]^*` has infinite order. Thus even the groups are not isomorphic in this case. The corrected boundary starts at `Gamma(U,O_U)^*`, where `U=G_{m,Y}`. This keeps the source's intended input, the unit `t` on the punctured line.

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: a torsor is a sheaf of sets with a simply transitive action; it is not a sheaf of modules with a zero. The displayed expression `T_t tensor_{Z/m} A` is therefore not a module tensor product. It now means the explicitly constructed contracted product along `phi:u -> theta^{-1}(u tensor 1_A)`. This repairs the type of the source's intended coefficient extension. A twist isomorphism alone does not specify a map from roots of unity to the twist: the unit of the coefficient algebra is used explicitly.

The relative spectrum is parenthesized around the quotient algebra. The etale roots sheaf is defined on its own site, while `def-pur-roots-of-unity-sheaf` is cited only for its actual pro-etale notion.
