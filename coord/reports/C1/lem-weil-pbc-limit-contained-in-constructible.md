## Reviewer fix (R-lcc1110)

The index universe `w` was independent of the scheme universe `u`; with `w` above `u` the
statement is false. Example: a strictly increasing chain of `u`-small fields `K_α` indexed by
an ordinal of size beyond `u` (e.g. `K_α = ℚ(x_β : β < α)`), stages `Spec K_α`. Every stage is
nonempty, but a nonempty `u`-small scheme mapping compatibly to all stages would have a residue
field containing every `K_α`, which is not `u`-small; so the empty scheme is a limit cone in
`Scheme.{u}`, `E = ∅` satisfies `himage`, and no stage maps into `E`. Fixed to
`{I : Type u}` (the form of Mathlib's AffineTransitionLimit, `Scheme.NoetherianApproximation`
and the landed lem-weil-pbc-limit-affine-transition), with `[Nonempty I]` added for the shared
directed-set form of that sibling (already implied by the supplied index `i`). Nothing else
changed; the file recompiled in 1.6 s.
