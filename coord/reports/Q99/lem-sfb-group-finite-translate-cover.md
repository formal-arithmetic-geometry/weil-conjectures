The geometric incidence calculation is as follows. Put C=H minus V over an algebraically closed fibre. For a missed point x the conditions are g_j^{-1}x in C. They are parametrized by (x,c_1,...,c_n) in H times C^n with g_j=x c_j^{-1}; the inverse map sends a tuple in the incidence locus to (x,g_j^{-1}x). This calculation works without commutativity. Integral fibres give dim C at most d-1, so the incidence source has dimension at most d+n(d-1), and its constructible projection has fibre-closure dimension at most that. The new statement includes this projection theorem, product dimension for the tuple space, and its geometric-fibre identification; none is hidden in the item's assembly.
