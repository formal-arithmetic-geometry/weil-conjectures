### Original step 5 — lem-sf-bounded-one-sided-tensor-realization

For a nonzero summand of the degree-$d$ total term one must have $r\le j\le s$, $u\le h\le v$, and $j+h=d$. These imply $r+u\le d\le s+v$. Thus every summand, and hence the total term, is zero if $d<r+u$ or $d>s+v$; only finitely many summands occur in any degree. The formula $\mathcal H^d=\ker(d^d)/\operatorname{im}(d^{d-1})$ from [def-weil-pbc-derived-categories] then gives zero cohomology in those degrees. Consequently $Q=B\otimes_A^LlM$ belongs to $D^b(Z_e,A)$ and to $D^+(Z_e,A)$, with lower bound $r+u$ and upper bound $s+v$. This is a vanishing bound; it does not require nonzero cohomology at either endpoint.
