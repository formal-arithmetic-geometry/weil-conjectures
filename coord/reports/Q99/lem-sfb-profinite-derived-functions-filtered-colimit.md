### Former step 1 → lem-sfb-profinite-free-functions-section-transport

Put $\mathcal C=*_{\mathrm{pro\acute et}}$ with the finite jointly surjective covering families specified in the statement. Products and covering pullbacks are profinite sets. A map into a profinite set descends uniquely along a finite jointly surjective family: the union of the sources is compact, the target is Hausdorff, and the surjection is a quotient map. Thus representables are sheaves. Sheafification on this site is strong symmetric monoidal for the objectwise tensor [det-def-sfb-sheaf-tensor-structure], so the section-action equivalence $E$ from internal $R$-modules to module sheaves over the section ring [def-weil-pbc-internal-abelian-sheaf-module-equivalence] applies; it preserves underlying abelian sheaves, sections and maps. By [lem-pe-internal-section-tensor-comparison] $E$ is symmetric monoidal from the relative tensor to $\otimes_R$. In $\operatorname{Mod}(R)$ the right adjoint of $E(R[P])\otimes_R-$ is $\mathcal Hom_R(E(R[P]),-)$ [lem-pc-sheaf-modules-tensor-closed], so uniqueness of right adjoints gives $E\circ H_P\cong\mathcal Hom_R(E(R[P]),-)\circ E$, naturally in $P$. As $E$ preserves underlying sheaves of sets, $E(R[P])$ is the free module sheaf on $h_P$, the sheafification of $V\mapsto\bigoplus_{h_P(V)}R(V)$. An equivalence preserves injectives and injective resolutions, so $E$ identifies the derived functors, the colimits and the comparison of the statement with those of $\operatorname{Mod}(R)$. Write $F_i=UM_i$ and $F=U(\operatorname{colim}_i M_i)=\operatorname{colim}_iF_i$. The cited forgetful-functor theorem gives this equality and exactness of $U$. Exact filtered colimits of abelian sheaves then give exact filtered colimits of module sheaves: exactness is tested on the underlying abelian sheaves.

### Former step 2 → lem-sfb-finite-cover-derived-presheaf-continuity

For every $W\in\mathcal C$ and $q\geq0$, obtain $\operatorname{colim}_iH^q(W,F_i)\cong H^q(W,F)$ from the existing cohomology-of-colimit-sites theorem, as follows. Adjoin a strict initial object $o$ to $I$ and extend the coefficient diagram by $F_o=0$ and its unique maps. The resulting small category $I^+$ is filtered: any finite diagram with vertices in $I$ has a cocone there, and any added vertex $o$ has its unique arrow to that cocone; for a diagram consisting only of $o$, use any object of the nonempty $I$. Every cocone on the original diagram extends uniquely across the zero object, so its colimit is still $F$. On $(I^+)^{\mathrm{op}}$ take the constant system of sites $\mathcal C$ with identity transition functors. All coverings are finite; the colimit category and site of the cited construction are $\mathcal C$, because the index is nonempty and filtered. In the cohomology theorem choose the index $o$ and the object $W$. Its category of arrows $j\to o$ in $(I^+)^{\mathrm{op}}$ has exactly one such arrow for each $j$, so its filtered colimit is precisely the colimit over $I^+$. All site pullbacks are identities. The theorem therefore gives the displayed isomorphism with the actual coefficient coprojections, not just an abstract group isomorphism.

### Former step 3 → lem-sfb-finite-cover-derived-presheaf-continuity

Let $\iota$ be inclusion of abelian sheaves into presheaves, and for an abelian sheaf $G$ put $h^q(G)=H^q(R\iota(G[0]))$, an abelian presheaf. By the definition through injective resolutions, $h^q(G)(W)=H^q(W,G)$ for every $W$, naturally in $G$ [def-weil-pbc-sites-and-cohomology], since kernels and cokernels of presheaves are computed objectwise; $h^q(G)=0$ for $q<0$ because the resolutions are nonnegative. Apply this to the coprojections $F_i\to F$. Presheaf colimits are computed objectwise, so the induced map $\operatorname{colim}_ih^q(F_i)\to h^q(F)$ is, at each $W$, exactly the isomorphism $\operatorname{colim}_iH^q(W,F_i)\to H^q(W,F)$ of step 2. Hence it is an isomorphism of abelian presheaves for every $q$ [def-weil-abstract-derived-category]. This uses one fixed degree at a time and never takes a product over cohomological degrees.

### Former step 4 → lem-sfb-free-functions-colimit-model-comparison
