STATEMENT-DISPUTED (source intermediate assertion only): Milne's printed p.74 says that the augmentation ideal is a direct summand of A, and uses this to infer injectivity of I_A tensor_A B -> B. The counit splits A as a k-vector space, not generally as an A-module. For the commutative Hopf algebra A=k[t]/(t^2) in characteristic 2, with Delta(t)=t tensor 1+1 tensor t, epsilon(t)=0 and S(t)=-t, the augmentation ideal I=(t) is not an A-module summand. Indeed an A-linear retraction r:A->I would satisfy r(t)=t r(1)=0, whereas a retraction has r(t)=t, a contradiction. Thus this sentence cannot prove the tensor injection. The item and the source's asserted basis-lifting conclusion remain true. I isolated the latter as a genuine statement-only node, with the stronger nilpotence hypothesis, and did not use the faulty argument anywhere in the item's steps.
