## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED (source intermediate claim, not the item): Milne Proposition 16.62, printed p.347, claims that a torus action on a smooth connected algebraic group with no invariant tangent vectors is strictly definite in the sense of Definition 13.40. Take H=G_a^2 over any field with S=G_m and t.(x,y)=(tx,t^{-1}y). The action is by group automorphisms and its tangent character spaces have weights +1,-1, so its scheme-theoretic invariant tangent subspace is zero. A basis of X*(G_m)=Z is either +1 or -1; neither puts both weights in its nonnegative cone. Equivalently no cocharacter pairs positively with both. Thus the strict-definiteness clause and its use to apply 13.41 to all of H in 16.63 are false. The item's split-unipotence conclusion is true in this example and is retained.

The correction retains Milne's valid unipotence argument and Rosenlicht criterion. Choose a cocharacter avoiding all zero pairings, take BOTH attracting subgroups, apply positive-weight coordinates separately, and multiply their affine spaces to obtain a dominant map. No positive cone for the entire tangent representation is asserted. This is a correction below the unit level to a source lemma, not a replacement of the blueprint's six-functor route. The node's proof_origin is agent and its split_report explicitly records the correction.
