### Previous Step 1

Retagging changes only the structural maps to $\operatorname{Spec}k$. The underlying schemes, the morphism $f$, and the chosen compactification $X\to\overline X\to Y$ are unchanged. Thus the identified small pro-etale ringed sites give exactly the same strict tower categories, exact pullback functors, and termwise tensor operations. This is an identity of the abelian tower categories of [sk-def-sp-coefficient-towers], so it induces an identity on complexes and on their ordinary derived categories, retaining the quotient transitions. The Tate sheaves of [def-coefficient-twists-detailed] are unchanged: on an object $U$ their roots of unity are the same units satisfying $u^{\ell^r}=1$, with the same power transition maps. The compactification construction of $F_\bullet$ is likewise the same underlying functor. Identify the right adjoints by the stipulated counit-preserving isomorphism; [det-sp-tower-gluing](2) supplies its uniqueness and the Hom bijection. This argument uses the identity of the entire tower construction, not faithfulness of finite evaluations on derived morphisms.

### Previous Step 2

The reviewed auxiliary comparison [lem-pur-orientation-ground-field-retagging] gives, under exactly the same underlying-site and counit-preserving adjoint identifications, the equality of normalized tower orientations a_{f^{\langle\sigma\rangle},\bullet}=a_{f,\bullet}, including all quotient-coefficient transition maps. Hence the orientation factor in the definition of the purity map is unchanged.

### Previous Step 3

For $K_\bullet$ in the stated constructible finite-Tor category, [det-sp-tower-gamma](2) defines $t_K$ as the adjoint transpose of $C_K:F_\bullet(f^*_\bullet K_\bullet\otimes^L\omega_{f,\bullet})\to K_\bullet$, where $C_K$ is inverse projection formula followed by $1\otimes\varepsilon_\bullet$ at the coefficient unit. The projection transformation is the compactification construction of [def-exchange-projection]: the direct-image projection map is the transpose of the pullback-tensor comparison followed by the ordinary direct-image counit, and it is followed by the extension-by-zero tensor comparison. Retagging leaves each of these constructions unchanged on the identical underlying sites and compactification of Step 1; the same applies to their tower construction used in [det-sp-tower-gamma](2), before any evaluation. The projection transformation is natural in the $\omega$ input, so the counit-preserving identification $\rho:G_{\mathrm{retag}}\xrightarrow{\sim}G_f$ also identifies $C_K$ for the two choices of $\omega=G(1)$. Explicitly, the two maps $t_{f,K}\circ(1\otimes\rho_1)$ and $\rho_K\circ t_{\mathrm{retag},K}$ have the same transpose: apply $\varepsilon_{f,K}\circ F_\bullet(-)$, use $\varepsilon_f\circ F_\bullet(\rho)=\varepsilon_{\mathrm{retag}}$, and the just-identified projection composite. Injectivity of the Hom bijection of [det-sp-tower-gluing], or the adjunct-counit identity [lem-weil-pbc-adjunct-counit-identity], makes these two maps equal. Step 2 gives $\rho_1\circ a_{\mathrm{retag}}=a_f$. Composing with $1\otimes a$ and the common twist reassociation therefore gives $\rho_K\circ\gamma_{\mathrm{retag}}(K_\bullet)=\gamma_f(K_\bullet)$, exactly the asserted equality under the stipulated identifications. The domain is the full constructible finite-Tor tower category in [det-sp-tower-gamma](2), so no normalization assumption is used.
