## Issues found and resolved

The item is correct. No statement dispute or statement change was necessary. The existing lem-sfb-finite-inertia-etale-affine-charts is superficially more general (algebraic stacks), but assumes a field base, whereas the item has arbitrary scheme base. It cannot be applied to this item. The new split follows the actual proof of Tag 0DUE rather than invoking that near-duplicate.

Care is needed with separatedness: the quotient-geometry node asserts absolute separatedness over Spec Z, and the cancellation node uses the same absolute convention. This is precisely the last paragraph of 0DUE and does not require S to be separated. An absolutely affine scheme is indeed separated over S; the point is that the proof uses the absolute property of the quotient stack rather than assuming separatedness of its base.

The source shrinks the initial finite-flat presentation to the stabilizer-preserving locus, which need not itself have affine source. The proof obtains a principal affine open containing its finite orbit by prime avoidance, then applies the EXISTING invariant-affine lemma to the groupoid RESTRICTED to that open locus. Applying that lemma to the original U would not force its answer into the desired locus. This domain check is explicit in steps 2--3.

No unfinished work remains in this one-level split. The four new statement nodes are deliberately left unsplit for later workers.
