## Argument allocation (verbatim returned proof)

### Returned step 1

Allocation: Parent assembly step 1 (unchanged).

Use the small representatives of the sites in [def-weil-size-convention], in a universe also containing the covering index set and the section sets and products used in descent. Put $\mathcal C=T_{\mathrm{Et}}$ and take its terminal object $T\to T$. The given family covers that object, and fibre products exist in $\mathcal C$, by [def-weil-pbc-etale-site]. By [lem-weil-pbc-etale-site-localization-open], $\mathcal C/T_a$ is the small etale site of $T_a$, and likewise on all overlaps. Thus the given $M_a$ and their transitions are sheaves and maps on the slice sites required for descent. Under these equivalences the structure sheaves agree: on an object $W\to T_a$ both assign $\Gamma(W,\mathcal O_W)$, with the same restrictions. Relocalization in [def-pur-localized-ringed-site] gives the same identifications on repeated slices; the slice over the terminal object is $\mathcal C$ itself.

### Returned step 2

Allocation: Parent assembly step 2 (unchanged), using existing set-sheaf descent.

Forget the monoid operations for the moment. Apply the existence clause of [lem-pe-set-sheaf-cover-descent] to the underlying set sheaves of $M_a$ and their given transition isomorphisms $\phi_{ab}$. The assumed cocycle is exactly its cocycle, including repeated indices. Obtain a sheaf of sets $M$ and isomorphisms $e_a:M|_{T_a}\xrightarrow{\sim}M_a$ with $e_b=\phi_{ab}e_a$ on every double overlap. These identifications induce the specified descent datum, rather than only isomorphic unlabelled local objects.

### Returned step 3

Allocation: lem-trf-monoid-sheaf-structure-gluing: multiplication, identity, naturality, laws, alpha and its monoid equations.

For $W\to T$ etale put $W_a=W\times_TT_a$. For $s,t\in M(W)$, transport their restrictions by $e_a$, multiply in $M_a(W_a)$, and transport back. The resulting sections of $M(W_a)$ agree on double overlaps because $\phi_{ab}$ is a monoid homomorphism. The equalizer condition of [def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaf-of-sets] gives a unique product in $M(W)$; glue the local identity sections in the same way. These operations commute with restriction, since each proposed restriction is the unique section with the indicated local values. Associativity, commutativity and both identity laws hold after restriction to every $W_a$, hence hold in $M(W)$ by the injective part of the same equalizer condition. Thus $M$ is a sheaf of commutative monoids and each $e_a$ is a monoid isomorphism. The local maps $\alpha_a e_a:M|_{T_a}\to\mathcal O_T|_{T_a}$ agree on overlaps by the assumed compatibility of structure maps. The morphism clause of [lem-pe-set-sheaf-cover-descent] glues them to $\alpha:M\to\mathcal O_T$. Its multiplication and identity equations hold locally, so the same separatedness argument proves that $\alpha$ is a monoid homomorphism.

### Returned step 4

Allocation: lem-trf-log-condition-fine-chart-locality: unique preimages of units, their gluing, and the log condition.

Check the log condition of [def-trf-fs-log-blowup] directly. For an etale object $W$ and $v\in\mathcal O_T(W)^\times$, its restriction to $W_a$ has a unique preimage $m_a\in M(W_a)$ under $\alpha$, since $(M_a,\alpha_a)$ is a log structure and $e_a$ is a monoid isomorphism. On every double overlap the two preimages agree by that same local uniqueness. The sheaf condition [def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaf-of-sets] glues them to $m\in M(W)$, and separatedness gives $\alpha(m)=v$. If two such preimages existed, their restrictions would be equal on all $W_a$, so they would be equal. Consequently the natural map $\alpha^{-1}(\mathcal O_T^\times)\to\mathcal O_T^\times$ is bijective on every object and therefore is an isomorphism of monoid sheaves. This makes $(M,\alpha)$ a log structure.

### Returned step 5

Allocation: lem-trf-log-condition-fine-chart-locality: composite etale covering, chart transport, finite generation, integrality and conditional saturation.

For each $a$ choose an etale covering $\{V_{a\lambda}\to T_a\}$ on which $M_a$ has its given fine charts. Composite coverings cover in the site [def-weil-pbc-etale-site], so the $V_{a\lambda}\to T$ form an etale covering. On each member transport its chart $P_{a\lambda}\to M_a|_{V_{a\lambda}}$ through $e_a^{-1}$. This is a chart for $M$: its composite to the structure sheaf is unchanged and its chart map is followed by an isomorphism of monoid sheaves. In particular the stalkwise pushout map in [def-trf-fs-log-blowup] is the old chart isomorphism followed by the stalk of $e_a^{-1}$. Each $P_{a\lambda}$ is still finitely generated and integral. Thus $M$ is fine. If the local charts were fine saturated, exactly the same charts remain saturated, so $M$ is fs. No single global chart, or Zariski-local chart, is asserted.

### Returned step 6

Allocation: lem-trf-monoid-sheaf-morphism-gluing: gluing underlying maps, multiplicativity, identity, equation over R and uniqueness, without a log axiom.

For the moreover clause apply the morphism clause of [lem-pe-set-sheaf-cover-descent] to the underlying set sheaves of $A,B$ and the maps $u_a$. It gives a unique set-sheaf map $u:A\to B$. For $s,t\in A(W)$ the sections $u(st)$ and $u(s)u(t)$ restrict to equal sections on $W_a$, by the homomorphism property of $u_a$; hence they are equal by [def-weil-pbc-etale-sheaf-of-sets]. The same argument gives $u(1)=1$ and $\beta u=\alpha$. Thus $u$ is the required monoid-sheaf map over the structure sheaf, and uniqueness already follows from uniqueness as a set-sheaf map. This argument assumes only the stated sheaf and monoid axioms of $A,B$, so it also applies when the source is an inverse-image monoid sheaf with the specified composite structure map; no log axiom for the source is used.

### Returned step 7

Allocation: lem-trf-monoid-sheaf-morphism-gluing: inverse gluing and composites equal to identities; parent step 6 supplies the particular comparison maps.

Finally let $(N,\gamma)$ be another fine log structure with identifications $f_a:N|_{T_a}\xrightarrow{\sim}M_a$ inducing this datum. The monoid-sheaf maps $f_a^{-1}e_a:M|_{T_a}\to N|_{T_a}$ agree on double overlaps and commute with the structure maps. By the moreover argument just proved (using the morphism clause of [lem-pe-set-sheaf-cover-descent]) they glue uniquely to a monoid-sheaf map $h:M\to N$ over $\mathcal O_T$. Glue the inverses $e_a^{-1}f_a$ in the same way. Both composites restrict to identity maps; uniqueness in that same morphism clause makes both composites identities. Hence $h$ is an isomorphism, and every isomorphism compatible with the identifications has precisely these local maps, so equals $h$. This proves uniqueness in the asserted, identification-preserving sense.
