The following is the mathematical justification to guide its later split; the node itself is intentionally statement-only. Restriction Res from k_E-module sheaves to A-module sheaves is exact, with right adjoint C = RHom_A(k_E,-) in the k_E-module category. Restriction commutes with compact support: choose a compactification and use the coefficientwise proper direct image and extension by zero. Uniqueness of composite right adjoints therefore gives C_Z a_{Z,A}^times = a_{Z,k_E}^times C_point. On the separably closed point these are module categories. Since A is self-injective, C_point(A) is Hom_A(k_E,A) in degree zero; its module is ann(varpi) = varpi^{n-1} A, identified with k_E by multiplication by varpi^{n-1}. Thus C_Z(omega_A) = omega_kE. The derived restriction–coinduction Hom adjunction, on each slice of the site and natural under restriction, now gives RHom_A(Res M,omega_A) = Res RHom_kE(M,omega_kE). This argument applies to all complexes via K-injective coextension and has no finite-Tor or constructibility hypothesis. It is a coefficient-change identification to be formalized, not the field-range or constructibility clause of this item.
