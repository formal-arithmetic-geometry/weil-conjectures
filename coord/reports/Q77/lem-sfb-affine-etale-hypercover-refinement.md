## Statement correction

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: arbitrary degreewise affine covers cannot constrain every summand of a single component-compatible simplicial refinement. This is an error in the imported node, not in Stacks 0DAV.

Take T = Spec C[t], and H the constant simplicial T-scheme, an etale hypercover with identity matching maps. Choose infinitely many pairwise disjoint pairs of distinct closed points {a_n,b_n}, n >= 1. In degree n prescribe the affine open cover D(t-a_n), D(t-b_n); in degree zero prescribe {T}. Suppose a refinement as originally stated existed. V_0 -> T is surjective etale, so some nonempty summand V_{0,i} has nonempty open image O in T (Stacks 03WT, morphisms.tex lemma-etale-open). Every nonempty open in Spec C[t] contains all but finitely many closed points: its complement is a proper closed set, hence is cut out by a nonzero polynomial and finite. Iterate the degeneracy [n] -> [0]. Component compatibility puts its restriction to V_{0,i} in a single summand of V_n. Naturality for the map to constant H says its composite with H_n = T is the original map V_{0,i} -> T. The original prescribed-cover clause therefore forces O to be contained in D(t-a_n) or D(t-b_n) for every n. O omits at least one point from each disjoint pair, hence infinitely many closed points, a contradiction. This does not assume a split refinement.

The corrected statement retains existence, affineness, component-compatible operators, all matching coverings, refinement of any H, arbitrary algebraic-space base change, and absence of qcqs assumptions. It replaces only the impossible last clause: at each attachment choose each new nondegenerate component inside the inverse image of one prescribed affine open of H_n. Degenerate copies retain the forced maps H(sigma) f_r. This is exactly the freedom available in Stacks 0DAV. An alternative sufficient assumption for the old all-summand version would be simplicially stable prescribed covering sieves; that additional hypothesis is not silently imposed here.
