## Audit findings and corrections

The imported statement is consistent with its source and was not changed. The original node had no proof steps and already warned that no colimit of q or q restricted to K' is assumed; replacing compactness by compactness of such an apex would therefore be invalid. I instead use HTT's two coslice left fibrations. I explicitly separate two technical facts that should not be hidden in the word pullback: restriction is itself a left fibration, and the strict pullback along the prescribed section represents the coherent homotopy fiber. Merely citing the definition of a left fibration or citing the existence of straightening without this comparison would not supply those facts.

HTT's incoming-edge inference after the total-space weak equivalence is expanded. A weak equivalence first supplies a component representative; it does not itself hand us the required directed edge. The proof extracts an edge zigzag using the quotient definition of realization and compresses it backwards with Lambda^2_1 and Lambda^2_0 lifts over the terminal poset vertex. This retains the original endpoint u exactly.

The current main-20261015 ancestor proof was read at literature/blueprint/main-20261015/blueprint/sec-six-functors/sk-thm-sf-package/prop-coef-integral-exceptional-adjoint.proof.md, paragraph 2. It is not a unit key node, so no unit realization fields were changed. The existing spaces-limit/filtered-colimit node was reused rather than recreated. The reused lem-cbc-relative-coslice-left-fibration is a generic tool from another unit, proposed in split_report.tool_uses; its acceptance in the maintainer-owned top-level tool_uses remains for the maintainer.
