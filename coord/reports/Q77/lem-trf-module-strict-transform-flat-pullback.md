### Original step 1

Put $B=S^b$, $E=b^{-1}V(\mathcal I)\subseteq B$, $X_B=X\times_SB$ with projections $p_X,q_X$, and $Y_B=Y\times_TT^b$ with projections $p_Y:Y_B\to Y$, $q_Y:Y_B\to T^b$; let $E_T=b_T^{-1}V(\mathcal I\mathcal O_T)\subseteq T^b$. Since $e$ is flat, the specified comparison $\operatorname{bc}_e:T^b\to T\times_SB$ [def-nagata-blowup-flat-base-change-map] is an isomorphism over $T$ by [thm-nagata-blowup-flat-base-change]; let $\beta:T^b\to B$ be its composite with the second projection, so $b\beta=e\,b_T$. Then $j^b=(j\circ p_Y,\beta\circ q_Y):Y_B\to X_B$ is well defined because $f\,j\,p_Y=e\,g\,p_Y=e\,b_T\,q_Y=b\,\beta\,q_Y$, and $p_Xj^b=jp_Y$. By the universal property and associativity of fibre products [def-pc-fibre-product], $Y_B\cong Y\times_T(T\times_SB)\cong Y\times_SB\cong Y\times_X X_B$, and under these identifications $j^b$ is the projection $Y\times_XX_B\to X_B$, i.e. the base change of $j$ along $p_X:X_B\to X$, with $p_Y$ the other projection. Exceptional loci: by [def-nagata-extended-ideal] the stalk of $\mathcal I\mathcal O_T$ at $t$ is $\mathcal I_{e(t)}\mathcal O_{T,t}$, which is proper exactly when $\mathcal I_{e(t)}\subseteq\mathfrak m_{e(t)}$ (the map of local rings is local), so $V(\mathcal I\mathcal O_T)=e^{-1}V(\mathcal I)$ as sets. Hence $D:=q_X^{-1}(E)$ is the set of points of $X_B$ lying over $V(\mathcal I)$, $D_Y:=q_Y^{-1}(E_T)$ the set of points of $Y_B$ lying over $V(\mathcal I)$ (via $e\,g\,p_Y=f\,j\,p_Y$), and therefore $D_Y=(j^b)^{-1}(D)$ and $Y_B\setminus D_Y=(j^b)^{-1}(X_B\setminus D)$.

### Original step 2

Put $H=p_X^*\mathcal F$ and let $K\subseteq H$ be the subsheaf of local sections whose restriction to $X_B\setminus D$ is zero; by [def-trf-module-strict-transform], $\mathcal F^b=H/K$ with its quotient map $\pi$. By [lem-trf-strict-transform-sheaf-kernel], $H$ and $K$ are quasi-coherent and $K=\ker\pi$. Likewise put $H_Y=p_Y^*(j^*\mathcal F)$ and let $K_Y\subseteq H_Y$ be the subsheaf of sections vanishing on $Y_B\setminus D_Y$, so $(j^*\mathcal F)^{T^b}=H_Y/K_Y$ with quotient map $\pi_Y$, by the same definition applied to $Y\to T$, the ideal $\mathcal I\mathcal O_T$ and $T^b$. From $p_Xj^b=jp_Y$ (Step 1) and the composition isomorphisms $(h\circ g)^*\cong g^*h^*$ of [def-pc-module-pullback] we get the canonical isomorphism $\gamma:(j^b)^*H=(j^b)^*p_X^*\mathcal F\cong(p_Xj^b)^*\mathcal F=(jp_Y)^*\mathcal F\cong p_Y^*j^*\mathcal F=H_Y$ of $\mathcal O_{Y_B}$-modules.

Cited nodes: def-trf-module-strict-transform, lem-trf-strict-transform-sheaf-kernel, def-pc-module-pullback.

### Original step 3

Claim: $(j^b)^*(K\hookrightarrow H)$ followed by $\gamma$ is injective with image $K_Y$. (a) $j^b$ is flat: it is the base change of the flat $j$ along $p_X$ (Step 1), so [lem-weil-pbc-flat-morphism-base-change] applies; hence for affine opens $W\subseteq Y_B$, $V\subseteq X_B$ with $j^b(W)\subseteq V$, $B'=\mathcal O(W)$ is a flat $A'=\mathcal O(V)$-module by [lem-weil-pbc-flat-morphism-affine-criterion]. (b) Charts. By [thm-nagata-blowup-principalizes-center] each point of $B$ has an affine neighbourhood $N$ on which $\mathcal I\mathcal O_B$ is generated by one section $c$, so $E\cap N=V(c)$. For $y\in Y_B$ choose such $N\ni q_X(j^b(y))$, an affine open $V\ni j^b(y)$ of $X_B$ inside $q_X^{-1}(N)$, and an affine open $W\ni y$ of $Y_B$ inside $(j^b)^{-1}(V)$ [lem-weil-pbc-affine-opens-basis]. With $a\in A'$ the image of $c$ we get $D\cap V=V(a)$, $V\setminus D=D(a)$, and by Step 1 $W\setminus D_Y=D(a_W)$ with $a_W$ the image of $a$ in $B'$. Such $W$ (inside any given neighbourhood of $y$) form a basis of the topology of $Y_B$. (c) Torsion description. For a quasi-coherent module $G$ on an affine $U$ and $u\in\mathcal O(U)$, a section $m\in G(U)$ vanishes on $D(u)$ iff $u^nm=0$ for some $n$: one direction is [lem-weil-pbc-affine-module-basic-open-localization](2), the other holds because $u$ is a unit on $D(u)$. Writing $\Gamma_u(N)=\ker(N\to N_u)$ for the $u$-power torsion of a module $N$, this gives $K(V)=\Gamma_a(M)$ with $M=H(V)$, and $K_Y(W)=\Gamma_{a_W}(H_Y(W))$. (d) Sections of the pullbacks. As $H$ and $K$ are quasi-coherent (Step 2), [lem-weil-pbc-module-pullback-affine-sections-bijective] gives bijections $M\otimes_{A'}B'\cong((j^b)^*H)(W)$ and $K(V)\otimes_{A'}B'\cong((j^b)^*K)(W)$, both $s\otimes t\mapsto t\,\eta(s)|_W$; by naturality of the unit $\eta$ they carry $\iota\otimes1$ to $(j^b)^*\iota$ on $W$, $\iota:K(V)\hookrightarrow M$. Composing with $\gamma$, identify $H_Y(W)$ with $M\otimes_{A'}B'$; the action of $a_W$ is that of $a\otimes1$. (e) Flatness. The sequence $0\to\Gamma_a(M)\to M\to M_a$ is exact, so by (a) and [lem-weil-pbc-flat-tensor-exact] so is $0\to\Gamma_a(M)\otimes_{A'}B'\to M\otimes_{A'}B'\to M_a\otimes_{A'}B'$. Since $M_a\otimes_{A'}B'\cong A'_a\otimes_{A'}M\otimes_{A'}B'\cong(M\otimes_{A'}B')_{a\otimes1}$ compatibly with the maps from $M\otimes_{A'}B'$ (elementary), the kernel of the last map is $\Gamma_{a_W}(M\otimes_{A'}B')=K_Y(W)$ by (c). Thus on every such $W$ the map $((j^b)^*K)(W)\to H_Y(W)$ is injective with image $K_Y(W)$. (f) Since these $W$ form a basis, the kernel of the sheaf map $\gamma\circ(j^b)^*\iota$ has zero sections on a basis, hence is zero, and its image subsheaf and $K_Y$ are two subsheaves of $H_Y$ with the same sections on a basis, hence equal (sheaf axiom). This proves the claim.

### Original step 4

Pullback $(j^b)^*$ is a left adjoint [def-pc-module-pullback], so it preserves cokernels: applying it to the cokernel sequence $K\to H\xrightarrow{\pi}\mathcal F^b\to0$ of Step 2 gives that $(j^b)^*\pi$ is a cokernel of $(j^b)^*\iota$. By Step 3, $\gamma$ carries the image of $(j^b)^*\iota$ onto $K_Y=\ker\pi_Y$; hence by the universal property of cokernels there is a unique morphism $\theta:(j^b)^*(\mathcal F^b)\to H_Y/K_Y=(j^*\mathcal F)^{T^b}$ with $\theta\circ(j^b)^*\pi=\pi_Y\circ\gamma$, and it is an isomorphism because both sides are cokernels of the same subobject transported by the isomorphism $\gamma$. The identity $\theta\circ(j^b)^*\pi=\pi_Y\circ\gamma$ is exactly the compatibility with the quotient maps from the ordinary pullbacks $(j^b)^*p_X^*\mathcal F\cong p_Y^*j^*\mathcal F$, and since $(j^b)^*\pi$ is an epimorphism it determines $\theta$ uniquely, which makes $\theta$ canonical.
