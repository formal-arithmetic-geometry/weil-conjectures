### Original step 1

Write $\varpi=\varpi_E$ and $B_k=(\mathcal O_E/\varpi^k)_T$. By [lem-weil-l-adic-extension-integers-dvr], $\ell=u\varpi^e$ for a unit $u$ and an integer $e\geq1$: $\ell$ is nonzero and has absolute value strictly less than $1$. Thus $(\ell^n)=(\varpi^{en})$, and the coefficient sheaves in the item are canonically $B_{en}$, with the same quotient maps. The completed sheaf is unchanged: a compatible family on the indices $en$ extends uniquely to index $k$ by projection from any $en\geq k$. Use $\mathscr S(B)$ for the strict varying-coefficient systems of [lem-pur-system-level-left-extension], and put $r(n)=en$, $U=U_r$. The category in the item is $\mathscr S(B_r)$, by the explicit system definition [sk-def-sp-coefficient-towers]. Use $\mathcal R_\varpi\dashv\mathcal L_\varpi$ for the actual unbounded derived adjunction of [lem-coef-derived-system-evaluation-calculus]. It has $\mathcal L_\varpi=R\lim\circ\operatorname{Res}$, not a categorical inverse limit in a triangulated category. Its reduction is computed from one flat-term K-flat representative $Q$ by $(Q\otimes B_k)_k$, so $U\mathcal R_\varpi=r_T$ with their actual quotient transitions.

### Original step 2

Here is the required derived cofinality argument, on towers over the single fixed ring sheaf $R_T$. Let $V(X)_n=X_{en}$. Its left adjoint is the exact functor $K(Y)_k=Y_{\lceil k/e\rceil}$, with the evident transition maps: a map $K(Y)\to X$ is determined by the maps $Y_n\to X_{en}$, and its other components are obtained by composing with $X_{en}\to X_k$. Exactness is levelwise. Hence $V$ preserves K-injective complexes: for acyclic $A$, a map $A\to VI$ transposes to $KA\to I$, which is null-homotopic since $KA$ is acyclic; the transpose of a null-homotopy is a null-homotopy [def-pur-kinjective-complex]. The functor $n\mapsto en$ on the opposite positive integers is initial: the indices with $en\geq k$ are a nonempty tail, and any two are joined through their maximum. By [lem-weil-pbc-initial-functor-limit], $\lim X\to\lim VX$ is an isomorphism respecting all selected projections. Apply this identity to a K-injective tower resolution. Since its restriction is still K-injective, the total-limit computation in [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-strict-tower-homotopy-limit] gives a natural isomorphism $R\lim X\xrightarrow{\sim}R\lim VX$ respecting the selected projections. This argument does not assume exact countable products.

Cited nodes: def-pur-kinjective-complex, lem-weil-pbc-initial-functor-limit, lem-weil-pbc-ringed-strict-tower-homotopy-limit

### Original step 3

The exact restriction $U:\mathscr S(B)\to\mathscr S(B_r)$ also has an exact right adjoint $J$, given explicitly by $(JC)_k=0$ for $k<e$, and $(JC)_k=\operatorname{Res}_{B_k\to B_{e\lfloor k/e\rfloor}}C_{\lfloor k/e\rfloor}$ for $k\geq e$. Its transitions are restrictions of the transitions of $C$. A system map $D\to JC$ is determined by its components $D_{en}\to C_n$: its $k$th component is $D_k\to D_{e\lfloor k/e\rfloor}\to C_{\lfloor k/e\rfloor}$, and it is zero for $k<e$. This constructs the inverse Hom bijections, including their naturality. Restriction of scalars is exact because it does not change the underlying abelian sheaf; exactness of varying systems is componentwise by [lem-pur-system-level-left-extension]. Consequently $U,J$ preserve quasi-isomorphisms. Applying their termwise unit and counit to complexes and then localizing, using [lem-sf-exact-functor-derived-triangulated], gives $U\dashv J$ on the ordinary unbounded derived categories. The identities descend from the identities on complexes. We have $UJC=C$ strictly. After forgetting to $R_T$, step 2 therefore identifies $\mathcal L_\varpi JC$ with $L_TC$, respecting the level-$n$ projections.

### Original step 4

Combine the two actual adjunctions, rather than taking a limit of degree-zero Hom sets:
$$\operatorname{Hom}(r_TM,C)=\operatorname{Hom}(U\mathcal R_\varpi M,C)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(\mathcal R_\varpi M,JC)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(M,\mathcal L_\varpi JC)\cong\operatorname{Hom}(M,L_TC).$$
The first bijection is step 3, the second is [lem-coef-derived-system-evaluation-calculus], and the last is step 2. They are natural in both variables, so this proves the ambient adjunction. Its unit is the map induced by the strict quotient maps $Q\to Q\otimes B_{en}$: the unit for $U\dashv J$ has identity components at $en$, and the uniformizer adjunction's unit has exactly the quotient components. Thus the resulting coherent map is the canonical derived-completion map. Its counit at level $n$ is the scalar-extension adjoint of $L_TC\to\operatorname{Res}C_n$, by the counit formula in that node and the projection compatibility in step 2. This specifies the natural maps, including their coherence; agreement of unrelated evaluated objects is not being used to choose a system map.

### Original step 5

To treat an arbitrary normalized tower $C\in D(\mathscr S(B_r))$, use the existing derived left extension $F=L_rC$ of [lem-pur-system-level-left-extension]. Its formula is
$$F_k=B_k\otimes^L_{B_{e\lceil k/e\rceil}}C_{\lceil k/e\rceil}.$$
In particular $F_{en}=C_n$ and the selected transitions are those of $C$. For adjacent $k+1\to k$, put $a=\lceil k/e\rceil$ and $b=\lceil(k+1)/e\rceil$. The cited node identifies the transition-adjoint with $B_k\otimes^L_{B_{eb}}C_b\to B_k\otimes^L_{B_{ea}}C_a$, induced by $B_{ea}\otimes^L_{B_{eb}}C_b\to C_a$. If $a=b$ this last map is the identity; if $a<b$ it is an isomorphism by normalization of $C$. Thus all adjacent transition-adjoints of $F$ are isomorphisms, and their composites give normalization for every pair of levels, using the same node's tensor-associator compatibility. After forgetting scalars, step 2 gives $\mathcal L_\varpi F\cong L_TC$, with the actual projections to $C_n$ identified. No boundedness or constructibility was used.

### Original step 7

For completeness, the unit clause holds even with the triangulated formulation of [def-weil-derived-complete], which does not itself choose the canonical unit. First $\mathcal R_\varpi M$ is normalized for arbitrary $M$: on its flat-term K-flat representative $Q$, [lem-pur-kflat-change-of-rings] makes $Q\otimes B_j$ K-flat over $B_j$, and $(Q\otimes B_j)\otimes_{B_j}B_i=Q\otimes B_i$ for $j\geq i$. The triangular identity $\mathcal L_\varpi(\epsilon_{\mathcal R_\varpi M})\circ\eta_{\mathcal L_\varpi\mathcal R_\varpi M}=1$ of [lem-coef-derived-system-evaluation-calculus] makes $\eta_{\mathcal L_\varpi\mathcal R_\varpi M}$ invertible.
