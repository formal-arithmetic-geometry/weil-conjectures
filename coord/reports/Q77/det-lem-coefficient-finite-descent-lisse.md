### Hypotheses and clauses

- `X : Scheme.{u}` is arbitrary. `[CompactSpace X]` is quasi-compactness;
  for a scheme this is equivalent to having a finite affine open cover.
  `[QuasiSeparatedSpace X]` is scheme quasi-separatedness, equivalent to the
  terminal morphism having quasi-compact diagonal. There is no noetherian,
  separated, finite-type, nonempty or characteristic restriction.
- `(ℓ : ℕ) [Fact ℓ.Prime]` fixes exactly a prime. `PadicAlgCl ℓ` is the fixed
  algebraic closure used by the coefficient dependency declarations.

  category is `Ab.{u + 1}`, retaining the framework's distinct site size convention.
- `Padic.FiniteCoefficientFields ℓ (PadicAlgCl ℓ)` is exactly the finite-dimensional
  intermediate-field subtype, ordered by inclusion. It is definitionally the
  `PadicAlgCl.FiniteIntermediateField ℓ` expected by the upstream inclusions.
- The first conjunct quantifies over EVERY
  `L : Mod (Sheaf (Scheme.ProEt.topology X) Ab.{u + 1}) (padicAlgClRingObj X ℓ)`.
  `IsLisseCoefficientModule (Profinite.padicAlgClRingObj ℓ) L` is precisely the
  supplied local finite-freeness condition. The existing definition quantifies a
  pro-etale cover and `Mod.IsFree` on each member, with independently chosen natural
  ranks. For a weakly etale member its inverse image is the slice restriction, with
  the canonical coefficient identification; thus this is the node's slice-site
  local-freeness, not a condition about a discrete constant sheaf.
- The existential `E`, `L_E` ranges over finite coefficient fields and their genuine
  internal module objects. Its conjunction asserts `L_E` lisse and
  `Nonempty ((Mod.extendScalars (padicSubfieldRingObjToPadicAlgCl X ℓ E)).obj L_E ≅ L)`.
  This is exactly the ordinary relative-tensor isomorphism in clause (1).
- The second conjunct quantifies every finite `E` and every pair `M,N` of its
  module objects, with the two lisse assumptions separately. Each finite `F` is
  accompanied by `h : E ≤ F`; `Mod.extendScalars (padicSubfieldRingObjMap X ℓ h)`
  constructs `M_F,N_F`. The closure versions use `padicSubfieldRingObjToPadicAlgCl`.
- Clause (2), surjectivity: EVERY closure-linear `ψ` has some `F ≥ E` and an
  `F_X`-linear `f` whose `finiteCoefficientHomToAlgCl` image is `ψ`.
- Clause (2), injectivity: representatives `f` over `F ≥ E` and `g` over `G ≥ E`
  with equal closure images become equal over a finite `H` containing BOTH `F,G`.
  `finiteCoefficientHomTransition` is ordinary scalar extension, with source and
  target identified by canonical tensor transitivity. Proof irrelevance identifies
  the two proofs of `E ≤ H`; no equality of the fields `F,G` is assumed.
- These last two assertions say exactly that the displayed set-colimit map is
  bijective. The node explicitly defines that colimit as representatives modulo
  the equivalence generated by extension to a larger field. In a directed poset,
  that equivalence is equality at a common upper field: a finite chain of generating
  relations can be moved to one common upper bound. Conversely eventual equality
  is a chain of two such relations. Canonical tensor transitivity makes evaluation
  invariant under transitions. Surjectivity on representatives is therefore
  surjectivity on the quotient, and the equal-image implication is exactly
  injectivity on that quotient. This encoding neither asserts injectivity of the
  disjoint union nor restricts representatives to one common initial field.
