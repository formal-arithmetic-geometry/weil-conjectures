For lem-pc-curve-local-scalar-principal-parts I read Stacks algebra.tex, Tags 00DT (algebra-lemma-chinese-remainder), 00JB (algebra-lemma-artinian-finite-length), and the Dedekind characterization 034X (algebra-lemma-characterize-Dedekind). The new statement is their geometric principal-parts consequence, not a claim that any of those tags alone states it. Here is the independent argument checked for this new unsplit node. On an affine neighbourhood Spec R of x, O = R_m. After extension, R tensor_k K is a smooth curve algebra and its local rings at the points above x are regular local rings of dimension one. Smooth geometrically connected curves are geometrically integral, so all rings embed in L. A = O tensor_k K is a localization of R tensor_k K, hence a Noetherian domain, but is generally NOT semilocal. Localize A at the complement of the union of the finitely many primes over t. The resulting ring B_0 is semilocal, with localizations precisely O_y. Its only primes are the generic prime and these height-one maximal primes; it is a regular semilocal dimension-one domain and equals their intersection B in L. The element t has positive valuation at every y, but need not be a uniformizer there. Thus B[t^{-1}] = L. The quotient A/t^nA is (O/t^nO) tensor_k K and is finite-dimensional, and hence Artinian. Its primes are exactly the points y. The Artinian product decomposition identifies it with the product of O_y/t^nO_y, with all nilpotents retained. Further localization at the complement above changes none of these Artinian local factors, giving A/t^nA = B/t^nB. The union of the t^{-n} lattices now gives V/A = L/B: injectivity gives V intersect B = A, and surjectivity is the ability to match any principal part modulo B. The same Artinian product decomposition, followed by these unions, gives L/B = direct sum_y L/O_y. Any finite tuple of fractions in the DVRs has a common t-power denominator, so this last map is onto. All comparison maps are the canonical inclusion or diagonal maps. This supplies exactly the two comparisons the proof uses; it contains no residue assertion.

I checked the six steps against their cited statements, with the node files open:

- Step 1 takes integrality, DVRs, finite closed fibre, common ambient field, all truncated-quotient and principal-parts comparisons from the geometry node. These are explicit clauses of that node. Geometric connectedness plus smoothness is retained; no perfectness of k is assumed.
- Step 2 uses the finite quotient, projection independence and trace formula from the abstract node, and the local commutator formula and admissibility from the DVR-calculus node. Tensoring split vector-space sequences preserves the kernels/intersections and quotients defining D/E. A basis of D/E proves the trace identity by the sum of diagonal entries. This is the elementary matrix argument, not an unproved assertion of residue compatibility. Finite sums in R clear a common power of t, giving admissibility even though R is not a field.
- Step 3 uses the geometry node's V intersect B = A and V/A onto L/B, and its finite-dimensional B/t^nB. These prove respectively the inverse-image lattice, surjectivity of B onto L/V, and admissibility of B. The exact-sequence node then gives the residue identity; the abstract formula with the identity projection proves the full-lattice residue zero. The quotient L/V is an R-module, not an L-module, and I explicitly use R = V throughout.
- Step 4 uses the geometry node's second principal-parts comparison to prove P onto W/j(L). The diagonal inclusion is injective because S is nonempty. It has inverse-image lattice B. Each local quotient by t^n is finite-dimensional, hence P is admissible. The same exact-sequence node applies over R = V. The full-lattice residue is again zero by the abstract formula. No weak approximation theorem is being silently assumed: its required precise comparison is the geometry node's stated isomorphism.
- Step 5 takes projection independence and the finite-potent commutator formula from the abstract and DVR nodes. A finite product of stable finite-dimensional trace subspaces has a concatenated basis, so its matrix trace is the sum of the blocks. This proves the finite-product assertion directly, without invoking unlicensed additivity of finite-potent traces. On f,g from F, restricting the algebra action gives literally the same multiplication operators as the local L-action, so there is no implicit coefficient-category comparison.
- Step 6 uses the differential definition for finite sums of f dg and the explicit linearity of residues in the DVR node. All sums are finite. The final equality is obtained by adding the previous identities, not by claiming that traces on one-dimensional cohomology spaces must agree automatically.

I tested the statement and new nodes in these cases. For K=k the principal-parts maps and the scalar trace comparison become identities. For a rational point and an arbitrary transcendental K/k, S has one point, but A can have extra maximal ideals; this is why the proof does not incorrectly call A semilocal. For a separable nonrational closed point split by K, the Artinian factors and trace blocks give the sum over distinct points. In characteristic p take k = F_p(a), C = P^1, x cut out by T^p-a, and K = k(b) with b^p=a. Then t=T^p-a becomes (T-b)^p. There is one point in S and O_y/tO_y has length p. The differential eta=dT/(T^p-a) becomes du/u^p and has residue zero; the common finite trace is zero as well (multiplication by u^{p-1} on the length-p quotient is nilpotent). For eta=T^{p-1}dT/(T^p-a), the scalar-extended Laurent coefficient is 1, so the single block has residue 1, not p: the common finite-quotient formula gives the original k-residue 1. Thus adding a multiplicity p to the sum would be false. The nilpotents are retained inside O_y/t^nO_y. Zero differentials and regular differentials give zero, and the abstract trace/sequence formulas remain valid on zero modules and full lattices.
