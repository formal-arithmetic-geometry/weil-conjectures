## Statement, route, and corrections

The statement is the second assertion of Stacks Tag 0F1F: an abelian sheaf on the small étale site of a finite-type scheme over a separably closed field, supported on closed points, is globally generated. I retained the statement and the current `main-20261015` route. The route approximates the sheaf by finitely generated subsheaves supported on finite closed sets, writes each such subsheaf as a direct image from its reduced finite support, proves generation componentwise using separably closed residue fields, and passes generation through the filtered colimit. No statement error or source error was found, and no hypothesis was added or dropped.

The imported proof expands the component and stalk arguments rather than moving the conclusion into a new node. It explicitly handles the empty support, the finite disjoint-union gluing of sections, the zero stalks off the support, and the fact that global generation gives finite integer linear combinations of germs rather than an isomorphism between sections and stalks. The smallness of the filtered index and the final-object interpretation of global sections are stated in the last step.
