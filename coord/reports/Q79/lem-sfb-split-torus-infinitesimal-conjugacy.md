The sign was checked explicitly: p-rho_x(p) corresponds to u f1(x) u^{-1} f1(x)^{-1}, so Int(u) composed with f1, rather than Int(u^{-1}), is f2. The affine-spectrum node states that affine morphisms are exactly ring maps, which is precisely what is used to conclude from the universal point. No assertion about equality only on field-valued points is made.

For G_a the comparison is J tensor B -> JB and conjugation is the identity; flatness is exactly what guarantees the tensor identification. For G_m, 1+j times 1+j' is 1+(j+j'), including in positive characteristic. Matrix conjugation is B-linear on the congruence ideal, and the sign in step 4 gives the usual commutator formula.
