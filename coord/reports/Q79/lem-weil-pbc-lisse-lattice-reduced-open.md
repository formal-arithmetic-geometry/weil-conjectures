## Review by Q79

I checked the statement against the first paragraph of Bhatt–Scholze 6.8.13, not against a claimed global lisse integral structure: global lattices can fail on a nonnormal scheme, and this conclusion only needs a nonempty open. The stronger noetherian, finite-dimensional and invertibility hypotheses of the item are retained. There are no newly stated lemmas to audit under B1–B8, no statement dispute, and no change of source meaning.

Step 1 uses the site identification and equality of coverings from `lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization`, not its clause about constant coefficients. The continuous coefficients agree on the slice because both evaluate to C(|Z|,A) or C(|Z|,E) on exactly the same scheme Z; this is written out. `lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-module-pullback-tensor` states the tensor comparison used to restrict the rationalization. Restricting a finite free basis and a cover proves preservation of local finite freeness directly. This avoids applying a theorem about internal condensed modules to sheaf modules or applying the noetherian immersion theorem to an arbitrary covering member. The off-unit localization result is declared in split_report.tool_uses as a generic site-theoretic tool.

Step 2 uses precisely the covering family and lattice pairs asserted by `lem-weil-pbc-lisse-lattice-etale-local`. Nonemptiness of X and joint surjectivity give a nonempty member; the scheme's affine basis gives B. We only retain one affine open, so no quasi-compactness of the original etale member, finite subcover, disjoint-union gluing, or descent datum is silently assumed.

Step 3 uses openness of the original etale map from `lem-weil-pbc-etale-morphism-open-map`, taking the image of its affine open B. Restricting source and target preserves the same affine-local etale condition stated in `def-weil-etale-morphism`. W is nonempty by construction and the map to its image is surjective. `lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-open-subscheme-noetherian` gives both local noetherianity and quasi-compactness of W; `lem-weil-pbc-locally-noetherian-quasi-separated` gives quasi-separatedness. `lem-weil-pbc-affine-scheme-qcqs` supplies both conditions for B. These statements really provide all hypotheses of 03S0; no separatedness of the original covering map is presumed.

Step 4 invokes exactly the finite locally closed partition and finite-etale base changes in `lem-weil-pbc-etale-stratified-finite`. It uses no lattice hidden in that node's proof. Step 5 is an elementary finite closed-cover argument written out in the item: minimality makes O nonempty, and density of S_a in its own closure makes O intersect S_a. This argument works for reducible spaces too; it does not assume a stratum is already open or a filtration has a preferred open first member.

Step 6 uses reducedness through the local-ring definition in `def-pc-reduced-scheme`, the closed-then-open definition of immersion in `def-weil-closed-immersion`, and exactly the image-containment factorization statement of `lem-weil-pbc-reduced-source-factors-through-closed-subscheme` (Stacks 0356). After choosing the open T in the immersion factorization, U is contained in T and is reduced, so that theorem really applies to U→T. This repairs the otherwise invalid inference from a topological stratum inclusion to a scheme factorization. I removed an unnecessary assertion about a closed immersion being a monomorphism; only the factorization is used.

Step 7 uses the base-change functor in `def-weil-pbc-finite-etale-category` to retain finiteness and etaleness, and `lem-weil-pbc-etale-covering-base-change` for surjectivity of the single-member cover. The factorization of step 6 identifies B×_W U with the iterated base change over S_a by the fibre-product universal property. Crucially B_U is an OPEN in B, so the lattice pair restricts by step 1; this avoids a separate closed-stratum coefficient or pullback claim. The monoidal inverse-image composition statement of `lem-weil-pbc-left-exact-site-inverse-image-tensor` is exactly the compatibility used for the two restrictions of L and the tensor formula.

Step 8 checks the topological noetherian hypothesis of `lem-weil-pbc-lisse-lattice-finite-etale-descent` rather than conflating it with quasi-compactness. The finite noetherian affine cover, `lem-sc-affine-noetherian-underlying-space`, and `lem-kun-finite-union-noetherian` give a noetherian underlying space. The descent node explicitly requires no descent datum on the given lattice, and gives the lisse A_U-model and rationalization asserted here. L|_U is lisse by step 1. Open permanence checks quasi-compactness both of U as a scheme and of the immersion j: the inverse image of every affine open V in X is the noetherian open U∩V and hence quasi-compact. This distinction was added during my review.

A disconnected or reducible X may lead to an open in only one component, which meets the statement. For the one-stratum partition, the minimal subfamily gives O=W and U=W. Empty covering members are harmless because a nonempty member is selected before choosing B. X empty is excluded exactly as in the statement. Reducedness is used only for the legitimate factorization to a potentially thickened stratum; no normality or irreducibility is assumed.
