## New nodes

lem-sfb-finite-kernel-group-fibres states the following supporting fact for arbitrary finite-type group schemes over any field: finite scheme-theoretic kernel implies that every scheme-theoretic residue-field fibre is finite, hence of dimension at most zero. It does not assert surjectivity or that the morphism is finite, flat, proper, or an isogeny; it needs no equal dimension. It is not a renamed clause of the item. The source is Milne I Proposition 7.1, first proof paragraph on page 32. The argument used there is valid in this generality: after an algebraically closed extension of kappa(y), an empty fibre stays empty; a nonempty finite-type fibre has a rational point a. The maps t -> a*t and x -> a^(-1)*x identify it with the base-changed scheme kernel on functors of points, hence as schemes. Finiteness of that kernel survives base change, and finiteness descends along the faithfully flat field extension, so the original fibre is finite. A finite algebra over a field is Artinian and its spectrum is zero-dimensional (empty allowed). This is the supporting argument retained for the future splitter, not a proof or proof dependency embedded in the new node.
