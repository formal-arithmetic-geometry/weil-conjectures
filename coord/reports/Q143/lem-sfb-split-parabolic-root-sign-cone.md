## Corrections and limitations

The original statement is mathematically sound and unchanged. Its proof was empty. A semisimple-only standard-parabolic node cannot supply exhaustion of all reductive parabolics; nor does the existing root parametrization node assert generation of arbitrary overgroups. I have made these missing structural facts explicit rather than treating the existing nodes as if they stated them.

The supplied source's p.452 definition of fundamental rational cocharacters is under a semisimplicity assumption. For the reductive item the proof extends the independent simple roots to a basis of the full rational character space, assigns zero on the added directions and clears denominators. It never assumes that simple roots span the full character lattice or that fundamental coweights themselves are integral. Equation (166) has been implemented as a root-character statement, not a statement about differentials of characters. Equality of arbitrary Lie algebras is not used as a subgroup equality criterion.

The new nodes remain unsplit as required. The entire requested item split and review are complete, subject to the required server dry run and delivery. No Lean was written and no compiler, Lake or build command was run.
