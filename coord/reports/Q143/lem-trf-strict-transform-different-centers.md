- The fixed isomorphism is `(φ : (I * D).blowup ≅ I.blowup)`. Its being over `S`
  is precisely `(hφ : φ.hom ≫ I.blowupMap = (I * D).blowupMap)`.
  This quantifies over the fixed over-base isomorphism in the node; it neither chooses
  a new isomorphism nor assumes any extra identification. Commutativity of ideal
  multiplication relates this order to the `D * I` order of the cited existence theorem.
- The module is `(F : X.Modules) [F.IsQuasicoherent]`. It need not be finite type.
- No nonzero local sections supported on `f⁻¹(D)` is
  `hF : Mono ((restrictAdjunction (f ⁻¹ᵁ D.support.compl).ι).unit.app F)`.
  This unit on an open `V` is restriction from `V` to
  `V ∩ f⁻¹(S \ V(D))`. A module-sheaf map is monic exactly when each section map
  is injective. Its kernel consists exactly of the sections vanishing off `f⁻¹(D)`.
  Thus this is the entire local condition, including all opens, not just global sections
  or a statement about the support of the whole sheaf.
- The two ambient schemes are `Limits.pullback f I.blowupMap` and
  `Limits.pullback f (I * D).blowupMap`. The helper `moduleStrictTransformCenterIso`
  is `asIso` of `pullback.map` using `id_X`, `φ.hom`, `id_S` and the commuting equations.
  It is precisely `id_X ×[S] φ`; all its data are supplied by the fibre-product API.
- To put both kernels inside the same ambient module `p*F`, the helper
  `moduleStrictTransformTransportedOpen` transports the product-centre complement
  `moduleStrictTransformOpen f (I * D)` by this isomorphism. It takes its preimage
  under the inverse isomorphism, which is exactly its image under `id_X ×[S] φ`.
  The helper `moduleStrictTransformTransportedRestriction` is restriction of
  `(tensorPullback (pullback.fst f I.blowupMap)).obj F` to this transported open.
  Because the ambient isomorphism commutes with `p`, this is the product-centre
  restriction after the canonical identification of both pullbacks of `F`.
- The conclusion is the equality
  `kernelSubobject (moduleStrictTransformRestriction f I F) =`
  `kernelSubobject (moduleStrictTransformTransportedRestriction f I D φ hφ F)`.
  `kernelSubobject` retains the inclusions into the common `p*F`, so this says
  equality of kernels, not just isomorphism of their underlying modules. The recorded
  strict transform is the cokernel of the kernel inclusion of its restriction map.
  In the abelian category of module sheaves, the kernel of this quotient is exactly
  that original kernel subobject. Consequently this is precisely equality of the
  two quotient kernels required in the node. No finite-type assumption on `I * D`
  is added: that follows locally from the Cartier generator and finite type of `I`.

Edge cases checked before writing:

- Empty `S` or `X`: no nonemptiness assumptions exclude them; the module categories
  and equality of kernel subobjects still make sense.
- `I = 0`: its blowup has empty standard charts; the resulting empty ambient causes
  no problem, and no nonzero ideal hypothesis was added.
- `D = top`, the empty Cartier divisor: the no-supported-sections condition is
  automatic, and multiplication leaves the ideal unchanged.
- `I = top`: both blowups are the base, and the equality is exactly vanishing of
  the supported-section kernel, as required by `hF`.
- `F = 0` is allowed. If all of `F` is supported on `f⁻¹(D)`, the hypothesis forces
  `F = 0`, exactly as the natural-language statement requires.
- The support hypothesis is substantive: on `Spec k[x]`, with `I = top`, `D = (x)`
  and `F` associated to `k[x]/(x)`, the centre-free kernel is zero whereas the
  product-centre kernel is all of `F`. In this case `hF` fails.
- No characteristic restriction, reducedness, Noetherianity, separation or flatness
  assumption has been introduced.
