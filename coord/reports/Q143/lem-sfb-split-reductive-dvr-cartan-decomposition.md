## Sources and proof plans for the new statements

`lem-sfb-complete-dvr-cocharacter-cartan`: Theorem 1.3 (1) implies (3), with Definition 3.4, states exactly this factorization for a connected reductive group over a complete DVR; its residue field need not be algebraically closed. I read pp.1 and 5-6, including the proof via extension of the equivariant torsor and Lemma 3.6. It is a genuine complete-case input to a descent argument. The original item is about an arbitrary DVR and a fixed split torus; the new statement needs completeness but allows nonsplit reductive groups and supplies a cocharacter rather than a fixed-torus point. The intended later split follows the equivariant-torsor argument in the cited paper, separating extension and complete equivariant trivialization, rather than silently assuming the algebraically closed version of Theorem 1.1.

`lem-sfb-complete-dvr-torus-cocharacter-conjugacy`: I read Remark 3.5, forward implication, p.5, which explicitly conjugates the cocharacter into the given maximal split torus by an integral element. The new node states only this conjugacy theorem, restricted to complete DVRs, including equality of group-scheme morphisms and hence all test-algebra points. It has no rational-point factorization conclusion. The existing field-conjugacy node has algebraically closed field hypotheses and therefore cannot justify this integral step. In the later split the integral conjugacy theorem must be proved from reductive-group structure over the local base, not from the algebraically closed field node alone.

`lem-sfb-split-reductive-local-quotient-points`: This is an agent-written auxiliary statement motivated by the root-coordinate approximation in Theorem 4.1, proof, p.7; it is not falsely attributed as a verbatim theorem of the paper. The proof plan is as follows. For a split reductive group over a local ring choose a split Borel, ordered positive/negative root parametrizations and integral Weyl representatives. Over the residue field, rational Bruhat factorization expresses a rational point using root coordinates, a torus point and a Weyl representative, since the group is split. Lift those additive coordinates and unit torus coordinates to the local ring. For a point over A/J, remove that lifted residue-field word; the remaining point reduces to the identity, so factors through the big cell U^- S U, whose complement is closed and misses the unique closed point of Spec(A/J). Lift its root and unit coordinates to A. The product is the required preimage. Existence of these integral root charts and Bruhat representatives is the substantive content to split later, not assumed elementary here. The assertion is stronger than the paper's approximation of an opposite unipotent factor; its stronger form is used only to avoid repeating parabolic notation in the item. It does not assert lifting for arbitrary smooth schemes or nonsplit tori. In particular no use of Hensel's lemma with a nonhenselian base is hidden in this input.
