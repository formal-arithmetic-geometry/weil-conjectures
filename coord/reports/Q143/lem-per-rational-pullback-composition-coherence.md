## Preservation of the original arguments

The following is the complete old proof, verbatim. It is retained as intended arguments for later workers, not hidden in new-node statements as proof sketches. Old Steps 1–2 belong to lem-per-localized-ordinary-pullback-normalization; old Steps 3–4 belong to lem-per-quotient-completed-compositor-unit. The ordinary part of old Step 7 belongs to the first and its reflected identity part to the second. Old Steps 5–6 and 8 are the explicit assembly proof of the item.

### Original step 1

Write $d_{g,n}:g^{-1}(A/\varpi^n)_X\to(A/\varpi^n)_{X'}$ for the finite constant-coefficient map. The definition of pro-etale inverse image and the adjunction-compatible site composition identify $d_{g\circ h,n}$ with $d_{h,n}h^{-1}d_{g,n}$: their adjoints both pull a constant section $a$ back to that same $a$ on $X''\times_XU$. This also proves the identity case and compatibility with the quotient transitions. By the definition of $c_g$, for every $n$,
$$\pi_{X'',n}c_hh^{-1}c_g=d_{h,n}h^{-1}d_{g,n}h^{-1}g^{-1}\pi_{X,n}=\pi_{X'',n}c_{g\circ h}.$$
The projections of the actual limit sheaf $B_{X''}$ are jointly monic, so $c_{g\circ h}=c_hh^{-1}c_g$, under inverse-image composition. The same argument gives $c_{\mathrm{id}}=1$. No inverse image is commuted with an inverse limit.

### Original step 2

Denote ordinary integral and rational composition by $a:P_hP_g\xrightarrow{\sim}P_{g\circ h}$ and $b:G_hG_g\xrightarrow{\sim}G_{g\circ h}$. Apply the ringed-derived monoidal comparisons to the ring maps from Step 1 and to their localizations. They are defined on flat-term K-flat representatives, and ordinary pullback preserves that class. The inverse-image/localization comparison is the specified one, preserves the unit, and satisfies its identity/composition equations. Let $\delta_g:F_{X'}P_g\xrightarrow{\sim}G_gF_X$ be balanced tensor flattening from the realization comparison. After these identifications both paths from $F_{X''}P_hP_gK$ to $G_{g\circ h}F_XK$ send the local generator $e''\otimes b'\otimes k$ to $e''\,\iota_{X''}(c_h(b'))\otimes k$, where $b'\in h^{-1}B_{X'}$ and $\iota_{X''}:B_{X''}\to E_{X''}$. Thus
$$\delta_{g\circ h}F_{X''}(a)=b\,G_h(\delta_g)\,\delta_{h,P_g}.$$
Equality holds on sheafified tensor complexes (the displayed balanced maps commute with restrictions and differentials), and hence on the derived functors. If $d_h:h^{-1}E_{X'}\to E_{X''}$ is the localized ring map, $b$ itself sends $e''\otimes e'\otimes k$ to $e''d_h(e')\otimes k$; its inverse inserts $1$. This is the formula in the statement, with $h^{-1}(e')$ acted on by $d_h$. Scalars have degree zero, so no sign is introduced.

### Original step 6

Abbreviate $F_{X''}$ to $F''$. Expand the second route using Step 5 and naturality of $\delta_h$ at $\gamma_g^{-1}:\widehat P_gK\to P_gK$:
$$\begin{aligned}
b\,G_h(\alpha_g)\,\alpha_{h,\widehat P_gK}
&=b\,G_h(\delta_g)\,\delta_{h,P_gK}\,F''(P_h\gamma_g^{-1})\,F''(\gamma_{h,\widehat P_gK}^{-1})\\
&=\delta_{g\circ h}\,F''(a_KP_h\gamma_g^{-1}\gamma_{h,\widehat P_gK}^{-1})\\
&=\delta_{g\circ h}\,F''(\gamma_{g\circ h,K}^{-1})\,F''(\widehat a_K)

\end{aligned}$$
The second equality is precisely Step 2; the third is the unit equation of Step 4 with invertible units canceled. This proves the requested equality of the two specified transformations on integral representatives, including their naturality in $K$.

### Original step 7

Write $e:P_{\mathrm{id}}\to\mathrm{id}$ and $e_E:G_{\mathrm{id}}\to\mathrm{id}$ for the specified ambient identities. $$e_E\alpha_{\mathrm{id}}=F(e\gamma_{\mathrm{id}}^{-1})=F(\widehat e).$$
This is the identity diagram for the specified maps. It also shows that conjugation by $\gamma$ preserves the normalization of the identity, just as the equation of Step 4 does for composition.

Finally $J_WQ=F_W$ with $J_W(f/\varpi^n)=\varpi^{-n}F_W(f)$.
