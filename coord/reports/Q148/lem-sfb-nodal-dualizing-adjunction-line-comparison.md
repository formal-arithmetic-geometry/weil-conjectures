Hypothesis and clause correspondence:

- Schemes and the structural morphism are `{X T : Scheme.{u}} (f : X ⟶ T)`.
  `[IsNoetherian T]` means a finite affine cover with Noetherian section rings:
  Mathlib's `IsNoetherian` extends local Noetherianity and compactness, and
  `isNoetherian_iff_of_finite_affine_openCover` identifies this with the node's
  convention. No Noetherian hypothesis on the total space is imposed separately.
- Properness, flatness and finite presentation are `[IsProper f] [Flat f]
  [LocallyOfFinitePresentation f]`. Properness supplies quasi-compactness and
  quasi-separatedness, so this is finite presentation, not merely local finite
  presentation. There is no smoothness, connectedness, irreducibility or genus
  assumption.
- `hfibres` quantifies over every algebraically closed field `Ω : Type u` and
  geometric morphism `t : Spec (.of Ω) ⟶ T`. Its scheme is the actual
  `pullback f t`, with structural morphism `pullback.snd f t`. The three
  conjuncts `Nonempty`, `IsReduced` and `.IsPureDimension 1` are precisely
  nonemptiness, reducedness and pure dimension one of the geometric fibre.
  Algebraic closures of residue fields are among these geometric points; the
  all-algebraically-closed-fields formulation also includes exactly the Ω-points
  at which the node explicitly imposes its completed-local-ring condition.
- The final fibre conjunct quantifies over `x : ↥(pullback f t)`, requires
  `IsClosed ({x} : Set ↥(pullback f t))`, and then requires that `x` be outside
  `(pullback.snd f t).smoothLocus`. Thus it is only the nonsmooth CLOSED
  points that must satisfy the nodal condition. The completion is
  `AdicCompletion (IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal ((pullback f t).presheaf.stalk x))
  ((pullback f t).presheaf.stalk x)`. The target of the ring equivalence is
  `MvPowerSeries (Fin 2) Ω` modulo the ideal generated by `X 0 * X 1`.
  `Nonempty (... ≃+* ...)` expresses existence of the stated ring isomorphism,
  rather than a selected nodal coordinate system.
- `hlci` says that every `x : X` belongs to an open `U` with
  `Nonempty (FlatLCIPresentation f 1 U)`. The dependency's actual structure
  includes an ambient open immersion into affine n-space over T, a closed
  immersion from U, equality of the structural morphisms, `n = c + 1`, and
  c ideal generators regular locally at every prime of the closed source.
  On the Noetherian ambient affine charts this is exactly the locally regular
  presentation in the node. It does not select a cover or exclude c=0.
- `W : NodalRelativeDualizingSystem.{u, w} f` is the relative dualizing
  module with comparisons specified in the node. Its `omega` is invertible,
  identified by `homologyIso` with H^-1 of the actual right-adjoint value
  f^! O, and its `baseChange` is the supplied coherent comparison system.
  Its normalized local comparisons and fundamental differential are retained.
- `K : FlatLCIAdjunctionSystem f 1` with `hK : K.IsNormalized` is the
  specified canonical adjunction line: its `line`, `localIso`, `baseChange`
  and determinant normalization are those of the dependency. This is the
  supplied normalized adjunction construction, not an arbitrary invertible
  module with freely chosen maps. The hypotheses W and K render the node's
  two “let ... be” clauses; their existence is given by the dependencies.
- `θ` is a simultaneous family of module isomorphisms
  `W.omega B ≅ K.line B`, for every `B : FlatLCIBaseChange f` with
  `IsNoetherian B.S'`. The original θ_f is its value at
  `FlatLCIBaseChange.identity f`. Both modules are invertible by the
  fields of W and K, so no duplicate invertibility assumptions are necessary.
- Clause (1) is the final equation. For any cartesian transition `(g,b)`
  from D to B, the left side is the actual tensor pullback of θ_B followed
  by `K.baseChange B D g b hc hg hb`, and the right side is
  `W.baseChange B D g b hc hg hb` followed by θ_D. With B the identity
  record and D the record of the chosen `X ×_T T'`, this is verbatim the
  square in (1). The equalities `hg`, `hb` only say that the transition
  commutes with the recorded maps to the original family; they are part of
  the definition of a base-change transition. All Noetherian new bases and
  arbitrary base morphisms are included; there is no flatness requirement
  on `b`. Admissibility of the base-changed family is the inherited fact
  explained in proof step 4, not an added assumption on B or D.
- Clause (2) is the same equation for a cartesian transition with base map
  the identity and total-space map an isomorphism over that base. In
  particular, take B the identity record and D the isomorphic family's
  cartesian record. This uses the dependency's comparisons along that
  isomorphism, not an independently chosen identification of either line.
  Arbitrary cartesian records are used specifically to retain these cases.
- No factorization is an argument of θ. The only presentation hypothesis
  is existence of local charts; no presentation data or chosen cover is
  passed to the comparison family. The docstring gives the normalized
  local construction, overlap argument and refinement independence from
  the six proof steps of the node. No uniqueness assertion or new
  normalization condition on θ has been added to the conclusion.
