## STATEMENT-DISPUTED: missing source conventions

The imported statement omitted Alper Section 2's convention that every algebraic stack and algebraic space has quasi-compact separated diagonal. The project's definitions def-sfb-algebraic-moduli-stack and def-trf-algebraic-space do not impose this. The omission makes the closure-equivalence assertion false.

An explicit counterexample is the etale algebraic-space quotient Y=A^1_K/Z over K=Qbar, for the free action by integer translations. The relation is the union of the graphs t -> t+n, n in Z; its projections are etale, and freeness makes it an equivalence relation, so the etale sheaf quotient is an algebraic space. Take X=Y and phi=id. The identity is quasi-compact and quasi-separated, its structure-sheaf map is the identity, and every section of every quotient algebra lifts etale locally (on affine charts it lifts with exponent 1). Hence it is an adequate moduli space under the project's broad definition. The K-points represented by 0 and 1/2 are distinct, since their difference is not an integer. In the atlas A^1_K, the inverse images of their singleton points are respectively Z and 1/2+Z. Each is an infinite Zariski-dense subset of A^1_K. Consequently both singleton points are dense in |Y| and their closures intersect, contradicting the original equivalence for id_Y. The diagonal of Y is not quasi-compact; the infinite relation is excluded by the source convention.

I corrected the item by explicitly requiring that the diagonals of X and Y over k are quasi-compact and separated, with the chart meaning written out. This is faithful to the exact source, not a finite-type or reducedness restriction. Universal submersiveness still permits arbitrary algebraic-space base changes, including non-quasi-separated ones. The only current direct citer is lem-sfb-adequate-locally-separated-universality; its broad statement does not currently supply these diagonal hypotheses. Its use of this item must be audited by the maintainer. I did not edit that other worker's node. In particular the same counterexample shows why broad stack conventions must not silently be identified with the Alper conventions. This is an upstream statement issue, not an authorized edit to any locked target.

## Supporting proof notes for the new unsplit nodes

These notes preserve the mathematical arguments without putting a proof in a statement-only node.

For flat base change, Alper Proposition 5.2.9(1) is exactly the source: Proposition 4.2.1(6) preserves adequate affineness and flat base change preserves the isomorphism O_Y -> phi_*O_X. The resulting adequacy quantifies over all algebras on the changed stack; it is not merely a lifting property for algebras pulled back from X. This will need splitting when that node is worked.

For rational-point topology, quasi-separated implies decent by Stacks 03JX. Stacks 07U5, with base S=Spec K and the field-valued point also K, says that each such image is closed. For injectivity, 0H1T factors the reduced point Spec K through the residual field Spec k(z), whose existence follows from 0H1R and decency. The two field maps K -> k(z) -> K have composite the identity. The second is a field embedding whose image contains the whole K through the first, so it is surjective; hence k(z)=K with the specified K-structure and the factorization is unique. Thus the point set map is injective. Neither a Jacobson hypothesis nor finite type is used.

For same-field existence, a quasi-compact smooth atlas gives quasi-compact |Z|. Its quasi-compact diagonal makes |Z| sober by Stacks 0DQQ; quasi-compact T0 spaces have closed points (the usual maximal-open/Zorn argument, or a closed point of a nonempty minimal irreducible closed subset). Let z be closed and give {z} its reduced closed substack G using 0509. The inertia of G is quasi-compact because it is a base change of its quasi-compact diagonal. Stacks 06RC gives a dense open substack of G which is a gerbe, and singleton topology makes that open all of G. The base space B of this gerbe is reduced and singleton; its diagonal is quasi-compact by the gerbe quotient diagonal calculation (0DQL, lemma-gerbe-diagonal-quasi-compact), hence B is decent and B=Spec L by 047Z. By 06R9 G -> Spec L is a universal homeomorphism. Closed immersion G -> Z and universal closedness of Z over K make G -> Spec K universally closed. For every K-scheme T, transfer closed subsets across the homeomorphism |G_T| -> |Spec L x_K T|: the induced map |Spec L x_K T| -> |T| is closed. Thus Spec L -> Spec K is universally closed. It is affine, hence integral by 01WM; L/K is algebraic, hence L=K because K is algebraically closed. The gerbe G -> Spec K is smooth and surjective by 0DN8/06QI. A nonempty smooth scheme chart of G is smooth over K, hence has a K-point (choose a nonempty finite-type affine open and use the weak Nullstellensatz). It supplies an object in G(K), and therefore in Z(K). This proof does not assume that Z/K, G/K or its original atlas is locally of finite type. Only the final gerbe atlas becomes smooth over K after the residual field calculation. The node remains unsplit, so each of these intermediate facts will be separately cited/split at its own later stage.
