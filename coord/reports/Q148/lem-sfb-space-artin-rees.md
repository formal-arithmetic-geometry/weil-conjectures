Edge cases checked mathematically before completing the statement:

- At n=c, exponent n-c is zero and the RHS is I^c F intersect N itself.
  At exponent zero the ideal power is the unit ideal; local membership in
  the sections of an actual submodule sheaf is global membership by the
  sheaf axiom. Thus I^0 M=M, including c=n=0.
- If I=0, every positive ideal power is zero. If I=O_X, all powers are the
  unit ideal and both sides are N. The zeroth power remains the unit ideal
  even for the zero ideal.
- If F or N is zero, both section subsets are the zero submodule.
- On an empty test, the empty covering sieve is allowed exactly when it is
  covering. Its vacuous local-membership condition yields every section;
  the module sheaf axiom there makes the section module trivial. For empty
  X the theorem therefore includes the case c=0; no nonemptiness is added.
- Tests need not be affine, quasi-compact or nonempty. The same bound works
  for all of them. All source universes and the small etale site's section
