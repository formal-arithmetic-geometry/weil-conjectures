### Original step 1

Put $E_U=\beta_U^{-1}(B_U^\times)$ and let $P(U)$ be the quotient of $R\times B_U^\times$ by the congruence generated by $(re,u)\sim(r,\beta_U(e)u)$ for $e\in E_U$. This is the pushout described in [def-trf-fs-log-blowup]: a pair of monoid maps out of $R$ and $B_U^\times$ agreeing on $E_U$ gives the map $[r,u]\mapsto f(r)g(u)$, which respects precisely these generating relations. Restriction sends $E_U$ into $E_V$, and sends each displayed relation to the same relation over $V$, so it induces $P(U)\to P(V)$; the identity and composition laws follow on representatives. The maps $[r,u]\mapsto\beta_U(r)u$ respect the relation, multiplication and restriction, hence give $P\to(\mathcal O_X,\cdot)$.

### Original step 9

The underlying map $P_U\to L_X|_U$ is locally surjective and locally injective by [lem-weil-pbc-site-sheafification-sections-local], applied to $P_X\to L_X$: the covering refinements of any $V\to U$ still lie over $U$. Thus [lem-cbc-set-presheaf-sheafification-local-bijection] identifies $L_X|_U$ with the set sheafification of $P_U$. By [lem-trf-commutative-monoid-sheafification], $L_U$ is another such sheafification.
