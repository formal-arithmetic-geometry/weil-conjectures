## Original proof retained verbatim

### Old step 1

Work with the datum and coefficient category of [thm-pur-weil-equivariance-of-purity-and-trace]. For $T=X$ or $Y$, abbreviate $F_T=L\Phi_T^*$ and $G_T=Lg_{T,\gamma}^*$. Use the specified natural comparison $\kappa_T:F_T\xrightarrow{\sim}G_T$ of [def-pur-scheme-geometric-frobenius]. At finite coefficients that definition gives it explicitly on $D(T_{\mathrm{proet}},A)$; the argument below only uses the comparison already supplied in the statement, in its stated coefficient category. Thus its naturality, for a morphism $h:M\to N$ in the category on $T$, is
$$G_T(h)\circ\kappa_{T,M}=\kappa_{T,N}\circ F_T(h).$$
By [def-pur-compact-support-transport], for every one of the objects carrying a datum the scheme-Frobenius form is the defined map $u_M:=\varphi_M\circ\kappa_{T,M}:F_T(M)\to M$. These are the given comparisons and transported objects in the chosen coefficient category; no new comparison or coefficient-category identification is being constructed.

### Old step 2

For any of these morphisms $h:M\to N$, its geometric equivariance means $h\circ\varphi_M=\varphi_N\circ G_T(h)$. Precompose this equality with $\kappa_{T,M}$ and use the naturality identity of step 1. Associativity gives the full calculation
$$h\circ u_M=h\circ\varphi_M\circ\kappa_{T,M}=\varphi_N\circ G_T(h)\circ\kappa_{T,M}=\varphi_N\circ\kappa_{T,N}\circ F_T(h)=u_N\circ F_T(h).$$
The bottom arrow is still $h$, the top arrow is now $F_T(h)$, and the two vertical arrows are exactly the old ones precomposed with their respective components of $\kappa_T$. For a strict datum, the inverse of $u_M$ is $\kappa_{T,M}^{-1}\circ\varphi_M^{-1}$: both inverse identities follow by associativity and cancellation of the adjacent inverse pairs. Thus the vertical isomorphisms are preserved as well as commutativity.

### Old step 3

Apply step 2 on $X$ with $M=Lf^*K(d)[2d]$, $N=Rf^!K$, $h=\gamma_{f,K}$ and the data $\varphi^{\mathrm{pur}}_{f,K}$ and $\varphi^!_{f,K}$. The purity square of [thm-pur-weil-equivariance-of-purity-and-trace] supplies precisely the geometric equivariance equality required by step 2. Hence
$$\gamma_{f,K}\circ u_{Lf^*K(d)[2d]}=u_{Rf^!K}\circ L\Phi_X^*(\gamma_{f,K}).$$
This is the required scheme-Frobenius purity square. If its source is written through the pullback-composition identifications, the equality $c_g\circ(Lf^*\kappa_Y)=(\kappa_XLf^*)\circ c_\Phi$ in [def-pur-scheme-geometric-frobenius], where $c_g$ and $c_\Phi$ are its pullback-composition identifications, ensures it uses the same specified comparison.

### Old step 4

Apply step 2 on $Y$ first with $M=Rf_!Rf^!K$, $N=K$ and $h=\operatorname{Tr}_{f,K}$, the exceptional counit; then with $M=Rf_!Lf^*K(d)[2d]$, $N=K$ and $h=\operatorname{Tr}^{\mathrm{sm}}_{f,K}$, the normalized smooth trace. Use in each case the compact-support transported datum specified in [thm-pur-weil-equivariance-of-purity-and-trace]. That theorem asserts geometric equivariance of both morphisms, including the exceptional-counit clause stated in words between its two displayed squares. Step 2 therefore yields
$$\operatorname{Tr}_{f,K}\circ u_{Rf_!Rf^!K}=u_K\circ L\Phi_Y^*(\operatorname{Tr}_{f,K}),\qquad \operatorname{Tr}^{\mathrm{sm}}_{f,K}\circ u_{Rf_!Lf^*K(d)[2d]}=u_K\circ L\Phi_Y^*(\operatorname{Tr}^{\mathrm{sm}}_{f,K}).$$
These are the remaining two scheme-Frobenius squares. Together with step 3 they prove all three clauses, using only naturality on the category on $X$ or $Y$, without applying Cartesian base change to the scheme-Frobenius square.
