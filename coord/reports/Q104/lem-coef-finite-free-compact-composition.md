### Returned Step 2

Regard $B_\bullet=(B_n)_n$, with its quotient transitions, as a strict $A_\bullet$-module system on every scheme $W$. It is normalized: for $m\ge n$, $B_m$ is free over $A_m$ on the reduction of the fixed $R$-basis of $S$, so $B_m\otimes^L_{A_m}A_n=B_m\otimes_{A_m}A_n=S/\pi^nS=B_n$. Its levels are constant sheaves of finite free $A_n$-modules, hence constructible of Tor amplitude $[0,0]$; thus $B_\bullet\in\mathscr N_W$ in the sense of [lem-pur-system-level-projection-formula-coherence], and $h^*B_\bullet=B_\bullet$ for every $h$. The realization [lem-coef-finite-adjunction-diagram-realization] supplies the strict-system functors $\mathcal F_h$ with their quotient transitions, and [lem-coef-semilinear-derived-sections] (applied to both ring systems $A_\bullet$ and $B_\bullet$) supplies simultaneous models of pullback, derived direct image, open extension, coefficient extension, tensor and their units and counits.

Restriction of scalars. Let $\operatorname{Res}$ be restriction of scalars from strict $B_\bullet$-systems to strict $A_\bullet$-systems and $T=B_\bullet\otimes_{A_\bullet}-$ its left adjoint. Since each $B_n$ is free over $A_n$, $T$ is exact levelwise and computes $-\otimes^L_{A_\bullet}B_\bullet$; $\operatorname{Res}$ is exact, and as the right adjoint of an exact functor it carries K-injective complexes to K-injective complexes. On module sheaves, $u^{-1}$, $u_*$ and $j_!$ commute with $\operatorname{Res}$ on the nose (the ring sheaves are constant, $u^{-1}(B_n)_W=(B_n)_{W'}$), and the sheaf-level adjunction units and counits are the same underlying maps. Computing $Ru_{*,B}$ on a K-injective $B_\bullet$-resolution therefore gives $\operatorname{Res}\,Ru_{*,B}=Ru_{*,A}\operatorname{Res}$, compatibly with units, counits and the direct-image compositors; hence $\operatorname{Res}\,\mathcal F_{h,B}=\mathcal F_h\operatorname{Res}$ and $\operatorname{Res}(\gamma_{g,f,B})=\alpha_{g,f,\operatorname{Res}(-)}$, since the compositor of [thm-sf-comp-composition] is built only from these maps.

Identification of the coefficient map. Let $\widetilde\alpha_h:T\mathcal F_hK_\bullet\to\mathcal F_{h,B}TK_\bullet$ be the specified system coefficient map, formed in the models of [lem-coef-semilinear-derived-sections] exactly as $c_h$ in [lem-pur-finite-free-compact-composition]: the $p_B^*\dashv Rp_{*,B}$ transpose of $T(\varepsilon_{p,A})$, then the open balancing $Tj_!\cong j_!T$. By the unit compatibility just recorded, $\operatorname{Res}$ of a $B$-transpose is the $A$-transpose of the restricted map, so under $\operatorname{Res}TP=P\otimes^LB_\bullet$, $\operatorname{Res}(\widetilde\alpha_h)$ is the transpose of $(\varepsilon_{p}\otimes1)\mu_p$ followed by the open tensor comparison, i.e. $\operatorname{PF}^{\mathrm{sys}}_{h,K_\bullet,B_\bullet}$ of [lem-pur-system-level-projection-formula-coherence]. This is an identity of the constructed system maps, not an inference from evaluations; its level-$n$ evaluation is $\alpha_{h,n}$ by Step 1.

### Returned Step 3

Apply the strict-system identity of [lem-pur-system-level-projection-formula-coherence] with $M=K_\bullet\in\mathscr N_X$ and $N=B_\bullet\in\mathscr N_Z$ (Step 2). Its left side is $\alpha_{g,f,K\otimes^LB}\,\mathcal F_g(\operatorname{PF}^{\mathrm{sys}}_{f,K,B})\,\operatorname{PF}^{\mathrm{sys}}_{g,\mathcal F_fK,B}$ and its right side is $\operatorname{PF}^{\mathrm{sys}}_{gf,K,B}(\alpha_{g,f,K}\otimes1)$, an equality in $D(\mathscr S_Z)$ before total inverse limit. By Step 2 these are $\operatorname{Res}$ of the two sides $\phi=\widetilde\gamma_{g,f,B}\,\mathcal F_{g,B}(\widetilde\alpha_f)\,\widetilde\alpha_g$ and $\psi=\widetilde\alpha_{gf}\,T(\widetilde\gamma_{g,f,A})$ of the item's equation, so $\operatorname{Res}\phi=\operatorname{Res}\psi$.

The item asks for $\phi=\psi$ as maps of $B_\bullet$-systems, and $\operatorname{Res}$ need not be faithful when $e>1$ (already for $n=1$, $e=2$, $B_1=A_1[v]/v^2$). The exact adjunction $T\dashv\operatorname{Res}$ of Step 2 passes to derived categories of systems, and a map $\chi:TX\to Y$ is determined by its transpose $\operatorname{Res}(\chi)\circ\eta_X$, with $\eta_X:X\to\operatorname{Res}TX$ the unit. Since $\operatorname{Res}\phi=\operatorname{Res}\psi$, the transposes agree, so $\phi=\psi$ in the derived category of $B_\bullet$-systems, before total limit. Under the equivalence of [lem-coef-semilinear-derived-sections] this is the asserted equality of the specified maps in the simultaneous section models. It is not inferred from equality of the level evaluations.
