I tested the statement on P^1 over an algebraically closed field: m=1 and rational point classes have trace 1. Over an imperfect separably closed field of characteristic p, a closed point of P^1 defined by T^p-a has residue degree p; its geometric pullback is p times a point, and the formula gives p, as required. If D=P^1_L is regarded as a k-curve for a finite purely inseparable extension L/k of degree p^r, its geometric base change is generically thick of multiplicity p^r; C=P^1_K and the trace is p^r times the usual geometric trace. This checks the very case that would invalidate omitting m. The trivial extension and characteristic-zero cases give m=1. The new normalization comparison is checked on smooth curves, on this nonreduced geometric base change, and on the identity field extension. Birational degree invariance is checked on the identity and on a cuspidal curve normalization: the finite normalization cokernel contributes the same Euler correction for a line bundle and the structure sheaf, so their degree difference agrees. The node does not claim the Euler characteristics themselves agree. An affine curve, a disconnected curve, the empty scheme, ell equal to the characteristic and n=0 are outside the item's explicit hypotheses; no such exclusions were added by the split.
