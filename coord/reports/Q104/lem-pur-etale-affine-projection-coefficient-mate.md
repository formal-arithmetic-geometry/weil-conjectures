### Original Step 1 -> lem-pur-etale-affine-integration-coefficient-equation

Write $X=\mathbf A^r_V$, $T_T=-\otimes_A^LB$ and $H_RK=\pi^*K(r)[2r]$. The coefficient definition gives finiteness of $A,B$. In each valuation ring $|\ell|=\ell^{-1}<1$, so $\ell$ belongs to its maximal ideal, generated by its uniformizer; therefore $\ell^mA=0$ and $\ell^nB=0$. Take a common annihilator $N=\ell^{\max(m,n)}$, invertible over $k$. The field $k$ is Noetherian since its only ideals are zero and the unit ideal; the finite-type theorem makes $V$ Noetherian. The coordinate-tower statement makes $X$ Noetherian and $\pi$ separated of finite type. The standard compactification stages are proper over standing bases, hence finite type; the same finite-type theorem makes them Noetherian. The chart-domain theorem applies to $A\to B$, with no flatness requirement: $T$, $H_R$ and the chosen compact-support functors preserve $D^b_{ctf}$, and site comparison is an equivalence there. These are the domains on which all following coefficient and site maps are evaluated.

### Original Step 4 -> lem-pur-normalized-mate-scalar-site-transport

Let $\gamma_{R,K}:H_RK\xrightarrow{\sim}\pi_R^\times K$ be exactly the integration-transposed map of vector-bundle purity. By its definition as an adjunction transpose,
$\epsilon_{R,K}\circ F_R(\gamma_{R,K})=z_{R,K}$.
Set $d_K=\gamma_{B,T_VK}\circ a_K\circ T_X(\gamma_{A,K}^{-1})$. Naturality of $q$ at $\gamma_{A,K}^{-1}$ gives
$$q_{H_AK}^{-1}\circ F_B(T_X\gamma_{A,K}^{-1})=T_V(F_A\gamma_{A,K}^{-1})\circ q_{\pi_A^\times K}^{-1}.$$
Using Step 3, compute
$$\epsilon_{B,T_VK}\circ F_B(d_K)=T_V(z_{A,K})\circ q_{H_AK}^{-1}\circ F_B(T_X\gamma_{A,K}^{-1})=T_V(\epsilon_{A,K})\circ q_{\pi_A^\times K}^{-1}.$$
The Hom bijection defining an adjunction transpose is injective, hence $d_K=b_{\pi,K}$. Each factor defining $d_K$ is invertible, so $b_{\pi,K}$ is invertible. Equivalently,
$$\gamma_{B,T_VK}^{-1}\circ b_{\pi,K}\circ T_X(\gamma_{A,K})=a_K.$$
This proves the requested reassociation under the specified integration-normalized identifications. The displayed formulas are natural in $K$ since their constituents are; $\pi_A^\times K$ lies in the comparison domain by its isomorphism with $H_AK$ from Step 1.

Original citations: thm-amb-vector-bundle-purity, def-weil-pbc-adjunction-mate.
