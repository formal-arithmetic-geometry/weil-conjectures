## New nodes

lem-sfb-finite-locally-free-top-cancellation: one local cancellation of an acyclic top degree, for an arbitrary integer r and without a lower bound on the complex. The intended argument, retained here for its future worker, is the single cancellation in 08DN (1): H^r=0 and P^{r+1}=0 make d^{r-1} surjective; trivialize the finite locally free target, lift its finitely many basis sections locally using 08C6, and obtain a section s. Write P^{r-1}=ker(d^{r-1}) direct-sum P^r. The kernel is finite locally free by 0BCI, since a scheme has local stalk rings. Keep the kernel as the term in degree r-1, erase degree r, and leave the lower terms unchanged; d^{r-2} factors through the kernel because consecutive differentials compose to zero. Inclusion is a chain map and its quotient is the contractible identity pair on P^r in degrees r-1,r. The local splitting gives an explicit contraction with s; hence inclusion is a quasi-isomorphism. This argument uses no boundedness below. Those local lifting and local-freeness inputs are substantial, which is why I did not mark cancellation elementary. Iteration and the final finite presentation stay in the item, rather than being hidden in this new node.
