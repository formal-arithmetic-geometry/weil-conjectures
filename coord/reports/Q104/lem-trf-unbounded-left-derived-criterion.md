## New-node argument and scope

The helper isolates the precise implicit claim in the last half of 0794. It assumes preservation of quasi-isomorphisms on bounded-above class complexes, not the item's acyclic-complex condition; it assumes supplied approximations to X and Y, not exact sequential colimits. It concludes compatible isomorphisms of their stage cohomology, not existence of any derived functor. Thus it is neither the item nor one of its clauses moved into another node.

For the future splitter: replace a stage by a class resolution of its good truncation when necessary. Each augmentation then represents a quasi-isomorphism onto the corresponding good truncation of its target. A common roof in the bounded-above homotopy category represents the map induced by tau_le_n(alpha). Resolve that roof by 05T7; both legs to the class stages are quasi-isomorphisms, so F of both legs is invertible on cohomology. Their quotient defines theta. Equality of roofs is checked after a further common quasi-isomorphism refinement, again bounded above by a good truncation and refined into the class by 05T7. F then makes the refinement invertible. To compare stage n with n+1, perform this same refinement for the two composite roofs from the n-th source to the (n+1)-th target; both represent the same map because the original augmentations and the good truncation maps commute. This proves the transition equation. To verify the square into unbounded Y, retain the homotopy-commuting squares of the common roof into tau_le_n(Y) and then Y. F preserves the homotopies because it is additive. This explains all the compatibility data in the helper, without assuming that alpha is a map of towers or that arbitrary class objects are projective. These details are a guide in this report; the unsplit node itself contains no proof.
