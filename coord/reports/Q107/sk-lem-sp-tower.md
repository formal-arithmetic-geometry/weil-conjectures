### Returned step 1

Put $O=\mathcal O_E$, $R=\mathcal O_{E,T}$ and $B_n=(O/\ell^n)_T$. The current coefficient-tower definition identifies $R$ canonically with $\varprojlim_n B_n$, compatibly with the finite projections; we use this identification, so that $B_n$ retains the definition's constant finite-ring meaning. Choose a uniformizer $\pi$ of $O$. The DVR statement gives $\ell=u\pi^e$ with $u$ a unit and $e\geq1$: the valuation is positive since the absolute value extends the $\ell$-adic one. Thus $O$ is Noetherian (every ideal is principal), and $(\ell^n)$ and $(\pi^j)$ are cofinal. We keep the given $\ell$-tower; we do not identify $\ell$ with $\pi$. Apply constant-completion flatness and its quotient assertion with $A=O,I=(\ell)$. This identifies $R/\ell^nR=B_n$ and makes multiplication by $\ell^m$ injective on $R$: tensor the injective map $\underline O\xrightarrow{\ell^m}\underline O$ with the flat $R$. Hence $K_m=[R\xrightarrow{\ell^m}R]$ in degrees $[-1,0]$ resolves $B_m$. The analogous assertion holds for $[R\xrightarrow{\pi^j}R]$ using $I=(\pi)$ and the cofinal identification of completions from the coefficient definition. These two-term complexes are K-flat: tensoring one with an acyclic complex gives a shifted mapping cone of a scalar map between acyclic complexes, which is acyclic by the long exact cohomology sequence. Each quotient $O/\pi^s$ is finite: its filtration by powers of $\pi$ has $s$ successive factors $\pi^iO/\pi^{i+1}O\cong O/\pi$, by multiplication by $\pi^i$ and cancellation in the domain $O$, and the DVR statement gives a finite residue field. In particular $O/\ell^n=O/\pi^{en}$ is finite.

### Returned step 3

The repleteness statement for every scheme is [det-pe-replete-epimorphism]. Here is its exact-products consequence used by the unit. Given a countable family of epimorphisms $M_i\to N_i$ of module sheaves, put $E_r=(\prod_{i\leq r}M_i)\times(\prod_{i>r}N_i)$ for $r\geq0$, with transition replacing the $(r+1)$st $M$ coordinate by its image in $N$. Each transition is epimorphic: lifting its one new coordinate is a local lifting problem for the given epimorphism. The inverse limit is $\prod_i M_i$, since each coordinate eventually is its $M_i$ coordinate; its projection to $E_0=\prod_i N_i$ is precisely $\prod_i(M_i\to N_i)$. Repleteness makes this projection epimorphic. Products preserve kernels by their coordinate universal property, so countable module products are exact. The existing countable-products statement also identifies them sectionwise for the coefficient sheaf $R$. Consequently derived products here are termwise products and commute with cohomology. The strict-tower limit has the natural Roos model $\operatorname{Cone}(1-s:\prod Q_n\to\prod Q_n)[-1]$. For fixed $m$, deleting the finite prefix $n<m$ does not change this model up to quasi-isomorphism, compatibly with all remaining projections. Indeed, write each product as its finite prefix plus tail. On the prefix the operator $1-s$ is an upper triangular finite matrix with identity diagonal; its inverse is the finite geometric sum in its strictly upper triangular part. Row elimination removes the prefix-to-tail block, leaving its contractible identity-cone and the tail Roos cone. This proves the needed tail comparison without an unstated cofinality theorem.
