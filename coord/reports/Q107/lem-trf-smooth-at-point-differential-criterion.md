### Old step 4

Assigned in full to lem-trf-fibre-cotangent-localization-generators.

Let $L=\kappa(\mathfrak Q)$. Because $\mathfrak Q$ contracts to $\mathfrak q$, every $a\in A\setminus\mathfrak q$ maps to a nonzero element of $L$; hence $A\to L$ extends to $A_{\mathfrak q}\to L$. $$\Omega_{F/k}\otimes_F L\cong M_{\mathfrak q}\otimes_{A_{\mathfrak q}}L.$$
Explicitly, on the right-to-left map, $(\omega/a)\otimes\lambda$ goes to $(1\otimes\omega)\otimes(\lambda/\bar a)$ after using the base-change isomorphism. $$\dim_L(\Omega_{F/k}\otimes_F L)\le r\le d=\dim_{\mathfrak Q}(\operatorname{Spec}F).$$

### Old step 6

Retained in assembly step 5.

By [lem-weil-pbc-principal-localization-open-immersion], $\operatorname{Spec}A_g$ identifies with an open neighbourhood $W_g=D(g)$ of $x$ in $W$.
