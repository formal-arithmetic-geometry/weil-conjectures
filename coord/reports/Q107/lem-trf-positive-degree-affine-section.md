Step 5 is the elementary evaluation argument written out from the rank-one local description in `def-sc-invertible-module` and the dimensions already proved. Taking a germ and reducing modulo the maximal ideal is a k-linear operation; fibres have k-dimension d_i, and concatenating bases gives dimension B for the finite product. An injective evaluation would send a basis to too many independent vectors; subtract two vectors with equal image to obtain a nonzero kernel vector. No surjectivity of evaluation is used and no statement about all powers is substituted for the one chosen n.
