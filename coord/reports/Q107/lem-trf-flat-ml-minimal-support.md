### lem-trf-ml-quotient-product-vanishing

The maps A tensor M to M tensor A sending a tensor m to m tensor a are well-defined by the tensor universal property; applying the swap twice fixes every pure tensor and therefore every tensor. ### lem-trf-flat-quotient-product-finite-lift

For each e, q(e)=0 if and only if e+N_j=0 for every j, if and only if e belongs to every N_j. Thus ker q=K, and K inclusion E followed by q is exact at E.
