Its TeX uses single backslashes in decoded strings. It is a genuine compatibility theorem, not an elementary renaming of a unit or an inverse, nor the item's projective-line trace theorem. The Elementary alternatives (adjunct expansion, cancelling splitting isomorphisms and equality of component families) remain explicit proof steps. Its generality was tested for identity maps, isomorphisms, an empty source, the trivial line bundle, level one and ramified coefficient extensions.
