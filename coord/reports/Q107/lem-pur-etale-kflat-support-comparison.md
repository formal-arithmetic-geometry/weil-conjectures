### Returned Step 2

For each pair $(p,q)$, tensor the degree-$q$ exact row with the flat sheaf $Q^p$. Flatness means that this tensor functor is exact, so $0\to Q^p\otimes S^q\to Q^p\otimes I^q\to Q^p\otimes C^q\to0$ is exact. In total degree $n$ take the sum over $p+q=n$. This sum is the filtered colimit of the finite subsums: the index consists of finite subsets of the integer-indexed set of pairs, ordered by inclusion. Each finite subsum is exact because kernels and cokernels of finite biproduct maps are computed componentwise; exactness of filtered colimits therefore proves exactness of the whole row. With the total differential $d_Q\otimes1+(-1)^p1\otimes d$, the inclusions and quotients commute with both differential terms. Consequently $0\to\operatorname{Tot}(Q\otimes S)\to N\to\operatorname{Tot}(Q\otimes C)\to0$ is a degreewise short exact sequence of complexes. This uses flat terms as well as K-flatness; no convergence of a double-complex spectral sequence is used.
