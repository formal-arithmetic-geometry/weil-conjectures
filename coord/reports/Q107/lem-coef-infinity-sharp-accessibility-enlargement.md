## Changes and proof route

The proof follows HTT Proposition 5.4.2.9. Choose an essentially small generating subcategory of lambda-compact objects. Its existing kappa-small colimits form an essentially small subcategory of kappa-compact objects. Reindex each original filtered presentation by a filtered poset; cover that poset by kappa-small lambda-filtered subsets, indexed by a small kappa-filtered poset. The existing local colimits assemble into a presentation of the original object. These are the defining clauses of kappa-accessibility.

The family of bounded colimits used here includes all existing kappa-small colimits of the chosen generators; it need not consist only of filtered ones. This is harmless: closure of compact objects applies to every existing bounded colimit, and the presentations constructed later use only filtered local colimits. No arbitrary colimit existence is inferred from accessibility.
