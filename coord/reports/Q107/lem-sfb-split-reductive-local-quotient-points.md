## Structural prerequisite and proof plan retained

For lem-sfb-split-reductive-local-gauss-atlas, Conrad's Definition 5.1.1 supplies the distinction between a split maximal torus and a split reductive triple: root and coroot sections must be constant and root lines free. Spec A is connected, so the sections are constant; root weight spaces are rank-one projective A-modules and therefore free over local A. This allows use of the relative theorem, without normality of A. Corollary 5.1.11 supplies integral normalizer representatives for the Weyl group, and Theorem 5.1.13 supplies the product U^- times S times U^+ as an open subscheme and root-product coordinates on U^+ and U^-. Choosing trivializations of the root lines gives the displayed Laurent-polynomial coordinate scheme, with d equal to twice the number of positive roots.

The remaining atlas assertion is a structural consequence, not a quotient-lifting lemma in disguise. Translate that open cell by the finitely many chosen Weyl representatives. On a geometric fibre the resulting charts cover: in the flag quotient these are the opposite big cells at the torus fixed points. One way to verify the cover is that its complement is a torus-stable closed subset of the projective flag variety; if nonempty it has a torus fixed point, whereas every fixed point lies in its own translated cell. Equivalently use the usual pivot/Bruhat chart cover. Fibrewise covering gives covering of H, and translations preserve open immersions.
