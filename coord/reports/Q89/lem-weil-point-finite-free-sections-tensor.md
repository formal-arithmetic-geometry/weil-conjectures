### Original step 2

The canonical residue map $\mathcal O_H\to\varprojlim_n A_{H,n}$ is an isomorphism. Indeed, choose integral representatives $x_n$ of a compatible family. For $m\geq n$, $|x_m-x_n|\leq|\varpi_H|^n$, with $0<|\varpi_H|<1$ by [lem-weil-l-adic-extension-integers-dvr]. By [lem-weil-l-adic-extension-complete], $x_n$ converges in $H$ to some $x$. The nonarchimedean inequality gives $|x|\leq1$ and $|x-x_n|\leq|\varpi_H|^n$; the DVR factorization identifies this last ball with $\varpi_H^n\mathcal O_H$, so $x$ has the prescribed residues. An element in all these balls has absolute value zero, proving injectivity. The map is a ring homomorphism because every residue map is one. Combining with step 1 gives precisely the constant-value isomorphisms in the statement, with their quotient projections.
