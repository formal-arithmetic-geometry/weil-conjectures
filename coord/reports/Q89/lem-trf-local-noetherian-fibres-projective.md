### Original step 1: lem-trf-projectivity-maximal-bad-reduced-quotient

We first record explicitly the base-change calculations used below. For any $R$-algebra $T$, put $S_T=S\otimes_RT$. Images $s_i\otimes1$ of finite algebra generators $s_i$ of $S$ generate $S_T$ over $T$: expand each $s$ as a polynomial in the $s_i$ and balance its coefficients in each pure tensor. If $U\hookrightarrow V$ is an injection of $T$-modules, the maps $U\otimes_T(S\otimes_RT)\simeq U\otimes_RS$, $u\otimes(s\otimes t)\mapsto tu\otimes s$, with inverse $u\otimes s\mapsto u\otimes(s\otimes1)$, and the same maps for $V$, identify its tensor with $S_T$ with tensoring the original injection over $R$ with $S$. Thus $S_T$ is $T$-flat by [def-weil-pbc-flat-ring-map]. These inverse formulas are well defined by the bilinearity relations [def-weil-pbc-module-tensor-product].

### Original step 2: lem-trf-projectivity-maximal-bad-reduced-quotient

Let $\mathfrak p'\subset T$ contract to $\mathfrak p\subset R$. The induced map $R/\mathfrak p\hookrightarrow\kappa(\mathfrak p')$ extends to a field embedding $\kappa(\mathfrak p)\hookrightarrow\kappa(\mathfrak p')$, since every nonzero denominator stays nonzero. Tensor balancing [def-weil-pbc-module-tensor-product] gives $S_T\otimes_T\kappa(\mathfrak p')\simeq(S\otimes_R\kappa(\mathfrak p))\otimes_{\kappa(\mathfrak p)}\kappa(\mathfrak p')$, sending $(s\otimes t)\otimes\lambda$ to $(s\otimes1)\otimes\bar t\lambda$ and inversely $(s\otimes a)\otimes\lambda\mapsto(s\otimes1)\otimes\bar a\lambda$. The same formulas hold after every further field extension of $\kappa(\mathfrak p')$. Hence all fibres of $T\to S_T$ are geometrically integral because every field extension of each original fibre $S\otimes_R\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ is a nonzero domain by hypothesis. In particular the hypotheses persist for $T=R/I$ and for the completion used later.

Cited nodes: def-weil-pbc-module-tensor-product. Source: literature/stacks-project/flat.tex, Tag 05FS, lemma-fibres-irreducible-flat-projective, proof, geometric integrality under base change, expanded.

### Original step 3: lem-trf-projectivity-maximal-bad-reduced-quotient

Assume the set of ideals $I\subseteq R$ for which $S/IS$ is not projective over $R/I$ is nonempty. The ascending chain condition gives a maximal member $I$. It is proper, since $S/RS=0$ is projective over the zero ring. Put $J=\sqrt I$. The ideal $J/I$ is finitely generated and every generator is nilpotent; if their nilpotence exponents are $e_1,\ldots,e_r$, every monomial of degree $1+\sum(e_i-1)$ vanishes, so $J/I$ is nilpotent. If $I\ne J$, maximality gives projectivity of $S/JS$, while Step 1 gives flatness of $S/IS$ over $R/I$. Applying [lem-trf-local-nilpotent-projective-flat-lift] contradicts the choice of $I$. Thus $I$ is radical. Replace $(R,S)$ by $(R/I,S/IS)$, retaining the hypotheses by Steps 1--2. The quotient is Noetherian because generators of the inverse image of an ideal descend to generators. We now have a nonzero reduced Noetherian ring $R$, with $S$ not projective but $S/JS$ projective over $R/J$ for every nonzero ideal $J$.
