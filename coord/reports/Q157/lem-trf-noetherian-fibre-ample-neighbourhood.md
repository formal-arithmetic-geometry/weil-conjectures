## Allocation of the complete returned argument

The following text is copied verbatim from all nine returned proof steps. It is retained here as task guidance only; the three new DAG nodes themselves are deliberately statement-only. No original argument is omitted or replaced by an unexplained shorter assertion.

### Original step 1 → retained parent empty-fibre case; proper-fibre preparation also serves the first new node

If $Z_s$ is empty, its underlying-space identification with $f^{-1}(s)$ in [def-pc-scheme-fibre] gives $s\notin f(Z)$. Properness makes $f(Z)$ closed by [def-weil-proper-morphism]. Choose an affine open neighbourhood $V\subseteq S\setminus f(Z)$ of $s$. Then $f^{-1}(V)=\varnothing$, and the two conditions defining ampleness in [def-weil-pbc-ample-invertible-module] are vacuous (the empty space is quasi-compact). This proves this case. Henceforth suppose $Z_s\ne\varnothing$. The fibre structure map $Z_s\to\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)$ is proper by [lem-weil-pbc-proper-base-change].

Cited statements: def-pc-scheme-fibre, def-weil-proper-morphism, def-weil-pbc-ample-invertible-module, lem-weil-pbc-proper-base-change.

### Original step 2 → lem-trf-fibre-embedding-lifted-coordinates

Apply [lem-trf-ample-fibre-section-lifting] to obtain $d_0$. Choose an integer $N\geq\max(1,d_0)$. By [lem-sc-ample-positive-tensor-power], $L_s^{\otimes N}$ is ample. The proper fibre map is locally of finite type by the definition of properness. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-ample-very-ample-immersion-affine-base] over the field $\kappa(s)$ to this invertible module. It gives $a\geq1$, $r\geq0$, an immersion $i_s:Z_s\to\mathbf P^r_{\kappa(s)}$, and an isomorphism $i_s^*\mathcal O(1)\cong L_s^{\otimes d}$, where $d=Na\geq d_0$. Here the polynomial-Proj model and the projective-bundle model, with their twists, are identified as stated in [lem-trf-projective-bundle-quotient-geometry]. Thus no assertion that every large power embeds is needed from the cited immersion node.

Cited statements: lem-trf-ample-fibre-section-lifting, lem-sc-ample-positive-tensor-power, def-weil-proper-morphism, lem-weil-pbc-ample-very-ample-immersion-affine-base, lem-trf-projective-bundle-quotient-geometry.

### Original step 3 → lem-trf-fibre-embedding-lifted-coordinates

The target projective space is separated over $\kappa(s)$ by [lem-trf-projective-bundle-quotient-geometry]. Consequently [lem-weil-pbc-proper-to-separated] makes $i_s$ proper, and [lem-weil-pbc-proper-immersion-closed] makes it a closed immersion. The quotient-functor description in [lem-trf-projective-bundle-quotient-geometry], for the free sheaf with $r+1$ basis vectors, associates to $i_s$ the surjection $\mathcal O_{Z_s}^{r+1}\twoheadrightarrow L_s^{\otimes d}$. Denote its coordinate sections by $t_0,\ldots,t_r$. They generate $L_s^{\otimes d}$, and their associated projective-space morphism is exactly $i_s$.

Cited statements: lem-trf-projective-bundle-quotient-geometry, lem-weil-pbc-proper-to-separated, lem-weil-pbc-proper-immersion-closed.

### Original step 4 → lem-trf-fibre-embedding-lifted-coordinates

For each $t_j$, apply [lem-trf-ample-fibre-section-lifting] with this same $d\geq d_0$ and choose a lift $u_j$ over an open neighbourhood $U_j$ of $s$. Intersect the finitely many $U_j$ and then choose an affine open neighbourhood $U=\operatorname{Spec}A$ inside their intersection. Restrict all $u_j$ to $Z_U=f^{-1}(U)$. These are sections of $L^{\otimes d}|_{Z_U}$ pulling back to $t_j$. The canonical tensor/pullback identifications, including their action on sections and residue-field values, are [lem-weil-pbc-invertible-pullback-tensor], iterated over the $d$ factors. Properness of $Z_U\to U$ follows from [lem-weil-pbc-proper-base-change].

Cited statements: lem-trf-ample-fibre-section-lifting, lem-weil-pbc-invertible-pullback-tensor, lem-weil-pbc-proper-base-change.

### Original step 5 → lem-trf-fibre-generators-projective-neighbourhood

Put $B=Z_U\setminus\bigcup_j(Z_U)_{u_j}$. The nonvanishing loci are open by [lem-weil-pbc-nonvanishing-section-trivializes], so $B$ is closed. On the fibre the $t_j$ generate, hence at each point some $t_j$ is nonzero by [lem-weil-pbc-invertible-generating-sections-fibre]. The residue-field pullback formula of [lem-weil-pbc-invertible-pullback-tensor] shows that a section $u_j$ vanishes at a point of $f^{-1}(s)$ if and only if its fibre restriction does: the residue-field extension is injective. Thus $B$ misses that whole fibre. As $Z_U\to U$ is proper, its image of $B$ is closed by [def-weil-proper-morphism] and misses $s$. Choose an affine open neighbourhood $U_1$ of $s$ in its complement. Over $Z_{U_1}$ the restricted $u_j$ have no common zero and therefore generate $L^{\otimes d}$, again by [lem-weil-pbc-invertible-generating-sections-fibre].

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-nonvanishing-section-trivializes, lem-weil-pbc-invertible-generating-sections-fibre, lem-weil-pbc-invertible-pullback-tensor, def-weil-proper-morphism.

### Original step 6 → lem-trf-fibre-generators-projective-neighbourhood

The generating tuple yields a surjection $\mathcal O_{Z_{U_1}}^{r+1}\twoheadrightarrow L^{\otimes d}|_{Z_{U_1}}$. Apply [lem-trf-projective-bundle-quotient-geometry] for the free rank-$(r+1)$ sheaf on $U_1$ to obtain $\varphi:Z_{U_1}\to P=\mathbf P^r_{U_1}$ and $\varphi^*\mathcal O_P(1)\cong L^{\otimes d}|_{Z_{U_1}}$. Its naturality under arbitrary pullback, applied to $\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)\to U_1$, identifies its fibre map with $i_s$: the restricted quotient has precisely the sections $t_j$ of Step 3. Properness of $Z_{U_1}\to U_1$ is [lem-weil-pbc-proper-base-change]. Since $P\to U_1$ is separated by the same quotient-geometry node, [lem-weil-pbc-proper-to-separated] makes $\varphi$ proper.

Cited statements: lem-trf-projective-bundle-quotient-geometry, lem-weil-pbc-proper-base-change, lem-weil-pbc-proper-to-separated.

### Original step 7 → lem-trf-projective-map-finite-near-embedded-fibre

Write $q:P\to U_1$ for the projection. For each point $p\in q^{-1}(s)$ the set $\varphi^{-1}(p)$ has at most one point. Indeed, the two fibre projections in [def-pc-scheme-fibre] identify $Z_s$ with the topological fibre of $Z_{U_1}\to U_1$ and $P_s$ with that of $q$; under these identifications $\varphi$ is $i_s$, which is injective on points by the definition of closed immersion [def-weil-closed-immersion]. Apply [lem-trf-proper-finite-fibre-finite-near] to the proper map $\varphi$ at each such $p$. It gives an open neighbourhood $W_p\subseteq P$ on which $\varphi$ is finite. Set $W=\bigcup_{p\in q^{-1}(s)}W_p$. By the finite-morphism clause of [lem-weil-pbc-closed-finite-etale-zariski-local], the restriction $\varphi^{-1}(W)\to W$ is finite. This application allows the empty fibre of $\varphi$ at a point outside the image of $i_s$.

Cited statements: def-pc-scheme-fibre, def-weil-closed-immersion, lem-trf-proper-finite-fibre-finite-near, lem-weil-pbc-closed-finite-etale-zariski-local.

### Original step 8 → lem-trf-projective-map-finite-near-embedded-fibre

The structural map $q:\mathbf P^r_{U_1}\to U_1$ is proper by [lemma-nagata-projective-space-base-change-proper], so $q(P\setminus W)$ is closed by [def-weil-proper-morphism]. Since $W$ contains $q^{-1}(s)$, this closed set misses $s$. Choose an affine open neighbourhood $V$ of $s$ in $U_1\setminus q(P\setminus W)$. Then $P_V=q^{-1}(V)\subseteq W$. The restricted $\varphi_V:Z_V\to P_V$ is finite: cover $P_V$ by its intersections with the $W_p$, restrict the finite morphisms there (finiteness on smaller affine opens is part of [def-weil-finite-integral-morphism]), and use [lem-weil-pbc-closed-finite-etale-zariski-local].

Cited statements: lemma-nagata-projective-space-base-change-proper, def-weil-proper-morphism, def-weil-finite-integral-morphism, lem-weil-pbc-closed-finite-etale-zariski-local.

### Original step 9 → retained parent affine-chart ampleness check

The sections $u_j|_{Z_V}$ still generate $L^{\otimes d}|_{Z_V}$. Their morphism $\varphi_V$ is the one given by sections in [def-weil-pbc-morphism-to-projective-space-by-sections], since both are classified by the same quotient in [lem-trf-projective-bundle-quotient-geometry]. Hence [lem-weil-pbc-projective-morphism-chart-preimage] gives $(Z_V)_{u_j}=\varphi_V^{-1}(D_+(T_j)_V)$. Because $V$ is affine, these standard projective-space charts are affine by [lem-weil-pbc-projective-space-standard-charts]. Because $\varphi_V$ is finite, its inverse images of these affine charts are affine by [def-weil-finite-integral-morphism]. Finally $Z_V\to V$ is proper by [lem-weil-pbc-proper-base-change] and therefore quasi-compact by [pqf-lem-proper-basic]; thus $Z_V$ is quasi-compact. The finitely many sections $u_j$ of the positive power $L^{\otimes d}$ now give an affine nonvanishing cover. This is exactly absolute ampleness of $L|_{Z_V}$ in [def-weil-pbc-ample-invertible-module].

Cited statements: def-weil-pbc-morphism-to-projective-space-by-sections, lem-trf-projective-bundle-quotient-geometry, lem-weil-pbc-projective-morphism-chart-preimage, lem-weil-pbc-projective-space-standard-charts, def-weil-finite-integral-morphism, lem-weil-pbc-proper-base-change, pqf-lem-proper-basic, def-weil-pbc-ample-invertible-module.
