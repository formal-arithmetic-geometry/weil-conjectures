This confirms that irreducible means irreducible among lisse subsheaves, not simple among arbitrary constructible sheaves, and that geometric constancy does not kill the Weil/Frobenius operator. There are no proof steps to audit. Here is the corresponding clause-by-clause statement-dependency audit, with the actual cited statements open:

1. `def-weil-finite-field-fiber` introduces the finite field, algebraic closure and geometric base change. `def-sbc-affine-space` constructs affine space and `def-sc-p1-standard` gives the two coordinate-ratio charts of P^1. These supply the actual spaces and infinity used here. `def-weil-ordinary-compact-cohomology` explicitly constructs cohomology on the geometric fibre; `def-weil-frobenius-detailed` fixes inverse-arithmetic, geometric Frobenius. This is not arithmetic cohomology over F_q.
2. `def-weil-weil-sheaf-complex` clause 3 gives Weil sheaves as heart objects with the Frobenius isomorphism. `def-pod-lisse-l-adic-sheaves` clauses 1–4 give finite-level lisse integral systems, rationalization, and the finite-extension colimit. All schemes here are separated and Noetherian with l invertible, as those definitions require: they are smooth finite-type curves over a finite field. No discrete Qbar sheaf was substituted for the adic convention.
3. `def-weil-punctual-weights` states the displayed eigenvalue equality. I did not take its filtration definition of mixedness as a synonym for the inequality. `def-wf-tate-twist` defines the twist. `lem-tw-tate-twist-weight-shift` states w-2n for arbitrary integer n, so n=-1 gives w+2 exactly, with no sign change of Frobenius.
4. The irreducible-lisse predicate itself introduces its nonzero and lisse-subobject requirements. `prop-wf-weil-sheaf-representation-equivalence` explicitly matches subobjects in both directions. For the general connected normal formulation, `cor-galois-lisse-representations` is the exact fibre equivalence, and `def-wf-fundamental-representation` describes adjoining the Weil operator: a lisse subobject descends precisely when its fibre subspace is stable under that operator as well as geometric monodromy. Thus it is Weil irreducibility, not geometric irreducibility. The nonsimplicity observation is elementary from the explicitly cited counit, extension-by-zero stalk formula and stalk detection of monomorphisms. At each finite coefficient level the counit is the identity off x and 0-to-F_x at x; these are injective. The compatible maps pass to adic systems and rationalization. Positive rank at x makes the inclusion proper, and its unchanged nonzero generic stalk makes it nonzero.
5. `def-wf-middle-extension` supplies ordinary sheaf direct image, deliberately distinguishing it from Rj_*. `lem-wf-boundary-stalk-invariants` states the exact invariants formula and Frobenius compatibility required for the missing-point clause. The new perverse theorem states exactly the characterization used for the j_!* comparison; I did not cite the definition of ordinary direct image as if it proved that comparison.
6. I read Stacks `pione.tex`, Tag 0BTD, label `lemma-normal-pione-quotient-inertia`, including its proof: the absolute generic Galois-to-pi_1 kernel is the closed normal subgroup generated by inertia, equivalently the strict-local generic groups. This is the source of the new general extension criterion, together with the adic fibre equivalence already in the DAG. For a smooth geometrically connected curve the geometric curve is normal integral, and the only non-generic points are closed points. Existing lissity on U kills its inertia; triviality at the remaining points gives precisely the missing condition. A future proof must carry this through integral finite levels and then rational/Weil descent, not suppose a Weil representation has compact arithmetic image. The node states this conclusion with all hypotheses and no proof sketch. Its specialization to C_0=U_0 union infinity has exactly one boundary point, is a smooth open of P^1, and imposes no purity or irreducibility assumption.
7. For the new perverse node I read the convention remark's second paragraph and the current curve-perverse proof's Steps 0–3. The source asserts the shifted ordinary image, not shifted derived Rj_*; the new node gives the precise intermediate-extension meaning rather than defining j_!* to be the desired answer. Its statement uses the support/cosupport convention node, introduces the geometric curve and complement, and requires both absence of boundary subobjects and absence of boundary quotients. No purity, irreducibility or unramifiedness is needed. The identification is on geometric constructible complexes and respects Weil descent, so it does not posit a triangulated derived category of Weil sheaves.
8. The geometric-constancy comparison takes the unit-to-trivial-representation assertion directly from `cor-galois-lisse-representations`, and the geometric/Weil distinction from `def-wf-fundamental-representation`. On the connected geometric open U, testing constancy on every connected open includes testing U itself; restriction of a constant finite-rank object gives the converse. This short argument needs no new node.

I tested the new assertions at j=id (no inertia condition, extension and intermediate extension are the original object), F=0, constant rank-one sheaves, geometrically constant sheaves with arbitrary invertible Frobenius, and rank-two nonsimple local systems. A sheaf with nontrivial inertia is still allowed in the perverse theorem; it is not assumed to be lisse after ordinary direct image. Adding a punctual direct summand to the proposed perverse extension violates the no-boundary-subobject/quotient condition and is excluded. For the warning about arbitrary sheaf simplicity, the zero sheaf and a component of rank zero are not used as the source's nonzero example.
