## Intended proof of the new node

Put R=O_{X,x} and S=O_{Y,y}. These are Noetherian local rings. The ordinary local map R->S is flat because g is flat. The stalk of its fibre at y is S/m_R S, by lem-sbc-scheme-fibre-stalk-quotient; it is a regular local ring because the fibre of g is regular (the identity field extension is among the finite extensions in the hypothesis).

Use the affine strict-local-ring comparison lem-exc-affine-strict-local-ring-comparison (whose statement has no excellence assumption) and the geometric-stalk description to identify A with R^sh and C with S^sh, using the separable residue closures inside Omega. The functoriality node's neighbourhood-compatibility clause identifies the composite R->A->C with R->S->C: choose compatible affine neighbourhoods, use equality there, and extend through the ordinary localization R. This verifies the actual A-action on C, not just existence of an unrelated flat map. The rings A,C are Noetherian by lem-sc-strict-henselization-noetherian and local by strict-henselization functoriality. The map h is already local by that functoriality statement.

The structural S->C is faithfully flat by lem-sc-strict-henselization-faithfully-flat. Its composite with the flat R->S is flat by lem-weil-pbc-flat-ring-map-comp. Thus C, with precisely its restricted canonical R-action, is flat over R. Apply lem-sfb-strict-henselization-module-flatness to the A=R^sh-module C: it is flat over A.

By lem-sc-ring-strict-henselization-local-maximal-ideal, m_A=m_R A. Therefore m_A C=m_R C. The ideal m_R S is contained in m_S because R->S is local. Apply lem-sbc-strict-henselization-surjective-quotient-core to S->S/m_R S, C=S^sh and the same residue closure (this quotient has the residue field of S). It identifies C/m_R C as a strict henselization of S/m_R S with its explicit quotient action. Since that Noetherian local ring is regular, lem-sc-strict-henselization-regular makes C/m_A C regular local. This completes the new node without any excellence or finite-type step.

The substantive input being isolated is the compatibility of the scheme-induced geometric-stalk map with algebraic strict henselizations, together with descent of its canonical flat module action and identification of its closed fibre. It is independent of every log object in the item. The elementary log pushout, ideal extension and dimension rearrangement stay as item steps, not new nodes.
