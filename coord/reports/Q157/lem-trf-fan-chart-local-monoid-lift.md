## New nodes

lem-trf-zariski-log-stalk-unit-extension states that extension from a Zariski log structure to the etale site changes the units in its stalk by a pushout and preserves the sharp characteristic; integrality and a compatible fs-chart face-quotient formula are included. Source: ILO VI 1.1, p.69, VIII section 3 opening convention, p.97, and VIII 3.3.2--3.3.4(ii), pp.106--107. These passages give the associated-log convention and the chart setting, not a separately numbered proof of this refinement; its source and the item proof are marked accordingly. No Stacks tag claims to state this log-geometric comparison.

A proof to retain for the future worker: on a Zariski prelog chart, the associated log stalk is the pushout of its monoid and the local units over elements mapping to units. The map from an ordinary local ring to its strict henselization is local, so it inverts exactly the same chart elements. Associativity of these pushouts gives the displayed extension-of-units formula, and quotienting the two pushouts by their unit groups gives the same sharp monoid. The construction is independent of a chart and commutes with restrictions, so glues. For fs etale charts the characteristic is the quotient of the localization at the unit face; extension by units preserves cancellation, and this gives integrality. This argument explains what must be established in splitting the new node; it is not put into the new node's statement or proof fields.
