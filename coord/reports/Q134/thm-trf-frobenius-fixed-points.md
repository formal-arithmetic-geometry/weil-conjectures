Thus the definition [def-weil-pbc-tor-modules] gives $\operatorname{Tor}^A_i(A/I,A/J)=0$ for $i>0$ and $\operatorname{Tor}^A_0(A/I,A/J)=k$. Its alternating length sum is $1$, since $k=A/\mathfrak m_A$ has only the submodules $0,k$ [def-weil-pbc-module-length]. Together with Step 7 this gives the asserted smooth-locus transversality and local Serre intersection multiplicity. If $d=0$, both parameter lists are empty, $I=J=0$ and $A=k$, and the same degree-zero complex gives the conclusion.
