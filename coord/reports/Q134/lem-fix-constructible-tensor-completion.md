### Returned step 5 — allocated to lem-fix-bounded-projective-tensor-complete

On each chart the coefficient unit $B$ is canonically complete. Indeed its reduction is $(A_n)$ in degree zero, with the usual surjective transitions on sections by the constant-module section theorem. The map $1-s$ on their product is surjective on every section set: given $(y_n)$ choose $x_1$ and recursively lift $x_n-y_n$ to $x_{n+1}$. Its kernel is $\varprojlim_n(A_n)=B$. Exact countable products and the Roos model therefore identify $\eta_B:B\to C(B)$ with an isomorphism. For exactness of the entire completion functor, consider on complexes the strict tower $F(P)_n=P\otimes_B(A_n)$ with its quotient transitions. This additive construction commutes with shifts and mapping cones (all cone sums are finite), and composing its image in the strict-tower derived category with $R\lim$ is exact by the strict-tower theorem. Every complex has a K-flat replacement; a quasi-isomorphism between K-flat complexes induces a quasi-isomorphism at each reduction by derived change of rings, hence in the strict-tower category because its kernels and cohomology are levelwise. The resolving-class theorem therefore gives an exact functor $C$ on $D(B)$, computed by these same K-flat towers. It is additive, so $\eta$ is invertible on every direct summand of a finite sum of $B$ (restrict its inverse along the summand inclusions and projections). It is then invertible on a bounded complex of such terms: start with a single term, and use successive finite brutal-truncation cone triangles and the long exact cohomology sequences to pass from two invertible components of a morphism of triangles to the third.

### Returned step 8 — allocated to lem-pur-integral-ambient-operation-models

Finally identify $C_W(H)$ with the tensor operation in the statement. Choose flat-term K-flat representatives $P\to M$, $Q\to N$; these exist by the resolution theorem. Derived monoidality for the identity of the topos equipped with $B\to(A_n)_W$ gives
$$H\otimes^L_B(A_n)_W\simeq (M\otimes^L_B(A_n)_W)\otimes^L_{(A_n)_W}(N\otimes^L_B(A_n)_W).$$
On the chosen representatives this is the balancing isomorphism
$$((p\otimes q)\otimes a)\longmapsto (p\otimes a)\otimes(q\otimes1).$$
Its inverse multiplies the two scalar factors. These maps preserve degree and commute with the tensor differentials, as all scalars have degree zero. They commute with quotient transitions by the identity $\rho_n(ab)=\rho_n(a)\rho_n(b)$, and send the quotient map to the map displayed in the item. The changed-ring representatives are K-flat, so they compute all finite-level derived tensors. Applying the functorial product mapping fiber identifies $C_W(H)$ with $M\widehat\otimes_R^LN$ and $\eta_H$ with exactly $\chi_{M,N}$, which is therefore invertible by step 7. All balancing and quotient maps commute with maps in $P,Q$; the tensor descent theorem extends this equality uniquely to the derived categories. Together with functoriality of the strict-tower derived limit, this proves naturality in both variables.
