## Extracted arguments retained for the new nodes

For the Roos node the chain-map equation is $b_e(1-s)=(1-s_e)a_e$ by the finite telescoping sum in each block. The map between the two product arrows is degreewise split surjective: extend a retained degree-zero family by zero off the retained indices; in degree one put the required value only at the first index of each block. Its kernel is contractible. With retained $x_{en}=0$, solve $x_i-t_ix_{i+1}=y_i$ backwards from the next retained zero coordinate; the zero block-sum condition on $y$ is exactly the condition at the first retained endpoint. The finite initial block is solved the same way. These are finite recursions, so they commute with the internal complex differential; the kernel differential in the Roos direction is an isomorphism of complexes and its total cone is contractible. Concatenating blocks concatenates the finite sums. Finite free tensor is a finite direct sum after choosing a checking basis and thus commutes with both products and $1-s$; the canonical comparison, its $B$-action and its augmentation normalization are independent of that basis.

For completion coherence, use ordinary flat-term K-flat representatives to identify $(P\otimes_AQ)\otimes_A A_n$ with $(P\otimes_A A_n)\otimes_{A_n}(Q\otimes_A A_n)$, compatibly with quotients and units; the finite tensor is over $A_n$, never over $A$. The completion adjunction fixes the completed counit from the ordinary one. For a complete target, precomposition with the unit is a Hom-bijection, so transposing the completed projection after that unit gives ordinary $(\varepsilon\otimes1)\mu$ followed by the target's completion map. To normalize the tower passage use the exact constant-diagram functor $\Delta$ left adjoint to total limit: on a K-injective strict tower $J$, $\operatorname{Hom}(\Delta P,J)=\operatorname{Hom}(P,\lim J)$, and acyclic $P$ shows that $\lim J$ is K-injective. Termwise inverse image commutes with $\Delta$. The composite adjunction Hom-bijections therefore identify the tower counit with the ordinary integral counit, using the canonical direct-image/limit comparison rather than uniqueness of arbitrary Roos cones. Applying the completion adjunction and the balancing identification gives the stated completed projection and its quotient-tower realization. This argument is deliberately a future unsplit auxiliary; it is not hidden inside the item's shortened proof step.
