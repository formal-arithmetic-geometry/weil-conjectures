Checked before drafting:

- Empty parameter list: `Ideal.ofList []` is zero, extension to parameters still has
  its usual meaning, and the quotient by the empty list is C_m. This is exactly the
  r=0 case; no positive-length guard is used.
- C=0: excluded by `ConnectedSpace (PrimeSpectrum C)` through its nonempty field,
  as required. No separate C-domain or C-nontrivial hypothesis is added.
- C=k×k over B=k: it is an etale regular ring but is disconnected, explaining why
  the connectedness hypothesis cannot be dropped from the domain conclusion.
- Empty fibres: reducedness and regularity of the zero fibre are permitted;
  quantification over its prime localizations is empty. No nontrivial fibre
  hypothesis is added.
- B a field: the only allowable parameter tuple at its prime is empty; the theorem
  specializes to the connected Noetherian ind-etale field-algebra assertion.
- A unit in the parameter tuple: `hjac` is incompatible with a maximal ideal of
  the nonzero C; the impossible case is excluded by the stated hypotheses, not a
  new tuple restriction.
- Arbitrary prime p of B containing J need not be maximal. `hparameters` quantifies
  over all primes exactly as the source argument requires.
