Every step is under 2000 characters.

Step 1 uses `lem-sc-affine-noetherian-underlying-space` exactly for the topological Noetherian property of Spec R. Its quasi-compactness is justified by the finite-union argument in the step. `pqf-def-locnoeth` supplies the definition of locally Noetherian; `lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian` applies because the base is now locally Noetherian and quasi-compact and V is of finite type by hypothesis. `lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-open-subscheme-noetherian` explicitly says every open of a Noetherian scheme is quasi-compact, so it really proves quasi-compactness of the later immersion, including inverse images of all affine charts. No assumption that a quasi-compact source alone makes every immersion quasi-compact is used.

Step 2 uses `lem-chow-segre` for finite products of projective spaces, by induction, with the zero remaining-factor case Y=S treated separately. `def-kun-projective`, `lem-kun-projective-implies-proper` and `def-weil-proper-morphism` give the closed diagonal of Y. The graph is its actual fibre-product pullback, so `lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-base-change` applies; the other closed immersion is the base change of the closed part of j. `lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-comp` combines them. `def-weil-open-immersion` identifies the last base-changed open. These statements supply the properties claimed without using the special affine-cover construction inside `lem-chow-graph`.

Step 3 uses the closed-immersion assertion of `lem-chow-segre` and the explicit multihomogeneous formula of `def-chow-segre`. I checked both equalities of actions on noncommuting h,k by substitution, and the equivariance equation with J_g=j g^{-1}. The final immersion argument uses the closed base-change and composition nodes again: for an open U_X through which J is closed, the complement of the closed image of X minus U_X is open in the ambient projective space and pulls back to U_X. Thus this is genuinely closed into an open, rather than only a pointwise embedding. The finite set E is nonempty even when n=0.

Step 4 uses `lem-chow-closed-model`, whose statement explicitly supplies the scheme-theoretically dense open immersion for a quasi-compact immersed scheme over an affine base. Step 1 checked the stronger condition that the immersion itself is quasi-compact. `lem-weil-pbc-scheme-image-qc-kernel` explicitly identifies its ideal on each affine chart as the kernel of the map to functions on the inverse image. The equivariant square has invertible vertical maps, which proves equality of these ideals under translation. Setwise invariance alone would be insufficient for a nonreduced M. I did not use the inaccurate phrase 'largest closed subscheme' in the old definition-node text as a universal-property theorem.

Pullback replaces F/z_e^d by rho_h(F)/rho_h(z_e)^d; all charts are permuted, so preservation of the ideal is checked on the entire standard cover.
