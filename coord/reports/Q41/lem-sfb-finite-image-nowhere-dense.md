## Supporting lemma argument retained for the next worker

Source: Tag 03HX, final paragraph, beginning with the finite union of closed subsets mapping isomorphically to the base. This is the actual closed-section case of the source proof, after its nontrivial finite-locally-free replacement, and not another statement of the arbitrary finite-morphism theorem. Let W_j cover W as in the new statement, and put A_j=A intersect W_j. If every A_j has empty interior in W_j, its image in X is closed with empty interior because W_j maps isomorphically to X. A finite union of closed sets with empty interior has empty interior: for two such sets B,C, any nonempty open O in B union C has O minus B open and contained in C, hence empty, and then O is contained in B, again impossible. Induction proves the finite-union assertion. The image q(A) is that finite union, hence closed nowhere dense.

Otherwise A_1 contains a nonempty open V of W_1. Process the remaining closed W_j one at a time. If V intersect W_j is proper in V, replace V by V minus W_j, a nonempty open; otherwise V is contained in W_j. Keep the indices of the latter kind in I_1 and the former kind in I_2, starting with 1 in I_1. At every stage V remains nonempty, contained in A, open in W_1, contained in all recorded good W_j and disjoint from all recorded bad W_j. When V is contained in W_j it is also open there: q(V) is open in X by the homeomorphism W_1 to X, and injectivity of W_j to X gives V=(q restricted to W_j)^{-1}(q(V)). Later shrinking preserves this property. At the end put U=q(V). For good j, W_j intersect q^{-1}(U)=V, again by injectivity. For bad j these intersections are disjoint from V. Thus V=q^{-1}(U) minus the union of the bad W_j, which is open in W, since U is open and that finite union is closed. This nonempty open lies in A, contradicting its nowhere density. That rules out the exceptional case and proves the lemma. The proof is retained here; the new node itself has proof [], proof_deps [] and proof_origin null as required for one-level delivery.
