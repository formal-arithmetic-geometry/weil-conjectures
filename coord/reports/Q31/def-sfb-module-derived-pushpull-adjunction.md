## Proof of lem-sfb-abelian-functorial-flat-resolution

For an abelian group M let P(M) be the free abelian group on its underlying set. On h:M→N send [m] to [h(m)]. This preserves identities and composition on generators. Evaluation π_M([m])=m is a homomorphism; both composites in its naturality square agree on every generator. It is surjective, since m is the image of [m], hence epimorphic. For any abelian group A the natural isomorphism P(M)⊗_Z A ≅ ⊕_(m∈M) A sends [m]⊗a to a in the m summand. Its inverse includes that summand by a↦[m]⊗a. Direct sums of short exact sequences of abelian groups are exact: kernels and images are checked coordinatewise, and each finitely supported target element lifts using finitely many choices of preimages. Thus P(M)⊗- is exact; tensor symmetry gives exactness on the other side. All indexing sets stay in the universe of M. This constructs the required functor and natural epimorphism.

This is the coefficient version of the free-cover argument in Part 4 of the current blueprint's `sk-prop-sfb-module-category.proof.md`. I also read the actual project realization: the tensor unit Z is a separator (maps Z→M detect every element), flat because tensoring with it is the identity. The copower-adjunction criterion constructs the same kind of flat cover functor; its counit is epimorphic by the separator criterion, and its values are flat by exactness of coproducts. These two descriptions establish the same existence statement, without requiring equality of chosen flat-cover functors.
