## Source error

The inherited source note already corrects the online text's all-vertices cover condition by requiring the entire spine. This is necessary: for m=3 the union F([0,1]) union F([2,3]) has all four vertices but omits edge [1,2]. Take the discrete outer nerve of the one-object category with endomorphism group C2, a Reedy fibrant Segal simplicial space. Mapping from F(3) gives the discrete set (C2)^3, while mapping from that disjoint union gives (C2)^2, and restriction forgets the middle arrow, so is not a weak equivalence. The item as received excludes this example. No further correction to its statement is needed.
