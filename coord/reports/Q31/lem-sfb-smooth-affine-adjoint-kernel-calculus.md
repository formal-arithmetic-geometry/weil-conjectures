## New-node construction for its future worker

This paragraph is report material, not a proof placed in the new unsplit node. Choose i,p with pi=1 and let e=ip. For every B, the image of e_B in B^n is canonically P_B, since tensoring the split equations preserves them without flatness. A pair U,V with eU=Ue=U and eV=Ve=V restricts to endomorphisms of that image. The equations UV=VU=e make these restrictions mutual inverses. Conversely, an automorphism a and its inverse give U=i_Bap_B and V=i_Ba^{-1}p_B, and the two constructions undo each other. This proves the representing bijection of the displayed finite polynomial quotient. Multiplication is (U,V)(U',V')=(UU',V'V), inversion swaps U,V, and identity is (e,e). These formulas give morphisms because they are polynomial and satisfy the displayed relations. The identity quotient sets U=V=e and is precisely A, with the stated finite list of ideal generators. Extending coefficients replaces e by its image and gives the identical presentation, proving arbitrary base change and the tower formula on entries. Independence of the splitting follows from the representing bijection, not an assumed choice of a global basis for P. The finite presentation is global, so no descent/locality theorem is hidden here.
