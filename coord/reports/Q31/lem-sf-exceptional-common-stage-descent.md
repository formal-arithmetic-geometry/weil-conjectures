Exactness of a functor alone does not ensure that the shift structure chosen on its right adjoint is compatible with the specified adjunction. To see the issue, use the clopen case Z=X, U empty and the identity adjunction on bounded complexes of E-vector spaces. This derived category is split: up to isomorphism every triangle is a finite sum of identity triangles and their rotations. The identity functor can be equipped either with the usual shift comparison or with twice that comparison, still sending these split triangles to distinguished triangles (on a rotated identity triangle the altered connecting isomorphism is absorbed at the third vertex). Counit preservation forces the comparison b to be the identity, which cannot intertwine the two different J shift comparisons. This is a defect only in permitting arbitrary exact shift structures, not in the intended geometric conclusion.

The correction explicitly uses the unique shifts induced by the specified adjunctions and the fixed shifts on their left adjoints, citing lem-weil-pbc-adjunction-shift-compatibility-unique.
