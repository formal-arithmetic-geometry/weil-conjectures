## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: arbitrary K-flat resolutions do not determine a morphism P -> P' of complexes or of the homotopy category. The corrected statement quantifies over any supplied comparison alpha with q' alpha homotopic to q, and independently asserts the canonical derived-category isomorphism for every pair of resolutions. Its identity, cocycle and agreement clauses make the original intended canonical independence precise. This is the intent of [Stacks 06YT](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/06YT) and [06YU](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/06YU), rather than a new hypothesis on the resolution-independence conclusion.

Explicit failure of the proposed lift already for Z-modules: take K_1 = Q[0], P = Q[0], q = identity. Put F_0 = direct sum of copies of Z with basis e_n (n >= 1), mapping e_n to 1/n!, a surjection F_0 -> Q. Let F_1 be its kernel and P' the complex F_1 -> F_0 in degrees -1,0, with augmentation q'. F_1 is torsion-free and thus flat over Z, so P' is bounded above with flat terms and is K-flat; P is flat and hence K-flat. Every map Q -> F_0 is zero: the image of 1 would be divisible by every positive integer, and no nonzero element of this free abelian group has that property. Consequently every chain map P -> P' induces zero in H^0 and cannot lift identity_Q even up to homotopy. This is also a counterexample for the actual pro-etale setup: use constant sheaves on Y = Spec(k) for k algebraically closed. The final object is connected, so sections of each constant sheaf there are its defining module; a map from the constant Q-sheaf to the constant F_0-sheaf is determined by the corresponding map of modules (constant-sheaf/global-sections adjunction). Exact constant sheafification preserves this resolution. Constant flat modules are flat sheaves, since tensoring with a constant localization is the filtered colimit of copies of the identity exact functor. For F_1 one can also use that the kernel is a free abelian group. This yields the same obstruction on the final object. The zero ring, zero complexes and empty scheme present no obstruction and are included.
