Edge cases checked before writing:

- Empty D: hcodim and the first conclusion are vacuous; i.ker is the unit ideal,
  locally generated by 1, so the Cartier conclusion holds.
- Empty T: a map i forces D empty; the conclusion still applies, including an
  empty base B. No point or nonempty chart is assumed globally.
- Identity immersion with nonempty T: stalk dimensions are finite by finite type,
  so hcodim fails; a zero ideal is not inadvertently declared Cartier.
- Hyperplane D in affine n-space over k: the dimension drop is one at all points
  of D, including generic points, and the coordinate is the expected generator.
- A codimension-two coordinate subspace fails hcodim and is excluded.
- B = Spec(k[e]/e^2), T = A^1_B, D the zero section: B is nonreduced and the
  coordinate still is a nonzerodivisor. The statement admits this case.
- Arbitrary characteristic: no characteristic restriction enters the argument.
