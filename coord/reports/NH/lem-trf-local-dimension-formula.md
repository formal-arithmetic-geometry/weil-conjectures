### Former step 1

By [pqf-def-locnoeth], $S$ has an affine open covering by opens whose rings of sections are Noetherian; choose a member $U$ of this covering with $s\in U$, so that $A=\Gamma(U,\mathcal O_S)$ is a Noetherian ring. Choose an affine open $V\subseteq f^{-1}(U)$ containing $x$ (affine opens form a basis of $X$) and put $B=\Gamma(V,\mathcal O_X)$. By [def-pc-integral-scheme], $A$ and $B$ are domains. By [lem-weil-pbc-affine-open-spec-points] let $\mathfrak p,\mathfrak q$ be the primes corresponding to $s,x$, and by [lem-weil-pbc-affine-open-stalk-localization] identify $\mathcal O_{S,s}=A_{\mathfrak p}$, $\mathcal O_{X,x}=B_{\mathfrak q}$, and likewise every stalk at a point of $U$ (resp. $V$) with the localization at its prime. Generic points: $\eta_S$ lies in every nonempty open, in particular in $U$; its prime $P$ has closure $V(P)$ in $\operatorname{Spec}A$ equal to all of $\operatorname{Spec}A$ (the closure of $\eta_S$ in $U$ is $\overline{\{\eta_S\}}\cap U=U$), so $P$ is contained in the prime $(0)$ of the domain $A$, i.e. $P=(0)$. In the same way $\eta_X\in V$ corresponds to $(0)\subset B$. Hence $R(S)=\mathcal O_{S,\eta_S}=A_{(0)}=\operatorname{Frac}(A)$ and $R(X)=\operatorname{Frac}(B)$. Dominance: since $X=\overline{\{\eta_X\}}$ and $f$ is continuous, $f(X)\subseteq\overline{\{f(\eta_X)\}}$, so $f(X)$ dense gives $\overline{\{f(\eta_X)\}}=S$; by the same argument as for $\eta_S$, $f(\eta_X)\in U$ corresponds to the prime $(0)$ of $A$, so $f(\eta_X)=\eta_S$. The stalk map $\mathcal O_{S,\eta_S}\to\mathcal O_{X,\eta_X}$ is a ring map out of the field $\operatorname{Frac}(A)$, hence injective, and its composite with the germ map $A\to\operatorname{Frac}(A)$ equals the composite of the ring map $\varphi:A\to B$ induced by $f$ with $B\to\operatorname{Frac}(B)$ (compatibility of $f^\sharp$ with germs, [def-weil-pbc-module-stalk]); both germ maps are injective, so $\varphi$ is injective and we regard $A\subseteq B$, with $\operatorname{Frac}(A)\subseteq\operatorname{Frac}(B)$ the map $R(S)\to R(X)$ induced by $f$. Finally, $f(V)\subseteq U$ and local finite type give that $B$ is a finite type $A$-algebra by [lem-weil-pbc-lft-affine-pairs], and $\mathfrak q\cap A=\mathfrak p$ because $f(x)=s$ and the point map $V\to U$ is contraction along $\varphi$ (equivalently: $\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak q)$ is the kernel of $A\to B_{\mathfrak q}/\mathfrak qB_{\mathfrak q}=\kappa(x)$, which by the local stalk map $\mathcal O_{S,s}\to\mathcal O_{X,x}$ of [def-weil-residue-field] is $\mathfrak p$).

### Former step 2

Apply [lem-trf-affine-dimension-inequality] to the Noetherian domain $A$, the finite type extension of domains $A\subseteq B$, and the primes $\mathfrak p,\mathfrak q$ (with $\mathfrak q\cap A=\mathfrak p$). By [lem-weil-pbc-localization-dimension-chains-below-prime], the Krull dimensions of $A_{\mathfrak p}=\mathcal O_{S,s}$ and $B_{\mathfrak q}=\mathcal O_{X,x}$ are the heights of $\mathfrak p$ and $\mathfrak q$. Residue fields: $\kappa(s)=A_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak pA_{\mathfrak p}\cong\operatorname{Frac}(A/\mathfrak p)=\kappa(\mathfrak p)$ [def-weil-residue-field], likewise $\kappa(x)\cong\kappa(\mathfrak q)$, and these isomorphisms are compatible with $\kappa(s)\to\kappa(x)$ and $\kappa(\mathfrak p)\to\kappa(\mathfrak q)$, both induced by $\varphi$; so $\operatorname{trdeg}_{\kappa(\mathfrak p)}\kappa(\mathfrak q)=\operatorname{trdeg}_{\kappa(s)}\kappa(x)$ [def-kun-transcendence-degree]. Generic term: every $a/b\in\operatorname{Frac}(A)$ is a root of the nonzero polynomial $bY-a$ over $A$, and likewise for $\operatorname{Frac}(B)$ over $B$, so $\operatorname{trdeg}_A\operatorname{Frac}(A)=\operatorname{trdeg}_B\operatorname{Frac}(B)=0$. The domain tower identity [lem-exc-domain-transcendence-degree-tower] for $A\subseteq B\subseteq\operatorname{Frac}(B)$ and for $A\subseteq\operatorname{Frac}(A)\subseteq\operatorname{Frac}(B)$ gives $\operatorname{trdeg}_A B=\operatorname{trdeg}_A\operatorname{Frac}(B)=\operatorname{trdeg}_{\operatorname{Frac}(A)}\operatorname{Frac}(B)=\operatorname{trdeg}_{R(S)}R(X)$ by Step 1. Substitution into the inequality of [lem-trf-affine-dimension-inequality] yields the displayed inequality.
