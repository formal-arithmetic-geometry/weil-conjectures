## Corrections and route

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported statement did not explicitly say that u and v are morphisms over k. Read literally as arbitrary scheme morphisms it is false: take d=0, k=C and X=Spec C, with u the identity and v induced by complex conjugation. Both are isomorphisms, hence flat and quasi-finite, but the conclusion with r=0 forces u=v. Corrected the wording to “quasi-finite flat k-morphisms”, faithfully making explicit the k-scheme context of SGA XVIII 2.6 and its coordinate-function proof. No geometric hypothesis or intended conclusion changed. Removed the unused proper-morphism statement dependency and added the definition of closed point. The original source-place description referred to “regular systems of parameters”; the argument actually uses systems of parameters in Cohen–Macaulay local rings, which need not be regular local rings. The new criterion defines the former explicitly; no use is made of the DAG definition of a regular system of parameters.

The item's ancestry is a seed-target refinement: `blueprint_refs.py ancestor lem-pur-flat-coordinate-chain` returns None. It serves SGA XVIII 2.9(b)'s independence of chart choice through XVIII 2.6, under the existing sk-thm-purity-transport binding. There is no imported blueprint ancestor to invent. Its existing ancestry and unit are retained. Searches of the whole DAG and blueprint for “parameter”, “system of parameters”, “coordinate”, “flat quasi-finite”, the relevant tags, and the candidate identifier found no duplicate of the germ criterion. The general smooth-base-change chart-existence nodes do not characterize a prescribed tuple and cannot replace it. The generic local algebra tools are explicitly declared in `split_report.tool_uses`; their use does not replace the SGA route.

Two details compressed by SGA are made explicit. The avoidance element is chosen regular on the original ring as well as on both first-parameter quotients, ensuring the middle quotient is Cohen–Macaulay. When a chain is lifted from a quotient, its final coordinates need only agree modulo the fixed entry; separate changes by multiples of that entry correct the exact endpoint without changing the generated ideal. Representatives of equal germs are chosen once and a single finite shrinking realizes every coordinate equality.

## What remains

Only the one-level split was requested. The new germ criterion remains an unsplit statement. Its future proof route is the one stated in SGA XVIII 2.6: the fibre local ring is the quotient by the centered coordinates; its zero dimension is quasi-finiteness at the closed point (Stacks 01TH); the polynomial local ring at the rational image is regular, and miracle flatness (00R4) gives flatness from the dimension equality. Conversely a flat quasi-finite chart has equal source/target local dimensions and zero-dimensional fibre. Openness of the quasi-finite and flat loci (01TI and 00RC) makes the property hold for the actual representatives on a neighbourhood. This argument is deliberately in the report, not inserted as a proof or hidden proof sketch in the new node's statement.

Primes of the quotient rings are pulled back to the original ring; none is maximal because a maximal-associated element would contradict a positive-depth regular element. The repeated quotient lemma, rather than an uncited completion theorem, shows the final quotient is zero-dimensional. Step 6 cites that same quotient lemma to verify all three induction rings are CM of dimension e-1. I used only the depth lower bound actually supplied by the quotient lemma, explicitly allowing truncation; no depth-upper-bound theorem is hidden. Its lifting argument keeps the omitted coordinate in the same position and corrects endpoint differences by its multiples, so it changes one coordinate at a time and preserves the parameter ideal. It does not assume an equivalence of tuples upstairs from equality modulo a parameter.

For Step 7 I read `def-weil-pbc-module-stalk`: it explicitly states that every stalk element has a representative and that equal germs agree after shrinking. The new criterion is applied to each prescribed representative, including the translated original charts, so no unstated stability-of-flatness theorem under coordinate changes is used. `def-weil-flat-morphism` says flatness is stalkwise, and `pqf-def-quasifinite` gives fibre isolation plus finite type. I also opened `lem-weil-pbc-lft-affine-pairs` (Stacks 01T2): applied to the structure map of X and the final affine open U it gives exactly the finite generation of Gamma(U,O) over k, which does not follow merely by choosing U affine. With `def-weil-finite-type-morphism`, its generators then prove the chart is finite type on its single affine source and target. Choosing the final open affine makes the finite-type check explicit; it avoids assuming an arbitrary open of an affine is affine.
