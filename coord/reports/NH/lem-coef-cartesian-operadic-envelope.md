1. **Subset-index fibration.** The cited definitions supply ordinary nerves, coherent simplicial conventions, and the right-outer-horn cartesian predicate. I wrote the subset condition and its inverse-image composition. The missing edge of an outer 2-horn exists by the equality of iterated inverse images; higher missing triangles are unique over the prescribed base maps. Thus the input to relative exponentiation is actually a cartesian fibration, not merely an ordinary category of tuples. The fiber order is reverse inclusion, an orientation used again in step 3.
2. **Actual relative exponential.** The relative-exponential statement explicitly asserts that Q is a quasicategory, q cocartesian, its fiber a functor quasicategory, and its edge criterion tests cartesian indexing edges. I instantiate its small base and total indexing categories in one universe, enlarging the target universe for C if necessary. The retraction over an arrow alpha has the correct crossing-arrow inclusion, proving transport is precomposition with inverse image. These are exactly the hypotheses and conclusion of the cited interface, with the explicit cartesian fibration of step 1.
3. **Product diagrams and tuple fibers.** The finite-poset theorem's right-hand statement computes j_* from A_{>=S}. With reverse inclusion, that index is the finite discrete set of singleton subsets contained in S. Consequently only finite products, not arbitrary finite limits, are required. Its right adjunction and unit/counit give the coherent extension and full faithfulness. I also cite the coherent-functor calculus for functor/full-subcategory closure and componentwise detection of equivalences; the unit comparison is not silently promoted from objectwise equivalences to a functor equivalence. 4. **Transport stability and symmetry.** The disjoint inverse-image calculation is written using mapping spaces, so it proves that the displayed maps are the product cone, including T empty. The cocartesian-straightening statement gives the intrinsic mapping-square criterion, which remains valid in a full subcategory stable under these chosen lifts. The Segal-classification statement supplies precisely the definition of a symmetric monoidal infinity category from such a cocartesian fibration with the inert fiber equivalences. It is not being used to invent coherence for an unconstructed product diagram. The fold and nullary operations have the stated Cartesian values.
5. **Full mapping spaces.** The cocartesian square, pulled back over the identity in the target base fiber, compares maps over alpha with maps out of its transported source. The same cited statement identifies simplicial and homotopy fibers. The finite-poset right-Kan adjunction then computes maps into the product diagram from its singleton restriction. Testing the target fiber on z=a and z=g yields the inverse of that vertical map when the arrow is cocartesian. Conversely an equivalence after a cocartesian lift preserves the square. Singleton restriction detects this equivalence by the cited right-Kan adjunction and unit/counit, and inverses in a finite product are coordinatewise. A functor carries the two inverse triangles to inverse triangles, so it preserves the component equivalences. Parameters and composition are supplied by the actual postcomposition operation on simplices.
