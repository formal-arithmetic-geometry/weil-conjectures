## Findings and corrections

The imported statement has been retained, including all-continuous coefficients, arbitrary totally disconnected normed fields, ordinary unbounded derived categories, constructible-only conservativity, and absence of condensed-flatness or completeness assumptions. Its empty proof has been replaced. The source place now explains the extraction instead of referring to an absent report as its argument.

The crucial conservativity proof concerns morphisms between finite local models. I did not assume that taking a cone preserves these models for arbitrary normed fields. On a common stratum cover the morphism is a matrix of continuous functions; every pointwise matrix is invertible, so its determinant has a continuous inverse. Open–closed localization then detects its kernel and cokernel, which need not have been proved constructible. This avoids any appeal to geometric-point conservativity for arbitrary pro-étale sheaves.

For the unbounded scalar comparison I resolve the input over F_Z. I do not replace internal scalar extension by an exact functor: F'_Z need not be flat over F_Z. After applying the exact fibre functor, abstract F'/F extension is exact, and this suffices. The balanced map and insertion-of-one unit are specified on that representative.

The totally disconnected realization node applies to arbitrary Hausdorff totally disconnected spaces, is natural in continuous maps and preserves finite products; these clauses are exactly what transports the ring operations. A metric normed field meets its hypotheses. Evaluating on the one-point spectrum gives F, through the constant map, not a discrete replacement of F.
2. The henselian definition gives strict henselianity of the field using I=0, without any extension theorem. The w-local hierarchy then makes its spectrum w-contractible. The sections-exact node permits arbitrary ring sheaves and modules; the module-pullback-exact node permits every condensed ring. 3. The point-sheafification node applies to arbitrary concrete coefficients with the listed sheafification hypotheses. The underlying-set sheafification node supplies those hypotheses for abelian groups. This is enough for the underlying abelian presheaf of a variable-ring tensor. The ringed-topoi tensor-pullback node states the natural tensor isomorphism for arbitrary ring sheaves, not just constants. Associativity and symmetry follow on the generating pure tensors; they are not taken from an uncited derived monoidality theorem.
4. The exact-functor argument uses the localization and exact-functor clauses of the derived-category node. Kernels and quotients give the cohomology identity directly, so no boundedness or enhanced-category assumption enters.
5. The finite-model node gives pro-étale covers with finitely presented scalar modules.
