### STATEMENT-DISPUTED: explicit typing of levels and schemes

The imported text says only $m\le n$ although its coefficient categories are defined only at positive indices: $m=0$ asks for the undefined stage $D^b_{ctf}(T,A_{E,0})$, rather than one of the finite coefficient stages in the source. It also leaves the schemes' scope inside “purity conventions”, while the actual exceptional functor definition explicitly requires finite-type schemes over the base field (and says every use in the blueprint has that scope). A smooth separated morphism by itself does not impose this: an infinite disjoint union of copies of $\operatorname{Spec}k$ mapping to $\operatorname{Spec}k$ is smooth separated of dimension zero and not finite type. The statement now spells out $1\le m\le n$ and separated finite-type $k$-schemes, and “pure relative dimension” as used by the diagonal theorem. This repairs the domain of the imported intended statement, rather than claiming a six-functor theorem outside its defined categories. All conclusions are unchanged.

### Source error

The original item blueprint's first proof step infers equality of arbitrary derived morphisms from fibrewise agreement using an isomorphism-conservativity lemma. This implication is false: on $\mathbf G_{m,k}$ with $k$ algebraically closed and $\ell\ne\operatorname{char}k$, the nonzero Kummer class of $t$ gives a nonzero morphism $\mathbf F_\ell\to\mathbf F_\ell[1]$ (after choosing a primitive root of unity), while every geometric-point pullback vanishes since vector spaces have no positive Ext. This source error is repaired below the unit level, following the unit's prescribed three-constituent/diagonal route; the chapter and unit are untouched.
