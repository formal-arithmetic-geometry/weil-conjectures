## Mathematical basis of the new comparison

The new node is an agent-formulated corollary, not a purported quotation of a numbered result in Hovey. I checked its content as follows. Factor a Kan fibration `p` by Hovey Theorem 3.5.9 as a boundary-lifting retraction followed by a minimal fibration. By Corollary 3.5.7, the latter is trivial over each simplex. After pullback over the specified simplex, project the given continuous filler and the prescribed simplicial boundary through the retraction. Local triviality reduces them to a continuous disk filling of a simplicial sphere in a Kan fiber. Hovey's horn contraction (Lemma 3.4.6) reduces that boundary to a based sphere representative; Proposition 3.6.3 detects its zero class by realization. Lemma 3.4.5, followed by the boundary-prism horn extension used in its proof, gives a simplicial extension with the original boundary, not merely a homotopic boundary. Finally lift this simplex through the boundary-lifting retraction with the original boundary in the total space prescribed. For dimension one the fiber issue is connectivity of the two endpoints; for dimension zero it is existence of a fiber vertex. This is a relative finite-simplex input, not the general Kan-target mapping-class comparison whose dependency graph is circular here.
