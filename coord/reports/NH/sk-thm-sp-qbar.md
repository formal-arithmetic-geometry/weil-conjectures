## What was wrong in the imported item

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the unqualified phrase “smooth of pure relative dimension d” omits the standing separated finite-type geometry and the invertibility of ell, and the literal equation `f_Qlb^* = f^{-1}` does not type as a definition on the common-stage category used in the proof.

An explicit counterexample to interpreting the geometry without the standing conventions is the affine line with doubled origin over the standing field. Its structure morphism is smooth of pure dimension one (both affine charts are affine lines), but is not separated: the overlap is the punctured affine line and the diagonal has a missing limit pair at the two origins. It cannot be an open subscheme of a proper scheme over that field, since such an open subscheme is separated. Thus a compactification as written in the unqualified imported statement cannot be chosen. The chapter's first sentence expressly says “smooth separated”; the coefficient setting says morphisms are separated of finite type and ell differs from the characteristic. The revised statement makes these existing conventions explicit; it does not impose an ambient exceptional-adjoint hypothesis or require a purity isomorphism as input.

For the typing check, an object of `def-weil-algebraically-closed-coefficients` is a pair `(E,K)` with K an object of the rationalization of the integral derived-complete category, and its arrows are common-stage classes. The ordinary inverse-image functor on sheaves accepts sheaves (or complexes), not these pairs and classes. `def-weil-derived-pullback-tensor-hom` actually defines the operation by completed integral pullback, raw rationalization, and the common-stage construction. The revised statement specifies that typed functor. The compact-support formula is likewise interpreted at finite torsion levels and then reconstructed and transported, as its existing definition requires. There is no identification with discrete non-torsion sheaves or with condensed Qlb-module objects.

The imported proof also used non-elementary facts without recording them: rational adjunction, both coefficient comparisons, their unit/counit compatibility, and compactification coherence. Three of its named sketch dependencies (`sk-prop-sp-rational`, `sk-prop-sp-unique-adjoint`, `sk-prop-sf-comp-coherence`) have no node file in the current shared DAG. I did not create aliases for these sketches or move the conclusion into a new lemma. Step 1 proves the needed rational adjunction by localizing the existing integral adjunction, following the rational blueprint proof. The proof fixes a compactification and establishes the adjunction for that choice, so a separate compactification-independence theorem is not a premise. The existing field-extension lemma supplies exactly the comparisons needed thereafter.

The original citation `sk-thm-sfb-qlbar-two-colimit` also asserts an identification with bounded constructible `D_cons` categories. I do not use that additional identification: the precise Hom formula and finite-stage representatives are already the definition of the common-stage category and the statement of its calculus theorem. This retains the same finite-extension-colimit route and avoids silently equating coefficient categories.
