I read the item's source Category.lean:134-218 together with the actual coefficient-module definition in Module.lean:50-79. Step 1 gives the two inverse section-action constructions explicitly. The ringed-site Grothendieck node says its conclusion for an arbitrary ring sheaf on a small model, and the pro-etale-site node supplies that model in a compatible coefficient universe. No topological Noetherian hypothesis is needed for this step.

The native framework supplies the scheme Frobenius automorphism; the new pullback node supplies exactness and the inverse-image comparison for its inverse, so this really is an autoequivalence of the same B-module category. Its extra derived-resolution clauses are unused. Step 3 uses only rank-zero local models and composition of trivializations with an isomorphism, as specified by the framework's constructibility sentence.

For Step 4 I opened all four cited statements: lft base change supplies precisely the geometric-fibre morphism, pqf-lem-locnoeth-lft applies because Spec(k) is locally Noetherian, the affine-open lemma gives a Noetherian coordinate ring, and the minimal-closed-subset lemma applies to the affine open as a Noetherian scheme.
