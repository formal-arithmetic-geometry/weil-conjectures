Products and subspaces are Hausdorff by separating unequal coordinates in the Hausdorff factors. The equation is closed since E is a Hausdorff topological field in its valuation topology. Projection is a continuous surjection: the norm inequality gives a quotient in O when b is nonzero, and forces a=0 when b=0. Condition (3) of `det-pe-stonean-projective` then gives a section because P is compact Hausdorff and extremally disconnected. Its second coordinate is the required continuous bounded quotient. For b identically zero, a is zero and the solution fibre is all of O; for nonzero b the fibre is its unique quotient. The elementary closed-equation and coordinate arguments are written out, so the step contains no uncited extension theorem.
