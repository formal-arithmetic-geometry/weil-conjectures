The important correction made while developing the proof is the overlap localization: Rprime tensor_R K is not generally the fraction field Kprime. For example a field R can sit locally inside a nontrivial valuation ring Rprime, and then tensoring with K=R changes nothing. I do not identify these rings. Instead the proof injects Rprime tensor_R Rprime into Kprime tensor_R Kprime using two distinct applications of flat tensor preservation. Since nonzero elements of R are inverted on both sides, the latter tensor product equals Kprime tensor_K Kprime. Equality there extends across the closed equalizer by injectivity. Thus the argument does not assume Rprime tensor_R K is a field, nor assume that the generic point is open, nor apply a properness criterion requiring quasi-compactness.

I also tightened step 4 during self-review: it now cites the exact affine flatness equivalence, and checks the covering definition using a finite principal-open cover of each affine open and the existing affine quasi-compactness leaf, rather than leaving the singleton fpqc assertion implicit.
