The source explicitly labels the further comparison as agent reconstruction. Here is the chart verification I checked. On the projective chart x_i nonzero, normalize a line vector so its i-th coordinate is 1 and write any nonzero lift as t times that vector. Localizing the homogeneous cone equations and dehomogenizing them cuts out exactly Y in that chart, also with its nilpotents. Thus the punctured cone is locally Y times Spec k[t,t^{-1}]. On overlap, t_j=(x_j/x_i)t_i; these are the tautological O(-1) transition functions. For homogeneous f of degree d, put a_i=f/x_i^d. Over Y_f in this chart, a_i is a unit and the slice equation is t^d=c/a_i. Its ring is O_Y[t]/(t^d-c/a_i), and t is automatically a unit because c/a_i is. The monic equation gives the free basis 1,t,...,t^{d-1}; positive rank makes the map surjective, and the overlap formulas glue it. This proves the stated finite locally free rank d assertion even when the derivative vanishes. It also checks naturality under the linear automorphisms used in the item. This is a reusable cone/line-bundle comparison, with no group, reductivity or stability hypothesis.

For lem-sfb-zero-dimensional-stabilizer-finite-orbit I read Milne Section 2.14 (printed p.44) and Propositions 7.4 and 7.11--7.15 (printed pp.139--143), Stacks varieties.tex Tag 06LH and descent.tex Tag 02VO. The orbit geometry already present in the DAG supplies the reduced locally closed orbit and faithful flatness. The stabilizer is a finite-type closed subscheme of affine G. Zero dimension gives finiteness by 06LH. Conversely finiteness gives finite k-points, and finite k-points force dimension zero by the Jacobson/Nullstellensatz property for finite-type schemes over algebraically closed k. For the additional assertion not contained in those dimension lemmas, the quotient identity sends (g,h) to (g,gh); its inverse sends (g1,g2) to (g1,g1^{-1}g2). The orbit map pulled back along itself is therefore the projection G times H to G. A finite k-scheme is finite locally free over the field, so this projection is finite locally free. Faithfully flat descent (02VO; the finite-type morphisms here are finitely presented over the noetherian field) gives the asserted finite locally free orbit map. It is surjective by orbit geometry. This reasoning permits nonreduced H and nonreduced ambient X. Unlike a zero-dimensional-scheme finiteness lemma it proves finiteness of a map onto an orbit which can have positive dimension.
