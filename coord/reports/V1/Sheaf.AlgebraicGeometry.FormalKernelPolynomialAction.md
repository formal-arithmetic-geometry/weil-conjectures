J^k/J^(k+1), compatible with restriction and the graded algebra operations, locally
spanning any finite family of sections. This is strictly the algebra-cover part of the

unique lifting, gluing, homogeneous monomial expansion, and all edge cases. No change
record or forced adaptation is needed.
