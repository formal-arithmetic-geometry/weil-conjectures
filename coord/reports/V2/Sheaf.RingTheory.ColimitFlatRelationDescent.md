## Where the brief, the DAG or earlier work was wrong

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: `HasColimit A` in `CommRingCat.{v}` is not enough to identify a
colimit indexed in an independent larger universe with the algebraic directed limit.
The original plan applies ring representative/equality descent without the necessary
forgetful-functor preservation hypothesis. Mathlib's ring forgetful preservation instance
is size-restricted. Its additive analogue can be made universe-independent by
`AddCommGrpCat.uliftFunctor`, which preserves all colimits. There is no corresponding
ring argument from that additive result.

Here is a mathematical counterexample in the standard Grothendieck-universe interpretation
of the independent Lean universes. Let U be the universe containing the stage rings,
and let κ be its inaccessible cardinal, so every U-small set has cardinality below κ.
Take a larger ordered abelian group Γ, the direct sum of κ copies of Z, ordered
lexicographically with the smallest nonzero coordinate most significant. Every interval
(0,γ), γ>0, has cardinality κ: if β is the leading coordinate of γ, all the positive
basis elements indexed above β lie in that interval. Let k=F₂ and let V be the valuation
ring of the Hahn series field k((Γ)); its residue field is k and its maximal ideal is m.
The construction takes place in the larger indexing universe.

For every ideal I properly below m, choose a positive-valuation element b in m\I and
write γ=v(b)>0. The κ distinct monomials t^δ with 0<δ<γ remain distinct modulo I:
the valuation of each monomial and of the difference of two distinct monomials is below
γ, so membership in I would force b into I by the valuation-ring divisibility relation.
Thus |V/I|≥κ. Any ring map V→S into a U-small ring has kernel either m or V; otherwise
V/I would inject into the U-small S. Consequently every such map factors uniquely
through the residue map V→k.

Index by the finite subsets of V. Let A_i be the k-subring generated by the finite
subset, represented in U (it is countable), with inclusion transition maps. Their
union is V. A cocone into any U-small ring is exactly a ring map from V, so the residue
maps exhibit k as the categorical colimit of A in the U-small ring category.
Set B and N to the constant diagrams k and the additive group of k, respectively;
set φ_i to the residue map, ψ=id, and use the usual scalar actions. All stated
compatibilities hold and N_infty=k is flat over A_infty=k.

At a stage containing a=t^γ for γ>0, use the one-term relation a·1=0 after applying φ_i.
Every later A_j is a domain and the image of a is nonzero. Any column equation a*c=0
therefore forces c=0. Every asserted row expansion is then zero, while the image of
x=1 in the constant N diagram is still one. This contradicts the original conclusion.
The elementary domain/row obstruction was also checked in Lean in the sandbox
scratchpad `DomainObstruction.lean`; the universe/Hahn construction is a mathematical
counterexample, not a fully formalized Lean refutation.

The source's usual algebraic directed-colimit proof is sound; the fault is the stronger
independent-universe categorical interface. The finite-relation theorem is used directly
by `Sheaf.RingTheory.IdealKernelElementDescent`; that module has the same independent
universe problem and should be reviewed before the original hole is treated as provable.
No unheld downstream module was edited or had its signature changed.

The Stacks source checkout is absent on this machine. The actual source was read at
https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00HK, https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00R6,
https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00D6, and https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00D7.
The theorem preserves the source's finite-data route. Empty row/column families are
handled by finite upper bounds including the original stage and by empty sums; zero
rings and zero modules require no extra nontriviality assumption.
