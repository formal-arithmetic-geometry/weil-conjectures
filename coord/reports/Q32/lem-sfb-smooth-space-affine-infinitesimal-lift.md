## Source error

The final paragraph of spaces-more-morphisms.tex, Tag 04AM, says that Omega_{X/S} has finite presentation from local finite presentation of f:X→Y. This inference is false for general S. For example take S=Spec(k), X=Y=Spec(k[x_1,x_2,...]) and f the identity, which is smooth; Omega_{X/S} is an infinite-rank free module and is not finitely presented. The translation module in that very paragraph correctly uses Omega_{X/Y}, which is zero in this example. The proof here consistently uses Omega_{X/Y}, as Tag 05ZF actually states. This corrects the source proof typo, not the item statement or its hypotheses.
