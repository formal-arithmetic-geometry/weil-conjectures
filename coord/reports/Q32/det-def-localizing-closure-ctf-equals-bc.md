### lem-sfb-scalar-model-finite-flat-resolution

Source read: Local models, the finite free resolution, flat scalar extension and derived tensor calculation.

Proof in words (1,106 characters):

Choose a finite surjection $R^n\twoheadrightarrow P$. In the DVR case [lem-sc-dvr-saturation-line-smith-normal-form] gives a finite basis of its kernel, so $0\to R^m\to R^n\to P\to0$ is exact. Passing to constant sheaves preserves this sequence by [lem-weil-pbc-site-pullback-abelian-exact]. Flatness of $B$ means precisely that tensoring it with this sequence remains exact [def-pc-flat-sheaf-modules], giving the asserted augmentation. Each finite free $B$-module is flat because its tensor functor is a finite direct sum of the identity, hence exact. Thus the two-term complex is K-flat by [lem-pc-kflat-closure]. The one-factor computation stated in [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-projection-mate-exact] identifies its derived tensor with $N$ with $[N^m\to N^n]$. In the field case take a minimal subset of a finite spanning set of $P$. A dependence with a nonzero coefficient solves for one of those vectors, contradicting minimality; this gives a finite basis. Extend its isomorphism $P\cong R^r$ to $B$ to obtain $B^r$ and then $N^r$. This includes $P=0$ and zero ranks; it makes no claim that $B$ is a field.

### lem-sfb-tor-interval-local-detection

Source read: Locality and Strata, exact restriction and open--closed detection of tensor cohomology. Supplementary source read: Stacks proetale.tex, Tag 09AH, label lemma-ses-associated-to-open; its quasi-compact open hypothesis is retained.

Proof in words (1,345 characters):

For the cover, [lem-weil-proetale-weakly-etale-localization] identifies its members' sites with slices. Restriction to a slice is exact [lem-pur-site-restriction-module-adjunction-exact] and commutes with derived tensor [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-projection-restriction], the latter applied with $f=1_X$. Fix an $A$-module $N$ and $q\notin[c,d]$. The cohomology sheaf of $K\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}N$ restricts to zero on every $V_i$. It is zero: for every object $W\to X$, the objects $W\times_XV_i$ cover $W$ and the sheaf uniqueness axiom makes a section zero if its restrictions are zero. For the open--closed pair, inverse image on module sheaves with inverse-image coefficient rings is exact by [lem-weil-pbc-site-pullback-abelian-exact]; hence it commutes with cohomology and is its own derived functor. By [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-derived-tensor-pullback], both inverse images of $K\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}N$ are the tensors of the restricted factors. Their $q$th cohomology is zero by the assumed common interval. Apply the short exact sequence in [lem-weil-pbc-proetale-ringed-open-closed] to $H^q(K\otimes_A^{\mathbf L}N)$. Its two outer terms vanish, so its middle term vanishes. This holds for every $N$ and $q$ outside the interval, proving both assertions. The quasi-compactness of $j$ is exactly the hypothesis of that short exact sequence.

### lem-sfb-stratified-tor-interval-devissage

Source read: Strata, with the corrected local affine induction retained from the returned head. The local-closed-stratum and pullback nodes were opened and their complete statements checked, not just their proof sketches.

Proof in words (1,601 characters):

Induct simultaneously for all qcqs schemes and all modules with at most $n$ strata. For $n=0$ the scheme is empty and the assertion holds. For $n>0$, let $x\in T$ lie in stratum $S$. Its scheme immersion factors as a closed immersion into an open $O\subseteq T$. The restricted partition on $O$ remains constructible by [lem-weil-pbc-constructible-open-restriction]. Apply [lem-sf-constructible-partition-local-closed-stratum] on $O$ to obtain an affine open $W\ni x$ inside $O$ such that $S\cap W$ is closed and $U=W\setminus(S\cap W)$ is quasi-compact. Give $Z=S\times_TW$ its actual stratum scheme structure. It is closed in the affine $W$, hence affine; $U$ is qcqs. Apply [lem-sfb-pullback-constant-model-constructible] to $Z\to T$ and $U\to T$, whose sources and target are qcqs. It gives the base-changed strata, covers, and the same scalar modules. Since a stratum immersion is a monomorphism, its self-base-change on $Z$ is $Z$; its intersections with the other strata are empty. Thus $M|_Z$ has a finite constant local model and has the required interval by hypothesis. On $U$ the selected stratum is empty, leaving at most $n-1$ strata, so induction applies. By [sk-prop-sfb-cartesian-coefficients], the pulled-back coefficient rings are the coefficient rings on $Z,U$, and module pullback is exact. Apply (ii) of [lem-sfb-tor-interval-local-detection] on $W$, then (i) on the open cover by all these $W$. This proves the assertion on $T$ and closes the induction. The case of one stratum uses the same argument, so an immersion with full image is never silently replaced by an isomorphism.

### lem-sfb-cohomology-tor-interval

Source read: Complexes, generalized from [-1,0] to a common arbitrary cohomology Tor interval [c,d].

Proof in words (991 characters):

Induct on $b-a$. If $a=b$, [lem-sf-derived-concentrated-single] identifies $K$ with $H^a(K)[-a]$; the shift rule of [det-def-sfb-constructible-tor-amplitude] gives $[a+c,a+d]$. For $a<b$, choose the triangle $K'\to K\to K''\to K'[1]$ of [lem-sf-derived-truncation-triangle] at $b$. Its truncation vanishings and [lem-sf-derived-homology-long-exact] give $H^q(K')=H^q(K)$ for $q<b$, and $H^q(K'')=H^q(K)$ for $q\geq b$: in these ranges the two adjacent terms of the long exact sequence are zero. Thus $K'$ is supported in $[a,b-1]$, while $K''\cong H^b(K)[-b]$ by [lem-sf-derived-concentrated-single]. Induction bounds $K'$ by $[a+c,b-1+d]$ and the shift rule bounds $K''$ by $[b+c,b+d]$. Both intervals lie in $[a+c,b+d]$ because $a\leq b$ and $c\leq d$. Enlarging a Tor interval only weakens the vanishing requirement. The extension rule in [det-def-sfb-constructible-tor-amplitude] now bounds $K$. This also covers zero $K$ and vanishing endpoint cohomology; no nonzero endpoint is needed.

### lem-sfb-square-zero-periodic-tensor

Source read: The examples, the displayed multiplication-by-ell periodic resolution, generalized to an element with square zero and annihilator equal to its principal ideal.

Proof in words (1,213 characters):

The kernel of multiplication by $t$ is $tR$ by assumption, and its image is $tR$ by definition; the kernel of $R\to F$ is also $tR$. This proves exactness at every term, while $t^2=0$ gives the complex equations. Put its free terms in all nonpositive cochain degrees. This is bounded above and flat, hence K-flat by [lem-pc-kflat-closure], applied to the point site in the scalar case. Tensoring with $F$ makes every differential zero because $tF=0$, so its cohomology is $F$ in every nonpositive degree. This computes derived tensor by the one-factor rule in [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-projection-mate-exact]. For any scheme $V$, pass the augmented resolution to constant sheaves; [lem-weil-pbc-site-pullback-abelian-exact] preserves its infinite exactness. Its terms $R_V$ are flat, so the same K-flat computation gives term $F_V$ and zero differential in every nonpositive degree. Finally, on a nonempty scheme a nonzero constant section cannot become zero after sheafification: that would require a covering on whose every member two distinct constants coincide, whereas some member of every cover of a nonempty object is nonempty. Thus $F_V\ne0$. For $V$ empty all these sheaves are zero, as the statement permits.
