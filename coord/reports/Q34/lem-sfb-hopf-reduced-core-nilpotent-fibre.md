  augmentation ideal and the fibre augmentation ideal are zero, exponent one works.
  Over an algebraically closed field the positive Frobenius power image is all of `k`.

  its augmentation ideal leaves `k`, and the induced counit's kernel is zero.

  for `r ≥ max(1,e)` the power image is the scalar subalgebra, while the quotient
  fibre retains a nilpotent augmentation ideal. The statement does not demand
  that this fibre or `A` itself be reduced.
- Additive and multiplicative groups in characteristic `p`: the image is the
  subalgebra generated by `t^(p^r)` (and its inverse in the multiplicative case),
