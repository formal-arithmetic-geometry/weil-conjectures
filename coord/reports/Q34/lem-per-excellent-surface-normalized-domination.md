## Source error

Stacks resolve.tex Tag 0BBT (resolve-lemma-dominate-by-normalized-blowing-up) is correct as a domination statement, but its proof needs these clarifications. Its second paragraph says codimension zero and one “in Y” where the open is an open of X: the cited 0BFP statement is about points of X. In the fourth paragraph the chosen u belongs to O_{Y,eta_C}, not the printed O_{X,C}; C is a subset of Y contracted to x, and has no generic-point local ring on X.

More seriously, the full fibre product Y ×_X X′ need not be integral, and its entire normalization need not be birational to Y. Take X=A²_k and Y=X′=Bl_0(A²_k). On two x-charts, the fibre product has equation x(t−s)=0: its dominating component is t=s, while x=0 is a vertical component. Globally the latter is E×_k E, with E=P¹_k. The normalization of the whole product therefore cannot be the birational normal surface asserted in the proof. The final split explicitly takes the reduced closure W of the common generic point and then normalizes W. The graph lemma supplies its proper birational projections and the injection on exceptional curves. This repairs the proof without changing the item statement or adding any hypothesis.
