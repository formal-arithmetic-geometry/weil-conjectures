## Maintainer review fix (2026-10-19)

The composition (consecutive-segment) clause pinned the initial comparison
`q.forward 0` only on its underlying scheme map. A log automorphism over the identity
(for instance `m ↦ m·φ(m̄)` on a log point) acts trivially on characteristics and
passes every `Comparison` field, so the clause did not fix the canonical
identification. The clause now also asserts `∃ g, g.IsIdentity ∧ HEq (q.forward 0) g`,
i.e. the initial component is the identity log morphism on sections. `lean.statement`
updated verbatim; per-file compile clean (3.0 s).
