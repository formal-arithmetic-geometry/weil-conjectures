First extend the scheme-target open construction to arbitrary space targets by an etale scheme cover of the target. The smooth-atlas construction in 06M3 gives the stack locus and its compatibility with representable stack-base change. I checked the delivery-errors B–E checklist with the sources and cited statements open. For step 1, lem-sfb-stack-diagonal-space-source-representability explicitly covers every algebraic-space source into a fibred groupoid with representable diagonal, without assuming a special atlas. def-sfb-algebraic-moduli-stack supplies exactly that diagonal. For step 2, the new node's conditions are checked individually. lem-sfb-representable-lqf-calculus-descent explicitly gives arbitrary base change and descent under a representable surjective flat locally finitely presented cover; this is exactly the single-cover target-local condition in the new node. It also gives open-source locality. Etale precomposition and detection are supplied by lem-a1-scheme-lqf-etale-source-locality, with both directions stated and no quasi-compactness restriction. lem-trf-space-fibre-products-etale-covers supplies the scheme intersections of representable etale charts and the base-changed covers used to apply those scheme assertions. lem-sfb-space-quasifinite-locus-open explicitly constructs the open Q for an arbitrary algebraic-space source over a scheme and explicitly states its equality under arbitrary scheme base change. Its chart definition makes its restriction locally quasi-finite; largestness uses only affine witnesses from pqf-def-quasifinite and the fact that an open in an open is open. Thus I use statements, not any hidden construction in another node's proof. Applying the generic node to h and a is legitimate by step 1, and yields the specific equality W_g = p^{-1}W_h.

For step 3, I read spaces-morphisms.tex Tag 04NV and morphisms.tex Tag 0397. The existing lem-trf-locally-quasi-finite-relative-dimension-zero states the scheme relative-dimension-zero criterion globally, so I do not falsely use it as a pointwise result: I first take an open V of dimension zero around a lift u, using the actual minimum formula in def-kun-topological-dimension-at-point. Every point v of V has dimension zero because V itself is an open neighbourhood and is zero-dimensional. Over Spec k the scheme fibre is V. The cited criterion therefore proves the entire V -> Spec k is locally quasi-finite. pqf-def-quasifinite then supplies its affine witness at u, and that same affine open is open in U. This works for every chart and every lift and hence gives precisely the all-chart definition of Q in lem-sfb-space-quasifinite-locus-open. No equality of stalk-ring dimension with neighbourhood dimension is used: I specifically rejected lem-sfb-space-local-dimension-chart-invariance for this purpose because it concerns stalk-ring dimension.

Step 4 uses only inverse-image membership and the defining property of the chosen open W_h from step 2. It is elementary, with no omitted geometry. Every used node has a verbatim quotation in citations.json. Generic tools outside this unit are declared in split_report.tool_uses; no unit step is replaced. No SGA source is used.

Small cases checked: the empty fibre supplies no z, so the hypothesis is vacuous rather than asserting an impossible neighbourhood. For a finite nonreduced k-algebra such as k[epsilon]/(epsilon^2), its spectrum has dimension zero and the argument correctly gives the whole finite locus without requiring reducedness or etaleness. For A^1 over k its closed and generic points both have neighbourhood dimension one, so the premise fails; using stalk dimension instead would wrongly include the generic point, which the proof avoids. A disjoint union of a finite k-scheme and A^1 isolates the zero-dimensional component. Infinitely many zero-dimensional components are allowed since neither the dimension-zero neighbourhood argument nor canonical-open descent requires quasi-compactness. For the new lemma, P = all maps gives the entire source and P = empty-source maps gives the maximal source-open with empty image, stable under the specified tests; arbitrary field-fibre criteria are not smuggled into its hypotheses. Inseparable field extensions cause no problem because the canonical-locus equality is for arbitrary base change, not just etale or flat maps.
