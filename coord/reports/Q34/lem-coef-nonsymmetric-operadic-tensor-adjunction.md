## Proof routes for the new unsplit nodes (not proofs installed in their JSON)

For projective cofibrancy: use the common base generators from finite-pattern-universe-comparison and the free-diagram formula in simplicial-model-coherent-diagrams. At j the generator is I(i,j) tensor a -> I(i,j) tensor b, using exactly the same smaller simplicial sets in both universes. Coproducts, pushouts and continuous transfinite sequences in the smaller cell presentation are the same marked sets on enlargement; for diagram categories they are computed objectwise. Therefore each cell and the entire composite are larger projective cofibrations. Retract equations remain the same equations. A trivial fibration between smaller base objects is characterized by lifting against the common generating cofibrations, so it remains a trivial fibration against the larger generating set too. This checks the replacement-arrow clause, not merely weak equivalence of its components. Fibrant smaller objects stay fibrant by finite-pattern-universe-comparison, giving the last clause.

For the enriched colimit: construct L by the enriched coequalizer of the two maps from the coproduct over i,j of I(i,j)^sharp times F_i to the coproduct over i of F_i. The maps are induced respectively by the action into F_j and by projection to F_i. Underlying simplicial sets are formed degreewise as quotient sets; the marking is the image of the markings of the summands, together with the degenerate edges. Every set in this construction is smaller. The map to the fixed base descends since each action is over that base. A map from this quotient to an arbitrary larger marked object is exactly a family of maps of the summands equalizing the action/projection pair and preserving their marked edges. Apply the same description with an extra (Delta^n)^sharp factor. Product with that factor preserves the coproduct and quotient construction, so the description gives the full enriched adjunction degree by degree and is compatible with simplex operators. Thus it applies to all enriched transformation simplices, not only ordinary maps. For empty I the construction is the initial empty object over the base. This argument contains no fibrant replacement or operadic colimit assertion.

For universe naturality: categories of components and mapping simplicial sets of the smaller index do not change under inclusion, so neither local Kan fibrancy nor its Dwyer–Kan comparison changes. The formula for Map^sharp over the base gives the same smaller mapping sets and compositions on all smaller objects, and finite-pattern-universe-comparison preserves fibrant objects and weak equivalences. Consequently the full simplicial inclusion of bifibrant objects induces j on coherent nerves. The rectification comparison is induced by enriched evaluation and restriction along u; those operations commute with inclusion, including the simplices representing enriched transformations. For non-bifibrant F first localize the objectwise weak equivalences in its resolution. The localization equivalence on entire functor infinity categories respects this commutative evaluation square, so the comparison is also compatible with the resulting coherent equivalences. Constant diagrams commute with inclusion. Applying the same square to the arrow t:F -> Delta A identifies the cocone as a transformation, including its coherences; the common weak-equivalence replacement of A does not change this identification. This proves naturality without assuming any cocone already colimiting. The item separately invokes the colimit comparison theorem.
