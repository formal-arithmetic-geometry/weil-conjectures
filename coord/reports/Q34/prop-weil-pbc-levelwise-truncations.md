Nor does vanishing of higher Tor by itself say that a pre-existing such arrow is an isomorphism. I replaced condition (1) by (TC), the explicit higher sheaf-Tor vanishing that the source immediately says it intends. This is sufficient for the truncation comparison; no unsupported equivalence or necessity assertion remains. The ambient upper torsion-free sufficient condition is retained and proved using actual quotient resolutions.
- The displayed split description of K_n for K=(A/varpi)[-(m+1)] is not an R_n-linear splitting. On a point, for n=2 the reduction is [A/varpi^2 --varpi--> A/varpi^2]. It has two residue-field cohomology modules, but is not their split sum in D(A/varpi^2): the two-term free complex is perfect whereas either residue-field summand has infinite projective dimension. The corrected statement asserts only those cohomology sheaves. That is all the counterexample needs. Its lower truncation really is a single residue-field object, and the explicit periodic resolution computes nonzero Tor in every degree.

The source's assertion of termwise support in [m+1,d_1] is false.
