## Proofs in words for the new nodes

### lem-pe-finite-coefficient-model-comparison

Use [lem-sf-module-object-ordinary-coefficient-equivalence] for the ring identification, section-action equivalence and finite constant models; it is exact, so it preserves cohomology. Since X is Noetherian it is qcqs and [sk-def-sfb-constructible] uses a global finite constructible stratification, just as [def-weil-proetale-constructible-sheaf] does. Over a finite ring A, a finitely presented module P is finite: it is a quotient of A^d. Conversely a finite module P is generated by its finite underlying set, so A^d surjects onto P, and its kernel is a subset of the finite set A^d and is therefore finitely generated. This supplies a finite presentation. Once the coefficient ring is constant, A_U tensor_A P is the constant module sheaf P_U: the tensor-unit isomorphism sends a tensor p to ap and has inverse p to 1 tensor p; sheafify these inverse maps of constant presheaves [def-weil-proetale-locally-constant-sheaf, def-weil-pbc-constant-sheaf]. On a nonempty trivializing object, a constant model has stalk P: a germ is locally a constant label, and two distinct labels cannot agree on a pointed neighbourhood. Thus the finite-stalk condition is exactly finiteness of the local models; empty objects contribute no condition and are discarded. The same strata and covers give both implications. Finally apply the exact coefficient equivalence degreewise: boundedness and every specified cohomological vanishing interval are unchanged.

### lem-pe-successive-quotient-linear-triangle

For n>1 form 0 -> R_(n-1) -> R_n -> R_1 -> 0, with first arrow [b] to [tb]. The first map is well-defined since t times t^(n-1)B is t^nB. If tb=t^nc locally, cancellation of t gives b=t^(n-1)c. The final kernel consists locally exactly of classes divisible by t. Quotient sheaf sections admit representatives locally, proving exactness. All maps are R_n-linear, with quotient actions on the outer terms [def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category]. Choose a K-flat resolution P of a complex representing K, with flat B-module terms [lem-pc-kflat-resolution]. The exact-functor definition of flatness [def-pc-flat-sheaf-modules] gives a degreewise short exact sequence of tensor complexes P tensor_B R_(n-1), P tensor_B R_n, P tensor_B R_1. Their differentials and maps are R_n-linear because R_n acts on the second factor. Exactness is detected on their underlying abelian sheaves by the kernel/cokernel description of module sheaves. Now [lem-weil-pbc-complex-ses-triangle] applied in Mod(R_n) gives the distinguished triangle. By [lem-pur-derived-change-of-rings], each tensor complex computes K_j in D(R_j); regarding that very complex as an R_n-complex computes its termwise restriction. This identifies all three terms and both displayed arrows without changing coefficient category or assuming K is bounded.

### lem-pe-finite-scalar-restriction-constructible

By [def-pc-sheaf-restrict-scalars], restriction changes only the action. Sectionwise kernels and sheafified cokernels have the same underlying abelian sheaves [def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category]; the induced scalar actions are precisely the restricted actions. Thus restriction is exact. Its termwise functor preserves quasi-isomorphisms and H^q is computed by the same kernel modulo image, giving the cohomology comparison [def-weil-abstract-derived-category]. A sheaf is zero exactly when its underlying abelian sheaf is zero, proving reflection as well as preservation of vanishing. For constructibility, retain the strata and covers in [def-weil-proetale-constructible-sheaf]. Pullback to a stratum commutes with scalar restriction: the inverse-image action is obtained by applying inverse image to the original action and precomposing with a, so both orders give the same action [def-weil-pbc-proetale-operations]. A constant A'-model M becomes the constant A-model obtained by the action a(x)m: this identity holds on constant presheaves, then on their sheafifications [def-weil-proetale-locally-constant-sheaf]. Its finite underlying set is unchanged, so it is a finite A-module. Hence the same local models witness constructibility after restriction. Apply this to every cohomology sheaf for bounded constructibility and retain any given vanishing interval.

### lem-pe-integral-completion-presentation

The canonical ring isomorphism of [det-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-x-integral-limit] is the one induced by the quotient projections; its n-th component is B -> R_n. Identify B with the limit by this map. A K-flat representative P [lem-pc-kflat-resolution] therefore computes both reductions by the identical complex P tensor_B R_n [lem-pur-derived-change-of-rings, def-weil-integral-derived-reduction]. If one keeps the ring isomorphism explicit, reindex the action by its inverse; this is an exact equivalence with inverse reindexing by the isomorphism, and the tensor balancing relations identify the two tensor complexes. This also preserves K-flatness, since tensoring and acyclic complexes correspond under the equivalence. On P, the quotient units and transitions are the same maps, so all reduction identifications preserve the augmentation. Under canonical completeness [sk-def-pe-derived-completion], let phi:K -> L be the canonical isomorphism to the strict-tower derived limit. Write its Roos triangle as L -> product Res K_n -> product Res K_n -> L[1] [def-weil-derived-limit]. The first map composed with phi is c=(c_n), the augmentation. Replace the last arrow delta_L by phi[1] inverse composed with delta_L; (phi,id,id) is an isomorphism from the resulting triangle on K to the original Roos triangle. It is distinguished by invariance under triangle isomorphism [def-weil-triangulated-category]. The first map is the specified c, giving exactly [def-weil-derived-complete] in that definition's global ell != p setting. This uses no arbitrary choice of a map into a nonfunctorial homotopy limit.
