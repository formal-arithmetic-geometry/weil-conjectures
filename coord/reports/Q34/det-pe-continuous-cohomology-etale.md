## Review by Q34

I checked the item against Definition 5.6.1 / Proposition 5.6.2 of Bhatt–Scholze and the source tags above. X is arbitrary; there is no qcqs, Noetherian, torsion or finite-ring assumption to add. Every F_n is a sheaf in degree zero, so the bounded-below comparison applies. The transition hypothesis means epimorphism of sheaves, not sectionwise surjectivity. Integer coefficients represent all abelian sheaves and do not change their category. There are no new-node statements to audit.

Step 1 uses the full levelwise abelian/evaluation statement of `lem-weil-tower-category-levelwise-abelian`, the actual module-sheaf definition, and the small-ringed-site bounded-below resolution theorem `lem-sc-bounded-below-injective-resolution`. The fixed-index covers of C times N give exactly one sheaf condition at each level; the off-index arrows give exactly the transitions. Thus this is genuinely a small site in the enlarged universe, and the resolution theorem applies. The unique integer action is obtained locally by repeated addition and gluing. I made the consequence “enough injectives” explicit: split finitely many negative contractible pairs, using the injectivity of their lowest term, so the original degree-zero object embeds into the remaining injective term. The same argument applies to the punctual site. This is not a separate unproved general diagram-category theorem.

Step 2 uses the sections/constant-sheaf adjunction stated by `def-weil-pbc-global-sections-functor`, the sheafified-constant-presheaf definition, and both clauses of `lem-weil-pbc-sheafification-exact`. That last node really asserts sectionwise limits as well as exact sheafification. Componentwise short exactness comes from the tower-category node. `lem-sp-right-adjoint-preserves-injectives` then applies because the displayed left adjoint is exact. This establishes the essential hypothesis for computing the composite on the same resolution; a mere spectral-sequence statement is not substituted for an isomorphism of derived objects.

Step 3 uses tower levelwise exactness, the same right-adjoint injectivity statement, and the natural transformation and injective-computation clauses of `lem-weil-pbc-right-derived-plus-injective`. I verified the prefix adjunction component by component, including the arrow from level n+1 to n, where the source is zero. Therefore the evaluated complexes really are injective resolutions, and their transitions really represent the original derived section maps. Step 4 uses that right-derived computation again on the bounded-below complex T(I), whose terms are injective by step 2. Both derived expressions are the identical termwise limit of that complex. `lem-weil-pbc-ringed-strict-tower-homotopy-limit` explicitly covers arbitrary small ringed sites and supplies the functor and Roos triangle on the punctual site with integer coefficients. It has no product-exactness or adic-coefficient restriction.

Step 5 uses the actual comparison geometric morphism statement and the arbitrary-ring theorem `lem-pe-constant-left-module-inverse-image`. The latter states exactness, agreement after forgetting scalars, and identification of the unit. Thus its specialization to integers is legitimate, unlike applying a finite-coefficient lemma. Exactness sends the zero cokernel of each transition to zero.

Step 6 uses bounded-below resolutions on the pro-etale tower site, the same evaluation adjunction/injective lemma from step 3, the natural right-derived comparison, and `sk-prop-sf-comparison`.
