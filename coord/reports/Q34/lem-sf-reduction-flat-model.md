## New node and the extracted argument

`lem-sf-ringed-kflat-single-tensor-comparison` is sourced to Stacks sites-cohomology.tex, Tag 06YU, `definition-derived-tor` and its immediately preceding construction, read together with the signed total-complex convention already defined in `def-pc-tensor-complex-sheaves`. The source constructs derived tensor from K-flat resolutions. With P K-flat, choose a K-flat resolution C -> M[0]. Tensoring this augmentation with P is a quasi-isomorphism: its cone is the signed total tensor of P with the acyclic cone, hence acyclic by K-flatness. The flat-resolution tensor model on both resolved factors therefore compares canonically to Tot(P tensor M[0]). In total degree q only the summand with second degree zero remains, so this last complex is canonically T_M(P), with differential d_P tensor 1; the second-factor differential is zero. Comparison through common refinements gives resolution independence, and the chain-level maps on the unique surviving summands are u^q tensor f, giving the stated naturality square. This construction, not a cohomological bound or a coefficient-reduction clause, is the separate work item. Its node intentionally has no proof or proof dependencies: this argument is a handoff in the report, for the worker who next splits it.
