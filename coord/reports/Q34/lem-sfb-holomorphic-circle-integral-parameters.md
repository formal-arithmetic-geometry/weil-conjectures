## Argument retained for the new unsplit lemma

This is a plan for the next worker, not a proof inserted into its statement. Choose a slightly larger closed parameter polydisc inside P (possible since Q_s is compact and P open). Cover the compact set K times that larger closed polydisc by finitely many product polydiscs on which the joint power series for g converges on a still larger product polydisc. Shrink each chart before taking this finite cover. Its local series show continuity and parameter holomorphy; the finite cover gives finite uniform bounds and permits continuity arguments under the parameter contour integrals. Define a_alpha(zeta) by the iterated parameter-circle Cauchy coefficient integrals on |eta_j-w0_j|=s. This definition uses the parameter circles, not the original zeta circle, and contains no pole z or H. Repeated one-variable Cauchy formulas identify the expansion with g; continuity on the compact product makes a_alpha continuous on K. Bound each coefficient integral using M=max_{K times Q_s}|g|, obtaining |a_alpha| <= M s^(-|alpha|). For rho<s, the sum of the supremum majorants is M times the product of d scalar geometric sums, namely M(1-rho/s)^(-d). This proves normal uniform convergence and provides the quantitative statement queued for further splitting. In dimension zero this is just the continuous function g on K. The finite cover is of a product containing K times the closed parameter polydisc, rather than an unjustified choice of one local series for the entire zeta circle.
