### Returned Step 1 → lem-trf-polynomial-simplicial-tensor-model

Write $C=s(P_\bullet)$ and $q:C\to B[0]$ for the given augmentation. By [lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence], $C^{-n}=P_n$, the differential is the alternating sum of faces, positive terms are zero, and $q$ is a quasi-isomorphism. Choose polynomial coordinates $P_n=A[X_i:i\in I_n]$. The definition [def-weil-pbc-mv-polynomial-ring] gives unique finite coefficient expansions $\sum_\alpha a_\alpha X^\alpha$. The tensor universal property [def-weil-pbc-module-tensor-product] gives inverse maps $P_n\otimes_A M\leftrightarrows\bigoplus_\alpha M$: $p\otimes m\mapsto(a_\alpha m)_\alpha$ and $(m_\alpha)_\alpha\mapsto\sum_\alpha X^\alpha\otimes m_\alpha$. Their composites are identities by coefficient expansion and balancing. These maps commute with maps of $M$. Thus tensoring with $P_n$ preserves exact sequences coordinatewise: a family in the kernel has a preimage chosen in only its finitely many nonzero coordinates. Hence every term of $C$ is flat, for arbitrary sets $I_n$.

Inputs: lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence, def-weil-pbc-mv-polynomial-ring, def-weil-pbc-module-tensor-product. Source: literature/stacks-project/cotangent.tex, Tag 08QP (lemma-flat-base-change), the free-module resolution s(P); Tag 08QI (remark-resolution), associated-complex convention.

### Returned Step 2 → lem-trf-flat-resolution-canonical-tensor-comparison

The complex $C$ is bounded above by degree zero and termwise flat. The ordinary-module theorem [lem-pc-module-bounded-flat-kflat] therefore makes $C$ K-flat. Since $q:C\to B[0]$ is a quasi-isomorphism, it is a K-flat resolution in the sense of [def-pc-module-kflat-complex].

Inputs: lem-pc-module-bounded-flat-kflat, def-pc-module-kflat-complex. Source: literature/stacks-project/cotangent.tex, Tag 08QP, computation using the free resolution; more-algebra.tex Tag 064K (lemma-derived-tor-quasi-isomorphism), bounded-above flat criterion.

### Returned Step 3 → lem-trf-polynomial-simplicial-tensor-model

Set $P'_\bullet=P_\bullet\otimes_A A'$ and $\epsilon'_n=\epsilon_n\otimes1$. The base change of the multiplication supplied by [def-weil-module-base-change] is $(p\otimes a')(r\otimes b')=pr\otimes a'b'$. Hence tensoring algebra maps gives algebra maps, and its identity and composition formulas preserve all simplicial and augmentation identities. The switch $p\otimes a'\mapsto a'\otimes p$ is well-defined by balancing, is its own inverse, and preserves this multiplication. Composing it with [lem-weil-pbc-polynomial-scalar-extension] yields an $A'$-algebra isomorphism $P'_n\cong A'[X_i:i\in I_n]$ sending $X^\alpha\otimes a'$ to $a'X^\alpha$. Finally any $\sum_j b_j\otimes a'_j\in B'$ lifts to $\sum_j p_j\otimes a'_j\in P'_n$ by choosing $\epsilon_n(p_j)=b_j$. Thus the tensor augmentation is degreewise surjective.

Inputs: def-weil-module-base-change, def-weil-pbc-module-tensor-product, lem-weil-pbc-polynomial-scalar-extension. Source: literature/stacks-project/cotangent.tex, Tag 08QP, base-changed polynomial algebras; Tag 08QI, polynomial and surjective conditions.

### Returned Step 4 → lem-trf-polynomial-simplicial-tensor-model

Degree $-n$ of both $C\otimes_A A'$ and $s(P'_\bullet)$ is $P_n\otimes_A A'$. Additivity of scalar extension on maps [def-weil-module-base-change] identifies their differentials: both send $p\otimes a'$ to $\sum_i(-1)^i d_i(p)\otimes a'$. Their augmentations both send $p\otimes a'$ to $\epsilon_0(p)\otimes a'$ in degree zero and are zero in negative degrees. Thus the degreewise identities give a chain isomorphism preserving the actual augmentation $q\otimes1$.

Inputs: def-weil-module-base-change. Source: literature/stacks-project/cotangent.tex, Tag 08QP, identity s(P) tensor A-prime = s(P tensor A-prime), including its augmentation.

### Returned Step 6 → lem-trf-flat-resolution-canonical-tensor-comparison

The ordinary-module counit theorem [lem-pc-kflat-scalar-extension-counit] makes $\gamma_C$ invertible. Naturality of $\gamma$ as explicitly supplied by [lem-pur-left-derived-from-kflat-class], applied to the homotopy image of $q$, gives
$$\gamma_{B[0]}\circ L(Q_Aq)=Q_{A'}(q\otimes1)\circ\gamma_C.$$
Since $q$ is a quasi-isomorphism, $Q_Aq$ and its image under $L$ are isomorphisms [def-weil-abstract-derived-category]. Thus $j=L(Q_Aq)\circ\gamma_C^{-1}:Q_{A'}(C\otimes_A A')\xrightarrow{\sim}L(B[0])$ is the resolution computation isomorphism, and the displayed equation says exactly $\gamma_{B[0]}\circ j=Q_{A'}(q\otimes1)$. By the hypothesis and this equation every cohomology map of $q\otimes1$ is an isomorphism. Step 4 gives this quasi-isomorphism for the specified augmentation of $s(P'_\bullet)$. Together with Step 3, this is precisely the polynomial-resolution criterion of [lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence].
