## Source and statement corrections

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: “an exact bifunctor” is not typed as a functor of Verdier triangulated categories with the current definition of `def-wf-weil-category`. That landed definition explicitly supplies a category of fixed pairs, with underlying commuting morphisms, and explicitly supplies no triangulated structure. The statement is now precise about what survives: it preserves equivariant triangle diagrams with distinguished underlying triangles, separately in each variable, with the canonical Hom shifts. No additional geometric or coefficient hypotheses are imposed. The underlying object, specified conjugation, morphism action, inverse variance, and separate triangle preservation are all retained.

Here is an explicit test showing why one cannot silently declare all underlying distinguished diagrams to be a Verdier triangulation. At X_0 = Spec F_q, consider degree-zero constant rational objects with Frobenius. Set V = E with identity Frobenius, and W = E e_1 + E e_2 with J(e_1)=e_1 and J(e_2)=e_2+e_1. The inclusion i:V -> W, i(1)=e_1, and quotient p:W -> V, p(e_2)=1, are equivariant. The triangle V -> W -> V -> V[1], with final map zero, is split and distinguished after forgetting Frobenius. Were every such diagram distinguished in a triangulation of the fixed pairs, its zero connecting map would force an equivariant section of p. But any lift of 1 is e_2+a e_1 and J(e_2+a e_1)-(e_2+a e_1)=e_1, so no such section exists. The corrected wording asserts the diagram-preservation property, rather than this false triangulation claim. This also explains the source's use of “exact” in the fixed-pair setting without silently changing its morphism groups to a derived equivariant category.

The imported proof also claimed the internal-Hom pullback comparison from exactness alone. An exact functor need not preserve internal Hom. Here the reason is stronger and present in the current definition: geometric Frobenius is a **scheme automorphism**, giving a ringed-topos equivalence with the specified completed-coefficient identification. Its comparison acts by transport of local maps and preserves K-injectives. The proof now uses that comparison, not an inference from mere exactness.

The old proof omitted constructible closure, both-variable morphism equivariance, and the exactness argument. These are now explicit. The tensor-Weil lemma is not needed: the sole Frobenius isomorphism defines a fixed pair, and the cyclic cocycle follows by grouping its iterated factors. I removed it from `proof_deps`, retaining all imported metadata and `blueprint_*` fields.
