## Why the new node is true and is a separate lemma

This is a coproduct theorem for arbitrary families in a derived category. It contains neither a tensor-variable projection map nor a constant module M, so does not restate the item or one of its clauses. The arbitrary-coefficient, derived-annihilator passage is not the one-line specialization of 0F11: objects annihilated by n need not be given as complexes of Lambda/n modules. It needs a genuine unbounded coefficient comparison and a derived scalar-reduction/retract argument, so is left as a separate statement-only node.

Here is the checked proof route for its future worker, kept out of the statement-only JSON. First apply the unbounded forgetful comparison of 0D6J (also the statement of `lem-kun-unbounded-abelian-exchange-comparison`, applied to f as an edge) to forget Lambda to abelian sheaves. Underlying abelian sheaves preserve all colimits by `lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-colimits-forgetful`; termwise coproducts compute derived coproducts by `lem-kun-grothendieck-derived-coproducts`, so this forgetting preserves the coproducts in question and is conservative. It suffices to prove the comparison for underlying abelian derived objects A_i with n id=0.

For each A_i form derived extension of scalars B_i = A_i tensor^L_Z Z/n, as an object of D(X_et,Z/n). The length-one free resolution [Z --n--> Z] of Z/n identifies its underlying abelian object with Cone(n:A_i -> A_i). Since n id_A_i=0, the corresponding distinguished triangle splits, making A_i a retract of Res(B_i), with no assertion that A_i itself lifts to Z/n coefficients. The natural coproduct comparison for the family (A_i) is a retract of the comparison for (Res B_i), by applying the source and target functors to these individual retractions and summing their maps. Tag 0F11(1) proves the comparison for the B_i in Z/n coefficients. Tag 0D6J(3) and the same termwise coproduct observation identify its forgotten comparison with that for the Res B_i, including the maps on each coprojection. Naturality of the comparison and conservation on forgetting finish the argument. This proof also covers arbitrary unbounded B_i; it does not use a nonconvergent hypercohomology spectral sequence. All indexing sets lie in the fixed coefficient-module universe.
