## Argument behind the new structural obligations

For the arithmetic cover, L19 Proposition 1.1.9, first bullet, p. 3, explicitly uses countably many finite subextensions. The existing source proof in NumberTheory/Padics/Extension.lean:76–134 explains the arithmetic: approximate a separable minimal polynomial over Q_l by one over Q, use a nearby algebraic-number root and Krasner's lemma, and obtain x in Q_l(b) for b algebraic over Q. Such b form a countable set. Enumerate these simple subfields, take successive finite composita with E, and obtain an increasing cover. Each term is closed as a finite-dimensional subspace over complete Q_l. This is not the countability of Omega as a set (Omega is uncountable). The node remains statement-only as required. Stacks Tag 09EI, more-algebra.tex lemma-krasner, was also read; it is a root-perturbation statement, not itself the desired countable-cover theorem, so I do not mislabel the cover with that tag.

For rectangular detection, the useful comparison is a partial resolution with n+1 colors, one free H-torsor H a_i of vertices for each color i. In degree k its chains are the finite sum over increasing (k+1)-element color sets of free K-modules on their tuples of vertices. Boundary deletes one coordinate with the usual sign. This is the augmented chain complex of the join of these n+1 nonempty sets; its augmentation is exact through degree n-1. To see that the exactness can be used continuously, choose the vertex a_i in each torsor: splitting the augmentation of K[H a_i] by that vertex decomposes its two-term augmented complex into a kernel plus a contractible summand. Tensor the n+1 two-term complexes. The resulting contractions below the top degree are finite insertions, deletions and differences of generators, with continuous dependence on all vertex parameters.

The homogeneous H-bar complex has free diagonal H-orbits with a continuous parametrization by (h_0, h_0^-1 h_1, ..., h_0^-1 h_k). Lift its augmentation recursively into the colored join up to degree n using those explicit contractions on the h_0=1 generators, then extend H-equivariantly. These are finite sums whose coefficients depend continuously on the parameters; one never averages over H. Map the join into the homogeneous G-bar complex by forgetting colors and keeping the increasing color order. This is a chain map. Compare its composite with the inclusion of the H-bar complex: the usual insertion-of-1 contraction in the augmented G-bar complex, applied on h_0=1 and then extended H-equivariantly, gives a homotopy through degree n. Pair with f. The join top-degree pairing is identically zero by the rectangular hypothesis, and pairing f with the degree-(n-1) homotopy gives t, with delta t = f restricted to H. Every expression is finite and continuous, for arbitrary topological coefficient spaces; no completed free modules or closure of boundaries enters. This explains why the structural node is smaller than the item: it involves no algebraic closure, finite extension, Baire argument, or retraction. Its proof is left to its later worker, not smuggled into its statement.
