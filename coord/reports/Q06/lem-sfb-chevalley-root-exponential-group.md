## Source error

Milne 23.72, printed pp.510-511, first describes T(r) as acting by scalars on weight spaces and then chooses the full basis-diagonal torus in its compressed proof. Arbitrary independent scalar actions are generally larger than the torus defined by the weight characters. For two copies of the standard SL2 representation, the correct weight torus is diag(t,t^-1,t,t^-1), of rank one. The full basis-diagonal torus has rank four. In characteristic zero, that full torus conjugates E12+E34 into independent E12 and E34 directions, so the group generated by it and the paired root maps has two SL2 directions, not the semisimple rank-one subgroup required for the single simple root. Thus one cannot simply apply 23.31 to that full torus as written. This does not refute the corrected item. Our separate open-cell assertion concerns the correct weight torus and removes this faulty inference.

The final sentence of Milne 23.72 also asserts an integral Lie-form identification without the needed qualification for its arbitrary-weight version. For the adjoint A1 representation in characteristic 2, the generated group is PGL2. Its Lie algebra pgl2 has derived subalgebra spanned by the two off-diagonal classes, of dimension 2: the class of diag(1,0) brackets with each off-diagonal class as the identity, and their mutual bracket is scalar and hence zero. The Chevalley lattice sl2(Z) reduces to sl2, whose derived subalgebra is instead the one-dimensional scalar line (h is central and [e,f]=h). Thus these Lie algebras are not isomorphic. The full-weight item already excludes the extra assertion; nothing here uses it. Every positive-characteristic assertion uses torus characters rather than their differentials, integral divided-power matrices rather than division by factorials in F, and subgroup schemes rather than point groups.

## Intended arguments for the unsplit prerequisites

These are planning notes, not proofs hidden in the new-node statements. The rank-one lemma is local to each rational sl2 string. On an admissible lattice, upper and lower divided-power matrices and the integral weight characters give the SL2 action by its Gauss charts. Their coordinate identities are verified in the rational representation and then in torsion-free integral coordinate rings, and survive reduction. Closed immersion of each root map must be proved scheme-theoretically, using the full-weight hypothesis to rule out disappearance of the root differential and torus equivariance to rule out a nontrivial kernel. The rank-one image together with T is the corresponding central-torus extension of an SL2 image; it has semisimple rank one. Weyl representatives conjugate the root matrices with the Chevalley signs. Root-height descent sends every root to a simple root, producing the finite words. The integral compatibility and the geometry of that image are genuine future work, which is why this is not an elementary proof step.

The open-cell prerequisite is a separate construction theorem: use the integral rank-one Gauss identities and ordered root commutator collection to obtain unique negative coordinates, the actual weight torus, and positive coordinates on the identity chart. Uniqueness and the inverse chart are required as morphisms, not merely on F-points. This does not follow by citing the already-reductive Bruhat theorem: reductivity of the generated group is not yet known. The new node deliberately states no reductivity or semisimplicity conclusion. Its root-tangent description is a consequence of its equivariant open immersion and does not claim that an arbitrary integral Lie form remains faithful after reduction.

The general recognition prerequisite is Milne 23.31 with Remark 23.33's preservation of simple coroots, and the usual rank-one subgroup compatibility from 21.11. It starts with smooth rank-one reductive subgroups already embedded in a smooth connected ambient group, plus linear independence and cross-root commutation. It contains no lattice, representation, exponentiation, or full-weight hypothesis. The empty-family convention explicitly retains T.
