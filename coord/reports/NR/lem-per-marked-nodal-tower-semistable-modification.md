## Statement dispute and correction

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported statement used the source's term “strict normal crossings” but defined only an etale-local normal crossings condition. Its definition of split fibres required geometric irreducibility but did not require their irreducible components to be smooth. Those two versions must not be identified.

Here is an explicit small-case obstruction to the intended strict conclusion under that weaker split hypothesis. Take A=W(k), k algebraically closed of characteristic p>=5, uniformizer pi=p, and the projective cubic y^2 z=x^3+x^2 z+pi z^3. Its generic fibre is smooth (the discriminant is -16 pi(4+27pi)), the special fibre is an irreducible rational nodal cubic with two rational branches y=+x and y=-x at the node, and the total surface is regular (at that node pi=y^2-x^3-x^2 belongs to the square of the maximal ideal, while x,y generate that ideal). Mark the point at infinity and take G trivial. This satisfies the imported weak splitting condition. Its closed fibre is normal crossings etale locally but is not a strict normal crossings divisor: its sole global irreducible component is singular. More strongly, a projective birational regular model with reduced strict special fibre cannot repair this over the same trait. Stacks Tag 0C5R, resolve.tex, lemma-proper-birational-regular-surfaces, read for this counterexample, factors such a map into point blowups. Before the node is touched its strict transform remains singular; the first blowup at that node creates a component of multiplicity two because pi has order two. That component's strict transform retains multiplicity two under further point blowups. Thus it cannot have the required reduced semistable special fibre.

I corrected the source-intended strict version, rather than silently proving the weaker etale-local predicate: “split” now requires smooth geometrically irreducible fibre components, and the boundary uses def-trf-strict-normal-crossings-boundary (regular global components and regular intersections of expected codimension). The strict semistable chart equation, the group, the fixed trait, projectivity, equivariance and birationality are unchanged. This is a missing-hypothesis correction, explicitly authorized by the worker brief. It also excludes the one-vertex self-loop fibre in the counterexample. The existing parent lem-per-trait-marked-nodal-alteration uses the same weaker imported definition and needs the same strict split refinement in its output before its invocation by lem-per-trait-semistable-galois-alteration is justified. Those other workers' files were not edited.
