## Source error

Bhatt–Scholze, *The pro-etale topology for schemes*, Lemma 4.2.13(1), asserts the unrestricted qcqs formula. I read its statement, its proof, the preceding construction paragraph, and Lemma 4.2.12 in the available PDF extraction. The proof identifies the inverse image as sheafification of W |-> F(pi0 W), correctly. It then tests a component coequalizer against profinite representables. That verifies a coequalizer in Prof, but does not verify the sheaf coequalizer required for arbitrary F. The implication “F is a sheaf, so it is enough to check that coequalizer in Prof” is the error. The affine right adjoint in Lemma 2.2.8 and the presheaf terminal-index computation do not use it.

Here is the maintainer's counterexample, with the two distinct coequalizers made explicit. Choose affine connected curves C_n = Spec A_n consisting of chains of n+1 affine lines with adjacent lines meeting in nodes. Choose f_n vanishing at the odd nodes and g_n vanishing at the even nodes, with their respective node sets disjoint, and with (f_n,g_n)=A_n. Put A = product_n A_n, V = Spec A and f=(f_n), g=(g_n). The Bezout witnesses are chosen componentwise, so (f,g)=A and U = D(f) disjoint-union D(g) is a finite Zariski, hence pro-etale, covering of V. Write X=pi0 U, Y=pi0 V, R=U x_V U and let R' be the image of pi0 R in X x_Y X. On the clopen fibre indexed by n, the components of D(f_n) and D(g_n) and their overlaps form a path of unbounded length as n grows. Thus the equivalence relation X x_Y X is not any uniformly bounded finite iterate of R'.

Let G be the sheafified coequalizer of h_{R'} => h_X on Prof/Y. The class of id_X equalizes the two maps from R', hence also those from pi0 R. It cannot descend to G(Y): such a descent, and the equality with id_X after pullback, would be witnessed after finite profinite coverings by a finite chain of the generating relation; quasi-compactness gives a uniform finite bound, contradicted by the chain fibres. A coequalizer tested only against representable profinite sheaves does not detect this obstruction. If the old natural evaluation formula held at V, U and R for G, sheaf descent for pi^{-1}G along U -> V would force the forbidden descent to G(Y). This refutes both the old item clause and the new helper, exactly as in E2/E7.

The corrected proof never asserts effectivity of that component relation. It proves bijectivity of the sheafification unit at an eligible W using its actual section W -> coprod W_i. The counterexample is therefore excluded for a stated reason, rather than hidden as an unsplit false obligation. The maintainer should record the BS 4.2.13(1) error in coord/HUMAN.md; this worker cannot edit coordinator files.
