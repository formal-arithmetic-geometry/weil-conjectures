The imported statement without the chapter convention would admit these data, while its claimed proof cannot type-check the smooth base-change input. The repair restores the source's convention, rather than inventing a hypothesis. All original noetherian assumptions are retained. The conclusion is the specified canonical ringed exchange in the ambient unbounded derived category, without a spurious bounded-constructible endpoint claim.
