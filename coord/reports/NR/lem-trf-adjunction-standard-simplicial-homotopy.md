## Source error

In the local Stacks 08ND proof, both displayed pairs of self-maps write the augmentation followed in notation by its section as e composed with h, while labeling the source and target as the resolution. With e:Z to const(Y) and h:const(Y) to Z, e composed with h is instead a self-map of const(Y). The self-map of the resolution that must be homotopic to its identity is h composed with e. Our proof consistently distinguishes ea=1 from ae homotopic to 1, so this typographical type error is not reproduced. It does not affect the lemma statement or the imported item statement.
