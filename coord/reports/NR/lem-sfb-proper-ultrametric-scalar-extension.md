The center-distance calculation makes these balls linearly ordered. Finite intersections, restricted to the compact ball for u=0, contain the center of a ball of least radius. Compactness gives an admissible value. The explicit formula g(u)+ab extends over U+Kx without changing C. Chain unions give upper bounds for graphs, and Zorn forces a maximal domain to be V.
