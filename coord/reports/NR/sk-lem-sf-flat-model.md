## New nodes

`lem-sfb-fully-faithful-thick-image`: statement, unsplit, no Mathlib match. Let an idempotent-complete triangulated category C be thickly generated by G, and let F:C→D be exact and fully faithful. Its essential image is thick and equals thick(FG). This is an ordinary categorical lemma with no scheme, coefficient or constructibility assumptions. Its statement explicitly defines all extra notions, including retracts, idempotent completeness, thick closure and essential image. It neither restates a sheaf clause of the item nor packages the whole item.

The new node serves the blueprint's paragraph-1 generation by shifts/extensions/direct summands after comparison and the complement's thick-closure formulation. Its `blueprint.from` names that place, with the item as ancestor, no intervening nodes, and `same_as: []`. Its source identifies the formulation as extracted by NR; Stacks 09SH (`homology.tex`, `lemma-karoubian`, lines 526–538) supplies the splitting criterion, not a literal assertion of the entire new lemma. I read that passage and Mathlib's exact full functor essential-image triangulated instance at `CategoryTheory/Triangulated/Subcategory.lean:726`, and the idempotent-splitting definition at `CategoryTheory/Idempotents/Basic.lean:54`.

For the later worker, the argument is: zero and shifts are in the essential image by exactness. Given a triangle between image objects, lift its connecting morphism using fullness, complete it to a triangle in C, and use the shift comparison and the two prescribed endpoint isomorphisms to identify the middle objects. Thus the image is triangulated. For a retract Y of F(X), lift the corresponding projector to e on X by fullness; faithfulness proves e²=e. Split e on X inside C, say X'→X→X' with ri=1 and ir=e. The image splitting and the given splitting onto Y are uniquely isomorphic (maps are the two cross composites); hence Y≅F(X'). This proves retract closure without assuming D idempotent complete. The thick image contains FG, so thick(FG) is contained in it. Conversely the inverse image under F of thick(FG) is thick and contains G; generation of C makes it all of C. This is the reverse inclusion. These are genuine lifted-cone and lifted-projector arguments, not a two-line instance of a definition. The node itself has no proof or proof sketch.
