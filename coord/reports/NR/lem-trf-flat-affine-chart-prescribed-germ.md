## Findings and changes

The live item was unsplit and had no proof. No released repair note or earlier sandbox split/report existed at the start. The statement is true as written, and is the preliminary shrinking argument of Stacks 056Y, not its entire hypersurface theorem. The proof of that preliminary argument does not use the subsequent hypotheses that the germ is in the maximal ideal and is fibre-regular; thus it applies to arbitrary germs, with the maximal-ideal conclusion proved conditionally.

The existing lem-sfb-relative-flat-affine-charts, reviewed on 2026-10-23, already gives the required local open flat locus, including its identification with the flat points of the actual morphism. It is used as a generic tool with Y=S and q=id_S; the identity's locally finite type hypothesis is verified from its affine coordinate ring being generated by the empty set. This avoids creating another scheme-flat-locus node or duplicating the existing algebraic openness theorem.

The proof explicitly restricts a germ representative to a new affine inside T intersect E. It never assumes an intersection of affine opens is affine. It applies the affine finite-presentation theorem only after that final shrink. The localized stalk-map formula and the maximal-ideal argument are written out, rather than asserted as an unexplained transport.
