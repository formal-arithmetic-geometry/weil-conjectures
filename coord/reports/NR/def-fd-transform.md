I made all these hypotheses explicit and retained the inverse-character construction. I spelled this out because the old displayed statements of both `def-nagata-compactification` and `def-weil-pbc-projection-compactification` impose quasi-compactness, whereas `def-nagata-compactification.blueprint_update` and the current source omit it. ## Review by NR

I checked delivery-errors B–E against the node files, not just the dependency names. The item is a definition, so its split consists of the notions its data uses, with empty proof/proof_deps/proof_origin as required by stages/1-dag.md section 3(2). Accordingly there are no proof steps whose citations need quotes, no hidden long proof steps, and citations.json is an empty list. I did not invent theorem steps for a definition or create a node for one clause of the defining formula.

For the kernel, I read `def-fd-kernel` and its stored blueprint_update alongside the current blueprint. The latter gives exactly a Lambda-module Artin–Schreier sheaf for an arbitrary global function and character over a fiber product with p=0. I take only this object, not its lissity, dualizability, or constructibility. Those extra facts are not needed to compose functors on the ambient derived categories. The stale closed audit of the kernel must not be read as a verification of the more general empty case: the source `fourierKernel` also carries the stronger CharP requirement. This does not change the mathematical definition used as a dependency here.

For the domain and codomain, `def-weil-pbc-derived-categories` explicitly defines D(T_et,R) of constant-ring module sheaves with no boundedness restriction. I read `prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact`: its clauses (1)–(3) identify inverse image of the constant ring, supply the module action, identify it with module pullback, and give exactness for arbitrary schemes and a commutative ring R. The statement of `lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-factorization` is for arbitrary abelian categories and gives the full unbounded derived functor, including the termwise representative. Thus its application to p1 and to j_! has all hypotheses. I avoided citing the pro-etale `def-sfb-finite-derived-pullback-iso`, and avoided treating the bounded-below clause of `def-weil-pbc-derived-inverse-image` as an unbounded module statement.

I read `def-kun-derived-tensor`: it has exactly arbitrary commutative Lambda, arbitrary scheme T, and small-etale module categories. Its K-flat construction is the tensor used here. In contrast `def-weil-derived-tensor` has a pro-etale statement, so I did not use it. No finite Tor-amplitude claim or preservation theorem is used to define tensor with the degree-zero kernel.

For compact supports, the current `def-nagata-compactification.blueprint_update` supplies j open and q proper with qj=p2, with no Noetherian or quasi-compact hypothesis. `def-weil-pbc-extension-by-zero` is for arbitrary open immersions and arbitrary commutative coefficients, and `lem-weil-pbc-extension-by-zero-exact` explicitly states preservation of quasi-isomorphisms under degreewise application. I read both. The statement of `def-weil-pbc-direct-image` supplies the additive sheaf direct image on module sheaves; its derived clause is only bounded below, and I do not use that clause on unbounded objects. Instead `def-weil-pbc-kinjective-resolutions` and `prop-weil-pbc-kinjective-resolutions-exist` give K-injectives for every complex in these precise module-sheaf categories; `lem-weil-pbc-kinjective-total-derived` then constructs the total derived functor and computes it on those resolutions. Its functor from the homotopy category is Qq_*; applying an additive functor termwise commutes with each entry of a cone differential matrix and its finite biproduct, so it carries cone triangles to cone triangles. This is the elementary verification needed to apply the cited lemma, without assuming direct image exact on the abelian category. The item's text records this verification. The composite Rq_* D(j_!) therefore is defined for all its declared objects. I avoided `def-sfb-finite-derived-lower-shriek` (pro-etale), `def-amb-etale-compact-support` (Noetherian), and `pur9-def-etale-unbounded-candidate` (finite coefficients over a field).

I read `def-weil-triangulated-category` for integer powers of the shift. The item additionally spells out the cochain shift and its action on morphisms, including the sign in the differential. The final formula is the composition of the specified functors, and its arrow formula is composition of their arrow maps. This supplies real data, not an unspecified object chosen from an existence result. No self-property of the transform is cited back into its definition, so no circularity is introduced through a well-definedness or inversion theorem.

The small cases were checked against these constructions. W may be empty (for example V empty); its zero global-section ring has p=0 even though CharP(_,p) fails for prime p. The module-sheaf category on W has only the zero object, and tensor and all the functors in the formula apply to it. Rank zero in the vector-bundle application gives shift [0], not [1]; arbitrary negative r has the same cochain sign convention. The character may be trivial, p may be 2, and the coefficient ring may be zero: none is excluded by the definition or by the cited general exact inverse-image/tensor/K-injective constructions. The input may be unbounded and nonconstructible. No nontrivial-character assumption, finite coefficient condition, field condition, or cohomological-dimension bound has slipped into the construction. These checks are also why the already rejected Lean attachment is not reused.

The dedicated unit-key node `sk-def-ft` already exists separately from this subordinate definition node and is recorded in same_as. Unit realization remains with that node; this delivery changes no unit record. The unit source and statement, and all maintainer-owned fields of the item, remain untouched. No new nodes were made: elementary composition and degree-zero placement are written directly, existing notions are reused, and no clause has been relabeled as a new lemma.
