Read universally, this is false: X_0 = Spec F_q, d = 0 and K = Lambda_X give the degree-zero point coefficient complex. The intended warning, already illustrated in the source by P^1, is that it **need not** be concentrated in degree zero. I corrected precisely these words, retaining the P^1 example. I also replaced “selects the top-degree summand” by the specified trace morphism inducing the normalized map on H_c^{2d}; a chosen splitting of the source complex is not part of the construction. This preserves the source's pairing and normalization, rather than weakening a duality assertion. Neither the chapter route nor the unit's pairing/perfectness construction changes.
