For lem-sfb-bruhat-simple-letter-specialization the local source supplies the Bruhat and Tits-system inputs, rather than a verbatim statement of simple-letter Zariski specialization. This is an agent-formulated auxiliary lemma. A proposed proof for its next worker is to form the reduced gallery variety using the rank-one subgroups B union B n_s B; it is an iterated projective-line bundle. Multiplication has dense reduced-word locus mapping onto B n_w B/B. Setting all factors except the j-th to the identity exhibits n_{s_j}B as the endpoint of a gallery, hence in the closure of that cell. The smooth quotient G -> G/B is open, so its inverse image of the closure of the cell equals the closure of its inverse image: every open neighborhood of a point over that closure maps to an open neighborhood meeting the cell. The inverse image is B n_w B. Rational points are dense in these smooth locally closed cells over an algebraically closed field. This proves the stated specialization in G. This proposed argument, including the gallery construction and its geometric properties, is deliberately not hidden inside an elementary step of the item: it is the new unsplit lemma. Existing semisimple simply connected Bott--Samelson and Schubert nodes can help prove it after a central-cover comparison, but their stronger hypotheses cannot be ignored here.
