### Argument assigned to lem-trf-simplex-word-evaluation-descent (old Step 1, retained verbatim)

Use the category $P$ and functor $J:P\to\Delta$ in [lem-sc-module-cup-godement-simplex-presentation-faithful]. Explicitly, an arrow of $P$ is a finite composable word in cofaces $\delta_i$ and codegeneracies $\sigma_i$, modulo replacement of either side of a simplex relation inside a word. The relations, with degree superscripts suppressed and only typed instances allowed, are $\delta_j\delta_i=\delta_i\delta_{j-1}$ for $i<j$, $\sigma_j\delta_i=\delta_i\sigma_{j-1}$ for $i<j$, $\sigma_j\delta_j=1=\sigma_j\delta_{j+1}$, $\sigma_j\delta_i=\delta_{i-1}\sigma_j$ for $i>j+1$, and $\sigma_j\sigma_i=\sigma_i\sigma_{j+1}$ for $i\leq j$. Here juxtaposition means ordinary composition, with the right factor first. Send $[n]$ to $U_n$; evaluate a word contravariantly, replacing $\delta_i$ by $d_i$, $\sigma_i$ by $s_i$, and reversing composition. Every displayed relation evaluates respectively to exactly the corresponding hypothesis on $d,s$, including the two separate identity relations. Consequently replacing a subword preserves its evaluated morphism: the surrounding words just compose the equality on both sides. Reflexivity, symmetry and transitivity then show that equivalent words have equal values. Empty words evaluate to identities and concatenation reverses evaluation. Thus evaluation gives a well-defined functor $E:P^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathcal D$.

### Argument assigned to lem-trf-simplex-presentation-unique-lifts (old Step 2, retained verbatim)

For a simplex map $\alpha:[m]\to[n]$, let $W(\alpha)$ mean that there exists $p:[m]\to[n]$ in $P$ with $J(p)=\alpha$. Since $J$ is a functor and is the identity on the degree labels, $W$ contains identities and is closed under composition: if $J(p)=\alpha$ and $J(q)=\beta$, then $J(qp)=\beta\alpha$. It contains all cofaces and codegeneracies by their specified images under $J$. Apply [lem-sc-simplex-morphism-property-generators] to this property $W$ to obtain a lift of every $\alpha$. By [lem-sc-module-cup-godement-simplex-presentation-faithful], any two such lifts with these endpoints are equal. Write $\widehat\alpha$ for this unique lift. Uniqueness gives $\widehat{1}=1$, $\widehat{\beta\alpha}=\widehat\beta\,\widehat\alpha$, and the lift of each coface or codegeneracy is the corresponding generator.

For the added inverse-functor clause in lem-trf-simplex-presentation-unique-lifts, the same uniquely defined lifts give K. J(K(alpha))=alpha is its defining property. For a presentation arrow p, p and K(J(p)) have the same endpoints and J-image, hence are equal by faithfulness. These give both strict inverse identities. This is the arrow-level statement needed for assembly, before any category D or operators are chosen.
