## Construction checked

Put J = I O_Y using the current kernel definition in `def-nagata-extended-ideal`: if i:V(I) -> X is the closed subscheme and p:Y times_X V(I) -> Y is its base change, J = ker(O_Y -> p_* O_{Y times_X V(I)}). This definition is not being cited for an image equality. For each natural n form the composite c_n: g^*(I^n) -> g^*(O_X) -> O_Y. By the statement of `lemma-nagata-flat-pullback-ideal-pow`, its image is J^n. Define beta_n by the canonical image corestriction, so the inclusion J^n -> O_Y composed with beta_n is c_n. This uses the lemma's stated image equality, not its affine proof and not an unstated claim that the map is injective.

The tensor formula in `def-pc-module-pullback` gives the local rule beta_n(b tensor a) = b g-sharp(a). It is balanced: replacing b g-sharp(r) tensor a by b tensor ra gives b g-sharp(r) g-sharp(a) in either case. Additivity and O_Y-linearity follow from the same formula. For homogeneous a in degree m and a' in degree n, both multiplication composites give bc g-sharp(aa') = (b g-sharp(a))(c g-sharp(a')). At degree zero the rule is b tensor r -> b g-sharp(r), so it preserves the algebra unit. Restrictions commute because restriction of b g-sharp(a) is res(b) g-sharp(res(a)); this is the compatibility in the definition of a morphism of ringed spaces. These are identities on tensor generators and hence hold after tensor product and sheafification. They do not require a separate new lemma.

The Rees algebra and its sheaf direct sum are precisely `def-nagata-sheaf-rees-algebra`. The adjunction stated by `def-pc-module-pullback` and the existing leaf `lem-weil-pbc-left-adjoint-preserves-colimits` identify g^*(direct sum I^n) with direct sum g^*(I^n), with their specified coprojections. The index is the small discrete category on the natural numbers; the source direct sum exists by the Rees definition, and the image cocone is a colimit by the leaf's statement. Thus the maps beta_n assemble into the displayed graded algebra map, with the multiplication and unit just checked. No arbitrary choice of data or inverse of an unproved isomorphism is used.
