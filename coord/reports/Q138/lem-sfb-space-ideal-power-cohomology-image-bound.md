## Source error

The 08AW proof writes the image-ideal algebra $\bigoplus I^n\mathcal O_X$ as $f^*\widetilde B$. Read literally with the image-ideal convention used in the preceding paragraph, that is not an isomorphism without flatness. For example, take the proper closed immersion $X=\operatorname{Spec}(\mathbf Z/2)\to\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf Z$ and $I=(2)$. The degree-one pullback of $I$ is $(2)\otimes_{\mathbf Z}\mathbf Z/2\cong\mathbf Z/2$, while the extended image ideal is zero. The lemma's finiteness assertion and the item remain true.

The new node makes no such identification. Its action is on the actual image submodules $J^rF\subseteq F$, through the genuine pullback of the base Rees algebra. For the later split of this input, use the surjective multiplication map from that pullback acting on $F$ onto $\bigoplus J^rF$. Local generators of $F$ in degree zero generate this module over the pullback algebra: products of images of elements of $I$ have coefficients in $I^r$, and conversely $I^r$ is the finite-sum product ideal. Thus the finite-type hypothesis of 08AT is the appropriate one; it does not require identifying pullbacks with images. This paragraph records the prospective proof argument, not an extra hypothesis or a proof sketch in the new statement.
