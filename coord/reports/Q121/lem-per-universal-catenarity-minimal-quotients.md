Step 3 uses `def-weil-pbc-finite-type-algebra`, which offers exactly the finite-polynomial-generators formulation required. The displayed map from A/p to C is well defined because those generators vanish in C. The assumption gives universal catenarity of A/p; the definition from `def-exc-excellent-ring` then gives catenarity of this specific finite type algebra C. No flatness, tensor-product identification, or contraction equality pB intersect A = p is used.

Its statement does not explicitly claim an order isomorphism; the step supplies that detail: image and preimage are monotone on subsets, and inverse monotone bijections reflect inclusion. Because J is contained in the lower endpoint, every prime in the whole interval is in the stated image.
