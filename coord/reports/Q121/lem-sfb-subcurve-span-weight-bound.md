For the degree cap, read Stacks curves.tex, Tag 0CCS, lemma-grd-inequalities, especially its nonzero section, effective zero divisor and restriction exact sequence. This proves h^0(M) <= deg(M)+1 whenever sections exist; their zero divisor has nonnegative degree, so a negative-degree line has no section. Tensor-degree additivity (varieties.tex 0AYX, lemma-degree-tensor-product) and the effective divisor degree (0AYY, lemma-degree-effective-Cartier-divisor) give deg(A^m(-qB))=me-qb, including inverse tensor powers. In a DVR frame, vanishing order q*n_P is exactly divisibility by the local equation of qB. Global sections are finite-dimensional by proper coherent finiteness. These ingredients give the new statement. No genus-at-least-two assumption is used.

For the defect lemma, read varieties.tex 0BXR, lemma-normalization-locally-algebraic, and 0C1R, lemma-prepare-delta-invariant; the existing reduced normalization/coherent support nodes give the finite normalization and finite-support quotient. Connectedness and dimension one exclude isolated zero-dimensional components, since these are open and closed. The nilradical N of the projective generically reduced X is coherent with finite support. Twist 0->N->O_X->O_D->0 and 0->O_D->nu_*O_tildeD->Q->0 by O(m). Tensoring a line preserves exactness in every local frame. By coherent.tex 0B3J and varieties.tex 0AYT, higher cohomology of the finite-support terms vanishes and h^0 of their twists is independent of m. Thus H_X(m)->H_D(m) is onto with bounded kernel, H_D(m)->sum H_E(m) is injective with bounded cokernel. The projection formula here follows directly in local frames of the invertible twist. Smooth proper normalized components have positive hyperplane degree; Riemann–Roch and high-degree vanishing give h^0(E,A_E^m)=me_E+1-g_E for large m. The last bound follows from these dimensions and the two defect bounds. Constants may depend on X, which suffices because we let m tend to infinity for one flag. No uniform regularity assertion is being smuggled into this lemma.
