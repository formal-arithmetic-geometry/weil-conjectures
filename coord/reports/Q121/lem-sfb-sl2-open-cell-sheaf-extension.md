## Proof plan for the new statement-only prerequisite

This plan is kept here, not in the unsplit node. Write P(Z)=product(b_i Z+d_i) and B=R[Z][1/P]. Polynomial rings are free as R-modules, hence flat; localization preserves flatness and composition of flat maps is flat (Stacks algebra.tex Tag 00HT, lemma-flat-localization; Tag 00HC, lemma-composition-flat, both read in full). Equivalently localization is a filtered colimit of free R-modules. B has the explicit finite presentation R[Z,W]/(W P(Z)-1), so the spectrum map is locally of finite presentation. For each prime p of R, every factor of P has nonzero reduction in kappa(p)[Z] by the unit-ideal equation; their product is nonzero because this polynomial ring is a domain. Localizing at its product is nonzero. More explicitly, map to kappa(p)(Z); P maps to a nonzero rational function, so this gives a prime of B contracting to p. Hence the spectrum map is surjective. Stacks Tag 00HQ, lemma-ff-rings (read the statement and proof), also identifies flatness plus this surjectivity as faithful flatness. At a prime Q of B, the induced localized map R_p to B_Q is flat, giving precisely the scheme-flatness definition; all localizations involved preserve flatness. The image of each factor has inverse (product of the other factors)/P in B. This proves exactly the new node, without any smooth-group structure theorem.
