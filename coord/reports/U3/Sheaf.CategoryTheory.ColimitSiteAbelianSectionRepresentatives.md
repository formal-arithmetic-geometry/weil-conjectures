`Sheaf.CategoryTheory.ColimitSiteAbelianStageImage` constructs, without any sorry, the additive
subpresheaf of the canonical inverse image generated by restrictions of stage images. Its section
groups are explicit `AddSubgroup.closure` subgroups; restriction precomposes the generating arrow.
The functor laws and natural inclusion are proved. No objects are selected from an unproved
existence theorem.

`Sheaf.CategoryTheory.ColimitSiteAbelianStageImageSheaf` isolates finite-cover descent, expressed
as the sheaf condition of that explicit presheaf. `Sheaf.CategoryTheory.ColimitSiteAbelianStageImageRefinement`
isolates refinement of finite additive expressions into one forward-stage section. The latter
requires only filteredness, the object/arrow set colimits, continuity and the unit equations;
it has no cover-finiteness, precoverage-stability or covering-pullback hypotheses. Its membership
hypothesis is substantive, not a repackaging of the original hypotheses: without finite-cover
descent this subgroup need not contain all inverse-image sections.

The proof combining the two is explicit: the identity-presentation unit equation puts each
unit value into the generated subgroup. Factor the unit through the subsheaf, transpose this
factorization by the adjunction, and use injectivity of the adjunction hom equivalence to prove
that its composite with inclusion is the identity. Evaluation proves subgroup membership of
any section, to which the pure refinement statement applies. Endpoint transports are proved
using cocone naturality and the supplied Arrow equalities; the original HEq is retained.

All four modules compile together with `lcompile.py --all`, with no compiler warnings. Reviewed
zero sections, empty covers (zero group by the sheaf condition), an empty source stage (no U),
and the identity presentation. Definitions and the combination proof have no sorry.
