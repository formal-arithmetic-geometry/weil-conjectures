### Former proof, retained verbatim

Old step 1:

Choose a presentation $B=A[x_1,\ldots,x_r]/(F_1,\ldots,F_m)$ as in [def-weil-pbc-finitely-presented-algebra]. Let $A_0\subseteq A$ be the $\mathbf Z$-subalgebra generated by all coefficients of the finitely many $F_j$, and let $B_0=A_0[x_1,\ldots,x_r]/(F_1,\ldots,F_m)$. Then $A_0$ is a finite type $\mathbf Z$-algebra and $B_0$ is finitely presented over $A_0$. There is an $A$-algebra isomorphism $B_0\otimes_{A_0}A\cong B$: the tensor pushout property [lem-weil-pbc-algebra-tensor-pushout-universal-property] gives the map sending $b_0\otimes a$ to $a b_0$ in $B$. In the other direction send each $a\in A$ to $1\otimes a$ and each $x_i$ to $x_i\otimes1$. All $F_j$ vanish because their coefficients lie in $A_0$, and the two composites fix $A$ and every $x_i$; the tensor uniqueness clause verifies the composite on the tensor product as well.

Uses: def-weil-pbc-finitely-presented-algebra, lem-weil-pbc-algebra-tensor-pushout-universal-property

Old step 2:

Put $Y_0=\operatorname{Spec}A_0$ and $X_0=\operatorname{Spec}B_0$, with structure morphism $f_0:X_0\to Y_0$. The finite generation of $A_0$ over $\mathbf Z$ and of $B_0$ over $A_0$ makes the corresponding affine morphisms finite type by [def-weil-finite-type-morphism]. Thus $Y_0$ is Noetherian by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian], including its explicit assertion for finite type $\mathbf Z$-schemes. Let $E_0\subseteq Y_0$ be the set of points at which the fibre of $f_0$ is geometrically irreducible.

Uses: def-weil-finite-type-morphism, lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian

Old step 3:

Fix a nonempty irreducible closed subset $Z\subseteq Y_0$. By [lem-sc-scheme-sober] it is $\overline{\{\xi\}}$ for its generic point $\xi$. Apply [lem-trf-geometric-component-count-generic-constancy] to $f_0$ and $\xi$. It supplies a nonempty relatively open $V\subseteq Z$ and $n\geq0$ such that every $t\in V$ has exactly $n$ irreducible components after extension of its fibre to a separable closure of $\kappa(t)$. For any topological space with exactly one irreducible component $C$, [lem-sc-irreducible-components-cover] says that every point belongs to $C$, so the whole space equals the nonempty irreducible subset $C$. Conversely, if the whole space is irreducible, it is itself a maximal irreducible subset and contains every irreducible subset, hence it is the unique component, by [def-weil-irreducible-components]. These arguments also show that a space with zero components is empty.

Uses: lem-sc-scheme-sober, lem-trf-geometric-component-count-generic-constancy, lem-sc-irreducible-components-cover, def-weil-irreducible-components

Old step 4:

For $t\in V$, the geometric-irreducibility definition [def-pc-geometrically-irreducible] implies that a geometrically irreducible fibre remains irreducible over the chosen separable closure, and therefore has one component by step 3. For the converse, if its extension to the separable closure has one component, it is irreducible and nonempty by step 3. Apply [lem-sbc-subset-geometric-irreducibility] with $k=\kappa(t)$, $X=(X_0)_t$, $A=|X|$ and $\Omega=\kappa(t)^s$. The separable closure is separably closed by [def-pd-separable-closure]; the cited lemma gives irreducibility and nonemptiness after every field extension, exactly geometric irreducibility. Consequently $E_0\cap V=V$ if $n=1$ and $E_0\cap V=\varnothing$ if $n\ne1$. This verifies the hypothesis of [lem-trf-noetherian-constructible-subset-criterion] for every irreducible closed $Z$. As $Y_0$ is Noetherian by step 2, that criterion gives constructibility of $E_0$.

Uses: def-pc-geometrically-irreducible, lem-sbc-subset-geometric-irreducibility, def-pd-separable-closure, lem-trf-noetherian-constructible-subset-criterion

Old step 5:

Let $r:\operatorname{Spec}A\to Y_0$ be induced by $A_0\hookrightarrow A$. The tensor isomorphism of step 1 and [lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra] identify $\operatorname{Spec}B\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ with the base change of $f_0$ along $r$, and identify its fibre at $\mathfrak p$ with $\operatorname{Spec}(B\otimes_A\kappa(\mathfrak p))$. Apply [lem-sbc-radicial-preparation-fibre-comparison] to this square with the identity universal homeomorphism of $X_0\times_{Y_0}\operatorname{Spec}A$ (its every base change is the identity, by [def-weil-universal-homeomorphism]). The lemma gives geometric irreducibility of the base-changed fibre at $\mathfrak p$ if and only if the fibre of $f_0$ at $r(\mathfrak p)$ is geometrically irreducible. Thus $E_{\mathrm{irr}}(B/A)=r^{-1}(E_0)$. Since $E_0$ is constructible, [lem-pur-scheme-constructible-preimage] makes this inverse image constructible. No flatness or Noetherian property of $A$ or $B$ has been used.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra, lem-sbc-radicial-preparation-fibre-comparison, def-weil-universal-homeomorphism, lem-pur-scheme-constructible-preimage
