### Returned step 3 — lem-trf-representable-sum-shriek-acyclic

For $U\in\mathcal E$ and an $R$-module $M$, define $L_U(M)(V)=\bigoplus_{a:V\to U}M$, restrictions sending the $a$-summand to the $a\circ b$-summand. A map $L_U(M)\to H$ is uniquely determined by its value on the identity summand at $U$: its value on $(a,m)$ must be $H(a)(f(m))$. Thus $\operatorname{Hom}(L_U(M),H)=\operatorname{Hom}_R(M,H(U))$. It follows directly from the lifting definition that $L_U(P)$ is projective when $P$ is a projective module. Every module has a free epimorphism, and $\bigoplus_{U,x\in H(U)}L_U(R)\to H$, $(a,r)\mapsto rH(a)(x)$, is surjective at $V$ because $x\in H(V)$ is hit by $(1_V,1)$. Direct sums of these projectives are projective: choose each lift and take their coproduct. Hence both module categories used below have enough projectives. Direct sums are exact: every element has finite support, and an element in a componentwise kernel has preimages in those finitely many components. In particular each $L_U$ is exact. The colimit universal property gives $\pi_!L_U(M)=M$, with every $a$-summand mapping identically to $M$.
