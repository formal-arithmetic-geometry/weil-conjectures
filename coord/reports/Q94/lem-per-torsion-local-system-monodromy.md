The previous steps incorrectly treated arbitrary R-modules as filtered unions of finite R-submodules, and cited the finite-coefficient lisse theorem without its finite-ring hypothesis. No finiteness of R is introduced.

The old citations.json contained paraphrases and cited statements in the wrong steps. It has been replaced by exact JSON statement excerpts, with the numbering of the repaired proof. The old extension step spoke about finite quotients of F'' unnecessarily; the free translation action of F' on F has quotient F'' directly and matches the torsor lemma's statement exactly.
