## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED (source inference only; the assigned item's statement is already corrected): the proof of More on Algebra Tag 09EU, `lemma-galois-relative`, says “Since $K=(K_1)^G$”. Under its normal-extension hypothesis this equality need not hold. For example, let $K=\mathbf F_p(u)$ and $E=K(u^{1/p})$. The degree-$p$ extension is normal because the minimal polynomial splits as $(X-u^{1/p})^p$, but every $K$-automorphism of $E$ is the identity. Thus $E^G=E\ne K$. With $L=K$, the reduced tensor is $E$ and $x=u^{1/p}$ is invariant but not in $K$, while $x^p=u$. This tests both the failed inference and the corrected conclusion. No statement change is necessary: the assigned item already asks for a positive power and expressly permits inseparability. Its text and statement dependencies are retained exactly.

The repair uses the existing Fields Tag 030M fixed-field decomposition: $S=E^G$ is purely inseparable over $K$, and $E/S$ is Galois. Instead of incorrectly identifying $S$ with $K$, step 4 raises its finitely many coefficients to a common characteristic power. The other detail omitted in 09EU is the common power killing the differences of a lift; step 2 spells it out for the finite group, without assuming the nilradical has a uniform nilpotence exponent. The source's stronger canonical-lifting-map assertion is not needed or claimed.
