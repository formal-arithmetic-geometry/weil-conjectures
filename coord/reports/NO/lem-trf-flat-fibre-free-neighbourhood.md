## Retained returned proof (verbatim)

### Old Step 1

Suppose first that $\mathcal F_x\otimes\kappa(x)=0$. The stalk $\mathcal F_x$ is finitely generated over the local ring $\mathcal O_{X,x}$ because $\mathcal F$ is of finite presentation. Apply the Nakayama generating criterion [lem-weil-pbc-nakayama-local-generators] to the empty family: its image generates the zero residue-fibre, so $\mathcal F_x=0$. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-module-finite-type-surjective-on-stalk] to the zero morphism $0\to\mathcal F$. Its stalk map is surjective because $\mathcal F_x=0$, and finite presentation gives finite type. After shrinking to an open neighbourhood $W$ of $x$, $\mathcal F|_W=0$, which is the required conclusion with $r=0$.

Cited nodes: lem-weil-pbc-nakayama-local-generators, lem-weil-pbc-module-finite-type-surjective-on-stalk

### Old Step 2

Assume now that $\mathcal F_x\otimes\kappa(x)\ne0$. Set $R=\mathcal O_{S,s}$ with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m_R=\mathfrak m_s$, $S_x=\mathcal O_{X,x}$ with $\alpha=f^\sharp_x:R\to S_x$ (a local homomorphism), use the identity map as the second local map $\beta:S_x\to S_x$, and let $M=\mathcal F_x$. Since $f$ is locally of finite presentation [def-sbc-locally-finite-presentation], there are affine opens $\operatorname{Spec}B\ni x$ of $X$ and $\operatorname{Spec}A\ni s$ of $S$ with $f(\operatorname{Spec}B)\subseteq\operatorname{Spec}A$ and $B$ a finitely presented $A$-algebra; with $\mathfrak p\subset A$, $\mathfrak q\subset B$ the primes of $s$, $x$, the ring $S_x=B_{\mathfrak q}$ is a localization of the finitely presented $A_{\mathfrak p}$-algebra $B\otimes_AA_{\mathfrak p}$, and $A_{\mathfrak p}=R$; so $S_x$ is essentially of finite presentation over $R$. Taking stalks at $x$ of a local presentation $\mathcal O_U^{\oplus m}\to\mathcal O_U^{\oplus n}\to\mathcal F|_U\to0$ (stalks are exact) shows that $M$ is a finitely presented $S_x$-module, and $M\ne0$ because $M\otimes\kappa(x)\ne0$. For the fibre, let $p:X_s\to X$ be the first projection and $x'\in X_s$ the point over $x$. By [lem-sbc-scheme-fibre-stalk-quotient], $p^\sharp_{x'}$ induces a ring isomorphism $S_x/\mathfrak m_RS_x\cong\mathcal O_{X_s,x'}$. By [lem-weil-pbc-module-stalk-pullback] applied to $p$ and $\mathcal F$, $(\mathcal F_s)_{x'}=(p^*\mathcal F)_{x'}\cong M\otimes_{S_x}\mathcal O_{X_s,x'}$ as $\mathcal O_{X_s,x'}$-modules, and under the ring isomorphism this is $M\otimes_{S_x}S_x/\mathfrak m_RS_x=M/\mathfrak m_RM$ as an $S_x/\mathfrak m_RS_x$-module. Flatness is invariant under such a compatible pair of isomorphisms, so the hypothesis that $(\mathcal F_s)_x$ is flat over $\mathcal O_{X_s,x}$ says that $M/\mathfrak m_RM$ is flat over $S_x/\mathfrak m_RS_x$; the other hypothesis says that $M$ is flat over $R$. Hence [lem-weil-pbc-criterion-flatness-fibre], applied to $R\xrightarrow{\alpha}S_x\xrightarrow{\mathrm{id}}S_x$ and $M$ (its $S$ and $S'$ both $S_x$), gives that $\mathcal F_x=M$ is flat over $\mathcal O_{X,x}$.
