### Hypotheses, constructions, and clauses

There are no mathematical restrictions on X or on its structure map.
`universe u`, `Scheme.{u}`, and `Profinite.{u}` implement the small-site size
convention, with `Ab.{u + 1}` for coefficients. There is no added field,
nonemptiness, quasi-compactness, properness or finiteness assumption.

* The point site is `coherentTopology Profinite.{u}`. The scheme site is
  `Scheme.ProEt.topology X` on `X.ProEt`, the exact recorded Mathlib declaration.
* The terminal base is `specZ = Spec (.of (ULift.{u} ℤ))`, the size-adjusted
  integer spectrum. `toSpecZ X = specULiftZIsTerminal.from X` is the structure
  map. The first conjunct quantifies every `X`, `Y` and `f` and states
  `f ≫ toSpecZ Y = toSpecZ X`, with Lean's order of composition.
* Part 1, the ring and its morphisms: `profiniteToCommRingCatOp.obj S` is
  `op (CommRingCat.of (LocallyConstant S ℤ))`. Its map is
  `(CommRingCat.ofHom (LocallyConstant.comapRingHom f.hom.hom)).op`.
  Thus it is literally precomposition with the continuous map. The ring
  operations are Mathlib's pointwise operations.
* `profiniteToScheme = profiniteToCommRingCatOp ⋙ Scheme.Spec` gives exactly
  the scheme in the node. `profiniteToProEt` packages its structure map as a
  weakly etale object, with maps `MorphismProperty.Over.homMk` of the same
  scheme maps. Its definition supplies identities and composition.
* The second conjunct is weak etaleness of the structure map for every S.
  The finite-set natural-isomorphism conjunct identifies the stages of
  `FintypeCat.toProfinite ⋙ profiniteToScheme` with `sigmaConst.obj specZ`:
  the coproduct of copies of Spec Z indexed by the given finite set.
  `Nonempty (IsLimit (profiniteToScheme.mapCone S.asLimitCone))` is the limit
  formula for every S. `S.asLimitCone` is Mathlib's specified cone on all
  discrete finite quotients, with projections the quotient maps. Together
  these assert the canonical inverse-limit formula, including the projections.
  The construction itself is fixed, rather than chosen from an existential
  description of a scheme.
* The following three conjuncts are respectively
  `PreservesFiniteLimits profiniteToProEt`, `CoverPreserving` for the coherent
  and pro-etale topologies, and `profiniteToProEt.IsContinuous` for those
  topologies. The coherent topology is generated by finite jointly surjective
  families, so the covering assertion includes precisely those families.
* Part 2: `profiniteToProEtOf X` is definitionally `profiniteToProEt` followed
  by `MorphismProperty.Over.pullback @WeaklyEtale ⊤ (toSpecZ X)`.
  Its objects are the stated fiber products, over X. For every X, the next
  conjunct identifies its finite stages in `Over X` with the coproducts of
  `Over.mk (𝟙 X)`. The accompanying `IsLimit` of its image of `S.asLimitCone`
  states the relative limit formula over X. This also fixes the comparison
  with all finite-quotient projections; it is not only an abstract isomorphism
  between unspecified limit objects.
* Part 3, direct image: `pointLowerStar` is exactly
  `profiniteToProEt.sheafPushforwardContinuous Ab ...`, hence restriction on
  objects and arrows and on morphisms of sheaves. In particular its sections
  are G(Spec LC(S,Z)).
* Part 3, inverse image: `pointUpperStar` is exactly
  `Functor.sheafPullbackConstruction.sheafPullback profiniteToProEt Ab ...`.
  Reading the Mathlib body gives `sheafToPresheaf ⋙ profiniteToProEt.op.lan ⋙
  presheafToSheaf`. Thus the construction in the node is reused literally.
  `Nonempty (pointUpperStar ⊣ pointLowerStar)` states the specified adjunction.
  Its next two conjuncts are strong monoidality (`Functor.Monoidal`) and
  braiding compatibility (`Functor.Braided`).
* The tensor structures are the localized objectwise coefficient tensor from
  `Sheaf.CategoryTheory.SheafTensor`, specifically

### Edge cases

The empty profinite set has the zero ring of locally constant functions and
empty spectrum. Its unique finite quotient is empty, giving empty coproduct
stages and the stated limit, so no nonempty assumption is introduced.
For the singleton profinite set, LC is ℤ and the test scheme is X, as required.
The finite-set description includes the empty finite set. The coherent cover
condition includes the empty covering family of the empty object.
The empty scheme X is allowed: all its test objects and coproduct stages are
empty. The quantified statements do not exclude it. Arbitrary non-quasi-compact
schemes and arbitrary scheme morphisms remain in scope. Zero abelian sheaves
and arbitrary characteristic impose no extra restrictions. Finite quotients
are in the same universe as S; the site morphism and object universes are not
identified with one another. Coefficients are correctly enlarged to u+1.
