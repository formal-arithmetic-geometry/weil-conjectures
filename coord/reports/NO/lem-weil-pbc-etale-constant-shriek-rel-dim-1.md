## Argument intended for the new geometric node

This is explanatory material for the next worker; the new node itself has no proof, proof dependencies or proof sketch inside its statement.

For each s in T_i, smoothness and geometric connectedness imply that the nonempty fibre (C_i)_s is integral. One can use `lem-sbc-smooth-connected-generic-geometry` over Spec kappa(s), a normal integral base whose only point is its generic point. A nonempty open (O_i)_s in this integral fibre is dense. Its proper closed complement has dimension at most zero: a chain of two irreducible closed subsets in that complement, followed by the whole integral fibre, would have length two, contradicting dimension at most one. For dimension zero the complement is empty. The complement fibre is a closed subscheme of a finite-type scheme over kappa(s), so `lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-dim-zero-artinian` makes it finite discrete, regardless of any nilpotents in that fibre.

For b in B, the finite etale fibre (T_i)_b is a finite disjoint union of spectra of finite separable extensions of kappa(b), by `lem-weil-pbc-etale-fibre-discrete-separable-points` and quasi-compactness of a finite morphism. The fibre Z_b is the finite disjoint union, over these points and the finite set I, of the boundary fibres just considered. Thus it is finite discrete. The reduced induced boundary has this complement as its support even when base change produces nilpotents. The total C is separated and of finite type over B, as a finite disjoint union of proper schemes over finite B-schemes, and so is its closed subscheme Z. The definition `pqf-def-quasifinite` applies to its isolated fibre points. Because B is Noetherian, finite-type coordinate algebras are finitely presented; separatedness supplies quasi-separatedness and finite type supplies quasi-compactness. Hence Z to B is of finite presentation. This argument supplies the assertion hidden by 'Again' in 0GKX.

The hypotheses matter: geometric connectedness prevents an open from meeting only one of two curve components; dimension at most one prevents a divisor in a surface from being a positive-dimensional boundary; the finite etale hypothesis makes the intermediate fibres finite discrete; Noetherianity supplies finite presentation for the reduced boundary. The statement introduces all schemes and maps and uses existing definition nodes for each notion.
