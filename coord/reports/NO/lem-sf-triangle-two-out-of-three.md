## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported node and the triangle clause of unit `sk-lem-sf-determination` omit compatibility of the natural transformation with the chosen shift isomorphisms of the two exact functors. Exactness of the functors does not impose this compatibility on every natural transformation between them.

There is an explicit counterexample. Let K be a field and C = D^b(fdVect_K). The cohomology functor identifies its underlying category with finite-support graded vector spaces: the components of a morphism are arbitrary degreewise linear maps, since higher Ext groups of vector spaces vanish. Take both functors to be the identity with its canonical exact structure. Define a natural endomorphism alpha of the identity to be identity on H^0 and zero on every other degree. Degreewise linear maps commute with this operation, so alpha is natural on all of C. The standard cone triangle of K[0] -> 0 is

    K[0] -> 0 -> K[1] --id--> K[1].

Its first component alpha_K[0] is identity, and its second component on the zero object is an isomorphism (the unique map 0 -> 0 is also its identity and inverse). Its third component alpha_K[1] is zero on the nonzero object K[1], so is not invertible. With cohomological indexing H^(-1)(K[1]) = K, which also fixes the shift convention explicitly. This example works over a coefficient field E occurring in the six-functor setting as well.

The failed inference is the last square of the purported map of image triangles. For shift structures phi and psi it needs

    alpha_X[1] o phi_X o Phi(w) = psi_X o Psi(w) o alpha_Z.

Naturality provides only alpha_X[1 as object] o Phi(w) = Psi(w) o alpha_Z; it does not identify alpha at the shifted object with the shift of alpha_X. In the counterexample these two endomorphisms of K[1] are respectively zero and identity. The corrected statement supplies exactly

    psi_X o alpha_(X[1]) = alpha_X[1] o phi_X.

This is the missing premise in the source's intended “map of triangles”, and the premise visibly assumed by the source implementation. It is now written as a mathematical equation, with all its objects introduced, rather than left as an unexplained Lean class name. The categories, exact functors, arbitrary distinguished triangle, and all three choices of the two known vertices are retained. The unused “In the setting of [def-sf-datum]” preamble and its statement dependency are removed: this is a general categorical lemma, its proof invokes no six-functor axiom, and the corrected statement introduces every object and hypothesis directly. It continues to apply to the six-functor categories in the source setting.

The imported proof also assumes cohomology functors without restricting the categories to derived categories. No such family is part of the stated general triangulated setting. The existing Stacks-014A Mathlib leaf is the categorical five lemma and supplies the intended argument in that generality. Finally, “a morphism whose cone has vanishing cohomology is zero” is erroneous wording: in a derived category its cone is zero and the morphism is an isomorphism; the identity of a nonzero object is already a counterexample to the literal wording. The replacement proof uses neither that sentence nor a nonexistent cohomology detection hypothesis.
