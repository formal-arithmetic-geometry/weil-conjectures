### Argument retained for lem-trf-prepared-generic-fibre-nonreduced

T is integral, hence irreducible by def-pc-integral-scheme. The universal homeomorphism q maps its generic point xi to a generic point of U (identity base change in def-weil-universal-homeomorphism). A nonempty open U of irreducible Y is dense: every nonempty open of Y meets U, by irreducibility. The closure of q(xi) in Y contains U and hence Y. By lem-sc-scheme-sober its generic point is eta. Apply lem-trf-geometric-reduced-fibre-locus-base-change to f and T -> U -> Y. Its statement is about reduced geometric fibres, so use lem-trf-geometric-reducedness-algebraic-closure-test on both fibres to obtain that P_xi is geometrically reduced iff X_eta is. The latter is false. Apply lem-pur-reduction-generic-fibre to h:P -> T at xi (the unique irreducible component of T is T), identifying R_xi with (P_xi)_red as schemes over kappa(xi). If P_xi were reduced, the canonical reduction map would be an isomorphism: on affines its map is quotient by the zero nilradical, by def-weil-pbc-scheme-reduction and def-pc-reduced-scheme. Its geometrically reduced reduction R_xi would then make P_xi geometrically reduced, a contradiction. Thus P_xi is nonreduced. This argument does not assume flatness of P -> T, which preparation does not provide.

### Argument retained for lem-trf-universal-homeomorphism-nonreduced-fibre-spreading

The projection h:P -> T is locally finite type by lem-weil-pbc-lft-base-change and quasi-compact by lem-pc-quasi-compact-base-change; hence finite type by def-weil-finite-type-morphism. Apply lem-trf-nonreduced-fibre-open with the given irreducible T, generic point xi, and nonreduced P_xi. Its statement imposes no Noetherianity, flatness, or reducedness condition on T. It gives a nonempty open W with all P_t nonreduced. Since q is a homeomorphism by def-weil-universal-homeomorphism, V=q(W) is nonempty open in U, and hence in Y. For y in V choose t in W with q(t)=y. By def-pc-scheme-fibre and def-pc-fibre-product, P_t is canonically X_y base changed from kappa(y) to kappa(t): both represent a map to X and a map to Spec kappa(t) agreeing over Y. The residue-field map is a field extension by def-weil-residue-field. If X_y were geometrically reduced, the kappa(t) case in def-pc-geometrically-irreducible would make P_t reduced, a contradiction. This proves all conclusions about W and its image V, with exactly the original final fibre-product argument.
