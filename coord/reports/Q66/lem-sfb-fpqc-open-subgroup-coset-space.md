## Review by Q66

I read the open-relation statement and proof in Stacks stacks-morphisms.tex 0DU1 and the free-action quotient in bootstrap.tex 06PH. On the representing cover the relation is literally the inverse image of the open H under (y,x) -> y x^{-1}; the maps of its groupoid are left multiplication, inverse and product. Thus both sources apply with exactly the stipulated flat and locally finitely presented objects. Neither assumes H normal. I retained the latest blueprint route through direct-images proof (3), the trait H-orbit construction, and did not substitute a trace or purity route.

For the first new node I read Rydh (5.17) and (5.3), distinguishing object descent from morphism descent and the former's quasi-compact-object hypothesis. Here is the agent construction that justifies the trait-specific extension, for the next worker who will split it. Refine the cover of affine S to affine T. Descent over the generic and closed fields gives etale schemes E_eta and E_s, arbitrary disjoint unions of spectra of finite separable fields. One can see field effectivity by choosing a field-valued point Spec L -> T_f: after pullback to T_f x_f Spec L the object is pulled back from the etale L-scheme; its separatedness and local quasi-finiteness descend and Stacks 0APK realizes it as a scheme over f. Etale algebraic spaces over fields are schemes, so this applies also to the original space datum. Field etaleness descends by flat descent on scheme charts.

For any finite union C of components of E_s, its pullback C' in E'_s is quasi-compact because T_s is affine. Choose a quasi-compact open W' of E' containing C'. The principal generic open of W' is quasi-compact, so its image in E_eta meets only finitely many components; choose a finite union D containing that image. Let Z' be the schematic closure in E' of E'_eta minus D'. Schematic closure commutes with flat base change here: the generic inclusion is quasi-compact (invert a uniformizer), and on affine charts the defining ideal is the kernel of the map to the corresponding localized quotient, a kernel preserved by flat tensor product. Consequently the closure is invariant under the overlap datum. Set U' = E' minus the union of Z' and (E'_s minus C'). Both removed sets are closed. The open W' avoids the generic set whose closure is Z', so Z' misses C'. Thus U' has special fibre C' and generic fibre D'. Both are quasi-compact, being pullbacks of finite etale schemes to the affine fibres of T; their two underlying subsets cover U', which is therefore quasi-compact. Its relative quasi-separatedness is inherited from E'/T. This U' is descent-stable, by flat compatibility of the closure. It descends by Rydh (5.17), since an affine faithfully flat cover is quasi-compact universally subtrusive. Such U' cover the special fibre; finite unions of generic-only components provide the remaining generic opens. Their intersections are quasi-compact by quasi-separatedness. Rydh (5.3) descends their overlap inclusions and cocycles and identifies them as open immersions; glue the descended spaces. This gives effectivity without making the whole E' quasi-compact. This is a substantive general trait lemma, not a restatement of a coset-space clause.
