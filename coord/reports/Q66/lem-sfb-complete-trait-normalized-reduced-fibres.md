## New node proof retained for its future worker

For lem-sfb-geonormal-normalization-field-extension follow varieties.tex, Tag 0C3N, proof of lemma-normalization-and-change-of-fields, especially the displayed tensor comparison and following normality paragraph. On affine charts, finite birational normalization B/A is an isomorphism after inverting a nonzerodivisor: choose finitely many module generators of B, write them in Q(A), and multiply their denominators. Under field extension this denominator stays regular by flatness. Hence the base-changed finite birational ring has the same total quotient ring as A_E. It is normal by geometric normality, so is its full integral closure. This comparison on principal localizations is the same inclusion of fractions, so glues and is compatible with towers. The statement uses the finite-module hypothesis explicitly; it assumes neither that A is a domain nor that geometric reducedness means geometric integrality. This argument is recorded here, not in the unsplit node's statement or proof fields.
