### Old Step 1 → parent Step 1; affine qcqs input of lem-trf-invertible-proper-noetherian-models

Choose an affine open neighbourhood $X^0$ of $x$. The restricted morphism $Y^0=f^{-1}(X^0)\to X^0$ is proper by [lem-weil-pbc-proper-base-change]. Its fibre over $x$ and the restricted invertible module give the original fibre and module. It suffices to produce the required open $U\subseteq X^0$, since every affine open of $U$ and its inverse image are the same in the original schemes. Rename this restriction $f:Y\to X$. The affine base $X$ is quasi-compact and quasi-separated by [lem-weil-pbc-affine-scheme-qcqs].
