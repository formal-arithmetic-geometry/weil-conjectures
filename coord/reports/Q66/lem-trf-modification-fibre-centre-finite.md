Since both fibres have pure dimension one, a point above a fibre-generic point must itself have transcendence degree one, hence be one of the finitely many component generics. Step 5 then proves O intersection K equals V localized at its contracted prime by an explicit unit/nonunit argument; the residue assertion uses the dimension/transcendence-degree theorem with dimension exactly one. Thus no unproved residue-field identification is hidden.
