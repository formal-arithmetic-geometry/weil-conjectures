### Original step 5: lem-trf-isomorphism-multiplication-cone-descent

For each $n$ the termwise map $j_n:C_n\to GD_n$ is multiplication $x\otimes a\mapsto ax$, with the scalar action transported through $c_n$. Its inverse is $i_n:x\mapsto x\otimes1$. Indeed $j_ni_n(x)=x$ and $i_nj_n(x\otimes a)=ax\otimes1=x\otimes a$ by balancing. These maps respect restrictions, so the formulas pass from the presheaf tensors to their sheafifications [def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product]. They are $\widehat R_X$-linear because $\pi_{X,n}c=c_nG(\pi_{Y,n})$, and commute with differentials. On a transition, both composites send $x\otimes a$ to $\bar a\,G(\tau_n)x$, since the coefficient structure maps commute with reduction. Thus $j:C\xrightarrow{\sim}GD$ is an isomorphism of strict tower complexes, not only a list of derived isomorphisms. It is natural in chain maps $Q\to Q'$.
