## Source error

I read `literature/stacks-project/weil.tex`, Tag 0FI4 (`section-chern`), Tag 0FI5 (`proposition-chern-class`) and Tag 0FI7 (`lemma-splitting-principle`). The line-kernel paragraph in the proof of 0FI5 prints the polynomial as a sum of terms `(-1)^i h^i c_i(F)`. As printed it is not the relation of the preceding definition: for a rank-one quotient it becomes `1 - h c_1(F)`, whereas its defining relation is `h - c_1(F)`. Taking F trivial gives the displayed wrong polynomial equal to 1, whose restriction to P(F) is nonzero, for example over Spec(k) with nonzero finite coefficients. I use the homogeneous monic relation `P_G(v) = sum_i (-1)^i c_i(G) v^(rank(G)-i)` throughout, including all base pullbacks. This agrees with the defining relation after multiplying the Stacks convention by its overall sign.

The later general-exact-sequence paragraph also prints both E' and F' as E/L and gives incompatible ranks. The intended quotients are E/L and F/L, of ranks r-1 and s-1. This split does not need that paragraph at all: its induction is on the given filtration, first taking its first line subbundle and then the quotient filtration. Thus no general Whitney lemma is introduced to conceal the item.

These are source-proof corrections, not a statement dispute and not a departure from the purity chapter or unit route. The main-20261015 localized-top-Chern proof, Step 4 (L4), requires the ordinary Chern-root equality; that is exactly the equality supplied here. The item is not the key statement of sk-prop-an, so no unit realized fields are changed.
