## Review by Q26

I checked the item against the sources above and reviewed delivery-errors B through E in both copies of those sections. This is a definition, so there are no numbered proof steps, no proof-step citations to verify and no long non-elementary steps. citations.json is empty for that reason. I did not replace the lifting condition by surjectivity on isomorphism classes: zeta', its marking and the specified comparison over A all remain quantified data, and the lifted comparison must restrict to that exact comparison. Arbitrary surjections of marked Artinian tests remain allowed, not only square-zero extensions.

I opened every statement dependency. def-weil-field supplies the nonzero field; def-weil-affine-spectrum supplies contravariant affine test maps; def-pc-scheme supplies schemes; def-sfb-stable-pointed-moduli-groupoids explicitly defines a category fibred in groupoids, its pullback arrows and their isomorphisms; def-weil-size-convention supplies the fixed universes; def-weil-pbc-noetherian-ring supplies the Noetherian predicate; def-weil-pbc-adic-completion defines completeness as bijectivity onto compatible residue sequences. The added lem-weil-pbc-artinian-local-noetherian-nilpotent states that every commutative Artinian local ring has a positive nilpotence exponent, with no condition on R or the degree of k. Its full statement, not an argument hidden in its proof, justifies N_A. Locality gives v(m_C) contained in m_A, so v(m_C^N_A)=0 by multiplication; the quotient map then exists. Evaluation comparisons are the specified coherent tower comparisons after pullback to a common higher level, not a claim that two chosen fibre objects are literally equal. No theorem about algebraic stacks, deformation smoothness, or effectivity is used to define the datum.

I tested the boundaries: A=k gives N_A=1 and evaluation at the marked first level; A=k[e]/(e^2) gives N_A=2; an identity test surjection requires extending the given comparison itself; C=k has a constant tower; the indexing is always positive, so C/m^0 never appears. The specified residue isomorphisms are R-algebra isomorphisms, and maps preserve them rather than choosing coefficient-field sections. The zero ring cannot be a marked local algebra because its residue would not be the field k. For a groupoid with automorphisms the prescribed comparison is retained, which is essential to the source diagrams.
