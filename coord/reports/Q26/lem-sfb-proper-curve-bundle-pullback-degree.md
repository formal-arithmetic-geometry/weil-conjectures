## Review by Q26

I read 0AYV at `varieties.tex:9669`, 089W at `coherent.tex:202`, 01E8 at `cohomology.tex:13843`, and the finiteness/coherence sources below, rather than relying on the blueprint's short proof.

For Step 1 I opened all five cited statements. `lem-weil-pbc-proper-over-separated` really concludes properness of f when X is proper over k and Y separated over k; I checked Morphisms 01W6, label `lemma-image-proper-scheme-closed`. `def-pc-curve` supplies precisely the integral separated finite-type dimension-one hypotheses. `lem-trf-birational-curve-quasifinite` actually states the dominant finite-generic-extension case, not only birationality; its hypothesis of local finite type follows from the just-proved properness. I read its source, Varieties 0AB6, label `lemma-quasi-finite-in-codim-1`. `pqf-lem-proper-lqf-finite` explicitly concludes finite from proper locally quasi-finite. The finite-morphism definition states affineness, which is all that the later cohomology argument needs.

For Step 2 I checked that finite type over the Noetherian field spectrum is enough for the cited Noetherianity theorem (Morphisms 01T6, label `lemma-finite-type-noetherian`). Invertible pullback is precisely clause (1) of `lem-weil-pbc-invertible-pullback-tensor`; I read Modules 0B8L, label `lemma-pullback-invertible`. The definition of an invertible module includes quasi-coherence and local rank one. Those give finite type in one generator, so `pqf-lem-coherent-ft-locnoeth`, read against Cohomology of Schemes 01XZ (`lemma-coherent-Noetherian`), applies to both structure and invertible sheaves. I did not infer coherence on a non-Noetherian scheme.

For Step 3 I opened `lem-weil-pbc-finite-pushforward-coherent` and read Cohomology of Schemes 01Y6 (`lemma-finite-pushforward-coherent`). Its required target local Noetherianity and source sheaf coherence are exactly Step 2, and its conclusion is coherence of F, not a generic-rank statement. I therefore added the latter's missing input as the new comparison node. I checked that node against 0AYZ's first proof sentence, together with the explanation of finite degree in Morphisms 02NX (`lemma-finite-degree`) and 02NY: on nonempty affine charts the comparison is S localized at R minus zero, with R -> S finite and injective domains, which is the fraction field of S. Indeed this localized domain is integral over the field Frac(R), hence a field, and has the same fraction field as S. This is the argument to split later, not a proof hidden in its statement. Alternatively the finite map is closed and its generic fibre has just the source generic point (02NX), so inverse images of target generic neighbourhoods are cofinal among source generic neighbourhoods. The germ map respects the actual generic stalk scalar map, not an arbitrary field embedding. Once this algebra isomorphism is cited, equality of dimensions and the field-degree definition in `def-pc-field-tower` are elementary.

Step 4 is a direct two-line local-basis calculation, with its overlap check included. I opened each of its three definition statements. Pullback has the adjunction unit used for f*e, the sheaf tensor is the sheafification of presheaf tensor products, and invertibility gives a local basis. The map m tensor a -> a f*m is bilinear and restricts on every trivializing open to the identity on f_*O_X; changing a basis by a unit changes source and target by that same unit. Thus this elementary step proves the comparison, rather than citing a definition as if it were a projection-formula theorem. I read 01E8's proof and supplied its omitted local calculation explicitly. No new node is warranted for this small argument.

For Step 5 I opened the affine-cohomology edge node: it really states vanishing of positive module direct images and isomorphisms H^p(Y,f_*N) -> H^p(X,N) for every p, with global-section scalar compatibility. Affineness is Step 1, quasi-coherence Step 2. Restriction of scalars along k and the k-morphism compatibility give k-linearity, so the Euler-characteristic definition justifies equality of alternating dimensions, not merely an equality of underlying abelian groups. I added an explicit second citation of `lem-weil-pbc-finite-pushforward-coherent` here to establish coherence of f_*f*M before taking its Euler characteristic, rather than leaving this as an implicit reuse of the Step 3 theorem. This uses the statement of the edge node, not its internal proof.
