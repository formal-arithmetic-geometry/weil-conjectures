## The argument and route

The input is an equivalence v:A→B strictly over S, with both A→S and B→S isofibrations. The proof factors the graph A→A×_S B into a trivial cofibration A→Z and a Joyal fibration Z→A×_S B. Both composites Z→A and Z→B are fibrations; their composites with A→Z are respectively the identity and v, so two-out-of-three makes them trivial fibrations. Their pullbacks along any R→S remain trivial fibrations by their right lifting property. The identity factorization then makes R×_S A→R×_S Z a weak equivalence, and its composite to R×_S B is exactly the requested induced map. This is a proof of the item, with the graph construction and all equations in its own steps; nothing is moved into a differently named pullback theorem.

I read the full source statement and proof of Kerodon 03PM, together with the statements of its cited 033L and 033T. They confirm exactly the two isofibration hypotheses and that no hypothesis on h or v as fibrations is needed. I chose the explicit factorization argument below the node level, which SETUP.md permits. I do not invoke or restate 033L/033T as hidden lemmas. There is no assertion that the Joyal model structure is right proper; the argument uses only pullbacks of trivial fibrations.
