## Intended proof of lem-sfb-finite-scalar-model-ordinary-quotient-comparison

Work locally on a pro-étale trivializing cover of M. Write M=B tensor over constant A with constant P. The scalar ring A is a Noetherian discrete valuation ring; for finitely presented P its torsion submodule is finitely generated and killed by a single power pi^N. The flatness of B over constant A, from lem-sfb-integral-quotient-resolution-normalization, shows that every M[pi^n] is killed by this same pi^N on this chart. This uses the bound for this chart only, not a bound on all charts.

The fibre of the strict cone-to-quotient map e_n has its only cohomology in degree -1, where it is M[pi^n]. Its transition from index n+1 to n is multiplication by pi: the degree -1 component of the displayed transition (pi,1). Canonical truncation identifies this fibre tower with the torsion-kernel tower shifted by one. Consequently, for each n the transition from m to n is zero whenever m-n is at least N. Its total derived inverse limit vanishes by lem-weil-pbc-ringed-strict-tower-homotopy-limit, and Rlim e is an isomorphism on this chart.

Restriction to a covering pro-étale object is exact and preserves products, and module products on these sites are exact; the normalized comparison of lem-cbc-exact-product-functor-strict-limit thus identifies restriction of Rlim e with this local calculation. Covering restrictions are conservative, so Rlim e is an isomorphism globally. The map e is defined on actual complexes and commutes with every module map and the degree-zero identity augmentations. This proves precisely the naturality and quotient-augmentation clauses. No completion assertion was inserted into this lemma.

Tests: M=0 gives zero towers. For a free model the kernel vanishes levelwise. For M=B/pi the kernel is nonzero at every level, but its transitions are zero; thus the proof allows torsion and does not falsely identify individual reductions with ordinary quotients. The same calculation with pi^N handles arbitrary bounded scalar torsion, including mixed free/torsion P. Different covering charts may have different N. The empty scheme presents no exception.
