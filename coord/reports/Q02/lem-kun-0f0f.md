## What was wrong

The imported proof asserted that every abelian etale sheaf is a quotient of a direct sum of copies of the constant sheaf Z, hence has a globally free-sheaf resolution. This is false. On Spec R, the sheaf corresponding to Z with the sign action of Gal(C/R) has no invariant sections, so every map from a direct sum of constant Z's is zero and cannot surject onto it. Even a bounded-below complex with free terms does not follow from finite sums and shifts: arbitrary sums and passage through truncations would need justification. I replaced the entire node-level proof by the source's stalk argument, keeping the unit's explicitly requested integral projection formula.

The old statement dependencies named the invertible-orders definition, although this statement permits all torsion including residue-characteristic torsion. I instead cite the proper-morphism definition, the actual bounded-below derived-category definition (which defines torsion cohomology), and the projection-map definition. No invertibility or constructibility hypothesis is introduced.
