## Findings and corrections

The inherited statement already carefully separates the proper all-sheaf cohomological-dimension bound from the quasi-excellent constructible-input bound. I retained that distinction and every hypothesis. Bhatt--Scholze Theorem 6.7.1 says more about cohomological dimension in its nonproper alternative than is needed here. I read ILO Introduction Theorem 1 and XIII Theorem 1.1.1 and 1.1.2 to use precisely eventual vanishing for a fixed constructible sheaf. I do not propagate an unverified uniform bound over arbitrary sheaves on an infinite-dimensional quasi-excellent base. There is no statement dispute in the delivered item.

Several tempting existing nodes do not apply: `thm-weil-pbc-direct-image-finiteness-proper` and `lem-weil-pbc-etale-proper-direct-image-finiteness` require Noetherian finite-dimensional schemes and invertibility; `lem-fin-proper-bounded-constructible` requires finite type over a field. Instead the proper case uses the general 0GL0 compactification-constructibility node with the identity compactification, the arbitrary-base 0A3V fibre bound, and the unrestricted torsion 095U estimate. I added the explicit module-to-abelian comparison, rather than assuming that forgetting scalars preserves injectives.

The nonproper part does not assume separatedness. I first prove the separated case via Nagata and the smaller open-immersion input, then write the relative two-open injective-complex calculation and the finite affine-cover induction. Intersections of affines are used only as quasi-compact opens, not as affine schemes. The compactifying proper map is locally finitely presented by `pqf-lem-lft-fp-locnoeth`; its properness supplies the remaining finite-presentation conditions. No new node is an elementary truncation/cover step, and neither new statement duplicates a whole alternative of the item: the open lemma needs genuine compactification, composition and nonseparated gluing before it proves that alternative.
