## Source error

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported rational statement and its opening proof paragraph do not justify the cutoff of the original immersion after refining the source. The retained delivered Step 1 repeated that error. Target refinement retains each fixed source cutoff, but source refinement replaces the original `a` by `a_k:S_k→X`, each with its own closure `Z_k` and complement `j_k`. The integral estimate gives `c_{a_k}=cd_l(j_k)+cd_l(S_k)+2`. Contravariant Ext induction gives the maximum of these numbers; it gives no inequality `c_{a_k}≤c_a`.

The maintainer's test refutes precisely that missing inequality. Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic different from l, pinch two distinct points of the affine plane to one point P and take `X=S` with `a=id`. The connected infinite Z-chain torsor has a rank-one lisse rational sheaf whose generator acts by multiplication by l. A lattice on its connected trivializing chain would be a constant valuation lattice, which multiplication by l does not preserve. Thus a source lattice refinement is genuinely necessary. Here the original cutoff is at most `4+cd_l(k)`, whereas the point stratum has `cd_l(j_P)≥3`: the punctured two-dimensional regular germs have nonzero degree-three cohomology, equivalently nonzero degree-four local cohomology at their origins by purity. Hence its cutoff is at least `5+cd_l(k)`. Refinement does not preserve the numerical original cutoff. This is a counterexample to the inference used by the proof, rather than a claim that we have exhibited a nonzero rational Ext group above every possible unrefined bound.

Correction implemented: choose a source lattice stratification once, take the maximum of the cutoffs of its actual refined strata, and state explicitly that the original cutoff is recovered when a finite coefficient model has a global source lattice. The source stratification and coefficient model are fixed before any target is chosen. For Qbar, restriction of scalars lets the fixed E_0 bound handle every finite coefficient stage without a new source refinement. This preserves the intended uniform-Ext theorem, whose existential bound may depend on its source sheaf; it corrects the unjustified particular numerical formula.
