## Source error — STATEMENT-DISPUTED

The original claim says that cohomology bounds `A in D^{>=a}` and `B in D^{>=b}` imply `A tensor^L B in D^{>=a+b}` for arbitrary commutative coefficients. Take `T = Spec(C)`, `Lambda = Z/4Z`, `A = B = (Z/2Z)[0]`, and `a=b=0`. The etale topos of this separably closed point gives the ordinary module example. A free resolution of `Z/2Z` has `Z/4Z` in every nonpositive degree, differential multiplication by 2 in negative degrees, and augmentation reduction modulo 2. It is exact in negative degrees because both the kernel and image of multiplication by 2 are `{0,2}`. Tensoring with `Z/2Z` makes every differential zero. Consequently `H^{-1}(A tensor^L B) = Z/2Z`, and indeed all its nonpositive cohomology groups are `Z/2Z`. This contradicts the proposed bound even with a finite coefficient ring. A field-coefficient example would conceal this error.

The original displayed abutment degree `p+q` and the ensuing inequality are therefore invalid. Merely changing the sign does not prove a uniform lower bound in this example.
