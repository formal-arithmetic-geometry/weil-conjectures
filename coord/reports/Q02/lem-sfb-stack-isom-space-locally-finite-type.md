5. `lem-weil-pbc-flat-lfp-open` states openness under precisely the flat and locally-finitely-presented hypotheses established in step 4. `lem-weil-pbc-affine-scheme-qcqs` supplies quasi-compactness for each affine open of the target. The finite subcover of the open images therefore verifies literally the finite-affine-image condition in `def-weil-pbc-fpqc-covering`. I checked that this proves singleton fpqc covering, not that the entire morphism is quasi-compact. This distinction matters for arbitrary atlases.
6. The lift isomorphisms conjugate an Isom element into the relation and back. The two displayed formulas are mutually inverse and natural for every test-scheme map, including nonreduced tests. `def-trf-algebraic-space` makes these natural maps morphisms of spaces, and `lem-trf-space-fibre-products-etale-covers` supplies their fibre products. 7. `lem-sfb-stack-finite-type-calculus` explicitly states stability under arbitrary base change between DM stacks and explicitly identifies its algebraic-space notions with the ordinary notions. I checked this clause rather than assuming the lemma applies to all algebraic stacks. The spaces involved are DM when viewed as discrete fibred groupoids: the existing space and groupoid definitions provide sheaf descent, representable diagonal and an etale atlas. This observation is included in the step. The result is applied only to the algebraic-space relation map and its pullback.
8. `lem-trf-space-chartwise-etale-permanence` explicitly preserves etaleness under arbitrary algebraic-space base change, so a scheme chart of I stays a chart after each base change. `def-pc-fibre-product` ensures Z_1 and Z_2 are schemes, and the finite-type convention tests the claimed property on those charts. `lem-weil-pbc-fpqc-descend-locally-finite-type` states exactly descent for scheme maps along an fpqc covering; I use it on Z_1/T_1 and then Z/T, with the covers proved in step 5.
