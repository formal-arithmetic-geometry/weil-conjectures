## Source error

In the proof of 084P the text says that $X\times_YX$ is “of finite type” over $X$. Only **locally** of finite type follows from its hypothesis, and only that is needed by 05WT. For example, take $X$ to be an infinite disjoint union of copies of $\operatorname{Spec}k$ and $Y=\operatorname{Spec}k$. Then $f$ is locally of finite type, while a fibre of the second projection is the infinite disjoint union and is not quasi-compact, so the projection is not of finite type. Our step 2 proves exactly local finite type. This is a source-proof typo, not a statement dispute: the imported statement already says the correct 084P first assertion, and is unchanged.
