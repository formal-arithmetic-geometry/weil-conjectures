## Original returned proof, retained in full

### Returned step 1

Suppose $f$ is smooth on an open neighbourhood $U$ of $x$. Apply [sbc-lem-smooth-criterion] to $f|_U:U\to S$: its fibre $U_s\to\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)$ is smooth. By [lem-weil-pbc-open-fibre-product], $U_s$ identifies with the open subscheme of $X_s$ lying above $U$, compatibly with the morphism to $\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)$. This open contains the point corresponding to $x$ by [def-pc-scheme-fibre]. It is therefore the required smooth neighbourhood in the fibre.

Uses: sbc-lem-smooth-criterion, lem-weil-pbc-open-fibre-product, def-pc-scheme-fibre.

Source: literature/stacks-project/morphisms.tex, Tag 01V9, lemma-smooth-at-point, (1) implies (2); Tag 01VB, lemma-base-change-smooth.

### Returned step 2

Conversely suppose $X_s\to\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)$ is smooth at $x$. By [def-sbc-locally-finite-presentation] choose affine neighbourhoods $W=\operatorname{Spec}A\subset X$ of $x$ and $V=\operatorname{Spec}R\subset S$ of $s$, with $f(W)\subset V$ and $A$ finitely presented over $R$. Write $\varphi:R\to A$ for the induced map and $\mathfrak q\subset A$ for $x$. By [lem-weil-pbc-affine-opens-induced-ring-map-spec-square], $\mathfrak p=\varphi^{-1}(\mathfrak q)$ represents $s$. By [lem-weil-pbc-affine-open-stalk-localization] and [lem-weil-pbc-spec-map-stalk-localization], the local map $R_{\mathfrak p}\to A_{\mathfrak q}$ is the given stalk map under ring isomorphisms. It is flat by hypothesis; no flatness away from $x$ is asserted.

Uses: def-sbc-locally-finite-presentation, lem-weil-pbc-affine-opens-induced-ring-map-spec-square, lem-weil-pbc-affine-open-stalk-localization, lem-weil-pbc-spec-map-stalk-localization.
