Step 4 again uses the section-producing smooth-cover lemma, with target a scheme T. The closed-subspace correspondence states the ideal-vanishing factorization criterion for each scheme test. Step 5 uses the surjectivity and continuity assertions of `lem-sfb-stack-point-topology`, including agreement with space and scheme topology, and the closure-of-range clause of `lemma-nagata-scheme-image-ideal-qc`.
