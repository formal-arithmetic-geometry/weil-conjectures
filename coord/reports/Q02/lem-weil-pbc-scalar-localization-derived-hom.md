## Proof of the new ordinary exactness lemma

Let P be the presheaf V ↦ R(V)[1/s|V], regarded as an R-module presheaf. Sectionwise tensor with P is localization: x tensor (a/s^n) ↦ ax/s^n has inverse x/s^n ↦ x tensor (1/s^n), with both identities following from balancing and the fraction relation. Ordinary module localization is exact by `lem-weil-pbc-localization-module-exact` (Stacks algebra.tex, Tag 00CS, proposition-localization-exact). Presheaf finite limits are computed sectionwise, so tensoring with P preserves them. By `lem-pc-sites-modules-sheafification-flat` (Tag 03ET, lemma-flatness-sheafification), P# = R_s is flat as an R-module sheaf. Here R# = R since R is already a sheaf. Its tensor functor preserves finite limits and is exact by `def-pc-flat-sheaf-modules`.

The identification of the underlying R-module sheaf of E(M) with R_s tensor_R M is the canonical sheafified tensor comparison of `lem-pc-sites-module-sheafification-tensor` (Tag 0GMW, lemma-sheafification-tensor), applied to P and the sheaf M. Its section tensor formulas are natural in M and maps. Tensor is additive by `lem-pc-sheaf-modules-tensor-closed`; addition of R_s-linear maps is the same addition on underlying sheaves, so E is additive. Restriction of scalars is exact by `lem-pur-sheaf-ring-coefficient-ordinary-adjunction` and reflects isomorphisms: an invertible underlying module map has an R_s-linear inverse, by applying the inverse to f(bx)=bf(x). Applying restriction to a finite-limit comparison of E gives the invertible comparison for tensor with the flat sheaf R_s, so E preserves finite limits. The same argument for finite colimits, or the ordinary scalar-extension left adjunction from that node, gives finite colimits. Natural isomorphisms of functors preserve these properties. This proof has no derived, compactness or boundedness step.
