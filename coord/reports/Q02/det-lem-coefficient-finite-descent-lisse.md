## Source error

The blueprint's proof (1) says `g_kk = 1` on `V_k ×_X V_k`. This is false for covers whose arrows are not monomorphisms. For example, let `X = Spec(k)` and `V = X ⊔ X`, a single affine etale covering object, and let `L` be the trivial rank-one coefficient module. Choose its local trivialization to be multiplication by 1 on the first component and by 2 on the second. On the cross component of `V ×_X V` the repeated-index transition is multiplication by 2 (or its inverse according to orientation), not 1. It pulls back to 1 on the diagonal. The proof now keeps all repeated-index transition maps and uses their full cocycle equation; the existing site-gluing theorem explicitly permits equal indices and does not impose the erroneous identity. This correction leaves the statement and the unit's transition-matrix route intact. There is no disputed conclusion requiring a statement weakening.

The original surjectivity step lacked both section descent and gluing citations, and neither imported step established finite common coefficient fields across several different overlaps. The finished proof cites these explicitly, shows the overlap equation, and handles injectivity for representatives at two different finite fields rather than merely a single map whose extension is zero.
