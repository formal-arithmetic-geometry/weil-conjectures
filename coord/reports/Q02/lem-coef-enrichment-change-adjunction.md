## Source error

This is a subscript typo: the preceding sentence names the underlying functor F_[1]:V→W, and g:W→V cannot be right adjoint to the map between the [0]-fibers (both terminal for monoidal infinity categories). The last paragraph of the proof also identifies the underlying adjunction on [1]-fibers. The new operadic statement uses the correctly typed f:V→W, exactly as the imported item already does; this does not change the item's statement.
