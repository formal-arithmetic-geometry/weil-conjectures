FDDK\xrightarrow{c_{DK}}D'FDK\xrightarrow{D'(c_K^{-1})}D'D'FK.
$$
Uncurrying its composite with $F\delta_K$ gives the factor-interchanged evaluation above: use the evaluation equation for $K$ and $DK$, and naturality of evaluation for $c_K^{-1}$. It is the same transpose that defines $\delta'_{FK}$, so
$$\delta'_{FK}=D'(c_K^{-1})\circ c_{DK}\circ F(\delta_K).$$
These equations explicitly identify the double-dual maps. Finally [lem-sf-proetale-constructible-origin] and [lem-sf-comparison-tor-amplitude] give an equivalence on the finite-Tor bounded constructible categories; $c$ identifies their duality functors. The etale biduality theorem [thm-weil-etale-geometric-biduality] then makes the displayed pro-etale double-dual map invertible and transports the etale anti-equivalence. This final application uses the already separate etale theorem, which has no dependency on the present site-comparison lemma.
