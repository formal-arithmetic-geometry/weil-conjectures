## Worker report

The module states the flatness conclusion of Stacks Algebra Lemma 00SV (2): for a
commutative base ring `R`, a fixed polynomial presentation by `f`, a prime `q` of the
quotient, and each `i ≤ c`, the localized polynomial ring modulo the first `i` equations
is flat over `R`. The hypothesis `h` is the fixed-presentation fibre-dimension condition
from the added dependency module. The prime `q` is represented by the corresponding
`PrimeSpectrum` point; its inverse image is formed with `PrimeSpectrum.comap`; localization
uses `Localization.AtPrime`; the initial ideal is generated by those `f j` with `j.val < i`.
The conclusion is `Module.Flat R` exactly, using Mathlib's flat-module notion.

The dependency declaration `Algebra.IsRelativeGlobalCompleteIntersection` from
`Sheaf.Paper.RelativeGlobalCompleteIntersection` is imported for the ring-map notion. It
is existential over presentations and therefore cannot render this lemma's specified
presentation. The dependency module
`Sheaf.Paper.RelativeGlobalCompleteIntersectionPresentation` supplies
`Algebra.IsRelativeGlobalCompleteIntersectionPresentation`, with the same `n`, `c`, and
`f`, quantifying every prime ideal and every nontrivial fibre, requiring `c ≤ n` and
`topologicalKrullDim (PrimeSpectrum (p.Fiber quotient)) = n-c`.

Edge cases checked: `c = 0` and `i = 0` give the localized polynomial ring; `i = c` gives
the full quotient localized at `q`; `c > n` makes the fixed-presentation condition force all fibres empty;
empty fibres are excluded by `Nontrivial`; arbitrary (including empty) prime spectra are
represented by the quantified `q`; and no universe restriction is imposed beyond the base
ring universe.

- Nonnegative numbers of variables and equations: `(n c : ℕ)`.

  declaration, with the canonical R-algebra structure.
- Ordered equations f_1,...,f_c: `f : Fin c → MvPolynomial (Fin n) R`.
  Index j in Fin c represents equation f_(j+1).
- The quotient S=P/(f_1,...,f_c):
  `MvPolynomial (Fin n) R ⧸ Ideal.span (Set.range f)`.
  It is determined by the given equations, with its canonical quotient algebra structure.
- This specified presentation is a relative global complete intersection:
  `h : IsRelativeGlobalCompleteIntersectionPresentation f`. Unfolding the delivered
  dependency gives every prime `p : Ideal R`, its `p.IsPrime` proof, and the condition
  `Nontrivial (p.Fiber S) → c ≤ n ∧ topologicalKrullDim (PrimeSpectrum (p.Fiber S))
  = (n-c : ℕ)`. The guarded inequality implements integer subtraction faithfully:
  a nonempty fibre cannot have negative dimension. Nontriviality is a premise of
  this implication, not an extra global hypothesis.
- Every prime q of S: `q : PrimeSpectrum S`. Its prime proof is part of the object;
  this is equivalent to quantifying an ideal of S with an IsPrime proof.
- Its inverse-image prime q' in P:
  `(PrimeSpectrum.comap (Ideal.Quotient.mk (Ideal.span (Set.range f))) q).asIdeal`.
  The map is exactly the quotient map, not the coefficient map or another presentation.
- Every integer index 0≤i≤c: `(i : ℕ) (hi : i ≤ c)` after q. No index is lost.
- The local polynomial ring: `Localization.AtPrime q'`, localization at the
  complement of q', as in the localization dependency.
- The extended initial-equations ideal:
  `Ideal.map (algebraMap P (Localization.AtPrime q'))
  (Ideal.span (f '' {j : Fin c | j.val < i}))`. Finite indexing starts at zero,
  so precisely the first i equations occur. Ideal.map is the ideal generated by
  the image, hence exactly extension to the localization.
- Flatness over R of the quotient by that extended ideal: `Module.Flat R (...)`.
  The inherited module structure is restriction along the canonical composite
  R→P→P_q'→P_q'/(f_1,...,f_i), as required by the node. Mathlib's flatness class
  is the dependency's tensor-injectivity notion, not faithful flatness.
