## New lemma: source and planned argument

For lem-per-strict-local-divisor-low-kummer I read the low-degree sentence of ILO XVI Proposition 3.4.2, printed p.245, and Stacks etale-cohomology.tex Tag 03PL, label lemma-kummer-sequence, plus more-algebra.tex Tags 0AFZ/0AG0, labels lemma-regular-local-Pic-zero and lemma-regular-local-UFD, including their proofs. The low-degree assertion follows as follows; this explanation is in the report, not in the unsplit child's statement or proof fields.

A regular local ring is a domain and a UFD. Its regular nonzero quotient R/(t) is a domain, so t is prime. Thus R[1/t] is a nonzero domain, hence its spectrum is connected, and its units are uniquely u t^a with u a unit of R and a an integer. Strict henselianity and invertibility of ell lift the simple roots of X^ell-u from the separably closed residue field, so every unit of R is an ell-th power. They also lift a primitive ell-th root of unity, giving the sheaf identification F_ell = mu_ell on Spec R. Stacks 0AFZ gives Pic(R[1/t])=0. The Kummer long exact sequence therefore identifies H1(V,mu_ell) with R[1/t]^*/(R[1/t]^*)^ell = Z/ell, and the class of t is its generator. This is more than an elementary few-line consequence of the item's cited statements: it requires regular-local Picard triviality and H1 Kummer theory as well as unit factorization and henselian lifting. It is strictly smaller than the item, since it proves no high-degree condition and no equivalence of derived isomorphisms. Existing Picard and UFD nodes will be reused when this new node itself is split.

The child was tested on k[[t]], k[[x,y]] with t=x, mixed-characteristic regular strict local rings with ell invertible, and ell=2. A field has no allowable nonzero nonunit t, so the hypotheses do not wrongly demand an H1 generator on an empty complement. A unit t, t=0, and a singular divisor t=x^2 are excluded for explicit reasons. In the x^2 case the Kummer generator can vanish mod 2, illustrating why the regular quotient condition matters.
