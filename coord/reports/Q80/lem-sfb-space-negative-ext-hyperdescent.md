## Source error

The precise failing inference in `spaces-simplicial.tex` is the invocation of `lemma-cartesian-module-derived-from-simplicial` (0D9T) in 0DLA and of `lemma-nullity-cartesian-modules-derived` / `lemma-hom-cartesian-modules-derived` (0D9R/0D9S) in 0DL9. These abstract module lemmas require flatness of the ring map for **every** order-preserving transition, including degeneracies. The proof of 0DHF correctly deduces flatness of augmentation components and faces from `hypercovering.tex`, `remark-P-covering` (0DER); that does not give flatness of degeneracies.

Explicit test: take the Cech hypercover of A^1_k -> Spec k. It is an fppf hypercover, with identity higher matching maps and flat projection faces. Its first degeneracy is the diagonal A^1_k -> A^2_k. On rings it is k[x,y] -> k[t], x,y -> t, whose quotient module k[x,y]/(x-y) is not flat. Tensoring the injective multiplication-by-(x-y) map on k[x,y] makes it the zero endomorphism of a nonzero module. Thus the omitted flatness inference is false even for a smooth cover of a point. This disputes a proof premise, not the imported statement or the unit's route.

Repair: use only the injective order maps, so that every transition is a composite of flat faces. The ringed total construction, realization and Hom inputs are stated explicitly for that diagram. The unbounded descent input is stated for the corresponding face total site. These are agent-supplied semisimplicial adaptations, identified as such in their sources; I do not claim Stacks states them verbatim. After descent the supplied degeneracy comparison follows by pulling face compatibility along a section and using theta_s Ls^*(theta_d)=1. The item writes this equation, rather than assuming nonflat tensor pullback is exact.

The intended next splits of the adaptations remain concrete. The total-site input uses the existing general total-pseudofunctor and total-systems statements and then equips the total topos with its coherent ring. The realization and Hom arguments use the free simplex resolution indexed by injections: on component m its terms are the finite sums indexed by [p] -> [m] injective, with p <= m, and the augmentation is the cellular chain complex of the m-simplex. Exact flat transitions give exact left adjoints to evaluation. Its finite partial totalizations give the same Postnikov argument as 0D9R--0D9T. The face-total quasi-coherent descent proof uses module descent and cohomological descent over the fppf hypercover and the affine cohomology bound zero used in 0DHF, now on the face total site; no degeneracy-flatness hypothesis is needed. These are genuine lower-level tasks, not claims proved by the present item.
