Prior step 4 argument, retained for continuity of the earlier work (superseded by the existing-node citation):

For any scheme morphism $f:U\to V$ on either indicated site, any $A$-module $M$, and module sheaf $N$ on $U$, the module adjunction gives natural bijections
$$\operatorname{Hom}(f^*\underline M_V,N)=\operatorname{Hom}(\underline M_V,f_*N)=\operatorname{Hom}_A(M,(f_*N)(V))=\operatorname{Hom}_A(M,N(U))=\operatorname{Hom}(\underline M_U,N).$$
Here the constant-module Hom formula follows directly from sheafification: a map from the constant module presheaf is determined by its value at the terminal object, an $A$-linear map into global sections. Conversely restrict such a map to each object and extend uniquely by sheafification. The resulting map is $A_V$-linear because sections of $A_V$ are locally represented by constants, where linearity is exactly $A$-linearity; equality then glues. Direct-image evaluation uses $U\times_VV\cong U$. The bijections are natural in $N$; applying them to identity maps gives mutually inverse comparison morphisms $f^*\underline M_V\cong\underline M_U$. They preserve the scalar action by construction. This proves the asserted constant-model comparison for $h$ and also for all morphisms used below, without assuming $M$ finite.
