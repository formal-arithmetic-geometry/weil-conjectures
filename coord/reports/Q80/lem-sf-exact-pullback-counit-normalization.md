### lem-sf-exact-pullback-tower-counit-comparison — old Step 1

Put $H(P)=Q_Y(FP)$ and $H_G(P)=Q_Y(GP)$, with the termwise extension of $m$ still denoted $m$. By the exact-functor derived-functor theorem, $(F_D,\xi)$ is a universal left derived pair for $H$. The pair $(L,Q_Y(m)\epsilon)$ is another: composition with the natural isomorphism $Q_Y(m)$ converts, bijectively, transformations into $H_G$ into transformations into $H$, and its inverse converts them back. Apply uniqueness of right Kan extensions to these two pairs. This gives a unique natural isomorphism $e:L\to F_D$ with $\xi_Pe_{Q_XP}=Q_Y(m_P)\epsilon_P$. In particular every $\epsilon_P$ is invertible, since the other three maps in this equation are invertible. This argument uses the specified counit, not just an abstract isomorphism between the functors.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-functor, lem-sfb-right-kan-extension-unique-iso.

### lem-sf-exact-pullback-tower-counit-comparison — old Step 2

Write $\mathcal T_T=\operatorname{Tow}(\operatorname{Mod}(B_T))$ and $\widetilde L$ for the specified left-derived levelwise $G$, with counit $\tau$. Kernels and cokernels in $\mathcal T_T$ are levelwise. Hence levelwise $F$, written $F^{\mathrm{Tow}}$, is additive and exact: evaluation of its kernel or cokernel comparison is the corresponding isomorphism for $F$. Likewise $m$ gives a natural isomorphism $m^{\mathrm{Tow}}:G^{\mathrm{Tow}}\to F^{\mathrm{Tow}}$, since its components commute with every tower transition by naturality of $m$. Apply the two cited universal comparison results to the abelian categories $\mathcal T_X,\mathcal T_Y$. They give a unique $E:\widetilde L\xrightarrow{\sim}F_D^{\mathrm{Tow}}$ with $\Xi_M E_{Q M}=Q(m^{\mathrm{Tow}}_M)\tau_M$ for every complex of strict towers $M$, where $\Xi$ is exact localization comparison. In particular this is a comparison in the derived category of strict towers.

Cited statements: lem-weil-tower-category-levelwise-abelian, lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-functor, lem-sfb-right-kan-extension-unique-iso.

### lem-sf-exact-pullback-tower-counit-comparison — old Step 3

Use the counit-normalized evaluation comparisons $v_n:\operatorname{ev}_n\widetilde L\cong L\operatorname{ev}_n$. On an objectwise K-flat flat-term representative $M$, the equation for $E$ evaluates to $Q(m_{M_n})\operatorname{ev}_n(\tau_M)$. The equation for $e$ after $v_n$ is $Q(m_{M_n})\epsilon_{M_n}v_n$, equal to that expression by the defining equation $\epsilon_{M_n}v_n=\operatorname{ev}_n(\tau_M)$. Thus evaluation of $E$ is $e\operatorname{ev}_n$ after $v_n$ and the exact-localization comparisons. Every derived strict tower has such a representative by the strict diagram replacement theorem; these equalities therefore hold on every object by naturality along its replacement isomorphism. The $v_n$ commute with transitions by the cited evaluation theorem, so these identifications respect all transitions as well.

Cited statements: lem-cbc-derived-tower-evaluation-normalization, lem-weil-pbc-diagram-kflat-replacements.

### lem-sf-exact-pullback-quotient-augmentation-square — old Step 4

Let $b:\Delta_YL\xrightarrow{\sim}\widetilde L\Delta_X$ be the counit-normalized constant-tower comparison. Let $c:\Delta_YF_D\xrightarrow{\sim}F_D^{\mathrm{Tow}}\Delta_X$ be the comparison obtained from the identity $\Delta_YF=F^{\mathrm{Tow}}\Delta_X$ on complexes by exact localization. Constant towers are exact because kernels and cokernels are levelwise. We have $E_{\Delta K}b_K=c_K\Delta_Y(e_K)$. To check it, represent $K$ by a K-flat flat-term $P$. Compose both sides with the target exact-localization comparison $\Xi_{\Delta P}$. The first becomes $Q(m_{\Delta P})\tau_{\Delta P}b_{Q P}=\Delta_Y(Q(m_P)\epsilon_P)$ by normalization of $b$. The second is the same expression by the defining equation for $e$, since $m_{\Delta P}=\Delta_Ym_P$. Cancel $\Xi_{\Delta P}$. Naturality along a K-flat replacement proves the equation for every $K$. This verifies the constant-tower compatibility in the strict derived tower category, without testing equality merely on its evaluations.

Cited statements: lem-cbc-derived-constant-tower-normalization, lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-factorization, lem-weil-tower-category-levelwise-abelian, lem-weil-pbc-diagram-kflat-replacements.

### lem-sf-exact-pullback-quotient-augmentation-square — old Step 5
