## Preserved original step 6

Fix $q$. Reorder $\widetilde C_q=A_q*F_q$ as $F_q*A_q$. The augmented bar row is then
$$R^V_{n,q}=F_q*A_{q,0}*\cdots*A_{q,n}*V_q\longrightarrow F_q*V_q=D^V_q.$$
Here all $A_{q,i}$ are labelled copies of $A_q$. For $n\geq1$, $d_i$ folds copies $i,i+1$ when $i<n$, and $d_n$ sends copy $n$ to $V_q$ by $v$; $s_i$ inserts an unused copy immediately after copy $i$. Define $h_n$ to insert an unused copy before copy $0$, shifting every old label up by one and fixing $F_q,V_q$. Define $h_{-1}:F_q*V_q\to F_q*A_{q,0}*V_q$ by the factor inclusions. The augmentation followed by $h_{-1}$ sends the $A_{q,0}$ factor into $V_q$. Thus $\varepsilon h_{-1}=1$, $d_0h_n=1$, and $d_1h_0=h_{-1}\varepsilon$: the last equality sends the old copy $0$ into $V_q$ on both sides. For the remaining face identity $d_{i+1}h_{n+1}=h_nd_i$ with $0\leq i\leq n$, each side sends an old copy with label $k\leq i$ to label $k+1$, copy $i+1$ to label $i+1$, and a copy with $k>i+1$ to label $k$; the two adjacent copies are folded by the identity functor of $A_q$. For $i=n+1$, both sides send the old last copy into $V_q$ by $v$ and send every other old copy $k$ to label $k+1$. For $s_{i+1}h_n=h_{n+1}s_i$ ($0\leq i\leq n$), both sides send an old copy $k\leq i$ to label $k+1$ and an old copy $k>i$ to label $k+2$. In all these equalities both sides fix $F_q,V_q$, so equality on coproduct factor inclusions proves equality of functors. Restrict these functors to $(x,y)$ mapping sets. The statement of [lem-coef-extra-degeneracy-contraction] now gives a section and simplicial homotopy inverse for the row augmentation. Realization preserves the product with $\Delta^1$ by [lem-coef-realization-finite-limits], so this homotopy realizes to a continuous homotopy. The row augmentation is therefore a weak homotopy equivalence, with no Kan assumption, by the definition in [lem-coef-kan-quillen-right-proper]. Finally [lem-coef-bisimplicial-diagonal-equivalence], in the exchanged direction, shows
$$\varepsilon_V:\operatorname{diag}R^V\longrightarrow D^V$$
is a local weak equivalence. The chosen free factors and the contractions need not be natural in $q$: only the bar augmentation is used as a map of bisimplicial sets, and it is natural by step 5.
