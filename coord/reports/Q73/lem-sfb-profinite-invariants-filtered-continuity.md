### Original step 1 → lem-sfb-profinite-action-colimit-cohomology

First construct the colimit without using invariants. On the finite-cover profinite site, [lem-weil-pbc-site-filtered-colimit-sections-finite-coverings] computes filtered abelian sheaf colimits on sections; [lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-colimits-forgetful] gives the same colimit with its $R$-module structure. Thus put $N(S)=\operatorname{colim}_i N_i(S)$. For $g\in C(S,J)$ define $g[m_i]=[gm_i]$. Equivariance of the transition maps makes this well-defined; the action laws, linearity and restriction compatibility follow by applying each equation to a representative. This is an internal action [def-sfb-condensed-action-module]. Its coprojections are equivariant. For an equivariant cocone, the unique underlying module map sends $[m_i]$ to its given image; this formula commutes with $g$ and all restrictions, so is equivariant. Consequently forgetting the action creates this filtered colimit, with the original coprojections.

Inputs: lem-weil-pbc-site-filtered-colimit-sections-finite-coverings, lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-colimits-forgetful, def-sfb-condensed-action-module.

### Original step 2 → lem-sfb-profinite-action-colimit-cohomology

Apply [lem-sfb-profinite-action-coherent-presentation] to $J$. Use its site $P_J$, ring $\mathcal R_J$ and exact additive equivalence $\Phi$. Put $F_i=\Phi(N_i)$ and $F=\Phi(N)$. An equivalence transports the colimit universal property: maps out of $\Phi(N)$ correspond, under its inverse, to maps out of $N$, so the transported coprojections make $F$ the module-sheaf colimit of the $F_i$. By [lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-colimits-forgetful], $F_{ab}=\operatorname{colim}_i(F_i)_{ab}$ in abelian sheaves on $P_J$. All objects and arrows are small by the presentation's universe convention. The objects $tS$ are representables in this finite-limit site, hence coherent by that lemma; all iterated fibre products of finite-cover members are still objects of the same site. This verifies the compactness and intersection requirements of the cohomology-continuity route, including for non-discrete $J$.

Inputs: lem-sfb-profinite-action-coherent-presentation, lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-colimits-forgetful.

### Original step 3 → lem-sfb-profinite-action-colimit-cohomology

Apply [lem-weil-pbc-cohomology-colimit-of-sites] to the constant diagram of sites $P_J$ indexed by $I^{\mathrm{op}}$, all transition and cocone functors being identities. This meets [lem-weil-pbc-colimit-of-sites]: $I^{\mathrm{op}}$ is cofiltered, all covers are finite, identity functors preserve covers and covering pullbacks, and the category colimit is $P_J$ (each object and arrow in a constant diagram has exactly its original representative). Use coefficients $(F_i)_{ab}$ and their original transition maps. Fix $i_0\in I$ and any $T\in P_J$. The cited theorem gives the comparison over $(i_0\downarrow I)$. Its colimit is the colimit over $I$, with the same coprojections: a cocone on the forward-arrow category extends at $j$ by choosing arrows $i_0\to k$ and $j\to k$ and composing its $k$-leg with the transition from $j$; two choices agree after taking a common target and equalizing the resulting parallel arrows, by filteredness. The same argument gives compatibility with every arrow of $I$ and uniqueness of the extension. Hence
$$\operatorname{colim}_i H^q(T,(F_i)_{ab})\ \xrightarrow{\sim}\ H^q(T,F_{ab}).$$
The explicit leg formula in the cited theorem makes the $i$-leg $H^q(T,(F_i)_{ab}\to F_{ab})$. In particular this holds at $T=tS$, and the maps respect restriction in $S$ because they are induced by the same coefficient maps.

Inputs: lem-weil-pbc-cohomology-colimit-of-sites, lem-weil-pbc-colimit-of-sites, lem-sfb-profinite-action-coherent-presentation.

### Original step 4 → lem-sfb-profinite-invariants-sheafified-cohomology

Identify the internal higher invariants, retaining sheafification. Choose an equivariant injective resolution $M\to Q^\bullet$ [lem-sfb-condensed-derived-restriction-calculus]. The exact equivalence $\Phi$ of [lem-sfb-profinite-action-coherent-presentation] sends it to an injective module-sheaf resolution, since the lifting property for monomorphisms transports under the equivalence. Its value on $tS$ is $\Gamma_{R,J}(Q^\bullet)(S)$. Module injectives on this ringed site are acyclic for abelian evaluation at every object [lem-sc-injective-module-abelian-acyclic-zariski]. Abelian sheaves on this small site have enough injectives [lem-weil-pbc-site-sheaves-enough-injectives]. The acyclic-resolution criterion [lemma-weil-pbc-leray-acyclicity] and its coefficient naturality [lem-sc-acyclic-comparison-natural] therefore identify
$$H^q(\Gamma_{R,J}(Q^\bullet)(S))=H^q(tS,\Phi(M)_{ab}).$$
These are presheaf identifications: compare the underlying abelian resolution with a single abelian injective resolution by a map of sheaf complexes. Evaluation of that same map at each $tS$ commutes with every restriction; its cohomology gives the displayed comparison. Naturality in $M$ follows from the cited comparison theorem. By exact abelian sheafification [lem-weil-pbc-sheafification-exact], the cohomology sheaf of $\Gamma_{R,J}(Q^\bullet)$ is the sheafification of $S\mapsto H^q(tS,\Phi(M)_{ab})$. This sheafification is therefore the underlying abelian sheaf of $R^q\Gamma_{R,J}(M)$, naturally in $M$. This formula does not assert exactness of evaluation on every profinite $S$.

Inputs: lem-sfb-condensed-derived-restriction-calculus, lem-sfb-profinite-action-coherent-presentation, lem-sc-injective-module-abelian-acyclic-zariski, lem-sc-acyclic-comparison-natural, lem-weil-pbc-sheafification-exact, lem-weil-pbc-site-sheaves-enough-injectives, lemma-weil-pbc-leray-acyclicity.

### Original step 5 → lem-sfb-profinite-invariants-filtered-continuity

Put $P_i(S)=H^q(tS,(F_i)_{ab})$ and $P(S)=H^q(tS,F_{ab})$. Step 3 says the presheaf map $\operatorname{colim}_i P_i\to P$ is an isomorphism. By [lem-weil-pbc-sheafification-exact], sheafification commutes with colimits. Applying it and step 4 gives an isomorphism on underlying abelian sheaves
$$\operatorname{colim}_i R^q\Gamma_{R,J}(N_i)\ \longrightarrow\ R^q\Gamma_{R,J}(N).$$
The underlying abelian sheaf of the module colimit is the abelian sheaf colimit [lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-colimits-forgetful]. Each leg is $R^q\Gamma_{R,J}(N_i\to N)$: in step 3 it is induced by that coefficient coprojection, and the step-4 comparison is natural in this map. Thus this is the canonical map in the statement. It is $R$-linear because the derived functor takes values in internal $R$-modules [lem-sfb-condensed-derived-restriction-calculus]. Its inverse is linear: $f^{-1}(ry)=rf^{-1}(y)$ follows by applying the injective linear map $f$. Hence it is an internal-module isomorphism. No uniform bound in $q$ was used.

Inputs: lem-weil-pbc-sheafification-exact, lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-colimits-forgetful, lem-sfb-condensed-derived-restriction-calculus.
