### Small-case checks and the extracted lemmas

For the product/projection lemma I checked $f=\mathrm{id}$, where its two routes reduce to the same $J_X(W)$, and $L=0$ or $V=0$, where both maps have zero source. For the mixed-product lemma the identity case reduces to the identity projection formula followed by $J_X$, and for a finite set over a point both routes send a pure tensor to the same tensor in the direct sum over the two coordinates. The multiplication identity sends $(v_x)_x\otimes(k_x)_x$ to $(v_x\otimes k_x)_x$ for a finite proper map to a point; this also confirms that $M$ must be on $X$ and that evaluation uses $M=D_XK$. These tests require neither $X$ nor $Y$ to be proper over $s$.
