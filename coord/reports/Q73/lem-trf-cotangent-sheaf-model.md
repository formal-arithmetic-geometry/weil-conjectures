### Original Step 1; assigned to lem-trf-polynomial-resolution-universal-evaluation

Put $\mathcal D=\mathcal C_{B/A}^{op}$, the category of polynomial $A$-algebras augmented to $B$ in the statement. Use its chosen small skeleton and transport the resolutions to it; the stipulated common infinite bound contains the sets of generators of their terms and of the standard resolution. A presheaf on $\mathcal C_{B/A}$ is a covariant functor on $\mathcal D$. By [lem-trf-derived-colimit-bar], $L\pi_!F$ is the left derived colimit of this functor. There is a sign convention to fix when using [lem-trf-simplicial-derived-colimit-computation]: the chain $U_n\to\cdots\to U_0$ in $\mathcal C$ becomes $T_0\to\cdots\to T_n$ in $\mathcal D$, with $T_i=U_i$. The former bar deletes the last vertex first, while the latter deletes the first vertex first. Thus $d_{\mathcal C,n}=(-1)^n d_{\mathcal D,n}$. Multiplication in degree $-n$ by $\lambda_n=(-1)^{n(n+1)/2}$ gives a chain isomorphism $j_F:K_{\mathcal C}(F)\to K_{\mathcal D}(F)$, since $\lambda_n=(-1)^n\lambda_{n-1}$. It is the identity in degree zero, respects augmentations, and commutes with every bar map induced by a functor and coefficient transformation. We use this explicit identification throughout.

Original cited inputs: def-weil-size-convention, lem-trf-derived-colimit-bar, lem-trf-simplicial-derived-colimit-computation.

### Original Step 2; assigned to lem-trf-polynomial-resolution-universal-evaluation

Let $P_\bullet\to B$ be any polynomial resolution specified in [lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence]. Regard it as a morphism of simplicial abelian groups to the constant simplicial group $B$. By the definition in that node every $P_n\to B$ is surjective, so the morphism is termwise surjective. The alternating complex $s(B)$ of constant $B$ has differential $\sum_{i=0}^n(-1)^i\mathrm{id}_B$ from degree $n$, which is zero for odd $n$ and the identity for even $n\geq2$; hence $H_0(s(B))=B$, $H_n(s(B))=0$ for $n\geq1$, and $s(B)\to B[0]$ is a quasi-isomorphism. Since $s(P_\bullet)\to B[0]$ is a quasi-isomorphism by the definition of resolution and factors through $s(B)$, the map $s(P_\bullet)\to s(B)$ is a quasi-isomorphism. By [lem-trf-surjective-qis-simplicial-group-trivial-kan] (Stacks Tag 08P1) the underlying map of simplicial sets $P_\bullet\to B$ is a trivial Kan fibration in the sense of [def-weil-pbc-trivial-kan-fibration]: surjective on vertices, with boundary fillers for all $n\geq1$. This applies to the standard resolution too, since [lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence] states that it is a resolution, and its terms surject onto $B$ by that definition.

Original cited inputs: lem-trf-cotangent-resolution-coherence, lem-trf-surjective-qis-simplicial-group-trivial-kan, def-weil-pbc-trivial-kan-fibration.

### Original Step 3; assigned to lem-trf-polynomial-resolution-universal-evaluation

For an object $T=A[E]\to B$ of $\mathcal D$, a simplex of $S_T=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal D}(T,P_\bullet)$ is a choice, for each $e\in E$, of a simplex of $P_\bullet$ mapping to the prescribed element $\epsilon_T(e)$ of $B$. This follows from the universal property of a polynomial algebra and commutes with all simplicial operators. A lifting problem for $S_T\to *$ against $\partial\Delta[n]\hookrightarrow\Delta[n]$ therefore gives, for each generator, a boundary lifting problem for $P_\bullet\to B$ over the constant simplex $\epsilon_T(e)$. Step 2 and [def-weil-pbc-trivial-kan-fibration] provide fillers; choose them for all generators and extend uniquely to an algebra map $T\to P_n$. For $n=0$ choose generator lifts by the same condition. Hence $S_T\to *$ is a trivial Kan fibration. By [lem-weil-pbc-trivial-kan-homotopy-equivalence] it is homotopy equivalent to a singleton. This includes the empty generator set and arbitrary sets of generators within the size bound.

Original cited inputs: def-weil-pbc-trivial-kan-fibration, lem-weil-pbc-trivial-kan-homotopy-equivalence.

### Original Step 6; assigned to lem-trf-polynomial-differential-reindexing
