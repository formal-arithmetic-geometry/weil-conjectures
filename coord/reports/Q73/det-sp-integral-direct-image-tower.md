has inverse $(q\otimes q')\otimes a\mapsto(q\otimes1)\otimes(q'\otimes a)$. These maps respect the tensor differential, since $a,b$ have degree zero, and commute with every quotient map. The total $Q\otimes Q'$ is K-flat: for an acyclic $V$, first $Q'\otimes V$ is acyclic and then $Q\otimes(Q'\otimes V)$ is acyclic, which equals $(Q\otimes Q')\otimes V$ by reassociation of the total tensor. Its terms are flat since tensoring by a tensor product of flat terms is a composite of exact functors, and sums of flat modules are flat. The derived tensor identifications on these models and their descent are [lem-weil-pbc-site-derived-tensor-descent]. Thus
$$\operatorname{red}_T(U\otimes^LV)\simeq\operatorname{red}_TU\otimes^L_{A_{\bullet,T}}\operatorname{red}_TV$$
as derived towers. The computation is the derived coefficient-extension tensor comparison on the simultaneous models of [lem-coef-semilinear-derived-sections], so it asserts a tower isomorphism rather than an arbitrary lift of level morphisms.
