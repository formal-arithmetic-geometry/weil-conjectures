### Old step 1 → lem-sfb-integral-coefficient-model-realization

By [lem-weil-l-adic-extension-integers-dvr], $A$ is a domain and every ideal is principal, so every ideal is finitely generated and $A$ is Noetherian. Its uniformizer is nonzero, hence multiplication by $\varpi^n$ is injective, with cokernel $P_n$ by the definition of that quotient. Thus $[A\xrightarrow{\varpi^n}A]$ in degrees $-1,0$, augmented to $P_n$, is the asserted finite free resolution. By [lem-weil-valued-dvr-finite-quotients], $P_n$ is finite. Since $|\ell|_E=\ell^{-1}<1$, the element $\ell$ belongs to the maximal ideal $(\varpi)$, say $\ell=\varpi a$ with $a\in A$. Consequently $\ell^n=\varpi^n a^n$ annihilates $P_n$. In particular $A/I$ is additive torsion, and every $P_n$ is perfect. These verify every algebraic hypothesis in [def-weil-pbc-adic-coefficient-datum].
