### Old step 1

Work in a common universe in which both sites are small. Their ordinary $A$-module categories are Grothendieck abelian by [prop-pur-kinjective-resolutions-exist], hence have enough injectives by [lem-sp-grothendieck-enough-injectives]. Apply [lem-sc-bounded-below-injective-resolution-existence] to choose a bounded-below injective module resolution $F_p\to J^\bullet$. The module adjunction is [def-weil-pbc-site-comparison-morphism], and its inverse image is exact by [lem-sf-comparison-pullback-exact]. Consequently [lem-sp-right-adjoint-preserves-injectives] shows that $I^\bullet=\nu_{T,*}J^\bullet$ is bounded below and termwise injective on the etale site. This uses preservation of injectives by direct image, rather than an assertion that inverse image preserves injectives.

Statement dependencies actually used in this argument: prop-pur-kinjective-resolutions-exist, lem-sp-grothendieck-enough-injectives, lem-sc-bounded-below-injective-resolution-existence, def-weil-pbc-site-comparison-morphism, lem-sf-comparison-pullback-exact, lem-sp-right-adjoint-preserves-injectives.

### Old step 2

Let $a:F\to I^\bullet$ be the ordinary comparison unit followed by $\nu_{T,*}(F_p\to J^\bullet)$. By the injective computation of right derived functors [lem-weil-pbc-right-derived-plus-injective], $I^\bullet$ represents $R\nu_{T,*}F_p$, and by [lem-pur-comparison-unit-injective-model], applied to $M=F$ in degree zero and the resolution $F_p\to J^\bullet$, $a$ represents the derived adjunction unit under this identification. That unit is an isomorphism by [thm-weil-pbc-site-comparison], with no constructibility hypothesis. Thus $a$ is a quasi-isomorphism and is a specified etale injective resolution of $F$. The direct-image formula [def-weil-pbc-site-comparison-morphism] gives $I^r(T)=J^r(T)$ and $I^r(V)=J^r(V)$, with identical restriction maps and differentials. Taking kernels gives $\Gamma_C(T_{\mathrm{et}},I^\bullet)=\Gamma_C(T_{\mathrm{proet}},J^\bullet)$, using [def-pur-support-cohomology].

Statement dependencies actually used in this argument: lem-weil-pbc-right-derived-plus-injective, lem-pur-comparison-unit-injective-model, thm-weil-pbc-site-comparison, def-weil-pbc-site-comparison-morphism, def-pur-support-cohomology.

### Old step 3

For completeness the degreewise surjectivity needed for localization also holds for module injectives. For an injective module sheaf $E$, [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-site-injective-module-injective-presheaf] makes its underlying module presheaf injective. For the monomorphism $j:V\to T$, the map of free presheaves $A[h_V]\to A[h_T]$ is injective: on each object its basis map is the injection $\operatorname{Hom}(-,V)\to\operatorname{Hom}(-,T)$. By the definition of injectivity [def-sp-injective-resolutions], restriction of Hom into $E$ is surjective. The free-presheaf Yoneda formula [lem-weil-pbc-free-yoneda-presheaf-module] identifies this with $E(T)\to E(V)$. Therefore in either topology the three complexes $\ker(E^\bullet(T)\to E^\bullet(V))$, $E^\bullet(T)$, $E^\bullet(V)$ form a degreewise short exact sequence when $E^\bullet$ is $I^\bullet$ or $J^\bullet$. The kernel and both arrows respect the $A$-actions.

Statement dependencies actually used in this argument: lem-weil-pbc-ringed-site-injective-module-injective-presheaf, def-sp-injective-resolutions, lem-weil-pbc-free-yoneda-presheaf-module.

### Old step 4

The support complex in each row computes the right-derived kernel functor of [def-pur-support-cohomology], with the ordinary module-coefficient interpretation of [lem-pur-support-site-comparison]; the middle complex computes global sections. The restrictions $I^\bullet|_V$ and $J^\bullet|_V$ are injective resolutions on their respective sites by [lem-weil-pbc-etale-restriction-injectives] and [lem-weil-pbc-proetale-restriction-injectives]. Thus the last complexes compute cohomology on $V$. These are precisely the injective localization models for the long exact sequences of [thm-pur-with-support-exactness]. The finiteness of $A$ makes every coefficient sheaf torsion, and the hypotheses of the pro-etale clause hold. No finiteness of the modules of sections is needed.

### Old step 5

The equalities in Step 2 identify the two short exact sequences of Step 3 term by term, including their inclusions and restriction arrows. They therefore give a morphism of short exact cochain complexes. Its support component represents the canonical supported comparison of [lem-pur-support-site-comparison]: first apply the etale derived support functor to the unit $F\to R\nu_{T,*}F_p$, represented by the augmentation $a$, and then use the kernel identity of Step 2. In the specified resolution $a:F\to I^\bullet$, the first operation is represented by the identity on the support complex of $I^\bullet$. The middle component is the global comparison of [lem-weil-pbc-comparison-global-cohomology] by the same unit computation. On $V$ it is the global comparison for $j^{-1}F$: restriction of $a$ is the adjunction unit there, since the direct-image restrictions are the same maps $J(T)\to J(V)$. The coefficient identification is the canonical $j^{-1}F_p\cong\nu_V^*(j^{-1}F)$ of [lem-weil-pbc-comparison-pullback]. Thus the three cohomology maps are exactly the specified $c_C^q,c_T^q,c_V^q$, rather than unspecified isomorphisms of their groups.

### Old step 6

Apply [lem-weil-pbc-complex-ses-long-exact-naturality] to this morphism of short exact complexes. The result is a morphism of the full long exact sequences, with $c_C^{q+1}\partial_{\mathrm{et}}^q=\partial_{\mathrm{pro}}^q c_V^q$. To see the sign explicitly, a cocycle on $V$ is lifted to a cochain on $T$, its differential lies in the support kernel, and the class of this differential is the connecting image. The termwise identifications send the chosen lift and its differential to the same cochains in the other row. Both boundaries therefore give the same class, with no negation. Naturality of the comparisons in [lem-pur-support-site-comparison] and [lem-weil-pbc-comparison-global-cohomology], and coefficient functoriality of [thm-pur-with-support-exactness], give naturality of all these squares in $F$. All complexes and their arrows are $A$-linear, so their induced cohomology and connecting maps are $A$-linear as well.

### Open-unit coherence to check in the section-model proof

Use the direct-image identity on the localized sites: restricting nu_T,* to V evaluates a pro-etale sheaf on the same etale V-objects as nu_V,* of its restriction. Define the inverse-image comparison as the adjunction conjugate of this identity, with orientation beta: nu_V^* j_et^-1 F -> j_pro^-1 nu_T^* F. The unit-conjugation equation is exactly the displayed equality in the new statement. Compose it with nu_V,* of h restricted to V. It identifies the restricted augmentation a|V with the open comparison unit followed by the resolution augmentation beta then h|V. The derived unit representative lemma identifies the resulting morphism in the derived category; evaluation and kernel functoriality identify it with u_V and u_C. For u_C use the normalized support map uniquely fixed by the kernel inclusion, rather than inferring equality of supported maps from a derived forgetting-supports map. That derived forgetting map need not be monic. This makes precise the coherence that old step 5 expressed in words.
