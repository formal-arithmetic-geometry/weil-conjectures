## Source error

The proof of Stacks 08QW (cotangent.tex, lemma-triangle-compute-lower-shriek), case i=2, proposes the object (P,Q') as a cartesian lift along Q to Q'. This need not be an object: take A=k, P=Q=k[x], B=C=k, all augmentations evaluating x at zero, and Q'=k with Q to Q' evaluation at zero. Both Q and Q' are polynomial over A, but Q'=k is not polynomial over P=k[x] via evaluation (every polynomial P-algebra has injective structure map). Hence the proposed lift fails its polynomial-over-P condition. There is also an A/B typo in that paragraph. I make no unsupported claim that a different cartesian lift cannot exist; it is the displayed construction that fails.

Step 4 gives a direct repair using the valid section Q maps to (A,Q), the natural arrow (P,Q) to (A,Q) in the opposite convention, the existing bar prism homotopy, and the initial object of the target coslice. The item statement needs no repair or weakening. This is a correction below the unit level, not a change to the unit's proof route. In case i=3 the source also writes the coefficient presheaf as being on C_{Qbar/C}; the typed base is B. Step 5 writes it explicitly on C_{Qbar/B}, by composition of the augmentation to the original C.

For the generic new node, 08PK describes the comma objects and morphisms with the arrow V to u(U), as our presheaf convention requires. Over the point its hypotheses apply to arbitrary small categories with these topologies. 08PH permits constant module coefficients, and 08PG supplies the bar model. The counit is the ordinary Kan cocone K(a) after the derived comparison, so the augmentation clause is typed. With u the identity, the comma has initial (V,1) and the augmentation contracts to K(V). With empty source it gives the zero complex. These tests also check the direction of the comma and the absence of an erroneous general counit-isomorphism claim.

For the polynomial-target node I read the entire 08QV proof. It chooses a polynomial simplicial resolution P_bullet of B over A and uses (P_n,P_n[E]). Its image is constantly (C,id); the relevant mapping simplicial sets are products of fibers of P_bullet to B and P_bullet[E] to B[E]. The polynomial map remains a trivial Kan fibration by finite products and filtered colimits; for infinite E, monomials mean finitely supported exponent functions E to N. 08Q9 then gives the evaluation for every constant R-module presheaf. The canonical augmentation is the one induced by the identity presentation, which is initial in the opposite polynomial-presentation category. The node states the conclusion only, without putting this proof sketch inside its statement (B1). The cases E empty (C=B), arbitrary infinite E, A=B, zero coefficient module and the zero ring do not require new hypotheses. The cardinal convention includes all these sets and their finite constructions.

Step 1 uses the actual statement of lem-trf-continuous-cocontinuous-derived-shriek: exact inverse image and the unbounded left derived adjoint with its canonical adjunction. Both continuity hypotheses hold for the generated trivial topology; the ring pullback is the identity for constant R. The displayed polynomial formulas and structural augmentations are objectwise algebra calculations, with no claim that a variable-ring adjunction has been replaced by extension of scalars.

Step 2 uses the new comma node's precise bar-computation and counit-augmentation clauses. The coefficient is K(u_i U), and K(a) has the correct direction to K(V). An objectwise quasi-isomorphism is a quasi-isomorphism of presheaf complexes because kernels and cokernels are objectwise.

I verified the section objects satisfy the polynomial conditions: P is polynomial over itself on no variables, and Q is polynomial over A for the second section. In both cases the natural arrow has the indicated opposite direction, and its image under u_i is the identity, so its coefficient restriction is literally identity. The retractions have ps=1. The augmentation contraction inserts the initial coslice vertex; in degree zero its boundary is (W,x)-(V,K(a)x), and higher faces cancel in pairs. Thus the steps derive their counit clauses rather than invoke new nodes restating them. They are elementary relative to the cited bar theorem, not claims of uncited homotopy-cofinality.

This identifies the comma category, not a strict fiber. The coefficient presheaf is on presentations of Qbar over B and uses its augmentation to the original C.
