## Findings and corrections

The item is an agent strengthening and says so already. Bhatt–Scholze Remark 7.4.8 uses Example 7.1.7, hence the colimit topology. Its proof cannot simply be quoted with the norm topology; GL_n(Omega) is not complete for that topology. I retained the statement and replaced that possible inference by a completion argument. There is no STATEMENT-DISPUTED change, no change to targets or coefficient definitions, and no unit-route replacement.

The parent's reviewed record contains a useful completion/universal-cover truth check. I made the completion bridge explicit differently: at the generic field the separable-closure Cech coordinates already give a K-valued continuous representation of G_k; complete-field frame monodromy shows that it kills the kernel of G_k -> pi1(X); the quotient gives the representation over K. This avoids an unproved reduction-to-Omega claim on an infinite universal cover. Essential surjectivity glues from a compact image torsor; it does not assert that a general compact set of Omega-valued functions lies in a finite field. The compact-matrix coefficient descent theorem is not needed here.

The existing coefficient-cartesianness node was deliberately not cited: its Omega convention is the finite-subextension/colimit coefficient. The generic continuous-field realization node applies to arbitrary totally disconnected normed fields, so it is the appropriate input.

All four new files have statements only, no proof, no proof_deps, and no informal proof embedded in the statement. The proof plans below explain their intended reduction without declaring them split.

For each new node:

- normal-generic-continuous-sections: its real additional input is that normality preserves the generic component quotient on affine pro-etale objects. This is stronger than the finite/discrete 09Z5 node and is left unsplit. Intended proof: locally write an ind-etale algebra as a filtered colimit of etale algebras. At each finite stage normal connected components are integral and their generic fibres are connected; idempotents and their refinements identify the profinite component spaces in the limit. Hence the generic component map is a homeomorphism. Continuous functions into a totally disconnected field factor through those spaces (BS 4.2.12). For general weakly etale objects use BS 2.3.4 refinement and sheaf descent. This is a geometric lemma about sections, not a restatement of monodromy. The empty object gives the zero ring on both sides; disconnected etale charts and noncomplete A are permitted. Normality is essential; a nodal curve can have disconnected generic branches over a connected local chart.
- field-separable-cech-coordinates: I checked BS's field case in 7.3/7.4 and HRS Example 3.1. Intended proof: the spectrum of a separably closed field is weakly contractible; select a section of a trivializing cover to obtain a basis. Finite separable Galois subextensions compute the Cech rings, and their inverse limits give G_k^q. Evaluation of the continuous ring sheaf gives C(G_k^q,A). Morphisms of finite free modules are their column matrices, and face maps give exactly the action equation. Thus no assumption that A is complete is hidden. For k separably closed, G_k=1 and all formulas reduce to ordinary A-vector spaces. Imperfect k uses k^s, not an unsupported etale algebraic closure.
- complete-group-torsor-monodromy: I checked the actual complete/Noohi criterion in BS 7.1.5 and the pushout to discrete G-sets in the proof of 7.4.7; this is the general group-theoretic lemma used by that proof, not a duplicate local-field representation theorem. Intended proof: push a torsor to every discrete continuous G-set, obtaining locally constant set sheaves. Infinite Galois theory reconstructs a continuous homomorphism into Aut(forget G-Set)=G. Conversely the compact profinite source has compact image; its profinite torsor is formed from finite quotients (BS 7.4.3) and extended to G. Fibre choices give pointed maps, and comparisons are detected on the discrete actions. Normality identifies pro-etale and etale pi1 by BS 7.4.10 and Stacks 0BQ3. Naturality follows from these functors, not from arbitrary choices of conjugacy classes. Small cases: G=1, G finite discrete, X a geometric point, and a nontrivial compact H. Completeness is explicitly retained; the lemma is not applied to GL_n(Omega).
