## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported assertion `H_{union_alpha Y_alpha} = union_alpha H_{Y_alpha}` for every increasing family is false if the indexing family is empty. Take K = Delta^0 and I the empty poset (one may also take I a singleton and K_i empty). The family with no members is increasing. Its union in N(I) is empty, so the left side is H_empty = K, whereas the right side is the empty simplicial set. These differ already in degree zero.

The corrected formula is `H_{union_alpha Y_alpha} = K union union_alpha H_{Y_alpha}`. This includes the empty family and specializes to exactly the old equality for every nonempty family, because each H_Y contains K. It preserves the source's initial stage and its continuity at every nonzero limit ordinal: HTT explicitly treats the initial stage separately and then nonzero limits. It changes no directedness, covering, or nonemptiness hypothesis on I, K or K_i.
