### Original step 1

*Step 1 (two members of $\Sigma$ lie in a third).* Let $\mathcal A_1,\mathcal A_2\in\Sigma$. For every open $W$ the map $\mathcal A_1(W)\times\mathcal A_2(W)\to\mathcal A(W)$, $(a,b)\mapsto ab$, is $\mathcal O_X(W)$-bilinear ($\mathcal A(W)$ is a commutative $\mathcal O_X(W)$-algebra containing $\mathcal A_1(W),\mathcal A_2(W)$), so it induces $\mu^p_W:\mathcal A_1(W)\otimes_{\mathcal O_X(W)}\mathcal A_2(W)\to\mathcal A(W)$, $a\otimes b\mapsto ab$; these commute with restrictions (restrictions of $\mathcal A$ are ring maps), so they form a map of presheaves of $\mathcal O_X$-modules from the presheaf tensor product [def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product] to the sheaf $\mathcal A$, which by the universal property of sheafification [def-weil-pbc-sheafification] factors uniquely as $\mu\circ t$ with $t$ the sheafification map and $\mu:\mathcal A_1\otimes_{\mathcal O_X}\mathcal A_2\to\mathcal A$ $\mathcal O_X$-linear. As $\mathcal A_1,\mathcal A_2$ are quasi-coherent, $\mathcal A_1\otimes_{\mathcal O_X}\mathcal A_2$ is quasi-coherent and $t_U$ is bijective for every affine open $U$ [lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-tensor-product-affine-sections]. Let $\mathcal A_3:=\operatorname{Im}\mu\subseteq\mathcal A$ (local sections of $\mathcal A$ locally in the image of $\mu$): it is a quasi-coherent sub-$\mathcal O_X$-module of $\mathcal A$ with $\mathcal A_3(U)=\mu_U((\mathcal A_1\otimes\mathcal A_2)(U))=\mu^p_U(\mathcal A_1(U)\otimes_R\mathcal A_2(U))$ for every affine open $U$, $R=\mathcal O_X(U)$ [lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-module-cokernel-image](1)-(3). So for affine $U$, $\mathcal A_3(U)$ is the $R$-span of the products $ab$ ($a\in\mathcal A_1(U)$, $b\in\mathcal A_2(U)$); it contains $1=1\cdot1$, is closed under multiplication since $(ab)(a'b')=(aa')(bb')$, contains $a=a\cdot1$ and $b=1\cdot b$, and lies in every $R$-subalgebra of $\mathcal A(U)$ containing $\mathcal A_1(U)\cup\mathcal A_2(U)$: it is the $R$-subalgebra generated by $\mathcal A_1(U)\cup\mathcal A_2(U)$. Hence if $x_1,\dots,x_m$ generate $\mathcal A_1(U)$ and $y_1,\dots,y_n$ generate $\mathcal A_2(U)$ as $R$-algebras, then $x_1,\dots,x_m,y_1,\dots,y_n$ generate $\mathcal A_3(U)$: $\mathcal A_3(U)$ is a finitely generated $R$-algebra. For an arbitrary open $W$ and $s,s'\in\mathcal A_3(W)$, every $x\in W$ has an affine open neighbourhood $V\subseteq W$ (affine opens form a basis of a scheme [def-pc-scheme]); $s|_V,s'|_V\in\mathcal A_3(V)$, which is closed under products, so $(ss')|_V$ lies in the image of $\mu_V$; thus $ss'$ is locally in the image of $\mu$, i.e. $ss'\in\mathcal A_3(W)$; likewise $1\in\mathcal A_3(W)$. So $\mathcal A_3(W)$ is an $\mathcal O_X(W)$-subalgebra of $\mathcal A(W)$ for every open $W$, and $\mathcal A_3\in\Sigma$. Finally $\mathcal A_k\subseteq\mathcal A_3$ ($k=1,2$): a section $s\in\mathcal A_k(W)$ satisfies $s|_V\in\mathcal A_k(V)\subseteq\mathcal A_3(V)=\operatorname{Im}(\mu_V)$ on every affine open $V\subseteq W$, so $s$ is locally in the image of $\mu$, i.e. $s\in\mathcal A_3(W)$. (Stacks: 'the image of $\mathcal A_1\otimes_{\mathcal O_X}\mathcal A_2\to\mathcal A$ is also a quasi-coherent $\mathcal O_X$-subalgebra of finite type (some details omitted) which contains both'; the omitted details are written out here.)

### Original step 2

*Step 2 (every section over an affine open lies in a member of $\Sigma$; this is (c)).* By [lem-weil-pbc-module-directed-colimit-finite-presentation] (Properties 01PK; $X$ is qcqs) applied to the underlying quasi-coherent module of $\mathcal A$ there are a directed set $I$ (nonempty), $\mathcal O_X$-modules of finite presentation $\mathcal F_i$ with transition maps, and $\mathcal O_X$-linear $\varphi_i:\mathcal F_i\to\mathcal A$ such that $(\mathcal A,(\varphi_i))=\varinjlim_i\mathcal F_i$ in $\mathcal O_X$-modules. Each $\mathcal F_i$ is quasi-coherent [lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-finite-type-affine](0), so [lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-symmetric-algebra] gives the quasi-coherent $\mathcal O_X$-algebra $\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal F_i)$ with $j_i:\mathcal F_i\to\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal F_i)$, and by its part (a) the algebra map $\psi_i:\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal F_i)\to\mathcal A$ with $\psi_i\circ j_i=\varphi_i$. By [lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-algebra-kernel-image-ideal](a), $\mathcal A'_i:=\operatorname{Im}\psi_i$ is a quasi-coherent $\mathcal O_X$-subalgebra of $\mathcal A$ with $\mathcal A'_i(U)=\psi_{i,U}(\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal F_i)(U))$ for every affine open $U$. For such $U$, $R=\mathcal O_X(U)$: $\mathcal F_i(U)$ is a finitely presented, hence finitely generated, $R$-module [lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-finite-type-affine](b), say by $f_1,\dots,f_n$; $\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal F_i)(U)\cong\operatorname{Sym}_R(\mathcal F_i(U))$ by the $R$-algebra isomorphism extending $j_{i,U}$ [lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-symmetric-algebra](b), so $\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal F_i)(U)$ is generated as an $R$-algebra by $j_{i,U}(f_1),\dots,j_{i,U}(f_n)$ [lem-weil-pbc-symmetric-algebra-finite-presentation](a), and $\mathcal A'_i(U)$ by $\varphi_{i,U}(f_1),\dots,\varphi_{i,U}(f_n)$. So $\mathcal A'_i\in\Sigma$ for every $i\in I$. Now let $U$ be affine and $a\in\mathcal A(U)$. By [lem-weil-pbc-quasi-coherent-module-colimit-affine-sections] (for $\mathcal A=\varinjlim_i\mathcal F_i$) $a=\varphi_{i,U}(x)$ for some $i$ and $x\in\mathcal F_i(U)$, hence $a=\psi_{i,U}(j_{i,U}(x))\in\mathcal A'_i(U)$. This proves (c). (Stacks proves the same via Lemma 05JS: there $\mathcal A=\varinjlim\mathcal A_{i,j}$ with $\mathcal A_{i,j}=\operatorname{Sym}(\mathcal F_i)/\mathcal I_{i,j}$ and the map $\mathcal A_{i,j}\to\mathcal A$ induced by $\psi_i$, so $\operatorname{Im}(\mathcal A_{i,j}\to\mathcal A)=\operatorname{Im}\psi_i=\mathcal A'_i$; the finite presentation of $\mathcal A_{i,j}$ is not needed for 05JT, only the first lines of the proof of 05JS, which are this step.)
