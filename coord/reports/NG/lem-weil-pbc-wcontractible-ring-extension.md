## Review by NG

I read Stacks `proetale.tex` at 0983 (`proposition-find-w-contractible`), 0984 (`remark-size-w-contractible`), 0981 (`definition-w-contractible`), and 0989 (`definition-fpqc-covering`). The item agrees with their combined conclusion: an arbitrary commutative ring is allowed, kappa is infinite and bounds its size, the resulting map is faithfully flat and ind-etale, the four-level power bound is retained exactly, and retractions are required for weakly-etale faithfully-flat maps. I also read the chapter and the proper-base-change unit. This is a supporting input on the existing qbar coefficient-continuity branch, not a replacement for any of the unit's three base-change proof steps.

For the size node I read 0976 (`remark-size-w`), 097S (`remark-size-T`), the first paragraph of 097X (`lemma-get-w-local-algebraic-residue-field-extensions`), and 0984. The named blueprint strict construction has the same size argument: w-localization has at most kappa finite algebraic data; there are at most kappa isomorphism classes of finite-presentation etale algebras over a ring of size at most kappa. Its finite tensor products and countable iteration still have size at most kappa. Retained localization is a localization of a w-localization of E, so stays within kappa. This is a bound on the specified C_A, not a claim that an arbitrary strict w-local extension is small. This construction-counting lemma is missing from the existing strict-cover property statement, and cannot be obtained merely by citing its proof.

For the topological node I read `topology.tex` 090D (`lemma-existence-projective-cover`) and 090E (`remark-size-projective-cover`). They give a compact Hausdorff extremally disconnected cover of any compact Hausdorff space, with the bound 2^(2^lambda) for a minimal cover. Such a space is totally disconnected: in a compact Hausdorff space one can choose an open neighbourhood whose closure avoids any specified different point; extremal disconnectedness makes that closure clopen. The extra profinite assumption on X specializes the theorem, and no irreducibility or nonemptiness is assumed. Mathlib's `CompHaus.projectivePresentation` and Gleason results cover existence but none of the read search hits states the size bound; hence I have not labeled the full node a Mathlib leaf.

For the ring-pullback node I read 097D (`lemma-construct-profinite`), 096C (`lemma-silly`), 096T (`lemma-ind-zariski-implies`), the paragraph after 096A (`definition-w-local`), and `topology.tex` 0906 (`lemma-spectral-pi0`). These give a genuine ring realization for an arbitrary continuous T-to-components map, the cartesian topological square, local-ring identifications, w-locality and preservation of the closed-point set. The closed points of a w-local spectrum map homeomorphically to its components; applying this to D identifies its maximal ideal set with T. Surjectivity of u is only required for surjectivity of the spectrum map. The ind-etale clause follows from the ind-Zariski building blocks: finite products of principal localizations are etale and their filtered colimit is ind-etale. This generic input has no extremal-disconnectedness or strict-henselianity assumption and asserts no retraction or cardinal bound.

For the geometric criterion I read 0982 (`lemma-w-local-strictly-henselian-extremally-disconnected`) including its equivalence of (1), (2), and (3). The node states implication (3) to (1); its hypotheses include all three geometric conditions and its conclusion allows every faithfully flat weakly-etale map. It does not replace the target's witness construction. In particular it cannot be applied to C before the extremally disconnected component cover has been chosen.

Step 1 uses the actual strict-cover definition and properties, plus the new size-only statement. The first introduces C_A; the second asserts faithful flatness, ind-etaleness, w-locality and maximal strict henselianity; the third alone supplies its bound. Step 2 uses the ring-pullback node's initial assertion about the profinite component space and its closed-point identification, and the topological node's quantified cardinal bound. The inequality |P| <= 2^kappa is obtained by injecting maximal ideals into the power set of C. Lambda=2^kappa is infinite, so the topological node's hypotheses hold.

Step 3 uses exactly the ring-pullback statement's supplied D, cartesian spectrum, local-ring isomorphisms, closed-point preservation and surjectivity under surjective u. Strict henselianity is transferred along an actual isomorphism at a maximal ideal, whose contraction is maximal by the closed-point assertion. The ind-etale-stability node explicitly states composition and the weakly-etale consequence; the ind-etale definition explicitly gives flatness. The spectrum-surjectivity node supplies surjectivity for A-to-C and the flat-plus-surjective criterion supplies faithful flatness for the composite A-to-D. Step 5 uses the ring-pullback node's local-ring and maximal-point identifications. The remaining work is written out: a local fraction is represented by a pair of elements of C; nonzero d stays nonzero at a maximal ideal containing its annihilator; thus D injects into the product of its maximal local rings. Bounding each factor by kappa and the number of factors by |T| gives the target's exact exponent. This step is elementary algebra and counting after the cited structural theorem; it does not invoke the final bound of 0984 as a black box.
