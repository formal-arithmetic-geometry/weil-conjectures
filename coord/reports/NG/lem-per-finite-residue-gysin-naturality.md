## Source error

ILO XVII, proof of Proposition 3.3.1, printed p.269, writes a canonical morphism from H^0 K to tau_{<=0} K. The canonical good-truncation morphism has the opposite direction: tau_{<=0} K to H^0 K. On the usual representative its degree-zero component is the quotient ker(d^0) to ker(d^0)/im(d^{-1}); reversing it would require a splitting. For example with coefficient ring R=Z/ell^2 and the two-term complex [R --ell--> R] in degrees -1,0, this quotient R to R/(ell) has no R-linear section: the elements killed by ell lie in ell R and project to zero. Step 7 uses the actual canonical projection. The trait cochain calculation proves that it gives an isomorphism on the required degree-two supported cohomology, so naturality still proves the intended reduction. This repairs the proof, with no change to the proposition or to the imported statement.
