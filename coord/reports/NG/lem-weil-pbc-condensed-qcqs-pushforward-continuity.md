For the new input, the source argument is: kernels evaluate sectionwise; epimorphisms of module sheaves are epimorphisms on underlying sheaves of sets, so weak contractibility makes evaluation surjective, giving exactness. An affine pro-etale object is quasi-compact as a site object by the covering definition, and 0935(2) makes evaluation preserve direct sums. For an arbitrary small diagram $M$, its colimit is the cokernel of the difference map $\bigoplus_{\alpha:i\to j}M_i\to\bigoplus_iM_i$, which on the arrow summand sends $x$ to its source inclusion minus the target inclusion of $M(\alpha)x$. Exactness and direct-sum preservation evaluate this presentation into the identical colimit presentation of the modules $M_i(W)$. This proves preservation of colimits and identifies all cocone legs. This argument belongs here as preparation for splitting the new node, not inside its statement.
