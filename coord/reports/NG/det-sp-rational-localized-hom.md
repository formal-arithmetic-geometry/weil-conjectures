STATEMENT-DISPUTED: “T a scheme” is too broad for the Hom formula. Let T be the disjoint union, indexed by positive integers n, of copies of Spec of an algebraically closed field of characteristic different from ell, and take K_0=L_0=O_{E,T} in degree zero. These are constructible in the source's affine-local definition. Integral endomorphisms are product_n O_E and rational endomorphisms are product_n E. The image of (product_n O_E) tensor_{O_E} E consists of sequences with a common bound on their denominators. The endomorphism with nth entry ell^{-n} is absent from that image. This is an explicit failure of surjectivity at q=0.
