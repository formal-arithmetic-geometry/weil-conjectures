### lem-weil-pbc-kflat-fixed-tensor-triangulated

Source: Stacks sites-cohomology.tex, Tag 06YU (definition-derived-tor and construction immediately before it); More on Algebra Tag 0FNH (item-shift-tensor). The cone/last-arrow calculation below uses the cone and connecting-map convention explicitly specified in def-weil-abstract-derived-category. Proof origin: agent expansion of these sources.

1. For a K-flat $A$ and a quasi-isomorphism $v:B\to C$, its cone $C(v)$ is acyclic by [lem-pur-quasi-iso-cone-acyclic]. Thus $C(v)\otimes A$ is acyclic by [def-pc-kflat-complex]. The unsigned reindexing $C(v)\otimes A\cong C(v\otimes1_A)$ is a chain isomorphism: on $B^{p+1}\otimes A^q$ the second differential in both complexes has coefficient $(-1)^p$, since the cone negates the tensor differential with first degree $p+1$. On the $C^p\otimes A^q$ summand both differentials agree directly. Hence $v\otimes1_A$ is a quasi-isomorphism by the same cone criterion. The graded swap $x_p\otimes y_q\mapsto(-1)^{pq}y_q\otimes x_p$ is a chain isomorphism: its two differential coefficients agree modulo two by $(p+1)q=pq+q$ and $p+p(q+1)=pq$. Therefore $1_A\otimes v$ is also a quasi-isomorphism.

   Cited statements: lem-pur-quasi-iso-cone-acyclic, def-pc-kflat-complex, def-pc-tensor-complex-sheaves.

2. The cited tensor-cone lemma gives $A\otimes C(v)\cong C(1_A\otimes v)$ compatibly with inclusion. Explicitly it is identity on $A^p\otimes C^q$ and $(-1)^p$ on $A^p\otimes B^{q+1}$; the off-diagonal differential $(-1)^pv$ is corrected by this sign. Its projection equals the second-factor shift map of [lem-weil-pbc-total-tensor-integer-shifts] after $1_A\otimes p$, since both are $(-1)^p$ on the shifted summand and zero on the other. Thus it also respects the connecting map $-p$. For $C(v)\otimes A$, the unsigned comparison of Step 1 respects inclusion and projection with the unsigned first-factor shift. Both tensor functors therefore preserve cone triangles, including the last arrow.

   Cited statements: lem-pc-tensor-mapping-cone-iso, lem-weil-pbc-total-tensor-integer-shifts, def-weil-abstract-derived-category, def-pc-tensor-complex-sheaves.

3. Tensor is additive by [lem-pc-sheaf-modules-tensor-closed], and its totalization preserves finite biproduct diagrams summandwise. Localize the two functors of Step 1 at quasi-isomorphisms using [def-weil-abstract-derived-category]. Every distinguished triangle is isomorphic to a localized cone triangle, so Step 2 proves exactness of the descended functors. Their restrictions to flat-term K-flat models identify them with $QA\otimes^L-$ and $-\otimes^LQA$ by [lem-weil-pbc-site-derived-tensor-descent]. The coherent shift maps and their naturality descend from the total-tensor shift leaf: precomposition with a localization is fully faithful on functor categories, so their equations remain equations. The same leaf identifies $H\otimes A[n]$ with $(H\otimes A)[n]$; for acyclic $H$ this proves K-flatness of $A[n]$, and its terms are just reindexed flat terms. Naturality also in the fixed argument descends using the resolving-model equivalence in the cited descent node, including inverses of quasi-isomorphisms. This supplies the asserted bifunctor shift structures and their fixed-variable naturality.

   Cited statements: def-weil-abstract-derived-category, lem-weil-pbc-site-derived-tensor-descent, lem-weil-pbc-total-tensor-integer-shifts, def-pc-kflat-complex, lem-pc-sheaf-modules-tensor-closed.
