## Faults repaired

The imported proof does not construct a complex. On the one-point topos, take Lambda=Z and F=Z[0], with N=0. Its free module in degree 0 is Z^(Z), with evaluation counit. The nonzero basis element k=[0] lies in the evaluation kernel. The imported degree -1 fibre product is ker(evaluation), and the basis element l=[k] in its free cover has differential k. Since F^-1=F^-2=0, the next imported fibre product is the entire degree -1 free module. Thus its degree -2 basis element [l] has second differential k, not zero. This is a counterexample to the imported construction, not to the lemma. Its claim that every kernel differential is surjective onto the entire next kernel also contradicts the square-zero equation unless the next differential vanishes. The correct surjectivity is onto cycles.

The replacement uses the cycles of the already constructed tail, exactly as in Stacks 05T7(1), and writes out the omitted induction checks. It operates on presheaves before sheafification, so its chosen free evaluations are sectionwise surjective; it never treats an arbitrary sheaf epimorphism as sectionwise surjective. I retained the same free-cover, descending-resolution and sheafification route, and made no change to the unit or chapter.

The statement now introduces T and Lambda and the definitions of complex, quasi-isomorphism and flatness. The coefficient macro kunZn is expanded to Z/nZ, with n quantified. This only makes the imported intended statement self-contained; the theorem is valid for every commutative coefficient ring, including n=0, so the final assertion is valid for every integer n. No additional restriction or weakening was introduced. No target statement is edited.

The imported proof cited the derived-category definition for the long exact sequence, which its statement does not supply. I instead prove sectionwise cohomology bijectivity by explicit cycle/boundary lifts and cite the existing exact-evaluation and exact-sheafification result.
