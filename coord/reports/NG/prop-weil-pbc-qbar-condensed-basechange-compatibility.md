### Continuity input and its proof for the next worker

Stacks 09A3 states the abelian-sheaf R Gamma result for a uniformly bounded-below family, not arbitrary unbounded objects. The new relative module version is justified as follows. A sheaf of Q-algebras R is flat as an abelian sheaf: tensoring factors through rationalization, and tensor of Q-vector sheaves is exact (sectionwise vector tensor followed by exact sheafification). Thus the free-R-module functor is exact, so its right adjoint, forgetting to abelian sheaves, preserves injectives and K-injectives by the complex Hom adjunction. It is exact and preserves sums by lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-colimits-forgetful. Consequently module derived sections agree after forgetting with abelian derived sections, and this forgetful functor detects isomorphisms. For an affine pro-etale V over U, the scheme W=T times_U V is qcqs. Derived evaluation on V of Rh_* is R Gamma(W,-); this is the ordinary slice-site derived evaluation, obtained by restricting a K-injective resolution (restriction to a slice has exact extension-by-zero left adjoint). Apply 09A3 on W to the underlying family with the stated common bound. Cohomology of Rh_* is the sheafification of these local derived-section presheaves, and direct sums commute with that sheafification. The comparisons are therefore isomorphisms on cohomology sheaves on the affine basis, hence in the derived category. This explains the adaptation in source.place; it does not pretend that Stacks states the module-relative formulation verbatim.
