### Old Step 1

Choose a flat-term K-flat resolution $P\to H$ of a complex representing $K$. Exact extension represents $j_{R!}K$ termwise and carries this resolution to a quasi-isomorphism. The total-tensor extension lemma gives K-flatness of $j_{R!}P$. Its terms are flat as well: for an exact sequence $A\to B\to C$ of $R$-modules, restrict it by exact $j^*$, tensor with flat $P^i$, and apply exact $j_{R!}$. The sheaf projection isomorphisms identify the resulting exact sequence with its tensor by $j_{R!}P^i$. Thus every flat-term K-flat representative, not just the chosen resolution, is sent to one.
