Natural-language proof for the unsplit model prerequisite: for a module sheaf F, the acyclic hypercover spectral sequence computes RΓ(W,F) by its face complex since every higher cohomology over U_p vanishes by exact evaluation. For a bounded-below E choose an injective resolution E -> J. Exact evaluation at each U_p makes each vertical map E(U_p) -> J(U_p) a quasi-isomorphism. The vertical filtration therefore makes Tot(E(U_bullet)) -> Tot(J(U_bullet)) a quasi-isomorphism. For each injective J^q, the hypercover face complex has only degree-zero cohomology J^q(W); the horizontal filtration identifies the latter total complex with J(W). Both filtrations converge since after shifting by N the double complex is first quadrant with finite diagonals. All maps arise from augmentation and the resolution lift, giving naturality in E and the stated R(W)-linearity. This is the module version of the full 09A2 proof, not a derived forgetful-functor assumption. For the existence prerequisite the complete source proof in 09A1 constructs finite-degree coverings by affine weakly contractible objects in the qcqs subsite, inductively extends a hypercover using 094K, and replaces the finite family in each degree by its finite disjoint union; the latter remains affine and weakly contractible and the inclusion into the full site preserves the hypercover.
