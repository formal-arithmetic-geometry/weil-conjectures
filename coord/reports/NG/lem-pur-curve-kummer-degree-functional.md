## Source error

In the displayed proof of XVIII Proposition 1.1.6, printed p. 489, the supplied reedition says that Kummer yields a map to `R^1 p_* Z/n(1)`. The degree map here must have target `R^2 p_* Z/n(1)`. For S the spectrum of an algebraically closed field and n>1 invertible, `H^1(P^1,mu_n)=Pic(P^1)[n]=0`, whereas the proposed source is Z/n and `H^2(P^1,mu_n)=Z/n`. Thus the printed R^1 cannot give the asserted isomorphism. Step 6 uses the correctly indexed projective-line decomposition and trace already stated in `lem-asd-ordinary-finite-p1-adjunction` and `def-amb-p1-trace`. The item's statement was already correctly indexed and has not changed.
