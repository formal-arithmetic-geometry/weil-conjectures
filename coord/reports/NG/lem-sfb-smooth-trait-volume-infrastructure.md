2. **Total space (Step 2).** I read morphisms Tag 01T6 (`lemma-finite-type-noetherian`), algebra Tag 07NF (`lemma-regular-goes-up`) and properties Tags 02IT/0569 (`lemma-characterize-regular`, `lemma-regular-normal`). 3. **Generic density (Step 3).** I read algebra Tag 0565 (`lemma-when-injective-covering`), morphisms Tag 01RE (the sheaf-injection characterization), and divisors Tag 07ZU (the complement of an effective Cartier divisor). `lem-pc-smooth-flat` supplies morphism flatness. `lem-weil-pbc-open-preimage-fibre-product` and `lem-weil-pbc-basic-open-pullback` identify the generic fibre with the required open, on the actual smooth affine charts. `lem-weil-pbc-smooth-ring-map-flat` supplies flatness of B over R on charts with base T. `lem-trf-flat-tensor-preserves-injection` then applies to the injective map multiplication by pi on the domain R. 6. **Generic closed-fibre stalk (Step 6).** I read schemes Tag 0HA1 (`lemma-local-ring-fibre`), the generic-point existence assertion 01IS, morphisms Tag 01RV (`lemma-integral-scheme-rational-functions`) and algebra 00PD(4). `lem-sc-scheme-sober` gives the component's generic point. `lem-sc-rational-functions-generic-stalks` explicitly says that an integral scheme's generic stalk is a field and applies to the integral open component from Step 5. `lem-sbc-scheme-fibre-stalk-quotient` supplies B/pi B as that field, so pi B is maximal. `lem-weil-pbc-affine-open-stalk-localization` identifies the base stalk with R, since R is already local. `def-weil-flat-morphism` then gives B flat over R; the flat-tensor node proves the image of pi is a nonzerodivisor.
