## STATEMENT-DISPUTED

The imported wording for positive-degree matching sheaves omitted the slice over the augmentation h_W. Read literally as limits of proper faces in sheaves on X, it gives M_1=h_U0 times h_U0, whereas the source requires M_1=h_U0 times_{h_W} h_U0. This is false even for X=Spec(k) and W=X disjoint union X. If U_0 covers W, the two components of h_W both have local lifts to h_U0. Thus the absolute product contains locally a pair with different images in h_W. The augmentation identities require every pair of faces of a 1-simplex to have the same image in h_W. The matching arrow cannot locally lift that pair, so cannot be an epimorphism. The corrected statement specifies the finite matching limit in sheaves OVER h_W, gives the degree-one formula, and keeps weak contractibility in the FULL site X_proet. This repairs the ambiguity to the intent of Stacks 09A1(2), rather than changing the target or the coefficient category.

Finite scheme disjoint unions of affines are Spec of the finite product of their rings; the empty case is Spec of the zero ring.
