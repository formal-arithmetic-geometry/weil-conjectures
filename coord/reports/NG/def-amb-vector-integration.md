## Corrections and scope

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported definition never binds `A`, never specifies the site of `R pi_!`, and refers to `def-coefficient-twists-detailed`, which now defines pro-etale inverse-limit twists. Its specified affine-space trace, however, is an ordinary-etale finite-coefficient map. Consequently the old text is not a closed, typed definition without restoring the coefficient and site conventions. For example, taking the old unbound `A` to mean `Z` gives no instance of its cited finite-coefficient affine-space trace. Nor can one omit invertibility: on an algebraically closed field of characteristic p, the ordinary-etale sheaf mu_p is zero, so with A=F_p the rank-one source using that twist is zero and cannot have the claimed affine-space isomorphism to A. These are scope/type failures of an unrestricted reading, rather than counterexamples to the intended finite invertible-torsion construction.

The repaired statement explicitly uses the hypotheses of `lem-amb-affine-space-top-trace`: finite commutative A, an integer N>=1 killing A and invertible on a Noetherian base, and ordinary-etale finite Tate sheaves. The source unit sk-prop-an Steps 6–7 constructs exactly the local counit traces and their sheaf gluing. No target statement changes.

The pro-etale twist dependency is replaced by `lem-asd-etale-finite-twist-pullback`. This is not an identification of coefficient categories: the item now constructs only the ordinary-etale map. The existing downstream `lem-pur-vector-integration-site-comparison` states the comparison of actual maps with the prescribed pro-etale twists; it depends on this definition and is deliberately not made an ingredient of it. This avoids a cycle.

The original sentence “These maps glue as morphisms of sheaves” concealed the compact-support restriction map, the reduction to a degree-zero sheaf and the conversion back to a derived morphism. The definition now specifies those operations, including the actual truncation roof. Its data are obtained by the existing unique sheaf-gluing statement, not chosen from an undetermined existence theorem.

The following are the actual construction stages and the statements they use.

First, `def-asd-vector-bundle-zero-section` supplies Zariski trivializations and the explicit invertible linear substitutions on overlaps; its Noetherian hypothesis is retained. `def-weil-pbc-derived-categories` supplies ordinary module-derived categories, shifts, constant coefficients and cohomology sheaves. `lem-asd-etale-finite-twist-pullback` supplies exactly the ordinary finite Tate sheaves and their pullback identifications; unlike the removed dependency, it is on the right site and accepts a general ring killed by N. Second, `def-amb-etale-compact-support` defines the chosen-compactification functor and `lem-amb-etale-compact-comparisons` gives the bounded-below independent notation. `thm-cbc-finite-torsion` gives the exchange isomorphism for a Zariski open base change and its pasting compatibility, with no smooth-base-change assumption. Each open of a Noetherian scheme is Noetherian, and all local traces satisfy the same finite-ring and invertibility hypotheses. `lem-amb-affine-space-top-trace` actually states both that the specified counit is an isomorphism and that its map commutes with arbitrary Noetherian base change. I use both clauses, rather than merely the existence of some purity isomorphism.

Third, `prop-weil-pbc-inverse-image-modules-exact` states exactness for constant-ring module inverse image, so kernels, images and quotients computing cohomology commute with restriction. Each nonzero-degree cohomology sheaf restricts to zero. `lem-weil-pbc-etale-cover-detects-iso` is stated for abelian sheaves: I explicitly apply it to the underlying abelian sheaf's map to zero, which suffices also for a module sheaf. I do not apply an abelian-only theorem directly to an unspecified module-derived category.

Fourth, on an overlap the actual coordinate substitution is in GL_d(O(W)), exactly the automorphism covered by `lem-amb-linear-trace-invariance`. The base-change equality in the affine-space lemma and pasting in the exchange theorem compare restrictions with those same overlap traces. Taking H^0 therefore gives agreeing module-sheaf morphisms. `lem-pur-sheaf-hom-cover-gluing` states gluing of precisely these linear maps on a covering family, including uniqueness. I read its Stacks source: sites-modules.tex, Tag 03EM, label lemma-internal-hom, and its cited Sites gluing statement Tag 04TQ. No derived-descent theorem is being assumed.

Finally, `lem-weil-pbc-truncation-cohomology` supplies the cohomology of tau_{<=0}; `lem-weil-pbc-truncation-descends` supplies the projection j_0 and naturality on the derived category. The inclusion b and the cycle-quotient projection c have identity H^0 and zero cohomology in all other degrees, so they are quasi-isomorphisms by the definition in `def-weil-abstract-derived-category`. This explicitly defines alpha=Q(c)Q(b)^(-1). The naturality of the projection and exactness of restriction identify the restrictions of alpha; `lem-weil-pbc-derived-single-fully-faithful` gives the bijection between sheaf maps and derived maps between degree-zero single objects, so each reconstructed local map is the specified a_i and uniqueness reduces to sheaf-gluing uniqueness. I read homology.tex Tag 0118's canonical-truncation formula and derived.tex Tag 06XS, lemma-negative-exts, especially its degree-zero clause. These existing statements justify the construction without a new lemma restating the item.

Passing to a common refinement of covers compares the same local maps by linear invariance, so uniqueness removes the choice of cover and frame.
