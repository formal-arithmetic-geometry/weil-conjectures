## Review by NG

I checked the mathematical statement against the affine presentation refinement described in its source field. The base is assumed to have a finite Noetherian affine cover, not merely finite dimension or local Noetherianity; step 1 uses the finite cover and finite-type quasi-compactness to keep the entire list of polynomial generator counts finite. Step 2 uses Noetherianity of each base coordinate ring. Step 3 is the only place where finite dimension of the base is needed. The geometric fibre calculation works over every field-valued point, so in particular over every algebraically closed one, without restricting the residue field extension or assuming algebraicity. No step assumes separatedness, flatness, catenarity, excellence, or affine intersections.

For step 1 I opened def-weil-finite-type-morphism: its statement explicitly permits a finite affine cover of the inverse image of each base affine. I opened def-weil-pbc-finite-type-algebra: it explicitly provides a surjective finite-variable polynomial evaluation map. Choosing the finite union of these covers and taking the maximum of its generator counts with zero is elementary. The same e is fixed before any geometric point is chosen.

For step 2 I opened lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-algebra-noetherian: it says that a finite type algebra over a Noetherian ring is Noetherian, with no field hypothesis. This is precisely the assertion taken, with R=R_a and S=S_ab. Its source is Algebra 00FN, also the ingredient named in the proof of Morphisms 01T6. The step leaves the finite source cover explicit rather than relying on an unexplained global equivalence of definitions.

For step 3 I opened lem-weil-pbc-open-subspace-dimension-monotone, lem-weil-pbc-spec-topological-krull-dimension-ring, lem-sf-noetherian-polynomial-dimension, and def-weil-pbc-krull-dimension-ring. The first applies to V_a inside the whole open Y; the second equates its spectrum dimension with dim R_a. The polynomial theorem requires R_a Noetherian, already given. The quotient inequality is proved inside the step: inverse images under a surjective ring map are prime, and a lifted element witnesses every strict inclusion. Thus the prime-chain definition gives the inequality without silently importing a separate quotient-dimension theorem. Zero rings and dimension bottom are retained.

For step 4 I opened lem-sc-scheme-sober and lem-weil-pbc-dimension-bound-open-cover. The former says generic points exist uniquely, and the step explains why that supplies both quasi-sobriety and T0, as required by the latter's actual statement. The latter permits any cover and bottom-valued dimensions; here it bounds the whole source by the common finite integer D+e, rather than merely asserting that finitely many finite-dimensional charts suffice without a common bound.

For step 5 I opened def-weil-affine-spectrum, lem-weil-pbc-open-fibre-product, and lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra. The first explicitly says Spec of a field has one point. The second explicitly identifies the image with the intersection of the inverse images of the chosen opens: with the full second factor this is exactly the inverse image of U_ab in F. This checks both openness and coverage; a base-change-of-open-immersion statement alone would not have provided the range identification. The third explicitly gives the tensor ring and both projection maps for affine base and factors. The factorization into V_a is the restriction of a locally ringed-space morphism whose entire image lies there; it introduces no additional lifting assertion.

For step 6 I opened lem-weil-pbc-algebra-tensor-pushout-universal-property, def-weil-module-base-change, and def-weil-pbc-mv-polynomial-ring. They provide the tensor multiplication/scalar inclusions, tensor module with balancing, and evaluation. Pure tensors generate the tensor module by its definition. This establishes the surjection by a few-line generator calculation, not by an uncited flatness or right-exactness assertion. It is elementary and is not promoted to a new node.

For step 7 I reopened the polynomial dimension and spectrum comparison nodes and the ring-chain definition. The field's two ideals show both Noetherianity and dimension zero directly. The strict prime-chain lifting argument from step 3 applies to the explicit surjection of step 6; it also allows zero fibre rings. All hypotheses of the polynomial theorem are checked for Omega itself, and this does not use a comparison of dimensions under field extension.
