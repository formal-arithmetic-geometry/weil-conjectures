## Review by Q108

I checked each proof dependency by opening its statement, and citations.json copies those statements verbatim for every step. I kept the statement unchanged and checked the following uses against their hypotheses.

Step 1: section-separated-closed-immersion applies to q=js because pq=1 and p is proper, hence separated; it does not assume that an arbitrary composition of an open and a closed immersion is closed. Closed-immersion-proper gives properness of s and q. The compact-support-square definition supplies the primed proper/open model. Open-closed-recollement explicitly calls the closed direct image exact in the ordinary etale module setting. These are exactly the facts used, with no purity hypothesis.

Step 2: proper-open-interchange-coherence allows a commutative noncartesian square. Its top open is the identity of B, its bottom open is j, and its two proper maps are s and q. Its resolution normalization specifies e. Etale-derived-direct-image-coherence specifies the compositor and the identity comparison, on arbitrary schemes. Their composite is precisely the inverse source map demanded by the item. No application of a Noetherian-only compactification-independent theorem is made on B'.

Step 3: etale-closed-constant-tensor gives the restriction-preserving supported constant exchange for an arbitrary scheme map. On exact closed direct images this is the same adjunction-mate arrow; the proper/open lemma explicitly states the base-change equation used, and def-cbc-exchange-map specifies the open-after-proper factorization. I wrote out the resulting equation after applying Rp'_*, including the beta_j factor and the naturality square for the proper exchange. I did not substitute a mere isomorphism of objects for the named arrows.

Step 4: the general mate-pasting lemma has both the pasting-along-adjunctions formula and the identity-mate formula. Exchange-forget-coefficients states the actual module exchange as the same counit-mate construction; it is used for its formula, not as a claim that a derived forgetful functor is faithful. Together with the normalized direct-image compositors this gives exchange for pq, which is identity after pq=1. The proof explicitly postcomposes and cancels r and r' to obtain the etale equation. This avoids the tempting but inapplicable strict-tower lemma and the abelian-only exchange-pasting interface.

Step 5: finite-compact-site-coherence applies to (s,h) on Noetherian schemes and arbitrary finite A. Its statement gives the precise equality between the two sites' specified proper/open composition maps, fixes the direct-image mate used in the comparison, and respects identity maps. Choosing the identity-open compactification for s and for hs gives exactly the source composite of Step 2. The immersion-comparison-normalization lemma supplies the actual closed counit normalization; its Noetherian requirement is checked in Step 6. Supported-constant-exchange-normalization identifies the restriction-preserving comparison on constants, and compact-support-comparison gives the canonical B_h. Although supported-constant-exchange-normalization uses the finite coefficient datum of def-pur-conventions, its assertion is for arbitrary schemes and uses only the ring A=A_E,n, not a structure map to the fixed geometric field; the same finite ring here is eligible. Its comparison is therefore used only at that level. The displayed equation r^p T=N r proves equality of source maps after inversion. I use neither a constructible-only theorem on arbitrary inputs nor an arbitrary-field extension of a finite-field hypothesis.

Step 6: finite-type-over-noetherian applies first over the field to B and X, then over B to the proper finite-type map p. A field is Noetherian directly because its ideals are 0 and 1. Noetherian-open-subscheme-noetherian makes each complement quasi-compact as a scheme; separated-implies-quasi-separated and qcqs-morphism-permanence make its inclusion quasi-compact. I corrected the first draft here to obtain quasi-separatedness of bar X from p and B, instead of silently invoking separatedness of their composite. The permanence statement explicitly includes arbitrary base changes. I also checked the case of a finite-type but nonseparated B': finite-type base-change stability and finite-type-over-noetherian make all primed schemes Noetherian, so the same composition-coherence node applies there. This corrects an initially narrower use of proper-composition-site-transport, whose separated-scheme hypothesis would not cover that case. Proetale-closed-exact therefore applies to s',q' even when B' is not Noetherian or quasi-compact. This is why the statement's underived s'_*A still agrees with Rs'_*A. The Noetherian immersion-comparison lemma is used only before base change.

Step 7: finite-level-proetale explicitly defines exchange as the transported etale proper/open exchange and allows arbitrary g and no qcqs hypothesis on the new base. Pullback-comparison supplies coherent endpoint identifications. Supported-constant-exchange-normalization explicitly gives the commuting square for the two supported-constant arrows. I wrote D^p S=T' N(D^e) and substituted the source identifications to obtain the equality of the actual maps; this is the missing calculation behind the word “transport.” On a non-finite-type B' the source is exactly the transported source stipulated by the item, so no finite-type composition theorem is applied there.
