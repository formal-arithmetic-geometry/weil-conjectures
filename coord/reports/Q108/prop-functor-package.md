Otherwise its assertion is the ambient completed isomorphism. Step 9 relabels the square explicitly and retains these hypotheses rather than silently asserting that the coefficient field alone supplies them. This clarifies this imported summary node; no target declaration is modified, and no unit or chapter route is replaced.
