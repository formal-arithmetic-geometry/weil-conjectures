### Original step 1

Fix a uniformizer $\pi$ of $R$. The DVR definition makes $R$ Noetherian. Each $X_i$ is finite type over $R$ (it is projective and finitely presented), hence Noetherian by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian]. The relative-dualizing assertion of [lem-sfb-nodal-log-canonical-reconstruction] gives an invertible $L_i$, without using positivity; its powers are invertible as well [def-sc-invertible-module]. For any such power $M$ on any of these flat families, multiplication by $\pi$ is injective on its stalks: over the generic point $\pi$ is a unit; at a special-fibre point the structure stalk is flat over $R$, so [lem-trf-flat-tensor-preserves-injection] applied to multiplication by $\pi$ on $R$ makes multiplication by $\pi$ on that stalk injective, and $M$ is locally that structure module. On an affine trivialization, $s|_{D(\pi)}=0$ means $\pi^rs=0$ for some $r$, so $s=0$. Consequently restriction $H^0(X_i,M)\to H^0((X_i)_K,M_K)$ is injective. The same argument applies to the common family used below.

### Original step 2

Let $x$ be any closed point of the special fibre of $X_i$, and put $A=\mathcal O_{X_i,x}$. The fibre-stalk quotient lemma identifies $B=A/\pi A$ with the local ring of that fibre at $x$. It is a reduced Noetherian local ring of dimension one, since the fibre is a reduced pure curve. Its maximal ideal $\mathfrak n$ is not associated to $B$: if $\operatorname{Ann}(z)=\mathfrak n$, then $z\ne0$; if $z$ is a unit then $\mathfrak n=0$ would make $B$ a field, while if $z\in\mathfrak n$ then $z^2=0$ contradicts reducedness. Thus [lem-weil-pbc-nonzerodivisor-if-maximal-not-associated], applied with the finite module $B$, supplies a nonzerodivisor in $\mathfrak n$, so $\operatorname{depth}B\ge1$. The flat local depth formula for the Noetherian local map $R\to A$ now gives $\operatorname{depth}A=\operatorname{depth}R+\operatorname{depth}B\ge2$: the uniformizer is an $R$-regular sequence of length one. Every stalk of $L_i^{\otimes m}$ at $x$ is free of rank one over $A$, and so also has depth at least two. This checks the boundary hypothesis needed for Hartogs extension without asserting normality of $X_i$.
