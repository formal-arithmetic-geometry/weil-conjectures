## Proof plans retained for the unsplit nodes

These plans are in the report only, as required for statement-only new nodes. The original steps are copied verbatim after the review as an additional lossless record.

For `lem-trf-monomial-rees-chart-comparison`, apply the existing `lem-trf-log-regular-monomial-tor` to nH for every n (0H=P). Its injective multiplication map has image J^n. The formula a tensor z^p -> a alpha(p) respects products, so the maps give a graded algebra isomorphism. Localize at z^h t and take degree zero. The degree-zero pieces are the direct system Z[nH] with transition z^h; its monomials are exactly p-nh, and after tensoring the resulting ring is A tensor Z[Q_h]. On the ordinary Rees side they are j/alpha(h)^n, so the ring is A[J/alpha(h)]. Further localizations have the same fractions, proving overlap compatibility. Use the existing chart-construction node and affine blowup cover to identify the whole affine fine blowup. No assumption that the chart map to Z[P] is flat is made.

For `lem-trf-etale-blowup-chart-descent`, flat base change of blowing up (Stacks 0805) compares each local blowup to B times_X X_i. Since A_i[J_i/a] embeds in (A_i)_a, the assumed non-zero-divisor a remains a non-zero-divisor of that chart; J becomes (a). On any flat T over Z_i, tensor the multiplication injection by the flat algebra to prove the extended ideal is still invertible. In particular this applies to Z times_X X_i times_X X_j, since X_j -> X is flat. Stacks 0806 now gives equality of the two X-maps to B. The existing fpqc-finer-than-etale and covering-base-change statements allow representable descent of these compatible maps to phi:Z->B. The inverse local isomorphisms agree on overlaps because they invert the base changes of this same phi. Descend them to psi:B->Z and check psi phi and phi psi on the covering; representable descent uniqueness proves the identities. Uniqueness of the descended map also gives canonicity. This is the full argument of the previous reader, not an appeal to unproved 'etale locality'.

For `lem-trf-finite-normal-blowup-recognition`, on integral affine opens of X the nonempty rings A[J/a] sit in Frac(A), are domains with that fraction field, and contain the common generic point. Their gluing gives B integral with field k(X). Normality makes Y reduced; its dense open V is isomorphic to irreducible U, making Y irreducible, hence integral. Finiteness makes the image closed and containing the dense open above U, so f is surjective. Each nonempty affine inverse image is Spec D for a finite C-algebra, and C and D embed in the common field k(X). D is integrally closed in that field, because its scheme is normal and integral. Finiteness gives D integral over C (existing finite-algebra-is-integral node). Conversely a field element integral over C is integral over D, so normality puts it in D. Thus D is precisely the integral closure of C. The inclusions in k(X) give compatibility on all overlaps, and the definition of normalization glues the unique B-isomorphism. This proves finiteness of normalization in this setting; it never assumes excellence or finiteness of arbitrary normalization.

## Original eight proof steps, preserved verbatim

### Original step 1

By [lem-trf-coherent-log-ideal-chart-construction], $J$ is finite type and quasi-coherent. By [lem-trf-log-regular-divisorial-structure], $U$ is open dense and every local log section restricts to a unit on $U$. Hence the nonempty ideal $\mathcal I$ gives $J|_U=\mathcal O_U$, so $J\ne0$. On any nonempty affine open of integral $X$, a monomial chart generator is nonzero wherever that chart is nonempty: it is invertible on its intersection with the dense $U$. This also rules out a zero denominator in the nonempty blowup charts below.

Cited nodes: lem-trf-coherent-log-ideal-chart-construction, lem-trf-log-regular-divisorial-structure

### Original step 2

Apply [lem-trf-coherent-log-ideal-chart-construction] to construct the fine unsaturated log blowup $(X_0,M_0)$ and the fs log scheme $(X',M')$, with a finite map $s:X'\to X_0$. The fs universal property is exactly the saturated log blowup of [def-trf-fs-log-blowup]. In a strict etale chart of $X$, write $E=\{h_1,\ldots,h_r\}$, $H=E+P$, and $Q_h=P[H-h]$. Its saturated charts have rings $A\otimes_{\mathbf Z[P]}\mathbf Z[Q_h^{\mathrm{sat}}]$. The saturated monoids are finitely generated by the chart-construction input and have the same group completion as $P$. Therefore [lem-trf-log-regular-toric-refinement] makes these charts log regular and locally Noetherian. These are etale-local conditions (the definition checks geometric strict local rings), so $(X',M')$ is log regular and locally Noetherian.

Cited nodes: lem-trf-coherent-log-ideal-chart-construction, def-trf-fs-log-blowup, lem-trf-log-regular-toric-refinement

### Original step 3

We identify the unsaturated chart with an ordinary blowup chart, rather than assuming that arbitrary blowups commute with nonflat base change. Set $L=\mathbf Z[H]\subset\mathbf Z[P]$, $J_A=(\alpha(h):h\in H)$. For every $n\geq0$, apply [lem-trf-log-regular-monomial-tor] to the monoid ideal $nH$ (with $0H=P$). It identifies $A\otimes\mathbf Z[nH]$ with $J_A^n$. The maps send $a\otimes z^p$ to $a\alpha(p)$; hence they preserve multiplication, since $\alpha(p+q)=\alpha(p)\alpha(q)$. They thus identify the base-changed graded ring $A\otimes\bigoplus_{n\geq0}L^nt^n$ with $\bigoplus_{n\geq0}J_A^nt^n$. Inverting $z^ht$ and taking degree zero expresses the first ring as the colimit of $A\otimes L^n$, with transition multiplication by $z^h$. Its elements are the fractions represented by $a\otimes z^{p-nh}$; their addition, multiplication and transition relations are precisely those of $A\otimes\mathbf Z[Q_h]$. On the second side the fractions are $j/\alpha(h)^n$ with $j\in J_A^n$, so the result is $A[J_A/\alpha(h)]$ as in [def-nagata-blowup-chart-ring]. These maps agree on the overlaps of the charts of one $\operatorname{Spec}A$, because further localization uses the very same fractions. With the affine cover of [prop-nagata-affine-blowup-chart-cover], they identify $X_0\times_X\operatorname{Spec}A$, which is the fine log blowup of the chart by the strict-etale compatibility in [lem-trf-coherent-log-ideal-chart-construction], with $\operatorname{Bl}_{J_A}(\operatorname{Spec}A)$ over $\operatorname{Spec}A$. Steps 4--5 globalize this to $B=\operatorname{Bl}_JX$.

Cited nodes: lem-trf-log-regular-monomial-tor, def-nagata-blowup-chart-ring, prop-nagata-affine-blowup-chart-cover, lem-trf-coherent-log-ideal-chart-construction

### Original step 4

Fix a family of strict etale charts $g_i:\operatorname{Spec}A_i\to X$ as in Step 3 covering $X$. Each $g_i$ is etale, hence flat, so [thm-nagata-blowup-flat-base-change] says that $\operatorname{bc}_{g_i}:\operatorname{Bl}_{J_{A_i}}(\operatorname{Spec}A_i)\to\operatorname{Spec}A_i\times_XB$ [def-nagata-blowup-flat-base-change-map] is an isomorphism over $\operatorname{Spec}A_i$; here $J\mathcal O_{\operatorname{Spec}A_i}=J_{A_i}$ by [lem-trf-coherent-log-ideal-chart-construction]. Composing with Step 3 gives isomorphisms $\tilde\varphi_i:X_0\times_X\operatorname{Spec}A_i\to B\times_X\operatorname{Spec}A_i$ over $\operatorname{Spec}A_i$; put $\varphi_i=\mathrm{pr}_B\circ\tilde\varphi_i$, an $X$-morphism. Invertibility: on the chart $\operatorname{Spec}A_i[J_{A_i}/\alpha(h)]$ the extended ideal of $J$ is generated by $\alpha(h)$. By [lem-trf-log-regular-divisorial-structure] applied to the log regular chart $\operatorname{Spec}A_i$, $\alpha(h)$ is a unit on a dense open of a scheme whose local rings are domains, so it is a non-zero-divisor of $A_i$, hence of $A_i[J_{A_i}/\alpha(h)]\subset(A_i)_{\alpha(h)}$. Tensoring the injection given by multiplication by $\alpha(h)$ with a flat algebra keeps it injective. So for every $T$ flat over $X_0\times_X\operatorname{Spec}A_i$, in particular $T=X_0\times_X\operatorname{Spec}A_i\times_X\operatorname{Spec}A_j$, the extended ideal $J\mathcal O_T$ is locally generated by one non-zero-divisor, hence invertible. On that $T$ the two $X$-morphisms $\varphi_i\circ p_1$ and $\varphi_j\circ p_2$ to $B$ therefore coincide, by the uniqueness clause of [thm-nagata-blowup-universal-property].

### Original step 5

By [lem-weil-pbc-fpqc-finer-than-etale] the family $(g_i)$ is an fpqc covering of $X$, and by clause (3) of [lem-weil-pbc-fpqc-covering-axioms] its base changes $(X_0\times_X\operatorname{Spec}A_i\to X_0)$ and $(B\times_X\operatorname{Spec}A_i\to B)$ are fpqc coverings. By Step 4 the morphisms $\varphi_i$ agree on the fibre products, so [lem-weil-pbc-fpqc-representable-descent] gives a unique $\varphi:X_0\to B$ with $\varphi\circ p_i=\varphi_i$; it lies over $X$ because both composites to $X$ agree after the covering, by the uniqueness in the same lemma. The base change of $\varphi$ along $g_i$ is $\tilde\varphi_i$, an isomorphism. The morphisms $\psi_i=\mathrm{pr}_{X_0}\circ\tilde\varphi_i^{-1}:B\times_X\operatorname{Spec}A_i\to X_0$ agree on $B\times_X\operatorname{Spec}A_i\times_X\operatorname{Spec}A_j$, where both are the projection composed with the inverse of the base change of $\varphi$ to that fibre product. Descent gives $\psi:B\to X_0$. Both $\psi\varphi$ and $\mathrm{id}_{X_0}$ restrict to the projections on the covering of $X_0$, and $\varphi\psi$ and $\mathrm{id}_B$ on the covering of $B$, so uniqueness of descent makes $\varphi$ an isomorphism over $X$, $X_0\cong B$. It is canonical, being the unique morphism compatible with the canonical chart isomorphisms $\tilde\varphi_i$.

### Original step 6

Over $U$, every element of $M$ is a unit. Thus $\mathcal I|_U=M|_U$, an ideal generated by the identity. The identity of $U$ has the fs universal property, so [def-trf-fs-log-blowup] identifies the restriction $b^{-1}U\to U$ with the identity as a log scheme. In particular its log structure is trivial. Conversely take a geometric point $x'\in X'$ with image $x\notin U$. There exists $m\in M_{\bar x}$ with nonunit structural image in $\mathcal O_{X,\bar x}$; otherwise the defining inverse-image-of-units condition of a log structure would make its characteristic zero. The induced homomorphism of strict local rings is local, so the image of $\alpha(m)$ is in the maximal ideal at $x'$. Its corresponding log section in $M'_{\bar x'}$ is therefore not a unit, by the same condition. Thus $x'$ is outside the triviality locus. This proves that the triviality locus of $M'$ is exactly $b^{-1}U$.

Cited nodes: def-trf-fs-log-blowup

### Original step 7

The ordinary blowup $B$ is integral, with function field $k(X)$. Indeed, on any integral affine open $\operatorname{Spec}A\subset X$, the nonempty charts supplied by [prop-nagata-affine-blowup-chart-cover] have rings $A[J/a]\subset A_a\subset\operatorname{Frac}A$ by [def-nagata-blowup-chart-ring], with $a\ne0$. They are domains with fraction field $\operatorname{Frac}A$ and contain the same generic point. This generic point is dense in each chart and the charts glue with it, hence $B$ is irreducible and reduced. The generic points agree on intersecting affine opens of integral $X$. No finiteness of normalization is being assumed here.

Cited nodes: prop-nagata-affine-blowup-chart-cover, def-nagata-blowup-chart-ring

### Original step 8

Apply [lem-trf-log-regular-divisorial-structure] to the log regular fs scheme of Step 2. It makes $X'$ normal and its triviality locus dense. By Step 6 that locus is $b^{-1}U\cong U$, which is nonempty and irreducible because it is a dense open in integral $X$. Its closure is therefore irreducible; normality implies reducedness, so $X'$ is integral. By Steps 2--5 the map $s:X'\to B$ is finite. It is closed, and its image contains the dense open $U$, so it is surjective; hence every nonempty affine open of $B$ has nonempty inverse image. It is the identity on the common dense open $U$, so its two function fields are canonically $k(X)$. For an affine open $\operatorname{Spec}C\subset B$, its inverse image is $\operatorname{Spec}D$ for a finite $C$-algebra $D$. These are domains inside $k(X)$ and $D$ is integrally closed there, by the cited normality statement. Thus $D$ equals the integral closure of $C$: each element of $D$ is integral over $C$ by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-algebra-is-integral]; and an element of $k(X)$ integral over $C$ is also integral over $D$ and hence belongs to $D$. By [def-sc-normalization] these are exactly the normalization rings. Their inclusions in the common field agree on all overlaps, giving the canonical identification $X'\cong B^\nu$ over $B$ and hence over $X$.

Cited nodes: lem-trf-log-regular-divisorial-structure, def-sc-normalization, lem-weil-pbc-finite-algebra-is-integral
