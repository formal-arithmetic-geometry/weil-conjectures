## Retained returned proof, verbatim

### Returned step 1

Write $u,v$ for the images of $(1,0),(0,1)$ and $e=g(1,0)$, so the defining relation is $u+v=e+g(0,a)$. Embed $P$ in $P^{\rm gp}$, using its integrality in [def-trf-fs-log-blowup], and put $H=\mathbf Z\oplus\mathbf Z\oplus P^{\rm gp}$. The assignments $u\mapsto(1,0,0)$, $v\mapsto(-1,1,a)$, $g(n,p)\mapsto(0,n,p)$ respect the relation and so give a homomorphism $h:Q\to H$. A representative $(x,y,n,p)\in\mathbf N^2\oplus\mathbf Z\oplus P$ maps to $(x-y,n+y,p+ya)$. Setting $k=\min(x,y)$ and applying the defining relation $k$ times changes this representative to $(x-k,y-k,n+k,p+ka)$, with at least one branch coordinate zero.

### Returned step 2

This reduction proves $h$ is injective: if two representatives have the same image $(m,t,b)$, their reduced representatives both have branch coordinates $m_+=\max(m,0)$ and $m_-=\max(-m,0)$, integer coordinate $t-m_-$ and $P$-coordinate $b-m_-a$. Their $P$-coordinates agree because $P\to P^{\rm gp}$ is injective. Thus they are the same representative after reduction and give the same element of $Q$. It also proves the exact image description $h(Q)=\{(m,t,b)\in H:b-m_-a\in P\}$: necessity follows from the reduced representative, and sufficiency uses $(m_+,m_-,t-m_-,b-m_-a)$.

### Returned step 3

The extension $h^{\rm gp}:Q^{\rm gp}\to H$ is an isomorphism. An inverse sends $(m,t,b)$ to $mu+te+g^{\rm gp}(0,b)$; the homomorphism $P^{\rm gp}\to Q^{\rm gp}$ here is the extension of $p\mapsto g(0,p)$ by the group-completion construction [def-trf-fs-log-blowup]. The composites are identities on the $u,e,P^{\rm gp}$ generators of $H$ and on $u,v,g(R)$ in $Q^{\rm gp}$, since $v=e+g(0,a)-u$. Hence the injectivity of $h$ implies that $Q$ embeds in its group completion. It is finitely generated: use $u,v,e,-e$ and the images of a finite generating set of $P$. If $r(m,t,b)\in h(Q)$ for an integer $r>0$, then $rb-(rm)_-a=r(b-m_-a)\in P$, since $(rm)_-=rm_-$. Saturation of $P$ implies $b-m_-a\in P$, so $(m,t,b)\in h(Q)$. These are exactly the three fs conditions in [def-trf-fs-log-blowup], proving $Q$ fine saturated.

### Returned step 4

In these group coordinates $R^{\rm gp}=\mathbf Z\oplus P^{\rm gp}$ and $g^{\rm gp}(n,p)=(0,n,p)$, so the map is injective. Projection $(m,t,b)\mapsto m$ descends to an isomorphism $Q^{\rm gp}/g^{\rm gp}(R^{\rm gp})\to\mathbf Z$: its inverse sends $m$ to the class of $(m,0,0)$, and subtracting $(0,t,b)$ shows that both composites are identities. This proves the cokernel clause without any assumption that $P$ is sharp or torsion-free.

### Returned step 5

Fix $A,\alpha,\varepsilon$ as in the statement, put $c=\varepsilon\alpha(a)$, and let $B=A[U,V]/(UV-c)$, with residue classes $\bar U,\bar V$. The map $(x,y,n,p)\mapsto\bar U^x\bar V^y\varepsilon^n\alpha(p)$ respects the defining monoid congruence because $\bar U\bar V=c$. It therefore gives a multiplicative map $Q\to B$ and, by extending on the free abelian monomial basis in the definition of $\mathbf Z[Q]$, a ring map $\mathbf Z[Q]\to B$. Its restriction to $\mathbf Z[R]$ is the prescribed map to $A$ followed by $A\to B$. Consequently $a_0\otimes[q]\mapsto a_0\cdot[q]_B$, extended additively, is a well-defined $A$-algebra map $F:A\otimes_{\mathbf Z[R]}\mathbf Z[Q]\to B$: the tensor balancing relation holds by that restriction equality, and multiplication holds on pure tensors.

### Returned step 6

Conversely let $D=A\otimes_{\mathbf Z[R]}\mathbf Z[Q]$. Send $U$ to $1\otimes[u]$ and $V$ to $1\otimes[v]$. Their product is $1\otimes[u+v]=1\otimes[g(1,a)]=c\otimes[0]$, by the monoid relation and tensor balancing. Polynomial evaluation therefore vanishes on $(UV-c)$ and defines an $A$-algebra map $G:B\to D$. The composite $FG$ fixes $A,\bar U,\bar V$, so fixes every polynomial class. The composite $GF$ fixes $A$, $1\otimes[u]$, $1\otimes[v]$, and every $1\otimes[g(n,p)]=\varepsilon^n\alpha(p)\otimes[0]$; these generate $D$ as an $A$-algebra because $u,v,g(R)$ generate $Q$ and its monomials form an additive basis of $\mathbf Z[Q]$. Thus $F,G$ are inverse, with exactly the generator images required in the statement. The construction also makes the isomorphism canonical.
