### Old step 1: Parent step 1

Choose degree-one algebra generators $x_1,\ldots,x_t$ of $S$ as in [def-pc-standard-graded-algebra]. Replace a finite set of module generators by the finite set of all their homogeneous components, denoted $m_j\in M_{d_j}$: their span contains the original generators and hence is $M$. Projecting an expression in these generators onto degree $n$ shows that $M_n$ is spanned over $k$ by $x_1^{e_1}\cdots x_t^{e_t}m_j$ with $\sum_i e_i=n-d_j$. There are finitely many such exponent tuples, including when $t=0$. Thus every $M_n$ is finite-dimensional. The same argument applies to every finite nonnegatively graded module over every standard graded algebra used below.

### Old step 7: lem-trf-graded-injective-multiplication-polynomial-lift

Write $P_C(X)=\sum_{j=0}^d a_j\binom{X}{j}$ with $a_j\in\mathbf Q$: the binomial polynomials have distinct degrees $j$ and nonzero leading coefficients $1/j!$, so coefficient elimination gives this expansion.
