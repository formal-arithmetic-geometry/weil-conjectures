Hypotheses and small cases: for rank zero the basis is empty and K=0, giving all of P, as every empty-source map factors through Z. If Z=X the ideal and coefficient lists are empty and again L=P. If D is a nonempty disjoint union of r copies of the base and Z is empty, J contains 1 with a unit coordinate and K is the unit ideal, giving the empty locus. Varying rank is harmless: for S=Spec(k x k) with D the first component and Z empty, the locus is the second component, the rank-zero part. For B_R=R[epsilon]/epsilon^2 and F*(x)=a+b*epsilon, Z=(x) gives exactly K=(a,b), so nilpotents are detected scheme-theoretically. For R=k[u], a=u, b=0, the non-flat quotient R'=R/(u) kills K and the whole base-changed parameter scheme is the locus, exactly as the tensor calculation predicts. P empty causes an empty cover and gives the empty closed immersion. None of these cases uses smoothness, connectedness, separatedness of P, or Noetherianity.
