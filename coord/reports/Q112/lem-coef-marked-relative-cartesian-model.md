## Statement repairs

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported statement quantifies over arbitrary small simplicial sets S but cites a definition that restricts a cartesian fibration to a map of quasicategories. For example S = the inner horn Lambda^2_1 is not a quasicategory, whereas id_S satisfies all relative horn lifting and cartesian-edge conditions. Under the cited restriction it is not an admissible test object, contrary to HTT's arbitrary-base convention. The corrected statement writes the horn definition over arbitrary simplicial sets explicitly, retaining the intended generality, the prescribed target of cartesian lifts, and the final edge {n-1,n} for cartesian horns. The cocartesian definition is the opposite one. No hypothesis was added to S.

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: read literally as an enrichment of the whole category, “Its Kan enrichment is Map^sharp_S” is false. Over a point take X = (Delta^0)^sharp and Y = (Delta^1)^sharp. Their sharp mapping simplicial set is Delta^1, which is not Kan: the outgoing outer 2-horn containing 0->1 and id_0 would require an edge 1->0. The repair says simplicial enrichment, Kan whenever the target is fibrant. The original intended Kan enrichment on fibrant objects is retained. This is HTT Remark 3.1.3.1 and Corollary 3.1.4.4, not a weakening made to avoid a proof.

The notation Fun_/S is also made explicit by its simplices; over arbitrary S its total source and target need not be quasicategories. Only the full subcategory on cartesian-preserving maps is asserted quasicategorical, as supplied by the relative mapping lemma.
