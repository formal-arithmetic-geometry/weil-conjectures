The proof uses the unrestricted diagram-model clause and then checks degreewise monomorphisms through cells.

During my citation audit I found that the long-exact-sequence existence statement alone does not give the commutative ladder needed for the five lemma. I added the existing `lem-weil-pbc-complex-ses-long-exact-naturality` citation and its verbatim clause. No new node was needed.
