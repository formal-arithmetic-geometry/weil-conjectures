## Source error

The proof of Stacks Tag 0DXQ (theorem-algebraization-formal-sections, algebraization.tex) says that opens inside V_0 correspond one-to-one with ideals J contained in J_0. This is not literally injective: in k[[t]], (t) and (t^2) define the same closed subset. The needed assertion is only existence of a defining ideal contained in J_0. In step 4 I take J=J'J_0, since V(J') contains V(J_0) and V(J'J_0)=V(J') union V(J_0). This repairs the proof wording without changing any theorem statement, hypothesis or route.
