## Source error

In `literature/stacks-project/constructions.tex`, Tag 01N8 (`lemma-converse-construction`), the uniqueness paragraph says that the opens indexed by `f in S_d` form a basis. For fixed d this is false: take S=F_2[x,y] with the standard grading and d=1. The only nonempty such opens are D_+(x), D_+(y), D_+(x+y). All three contain the degree-two closed point defined by x^2+xy+y^2. Hence none is contained in the nonempty complement of that point, so they are not a basis. This is a flaw in that proof sentence, not in the lemma or the item. Step 7 repairs it: both maps restrict to W_a, the homogeneous fraction identity forces the entire ring map A_(a) -> Gamma(W_a,O), and the affine Hom bijection gives equality of scheme morphisms. The cover then suffices. The omitted verification for alpha and the all-degree diagram is supplied explicitly in steps 5 and 6.
