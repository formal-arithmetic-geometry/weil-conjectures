## Argument of the new node

Read Bhatt–Scholze, Lemma 3.4.12, proof of (1) iff (2), pp.22–23, and its preceding scalar-tower setup. For a K-flat representative P, regularity of t makes [R --t^n--> R] in degrees -1,0 a resolution of R/t^n. Tensoring the cone-to-quotient augmentation with P gives the levelwise quasi-isomorphism to the specified quotient-tensor tower. The transition on the two-term resolution is t on the degree -1 copy and identity on the degree-zero copy, so its map to the next quotient is the actual quotient transition. The levelwise cone triangles therefore have source the repeated-t tower and target the constant identity tower, with nth arrow t^n. Strict Roos commutes with cones and sends this diagram to a distinguished triangle. The constant identity tower contracts to P by evaluation at its first term; on that term the arrow is t, so the first arrow is t times the first evaluation, as the node states (not just first evaluation, since the index starts at n=1). The other arrow is exactly the reduction augmentation: quotient components in the product coordinate and zero in the shifted coordinate. Exact products and the strict-resolution Roos comparison identify this augmented model with total derived limit, compatibly with the unit. This is a genuine tower/derived-object comparison, not a second formulation of kernel/cokernel closure; its future split must justify these comparisons. I did not put this argument into the new node's statement or give it proof steps.
