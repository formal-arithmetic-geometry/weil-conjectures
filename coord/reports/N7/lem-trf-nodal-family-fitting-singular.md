## Arguments transferred to the new statements

For the relative-differential child, cover X, S and S' by compatible affine opens U=Spec A, V=Spec R, V'=Spec R'. The fibre-product affine formula gives A'=R' tensor_R A. On these charts the canonical derivation sends a to d(1 tensor a), and the existing ring differential base-change theorem identifies Omega(A'/R') with A' tensor_A Omega(A/R). Sheafify this A'-linear comparison on the pulled-back affine charts. On smaller charts the maps agree on every generator da by the defining derivation formula, so uniqueness in the differential universal property identifies the restrictions; this is the actual gluing obligation now stated by the child. The affine model of differentials gives quasi-coherence. When A is finite type over R, the differentials of finitely many algebra generators generate Omega(A/R); the same holds on base-changed charts. Thus the finite-type hypothesis needed by Fitting formation is established, without a flat or Noetherian base. Sources: Morphisms 01UT, 01V0 and 01V2, with the quasi-coherence discussion preceding 08S2.

For the Fitting child, the kernel-minor ideals for affine section modules are independent of the chosen finite surjection and commute with localization, by the existing ring-level Fitting node. They therefore define a quasi-coherent ideal whose affine sections are exactly those ideals; it both satisfies the bounds in the largest-ideal definition and contains every quasi-coherent ideal satisfying them. This proves its identification with that particular construction. For A to B on compatible affine charts, tensoring A^n/K gives B^n divided by the submodule generated by the images of K: maps out of these quotients are exactly the balanced maps out of B times A^n/K. No injectivity of B tensor K is claimed. Multilinearity of determinants identifies the minors generated by these image rows with the extended ideal; the convention n<=r gives the unit ideal on both sides, including empty matrices. The localization equalities make the affine identifications compatible on overlaps, giving the equality of ideal sheaves. Affinely, B tensor_A (A/I)=B/IB gives the closed-subscheme comparison; its maps are the quotient maps, so these comparisons glue with the prescribed closed immersions. This retains every presentation/minor/gluing argument in old step 1 while exposing it for a separate worker. Sources: Divisors 0C3C and 0C3D, and the scheme-theoretic interpretation in 0C3I.
