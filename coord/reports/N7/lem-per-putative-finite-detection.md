## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the unqualified assertion that schemes obtained by etale restriction are again Noetherian is false. For example, let X be the spectrum of an algebraically closed field of characteristic different from ell. The infinite disjoint union of copies of X is etale over X and is not quasi-compact, hence is not Noetherian in this project's convention. I changed only “Etale restriction” to “Etale restriction to Noetherian etale schemes”. ILO XVII Proposition 2.2.1 expressly assumes both etale schemes Noetherian excellent.
