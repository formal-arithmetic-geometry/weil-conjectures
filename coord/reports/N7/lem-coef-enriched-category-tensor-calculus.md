## Source error

Haugseng's introduction expressly corrects the colimit argument in the older Gepner--Haugseng Corollaries 4.3.15--4.3.16: separate preservation by multiplication is not preservation of all colimits in the product category. Tensor multiplication Ab x Ab -> Ab takes the coproduct of (Z,0) and (0,Z) to Z although the coproduct of their images is zero. Step 3 uses Haugseng's repaired Corollary 9.2 / 8.17. The external-product and completion results cited from Gepner--Haugseng do not use the erroneous colimit inference as a substitute for this repaired theorem.
