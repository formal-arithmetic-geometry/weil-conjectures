## Source error

Milne 13.27, printed p.263, second paragraph, says that `(A/a')_m` is a quotient of `(A/a)_m`. Since a' is contained in a, the natural surjection actually goes from `(A/a')_m` to `(A/a)_m`. This is a direction error in the proof, not a false theorem. For the direction alone, take A=k[t] with its usual grading, a'=(0), a=(t), m=(t): k[t]_(t) surjects onto k, and k cannot surject as a k-algebra onto k[t]_(t). Stacks 00NR states and proves the surjection in the correct direction. Step 3 uses its existing independent-kernel-generators consequence and a written coefficient calculation instead of reproducing Milne's reversed inference. The displayed kernel basis quotient in that paragraph should have denominator m^2; the final negative-degree calculation also has inconsistent coefficient names. The proof consistently uses the cotangent quotient m/m^2 and coefficients c_i. The item's statement remains exactly unchanged.
