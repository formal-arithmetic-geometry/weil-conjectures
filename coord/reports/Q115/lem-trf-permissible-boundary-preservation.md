### Previous step 1

Under this isomorphism [lemma-nagata-strict-transform-restrict] identifies each strict transform with its original boundary restricted to $X\setminus M$, compatibly with projection. All the asserted regularity and boundary properties on this open are therefore inherited from $X,B,H$.

### Previous step 2

Fix $y\in E$ and $x=b(y)$. Choose an actual étale neighborhood $v:W\to X$, a point $w$ over $x$, and parameters in $R=\mathcal O_{W,w}$ such that $B$ has equation $t_1\cdots t_r=0$ and $M$ has ideal $(t_j:j\in J)$.
