## Argument reserved for the new statement-only node

For a reduced Noetherian ring A, Tag 02LX (algebra.tex, lemma-total-ring-fractions-no-embedded-points), with Tag 00EW (lemma-reduced-ring-sub-product-fields), identifies Q(A) with the finite product of the fields A_q for the minimal primes q. I read the statements and proofs in algebra.tex. After localization at S=A minus p, a factor with q not contained in p becomes zero: choose s in q minus p, whose image in the field A_q is zero, and localization inverts it. A factor with q contained in p is unchanged, because every element of S has nonzero image in A_q. Localization of a finite product is the product of the localizations: send a fraction to its coordinate fractions, and conversely multiply the finitely many denominators to get one common denominator. The two fraction constructions are inverse by the localization equality relation. Thus S^{-1}Q(A) is the product of A_q for q contained in p. The localization prime correspondence (Tag 00E3, lemma-spec-localization) identifies these q with the minimal primes of R=A_p, and repeated prime localization identifies R_{qR} with A_q. R is reduced Noetherian. Applying 02LX to R identifies its total fraction ring with exactly this same product. Both maps are induced by the maps from A, so their composite is an R-algebra isomorphism and sends (a/1)/(s/1) to the image of a/s. This is a ring-theoretic comparison with independent hypotheses, not any clause of a scheme normalization theorem. Its proof is recorded here only; its unsplit node has no proof and no proof dependencies.

The new-node source field uses 02LX as the underlying product result and expressly labels the prime-localization consequence as agent-formulated; it does not claim 02LX itself states the new theorem. Whole-DAG tag/concept searches, blueprint_refs searches for normalization, total ring, total quotient, localization total and finite normalization quotient, and concept searches of project Lean were read before naming the node. Existing finite-type/curve normalization nodes have stronger geometric hypotheses, and product-description nodes give Q(A), not its comparison after localization at an arbitrary prime.
