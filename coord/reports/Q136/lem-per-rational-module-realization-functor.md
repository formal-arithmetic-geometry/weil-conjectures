### Derived-localization input (old Steps 2, 3, 7, 8)

Old Step 2: On the small site in the stipulated universes apply [lem-weil-pbc-scalar-localization-sheaf-exact] with $R=B_X$ and $s=\varpi$: $T(M)=E_X\otimes_{B_X}M$ is additive and exact. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-factorization] to obtain the triangulated termwise functor $D(T)$. By [lem-pur-derived-change-of-rings], $F_X$ is computed by $T(P)$ for a K-flat resolution $P\to K$. Exactness implies that $T(P)\to T(K)$ is a quasi-isomorphism. Consequently $F_X$ is canonically $D(T)$: the termwise factorization already inverts all quasi-isomorphisms and has the derived universal property. This identification is natural, so the triangulated structure and the degree-zero restriction are those of $D(T)$.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-scalar-localization-sheaf-exact, lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-factorization, lem-pur-derived-change-of-rings.

Old Step 3: For a complex representing $K$, [lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-complex-cohomology] applied to $T$ identifies $H^r(TK)$ with $T(H^rK)$ by its cycle and boundary maps. Thus $\mathcal H^r(F_XK)\cong E_X\otimes_{B_X}\mathcal H^r(K)$ for every integer $r$. This comparison is natural for cochain maps. It also commutes with inverses of quasi-isomorphisms, since both sides send them to isomorphisms; hence it is natural for derived morphisms represented by roofs. In particular, localization preserves any cohomological vanishing interval of $K$.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-complex-cohomology.

Old Step 7: The ordinary tensor functor $T$ is $A$-linear because $1\otimes(af)=a(1\otimes f)$ on pure tensors. By [lem-cbc-exact-linear-derived-functor], $F_X=D(T)$ is $A$-linear on derived morphisms. Define $J_X(QK)=F_XK$. In the Hom localization of [def-weil-pbc-rational-category] set $J_X(f/\varpi^n)=\varpi^{-n}F_X(f)$. If $f/\varpi^n=g/\varpi^m$, there is $t\geq0$ with $\varpi^t(\varpi^m f-\varpi^n g)=0$; applying $F_X$ and multiplying by the inverse of $\varpi^{t+n+m}$ gives equality of the two proposed images. The product of images of $f/\varpi^n$ and $g/\varpi^m$ is $\varpi^{-n-m}F_X(gf)$, and identities map to identities. Addition uses the same common-denominator calculation. This gives a well-defined functor factoring $F_X|_{\mathcal C_X}$. By [lem-weil-l-adic-integers-localization-uniformizer], every scalar of $E$ is $a/\varpi^m$; the displayed formula sends $(a/\varpi^m)(f/\varpi^n)$ to $(a/\varpi^m)J_X(f/\varpi^n)$, proving $E$-linearity.

Cited statements: lem-cbc-exact-linear-derived-functor, def-weil-pbc-rational-category, lem-weil-l-adic-integers-localization-uniformizer.

Old Step 8: The integral characterization [lem-weil-pbc-integral-stratified-perfect-characterization] makes $\mathcal C_X$ a full triangulated subcategory, closed under shifts and cones. Its shifts are $A$-linear by [lem-weil-linear-abelian-derived-category]. Therefore [lem-weil-linear-scalar-localization-triangulated] applies: every distinguished triangle of $\mathcal R_X$ is isomorphic to $Q$ of an integral distinguished triangle. The shift isomorphism of $F_X$ is natural also for fraction morphisms: naturality for $f$ may be multiplied by $\varpi^{-n}$, since shifts on both sides are linear. It consequently defines the shift comparison of $J_X$. On an image integral triangle $J_X$ agrees with the triangulated functor $F_X$, so it gives a distinguished triangle; it does so on every isomorphic triangle as well. Thus $J_X$ is triangulated. Together with Steps 3–6 this proves all the asserted cohomology and constructibility clauses.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-integral-stratified-perfect-characterization, lem-weil-linear-abelian-derived-category, lem-weil-linear-scalar-localization-triangulated.

### Stratum-model input (old Steps 4, 5, 6)

Old Step 4: For $K\in\mathcal C_X$, [lem-weil-pbc-integral-stratified-perfect-characterization](iii) gives bounded cohomology and, for each $r$, a finite stratification with immersions $h:S\to X$ such that $h^{-1}\mathcal H^r(K)$ is pro-etale locally $B_S\otimes_A N$, $N$ finitely presented. Fix one such model on a covering object $U$ of a stratum. By [lem-weil-pbc-local-derived-restriction-comparisons], inverse image to $S$ and restriction to $U$ are exact and commute with cohomology. Inverse image along $h_{\mathrm{proet}}$ and restriction to the slice over $U$ are geometric morphisms, so [lem-weil-pbc-localization-inverse-image-coherence] (with $R=B_X$, $t=\varpi$, and its clause on a specified identification $h^{-1}B_X\cong B_S$, here the one of Step 1) gives $h^{-1}(E_X\otimes_{B_X}M)\cong E_S\otimes_{B_S}h^{-1}M$ and the same for restriction to $U$, compatibly with the scalar maps. Applied to $M=\mathcal H^r(K)$ and Step 3, $h^{-1}\mathcal H^r(F_XK)|_U\cong(E_S\otimes_{B_S}(B_S\otimes_A N))|_U$. Here and below the model formulas on $S$ mean their restrictions to the chosen covering objects; a global trivialization on $S$ is not claimed.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-integral-stratified-perfect-characterization, lem-weil-pbc-local-derived-restriction-comparisons, lem-weil-pbc-localization-inverse-image-coherence.

Old Step 5: Write $N_S$ for the constant module sheaf of $N$ over the constant ring $A_S$; as in [def-weil-pbc-continuous-constructible-module], $B_S\otimes_A N$ means $B_S\otimes_{A_S}N_S$ along the constant-section map, and $E_S\otimes_E V$ means $E_S\otimes_{E^c_S}V_S$ for the constant ring sheaf $E^c_S$ of $E$. The constant-section maps satisfy $(A_S\to B_S\to E_S)=(A_S\to E_S)$, both sending $a$ to the constant function $a$. By [lem-pur-sheaf-ring-coefficient-ordinary-adjunction] each change of rings [def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product] is left adjoint to restriction of scalars, with Hom correspondence $f\mapsto(x\mapsto f(1\otimes x))$. Restriction along $B_S\to E_S$ followed by restriction along $A_S\to B_S$ is restriction along $A_S\to E_S$. Hence $E_S\otimes_{B_S}(B_S\otimes_{A_S}-)$ and $E_S\otimes_{A_S}-$ are both left adjoint to the same functor, and uniqueness of adjoints gives a natural isomorphism $E_S\otimes_{B_S}(B_S\otimes_{A_S}N_S)\cong E_S\otimes_{A_S}N_S$, given on local pure tensors by $e\otimes(b\otimes n)\mapsto eb\otimes n$. No flatness of $N$ is used. The same adjunctions show that $E_S\otimes_{A_S}-$ preserves cokernels.

Cited statements: def-pur-sheaf-tensor-product, lem-pur-sheaf-ring-coefficient-ordinary-adjunction, def-weil-pbc-continuous-constructible-module.

Old Step 6: Choose a finite presentation $A^m\xrightarrow{M}A^n\to N\to0$. By [lem-sfb-constant-finite-free-presentation-extension] (with $R=A$, the $A_S$-algebra $E_S$) the constant-sheaf functor is exact, $E_S\otimes_{A_S}(A^s)_S\cong E_S^s$, and the extension of $M$ is the matrix $M_{E_S}$. With Step 5, $E_S\otimes_{A_S}N_S\cong\operatorname{coker}(M_{E_S}:E_S^m\to E_S^n)$, and likewise $V=E\otimes_AN\cong\operatorname{coker}(M_E)$. Over the field $E$, row and column reduction gives $P\in GL_n(E)$, $Q\in GL_m(E)$ with $PMQ=\begin{pmatrix}I_\rho&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}$. Constant sections $E\to\Gamma(S,E_S)$ form a ring map, so $P,Q$ stay invertible over $E_S$, and both cokernels are free of rank $d=n-\rho$: $V\cong E^d$ and $E_S\otimes_{A_S}N_S\cong E_S^d$. The same node with $R=E$, the $E^c_S$-algebra $E_S$ and the isomorphism $E^d\cong V$ gives $E_S\otimes_E V\cong E_S^d$. Hence the local model of Step 4 is $E_S\otimes_E(E\otimes_A N)\cong E_S^d$. Since Step 1 identifies $h^{-1}E_X$ with $E_S$, the ringed inverse image of [def-weil-pbc-continuous-constructible-module] is $h^{-1}$ here, so every $\mathcal H^r(F_XK)$ is constructible with the finitely presented $E$-modules $E^d$; Step 3 gives boundedness. Checks: $N=0$ and $N=A/\varpi^k$ give $d=0$; $N=A^r\oplus A/\varpi$ gives $d=r$. No torsion-freeness of $N$ is used.

Cited statements: lem-sfb-constant-finite-free-presentation-extension, def-weil-pbc-continuous-constructible-module.
