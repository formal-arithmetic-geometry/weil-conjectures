The potentially misleading phrase “span determined up to a contractible space of choices” concerns arrows equipped with the homotopies identifying their composites with the given restricted cocones. It does not claim that an entire bare mapping space Map(Y,X) is contractible. Step 2 makes this distinction explicit.

Ordinary strict agreement or equality of homotopy classes would omit the path on A. Steps 3–4 use a homotopy pullback of spaces and write out the path obtained from the two comparison homotopies. Step 6 constructs an actual cocone, then verifies its actual composition map. It does not merely assert that its vertex represents some abstractly equivalent functor.
