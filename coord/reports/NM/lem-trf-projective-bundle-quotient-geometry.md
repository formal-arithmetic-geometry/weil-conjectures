### Old step 1; assigned to lem-trf-symmetric-proj-tautological-chart-gluing

Work over an affine open $U=\operatorname{Spec}R\subseteq S$ and put $M=\mathcal E(U)$, $A=\operatorname{Sym}_R M$. The canonical graded algebra [def-trf-canonical-symmetric-graded-algebra] has degree-$d$ part spanned by products of $d$ elements of $M$. Thus the images $e=\iota(m)$, for all $m\in M$, generate $A_+$ as an ideal. By [lemma-nagata-relative-proj-exists], $p^{-1}U=\operatorname{Proj}A$; by [prop-nagata-proj-standard-open], its opens $D_+(e)=\operatorname{Spec}H_e$, $H_e=(A_e)_0$, cover it. On base overlaps choose affine refinements using [lem-weil-pbc-affine-opens-basis]. All subsequent comparisons use the restriction maps of this canonical algebra and the compatible affine identifications in the relative-Proj existence statement.

Uses: def-trf-canonical-symmetric-graded-algebra, lemma-nagata-relative-proj-exists, prop-nagata-proj-standard-open, lem-weil-pbc-affine-opens-basis.

### Old step 2; assigned to lem-trf-symmetric-proj-tautological-chart-gluing

On $D_+(e)$ the affine twist [def-weil-pbc-proj-twisting-sheaf] is $(A_e)_1$. Multiplication by $e$ identifies it with $H_e$: its inverse sends $a/e^k$ of degree one to $a/e^{k+1}$ of degree zero. The composites are identities in the localized ring, including when a chart is empty. On $D_+(e)\cap D_+(f)$ its generators satisfy $e=(e/f)f$, with $e/f$ a unit; this overlap is $D(f/e)$ inside the $e$ chart by [lem-weil-pbc-proj-basic-open-intersection]. These identifications are the restriction maps of the affine twisting module, since both act on homogeneous fractions. On changing the base affine, the coefficient restriction sends $a/e^k$ to $\overline a/\overline e^k$, and sends the frame $e$ to $\overline e$. Hence the local free modules and their inverses agree under base restriction, by the canonical-algebra restriction data of [def-trf-canonical-symmetric-graded-algebra]. Their cocycle on every triple overlap is $(e/f)(f/g)=e/g$. Glue them by [lem-weil-pbc-site-module-sheaf-gluing] on the Zariski open site of $P$. This gives exactly the specified affine twists, a global module $\mathcal O_P(1)$ locally free of rank one, hence invertible by [def-sc-invertible-module].

Uses: def-weil-pbc-proj-twisting-sheaf, lem-weil-pbc-proj-basic-open-intersection, def-trf-canonical-symmetric-graded-algebra, lem-weil-pbc-site-module-sheaf-gluing, def-sc-invertible-module.

Source: literature/stacks-project/constructions.tex, Tags 01NR (lemma-glue-relative-proj-twists), 01MS (lemma-when-invertible), and 01OB; degree-one localization calculation.

### Old step 3; assigned to lem-trf-symmetric-proj-tautological-chart-gluing

The section $m\in M$ maps on each chart to $\iota(m)/1\in(A_e)_1$, the degree-one section map of [def-weil-pbc-proj-twisting-sheaf]. These maps respect localization and restriction on the base, so define $\mathcal E\to p_*\mathcal O_P(1)$. Adjunction [lem-pc-module-pullback-adjunction] gives $q_P:p^*\mathcal E\to\mathcal O_P(1)$. On $D_+(e)$, it sends the pulled-back $m$ with $\iota(m)=e$ to the frame $e$ of Step 2. It is therefore surjective on that chart, hence on their cover and thus globally. For an arbitrary $a:T\to P$ over $S$, tensor pullback [def-pc-module-pullback] takes a local frame to a local frame. Pulling back these local surjections consequently gives a surjection $h^*\mathcal E\to a^*\mathcal O_P(1)$ onto an invertible module.

### Old step 7; assigned to lem-trf-line-quotient-chart-inverse-pullback
