## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported phrase “pullback of the raw datum to this stage” does not define a common-base pullback for independent absolute chart subalgebras. Reading it as arbitrary extensions commuting with coefficient inclusions makes the proposed equality transport false. At the old stage take both maps to be the identity of Z; at the larger stage Z[t], the maps t -> t and t -> t+1 extend the same old map and commute with the old inclusions, but differ. This is a counterexample to the imported proof's transport inference, rather than to Nagata approximation.

The corrected statement specifies the raw coefficient construction that the source's finite closure and “restricted overlap map” language intends. An index is a finite closed restriction record. Its rings are actual subalgebras of the original chart rings. For each selected original restriction A_b -> A_a[1/s], its stage map is the factorization of that same restriction through B_a[1/s], on the whole B_b. Every new generator is included in the closure and has its image recorded. Refinement is inclusion of these closed records. The finite upper bound is selected in that raw-record directed set. A closed record does not assume any finite-stage gluing equation. Thus the construction is not an index set of already valid gluings.

This replaces the ambiguous meaning of the named datum; it does not assume a cartesian comparison that the source lacks, and it does not drop any conclusion. It permits arbitrary finite coefficient enlargements, after applying the same closure operation, rather than requiring the original chart rings to be finitely generated. To see that this really uses the existing construction, put each extra seed x onto the initial finite basic-open list by adding D(x) to the diagonal cover, without deleting the old cover. Apply the coefficient-stage definition and coefficient-descent lemma to that enlarged selected presentation. Concatenating two finite seed lists and doing this again gives an upper record. This is the same-unit construction of orders 332, 334 and 335, instantiated with more finite choices, not an application of the later valid-refinements theorem. The coefficient construction and its existing descent lemma remain responsible for existence of their finite closed lists and restricted maps; this item proves the equations of those maps.

The concrete equations in the conclusion are unchanged: diagonal domain and identity, both inverse directions, triple domain equality, and triple cocycle equality. No hypothesis on S or any original A_a has been strengthened. The added text defines the previously underspecified coefficient indices and maps rather than putting their gluing identities among the hypotheses. The unit's absolute-approximation step and its later relative descent/separatedness steps are unchanged. No target statement or target definition was edited.

I checked the three direct DAG citers of the item. `def-nagata-noetherian-stage` needs precisely this whole-overlap gluing datum at the selected stage; every one of its displayed axioms is still supplied. `lemma-nagata-glue-noetherian-stage` still has finite-type coefficient charts and the same chart inclusions; its separate affineness assertions are not claimed as consequences of this item alone. `lemma-nagata-valid-refinements-directed` still has an initial valid stage and can enlarge finite seeds within this raw index system.
