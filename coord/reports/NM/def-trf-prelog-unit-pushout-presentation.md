This is a definition split, so there are no numbered proof steps in its JSON. The following are the construction checks, each from the displayed formulas and the cited statements, rather than hidden lemmas or a proof moved to a new node:

1. **Quotient and restrictions.** A monoid congruence is explicitly defined as a multiplicatively stable equivalence relation; its quotient multiplication and identity are induced from the product. A ring map sends a unit a with inverse b to a unit with inverse f(b). Naturality gives gamma_V(M(f)e) = f^sharp(gamma_W(e)), so the images of the generating related pairs are generating related pairs. Thus restrictions descend; the identity/composition equations hold on representatives. The structural formula is constant on a generator since gamma(me)a = gamma(m)gamma(e)a, and its naturality follows from naturality of gamma. These are elementary substitutions, not new nodes. Neither M nor the monoids are assumed cancellative or integral.

2. **Same quotient as the existing interface.** I read `LogRegularPair.lean:159,167` and `ZariskiPrelogLogificationComparison.lean:49--92`. The code generates its congruence by (e,1)~(1,a) when gamma(e)=a in the ring. Such an a is precisely the unique unit representing gamma(e). Multiplying this relation by (m,b) gives (me,b)~(m,gamma(e)b). Conversely the latter relation at m=1,b=1 gives the code's generator. Thus the least congruences are equal; there is no new or weaker quotient notion. This two-line generator check is elementary.

3. **Pair naturality and sheafification encoding.** Each quotient class has a pair representative. The pair naturality equation therefore says exactly that the maps q_W are the components of a natural transformation eta; equality on representatives is equality on the quotient. Likewise a family r_W satisfying pair naturality is exactly a natural map P_gamma→H. Consequently the universal field “there exists a unique h with all h_W q_W=r_W” is exactly bijectivity of precomposition with eta. The inclusion is forced to be eta([m,1]); its monoid and naturality equations follow by substitution. This establishes the two encodings with exactly the same maps, rather than replacing them by an unspecified isomorphic sheaf. The statement of `lem-trf-commutative-monoid-sheafification` was opened: it gives precisely this universal-property convention for every monoid sheaf H and, separately, existence. Only the convention is used in this definition; existence/logification is not smuggled into the fields.

4. **Slice and log universal predicates.** I read `.Restricts:295--309` and `.EtaleUniversal:167--177` in the existing interface. The former quantifies over every slice and requires literally the original pair maps and inclusion; the item now says this explicitly and asserts no existence theorem. The latter quantifies over every compatible natural prelog map and asks for a unique structurally compatible sheaf map. This is exactly the displayed bijection defining HasPrelogUniversalMapping once alpha_L i=gamma, and only for sheaf M is it the existing EtalePrelogStructure interface. I checked this restriction instead of treating a presheaf as a sheaf.

5. **Section unit map.** The statement of `def-trf-fs-log-blowup` gives an isomorphism of the inverse-image sheaf of ring units with O^×, not directly an arbitrary inverse on all sections of K. Objectwise its inverse sends a to an element k_a. The elements k_a k_(a^-1) and 1 have structural value 1 and belong to the inverse image, where alpha is injective, so they agree. Thus k_a is a monoid unit. Conversely every monoid unit has unit structural value. This identifies that inverse-image group with K(W)^× and justifies the inverse units map used in the definition. This elementary argument uses only the stated log axiom, with no chart or saturation assumption.

6. **Presheaf stalk and size.** I opened every relevant dependency: `def-weil-size-convention` permits a small site skeleton; `def-weil-pbc-pointed-etale-neighbourhoods` specifies the objects, lifts and arrows; `lem-weil-pbc-pointed-etale-neighbourhoods-cofiltered` supplies nonemptiness, common refinements and equalizers for every scheme and geometric point, without a Noetherian hypothesis. Over a small site skeleton, all lifts and arrows form sets in the containing universe. For any original pointed object (W,w), choose a site isomorphism W'→W with W' in the skeleton and lift w by its inverse. The inclusion of pointed skeleton objects is fully faithful and every pointed object is isomorphic to one of them, so its opposite is the required final filtered model. The statement of `lem-trf-commutative-monoid-filtered-colimit-underlying` was opened: it supplies both colimit existence and the underlying-set colimit for a small filtered category with coefficient sets in the containing universe. All those hypotheses are now explicit in the item. The old sheaf-only wording of `def-weil-pbc-geometric-stalks` is used for the index convention, not claimed to itself state presheaf colimit existence.

7. **Induced stalk map and edge cases.** The composite of v_W with each target germ map is a cocone by naturality of v. The monoid-colimit universal property therefore defines v_t, with exactly the representative formula in the item. No descent comparison between different sites is used. The empty scheme has no geometric point, so that clause is vacuous, while all presheaf formulas still make sense. At an empty etale object the function ring is the zero ring, whose unit group is trivial; the quotient collapses E=M and becomes the trivial monoid, as the generators show. If gamma takes all values in units, [m,a]=[1,gamma(m)a], giving P_gamma(W)=B_W^×. For trivial M this also reduces to B_W^×. For a noncancellative monoid (e.g. {1,e} with e²=e mapped to 0 in a field), the quotient and map checks still use only congruences and do not cancel anything. The compatibility counterexample above checks the one typing repair explicitly.
