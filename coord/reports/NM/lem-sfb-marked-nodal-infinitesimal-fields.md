Edge cases checked mathematically before writing:

- `n = 0`: the marked stabilizer intersection is top; no nonemptiness of Fin n is assumed.
- Smooth C and no nodes: normalization is the identity; E consists precisely of the marks.
- P1 with zero, one, two or three marks: dimensions are 3, 2, 1 and 0; stability uses the
  integer expression -2 + #E, and three marks force triviality.
- A smooth elliptic curve: a nonzero translation vector field has no fixed point; one
  mark kills it. A genus at least two component needs no special points.
- An irreducible nodal cubic: the two branches on its normalized P1 are counted separately;
  the unmarked field z d/dz survives, while an additional smooth mark kills it.
- Reducible curves and self-nodes: components are indexed canonically; preimages do not
  collapse a self-node into one point. Properness makes the index finite.
- Characteristic two: the proof plan does not divide by two. Idempotent derivations vanish
  in every characteristic, and the local uv calculation and additive composition work.
- Empty C is excluded exactly by ConnectedSpace, as in the node's definition dependency.
- The genus-zero zero bound uses extended cardinality and does not accept an infinite
  fixed set by the convention that ncard of an infinite set is zero.

## New nodes

`Sheaf.AlgebraicGeometry.CurveVectorFieldsFixingPoints`, defines exactly the additive
subgroup of relative sheaf derivations preserving the specified point ideals. It reuses

queries and results. Its dependencies are scheme, relative differentials and residue field.

automorphisms over the constant dual-number base fixing the closed fibre and every marked
section. The generic map stabilizer helper specializes Mathlib's stabilizer through
Aut.toEnd; the definition intersects and pulls back those subgroups by the forgetful
functor's mapAut. Source: more-morphisms.tex 02H5 and curves.tex section-vector-fields.

automorphism subgroup. No chosen map, existence-only datum or hollow object is used.
