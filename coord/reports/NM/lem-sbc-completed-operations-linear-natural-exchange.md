## Proof in words for lem-sbc-ambient-operations-scalar-linearity

Source: Stacks dga.tex Tag 09MK, definition-functor-linear-categories, and derived.tex Tag 070I, lemma-K-injective; the elementary sectionwise scalar calculations and their combination are agent supplied. The exact-functor descent input is the existing lem-cbc-exact-linear-derived-functor; it is also Mathlib's `CategoryTheory.Functor.instLinearDerivedCategoryMapDerivedCategory`, ExactFunctor.lean:122. That instance alone does not handle the generally nonexact right adjoint, so the new node is not a Mathlib leaf.

For a module-sheaf map $s$, module direct image sends $as$ to $a(u_*s)$ by its section formula. Module pullback sends a tensor generator under $as$ to $a$ times its image under $s$, since the coefficient isomorphism respects the given maps from $A$. Both operations preserve sums. In particular the exact module inverse image is additive and $A$-linear.

The induced derived-category structures and the localization functors are $A$-linear by lem-weil-linear-abelian-derived-category. The exact pullback descends as an $A$-linear functor by lem-cbc-exact-linear-derived-functor; its exactness and identification with $Lu^*$ are in def-weil-pbc-proetale-operations.

For $Ru_*$ choose K-injective representatives $J,J'$ of two input objects, available in the operations construction. By lem-weil-pbc-kinjective-hom-derived, every morphism between them in the unbounded derived category is represented by a homotopy class of chain maps $J\to J'$. Under this bijection, addition and scalar multiplication are represented by those same operations on chain maps, because localization is linear. Termwise $u_*$ preserves these operations and chain homotopies. Therefore the already-defined right derived functor satisfies $Ru_*(s+t)=Ru_*s+Ru_*t$ and $Ru_*(as)=aRu_*s$. Naturality of the canonical resolution identifications makes this independent of the two chosen representatives. No claim about scalar linearity of an arbitrary resolution functor is required.
