## Source error

Tag 06SZ's displayed identity is written with A/(J intersection K), A/J and A/K over A/I, although J,K,I are ideals of B and A=B/I. These quotients are not typed with those meanings. The correct identity is B/(J intersection K) = B/J times_A B/K when J+K=I. This is a proof typo, not a false item statement. For instance, B=k[epsilon]/(epsilon^2), I=(epsilon), J=I, K=0 gives the correct product B/I times_A B = B, not A. Step 3 explicitly constructs the correct isomorphism, including both its kernel and surjectivity, instead of propagating the typo. No target statement or imported statement was changed.
