We use this natural identification, with its transported action, whenever identifying module pullback with inverse image below. The component formula in [def-weil-pbc-adjunction-mate] gives precisely
$\gamma_K=R'(T_X\epsilon_K)\,R'(c_{f,RK})\,\eta'_{T_YRK}$.

### Returned step 2

### Returned step 5

For finite admissible $A$, the discrete condensed ring $\underline A$ is the sheafification of the constant ring: every continuous $S\to A$ is constant on a finite clopen partition. A finite ring is Noetherian since each ideal is generated by its finite underlying set. Thus [det-pe-finite-tor-comparison-noetherian] supplies $K'\in D^b_{\mathrm{ctf}}(X_{\mathrm{ét}},A)$ with $\nu_X^*K'\simeq K$. Define $K'_B$ by [lem-pur-derived-change-of-rings]. Its underlying $A$-complex is $K'\otimes_A^LB_X$, so finite Tor amplitude puts its cohomology in a finite interval. Both $K'$ and $K'_B$ are therefore bounded below. Its displayed equality identifies the pro-étale $\gamma$, after the canonical $\delta_X$ identification, with $\beta^B\nu_Y^*(\gamma^{\mathrm{ét}})\delta_Y T_Y((\beta^A)^{-1})$. The $\delta$ and $\beta$ maps are isomorphisms by the cited node. Consequently invertibility of the étale coefficient comparison implies invertibility of the required pro-étale comparison.

The monoidal natural isomorphism of [sk-def-sfb-pullback-from-point-comp] identifies $f^*F_Y$ with $F_X$: its compatibility with the action map $\Lambda\otimes F\to F$ makes it module-linear, and naturality makes it commute with the differentials. Apply the exact natural projection transformation of [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-projection-mate-exact] to the second variable $F_Y$. It is the identity for $F=\Lambda$, hence an isomorphism for every finite direct sum of shifts of $\Lambda$. Its compatibility with triangles, including connecting maps, is explicitly part of the cited node. Build $F$ by the successive brutal truncation triangles, adding its single finite-free terms one at a time. Induction on the number of nonzero terms and [lem-sf-triangle-morphism-iso] prove the projection is an isomorphism for $F$. By [sk-prop-sfb-cartesian-coefficients](2), for $\mathcal O_E\to\mathcal O_F$ and $E\to F$, the second coefficient sheaf is finite free over the first on every scheme. For these two maps, apply the same projection transformation directly to $P=\Lambda'_Y$ in degree zero. Choose a finite-free basis on $Y$, so $P\simeq\Lambda_Y^{\oplus r}$; pull back this basis to identify $f^*P\simeq\Lambda'_X$ using [sk-prop-sfb-cartesian-coefficients](1). Naturality with the $r$ summand inclusions identifies its projection map with $r$ copies of the unit-coefficient identity. It is therefore invertible, and step 3 proves these cases of $\gamma$. This uses finite freeness of the coefficient sheaf on $Y$, exactly as stated in the cited node.

### Returned step 8

For a listed continuous $O=\mathcal O_E\to A$ with $A$ finite, apply [lem-sfb-finite-integral-condensed-resolution]. It supplies an exact sequence $0\to\underline O^r\xrightarrow\phi\underline O^r\to\underline A\to0$ with finite free terms. Pulling it back by $p_Z^*$ preserves exactness by [lem-weil-pbc-site-pullback-abelian-exact] and preserves the free terms by the monoidal left-adjoint description in [sk-def-sfb-pullback-from-point]. This yields a two-term finite free resolution of $A_Z$ in degrees $-1,0$. Finite free modules are flat (tensoring with them is a finite sum of the identity). Thus [lem-pc-bounded-above-flat-kflat-site] makes the resolution K-flat, so it computes tensoring with $A_Z$. The criterion is Stacks Tag 06YQ, label lemma-bounded-flat-K-flat, not the existence theorem 06YS named in that dependency's bibliography. Apply step 7 to this pulled-back two-term complex. Its projection transformation is invertible; step 3 identifies it with the restriction of $\gamma$ and detects invertibility. This proves the $O\to A$ case. When $A=0$, the cited resolution allows $r=0$ and both sides vanish.

### Returned step 9

We record explicitly the colimit calculation used for the last two coefficient maps. By [lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-colimits-forgetful], module colimits and their underlying sheaf colimits agree. By [lem-weil-pbc-site-abelian-sheaves-filtered-colimits-exact], filtered colimits of these modules are exact. If $S_Z=\varinjlim_i P_{i,Z}$ with each $P_{i,Z}$ finite free over $R_Z$, then $S_Z\otimes_{R_Z}M=\varinjlim_i(P_{i,Z}\otimes_{R_Z}M)$ by the balanced-coequalizer construction in [sk-def-sfb-change-of-scalars]: an $R_Z$-balanced bilinear map out of $(\varinjlim_iP_{i,Z})\times M$ is exactly a compatible family of balanced bilinear maps out of $P_{i,Z}\times M$. The tensor universal property therefore gives the displayed isomorphism, with each colimit coprojection corresponding to its tensor with $M$. Each functor on the right is exact, so their filtered colimit is exact: $S_Z$ is flat. Consequently ordinary termwise tensor with $S_Z$ sends quasi-isomorphisms to quasi-isomorphisms and computes derived scalar extension, by its K-flat definition [sk-def-sfb-derived-extension-of-scalars]. It also commutes with cohomology, as does the termwise filtered colimit. To apply bounded continuity, choose a representative $C$ of $K$ and a degree $a$ below its cohomology; replace it by the good lower truncation, with $\operatorname{coker}(d^{a-1})$ in degree $a$ and zero below $a$. The map to this truncation is a quasi-isomorphism because its cohomology agrees in every degree. Tensoring its terms with each flat $P_{i,X}$ gives complexes zero below the same $a$. Thus every colimit diagram below meets the literal common lower bound required by [det-pe-bounded-continuity-direct-image].

### Returned step 11

For $E\to\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$, [sk-prop-sfb-qlbar-colimit-x] presents the target coefficient sheaf as the filtered colimit of the $F_Z$ for finite extensions $F/E$ inside $\overline{\mathbf Q}_\ell$.
