## Supporting-node argument and scope

The new node is a general homological comparison for arbitrary left exact additive F and arbitrary nonnegative cosimplicial complexes. It mentions neither covers, topoi, H, adjunction units nor this item's conclusions. It is the non-elementary missing operation used twice in the item, rather than a renamed clause of it. The intended proof of the new node is to resolve the object K in the abelian category Fun(Δ,Ch^{≥0}(A)), adding a resolution degree s≥0. This category is Grothendieck: limits/colimits and exactness are objectwise; generators can be obtained from left Kan extensions of the disk-complex generators. Evaluation at ([n],q) has an exact left adjoint (the free representable diagram combined with the nonnegative disk complex in degrees q,q+1), so it preserves injectives. Consequently the resulting J^{n,q,s} has injective evaluated terms and exact augmented s-columns. Its total over q,s is an injective replacement of K([n]); its total over n,q,s is an injective replacement of T(K). These assertions use the finite-diagonal augmentation theorem, with s the resolution index. Apply F: the total computes RF on the total, each fixed-n total computes RF on that column, and the two rebracketings agree by finite biproducts, with differential δ+(-1)^n d_q+(-1)^{n+q}d_s. There are (r+1)(r+2)/2 triples in degree r≥0 and none in negative degrees. Comparison maps of resolutions taken in the diagram category preserve all cosimplicial operators; their homotopies, signed by (-1)^{n+q}, give total homotopies. Resolving a diagram map and its augmentations gives the asserted natural comparison and augmentation equation.
