## Proof plan for the new unsplit lemma

Conrad Proposition B.2.5 and Corollary B.2.6 identify the conjugacy classes of two existing lifts with first regular cohomology of the diagonalizable special-fibre group with coefficients in the Lie module tensored with the square-zero ideal. Remark B.2.4 gives the vanishing, without assumptions on the coefficient module or invertibility of character torsion. Equivalently, subtract the two homomorphisms in the additive congruence kernel, obtaining a regular crossed homomorphism. A diagonalizable comodule is a direct sum indexed by the character group: expansion against the free basis and the coaction identity give the projections. The crossed homomorphism defines an extension of that comodule by the trivial rank-one comodule. Lift 1 and take its degree-zero component to obtain an invariant lift of 1; this splits the extension and expresses the crossed homomorphism as a coboundary. Conjugation by the corresponding congruence-kernel point identifies the two homomorphisms. This is background for the queued new lemma, not a hidden proof inside its statement. In the new node neither homomorphism is required to be an embedding; this is precisely what the source's conjugacy classification allows.
