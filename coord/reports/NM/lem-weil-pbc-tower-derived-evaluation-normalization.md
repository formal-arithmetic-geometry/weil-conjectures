### Old step 1 → item step 1

Write $Q$ for each localization, suppressing its exact-functor comparison isomorphisms. The ordinary tower adjunction $F^{\rm Tow}\dashv G^{\rm Tow}$ is defined by applying the ordinary Hom bijection at each level: naturality of that bijection says exactly that compatible level maps correspond to compatible level maps. Its unit and counit are levelwise the original ones. Levelwise exactness makes $F^{\rm Tow}$ exact, and evaluations and $F,F^{\rm Tow}$ therefore induce termwise exact derived functors. The additive adjunctions also induce adjunctions on homotopy categories. These identifications retain the ordinary units and counits, not arbitrary adjunctions with the same functors.

Uses: lem-weil-tower-category-levelwise-abelian, lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-factorization, lem-weil-pbc-termwise-adjunction-homotopy.

### Old step 2 → lem-weil-pbc-kinjective-exact-square-comparison

The assumed Grothendieck property gives K-injective resolutions in $\mathcal A,\mathcal B$ and both tower categories. For a K-injective tower complex $J$ in $\mathcal A$, evaluation preserves K-injectivity, so $e_nJ$ is K-injective. The total-derived computation theorem makes both $a_n(J):=e_n(\eta_{G^{\rm Tow},J})$ and $b_n(J):=\eta_{G,e_nJ}$ invertible. Both have source $Q(G(e_nJ))$, by the strict ordinary evaluation identity. Put $A_n=e_n\mathcal G^{\rm Tow}$ and $B_n=\mathcal G e_n$ and define $\delta_n(J)=b_n(J)a_n(J)^{-1}:A_n(QJ)\to B_n(QJ)$. Thus $\delta_n(J)a_n(J)=b_n(J)$, the required normalization.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-kinjective-functorial-existence, lem-weil-tower-evaluation-kinjective, lem-weil-pbc-kinjective-total-derived.

### Old step 3 → lem-weil-pbc-kinjective-exact-square-comparison

For a homotopy class $k:J\to J'$ between K-injective tower complexes, naturality of the two universal derived units gives
$$A_n(Qk)a_n(J)=a_n(J')Q(G(e_nk)),\qquad B_n(Qk)b_n(J)=b_n(J')Q(G(e_nk)).$$
Substituting $\delta_n= b_na_n^{-1}$ yields $B_n(Qk)\delta_n(J)=\delta_n(J')A_n(Qk)$. Additive functors respect homotopies, so this calculation uses the homotopy class of $k$. It proves naturality on the full subcategory of K-injective complexes in the homotopy category.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-kinjective-total-derived, lem-weil-pbc-termwise-adjunction-homotopy.

### Old step 4 → lem-weil-pbc-kinjective-exact-square-comparison

For each $M\in D(\operatorname{Tow}(\mathcal A))$ choose an isomorphism $u:M\xrightarrow{\sim}QJ$ with $J$ K-injective, using a representative of $M$ and a K-injective resolution. Define
$$\beta_{n,M}=B_n(u)^{-1}\delta_n(J)A_n(u).$$
For another choice $u':M\to QJ'$, full faithfulness of localization on K-injectives gives a unique homotopy class $k:J\to J'$ with $Qk=u'u^{-1}$. Step 3, multiplied by $B_n(u')^{-1}$ on the left and $A_n(u)$ on the right, proves that the two definitions coincide. For any $h:M\to N$ and choices $u:M\to QJ$, $v:N\to QJ'$, use the unique class $k$ with $Qk=vhu^{-1}$ in the same equation; it gives $B_n(h)\beta_{n,M}=\beta_{n,N}A_n(h)$. Thus $\beta_n$ is a natural isomorphism. Taking $u=1$ proves its normalization. Any normalized comparison equals $\delta_n$ at $QJ$ by cancellation of $a_n(J)$; naturality with respect to $u$ forces the displayed formula at $M$. This proves uniqueness.

Uses: def-weil-abstract-derived-category, lem-weil-pbc-kinjective-functorial-existence, lem-weil-pbc-kinjective-hom-derived.

### Old step 5 → item step 3

Fix a tower transition $t:e_m\to e_n$ ($m\geq n$). Denote the induced transitions $A_m\to A_n$ and $B_m\to B_n$ by $s_A,s_B$. At $QJ$, naturality of evaluation transitions with respect to the tower morphism $\eta_{G^{\rm Tow},J}$ and naturality of $\eta_G$ with respect to $t_J:e_mJ\to e_nJ$ give
$$s_Aa_m=a_nQ(Gt_J),\qquad s_Bb_m=b_nQ(Gt_J).$$
Consequently $s_B\delta_m a_m=b_nQ(Gt_J)=\delta_n s_Aa_m$. Cancel the invertible $a_m$. This is transition compatibility at $QJ$; conjugating by the functorial images of $u:M\xrightarrow{\sim}QJ$ proves it at every $M$. The calculation applies to every transition, including identity transitions.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-kinjective-total-derived.

### Old step 6 → lem-weil-pbc-exact-square-derived-counit

Apply the normalized derived-adjunction theorem to $F\dashv G$ and $F^{\rm Tow}\dashv G^{\rm Tow}$, using all source homotopy objects as the left resolving class. Identity resolutions verify the resolving condition; exactness of the left functors ensures that their localized termwise functors invert quasi-isomorphisms. K-injective resolutions give the right derived pairs. The theorem's Hom-transpose formula specifies the canonical adjunctions. Write $\varepsilon^0$ for the ordinary counits and $\varepsilon^D$ for these derived counits. For a K-injective target $I$, apply that formula to the ordinary map $\varepsilon^0_I:F(GI)\to I$. Its ordinary transpose is $1_{GI}$, so its derived transpose is $\eta_{G,I}$. By the definition of an adjunction counit as inverse transpose of the identity, the inverse transpose of $\eta_{G,I}$ is $\varepsilon^D_{QI}F(\eta_{G,I})$. Therefore
$$\varepsilon^D_{QI}F(\eta_{G,I})=Q(\varepsilon^0_I).$$
The same argument in the tower categories gives $\varepsilon^{D,\rm Tow}_{QJ}F^{\rm Tow}(\eta_{G^{\rm Tow},J})=Q(\varepsilon^{0,\rm Tow}_J)$. All the exact-localization identifications are those of step 1.

Uses: lem-sfb-resolving-derived-adjunction-normalization, lem-weil-pbc-exact-functor-derived-factorization, lem-weil-pbc-kinjective-functorial-existence, lem-weil-pbc-termwise-adjunction-homotopy.

### Old step 7 → lem-weil-pbc-exact-square-derived-counit

At a K-injective tower $J$, evaluate the tower counit equation of step 6. Since the ordinary tower counit is levelwise the ambient one, it reads
$$e_n(\varepsilon^{D,\rm Tow}_{QJ})F(a_n(J))=Q(\varepsilon^0_{e_nJ}).$$
For the proposed right-hand side of the claimed identity, normalization of $\beta_n$ and the ambient equation of step 6 give
$$\varepsilon^D_{e_nQJ}F(\beta_{n,QJ})F(a_n(J))=\varepsilon^D_{e_nQJ}F(b_n(J))=Q(\varepsilon^0_{e_nJ}).$$
Here $e_nJ$ is K-injective, as established in step 2. Cancel the isomorphism $F(a_n(J))$. This proves the required counit equality at $QJ$.

Uses: lem-weil-tower-evaluation-kinjective, lem-sfb-resolving-derived-adjunction-normalization.

### Old step 8 → lem-weil-pbc-exact-square-derived-counit

Let $C(M)=e_n(\varepsilon^{D,\rm Tow}_M)$ and $D(M)=\varepsilon^D_{e_nM}F(\beta_{n,M})$. They are natural transformations $F A_n\to e_n$, by naturality of the counits and step 4. For $u:M\xrightarrow{\sim}QJ$, naturality gives $e_n(u)C(M)=C(QJ)F A_n(u)$ and $e_n(u)D(M)=D(QJ)F A_n(u)$. Step 7 identifies the right sides; cancel $e_n(u)$ to obtain $C(M)=D(M)$. Thus the normalized comparisons have the stated counit compatibility for every derived tower object.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-kinjective-functorial-existence.
