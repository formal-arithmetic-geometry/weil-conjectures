The universal property [def-pc-fibre-product] identifies it with $U\times_V\operatorname{Spec}k$: a map into either object is exactly a map into $U$ and a map into $\operatorname{Spec}k$ whose composites into $Y$ agree; both composites factor through the open $V$, where agreement is equivalent. Thus [lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra] gives $U_k\cong\operatorname{Spec}(B\otimes_A k)$ over $k$. Inverse images of a covering family cover: the inverse image of its union is the union of its inverse images. We use these chart identifications and this covering observation below.

Original step 2: Assume (1), and fix any affine pair $U,V$ as in step 1. By [lem-weil-pbc-flat-morphism-affine-criterion], $A\to B$ is flat.
