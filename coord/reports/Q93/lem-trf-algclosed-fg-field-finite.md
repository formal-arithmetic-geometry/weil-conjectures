## Retained argument for the unsplit binomial input

This is an agent-written supporting argument, rather than a claim that Stacks 030F or 09GH itself proves the obstruction. Put \(H=F(t_2,\ldots,t_r)\), with \(H=F\) for \(r=1\). Algebraic independence says \(t_1\) is transcendental over \(H\): any nonzero polynomial relation over \(H\) can be multiplied by a common nonzero denominator in \(F[t_2,\ldots,t_r]\) to yield a nonzero polynomial relation in the original independent parameters. Hence evaluation identifies \(H[T]\) with \(H[t_1]\) and its fraction field with \(K=H(t_1)\). In the domain \(A=H[T]\), the ideal \((T)\) is prime because its quotient is \(H\). For \(f=X^m-T\in A[X]\), \(m\ge1\), the leading coefficient is \(1\), every lower coefficient belongs to \((T)\), and the constant coefficient \(-T\) does not belong to \((T)^2=(T^2)\), as is seen by its coefficient of \(T\). The polynomial is primitive because it is monic. The signature of `Polynomial.irreducible_of_eisenstein_criterion` was read at `Mathlib/RingTheory/Polynomial/Eisenstein/Criterion.lean:175--180`; it gives irreducibility in \(A[X]\). The monic Gauss lemma, read at `Mathlib/RingTheory/Polynomial/GaussLemma.lean:149--150`, gives irreducibility over the fraction field (the polynomial ring over a field is a PID and hence integrally closed). Transport along the explicit evaluation identification gives \(X^m-t_1\) irreducible over \(K\). This argument is retained here for the next worker; the new node itself remains statement-only as required.
