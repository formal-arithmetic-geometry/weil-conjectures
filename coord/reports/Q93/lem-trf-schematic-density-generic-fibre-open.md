### Returned step 1

Retained in parent step 1.

Replace $Y$ by its reduction and $X,U$ by their base changes. By the definition of reduction, on an affine chart this replaces $A$ by $A/\sqrt{0}$. Every map $A\to\kappa(y)$ kills $\sqrt{0}$, and the local quotient $(A/\sqrt{0})_{\mathfrak p/\sqrt{0}}$ has residue field $\kappa(\mathfrak p)$. Thus the point sets, residue fields, and fibre products defining $X_y,U_y$ are unchanged. Finite type survives this base change. The reduced irreducible base is integral by the integral-scheme characterization. Restrict to a nonempty affine open of it, obtaining $Y=\operatorname{Spec}A$ with $A$ a domain and fraction field $K$. The open eventually found here is also an open of the original base.

### Returned step 2

Finite affine cover retained in parent step 2; Noetherian generic fibre, quasi-compactness and selection of total-space principal opens assigned to lem-trf-generic-open-principal-multiplication-data.

Finite type over affine $Y$ gives a finite affine cover $X=\bigcup_{r=1}^N X_r$ with each $X_r=\operatorname{Spec}B_r$ finite type over $A$. The sheaf injection on the generic fibre restricts to each $(X_r)_\eta$; after proving the result on each chart, intersect the finitely many base opens. Fix one chart and write $B=B_r$. Its generic fibre is $\operatorname{Spec}(B\otimes_A K)$. A field is Noetherian since its only ideals are $0$ and $K$, so finite-type permanence makes $B_K$ Noetherian. Its spectrum is Noetherian and every subset, in particular $U_\eta\cap(X_r)_\eta$, is quasi-compact. Cover $U\cap X_r$ by principal opens $D(g)\subseteq U\cap X_r$ using the principal-open basis on $\operatorname{Spec}B$. Their inverse images cover $U_\eta\cap(X_r)_\eta$; quasi-compactness chooses finitely many $g_1,\ldots,g_m\in B$. Hence $D(g_i)\subseteq U\cap X_r$ holds on the total chart, and their generic fibres cover its $U_\eta$.

### Returned step 3

Generic injectivity assigned to lem-trf-generic-open-principal-multiplication-data; definition and finite generation of M retained in parent step 2.

Apply the affine density criterion on the generic fibre. It gives injectivity of $\mu_K:B_K\to B_K^{\oplus m}$, $b\mapsto(g_1b,\ldots,g_mb)$. Define $\mu:B\to B^{\oplus m}$ by the same formula and $M=\operatorname{coker}\mu$. The images of the $m$ standard basis vectors generate $M$ as a $B$-module, including $M=0$ when $m=0$.

### Returned step 4

Entire square-zero construction, its finite type generators, localization splitting and flat summands assigned to lem-trf-algebra-module-simultaneous-generic-flatness.

Obtain the simultaneous flatness needed in Tag 0573 from the existing algebra generic-flatness tool as follows. Form the square-zero algebra $E=B\oplus M$ with $(b,u)(c,v)=(bc,bv+cu)$ and unit $(1,0)$; associativity follows by expanding each side to $(bcd,bcw+bdv+cdu)$. If $b_j$ generate $B$ over $A$ and $u_i$ generate $M$ over $B$, then $(b_j,0)$ and $(0,u_i)$ generate $E$ over $A$: products with $(b,0)$ give every $(0,bu_i)$. Apply algebra generic flatness to $A\to E$, obtaining nonzero $a\in A$ with $E_a$ flat over $A$. Fractions identify $E_a=B_a\oplus M_a$ as $A$-modules. For any injection $P\to Q$, its tensor with $E_a$ is injective by flatness; the maps on the two summands are therefore injective separately. By the definition of flatness, both $B_a$ and $M_a$ are flat over $A$. All subsequent arguments are on $D(a)$, where tensoring with $\kappa(y)$ or $K$ is unchanged by this localization. No finite-presentation conclusion is needed.

### Returned step 5

Entire torsion-free argument, embedding into B_K, kernel vanishing and fraction description of the localized cokernel assigned to lem-trf-localized-generic-multiplication-universal-injectivity.

The localized map $\mu_a:B_a\to B_a^{\oplus m}$ is injective. Indeed, for nonzero $s\in A$, multiplication by $s$ on $A$ is injective; tensor with the flat $A$-module $B_a$ to see that multiplication by $s$ on $B_a$ is injective. Thus $B_a$ is torsion-free. The map $B_a\to B_K$ is injective: a fraction mapping to zero is killed by a nonzero base scalar, and hence is zero. If $b\in\ker\mu_a$, its image in $B_K$ lies in $\ker\mu_K=0$, so $b=0$. Fractions also identify $\operatorname{coker}\mu_a=M_a$: every localized cokernel class lifts from a fraction in $B^{\oplus m}$, and a class is zero precisely when a power of $a$ puts its numerator in $\operatorname{im}\mu$. Consequently $0\to B_a\xrightarrow{\mu_a}B_a^{\oplus m}\to M_a\to0$ is exact.

### Returned step 6

Flat-quotient tensoring and localization/base-extension identifications assigned to lem-trf-localized-generic-multiplication-universal-injectivity; affine criterion and W_y subset U_y section argument retained in parent step 4.

For $y\in D(a)$ tensor this short exact sequence over $A$ with $\kappa(y)$. The flat-quotient tensor theorem applies because $M_a$ is flat over $A$. It makes the map $B\otimes_A\kappa(y)\to(B\otimes_A\kappa(y))^{\oplus m}$, $b\mapsto((g_i\otimes1)b)_i$, injective. The affine criterion says that $W_y=\bigcup_i D(g_i\otimes1)$ has injective structure-sheaf restriction from $X_y$. Since the total-space opens $D(g_i)$ were chosen inside $U$, we have $W_y\subseteq U_y$. A section on any open of $X_y$ vanishing on its intersection with $U_y$ vanishes on its intersection with $W_y$, and hence is zero. This proves the required sheaf injection for the chart.

### Returned step 7

Retained in parent step 5.

For each chart take the resulting nonempty base open, and intersect all finitely many of them. This intersection contains the generic point: every nonempty open of an irreducible scheme contains its generic point. Thus it is nonempty. For a fibre over this intersection, the chart fibres cover $X_y$. A section in the kernel of $\mathcal O_{X_y}\to(j_y)_*\mathcal O_{U_y}$ restricts to zero on every chart by the previous step, and is zero by the sheaf axiom. The same applies on every open of $X_y$, giving the claimed sheaf injection. If there are no charts, $X$ is empty and any nonempty affine base open works.
