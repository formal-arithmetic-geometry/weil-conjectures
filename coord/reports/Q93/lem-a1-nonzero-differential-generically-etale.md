### Old step 2 → lem-a1-map-over-base-finite-presentation

The morphism $h$ is locally of finite presentation. Around any source point choose affine charts $\operatorname{Spec}A\to\operatorname{Spec}B$ with $A=k[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/(F_1,\ldots,F_r)$ finitely presented over $k$, using smoothness and [def-sbc-locally-finite-presentation]; the chart can be made small enough to map into the target affine by principal localization, which adjoins one variable and one relation. Choose $k$-algebra generators $b_1,\ldots,b_m$ of $B$ and polynomial representatives $G_i(x)$ for their images in $A$. Then $$A\cong B[x_1,\ldots,x_n]/(F_1,\ldots,F_r,b_1-G_1,\ldots,b_m-G_m).$$ Evaluation gives the map to $A$; the reverse map sends each $x_j$ to its class. The finite relations of $A$ hold, and every element of $B$ is a polynomial in the $b_i$, so both composites fix all generators. This is a finite presentation for the original $B$-algebra map. In particular $h$ is locally of finite type [def-weil-finite-type-morphism].

Uses: def-sbc-smooth-morphisms, def-sbc-locally-finite-presentation, def-weil-finite-type-morphism

### Old step 3 → lem-a1-differential-sheaf-transitivity-exact

### Old step 4 → lem-a1-smooth-curve-cotangent-generic-isomorphism (generic invertibility); assembly step 2 (unramifiedness)

Assume $dh\ne0$. Trivialize both rank-one sheaves on affine neighbourhoods. The map there is multiplication by a regular function $a$ in a domain. If its generic stalk were zero then $a$ would be zero in the fraction field, hence zero on each such affine, forcing $dh=0$. Thus on some nonempty affine chart $a\ne0$. Its principal open $D(a)$ is nonempty (it contains the generic point) and multiplication by $a$ is invertible there. Denote this open by $V$. By Step 3, $\Omega_{V/Y}=0$. ### Old step 5 → lem-a1-unramified-curve-map-nonconstant

The map $h|_V$ is nonconstant. Suppose its image were the single point $y$. Every point of $V$ is a specialization of its generic point, so $y$ is the image of that point as well. If $y$ is the generic point of $Y$, the local homomorphism at a point $v$ corresponding to a maximal ideal in any nonempty affine chart of $V$ would embed $k(Y)$ into $\kappa(v)$. This is impossible: [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-domain-dimension-trdeg] gives $\operatorname{trdeg}_k k(Y)=1$ and dimension one for $\mathcal O_{V,v}$, whereas [lem-weil-pbc-dimension-at-point-local-ring-trdeg] gives $\operatorname{trdeg}_k\kappa(v)=0$. If $y$ is not generic, it is closed: in a dimension-one integral scheme every nongeneric point is closed, since an intermediate specialization would give a prime chain of length two. On an affine neighbourhood $\operatorname{Spec}B$ of $y$ with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, take a nonempty affine $W=\operatorname{Spec}A\subset V$. Every prime of $A$ contains the image of $\mathfrak m$; since $A$ is a domain this image is zero. Thus the original map factors through the field $K=B/\mathfrak m=\kappa(y)$. Derivations of $A$ over $B$ and over $K$ are identical because $B\to K$ is surjective, so $\Omega_{A/K}=\Omega_{A/B}=0$. The $k$-generators of $A$ also generate it over $K$, hence $W\to\operatorname{Spec}K$ is unramified by [def-weil-unramified-morphism]. Apply [lem-weil-pbc-unramified-algebra-over-field-product]: $A$ is a finite product of fields and, as a domain, is a field. Its spectrum has dimension zero, contradicting Step 1. Both possible singleton images are excluded.

Uses: lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-domain-dimension-trdeg, lem-weil-pbc-dimension-at-point-local-ring-trdeg, def-weil-unramified-morphism, lem-weil-pbc-unramified-algebra-over-field-product

### Old step 7 → assembly step 4; lem-a1-smooth-curve-cotangent-generic-isomorphism (nonzero stalk)
