## New-node proof plan

The new node generalizes the returned one-term argument to a sheaf of algebras over any commutative scalar ring; no discrete-valuation or completeness hypothesis is needed. On degree-zero modules, the derived-category embedding is fully faithful, so a rational map between such objects is a finite sum of actual module maps with scalar denominators. Both ordinary sheaf Hom into $R$ and the Hom complex into the fixed $I$ act by precomposition and hence extend linearly on this full subcategory. Their natural comparison is therefore natural for the rational splitting maps. For $R^m$ it is a quasi-isomorphism because ordinary internal Hom from $R^m$ is a finite power of the identity. The arrow $c_F$ is a retract of this finite-free arrow, so its inverse is obtained by composing the inclusion into the finite-free target, the inverse finite-free comparison, and the projection onto the summand. The two inverse identities follow from naturality and $pi=1$. The same underived Hom splitting exhibits its source as a finite-free summand. This is a genuinely separate localized one-term comparison, not the bounded dual-perfectness assertion of the item. Its cases $m=0$, $F=R$, $F=R\oplus R/t$, $t=0$ (the zero scalar-localized category), and invertible $t$ were checked; in the last case the hypothesis is an actual finite-projective splitting.
