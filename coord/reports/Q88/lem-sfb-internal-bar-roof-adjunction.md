### Former step 10

Perform the following calculation with morphism groups in $\mathcal E$. Put $C^n=\prod_{p\geq0}\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal E}(P_p,I^{n-p})$; its nonzero factors have $0\leq p\leq n$, so the product is finite, and it is zero for $n<0$. This is a direct calculation of the cochain and shift definitions, with no internal-Hom derived comparison theorem used.
