## Arguments for the new statement-only inputs

These paragraphs explain why the inputs are true and how the sources are specialized; they are not proof fields on unsplit nodes.

For the special-neutral subgroup, the special fibre of a smooth locally finite-type group has an open and closed finite-type identity component, compatible with field extension. Its complement is closed in the closed fibre, hence closed in J; U is therefore open. The multiplication and inverse preserve U on each fibre (on the generic fibre U is all of J, on the special fibre it is the identity subgroup), so the morphisms factor through U. Its identity section factors through U too. To see quasi-compactness without claiming J is quasi-compact, take finitely many affine neighbourhoods covering the quasi-compact special identity component. Their union together with the quasi-compact generic fibre covers U. Over the Noetherian trait, local finite type and this finite cover imply finite type. A datum-compatible group isomorphism preserves the fibrewise identity components, including after residue-field extensions, so it preserves U. Over a strictly henselian DVR, every component of the smooth special fibre contains a rational point: a nonempty smooth finite-type open has a separable closed point, and the residue field is separably closed. Such a point lifts to a section by the henselian smooth-point lifting property. Its translate of U contains that entire special component; all translates contain the entire generic fibre. Hence they cover J. This is Raynaud's G^{00} plus the smooth translate argument. It does not claim that every closed point over an imperfect separably closed field is rational.

For generic-point approximation, choose an affine open of V containing the image of v. A point there is given by finitely many elements of F satisfying finitely many relations. Write them as fractions of elements of D. Since D is the filtered pointed-etale colimit, numerators, denominators and the finitely many relations occur at one pointed etale stage; equivalently apply 01ZM's morphism approximation after inverting the uniformizer. Refine the stage to the normal component through the chosen geometric point. It is an integral etale quasi-finite scheme over the trait, so its special fibre is finite. Remove all other points of that special fibre by principal shrinkings not removing the chosen point, and discard the other normal components. The resulting affine scheme has only its generic point and its chosen closed point, hence its coordinate ring A is a local DVR, etale of finite presentation over R. The map A→D is local; it is injective because its kernel is a prime not equal to the maximal ideal (the nonzero uniformizer has nonzero image), and A has only those two primes. It is flat because D is torsion-free over the DVR A, and faithfully flat because it is flat and local. The finitely many coefficients now lie in Frac A and define v_A, with precisely the required pullback. Thus this input includes the local-DVR refinement and is more than the generic 01ZM morphism statement. It does not assume that a nonhenselian DVR has a cofinal system of finite etale local extensions.

For effective-chart gluing, the equalizer functor X is an fppf sheaf: a compatible family on an fppf cover gives maps to Y after base change to S', they glue uniquely by the representable sheaf property 023Q, and compatibility with alpha is an equality checked after that cover. The cocycle identifies its restriction to S' with h_Y: for T/S', a map T→Y is extended across T×_S S' using alpha and recovered along the diagonal; the cocycle gives compatibility and inverse formulas. For a fixed i and test T→X_{T_i}, the inverse image of U_i in T×_{T_i}S' is an invariant open. Because q_i is fpqc, its quotient topology and base-change stability descend this open to an open W⊂T. On W the section to U_i corresponds, through the supplied effective datum, to a unique map W→Z_i by 023Q; conversely a map to Z_i has image in that open. This proves Z_i→X_{T_i} is representable by open immersions, without first assuming X is algebraic. Composing with T_i→S makes Z_i→X representable etale. Their coproduct becomes surjective over S' because the U_i cover Y, and hence is surjective on every scheme test after the fpqc base change; the representable etale map is thus a surjection. This is an actual etale scheme atlas, or one may apply the bootstrap theorem 04S6. No fppf descent theorem is applied to the non-fppf cover S'→S. The supplied Z_i are the precise added hypothesis missing from the rejected broad node.
