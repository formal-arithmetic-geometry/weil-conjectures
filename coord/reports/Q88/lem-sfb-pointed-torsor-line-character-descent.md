I read the item against the Stacks statements above. The classification is a consequence under exactly its written hypotheses: X need not be reduced, proper, connected, finite type or smooth, and the finite group scheme need not be etale. Crucially the global-functions hypothesis quantifies over all k-schemes S, so it can be used with S=G_T, including nonreduced G_T. Merely knowing Gamma(X,O)=k would not justify that step; I did not replace the written hypothesis with that weaker one.

Step 1 uses the actual affine-preimage meaning of finite in `def-weil-finite-integral-morphism` and the affine-open formulation in `def-weil-pbc-fpqc-covering`: the single inverse image of each affine open is already a suitable affine covering member, with full image by surjectivity. It then cites exactly the arbitrary base-change assertion of `lem-galois-fpqc-covering-base-change`. Flatness is supplied by the item. Thus subsequent applications of module descent really have an fpqc cover, even when T is arbitrary. No use of finite flat implying locally free over a non-Noetherian base is made.

Step 2 uses tensor pullback composition as stated in `def-pc-module-pullback` to type the restriction to x0. I checked the scalar assertion directly: a sheaf-module endomorphism of O multiplies by its value on 1 on every open, with inverses corresponding to units. The global-functions isomorphism for S=T is a ring isomorphism, so it carries units bijectively; evaluation at x0 is its inverse. The ratio of two normalized frames therefore equals 1. No theorem on an unspecified automorphism sheaf is hidden here.

Step 3 uses the same pullback-composition assertion to form the canonical comparison c on the double overlap. The expression d=(p2* tau)c(p1* tau)^(-1) is well typed. The torsor isomorphism is an assumption of this item, not a theorem borrowed from the proof of a definition. Applying the written hypothesis at S=G_T yields the unique unit b. Its explicit formula by restriction at x0 verifies that the multiplier is independent of X on the full scheme.

Step 4 writes the triple-overlap identification and the cocycle equation with its order. Substituting the factorization and pulling back along x0 gives m*b=pr2*b pr1*b on the whole G_T times_T G_T. This is not a deduction from k-points. The coordinate description in `def-sc-gm-ga`, together with the spectrum correspondence of `def-weil-affine-spectrum`, turns b into the T-morphism by t->b and t^(-1)->b^(-1) on affine charts; the formulas agree on overlaps. `def-sc-group-scheme` states the multiplication criterion for a group homomorphism. The identity and inverse follow by the displayed unit calculations. The convention is precisely the multiplier itself, not an accidentally reversed character.
