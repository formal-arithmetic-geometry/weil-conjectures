## Algebra behind the new node

This is the argument intended for the new statement-only node, recorded here without splitting that node. Let D=Z[Q]. Fix n and suppose every cyclic monomial quotient D/Z[I] is Tor_n(M,-)-acyclic. Well-order T\U. Starting with U, adjoin the translates t+h(Q) one at a time, omitting a translate when already present. At a successor stage, the added subquotient has basis the elements of t+h(Q) not in the old invariant subset. Because h is injective, q maps bijectively to t+h(q); the elements already present have inverse image a monoid ideal I_t in Q. Thus the successive subquotient is exactly D/Z[I_t], including a zero subquotient when I_t=Q. At limit stages use unions. The Tor exact sequence shows that acyclicity is closed under extensions, and Tor commutes with these filtered unions (Stacks algebra.tex, Tag 0BNF, lemma-tor-commutes-filtered-colimits). Hence the Tor_n vanishing for cyclic monomial quotients gives Tor_n(M,Z[T]/Z[U])=0 for all such pairs.

For n=1 the cyclic hypothesis is exactly the new statement's premise. For the induction, assume the conclusion at degree n for all pairs. In particular Tor_n(M,Z[I])=0, taking G=Q^gp, T=I and U empty (Q embeds in its group because h is injective). The exact sequence 0 -> Z[I] -> D -> D/Z[I] -> 0 gives Tor_(n+1)(M,D/Z[I])=0 since positive Tor with the free module D is zero. Apply the preceding filtration argument at degree n+1. This proves the assertion in every positive degree. The long exact sequence and dimension shifting are the ordinary resolution argument behind Stacks Tag 00M0, lemma-long-exact-sequence-tor. No finite generation or Noetherian hypothesis is needed for this algebra lemma: limit stages are genuine filtered colimits and h-injectivity ensures that each orbit is free as a Q-set.

This is a real lemma rather than a clause renamed: it removes all log structures, local rings, coefficients, and fine/saturated conditions, and replaces geometry by an explicit Tor-one premise. Establishing its transfinite filtration and degree induction is not a two-line elementary inference. In contrast, the coefficient and Tor-independent change-of-rings comparisons are short free-resolution calculations and remain steps of the item.
