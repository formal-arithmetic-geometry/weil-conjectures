I checked each step against the actual statements it cites, rather than against their proofs. All sixteen step/node pairs have a verbatim statement quote in citations.json. No new node requires a separate source or search audit.

Step 1 uses the 2-fibre-product and representability definitions of def-sfb-stable-pointed-moduli-groupoids. These apply to g after the arbitrary scheme test T to B. The refinement node's hypothesis is a small jointly surjective family of flat lfp maps of spaces; our singleton Y to T satisfies exactly this, since field lifting produces a point above each point of T. There is no assumed quasi-compactness.

Step 2 uses def-trf-algebraic-space, which explicitly supplies a surjective etale scheme atlas U of X and says that Y is a sheaf. It uses def-pc-fibre-product only for a fibre product of schemes U and V over the scheme T. The full statement of lem-sfb-space-fppf-finite-affine-refinement supplies finite affine summands mapping to Y, an affine coproduct V-prime, and the singleton fpqc covering of V. It applies to each affine open V of arbitrary T; it does not require T itself affine. The map from the disjoint union to the sheaf Y is obtained by sheaf gluing on its disjoint open cover, so it is actually available for the next base change.

Step 3's comparison of two 2-pullbacks is written on objects and arrows using the triple definition in def-sfb-stable-pointed-moduli-groupoids; the two functors are inverse and compatible with restrictions. This avoids a hidden comparison lemma. The existing stack calculus explicitly provides base change of the two P properties from f_C along the scheme V-prime to C. The statement of lem-trf-space-representable-etale-scheme-tests supplies base change of surjective etale maps and includes identity charts. def-pc-fibre-product ensures U-prime is a scheme, since U and V-prime both map to T. Finally lem-sfb-space-local-properties-chart-test really says P(X-prime to V-prime) iff P(U-prime to V-prime) for a surjective etale source atlas and identity target chart, separately for flatness and local finite presentation. No smooth atlas was incorrectly treated as etale.

Step 4 applies the statements of lem-weil-pbc-fpqc-descend-flat and lem-weil-pbc-fpqc-descend-locally-finite-presentation to U_V to V, both schemes, and the singleton fpqc scheme cover produced in Step 2. Their statements allow arbitrary sources, so no affineness or quasi-compactness of U_V is smuggled in. The base-changed map is precisely U-prime to V-prime, with its projection fixed by the fibre-product formula.
