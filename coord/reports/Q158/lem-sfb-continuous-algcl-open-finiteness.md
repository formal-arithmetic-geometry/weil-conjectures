### Returned step 2

Apply [lem-sfb-continuous-algcl-stratified-finite-models] to $L$ on $S$. Refine its finite stratification to a good one by [lem-sfb-noetherian-good-stratification-refinement], realizing each refined locally closed subset inside its original scheme stratum by a locally closed pullback, so the same finite-field models actually restrict along scheme morphisms. Since closures are unions of strata, a finite good stratification has an open layer and a closed complementary union of strata. The exact sequence $0\to v_!v^*M\to M\to i_*i^*M\to0$ of [lem-weil-pbc-proetale-ringed-open-closed] therefore gives, by induction on the finite number of strata, a finite extension filtration with pieces $b_!(B_{S_b}\otimes_{A_{E_b,S_b}}L_b)$. At an open layer consisting of several disjoint strata, take their finite direct sum. Applying the exact $a_!$ (open extension followed by closed direct image) gives the corresponding finite triangle filtration of $a_!L$. Thus it is enough to treat $G=a_!b_!(B_V\otimes_{A_{E,V}}N)[0]$ with $b:V\hookrightarrow S$ locally closed and $N$ a lisse $A_{E,V}$-module for one finite $E$. This induction ends with the empty closed complement, which contributes zero.

### Returned step 3

Put $C=(a_!b_!N)[0]$. The old strata of $V$, together with the zero model off $V$, at each of the two immersions prove that $C$ is bounded constructible over $A_E$ by [lem-sfb-scalar-model-immersion-extension]. $$B_U\otimes^L_{A_{E,U}}a_!b_!N\ \cong\ a_!b_!(B_V\otimes^L_{A_{E,V}}N)\ \cong\ G.$$
The comparison carries the $B_U$-action to the action through its restriction to $V$: on a local pure tensor it sends $b\otimes n$ to the same product $bn$. It is therefore an isomorphism of $B_U$-complexes, not just of the restricted $A_E$-complexes.
