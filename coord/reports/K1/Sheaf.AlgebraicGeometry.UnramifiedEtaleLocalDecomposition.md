## Where the brief, the DAG or earlier work was wrong
STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the first worker's exclusion helper is false. Take a nonempty field spectrum with `X = S = U`, identity morphisms, one listed point `x = s = u`, every `m i = 0`, `W = top`, and the empty family `V`. Every proposed core hypothesis holds, but its claimed exclusion says that the listed point differs from itself. The proposed core itself permits this trivial empty-family choice for every input and contains no connection between the pieces and the listed points. It cannot support exclusion.

Both proposed new nodes also depended on the parent theorem they were intended to prove, creating a circular proof-dependency route. Their statements were not self-contained, and the exclusion helper lacked a complete docstring proof. The prior affirmative second-reading report was incorrect and has been superseded. The replacement makes distinctness, unique purely inseparable fibre points, and exhaustion of listed lifts explicit. It retains the original geometric route rather than replacing the target by a renamed theorem.

Its route is finite-one-point closed immersion, proper-over-separated, deletion of closed intersection images, and open base change. It realizes the geometric parts of steps 2–4 of the parent's plan. It is distinct from `finite_unramified_one_point_closed`, which concerns only one piece and does not prove disjointness or ambient closedness.
