## Dependency modules

`def-weil-pbc-special-cocontinuous-functor`: its old declaration requires source and target
precoverages to satisfy `Precoverage.IsStableUnderComposition`. This is too strong for the
standard finite coverings used here. For a concrete counterexample, take S = Spec(Q).
Index the identity covering of S by the positive integers, repeating the identity arrow.
Its presieve is the singleton identity and is therefore a finite covering. At index n
refine by the singleton finite étale surjective cover Spec(Q(2^(1/n))) → S, restricting
for example to odd primes n. Their domain fields have unbounded degrees, giving infinitely
many distinct arrows. The indexed composite presieve is infinite, so is not a standard
finite covering. Ordinary pretopology transitivity instead chooses a cover for each
DISTINCT arrow of the covering presieve and has no such problem. Thus this class is not
an extra hypothesis one may assume for the affine site.

`Sheaf.CategoryTheory.SpecialCocontinuousCoverings` states the exact dependency notion:
(a) cocontinuity on generated topologies, (b) preservation of actual covers and pullbacks
of covering arrows, (c) local equality on a source cover, (d) local lifting on a source cover,
and (e) target-object covering by image objects. Its definition uses the previous declaration's
five clauses verbatim and omits only the restrictive precoverage class binders; those are
not part of Stacks 03A0 or the dependency's statement. A new full name avoids changing the
already landed declaration. Only the dependency's lean record changes, with the required
note; its mathematical statement and all other fields are untouched. This reports the
process/API fault upward through the delivery report, without editing any repository tool.
