## Statement corrected — STATEMENT-DISPUTED

The literal imported statement introduces neither the characteristic/coefficient conditions nor a nontrivial character. Without the latter it is false: take rank one, the trivial character, and any distinct geometric points x,y. Both evaluation kernels are constant. Compact integration along the middle affine line gives a nonzero constant complex on all of V×V, whereas the proposed delta complex has zero stalk at (x,y). Thus the missing condition is mathematically necessary, not an optional strengthening to simplify a proof.

The correction spells out the intended Fourier setting: k is the fixed algebraic closure of the finite field, p and ell are distinct, the fixed prime-field character Psi is nontrivial, and psi on the finite-field model is Psi composed with the finite field trace. Over the algebraic closure the sheaf is the pullback of the prime-field sheaf, not a sheaf constructed from a nonexistent finite-field trace k→F_p. This is exactly the convention of `def-fd-artin-schreier-sheaf` and the already repaired `lem-fd-cohomology-affine-line-character`, and agrees with Laumon (0.1)–(0.3), (1.2.1). Both displayed conclusions are retained, for every r≥0. Reflection is explicitly y↦−y. The twist now cites the coefficient-stage definition instead of only an E-valued Weil-category definition.

There are two proof omissions, rather than changes of route. First, the geometric fibre over an arbitrary point is affine space over its algebraically closed residue field, not necessarily over the fixed k. I implement the same linear-form calculation over the principal opens D(c_i) with an explicit regular inverse coordinate change; this avoids an unprovided extension of the finite-field-descended duality theorem to arbitrary geometric residue fields. Second, equal pointwise fibres alone do not identify the convolution as a constant sheaf along the diagonal. I compute its restriction on the entire closed diagonal using compact base change, and apply the actual open–closed unit map. This keeps the unit's kernel/linear-form/Thom/duality route and fills its final inference. No chapter or unit statement or route was edited.
