## Argument intended for the new statement-only node

Read ILO VIII 3.2.9 and 3.2.17--3.2.18, printed pp.104--105, together with the chart definitions and the proof comparing monoid fractions. The comparison of the ordinary blowup with monoid charts works over Z: Z[Q] embeds in Z[G], and the chart of the ideal generated by z^e at z^a has ring Z[Q][z^(e-a)] = Z[Q[I-a]]. One can verify this inside the Laurent polynomial domain Z[G]. Saturation gives a finite algebra on each chart, using the existing finite-saturation statement, and the overlap localizations agree. Thus the saturated realization is finite over the ordinary toric blowup; the latter is projective, so the former is proper. Gluing and properness are compatible with ordinary scheme base change. In particular, AFTER constructing the proper toric scheme over Z, arbitrary tensoring with A preserves properness, even though the result need not be the ordinary blowup of the extended ring ideal in A. Repeat for the finite tower, keeping the monoid charts inside the original G. Inverting P inverts every chart monomial and every monomial ideal generator, so every stage is the identity over Spec S^(-1)A. This is a genuine generic chart-comparison lemma; the item uses its statement, and does not count this report sketch as a proved node.
