### lem-trf-closed-evaluation-naturality

Original step 1 (832 characters):

Write $H(X,Y)=R\operatorname{Hom}_R(X,Y)$. Apply the closed symmetric monoidal structure of [lem-sf-ringed-derived-closed-symmetric-monoidal] on the one-object site with the trivial topology and constant ring $R$. Its modules are precisely $R$-modules; this site is small. Put $A=H(L,M)$ and $B=H(K,L)$. Form $A\otimes^L K\otimes^L B\to A\otimes^L B\otimes^L K\to A\otimes^L L\to M$, swapping $K,B$ and then applying the two counits. Transpose it under $-\otimes^L B\dashv H(B,-)$ to obtain $e:A\otimes^L K\to H(B,M)$. By naturality and involutivity of the symmetry, uncurrying instead with $B$ on the left gives exactly the composite in the statement: move $B$ past $A$, then evaluate $B\otimes^L K$, then evaluate $A\otimes^L L$. Thus this is a specified canonical map, without choices of an inverse or of a finiteness hypothesis.

Original step 2 (832 characters):

The closed adjunction in [lem-sf-ringed-derived-closed-symmetric-monoidal] is natural in all its variables. For $u:K\to K'$, the two uncurried composites reduce to evaluating $f\circ u$ on $x$, or $f$ on $u(x)$; the equality is the first-variable counit naturality. ### lem-trf-double-hom-evaluation-shift

Original step 5 (1339 characters):

Check the signed comparisons on the representatives of step 3. With the Hom differential of [def-pur-hom-complex], the first-variable comparison $s_{P,m}:\operatorname{Hom}(P[m],J)\to\operatorname{Hom}(P,J)[-m]$ multiplies a homogeneous map of degree $q$ by $(-1)^{mq}$ and reindexes its source. Substitution in $d(f)=d_Jf-(-1)^qfd_P$ verifies this chain identity; replacing $J$ by $I$ gives the outer comparison. Their signed composite for $G(P[m])\to G(P)[m]$ is
$$\chi_{P,m}=(-1)^m s_{H(P,J),-m}\circ H(s_{P,m},I)^{-1}.$$
On $g\in G(P[m])^n$ tested at $f\in H(P,J)^c$, this reads $(\chi_{P,m}g)(f)=(-1)^{m(n+c)}g(f)$, where $f$ on the right is read in $H(P[m],J)^{c+m}$. Indeed the two reindexings contribute $m(c+m)+mn$, and the reversal contributes $m$; $m^2+m$ is even. Tensor shift has multiplier $(-1)^{ma}$ on $h\otimes x$. If $x\in P^b$, its degree in $P[m]$ is $b-m$ and $n=a+b-m$. Consequently
$$(b-m)(c+m)+m(n+c)\equiv bc+ma\pmod2.$$
The left side is the sign of $\chi_{P,m}E_{P[m]}$, and the right side that of $E_P[m]$ after tensor shift. Hence the shift square commutes for every integer $m$. These are the signed exactness comparisons of step 4, not unsigned reindexings. The identity passes to $D(R)$ through the representative comparisons of [def-pur-derived-internal-hom] and [lem-weil-pbc-site-derived-tensor-descent].
