These give the two new algebraic statements: a filtered union of finite etale subalgebras is the maximal weakly etale subalgebra over a field, and that maximal subalgebra commutes with every field extension. A finite set is contained in one stage, not merely in some unspecified algebraic extension. Every idempotent generates a quotient of k x k and belongs to the union. Images of finite etale algebras are quotients, which over a field are again finite etale. I did not put proof sketches into the two new statements.
