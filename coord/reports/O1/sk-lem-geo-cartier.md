## Unfinished work

The new generic homological lemma is deliberately statement-only and remains unsplit, as required for a one-level split. Its future proof is the following. Compute the underlying derived reduction using the two-term free resolution [Z --n--> Z]. The two hypotheses give cohomology F/nF only in degree zero for F, and ker(n on B) only in degree minus one for B. Canonical truncation in the module derived category gives the two identifications, with the second sign normalized so the scalar-extension unit is the boundary of 0 -> H -> B --n--> B -> 0. Apply the exact derived reduction functor to the extension boundary. Its adjunction unit naturality, precomposed with s:Z -> F, identifies the adjunct of the resulting R-linear composite with kappa_B[1] composed with partial_S composed with s. This is the claimed Kummer connecting image. The construction uses functors and canonical truncations, so morphisms of extensions and exact geometric inverse image commute with it. This includes the module-linearity and adjunction normalization that a bare abelian cone computation would omit.
