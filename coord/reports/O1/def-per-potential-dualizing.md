## Statement typing: STATEMENT-DISPUTED

Read literally, the imported equation `p_y = p_x(1)[2] ∘ sp_{y,x}` composes pinnings on different residue-field etale sites, whereas Expose XVII Definition 1.4.1 defines the transition in `D^+(Lambda)` at geometric points. For example, on a strictly henselian trait the generic residue-field category still carries its nontrivial absolute Galois action, while the closed geometric fibre does not; the displayed residue-field morphisms are not morphisms of one category. Definition 2.1.2 uses the geometric specializations in precisely this sense. The corrected equation explicitly uses `p_bar_y` and `p_bar_x`, the geometric fibres of the original residue-field pinnings. This repairs its typing and retains all residue-field pinnings and their Galois information. I also require the dimension function for every codimension-one *geometric* specialization, as in Definition 2.1.1.

The imported residue degree was ambiguous about the notation `kappa(x)`. Its correct denominator is the residue field of the closed point after strict localization, not the original scheme's residue field. The corrected definition names these two local-curve points `z_0` and `tilde z_0`. For a trait already normal the degree is one.
