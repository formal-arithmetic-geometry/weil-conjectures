### lem-coef-compact-support-quotient-cocycle — returned Step 1

Choose a K-flat flat-term representative $P$ using [lem-pc-kflat-resolution]. By [lem-pur-derived-change-of-rings] its tensor reductions compute every $K_n$ independently of this choice. Apply [lem-sfb-successive-derived-quotient-normalization] to $\widehat R_X\to B_{X,m}\to B_{X,n}$: its balancing isomorphism is $\beta_{m,n}$, represented by $(x\otimes u)\otimes v\mapsto x\otimes q_{m,n}(u)v$. The representative-normalized adjunction [lem-pur-sheaf-ring-derived-coefficient-adjunction] identifies the quotient map with $\rho_{m,n}=\operatorname{Res}_{m,n}(\beta_{m,n})\eta_{m,n,K_m}$. Indeed both send $x\otimes u$ to $x\otimes q_{m,n}(u)$. Naturality of the balancing isomorphism and the adjunction makes this independent of $P$. Identity quotients act identically; the two composites for $r\ge m\ge n$ both send $x\otimes u$ to $x\otimes q_{r,n}(u)$, proving the input cocycle.

### lem-coef-compact-support-quotient-cocycle — returned Step 2

Write $\sigma_{m,n}:F_m\operatorname{Res}_{m,n}\xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{Res}_{m,n}F_n$ for [lem-sf-compact-support-restriction-of-scalars], with the fixed compactification $c_0$ throughout. Define $\tau_{m,n}=\sigma_{m,n,K_n}F_m(\rho_{m,n})$, as in the statement. Expand $\operatorname{Res}_{r,m}(\tau_{m,n})\tau_{r,m}$. Naturality of $\sigma_{r,m}$ moves $F_m(\rho_{m,n})$ past $\sigma_{r,m,K_m}$; the resulting expression is $(\operatorname{Res}_{r,m}\sigma_{m,n,K_n})\sigma_{r,m,\operatorname{Res}_{m,n}K_n}F_r(\operatorname{Res}_{r,m}\rho_{m,n}\circ\rho_{r,m})$. The comparison-composition clause of the cited lemma and Step 1 identify this with $\sigma_{r,n,K_n}F_r(\rho_{r,n})=\tau_{r,n}$. For identity coefficients its open comparison is the identity on underlying extension-by-zero sheaves, and its proper comparison is the inverse of the identity resolution map, so $\sigma_{n,n}=1$ and $\tau_{n,n}=1$.

### lem-coef-sequential-varying-ring-realization — returned Step 5

Construct representatives inductively, using [lem-pc-quasi-isomorphisms-multiplicative-system]. Choose a complex $C_1$ representing $M_1$. Suppose $i_n:[C_n]\xrightarrow{\sim}M_n$ has been chosen. Choose any $B_{Y,n+1}$-complex $D$ representing $M_{n+1}$, with $d:[D]\xrightarrow{\sim}M_{n+1}$. In $D(B_{Y,n+1})$ consider $(\operatorname{Res}_{n+1,n}i_n)^{-1}\tau_{n+1,n}d:[D]\to[\operatorname{Res}_{n+1,n}C_n]$. The cited calculus represents it by a roof $D\xleftarrow{s}C_{n+1}\xrightarrow{t_n}\operatorname{Res}_{n+1,n}C_n$ in the homotopy category, with $s$ a quasi-isomorphism. Choose chain-map representatives of $s,t_n$ and put $i_{n+1}=d[s]$. Then $(\operatorname{Res}_{n+1,n}i_n)[t_n]=\tau_{n+1,n}i_{n+1}$. This induction never changes an earlier $C_n$, and every $C_{n+1}$ remains a complex over $B_{Y,n+1}$. The maps $t_n$ give the required adjacent transitions; induction on $m-n$ using Step 2 shows their composites represent every $\tau_{m,n}$.

### lem-coef-sequential-varying-ring-realization — returned Step 6

Restrict $C_n$ to $\widehat R_Y$ and restrict $t_n$ along $\widehat R_Y\to B_{Y,n+1}$. Scalar restriction is termwise and leaves underlying abelian sheaves unchanged [def-pc-sheaf-restrict-scalars], and $q_{n+1,n}\pi_{n+1}=\pi_n$, so the targets are exactly $\operatorname{Res}_n C_n$. Define the map from level $m$ to $n$ as the finite composite of the adjacent maps; associativity gives a strict inverse sequence. In cochain degree $r$ take the tower $(\operatorname{Res}_n C_n^r)_n$, with differential $(d_{C_n}^r)_n$. Each $t_n$ is a chain map, so this differential is a tower morphism, and its square is zero levelwise. Localizing this complex of towers gives $\mathcal M_\bullet\in D(\operatorname{Tow}_{\widehat R_Y}(Y))$ [def-weil-pbc-adic-coefficient-datum, def-weil-abstract-derived-category]. Its evaluations and transitions are precisely those proved in Step 5. Together with Steps 2--4 this is every datum of [def-coef-normalized-dvr-tower]. Finally, the adjacent maps coincide term for term with the transition formula of [def-weil-integral-rational-qbar-compact-support], since both use $F_{n+1}(\rho_{n+1,n})$ followed by the same $\sigma_{n+1,n}$.
