### Edge cases checked before and during writing

* Empty Z or empty geometric fibre: the presheaf sum is zero; no lift or nonempty
  hypothesis enters SumSheafification or exchange_summand. Exact extension preserves
  zero, and the finite-sum theorem explicitly allows empty I.
* Identity j or h: restriction is identity up to its literal functor/unit transport;
  the mate is the unit followed by the evaluation counit, with the triangle identity
  giving the expected comparison. The normalized choice does not omit identities.
* Arbitrarily many sheets: maps from each direct-sum summand still determine the
  sheafification; tensor commutes with arbitrary direct sums. No product replaces
  the direct sum and no cardinal bound is imposed beyond the existing convention.
* Nonflat h, e.g. k[epsilon]/(epsilon²) to k on structural rings: ordinary tensor
  distributes over sums without being exact. The derived comparison uses adapted
  K-flat resolutions, so it does not assume the structural scalar map is flat.
* Nonseparated étale j: only representable étaleness is required. Chart lifts may
  be numerous and different; no uniqueness or separatedness of lifts is assumed.
* Unbounded complexes: indices are ℤ. Total K-flat tests use direct sums, not finite
  degree truncations. Shifts use the same (-1)^n sign on both sides.
* Zero modules and zero complexes satisfy the sum/counit formulas; finite sums
  and arbitrary integer shifts remain within the stated adapted class.
* Coefficient/site sizes: Modules remain in Type u while site objects can be in
  Type (u+1). The preexisting small-model equivalence is used for K-flat tensors
  and derived localization, not an extra universe equality or coefficient lift.
