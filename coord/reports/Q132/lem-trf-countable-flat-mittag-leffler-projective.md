### Old step 3 → lem-trf-countable-flat-ml-sequential-presentation

Enumerate $J$ by a sequence $j_1,j_2,\ldots$, repeating entries when $J$ is finite. Recursively choose $k_1=j_1$ and $k_{n+1}\in J$ above both $k_n$ and $j_{n+1}$. Set $G_n=F_{k_n}$ and use the inherited transitions. These indices are cofinal. To verify the colimit directly, a compatible family $a_n:G_n\to T$ extends to $F_j\to T$ by $a_n f_{j,k_n}$ whenever $j\le k_n$. Two such choices agree by passing to a larger sequence index; the same computation proves compatibility with every arrow of $J$. Conversely, restriction recovers $a_n$. The universal property in [def-weil-pbc-category-colimit] therefore exhibits $P$ as $\operatorname{colim}_{n\ge1}G_n$.

Cited statements: def-weil-pbc-category-colimit.

Cited statements: lem-trf-mittag-leffler-hom-systems, def-weil-pbc-mittag-leffler-system.

### Old step 5 → lem-trf-sequential-free-colimit-hom-exact

Fix an arbitrary short exact sequence $0\to N_1\xrightarrow{a}N_2\xrightarrow{b}N_3\to0$. For every $n$ the induced sequence $0\to\operatorname{Hom}_R(G_n,N_1)\to\operatorname{Hom}_R(G_n,N_2)\to\operatorname{Hom}_R(G_n,N_3)\to0$ is exact. Indeed, choose a basis identifying $G_n$ with $R^{s_n}$; Hom becomes the finite power $N^{s_n}$. Injectivity and equality of kernel with image hold coordinatewise, and a tuple in $N_3^{s_n}$ lifts by choosing a preimage in $N_2$ of each of its finitely many coordinates. Precomposition with $G_n\to G_{n+1}$ commutes with postcomposition by $a,b$, so these sequences form a short exact sequence of inverse systems.

Cited statements: elementary coordinate argument.

### Old step 6 → lem-trf-sequential-free-colimit-hom-exact

Hence part (3) of [lem-weil-pbc-mittag-leffler-limit-exact], applied to the underlying abelian groups, gives the short exact sequence of their inverse limits. The limit maps are $R$-linear because all component maps are; exactness of underlying groups is exactness of the $R$-modules.

Cited statements: lem-weil-pbc-mittag-leffler-limit-exact.

### Old step 7 → lem-trf-sequential-free-colimit-hom-exact

For every $N$, map $h:P\to N$ to $(h\iota_n)_n$, where $\iota_n:G_n\to P$ are the colimit maps. The universal property [def-weil-pbc-category-colimit] gives a unique inverse map from every compatible family. Addition and scalar multiplication agree after restriction to every $G_n$, so this is an $R$-linear isomorphism $\operatorname{Hom}_R(P,N)\cong\varprojlim_n\operatorname{Hom}_R(G_n,N)$. For $v:N\to N^\prime$, the equality $(vh)\iota_n=v(h\iota_n)$ proves naturality. These isomorphisms therefore identify the exact sequence of step 6 with the sequence obtained by applying $\operatorname{Hom}_R(P,-)$ to the arbitrary short exact sequence in step 5. Consequently this Hom functor is exact, so $P$ is projective by [def-weil-pbc-projective-module].

Cited statements: def-weil-pbc-category-colimit, def-weil-pbc-projective-module.
