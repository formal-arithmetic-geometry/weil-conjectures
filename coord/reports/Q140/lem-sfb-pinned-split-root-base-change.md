## New-node proof guidance

For the new augmented-algebra lemma, put Q = A tensor_(B,u) Omega_(B/A). Smoothness in Stacks 00T2 gives Omega finite projective over B. Tensor a finite-free splitting by A to make Q a summand of A^n. Universal differentials (00RO) and the tensor universal property identify Der_A(B,A) with Q*. Stacks 00RV, followed by the augmentation C tensor_A B -> C, identifies Q_C with C tensor_A Q. A finite dual basis q_i,f_i of Q gives the inverse to C tensor_A Q* -> Hom_C(C tensor_A Q,C): F maps to sum_i F(1 tensor q_i) tensor f_i. The two composites equal the identity by q = sum_i f_i(q)q_i and the dual formula. These formulas also exhibit Q* as a finite-free summand. Evaluation on c' tensor b gives exactly cc'f(d(b)), which proves augmented-algebra naturality by precomposition and tower compatibility by associativity of scalar multiplication. Neither flatness nor Noetherianity enters. This guidance stays in the report; the new unsplit node contains a statement only and has no proof or proof dependencies.

The general augmented statement takes more than a one-line specialization of any hit: one must pull differentials back along a specified augmentation, identify derivations with its dual, establish finite projectivity of that pullback, and identify the actual natural comparison. Mathlib's KaehlerDifferential.tensorKaehlerEquiv/Base only treats differentials. `IsBaseChange.toDualBaseChange` in LinearAlgebra/Dual/BaseChange.lean additionally assumes a finite free module; `dualTensorHomEquiv` treats a finite projective module already supplied. The Lie.Derivation/BaseChange constructions act on an already defined Lie algebra, not on a smooth algebra's identity tangent. All searches were run with msearch.py and read.
