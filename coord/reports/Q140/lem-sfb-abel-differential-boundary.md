Neither asserts the sought differential comparison.

Step 1 uses the exact projective-implies-proper statement of lem-pc-projective-proper, the Noetherian integrality statement of lem-sc-smooth-connected-curve-local-rings, and geometric integrality with a point from prop-pc-xk-integral.
