Here is the ring argument that the new statement-only node records for its later worker. For a character over Spec A, write its coordinate as a finite Laurent sum f=sum_m a_m x^m. The Hopf equation Delta(f)=f tensor f gives a_m^2=a_m and a_m a_n=0 for m unequal to n; the counit gives sum_m a_m=1. Thus the D(a_m) form a finite clopen partition and f is exactly x^m on D(a_m). Conversely such an orthogonal-idempotent Laurent sum is group-like and a unit, with inverse sum_m a_m x^(-m). Do this for the finitely many target coordinates and intersect the resulting finite clopen partitions. It gives the locally constant matrix and its uniqueness by coefficients. A matrix substitution composes by multiplication of matrices and commutes with every ring map. Invertible homomorphisms therefore give pointwise inverse integer matrices, and conversely their inverse matrices define inverse morphisms. The affine calculations glue over every scheme. The point formula follows by mapping the Laurent coordinates to units, using the affine-spectrum universal property, then gluing over affine opens. This is the non-elementary Hopf-algebra calculation isolated as the new node; it is not an additional clause moved out of the item.
