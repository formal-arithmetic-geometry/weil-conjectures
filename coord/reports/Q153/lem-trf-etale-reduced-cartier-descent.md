### Old step 1

Let $i:D\hookrightarrow X$ be the reduced induced closed immersion with ideal sheaf $I_D=\mathcal I_Z$, and let $v:W\to X$ be étale. In the fibre product $D\times_XW$ with projections $p:D\times_XW\to D$ and $q:D\times_XW\to W$, the projection $q$ is a closed immersion with ideal $\operatorname{im}(v^*I_D\to\mathcal O_W)$ by the base-change ideal formula; since $v$ is flat (an étale morphism is flat by definition) this arrow is injective and $q$ has ideal $v^*I_D$. The other projection $p$ is the base change of the étale morphism $v$ along $i$, hence étale by stability of étale morphisms under base change (Tag 02GO). As $D$ is reduced (clause (2) of the reduced-induced lemma), $D\times_XW$ is reduced by the étale-reducedness proposition applied to $p$; here $D$ is locally Noetherian, being a closed subscheme of the locally Noetherian $X$, so the Noetherian-local form of Tag 025O also applies. Image of $q$: by the description of points of a fibre product, a point $w\in W$ lies in $q(D\times_XW)$ iff there is $d\in D$ with $i(d)=v(w)$ and $\kappa(d)\otimes_{\kappa(v(w))}\kappa(w)\ne0$; since $i$ is a closed immersion with image $Z$, $\kappa(d)=\kappa(v(w))$ and the tensor product is $\kappa(w)\ne0$, so $q(D\times_XW)=v^{-1}(Z)$. Thus $q$ is a closed immersion with reduced source and image $v^{-1}(Z)$, and the uniqueness clause (3) of the reduced-induced lemma identifies $D\times_XW$ with the reduced induced closed subscheme on $v^{-1}(Z)$ (its ideal is $\mathcal I_{v^{-1}(Z)}$).

### Old step 2

If $D$ is an effective Cartier divisor, its ideal $I_D$ is an effective Cartier ideal, meaning that it has a generator which is a non-zero-divisor on an open cover. Every étale morphism is flat, so the flat pullback theorem applies to $v$ and shows that the pullback ideal $I_D\mathcal O_W$ is an effective Cartier ideal; by the first step this is the ideal of $D\times_XW$. Thus every member of any jointly surjective étale covering has effective Cartier pullback.

### Old step 3

Conversely, suppose $D\times_XW_i$ is effective Cartier for every member of a jointly surjective étale family $\{v_i:W_i\to X\}$. This family is a covering of $X$ in the small étale site of $X$, hence an fpqc covering of $X$ by the comparison lemma (each $v_i$ is flat, and every affine open of $X$ is a finite union of images of affine opens of the $W_i$). Since each $v_i$ is flat, the base-change ideal formula identifies the ideal of $D\times_XW_i$ with $v_i^*I_D$. By the effective-Cartier/invertible-ideal lemma these ideals are invertible, i.e. each $v_i^*I_D$ is finite locally free of rank one. Clause (2) of fpqc descent for quasi-coherent modules (reflection of finite local freeness of rank $r=1$) applied to the quasi-coherent $\mathcal O_X$-module $I_D$ shows that $I_D$ is finite locally free of rank one, i.e. an invertible ideal. The invertible-ideal/effective-Cartier lemma then shows that $I_D$ is an effective Cartier ideal, so $D$ is an effective Cartier divisor. With step 2 this proves the equivalence; for $Z=\emptyset$ both sides hold, the ideal being $\mathcal O_X$ (generated by the non-zero-divisor $1$).
