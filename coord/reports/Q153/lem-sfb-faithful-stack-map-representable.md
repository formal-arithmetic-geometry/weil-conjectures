With the comparison oriented from the test object to F(x), the equation for an arrow is F(a) alpha = alpha'. Choosing the test object F(x), alpha = identity and alpha' = F(a) makes both proposed arrows lift. An equivalence with a discrete groupoid is injective on hom sets (apply its inverse and conjugate by its unit), so their equality really implies a=b.
