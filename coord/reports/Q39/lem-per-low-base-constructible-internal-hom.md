## Review by Q39

I checked delivery-errors B–E against both JSON files and the sources, including the meaning and hypotheses of every cited statement. The item is a theorem about ordinary small-etale constant F_ell modules; no site comparison or scalar-change assertion is silently used. All new-node objects are introduced, its eight statement dependencies define the notions named there, and it has no proof, proof dependencies or proof sketch in its statement. Its source has a precise permitted theorem number; there is no Stacks tag for this low-dimensional finiteness theorem, and I did not invent one. No forbidden source route is cited.

Step 1 uses exactly the kernel/cokernel/extension closure in thm-constructible-serre and the derived triangulated-subcategory conclusion in lem-sbc-cohomology-in-weak-serre-subcategory. The hypotheses agree because F_ell is finite and the categories are ordinary module sheaves. The finite-type-over-Noetherian statement supplies Noetherianity of X. The induction quantifies over all closed subschemes with a given support, so it handles nonreduced schemes and does not incorrectly induct over nilpotent thickenings. Quotient presentations prove the inherited finite type and Noetherian hypotheses; restricting the diagonal proves separatedness.

Step 2 applies the new sheaf-level direct-image lemma only to an open into a scheme still of finite type over the same S. It explicitly uses finite truncation triangles and triangulated right-derived direct image, with enough injectives supplied by lem-weil-pbc-module-sheaves-enough-injectives. I opened those statements: the derived-functor lemma is for any additive left exact functor of abelian categories, so it also applies to these module sheaves. Only finitely many sheaves appear; their individual bounds suffice. This repairs the tempting but unsupported passage from constructible-dependent bounds to a bound for all sheaves.

Step 3 uses only the dense-open finite local constancy promised by prop-constructible-generic-constancy, exact module inverse image, and preservation of constructible sheaves under pullback. Intersecting finitely many dense opens is justified using the generic points of the Noetherian scheme. The closed complement has strictly smaller support, the induction parameter. No regularity of the complement is asserted or needed: regularity is a hypothesis on S, which stays fixed.

Step 4 is an elementary local calculation, not a new lemma. The exact-restriction statement in lem-weil-pbc-ringed-internal-hom-exact-restriction explicitly includes every slice site, so it permits an etale cover trivializing F. The internal-Hom definition computes Hom(A^r,I) as I^r. Finite-dimensionality over F_ell gives finite local freeness, and a common finite stratification proves constructibility of the ordinary Hom. I did not cite lem-sf-hom-finite-projective, whose fixed-field setting would not fit this item, or lem-sf-ext-sheaf-constructible with its different coefficient presentation. The degree calculation is written out, so no site-perfect replacement theorem is assumed.

Step 5 uses the two actual distinguished-triangle clauses of lem-sf-hom-triangulated, the stated truncation triangles, and the long exact sequence in lem-sf-derived-homology-long-exact. A shift H^q[-q] places the sheaf in degree q; Hom from that sheaf to H^r[-r] is in degree r-q. This checks the signs and proves the finite interval [c-b,d-a] on U. Closure under extensions is supplied by Step 1, not an unspecified finiteness theorem.

Step 6 uses the full statement of lem-per-etale-internal-hom-localization, which explicitly permits any commutative ring, bounded first argument and bounded-below second argument without dualizing hypotheses. Taking its first argument to be the constant unit gives the supported triangle; Hom(A,I)=I is the defining computation. The counit is exactly the i^*i_* counit of prop-openclosed-lowerstar-fully-faithful, on the ordinary etale site. Naturality in scalar multiplication makes it module-linear. Exactness of inverse image and constructible pullback then give bounded constructibility of Ri!N. Thus no exceptional-preservation or purity lemma has been smuggled in.

Step 7 applies the induction to the bounded constructible pair just proved to exist on the proper closed complement. The cited finite-pushforward theorem asserts constructibility on Noetherian schemes, and a closed immersion is finite (affine quotient presentations); the module actions do not alter its finite local constructibility. Exactness of this i_* is explicitly in the localization statement. The same internal-Hom triangle, now with first argument M, has both outer terms bounded constructible. Step 1 concludes. This is the actual argument, not application of a new node stating the desired Hom theorem.
