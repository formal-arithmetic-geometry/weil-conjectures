### Returned Step 25: lem-coef-cartesian-marked-lift-generation

We prove the marking-reflection assertion, using both directions of Step 22's criterion. First record its consequence for equivalences over an identity of the base. If $u$ is an equivalence in a fiber, postcomposition by $u$ on every mapping space has homotopy inverse given by an inverse edge, by associativity and the inverse triangles. Composing an edge $b$ before or after such fiber equivalences changes its Cartesian mapping square by equivalences of corners, with the same base corners. The path-space homotopy-pullback criterion is invariant under these changes by [lem-coef-kan-homotopy-pullback-invariance]; the coherent comparison homotopies enter the path coordinate by concatenation, and the inverse fiber equivalences give inverse comparisons up to the associativity and inverse-triangle homotopies. The iff direction therefore says that the new edge is Cartesian exactly when $b$ is. This establishes the closure and reflection under fiber equivalences used here; it is not an extra assertion of the mapping-fiber lemma.

Every $y\in Y_j$ is equivalent within $Y_j$ to some $q(a)$, $a\in(A_j)_0$. Indeed rigidification detection makes $\mathfrak C[A_j]\to\mathfrak C[Y_j]$ essentially surjective on $\pi_0$, and Step 23 identifies the latter category with $hY_j$. Given a Cartesian $d:x\to y$ over $i\to j$, let $c=q(t_{j,i}a)$ and choose a fiber equivalence $v:q(a)\simeq y$.
