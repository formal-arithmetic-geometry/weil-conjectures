For step 2, I checked the actual statements despite the misleading filename `constructible-in-retrocompact-open`: it is precisely the 09YJ transitivity theorem for constructible subspaces. An affine open B in an affine A is quasi-compact, and A is spectral, so B is retrocompact and constructible. The 09YI statement, with E=B and E'=C, restricts C to B. The 09YJ statement extends a constructible subset of B to A. These give both inclusions of the induced topology.
