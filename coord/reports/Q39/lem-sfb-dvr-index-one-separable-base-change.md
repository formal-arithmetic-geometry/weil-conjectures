## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported unconditional assertions that L tensor_K N is reduced and that B tensor_A R equals C are false for arbitrary DVRs. These two assertions are also made in the cited Stacks Tag 09EQ itself. The local index-one and separable-residue conclusions are true without extra hypotheses. I retain every local conclusion under exactly the original assumptions, and retain the two global conclusions conditional on T = B tensor_A R being Noetherian. This is not a silent change or a claim that the original source already includes that condition.

Here is an explicit family of counterexamples to both global assertions. Choose a prime p and a power series f in t F_p[[t]] transcendental over F_p(t). Such series exist because the algebraic closure of the countable field F_p(t) is countable and F_p[[t]] is uncountable. Put

    K = F_p(t, f^p),    L = F_p(t, f),
    A = K intersect F_p[[t]],    B = L intersect F_p[[t]].

Both intersections are the valuation rings for the restrictions of the t-adic valuation. Their value groups are Z, since they contain t, and their residue fields are F_p: they lie in F_p[[t]] and contain the constants. Thus A and B are DVRs, t generates both maximal ideals, A -> B is injective and local, its ramification index is one, and its residue extension is the identity. The transcendence of f over F_p(t) implies f^p is not a p-th power in the rational function field F_p(t, f^p); consequently L/K is purely inseparable of degree p.

Take N = L. The integral closure R of A in L is B. Indeed b^p belongs to K and has nonnegative t-valuation for each b in B, hence b^p is in A and b is integral over A; conversely an element integral over A lies in the valuation ring B. The algebra L tensor_K L is L[X]/((X-f)^p), with X representing f in its second factor. Its reduction E is L and C is B. In B tensor_A B the element epsilon = f tensor 1 - 1 tensor f satisfies epsilon^p = 0 and is nonzero: after inverting t it is the nonzero class X-f in that displayed algebra. The canonical map B tensor_A B -> B kills epsilon. This disproves reducedness and injectivity of the proposed comparison. Meanwhile all the claimed local properties reduce to the identity B -> B and hold. The repaired proof also shows that T in this example cannot be Noetherian.
