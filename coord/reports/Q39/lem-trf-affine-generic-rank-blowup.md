### lem-trf-affine-fp-approximation-strict-transform

Tag 0810, lemma-helper-blowup-affine-space, first proof paragraph: finite-presentation replacement and comparison of the two power-torsion quotients.

Old step 1: Choose a surjection $v:B^n\twoheadrightarrow N$. The kernel of $v_h$ is finitely generated: take a finite presentation $B_h^t\xrightarrow A B_h^m\xrightarrow w N_h\to0$ from [def-sc-finitely-presented-module], and lift $v_h$ and $w$ to maps $u:B_h^n\to B_h^m$ and $z:B_h^m\to B_h^n$ with $wu=v_h$ and $v_hz=w$. Then $\ker(v_h)$ is generated by the columns of $zA$ and $1-zu$: for $x\in\ker(v_h)$ write $ux=Ay$ and $x=zAy+(1-zu)x$. Clear denominators, including powers of $h$ needed to make their images under $v$ zero, obtaining a finitely generated $K\subset\ker v$ with $K_h=\ker(v_h)$. Set $M=B^n/K$. Thus $M$ is finitely presented, maps onto $N$, and $M_h=N_h$.

Cited statements: def-sc-finitely-presented-module.

Old step 10: Finally $M\twoheadrightarrow N$ induces a surjection $M^*\twoheadrightarrow N^*$. It becomes an isomorphism after inverting $a$, since $h$ is then invertible and $M_h=N_h$. Each element of its kernel is therefore killed by a power of $a$, while multiplication by $a$ on $M^*$ is injective by its defining power-torsion quotient: if $ax$ is power torsion then so is $x$. Hence the kernel is zero. The free open from steps 7--9 transfers to $N^*$. Because $g\notin\mathfrak p^*B^*$, its image in the domain fibre is nonzero by the injection proved as in step 3. This proves every clause for the original finite module $N$.

Cited statements: elementary calculation using previous displayed constructions.

### lem-trf-affine-generic-rank-coefficient-ideal

Cited statements: lem-trf-affine-geometrically-integral-projective, lem-trf-fitting-ideal-independence-base-change.

Old step 3: For a prime $\mathfrak p$ of $R$, flatness [def-weil-pbc-flat-ring-map] makes $B/\mathfrak pB$ flat over the domain $R/\mathfrak p$, hence injective into its localization $B\otimes_R\kappa(\mathfrak p)$: tensor the injections given by multiplication by nonzero elements of $R/\mathfrak p$. Cited statements: def-weil-pbc-flat-ring-map, lem-trf-fitting-ideal-independence-base-change.

Old step 8: Assume $r>0$ and put $L=\operatorname{Fit}_{r-1}(M)$. Cited statements: lem-trf-fitting-ideal-independence-base-change, lem-trf-affine-generic-localization-intersection.
