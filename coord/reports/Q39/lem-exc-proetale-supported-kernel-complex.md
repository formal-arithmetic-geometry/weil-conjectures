Here is why the helper is true and smaller than the item. The two ring maps
`c_f^S ∘ f^{-1}(varphi_Y)` and `varphi_X ∘ c_f^R` agree: their adjoints agree on constant sections by the definitions, hence on the locally constant functions defining the coefficient presheaves (decompose into their open fibres), and hence on the sheafifications. Both orders of pulling back a module action and restricting it along `varphi` therefore give the same action of `R_X` on the same abelian inverse image. Applying the finite-product-preserving inverse image to the action composed with `varphi × id` verifies this equality, including restrictions and module morphisms. This constructs alpha. For beta, evaluate on an object V of the target site: both sides have the module M on `X ×_Y V`, with `R_Y(V)` acting through the same composite into `S_X(X ×_Y V)`. This constructs beta.

Finally the lifted module adjunction is the abelian adjunction restricted to linear maps: naturality of the abelian unit with respect to the action proves its linearity, and the corresponding product calculation proves the converse direction of the linearity equivalence in 03D3. Thus its underlying unit is the specified abelian unit. Under alpha and beta both sides of the helper's unit equation have that same underlying abelian morphism; faithfulness of forgetting module structure proves the equation. For the commutative tensor-formula inverse image, the canonical map `m tensor r` to `c_f^{-1}(r)m`, inverse `m` to `m tensor 1`, is the normalized adjunction comparison, as explicitly stated in `def-weil-pbc-proetale-operations`.
