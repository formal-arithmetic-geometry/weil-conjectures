## Proof guidance for the two new independent categorical tasks

For cochain-universal-counit-transport, precomposition by the homotopy quotient is fully faithful on functor categories. Given any cochain test transformation, lift it uniquely through that full precomposition functor, then use the homotopy right-Kan property to get its unique factor. Faithfulness proves the factorization equation and uniqueness. Conjugate this extension along quotientCompQhIso and the mapped-homotopy factor isomorphism. On each complex the conjugation is by isomorphisms, so it both preserves and reflects invertibility. This is not the definition of the cochain counit: the definition node only names its formula, and asserts no universal property. The private complex_universal proof in upstream DerivedExtensionRingEquivRawTransportIso was read; it has no public declaration covering this full normalized statement, and is not an attachment. The argument applies to any additive functor, not just scalar extensions or equivalences.

For augmented-derived-tower-unit, q'G inverts W because it is isomorphic to DG q. Thus RF DG with comparison RF(alpha) is a universal left-derived factor for RF q'G, even though RF is arbitrary. Lift gamma_G q(nu) to v using this factor. Lift q'(eta) alpha through beta to t. Their composite is c. In its normalization, replace RF(beta)RF(t) by RF(q'(eta)alpha), use v's factorization, and then naturality of gamma on eta. The result is exactly gamma_T q(F(eta)nu), with all functors and types retained. This needs two distinct universal lifts plus the naturality calculation. It does not assume a cochain adjunction, invertible beta or invertible gamma. For strict towers, ordinary limit projections identify F(eta)nu with ell on the nose; naturality in the differential makes ell a cochain map. If the derived constant/limit adjunction is used, the same factor equations identify c with the adjunct of the lifted augmentation. This preserves the Q1 normalization rather than choosing an arbitrary completion map.
