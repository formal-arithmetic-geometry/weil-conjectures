It proves properness of the full possibly nonreduced P^0.

6. I checked every assertion needed from the component input: extension-of-field compatibility, clopenness, and smooth connected integral separated finite-type reduction. A normalized family on a connected smooth parameter maps into exactly one side of the clopen decomposition, so the equivalence relation generated by pairs of fibres preserves the zero-component condition. Conversely the pullback of the universal family to (P^0_Omega)_red connects any Omega-point to zero using a single allowed parameter variety. Algebraically closed points factor through the reduction. For an arbitrary T, membership at every geometric point is the open-subscheme factorization criterion, which also works for nilpotent T. This proves precisely the specified smooth-parameter algebraic equivalence, rather than replacing it by a definition involving P^0.

7. lem-sc-rational-tangent-cotangent-duality is the general rational-point theorem (not the curve Picard tangent theorem); def-sc-scheme-tangent-space states dual-number scalar and two-parameter addition. Line bundles on Spec of a local ring are trivial by the trivializing-open argument written in the item. Hence the representing functor yields precisely this kernel.
