For each of the eleven steps I opened every cited node and read its statement, then made the following hypothesis and conclusion checks.

1. The two valuation-ring nodes assert that the valuation ring is a DVR with finite residue field, give the unit-times-uniformizer decomposition and finite quotients. These are enough to prove that A is finite Noetherian and killed by ell^n: the extended ell-adic absolute value puts ell in the maximal ideal, so its uniformizer exponent is positive. I wrote that argument rather than asserting that any finite ring is ell-primary. The finite-module/finitely-presented equivalence is the two finite-free-presentation arguments actually written in the step.
2. The finite-type-over-Noetherian and Noetherian-open nodes establish the scheme finiteness needed for the ambient support and generation tools. The local-Noetherian quasi-separatedness node and qcqs permanence have the correct source and target hypotheses. The immersion node supplies local finite type; Noetherian quasi-compactness upgrades it to finite type. Closed subschemes are covered by affine quotients of the Noetherian affine charts; their closed underlying subsets are quasi-compact. Open subschemes are covered by the stated Noetherian-open result. Thus the factorization schemes are finite type over the original field, with no replacement by a perfect or closed field.
3. The Noetherian global-constructibility node says exactly that affine-local and finite global stratification definitions agree, separately on the pro-etale site. Step 1 supplies finite = finitely presented values. The weak-Serre node gives all four closure properties, and the cohomology-in-weak-Serre node gives the bounded triangulated subcategory. Retract closure is proved explicitly using the kernel of 1 minus the idempotent on cohomology. This supplies the direct-summand closure needed later.
4. The exact inverse-image and exact-functor/cohomology nodes give interval preservation. The base-changed-partition node supplies actual scheme fibre products, not merely subsets, and the constructible-preimage node applies because the source is Noetherian, making every inverse image of an open retrocompact. Pulling back the covering trivializations preserves their constant finite module values. I corrected the wrong intersection-node citation here before delivery.
5. The finite-ring-origin node gives an actual bounded constructible etale complex, and preserves the interval on return. `thm-dg-finite-type-constructibility` states the ordinary direct-image result over any field for a finite ring killed by an invertible integer, including nonseparated morphisms; these are exactly the hypotheses from steps 1–2. The direct-image comparison node requires qcqs and bounded below, both checked. I use this sheaf-valued statement, not a finite global Galois cohomological dimension assertion, which would be wrong for some fields in the item.
6. The support-triangle node asserts only the ambient triangle; it does not assume preservation. The open-closed node supplies exact closed direct image and g^* g_* = identity with quasi-compact complementary open. Those hypotheses hold on the Noetherian schemes from step 2. Steps 3–5 consequently prove preservation of closed exceptional inverse image inside the argument.
7. The perfect-duality node is for an arbitrary ringed site, not only a field or a fixed characteristic. It supplies the tensor-Hom formula for a degree-zero locally projective local system. The restriction node is restriction to site objects, which is precisely the trivializing cover used here. The transpose-idempotent calculation proves that the dual is a degree-zero projective local system, exact tensor preserves the same interval, and finite-module tensor on every original stratum remains finite. I do not assert closed-stratum base change for arbitrary derived Hom.
8. The generation node's clause (3) explicitly covers finite commutative rings and Noetherian schemes on either small site, and states generation by locally closed extensions of finite-projective local systems under cones, shifts and summands. It uses the identical ordinary constant-coefficient Tor-amplitude convention as this item. Its factorization convention is open into the closure followed by closed, and that order is retained. Only K's finite Tor is needed; replacing it by its cohomology would be invalid over a nonregular finite ring.
9. The stratum-ambient-Hom node states the internal sheaf Hom adjunction formula for all complexes, on the pro-etale site with a Noetherian ambient scheme. I apply it twice to a closed and then an open immersion, with identity auxiliary factors. The closed direct-image exactness is in the cited ringed open-closed statement. The target of each inverse image and direct image is checked in the order of the displayed formula. Steps 4–7 therefore give precisely the bounded-constructible conclusion for each generator, without commuting closed restriction with Hom.
10. The Hom exact-restriction node explicitly includes contravariant triangle preservation and the signed shift rule. Functoriality preserves a split retraction, and step 3 proves the needed retract closure of the output class. Thus the subcategory containing the generators meets every closure condition of the cited generation theorem and contains K. M's Tor hypothesis is retained even though bounded constructibility of M already suffices here.
11. The finite-ring-origin node applies once more to the output, giving the requested etale origin as an additional conclusion rather than presupposing it. Its ring is the same finite A and its interval is the one established in step 10.
