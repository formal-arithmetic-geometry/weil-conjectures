## STATEMENT-DISPUTED: discarded draft lemma

The proposed `lem-per-exceptional-basechange-mate` was false as written and is excluded. Take an algebraically closed field k of characteristic different from ell, f the closed immersion of 0 into A^1_k, g=f, and M=Lambda on A^1. The asserted formula would identify Lambda(-1)[-2] with Lambda on the point. The former is the ordinary closed-point purity costalk; their nonzero cohomology degrees differ. Compact-support base change still holds. Taking a right adjoint of that isomorphism instead gives the comparison involving direct images, not the asserted pullback formula. This is an error in the earlier uncommitted draft, not a dispute of the imported item. Its false proof route has been repaired, rather than used as a new assumption.
