## Proposed arguments for the new statement-only nodes

`lem-pe-profinite-sections-finite-quotient`: injectivity and zero composite are pointwise. A continuous function h:S→T1 in the kernel factors continuously through the subspace ker(g), and composing with the assumed homeomorphism inverse gives its unique continuous preimage in T0. For a continuous k:S→T2, choose one preimage in T1 of each element of the finite discrete T2. The resulting set section is continuous because its domain is discrete; its composite with k is a continuous lift. Pointwise operations make the two maps C(S,A)-linear and the construction commutes with restriction. The section sequences are therefore short exact. Kernels of abelian sheaves are sectionwise and sheafification preserves cokernels by `lem-weil-pbc-sheafification-exact`. For internal modules one can use `def-weil-pbc-internal-abelian-sheaf-module-equivalence` (underlying sheaves unchanged) and `def-weil-sheaf-of-modules-category` (kernels sectionwise and cokernels sheafified); the needed smallness and monoidal sheafification on the profinite site are furnished by `lem-weil-pbc-sheaf-tensor-closed`. This proves the condensed conclusion, rather than assuming epimorphisms of arbitrary sheaves are sectionwise surjective.

`lem-pe-scalar-cone-tensor-model`: the tensor unit is flat since tensor by it is isomorphic to the identity. Its cone under b is K-flat (the unit and closure under cone, as in the complete source proof `kFlat_scalarCone`); this is independent of b being regular. In the tensor order P_b⊗C the terms are C^q⊕C^{q+1} and the differential is (x,y)↦(d x+b y,−d y), exactly the chosen cone. Braiding interchanges C⊗P_b and P_b⊗C with the Koszul sign (−1)^{pq}; substituting into the total differential verifies the chain identity. On P_b⊗C the cone identification is the identity after unit identifications, so it commutes with every chain map. This is precisely the source's `tensorScalarConeIso'` and its naturality theorem. The K-flat comparison makes Q(C)⊗^L Q(P_b)→Q(C⊗P_b) invertible; composing gives the displayed natural derived comparison (`isIso_μ_scalarCone`, `tensorScalarConeIso` and its naturality theorem). The left side factors through localization in C. Consequently the naturally isomorphic right-side functor also inverts quasi-isomorphisms; the localization universal property descends the functor and its natural isomorphism. This fixes a functorial cone model, rather than choosing unrelated cones in a triangulated category.

Both arguments are individually well below 3000 characters. They are report material only; no proof steps have been inserted into the new unsplit nodes.
