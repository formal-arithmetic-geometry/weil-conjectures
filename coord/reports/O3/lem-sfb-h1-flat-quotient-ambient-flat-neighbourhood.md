## New lemma's mathematical argument (for its later worker)

Read divisors.tex Tag 062U, label lemma-flat-at-x. For every finitely generated ideal J of A, compare the exact sequence 0 -> C --t--> C -> C/tC -> 0 with its tensor product over A with J. Flatness of C/tC makes the lower row exact, including its leftmost injection, and makes the right vertical multiplication map (C/tC) tensor J -> C/tC injective. A diagram chase shows tK=K for K=ker(C tensor_A J -> C). The module C tensor_A J is finite over C because J is finite over A; K is finite because C is Noetherian. Since t belongs to the maximal ideal, Nakayama gives K=0. The finitely generated ideal criterion then proves A-flatness of C. The proof never requires A to be local or Noetherian, which justifies the stated algebraic generality of the new lemma. The node itself contains no proof steps or proof dependencies.
