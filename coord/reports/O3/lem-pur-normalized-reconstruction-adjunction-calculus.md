### Former step 1 → lem-pur-normalized-actual-reconstruction-maps

For each scheme W, use the specified reduction/total-limit adjunction $\mathcal R_W\dashv\mathcal L_W$ of [lem-coef-derived-system-evaluation-calculus]. On a flat-term K-flat representative $Q$ of an ambient complex, the component of $\mathcal R_W$ is $Q\otimes_{\widehat R_W}B_{n,W}$; associativity of scalar extension identifies its further reduction from level $m$ to level $n$ with $Q\otimes_{\widehat R_W}B_{n,W}$. Thus $\mathcal R_W$ lands in $\mathscr N_W$, and its unit and counit are the specified maps of the evaluation-calculus statement.

### Former step 2 → lem-pur-normalized-actual-reconstruction-maps

Since evaluations detect isomorphisms in [lem-coef-derived-system-evaluation-calculus], $e_N:\mathcal R_W\mathcal L_WN\to N$ is a natural isomorphism.

### Former step 5 → lem-pur-adjoint-equivalence-conjugation-calculus

Given $F\dashv G$ with counit $\epsilon$, compose the three adjunction bijections for $\mathcal L_X\dashv\mathcal R_X$, $F\dashv G$, and $\mathcal R_Y\dashv\mathcal L_Y$. This proves $\bar F=\mathcal L_YF\mathcal R_X\dashv\bar G=\mathcal L_XG\mathcal R_Y$. Tracing the identity of $\bar G K$ through these bijections gives the counit at $K$ as
$$\bar\epsilon_K=u_K^{-1}\circ\mathcal L_Y(\epsilon_{\mathcal R_YK})\circ\mathcal L_YF(e_{G\mathcal R_YK}).$$
Here the last comparison is the counit $e$ of $\mathcal R_X\dashv\mathcal L_X$, and the first comparison is the inverse of the unit $u_K$ of $\mathcal R_Y\dashv\mathcal L_Y$.
