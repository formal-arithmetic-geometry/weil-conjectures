## Statement corrected

STATEMENT-DISPUTED: the imported higher-coefficient recipe applied K tensor^L_{O_E} A_{E,m} directly to K with rational or algebraically closed coefficients. The rational category in def-weil-rationalization-karoubi is a scalar localization; it has integral object representatives but no functor back to finite torsion coefficients. Even on a point, the ordinary derived tensor E tensor^L_{O_E} O_E/(varpi^m) is zero, since varpi is invertible on E; the identity exchange of a nonzero rational constant sheaf cannot be recovered from those zero objects. The corrected definition first reduces an integral representative, forms the integral map, then scalar-localizes that map, and finally takes its common-stage class. This agrees with the source's coefficient-by-coefficient construction and does not restrict the objects or change the mate or compactification formula. No Karoubi completion is used, matching the current coefficient nodes.

The original identification with def-exchange-projection also needed qualification. For X_0=Spec(k_0), its geometric fiber Spec(k) is not finite type over k_0: an algebraically closed algebraic extension of a finite field is infinite, whereas a field finite type and algebraic over k_0 is finite. Moreover g_s=Spec(gamma) is generally not k-linear. Hence there is no immediate application of that node's finite-type-over-S setting at S=Spec(k_0) or S=Spec(k). The definition now takes the explicitly typed ambient ringed mate and open-square exchange as its construction, and cites def-exchange-projection for precisely its compactification recipe. It makes no assertion of a new general base-change theorem.

Finally, comparison of underlying compact-support functors does not alone state that their exchange transformations are intertwined. I retained the compactification c as data, wrote the conjugation by C_{d,c} explicitly for a previously chosen model d, and left refinement compatibility as the separate property it is in the source (def:compact-support-exchange). The finite compactification-independent notation is used only on the etale-origin domain already declared by def-compact-support-direct-image-detailed and prop-compactification-coherence-detailed; the relative Rp_*j_! construction remains available in the ambient category. These repairs correct missing construction data rather than weaken a source theorem; this item is a definition and has no invertibility assertion.
