### lem-coef-closed-scalar-domains

Returned step 1 (1751 characters); source: literature/blueprint/master/blueprint/sec-six-functors/sk-prop-sf-biduality-general/lem-coef-finite-closed-shriek.md, Proof, paragraph 3: finite-Tor domains; explicit scalar-extension argument.

Derived change of finite coefficients preserves the domain used here. Indeed [lem-sf-proetale-constructible-origin] and [lem-sf-comparison-tor-amplitude] descend any $K\in D^b_{ctf}(T,A)$, for a separated finite-type $k$-scheme $T$, to a bounded constructible etale $K'$ with the same Tor interval $[a,b]$, where $a\leq b$ is part of the finite-Tor definition (for the zero object one may take $a=b=0$). The schemes in question are Noetherian by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian]: the base $\operatorname{Spec}k$ is Noetherian since its coordinate ring is a field and its underlying space is a single point. By [lem-sf-etale-flat-constructible], choose an etale model $P\cong K'$ with flat constructible terms, zero outside $[a,b]$. The complexes $P$ and $P\otimes_A B$ are K-flat by clause (1) of [lem-kun-kflat-criteria]; [lem-pur-kflat-change-of-rings] gives flat $B$-module terms for the latter, and [lem-pur-derived-change-of-rings] identifies it with $K'_B$. On a stratum and an etale covering trivializing a term of $P$, its tensor with $B$ is constant with value $F\otimes_A B$, a finite $B$-module because both $F$ and $B$ are finite. Thus the new terms are constructible; [thm-constructible-serre] makes their cohomology constructible. The reverse implication of [lem-sf-etale-flat-constructible] gives Tor interval $[a,b]$. The tensor comparison of [lem-weil-pbc-ringed-topoi-derived-tensor-pullback], applied to $\nu_T$ with constant $A$ coefficients and the constant module $B$, identifies $\nu_T^*(K'_B)$ with $K_B$, respecting the $B$ action. Finally [lem-sf-comparison-tor-amplitude] transports this Tor interval and constructibility to the pro-etale site. This argument assumes no finite projective dimension of $B$ over $A$.

### lem-coef-localization-first-transpose

Returned step 5 (1162 characters); source: literature/blueprint/master/blueprint/sec-six-functors/sk-prop-sf-biduality-general/lem-coef-finite-closed-shriek.md, Proof, paragraph 2: identify the first comparison and reflect its isomorphism.

Rotate the two distinguished triangles of step 3 so that their first two vertices are the middle and third vertices. The commutative square from step 4 and axiom TR3 of [def-weil-triangulated-category] extend $(1_{M_B},d_j)$ to a morphism of the rotated triangles; unrotate it. Thus there is a first component $a:(i_{*,A}N)_B\to i_{*,B}Ri_B^!M_B$ satisfying $\varepsilon_B a=(\varepsilon_A)_B$.
