## Source error

The imported Proof (Only if) inferred pseudo-coherence of C from 0-pseudo-coherence of its cohomology. [064T](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/064T) distinguishes finite generation from full pseudo-coherence, while [066B](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/066B) needs the stronger hypothesis in its pseudo-coherent clause. Step 2 instead cites the existing Noetherian criterion [066E](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/066E), after step 1 verifies its exact hypotheses. This is a repair of the proof, not a dispute of the equivalence.

The imported counterexample paragraph claimed an infinite resolution using multiplication by pi in every degree for every n. For E = Q_l and n = 3, two successive maps multiply by l squared, nonzero modulo l cubed: the displayed sequence is not even a complex. For n = 1 the coefficient ring is a field and its residue module is free, so the asserted positive Tor groups are zero. For n > 1 the intended resolution alternates multiplication by pi and pi^(n-1); tensoring it with the residue field gives zero differentials. The especially simple valid example is Z/l^2 and its residue field. This explanatory paragraph is removed from the proof because the theorem needs neither a counterexample nor this additional resolution assertion. The theorem still includes n = 1.

There is also a notation collision in the inherited definitions: def-weil-point-finite-cohomology-complexes defines D_fin by bounded finite cohomology, while def-trf-categorical-trace uses the same symbol for perfect complexes. The item's conclusion already explicitly selects def-trf-categorical-trace, and we preserve that selection. They cannot be silently identified: over Z/l^2 the residue module belongs to the former category but not the latter. The report flags the definitions for the maintainer; neither other node is edited.
