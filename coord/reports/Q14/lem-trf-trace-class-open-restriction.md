### Returned step 1

Write $j=j_W$, $i=i_F$, $i_W:F_W\to W$ and $d:F\to X$ for the other fixed-square projection of [def-trf-fixed-scheme]; put $d_W=dj'$. By [def-trf-fixed-scheme], a map from a test scheme $T$ to $\operatorname{Fix}(cj)$ is a pair $(w:T\to W,x:T\to X)$ satisfying $cjw=\Delta_Xx$. The same pair specifies a map to $W\times_C F$. Thus $F_W=W\times_C F=\operatorname{Fix}(cj)$, with $ji_W=ij'$ and $ci=\Delta_Xd$; the two Cartesian squares paste to the fixed square for $cj$. The pullback of the open immersion $j$ is open by [lem-weil-pbc-open-immersion-base-change], so $j'$ is an open immersion, retaining the scheme structure, including nilpotents.

Here all schemes and maps remain in the finite-type separated setting. For $Z=X\times_sX$, products of affine charts have coordinate rings $B\otimes_k B'$, generated by the finite generating sets of $B$ and $B'$; its diagonal is the product of the closed diagonals of the factors. Thus $Z$ is separated of finite type over $k$. Next, $C$ is Noetherian by [lem-weil-pbc-finite-type-over-noetherian] (a field is Noetherian), and its open $W$ is Noetherian, hence quasi-compact, by [lem-weil-pbc-noetherian-open-subscheme-noetherian]. Cover $C$ by finitely many affine opens $\operatorname{Spec} B$ of finite type over $k$. Principal opens $D(f)$ in these charts contained in $W$ cover $W$; choose a finite subcover. Each has ring $B_f=B[t]/(ft-1)$, so $W$ is of finite type over $k$. The map $i$ is a closed immersion: it is the base change of the closed diagonal of separated $X$, by [lem-weil-pbc-closed-immersion-base-change]. On each affine chart of $C$, its source is $\operatorname{Spec}(B/I)$, so $F$ is of finite type; the same open argument gives this for $F_W$. Separatedness of these subschemes is inherited by restricting the closed diagonals. Every $k$-map between the separated finite-type schemes just displayed is separated of finite type by [lemma-nagata-finite-type-to-separated-target] for finite type, and by restricting the source's closed diagonal for separatedness. The statement of [def-trf-admissibility] supplies the constructible exceptional adjunctions for all these maps. We use only $D^b_{ctf}(-,A)$ throughout. In particular [prop-functor-package], including its finite-stage preservation and clause (6) in relative dimension zero, gives $Rj^!\simeq Lj^*$ and $Rj'^!\simeq Lj'^*$, with the specified normalized open comparisons.

### Returned step 2

Put $Z=X\times_sX$, $P=K\boxtimes_A^L D_XK$, $H_c=R\mathcal Hom_A(Lc_2^*K,Rc_1^!K)$, and let $\widetilde u:A_C\to H_c$ and $\widetilde u_W:A_W\to H_{cj}$ be the mates of [def-trf-trace-class-source]. Apply [lem-trf-exceptional-internal-hom-exchange] to $j$, $M=Lc_2^*K$ and $N=Rc_1^!K$, which belong to $D^b_{ctf}(C,A)$ by [prop-functor-package]. With the composition isomorphisms of [def-trf-admissibility] it gives $E_j:H_{cj}\xrightarrow{\sim}Rj^!H_c$. Let $a:A_W\xrightarrow{\sim}Rj^!A_C$ be the constant-pullback comparison followed by open purity. Then
$$E_j\widetilde u_W=Rj^!(\widetilde u)\,a.\tag{1}$$
To check the particular morphism in (1), use the defining tensor–Hom transpose and exceptional adjunction. For a test object $T\in D^b_{ctf}(W,A)$ the comparison is represented by
$$
\operatorname{Hom}_W(T,H_{cj})
=\operatorname{Hom}_W(T\otimes^L Lj^*M,Rj^!N)
=\operatorname{Hom}_C(Rj_!(T\otimes^LLj^*M),N)
=\operatorname{Hom}_C(Rj_!T\otimes^LM,N)
=\operatorname{Hom}_C(Rj_!T,H_c)
=\operatorname{Hom}_W(T,Rj^!H_c).
$$
The middle equality is clause (5) of [prop-functor-package]. For $T=A_W$ and $u|_W=Lj^*u$ through the stated identifications, its image in $\operatorname{Hom}_C(Rj_!A_W,H_c)$ is $\widetilde u\circ\varepsilon_j$, where $\varepsilon_j:Rj_!A_W\to A_C$ is the open counit. Transposing back gives (1), by naturality and the triangle identity. This checks the actual restricted coefficient map rather than choosing an arbitrary internal-Hom isomorphism.

Let $\xi_c:H_c\xrightarrow{\sim}Rc^!P$ and $\xi_{cj}:H_{cj}\xrightarrow{\sim}R(cj)^!P$ be [thm-trf-verdier-duality]. Under $R(cj)^!=Rj^!Rc^!$,
$$\xi_{cj}=Rj^!\xi_c\circ E_j.\tag{2}$$
Indeed the exceptional tensor–Hom transposes are those just displayed, now with $M=Lp_2^*K$ and $N=Rp_1^!K$ on $Z$. For the composite $cj$, the successive Hom bijections through $Rj_!$ and $Rc_!$ equal the single bijection through $R(cj)_!$, because the composite exceptional adjunction is the one specified in [def-trf-admissibility]. The two projection-formula identifications agree by the composition/projection coherence of [fix-prop-coherence]. Thus both sides are the same tensor–Hom transpose. Finally both apply the same inverse external-duality map on $Z$, as stipulated by [thm-trf-verdier-duality]. This proves (2), with no extra symmetry permutation or sign.

### Returned step 3

Use the objectwise trace map specified in the statement of [lem-trf-trace-map-from-external-duality]:
$$\varphi_c=Ri_*\gamma_d\circ\beta_c\circ Rc^!e_K\circ\xi_c:H_c\to Ri_*\omega_F,$$
where $e_K:P\to R\Delta_*\omega_X$ depends only on $X,K$, $\beta_c:Rc^!R\Delta_*\to Ri_*Rd^!$ is fixed-square dual base change, and $\gamma_d:Rd^!\omega_X\to\omega_F$ is dualizing composition. Thus the same $e_K$ occurs for $cj$.

For the Cartesian restriction square of Step 1, [thm-sf-dual-base-change] gives
$$\beta_j:Rj^!Ri_*\xrightarrow{\sim}Ri_{W*}Rj'^!.$$
Its vertical-pasting clause, with vertical maps $j,c$ and bottom horizontal map $\Delta$, says explicitly
$$\beta_{cj}=(Ri_{W*}\operatorname{comp}_{j',d})\circ(\beta_j Rd^!)\circ(Rj^!\beta_c),\tag{3}$$
under the compositors $R(cj)^!=Rj^!Rc^!$ and $R(dj')^!=Rj'^!Rd^!$. These are precisely the fixed-square exchanges appearing in the trace maps. The arrow $Bc$ of [lem-trf-trace-map-from-external-duality] is, by that statement, the right-adjoint form in clause (2) of [thm-trf-fixed-point-base-change], which that clause takes from the exchange of [def-trf-admissibility]: the conjugate $Ri_*Rd^!\xrightarrow{\sim}Rc^!R\Delta_*$ of the compact-support exchange $L\Delta^*Rc_!\xrightarrow{\sim}Rd_!Li^*$, read in the inverse direction. By [thm-sf-dual-base-change] the fixed-square $\beta_c$ is the inverse of that same conjugate, so $Bc=\beta_c$, and likewise $B(cj)=\beta_{cj}$. Functoriality of dualizing composition in [thm-trf-dualizing-complex-composition] gives
$$\gamma_{dj'}\operatorname{comp}_{j',d}=\gamma_{j'}\circ Rj'^!\gamma_d.\tag{4}$$
Insert (2), (3), and (4) into the formula for $\varphi_{cj}$. Naturality of $\beta_j$ moves $Rj^!Ri_*\gamma_d$ across $\beta_j$, giving the fully typed identity
$$
\varphi_{cj}
=Ri_{W*}\gamma_{j'}\circ\beta_{j,\omega_F}\circ Rj^!\varphi_c\circ E_j
:H_{cj}\longrightarrow Ri_{W*}\omega_{F_W}.\tag{5}
$$
Here $\gamma_{j'}:Rj'^!\omega_F\xrightarrow{\sim}\omega_{F_W}$ is exactly the dualizing-complex comparison used in the statement's restricted section. Formula (5) proves the compatibility of the objectwise trace construction, by pasting its individual arrows.

### Returned step 4

By [def-trf-trace-class] and the displayed formula for $\mathfrak t_{c,K}$ in [lem-trf-trace-map-from-external-duality], the trace section $\sigma=\operatorname{Tr}_c(u)$ is the ordinary $Li^*\dashv Ri_*$ adjoint of $v=\varphi_c\widetilde u:A_C\to Ri_*\omega_F$. Indeed the global-sections adjunctions used in that definition are natural, and its direct-image compositor identifies the global section of $\varphi_c\widetilde u$ with the same $i$-adjoint section on $F$. The analogous description holds for $cj$.

Combining (1) and (5) therefore gives
$$
\varphi_{cj}\widetilde u_W
=Ri_{W*}\gamma_{j'}\circ\beta_{j,\omega_F}\circ Rj^!v\circ a.\tag{6}
$$
We now compute the $i_W$-adjoint of (6), including the normalization of $\beta_j$. Use the normalized open-purity identifications $Rj^!=Lj^*$ and $Rj'^!=Lj'^*$ of [prop-functor-package], so $a$ is the constant-pullback identification. Write
$$b_T:Li^*Rj_!T\xrightarrow{\sim}Rj'_!Li_W^*T$$
for compact-support base change in the restriction square, and $\nu'_Q:Q\xrightarrow{\sim}Lj'^*Rj'_!Q$ for the open adjunction unit. By the inverse-conjugate assertion of [thm-sf-dual-base-change], for $T\in D^b_{ctf}(W,A)$ and $S\in D^b_{ctf}(F,A)$ the action of $\beta_{j,S}$ on Hom is the following specific chain:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Hom}_W(T,Lj^*Ri_*S)
&\cong\operatorname{Hom}_C(Rj_!T,Ri_*S)\\
&\cong\operatorname{Hom}_F(Li^*Rj_!T,S)\\
&\xrightarrow{\;(-)\circ b_T^{-1}\;}
\operatorname{Hom}_F(Rj'_!Li_W^*T,S)\\
&\cong\operatorname{Hom}_{F_W}(Li_W^*T,Lj'^*S)\\
&\cong\operatorname{Hom}_W(T,Ri_{W*}Lj'^*S).
\end{aligned}
$$
The first two and last two arrows are the displayed ordinary and open exceptional adjunctions, with their composite normalization from [def-trf-admissibility]; the composition and square comparisons are those of [fix-prop-coherence]. The generic tool [lem-sf-open-tensor-normalization], applied with its open immersion equal to $j$ and its base-change map equal to $i$, identifies $b_T$ with exactly the normalized extension-by-zero base change. Its assertion about the inverse adjunct therefore specifies the middle arrow, including its open-unit normalization.

Take $T=A_W$, $S=\omega_F$, and write $v=Ri_*\sigma\circ\eta_i$, where $\eta_i:A_C\to Ri_*Li^*A_C=Ri_*A_F$ is the ordinary adjunction unit. The input $Lj^*v$ maps, after the first two arrows, to $\sigma\circ Li^*\varepsilon_j$, where $\varepsilon_j:Rj_!A_W\to A_C$ is the open counit; this follows by naturality of $Li^*\dashv Ri_*$ and its triangle identity. After the middle arrow and the $j'$-adjunction its image is exactly
$$
Lj'^*\sigma\circ Lj'^*Li^*\varepsilon_j\circ Lj'^*(b_{A_W}^{-1})\circ\nu'_{A_{F_W}}.
$$
The last three arrows compose to $1_{A_{F_W}}$: under the specified pullback comparisons, the open-unit normalization of $b$ identifies $Lj'^*(b_{A_W}^{-1})\circ\nu'_{A_{F_W}}$ with $Li_W^*\nu_{A_W}$, where $\nu_{A_W}:A_W\to Lj^*Rj_!A_W$ is the open unit. The remaining composite is $Li_W^*(Lj^*\varepsilon_j\circ\nu_{A_W})=1$ by the open adjunction triangle identity. Thus the $i_W$-adjoint is $Lj'^*\sigma$ before applying $\gamma_{j'}$. Naturality of $Li_W^*\dashv Ri_{W*}$ with respect to $\gamma_{j'}$ gives the $i_W$-adjoint of (6) as

$$
A_{F_W}\simeq Lj'^*A_F\xrightarrow{Lj'^*\sigma}Lj'^*\omega_F
\simeq Rj'^!\omega_F\xrightarrow{\gamma_{j'}}\omega_{F_W}.
$$
This is exactly $j'^*\operatorname{Tr}_c(u)$ as defined in the statement. The left side is $\operatorname{Tr}_{cj}(u|_W)$ by the first paragraph, proving the assertion. If $F_W$ is also closed in $F$, the very same composite is the restricted section of [def-trf-local-term]; this last clause is equality of the defining maps and introduces no integration or properness assumption.
