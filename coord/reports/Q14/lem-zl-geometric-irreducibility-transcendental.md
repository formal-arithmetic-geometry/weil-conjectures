## Review by Q14

I reviewed the written JSON with Tag 0G31 open, then checked every cited node's statement, not its proof or title. Both implications retain arbitrary field extensions, arbitrary characteristic and no finite-generation, perfectness, separability or reducedness condition. The source proves the one-variable equivalence; the item also asks for finitely many variables, and step 5 supplies an induction with its claim, base and step stated explicitly. I kept `proof_origin.by = agent` to make the supplied omitted details explicit, while recording the Stacks document and the precise route followed.

1. Step 1 uses `def-pc-geometrically-irreducible` for the scheme property and `lem-weil-pbc-fibre-product-affine-spectra` for the actual spectrum of the tensor ring. The latter states the fibre product for arbitrary commutative algebras, so no finite-type hypothesis enters. The rational-function and algebraic-independence definitions justify interpreting a transcendental element as the formal variable. A definition alone is not cited for the affine base-change theorem.
2. Step 2 uses the definitions of polynomials, rational functions and localization, together with `lem-weil-pbc-algebra-tensor-base-change-universal-property`. The latter's statement gives the algebra-homomorphism bijection and its pure-tensor formula, exactly what is needed to construct the displayed maps. The inverse on a fraction is written coefficient by coefficient; the tensor and fraction relations show the two composites are identities. This short explicit calculation is marked elementary. It makes no claim that $K\otimes_k k(t)$ is already $K(t)$.
3. Step 3 uses the geometric property and the affine base-change formula to get irreducibility of $A=K\otimes_k F$. The map to which `lem-weil-pbc-localization-base-change` applies is $R\to K(t)$ with base algebra $A$, not $F$: a map from $K$ into $F$ has not been assumed. Its statement gives a localization after this base change. The tensor universal-property node permits the two displayed mutually inverse maps to $B$. The statement of `lem-pur-field-vector-space-tensor-nontrivial` applies over $k(t)$, because both factors are fields and therefore nonzero vector spaces. It gives nonzero $B$ even when the tensor has nilpotents.
4. Step 4 takes the unique minimal prime of $A$ from `lem-weil-pbc-spec-irreducible-components-minimal-primes`, using that the whole irreducible space is its only maximal irreducible subset. `lem-weil-pbc-minimal-prime-over-proper-ideal` applies to $(0)\subsetneq B$ because step 3 proved $B\ne0$. `lem-weil-pbc-prime-contains-minimal-prime` shows every prime of $A$ contains its unique minimal prime. `lem-weil-pbc-localization-spectrum-embedding` explicitly gives both the image consisting of primes avoiding the denominator set and the subspace topology. Thus this image contains the generic prime. The closed-cover argument in the step proves irreducibility from that containment directly, using `def-weil-irreducible-components`; there is no uncited theorem that arbitrary nonempty subspaces preserve irreducibility.
5. Step 5 uses the algebraic-independence definition and rational-function description. The denominator-clearing argument proves transcendence of the next variable over the previously adjoined field; a nonzero relation remains nonzero because that previous polynomial ring injects by algebraic independence. It then applies the forward implication already proved in this same proof. No new lemma stating the item's own finite-variable clause is introduced.
6. Step 6 repeats the explicit tensor/fraction construction from step 2 for $k'/k$. Its cited field-extension definition includes injectivity of the coefficient map; the polynomial definition makes that injectivity coefficientwise. In a polynomial ring over the field $k'$, the product of nonzero polynomials has nonzero product of leading coefficients. Hence its fraction field detects zero numerators, and the localization definition gives injectivity of $E\to k'(t)$. This is a short direct calculation, not an appeal to an unstated localization theorem or an identification of $E$ with the full rational function field.
7. Both are fields, so the flatness hypotheses hold. The algebra tensor universal-property node is used for the displayed reassociation maps; balancing shows they are inverse. This proves exactly the map omitted in the Stacks proof, with its action on $a\otimes b$ recorded, and does not require linear disjointness or separability.
