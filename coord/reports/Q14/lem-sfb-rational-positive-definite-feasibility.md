For rational-linear-system-parametrization the intended argument is ordinary elimination of the augmented rational matrix. Row swaps, adding a rational multiple of one row to another, and dividing a row by a nonzero rational pivot have rational coefficients and invertible rational inverses. Each such operation gives equivalent equations over both Q and R. Process finitely many columns, using a nonzero entry as pivot where possible and eliminating that column in other rows, to obtain row-reduced equations. A row with zero variable coefficients and nonzero constant would contradict the given real solution, so none occurs. The pivot variables are rational affine expressions in the nonpivot variables. Set all free variables to zero for x0, and for each free variable set it to one and the others to zero in the homogeneous equations for v_j. The homogeneous directions have Av_j=0. Every real solution has its own real free coordinates t_j and is x0+sum t_j v_j, because substitution recovers every pivot coordinate. The construction also works when no equation or no variable is present. This auxiliary has no positivity hypothesis and concludes no strict feasibility; it is strictly a linear-equation input, not a renaming of a clause of the item.

For positive-definite-entrywise-neighbourhood, if n=0 take epsilon=1. If n>0, the continuous quadratic polynomial z ↦ z^T G z has a minimum c on the compact Euclidean unit sphere. Every vector on that sphere is nonzero, so the attained minimum satisfies c>0. For any z, homogeneity after division by its Euclidean norm gives z^T G z >= c sum z_a^2 (z=0 separately). Take epsilon=c/(2 n^2)>0. For D=H-G with every |D_ab|<epsilon, the triangle inequality and |z_a| <= ||z||_2 show |z^T D z| <= epsilon n^2 ||z||_2^2. Thus z^T H z >= (c/2)||z||_2^2>0 for z nonzero. The statement explicitly requires H symmetric: there is no mistaken claim that real symmetric positive definite matrices are open in the entire ambient space of all matrices. Compactness, minimum attainment, and the uniform coercivity argument are the substantive content of this unsplit node, not hidden assumptions in the item's proof.

For real-rational-interval-density I read exists_rat_btwn directly in Mathlib/Algebra/Order/Archimedean/Basic.lean:371–374 and its Archimedean ordered-field context.
