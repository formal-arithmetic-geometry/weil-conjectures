## Argument retained for the new local algebra node

For a flat local Noetherian-domain map A -> B with normal A and integral nonempty fibres, faithful flatness makes A inject into B and contracts extended ideals. The fibre assumption and flatness imply pB is prime for each prime p of A: B/pB is flat, hence torsion free over A/p, and embeds in its integral generic fibre. Write a generic unit as f=a/r, and its inverse as a'/r', with a,a' in B and nonzero r,r' in A. Thus aa'=rr'. For the finitely many height-one primes p_i minimal over rr', A_(p_i) is a DVR; p_i B is prime and its localized maximal ideal is generated by a uniformizer, so B_(p_i B) is a DVR with ramification index one. Let e_i,e'_i be the orders of a,a' there. Their sums are the orders of rr'. Take the symbolic-power intersections I=intersection p_i^(e_i) and I'=intersection p_i^(e'_i). The normal-domain height-one intersection theorem identifies the intersection with summed exponents with (rr'). Flat extension commutes with these finite intersections and these symbolic powers. Consequently a belongs to IB and a' to I'B; aa'=rr' and II' contained in (rr') force II'B=(rr')B, hence II'=(rr') by faithful flatness. The product-principal ideal lemma makes I and I' invertible. Since A is local, I=(g), I'=(g'), and gg' differs from rr' by a unit. Writing a=ug and a'=u'g' in B gives that u,u' are units. Therefore f=(g/r)u as required. This is the affine paragraph of 0BD7 with its letters renamed. For uniqueness, if h=a/b in K is a unit of B, faithful flatness gives a in bB intersect A=bA, and the same argument for h inverse gives h,h inverse in A. Thus h is a unit of A. The new node does not assert divisor descent or group-scheme normality.
