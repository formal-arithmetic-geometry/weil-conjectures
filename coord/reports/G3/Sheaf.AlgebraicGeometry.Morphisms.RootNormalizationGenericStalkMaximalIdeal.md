## Where the brief, the DAG or earlier work was wrong

The earlier reverse-inclusion theorem contained exactly the nonautomatic content of
the original equality. It was correctly rejected. This delivery instead proves the
root-prime geometry independently, and uses a first-order local calculation plus
finite generation and Nakayama. The remaining lemma is uniform over points at the
root prime and does not include D, j, eta, their regularity/integrality, or their support
and generic-point assumptions. It is still a substantial tame ramification item;
no claim is made to have formalized Abhyankar here.

The original single DAG dependency, normalization-stalk base change, supplies the
localization comparison but not the Galois degree formula or Abhyankar's lemma.
The new cotangent node records these missing arguments explicitly. Searches found
general localization, generic-point, and Nakayama tools, but no applicable existing
root-torsor tame theorem. LeanSearch returned HTTP Internal Server Error; LeanFinder,
Loogle, and local source grep were used instead. Upper unramified and generic-etale
lemmas were not imported because they depend on the present item.

Checked edge cases: n=1 is included in the proved generic-prime calculation and in
the tame proof plan. The existence of eta or x excludes the relevant empty cases;
a unit g would make H/(g) the zero ring, excluded by IsDomain. Nonreduced or otherwise
irregular H away from (g) is allowed: the local proof uses only H_(g), which is a DVR
because (g) is prime and generated by the regular element g. The source cotangent
lemma uses the exact structural stalk map, with no chosen object or new definition.

Read Stacks Tags 0BRM, 09EB, 0EYG and 09E7 against the remaining proof plan.
These provide respectively tame base-change index one, the Galois degree formula,
the root-divisor construction, and the formally-smooth/index-one criterion.
