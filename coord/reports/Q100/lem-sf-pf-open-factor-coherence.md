### lem-sf-open-roos-unit-adjunction

Original step 1:

First check the hypotheses of the completion and open-extension results. The finite-type-over-Noetherian lemma applied to $\operatorname{Spec}k$ makes all four schemes Noetherian. The locally-Noetherian quasi-separatedness lemma makes them qcqs. Every inverse image of $U$ in an affine open of $X$ is a Noetherian open subscheme and therefore quasi-compact; thus $j$ is quasi-compact, and the same argument applies to $j'$. The coefficient theorem makes $R$ a principal ideal domain with finite residue field. Every ideal is generated by one element, so $R$ is Noetherian. Each $A_n$ is finite by the finite-quotient theorem, and $[R\xrightarrow{t^n}R]$ is a finite free resolution of $A_n$, since $t$ is nonzero in the domain $R$. Thus $(R,(t))$ satisfies the adic coefficient datum. Open slice localization identifies $(A_n)_X|_U=(A_n)_U$ with the constant-section normalization. Restriction preserves limits, so it also identifies $B_X|_U=B_U$, respecting every quotient projection; likewise for $j'$.
