The explicit equality condition proves that it represents precisely the desired compatible pairs. lem-pc-yoneda states the natural Hom/section bijection needed to turn the projections into scheme maps. Step 2 uses the scheme-test definition twice: Q over T tests g and P over Q tests f. The compatible-pair bijection is written out and makes the composite test P over T. Clause (2) of lem-weil-pbc-etale-composition-cancellation states precisely etale composition. Step 3 takes only existence of the space fibre product from lem-trf-space-fibre-products-etale-covers. Its base-change theorem for covering families is not needed. The two displayed inverse compatible-pair maps identify each scheme test with the original test of f; the scheme-test definition then gives both etaleness and surjectivity. This avoids circular reliance on chartwise permanence (the parent item depends on this one).

Step 4 writes an inverse to every test projection for an isomorphism. Choosing its representation by the identity scheme map avoids any hidden transport-of-properties theorem. The definition of open immersion verifies that an identity is open, clause (1) of the composition/cancellation node makes it etale, and the definition of surjectivity makes it surjective.

Testing along the identity proves the converse without descent. lem-weil-pbc-etale-covering-base-change states joint surjectivity as well as etaleness and applies to the singleton family of a surjective etale r. No extension to merely pointwise-surjective natural transformations is used.
