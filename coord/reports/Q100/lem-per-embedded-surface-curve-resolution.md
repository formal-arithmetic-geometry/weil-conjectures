## Source error and STATEMENT-DISPUTED repair

The imported SNC gloss incorrectly requires every reduced component to be a curve (dimension one). The hypotheses do not make X equicodimensional at closed points. For an explicit counterexample, take A=k[x,y], p=(x), q=(x-1,y), S=A\(p union q), R=S^{-1}A, X=Spec R, and Z=V(x). This is an affine integral regular excellent Noetherian scheme of dimension two, so O_X is ample. Its closed point pR has height one, and Z=Spec(R/(x)) is a single reduced point, an effective Cartier divisor of dimension zero. Blowing up that reduced point is the identity because its ideal is invertible; repeated allowed blowups cannot turn this component into a curve. Thus the imported gloss is false. The corrected statement uses regular codimension-one components and the local parameter definition of SNC, as in Stacks 0BIA. It adds no input hypothesis. Steps 3 and 7 explicitly handle the zero-dimensional components. This is a repair of the source's intended SNC statement, not a change of route.

Separately, the last paragraph of the Stacks proof 0BIC says to blow up points where “more than 3” components meet immediately after discussing r>2; three transverse distinct lines through the origin show that leaving triple points is insufficient. Step 6 uses more than two and gives the separation argument. This corrects that proof typo without changing the Stacks theorem.
