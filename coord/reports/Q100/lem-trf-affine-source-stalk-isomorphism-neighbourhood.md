The comparison's future proof is the actual missing middle argument: since T has finitely many components and h has dense image, Y is the finite union of the closures of h(eta). No h(eta) can lie properly below another such generic image, because then f(eta) would be a specialization of a different member of X^0. Thus these closures are exactly Y's components and h gives a bijection on generic points. Given a generic xi of Y generalizing y, lift it to eta in T^0. Its image theta in X is generic and generalizes x. The selected stalk isomorphism lifts theta to a generic eta′ generalizing t. The two generic points have the same image in X, hence eta=eta′ by injectivity. Therefore every generic point through y lifts through t. If a germ in O_Y,y dies in O_T,t, it dies after localizing at every such eta′. The maps from the reduced minimal-prime local rings O_Y,xi (fields) into those source fields are injective, so the germ vanishes at every minimal prime of O_Y,y. Their intersection is its nilradical, zero because Y is reduced. This proves the second stalk map injective; as its composite with the first is bijective, it is also surjective and then the first map is bijective.
