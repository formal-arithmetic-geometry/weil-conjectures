For smooth connected G and N, their product is geometrically reduced and geometrically connected, so the generated subgroup is smooth and connected. Its containment in N comes from normality of N and minimality. Normality of [G,N] comes from conjugation preserving relative commutators: a[g,n]a^{-1}=[aga^{-1},ana^{-1}], with ana^{-1} in N. These statements are scheme-theoretic: one can check the conjugation factorization after an algebraic closure on the geometrically reduced source G times [G,N], then descend it. For inversion stability of the generating map, [g,n]^{-1}=[g^{-1},gng^{-1}]. These are the structural ingredients of the new unsplit node, not hidden extra assumptions about K. Its minimality is explicitly among all closed subgroup schemes K, even nonsmooth ones; this follows from the minimality in 2.46.
