## Statement correction: STATEMENT-DISPUTED

The imported statement says that the divisorial log structure of B is fs log regular, but does not expressly say that its triviality locus equals U=X\B. If the bracketed reference def-trf-log-regular-pair is intended to impose that equality, the statement was merely ambiguous. Under the literal assertion about the log scheme alone it is false.

Counterexample: take X=A^2_F, B={(0,0)}, U=A^2_F\{(0,0)}, and the trivial group. The divisorial log structure O_X intersect j_*O_U^times is O_X^times: normality extends regular functions across the codimension-two complement, and a nonunit on a regular local scheme cannot have its entire zero locus supported only in codimension two. This remains true on etale neighborhoods. Thus this log structure is trivial, fs, log regular and log smooth over F. Its only nonempty coherent log ideal is locally the whole monoid, since every section is a unit. Every saturated log blowup is the identity by its universal property. Consequently the final scheme is still A^2_F with B_n the origin. That codimension-two closed set cannot be the inverse image of a coordinate hyperplane union under a smooth map to A^r_F with all r branches through the point: r=0 gives empty boundary and r>0 gives a divisor.

Correction made explicitly in the statement: the divisorial log structure has **triviality locus exactly U**, hence (X,B) is a log regular pair. This is precisely ILO VI 1.4, printed p.69, and def-trf-log-regular-pair, already among statement dependencies. It is the hypothesis delivered by the parent's marked-nodal-relative-log-charts input, and it makes the final boundary clause meaningful. No perfection or separatedness condition was added. No target statement was changed.
