## Proof arguments for the statement-only inputs

For normalization-jet-codimension: the finite normalization sequence has a finite-length quotient (Stacks varieties.tex, Tag 0C1R, lemma-prepare-delta-invariant; also read the existing reduced-curve-normalization-genus and finite-curve-quotient-stalk-euler statements). Tensor by L^m and take sections: its injection has cokernel of dimension at most delta=length of the fixed quotient. On each smooth normalized component a line bundle of nonnegative degree a has h0 at most a+1: choose a+1 distinct points, and a section vanishing on them would have an effective zero divisor of degree exceeding a. Riemann--Roch gives h0(L_i^m)>=me_i+1-g_i. Thus, with N_i=sum n_q<=me_i, the normalized simultaneous-jet codimension is at least N_i-g_i. Passing from all normalized sections to the subspace of sections descending to D loses at most delta in codimension. Sum over components to obtain C=delta+sum g_i, uniformly in m and the orders, even when N_i=me_i and even for two points on one component. This is a section-space comparison, with no weighted flag or singularity restriction, and is not a renamed clause of the item. Read Stacks curves.tex Tag 0BS6, lemma-rr, and the normalization exact sequence at varieties.tex Tag 0C1R; the complete new statement is the agent's synthesis, rather than a purported verbatim Stacks theorem.

For branch-multiplicity-tangent-criterion: write the completed normalization as a finite product of k[[z_q]], as in the read existing normalized-curve-branch-coordinates statement. The finite normalization defect has bounded length. Comparison of the m_p-adic Hilbert polynomials through a finite module and its finite-length quotient preserves the linear coefficient (the intersection filtration is equivalent up to a bounded shift by Artin--Rees). In each DVR the extended ideal is (z_q^{b_q}), so this coefficient is sum b_q. A general hyperplane through p contains no component and has order exactly b_q at each of the finitely many branches (avoid finitely many proper linear subspaces over the infinite field k). Its effective divisor on C_i has total degree e_i, so sum b_q<=e_i. The coefficient-of-z_q map of affine coordinates is zero exactly when b_q>=2. When all b_q=1 and their sum is two there are precisely two smooth formal branches. With distinct tangent directions, the ideals of the branches in the completed regular ambient local ring have sum the maximal ideal: their cotangent annihilators span, then Nakayama applies. Their union therefore has ring k[[u]] times_k k[[v]], equal to k[[u,v]]/(uv). Conversely that ring has two independent tangent directions, preserved by a closed immersion. If the two directions coincide, a linear form vanishing on their line has zero constant and linear coefficient on each branch, hence order >=2. This local criterion is not a Hilbert-weight bound. Read curves.tex Tags 0C1U--0C1W, the coordinate-axes discussion and multicross-algebra lemma, together with the existing ordinary-point-delta-branches, coordinate-axes-normalization and normalized-curve-branch-coordinates statements.
