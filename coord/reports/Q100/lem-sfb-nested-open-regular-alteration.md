### Original step 1: lem-sfb-cover-nested-cartier-preparation

Put $K=k(T)$. Choose a point of the nonempty finite etale generic fibre $H_K$ and let $F$ be its residue field; $F/K$ is finite. Let $q:X\to T$ be the normalization of $T$ in $F$. By the normalization-in-a-residue-field node, $X$ is normal integral with function field $F$; by finiteness of normalization over a field, $q$ is finite. It is proper by finite-proper, and its image is closed and contains the generic point, hence it is surjective. Over an affine open of $S$, the integral-closure description of normalization is unchanged on localization. Thus $X_S$ is the normalization of $S$ in $\operatorname{Spec}F$. Apply the relative-normalization universal property to $\operatorname{Spec}F\to H\to S$, whose second arrow is integral: this supplies $X_S\to H$ over $S$. This argument uses a field factor of $H_K$, not an unsupported assertion that connected $H$ is integral.

### Original step 2: lem-sfb-cover-nested-cartier-preparation

The scheme $X$ is separated finite type over $k$, hence Noetherian. Set $U=q^{-1}S\subseteq U^\circ=q^{-1}T^\circ$; both are dense quasi-compact opens. Choose finite-type quasi-coherent ideals $\mathcal I,\mathcal J$ with $V(\mathcal I)=X\setminus U$, $V(\mathcal J)=X\setminus U^\circ$. Let $b_1:B_1=\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal I}(X)\to X$, $b_2:B=\operatorname{Bl}_{\mathcal J\mathcal O_{B_1}}(B_1)\to B_1$, $b=b_1b_2$. As $\mathcal J|_U=\mathcal O_U$, $b_2$ is a $b_1^{-1}(U)$-admissible blowup. The boundary-divisor lemma, with its clause on a further admissible blowup, makes $I_B=\mathcal I\mathcal O_B$ effective Cartier with support $B\setminus b^{-1}U$ and $b^{-1}U$ scheme-theoretically dense in $B$; the Cartier-total-transform lemma gives the same Cartier property. Applied to $B_1$, $b_1^{-1}U^\circ$ and $\mathcal J\mathcal O_{B_1}$, it makes $J_B=\mathcal J\mathcal O_B$ effective Cartier with support $B\setminus b^{-1}U^\circ$. The blowup-complement theorem makes $b_1$ an isomorphism over $U$ and $b_2$ one over $B_1\setminus V(\mathcal J\mathcal O_{B_1})\supseteq b_1^{-1}U$, so $b^{-1}U\to U$ is an isomorphism and $b^{-1}U$ is integral with function field $F$. Hence $B$ is integral: it is nonempty, and for a nonempty affine open $V\subseteq B$ density makes $\mathcal O_B(V)\to\mathcal O_B(V\cap b^{-1}U)$ injective, so $V\cap b^{-1}U\neq\varnothing$; sections of the integral $b^{-1}U$ over a nonempty open inject into $F$, because they do on each nonempty affine of a cover (affine-fraction lemma), so $\mathcal O_B(V)$ is a domain. Thus $k(B)=F$ and $b$ is dominant. Blowup properness and proper composition make $b$ proper. Empty complements give unit ideals and identity blowups.

### Original step 4: lem-sfb-alteration-cartier-subboundary

Pull back $I_B,J_B$ to $Y$. Both extended ideals are still effective Cartier: a local generator is nonzero in the function field of $B$, hence remains nonzero in $k(Y)$ by dominance, and every local ring of $Y$ is a domain. Their supports are $Y\setminus h^{-1}S$ and $Y\setminus h^{-1}T^\circ$; the first is exactly $|D|$. To check that the second is a union of whole components, work locally at $y\in Y$. The Gabber boundary has parameter equations $t_1,\ldots,t_r$ there. A generator $f$ of the second ideal becomes a unit off $D$, because its zero set is contained in $|D|$. In $\mathcal O_{Y,y}[1/(t_1\cdots t_r)]$ it is therefore a unit. The regular-local-UFD boundary-factorization node gives $f=v\prod t_i^{n_i}$ with $v$ a unit and $n_i\ge0$. Consequently its support is locally a union of the branches of $D$. At every point of this support at least one branch through the point is selected, and its generic point also lies in the support. Since the support is closed, it contains that whole $D_a$. Conversely every selected component lies in the support. Thus, with $A_{\rm out}$ the components whose generic points lie in it, the support is $\bigcup_{a\in A_{\rm out}}D_a$. This proves both required open identities without asserting normal crossings for any later shrinking.

### Original step 6: lem-sfb-proper-generic-radicial-etale-factorization

Let $K'\subseteq L$ be the separable closure of $K$ in $L$. The separable-first lemma gives $K'/K$ finite separable and $L/K'$ finite purely inseparable. Choose a primitive element $\alpha\in K'$ and write its monic separable minimal polynomial as $g\in K[T]$. Apply the localized separable-polynomial model lemma and replace $A$ by the resulting nonzero principal localization, so $g\in A[T]$ and $g$ is coprime to $g'$. Put $A'=A[T]/(g)$. Division by the monic polynomial shows that $1,T,\ldots,T^{d-1}$ is an $A$-basis, where $d=\deg g=[K':K]\ge1$, and the map $A'\to K'$ sending $T$ to $\alpha$ is injective: a representative of degree less than $d$ which vanishes at $\alpha$ is zero over $K$, hence over the domain $A$. Thus $A'$ is a domain with fraction field $K'$. The Bezout relation makes $g'$ a unit in $A'$, so the standard-etale monic-derivative lemma says $\operatorname{Spec}A'\to\operatorname{Spec}A$ is etale. Its displayed free basis makes it finite and surjective of constant degree $d$.
