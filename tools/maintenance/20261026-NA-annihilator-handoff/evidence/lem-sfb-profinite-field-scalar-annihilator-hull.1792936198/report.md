## Failed constructions and what remains

There is a useful degree-zero inverse-limit description. Choose a decreasing sequence of clopen neighborhoods V_n of the zero set using inverse images of sufficiently small clopen balls around 0 in E. Let M_n=e_{V_n}R_E, a retract of R_E, and use the inclusion M_{n+1}->M_n. On every test Q->P, the inverse limit consists of continuous functions whose support is contained in every inverse image V_n, so it is the actual K_a. This calculation does not identify ordinary inverse limit with its derived inverse limit.

In fact the missing Roos surjectivity is false for this particular tower. Take P=beta N, index N by positive integers, a(k)=ell^k, and V_n=closure({k>=n}). Each V_n is clopen, and their intersection is the boundary. Set y_n=ell^{-n} e_{V_n}, a section of M_n on P. If x_n in M_n on any surjective profinite cover Q->P solved x_n-x_{n+1}=y_n, evaluating over k in N would force

    x_1(q)=sum_{n=1}^k ell^{-n}  whenever q lies over k.

The ell-adic norm of this value is ell^k, by the strict inequality between the norms of the summands. The image is unbounded. But a continuous E-valued function on the compact space Q has bounded image. Every surjective cover meets every fibre over k, so no such cover can repair this obstruction. A finite jointly surjective family has a profinite coproduct with the same argument. Thus 1-shift on the product is not even locally surjective. The cone has a nonzero degree-one obstruction and cannot simply be declared K_a[0].

A second attempted route was the continuous dual of a free resolution of the closed-zero-locus quotient. This would require a relative derived continuous-duality/Ext-vanishing theorem and a genuine presentation of the vanishing ideal by allowed free modules. Neither is in the cited statements. Merely asserting that the continuous E coefficient is injective, or that all solid relative modules are generated by these products, would insert an unproved substantial theorem and might change the ambient category. I did not create such nodes on speculation.
