    leanindex text cache: keyed SQLite store instead of a whole-file pickle; machine-wide slots for check-one
    
    The 1.4 GB pickle was loaded whole by every process (3.5 GB RSS, ~70% of a lander check) and rewritten whole at
    exit (disk full twice on 2026-10-27). Rows are now read per key and inserted per new key (WAL, concurrent-safe).
    textcache.use_shared is a no-op: leanindex reads SHEAF_LEANINDEX_CACHE itself. batch_land check-one takes one of
    SHEAF_CHECK_SLOTS (16) flock slots, so orphaned pools after a lander restart cannot double the checks.
