## Cross-case observations

- Five cases have a controlled change that removes the expensive check while also removing the predicted structural mismatch. In `RealPushforward` and `OrderedSpectralRawLattices`, the control aligns a data or instance representation. In the three helper-application cases, a typed proof argument matches the instantiated expected proposition or removes its specific scheme/domain mismatch.
- The helper-application cases require reconstructing a dependent Pi domain after all preceding actual arguments: argument 26, 49, and 39 respectively. Comparing the original proof with the generic uninstantiated helper binder would miss the conversion being timed.
- Two final-exact cases and the explicit-`change` case have exact first divergent heads but lack a successful one-factor control for the downstream cost. `ImageHomCounit` source-spelling edits leave the elaborated pair unchanged. The integral local-instance edit also leaves its first divergent pair and approximately 85-second cost unchanged. `RealPurity` has a fast alternate local proof, but it changes the proof construction rather than only the ring-instance leaf.
- The first divergent paths are ordinary applications or, in `OrderedSpectralRawLattices`, let-bound values and a later forall body. Source `.app`, `.hom`, and `.left` syntax does not by itself establish an `Expr.proj` trigger. No case isolates recursor computation as the initial cause.
- Reducibility hints and regular heights predict candidate unfoldings; they are not elapsed-time estimates or traces. The counts of routine `Functor.obj`, `Add.add`, `PresheafOfModules.obj`, and module-instance unfolds reported in earlier diagnostics describe work after the conversion begins. They do not identify the first Kernel decision.
- The current checked theorem in `RealPurity` is fast, while its historical `After.lean` still times out on this toolchain. The corpus does not attribute that speed to the historical edit. All rows distinguish complete proofs from checked local applications or proofs followed by diagnostic `sorry`.

## Interpretation and remaining questions

The confirmed pattern is a mismatch inside a high-level pair whose printed mathematical form conceals a different stored polynomial, site, scheme, or typeclass path. The strongest causal evidence comes from controls that preserve the obligation and show the predicted elaborated change along with a large timing reduction. A shared outer head is therefore useful only when its corresponding dependent arguments align.

The unresolved cases narrow the next experiment: `ImageHomCounit` needs a control that actually changes the raw/wrapper pair at the final equality; the integral case needs an aligned `HasDerivedCategory` instance pair in the same `change` obligation; `RealPurity` needs a valid one-path ring-instance control or an independent comparison of the instance leaf in its enclosing polynomial context. These are open questions, not claims that an abbrev, proof irrelevance, or a particular Kernel cache caused the observed timeouts. The reports separate the observed expression and timing facts from predictions about internal reduction order.
