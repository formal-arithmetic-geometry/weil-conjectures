\chapter{Zeta functions and finite Frobenius point counting}
\label{chap:weil-zeta-point-counting}

This chapter assembles the counting input to the zeta function of a variety
over a finite field.  The starting point is purely combinatorial: a set with a
self-map whose sets of periodic points are finite, the geometric Frobenius
being the case of interest.  Unless stated otherwise, all coefficient, degree,
period, divisor and iteration indices lie in $\mathbf N$.
